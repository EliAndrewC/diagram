- "Ferries and fords (watashi)" / "Moving goods: ... river landings (kashi)": where a road crosses a river vs where freight is loaded onto boats.
- "Highways and what lines them (kaido)" (ways/130's teahouses) / urban-features "Teahouses, eating houses and drinking houses (chaya)": the road's rest-stop teahouses vs a town's eating houses.
