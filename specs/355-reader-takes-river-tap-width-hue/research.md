# Research: feature 355

## R1 - the taper's length and hues

- The length, `~30 ft`, is the GM's filed entry's own figure ("taper its first `~30 ft` from the brook's hue to its own"). It is a
  map drawing convention for where a dug ditch leaves a brook, not a historical measurement; no source is owed for a hue
  transition, and none was searched.
- The hues are the map's own: the brook's bed `#9CB4C8` (`Settlement.stream`) and the supply hue `#6C9CBE`
  (`settlement/water_ways/water.py` `SUPPLY_HUE`).
