## Lead 2. Koyaizu Nobuo, 『近世賃金物価史史料』 (成工社出版部 1998; 改訂版 2006; 『新修』 2018): the book is NOT PUBLIC. ATTESTED ONLY FOR its nail (釘) entries, through a public paper.
- Book: NDL pid 14107062 (1998 ed.) is `ndl_only, inlibrary`. The 2006 and 2018 editions are not digitized.
- Public derivative: 平山育男 (Hirayama Ikuo), 「『近世賃金物価史史料』に見る釘の価格」, 長岡造形大学研究紀要 16 (2019), pp.55-59. Record https://nagaoka-id.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/228; PDF https://nagaoka-id.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/228/files/p55-59.pdf (public).
  - The book's scope (p.55): 「小柳津信郎による『近世賃金物価史史料』は、17世紀末期の元禄期以後における賃金と物価についての史料収集を行った労作である。同書では全国各地で出版された315件に及ぶ市史及び資料集などを渉猟し、金・銀・銭相場、給金に始まり、１年間の食料の見積、食費、鍋・釜など83件について価格を記載するもので、この中に"釘"の項目が存在する。"釘"について小柳は、元禄10（1697）年から文久２（1862）年に及ぶ130件の記事を挙げて」
    (translation) "Koyaizu Nobuo's 『近世賃金物価史史料』 is a laborious collection of sources on wages and prices from the Genroku era (late 17th c.) on. It combs 315 municipal histories and source collections published around the country and gives prices for 83 items, beginning with gold/silver/zeni rates and wages (給金), then a year's food estimate, food costs, pots and kettles, and so on. Among them is an entry for nails, for which Koyaizu lists 130 records from Genroku 10 (1697) to Bunkyu 2 (1862)."
  - The paper's 表1 (p.56) reprints all 130 nail records: era, year, nail size, count, price as in the source, and source. It adds conversions per 100 nails, a column ⑤ 「銀1匁当たり銭[文]」 for each year, and a rice-price index. The table is an image with no text layer; I read it from a 220 dpi render. Rows as printed (番号, 元号, 西暦, 釘の種類, 本数, 価格, 出典):
    「1 元禄10 1697 6寸釘 54本 336文 田原藩日記」 (translation) "No.1, Genroku 10 (1697), 6-sun nails, 54, 336 mon, source 田原藩日記"
    「7 正徳3 1713 6寸釘 100本 10.5匁 豊川市史」
    「63 安永7 1778 大6寸釘 100本 624文 成田市史」
    「64 天明元 1781 6寸釘 28本 84文 猿投神社近世史」
    「66 寛政5 1793 4寸釘 20本 30文 韮山町史」
    「72 文化2 1805 4寸釘 600本 1672文 富津市史」
    「74 文化2 1805 2寸釘 1000本 900文 揖宿史談」
    「75 文化9 1812 5寸釘 200本 3匁3分 藤沢市史」
    (Rows 78-130, up to 1862, are on p.57 and were not transcribed.)
  - Findings (p.56-57): 「呼称長２寸の釘は…16件が掲載されており…物価換算した釘100本当たり価格における平均価格は銀立で0.93匁、銭立で86銭」 (translation) "16 records for 2-sun nails; the mean price per 100, deflated by rice, is 0.93 monme of silver or 86 mon." For 4-sun nails: 「26件…平均価格は銀立で3.60匁、銭立で334銭」 (translation) "26 records; mean 3.60 monme or 334 mon." Note: these means are rice-deflated to 1697 = 1. They are not nominal prices.
- Evidence date: 1697-1862, Edo period, many provinces. The source of each figure is a municipal history (市史) quoting a local document.

## Lead 4. Gamble's own 1943 article and Ting Hsien are NOT PUBLIC.
- (a) Allen, Bassino, Ma, Moll-Murata and van Zanden, LSE Economic History WP 123 (archived: https://www.lse.ac.uk/asset-library/information/wp123.pdf), Appendix I B and Appendix Table I, pp.34-36. "The wage series in Gamble, 'Daily Wages' which spans almost the entire nineteenth century, was derived from detailed account books of a fuel store in rural Beijing. ... We chose the annual average wage series (January through December) ... This wage series in copper cash is in the first column." Caveats as printed: "Gamble remarked that one tiao in that location was equal to 500 copper cash before 1860 and 100 copper cash after 1860"; "as indicated by Gamble on p. 41, workers were also given additional food."
  Rows of "Appendix Table I. The Gamble Rural Beijing Wage Series in Copper Cash and Silver Taels, 1807-1902" (year: copper wages in cash (wen) / copper cash per silver tael / silver wages in taels):
  1807: 81 / 979 / 0.041; 1820: 95 / 1,159 / 0.041; 1830: 96 / 1,329 / 0.036; 1842: 100 / 1,439 / 0.035; 1850: 94 / 1,997 / 0.024; 1856: 110 / 4,970 / 0.011; 1860: 255 / - / -; 1865: 265 / 5,180 / 0.026; 1870: 287 / 5,576 / 0.026; 1880: 410 / 8,510 / 0.024; 1885: 395 / 7,573 / 0.026; 1890: 393 / 7,254 / 0.027; 1894: 443 / 6,722 / 0.033; 1900: 422 / 5,312 / 0.04; 1902: 470 / 6,079 / 0.039.
  (The cash figures before and after 1860 are in different local units of account. The silver column is the authors' derivation: col.1 / (col.2 x 2).)
- (b) Gamble and Burgess, Peking: A Social Survey (1921), public domain. Text: https://archive.org/stream/pekingsocialsurv00gambuoft/pekingsocialsurv00gambuoft_djvu.txt. The table was read from the page image (leaf n468), https://archive.org/download/pekingsocialsurv00gambuoft/page/n468_w1400.jpg. The survey was taken 1918-19.
  - p.196: "Investigation showed only two strikes of this sort in Peking, and both of these were in 1885 ... The Jade Workers asked that they be paid 6 cash instead of 4 for making a hole in the mouth-piece of a pipe. Their demand was refused ..." Also: "the wages of the Jade Workers have risen a full 100 percent in the last 15 years."
  - p.184: "Even in the last twenty-five or thirty years the increase is only from 60 to 150 percent." Footnote: "See Appendix, schedule of Gild Wages."
  - p.431, "GILD WAGES / The Comparison of Present Day Wages with Those of the Past". Columns: NAME, YEARS AGO, PAST WAGES, PRESENT WAGES, PERCENT INCREASE. Rows as printed:
    Barbers 50, $3.00, $5.00-10.00, 65-230 | Bone and horn 25, 4.00-12.00, 6.00-18.00, 50 | Butchers, pig 30, 2.00, 3.00-4.50, 50-125 | Butchers, sheep Olden Time, 2.50, 3.50-6.00, 40-140 | Carpet 3, 7.00-20.00, 4.50, (1)35-75 [note 1 = Decrease] | Coal Olden Time, 2.00-3.50, 3.00-4.00, 15-50 | Confectioners 30, 2.00-3.00, 3.50, 18-75 | Cooks 25, 35c. a day, 60c. a day, 70 | Drugs 30, 3 Tls., 4-6 Tls., 35-100 | Dyeing 30, 2.50, 4.50, 80 | Fertilizer 50, 3.00-3.50, 6.00, 70-100 | Gold foil 30, Piece Work, Piece Work, 40 | Hat 30, 3.00, 4.00-5.00, 33-66 | Incense and cosmetic 30, 1.20, 2.50, 110 | Jade 15, Piece Work, 72c.-2.00) a / 4.00-5.00) day, 100 / 100 | Paper-hangers No Report, No Report, 57c.-87c. a day | Painters 25, 40c. a day, 65c. a day, 60 | Pawn brokers Olden Time, 4 Tls., 4-7 Tls., 0-75 | Shoemakers 30, 2.00-3.00, 3.50-5.00, 70 | Smelters 25, 3-8 Tls., 5-15 Tls., 65-85 | Soap 30, 4.15, 6.00, 45 | Tailors 25, 60c. piece wk., 1.00 piece wk., 65 | Undertakers 30, 3.00-4.00, 3.00-6.00, 0-50 | Water-carriers 30, 2.25, 3.00-4.00, 35-75.
