published standard rather than a deviation, a one-line change. The GM, 2026-09-29:

> Yes, I think I would like to follow the published standard rather than deviate, so let's go ahead and adopt that, especially since you say it would be a one-line change. And then the record can still call it a standard instead of a deviation. Thanks.
