# Rubric - task R: the Ubame servants' quarters (one dormitory, or a door a household?)

Frozen before the first run (spec FR-008, SC-002). Each criterion is scored 0-4 on its anchors, for A and for B separately;
the total is the weighted sum (maximum 4 x 10 = 40). **Pass line: a total of 24 or more AND at least 2 on criteria 1 and 3.**

| # | criterion | weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The answer, and whether the sources read support it | 3 |
| 2 | Breadth and honesty of the search | 2 |
| 3 | Citation correctness | 2 |
| 4 | What the map should draw | 1 |
| 5 | Clarity for the casual reader | 2 |

## 1. The answer, and whether the sources read support it (weight 3)

- **4** - A clear answer to the question as asked (one door per household / one dormitory / both forms as a knob / the record
  silent), every sentence of it resting on a quoted passage that says it, and the period and place of each source stated.
- **3** - A clear answer, well supported, with one claim resting on inference that is labeled as inference.
- **2** - An answer, but part of it rests on a source that does not quite say it, or on a later period stated as if it were earlier.
- **1** - An answer mostly by inference, or one that misstates what a source says.
- **0** - No answer, or an answer the cited passages contradict.

## 2. Breadth and honesty of the search (weight 2)

- **4** - Japanese AND Chinese sources searched (and English), several kinds (surviving buildings or plans, museum or government
  pages, scholarship), the searches listed with terms and date; where nothing was found it says so as an absence note.
- **3** - Two languages and more than one kind of source; the search recorded.
- **2** - One language, or one kind of source; the search partly recorded.
- **1** - A thin search, not recorded.
- **0** - No evidence of a search beyond what the record already held.

## 3. Citation correctness (weight 2)

Judged from the entry and its notes as written, and from the check report where the output includes it.

- **4** - Every footnote quotes its passage verbatim (in English translation marked as one, the original after), from a page the
  reader can open; every assertion that needs a footnote has one; no source is cited that the entry could not read.
- **3** - One minor flaw (a translation mark missing, a quote trimmed without an ellipsis).
- **2** - A note that does not quite support its assertion, or one assertion left without a footnote.
- **1** - Several such flaws.
- **0** - A citation that misrepresents its source, or quotes not taken from the page.

## 4. What the map should draw (weight 1)

- **4** - Says plainly what the Ubame sheet should draw (a door per bay, one door, or a knob between them), with the drawing
  decision labeled in the project's four classes.
- **2** - The consequence for the map is implied but not stated, or stated without its class.
- **0** - Nothing about the map.

## 5. Clarity for the casual reader (weight 2)

- **4** - The heading is the question a reader would ask; the entry opens with the answer in plain words; terms are glossed;
  short; nothing about what the entry used to say or how the session worked.
- **3** - Clear, with one lapse (an unglossed term, a long sentence of method in the reader's prose).
- **2** - Readable but reads as a research log rather than an answer.
- **1** - Hard to follow.
- **0** - Unreadable, or mostly process.
