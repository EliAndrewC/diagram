## What the surveys measure, by type

**Groves per village**: about two (Fujian, province-wide, reported by Chen et al. 2020 from Xu et al. 2012; two parcels each in two Buyi villages, Yuan and Liu 2009).

**The back grove (the grove behind the village)** - the one measured distribution: Hong Kong's AFCD survey, 115 woods, GIS-drawn boundaries (NCSC 9/06, 2006), all of the behind-the-village crescent form. The record's own arithmetic over its Annex: median 0.99 ha, mean 1.10 ha, quartiles 0.47-1.31 ha, range 0.06-5.32 ha; 58 of 115 under 1 ha, 45 between 1 and 2 ha, 12 at 2 ha or more, 5 at 3 ha or more. Fujian's lineage villages are the tail: forests listed as over 3 and over 6 ha for two Youxi County villages and Lingtou's over 20 ha in total (Chen 2020), a 22 ha houlongshan (Coggins and Minor 2018), 133 ha at Letu (Yuan and Liu 2009).

**The water-mouth grove**: NO Chinese measurement readable. Analogue: the Korean village-entrance grove (동구숲, the sugumagi that blocks the water mouth) averages 0.71 ha over 42 surveyed and is the commonest type (Park et al. 2013, 100 groves); a 24-grove Namwon sample averages 0.22 ha, range 0.025-0.63 ha (Kweon and Youn 2021).

**Trees**: no Chinese grove count readable. Density 3,403 plants/ha in well-protected Pearl-delta patches (Hu 2011 abstract); basal area 30-52 m²/ha (Ma 2017); Korean groves "less than 10 to more than 100" trees each (Kweon and Youn).
