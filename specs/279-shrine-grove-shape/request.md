# Feature 279 - the shape of a village shrine's grove - the GM's request

The GM, 2026-09-28, on the Hoshigaoka village map:

> Now, I do have a question about what is essentially a rectangle of trees surrounding this country shrine. I understand why the farmhouses do not have trees surrounding them on all sides because that shade would be a problem for their gardens and threshing yards and such. But it looks very strange when I look at the village map to see just a rectangle of trees. like was that a deliberate decision as a result of research as in historical people made sure that the trees were cut down and then the ones that were left formed what is basically a perfect rectangle that feels unlikely For that matter, was it generally the case that country shrines were surrounded by trees on all sides? Like, was that a research finding that we had? I'm just trying to figure out what I'm looking at. And why it was rendered that way.

The session's answer (summarized): the rectangle is not a research finding. Research 124 found the precinct's
size (the registers' band) and that buildings cover a fortieth to a seventh of it; that the rest is wood is a
recorded guess, and the GM ruled (2026-09-27) to draw a grove. No source read gives a grove's outline or says
a village shrine was wooded on every side. The rectangle is the frame the crowns were thrown into - the 137 by
215 ft precinct box from the well to the outermost arch - and the record never labeled its shape.

The session proposed: (1) a research pass on the shape of village shrine woods - irregular remnant stands,
backed against a slope with a more open front, or wooded all round - a knob where the sources support more
than one form; (2) redraw the outline from it, or at least label it a guess.

The GM, 2026-09-28:

> Yes, please go with your suggestion, doing the research pass and then recording your findings and then making updates. I understand that this does involve editing the hand-drawn map, but that's fine because we are correcting something which looks very visually wrong. And so that is the kind of thing that is worth fixing even in advance of switching over to scripted map generation for villages. it is especially important to know what tunable knobs we will have for villages when we eventually go to start scripting them. Thanks.
