I worked through all 44 claim keys. The record already answered or had searched most of them. Chinese county-yamen sources answer 5 of the magistracy claims outright, and 2 more only in part. Three findings contradict figures or rules now in the docs:

- **The armory:** Pingyao's yamen kept one as routine, not only in bandit country.
- **The prisoners' gate:** prisoners went through a side door of the inner ceremonial gate, not a separate outer service gate.
- **The arch:** the one village arch measured is about 8 ft between its posts, against the doc's 10.3 ft clear.

## Sources that bear

**A. Pingyao county yamen, archived Baidu Baike copy** (`/diagram/.specify/source-archive/42/429e9a2d5a67/`, baike.baidu.com/item/平遥县衙/2409876). Evidence: Ming 1368 and 1619, and Qing. It is a modern description of the historic layout.

- 「重修大堂五间，其中东梢间为钱粮库，西梢间为武备库……在兵、刑、工房后修有公廨房，以北修县仓。」 (translation) "The great hall was rebuilt at five bays, its east end bay the money-and-grain store, its west end bay the armory … behind the war, justice and works offices, official quarters were built, and north of them the county granary." (1619)
- 「西部有申明亭，往北有重狱、女狱、轻狱……督捕厅和"马号……"」 (translation) "In the west part is the Shenming pavilion, and further north the heavy prison, the women's prison, the light prison … the arrest office and the 'stables …'" The page's text is garbled from 马号 on.

**B. Pingyao, 163.com article** (https://www.163.com/dy/article/DD102NPC0524CT3H.html). Same evidence, Ming and Qing.

**C. Neixiang county yamen, archived WeChat Baike copy** (`/diagram/.specify/source-archive/bb/bbe0f9f80ace/`, baike.weixin.qq.com/v195939.htm). Evidence: Qing.

- 「大堂……堂前有宽阔的月台」 (translation) "The great hall … before it is a broad terrace."

**D. Neixiang, Henan Daily via China Daily, 2021** (https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202103/12/WS604b0287a3101e7ce9743b4c.html). Evidence: buildings of the Qing Guangxu reign.

- 「进大门向北，依次是两个长方形院落，第一个院落东西侧都设置了功能性建筑，西侧设寅宾馆、膳馆、监狱，东侧设衙神庙、土地祠、三班院。」 (translation) "North of the main gate come two oblong courts. The first has working buildings on both sides: on the west the guest lodge, the refectory and the prison; on the east the yamen god's temple, the earth-god shrine and the runners' quarters."

**F. Sayama jin'ya, in the record** (0101 notes, key `sayama-jinya-uematsu`). Evidence: Edo, as the page describes it; I did not check the date further.

**NOT ATTESTED, the evidence pointing the other way:**
