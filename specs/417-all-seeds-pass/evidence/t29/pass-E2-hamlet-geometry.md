## Reading

**Seating (claims 1-4): ATTESTED ONLY FOR the qualitative split between grown and planned settlements.**
- A clustered settlement that grew by itself sat irregularly, in a lump or a jumble: kotobank, ja.wikipedia (Japan), ARIC (Nara-Heian Japan), and Smith (Qing North China, "just growed", crowded).
- Regular ranks are attested only where the settlement was planned: the Guangfu comb layout from the mid-Ming on (houses in ranks facing one way behind a front lane and a pond), and 0033's planned rows. Kotobank states the rule outright: 「計画的な開発によるものは規則的となる」. That backs 0081's back-lane/side-lane reading, now marked a GUESS, and claim 4's "jitter on an alleys hamlet only".
- The lean toward the field: houses facing and keeping to the water are attested in pass B (Takamine, Miyagi, before 1912, with the grant going back to 1640) and in 0031 (the levee beside the paddy). There is no lean factor.
- Every magnitude (0.1 pitch, 0.75, 1.45 pitches, 22%/16%, half of 0.25 pitch, the along-the-field growth) is a tolerance no source speaks to.

**Lanes (claims 5-6): ATTESTED that lanes were crooked and wound round what stood; NOT ATTESTED that corners were rounded.**
- Smith: village streets were never straight and took "sudden and inexplicable turns". Field roads followed each owner's land edge and "wind around", making long detours.
- Tonami's pre-consolidation roads were winding (modern evidence, scattered form).
- Against these, Guangfu lanes ran straight and at right angles.
- **Contradiction to flag:** Smith's "sudden" turns lean against claim 5's picture of corners eased up to 24 ft off the apex. That easing is a drawing smoothing, not something the world shows.
- No source gives how far a path bowed round an obstacle. Smith's "great distance to reach a place not far off" concerns field roads, so it applies to claim 6 only by analogy. 10-40 ft is unsourced.

**Bamboo slide (claim 7): SILENT.** 0075 has the sides bamboo took and one Musashino farm's madake "all along its south side". If anything, a strip ran the whole side, so where a 22 ft strip sits along that side is a drawing choice. Nothing new was found.
