## The session's proposal (context, not the GM's words)

The bare strip is a leftover: when reeds were thrown blade by blade each tuft was kept a blade's length plus 2 ft off every
watercourse so no blade crossed the water - about 9 ft bare each side - and the tiles kept the rule though a tile needs none.
The record already says reeds grow right at a ditch's edge. Proposed: (1) the reed tile runs to the water's drawn edge (the
stream draws over the marsh, so the water stays clear); (2) a narrow band, about 6 ft each side, of a tighter, slightly darker
reed tile lines the bank, framing the stream; (3) only if the stream still gets lost, a thin dark outline on it, judged by eye
after (2). The same for the irrigation ditches where they cross a marsh; nothing changes in the scrub, where grass already runs
to a stream's bank. Built as a spec-kit feature and landed.
