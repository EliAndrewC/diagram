# Feature 413: a tooltip converting every historical amount of money in the record (diagram#14)

**Feature Branch**: none (main, in a session clone)

**Created**: 2026-10-24

**Status**: Draft

**Affects**: research record, tooling

**Approved**: the GM, 2026-10-24, "anything that gets filed, go ahead and work it"

The GM asked for what gm-assistant files on this repository to be worked (his words, quoted above); a request gm-assistant
opens for him is approved by his asking (GM 2026-10-18: "you should treat features that I have asked to be filed as automatic
approval, and therefore you should not ask in the future"). The request itself quotes the GM, 2026-10-24: "I think that I am
going to want a tooltip where we don't just explain that this was the copper coin, but we translate the amount of money based
on different ways of understanding it."

**Input**: `request.md` (issue #14 and its thread, verbatim)

## Why

The record states prices in the money of their era (20 mon, 3 monme, 15-20 ryo, 0.04 tael). A reader who hovers "mon" gets
the glossary's definition of the coin, not what 20 of them meant. gm-assistant now publishes, per era and per coin, what ONE
unit was worth in grams of silver, in goods dollars (what it bought), in wage dollars (the same share of a laborer's day) and
in Rokugani sen (`webapp/l7r/money/data/conversions.json`, generated by its `python3 -m l7r.money export`). This feature
carries that table into the record and shows the conversion of each marked amount on hover.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - hovering an amount shows what it was worth (Priority: P1)

A reader of a question's page hovers "20 mon" in a sentence about Kyoto in 1750 and sees, in the record's usual hover box:
about $4.07 in goods, $41.71 in wages, 4.2 sen in Rokugan; the era the rates are from; how much of the basket that era
prices; and its confidence line.

**Independent test**: build the record; open a question with a marked amount; the box shows those figures for the worked
example of the issue (Kyoto 1745-54, 20 mon = 0.94 g of silver = about $4.07 goods, $41.71 wages, 4.2 sen).

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. A marked amount whose era has a row shows goods dollars, wage dollars and Rokugani coin, each the per-unit figure times the
   amount; a range ("15-20 ryo") shows both ends.
2. Rokugani coin follows gm-assistant's `conversions.rokugani()` (in `conversions.py`; the JSON has no Rokugani display format - its `_format` key describes the fields): under
   10 sen, a tenth of a sen ("4.2 sen"); otherwise the two largest coins it needs, rounded, with "about" when rounded (its
   `coins.everyday()`), 10 sen a zeni, 10 zeni a bu, 10 bu a koku.
3. When the amount was paid in another place or time than its era row (marked so by the author), the box says so in one line,
   "at Kyoto 1745-54 prices" (the asker, #14, 2026-10-24).
4. An amount not marked, or marked with an era that has no row yet, keeps today's glossary tooltip only.
5. The glossary tooltip on the coin's word inside a marked amount is not lost: the box shows the conversion and the coin's
   definition under it.

### User Story 2 - the author marks the era; the build refuses a bad mark (Priority: P1)

The author of a question marks an amount with the era whose rates convert it. Choosing the era is the author's judgment, never
the build's: there is no automatic nearest-era fallback (the asker, #14: "no automatic fallback in the build. Choosing the era is
a judgment the author makes while marking the amount, not something the build infers").

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. `make record CHECK=1` refuses, naming the fragment and the amount: an era id the vendored table does not have and that is not
   declared as awaiting (FR-008); a coin the era's row has no unit for; an amount that is not a number or a range of two numbers; a marked
   amount with no era on it or on an ancestor; a `data-paid` that names no year.
2. A borrowed era is marked with `data-paid` and its tooltip says so; the build refuses no distance in years. The asker's first
   default ("within about 25 years") was withdrawn: the GM, through the asker on #14 (2026-10-24), "had no basis, and I withdraw
   it ... no numeric threshold in the record checks yet"; the closeness rule is diagram#15's research. The money system is held
   by the coin being one of the era's units.

### User Story 3 - the table stays current (Priority: P2)

A make target refreshes diagram's copy of the table from gm-assistant; a check says when the copy is stale.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. `make money-conversions` copies `webapp/l7r/money/data/conversions.json` from gm-assistant's main checkout
   (`/host-l7r-repo/gm-assistant`, read only) into `research/assets/money-conversions.json`; `CHECK=1` writes nothing and exits 1
   while the copy differs, naming the command. No network at build or gate time.
2. When the copy changes (a new era lands), the amounts already marked with that era light up with no edit to the questions.

### User Story 4 - the record's existing amounts are marked (Priority: P2)

Every amount already in the record - every fragment under `research/questions/`: questions, drawing pages and their notes,
not only the money questions (the issue: "each money amount marked with its era") - whose place and years fall within an era
that has a row, today `kyoto-1745` and `tokyo-1882`, is marked in this feature where its author judges the era close enough (the 1897 Meiji shrine
rules' yen in 0216 are one). Amounts in eras without a row are left for feature 412, which defines those eras and marks their
amounts, record-wide, as it lands them.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. Every amount in any fragment in a coin of a built era's row is decided: marked with the era its author judges close enough,
   or passed over in `backfill.md` with its reason; where the reason is that no built era is close enough, it states the amount's
   date (for a `*` line, the years its block names) and that the author judged no built era close enough to borrow, pending
   diagram#15's measured rule (a script sweeps every fragment and lists the candidates).
2. Marking changes no visible words, so it owes no record check (`scripts/record/record_units.py`: words are text with markup
   normalized away); `make record-owed` names nothing for these edits.

### Edge cases

- A rate ("60 monme per 10 da") converts the money part only: the amount marked is "60 monme".
- An amount inside a quotation is marked the same way; the quotation's words are unchanged.
- An amount in a table cell: the column may declare the coin and era for its cells (a mark on the cell).
- A pre-1882 Tokyo amount (Edo) is not marked with `tokyo-1882`: Edo's mon is not the yen's money system.
- An amount in copper cash or taels has no era row yet (China): left unmarked until 412 lands one.

## Requirements

- **FR-001**: Diagram vendors gm-assistant's conversion table as `research/assets/money-conversions.json`, refreshed by
  `make money-conversions` from gm-assistant's main checkout; `CHECK=1` reports a stale copy without writing.
- **FR-002**: An amount is marked `<span class="money" data-amount="<n or n-m>" data-coin="<unit>">...</span>`, its era given by
  `data-era="<era id>"` on the span or on its nearest ancestor that carries one; a borrowed era also carries
  `data-paid="<place> <year or years>"`.
- **FR-003**: The site build carries the table as a derived script beside the glossary's, loaded by every page, and refuses
  every bad mark of US2 in one run, naming each.
- **FR-004**: The hover box shows, per marked amount with a row: goods dollars, wage dollars and Rokugani coin (gm-assistant's
  format), the era's place and years, its basket coverage and goods left out, and its confidence; a borrowed era's line "at
  <place> <years> prices"; the coin's glossary definition beneath.
- **FR-005**: An amount with no row shows the glossary tooltip only; nothing falls back to another era.
- **FR-006**: Every amount in the record's fragments in the two eras with rows is marked (US4), changing no visible words.
- **FR-008**: An era id gm-assistant has not built yet may be marked only if it is listed, with its place and years, in
  `research/assets/money-eras-awaiting.json`; this feature creates the list (empty), feature 412 adds each era it defines and
  removes it once the vendored table carries its row.
- **FR-007**: The reply on #14 says how amounts are marked, how the copy is refreshed, and how to verify it; gm-assistant
  verifies and closes the issue.

## Success Criteria

- **SC-001** (FR-002, FR-003, FR-005, FR-008): a test builds a fragment with a good mark, each kind of bad mark of US2, an
  unmarked amount and one marked with an era the awaiting list holds; the build refuses exactly the bad ones, naming each.
- **SC-002** (FR-004): a test computes the box's figures for 20 mon at `kyoto-1745` and gets the issue's worked example (about

- **SC-004** (FR-006): the candidate script, sweeping every fragment under `research/questions/`, lists no unmarked amount in the two eras that the task did not pass over with a
  reason; `make record-owed` names nothing for the marking.
- **SC-005** (FR-007): the reply's URL on #14 is in the task's verify line.

## Assumptions

- The vendored copy is committed, so the record builds offline; gm-assistant's main checkout is read, never written, and no git
  runs against it.
- The figures are computed in the page's script from the per-unit row, as the issue says ("Multiply by the amount shown").
- The site build lives in the engine (`l7r/diagram/interactive/record/`), so this lands on the gated route.
