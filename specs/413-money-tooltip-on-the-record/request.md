**The data**: gm-assistant publishes the conversion table at `webapp/l7r/money/data/conversions.json` on its `main`
(https://github.com/EliAndrewC/gm-assistant/blob/main/webapp/l7r/money/data/conversions.json). It is generated by

as the research of the companion issue lands) and per coin (`mon`, `monme`, `kanmon`, `ryo`; `yen`, `sen`, `rin`), it
gives per ONE unit: `silver_g`, `goods_dollars`, `wage_dollars` and `rokugan_sen`, plus the era's `basket_coverage`
("the whole basket", "9 of 12 goods", "rice alone"), `goods_left_out` and `confidence`. Multiply by the amount shown.

**What it needs from the record**: each money amount marked with its era (the place and decade it was paid in), so the
build can pick the row; an amount whose era has no row shows only today's glossary tooltip. Rokugani coin: 10 sen a
zeni, 10 zeni a bu, 10 bu a koku; under 10 sen, a tenth of a sen (gm-assistant's `conversions.rokugani` is the format).
Terms: "goods dollars" (what it bought) and "wage dollars" (the same share of a laborer's day, at a US unskilled day),
the GM's names as of 2026-10-24.

**Worked example** (Kyoto 1745-54 row, standard basket, 2026-10 prices): 20 mon = 0.94 g of silver = about $4.07 in
goods, $41.71 in wages, 4.2 sen in Rokugan.

How diagram vendors the file (a copy refreshed by a make target, or a fetch at build time) and how amounts are marked are
diagram's to decide.
