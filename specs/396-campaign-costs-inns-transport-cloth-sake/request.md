### 2. Hiring transport

- **Question**: what were the official post-station rates (*dachin*) per ri for a pack horse and for porters, and what
  did a hired horse with a driver, or a cart, cost per day? Did the hirer pay for the return?
- **Figure needed**: rate per ri and per day, with the wage of the same period.
- **Beside it**: ledger guess: horse, cart and driver 35-55 sen a day, return days paid.

### 3. Cloth and kimono

- **Question**: what did a *tan* of cotton cloth and of plain silk (and pongee, *tsumugi*) cost, how long was a *tan*,
  and what did a new plain kimono, a used one, and the sewing of one cost? Silk bedding if found.
- **Figure needed**: price per *tan* and per garment; the *tan*'s length; silk-to-cotton ratio.
- **Beside it**: the PPP basket's cotton at 5.83 g silver a meter (Bassino & Ma, Kyoto 1745-54); ledger guesses: cotton
  kimono length 312 sen (12 m), silk 4-10x, a plain cotton kimono 3.5-4.5 bu, silk 1.3-3.3 koku.

### 4. Sake and drink

- **Question**: what did sake cost by the *go* or *sho* from a brewer or shop, and by the cup or flask in a tavern
  (*izakaya*, teahouse)? What was the markup?
- **Figure needed**: retail and tavern price per *go*, with the wage of the same period.
- **Beside it**: ledger guesses: retail 1-2 sen a cup (one *go*), tavern 2-4 sen; a night for six 4-8 zeni. Canon: sake
  brewing is licensed and taxed (`setting/l7r.md` "The Ministry of Revenue").
