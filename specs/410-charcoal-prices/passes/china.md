## 1. Gao Shouxian 高寿仙 2022, 故宫学刊 (record key gao-shouxian-jiuqing-wuliao; archived)

https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/02/10/u63e60647c73ae.pdf - a modern secondary work tabulating Beijing's official
assessments of 1615 (《工部厂库须知》) and 1736 (《九卿议定物料价值》); prices "均指白银，单位为两".

- Basis: 「这份物料价值清单系以市场价格为基础，经过工部和九卿反复斟酌确定的，应当能够大致反映当时北京市场的价格水平」 (Gao's judgment).
- Table 2, Beijing 1736: 「炭 炭每百斤0.225；黑炭每百斤0.5/0.6；白炭每百斤1.1；红螺炭每百斤1.06 / 头号炭饼（重12两）每个0.012……」;
  firewood 0.162/0.3 per 100 jin; coal 0.26/0.27; 「黑炭筐（装60斤）每个0.0195」.
- Table 22, 1615 // 1736: 「炭每百斤0.12-0.75//0.225；白炭每百斤0.49-1.113//1.1」 (only coal bracketed as from 宛署雜記).
- Table 23 wages, Beijing 1736: craftsmen 154 cash, 壮夫 75 cash; Gao at 800 cash a tael: 0.1925 and 0.09375 tael.
  Ming 1615 wages 0.03-0.07 tael a day.
- Reading: ATTESTED ONLY FOR official assessed prices. White 1.1 against black 0.5/0.6 per 100 jin, Beijing 1736: about 2:1
  (arithmetic). A laborer's day buys about 16-19 jin of black, 8.5 of white (arithmetic).
