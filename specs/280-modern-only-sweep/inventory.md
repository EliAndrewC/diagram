# Inventory - feature 280, the modern-only sweep

Built from seven Opus audits run on 2026-09-28 (`/tmp/l7r-check/280-audit/out-1` to `out-7`). They read a copy of
the record taken from feature 269's clone, which is main plus 269's unlanded research, together with the engine,
the kinds, the knobs and the pool, legacy and magistracy maps on main. Auditors 1 to 6 went page by page and put
every section in one of four classes: candidate, clean, excluded (already ruled or handled by another feature) or
passed over (named only because a rule required it, then judged not modern). Auditor 5 also read the Mode A compound
kinds and plans, and auditor 7 read every kind, knob form and map feature. Each candidate row became one item. A kind
whose Entry section is itself a page candidate is folded into that section's item. Weak rows are kept, since a weak
candidate costs one search. Groups are research sessions of at most four questions (feature 274's cap). A question is
each distinct section a group's items name, plus one for each item that names none.

The letter at the end of each item is its strength:
- S (strong): the record itself dates the drawn form as modern, or its only sources are modern.
- M (mixed): a modern figure stands beside period evidence, or the modern part is a degree.
- L (weak): likely to clear on one search.

Map shorthand: "scripted hamlets" means the five pool hamlets (inashiro, kashikawa, kuwabata, mizuguchi, sawada).
"The six legacy towns and cities" means hirameki, hoshizora, ubame, minami, nagahara and tango. "The magistracy
sheets" means county-magistracy-example, hayakawa, ochiba, ochiba-roundtrip-test and ubame.
