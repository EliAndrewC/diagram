# The GM's rulings of 2026-09-29 on the four forms 280 left waiting (verbatim)

The four were forms the GM had ruled in knowingly before the 2026-09-28 rule against anything attested only in modern
times. Recorded in `outcomes.md` (M50, M52, M53, M64), `research/vegetation/152`, `research/archetypes/020`,
`research/religion-and-death/090` and `710`, and the frozen maps' owed lists in `future-work/`. The engine is unchanged:
it already draws all four.

**M50, the bamboo map symbol - kept, a map drawing convention.**

> Yes, my ruling is that the bamboo symbol is fine because it is a glyph rendering convention and represents a thing which is real on the map. I don't really see why it would be an issue.

**M52 and M53, the lotus overlay - kept.** The record (archetypes/600) has lotus fields and lotus-growing districts before
modern times (Kadoma and Iwakuni in the Edo period, Wu county in the Ming and Qing), so both of the GM's conditions are
met; the band's drawn size is a legibility convention.

> I do not want things on our maps which did not exist in the pre-modern period. So if the lotus was not present at all in the pre-modern era, then we should not have it. On the other hand, if it was there, but we have had to make it slightly bigger in order to make it legible on the map, then that is fine as a glyph rendering convention. and you mention villages marked as lotus growing. So if that was a thing in pre-modern times, then we should keep it. But if that is only a modern thing, then we should get rid of it.

**M64, the avenue of arches at any shrine - kept, a deliberate deviation.** Feature 280 had written in religion-and-death
710 that an ordinary shrine gets a single arch; the engine never changed (it rolls one, three or seven arches by tier,
`TORII_WEIGHTS`, GM 2026-07-21), and 710 and 090 now say what the maps draw.

> With regards to Tori arches, my earlier ruling stands. They are something that exists in this setting. Even though they were not present at common local shrines in the pre-modern era in real life. That's fine. This setting is a mishmash of different periods, and Tory arches were a thing that existed historically, and so we are simply deploying them in a way which is true to this setting as a documented deviation from the historical norm.
