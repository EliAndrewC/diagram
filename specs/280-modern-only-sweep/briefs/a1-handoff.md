## Left open

- Whether the lotus overlay becomes a knob (ordinary village at the low end / lotus-district village at the upper part) is the GM's call: it narrows the GM's own liberty of 2026-07-19, so the record states the finding and raises it without a rule.
- The Iwakuni pages disagree on who began it (the ALIC report: the lord's order; the city tourism page: a Muronoki farmer), and ja.wikipedia dates the Bitchu variety to early Meiji while the ALIC report has Bitchu seed lotus in 1796; the record cites only what does not rest on either point.
- Seed lotus (Hunan) is a different crop from the root lotus the maps draw; it is cited only as rice paddies given over to lotus before 1912.
- Qimin Yaoshu on ctext.org refused the fetch (anti-scraping page); Chinese Wikisource was used instead. Kanazawa's Kaga-lotus PDF had no text layer and the Aichi Tatsuta page was 404; neither is cited.
