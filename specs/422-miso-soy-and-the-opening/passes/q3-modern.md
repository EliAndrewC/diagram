## Reading
**ATTESTED** for both.

**(a) Miso.**
- The cheapest plain household pack is Shirakiku miso on Amazon:
  - **$11.83 a kg** for the red 2.2 lb bag ($11.80).
  - **$10.64 a kg** for the white two-pack of 2.2 lb bags ($21.28).
- Walmart, the retailer 0299 used, has no own-brand miso and nothing near household size with a price. Its cheapest paste it sells itself is Hikari Aged Red, 10.5 oz for $4.98, **$16.73 a kg**.
- I suggest $10.64–11.83 a kg for the household size, with Walmart's $16.73 for a small tub.

**(b) Naturally brewed soy sauce.**
- Walmart's own **Great Value Naturally Brewed Soy Sauce**, 15 fl oz for $1.73, is **$3.90 a liter**. It is the cheapest brewed soy sauce found, but only in a 15 fl oz bottle.
- The nearest household size is the **Kikkoman 1 gallon at $26.77, $7.07 a liter**, whose printed name does not say "naturally brewed". Kikkoman 40 oz is $6.86 a liter.
- Kroger's own basic soy sauce is a hydrolyzed (non-brewed) product, per Tasting Table, but its price is unread.
