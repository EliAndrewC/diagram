## 1. First records and objects

- **Hou Hanshu 86**, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷86, on the Ailao of the southwest (Han period).
  - 「知染采文繡，罽毲帛疊，蘭干細布，織成文章如綾錦。」 "They know dyeing and embroidery; wool rugs, 帛疊 cloth, lan'gan fine cloth, woven with patterns like damask." (translation)
  - The text does not say cotton; Zurndorfer reads it so. Qu Dajun glosses 蘭幹 as ramie (卷15, below).
- **Hou Hanshu 86**, on Hainan, end of Han Wudi.
  - 「武帝末，珠崖太守會稽孫幸調廣幅布獻之，蠻不堪役，遂攻郡殺幸。」 "At the end of Wudi's reign the Zhuya governor Sun Xing levied broad-width cloth to present to the throne; the Man could not bear the service, attacked the commandery and killed him." (translation)
  - The text says only 布.
- **Zurndorfer, Helsinki [DRAFT]**, https://www.lse.ac.uk/Economic-History/Assets/Documents/Research/GEHN/Helsinki/HELSINKIZurndorfer.pdf.
  - "around 200 BCE, official documents recorded the existence of cotton cloth in what is now Yunnan. Recent archaeological research on mummies found in the Tarim Basin has also uncovered cotton textiles, dating from the first millennium BCE, whose origin may be traced to India"
- **Chen Guodong, 故宮學術季刊 34:1**, https://www.npm.edu.tw/NewChineseArtDownload.ashx?bid=3896 (modern, on Han finds).
  - 「目前中國境內已知最早的棉織物於 1959 年出土於新疆民豐」 "The earliest cotton fabric known so far within China was excavated in 1959 at Minfeng, Xinjiang" (translation)
- **Liang shu 54**, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/梁書/卷54, on Gaochang/Turfan (6th c.).
  - 「多草木，草實如繭，繭中絲如細纑，名爲白疊子，國人多取織以爲布。」 "Many plants; a plant's fruit is like a cocoon, and the floss within like fine thread; it is called baidie seed, and the people mostly take it and weave it into cloth." (translation)
- **Pelliot, Notes on Marco Polo I, p.507**, https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/toyobunko/III-2-F-c-104/V-1/page/0507.html.en.
  - "Even during the T'ang dynasty, we have no texts referring to cotton cultivation in China proper."
  - On the Turfan question: "no cotton cultivation can be traced which might have passed from the Turfan region into Kan-su, Shan-hsi, or Shàn-hsi."
  - In 736, Chang Shou-chieh: "Po-tieh is woven from mu-mien (cotton), which does not exist in China."

## 2. Import and tribute

- **Qiu Jun, 大學衍義補 22** (1480s), https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/大學衍義補_(四庫全書本)/卷022.
  - 「蓋自古中國所以為衣者絲麻葛褐四者而已漢唐之世逺夷雖以木綿入貢中國未有其種民未以為服官未以為調宋元之間始傳其種入中國闗陜閩廣首得其利」
  - "From antiquity China clothed itself with silk, hemp, kudzu and coarse wool only. In Han and Tang the distant barbarians offered cotton as tribute, but China did not have the seed, the people did not wear it, and the officials did not levy it. Between Song and Yuan the seed first came into China, and Guanzhong-Shaanxi, Fujian and Guangdong were first to profit." (translation)
- **Chen Guodong** (above).
  - 「從東漢到南宋，一千多年間，印度棉布偶爾還是以貢品的名目寫入中國的歷史。不過，至晚在南宋時，中國也已經有規模地自印度進口棉布」 "From Eastern Han to Southern Song, for over a thousand years, Indian cotton cloth occasionally entered Chinese history under the name of tribute; but by Southern Song at latest, China was also importing cotton cloth from India on a considerable scale." (translation)
- **Liang shu 54** (6th c., southern-seas embassies).
  - 「普通三年，其王頻伽復遣使珠貝智貢白鸚鵡、青蟲、兜鍪、琉璃器、吉貝、螺杯、雜香、藥等數十種。」 "In Putong 3 [522] its king Pinjia again sent the envoy Zhubeizhi with tribute of white parrots, green insects, helmets, glass vessels, jibei, conch cups, aromatics, drugs and others, several tens of kinds." (translation)
- **Zizhi tongjian 159**, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/資治通鑑/卷159, Liang Wudi, under the year 545.
  - 「身衣布衣，木綿皁帳」 "He wore cloth garments and used a black cotton canopy." (translation)
  - This is austerity at court. Pelliot (p.521) cites this "black tent of cotton" as first-half 6th century.

## 3a. Frontier cultivation

- **Yunnan, Pelliot p.511**, https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/toyobunko/III-2-F-c-104/V-1/page/0511.html.en. "cotton cultivation was thus still restricted to Yün-nan and Tongking, or at least its beginnings in other regions remained unnoticed" (Tang).
- **Northwest, Pelliot p.511** (same page). "The cotton cultivation attested in the first half of the 6 th cent. in the region of Turfan by the Liang shu has hitherto found no counterpart in any other oasis of Chinese Turkestan."
- **Northwest, Pelliot p.507.** Tang tribute of "tieh cloth" is listed only for Hsi-chou (Turfan), statutes of 713-741.
- **Hainan and Guangdong, 嶺外代答 卷6** (Zhou Qufei, 1178), https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/嶺外代答_(四庫全書本)/卷06.
  - 「雷化廉州及南海黎峒富有以代絲紵」 "In Lei, Hua and Lian prefectures and the Li districts of Hainan, [it is] plentiful, and used in place of silk and ramie." (translation) Pelliot p.513 reads 富有 as "some of the rich".
  - 「其長者黎人用以繚腰」 "The long pieces the Li wind round their waists." (translation)
- **Hainan, 諸蕃志 卷下** (1225), https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/諸蕃志/卷下.
  - 「婦人不事蠶桑，惟織吉貝、花被、縵布、黎幕」 "The women do not do sericulture; they only weave jibei, patterned quilts, cloth and Li curtains." (translation)
- **Pelliot p.514.** "in 1225, Hai-nan was the principal source of the export of cotton towards Fu-chien."
- **Fujian and Guangdong, Pelliot p.514**, quoting 文昌雜錄 of 1085. "Beginning with Min (= Fu-chien) and Kuang[-tung] and more to the south, there is much cotton (mu-mien). The local peasants (t'u-jên) emulate each other in planting it".
- **Pelliot p.515.** "cotton cultivation was already extensively practised in Kuang-tung and Fu-chien at the end of the 11th century."

## 3b. Yangzi valley, separately

- **Liu Pingsheng and Ge Jinfang**, https://www.aisixiang.com/data/121418.html, on 孝宗's reign (1163-1189).
  - 「约在孝宗统治时期, 棉花种植又自岭南传至两浙等地。」 "About the reign of Xiaozong, cotton growing passed from Lingnan to Liangzhe and elsewhere." (translation)
  - They rest this on poems by Lin Guangchao (at Tonglu) and Fang Kui (Yangzhou).
- **Pelliot p.521.** "the cotton which was cultivated in Chiang-nan from the 12th cent."
- **Pelliot p.520**, on Wang Zhen: cultivation before 1276 had reached "the whole of the region of the lower Yang-tzŭ, but also … Ssŭ-ch'uan".
- **Hu Sanxing's Tongjian note** (late 13th c., quoted by Liu and Ge). 「木棉, 江南多有之。」 "Cotton is common in Jiangnan." (translation)
- **農桑輯要 卷2**, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/農桑輯要_(四庫全書本)/卷2 (1273 preface).
  - 「苧麻本南方之物木棉亦西域所産近嵗以來苧麻藝于河南木棉種於陜右滋茂繁盛與本土無異」 "Ramie is by origin a southern thing and cotton a product of the Western Regions; in recent years ramie has been planted in Henan and cotton in Shaanxi, flourishing no differently than in their native lands." (translation)
- **Wang Zhen 木棉圖譜敘** (1313), read only as quoted in 農政全書 35, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/農政全書/卷35.
  - 「夫木棉產自海南，諸種藝製作之法，駸駸北來。江淮川蜀，既獲其利；至南北混一之後，商販於此，服被漸廣」 "Cotton comes from the south seas; the arts of planting and making came steadily north. The Jiang-Huai and Sichuan have profited, and since the unification of north and south, merchants deal in it, and its wearing grows wider." (translation)
- **Tao Zongyi, 輟耕錄 24** (late Yuan), https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/南村輟耕錄_(四部叢刊本)/卷之二十四.
  - 「閩廣多種木綿紡績為布名曰吉貝松江府東去五十里許曰烏𭰖涇其地土田磽民食不給因謀樹藝以資生業遂覔種於彼…國𥘉時有一嫗名黄道婆者自厓州来乃教以做造捍弹紡織之具」
  - "Fujian and Guangdong plant much cotton and spin and weave it into cloth called jibei. Some fifty li east of Songjiang is Wuniqing, where the soil is poor and the people's food insufficient; so they sought to plant it for a living, and got seed from there. … In the early dynasty an old woman named Huang Daopo came from Yazhou and taught the making of tools for ginning, bowing, spinning and weaving." (translation)
  - **Conflict on the date:** People's Daily 2024, https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbwap/html/2024-06/14/nw.D110000renmrb_20240614_2-17.htm, says 「元贞年间（1295年—1297年）」, and quotes Kangxi 松江府志: 「宋时乡人始传其种于乌泥泾镇。」 "In Song times villagers first brought the seed to Wuniqing." (translation)

## 4. Commercial crop and the state

- **Song.** Zurndorfer [DRAFT], citing Billy So: "Already in the early Song period, cotton (of the perennial variety) had become a commercialized crop in Fujian"; annual quota "5000 bolts".
- **Liu and Ge**, on Southern Song trade: 《宝庆四明志》 lists 「木 (锦) [棉]布、吉[贝]布、吉贝花」 among the "粗色" goods on ships from Fujian, Guangdong and Hainan.
- **Conflict on 農桑輯要:** Zurndorfer calls it Song ("In its waning days, the Song state issued…"). Pelliot p.515 says "prepared by order of Qubilai".
- **Yuan 1289**, 元史 15, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/元史/卷015.
  - 「置浙東、江東、江西、湖廣、福建木綿提舉司，責民歲輸木綿十萬匹，以都提舉司總之。」 "Cotton superintendencies were set up in Zhedong, Jiangdong, Jiangxi, Huguang and Fujian; the people were charged to deliver 100,000 pieces of cotton yearly, under a chief superintendency." (translation)
- **Yuan 1296**, 元史 93, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/元史/卷093.
  - 「成宗元貞二年，始定徵江南夏稅之制。」 and 「夏稅則輸以木綿布絹絲綿等物。」 "In Yuanzhen 2 of Chengzong the Jiangnan summer tax was first fixed … the summer tax to be paid in cotton cloth, silk, floss and the like." (translation)
  - **Conflict:** Qiu Jun says 「然是時猶未以為征賦故宋元史食貨志皆不載」 ("not yet levied in Song-Yuan"). The Yuan shi contradicts him.
- **Early Ming**, 明史 78, archived (6f/6f29c857fab4).
  - 「太祖初立國即下令，凡民田五畝至十畝者，栽桑、麻、木棉各半畝，十畝以上倍之。麻畝徵八兩，木棉畝四兩。…不種麻及木棉，出麻布、棉布各一疋。」 "On founding the state Taizu ordered: of private land of 5 to 10 mu, half a mu each to mulberry, hemp and cotton; double above ten mu. Eight liang per mu levied on hemp, four on cotton … those not planting hemp and cotton to pay one bolt each of hemp and cotton cloth." (translation)
  - The text gives no year; Pelliot p.522 dates it 1368. In Hongwu 9: 「棉薴一疋，折米六斗…麻布一疋，折米四斗」.
- **Hongzhi tax list** (明史 78), cotton and bast still side by side: 「曰棉花折布，曰薴布，曰土薴，曰紅花，曰麻布…曰粗租麻布，曰課程棉布，曰租薴布」.
- **State demand, Zurndorfer Pune [DRAFT]**, https://www.lse.ac.uk/Economic-History/Assets/Documents/Research/GEHN/GEHNConferences/conf8/PUNEZurndorfer.pdf.
  - "as high as fifteen million bolts (pi) per year before 1400 (Yan Zhongping:15-17)"
  - "In the second half of the fifteenth century, cotton textile production in Songjiang had become completely commercialized"
- **Songjiang, 農政全書 35**, editor's note on an event of 乙卯 (1615).
  - 「海上官民軍灶，墾田幾二百萬畝；大半種棉，當不止百萬畝。」 "On the Shanghai coast official, private, military and salt-household land is nearly two million mu; more than half is in cotton, not less than a million mu." (translation)
- **Songjiang, 閱世編**, archived (6d/6ddbacd28ebf), late 17th c.
  - 「吾邑地產木棉…故種植之廣，與粳稻等。」 "Our county produces cotton … so it is as widely planted as rice." (translation)

## 5. Normal clothing, by century, region and class

- **Baseline, Zurndorfer Helsinki [DRAFT]:** before Song, dress rules rested on "the distinction between silk and hemp, the latter of which was worn by the majority of inhabitants".
- **Late 15th c., Qiu Jun:** 「至我朝其種乃徧布於天下地無南北皆宜之人無貧富皆賴之其利視絲枲蓋百倍焉」 "In our dynasty the seed has spread across the realm; it suits land north and south, people rich and poor all depend on it; its profit is a hundred times that of silk and hemp." (translation)
- **1637, 天工開物·布衣**, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/天工開物/布衣.
  - 「凡棉布禦寒，貴賤同之。棉花古書名枲麻，種遍天下。」 "Cotton cloth for keeping out the cold is the same for high and low. … it is planted across the realm." (translation)
  - 「凡棉布寸土皆有，而織造尚松江，漿染尚蕪湖。」 "Cotton cloth is everywhere; for weaving Songjiang is prized, for sizing and dyeing Wuhu." (translation)
  - 「織機十室必有」 "Of ten households, some are sure to have a loom." (translation; this is his phrase, not a count)
- **Hemp and ramie did not vanish, 天工開物·夏服**, archived (f2/f20fa1679d67), 1637.
  - 「凡苧麻無土不生…績為當暑衣裳、帷帳。」 "Ramie grows on any soil … it is made into hot-weather clothes and curtains." (translation)
  - 「凡布衣縫線，革履串繩，其質必用苧糾合。」 "Thread for sewing cloth garments and cord for leather shoes must be of twisted ramie." (translation)
- **Modern summaries.**
  - Guo Weidong, https://fh.pku.edu.cn/docs/2018-11/20181110172100744658.pdf: 「元明时期棉布取代麻丝成为中国人最重要的衣被材料」 "In Yuan-Ming, cotton cloth replaced hemp and silk as the Chinese people's most important clothing and bedding material." (translation)
  - Pelliot p.522: "one century after Polo, the use of cotton was universally adopted." This is his own gloss.
- **Jiangnan.** See Songjiang above. Zurndorfer Pune [DRAFT]: "it was claimed in local gazetteers that this prefecture 'clothed the empire'".
- **North China, 農政全書 35.**
  - 「今北土之吉貝賤而布貴，南方反是；吉貝則汎舟而鬻諸南，布則汎舟而鬻諸北。」 "Now in the north raw cotton is cheap and cloth dear, in the south the reverse; raw cotton goes by boat to be sold in the south and cloth goes by boat to be sold in the north." (translation)
  - 「而數年來，肅寧一邑所出布疋，足當吾鬆十分之一矣。」 "In recent years the cloth of Suning county alone comes to a tenth of our Songjiang's." (translation; c. 1620s)
  - Zurndorfer Helsinki [DRAFT]: Suning's cellar spinning spread "in the last decades of the Ming", and "Within several decades, northern cloth competed with Jiangnan textiles."
- **South, 廣東新語 15**, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/廣東新語/卷15 (late 17th c.).
  - 「史稱粵多果布之湊，然亦夏布若蕉葛、苧麻之屬耳。冬布多至自吳、楚。松江之梭布，咸寧之大布，估人絡繹而來，與綿花皆為正貨。粵地所種吉貝，不足以供十郡之用也。蕉布與黃麻布為嶺外所重，常以冬布相易。」
  - "The histories say Guangdong has a confluence of fruit and cloth, but these are summer cloths, of banana, kudzu and ramie. Winter cloth mostly comes from Wu and Chu: Songjiang's and Xianning's cloth, brought by merchants without end, and raw cotton, are all staple goods. The jibei grown in Guangdong is not enough for the needs of its ten prefectures. Banana and jute cloth are valued in Lingnan, and often traded for winter cloth." (translation)
  - On kudzu: 「故今雷葛盛行天下。」 "So now Leizhou kudzu is popular across the realm." (translation)
- **Class, 閱世編·冠服** (the Ming, as Ye Mengzhu remembered it).
  - 「其華而雅重者，冬用大絨繭綢，夏用細葛，庶民莫敢效也；其樸素者，冬用紫花細布或白布為袍，隸人不敢擬也。」 "The grand wore thick wild silk in winter and fine kudzu in summer, which commoners dared not copy; the plain wore robes of fine nankeen or white cloth in winter, which servants dared not imitate." (translation)
  - 「若寒士則惟以白布袍為常服」 "Poor scholars had only the white cloth robe as ordinary dress." (translation)
  - 「其僕隸、樂戶，止服青衣」 "Servants and entertainers wore only blue-black." (translation)
- **Observers.**
  - Staunton, 1790s, archived (bc/bc6b5f14789d): "among the lower orders of which cotton is universally worn by both sexes."
  - Fortune, 1840s, archived (d7/d71fce34d07c): "the blue cotton cloth, which forms the principal article of dress of the poorer classes."

  - Songjiang coast, c.1615: more than half of nearly 2 million mu (農政全書 above).
  - Cotton zone of southern Jiangsu, late Ming: 50%, rising to about 70% by mid-Qing, in 「棉作区棉田的比重从明代后期的50%上升到清中期的70%左右」 (archived d9/d951120227a7, reporting Li Bozhong; cotton fields' share within the cotton zone).

## Reading, sub-question by sub-question

**(1) ATTESTED.** Cotton cloth is known in Han texts and Xinjiang finds. The oldest unambiguous statement of a cotton plant woven into cloth is the 6th-century Liang shu on Turfan.

**(2) ATTESTED.** Tribute and elite import from the southern seas and Central Asia ran from Han to Song, with commercial Indian imports by the Southern Song. Qiu Jun says commoners did not wear it in Han-Tang.

**(3) ATTESTED.** Frontier growing is shown for Turfan by the 6th c., Yunnan under Tang, and Fujian-Guangdong-Hainan by the late 11th c. Reaching the Yangzi by the 12th c. is a modern reading of Song poems, supported by 13th-c. notices. Yangzi cultivation is firm by the 1270s-1280s (Nongsang jiyao, Hu Sanxing).

**(4) ATTESTED.** It was a commercial crop in Fujian and Guangdong by the 11th c. In the Yangzi the state's part is direct: 1289 superintendencies, 1296 summer tax, then the Ming planting order and commutations. Songjiang was fully commercial by the late 15th c. Modern estimates of state consumption are second hand and from a marked draft.

**(5) ATTESTED ONLY FOR** the general statements of "rich and poor" (Qiu Jun, late 15th c.) and "high and low" (Song Yingxing, 1637), and for winter wear. On this evidence cotton had become the ordinary clothing of commoners in Jiangnan and the cotton belt by the 15th-16th centuries, reaching the north's own weaving only in the last Ming decades. It was still not the summer wear of the far south in the late 17th c., where kudzu, banana and ramie were worn and winter cotton was imported. Ramie stayed as hot-weather cloth and sewing thread in 1637. No premodern share of households or of clothing was found. The acreage figures cover only Songjiang and southern Jiangsu.
