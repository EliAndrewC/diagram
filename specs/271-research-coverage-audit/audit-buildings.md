# Audit D - buildings and religious places (feature 271 FR-001)

Domain: buildings and religious places as building plans (Mode A sheets) and as map footprints at every tier - the
magistracy compounds and the country shrine in `pool/`, the `buildings`, `religion-and-death` and `presentation`
pages, the `compound_kinds` sheet modals, and the houses, temples, inns, dojos and mansions the settlement tiers draw
or will need.

Tiers: H hamlet, V village, T town, C provincial city, K capital, M a Mode A sheet (building plan). Status is judged
against the record on main in this clone, EXCEPT where feature 267's clone (`diagram-buildings`) has written the
answering question: those are named `267:<page>/NNN` and judged on that fragment (267 T11-T17 still open, not yet on
main). 268's questions (religion-and-death 080-128) are on main. 269's items are claims to EDIT an existing thin
section, so a row owned by 269 keeps the status the section has today. `presentation/010-070` are drawing conventions
(the GM's rulings) and owe no source; they are listed THIN-by-design so the audit is complete, owner 271, and need no
research pass.
