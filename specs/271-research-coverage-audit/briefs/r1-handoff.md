## Items

- A137/D88 ACCURATE (partly) - 130 now cites grave tending: graves and the paths to them were cleaned on nanuka-bon, the day that opens Bon (kotobank-nanukabon, ndl-crd-nanukabon), and the grass round graves was cut before Bon in some regions (yakult-bon-kusakari). A village shrine was cleaned by representatives its own households chose, sometimes in rotation (kotobank-ujiko). The canon adds weeding at the equinox festivals. 140 now cites that tending came in bouts at set seasons. How far out the clearing reached, and where its edge fell, stays SILENT (absence notes updated, 2026-09-27); the ragged edge stays the GM's ruling - no change to what the generator draws (the collar sizes and the bays-only outline stand).
- A143 KNOB - 400: the household's own grave (yashiki-baka) is attested beside the shared ground: formerly common, with branch-house graves beside the main house's (kotobank-haka). Yanagita finds it common in Kanto, the northeast and the south Kyushu hills; today it is said to be many in Kanto and few in Kansai. On the plot it sits at a corner, the oldest at the back in the house grove, and when moved it went onto the household's own field or wood nearby (nireyama-yashiki-bochi, eisi-yashikibaka). No share is readable (absence note). - Generator: a hamlet should roll per seed whether its farmsteads keep household graves (a few stones at a back corner of the house plot, in or against the grove, or on the household's field beside it). The shares, about half of hamlets and a third of their farmsteads, are labeled guesses in 400's spec. Nothing draws this today.
- C157 KNOB + SILENT - 410 (pointed at from 333): "one per seat" is the Song order. The Song ground was a field, not a mound: 3-5 qing for each capital-district county, about 42-70 acres on this page's arithmetic (songshi-178-wikisource, units from chinese-units-enwiki and mu-land-enwiki). It was fenced, planted with trees, laid in shallow graves in neat rows at 8-9 chi (about 8-9 ft) a burial, with a keeper monk's house (chnmus-louzeyuan-bricks, gongyishibao-louzeyuan, louzeyuan-zhwiki). A Qing county instead kept several small charity grounds of 1-3 mu, about 80-140 ft on a side (sohu-xuancheng-yizhong). The Japanese-form mound's 10-30 ft stays a guess (SILENT; the absence note now names the pages searched). - Generator: a seat drawn in the Chinese form (funerary.py already has the organic=False Chinese mode for burial grounds) should roll between ONE large fenced ground of rows with a keeper's house (a few hundred ft on a side, a labeled calibration) and two to four small charity grounds by different roads out. The default Japanese-form `ossuary` mound is unchanged.
