## Items

- C27 ACCURATE (calibrated) - preserved provincial seats run from about 1 ha (Takayama, a Japanese provincial office, 9,807 m2 less its plaza) to 3-7 ha (Chinese prefectural and provincial yamen: Nanyang 36,000 or 72,000 m2, Baoding 30,000, Huozhou 38,500); the drawn cities' ~1.5 ha (Minami 525x300 ft, Nagahara/Tango 436x366 ft) is inside the span - the governor's compound kind can say its size is attested; a generated provincial city should roll the compound between about 1 and 3.6 ha (never below the largest county compound) rather than fix it; the frozen three need no change.
- C28 ACCURATE - the governor's compound holds the county magistracy's program in the same order (gate and guards, office hall with the courtroom, clerks' rooms flanking the front court, a second hall, the residence behind the inner gate) plus side routes for archive, stores and granary, the shrine and the jail; it grows by added courts (two to five), not by larger halls (Qing rule: "a senior official has the full set, a junior one reduced in order") - the city map is unchanged (walls, interior implied); a governor's Mode A sheet, when one is drawn, follows 210's spec.
- C29 KNOB - the compound faces SOUTH in both traditions (cited); the approach is two forms: an axial ~30 ft avenue south from the gate (Shimotsuke kokuga 9 m avenue, Suō's Suzaku-oji) or a cross street past the gate with a screen wall facing it (Ming-Qing yamen) - a generated provincial city should open the governor's gate south and roll the approach; the drawn Minami and Nagahara open north and Tango west, which history does not support (frozen exhibits, left alone; the governor's-compound kind should not claim the gate side as accurate). towns/010's gate-to-yamen axis stays a guess for a county seat, but it now has an attested Japanese provincial counterpart - see "owed" below.
- C30 SILENT (size) / ACCURATE (program and placement) - no readable page gives a ministry compound's dimensions (absence note); below the capital the six were equal rooms (two bays each) inside the yamen; at the Ming-Qing capital they were separate compounds built one by one, five east of the Daqing gate and Justice west beside the Censorate and the Court of Judicial Review; a ministry holds a hall, bureau rooms (Revenue: 14 by province) and a document store, with treasuries, granaries and mint as separate offices - ministry kind: its size stays a labeled guess (the drawn 114x78 to 140x95 ft); its interior should be hall + bureau rooms + document store, never a treasury or granary.
- C38 ACCURATE (thin section filled) - 060 now cites the watch hut's size (6 x 9 shaku, about 6 x 9 ft, eaves ~10 ft), two ward-paid watchmen, the gate shut at about 10 pm with a questioned wicket - the guard-box kind can give its real size; the drawn box should be about 6 x 9 ft if it is drawn larger (not measured here).
- C33 KNOB - two forms: the jail as a walled court at the southwest of the governor's compound outside the inner gate, with inner, outer and walled women's wards (China; every seat had one, a prefecture its own jail office); or a compound of its own on a residence plot (Chōshū's Noyama/Iwakura jails at Hagi, two across a road, 12 cells; Edo's Tenmachō), the magistracy keeping only a holding cell - a provincial city should roll which; in-compound size is a guess (no readable area); a separate jail is a samurai residence plot, never Tenmachō scale.
- C34 ACCURATE + setting-canon - a city did keep its own urban magistracy (Edo's two town magistracies, ~9,000 m2 each, inside the outer-moat gates, residence inside, 25 yoriki and 100 doshin each; a Chinese prefectural city held a county yamen too), and the staff lived OUT in a group residence of plots (~300 tsubo yoriki, ~100 tsubo doshin; Hatchobori ~40,000 tsubo for 50 + 200); the setting puts street policing, courts and jails under the Ministry of Justice, so this project reads a provincial city's town magistracy as its Ministry of Justice office - no extra magistracy compound in a provincial city; constables' group residence of equal plots (~100 ft and ~60 ft square) near it; a domain capital's Imperial Magistrate compound is a magistracy with its residence inside, size a guess below Edo's; Clan/Family magistrates not drawn until the GM asks. programs.md knob 1 can cite 250.
