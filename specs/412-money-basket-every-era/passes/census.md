## Era summary

Basket key: R rice, Sb soybeans, BW barley/wheat, BM buckwheat/millet, Ob other beans, F fish, EO edible oil, CC cotton cloth, LO lamp oil, S salt, FU fuel, RR room rent. Row counts are approximate (rows above, grouped).

### Japan, market eras
1. Japan, Kyoto, 1745-1754 (market; Bassino-Ma reconstruction): about 11 rows. R, Sb, BW, BM, Ob, F, EO, CC (cloth price is Osaka 1840s), LO. Wage: yes (2.82 g). Coin rate: none (all silver). No S, FU, RR.
2. Japan, Kyoto, 1741-1867 sparse wages (Mitsui): about 12 rows (1741, 1791, 1802-04, 1815-29, 1851, 1865-67). Wage only (0.92 monme 1802-04; 2.55 g 1791; 3.37 g 1851; 4.11-4.26 g 1865-67). No goods, no rate.

### Which eras have both a rice price and an unskilled wage
Only Kyoto 1745-1754 (R 1.49 g/kg; 2.82 g/day) and, loosely, Songjiang (rice but no wage) and Canton (rice 0.024 tael/kg and wage 0.08-0.1 tael) mid-1700s. Salt plus an unskilled wage together: Kashimo 1774 (60 mon) and Nagata/Otsukawa 1842 (132 mon), and Beijing 1736 (75 cash). Charcoal plus an unskilled wage: Beijing 1736, Zhili 1769, Nagata 1842 (nearby only).

### Staples of the basket (soybeans, barley/wheat, buckwheat/millet, other beans, fish, edible oil, lamp oil)
Appear only in Kyoto 1745-1754 (0249). No other era or place in questions 0248-0283 prices any of them.
