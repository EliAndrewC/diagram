### `religion-and-death.html` fn-66 (searched 2026-09-12)

*Under the heading:* Does a graveyard stand beside its temple or its shrine?

**The paragraph:** Why the graveyard is the hall's own yard. The state merged the Buddhist strand with Fortune worship, so ANY temple may host a graveyard, and a graveyard is a parish ground that sits inside a temple precinct. A village SHRINE hosts one too, which is the churchyard rule and the thing most likely to look wrong at first glance: real-world Shinto shuns death pollution (kegare), but this setting's country shrines are officially of the Buddhist-analog order even while they house Fortune deities, and the resident brother performs the funerary rites.
