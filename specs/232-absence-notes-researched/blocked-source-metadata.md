## Where the identity is uncertain

**Item 1, GB 50288-2018.** The identity is firm: the Chinese title 灌溉与排水工程设计标准, the official English title *Design standard for irrigation and drainage engineering*, MOHURD announcement 2018 no. 32 of 2018-03-16, effective 2018-11-01, superseding GB 50288-99, with clauses 20.4.2 and 20.4.3 mandatory. Three things could not be settled. The page count disagrees between sources: the mirrored scan is 210 pages while the antpedia vendor listing says 340, which most likely means one counts the standard alone and the other the standard bound with its 条文说明 (explanatory provisions), but that was not verified. The issuing body is reported two ways: the announcement itself is signed by MOHURD alone, while the 工标库 record lists MOHURD together with SAMR, and some secondary summaries say AQSIQ instead; only MOHURD is confirmed from the announcement text. And the MOHURD portal, which the announcement says carries the standard openly, did not resolve from this container, so the claim that an official free copy exists could not be checked. The free national standards portal is a confirmed NO, and structurally so: its own notice excludes engineering-construction standards from the full-text system, which puts the entire GB 50xxx series outside it. The openly reachable full copy is a third-party mirror (eia543.com), a scanned image PDF with no text layer, so a quotation from it would have to be transcribed by eye rather than machine-checked.

**Items 11 and 12 are one document, not two.** The ScienceDirect PII S1001627920300706 and the truncated ResearchGate title resolve to the same paper, Alomari et al. 2020 in the *International Journal of Sediment Research*. The open manuscript at the Universiti Putra Malaysia repository is registered as the best open location by both Unpaywall and OpenAlex, but that host did not answer on the one attempt made here, so the link is recorded on the strength of the index rather than verified.

**Item 18 is a subject reconstruction, not a recovered citation.** The project recorded only "an MDPI paper and two engineering repositories" with no titles, so these are the best candidates found by subject search rather than the items originally consulted. The closest direct match on angle against sediment is Salman and Mhammad 2020 in the *Al-Rafidain Engineering Journal*. For the MDPI item there are two plausible readings: Baltazar et al. 2021 in *Water* holds the alignment fixed at 90 degrees and varies a vane field, while Ali et al. 2019 in *Water* comes from Alomari's own Universiti Putra Malaysia group; which one the note meant cannot be determined. The Herrero Casas 2013 doctoral thesis at the Universitat Politecnica de Catalunya is the most substantial open treatment of bedload partition at a diversion and is the likeliest of the two "engineering repository" items, the other almost certainly being the Universiti Putra Malaysia copy of item 11.

**Item 19 is also a subject reconstruction.** No title was recorded, so the ScienceDirect paper named here (He, Li and Zhang 2015 on Chinese forest cover 1700-2000) is an inference from the subject description. It covers the historical deforestation half well and the "what the slopes carry now" half not at all, which is why a second paper is listed for the present cover. Of the two candidates for that half, the closer thematic match (Chen et al. 2021 in the *Journal of Environmental Management*) has no open version, while the one that is open (Tang et al. 2021 in *Ecological Indicators*) is narrower.
