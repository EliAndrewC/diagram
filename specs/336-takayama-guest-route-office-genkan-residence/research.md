# Research - feature 336: Did Takayama's guest route from the office genkan to the residence run indoors?

## R1 - the research pass (2026-10-10, an agent, read-only)

**Method** (observed 2026-10-10; method: a sonnet agent's search of the record, the archive copies and the web, reading
and reporting only).

- **shirobito.jp review**, https://shirobito.jp/castle/1717 (the record's `shirobito-1717-takayama`), a visit of 2025-11-16:
  「順路は政庁である御役所の各部屋をめぐって、代官・郡代らの役宅へと続いています。」 ("The route goes round the rooms of the
  government office, the goyakusho, and continues on to the residence of the intendants and district intendants",
  translation the agent's). The modern visitors' route.
- **Gifu Prefecture, 高山陣屋管理事務所 facility table (平成24年度)**, https://www.pref.gifu.lg.jp/uploaded/attachment/39687.pdf:
  「御役所 754.75㎡ 文化13年（1816年）… 郡代役宅 597.30㎡ 平成8年3月復元 / 渡廊下 12.78㎡」 (the office 1816; the district
  intendant's residence restored March 1996; a covered corridor 12.78 m², undated, its ends unstated).
- **Japanese Wikipedia 高山陣屋**: the residence was restored in 1996 from an 1830 drawing (「1830年（天保元年）の絵図を基に」).

**Answer** (observed 2026-10-10; method: the sources above): indoors, probably through the office and a covered corridor;
the Edo-period route is not recorded in anything read. Not reached: the 1992 survey report (上嶋善治), the 1830 drawing.
