## What stands, and why

`household_reach` still lays the four garden sides' homesteads at each seat it measures (416 measures, 1,216 fixture layouts at
40 households): the reach must be the placer's own (it rolls a homestead's parts at the seat offered, feature 297 plan C and
research R9), and the layouts it builds are the placer's cache for that seat. The `px` left is spread over the layout's helpers,
none over 16,412 calls. Cutting the layouts themselves means a reach not rolled at the seat - the household-keyed roll 297
withdrew - so the tenfold is not met on this chain: put to the round arbiter (`ask:427-T12-r4`).

**Ruled** (round arbiter, `ask:427-T12-r4`, accept, 2026-10-26): T12 closes at 4.8x, a red exit recorded; US4's tenfold is judged on
the homestead parts family (T07 and T12 together) when it closes, and SC-002 stands. The `px` scale factor and any exact
pruning of the four sides are optional `found.jsonl` rows.
