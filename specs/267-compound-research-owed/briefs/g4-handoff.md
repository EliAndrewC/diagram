- R32 SILENT (on size) - the black pine is 15-40 m untended, the lead tree of the Japanese garden, kept by pinching and thinning twice a year; the mikoshi pine at the wall, seen from outside, is attested by 1603-04; no page gives a pruned garden pine's crown (absence) - `garden pines`: H - pines in the garden ACCURATE (a pine at the wall showing over it too); the 4-6 ft crowns read as young or tightly pruned trees and are a GUESS, as would be anything up to 15-30 ft; the kind's own story (a garden kept by generations) argues for wider crowns.
