R36 KNOB - several kami in one hall (aidono) was ordinary, and three forms are attested: a bay and a door for each (the two-bay shrine, halls joined under one roof, the three-door altar shelf, main kami in the middle then right), one altar with the lesser kami ranked behind it (the one-door shelf), or a small shrine of its own in the precinct; nothing read says which was usual or describes a residence's or office's shrine - `shrine altar` can move from deviation/"Sources: not recorded" to accurate for Ochiba's and Hayakawa's several altars side by side in one hall (the first form), cited to kotobank-aidono, genbu-honden-styles and tokyo-jinjacho-kamidana, Entry religion-and-death 'When one shrine hall serves several kami, does each have its own altar?'; `compound shrine` gains that Entry for the second-shrine form.
R37 SILENT - no page read gives a distance from any torii to its hall (absence note dated 2026-09-27); what IS read is that the torii marks the precinct's boundary and that a household shrine only "may even have" one - `torii` must stop calling the ~20 ft "the record" (it is this project's calibration, now labeled as such at 090) and label the distance a guess; Hayakawa's and Ubame's ~5 ft short gaps are as supported as the 20 ft, and a compound shrine with no arch is also attested.
R38 KNOB - one torii at the precinct's boundary covers the subsidiary shrines inside it (Jinja Honcho), and a subsidiary shrine may also have its own (Banshi-sha at Kitano Tenmangu) - `torii` on Hayakawa, one arch before two shrines on one shrine ground, is attested; cite jinjahoncho-keidai and jawiki-torii and add Entry 'How far before a small shrine does its torii stand, and can two shrines share one?'.
R39 SILENT - morijio is attested from about 1838-40 at the doorways of the customer trades (restaurants, assignation houses, variety halls), shaped as a cone or three-sided pyramid, and today usually set as a pair outside the door; no page read puts it at a samurai residence or an official's gate - `salt wards` on Hayakawa keeps the label guess for its use at a compound, but its form, pair and outside placement can now be cited (kotobank-morijio, jawiki-morijio, ise-miyachu-morijio) and its Entry becomes religion-and-death 'Were cones of salt set at the doors of an official's compound?' instead of "no dedicated entry - recorded as silent".
