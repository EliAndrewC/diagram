R16 ACCURATE (depth and horse count SILENT) - a warrior's stable was a freestanding shoin-style building, the formal one three ken long and gable-entered, the classic ones 3, 5 or 7 bays, with stalls one bay (about 6 ft) wide on board floors, an attendants' room (tozamurai) and an earth-floored fodder room; Hikone's has 21 bays; a Kinki farm stall is about 3 m square; a warrior stall's depth and a county post's horse count were not found - `stables` on O H U P: a small building of its own, stalls about 6 ft wide on board floors, room for grooms and fodder; stall depth and horse count are GUESSES.
R17 KNOB (a north seat SILENT) - the Boso-no-mura middle-rank house's vegetable garden is about 1 se (about 1,070 sq ft) west of the house; the Yokota house at Matsushiro (150 koku) had about half its 1,012-tsubo grounds in vegetable field; the formal garden was to the south; a vegetable garden north of the house was not found - `vegetable garden` on O H U: size rolled per settlement between a soup-greens plot and a field over half the grounds; seated off the south; a north seat is a GUESS (the one sided case is west).
