chosen per sheet by its story and its current drawing, the three sheets differing where the record allows (D6); the
choice is recorded in each sheet's notes ("Knob settings") and its modal text ("Map notes / Features").

## Redraws a finding requires on every sheet it names

- The KITCHEN is part of the house on every sheet (research 360/370: the kitchen door is the house's service door;
  the Takayama residence lists its kitchen, well and bath among its own rooms) - joined to the residence as an ell or
  by a short covered corridor (building-review, pass 2).
- The CEREMONIAL MAIN GATE is the widest opening in each wall (buildings.md checklist): the lesser gates narrowed where
  R26's new widths had tied or passed them (pass 2).
- A guardroom in the GATE RANGE is drawn as a nagaya-mon: the range is the wall line, the wall broken along it, the
  passage through it (research 420; pass 2, Hayakawa and Ubame).


- R09 bath: a small addition to the kitchen range (the Takayama residence's bath stood with its well and kitchen), not
  a freestanding pavilion - O H U.
- R20 clerks: H's freestanding clerks' duty room removed; the clerks' room becomes a room of H's office hall (O and U
  already draw it so).
- R08 privy: H's latrine between the residence blocks becomes a guest privy at the rear of the zashiki on a board
  veranda, and a family privy toward the living rooms - neither a yard building.
- R23 kneeling: mats - the accused's at the center, the plaintiff's behind to one side, the village officials' behind
  to the other - O H U.
- R24 cell: 12 by 10 ft, the small end of the attested span (18 by 15 ft drawn) - O H U.
- R42 landing: H's pier becomes steps cut in the revetment across the bank street, reached from a gate in the wall.
- R44 steelyard: U's balance beam is a steelyard (done, the kind renamed `steelyard`).
- R50: U's notes' "the standard practice wherever two jurisdictions must transact regularly" is cut (not attested).
