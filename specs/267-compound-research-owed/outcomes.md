- **R04 ACCURATE** (G1) - the lord's suite beyond the study is, in the sources, a room for working by day and a bedroom (the naka-oku, a modern term; Edo drawings count it in the omote), and the daimyo mansion's goza-no-ma stands on the omote side; the Matsue master's living room is set apart from the zashiki - `lord's quarters`: working room plus bedroom, behind the reception, before the family.
