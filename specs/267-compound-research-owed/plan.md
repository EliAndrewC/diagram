# Implementation Plan: The compound research owed

**Feature**: `267-compound-research-owed` | **Date**: 2026-09-27 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Fifty-three owed questions ([`inventory.md`](inventory.md), R01-R53), researched by the process feature 250 landed:
ten groups, each a WRITE session then CHECK sessions, every one a fresh headless session from a written brief
(`make page-session`), the state between them carried in each group's handoff file, never in a context. This session
orchestrates: it writes the briefs, queues the sessions, and - once a group's record is checked - rewrites the map
kinds and the sheets its outcomes bear on, which the research sessions do not touch.

## Decisions

- **D1 - The research runs in page sessions, not here** (FR-005). Feature 250 measured the main session at 87% of
  every token when it did the research itself; a session that does one group and ends never grows that far. The
  briefs are generated by `briefs/gen.py` on 250's pattern (`write <G>` from the inventory; `checks <G>` from the
  handoff, two questions a session, the new registry keys with the last), and queued as `then:` steps.
- **D2 - Ten groups, at most eight items each** (inventory; G8 is the grave island alone): G1 and G3 were split (thirteen with R53, and ten items) because a
  write session reads every source it cites; the groups run ONE AFTER ANOTHER in this clone (the runner's rule: do not
  edit the clone while they run), so no two sessions write at once.
- **D3 - Each group owns a prefix range on its page** (`gen.py GROUPS`), so questions never collide across groups.
- **D4 - No section on 265's derived list is edited while 265's task for its page is open** (FR-006; the list is the
  spec's Edge Cases, and every brief carries it). The research sessions write their findings in their own questions
  and name any edit a finding owes such a section in the handoff; the edit itself - R27 in buildings 070, R42 in
  river-cities 040, R46 in ways 020, and any a handoff names - is made in T17 once 265's task for that page is ticked.
- **D5 - An item's outcome is one of four**, recorded in the handoff line `R<nn> <OUTCOME> - ... - ...` and gathered
  into `outcomes.md`: ACCURATE, KNOB, SILENT (an absence note, dated search), CONTRADICTION-RESOLVED (FR-001). A
  CONTRADICTION-RESOLVED item CORRECTS the wrong section, cited, in its write session, on any page and at any prefix -
  except a section on 265's list, whose correction the handoff states for T17 - and the corrected section is a
  `SECTION=` line of the handoff, so the check sessions check it (plan review B1). G1 ran on a brief that only named
  such corrections: the orchestrator reads its handoff's CONTRADICTION-RESOLVED lines and makes and checks any
  correction it named but did not make.
- **D6 - The kinds and the sheets follow in this session, per group, after its checks** (FR-003, FR-004): each kind's
  `Entry:`, label and prose rewritten from its item's question; `entry-drift` on a bundle
  (`make check-bundle ... FOR=entry-drift KIND=<class>`); a sheet a finding changes is redrawn, its picture and pack
  audit measured and `building-review` run; a KNOB is recorded in each sheet's notes as the form it takes - the three
  sheets taking different forms where the record allows it.
- **D7 - The GM's items go through `escalation-check`** (FR-008), with any research result the GM must rule on.
- **D8 - `future-work/compounds.md` "Research owed" is rewritten at the end** to what remains, each with why (FR-007).
- **D9 - The placer's hand-sheet path is fixed and the revised sheets are seated at the standard** (2026-09-27,
  after spec-fidelity BLOCKED the first D9 - re-taking the three sheets into feature 266's caption ledger - as an
  exception to 266 FR-013 that the GM's request does not support, and then blocked keeping a hand seat on a TIE).
  Revising a sheet takes it off the ledger and holds every caption to the one placer's standard seat, so the placer
  was fixed until it can. Measured 2026-09-27 on the tree (`make seat-label SHEET=<sheet>`, then `WRITE=1` twice and a
  byte compare): Ochiba 48 captions, Hayakawa 62, Ubame 60, each 0 off seat, each second write byte-identical; every
  `make pack-audit` check green on all three. The placer's rules, each with a unit test (`tests/tools/test_seat_label.py`,
  `test_seat_label_hand.py`): a self-tagged caption keeps its `data-kind`, letter-spacing and parent frame; side-by-side
  names in one group are separate captions; a group naming several things gives each name its own shape; a leader is
  matched to the caption it reaches; a face's width follows caps, bold and spacing; the rewrite loops to a fixed
  point. Ink is weighed as a reader meets it: ink INSIDE what a caption names is ink it avoids (`Obstacle.inner`; a
  generated map sets none, so the settlement engine is unchanged), a ground's drawn border is ink, dark ink weighs
  double, anything painted after a caption weighs as much as another name (it hides it), and the building that holds
  a named room is waived for that room's name; a light name set down off its dark roof takes the dark caption ink.
  Where no seat is free, the caption's own seat is kept ONLY when it covers strictly less than the standard's pick; a
  tie goes to the standard's own order (266 FR-007: the nearer ring, then rank). The seated sheets then had to pass
  every `pack_audit` check, which found reading defects in the audit, fixed there: a wrapped caption's tspans and a
  quarter-turned label are read as drawn, a tagged caption is paired with a structure of its own kind, and a group
  label is measured from its glyph's edge. Five captions had no seat at all and were changed by hand, each recorded
  in its sheet: Ubame's OUTER COURT dropped (no seat clears its buildings), Hayakawa's shrine note and Ubame's
  "(his letters)" moved to the notes and the page, two sub-lines wrapped. Hoshigaoka moved 3 px under the tie rule.
  `tests/fixtures/caption_ledger.json` is empty.
