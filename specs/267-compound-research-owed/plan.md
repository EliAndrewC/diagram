# Implementation Plan: The compound research owed

**Feature**: `267-compound-research-owed` | **Date**: 2026-09-27 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Fifty-three owed questions ([`inventory.md`](inventory.md), R01-R53), researched by the process feature 250 landed:
ten groups, each a WRITE session then CHECK sessions, every one a fresh headless session from a written brief
(`make page-session`), the state between them carried in each group's handoff file, never in a context. This session
orchestrates: it writes the briefs, queues the sessions, and - once a group's record is checked - rewrites the map
kinds and the sheets its outcomes bear on, which the research sessions do not touch.

## Decisions

- **D1 - The research runs in page sessions, not here** (FR-005). Feature 250 measured the main session at 87% of
  every token when it did the research itself; a session that does one group and ends never grows that far. The
  briefs are generated by `briefs/gen.py` on 250's pattern (`write <G>` from the inventory; `checks <G>` from the
  handoff, two questions a session, the new registry keys with the last), and queued as `then:` steps.
- **D2 - Ten groups, at most eight items each** (inventory; G8 is the grave island alone): G1 and G3 were split (twelve and ten items) because a
  write session reads every source it cites; the groups run ONE AFTER ANOTHER in this clone (the runner's rule: do not
  edit the clone while they run), so no two sessions write at once.
- **D3 - Each group owns a prefix range on its page** (`gen.py GROUPS`), so questions never collide across groups.
- **D4 - Feature 265's five buildings sections are not edited** (FR-006; the briefs say so): an item belonging there
  (R27, the wall thickness, in 070) is written as its own question linking it.
- **D5 - An item's outcome is one of four**, recorded in the handoff line `R<nn> <OUTCOME> - ... - ...` and gathered
  into `outcomes.md`: ACCURATE, KNOB, SILENT (an absence note, dated search), CONTRADICTION-RESOLVED (FR-001).
- **D6 - The kinds and the sheets follow in this session, per group, after its checks** (FR-003, FR-004): each kind's
  `Entry:`, label and prose rewritten from its item's question; `entry-drift` on a bundle
  (`make check-bundle ... FOR=entry-drift KIND=<class>`); a sheet a finding changes is redrawn, its picture and pack
  audit measured and `building-review` run; a KNOB is recorded in each sheet's notes as the form it takes - the three
  sheets taking different forms where the record allows it.
- **D7 - The GM's items go through `escalation-check`** (FR-008), with any research result the GM must rule on.
- **D8 - `future-work/compounds.md` "Research owed" is rewritten at the end** to what remains, each with why (FR-007).

## Constitution check

- XII: every finding is read (source-reader) before it is written, quoted, checked (quote-check, record-format,
  source-applicability), and labeled one of four classes; a silent record is labeled a guess with its search.
- XIII: sheets redrawn are measured; the gate runs before each landing.
- XVI: every inventory item is researched; the three "for the GM" items and the three "not research" items are
  separated with the reason, and put to the spec review.
