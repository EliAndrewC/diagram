# Inventory of parts and fabric (measured 2026-09-27)

Every element drawn inside or against a feature on the five pool magistracies that took its parent's kind before this
feature, read element by element from the sheets (and, for the two placer sheets, `compound.py` `emit_svg`). Each row
is a PART (it gets the kind named) or FABRIC (it stays the parent's; spec, "What counts"). Parts already separate
before this feature (every well, the dais, the clerks' room label, the guest-room label, the ancestral-alcove labels,
the fox-fire lantern, the fox relics) are unchanged, except that a room's floor now lights with its label (FR-002).

## Fabric - stays the parent's

Every building's outline and fill; the dashed room dividers (office halls, residences); stall divisions (stables);
bay and partition lines (barracks, quarters, nagaya, charcoal store, granary); the cell's lattice; the granary's slat
hatch; the shrine halls' vermilion edging and Ochiba's altar rail; the colonnade hatch and posts (cinnabar workshop,
weighing floor); the dock's plank lines; the river's flow chevrons; the bath's steam mark; every court's and garden's
ground; the shrine grove's trees (the grove is its trees).
