# Pass: Edo salt prices (retail by sho / bale; 塩問屋 bale price; Ako salt), 1700s-1850s

## 1. Sources that bear

### A. 『諸問屋再興調』巻十之二 (NDL 811-20), koma 38 - THE KEY FIND
- URL: https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548694/1/38 (image: https://dl.ndl.go.jp/api/iiif/2548694/R0000038/full/full/0/default.jpg)
- Publicly readable: YES. Images served without login; series rights code "pdm". The series TOC files the salt wholesalers
  under 十之一 (pid 2548693), but they are bound in 十之二 (pid 2548694), koma 11-25 and 32-38, plus 70-71.
- Date of evidence: 寛政3年8月7日 (1791). This is a copy, made for the 1851 guild restoration, of the Edo 下り塩仲買行事's
  answer to the city magistrate (地田筑後守's office), quoting the prices they sold at from 7/28 that year.
- Text: NDL OCR (lab.ndl.go.jp fulltext-json), checked by me against the page image at 3000 px. The heading, the bale size and
  the per-ryo bale counts read clearly. The 下札 (actual-fill) figures are cursive and harder to read; take that part from the
  OCR with caution.
  > 乍恐以書附奉申上候赤穂塩先々売直段書上候様被為仰付候処当時船間ニ付七月廿八日ゟ売直段左ニ奉申上候一赤穂塩但壱俵ニ付三斗五升入
  > 三斗五升ゟ春冬三斗弐升位下ケ札夏秋三斗弐升ゟ三升宜金壱両ニ付九俵半ゟ当時船間に付九俵弐二分ゟ九俵迄商内も少々斗七候右は七月廿八日
  > 赤穂塩壱艘入船仕私共買取右直段に先きゝ売渡申候御尋被為遊候に付奉申上候以上寛政三亥年八月七日下り塩仲買行事伊勢町濱川岸新兵衛店喜兵衛…
  The image shows "九俵弐三分ゟ九俵迄", where the OCR has "九俵弐二分".
  (translation) "With respect, we report in writing. We were ordered to write up the prices at which Ako salt has been sold.
  Because few ships are in at present, we report below the selling prices from the 28th of the 7th month. Item: Ako salt,
  each bale holding 3斗5升 [35 sho]. The attached tag (下ケ札) reads from 3斗5升 to about 3斗2升 in spring and winter, and
  from 3斗2升 to [三升宜 - illegible; OCR garbled] in summer and autumn. From nine and a half bales per ryo of gold; at present,
  because few ships are in, from 9.2-9.3 bales down to 9 bales; trade [商内も少々斗七候 - garbled; untranslated]. These are the
  prices at which we bought one shipload of Ako salt that came in on the 28th of the 7th month and sold it on. We report this
  because you asked. Kansei 3, 8th month, 7th day. The monthly officers of the 下り塩仲買 (the dealers in salt shipped down
  from the west)..."
  The verdict rests only on the parts that read clearly: 壱俵ニ付三斗五升入 / 金壱両ニ付九俵半ゟ / 九俵弐三分ゟ九俵迄 /
  寛政三亥年八月七日.
- My arithmetic, not the source's: 9 to 9.5 bales of 32-35 sho comes to about 290-330 sho of Ako salt per ryo of gold.
  This is the brokers' price to retailers, not a street price. To put it in mon, use the ryo-to-mon rate already on record
  for 1791; this pass did not source one.
- Koma 37 (the facing spread, read on the image) holds only the brokers' list of members, dated 寛政二戌年三月; it gives no
  price. I found no answer from the 問屋 (wholesalers) themselves to the 1791 inquiry; see the leads.

### B. Same volume, koma 71: the wholesalers' commission (the year is a lead, see below)
  > 毎朝市立商之儀売行手板両様相用ひ売引仕候銭金壱両に付銀三匁宛荷主ゟ受取内壱匁は仲買江分ケ遣は残弐匁に而致相続候
  (translation) "In the daily morning market we trade by both the sales ledger and the hand-board. We take 3 monme of silver
  per ryo of gold from the shipper; 1 monme of that goes to the brokers, and we live on the remaining 2 monme."
  This appears among the four Kitashinbori 下り塩問屋's agreed rules (松本屋/長嶋屋/渡辺屋/秋田屋). The rules cite 享保度 and
  天明度 practice, so they date from the 1780s at the earliest; read from the OCR only.

### C. kotobank 塩問屋 (改訂新版世界大百科事典, 渡辺則文) https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%A1%A9%E5%95%8F%E5%B1%8B-1170789 - public. Gives bale weights; no price.
  > 天保ころの問屋口銭は斎田塩1俵（6貫500目）につき3厘，赤穂塩1俵（9貫300目）につき5厘であった。
  (translation) "Around the Tenpo era the wholesalers' commission was 3 rin per bale of Saida salt (6 kan 500 me) and 5 rin per
  bale of Ako salt (9 kan 300 me)."
  > 下り塩問屋は江戸北新堀町の秋田屋，長島屋，渡辺屋，松本屋の4軒に限定され，塩廻船を一手に引き受けていた。
  (translation) "The 下り塩問屋 were limited to four houses in Kitashinbori-cho, Edo - Akitaya, Nagashimaya, Watanabeya and
  Matsumotoya - which handled all the salt ships."
  Period described: Tenpo (1830-44). The site is a modern encyclopedia describing premodern practice. Earlier reads of this
  page were logged as "rejected: no price"; it remains useful for the bale size.
  Caution: this is a Tenpo-era bale measured by weight (9貫300目). Source A's 1791 bale is measured by volume (3斗5升).
  The bales differ by decade and by unit; do not convert one into the other.

## 2. Verdict
**ATTESTED ONLY FOR** the brokers' (仲買) price of Ako salt in Edo in 1791: 9 to 9.5 bales per ryo of gold, with a bale
nominally of 3斗5升 and actually holding about 3斗2升 (source A, an Edo document of 寛政3年8月 preserved in the
publicly readable 諸問屋再興調). I found no Edo **retail** price per sho, no 1800-1850s Edo salt price series, and no
wholesalers' (問屋) bale price, only their commission (sources B and C).

The four named leads:
- 東京市史稿 産業篇 54 (2013) and 55 (2014): **NOT publicly readable**. The NDL Search records (R100000002-I024474960 and
  -I025481945) list paper copies only, with no dl.ndl.go.jp link. They are recent prints of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government
  (東京都).
- The 1838 諸色相場付: not found online.
- 守貞謾稿: NDL holds 類聚近世風俗志 (pid 991467), but the lab full-text API refuses that pid ("This PID is not allowed")
  and the dl.ndl in-item search endpoints returned errors. Unread; **SILENT**.
- 廣山堯道『塩の日本史』 (4th ed. 2025, 雄山閣): print only. No page quotes the 5-6 mon to 300 mon (Ichinoseki) price ladder;
  **SILENT**.
- Ako: the price in A is for Ako salt in Edo. I found nothing for the price at Ako itself.

The 1842 Edo 物価書上 (Oishi, 学習院経済論集 2-2; https://glim-re.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/202/files/keizai_2_2_69_128.pdf,
public) has 58 commodity tables and **none for salt**; the 塩-looking heading (13) is 鰻 (eel), checked on the page image p.86.

- Pages rejected: a Toyo Univ. paper on an Osaka sardine-fertiliser (干鰯) merchant; the Meiji Univ. museum leaflet on 1866

  is for salt only; it does not withdraw the session's citation shomonya-saiko-1851-tools.

  table 1, whose text layer is garbled (the page image would need to be read).
