## Housekeeper and House Steward: both found

- **Housekeeper**, lines 1860-1862, in THE HOUSEKEEPER section (1661): "[The Salary of the Housekeeper is from twenty-five to fifty guineas per annum, dependent on the extent of the family, and the nature of the business she undertakes.]" The index confirms it (22193, "salary of, 59"). The book also lists a "House-Keeper 24" guineas in the country-gentleman table (375).
- **House Steward**, line 14618, in THE HOUSE STEWARD section (14552): "Salary from 100l. to 250l. and upwards." The index confirms it (22203, "Salary, 338").
