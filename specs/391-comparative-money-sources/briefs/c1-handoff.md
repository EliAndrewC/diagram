## 0252 - servants' wages (all ATTESTED unless marked)

- Housekeeper 25-50 guineas a year (index p. 59); house steward £100-£250 and upwards (index p. 338)
- Lady's maid 18-25 gns with tea and washing; head nurse £18-£25, perquisites at christenings; governess £25-£120
- Upper house maid 12-16 gns; under house maids £10-£12; under nurse 10-12 gns; nursery maid 6-10 gns; still-room maid 8-12 gns; servant of all work 8-12 gns (12-14 gns in the income list, index p. 3)
- Kitchen-maid 12-14 gns; scullion 8-12 gns; laundry maid £8-£15; dairy maid £8-£12 + perquisites (1d./lb butter, 1½d. a fowl, 2d. a duck, goose or turkey, 3d. a score of eggs)
- Cook (woman): SILENT - the book states no wage; the 12-14 gns are the kitchen-maid's

- Butler £50-£80 large family, £30-£50 smaller, with perquisites (index p. 342); valet £30-£60, cast-off clothes (index p. 364)
- Footman 20-30 gns with liveries (index p. 384); under butler 16-25 gns; lady's footman £18-25 gns; under footman 16-20 gns; hall porter £24-£30; steward's room boy £8-£12
- Head coachman 25-36 gns with liveries; under coachman £20-24 gns; groom £22-£25; groom and valet about £50 living out; postillion 16-20 gns; stable boy £8-£12 living in; stable helpers 16-21s. a week
- Head gardener £50-£100, cottage, vegetables and fuel; lone gardener a guinea a week; under gardeners 16-21s. a week and vegetables; chamber (sick) nurse in London 10s. 6d.-15s. a week
- Country gentleman's list (net income £16,000-£18,000, expenses under £7,000; no page in the Gutenberg text): every figure as printed, in guineas; head gamekeeper 70 gns + 13s./week board wages; gardener 40 gns + 13s./week
- Board wages 10s. a week women, 12s. men; 10s. a head average; a pot of ale a day for men, a pint for women, besides table beer (index p. 8)

## 0253 - the division of income (all ATTESTED)

- The rule (33 / 25 / 25 / 12½ / 4½ percent) with the heads each pays for, and the table for £1,000-£10,000 (index: Expenses, Tables of, p. 4)
- Smaller incomes: ¼-⅓ of the expense for each parent, 1/10-1/12 for each child
- Servants kept at each income, £100 to £5,000 (index: Incomes, table of, p. 3)
- £150-£200 a year keeps a man, wife, two or three children and a servant girl (the paragraph opens with Cobbett)
- The greater houses' extra servants (land stewards, gamekeepers, jockeys and others)
