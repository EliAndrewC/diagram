# Research coverage of the Mode A kinds (measured 2026-09-26)

A measurement, not a decision. For every proposed kind on the three hand-drawn magistracy sheets
(Ochiba, Hayakawa, Ubame), this lists the research sections that actually say something about that
kind of thing, what each one establishes, the matching `types.json` item (tier `magistracies` unless
noted), and a proposed label under the rule the brief set:

- a section grounds it -> **accurate**
- the setting's canon or the map's story makes it, and it differs from history or has no historical
  counterpart -> **deviation**
- drawn larger or recolored for legibility where the record says so -> **convention**
- otherwise the `types.json` class if there is one, else **guess** (record silent).

Where the facts split one kind across two labels, the row gives the main label and names the part
that takes the other.

**How it was measured.** All 23 `research/buildings/` fragments were read in full. Every other page
directory (archetypes, cities/*, fields, homesteads, presentation, religion-and-death, settlements,
towns, urban-features, vegetation, water, ways; 265 fragments) was searched for the kinds' keywords
(kosatsu/notice board, chinju/grove, bath, kennel/dog, stable, weigh, tally, boundary stone,
landing/jetty/dock/wharf/barge, torii, charcoal, kitchen/vegetable garden, cart, gatehouse/monban,
nagaya/barracks, kura/granary, magistra*, engawa/genkan/veranda, privy/latrine, salt, parley, guest,
karo/retainer, altar, jin'ya/yamen). Every hit that could plausibly speak to a kind was read, and a
section is listed only where its text is about that kind of thing. `*.notes.html`, `_front`/`_tail`/
`_citations-*` files and the assembled pages were not used. Also read: `l7r/diagram/buildings/types.json`,
`buildings.md`, `buildings/programs.md`, the three `*.notes.md`, and a grep of
`/host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md` (plus `budgets.md` where l7r.md was silent).
