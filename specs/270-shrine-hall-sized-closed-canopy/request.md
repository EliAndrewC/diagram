# Where this feature comes from

Feature 268 (Hoshigaoka's country shrine in its grove) closed by asking the GM two questions: whether tree crowns
may overlap one another inside a shrine grove (the pack audit's `trees_overlap` forbade any two canopies to
overlap, so the grove drew at 42% canopy), and - carried since 2026-09-20 - whether the map's 60 by 48 ft
footprint for the shrine hall is a real measurement or the map glyph's size. The GM answered on 2026-09-27:

## The GM's words (2026-09-27)

> Yes tree crowns may overlap, I never said they couldn't - my rule was that trees were overlapping in ways which didn't make sense before, not that their crowns can't overlap with anything.
>
> And the current shrine hall dimenions are NOT something that came from me, so we should presume they are NOT a real measurement as a default, and should size the shrine based on our actual research.
