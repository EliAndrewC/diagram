## The research pass, and the GM's two rulings on it (same day)

Four background readers searched public pages; their reports, with every verbatim quote, are in
`reader-reports/`. The session reported (after `escalation-check`): no fence around a whole village
precinct is attested (fences ring the sanctuary, and date from Taisho/Showa donations); a village
shrine's precinct is typically 150-650 tsubo, of which buildings cover about 1.5-14%, the rest largely
wood; no source supports a 30 ft torii pitch (donation rows stand nearly post to post; the one small
rural row estimable works out at about 3-4 m); the sacred tree is near-universal, guardian dogs,
lanterns and strength stones are late-Edo donations, a farmers' stage and a sumo ring are attested but
not general. The session said it would drop the fence, turn the crop check back on for every sheet on a
map, and add the features as the research directs. It asked two questions; the GM answered:

1. *"The research says a precinct this size is mostly wood, but the village map draws open grazing
   land around the shrine, and your 2026-09-20 ruling is that the sheet shows what the map shows. What
   should the shrine's ground be?"* - the GM chose **"Add grove to map (Recommended)"**: *"Hand-edit the
   frozen village map, as was done for the seven arches: a grove around the shrine, plus the sacred
   tree and a stone basin by the approach. The sheet then draws them, with a small swept clearing at
   the hall."*
2. *"The research contradicts your preference for ~30 ft village avenues (settlement/_geom/walls.py:28,
   which also holds the invented ~20 ft general pitch). How far should the change reach?"* - the GM
   chose **"All maps, ~10-13 ft"**: *"Tighten the generator's avenue pitch everywhere and move
   Hoshigaoka's seven arches on the map and the sheet. At 2 ft per pixel the village map's arch glyphs
   would nearly touch, so the drawing floor needs a look."*
