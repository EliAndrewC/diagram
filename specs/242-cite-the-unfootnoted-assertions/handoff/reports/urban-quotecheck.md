## ABSENCE and GROUNDS notes

**One GROUNDS note, and it is the failure the form exists to prevent.**

- **fn-176** - `no source is owed: measured on our own maps`, on: *"Capacity is never the constraint (...) - **digging cost and morning queueing are.**"* That is a claim about **how a premodern settlement was built and lived in** - what actually limited how many wells a community sank - not a number measured off this project's drawings. Under the feature-235 prohibition it owes a citation or an absence note. It is also recoverable: **fn-150**'s own absence note, four lines earlier in the same paragraph, records that 「ja.wikipedia 井戸 gives communal ownership of wells as the rule because digging was costly」, so the digging-cost half has a readable source the grounds note walks past.

**Absence notes - 23 in scope, all in the right form** (`no publicly readable source (searched DATE: ...)`, no key, no link), each naming what was read and when. Two observations:

- **A doubled prefix in three of them**: fn-169 (urban-features) and fn-235, fn-236 (capitals) all read `(searched 2026-09-14: searched 2026-09-14: ...)`. Cosmetic, but it is in the reader-visible half of the note.
- **fn-169** and **fn-235** state reasons that are arguably GROUNDS rather than ABSENCE: fn-169's reason is that the characterization *"is this page's own"* (the record's own silence), and fn-235's is that *"the distance is this project's own measurement on a modern map"*. Neither is a research question left open, which is what an absence note is supposed to mean. fn-235's measurement is on a **third-party modern map**, not on our drawings, so the absence note is defensible; fn-169's reads as a decision wearing a backlog item's clothes. The session should decide; I do not.
- **Seventeen of the nineteen new absence notes** in fn-119-195 say *"no web query could be run in this pass, the session's search allowance being spent"* and then name the pages actually read. Honest and correctly formed - but the record should know that this batch's "searched" means "read these pages", not "ran a search".

Every absence note sits on a sentence that genuinely owes something findable (a seasonality, a rate, a dimension, a count), and none carries a claim the record labels as settled.
