2. 欽定物料價值則例, Hunan provincial edition, 卷一 (長沙府 長沙縣/善化縣). It is the same 1769 compilation in the same
   prescribed format. A public scan is on Wikimedia Commons (PD-scan),
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SSID-11354707_欽定物料價值則例_第1卷.pdf, PDF pages 38-39. The evidence is
   Qianlong. I read it from the page images: there is no text layer, and `make source-pages` could not read the PDF.
   - p.38: 「運價　夫運捌拾斤每里銀叁釐」 (translation: "Transport costs. Porter carriage, 80 jin, per li, silver 3 li [0.003 tael].")
   - p.39: 「船運壹百斤每拾里銀貳釐」 (translation: "Boat carriage, 100 jin, per 10 li, silver 2 li [0.002 tael].")
     Note beneath: 「以上貳欵係原定成規該處現送册開運價相符今照册開載嗣後修建工程将物料斤重道路里数於估銷册内分晰登明」 (欵 = 款, "item"; glyph forms as printed)
     (translation: "The above two items are the originally fixed rules and agree with the transport rates in the register
     this place has now sent; they are entered as the register gives them. Hereafter, for construction works, the weight of the
     materials and the li of road are to be entered separately in the estimate-and-reimbursement registers.")
   So the original states its unit inside each row (每里, 每拾里). The unit is not fixed across the series and can differ
   from row to row within one district.

## Verdict

SILENT on the unit in the Zhili original. I found no public scan or authoritative transcription of the Zhili edition. The
only copy cited is Tsinghua's, and it is not among the 物料價值則例 scans on the NLC 數字古籍, Internet Archive, Wikimedia
Commons or the National Archives of Japan. On how the series states its haulage unit, the evidence is ATTESTED ONLY FOR the
Hunan edition. There the original writes the unit into each row: porter "per li" and boat "per 10 li" for 長沙/善化. My
reading, not attested: the ch. 13 figures are identical in every district, and their unit label changes between 百里 and 里
even inside 青縣 (28b against 29a). That points to inconsistent entry by the transcribers, not to Tianjin really differing.
Arithmetic on the transcription (not a source) favours "per li". Read as 每百里, Tianjin's porter rate would be 0.006 tael
for 100 jin over 100 li. Elsewhere in Zhili the porter rows are 0.2-0.22 per 100 li (冀州, 廣平, 霸州). Tianjin's figure
would also sit far below its own 0.09 day wage for 夫 (9a). Read as 每里, it comes to 0.6 per 100 li, close in size to the Hunan original's
0.003 per li for 80 jin (長沙). The Bochum Hunan sheet gives 0.005 per li for 靖州, but that is a transcription, not the original. Hunan's 每拾里 boat row also means the Zhili boat row might be per 10 li, which the transcription
flattens.
