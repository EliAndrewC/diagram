**1. Is each factor derived?** No, one is not.
- **The sites tried.** derivation.md line 33 derives 2.13 as 1 / p. The contract's p = 0.47 (contract.md line 6) is the share of reach sites "clear of the homestead parts, the ways and the water for a clump's 22 ft box". A site must also pass "on its house's own bank" (algorithm line 18). A site whose box is clear but which sits across a water course from its house fails that test and still counts as tried. Its share, q, is neither measured nor argued to be zero. So the sites tried are 1 / (p(1 - q)), and only p is derived. Two ways to close it: measure the share of reach sites that pass both tests, or state that q is 0 on the reference rolls.

The box goes first unless q > 0.28, which is one more reason q needs stating.
