**2. Counter coverage (S5).** Every loop over a growing set has a counter where it runs:
- the bounding-box pass (`test@fringe`);
- filing and sorting in `face` (`2n`, plus one per comparison);
- in `pieces`: turning the face, the strip's points and the boxes, one per point;
- `clip`, `signed_area`/`inside` (n each), `crossings` (4n), the ring walk (`run+1`), and the sorts of crossings and spans.

What is left uncounted is constant-bounded: the four corners of each bed, at most four bed sides walked round, and the `past` folds. The channels stay at 6 points at both sizes (`read@dike` minus `test@fringe`).

Weight note: `crossings()` tallies 4 per edge but also runs `within(sides, a)` and `within(sides, b)` on every edge. Those are two more point-in-bed tests that go unweighted. Their loop has its counter, so this is not UNCOUNTED.
