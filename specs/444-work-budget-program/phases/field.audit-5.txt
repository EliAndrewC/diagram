**The ruling on `fit()`'s passes beyond `s.len()`.** Its loop runs up to n+1 passes over the half-row's lengths. Even when no length clamps (one full pass, then a second pass whose sum returns), it makes about 4n reads per half-row against the n counted, so at least 3 uncounted reads per basin. That is my estimate from the code, not a measurement. A `*work +=` per length read inside `fit` would settle it, or a one-pass water-fill could replace the loop.
