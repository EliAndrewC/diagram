**2. Is there a standard, cheaper algorithm?**
- **Order of laying:** the algorithm files each lane's corridor in the parts index at "lay". After that it trims the ends back and pulls each lane taut and clears its kinks ("settle"). That is placing and then repairing (S4), and any re-filing of the corridor is uncounted. Where each run ends is known from the walk, so trimming and the taut pull can happen before the lane is filed. The derivation should be in that order, or say why it cannot be.
- **The ring:** placed parts lie inside the hull, and the ring lies outside the gap bands. Only ring cells within 8 ft of the hull need the parts index; the rest need the raster alone. This is likely LOOSE once the 9 is broken down.
