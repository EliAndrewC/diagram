# T18 - the research pass on the largest single paddy field (feature 444 FR-018)

The `research-pass` agent's report, 2026-10-31, kept as the write brief's source. Verdict: ATTESTED - one contiguous tract watered
from one intake, pond or canal ran from about 1 to about 45 ha, typically a few to a few tens of hectares; the largest found is
Fei's 986-mow yu (about 60 ha), which Fei says was divided into smaller drainage units for working; larger settlements had several
tracts; above about 100 ha the sources describe a multi-village canal divided into channels and village shares, not one field.
