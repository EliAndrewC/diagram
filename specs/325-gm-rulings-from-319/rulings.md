# Rulings needed from the GM (deferred from feature 319)

### R1. Which season do a hamlet's dry-field crops show? (found 2026-10-05, feature 319's field-crop modals)

- **What the map does:** each dry-field crop - millet, buckwheat, barley, soy - is drawn in its own ripest color, so barley
  shows the gold of its May harvest beside crops of high summer on one sheet.
- **What the record says:** the field-crop pages give each crop's season and color; none says a map shows one season.
- **Why the guidelines do not settle it:** whether drawing every crop at its ripest is a deliberate map drawing convention or a
  drift to be fixed is the same kind of call the GM made on the farmhouse's single roof (2026-10-04, "NOT a deliberate
  convention"); a drawing convention is recorded only where a drawing page records it as one (dev/modals.md D3), and no page
  does. A labeled guess cannot settle it: it is a choice of how the sheet is drawn, not a missing fact.
- **The options:** (a) a deliberate convention - each crop at its ripest so it reads; the Depiction tabs say so with the real
  season; (b) one season for the whole sheet (high summer), barley then drawn as stubble or a following crop; (c) a season
  knob rolled per map.
- **Until ruled:** the map draws (a); the modals say the colors and the real seasons and do not call it deliberate.
