## Your items

1. **0340, Sanuki white sugar** (item 1): Kodama's record gives white sugar its own freight rate (2 bu 2 rin raised to 2 bu 6 rin of the price) and the cask rate of 2 monme 5 bu as a separate item. 2. **0340, Izumoya** (item 2): Oyano's note 36 says the sale of a million kin was GUARANTEED by a secret agreement; say that, not that the house "had" it. Give the 1822 Horigome lots as Iwata has them (nine wholesalers, seven of Kyoto and two of Osaka, in a year that made about 7 percent before a year that lost about 24). Say that the Aida house's sales of 1735-1765 were to a guild holding the buying of safflower to itself, and that its years around 1736 are in gold coinages of unequal worth.
