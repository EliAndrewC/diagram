1. **OVERSIZED.** The anchor is one stall of a half to one tatami (3 x 6 ft), plus a urinal and vestibule. That fits inside 1 ken x 1.5 ken (6 x 9 ft). The sheet's own notes call this a single household privy, so a 14 ft square is too big. The honest size is about 6 x 9 ft.
2. **Anchor quality.** I found no public page that gives a quotable privy dimension. I searched Edo setchin sizes in English and in Japanese, and fetched one farmhouse page. The unit pages and the farmhouse page give units but no privy size. The anchor is therefore derived from the tatami and ken modules, not a known measurement.
3. **No exemption applies.** A 14 ft box is not a small legibility bump for a glyph. It is also a building that stands 10 ft from the kitchen door.
4. **Band.** I recorded 5-9 ft per side for a single-household privy. Larger shared privies, such as a nagaya's or an official hall's, would need their own program entry.
