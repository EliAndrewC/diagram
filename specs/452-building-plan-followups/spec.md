# Feature Specification: the building-plan follow-ups

**Feature Branch**: `452-building-plan-followups` (no branch; work on `main` in the clone)

**Created**: 2026-11-24

**Status**: Draft

**Affects**: magistracy, country shrine, research record

**Approved**: the GM, 2026-11-24, "Okay, can we move all 17 of those into their own separate feature?"

**Input**: `request.md` (the GM's words, verbatim).

## The request

Told that feature 449 held 17 open follow-ups beside its deletion close (T158), none a defect in ported code, the GM said
(`request.md`, 2026-11-24):

> Okay, can we move all 17 of those into their own separate feature?  It seems like they all have to do with hand-drawn diagrams rather than scripted maps, is that correct?

All 17 concern building plans (Mode A): the magistracy compound sheets - three hand-drawn (Ochiba, Ubame, Hayakawa) and two
generated by the Rust compound generator (the county example, the round-trip sheet) - the country shrine's burial ground, and the
research pages, pop-ups and claims behind them. None concerns a scripted settlement map (Mode B). This feature holds them, so that
449 closes on its port.

## Why

Each of the 17 was found by a review or a search pass while 449 ported the engine to Rust (building-review, glyph-check,
impl-drift, record-format and research-pass rounds of 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24). None is a defect in the ported code; each is a
sheet, a page or a claim that does not yet say or draw what the record holds. They were filed on 449 to land before its close.
Moved here, they keep their full text, their evidence and their ids' history (each task names its 449 number), and 449 closes
without them.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - the three open research questions answered (Priority: P1)

A reader of the county example asks how its menservants lodged, whether its hearing court was screened from its lodgings, and how
long a junior officials' rowhouse ran. Each answer is in the record, cited, and the sheet and its pop-up follow it - or the record
says no source gives it and labels the drawn value a GUESS.

**Independent Test**: Each question's page in `research/questions/` carries the finding or the absence; the county sheet's notes
cite it.

### User Story 2 - the sheets drawn to what the record holds (Priority: P1)

A reader who knows the forms finds the stables' stalls with their rear doors and the grooms' room at the depth the source gives,
the compound shrine and the burial ground in their attested forms, and Ubame's household able to reach its door without walking
round the writing pavilion.

**Independent Test**: The sheets each such task names, redrawn, pass their checks and the review their occasion owes.

### User Story 3 - the claims, docs and notes say what the pages and drawings say (Priority: P2)

A reader of `docs/buildings.md`, `docs/building-programs.md`, a drawing page or a sheet's notes is told what the record and the
drawing hold - no cart gate no source has, no guessed size given as a page's, no citation of a pass no reader can open, no stale
log line.

**Independent Test**: `make claims-report` lists no open finding on the claims these tasks restate; each restated note or page
matches its sheet as drawn.

### Edge Cases

- A research pass finds no source: the record says so and the drawn value is labeled a GUESS; the task closes on that.
- A restatement and a redraw meet on one sheet (the county example's junior retainers' quarters, named by four tasks): the drawing
  is measured first and the texts restated to it.
- A fix to a sheet's svg or notes re-owes that sheet's review (the reason the T351 bundle made none): each such task declares its
  occasion when it lands.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

- **FR-001** (research questions answered into the record; T001-T003, from 449 T386, T387, T389): each question gets a research
  pass, and its finding - or the absence - goes into the record, cited; the county example's sheet, notes and pop-up follow it.
- **FR-002** (stable details drawn to the source; T004-T005, from 449 T404, T405): the JAANUS umaya details the record lacks are
  added to the stables' page, and the stables on the generated and hand-drawn sheets are redrawn to them, room first.
- **FR-003** (the Ubame layout item; T006, from 449 T408): Ubame's kitchen-garden bed is seated so the household's way from its
  rooms to its door is not forced round the writing pavilion.
- **FR-004** (claims and docs restated to their pages; T007-T014, from 449 T374, T390, T393, T396, T411, T412, T413, T414): each
  claim in `docs/buildings.md`, `docs/building-programs.md` and the county sheet's research strings is restated to the page it
  cites, labeled as the record labels it. Two of them change the drawing as well as the docs: the compound shrine's material
  (T012) becomes a knob rolled per sheet from 0220's two attested forms, or a recorded DEVIATION, and the claim says which; the
  country shrine's burial ground (T013) is drawn with the taller memorial stones and the row of six stone jizo at its entrance that
  0235's drawing page gives.
- **FR-005** (record and notes brought to their drawings; T015-T017, from 449 T380, T406, T410): each sheet's notes and comments,
  and each drawing page, say what the drawing does; the record's untranslated term gets its gloss once a source gives it.
- **FR-006**: Every task keeps its full text and evidence from 449's line and names its 449 number, its research class corrected
  to the standing rule (Decisions Recorded); nothing here changes a scripted settlement map (Mode B).

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

- **SC-001** (FR-001): Each of the three questions has its finding or its absence on a page of the record, checked by
  `source-reader`, `quote-check` and `source-applicability`, and the county sheet cites that page.
- **SC-002** (FR-002, FR-003, FR-004): The sheets FR-002 and FR-003 change, and the sheets T012 and T013 redraw under FR-004,
  are redrawn, pass their checks, and carry their occasion's review.
- **SC-003** (FR-004, FR-005): `make claims-report` lists no open finding on the claims these tasks restate, and no note, comment
  or drawing page they name contradicts its sheet as drawn.
- **SC-004** (FR-006): Every task line names its 449 number, each `research: physical` task closes with its five boxes ticked, and
  no pool map of Mode B moves at any of this feature's landings.

## Decisions Recorded

- The 17 are moved, not re-filed from scratch: each 449 line's text and evidence is kept whole in this feature's `tasks.md`, and
  each 449 line is closed as withdrawn, pointing to its task here (the GM 2026-11-24).
- The research class is corrected in the move to the standing rule (root CLAUDE.md, Development workflow: every task is
  `research: rendering` or `research: physical`; `docs/spec-kit-and-reviews.md`): 449 classed all 17 `research: rendering` as
  tooling, but T001-T006, T012, T013 and T017 are about how a place was built or lived in, so each is `research: physical` with its
  five boxes. Where the record already answers a box - T012's from 0220, T013's from 0235 - it is ticked with its pointer to that
  page, not researched again.
- The tasks are grouped and numbered by kind, in the order of the GM's request: research questions, stable details, the layout
  item, claims and docs, record and notes.

## Assumptions

- "Hand-drawn diagrams" in the GM's question is read as the building plans (Mode A), the generated compound sheets included, since
  the session's answer, which the request records, named them so.
