# Feature 283 - a garden's sun, checked on hand-drawn sheets - the GM's request

The GM, 2026-09-28, on the Hoshigaoka shrine sheet:

> I see a kitchen garden next to the shrine, but does that kitchen garden have enough clearance from the trees? Like, I know that on our maps, we have some actual rules based on sunlight calculations, where a garden needs a certain amount of sunlight per day. And so if you put a garden in the middle of a bunch of trees, then it won't really be able to grow very well because of the sunlight requirements. Have you done the math on this? That feels like it should be an automated check. that gets run on hand-drawn diagrams that have gardens for distance between the gardens and things that are known to produce shade, such as buildings and trees.

The session's answer (2026-09-28): no. Measured then - observed 2026-09-28; method: the sheet's garden rect, the
hall as a 20 ft block (the minka ridge research homesteads 030/043 uses) and the wood's crowns as 10 m and 15 m
columns, shadows cast every half hour at 38N in the shoulder month (declination -8.5) - the 14 by 23 ft bed has at
least half its area in sun for only 2.0-2.5 hours: the hall shades it until about 10, the wood's west flank from
about 13:00 to dusk. The engine's rules for scripted maps (39 ft open south of a bed; 50 ft clear of a tall belt west
and southwest - research homesteads 040, 043) would not have seated it there; no check applies them to hand sheets.

Measured next (observed 2026-09-28; method: the same shadow cast over every sheet's `vegetable garden` rect, buildings
at 20 ft, walls at 1.6 m, trees at 10 m, a half-hour counted when at least half the bed is lit): Hoshigaoka shrine 2.5 h;
Ubame 0 h, Hayakawa 0 h, Ochiba 0.5 h (strips along the north wall about 11 ft north of the residence); the county
example 9.5 h. The GM was asked how to fix the three magistracies - move them all, shrine now and magistracies later, or
declare half-shade beds - and answered (2026-09-28): "Move them all (Recommended)" - re-seat each magistracy garden where
the check passes, preferring the west side of the residence (the one side the record attests), and the shrine's the same.

The GM was asked (2026-09-28, after the spec's first reading) whether the check should also cover the frozen hand-drawn
settlement maps (the Hoshigaoka village, Ubame town, Minami and the other legacy maps), whose farmhouses draw kitchen
gardens, or only the hand-drawn building sheets, and answered: "Sheets only (Recommended)" - the legacy maps get the sun
rules when each is converted to scripted generation, whose placer already enforces them.
