That is probably the point at which you should stop and present your findings to me. since there will be a task for this feature, which is me taking acceptance of the work, and that is not a task that you are able to mark for yourself. Also, I don't want you adding new categories of things to these maps without running them by me first, at least not in this specific case. With this in mind, please proceed with creating and then working on this new SpecKit feature. Thanks.
