# The GM's rulings on 269's escalated findings (verbatim, 2026-09-28)

> Okay, I would like to use the pre-modern spacing for mulberry density rather than modern spacing.
>
> For the dike crops, it sounds like we should add tea dikes as an option, as in because it is attested, it should be a tunable knob that allows us to indicate that some of these have it. We should avoid anything that appears only on modern lists. So for example, no bananas and no vegetable dykes.
>
> Because the modern count in which even smaller communities like hamlets have shrines reflect a wealthier and even post-industrial society, then I do not want our pre-modern villages and hamlets to reflect those counts. Thus, I do want to keep my ruling that hamlets should not have small shrines. Except for personal family shrines, which we already do have, and which are rare.
>
> We should eliminate anything which is only modern, and this includes the duck pen, apparently. I didn't know that this was a modern thing when I asked to have it added, so we should get rid of it. However, we should make sure that whatever write-ups we have of ponds or rice paddies which has ducks or other animals living there, mentions this. So like if somebody clicks on a pond and there would be ducks in the pond, then the write-up will mention that.
>
> As for the headsman's gate, Yes, absolutely, we should have that. Now, with that being said, I don't want you to update the hand-drawn maps with this, but I do want it recorded in the research, and I do want it to be the case that when we begin scripting our village generation as we have scripted our hamlet generation, then we should absolutely make sure that the village headsman's house is gated if that was a headsman's right.

## What each ruling means for 269

- **B33, mulberry density.** Keep the premodern spacing. The drawn ~23 sq ft a bush IS the premodern figure (late-Qing
  mid-trunk, about 300 a mu, one tree to about 24 sq ft: archetypes/220). The undated root-cut 8,000-10,000 a mu is not
  used. No map change. archetypes/220 and the MulberryDike write-up state it as the GM's ruling.
- **B34, dike crops.** Add a tea dike as a knob option (attested). Drop cane, banana and vegetable from what a hamlet may
  roll: none has a premodern attestation (cane is on one undated modern list only). Fruit and mulberry stay. This means
  engine work (`meta.dike_crop` options, a TeaDike glyph and kind), the SugarcaneDike, BananaDike and VegetableGround
  kinds retired, and archetypes/230 recording the ruling.
- **B35, a hamlet's shrine.** The GM keeps the ruling: hamlets draw no small shrine, only the rare household shrine they
  already have. Modern counts reflect a wealthier, post-industrial society. archetypes/260 and religion-and-death/210
  record the ruling and its reason. No map change.
- **B32, the duck pen.** Remove it: it is a modern form, and only-modern forms are eliminated. `pondstock.py`'s pen and
  the DuckPen kind go. Every write-up of a place where animals lived says so. The research puts premodern delta ducks
  herded in the coastal rice (and sand) fields, not at the fish ponds (archetypes/210). So the paddy write-up on a
  dike-pond or delta hamlet mentions the herded ducks, and a fish pond's write-up names its fish, as the record allows.
- **B19, the headman's gate.** The GM confirms the gate: the nagayamon was the headman's right. Hand-drawn maps are NOT
  changed. homesteads/520 and homesteads/110 record the ruling. The village tier's scripted conversion MUST gate the
  headman's house, recorded in `future-work/farming-communities.md` and `migration-plan.md` beside the village's
  funerary grounds (owed at conversion).
- **General rule, recorded in docs/research-doctrine.md:** anything attested only in modern sources is eliminated
  from the premodern maps; the GM, 2026-09-28.
