About: A Song-dynasty Chinese farming manual tells a farmer who digs a pond to plant its bank sparsely with mulberry and
cudrania, so that cattle can be tied there in the shade; the cattle tread the bank firm, and the trees grow well on the pond's
water. This hamlet's tail pond has that bank.

How many trees a planted bank carried, and how far apart, is not recorded.

Guesses:
- That half of tail ponds have the planted bank and half the bare one: no account gives a share between them.
- One tree about every 30 ft of bank: the manual says only "sparsely".

Depiction: The map draws a few round green crowns on the bank beyond the reeds, mulberry and cudrania in turn, each left out
where it would fall on a roof, a wellhead, a yard's or a bed's sun, a lane, the crop or the water.

Name: A planted pond bank
