About: On a row village with farms on both sides of its road, each farm could keep its own grove on its windward sides,
whichever side of the road it stood. At Ogawa on the Musashino plateau, a farm settled in the Kanbun era (1661-1673) had
its road on the north, and still kept its grove north of its house, mostly zelkova and rather thin.

Guesses:
- That a row village takes this form or the leeward row sheltered by the grove row across its road at even odds: both are
  attested, and neither is counted against the other.

Depiction: The map draws every farm of the row with its own grove on its windward sides, on both sides of the street.
