About: Where the brook a hamlet tapped curved, the intake stood on the outside of the bend, on its concave bank, where the
current runs deep against the bank and silt does not settle. A modern irrigation manual puts an offtake on the outside of a
bend where no straight reach serves, and a Japanese torrent-river intake of 1974, sited just below the middle of a bend's
concave bank, took in little sediment; the old intakes without a dam opened a mouth on the concave side of a bend.

Guesses:
- That a hamlet's intake stands on a bend half the time: no source counts how often an intake was sited on a bend and how
  often on a straight reach.

Depiction: The map draws the brook's bend at the intake bowing away from the fields, the intake at its apex on the fields'
bank, so the brook runs down the fall just there.
