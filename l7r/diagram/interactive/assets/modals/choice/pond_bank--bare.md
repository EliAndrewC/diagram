About: A pond's bank could be left bare of trees. The same Song-dynasty manual that plants a bank with mulberry has the
cattle tread it firm, and today a pond's embankment is kept clear of grass and trees, mown and burned, so that it keeps its
strength. This hamlet's tail pond has a bare bank.

Guesses:
- That half of tail ponds have the bare bank and half the planted one: no account gives a share between them.
