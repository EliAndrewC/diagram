
## T227 builder, 2026-11-25 - new frictions
- `make rust-test CRATE="a b"` takes ONE crate (the two were joined into one manifest path); `make crate-checks CRATE="a b"` takes several.
- `make task-add` sat behind a running crate-checks (the rust run lock) past the 120 s foreground timeout; run it backgrounded or before a long check.
- `make crate-checks` reported exit 0 to the background notification while its log said `Error 1` (piped through the log redirect and `echo rc`); read the log's tail, not the notification.
- The armory audit test in rust/mode_a needed both gitignored magistracy renders (the known friction): `make compound SPEC=pool/magistracies/<m>/<m>.compound.json` for county-magistracy-example and ochiba-roundtrip-test, then closing-checks again.
