# Design notes: Hayakawa County Magistracy

**Subject**: seat of County Magistrate Hida no Reiji Hajime - Hayakawa county, Nagahara province, Reiji domain (Crab Clan). A rich river-adjacent county UPSTREAM of Nagahara provincial city; most barges stop down there. Validation instance of the generic magistrate's-manor program (every knob deliberately set differently from Ochiba).

**Program type**: magistrate's manor (county magistracy) - see `docs/buildings.md` "Compound programs".

**The magistrate** (from his Obsidian Portal record): Honor 1.5, XP 145 (young for the post), Permanent Wound, balding, judgmental, resentful about another's promotion, superstitious, sweaty. A low-honor incentive-driven official on one of the richest postings in the province, resentfully watching the barges glide past toward the prestigious city downstream. His synthesized backstory (in his OP GM-only notes, 2026-07, Claude-native): he was meant to sit the provincial ministry examinations the same spring the thaw-flood took the grain barge with his father (the previous magistrate) aboard; he leapt in after him and the current broke his leg against the revetment stones. He believes Hayakawa's wealth is borrowed from the river, which keeps its own ledger - his father died of arrears, so his bench punishes carelessness on the water savagely while coin settles dry-land cases. His cousin Hida no Reiji Fumito later took the ministry post he wanted (Governor Asuka never acknowledged his petition). The salt wards are Yasuki sea salt (never salt that touched the river), set by his own hand in strict order, main gate last - the night his father died, the landing-gate ward was found washed away though no rain had fallen. Full detail in the OP "Salt Wards" GM-only section.

## Knob settings

1. **Tier**: county magistracy (baseline).
2. **Posting wealth**: RICH - detached guest house, mature garden, fine timber. (The wealth shows in the residence side, not the bench: the hearing court itself is ordinary county size.)
3. **Granary weight**: STAGING NODE - single kura; the county's tax grain barges downstream to Nagahara city. The granary, a kura on posts, stands beside the tally office and the landing gate, and the landing steps are across the bank street from that gate - the shortest bale-haul.
4. **Garrison emphasis**: minimal (quiet rich county) - no watchtower, no armory; the river watch post at the landing is the security particular.

## The river logistics story

- Customs/tariffs are deliberately ABSENT: the Yasuki Taka tariff system collects at the provincial city gates downstream, NOT at county landings. This magistracy's river work is OUTBOUND - the tally office does barge manifests and seals; the river watch looks for gate-runners (bypass smuggling around Nagahara's tariff gates is the natural local crime).
- The tax barge is a HIRED town boatman's vessel under the magistracy's seals (offices owned no hulls); drawn at takasebune proportions (~47 x 7 ft).
- Stone revetment at the landing (swift river with a flood history - the river kami's altar in the shrine says so).
- The river band on the map is a margin crop of a wider river - tolerated; the drawn ~43 ft is the near-bank reach only.

The small square in the Fire Dragon shrine's corner is Ebisu's altar, kept from the shrine it was before.
- **torii**: The shrine's one torii, before the door of the shrine in the inner court's north-east corner.
- **ancestral alcove**: Hayakawa has stayed in Hajime's own line, so the alcove beside the reception holds the tablets of its past magistrates, his forebears.
- **bath**: Hajime enlarged the bath: at about 18 by 12 ft it is larger than a house this size would need.
- **guest quarters**: A rich posting, Hayakawa lodges its guests in a guest house of their own, in the annex the ninth magistrate added; it stands in the inner court, the private depth of the household, because its guests - imperial inspectors, the lineage's chancellors - outrank a county magistrate. They come and go by their own river door into the guest garden. The annex has no privy: the household staff serve its guests with chamber pots.
- **river**: The Hayakawa, the "swift river", has a history of floods. The county lies upstream of Nagahara city, and most barges on the river go on down to the city.
- **river landing**: The county's tax grain leaves for Nagahara city from here, carried out of the east landing gate and down the steps. No customs are collected at the landing: tariffs are taken at the city's gates downstream, where goods are sold.
- **residence**: The lineage has added to the house over generations: the later of its two halls holds the reception at its east end, with Hajime's rooms behind it.
- **garden**: A guest of rank takes no genkan here: from the middle gate a path of stepping stones crosses the garden to a stone at the reception's veranda.
- **latrine**: The family's privy stands at the corridor end, toward their own rooms. The servants' range keeps one at its west end, by the west service gate; the inner court has one in its north-west corner, reached from the kitchen postern, for the kitchen and the house's working servants by day.
- **hearing court**: Its floor is the river's own cobbles.
- **gatehouse**: The gate range is a long gatehouse standing in the wall line, with the main gate's passage through it toward its east end; the guardroom opens onto the passage from the west side, the gatekeepers lodge in the long room beyond it, and a store takes the east end.
- **cart yard**: Hayakawa has two: the open ground inside the west service gate, where the horses are led out and the carts stand, and the ground below the granary, where the bale carts wait their turn. They load at the granary's one door, on its north face, from the bale yard the practice ground doubles as.
- **residence corridor**: The corridor joins the two halls; the kitchen stands against the west block with no corridor.
- **granary**: Hayakawa's granary stands on posts beside the tally office, its one door toward the bale yard and the landing gate.
- **tax barge**: Bound for Nagahara city, carrying the county's grain under the office's seals.
- **river watch**: The watch looks out for gate-runners: cargo meant for sale in Nagahara city, put ashore upstream to be carried in around the city's tariff gates.
- **servants' quarters**: The menservants lodge in the range on the street front, west of the gate range, its doors on the outer court by the stable; the grooms keep the stable's own room; the maids and the kitchen hands sleep in the maids' room under the main roof, at the kitchen end. The senior retainers keep their own houses in town, and the barracks holds only the duty watch.
- **vegetable garden**: The bed stands west of the house, beside the kitchen yard, where nothing to the south shades it.
- **storehouse**: Hayakawa keeps two, on the north wall east of the house, behind the garden.
