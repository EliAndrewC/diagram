About: A sumo ring, the dohyo, was the ring the wrestlers met in. Sumo was offered at shrines across Japan, and a rite
consecrated the ring for the sumo offered there, in the same way as elsewhere; that rite is described only as it is performed today, with no date for its beginning. In Rokugan sumo bouts are held on the 30th day of the
seventh month, to protect the rice harvest and to honor the rice god.

What a village shrine's ring was like - how it was made, whether it stood all year or was made for the day - is not
recorded, nor when sumo at village shrines began.

How big a village's ring was, and how many village shrines kept one, are not recorded.
