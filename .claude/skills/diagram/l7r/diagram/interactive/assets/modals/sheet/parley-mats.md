About: The kneeling mats in Ubame's parley room, two on each side of the border that runs across its floor, facing each other
across the line, so that each party kneels on its own soil while they talk.

In Rokugan some parley rooms are laid out just so, the two sides kneeling on mats either side of a line marked across the floor.
History records no such room: where two powers dealt regularly across a border, each kept a post on its own side of the line, as
at Kyakhta between Russia and Qing China, or one kept a compound on the other's ground, as Tsushima's officers did at the Japan
House in Pusan.
