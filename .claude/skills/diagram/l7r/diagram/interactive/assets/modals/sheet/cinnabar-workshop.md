About: An open-sided workshop under a roof on posts, where the Fox Clan's threshold stones are painted with their fox-tracks in
cinnabar, the red mineral ground for vermilion. River-stones the size of two fists and the small lacquer bowls that become
Pact-Bowls are laid out to dry under its roof.

The County Magistrate of Ochiba is a Fox priest and keeps the Fox Clan's road wardings along the Imperial road, so the work of
making them is the shrine's as much as the office's: the workshop adjoins the compound's shrine hall rather than standing in the
service yard.

Guesses:
- Its size, about 37 by 24 ft, and its open sides: nothing in the setting gives the workshop a size or a form, so both are the
  plan's own.

Depiction: The plan hatches the workshop to show a roof over open sides, with a dot for each of its six posts; the stones and
bowls drying beneath it are their own feature.
