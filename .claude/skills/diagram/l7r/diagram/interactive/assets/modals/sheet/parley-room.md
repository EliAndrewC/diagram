About: A room built into the compound's border wall, with the Fox border running across its floor. The two sides meet here, each
kneeling on its own ground, and settle their business without either stepping onto the other's soil; a door on each side lets
each party in from its own lands.

In Rokugan certain negotiations take place in such rooms, built on a border so that each clan negotiates from its own domain
while still sipping tea together. Some seat the parties at a table set precisely on the line; in others the door that receives
the visiting delegation is itself the border; in others still the two sides kneel on mats either side of a line marked across
the floor.

No such room is known from history. Where two powers dealt regularly across a border, each kept a post on its own side, as Russia and
Qing China did at Kyakhta, or one kept a compound on the other's ground, as Tsushima's officers did at the Japan House
in Pusan, with the Korean side's banquet hall beside it.

Depiction: The plan builds the room into the border wall, astride the dashed line, with a door at each end; its kneeling mats are
their own feature.
