About: River-stones and small lacquer-black bowls laid out to dry under the roof of Ochiba's cinnabar workshop.

The stones are the Fox Clan's vermilion threshold stones in the making: river-stones the size of two fists, painted with cinnabar
fox-tracks, to be set along the Imperial road in and beside Fox lands. The bowls are Pact-Bowls, which are buried beneath the
stone at a County Magistrate's checkpoint beside those lands, holding rice, three copper coins and a tuft of fox fur, and renewed every seventh year in a ceremony attended by the magistrate
and a Kitsune emissary. The County
Magistrate of Ochiba, a Fox priest, keeps these road wardings, and the workshop beside the shrine is where they are made.

Depiction: The plan draws the stones as gray dots and the bowls as black ones, in a row under the workshop's north posts, each
larger than a two-fist stone or a small bowl would be so that it shows.
