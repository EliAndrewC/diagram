  2. kaifeng-flood-1642-enwiki - 「300,000 of the 378,000 residents were killed by the flood and ensuing peripheral disasters such as famine and plague.」 (English.   4. pan-gate-enwiki - 「Pan Gate at the southwest corner of the city's central historic district, with the water gate connecting the city's inner canals and former defensive moat with the nearby Grand Canal.」 (English; supports a water gate admitting a canal at Suzhou; that this is the only way water enters a wall is our inference, not on the page) back
