  • dosojin-jawiki - 「Dosojin are enshrined chiefly at the entrances and exits of villages, at passes, crossroads, the foot of bridges and other spatial and social boundaries, and their role was to stop defilement and disaster from coming in with people's movements.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「A god enshrined, chiefly in the form of a stone monument or stone statue, at a settlement's edge, in the village center, at the boundary between inside and outside the village, at crossroads, at three-way forks and similar places」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • matsumoto-dosojin - 「The total number of dōsojin stones in Matsumoto city exceeds 600」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「In Matsumoto city, most dōsojin are enshrined at the entrance to a settlement (the village boundary).
