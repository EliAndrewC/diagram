• atariya-nisho - 「Sun plants: they like a place in direct sunlight all day (about 6 hours or more), and do not grow in shade.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一日中（およそ６時間以上）直射日光が当たるところを好み、日陰では育ちません。」); and 「Half-shade plants: they like a place in direct sunlight for about half a day (about 3 to 4 hours)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「半日（およそ３～４時間）くらいは直射日光が当たるところを好み」); and of shade plants, 「they grow even on 1 to 2 hours of sunlight a day」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一日１～２時間の日照でも育ちます。」)
  • jasaga-hiatari - 「Those that like full sun: bitter melon, cucumber, tomato, chili, eggplant, green pepper, daikon, carrot, pumpkin, cabbage, maize, sweet potato, cauliflower, beans, and so on」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日当たりを好むもの ゴーヤ・キュウリ・トマト・トウガラシ・ナス・ピーマン・ダイコン・ニンジン・カボチャ・キャベツ・トウモロコシ・サツマイモ・カリフラワー・豆類など」)
