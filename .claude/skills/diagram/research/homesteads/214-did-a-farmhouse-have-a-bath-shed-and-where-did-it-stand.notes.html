  • mizu-no-bunka-11-furo - 「The target of Meiji-period housing improvement was the water areas; toilets and baths in particular became targets of improvement as unsanitary. Basically, because they are unsanitary, you separate them from the main house. Sometimes they are linked by a corridor, but sometimes they stand alone outdoors. Farmhouses are like that.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Oba Osamu, interviewed)
