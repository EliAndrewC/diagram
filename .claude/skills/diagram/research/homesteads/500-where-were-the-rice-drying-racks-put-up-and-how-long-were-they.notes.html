  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - of drying in heaps: 「it is seen in the regions along the Seto Inland Sea coast to begin with, and in northern Kyushu, the Chubu region, parts of the Tohoku region and so on」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「瀬戸内海沿岸地域をはじめ、北九州、中部地方、東北地方の一部などでみられる」)
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - of drying on stakes: 「stakes are set up in a row at fixed intervals on the paddy's bunds」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「田の畦(あぜ)に棒杭を一定の間隔に立て並べ」); 「from low regions of 1 to 2 meters to tall regions reaching 2 to 3 meters」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「1～2メートルの低い地方から2～3メートルに及ぶ高い地方もある」); 「common in eastern Japan from the Kinki region east to Hokkaido, especially the Tohoku region」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「近畿地方以東北海道までの東日本、とくに東北地方に多い」)
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「sheaves are hung on bamboo, poles, ropes and the like laid across」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「横に渡した竹、棒、綱などに稲束をかける」); 「sheaves can be dried even where the paddy floor is wet or in wet-paddy districts, and it is widely done in rainy regions throughout the country.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「田面が湿っている場合や湿田地帯でも稲束の乾燥ができ、全国的に雨の多い地域で広く行われる。」)
  • hasa-jawiki - 「Most are about a little above head height, where the ears are easy to hang, but some are built on a large scale, hung using a ladder, and they are mostly built on the fields after the harvest.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「稲穂などがかけやすい身長より少し上程度のものがほとんどであるが、中には梯子を使って掛けるような大掛かりなものも作られ、主に収穫後の田畑に作られることが多い。」)
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「Besides being built on the paddy, a multi-tier rack is sometimes built around the dwelling, the sheaves being carried there.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「多段架は田の上につくるほか、稲束を運んで住居の周りにつくる場合もある。」)
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「In the Niigata region, alders are planted beforehand on the bunds at fixed intervals, the lower branches are cleared, and their trunks are used as posts to build a multi-tier rack.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「新潟地方ではあらかじめ畦にハンノキを一定間隔で栽植しておき、下枝を払ってその幹を支柱として多段架を組む。」)
  • joetsu-yoneoka-hasagi - 「Hasa trees were used when drying the cut rice by hanging it, and were the ordinary landscape of Niigata prefecture's farmland.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「はさ木は、刈り取った稲を干して乾燥させるときに使われたもので、新潟県の田園の一般的な風景でした。」)
  • niigata-manganji-hasagi - 「it was made when, from 1943 (Showa 18) to 1945 (Showa 20), about 250 hectares of paddy in the district were consolidated, and 26 farm households concerned cooperated to transplant hasa trees from various places onto this farm road.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「昭和18（1943）年から20（1945）年にかけて同地区の水田約250ヘクタ－ルを区画整理したときに26軒の関係農家が協力して各地の稲架木をこの農道へ移植したものです。」)
