  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - of drying in heaps: 「it is seen in the regions along the Seto Inland Sea coast to begin with, and in northern Kyushu, the Chubu region, parts of the Tohoku region and so on」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - of drying on stakes: 「stakes are set up in a row at fixed intervals on the paddy's bunds」 (translated; ); 「from low regions of 1 to 2 meters to tall regions reaching 2 to 3 meters」 (translated; ); 「common in eastern Japan from the Kinki region east to Hokkaido, especially the Tohoku region」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「sheaves are hung on bamboo, poles, ropes and the like laid across」 (translated; ); 「sheaves can be dried even where the paddy floor is wet or in wet-paddy [shitsuden] districts, and it is widely done in rainy regions throughout the country.」 (translated; )
  • hasa-jawiki - 「Most are about a little above head height, where the ears are easy to hang, but some are built on a large scale, hung using a ladder, and they are mostly built on the fields after the harvest.」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「Besides being built on the paddy, a multi-tier rack is sometimes built around the dwelling, the sheaves being carried there.」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「In the Niigata region, alders are planted beforehand on the bunds at fixed intervals, the lower branches are cleared, and their trunks are used as posts to build a multi-tier rack.」 (translated; )
  • joetsu-yoneoka-hasagi - 「Hasa trees were used when drying the cut rice by hanging it, and were the ordinary landscape of Niigata prefecture's farmland.」 (translated; )
  • niigata-manganji-hasagi - 「it was made when, from 1943 (Showa 18) to 1945 (Showa 20), about 250 hectares of paddy in the district were consolidated, and 26 farm households concerned cooperated to transplant hasa trees from various places onto this farm road.」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「long ones reach as much as 100 meters, and besides straight ones there are zigzag and コ-shaped [three-sided] ones and so on. Depending on the region there are ones of two or three tiers, and there are also regions that build them into tall wall-like structures of 10 to 15 tiers」 (translated; ) (of the single-tier rack, then the multi-tier ones)
  • kotobank-hasa-nipponica - 「it is said that rice dried slowly and thoroughly, usually over 20 to 30 days, turns out of good quality and taste」 (translated; ) (of drying the sheaves before threshing)
