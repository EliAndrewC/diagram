  • toyoko-kaki - 「Miyazaki Yasusada, an agronomist of the Edo period, is said to have encouraged the planting of persimmon trees around the house and homestead.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代の農学者　宮崎安貞は、家屋敷の周りに柿の木を植える事を奨励したとの事。」)
  • toyoko-kaki - 「In summer it casts shade over the house, in autumn its turning leaves are beautiful, birds do not even build nests in it, and insects do not settle on it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「夏は家に木陰を作り、秋の紅葉は美しい、鳥が巣を作る事もなく、虫はつかない。」)
