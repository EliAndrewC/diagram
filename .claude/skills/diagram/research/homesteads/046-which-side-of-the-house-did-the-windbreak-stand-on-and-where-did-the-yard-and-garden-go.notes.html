• yashikirin-jawiki - 「On the west side and the south side, tall and sturdy trees, chiefly sugi, were planted in preparation against wind and snow. On the south side there were the storehouse (kura), the barn (naya) and the like, and fruit-bearing plants such as fig, grape and persimmon, and bamboo and the like, were planted.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the page's passage on the Tonami plain: on the south were the storehouse and barn, with fruiting plants - fig, grape, persimmon - and bamboo.)
  • tonami-yashikirin-haichi - 「Looking at which trees grow on which side of the homestead, we found that from the south round to the west of the house there are many tall trees such as sugi, kashi and keyaki, and that from the west round to the north sugi, enoki, alder and the like are often mixed with bamboo. And on the east side, which is the front of the house, there is little grove, and we often saw flowering trees and persimmon, peach and the like planted there.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • tonami-yashikirin-haichi - 「It held off the strong south wind that blows in early spring and the cold seasonal wind of winter.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
