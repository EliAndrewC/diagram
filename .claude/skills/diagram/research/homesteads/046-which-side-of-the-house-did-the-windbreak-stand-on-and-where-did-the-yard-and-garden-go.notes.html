• yashikirin-jawiki - 「On the west side and the south side, tall and sturdy trees, chiefly sugi, were planted in preparation against wind and snow. On the south side there were the storehouse (kura), the barn (naya) and the like, and fruit-bearing plants such as fig, grape and persimmon, and bamboo and the like, were planted.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「西側や南側には風や雪に備えて杉を中心に背の高く丈夫な木が植えられていた。南側には蔵や納屋などがあり、無花果や葡萄、柿などの果実がなる植物や竹などが植えられていた。」) (the page's passage on the Tonami plain: on the south were the storehouse and barn, with fruiting plants - fig, grape, persimmon - and bamboo.)
  • tonami-yashikirin-haichi - 「Looking at which trees grow on which side of the homestead, we found that from the south round to the west of the house there are many tall trees such as sugi, kashi and keyaki, and that from the west round to the north sugi, enoki, alder and the like are often mixed with bamboo. And on the east side, which is the front of the house, there is little grove, and we often saw flowering trees and persimmon, peach and the like planted there.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷のどの方向にどんな木が生えているかを調べてみると、家の南側から西側にかけては、スギ、カシ、ケヤキなどの高木が多く、西側から北側には、スギ、エノキ、ハンノキなどにタケが混ざっていることが多いと分かりました。また、家の正面に当たる東側には屋敷林が少なく、花木やカキ、モモなどが植えられているのを多く見かけました。」)
  • tonami-yashikirin-haichi - 「It held off the strong south wind that blows in early spring and the cold seasonal wind of winter.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「それは、春先に吹く強い南風や冬の冷たい季節風を防いでくれました。」)
