• shimogoe-jawiki - 「For rotting down to ripeness, a container from which the liquid cannot leak is used - such as a jar buried in the ground, or a pit finished with plaster (the koedame).」 (translated; ); 「Fresh night soil is harmful to crops, and when it is to be used as fertilizer it is stored for a fixed period and left to rot to ripeness.」 (translated; ) (For ripening, a vessel that does not leak is used - a jar buried in the ground or a plastered pit (the koedame); fresh night soil is harmful to crops and is stored for a period to ripen before use as fertilizer.)
  • jawiki-koedame - 「A pit, or else a largish water jar. The pit kind resembles a well in appearance. The jar kind is often unglazed earthenware, and one of about 1 to 1.5 meters in mouth diameter is buried in the ground and used.」 (translated; ) (the pit looks like a well; the jar is usually unglazed, about 1-1.5 m across the mouth, buried in the ground for use.)
