Did a farmhouse have a bath shed, and where did it stand?

Sources: mizumaki-goemonburo, sugiura-1973-fuzoku (the rates)

The bath shed is one of the farmstead's fixtures the GM asked to see drawn; the whole set, and how many of each the map draws, is at The farmstead's fixtures.

The bath shed - its use attested, its placement not found. Mizumaki museum: the goemon-buro "widely used in farm villages centered on self-sufficiency" (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「自給自足を中心とした農村で多く使われた」); that it spread into western-Japan homes in the late Edo is a GUESS that no readable page supports. Sugiura: 0.29 bath SHEDS per household over all houses, and a bath INSIDE the house in 20 of 38 pre-1944 houses - two forms, so only the shed share is drawn (on 20-45% of homesteads) and the rest bathe indoors, undrawn. Where the shed stood no source says: seated at the back wall or a flank (GUESS), 6 x 6 ft (GUESS).
