How much open ground does a kitchen garden keep to its south and east?

Sources: daikon-jawiki; derived from the threshing-yard entry

This question is the shade a garden takes from the houses around it and from a neighbor's grove. The GM's note that prompted it, and the shade the village's windbreak belt throws on the gardens in the afternoon, are in The garden's sun, and how far the windbreak shades.

The garden takes the yard's corridor - one number, not two. A dooryard garden's binding season is the shoulder month too: daikon (and, in this page's reading, the other autumn greens) is in the ground under the same 28 deg 9am sun the threshing yard works under, so a minka's ~20 ft ridge shades a bed 39 ft off exactly as it shades a yard. The growing-season figure is shorter (a 60-70 deg noon sun in the 4th-7th months throws 7-12 ft), so the shoulder month binds, as it does for the yard. Rule: 39 ft of clear ground south of every bed, whichever is placed first, the bed or the house that would shade it. The bed may stand on any of four sides of its house (SE/SW/E/W), so a bed that the SE side would put in shadow moves to the other flank.

And the morning sun, which a neighbor's grove takes. The bed sits on a sheltered flank, and a neighbor's windward belt stands against that neighbor's west wall - which is to say just EAST of our bed, in the way of the rising sun. Morning light is what a kitchen bed lives on, and the fix is placement rather than felling a windbreak: the GM asked in 2026-07 to "move it a bit south". Two things keep the east open. The communal copse keeps a sun lane to its east as well as to its south, so no clump of it is ever seated directly east of a bed; and once every homestead grove is drawn, an east-shaded bed is nudged a little south into open ground, so the trees fall to its northeast instead. It is a best effort and not a slot - most farms keep the plain east bed, and a bed hemmed in to the south by paddy, lane or neighbor stays where it is - so the rule is broken only where a clear southward shift existed and was not taken.
