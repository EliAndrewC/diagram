• kotobank-yoriai - 「The meeting was often held at the house of a village official or office-holder such as the nanushi, the shoya or the ward head, but in the Kinki region facilities called kaisho or kaigisho were set up from early times.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寄合は名主，庄屋あるいは区長などの村役人や役職者の家を会場とすることが多かったが，近畿地方では古くから会所（かいしよ）とか会議所と呼ばれる施設が設けられていた。」)「The kaisho was often within the precinct of the village's tutelary shrine, suggesting a tradition of deciding matters before the gods.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「会所はしばしば村の鎮守の境内にあり，神前でものごとを決める伝統をうかがわせる。」) (the Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry, revised edition, by Fukuta Ajio)
  • kotobank-mura-yoriai - 「For the place of the village meeting, in old times the office-house of a village official, or a temple, a shrine or a nenbutsu hall and the like were used, and there were places that held it at the festival lodging after the festival, but gradually meeting halls and the like became general.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村寄合の場には古くは村役人の役宅か寺院神社念仏堂などを用い、「祭宿」で祭事後開く所もあったが、しだいに集会所の類が一般的となっていった。」) (the Nihon Daihyakka Zensho entry by Takeuchi Toshimi)
  • kotobank-mura-yoriai - 「The full members of the village meeting were the representatives (the heads) of the households, the basic units that made up the mura; all were required to attend as a duty and to join in the deliberation and decision, and attendance by proxy was permitted only to an adult heir.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村寄合の「正員」は、「ムラ」構成の基本単位である「家」の代表者（家長）で、全員、義務として出席し、協議決定に加わることが要請され、成人の「跡取り」に限って代理出席が許された。」)「The matters discussed were the year's plan for running the shared life, with its budget and accounts, and urgent matters as they arose; the regular meeting was usually held twice a year, and in an emergency one was held as needed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「協議事項は、協同生活運行の年間計画とその予算決算や臨時的な重要事項で、定例会は通例年2回、緊急な場合には臨時に開かれた。」)
  • kotobank-mura-yoriai - 「The early-modern village meeting was convened under the lead of the village officials (the leading men) and valued as the occasion for deliberating, deciding or announcing everything in the shared life - paying the tax, handling the village's charges, the village festival and more - and it was made up mainly of the honbyakusho stratum, but later it seems generally to have come about that the komae, hirakata and mizunomi also took part as their strength grew.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「近世の村寄合は村役人（重立衆）の主導のもとに参集され、貢租納入、村掛費用の処理そのほか村祭礼などの協同生活万般にわたる協議決定あるいは示達の場面として重視され、「本百姓」層主体の構成であったが、のちには「小前(こまえ)」「平方(ひらかた)」「水呑(みずのみ)」も力を増すまま参加する形勢を一般に呈してきたようである。」)
  • citang-zhwiki - 「The ancestral hall, also called the family temple, the ancestors' hall or the public hall, is a shrine founded on the lineage system for enshrining and sacrificing to the ancestors; it also serves for the family's publicity, for enforcing the clan's rules and household law, and for deliberation and feasting.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「祠堂，又称家庙、祖堂、公厅，是基於宗法制度，用于供奉和祭祀祖先的祠，其又具有从事家族宣传、执行族规家法、议事宴饮的功能。」)
