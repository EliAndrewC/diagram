Where in the village did the headman live?

Sources: kotobank-shoya (the office held by an old family), kusawake-jawiki (the founding houses and their standing), l7r-median-domain (the setting's canon)

In the setting, in the district's main village. A village district's headman lives in its main village, never in one of its hamlets.

In the old family's house. A headman was sometimes chosen by election or agreement of the landholding farmers, but in most cases an old family of the village held the office by inheritance. The houses said to have founded a village - whether or not they truly did - held the village offices such as headman generation after generation - and where they did not, still spoke as the head house of several branch houses - and held the first place at the village's places of worship and in the use of its shared land and water.

Where that house stood, nothing read says. No page read places the headman's house at the village's center, at its oldest spot, beside its shrine or on its road. That the founding house is likely to be among the first houses built, and so near the village's oldest ground, is this page's reading of the two findings above, not a statement of any source.

What this means for the map. Where the headman's house stands on a map is a GUESS. The reading above makes the settlement's oldest cluster, where one can be told, the likeliest place for it; nothing read supports putting it on the road for the road's sake or beside the shrine.
