Why does a persimmon stand beside so many farmhouses?

Sources: toyoko-kaki, uekipedia-kaki

The persimmon is one of the farmstead's fixtures the GM asked to see drawn; the whole set, and how many of each the map draws, is at The farmstead's fixtures.

The persimmon. "so a persimmon tree is planted in every dooryard" (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「だからどこの庭先にも柿の木が植えてある」) (toyoko-housing) - hence a persimmon in every dooryard; and "the Edo-period agronomist Miyazaki Yasusada is said to have encouraged planting persimmon trees around the house lot" (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代の農学者　宮崎安貞は、家屋敷の周りに柿の木を植える事を奨励したとの事」) - the Edo agronomist Miyazaki Yasusada is said to have urged planting them round the homestead; "in summer it makes shade for the house" (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「夏は家に木陰を作り」) - it shades the house in summer, so it stands BESIDE the house (which side, a GUESS: the flank, then a front or back corner). Height 3-20 m (uekipedia); crown width - no source gives one - drawn 18 ft across (GUESS); drawn on 80-95% of homesteads. Its fruit dots are a RENDERING convention naming the tree, not a season.
