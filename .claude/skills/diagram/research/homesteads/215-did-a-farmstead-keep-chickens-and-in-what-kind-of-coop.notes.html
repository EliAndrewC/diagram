  • qimin-yaoshu-yangji - 「For the chickens' roost it is best to set a cage on the ground with a perch placed inside. Although their crowing is not clear, they are safe and settled and fatten easily, and they are also spared the menace of foxes. If they are left to the trees, then at one encounter with wind and cold the big ones will be harmed and grow thin, and the small ones may die.」 (translated from the Classical Chinese by this project; original: 「雞棲宜㧻地為籠内著棧雖鳴聲不朗而安穏易肥又免狐狸之患若在之樹林一遇風寒大者損瘦小者或死」) (though their crowing is less clear, they are safe and fatten easily and are spared the fox; left to the trees, one spell of wind and cold and the big ones waste, the small ones may die.)
  • pitt-zhengzhou-coop - "The square-shaped structure had six niche-like openings in the west wall, with some eggshell fragments found inside them" (the source's own English; no dimensions on the page) / "a chicken coop dating to the late Ming Dynasty (c. early 17th century)" / "during a salvage archaeological excavation in Zhengzhou, China"
  • zhwiki-liuchu - 「Refers to these six kinds of fowl and livestock: horse, ox, sheep, chicken, dog and pig.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「指馬、牛、羊、雞、狗、豬這六种禽畜」)
