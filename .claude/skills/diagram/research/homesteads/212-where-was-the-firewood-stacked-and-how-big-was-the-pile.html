Sources: boso-no-mura-kigoya, sugiura-1973-fuzoku (the rate), 326woods-stack (the stack's height)

The woodpile is one of the farmstead's fixtures the GM asked to see drawn; the whole set, and how many of each the map draws, is at The farmstead's fixtures.

The woodpile - the shed attested, the stack's wall a GUESS. The Boso-no-Mura reconstructed farmstead lists a 木小屋: "a building for storing the charcoal, firewood and the like that are used as fuel" (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「燃料として使う、炭やたきぎなどを収めておく建物です」) (a building holding the charcoal and firewood used as fuel); where the pile stood relative to the house the page does not say. Sugiura counts 0.76 firewood SHEDS per pre-1944 household; the open stack under the eaves is the simpler form and the one drawn (that it is cheaper and older is this page's reading): 10 x 3.5 ft in plan, split logs stacked head-high - the 1.5 m height is modern stacking practice (326-woods, a modern page; that the older stack stood as high is a GUESS). Wall: the back wall or the kura's outer wall, out of the rain - a GUESS; drawn on 75-95% of homesteads.
