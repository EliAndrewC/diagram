  • ndl-crd-shakkogyu - 「In Sanuki cattle were mainly used for cultivation. At the June and November cultivation times draft cattle ran short, so there was a custom of borrowing cattle for that period from neighboring Awa. ... The mountain villages of Awa have much grassland, so cattle are easily grazed and they kept more plow cattle than their paddies needed, which is why they lent cattle to Sanuki in the busy farming season. They were called kometori-ushi (rice-taking cattle) because the cattle were lent and one koku of rice was taken in return. ... At times, as many as several hundred cattle came over the passes on the Awa-Sanuki border.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「讃岐では耕作に主として牛を使用していた。６月と11月の耕作時には耕牛が不足するため、となりの阿波からその期間牛を借入れる風習があった。 ... 阿波の山村には草生地が多いので、牧牛がし易いため水田に比して耕牛を多く飼っていたので農繁期の讃岐へ牛を貸すわけである。 ... コメトリウシといわれたのは牛を貸してかわりに米１石をもらって行くからである。 ... 多いと きは阿讃の国境の峠を越えて数百頭の牛がきていた。」)
