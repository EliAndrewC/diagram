• kotobank-minka - 「In the Edo period, the state of farming villages near Utsunomiya in the Kanto region becomes clear from records in which, when the Tokugawa shogun made his pilgrimage to the Toshogu at Nikko, floor plans of every house in the village were drawn up to lodge his retainers.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代に入って、関東地方の宇都宮近郊の農村のようすが、徳川将軍が日光の東照宮に参詣(さんけい)したときに、家来たちの宿泊のために村中の家の平面図を書き出させた史料から、明らかになる。」)「The eighteenth-century records among them show that in the villages of the time houses of around 20 tsubo (about 66 square meters) were the most common, there were also houses of 4 to 5 tsubo, and many small houses of under 10 tsubo, while large houses were few」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「それらのうちの18世紀の史料をみると、当時の農村は20坪（約66平方メートル）前後の家がもっとも多く、4～5坪の家もあり、10坪に満たない小さな家も多かったのに対して、大きな家はわずかであった」) (the Nipponica entry by Hirai Kiyoshi; the feet are this page's conversion)
