• chimei-jawiki - 「Making place names from the features of the terrain is one of the many common ways of naming. It is especially common for small-scale terrain and for names given in old times.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「For example, naming a place X-tani if it is a valley, or X-ga-oka if it is a hill」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「many have several competing theories with none settled, or can only be called entirely unknown」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • yaji-tani-sawa - 「The 谷 place names recorded number 20,422, amounting to 3.95% of all place names recorded nationwide.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「On the other hand, 沢 place names number 21,746, amounting to 4.21% of place names nationwide.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「Place names with 谷 read tani or dani number 13,484, and most of them are in western Japan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「Place names with 谷 read ya are common on the Pacific side of eastern Japan; as terrain, they are common where shallow valleys cut into uplands or low mountains, but 22% are in western Japan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「For place names ending in 沢, the concentration in eastern Japan, counting as far as the four eastern Chubu prefectures, is 95.7%; adding the three prefectures of Toyama, Aichi and Gifu, it is 97.5%.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「Today sawa means a mountain valley in eastern Japan, and a marsh in western Japan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (read from the scanned PDF; the originals are this project's transcription of the scan)
