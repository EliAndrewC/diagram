• chimei-jawiki - 「Making place names from the features of the terrain is one of the many common ways of naming. It is especially common for small-scale terrain and for names given in old times.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「地形の特徴から地名を作るのは、数多い一般的な命名方法である。特に小範囲の地形、古い時代に付けられた地名に多い。」); 「For example, naming a place X-tani if it is a valley, or X-ga-oka if it is a hill」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「たとえば地形が谷だと○○谷としたり、丘だと「〇〇が丘」などとする命名法である。」); 「many have several competing theories with none settled, or can only be called entirely unknown」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「諸説あって定まらないもの、まったく不明とするしかないものも多い。」)
  • yaji-tani-sawa - 「Place names with 谷 read tani or dani number 13,484, and most of them are in western Japan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「谷タニ・ダニ」地名は13,484件でその大半は西日本にある。」); 「Place names with 谷 read ya are common on the Pacific side of eastern Japan; as terrain, they are common where shallow valleys cut into uplands or low mountains, but 22% are in western Japan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「谷ヤ」地名は東日本の太平洋側に多く、地形的には台地や低い山地を刻む浅い谷が入っているところに多いが、西日本にも22%ある。」); 「For place names ending in 沢, the concentration in eastern Japan, counting as far as the four eastern Chubu prefectures, is 95.7%; adding the three prefectures of Toyama, Aichi and Gifu, it is 97.5%.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「＊沢」地名では中部地方東部4県までの東日本の集中率が95.7%になり、さらに富山・愛知・岐阜の3県を加えると東日本の集中率が97.5%になる。」); 「Today sawa means a mountain valley in eastern Japan, and a marsh in western Japan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「沢は現在、東日本では渓谷を、西日本では沼沢を指す。」) (read from the scanned PDF; the originals are this project's transcription of the scan)
