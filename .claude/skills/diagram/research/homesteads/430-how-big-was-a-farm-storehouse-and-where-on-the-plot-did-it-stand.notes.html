  • boso-no-mura-kazusa - 「Dozo: a gable-entry two-story dozo whose whole exterior, roof included, is earth wall」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「土蔵 妻入り二階建ての土蔵で、屋根を含めた外壁全体を土壁とし」)「Because of the 'okiyane' structure, in which a tiled roof sits on top of the earth-walled roof, the gap between them regulates temperature and humidity.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「土壁の屋根の上に瓦葺きの屋根が乗る「置き屋根」構造のため、間の隙間により温湿度の調整が図られることになります。」)「It reproduces a headman-class farmhouse by traditional building methods.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「名主クラスの農家を伝統的な工法で再現しています。」)
  • sekkei-sya-sayama-kura - 「As is often said, a kura is built when money has accumulated.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「蔵はよく言われるように、お金が貯まったら、建てられる。」)「So a kura is built as an ancillary building of the main house.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「だから蔵は主屋の付属棟として建築される。」)「A site is probably set aside for it, but conditions change before it is actually built, so various placements arise, in front of the main house or behind it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「予定地として決めておくのであろうが、実際に建てるまでには条件が変ってくるので、主屋の前に置いたり裏に置いたりいろいろな配置が生ずる。」)「In areas with a seasonal wind there tend to be few examples built touching the main house on its leeward side, but they are placed regardless of east, west, south or north.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「季節風のある地域では主屋の風下側に接して造る例は少ない傾向であるが、東西南北に関係なく配置されている。」)
