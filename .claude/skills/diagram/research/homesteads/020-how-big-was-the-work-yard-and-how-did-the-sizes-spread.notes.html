  • kitamoto-mushiro-niwa - 「At harvest time straw mats (mushiro) were spread over the whole of the yard, and the size of the yard was often expressed by the number of these mats. Incidentally, two mats correspond to one tsubo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「収穫期には庭一面にムシロが敷かれ、しばしば庭の広さはこのムシロの枚数で表現された。麦の耕作面積と関係していて、普通四〇〜六〇枚、中には百枚を越す家もあった。ちなみに、ムシロ二枚が一坪にあたる。」) (40-60 mats usually, over 100 for some; two mats to a tsubo (the tsubo figures are the record's arithmetic on this).)
