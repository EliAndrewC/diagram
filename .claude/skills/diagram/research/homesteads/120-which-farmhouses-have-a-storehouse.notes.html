• dozo-jawiki - 「They were built in great numbers to prevent fire and theft, and later also became a symbol of affluence」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「火災や盗難防止のために盛んに建てられ、後に裕福さの象徴ともなった」); kotobank-dozo - 「Pillars are set at intervals of about one meter, a ground of round bamboo and palm rope is made outside them, and wall earth is applied many times over to a thickness of 20 to 30 centimeters, finished on top with lime plaster or the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「柱を約1メートル間隔に立て、その外側に丸竹としゅろ縄で下地をつくり、壁土を何回も塗って20～30センチメートルの厚さとし、その上を漆喰(しっくい)などで仕上げる」) (that only the well-off had one is this page's reading; the Sugiura count in the naya-jawiki note gives the prevalence)
  • sekkei-sya-sayama-kura - 「As is often said, a kura is built when money has accumulated.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「蔵はよく言われるように、お金が貯まったら、建てられる。」)「However, it is said that in this area minka came to own dozo probably only from around the bakumatsu, so the history is relatively short.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ただ、この地域で民家が土蔵を持つようになるのは、おそらく幕末頃からで歴史は比較的浅いといわれる。」) (an architect's survey of Sunagawa-shinden, western Musashi; the bakumatsu is the last years of the shogunate)
  • naya-jawiki - 「In a farmhouse it means a shed in which crops and farm implements and the like are kept.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「農家では農作物や農機具などを収納しておく小屋を指す。」); doma-jawiki - 「In farmhouses the doma was a space for taking meals after farm work, for drying crops, and for weaving straw sandals」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「農家では農作業後に土間で食事をとったり、作物を乾燥させたり、わらじを編んだりする空間だった」); sugiura-1973-fuzoku - 「Table 5.
