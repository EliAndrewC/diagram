• kayabuki-jawiki - 「Basically the roof is made a steeply pitched one in order to prevent rain leaks, but」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「基本的に雨漏りを防止するために急勾配の屋根にするが」)「When the stems and the like of the plants used are thick, the gaps become larger and rain leaks come more easily, so a steeper pitch is required.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「使用する植物の茎などが太いと隙間が大きくなり雨漏りがしやすいため、より急勾配が要求される。」) (Basically the roof is made steep to prevent leaks; when the stems used are thick the gaps widen and leaks come easier, so a steeper pitch is required.)
  • gassho-jawiki - 「Building in the gassho manner produces a broad space in the roof loft free of king posts」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「合掌造りにすることで屋根裏に小屋束のない広い空間が生まれる」); gassho-jawiki - 「It led to two or three levels of space being secured in the loft, which, besides being a measure against heavy snow, suited sericulture as well」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋根裏に二層もしくは三層の空間を確保することにつながり、豪雪への対策以外に養蚕業にとっても都合が良いものであった」) (the page gives snow and rain as the reason for the steep pitch, and the loft's demand as what made the gassho roof steeper still)
