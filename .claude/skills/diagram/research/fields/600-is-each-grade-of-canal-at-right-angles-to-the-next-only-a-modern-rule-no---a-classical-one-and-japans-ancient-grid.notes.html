• zhouli-zhushu-suiren - 「Drawn with the fields running south, the sui runs lengthwise and the gou crosswise, the xu lengthwise and the hui crosswise; nine hui make a chuan, which surrounds the outside.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「以南畝圖之，則遂從溝橫，洫從澮橫，九澮而川，周其外焉。」)「Where the fields are divided finely north-south, each row is one fu; every ten fu there is a crosswise gou at the head. Ten gou make a hundred fu, and on the east side there is a north-south xu. Every ten xu there is a crosswise hui on the south side, and nine hui make the great chuan on all four sides.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「其田南北細分者，是一行隔為一夫，十夫則於首為橫溝。十溝即百夫，於東畔為南北之洫。十洫則於南畔為橫澮，九澮則於四畔為大川。」) (the first is the note on the Suiren passage, the second the sub-commentary on it; sui, gou, xu, hui and chuan are the channels of the ladder from the smallest up, and a fu one farmer's allotment. The same edition's Wikisource index page, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%91%A8%E7%A6%AE%E8%A8%BB%E7%96%8F, files it under 「Han dynasty, Tang dynasty, Zheng Xuan, Jia Gongyan」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「漢朝 唐朝 鄭玄 賈公彥」))
