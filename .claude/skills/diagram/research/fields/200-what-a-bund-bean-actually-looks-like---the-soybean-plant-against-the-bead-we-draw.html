What a bund bean actually looks like - the soybean plant against the bead we draw

Sources: nabunken-azemame, wikipedia-soybean, cropfarming-soybeans

What the research found. The practice, from the Nara National Research Institute for Cultural Properties' Asuka pages: 「They are soybeans, also called "azemame" (畦豆, bund beans)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「『畦豆（あぜまめ）』とも呼ばれる大豆です」) and 「Azemame are sown as seed on the bund after the rice is transplanted, and are harvested at the same time as the rice cutting」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦豆は田植え後に畦に種がまかれ、稲刈りと同時に収穫されます」) - a soybean sown on the bund after transplanting and harvested with the rice; once grown all over Japan, mostly gone with land consolidation and herbicide, still grown at Asuka; the photo caption has them 「azemame growing in a long unbroken row along the bund of a paddy where the rice cutting too is near」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「稲刈りも近い田んぼの畦にずらっと育っている畦豆」) - growing in a row along the bund. The plant: "fully mature soybean plants are generally between 50 and 125 cm (20 and 50 in) in height" (Wikipedia, Soybean, Description); "The plant is erect and bushy, with branches coming off a central stem" and "The leaflets are broad, pointed, and medium green" (cropfarming.org - a trade site, the one page read that states the leaf color; Wikipedia's Description says nothing about color). Not found: a figure for one plant's spread on the bund - the Iowa State extension page on row spacing discusses canopy-closure timing, not plant width - so the record does NOT say how wide a bund bean stands. Not used, because only search snippets carry them: azemame roots firming the bund, miso from azemame in Noto, and nitrogen to the paddy (the last contradicted by 'Nitrogen - a flooded paddy makes its own' above).

The convention, stated for the reader. We draw each plant as a round bead about 3 ft across, in a single row on the bund - and at least two beads on any stretch of bund that carries them, because a single bead does not read as a row of anything - in a deep pine green chosen so the beads read against the pale rice; real foliage is medium green, and the bead is drawn darker than the plant. The plant is a knee-to-waist-high bush (50-125 cm); the bead says only where it stands. The GM chose the color on 2026-08-15 from a rendered ladder of three candidates, because the old olive sat between the rice green and the bund brown and vanished at map zoom; the two darker candidates on the ladder were declined with it, a hunter green that grayed out and a forest green that read as black against the near-black bund stroke.
