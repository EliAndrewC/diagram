• satoyama-levee-grassland - 「Farmers generally maintain levee grasslands by periodic mowing or herbicide application.」「In the studied paddies, farmers managed grasses on the levees by mowing two to five times per year and no herbicide was used.」 (the source's own English; the paper's whole subject is the semi-natural grassland a paddy levee carries, and mowing two to five times a year is the measure of how hard it grows); nabunken-azemame - 「Azemame are sown as seed on the bund after the rice is transplanted, and are harvested at the same time as the rice cutting」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦豆は田植え後に畦に種がまかれ、稲刈りと同時に収穫されます」) (so the beans stand on the bund through the season the paddies are drawn in).
