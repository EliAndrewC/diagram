Plot sizes, pond sizing and acreage from population

Sources: kokudaka-jawiki (one koku a year; real stipends ~1.8), gokogomin-kotobank (40% then 50% - the "~45%" is the midpoint), kokumori-jawiki, kochi-seiri-jawiki, king-forty-centuries

  • Plots: pre-modern 0.02-0.25 acre, irregular, about 9 scattered plots per household (the ~9 is unsourced) (fragmented holdings were normal).11029 STRAIGHT rectangular channels/plots are post-1900 consolidation (the Tedori fan's ditches were only straightened in the early 1900s)30 - the organic waver is period-correct.111

    Which figure is which, because two different things get called a plot. A leveled, diked CELL is one basin that has to hold water at an even depth, and that is physically small: the maps target about 0.05 acre for it (roughly 200 m2, a 47 ft square), derived from real feet at whatever scale the sheet is drawn to, so a basin is the same real size on a hamlet map and on a city map. The older drawing, still used by the maps that have not been rebuilt on the water-first engine, subdivides a field recursively to a PARCEL grain instead - about 0.10 acre at the finer setting the villages use, about 0.19 acre at its default. All three figures sit inside the 0.02-0.25 acre band above, and neither grain is a legibility inflation: the coarser one is a parcel, which a holding really was, and the finer one is the cell the water-first engine derives. A parcel subdivides into several cells on any slope, which is why the cell sits below the surveyed-parcel figures.

  • Pond sizing (the rule): sole-storage tameike run ~2,000-2,500 m3 of storage per irrigated ha (typical depth 2-4 m); a STREAM-FED pond refilling 1-2x a season is comfortable at ~1,200-1,500 m3/ha.109 Hoshigaoka: 31.8 ha of paddy gives about 1.5 ha of pond surface, roughly 47,000 m3 at about 3 m deep, or about 1,470 m3/ha, plus a feeder stream. Pond area is kept proportional to command area.

  • Acreage from population (the sizing rule): a person eats ~1 koku/yr;31 the assessed rate for middling paddy ~1.3 koku/tan (the middle-grade kokumori, a tax rate rather than a measured yield);108 coarse grain fills part of the diet while ~45% of rice goes to tax32 -> ~0.8-1.0 tan gross paddy per person -> 350 inhabitants ~ 280-350 tan = 69-86 acres (+ dry margins later). Kikuta lands at ~79 acres / ~600 plots.
