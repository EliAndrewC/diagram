Where do the samurai estates sit when the paddy has the near ring?

Sources: the GM's setting notes on land tenure and the samurai country estate; the GM's ruling on approach lanes.

The fans claim the near ring BEFORE any country estate is placed, and the gentry keep the pockets left over - most of their estates stand farther out in the rural district, off the sheet altogether. This rests on the GM's setting notes rather than on the historical record. It means a city map showing as few as ONE estate, even one whose grounds run off the frame, is the honest signal rather than an omission; a ring of estates crowding the wall would be the error.

The decision: estate DRIVEWAYS are not drawn. The GM ruled them out because the long worn approach lanes read on the sheet as fat roads laid across the countryside, and the drawing already held that an approach lane is not a thing drawn at this scale. The manors therefore stand as clean walled compounds with their approaches implied off the map.

The rule the map follows: a CITY map shows at least one samurai estate; one of them cut by the frame edge is legitimate and says its land continues beyond. Estates cluster on the side of the map that faces the capital, stand at least about 600 ft apart so they never read as a row, and vary in size. No estate is drawn with a driveway.
