• warichi-kotobank - 「In the Edo period, dividing a village's farmland by the number of cultivators and allotting it, having each farm it for a fixed period, and re-dividing it when the term ended. A system carried out to even out the tax burden and the quality of the soil. It was practiced where production was unstable, such as land damaged by rivers.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代、一村の農地を耕作者の数に区分して割り当て、一定期間各自に耕作させ、満期の後に分割しなおすこと。貢租の負担や地味の良否を平均化するために行なわれる制度。川欠地など生産力の不安定なところで行なわれた。」) (精選版 日本国語大辞典, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten)
  • warichi-seido-kotobank - 「It is divided into several groups of land (danchi, wari) by paddy or dry field, soil quality, terrain, the expected frequency of floods and so on. Next it is gathered into the number of shares (kuji) the village has set. In doing so each group of land is split into that number of shares, and one from each is combined to make one share. Lots are then drawn by the landholding farmers only」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「田畑別、地味、地形、予測される水害などの頻度などに応じていくつかの耕地グループ（団地、割）に分ける。次に、村として定められた数の持分単位（鬮）にまとめる。その際、各耕地グループごとに鬮の数に分け、その一つずつをあわせて鬮1本分とする。そのうえで、高持百姓のみで抽選を行い」) (日本大百科全書, the Encyclopedia Nipponica)
  • jochi-kotobank - 「In the Edo period, the precincts, fields and house land of temples and shrines that the lord exempted from tax and levies.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代，領主から貢租・課役を免除された寺社の境内・田畑・屋敷地。」) (山川 日本史小辞典, the Yamakawa dictionary of Japanese history)「There were cases where at the land survey it was left unmeasured and never included in the village assessment, and cases where its yield was entered in the survey register and it was then separately exempted as jochi.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「検地の際に縄除(なわよけ)して村高のなかに最初から含めない場合と、検地帳にはその土地の石高(こくだか)を記載したうえで改めて除地として年貢免除をする場合とがあった。」) (日本大百科全書, the Encyclopedia Nipponica)
