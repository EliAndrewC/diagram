• tabayashi-1987 - 「Representative of this type of irrigation is a system on the Tedori alluvial fan in the Hokuriku district along the Sea of Japan (TABAYASHI, 1978). The Tedori alluvial fan, which covers about 150 square kilometers, was formed by the Tedori River whose length is about 71 kilometers. The fan is almost completely covered with paddy fields.」 (English)
  • japanese-wiki-corpus - 「The basic unit of jori is about 109-meters square」「The section in which the basic unit tsubo is aligned 6x6 (in other words, six-cho square) was called 'ri.'」「Each tsubo is subdivided equally into 10 sections, and these sections were called 'dan'」「hokaku sen that divide jori are located north-south and east-west directions, but there are quite a few cases in which hokaku sen are at a tilt depending on the regions」「first appearance of land representation using jori was in 743, the mid Nara period」 (English; the page dates the first appearance of jori land notation to 743 (8th century) and says remains exist "throughout Japan except Hokaido and Okinawa". Tabayashi corroborates the plains association: 「This area was developed early and the Jori grid pattern can be found.」 (Saga plain, Fig. 5 caption))
  • jori-jawiki - 「In western Japan, where regularly shaped parcels laid out by jori are widespread, land readjustment was unnecessary, and the cases carried out were limited to some poorly drained land.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「条里による整形区画の広がる西日本においては土地区画整理が必要でなく、実施例は一部の排水不良地に限られた。」) (the surviving grid and its regularity; 「semi-regular」 is this page's hedge, and the page describes land division rather than a village's paddies at village scale)
