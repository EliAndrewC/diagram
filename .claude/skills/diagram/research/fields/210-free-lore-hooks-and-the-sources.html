Free lore hooks, and the sources

Sources: tabayashi-1987, maff-water-history, jsslkx-002-2021

  • Free lore hooks from the sources: drought rotation in fixed village turns; water-heads (mizugashira) elected to run the flow; supply turns timed by BURNING INCENSE STICKS (senkomizu); upstream villages leveraging position in water disputes; a village trading pond-management duty for water rights.
  • The comb layout also rests on codified Chinese canal doctrine, which an openly published provincial design guideline states in full: every grade of canal on the higher ground within its own command area, mains and branches along the contour lines or the watershed divides, the laterals below them crossing the contours, and two or three grades of fixed canal in the field with successive grades at right angles on flat ground.
