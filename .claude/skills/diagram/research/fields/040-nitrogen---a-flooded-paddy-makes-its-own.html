Nitrogen - a flooded paddy makes its own

Sources: azolla-enwiki (Azolla on the flooded paddy releasing nitrogen, and its cyanobacterial symbiont), genge-jawiki

  • Nitrogen - the paddy makes its OWN, so soy is food not fertilizer. A flooded paddy is near self-sustaining for nitrogen: the standing water hosts N-fixing cyanobacteria + azolla12 and the irrigation water carries in silt/nutrients from upstream - which is why paddies crop continuously for centuries13 where dry-field monoculture exhausts the soil. Legumes entered as winter green manure grown IN the drained paddy (renge / Chinese milk vetch, plowed under before spring flooding) + applied night soil / ash / fish-and-oilseed cake - NOT soy on the margins washing in.121 So the ~6% soy is a food crop (dry fields, and characteristically on the paddy bunds - aze-mame, "ridge beans"),14 NOT the paddy's nitrogen supply.
