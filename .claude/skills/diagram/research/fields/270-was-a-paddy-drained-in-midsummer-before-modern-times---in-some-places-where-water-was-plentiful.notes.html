  • horikawa-2011-nakaboshi - 「Nagata (1964) examined the irrigation methods recorded in the "Outline of paddy and upland rice cultivation (Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, Agricultural Affairs Bureau, 1933)" and showed that nakaboshi (doyoboshi in the original) was carried out in 22 prefectures. However, before the war its spread was limited to some regions with plentiful water (Tanaka, 1970).」 (translated; )
