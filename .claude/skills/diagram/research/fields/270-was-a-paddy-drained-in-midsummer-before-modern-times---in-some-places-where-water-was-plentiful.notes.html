  • doyoboshi-jawiki - 「Doyoboshi of the paddy is also called nakaboshi and the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水田の土用干しは中干しなどともいい」); 「in farming, draining the water from the paddy around the summer doyo, done to make the rice strong against wind and to make the ears fill well」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「農業においては夏の土用のころ田（水田）の水を抜き、風に強く、穂をよく実らせるために行うこと」); 「It differs by region, but about one week is the guide.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「地域によっても違うがおよそ一週間が目安。」)
  • horikawa-2011-nakaboshi - 「Nagata (1964) examined the irrigation methods recorded in the "Outline of paddy and upland rice cultivation (Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, Agricultural Affairs Bureau, 1933)" and showed that nakaboshi (doyoboshi in the original) was carried out in 22 prefectures. However, before the war its spread was limited to some regions with plentiful water (Tanaka, 1970).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「永田（1964）は「水稲及陸稲耕種要綱（農林省農務局，1933）」に記載されている灌漑方法を検討し，22県で中干し（原文では土用干し）が実施されていることを示した。但し，戦前にはその普及は用水の豊富な一部の地方に限られていた（田中，1970）。」)
