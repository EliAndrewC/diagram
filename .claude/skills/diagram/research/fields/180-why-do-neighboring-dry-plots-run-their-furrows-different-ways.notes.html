• fao-aina-ridging - 「Ridge and Mound Tillage The ridge-furrow system is a commonly used physical conservation practice. Ridge-and-furrow systems when aligned parallel to the contour lines have the dual purpose of erosion control and surface drainage. Their advantages are greater, the less steep the terrain and the more permeable the soil.」「Generally, for small landholders with only hand implements or animal traction and low-value subsistence crops, the ridge-furrow system used along the contour is a satisfactory method of enhancing infiltration and reducing runoff.」「Physical conservation measures such as terraces and contour bunds are considered of secondary importance in runoff management for small-scale farmers but it may be necessary for large-scale mechanized farming to cope with larger exceptional runoff on steep cultivated lands.」「Because of prohibitive costs of construction of terraces, other methods such as contour hedges, straw barriers and buffer strips of grass or alley cropping are often suggested for use on steep cultivated lands.」 (the source's own English. So contour ridging is not a steep-ground measure; a gentle margin is where its advantage is greatest)

  • zgkpw-longzuo - 「To suit the rainy, low-lying character of the south, the ancestors localized ridge culture, raising and widening the ridge tops into the "high ridge, deep furrow" pattern, which both strengthened drainage and kept the soil from becoming heavy and compacted」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「为适应南方多雨、地势低洼的特点，先民对垄作法进行了本土化改造，将垄面加高加宽，形成“高垄深沟”模式，既增强排水能力，又能防止土壤黏重板结」); 「As a crystallization of the wisdom of traditional Chinese agriculture, ridge culture embodies the ancestors' agricultural philosophy of "suiting measures to local conditions and following nature."」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「作为中国传统农业的智慧结晶，垄作法体现了先民“因地制宜、顺应自然”的农业哲学。」) ; of dry crops on ridges, 「In the Qimin Yaoshu, Jia Sixie of the Northern Wei recorded in detail the ridge-culture rules for different crops such as soybeans and millet」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「北魏贾思勰（xié）在《齐民要术》中，详细记载了大豆、粟等不同作物的垄作规范」) and 「set out the tillage principle "whenever sowing the five grains, first raise the ridges", establishing ridge culture as the core technique of northern dry-land farming」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「提出“凡种五谷，先须起垄”的耕作原则，将垄作法确立为北方旱作农业的核心技术。」) (the second sentence sums up ridge culture as a whole; the page says nothing of which way rows ran)
