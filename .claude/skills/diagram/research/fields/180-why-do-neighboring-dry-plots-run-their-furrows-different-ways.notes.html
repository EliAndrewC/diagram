• fao-aina-ridging - 「Ridge and Mound Tillage The ridge-furrow system is a commonly used physical conservation practice. Ridge-and-furrow systems when aligned parallel to the contour lines have the dual purpose of erosion control and surface drainage. Their advantages are greater, the less steep the terrain and the more permeable the soil.」「Generally, for small landholders with only hand implements or animal traction and low-value subsistence crops, the ridge-furrow system used along the contour is a satisfactory method of enhancing infiltration and reducing runoff.」 (the source's own English. The same chapter assigns steep cultivated land to a different family of measures - terraces, contour bunds, contour hedges, straw barriers and grass buffer strips - so contour ridging is not a steep-ground measure; a gentle margin is where its advantage is greatest)
