First collect and store the water at the topmost place so it is not lost. Then, from the bottom, let each part off to dry and weed it as it dries.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「且耘田之法，必先審度形勢，自下及上，旋乾旋耘。先於最上處收滀水，勿致水走失。然後自下旋放令乾而旋耘。」) (the chapter on weeding, 薅耘之宜篇第八)
  • chenfu-nongshu-juanshang - 「In making the bund, width is what matters; then the water can be kept at the right depth. If, just after the seed is sown, a sudden wind comes, quickly let the water off, so the waves do not wash the seed and heap it up; if a sudden heavy rain comes, add a little water, since the downpour otherwise floats and tosses the seed and lifts the grain's roots; if it is sunny, keep the water shallow, so the sun can warm it. But shallow must not be too shallow: too shallow and the skin of the mud dries hard. Deep must not be too deep: too deep and the seed is submerged, soaked to the heart, and wilts and yellows.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「作堘貴闊，則約水深淺得宜。若纔撒種子，忽暴風，卻急放乾水，免風浪淘蕩，聚郤穀也；忽大雨，必稍增水，為暴雨漂颭，浮起穀根也；若晴，卽淺水，從其曬暖也。然淺不可太淺，太淺卽泥皮乾堅。深不可太深，太深卽浸沒沁心而萎黄矣。」) (the chapter on seedlings, 善其根苗篇, on the seedbed just after sowing)
