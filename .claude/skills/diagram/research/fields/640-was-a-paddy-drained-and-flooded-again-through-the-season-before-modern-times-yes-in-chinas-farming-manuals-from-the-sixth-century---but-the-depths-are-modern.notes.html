When the weeding is done, let the water out and sun the roots to make them firm. Irrigate according to whether the season is wet or dry. First collect and store the water at the topmost place so it is not lost. Then, from the bottom, let each part off to dry and weed it as it dries.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (the chapter on weeding, 薅耘之宜篇第八)
  • chenfu-nongshu-juanshang - 「In making the bund, width is what matters; then the water can be kept at the right depth. If, just after the seed is sown, a sudden wind comes, quickly let the water off, so the waves do not wash the seed and heap it up; if a sudden heavy rain comes, add a little water, since the downpour otherwise floats and tosses the seed and lifts the grain's roots; if it is sunny, keep the water shallow, so the sun can warm it. But shallow must not be too shallow: too shallow and the skin of the mud dries hard. Deep must not be too deep: too deep and the seed is submerged, soaked to the heart, and wilts and yellows.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (the chapter on seedlings, 善其根苗篇, on the seedbed just after sowing)
