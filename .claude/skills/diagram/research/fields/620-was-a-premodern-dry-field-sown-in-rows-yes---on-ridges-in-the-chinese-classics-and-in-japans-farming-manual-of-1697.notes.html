  • hanshu-daitian-wikisource - 「Zhao Guo was made Commandant for Searching out Grain. Guo could make the alternating field, three furrows to a mu. They change places each year, hence the name; it is an ancient method. Hou Ji began the furrowed field: two spades make a pair; one chi wide and one chi deep is called a quan, running the length of the mu. Three quan to a mu, three hundred to a farmer, and the seed is sown in the furrows. Once the seedlings put out leaves, the weeds of the ridges are hoed little by little, and the ridge soil is pushed down to bank the roots.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「以趙過為搜粟都尉。過能為代田，一畝三甽。歲代處，故曰代田，古法也。后稷始甽田，以二耜為耦，廣尺深尺曰甽，長終畝。一畝三甽，一夫三百甽，而播種於甽中。苗生葉以上，稍耨隴草，因隤其土以附根苗。」) (the Book of Han, the treatise on food and money; a chi, the Chinese foot, and the dimensions are given as the ancient method Zhao Guo revived)
