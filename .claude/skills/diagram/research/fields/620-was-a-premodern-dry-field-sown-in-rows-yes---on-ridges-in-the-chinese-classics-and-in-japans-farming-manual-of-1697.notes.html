  • hanshu-daitian-wikisource - 「Zhao Guo was made Commandant for Searching out Grain. Guo could make the alternating field, three furrows to a mu. They change places each year, hence the name; it is an ancient method. Hou Ji began the furrowed field: two spades make a pair; one chi wide and one chi deep is called a quan, running the length of the mu. Three quan to a mu, three hundred to a farmer, and the seed is sown in the furrows. Once the seedlings put out leaves, the weeds of the ridges are hoed little by little, and the ridge soil is pushed down to bank the roots.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (the Book of Han, the treatise on food and money; a chi, the Chinese foot, and the dimensions are given as the ancient method Zhao Guo revived)
