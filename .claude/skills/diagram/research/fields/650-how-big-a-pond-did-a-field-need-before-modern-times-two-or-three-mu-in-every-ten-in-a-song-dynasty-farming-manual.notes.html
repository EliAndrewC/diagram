• kotobank-chenfu-nongshu - 「A Chinese agricultural book of the Song dynasty, by Chen Fu, in 3 books. Completed in Shaoxing 19 (1149).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中国，宋代の農書。陳旉の著。3巻。紹興19（1149）年完成。」) (the Britannica International Encyclopedia's entry)
  • chenfu-nongshu-juanshang - 「For high fields, look at the lay of the land; at the place where the high water gathers, measure what is needed and dig a pond, roughly giving up two or three mu of every ten mu of field to store water. At the turn of spring and summer, when the rains come in season, have its bank high and large and its middle deep and wide, so that it is broad enough to hold them. On the bank, plant mulberry and cudrania sparsely, where oxen can be tethered.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「若高田視其地勢，高水所會歸之處，量其所用而鑿為陂塘，約十畝田卽損二三畝以潴畜水；春夏之交，雨水時至，高大其隄，深闊其中，俾寬廣足以有容；隄之上，疎植桑柘，可以繫牛。」) (the chapter on the lie of the land, 地勢之宜篇第二)
