  • chenfu-nongshu-juanshang - 「For high fields, look at the lay of the land; at the place where the high water gathers, measure what is needed and dig a pond, roughly giving up two or three mu of every ten mu of field to store water. At the turn of spring and summer, when the rains come in season, have its bank high and large and its middle deep and wide, so that it is broad enough to hold them. On the bank, plant mulberry and cudrania sparsely, where oxen can be tethered.」 (translated; ) (the chapter on the lie of the land, 地勢之宜篇第二)
