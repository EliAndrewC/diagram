• kotobank-warazuka - 「Straw bundles, after threshing, piled up in a cylinder on the reaped paddy or on the bunds. Wara-nio. [Season: autumn]」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「脱穀後のわら束を刈田やあぜに円筒形に積み上げたもの。わらにお。《季 秋》」) (the Digital Daijisen)
  • kigosai-warazuka - 「There are various forms depending on the region, such as making it cylindrical around a central pole.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「棒を中心に円筒形にするなど、地方により様々な形がある。」)
  • kigosai-warazuka - 「The straw of the rick is chopped and used as compost, or used for straw goods such as bales and rope.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「藁塚の藁は刻んで堆肥として利用したり、俵や縄などの藁製品に用いたりする。」)
  • hirogawa-genryu-noka - 「The threshed straw was stacked in the paddy and kept until the next year, for feed for cattle and horses, for manure, and sometimes for fuel.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「脱穀した藁は田圃に積んで翌年まで牛馬の餌に、肥料に、時には燃料にと保存しました。」); of the period described, 「Among farm households of the Meiji, Taisho and early Showa periods, a life in keeping with the natural environment was the norm.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「明治、大正、昭和初期の農家では自然環境型の生活が普通でした。」) (the page reckons early Showa as running to about 1960)
