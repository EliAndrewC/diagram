• aze-jawiki - 「A bund set between a paddy and an open channel flowing beside it is sometimes distinguished by the name kohan (ditch bund). The channel is often public land, and the boundary is sometimes set at the paddy-side toe of the slope or the water line.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「田んぼの脇に流れる開水路との間に設けられる畦は、溝畔と呼ばれ区別されることがある。水路は公共の土地であることが多く、地境は田んぼ側の法尻あるいは法尻あるいは水張ラインに設定されることがある。」) (the doubled phrase is the page's own)
  • no source is owed: follows from the definitions
  • bungotakada-tagoshi - 「Modern paddies draw water from a supply canal and let the water out into a drain.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現代的な水田は、用水路から水を引き、排水路に水を出しています。」)「But Tashibu-no-sho Osaki keeps tagoshi irrigation, which passes the water from paddy to paddy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「しかし、田染荘小崎では水田から水田へ水を伝える「田越し」灌漑を残しています。」)
  • suido-ishizue-kochi-seiri - 「The plot was one tan (10 a) of 30 ken by 10 ken (about 54 m by 18 m), and every plot touched the irrigation and drainage channel and a road.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「区画は30間×10間(約54m×18m)の１反(10a)で、すべての区画が用排水路と道路に接していた。」) (the Konosu model, begun in 1902)
