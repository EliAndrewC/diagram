  • suido-ishizue-kochi-seiri - 「In western Japan, because regular plots made up to the Meiji period, such as the jori division, were widely distributed, it was carried out only on small areas, chiefly as drainage by buried drains under poorly drained paddies on inland valley-bottom lowlands.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「西日本では、条里地割など明治期までの整形区画が広く分布していたため、わずかに内陸部の谷底低地の排水不良田に小面積で暗渠排水を主として行われた。」) (the page goes on to say that pumps and ponds were later added there)
