• kotobank-shitsuden - 「A paddy with poor drainage, which is in an excessively wet state even during periods when wet rice is not being cultivated. Also, a paddy on which a second crop such as barley cannot be grown.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水はけが悪く、水稲を栽培していないときでも過湿な状態の水田。また、麦などの裏作のできない水田。」) (the Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
  • kotobank-kanden - 「A paddy whose drainage is good, and which, when water is not let in, dries out and becomes like a dry field.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水はけのぐあいがよく、水を入れないときには乾いて畑の状態になる田」) (the Digital Daijisen entry)
  • kotobank-shitsuden - 「The productivity of shitsuden (湿田, wet paddy) is generally low」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「湿田の生産性は概して低い」) (改訂新版 世界大百科事典) (shitsuden productivity is generally low)
  • kotobank-kanden - 「From the Meiji period onward, the conversion of wet paddies (shitsuden) into dry paddies (kanden) was carried out as a national undertaking, and it is said that more than 2/3 of the paddy fields in the country became dry paddies」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「明治以降，国家的事業として湿田の乾田化が行われ，全国の水田の2/3以上は乾田となったといわれる」) (改訂新版 世界大百科事典) (as the file's gloss)
  • kotobank-yatsuda - 「Paddy fields distributed in the low ground of valleys formed by the dissection of a plateau, that is, in yachi (谷地, valley bottoms).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「台地が開析されてできた谷間の低地すなわち谷地に分布する水田。」)「Generally they are wetland and were opened up long ago, but, not being blessed with abundant water sources, many of them became rain-fed paddies.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一般に湿地で古く開発されたが，豊かな水源に恵まれないため，それらの多くは天水田となり」) (世界大百科事典)
  • kotobank-fukada - 「A paddy with deep mud. Swamp paddy (numata). Fuke. Fuketa. Opposite: asada (a shallow paddy).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「どろの深い田。沼田。ふけ。ふけた。⇔浅田。」) (デジタル大辞泉)「A paddy with deep mud. A paddy that is deeply miry underfoot. Swamp paddy (numata). Fukeda.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「泥の深い田。深くぬかる田。沼田。ふけだ。」) (精選版 日本国語大辞典) (a paddy with deep mud; swamp paddy; antonym asada)

  • fao-rice-water - 「Typically, parts of seepage and percolation losses as well as surface runoff can be re-used, i.e., recycled within the system.」 (English; verbatim)
