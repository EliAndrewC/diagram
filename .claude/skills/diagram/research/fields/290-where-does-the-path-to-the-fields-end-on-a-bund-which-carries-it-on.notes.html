• kotobank-nodo - 「A road made, for farm work, between farmhouses and cultivated land and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「農作業のために農家と耕作地の間などに設けた道。」) (the Digital Daijisen's definition)
  • kotobank-aze-sekai-daihyakka - 「A small embankment made by heaping up earth between paddy and paddy. It holds water, and serves as boundary and path.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水田と水田との間に土を盛り上げてつくった小さな堤。水をためるとともに、境界・通路とする。」) (the Digital Daijisen's entry on the same page)
  • aze-jawiki - 「Those that also serve as the path for going around the paddies are called azemichi or nawate (also written 縄手).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水田を回る際の道としての役割も持っているもののことを、畦道（あぜみち）、畷（なわて、縄手とも）という。」)
  • kotobank-nawate - 「(2) A road between paddies; an azemichi; a paddy path; a nawate road. [Ten-volume Wamyosho (c. 934)]」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「② 田のあいだの道。あぜ道。たんぼ道。なわて道。〔十巻本和名抄（934頃）〕」) (the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, with the source of its earliest example)
  • kotobank-keihan - 「It also serves as passage for weeding and fertilizing, as a resting place, and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「また、除草・施肥のための通行、休憩場所などの機能もある。」) (the farm ministry's glossary, of the bund)
  • aze-jawiki - 「The paddy bunds of that time were mainly of the form called "small-plot paddies": many small bunds, the smallest about 2-3 m on a side, lined up in order inside large bunds that served as both azemichi and dike. They were built throughout the country from the Yayoi to the Kofun period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「当時の田畦は、畦道と堤の機能を兼ねた大畦畔に囲まれた中に、1辺が最小で2・3メートル規模の多数の小畦畔が整然と並ぶ「小区画水田」と呼ばれる形態のものが主流で、弥生時代から古墳時代にかけて全国的に造られた」)
  • aze-jawiki - 「However, some azemichi were once village roads (ridō).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ただし、畦道の中には里道であったものも含まれる。」)
