  • kotobank-aze-sekai-daihyakka - 「A small embankment made by heaping up earth between paddy and paddy. It holds water, and serves as boundary and path.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Digital Daijisen's entry on the same page)
  • aze-jawiki - 「Those that also serve as the path for going around the paddies are called azemichi or nawate (also written 縄手).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kotobank-nawate - 「(2) A road between paddies; an azemichi; a paddy path; a nawate road. [Ten-volume Wamyosho (c. 934)]」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, with the source of its earliest example)
  • kotobank-keihan - 「It also serves as passage for weeding and fertilizing, as a resting place, and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the farm ministry's glossary, of the bund)

They were built throughout the country from the Yayoi to the Kofun period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
