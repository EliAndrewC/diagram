  • senjochi-jawiki - 「Fan middle: because the river sinks underground, the water table is low and the land is water-poor, so it is hard to use as paddy. It was therefore often undeveloped coppice,」 (translated; )
  • senjochi-kotobank - 「Because a fan's underground water rises as springs at the fan toe, settlements form there, and nearby paddy has often developed early.」 (translated; )「The fan middle, however, has deep groundwater; its development came late and it was long left as wilderness.   • senjochi-kotobank - 「Where fans were cleared from early times, as in Kinki and Kofu, the river channels were also fixed early, and many became raised-bed rivers as the gravel built up within the channel.」 (translated; ) (ブリタニカ国際大百科事典 小項目事典, the Britannica short-entry encyclopedia, on the same page)
