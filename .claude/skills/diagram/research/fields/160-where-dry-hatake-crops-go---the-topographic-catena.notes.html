• satoyama-enwiki - 「Satoyama (里山) is a Japanese term applied to the border zone or area between mountain foothills and arable flat land.」 (the source's own English)「satoyama contains a mosaic of mixed forests, rice paddy fields, dry rice fields, grasslands, streams, ponds, and reservoirs for irrigation.」 (English; the page does not say which element sits on which ground)

  • satoyama-jawiki - 「In the Yoshino mountains of Nara prefecture, the mountains are divided, in order from nearest the village and lowest in elevation, into 'satoyama', 'uchiyama', 'okuyama' and 'dake', and what counts as 'satoyama' is the dry fields and coppice woods on the slopes around the settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「奈良県の吉野山地では、山を村落から近く標高が低い順に「サトヤマ」「ウチヤマ」「オクヤマ」「ダケ」と区分しており、「サトヤマ」に該当するのは集落の周囲の斜面にある畑や雑木林である」)「Broadleaf woods: every 10 to 20 years they were cut, leaving the roots, and used for firewood and charcoal.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「広葉樹林の場合：10年から20年ごとに根を残して伐採され、薪や木炭に利用された。」)「Looking back through history, by the early modern period most of Japan's satoyama had become red pine forest, or grass-covered or bare hills.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「歴史を遡ると、近世までに日本の里山の大半はアカマツ林、あるいは草山、禿げ山となっていた。」)
  • sanson-jawiki - 「Because the Shō River running across the Tonami plain often flooded before the Edo period, the people who settled this district chose the parts of the plain slightly higher than their surroundings to build their houses on, and made the ground around them paddy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「砺波平野を流れる庄川は江戸時代以前にはしばしば氾濫したため、この地域に住みついた人々は平野の中でも若干周囲より高い部分を選んで家屋を建て、周囲を水田とした」)
  • shizen-teibo-jawiki - 「For that reason, on alluvial lowland, long-established settlements are sited first of all on the natural levee, which is also used as dry field.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そのために沖積低地において、古くからの集落はまず自然堤防上に立地し、また畑として利用される」)
  • kohai-shicchi-jawiki - 「In the narrow sense, it is the lower, wetter, marsh-like part of the low flat ground behind the natural levee (the back lowland).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「狭義には、自然堤防の背後の低平地（後背低地）のうち、より低湿で湿地状の部分をいう」)「In regions such as Japan where wet-rice growing predominates, it was reclaimed and has traditionally been used as paddy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本のように水稲栽培の卓越する地域では、開墾され伝統的に水田として利用されてきた。」)
