Which district and county does this settlement belong to?

Sources: l7r-median-domain (the Place Names convention: a village and its district carry one name)

A village IS its district, and a map never says otherwise. The GM, 2026-08-29: "the name of the village is the name of the village district ... So a village should never be listed as belonging to a village district with a different name. In fact, there is no reason to ever say what village district a village belongs to." A village and the district it heads share one name, always. What a village's card does say is its county, which every village belongs to.

A hamlet is the other case: it genuinely belongs to a district named for a different place - the village at the district's head - and it says so, which is also how a reader learns which village the hamlet's lanes lead to.

The shared name is a deliberate departure from how such places were actually named, and the maps do not flag it: it is set out in the GM's own core materials and has been reviewed with the players, so a reader of these maps already knows.

The rule the map follows: a village map names its county and never names a village district; a hamlet map names the district it belongs to, and the direction the district's village lies in.
