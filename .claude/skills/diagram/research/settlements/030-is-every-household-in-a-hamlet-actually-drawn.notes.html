  • kotobank-inkyoya - 「Many house plots registered in the land-survey registers had, inside the plot enclosure, huts, gate-houses, retirement houses and so on along with the main house; weak small farmers (small farmers in the process of becoming independent) under the protection and control of the master family lived in the huts, gate-houses and retirement houses, and the master family lived in the main house. Even where weak small farmers of hut-dwelling, gate-house-dwelling or retirement status had families and their livelihood was, for the time being, independent and separate from the master's, in the house-count, people-and-horse registers (the basic registers for levying labor dues) the life inside one house plot was grasped as one hearth」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「検地帳に登録された屋敷は，その多くが屋敷囲いの内部に母屋（おもや）とともに小屋，門屋（かどや），隠居屋などを備え，小屋，門屋，隠居屋には主家の庇護･支配を受ける弱小農民(自立過程の小農)が起居し，母屋には主家が住いした。小屋住み，門屋住い，隠居身分などの弱小農民が家族をもち，その生計が主家のそれから一応独立分離している場合でも，家数人馬改帳（いえかずじんばあらためちよう）(夫役徴集の基礎帳簿)では，1屋敷の内部の生活は1竈（かまど）として把握され」) (from Heibonsha's Sekai daihyakka jiten entry on the house plot, quoted on the page; the entry places this in the early part of the Edo period)
