  • kotobank-inkyoya - 「Many house plots registered in the land-survey registers had, inside the plot enclosure, huts, gate-houses, retirement houses and so on along with the main house; weak small farmers (small farmers in the process of becoming independent) under the protection and control of the master family lived in the huts, gate-houses and retirement houses, and the master family lived in the main house.
