• guangdong-xinyu-22 - 「Jiujiang township raises fry; fry ponds are found only in Jiujiang. Elsewhere they mostly raise grown fish, and if they do raise fry, the fry do not thrive.」 (translated; )
  • guangdong-xinyu-22 - 「The land of Jiujiang is like a chessboard, over thirty li around; its black lines are the dikes, and the squares are the ponds. Of the pond water, seven-tenths raises fry and three-tenths raises grown fish. Fry water is turbid; grown-fish water is clear. From the color of the water one knows what kind of fish is raised.」 (translated; )
