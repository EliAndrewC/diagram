How our maps draw the boatmen's shrine at the landing (funadama)

At a landing, as at Hayakawa, our maps draw a small shrine at the head of the landing: the boat's guardian spirit given a shrine on land, as it sometimes was.

  • Our maps draw no boatmen's water-god shrine.
    Our maps draw nothing attested only in modern practice, and the boatmen's water-god shrine is known only from a twentieth-century record of custom, with nothing read placing boatmen keeping one before 1868.
  • Where does the shrine stand, and how big is it?
    A small shrine at the head of the landing is a GUESS: the boat's guardian's shrine on land is not recorded standing at a landing itself, nor with a size.
