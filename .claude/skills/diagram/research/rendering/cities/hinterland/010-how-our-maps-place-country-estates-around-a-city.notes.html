• heino-bunri-jawiki - 「the shift from warriors living on the land to their gathering in the castle town and the lord's direct rule of the farmers」 (translated; )
  • kadota-kotobank - 「Rice land around the residence of a medieval local lord; the name is thought to come from its typical form of spreading before the residence's gate. ... and because mostly well-established paddy was chosen ... In general, ... it was land the lord managed directly and had worked by dependents, the farmland over which the local lord's control was strongest.」 (translated; ) (the Encyclopedia Nipponica)
  • siheyuan-zhwiki - 「The main gate, also called the street gate or house gate, is the passage by which a Beijing siheyuan communicates with the outside world; it is generally built at the southeast side of the whole compound, to take the meaning of "the purple qi comes from the east"; another account is that it occupies the xun position of the eight trigrams, that is, the wind position, where the gentle and moistening winds blow in, so as to bring in the southeast wind and block the northwest wind of winter; it is an auspicious position, embodying the principle of "a kan house with a xun gate".」 (translated; ) (a Beijing courtyard house, not a country estate: that an estate's formal gate follows the same rule is this record's reading)
  • kotobank-shimoyashiki - 「An east-west street in the southern part of the castle town's outer town (soto-machi), below Mt. Magata.」 (translated; ) and 「A samurai district also called Soto-no-Shimo-yashiki-chō; street width 3 ken, total length 237 ken. Both sides were the Inada family's lower residence and their retainers' residences (per 'Michigusa' and others).」 (translated; ) (the Nihon Rekishi Chimei Taikei's entry, of which Kotobank prints only a part; the quoted words do not name the town - that it is Sumoto rests on where the page files the entry, under Sumoto's castle town, which is not quoted here. It does not say what the houses held. The feet are this page's arithmetic at about six feet to the ken.)
  • kotobank-shimoyashiki - 「In castle towns other than Edo too, there are cases of domain lords and upper-ranking retainers setting them up besides the castle or main residence.」 (translated; ) (the Yamakawa entry; "there are cases" is its own hedge, and it gives no count and no place.)
  • l7r-median-domain - 「Village districts are farming communities populated by peasant farmers with no samurai living there. At most a samurai family might maintain a country estate close to some farmland, but the estate would never be considered to be part of the village/hamlet」 (the setting's canon, "The Median Domain")
  • l7r-median-domain - 「Isolated farms that *are* taxed, including samurai country estates, get tallied under」 (the setting's canon, "The Median Domain"; the sentence goes on to name the settlement they are tallied under, which the search that read it cut off)
