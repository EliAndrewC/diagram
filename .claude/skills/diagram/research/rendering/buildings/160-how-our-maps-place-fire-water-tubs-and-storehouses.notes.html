  • tfd-hongou-fire-history - 「As a matter of daily habit, fire-fighting equipment such as fire-prevention water and fire-beaters was kept on the roof. The fire-beater was soaked in water and used to beat out sparks when they fell on one's own house; the names vary - yosui-oke (water bucket), tensui-oke (rainwater bucket), chinka-sui (fire-quenching water) - but the common people habitually kept water ready and placed it at the front entrance and on the roof.」 (translated; )
