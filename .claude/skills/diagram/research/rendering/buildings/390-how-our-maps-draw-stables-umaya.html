How our maps draw stables (umaya)

An official's compound on our maps keeps its horses in a small stable of its own, a few bays long, with room in it for the grooms and the fodder, as a warrior's stable had.

  • The stalls are drawn about one ken (~6 ft) wide, on board floors.
    A warrior's stall was one bay wide, on a raised timber floor, and a bay is taken as one ken.
  • How deep is a stall drawn?
    Its depth, and so its area, is a GUESS: no page we read gives a warrior's stall its depth.
  • How many horses does the stable hold?
    That number is a GUESS too: no page we read gives the horses of a county magistrate's post.
