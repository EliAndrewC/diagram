How our maps draw a parley room on a border

A border posting on our maps has a parley room built into the border wall, with the border line running across its floor, so that the two sides meet in one room, each kneeling on its own ground. No such room is recorded: it is a deliberate deviation, this setting's own.

  • The parley room on the line is a deliberate deviation.
    This project makes the door that receives a delegation from across the border the border itself, so the room stands astride the line. The historical forms it departs from are a post on each side of the line, facing each other across it, as Russia and Qing China built at Kyakhta, or a compound one side set aside for the other's officers, with a reception hall beside it, as at the Japan House where Tsushima dealt with Korea.
  • The kneeling mats, two a side across the line, belong to the same deviation.
    No page we read seats two parties across a border line in one room.
