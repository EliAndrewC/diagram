How our maps draw a border crossing court

A border posting on our maps keeps a court behind its gate, and in this setting that court is where a delegation from across the clan border is received. Half of that is history and half is this project's own.

  • The court as the ground those crossing pass over is accurate.
    At the surviving Nuruyu post the road entered by the gate and ran on in front of the inspection hall that faced it, so the ground between gate and hall was the crossing ground.
  • A formal reception room in the officer's house is accurate, even at a plain post.
    Nuruyu's officer's house is a farmhouse in form, but it has a reception room in the shoin style.
  • Keeping the court for delegations of rank from across the border is a deliberate deviation.
    Receiving a neighboring clan's delegations there with ceremony is this setting's own use of the court, built on the examining ground: no page we read describes a border post that kept a court for receiving the neighboring domain's officials.
