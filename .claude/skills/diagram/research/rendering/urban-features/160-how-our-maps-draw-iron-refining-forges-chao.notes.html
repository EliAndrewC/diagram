• wagner-ming-iron - "If they are making wrought iron then when the cast iron flows out it is led into a rectangular hearth which is constructed a few chi away and a few cun lower, up against a short wall. While the iron flows into the hearth, several persons holding willow poles stand in a row on the wall. Earlier they have taken wuchaoni and dried and sieved it so that it is as fine as flour. One of the men quickly spreads this while the others quickly stir with the willow poles and [the iron] is immediately fined into wrought iron. The willow poles burn down two or three cun each time fining is done, and after they have been used twice they are replaced."
