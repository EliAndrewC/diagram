How our maps draw teahouses, eating houses and drinking houses (chaya)

Our maps draw every teahouse, eating house and drinking house as the ordinary shop, and tell them apart by where they stand: by a temple's gate, along the road through a town and at its ends, and at the foot of a bridge. How many a town keeps follows its mix of shops, at How our maps draw shops and trades in towns and villages.

  • Every eating or drinking house is drawn as the generic shop, a map drawing convention.
    A Song dynasty wine shop's gateway of colored silk and an Edo tea stall's reed screens are too small to show at a map's scale, so neither is drawn.
  • Our maps set a town's teahouses beside a temple's gate, as Edo's stood.
    In Edo the tea stalls sprang up before the gates and in the precincts of temples and shrines, and the maps place them where What kind of shops stand at a temple gate? places a temple's shops.
  • Our maps line the road through a town with teahouses, thickest at its ends.
    The travelers' teahouses of the highways moved in to the edge of the post towns, and the maps line the road as What lines the Imperial road where it runs through the city? does.
  • Our maps set drinking houses at the foot of a bridge, this project's placement.
    In Osaka drinking houses are said to have set up shop even on the bridges, and the maps set them where the bridge meets the street.
  • A village keeps no drinking house unless a road through it carries travelers, this project's decision.
    Drinking houses reached the farming villages only in the later eighteenth century, so a village map draws none. Long before that the tea stalls along the highways sold sake and food to travelers, so a village on a traveled road may keep one; that exception is this project's choice.

The rule the map follows: a town's teahouses, eating houses and drinking houses are shops in the ordinary rows, set where the crowd passes - beside a temple's gate, at the ends of the road through the town and along it, and at the foot of a bridge. A village map draws no drinking house unless a road through the village carries travelers.
