How our maps draw a relief house

Two forms of relief house are attested, so a city rolls between them as a knob. In the Song form, every provincial city and capital keeps a poorhouse and an infirmary, and a county seat keeps them too. In the Japanese form, only a capital keeps one, a single longhouse standing in the ruler's herb garden, and a provincial city has none.

  • A pharmacy is a shop among the shops and is not drawn apart.
    Song Hangzhou's official pharmacies are named among the city's famous shops, so in either form the map draws a pharmacy as one of the shops.
  • How big is a relief house on our maps?
    One longhouse about 25 ft deep and 110 ft long. It is a GUESS, reckoned from the Edo infirmary's forty beds: forty sleepers at two mats each, doubled for the rooms and passages, since no floor area was found for any relief house.
  • Where do the Song form's two houses stand?
    The poorhouse and the infirmary are drawn side by side in one walled yard. That is a GUESS: the Song orders put both in each seat but say nothing of where they stood or whether they shared a wall.
