How our maps size kitchen gardens

Our maps draw a farm's kitchen garden at its true size, as they draw its house and its yard, and hold each household's beds together to between 10 and 140 sq m (~108-1,507 sq ft): from a few tsubo up to about 1.4 se. That band is the guess the research makes at a kitchen bed's size (Kitchen gardens beside farmhouses (yashikibatake)).

  • Nothing on a farm is enlarged to make it read.
    The beds are drawn at true size at every scale, exactly as the house and the work yard are, so their area band is a real quantity, not a drawing habit.
  • A household's beds are summed.
    A garden split into several beds is still one household's garden, so it is the beds together that the band holds.
  • Below the band a bed reads as no garden at all, and above it as a field.
    That is why the band stops where it does.
  • How big are the gardens on our maps?
    The drawn hamlets land at about 35-115 sq m (~377-1,238 sq ft) per household, centered near 55 sq m (~592 sq ft), about half a se - inside the band.
