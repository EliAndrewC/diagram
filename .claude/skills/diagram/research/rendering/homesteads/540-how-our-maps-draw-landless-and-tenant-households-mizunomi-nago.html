How our maps draw landless and tenant households (mizunomi, nago)

Most farmers in the empire our maps are set in are tenants, so on our maps a tenant's house is an ordinary farmhouse, not a poor one. The households that hold no land at all, or rent their house as well as their fields, are drawn in the two forms the record finds.

  • Where a poorer household lives is a knob with two settings.
    It is either a hut or gate-hut standing inside a larger farm's enclosure, drawn as a small outbuilding that is lived in, or a small house of its own at the edge of the settlement, at the margin of the fields or the foot of the hills. Both are attested, so neither is chosen over the other.
  • How many of a map's households are drawn each way, and how many are drawn at all?
    This is a GUESS: the pages we read give the two forms, but not how many households lived in each.
