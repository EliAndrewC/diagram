• kotobank-miyaji-shibai - 「In the Edo period, a booth-built play performed under a temporary license in the precincts of a shrine or temple.」 (translated; ); edo-sanza-jawiki - 「Conversely, a playhouse without a yagura was called a miyachi-shibai, and various restrictions were imposed on it: that it be a simple booth structure, that no roof be put on anything other than over the stage, that it not use fittings such as a draw curtain, a revolving stage or a hanamichi.」 (translated; ) (the play in a precinct, with the regulation beside it that put no roof on anything but the stage; the Chinese temple opera stage could not be reached, and the audience's position between stage and hall is this page's)
