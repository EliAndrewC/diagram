How our maps house a town's laborers

Our maps draw two kinds of laborer's house: an ordinary one, 34 by 24 ft, and a master laborer's, 50 by 34 ft. In a city both stand behind the shops on the street, on the lanes of the back lots. In a town, how many of its laborers live in back tenements and how many on plots of their own is rolled per town.

  • An ordinary laborer's house stands for a whole tenement row, not one family's home.
    It is drawn 34 by 24 ft, about 816 sq ft, and one back-tenement unit was about 9 by 12 ft, some 108 sq ft, so the drawn house is the size of a row of seven or eight units.
  • In a city, a laborer lives in a one-story back tenement on a lane behind the street-front shops.
    About 9 by 12 ft a household, as the common one-story unit was, the units one row deep or two by the width of the shop in front.
  • In a town, how many laborers rent is a knob rolled per town.
    The share of urban residents who rented ran from 20 to 30% in the north-east and on the Japan Sea coast to 50 to 60% around the Inland Sea. In a town at the low end most laborers have a small house on a plot of their own, and a back tenement appears only behind the larger shops. In a town at the high end the back tenement is the laborer's usual home, as in a city.
  • Giving a low-share town's laborers plots of their own is a calibration, not a finding.
    The tenant shares are of all urban residents, not of laborers alone, so how many of a town's tenants were laborers is this project's calibration against those shares.
  • A town on the Chinese model houses its laborers by GUESS.
    No page we read says where a Chinese county town's laborers lived.
  • The larger laborer's house is a master laborer's.
    The setting sets a small band of rich master laborers among its laborers, one in twelve in a town and one in eight in a city. Our maps make the master laborer a labor boss who finds work for laborers and houses some of them, as the early-modern placement broker found hired hands their places and lodged them until placed.
  • A master laborer's house stands among the laborers' housing, not on the shop street.
    It takes a lot on a back street, among the tenements; how many there are and where they front is at Where do the shops stand, and where does the poor housing go?.
  • How big is a master laborer's house?
    Our maps draw it 50 by 34 ft. That is a GUESS: no page we read gives the size of a labor broker's house, a tenement's house manager's or a post town's porters' office.
