• kotobank-fumiguruma - 「Japanese paddy irrigation is generally done easily by channels or by setting up a simple weir, so its degree of dependence on lifting by wheels was low.」 (translated; ) 「the dragon-bone wheel used in the 17th century was hard to make and did not spread, and the treadle wheel that appeared in its place in the 18th century also spread only in the downstream reclaimed lands and new-field districts where making new channels was difficult.」 (translated; ) (the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, older edition, entry 水車 (suisha, "water wheel"), as excerpted on this page; it dates the treadle wheel to the 18th century where the same page's main entry gives the Kanbun era, 1661-73)
  • kotobank-fumiguruma - 「a human-powered water-lifting machine peculiar to Japan, used almost exclusively for paddy irrigation」 (translated; ) 「It is not installed fixed; it can be carried to wherever it is needed and used. It was generally used for lifts of about 1 m or less from the channel to the paddy surface, but for higher paddies two or three might be used in a row.」 (translated; ) 「whereas most dragon-bone wheels needed the labor of two people, the treadle wheel needed only one.」 (translated; ) (Horio Hisashi, the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, revised edition)
  • tiangong-shuili - 「Wherever by a river a noria is made, a weir blocks the flow and turns it beneath the wheel, driving the wheel round; it draws the water into its tubes and tips each one into the trough, and the water flows into the fields. Day and night without rest, a hundred mu are without worry.」 (translated; ) 「At lakes and ponds whose water does not flow, it is turned on a turntable by an ox, or trodden round by several people gathered. The pump's body is two zhang at its longest, and half that at its shortest. Inside, boards are strung on a dragon-bone chain and carry the water upward against its flow.」 (translated; ) 「In general one person's full day waters five mu of fields, and an ox twice that. On shallow ponds and small ditches that will not take a long pump, a pump of a few chi, turned fast by one person's two hands, waters only two mu in a full day's work.」 (translated; ) 「With the well-sweep and the windlass the result is smaller still.」 (translated; )
