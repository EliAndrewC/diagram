  • sumida-gawa-jawiki - 「Once the Edo period began, the stretch downstream of the area around Azuma Bridge was also called the Okawa (大川).」 (translated; ) ; 「It is recorded as "Sumida-gawa" (住田河) in a Daijokan edict of 835 (Jowa 2), and was also called the "Miyato-gawa" (宮戸川) among other names.」 (translated; ) ; 「It was also called the Miyato-gawa and the Asakusa-gawa.」 (translated; ) ; 「Miyato-gawa (the former name of the Sumida-gawa around Asakusa)」 (translated; ) (In the Edo period the stretch below Azuma Bridge was also called the Okawa; an 835 Daijokan edict records it as 住田河 (Sumida-gawa, "Sumida river") and it was also called the Miyato-gawa; it was also called the Miyato-gawa / Asakusa-gawa; the Miyato-gawa was its name around Asakusa.
