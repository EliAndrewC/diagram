Suppose twenty villages with some thousand households. In one district there are five shrines. Each takes three or five villages as its parish. Of these five shrines, the one that should stand first - whether listed in the Engishiki, or holding an earlier rank, or the one to which natural faith returns - is to be made the district shrine.
