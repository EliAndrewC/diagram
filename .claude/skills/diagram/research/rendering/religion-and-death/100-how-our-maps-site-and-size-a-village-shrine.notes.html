The fengshui literature on water mouths reached only village-by-village inventories, with no general statement to quote. A Taiwanese thesis on fengshui siting speaks of temples' good siting in general, and a Japanese study of the tutelary shrine and village structure is about its social place. The preface of an edited volume on the sacred space of Chinese villages and market towns states the water mouth's importance for one Huizhou village it introduces. A survey of Zhejiang villages' entrances names temples at water mouths and entrances and reads them as auspicious sites. None of them states that the earth god's shrine took the water mouth as a rule.
