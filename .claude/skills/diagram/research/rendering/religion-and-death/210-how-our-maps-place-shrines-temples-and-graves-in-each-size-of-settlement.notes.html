• dosojin-jawiki - 「Enshrined at spatial and social boundaries - a village's entrance and exit, a mountain pass, a crossroads, the foot of a bridge, and the like.」 (translated; ); and 「A deity enshrined mainly in the form of a stone monument or stone image at a settlement's boundary or a village's center, at the border between inside and outside the village, at a road crossing, at a three-way junction and the like.」 (translated; )
  • sanko-densetsu-jawiki - 「At Mikurahana there is a dragon-god shrine near a well called Binsui-ire, and the people of Amasegawa call November 20 Dragon God Day and hold a festival」 (translated; ) (a water-deity shrine rather than a wayside one)
  • sando-jawiki - 「In the narrow sense it denotes only the passage inside the sacred bounds, such as within the torii or the temple gate; in the broad sense it means the whole of the road running to the shrine or temple from a place of heavy foot traffic such as a highway.」 (translated; ), 「Generally, there are also many places where the sando is recognized together with the torii-front town or gate-front town that arose of its own accord around an influential shrine or temple,」 (translated; ) and 「Also, places where the line of a highway was bent so that it came close to a shrine or temple for the sake of pilgrimage were not rare either,」 (translated; )
  • inari-jinja-jawiki - 「At the entrance of a Shinto Inari shrine a vermilion torii usually stands. Vermilion lacquer is widely used on shrine buildings and torii, and has become a symbol of Inari.」 (translated; ) (shrine buildings and torii; the page does not speak of a small wayside shed)
  • hokora-jawiki - 「A simplified form of shrine, set up in places difficult for a person to enter, or on land owned by a settlement or by an individual.」 (translated; )
  • akiba-jinja-jawiki - 「In the case of a small shrine, because the deity is also a fire-quelling one, stone-built shrines and the like, which do not burn easily, are seen.」 (translated; ) (the material, against the vermilion wooden shed the map draws)
