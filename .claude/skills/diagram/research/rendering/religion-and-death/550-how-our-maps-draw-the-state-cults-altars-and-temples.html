How our maps draw the state cult's altars and temples

No map draws a City God temple, a Confucian temple or a state altar, in a county town or in a city. This is a deliberate deviation. Every Chinese seat of local government kept a City God temple, and every county school a Confucian temple, but the setting our maps are drawn for names neither, nor any state altar.

  • The Ministry of Rites' temple takes the state cult's place at a seat.
    The setting puts its own ladder of temples at every seat: the preceptor's town monastery in a county town, the provincial abbot's subordinate temple in a provincial city, and the grand abbots' temples in a domain capital.
