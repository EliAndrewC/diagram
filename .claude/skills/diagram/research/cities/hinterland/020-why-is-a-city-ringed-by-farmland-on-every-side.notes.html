• chang-morphology-walled-capitals - 「Since urbanization developed to serve the basic economic, political, and social needs of the rural population, it was natural for cities to grow up along the navigable waterways that were an important means for connecting them with rural communities or with other cities.」 (the market half; garrison of the land that feeds it is this page's, the chapter giving only soldiers quartered in the gate towers)
  • kohai-shicchi-jawiki - 「Because micro-high ground is distributed along the channel, the flood water drains away poorly, and mud is deposited by standing for long periods.」 and 「As the deposition of mud in the back lowland proceeds, it becomes land with poor drainage and gradually turns into wetland.」 and 「In regions where wet-rice cultivation predominates, as in Japan, it has been reclaimed and traditionally used as paddy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「流路沿いには微高地が分布するため氾濫水は排水しづらく、長期間にわたり湛水することにより泥の堆積が生じる。」 and original: 「後背低地への泥の堆積が進行すると、排水不良の土地となり、次第に湿地化する」 and original: 「日本のように水稲栽培の卓越する地域では、開墾され伝統的に水田として利用されてきた。」)
  • shizen-teibo-jawiki - 「The natural levee forms a slight rise relative to the back marsh that spreads on the channel side and behind it, and within the low, wet alluvial lowland it is well drained. For that reason, in an alluvial lowland, long-established settlements are sited first of all on the natural levee, and it is also used as dry field.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「自然堤防は流路側および背後（流路と反対側）に広がる後背湿地に対してわずかな高まりとなり、低湿な沖積低地の中では水はけが良い。そのために沖積低地において、古くからの集落はまず自然堤防上に立地し、また畑として利用される。」) (the settlement rule of a wet lowland, from the rise it is built on. The low ground it names is a natural back marsh, not the strip below a dug collector, so the record is carrying the principle across to a feature the page does not name.)
