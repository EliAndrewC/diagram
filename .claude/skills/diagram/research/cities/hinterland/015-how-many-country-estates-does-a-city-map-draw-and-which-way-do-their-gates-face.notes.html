• siheyuan-zhwiki - 「The main gate, also called the street gate or house gate, is the passage by which a Beijing siheyuan communicates with the outside world; it is generally built at the southeast side of the whole compound, to take the meaning of "the purple qi comes from the east"; another account is that it occupies the xun position of the eight trigrams, that is, the wind position, where the gentle and moistening winds blow in, so as to bring in the southeast wind and block the northwest wind of winter; it is an auspicious position, embodying the principle of "a kan house with a xun gate".」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「大门又叫街门、宅门，是北京四合院与外界沟通的通道，一般都修筑在整个院落的东南侧，以取“紫气东来”之意，也有说法是占据八卦中的巽位，即风位，是和风、润风吹进的位置，以引进东南风，挡住冬天的西北风，是吉祥之位，体现“坎宅巽门”的原则。」) (a Beijing courtyard house, not a country estate: that an estate's formal gate follows the same rule is this record's reading)
