  • xunjiansi-zhwiki - 「in the Yuan, Ming and Qing periods, a grassroots body under the county yamen or a county-level yamen」 (translated; ) ... 「its official rank was not high; taking the Qing as the example, only the ninth grade」 (translated; ) ... 「in the twentieth year of Hongwu, against the wokou, five guards, twelve battalions and forty-two xunjiansi were set up in Fujian. Prefectures, departments and counties also had xunjiansi, which were under the local official」 (translated; )
  • historist-kido - 「In the early modern period, in cities everywhere, they were set at both ends of the street of each two-sided block. A watch house was attached to the kido, where the kido keeper lived. The first function of the kido was keeping the peace, and they were shut at night and in emergencies such as riots.」 (translated; ) ... 「some cities, such as Sendai in Mutsu and Matsuyama in Iyo, had no kido for each separate block, and set kido only at the entrances of the castle town and at the boundaries between the samurai and townsmen's quarters」 (translated; )
  • kitsuki-bansho - 「They watched the people and goods going in and out, closed the great doors when the hour came, and, as at a barrier station, forbade coming and going late at night. The Kitsuki castle town had six bansho, and one could not come and go freely.」 (translated; ) ... 「Looking at old maps, outside the bansho there were no houses, only a single road.」 (translated; ) (of the Babao entrance, the town's front entrance on its highways)
  • kitsuki-bansho - 「At the north foot of that earthen bridge was a bansho.」 (translated; ) ... 「The fish quarter lay outside this bansho, and was outside the enclosure.」 (translated; ) ... 「at night, even when the bansho was shut, a small gate was opened for the people of the fish quarter.」 (translated; )
  • kuchidome-bansho-jawiki - 「A kuchidome bansho is a bansho that each domain set up in the Edo period at its own borders and at key points of traffic. It corresponds to the sekisho of the Edo shogunate.」 (translated; ) ... 「Its main duty was watching the travelers and goods going in and out of the domain.」 (translated; ) ... 「The bansho's hours of opening were from the sixth hour of dawn (sunrise) to the sixth hour of dusk (sunset).」 (translated; ); on the duty, 「a tax on the goods passing, the so-called」 (translated; ) kuchiyakugin 「(something like a customs duty today) - collecting it was also an important role of the kuchidome bansho」 (translated; )
