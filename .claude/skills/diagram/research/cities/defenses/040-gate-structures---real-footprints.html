Gate structures - real footprints

Sources: xian-wall-zhwiki (the mamian and the wall itself, measured), ditai-zhwiki (that the mamian is the projecting tower also called a ditai), pingyao-yingxunmen-zhwiki (a county seat's gate tower, measured), pingyao-enwiki (that Pingyao was a county town),guangjimen-prdculture (a prefectural seat's gate tower, measured), beijing-chengmen-zhwiki (the capital's gate towers, great and small, measured)

A gate guard duty room is a small building of one to three bays, about 15 to 35 ft; a gate inspection hall (a sekisho or a lijin guard post) about 25 to 45 ft; the wall's MAMIAN bastion (马面, also called a ditai or enemy platform - a tower-like structure projecting from the wall) about 65 by 40 ft (Xi'an's are about 20 m wide and project about 12 m, on a wall 12 m high and 15 to 18 m across at the base), the tower building on it about 30 to 40 ft; a corner tower about 50 to 66 ft; a masonry gatepost about 15 ft across by 24 ft along the opening (the guard-room, inspection-hall, tower-building, corner-tower and gatepost figures unsourced).

The GATE tower (a 城楼) over the opening is measured at four gates, and its size follows the gate's rank. Pingyao's four main gate towers, at a county seat, are five bays (14 m) wide and four bays (10 m) deep - 46 by 33 ft; Chaozhou's Guangji Gate tower, at a prefectural seat (a rank no page read states), stands on the wall with a ground story 25.8 m wide and 13 m deep, 85 by 43 ft; the capital's Yongding Gate, at the middle of the outer city's south wall, carries a tower of five bays (24 m) by three (10.5 m), while Zuo'an Gate, at the east end of the same wall, carries a tower of three bays by one, 16 m by 9 m - 52 by 30 ft - and the outer city's towers are all lower and smaller than the inner city's. So the band runs from about 46 by 33 ft at a county seat's main gate to about 85 by 43 ft at a prefectural seat's, and a three-bay tower of 52 by 30 ft stands at a side gate of the capital's outer city.

The rule the map follows: the buildings at a gate are drawn at their real sizes and not as fixed markers - a guard house about 34 by 20 ft, an inspection hall about 44 by 22 ft, a masonry gatepost 15 ft across by 24 ft along the opening, a gate tower about 52 by 30 ft, and the wall's own tower as a mamian bastion about 65 by 40 ft: a rectangle PROJECTING from the curtain, longer along the wall than deep, with the tower building set back on it. The mamian and the gate tower are attested: the gate tower's 52 by 30 ft is the measured three-bay tower of a side gate of the capital's outer city, its width inside the band from a county seat's main gate to a prefectural seat's. The gatepost's is a GUESS, because no readable page gives it, and the guard house and the inspection hall are estimates, since no page read gives either one's bay count or size. Each is drawn at the legible upper end of its band and no further - a real value, never one inflated to read better; strokes keep their own legibility floor and the footprints take no license.
