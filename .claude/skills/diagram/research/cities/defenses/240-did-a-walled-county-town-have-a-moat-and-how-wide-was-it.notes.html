  • hori-jawiki - 「A settlement surrounded by a moat, to keep out wild animals such as wolves, is called a kango settlement.」 (translated; ) ... 「In the kango settlements of the Yayoi period, an earth bank was built outside the moat」 (translated; ) ... 「In the jinaicho formed in the Muromachi period too, moats were dug around for the self-defense of their members.」 (translated; )
  • sogamae-jawiki - 「The medieval city of Sakai was surrounded on three sides by a moat about 3 m deep and 10 m wide, and set up kido gates to prepare its defense.」 (translated; )
