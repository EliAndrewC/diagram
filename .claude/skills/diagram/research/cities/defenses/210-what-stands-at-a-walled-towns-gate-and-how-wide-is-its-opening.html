A tower over the opening. On the Chinese model a town's gate carries a gate tower built over it. Pingyao's six gates each carry their gate tower; Shanghai, walled in a hurry in 1553, had towers for defense built at its gates four years later, when the wall was also widened. A historian of the Chinese city wall puts the rule in one line: the size of a gate tower is in proportion to the scale of its wall. So a county gate is the city's gate made smaller, not a different thing.
