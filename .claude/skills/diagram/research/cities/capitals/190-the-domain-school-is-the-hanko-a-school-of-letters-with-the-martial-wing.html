The domain school is the hanko: a school of letters WITH the martial wing

Sources: hanko-jawiki (255 schools; 武芸 and 演武場), nisshinkan-jawiki (弓道場・武道場 at the reconstruction)

The question (GM 2026-08-09): is the "Domain School" an office-like building (ministry glyph) or the capital equivalent of the provincial martial hall?

Both, because that is what a hanko WAS: a school of LETTERS first, with a martial wing (bugeijo) on the same grounds - Aizu's Nisshinkan, Mito's Kodokan and Kagoshima's Zoshikan all pair lecture halls with fencing floors and an archery range. The provincial martial hall is exactly that martial wing at the tier below; the capital shows the whole institution. Decision: a dedicated hanko glyph - the martial-hall drawing (state violet, dojo hall with kamiza, archery lane with azuchi) plus the larger civil lecture hall.
