The domain school is the hanko: a school of letters WITH the martial wing

Sources: hanko-jawiki (255 schools; 武芸 and 演武場), nisshinkan-jawiki (the archery and martial-arts halls of the rebuilt school), kodokan-ibaraki-about (Mito's halls of letters and of arms, and its archery ground), meirinkan-jawiki (Hagi's martial halls around its Confucian hall)

The question (GM 2026-08-09): is the "Domain School" an office-like building (ministry glyph) or the capital equivalent of the provincial martial hall?

Both, because that is what a hanko WAS: a school of LETTERS first, with a martial wing (bugeijo) on the same grounds - Aizu's Nisshinkan, rebuilt today, has its archery hall and martial-arts hall; Mito's Kodokan set a hall of letters and a martial hall to either side of its main hall, with an archery ground among its grounds; and Hagi's Meirinkan ringed its Confucian hall with sword, spear and gunnery halls. How much ground a school took, and what else stood on it, is its own question. The provincial martial hall is exactly that martial wing at the tier below; the capital shows the whole institution. Decision: a dedicated hanko glyph - the martial-hall drawing (state violet, dojo hall with kamiza, archery lane with azuchi) plus the larger civil lecture hall.
