• bodaiji-jawiki - 「A bodaiji (菩提寺) is a temple whose sect a household has been devoted to for generations and at which it holds memorial services for the enlightenment (bodai) of its ancestors.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「菩提寺（ぼだいじ）とは、一家が代々その寺の宗旨に帰依して、先祖の菩提を弔う寺院のこと[1]。」)
  • teramachi-jawiki - 「In the Edo period and earlier, with the intent of making them a line of defense against an enemy attack, temples and graveyards were gathered at the outer edge of the town in many castle towns.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代以前、敵襲に際して防衛線とする意図で、多くの城下町において寺院や墓地が市街の外縁にまとめられた。」)
  • jokamachi-jawiki - 「Furthermore, the teramachi were placed on the outer ring of the castle town, lined with broad temples, and took a part in strengthening the town's defense.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「さらに、寺町は、城下町の外郭に配されて広い寺院が建ち並び、都市防衛強化の一翼を担った。」)
  • kotobank-teramachi - (日本歴史地名大系) of Fukuchiyama, 「temples were concentrated as the northern guard when the castle town was built」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城下町建設時に北方の固めとして寺院を集中させたもの」); of Shibata, 「a quarter formed by placing temples on the southeast outer ring of the Shibata castle town, outside the Shibata river and the moat that surround the town. It lay at the place corresponding to the town's entrance, and also had a role in the town's defense.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「新発田城下の東南外郭、城下を囲む新発田川と堀の外に寺院を配置して形成された町。城下の入口に相当する場所にあり、都市防衛的役割もあった。」); and of Yonago, 「a block of the sand-hill country northwest of the castle was set aside for temples and formed the castle town's defense line」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城の北西方向の砂山地帯の一画が寺院の予定地とされ、城下の防御線を形成した」)
  • kotobank-teramachi - (日本歴史地名大系) of Tokushima, 「it is generally held that placing a teramachi within a castle town was for its use in massing troops in wartime, and for its carrying an important defensive function」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一般的に城下町のなかに寺町を配するのは戦時下における兵力の屯集に利用されるとともに、防衛上重要な機能を担うことにあったとされており」); and of Matsue, 「temples run in a long line from south to north, and when the castle town was built they took the role of a defensive position against enemies entering from the east or the south, or of a gathering ground for the army when it went out on campaign」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南北に寺院が長く連なるが、城下建設にあたって東方ないし南方から進入する敵を防ぐ防衛陣地、あるいは出陣の際の軍勢集結地としての役割を担っていた。」)
  • kotobank-teramachi - (日本歴史地名大系) of Hikone, 「though it is called Teramachi, the mid-Edo map of samurai residences and the Tenpo map of the castle town both show only samurai houses, with just three temples, Raigoji and others, to the north of it」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺町といっても江戸中期の侍屋敷図および天保城下絵図ともに武家の屋敷ばかりで、その北方に来迎（らいごう）寺など三ヵ寺があるのみ。」)
  • wakayama-teramachi-nwn - 「Temples, whose tile-roofed buildings within the precinct were sturdy and which were moreover enclosed by stone walls and earthen walls, became forts in an emergency. What is more, stone walls and the like could be built and repaired without obtaining the shogunate's permission」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺院は、境内の瓦葺きの建物が頑丈なうえに、石垣や土塀で囲まれているので、有事の際には砦（とりで）になりました。しかも石垣などは、幕府の許可を得ずに、築いたり修復したりできたので」) and 「by gathering many temples to form a "teramachi", a defensive wall for the castle town was completed」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「こうして寺院を多く集め「寺町」を形成することで、城下町の防御壁ができあがったのです。」)
  • miyazu-teramachi - 「At Kanazawa, which places three teramachi, a function of watching the Ikko sect was stressed, and because the broad precincts of temples suit military operations, defensiveness against outside enemies has been assumed as the general function of the teramachi. However, looking at the teramachi of various parts of Japan, there are examples that include the domain lord's family temple (bodaiji) and examples close to pleasure quarters or ports, so their function seems to have changed by region and in the course of history.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「三か所の寺町を配する金沢では、一向宗に対する監視機能が重視され、また、寺院の広い境内は軍事的な作戦行動に向くことから、寺町の一般的な機能として、外敵に対する防御性が想定されてきました。ただし、日本各地の寺町をみると、藩主の菩提寺（ぼだいじ）が含まれている例や、花街や港に近接する例もあり、その機能は地域や歴史の流れの中で変化がみられるようです。」)
  • miyazu-teramachi - 「In the southwest of the former Miyazu castle town, as many as twelve temples are concentrated.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧宮津城下町の南西部には、一二におよぶ寺院が集中して展開します。」)
  • mano-2016-takada-teramachi - 「What is held at present to be almost the settled view is that they were defensive works for the castle town and for the castle itself ...
