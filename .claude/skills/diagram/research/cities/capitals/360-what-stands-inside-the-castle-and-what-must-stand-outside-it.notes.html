• honmaru-jawiki - 「The early-modern honmaru held the oku-goten, where the castle's lord and family lived, and the omote-goten, the seat of government, and as needed had a multi-story keep and turrets built in a row」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「近世における本丸は、城主とその家族が暮らす奥御殿と政庁である表御殿を置き、必要に応じて重層な天守や櫓を建て並べ」)
  • homemate-sannomaru - 「The third bailey held residences where senior retainers lived, places for keeping horses, a privy for the castle's lord, storehouses and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「三の丸には重臣が暮らす屋敷や、馬を飼育する場所、城主のための厠、蔵などが置かれた。」)
  • kotobank-jomai - 「In the Sengoku and Edo periods, granary rice that the shogunate or the domains kept inside the castle against war and famine.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「戦国時代・江戸時代、幕府や諸藩が軍事・凶荒に備えて城中にたくわえておく蔵米。」) and 「The castle rice was of the same nature as the castle-attached arms: it was handed on when the castle's lord changed, and the lord managed it and replaced it year by year.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城米は城付武具と同性格で，城主の交代にも引き継がれ，城主が管理し年々詰替えをした。」)

  • fukuokajyo-sannomaru - 「Though the layout of the estates changed over time, great retainers' estates and the like stood there from the early Edo period to the end of the shogunate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「時期によって屋敷割に変化があるものの、江戸時代初期から幕末まで大身屋敷などが置かれていました。」) (the page says this of the eastern part of Fukuoka's third bailey; it says the western part first held middle and lower retainers' houses)
  • jomai-jawiki - 「later it meant the rice stockpiled by the shogunate and the fudai domains and others as military provisions.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「後に兵糧米のために幕府・譜代諸藩などに備蓄された米のことを指した。」) and 「under the system of Jokyo 2, 442,000 koku of castle rice was stockpiled across the country, and it was to be stockpiled dispersed among 68 places in all, such as the castles held directly by the shogunate - Edo, Osaka, Nijo and Sunpu castles - the castles of the fudai domains, and post stations on the Tokaido」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「貞享2年の制によれば、全国に44.2万石の城米が備蓄され、江戸城・大坂城・二条城・駿府城と言った幕府直轄の城や譜代諸藩の城、東海道宿駅などの計68ヶ所に分散して備蓄されることになっており」); bunka-kawashiri-komegura - 「The Kumamoto domain's Kawashiri rice-granary site is the site of the granary that in the Edo period was the collection point for the Kumamoto domain's tribute rice, and it also served as a naval port.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「熊本藩川尻米蔵跡は，江戸時代，熊本藩の年貢米の集積場となった米蔵跡であり，また軍港としても機能した。」); kotobank-horokusei - 「A system under which retainers were paid kuramai (stipend rice) by their lord」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「家臣が領主から蔵米(俸禄米)を給与された制度」) (the siege stock in the castle and the tribute granary at the port are two attested institutions; that stipends were paid from the port granary in particular is this page's reading)
