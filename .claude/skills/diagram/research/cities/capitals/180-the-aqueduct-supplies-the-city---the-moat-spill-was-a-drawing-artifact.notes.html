• tamagawa-josui-jawiki - 「Water was drawn from the Tama River at the Hamura intake weir, flowed east across the Musashino plateau, and passed through the "water-watch station" (mizubanya) attached to the Yotsuya Okido, which stood near the present-day Yotsuya 4-chome intersection, and was distributed into the city of Edo. The roughly 43 kilometers from Hamura city to the Okido are all open-cut, and below the water-watch station it is an underground conduit using wooden pipes and stone pipes.」 (translated; )
