• fukui-bushi-jutaku - 「"Suganuma go-ke ezu" (Plan of the Suganuma House), 1839 ... This is the mansion plan of the Suganuma family, holders of a 1,000-koku fief, near the Yanagi gate (today, in the vicinity of the courthouse). The site was about 1,000 tsubo, 28 ken (about 59 m) north-south and 32.5 ken (about 51 m) east-west. The north side faces the street, and there is a front gate (omote-gomon) there.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「菅沼御家絵図」1839年 ... 柳御門近く（現、裁判所付近）の知行千石の菅沼家の屋敷図です。南北28間（約59 ｍ）、東西32.5間（約51 m）の約千坪の敷地がありました。北側が通りに面し、表御門があります。」) and 「The Suganuma family were of yoriai-seki rank ... from the Jōkyō halving of stipends (1686) onward, they held 1,000 koku.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「菅沼家は寄合席格で ... 貞享の半知（1686年）以降、1千石となりました。」) ("'Suganuma house plan', 1839 ... the plot plan of the Suganuma family, 1,000 koku, near the Yanagi gate: about 1,000 tsubo, 28 ken north-south, 32.5 ken east-west, facing the street on the north with its front gate." / "The Suganuma were of yoriai rank ... 1,000 koku from the 1686 halving.")
  • shibata-ashigaru-nagaya - 「A nagaya in which the low-ranking samurai of the former Shibata domain lived, eight dwellings joined together in one building. One dwelling is an entrance hall, two six-mat rooms, a three-mat room and a kitchen - quite cramped, and plain in feel.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧新発田藩の下級武士の住んでいた長屋で八軒を一棟に連らねている。一軒分は玄関に六畳二室、三畳、台所でかなりせまく、素朴な感じがする。」)
  • shibata-ashigaru-nagaya - 「Length along the ridge 43.6 m, depth between the beams 7.3 m, hipped roof (yosemune-zukuri), thatched, with an eave on the east face, shingled (kokera-buki).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桁行43.6m、梁間7.3m、寄棟造、茅葺、東面庇付、こけら葺」)
  • matsue-bukeyashiki-about - 「The main house is about 67 tsubo; between the front part, running from the shikidai entrance (the entrance for visitors) to the reception room, and the rear part of private life, the construction and the materials are markedly distinguished, showing the strictness of the samurai separation of public and private.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「主屋はおよそ67坪で、表側である式台玄関(来客用玄関)から座敷に至る部分と、裏側である私生活の部分では造りも材料も特に区別がされ、武家の公私の別の厳しさを示しています。」); matsue-bukeyashiki-about - 「Retainers of the domain of about 500 to 1,000 koku lived here in succession, changing over as residences were reassigned.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「500～1,000石程度の藩士が屋敷替えによって入れ替わり住んでいました。」) (the conversion to square meters is this project's)
