• jokamachi-jawiki - 「Terrain such as rivers was skillfully used, and moats were dug, earthen ramparts and stone walls built」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「川など地形を巧みに利用するとともに、堀を掘り、土塁や石垣を築き」) (the article's own default is the castle at the center; that a flank position goes with a river or the sea is this page's reading of the cases listed)
  • okayama-castle - 「His son, Hideie, determined to place Honmaru (main enclosure and keep) on the hill of Okayama and built Okayama Castle that stands to the present day. ... In addition, the branching channels of the Asahi River were devised as they are today to protect the north and east sides of the castle and utilized to create moats, and a castle town was built that stretched long in the north-south direction between the moats.」
  • kitsuki-castle-jawiki - 「It is a natural stronghold, enclosed by the Takayama River to the north and Morie Bay to the east.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「北は高山川、東は守江湾に囲まれた天然の要害である。」); kitsuki-castle-jawiki - 「It was built atop Daiyama, at the mouth of the Yasaka River.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「八坂川の河口にある台山（だいやま）の上に築かれた。」); kyu-shokyoto-kitsuki - 「Centered on Kitsuki Castle, there are groups of samurai residences on the high ground to the north and south, with the merchants' town sandwiched in the valley between them.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「杵築城を中心に、南北の高台に武家屋敷群があり、その谷間に商人の町が挟まれています。」)
