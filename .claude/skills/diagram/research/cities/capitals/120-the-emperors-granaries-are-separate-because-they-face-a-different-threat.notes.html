• kotobank-jomai - 「In the Sengoku and Edo periods, granary rice that the shogunate or the domains kept inside the castle against war and famine.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「In the narrow sense it means the provisions stored at the shogun's own castles, at the post stations of the Tokaido, and at the castles of the fudai daimyo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the fudai daimyo are the shogun's hereditary vassal lords)
  • kotobank-jomai - 「The castle rice was of the same nature as the castle-attached arms: it was handed on when the castle's lord changed, and the lord managed it and replaced it year by year.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kotobank-asakusa-okura - 「The Asakusa Okura mainly received, by ship, the tax rice and purchased rice of the shogunal lands across the country, and the rice it held was issued as kirimai to the hatamoto and gokenin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (kirimai is stipend rice; the hatamoto and gokenin are the shogun's own retainers)
  • kotobank-asakusa-okura - 「An inlet on the right bank of the Sumida (today Kuramae 1 and 2 chome, Taito ward, Tokyo) was filled in, eight boat-entry channels were cut along 344 ken (about 625 m) of the river side, and a site of 36,648 tsubo (about 12 ha) in all was made.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • jtb-takayama-jinya - 「Within the grounds is the storehouse (onkura) moved from the third bailey of the former Takayama castle; it was once a rice store that held the tax rice paid in by the nearby villages.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Takayama Jin'ya was the shogun's intendant's office for Hida province)
  • kotobank-kurayashiki - 「Most of the kurayashiki were concentrated near Nakanoshima and the Tosabori and Edobori rivers, convenient for water transport」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the case is the daimyo's Osaka warehouse compounds, cited for their waterside siting)
