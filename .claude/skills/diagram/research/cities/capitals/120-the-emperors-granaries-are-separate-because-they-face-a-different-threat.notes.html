• kotobank-jomai - 「In the Sengoku and Edo periods, granary rice that the shogunate or the domains kept inside the castle against war and famine.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「戦国時代・江戸時代、幕府や諸藩が軍事・凶荒に備えて城中にたくわえておく蔵米。」) and 「In the narrow sense it means the provisions stored at the shogun's own castles, at the post stations of the Tokaido, and at the castles of the fudai daimyo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「狭義には幕府直轄城地，東海道宿駅，および譜代大名城地に詰められた兵粮米をさす。」) (the fudai daimyo are the shogun's hereditary vassal lords)
  • kotobank-jomai - 「The castle rice was of the same nature as the castle-attached arms: it was handed on when the castle's lord changed, and the lord managed it and replaced it year by year.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城米は城付武具と同性格で，城主の交代にも引き継がれ，城主が管理し年々詰替えをした。」)
  • kotobank-asakusa-okura - 「The Asakusa Okura mainly received, by ship, the tax rice and purchased rice of the shogunal lands across the country, and the rice it held was issued as kirimai to the hatamoto and gokenin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「浅草御蔵にはおもに全国の幕領の年貢米や買上米を回漕して収納し，収納米は旗本・御家人の切米(きりまい)として支給。」) (kirimai is stipend rice; the hatamoto and gokenin are the shogun's own retainers)
  • kotobank-asakusa-okura - 「An inlet on the right bank of the Sumida was filled in, eight boat-entry channels were cut along 344 ken (about 625 m) of the river side, and a site of 36,648 tsubo (about 12 ha) in all was made.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「隅田川右岸の湾入部（現，東京都台東区蔵前1・2丁目）を埋め立て，川側344間（約625m）の間に8本の船入り堀を設けて，総面積3万6648坪（約12ha）の敷地が造成された。」)
  • jtb-takayama-jinya - 「Within the grounds is the storehouse (onkura) moved from the third bailey of the former Takayama castle; it was once a rice store that held the tax rice paid in by the nearby villages.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「敷地内には、旧高山城三ノ丸から移築した御蔵（おんくら）があり、かつては近隣の村々から納められた年貢米を収納する米蔵であった。」) (the Takayama Jin'ya was the shogun's intendant's office for Hida province)
  • kotobank-kurayashiki - 「Most of the kurayashiki were concentrated near Nakanoshima and the Tosabori and Edobori rivers, convenient for water transport」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「蔵屋敷の多くは、水運に便利な中之島や土佐堀川、江戸堀川付近に集中していた」) (the case is the daimyo's Osaka warehouse compounds, cited for their waterside siting)
