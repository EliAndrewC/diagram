The aqueduct supplies the CITY - the moat spill was a drawing artifact

Sources: tamagawa-josui-jawiki (the open cut hands off at the Yotsuya Ōkido 水番所 to buried wooden and stone pipes), edokara-yosuibaki

The question (GM 2026-08-09): the aqueduct exists because a capital outgrows its wells - does the cut supply the city, or feed the city moat?

It supplies the city. The system is Edo's: OPEN cut outside the wall, BURIED pipe inside it, the GATE as the boundary; what a resident sees inside is the draw-basins, with the wells. The open cut therefore ENDS at the gate, in the historical furniture: a TERMINAL BASIN (head-tank) at the gate end, the settling tank where the open cut hands off to the buried pipe - Edo's josui ended in exactly such tanks. The glyph draws it standing clear of the moat, so the drawing says "supply enters here", not "stream joins moat" (any real josui also spilled surplus somewhere, and the moat is where a surplus WOULD go).
