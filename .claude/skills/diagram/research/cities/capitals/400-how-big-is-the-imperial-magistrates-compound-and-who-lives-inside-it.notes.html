• machi-bugyo-jawiki - 「The town magistrate's official residence was inside the magistracy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町奉行の役宅は奉行所内にあった。」)
  • edo-ashigaru-bugyo-yakutaku - 「It combined the function of a bansho (an office) with that of a yakutaku (a residence).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「番所（役所）としての機能と、役宅（住居）としての機能を兼ね備えていました。」), 「It divides broadly into two areas, the office on the east side and the residence on the west.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「東側は番所、西側は役宅と、大きく二つのエリアに分かれています。」) and 「In the part projecting on the west side of the north face were the row houses where the magistrate's retainers lived, the rooms of the chugen (servants) and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「北面西側に突出している部分には、奉行の家来が住む長屋や、中間部屋などがありました。」) (the page describes the North magistracy and says the South was built much the same)
  • kazusa-bugyosho - 「It seems the North and South magistracies each had a town magistrate's mansion of over 1,000 tsubo standing on a vast site of 2,500 to 2,600 tsubo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南北の奉行所はそれぞれ二千五～六百坪の広大な敷地に千坪余りの町奉行屋敷が建っていたようです。」) (the page hedges its figure with "it seems"; 1 tsubo is about 3.3 m^2, or 35.6 square feet)
  • takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「The Takayama Jin'ya is the intendant's office, the Hida district intendant's office (jin'ya), set up by the Edo shogunate to administer Hida province as its direct territory.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高山陣屋（たかやまじんや）は、江戸幕府が飛騨国を直轄領として管理するために設置した代官所・飛騨郡代役所（陣屋）。」)
  • tabiiro-takayama-jinya - 「within grounds exceeding 3,000 tsubo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「3000坪を超える敷地内で」) (that the grounds hold the office's rice storehouse is jtb-takayama-jinya's, quoted at the Emperor's granaries above)
  • machi-bugyo-jawiki - 「There were 25 yoriki and 100 doshin in each of the South and North magistracies.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「与力は南北奉行所に25名ずつ、同心は100名ずついた。」) (the yoriki were the magistracy's assistant officers, the doshin its constables)
  • orange-hatchobori-yoriki - 「The granted estates of the town magistracy's yoriki were about 200 to 300 tsubo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町奉行所与力の拝領屋敷は２００坪から３００坪くらいで」) and 「The residential plots in the Hatchobori group housing were somewhat over 100 tsubo per doshin」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「八丁堀組屋敷での居宅地は同心一人当たり約１００坪余であるが」) (the page quotes the Edo-Tokyo encyclopedia for the first; it places only the constables' plots at Hatchobori by name)
  • edo-hantei-jawiki - 「Lower-ranking samurai lived in row houses (nagaya) and the like built inside the domain estate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「下級の武士は藩邸内に設けられた長屋などに居住した。」)
