• edo-hantei-jawiki - 「Under the rule of Genbun 3 (1738), daimyo of 10,000 to 20,000 koku were given 2,500 tsubo, those of 50,000 to 60,000 koku 5,000 tsubo, those of 100,000 to 150,000 koku 7,000 tsubo, and so on. In practice many estates were larger than this standard, with cases such as the Kaga domain, whose upper estate alone reached 100,000 tsubo, and it was not strictly applied.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「元文3年（1738年）の規定では、1 - 2万石の大名で2500坪、5 - 6万石で5000坪、10 - 15万石で7000坪などとされていた。実際にはこの基準より広い屋敷も多く、上屋敷だけで10万坪にも達した加賀藩などの例もあり、厳密な適用はされていなかった。」)
  • fukui-bushi-jutaku - 「For middle and lower retainers, the area of the estate plot was fixed according to the stipend.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中・下級藩士は、禄高に応じて屋敷地の面積が定められていました。」) and 「It shows that the same stipend was given the same plot area in practice.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「同じ禄高同士では、屋敷地面積を揃えて運用していたことがわかります。」) (the page's table leaves the upper ranks' plot areas unset)
  • fukui-bushi-jutaku - 「There was a site of about 1,000 tsubo, 28 ken (about 59 m) north-south and 32.5 ken (about 51 m) east-west.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南北28間（約59 ｍ）、東西32.5間（約51 m）の約千坪の敷地がありました。」) and 「it is known that the building walls facing the street were row houses and the like in which servants lived」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「通りに面した建物の壁は奉公人が暮らす長屋などであったことがわかり」) (the house is the Suganuma, of 1,000 koku; the same page elsewhere gives the plot as 915 tsubo)

  • kanazawa-honda-kamiyashiki - 「The Honda upper estate was the residence of the head of the Honda house, one of the Kaga Eight Houses, senior retainers of the Kaga domain; it was also where that house's retainers came to serve and ran the house's affairs, and it boasted a site of over 10,000 tsubo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「本多家上屋敷は、加賀藩の重臣だった加賀八家本多家当主の居屋敷で、同家の家臣が出仕して家政を執り行う場でもあり、一万坪余もの敷地面積を誇りました。」)
