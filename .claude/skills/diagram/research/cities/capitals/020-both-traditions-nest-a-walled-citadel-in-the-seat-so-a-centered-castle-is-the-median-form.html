Both traditions nest a walled citadel in the seat, so a CENTERED castle is the median form

Sources: jokamachi-wiki-corpus, baidu-yangzhou-city-site, mnwhstq-quanzhou-walls, sogamae-jawiki

Japan. A jokamachi was established4 "with the lord's castle in the center," and the town was zoned in concentric rings by status, closeness to the castle tracking rank:5

  • samurai innermost6, "retainers with higher position had a closer location to the castle" (surviving place names Sange, Kamiyashiki-cho)
  • ashigaru outside the townsmen district (Ban-cho, Teppo-cho)100
  • merchants and artisans outside the samurai town, segregated by trade (Gofuku-machi drapers, Kaji-machi smiths)7
  • teramachi at the outer rim, where8 "spacious temples formed part of the city defenses"

Two further details drive rules of their own: main roads were deliberately routed past the castle's front rather than its rear,9 "to indicate the glory of the ruler"; and total enclosure of the whole town (sogamae - moat plus earth mounds) became increasingly common, Odawara and Osaka being the exemplars.10 The sogamae is what makes our single-rampart capital honest: a castle town that walls its whole population is an attested form, not a convenience.233

China. The prefectural seat nested the same way - yacheng (the administrative city) inside zicheng (the inner city) inside luocheng (the outer city).145 Tang Yangzhou's luocheng was the residential and commercial city, its zicheng the seat of the offices.118

The decision. The centered castle is not an import we are choosing; it is what both traditions do, so the centered castle is the DEFAULT and the edge castle is the variant.
