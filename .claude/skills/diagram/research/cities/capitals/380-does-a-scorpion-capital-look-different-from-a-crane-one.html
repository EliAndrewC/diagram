Does a Scorpion capital look different from a Crane one?

Sources: the GM's ruling of 2026-08-08; jokamachi-jawiki (one zoning rule, rank by distance from the castle, and trade wards named for their trades)

Not in its bones. The GM ruled on 2026-08-08 that clan identity changes labels only: "all clans fundamentally have the same needs and the same shared material reality", so the tier's layout, program and proportions are clan-independent. The setting's own notes, searched for it, describe the clans' temperaments, wealth and politics and never how a clan lays out its capital. The history agrees in its broad lines: the castle towns of Japan's many domains followed one zoning rule, in which, basically, the higher a retainer's status the closer to the castle their house stood, and merchants and artisans were settled by trade into wards that kept names like Gofukumachi and Kajimachi. What varies is dedications, names and the character of an individual city - a Scorpion seat's two sovereign temples are dedicated to that clan's patron fortunes, its lineage compounds carry that house's names, its trade wards are named for their trades - while the ring, the castle, the avenue, the wards and the caste proportions are the ones any other clan's capital would build. The map gains nothing from clan-specific layout rules, and the honest reason to say so out loud is that inventing them is the obvious thing to do and it would be wrong.

The rule the map follows: a capital's clan changes only names, dedications and labels - the patron fortunes of the two sovereign temples, the lineage names on the compounds, the flavor of the individual city. No layout rule, no count and no proportion varies by clan.
