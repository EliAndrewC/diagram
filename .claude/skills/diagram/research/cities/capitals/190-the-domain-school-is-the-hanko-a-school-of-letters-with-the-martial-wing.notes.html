  • nisshinkan-jawiki - 「This facility serves at once as a museum, a dōjō (an archery hall and a martial-arts hall), a training and lodging facility, a film studio location and so on ...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「Until the age of fifteen they belonged to the sodokusho, the recitation hall (the elementary school), and studied etiquette, calligraphy, martial arts and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「in March 1987 (Showa 62) it was fully restored and opened as the Aizu domain school Nisshinkan in Kawahigashi, Aizuwakamatsu (Kawahigashi, Kawanuma district, when it opened)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kodokan-ibaraki-about - 「placing the hall of letters on the north side of the main hall (the school palace) and the Shizendo, and the martial hall on the south side」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「a riding ground, a drill ground, an archery ground, a gunnery ground and so on were also laid out」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • meirinkan-jawiki - 「it was laid out around the Taiseiden, which held the wooden tablets of Confucius, Mencius and others, and to its left and right the martial halls - the sword hall, the spear hall, the gunnery hall and so on - formed the outer ring of the school」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
