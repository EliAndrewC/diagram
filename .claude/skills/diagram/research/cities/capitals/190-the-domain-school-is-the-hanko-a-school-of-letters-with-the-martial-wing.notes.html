• hanko-jawiki - 「As a nationwide tendency, the martial arts too were encouraged at the hankō: pupils entered at seven or eight, first learned letters, later studied the martial arts, and graduated from fourteen or fifteen to about twenty.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「全国的な傾向として、藩校では武芸も奨励され、7歳・8歳で入学して第一に文を習い、後に武芸を学び、14歳・15歳から20歳くらいで卒業する。」) and 「In their period of expansion they came to 255 schools nationwide, founded in almost every domain.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「発展期には全国に255校に上り[3]、ほぼ全藩に設立された。」) ("As a nationwide tendency the hankō also encouraged the martial arts: pupils entered at 7 or 8, learned letters first, then the martial arts, graduating between about 14-15 and 20." / "At their height they reached 255 schools nationwide, founded in nearly every domain.")
  • nisshinkan-jawiki - 「This facility serves at once as a museum, a dōjō (an archery hall and a martial-arts hall), a training and lodging facility, a film studio location and so on ...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「この施設は、博物館・道場（弓道場・武道場）・研修や宿泊施設・映画撮影所などを兼ねており」) and 「Until the age of fifteen they belonged to the sodokusho, the recitation hall (the elementary school), and studied etiquette, calligraphy, martial arts and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「15歳までは素読所（小学）に属し、礼法、書学、武術などを学んだ。」)
