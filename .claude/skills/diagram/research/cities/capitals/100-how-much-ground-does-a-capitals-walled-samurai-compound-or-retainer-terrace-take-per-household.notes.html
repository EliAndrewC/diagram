• fukui-bushi-jutaku - 「"Suganuma go-ke ezu" (Plan of the Suganuma House), 1839 ... This is the mansion plan of the Suganuma family, holders of a 1,000-koku fief, near the Yanagi gate (today, in the vicinity of the courthouse). The site was about 1,000 tsubo, 28 ken (about 59 m) north-south and 32.5 ken (about 51 m) east-west. The north side faces the street, and there is a front gate (omote-gomon) there.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The Suganuma family were of yoriai-seki rank ... from the Jōkyō halving of stipends (1686) onward, they held 1,000 koku.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shibata-ashigaru-nagaya - 「A nagaya in which the low-ranking samurai of the former Shibata domain lived, eight dwellings joined together in one building. One dwelling is an entrance hall, two six-mat rooms, a three-mat room and a kitchen - quite cramped, and plain in feel.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); the page's title and designation: 「Former Shibata domain ashigaru row house」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「Important Cultural Property」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shibata-ashigaru-nagaya - 「Length along the ridge 43.6 m, depth along the beams 7.3 m, hipped roof (yosemune-zukuri), thatched, with an eave on the east face, shingled (kokera-buki).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • matsue-bukeyashiki-about - 「The main house is about 67 tsubo; between the front part, running from the shikidai entrance (the entrance for visitors) to the reception room, and the rear part of private life, the construction and the materials are markedly distinguished, showing the strictness of the samurai separation of public and private.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); matsue-bukeyashiki-about - 「Retainers of the domain of about 500 to 1,000 koku lived here in succession, changing over as residences were reassigned.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the conversion to square meters is this project's)
