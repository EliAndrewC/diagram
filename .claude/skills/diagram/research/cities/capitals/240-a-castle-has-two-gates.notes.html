• nihon-no-shiro-jawiki - 「At the gate-mouth on the castle's front side - in early-modern castles usually the south - were set the ote-mon or oute-mon (the great gate); at the gate-mouth at the rear was set the karamete-mon (the rear gate).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城の正面（近世城郭では通常は南）の虎口には大手門・追手門（おおてもん）、裏の虎口には搦手門（からめてもん）が構えられた。」)
  • karamete-jawiki - 「The karamete-mon is one of the castle gates: the gate of the karamete entrance at the rear, opened opposite the ōte-mon.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「搦手門（からめてもん）は城門の一つで、大手門に対して開かれる裏手にあたる搦手口の門。」)
  • homemate-karamete - 「Japanese castles are designed so that, in a siege operation, the tactic of drawing the enemy in near the ōte-mon and running them down with castle troops who sortie out from the karamete-mon can be carried out」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本の城は、籠城作戦の際に大手門付近に敵を寄せ付け、搦手門から打って出た城兵で追い詰める、と言う手法が実行できるように設計されている」) (the encyclopedia article cited above frames the rear gate as an escape route; the glossary states the sortie doctrine)
  • edo-36-mitsuke-jawiki - 「The "thirty-six mitsuke of Edo castle" is a listing of the 36 principal ones among the mitsuke (watch guardhouses) placed at Edo castle. Many were installed as a single unit with a bridge thrown across the moat.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸城三十六見附（えどじょうさんじゅうろくみつけ）とは、江戸城に置かれた見附（見張り番所）のうちの主な36か所を挙げたもの。堀に架けられた橋と一体的に設置されたものが多い。」) and 「In fact there seem to have been many more watch stations at Edo's castle gates (various accounts give 66, 90 and so on), but according to the records of the bakufu's works office there were 26 gates with masugata gatehouses in the outer enceinte ... and for the sake of how it sounded, ten notable gates were added to the 26 masugata gates to make the "thirty-six mitsuke" ...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「実際には、江戸の城門の見張り場所自体はもっと多数あったようだが（66、90など諸説あり）、枡形門を持つ見附は、幕府作事方の資料によると外曲輪に26門あり ... 語呂の関係から、枡形の26門に、目ぼしい10門を足して「三十六見附」とし」) (many were built as one with a bridge over the moat." / "In fact there seem to have been many more watch-posts (66, 90 by various accounts); by the bakufu works office's records there were 26 masugata gates in the outer works ... for the sake of the ring of it, 10 notable gates were added to the 26 to make 'thirty-six'.")
