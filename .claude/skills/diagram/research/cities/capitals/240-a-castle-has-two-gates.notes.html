• nihon-no-shiro-jawiki - 「At the gate-mouth on the castle's front side - in early-modern castles usually the south - were set the ote-mon or oute-mon (the great gate); at the gate-mouth at the rear was set the karamete-mon (the rear gate).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • karamete-jawiki - 「The karamete-mon is one of the castle gates: the gate of the karamete entrance at the rear, opened opposite the ōte-mon.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • homemate-karamete - 「Japanese castles are designed so that, in a siege operation, the tactic of drawing the enemy in near the ōte-mon and running them down with castle troops who sortie out from the karamete-mon can be carried out」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the encyclopedia article cited above frames the rear gate as an escape route; the glossary states the sortie doctrine)
  • edo-36-mitsuke-jawiki - 「The "thirty-six mitsuke of Edo castle" is a listing of the 36 principal ones among the mitsuke (watch guardhouses) placed at Edo castle. Many were installed as a single unit with a bridge thrown across the moat.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「In fact there seem to have been many more watch stations at Edo's castle gates (various accounts give 66, 90 and so on), but according to the records of the bakufu's works office there were 26 gates with masugata gatehouses in the outer enceinte ... and for the sake of how it sounded, ten notable gates were added to the 26 masugata gates to make the "thirty-six mitsuke" ...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (many were built as one with a bridge over the moat." / "In fact there seem to have been many more watch-posts (66, 90 by various accounts); by the bakufu works office's records there were 26 masugata gates in the outer works ... for the sake of the ring of it, 10 notable gates were added to the 26 to make 'thirty-six'.")
