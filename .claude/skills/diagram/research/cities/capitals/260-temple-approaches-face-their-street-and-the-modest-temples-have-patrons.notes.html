• sando-jawiki - 「In the narrow sense it denotes only the passage inside the sacred bounds, such as within the torii or the temple gate; in the broad sense it means the whole of the road running to the shrine or temple from a place of heavy foot traffic such as a highway.」 (translated; ), 「Generally, there are also many places where the sando is recognized together with the torii-front town or gate-front town that arose of its own accord around an influential shrine or temple,」 (translated; ) and 「Also, places where the line of a highway was bent so that it came close to a shrine or temple for the sake of pilgrimage were not rare either,」 (translated; ) ("Narrowly it means only the path inside the sacred bounds of the torii or gate; broadly it means the whole way from a busy place such as a highway to the shrine or temple." / "Sandō are often recognized together with the torii-front and gate-front towns that grew up around influential shrines and temples." / "It was not rare for a highway to be bent toward a shrine or temple for the convenience of pilgrims.")
  • bodaiji-jawiki - 「A bodaiji (菩提寺) is a temple whose sect a household has been devoted to for generations and at which it holds memorial services for the enlightenment (bodai) of its ancestors.」 (translated; ) and 「For example, the Tokugawa house's Kan'eiji and Zojoji are generally called bodaiji, while the Date house's Zuihoji is called a kogein, and so on.」 (translated; )
