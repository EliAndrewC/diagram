• funabansho-jawiki - 「A funabansho is an office that, in the Edo period, domains with a coastline set up at the landings of routes that boats often traveled and at other key points, to inspect passing boats and to collect taxes, among other duties.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「船番所（ふなばんしょ）とは、江戸時代に海岸線を持っていた藩は、船が良く航行する航路の河岸、そのほかの要所に設けて、通行する船を検査し、税の徴収などにあたった役所。」) The article is a stub, flagged since 2019 as lacking sources.
  • koto-nakagawa-funabansho - 「The Nakagawa funabansho was set up on the 6th day of the 6th month of Kanbun 1 (1661) on the north bank at the Nakagawa mouth of the Onagi river. It was a checkpoint for river-boat inspection, examining the people and goods entering and leaving Edo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中川船番所は、寛文元年（1661年）6月6日小名木川の中川口北岸に設置され、江戸に出入りする人と物資を査検した川船改のための関所である。」)
  • koto-nakagawa-funabansho - 「As to its size, the Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko records a little over 26 ken east-west (about 47 m) and a little over 17 ken north-south (about 31 m).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「規模については、『新編武蔵風土記稿』に東西26間余（約47メートル）、南北17間余（約31メートル）と記されている。」)
  • koto-nakagawa-funabansho - 「According to the illustration in the Edo Meisho Zue, a wooden palisade ran around the station, ten spears were kept there, and a watch hut was built at the edge of the Onagi river.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「また、『江戸名所図会』の挿絵によると、番所の周囲には木柵がめぐらされ、槍10筋が装備されており、小名木川縁には番小屋が建てられている。」)
