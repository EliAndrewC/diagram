• takasegawa-kyoto-jawiki - 「an entirely artificial canal, begun in 1611 and completed in 1614, about 8 m wide and normally about 30 cm deep」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「1611年に高瀬川を開削開始し、1614年に完成した川幅8m水深通常30cm程度の全くの人工運河である」)
  • kyoto-city-takasegawa - 「The boats' length averaged 13 meters, and their width was 2 meters.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「舟の長さは平均13メートル，幅は2メートル」) (that 8 m is about four boats wide is this page's arithmetic)
  • kotobank-dotonbori - 「north to south, 20 ken on the east side of the canal and 34 ken on the west side (Tenmei 7, the "Matsudaira Iwami-no-kami dono o-hatsuiri ni tsuki sashidashi o-oboegaki", held by Kyoto University)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南北は堀の東側で二〇間、西側で三四間（天明七年「松平石見守殿御初入ニ付差出御覚書」京都大学蔵）」) (Tenmei 7 is 1787; the meters are this page's conversion at a ken of about 1.82 m)
  • osaka-city-dotonbori - 「the western Dotonbori section is about 50 meters and the eastern Dotonbori section about 30 meters」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「道頓堀西部区間が約50メートル、道頓堀東部区間が約30メートル」)
  • sohu-pingjiang-suzhou - 「These rivers were for the most part cut by hand; both banks of the channels are faced with solid stone revetments, and some banks also carry wooden or stone railings.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「这些河流大都由人工开凿，河道两岸都砌有坚固的石驳岸，岸边有的还设有木石栏杆。」)
  • guidetokyo-edo-horiwari - 「This waterway was made for the defense of the castle, but at the same time it also served as a drainage channel and a canal. On the castle side stone walls were built, while the goods landing on the east side was called the Shirobe kashi.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「この水路は、城の防衛を目的につくられたが、同時に排水路や運河の役割も果たしていた。城側城廓には石垣が組まれていたが、東側の物揚場は城辺河岸と呼ばれていた。」) (the waterway is Edo's outer moat)
  • sohu-pingjiang-suzhou - 「houses in the city often have their front door on the street and their back door on the river, with a water landing before the door or behind the house, whose stone steps lead down to the water, for boats, drawing water and washing」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「城内居舍常常是前门临街、后门沿河，门前宅后设有水埠码头，有石级踏步通至河面，以便航运、汲水和洗涤」)
  • kashi-jawiki - 「From the Edo period on, merchants running wholesale houses and their storehouses gathered at a kashi, forming a single commercial settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代に入ると河岸には問屋を商う商人やその蔵が集まり、一つの商業集落を形成していた。」)
  • kyoto-city-takasegawa - 「The Takase River's boat-entry basins are the places for loading and unloading goods and for turning boats around.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高瀬川の舟入は、物の積み下ろしと舟の方向転換の場所です。」); 「at first seven were made between Nijo and Shijo, but by the end of the seventeenth century they had grown to nine, and by the middle of the eighteenth century they had fallen to seven, and so it remained until Meiji」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「当初は二条～四条間に七か所作られましたが、十七世紀末には九か所に増え、十八世紀中ごろには七か所に減り明治に至りました」) (the second passage counts those basins)
