  • zakoba-jawiki - 「the Zakoba fish market, which stood on the left (east) bank of the upper Hyakkenbori river」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「百間堀川上流左岸（東岸）にあった雑喉場魚市場」)
  • zakoba-jawiki - 「Zakoba, near also to the river mouth where the Aji and Kizu rivers, Osaka's two great waterways, divide, and so favorable for carrying fish」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「大坂の二大水路である安治川・木津川の分流点である川口にも近く、魚荷の輸送に有利な雑喉場」); 「The Zakoba fish market met three times a day, morning, noon and evening」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「雑喉場の魚市は朝、昼、夕と1日に3回立ち」)
  • kotobank-zakoba - 「in the area of Zakoba-machi facing the Hyakkenbori river, and together with the Dojima rice market and the Tenma produce market (now Kita ward) it was called one of Osaka's three great markets」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「雑喉場町の百間堀（ひやつけんぼり）川に面した地域にあり、堂島（どうじま）米市場・天満（てんま）青物市場（現北区）とともに大坂三大市場と称せられ」); 「the river-fish market at the Kyobashi bridgehead (now Miyakojima ward)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「京橋（きようばし）詰の川魚市場（現都島区）」)
  • tenma-aomono-jawiki - 「It stood along the Okawa, in what are now Tenjinbashi 1-chome and Tenma 4-chome and 3-chome.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現在の天神橋1丁目・天満4丁目・同3丁目の大川沿いにあった。」) (it moved there in 1653, from an earlier site at Kyobashi)
  • chuo-uoichiba-ezumen - 「the piers at the landing's edge from Nihonbashi to Edobashi (12 places), the back sheds along the landing for holding fish and shellfish for a time and the front sheds where they were sold (50 buildings), aqueduct wells (25 places), and the shopfronts of Honfunacho and the display stalls projecting into the street (126 places)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本橋から江戸橋に至る河岸端の桟橋(12カ所)、魚介類を一時保管する河岸沿いの裏納屋と売り場の表納屋(50軒)、上水井戸(25カ所)、本船町の表店と往還にせり出した見世棚(126カ所)」); 「the display stalls, many with a narrow frontage of 3 shaku」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「狭小な3尺間口が多い見世棚」) (the fifty counts back and front sheds together, and the 126 the shopfronts and stalls together; the page dates the scroll to 1842)
  • chuo-uoichiba-ezumen - 「the ita-bune (a flat board about 2 shaku 3 sun (about 70 cm) wide and 5 shaku (about 150 cm) long)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「板舟」(幅2尺3寸〈約70cm〉・長さ5尺〈約150cm〉ほどの平板)」); 「the plan carries no record of them ... it is not hard to imagine」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「記載はありません…想像に難くありません」)
  • kotobank-uogashi - 「it means a kashi with a fish market that wholesales fish and shellfish to ordinary traders; they are found in every part of the country」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「魚介類を一般業者に卸売りする魚市場のある河岸(かし)をいい、全国各地にみられる。」) (the Nipponica's entry)
