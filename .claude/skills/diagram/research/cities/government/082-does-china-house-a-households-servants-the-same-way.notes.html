• siheyuan-zhwiki - 「A large siheyuan is walled in from the outside; the walls are tall and high, and no windows are opened in them, in order to display its privacy.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「大四合院从外边用墙包围，墙壁高大，不开窗户，以显示其隐秘性。」) (the blank high wall and, on the same page, the gate on the southeast side; the FIELD of such compounds and the single notch are this page's)
  • daozuofang - 「The daozuofang is the southernmost row of rooms in the whole siheyuan; because its doors and windows all face north, the daylight is poor, and it therefore generally serves as guest rooms or as rooms in which the servants live. Its easternmost room is the sishu (family school) and its westernmost the privy; the rooms between them are generally lived in by the servants.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「倒座房是整个四合院中最南端的一排房子，因其门窗都向北，采光不好，因此一般作为客房或者下人居住的房屋。其最东为私塾，最西为厕所，其间的房子一般为仆人居住。」) 「In the Beijing siheyuan, on one side of the daozuofang the main gate opens to the south; its eaves wall abuts the hutong and generally has no windows.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「在北京四合院中，倒座房一侧朝南开大门，其檐墙临胡同，一般不开窗。」)
  • siheyuan-zhwiki - 「Its eaves wall faces the hutong, and it generally has no windows.」 and 「Its easternmost room was the private school and its westernmost the privy; the rooms between them were generally occupied by servants.」 and 「The rear building is usually the house of the innermost courtyard, near the boundary of the compound, and usually the owner's daughters lived there.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「其檐墙临胡同，一般不开窗。」 and original: 「其最东为私塾，最西为厕所，其间的房子一般为仆人居住。」 and original: 「后罩房通常是最裏一进院子的，靠近院落边界的房子，通常主人的女儿居住。」) (the courtyard-house article, for the two halves the entry rests on: the south row's windowless back IS the compound's street wall, and the rear row is family rather than staff.)

  • neixiang-yamen - 「Entering the main gate and heading north, there are two rectangular courtyards one after the other; in the first courtyard functional buildings are set on both the east and the west sides - on the west the Yinbin guest hall, the dining hall and the jail, on the east the yamen god's temple, the earth-god shrine and the Three Corps (sanban) courtyard.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「进大门向北，依次是两个长方形院落，第一个院落东西侧都设置了功能性建筑，西侧设寅宾馆、膳馆、监狱，东侧设衙神庙、土地祠、三班院。」)
