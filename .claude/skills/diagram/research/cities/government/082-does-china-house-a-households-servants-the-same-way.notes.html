• siheyuan-zhwiki - 「A large siheyuan is walled in from the outside; the walls are tall and high, and no windows are opened in them, in order to display its privacy.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (the blank high wall and, on the same page, the gate on the southeast side; the FIELD of such compounds and the single notch are this page's)
  • daozuofang - 「The daozuofang is the southernmost row of rooms in the whole siheyuan; because its doors and windows all face north, the daylight is poor, and it therefore generally serves as guest rooms or as rooms in which the servants live. Its easternmost room is the sishu (family school) and its westernmost the privy; the rooms between them are generally lived in by the servants.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) 「In the Beijing siheyuan, on one side of the daozuofang the main gate opens to the south; its eaves wall abuts the hutong and generally has no windows.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • siheyuan-zhwiki - 「Its eaves wall faces the hutong, and it generally has no windows.」 and 「Its easternmost room was the private school and its westernmost the privy; the rooms between them were generally occupied by servants.」 and 「The rear building is usually the house of the innermost courtyard, near the boundary of the compound, and usually the owner's daughters lived there.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (the courtyard-house article, for the two halves the entry rests on: the south row's windowless back IS the compound's street wall, and the rear row is family rather than staff.)
