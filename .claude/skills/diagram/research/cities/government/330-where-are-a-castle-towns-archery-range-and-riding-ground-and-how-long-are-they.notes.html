  • kotobank-baba - 「in the Ikeda family's time, a riding ground 5 ken wide on the riverside embankment running two and a half cho north from beside the castle's main-gate moat to the Zuio-ji temple.   • kotobank-yaba - 「in samurai houses the length was fixed at 33 bow-lengths (about 76 meters) and the width likewise at one (about 2.3 meters); at the shooting place an azuchi was built and the target hung on it.
