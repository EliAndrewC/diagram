How was a samurai household's live-in staff hired?

Sources: buke-hokonin-wiki (who took service, on what terms, and the brokers), kotobank-degawari (the changeover day), kotobank-hitoyado (who was hired through the placement brokers, and when the trade arose)

The domestic servants who lived inside a samurai household's walls (where in the plot they lived is the question before this one) were live-in staff on contracts of a year or half a year, or hired by the day (the degawari changeover, the 5th day of the 3rd month, set by a shogunal decree that the two reference works date to 1668 and to 1669), hired for wages from peasant and townsman households, more and more through placement brokers, a trade that grew up with the cities. So a castle town's servant population is partly recruited from the chonin-chi, and a household with no connections of its own turned to the brokers.
