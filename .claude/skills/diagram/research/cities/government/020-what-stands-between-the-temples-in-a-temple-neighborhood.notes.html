• hokora-jawiki - 「A simplified form of shrine, set up in places difficult for a person to enter, or on land owned by a settlement or by an individual.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「神社の簡略形で、人が立ち入ることが難しい場所や、集落や個人が所有する土地に設置され」) (small, on community or private land, at roadsides and junctions, with no resident priest; 「tended by the neighborhood」 is on no page read)
  • akiba-jinja-jawiki - 「In the case of a small shrine, because the deity is also a fire-quelling one, stone-built shrines that do not burn easily are seen. They are often small shrines, and in some cases several of them are set up within a single town block.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「祠の場合は火伏せの神でもあるため、燃えにくい石造りの祠などが見かけられる。小さな祠であることが多く、一つの町内に何箇所も設置されている場合もある。」) (the density the map needs - several within one town block - and the reason a block would have one. The same sentence gives the material as stone, against the vermilion wooden shed the map draws.)
