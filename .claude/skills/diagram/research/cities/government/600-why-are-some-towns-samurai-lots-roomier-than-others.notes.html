  • harakata-sashiko-jawiki - 「The Uesugi gave the harakata-shu residences of 150 tsubo (about 495 square meters) in total area, but the back half was a vegetable field and they were forced to live self-sufficiently」 and 「the frontage was fixed by status at 6 ken (about 11 meters) to 8 ken, the depth at about 25 ken」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「上杉氏は原方衆に対し、総面積150坪（約495平方メートル）の屋敷を与えた[3] が、裏半分は野菜畑とし、自給自足の生活を余儀なくされた[5]。」 and original: 「間口は身分に応じて6間（約11メートル）から8間[5]、奥行は約25間と定められていた」) (the square feet are the record's conversion; the two measures come from different references of the article)
