  • denken-kakunodate - 「The preservation district lies near the center of the former samurai town and is the residential land of upper and middle samurai.」 and 「together with the lot division of the end of the Edo period and the house, gate and storehouse arrangement of the residences, it keeps the character of a samurai town well」 (translated; )
