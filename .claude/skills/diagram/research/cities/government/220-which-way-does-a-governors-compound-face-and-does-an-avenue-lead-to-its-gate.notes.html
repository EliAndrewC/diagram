• qing-yamen-tushuo - 「by the ideas of fengshui the yamen usually stood at the center of the city ... meaning 'governing from the middle'. Hence too the common saying 'the yamen's gate opens to the south'.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「衙署通常都位于城市中央…有"居中而治"之意。故又有"衙门口朝南开"的俗谚。」) and 「from south to north in order the screen wall, the main gate, the ceremonial gate, the admonition pavilion」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「自南而北依次为照壁、大门、仪门、戒石亭」)
  • bjd-qing-yamen - 「sits to the north facing south」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「坐北朝南」) (the Zhili governor-general's yamen at Baoding)
  • kokuga-jawiki - 「Since the main hall was usually built toward the north, facing south, the side halls were placed east and west」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「正殿は北寄り正面に南向きに建てられることが多かったので、脇殿は東西に置かれ」)
  • kokuga-jawiki - 「The government-hall district has a one-cho plot and is enclosed on four sides by a fence ... A gate is set in the middle of each side, and an avenue 9 meters wide runs south from the south gate. ... various office buildings are supposed to have been placed on its west and south sides.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「政庁域は一町の地割り区画をもち四周を塀で囲み…各辺の中央には門を設け、南門から幅9メートルの大路が南に走っている。…西側や南側に各種の官衙建物が配置されていたと想定されている。」) (the Shimotsuke provincial office) and 「the 'Suzaku-oji' runs north-south through the middle of the kokuga district」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「国衙域中央を南北に「朱雀大路」が走り」) (the Suō provincial office)
  • sohu-wuzhou-yamen - 「the screen wall faces the main gate, set across the street」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「照壁面向大门 , 隔街而设」) and 「Memorial arch: also called the xuanhua arch. Outside the yamen, by the street」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「牌坊：又称宣化坊。在衙门外 , 临街」)
