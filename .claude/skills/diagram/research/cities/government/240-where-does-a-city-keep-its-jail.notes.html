• siyu-zhwiki - 「In the early Ming every prefecture, department and county across the country had a jail; administrative units at prefecture level and above had a jail office to manage the business of the jail.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「明初全国府州县各地皆设有监狱，府级以上行政单位，设有司狱司来管理狱政事务。」) and 「as for departments, counties and such administrative units, no official dedicated to managing the jail was appointed」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「至于州、县等行政单位，不设专职管理监狱的职官」)
  • qing-yamen-tushuo - 「the jails of local yamen were all set southwest of the great hall, outside the ceremonial gate ... and so it was called the 'South Jail'.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「地方衙门的监狱都设在大堂西南仪门之外…故称之为“南监”。」) (the book's reason for the direction, from divination, is left out: it is the book's gloss)
  • iolaw-mingqing-jails - 「each jail has an inner jail to hold the condemned and an outer jail to hold those sentenced to penal servitude, exile and below; women are set in a separate room, called the women's jail」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「各监有内监以禁死囚，有外监以禁徒、流以下，妇人别置一室，曰女监」), 「department and county jails were divided into four grades, the light jail, the outer jail, the inner jail (heavy jail) and the dark jail (black jail), and a women's jail was set up besides」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「州县监狱划分为软监、外监、里监（重监）、暗监（黑狱）四个层次，另外设置女监」) and 「the women's jail was set beside the outer jail, with its own courtyard wall」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「女监设在外监之侧，有专门的院墙」) and 「the persons chiefly responsible for department and county jails were the department and county magistrates, so these jails were in fact run by the magistrates themselves on the basis of the code and its regulations」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「州县监狱的主要责任者是知州、知县，因此，州县的监狱实际是在律例的基础上，由知州、知县亲自负责具体实施的」); for the small jail, the paper's note 5 quotes the Kangxi gazetteer of Wengyuan county: 「jail: two rooms, heavy and light; the heavy jail to the right inside the ceremonial gate, the light jail to the right of the county hall」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「监，轻重二间，重监在仪门内右，轻监在县堂右」)
  • noyamagoku-jawiki - 「The Noyama jail was a jail compound set up at Hagi by the Chōshū domain in the Edo period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「野山獄（のやまごく）は、江戸時代に長州藩によって萩に設けられた獄屋敷である。」), 「the Noyama jail, the upper jail holding those of samurai status, and the Iwakura jail, the lower jail holding those not of samurai status」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「士分…を収容する上牢である野山獄と、士分以外の者を収容する下牢である岩倉獄」), 「the residences faced each other across a road」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷は道路を隔てて向かい合わせ」), 「the residences were confiscated, and later the residence sites were rebuilt as jails」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷が没収され、後に屋敷跡は牢獄に建て替えられた」) and 「Twelve single cells, six facing six, and an inspection hut and an execution ground were also provided.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「12室の独房が6室ずつ向かい合わせの構造で、検視小屋や刑場も設けられた。」) (the article gives the cell count for the Noyama jail, the upper jail; it gives no layout for the Iwakura jail)
  • edo-ashigaru-kitamachi - 「In this area the functions concerned with lawsuits and trials were concentrated: the judgment place and the white-sand court, the inner interrogation room, the interrogation room, the stations of the pardon-register compilers and the precedent clerks, the litigants' waiting room and benches, the station of the jail doshin, the temporary jail and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このエリアには、裁許所と白洲、内詮議所、詮議所、赦帳撰要方や例繰方の詰所、公事人溜りや腰掛、牢屋同心の詰所や仮牢など、公事吟味に関する機能が集中していました。」) (the North town magistracy of Edo)
