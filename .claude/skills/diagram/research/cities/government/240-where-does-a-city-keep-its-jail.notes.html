• siyu-zhwiki - 「In the early Ming every prefecture, department and county across the country had a jail; administrative units at prefecture level and above had a jail office to manage the business of the jail.」 (translated; ) and 「as for departments, counties and such administrative units, no official dedicated to managing the jail was appointed」 (translated; )
  • qing-yamen-tushuo - 「the jails of local yamen were all set southwest of the great hall, outside the ceremonial gate ... and so it was called the 'South Jail'.」 (translated; ) (the book's reason for the direction, from divination, is left out: it is the book's gloss)
  • iolaw-mingqing-jails - 「each jail has an inner jail to hold the condemned and an outer jail to hold those sentenced to penal servitude, exile and below; women are set in a separate room, called the women's jail」 (translated; ), 「department and county jails were divided into four grades, the light jail, the outer jail, the inner jail (heavy jail) and the dark jail (black jail), and a women's jail was set up besides」 (translated; ) and 「the women's jail was set beside the outer jail, with its own courtyard wall」 (translated; ) and 「the persons chiefly responsible for department and county jails were the department and county magistrates, so these jails were in fact run by the magistrates themselves on the basis of the code and its regulations」 (translated; ); for the small jail, the paper's note 5 quotes the Kangxi gazetteer of Wengyuan county: 「jail: two rooms, heavy and light; the heavy jail to the right inside the ceremonial gate, the light jail to the right of the county hall」 (translated; )
  • noyamagoku-jawiki - 「The Noyama jail was a jail compound set up at Hagi by the Chōshū domain in the Edo period.」 (translated; ), 「the Noyama jail, the upper jail holding those of samurai status, and the Iwakura jail, the lower jail holding those not of samurai status」 (translated; ), 「the residences faced each other across a road」 (translated; ), 「the residences were confiscated, and later the residence sites were rebuilt as jails」 (translated; ) and 「Twelve single cells, six facing six, and an inspection hut and an execution ground were also provided.」 (translated; ) (the article gives the cell count for the Noyama jail, the upper jail; it gives no layout for the Iwakura jail)
  • edo-ashigaru-kitamachi - 「In this area the functions concerned with lawsuits and trials were concentrated: the judgment place and the white-sand court, the inner interrogation room, the interrogation room, the stations of the pardon-register compilers and the precedent clerks, the litigants' waiting room and benches, the station of the jail doshin, the temporary jail and so on.」 (translated; ) (the North town magistracy of Edo)
