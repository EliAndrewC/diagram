• jishinban-jawiki - 「Both the kidoban hut and the jishinban hut were built abutting the kido that were set up at both ends of the main street of each cho, the kido watchman being stationed in one and the jishinban in the other.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「木戸番屋・自身番屋ともに、各町内の大通りの両端に設置された木戸に接して建てられており、一方に木戸番が、もう一方に自身番が詰めた」) and 「the cost of running it was borne by each cho」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「運営の費用は各町が負担した」) (the box abutting the ward's gate, run and paid for by the town; which flank is this page's placement, the page putting the two posts at opposite corners of the crossroads)
  • kidoban-jawiki - 「there were two kidoban」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「木戸番が2人いた」), 「they closed the kido at the fourth hour of night (about 10 pm)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「夜の四ツ時（午後10時ごろ）に木戸を閉めた」), 「after being examined by the kidoban, they passed through the wickets on the left and right of the kido」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「木戸番に改められた上で、木戸の左右にある潜り戸から通る」), 「the watch hut was fixed at a span of 6 shaku, a length of 9 shaku and an eaves height of 1 jo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「番屋は梁間6尺、桁行9尺、軒高1丈」), 「the watchman lived in, and the rule was that he be a man without wife or children」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「番人は住み込みで、妻子の無い者という決まりだった」) and 「the kidoban's pay was paid by each ward」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「木戸番の給金はそれぞれの町内から支払われた」) (a shaku is about 30 cm and a jo ten shaku; the page adds that later in the period huts were enlarged to house families)
