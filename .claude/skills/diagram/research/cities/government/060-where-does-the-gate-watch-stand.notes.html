• jishinban-jawiki - 「Both the kidoban hut and the jishinban hut were built abutting the kido that were set up at both ends of the main street of each cho, the kido watchman being stationed in one and the jishinban in the other.」 (translated; ) and 「the cost of running it was borne by each cho」 (translated; ) (the box abutting the ward's gate, run and paid for by the town; which flank is this page's placement, the page putting the two posts at opposite corners of the crossroads)
  • kidoban-jawiki - 「there were two kidoban」 (translated; ), 「they closed the kido at the fourth hour of night (about 10 pm)」 (translated; ), 「after being examined by the kidoban, they passed through the wickets on the left and right of the kido」 (translated; ), 「the watch hut was fixed at a span of 6 shaku, a length of 9 shaku and an eaves height of 1 jo」 (translated; ), 「the watchman lived in, and the rule was that he be a man without wife or children」 (translated; ) and 「the kidoban's pay was paid by each ward」 (translated; ) (a shaku is about 30 cm and a jo ten shaku; the page adds that later in the period huts were enlarged to house families)
