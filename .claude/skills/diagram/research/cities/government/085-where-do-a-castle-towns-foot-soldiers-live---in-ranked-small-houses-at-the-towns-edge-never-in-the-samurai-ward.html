Where do a castle town's foot soldiers live? In ranked small houses at the town's edge, never in the samurai ward

Sources: bukeyashiki-wiki, jokamachi-jawiki, hikone-ashigaru, jta-ashigaru-kaga, jta-nagayamon, kotobank-bukeyashiki, shibata-ashigaru-nagaya

Small uniform houses in ranks DO exist in a castle town - but never in the samurai ward, whose servants are the subject of the servant-housing question. Ashigaru, kachi and doshin got individual plots in dedicated collective blocks (kumi-yashiki) at the town's EDGE: castle towns often put them on the fringe beyond the townspeople's quarter, and Hikone ringed the whole town with ~700 households as a defensive screen. Files of about ten identical plots per row, and the enclosure marks the rank - earthen wall for middle and up, board fence for lower, hedge for the lowest (that they were forbidden walls is on no page read). Form is a terraced range by default (Shibata's ICP ashigaru-nagaya: 8 households under one thatch roof, 143 x 24 ft (梁間, a depth of 7.3 m), 18 ft frontage each) and detached-with-garden only in rich domains (Kaga, Hikone - both flagged as unusual in their own documentation).

Not drawn at the city tier: ashigaru blocks as a distinct texture at this tier. The edge-of-town kumi-yashiki is a real and attested pattern and would be the correct home for any genuinely ranked small housing: ranks of uniform small dwellings belong OUTSIDE the ward on the town fringe, not inside the fence.
