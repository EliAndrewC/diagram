Where do a castle town's foot soldiers live? In ranked small houses at the town's edge, never in the samurai ward

Sources: bukeyashiki-wiki, jokamachi-jawiki, hikone-ashigaru, jta-ashigaru-kaga, jta-nagayamon, kotobank-bukeyashiki, shibata-ashigaru-nagaya, l7r-budgets (the setting's ashigaru are peasants)

Small uniform houses in ranks DO exist in a castle town - but never in the samurai ward, whose servants are the subject of the servant-housing question. Ashigaru and kachi lived in dedicated collective blocks (kumi-yashiki) at the town's EDGE, as a rule usually not on plots of their own but in shared row-houses, two dwellings to a building or a row of several, each a narrow earth floor and about two rooms with no alcove: castle towns often put them on the fringe beyond the townspeople's quarter, and Hikone ringed the whole town with ~700 households as a defensive screen. Files of about ten plots per row, each 165 or 230 m², and the enclosure marks the rank - earthen wall and longhouse gate for senior retainers (the gate from middle rank up), and below them an earthen wall, board fence or hedge, which the dictionary offers as alternatives without tying any to a rank, with a hedge for the lowest in Kaga (though no page read says the lowest rank were forbidden a wall). Form is a terraced range by default (Shibata's ashigaru-nagaya, an Important Cultural Property: 8 households under one thatch roof, 143 x 24 ft (梁間, a depth of 7.3 m), 18 ft frontage each) and detached-with-garden only in a few domains (Hikone, and Kaga, which the Japan Tourism Agency's entry on it calls rare).

The setting departs here. In Rokugan the ashigaru are peasants, not samurai - the setting's own DEVIATION from historical Japan, where they were the lowest stratum of the samurai class. So the setting's foot soldiers have no historical claim on a samurai foot soldiers' quarter: they are levies who live as the peasants they are.

Not drawn at the city tier: ashigaru blocks as a distinct texture at this tier. The edge-of-town kumi-yashiki is a real and attested pattern and would be the correct home for any genuinely ranked small housing: ranks of uniform small dwellings belong OUTSIDE the ward on the town fringe, not inside the fence.
