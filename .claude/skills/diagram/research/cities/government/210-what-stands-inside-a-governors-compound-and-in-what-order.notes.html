• qing-yamen-tushuo - 「Civil and military officials of every province all have a yamen; the rule is that the place of business is the great hall and the second hall, outside them the main gate and the ceremonial gate, the place of rest the inner rooms and the side rooms, and the place where the clerks work the clerks' rooms. A senior official has the regulation complete; a junior one reduces it in order.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「各省文武官皆设衙署，其制，治事之所为大堂、二堂，外为大门、仪门，宴息之所为内室、为群室，吏攒办事之所为科房。官大者规制具备，官小者依次而减。」) (the Qing regulation, quoted by the book)
  • qing-yamen-tushuo - 「inside the yamen there was also an archive for keeping documents and records, and granaries and storehouses, an armory, a jail and the like were indispensable too. The great majority of local yamen also had an official residence attached」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「衙署内还有架阁库用来保存文牍、档案，而仓库、军器库、监狱等设置也必不可少。绝大多数地方衙署还附设官邸」)
  • bjd-qing-yamen - 「outside the main gate there were originally the outer gate, the screen wall, the flagpoles ... the east and west guard rooms」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「大门以外，原有辕门、照壁、旗杆…东西班房」), 「from south to north in order the main gate, the ceremonial gate, the admonition arch, the great hall, the second hall, the inner-residence gate, the official residence, the upper rooms, the rear storehouse ... five courts in all」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「自南而北依次为大门、仪门、戒石坊、大堂、二堂、内宅门、官邸、上房、后库…共五进院落」), 「the inner-residence gate, the dividing line between the front court and the rear residence」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「作为前朝后寝分界线的内宅门」) and 「The third hall, also called the official residence, is the governor-general's study and inner signing room (office). The fourth hall, also called the upper rooms, ... is where the governor-general and his family lived.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「三堂又称官邸，是总督的书房和内签押房（办公室）。四堂又称上房…是总督及其家眷生活居住的地方。」) (the Zhili governor-general's yamen at Baoding)
  • bjd-qing-yamen - 「22 m long, 10 m deep」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「长22米，进深10米」) (the great hall of the Zhili governor-general's yamen)
  • sohu-wuzhou-yamen - 「The six rooms are the personnel, revenue and rites, and the war, justice and works rooms on both sides of the path between the ceremonial gate and the great hall inside the yamen」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「六房，即衙署内仪门到大堂之间雨路两侧的吏户礼、兵刑工」), 「on the east, from north to south, the three rooms of personnel, revenue and rites, and on the west the three rooms of war, justice and works, generally two bays to each room」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「东面的由北向南依次为吏、户、礼三房和西面的兵、刑、工三房，一般每房各二间」) and 「according to how heavy or light the business of each place was, some yamen did not set up only the six rooms but also set up a dispatch room, an accounts room, a storeroom, a hall room and so on」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「依各地事务繁简,有些衙署并非只设六房,还设有承发房、账房、 库房、堂房等」)
  • sohu-wuzhou-yamen - 「Inside the yamen were set a temple to the great immortal, a temple to the horse king, a temple to the jail god, a temple to the yamen god and so on.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「衙门里设大仙庙、马王庙、狱神庙、衙神庙等。」) (the page's next sentence puts shrines generally in the southeast of the yamen, but it follows a sentence on temples to former worthies and may mean only those, so no side is taken from it)
  • bjd-qing-yamen - 「within the yamen are over 50 buildings and over 600 rooms, divided into east, middle and west routes; the middle route has the great hall, the second hall, the rooms of the six sections and so on ... the west route is the military and police office」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「衙内有房屋50余幢、600余间，分东、中、西三路，中路有大堂、二堂、六科用房等…西路为军捕厅」) (the Huai'an prefectural yamen)
  • kokuga-jawiki - 「Since the main hall was often built toward the north, facing south, the side halls were placed east and west, and were called the east side hall and west side hall respectively. The center was made an open court.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「正殿は北寄り正面に南向きに建てられることが多かったので、脇殿は東西に置かれ、それぞれ東脇殿・西脇殿と呼ばれた。中央を広場とした。」) and 「Before and behind the main hall, a front hall and a rear hall are set.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「正殿の前後には、前殿・後殿が設置される。」)
  • takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「Surviving buildings: the front gate and gatehouse, storehouses one to four and nine to twelve, the book storehouse.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現存建物 表門・門番所、一～四番蔵・九～十二番蔵、書物蔵。」) and 「The gundai office was rebuilt in 1816 (Bunka 13) in parts including the entrance, the examination room, the office and the great hall.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「郡代役所は玄関・吟味所・御役所・大広間などの部分に1816年（文化13年）に改築。」) and 「Because it is the only surviving jinya, a policy of preserving it as a cultural property was set out.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現存する唯一の陣屋であることから文化財として保存する方針が示された。」)
