  • chongming-yanwuchang - 「"the drill ground is outside the east gate, reckoned at about one li of ground"」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「“教场在东门外，计地一里许”」), with 「a review hall of three bays, a rear hall of three bays, a tea hall of three bays ... one commander's platform, one gate」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「演武厅三间，后堂三间，茶厅三间...将台一座，辕门一座」), and 「earthen walls on all four sides, with a review hall of three bays at the middle of the north end」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「四周为土墙，北首居中有演武厅三间」) (the gate to the south of the wall, and the ground founded by the county magistrate in 1586, are in the same article)
  • chongming-yanwuchang - 「one square li around, occupying an area of 37.5 mu」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「方圆1平方里，占地面积37.5亩」) (the article's own reckoning from the gazetteers; a mu is about 667 m², so 37.5 mu is about 2.5 ha)
