• fuguo-zhwiki - 「In the late Qing it was common for a prefecture to have one attached county」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「晚清时，以一府有一个附郭县为常见」), 「larger prefectural or provincial cities often had two or even three attached counties」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「较大的府城或省城常有两个甚至三个附郭县」) and 「Within one city there was both a prefectural yamen and a county yamen, each with its own supporting institutions such as the school and Confucian temple.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「在一城之内，既有府衙，也有县衙，并有各自的配套机构如学宫文庙等。」)
  • machi-bugyo-jawiki - 「a post in charge of the administration and justice of the urban areas (machikata) within the domain」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「領内の都市部（町方）の行政・司法を担当する役職」) and 「not only the shogunate but the domains too set up this post」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「幕府だけでなく諸藩もこの役職を設置した」)
  • kotobank-machibugyosho - 「In 1631 (Kan'ei 8) the North town magistracy moved inside the Tokiwabashi gate, and in 1698 (Genroku 11) the South town magistracy inside the Kajibashi gate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寛永八年（一六三一）に北町奉行所が常盤橋（ときわばし）御門内へ、元禄一一年（一六九八）に南町奉行所が鍛冶橋（かじばし）御門内へ移転。」) (the first two offices of 1604 stood one inside the Gofukubashi gate and one inside the Yayosu riverbank)
  • narukuchiya-bugyosho - 「The South town magistracy's residence was some 2,720 tsubo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南町奉行所の屋敷は2720坪ほど」) and 「The North town magistracy had a site of about 2,630 tsubo and more」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「北町奉行所は約2630坪余り」) (a blog repeating the figures without naming their source; a tsubo is about 3.3 square meters)
  • machi-bugyo-jawiki - 「The town magistrate's official residence was inside the magistracy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町奉行の役宅は奉行所内にあった。」) and 「There were 25 yoriki and 100 doshin in each of the South and North magistracies.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「与力は南北奉行所に25名ずつ、同心は100名ずついた。」)
  • edo-ashigaru-kitamachi - 「both South and North were of nearly the same construction」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南北ともにほぼ同じつくり」), 「the east side is the office, the west side the residence」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「東側は番所、西側は役宅」), 「in the north-east area, the yoriki office and the doshin office」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「北東エリアには、与力番所、同心番所」) and 「The south-east area is especially wide ... the judgment place and the white-sand court, the inner interrogation room, the interrogation room ... the temporary jail」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南東エリアはひときわ広く…裁許所と白洲、内詮議所、詮議所…仮牢など」)
  • doshin-jawiki - 「These residences were on the scale of about 300 tsubo for a yoriki and about 100 tsubo for a doshin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「この屋敷は、与力が約300坪、同心が約100坪程度の規模だった。」) and 「The group residences were placed at Hatchobori」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「組屋敷は八丁堀に置かれ」)
  • chuo-kanko-hatchobori - 「The Hatchobori group residence was a wide block-granted residence (about 40,000 tsubo in the Kaei era), granted in one lot by the shogunate to the yoriki and doshin as a group」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「八丁堀組屋敷は広い大繩屋敷（嘉永時代には約4万坪）で、与力・同心グループとして幕府から一括拝領」) and 「the yoriki's granted land was divided for 50 yoriki, the doshin's granted land for 200 doshin」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「与力拝領地は与力50人分に、同心拝領地は同心200人分」)
  • narukuchiya-bugyosho - 「The yoriki and doshin group residences' grounds: a yoriki was given a site of 300 tsubo, a doshin one of 100 tsubo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「与力・同心組屋敷の敷地は、与力で300坪、同心は100坪の敷地が与えられていました。」) and 「they let part or all of the residence to others and took rent」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷の一部もしくは全部を人に貸して店賃を取っていた」)
