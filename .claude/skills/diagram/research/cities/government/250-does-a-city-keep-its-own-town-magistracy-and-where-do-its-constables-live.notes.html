  • kotobank-machibugyosho - 「In 1631 (Kan'ei 8) the North town magistracy moved inside the Tokiwabashi gate, and in 1698 (Genroku 11) the South town magistracy inside the Kajibashi gate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the first two offices of 1604 stood one inside the Gofukubashi gate and one inside the Yayosu riverbank)

  • machi-bugyo-jawiki - 「The town magistrate's official residence was inside the magistracy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「There were 25 yoriki and 100 doshin in each of the South and North magistracies.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • edo-ashigaru-kitamachi - 「both South and North were of nearly the same construction」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ), 「the east side is the office, the west side the residence」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ), 「in the north-east area, the yoriki office and the doshin office」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The south-east area is especially wide ... the judgment place and the white-sand court, the inner interrogation room, the interrogation room ... the temporary jail」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • doshin-jawiki - 「These residences were on the scale of about 300 tsubo for a yoriki and about 100 tsubo for a doshin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The group residences were placed at Hatchobori」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • chuo-kanko-hatchobori - 「The Hatchobori group residence was a wide block-granted residence (about 40,000 tsubo in the Kaei era), granted in one lot by the shogunate to the yoriki and doshin as a group」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「the yoriki's granted land was divided for 50 yoriki, the doshin's granted land for 200 doshin」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • narukuchiya-bugyosho - 「The yoriki and doshin group residences' grounds: a yoriki was given a site of 300 tsubo, a doshin one of 100 tsubo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「they let part or all of the residence to others and took rent」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
