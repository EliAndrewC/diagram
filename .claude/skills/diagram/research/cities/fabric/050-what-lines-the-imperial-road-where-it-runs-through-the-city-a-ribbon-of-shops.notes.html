• jokamachi-jawiki - 「Once it entered the castle town, by arranging houses along both sides of the trunk road with no gaps, the castle was made hard to see」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城下に入ると幹線道路の両脇に家屋を隙間なく配置させることで城を見え難くして」); shukuba-jawiki - 「Inns, cheap wood-fee lodgings, teahouses, shops and the like stood built up in a row.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旅籠、木賃宿、茶屋、商店などが建ち並び」) (both sides built up - the castle-town page's own reason for it is to hide the castle from the road, not frontage - and what lined them is the post-station page's, which describes a post station rather than a city through-road)
