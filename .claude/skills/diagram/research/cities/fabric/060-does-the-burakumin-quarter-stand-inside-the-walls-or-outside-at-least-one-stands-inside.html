Does the burakumin quarter stand inside the walls, or outside? At least one stands inside

Sources: burakumin-enwiki; the GM's setting notes for the burakumin caste and its trades

The burakumin handle what a city cannot do without and will not touch: the corpses, the hides and the leather, the sandals, the executions. A city shut up under siege needs those hands as much as it needs its granary, so however many burakumin live on the cheaper ground outside the wall, a neighborhood of them, on these maps, always sits within it.

The rule the map follows: a walled provincial city draws at least one burakumin neighborhood inside the walls. Not all of a city's burakumin need be inside - extramural rows are usual - and the intramural neighborhood is the floor, not the whole.
