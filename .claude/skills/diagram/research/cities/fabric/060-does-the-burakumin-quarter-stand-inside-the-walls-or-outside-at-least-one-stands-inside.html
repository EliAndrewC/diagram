Does the burakumin quarter stand inside the walls, or outside? At least one stands inside

Sources: burakumin-enwiki (the caste's trades), kotobank-hisabetsu-buraku (the ground the quarters were given), chori-jawiki (the Shinano castle towns' quarters at their entrances); the GM's setting notes for the burakumin caste and its trades

The burakumin handle what a city cannot do without and will not touch: the corpses, the hides and the leather, the executions. A city shut up under siege needs those hands as much as it needs its granary, so however many burakumin live outside the wall, a neighborhood of them, on these maps, always sits within it.

This is a deliberate deviation. The history puts the quarter at the town's edge, not inside it: the lords who took the leather workers under their control housed them, as far as the record can tell, at the edge of the castle town, at a chokepoint on the roads, or on a riverbank, and Matsumoto, for one, gathered its kawata at the entrances of the castle town. The evidence in full, and what it means for a town, is where a town's burakumin quarter stands. The walled city's intramural neighborhood is the GM's ruling for this setting, recorded where the tanning yard's direction is: the caste's duties in town keep some of it within the wall.

The rule the map follows: a walled provincial city draws at least one burakumin neighborhood inside the walls. Not all of a city's burakumin need be inside - extramural rows are usual - and the intramural neighborhood is the floor, not the whole.
