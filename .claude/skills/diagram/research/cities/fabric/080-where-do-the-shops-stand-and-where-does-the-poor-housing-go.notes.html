• nagaya-jawiki - 「In the Edo period, in urban areas and especially in dense places such as Edo, merchant houses of the middle stratum and above kept independent shops on the main street, but the rest of the townspeople, the craftsmen and so on almost all lived as renters in the nagaya of the back streets.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代において都市部、とくに稠密な江戸などでは中層以上の商家などは表通りに独立した店を構えていたが、それ以外の町人、職人などはほとんどが裏町の長屋に借家住まいだった。」)
