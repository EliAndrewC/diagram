  • kyototuu-unagi - 「Toyotomi Hideyoshi taxed houses according to the width of their frontage, under the name jiguchi-sen, so it is said that people narrowed the frontage and built deep 'eel's bed' style machiya to save on tax.」 (translated; ) (Note the page's own hedge といわれる ("it is said").)
  • nagaya-jawiki - 「In the Edo period in urban areas, especially dense ones like Edo, merchant houses of the middle level and above kept independent shops on the main street, but the rest of the townsfolk and craftsmen almost all lived as tenants in nagaya in the back streets.」 (translated; )
