• kotobank-machikido - 「A wooden gate set up for security in the various machi of Edo. It was closed at night.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸の町々に警備のために設けられていた木戸。夜は閉ざされた。」)
  • kotobank-machiyakunin - 「The monthly duty: the house-owning townsmen of the machi, or the landlords' managers, took charge of the machi's administrative business in turn every month.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「月行事 町の家持町人ないしは家守(やもり)（地主の差配人）が毎月順番で町政事務を担当した。」)
  • kotobank-machiyakunin - 「The ward levy: the costs of running the machi were the ward levy collected from the landowners and house-owners, and the expenditure ranged widely - gold and silver paid to the shogunate such as the provincial levy, festival costs, first offerings to the gods and buddhas, the end-of-month money to the town elders, the headman's fee, the upkeep of the time bell, the wages for dredging the canals, the share of the water-supply works, the costs of the fire brigade's standard, and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町入用 町の運営の費用は、地主・家持から徴収される町入用であり、支出としては、国役など幕府に納入する金銀、祭礼の入用、神仏への初穂、町年寄への晦日銭(みそかぜに)、名主の役料、時の鐘の維持費、堀浚(ほりさら)い賃、上水普請(ふしん)の分担金、火消の纏(まとい)入用など多岐にわたった。」)
  • kotobank-machiyakunin - 「However, Edo's town elders were hereditary in the three houses of Naraya, Taruya and Kitamura, and were not chosen by the townsmen.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「しかし、江戸の町年寄は奈良屋、樽(たる)屋、喜多村(きたむら)の3家の世襲であり、町人によって選出されたのではない。」)
  • kotobank-edo-machinanushi - 「For reasons of cost they were not placed in each machi; it was usual for one nanushi to govern from two or three to a dozen and more machi, and there are said to have been two hundred and some in all of Edo.」 and 「Under the direction of the town elders, he handled the business of the machi he governed at his own house.」 and 「The post of machi-nanushi was hereditary, and he was not allowed to take up another trade; instead he received a salary from the machi.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「経費の関係で町ごとには置かれず、一人の名主が二、三町から十数町を支配するのが普通で、江戸全部で二百数十名いたといわれる。」 and original: 「町年寄指揮の下に、自宅で支配町の事務を処理した。」 and original: 「町名主の職は世襲で、他業につくことは許されず、そのかわり町から給料を受けた。」)
  • kotobank-edo-machinanushi - 「It was the rule that he lived in a machi he governed, and he was not permitted another occupation.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「支配する町に居住することが原則で、兼業は認められなかった。」) (a second encyclopedia on the same page)
  • kotobank-jobosei - 「Under the ritsuryo system, by the provisions of the household code, under the capital office, one ward head was appointed for each ward, and one ward superior for every 4 wards of each row, and they saw to the checking of households, the supervision of wrongdoing, and the pressing of the labor levies.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「律令制では，戸令の規定によって，京職の配下として，各坊に坊長1人，各条の4坊ごとに坊令1人が任ぜられ，戸口の検校，姧非の督察，賦徭の催駈に当たった。」)
  • lijia-zhwiki - 「110 neighboring households in a Chinese area were organized as one li; a li governed ten jia, and one jia was ten households in all.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「将中国地域相邻的110户人家，编为一“里”，里辖十“甲”，一“甲”共十户。」) (the page's next sentence makes up the 110 as ten heads of the li plus a hundred households; the count for a city of 600 households is the record's arithmetic)
