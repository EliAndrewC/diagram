• thepaper-city-fields - 「The most surprising thing may be this: for a long time, Chinese city residents not only grew rice and vegetables in the city, but even, astonishingly, kept pigs and chickens.」 and 「Similar conditions were extremely common in Chinese cities; one can say that until two generations ago, fields and gardens could be seen inside the city in almost every Chinese city.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「最令人惊诧的或许是：在很长一段时间里，中国的城市居民不仅会在城里种稻种菜，甚至居然还会养猪养鸡。」 and original: 「类似的情形在中国城市中极为普遍，可以说直至两代人之前，几乎所有中国城市都可以在城内看到田园景象。」) (an essay by the writer Wei Zhou in The Paper's history column, 2016)
  • thepaper-city-fields - 「Before the Northern Song, the urban economy of our ancient cities was not yet very developed; this is reflected in vegetable growing, in that city residents mostly used various idle plots inside the city to grow vegetables for their own use.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「在北宋以前，我国古代的城市经济尚不太发展，这一点反映在蔬菜种植上，就是市民用菜多是利用城内各种空闲地段种菜自用。」) (the essay quoting Zhou Baozhu's book on the Qingming scroll; the words are Zhou's)
  • kotobank-jokamachi - 「All the retainers were gathered under the castle, and further, for the economic development of the castle town, besides merchants from outside the domain, the merchants and artisans living in the countryside of the domain were also gathered into the castle town, and the separation of warrior from farmer and of farmer from merchant was carried out (though among the townsmen of small provincial castle towns there were also those who farmed alongside their trade).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城下にはすべての家臣が集められ，さらに城下町経済発展のため領外商人に加え領内在方居住の商工業者も城下町に集められ，兵農分離，農商分離が行われた（ただ地方小城下町の町人には商売のかたわら農業を営むものも存在した）。」)
