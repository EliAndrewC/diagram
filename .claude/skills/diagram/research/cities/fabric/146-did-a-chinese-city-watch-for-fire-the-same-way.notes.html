• dongjing-menghualu-wikisource - 「Every ward and lane, at intervals of about three hundred paces, has one military patrol post, with five post-soldiers, who patrol and keep watch by night and take charge of public business. Also, at a high place, a fire-watch tower is built of brick, and on the tower there are men keeping lookout. Below are several official rooms, where more than a hundred soldiers are quartered, together with the gear for fighting fires such as large and small buckets, sprinklers, hemp swabs, axes and saws, ladders, fire-forks, great ropes, and iron grapnels.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「每坊巷三百步許，有軍巡鋪屋一所，鋪兵五人，夜間巡警收領公事。又於高處磚砌望火樓，樓上有人卓望。下有官屋數間，屯駐軍兵百餘人，及有救火家事，謂如大小桶、酒子、麻搭、斧鋸、梯子、火叉、大索、鐵貓兒之類。」) (the Northern Song capital at Kaifeng, in a memoir of the city.)
  • chinanews-ancient-firefighting - 「Ming local government at the base level had a zongjia responsible for fire and theft. According to the Wanshu zaji, the county town of Wanping in Beijing then had resident organizations of fang and pu, each pu with a head and three to five firemen, all managed by the zongjia. They kept watch by night in turn, striking clappers and ringing bells to remind people to beware of fire and theft. In some places a patrol post (watch post) was also set up, where the zongjia and the firemen could rest and shelter from rain and snow. In the patrol post were kept knives, spears and other weapons, and fire-hooks, buckets and other firefighting equipment.」 and 「In the eighth year of Qianlong (1743) the governor of Hunan, Jiang Bo, instructed every department and county to organize volunteer fire companies specifically responsible for putting out fires. These companies were all to be registered on rolls and reported to the authorities, which issued them a fixed sum of silver each year.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「明代地方基层政权设有总甲负责火盗，据《宛署杂记》记载，当时，北京宛平县城内设有坊、铺等居民组织，每铺设铺头、火夫三五人，统由总甲负责管理。他们轮流值夜，击柝振铃，提醒人们注意火盗。有的地方还设置巡警铺(更铺)，以供总甲、火夫们休息、躲避雨雪之用。巡警铺内备有刀、枪等兵器和火钩、水桶等救火器具。」 and original: 「乾隆八年(1743年)，湖南巡抚蒋薄指示各州县组织救火义役，专门负责扑救火灾。这些救火义役都要登记造册，上报官府，由官府每年发给一定的银两。」) (a news-agency feature on firefighting in the Chinese past, quoting the Ming Wanshu zaji for the county town. It carries an organized Ming night watch, described for Wanping in Beijing, patrol posts set up in some other places, and fire companies ordered for every department and county of Hunan in 1743; it does not carry a standing lookout tower outside the capital.)
  • xiancheng-zhwiki - 「In the Song, because of the separatism of the regional military governors of the late Tang and the Five Dynasties, the building of walled cities was not encouraged in regions other than the borders.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「宋朝时，因鉴于晚唐、五代藩鎮割據之故，边境地区之外的地区不提倡修建城池。」) / 「By the late Ming, it is estimated that more than two-thirds of the county seats in the whole country had walls.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「到明后期，估计全国三分之二以上的县治均筑有城垣。」) / 「By the end of the Qing, the great majority of prefectural, departmental and county seats had walls built」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「至清末，绝大部分府州县治所均建筑有城垣」) (the page also says that from the Sui to the middle Tang most seats reused older walls or had none.)
