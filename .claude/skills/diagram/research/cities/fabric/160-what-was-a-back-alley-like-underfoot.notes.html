• roji-jawiki - 「The entrance of the roji lay between one merchant house and the next, in the row facing the main street.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「路地の入口は表通りに面して並ぶ商家と商家の間にあった。」)
  • ginza-machidukuri-machiwari - 「Shops stood in a row on the street side, and as you went in along the roji, the nagaya that were the craftsmen's homes stood in rows on both sides.」 and 「Slip into a small roji between shops some three to five ken wide at the front, and there the nagaya stand in a row.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「通り側に店が並び、路地を入っていくと両側に職人たちの住まいである長屋が並んでいました。」; original: 「間口３間～５間ばかりのお店の間の小さな路地をひょいと入っていくとそこに長屋が並んでいる。」)
  • roji-jawiki - 「At the entrance from the main street into the roji, a gate with a door, called the roji kido (nagaya kido), was set up.」 and 「[it was] opened at ake-mutsu and closed at kure-mutsu (about 6 p.m.).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「表通りから路地に入る入口には路地木戸（ろじきど）（長屋木戸（ながやきど））と呼ばれる、扉のある門が設置されていた。」; original: 「明六つ（あけむつ）に開けられ暮六つ（くれむつ）（午後6時頃）に閉められた。」)
  • roji-jawiki - 「In the middle of the roji, drain boards (dobuita) were laid in a line, and beneath them a small ditch six to seven sun (18 to 21 centimeters) wide was dug」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「路地の中央には溝板（どぶいた）が並べられており、その下には幅が6寸から7寸（18センチメートルから21センチメートル）の小さなどぶが掘られていた」)
  • ginza-machidukuri-machiwari - 「The privy and the well were shared.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「便所と井戸は共同でした。」)
