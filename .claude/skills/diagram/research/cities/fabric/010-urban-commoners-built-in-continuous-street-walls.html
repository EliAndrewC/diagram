Urban commoners built in continuous street walls

Sources: kyomachiya-jawiki, kyototuu-unagi, nagaya-jawiki, udatsu-jawiki, bukeyashiki-wiki, roji-jawiki, jokamachi-jawiki, machiya-shoka-jawiki

  • What the history says. Urban commoners in both reference cultures did not build detached-with-yard.77 Machiya street frontage was continuous - party walls or touching eaves,54 because street frontage was taxed and precious;1 back-lot nagaya3 were literally ONE ROOF over a row of family units;2 Chinese county-seat courtyard housing shared rammed-earth or brick party walls in continuous street walls.55 Detached-with-yard was a samurai (and rural) form.56

The frontage tax is disputed. "street frontage was taxed" is the popular explanation of the narrow lot (kyototuu-unagi carries it), and ja.wikipedia 京町家 disputes it outright - Edo-period Kyoto assessed a lump sum per town, not per frontage.4 The continuous built edge is read either way; the tax is not what it rests on.

The rule the map follows: a city's commoner housing - the laborer, servant and burakumin dwellings and the small merchant houses - goes down as contiguous terraces rather than as separate buildings standing in their own clearings. At least 55% of those dwellings touch a neighbor, and the median gap from one to its nearest neighbor is no more than 6 ft ; the touching units in a row keep a hairline seam between their outlines because that is what they were, independent houses grown together.75 Back-to-back rows keep the real eave and drainage gap of about 3 to 6 ft, an idobata court opens every second row, and the block's wells are drawn before the rows so the terraces break into courts around them; the quarter's alleys go down first and are fronted tightly, while a real street keeps a band clear for the shop frontage.76 Obstacles break a terrace naturally and the occasional gap is honest, but it is not the default texture. The samurai quarter is the exception and keeps detached compounds with yards - the one district that did build detached, and the contrast that makes the terraces read.

The departure the sheet discloses: a true row shared party walls, a gap of zero, and the drawn market frontages do not.74 Inside a residential terrace the units touch; it is the frontage rows that carry the wider joint, and the gap the sheet draws between neighboring shops, with the reason for it, is in the next entry, on machiya row density.
