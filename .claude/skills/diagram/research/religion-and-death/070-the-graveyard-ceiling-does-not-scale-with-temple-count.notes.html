• ryobosei-jawiki - 「Often a communal graveyard belonging to each settlement.」 (translated; ); kotobank-kyodo-bochi - 「When living becomes settled, a communal graveyard is often formed separately from the dwellings.」 (translated; )
  • osaka-nanabaka-jawiki - 「Osaka's streets, damaged in the Summer Siege of Osaka, were remodeled as part of their rebuilding. As one part of this, for military reasons, a measure was carried out to consolidate the graveyards scattered in the city onto the outer rim of Osaka. The Awaza, Watanabe, Tsumura and Kami-Namba graveyards were merged into the Sennichimae graveyard, the Uemachi plateau's graveyards into the Kobase graveyard (now Jonan-teramachi, Tennoji Ward), and Tenma's graveyards were divided among the Umeda, Hama and Yoshiwara graveyards. Later the Gamo and Tobita graveyards were added, and they came to be called the "Seven Graveyards of Osaka".」 (translated; ) The page gives no reading for 小橋, and adds that accounts of which grounds made the seven differ by period; the measure belongs to the rebuilding after the siege of Osaka.
  • osaka-nanabaka-jawiki - 「The largest of the seven was the Sennichimae graveyard, made in Genna 7 (1621).」 (translated; ); 「Afterwards Hozenji (1637) and Chikurinji (1648) were built」 (translated; )
  • teramachi-jawiki - 「Before the Edo period, in many castle towns temples and graveyards were gathered on the outer edge of the town, with the intent that they serve as a defensive line against enemy attack.」 (translated; )
