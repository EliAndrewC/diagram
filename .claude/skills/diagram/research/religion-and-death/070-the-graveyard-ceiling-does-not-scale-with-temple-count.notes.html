• ryobosei-jawiki - 「Often a communal graveyard belonging to each settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「集落ごとの共同墓地であることが多く」); kotobank-kyodo-bochi - 「When living becomes settled, a communal graveyard is often formed separately from the dwellings.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「生活が定住的になると，住いと別に共同墓地が形成されることが多い。」)
  • osaka-nanabaka-jawiki - 「Osaka's streets, damaged in the Summer Siege of Osaka, were remodeled as part of their rebuilding. As one part of this, for military reasons, a measure was carried out to consolidate the graveyards scattered in the city onto the outer rim of Osaka. The Awaza, Watanabe, Tsumura and Kami-Namba graveyards were merged into the Sennichimae graveyard, the Uemachi plateau's graveyards into the Kobase graveyard (now Jonan-teramachi, Tennoji Ward), and Tenma's graveyards were divided among the Umeda, Hama and Yoshiwara graveyards. Later the Gamo and Tobita graveyards were added, and they came to be called the "Seven Graveyards of Osaka".」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「大坂夏の陣によって戦災を被った大坂市街に、復興を兼ねた都市改造が行われた。そのひとつとして、軍事的な配慮のため市内に散在していた墓地を、大坂市街の外縁部に整理集約する施策が行われた。阿波座、渡辺、津村、上難波の墓地を千日前墓地に、上町台地の諸墓地を小橋墓地（現在の天王寺区城南寺町）に統合し、天満の墓地は梅田墓地、浜墓地、葭原墓地に分割統合された。のちに蒲生墓地、鳶田（飛田）墓地を加えて「大阪七墓」と呼ばれるようになった」) The page gives no reading for 小橋, and adds that accounts of which grounds made the seven differ by period; the measure belongs to the rebuilding after the siege of Osaka.
  • osaka-nanabaka-jawiki - 「The largest of the seven was the Sennichimae graveyard, made in Genna 7 (1621).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「七墓の中で最も規模の大きい墓地が元和7年（1621年）に造成された千日前墓地である」); 「Afterwards Hozenji (1637) and Chikurinji (1648) were built」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その後、法善寺（1637年）、竹林寺（1648年）が建立され」)
  • teramachi-jawiki - 「Before the Edo period, in many castle towns temples and graveyards were gathered on the outer edge of the town, with the intent that they serve as a defensive line against enemy attack.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代以前、敵襲に際して防衛線とする意図で、多くの城下町において寺院や墓地が市街の外縁にまとめられた。」)
