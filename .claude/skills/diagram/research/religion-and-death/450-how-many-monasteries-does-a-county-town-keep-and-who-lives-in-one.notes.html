  • eijuji-shinshiro - 「Centered on the forecourt, the kuri and study are placed on the east side, and on the west side the dormitory, the meditation hall and the tutelary shrine hall, in order from the north」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「前庭を中心にして東側に庫裡及び書院、西側に衆寮、禅堂、鎮守殿が北から順に置かれ」); and 「at temples of such ranks as the jōgōe-chi, the kata-hōdōe-chi and the zuiie-chi, it was laid down that the training retreat be held in that period, and such temples had to have a full set of buildings - main hall, kuri, meditation hall, dormitory, gate and the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「常恒会地、片法幢会地、随意会地などの格式の寺院では、その間に結制を行うことが定められ、そうした寺院では本堂、庫裡、禅堂、衆寮、山門などの伽藍を整える必要があった。」) (Eijuji, Shinshiro, Aichi, a Sōtō temple of those ranks; the requirement is for the ranked temples that held the retreat, not for every temple)
