• jinjahoncho-keidai - 「The torii is put up at the boundary dividing the inside of the shrine from the outside, and the inside of the torii is revered as sacred ground where the kami dwells.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「鳥居は神社の内と外を分ける境に立てられ、鳥居の内側は、神さまがお鎮まりになる神域として尊ばれます。」)
  • jawiki-yashikigami - 「Most household guardian shrines are small shrines of stone or wood. They almost never have a hall like an ordinary shrine's. Where they are carefully worshipped, a hall on the scale of a subsidiary shrine may be built, and they may even have a torii.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷神の多くは石造か木造の小祠である。普通の神社のような社殿を持つことはまずない。丁寧に祭祀されている場合は末社程度の規模の社殿を建てられ、鳥居までも持つこともある。」) The article is flagged as short of sources and possibly original research.
