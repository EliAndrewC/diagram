  • danka-terauke-encyclopedia - 「Temple membership was not an individual affair; rather, the unit of religious affiliation was the emergent unit of social organization, the "household" (ie).」; 「For each household, the main benefit of membership was the funerary and ongoing memorial services that temples provided for all household members.」 (the source's own English) The arrangement described is the Tokugawa danka system, a state-mandated anti-Christian registration.
