Who kept a Chinese village temple? Resident monks at some, nobody at the earth god's shrine

Sources: mianning-village-temples, tudimiao-zhwiki and temple-chinese-enwiki (China)

China, the setting's first model, has both forms side by side. The village temples of a Qing county in Sichuan "generally engaged monks and Daoists to reside and keep watch, responsible for guarding the property, cleaning, and managing the temple's incense offerings", under heads "chosen by election from among the congregation"; the earth-god shrine proper, "from a few square meters at the small end to over ten", housed nobody, and popular temples are "established and administered by local managers, village communities, lineage congregations". The Japanese form the country-shrine sheet draws, the monk living at the shrine, is under "Does the country monk live at the shrine?".
