• terauke-seido-jawiki - 「At the temples a sect-and-population register, corresponding to today's household register, was compiled, and for travel or a change of residence its certificate (the terauke certificate) was required.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺院では現在の戸籍に当たる宗門人別帳が作成され、旅行や住居の移動の際にはその証文（寺請証文）が必要とされた。」)
  • shumon-ninbetsu-jawiki - 「The compiling of the register was to be done every year, town or village by town or village, by the nanushi or shōya headmen and the town elders」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「改帳の作成は、町村毎に名主や庄屋、町年寄が毎年行うこととされていた」)
  • jaanus-shamusho - 「No distinction was made between public and private affairs and business was frequently carried on at the private home of the administrator, keieisha 経宮者, especially at small shrines.」 (the source's own English; the characters are the page's own gloss)
