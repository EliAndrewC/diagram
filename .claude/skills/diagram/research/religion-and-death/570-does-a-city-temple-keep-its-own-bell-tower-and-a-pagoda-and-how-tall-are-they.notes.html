• shoro-jawiki - 「a building set up within a temple for hanging its bonsho」; 「In the garan of Japan's Nara period it was built so as to face the sutra house east and west.」; 「But from the Heian period on, its placement in the garan became much less strict.」; 「In the Muromachi period there are also cases in which it was joined to the main gate and became a bell gate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺院内において梵鐘を吊るすために設けられた建物をいう」; original: 「日本の奈良時代の伽藍では経蔵と東西に向き合うような形で建てられた」; original: 「しかし平安時代以降になると伽藍での配置はさほど厳格ではなくなった」; original: 「室町時代になると、山門と一体化し、鐘門となった事例もある」) (a bonsho is a temple's great bronze bell)
