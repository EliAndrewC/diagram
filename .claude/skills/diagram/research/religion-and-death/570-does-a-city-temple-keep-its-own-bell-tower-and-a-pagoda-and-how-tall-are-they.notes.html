• shoro-jawiki - 「a building set up within a temple for hanging its bonsho」; 「In the garan of Japan's Nara period it was built so as to face the sutra house east and west.」; 「But from the Heian period on, its placement in the garan became much less strict.」; 「In the Muromachi period there are also cases in which it was joined to the main gate and became a bell gate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (a bonsho is a temple's great bronze bell)
  • ryosenji-shoro - 「One bay long and one bay deep, with a skirted irimoya roof of cypress bark.」; 「From the handling of its details it is held to have been built in the early Muromachi period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • zhihuasi-zhwiki - 「Its present site is No. 5 Lumicang Hutong, Dongcheng district, Beijing.」; 「Bell tower: east of the Zhihua gate, outside it, of two stories. Its form is the same as the drum tower's. In the bell tower hangs a bronze bell cast in the ninth year of Zhengtong (1444), 1.05 meters across the mouth and 1.60 meters tall」; 「Drum tower: west of the Zhihua gate, outside it, of two stories. A hip-and-gable roof of black glazed tile; its width and depth are both 7.1 meters.」; 「The central line of the Zhihua temple runs, from south to north: the mountain gate, the Zhihua gate, the Zhihua hall, the Wanfo pavilion (the lower story the Rulai hall, the upper story the Wanfo pavilion), the Dabei hall, the Wanfa hall.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (the Zhihua gate is the temple's inner gate, behind its main gate)
  • tokinokane-list-jawiki - 「Shinjuku, Shinjuku ward, Tokyo (Tenryuji)」; 「Asakusa, Taito ward, Tokyo (Sensoji)」; 「Takasu-cho, Kaizu-cho, Kaizu city, Gifu prefecture (Nionji)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (entries of the list of time bells, each naming where the bell hangs)
  • kokubunji-edict-jawiki - 「in 740 (Tenpyo 12) he issued an edict to copy ten sets of the Lotus Sutra and build seven-story pagodas.」; 「build a seven-story pagoda in each province」; 「set up one provincial monastery and one provincial nunnery in each province」; 「on the 14th of the second month of Tenpyo 13 (741) (the date follows the Ruiju Sandai Kyaku), the "edict founding the provincial temples" was issued by Emperor Shomu.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kohfukuji-sanjunoto - 「Size: height 19.1 m; first story: three bays square, 4.8 m」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the same page: Kamakura period, a national treasure)
  • sanjunoto-jawiki - 「about 24 m tall, the oldest surviving three-story pagoda」; 「the east pagoda, about 34 m tall」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the first is Hokkiji's row of the page's table, the second Yakushiji's)
  • gojunoto-jawiki - 「54.8 m (the tallest in Japan before the modern era)」; 「16.1 m (the lowest in Japan of five-story pagodas standing outdoors」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the first is Toji's entry, the second Muroji's)
