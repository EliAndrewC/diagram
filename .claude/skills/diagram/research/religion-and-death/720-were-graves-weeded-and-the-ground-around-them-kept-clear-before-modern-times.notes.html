Those who bow, those who pour libations, those who wail, those who clear the weeds from the grave and add earth to it - after burning the paper ingots, they set paper money on the grave's top. Where in view there is no paper money, it is an orphaned grave.」 (translated; ) (book 2, the section on the spring grounds)
