• kotobank-morijio - 「In customer trades such as restaurants, assignation houses and variety halls, heaping salt small at the doorway to celebrate good fortune.」 (translated; ); its earliest cited use: 「a ninjōbon, Shunshoku Magaki no Ume (about 1838-40), book 5」 (translated; ) (the Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
  • jawiki-morijio - 「Morijio is a custom of heaping salt into a three-sided pyramid or a cone and setting it at the entrance or inside the house.」 (translated; ) The article flags itself as citing no verifiable sources, or too few.
  • ise-miyachu-morijio - 「When morijio is set at an entrance such as the front door, we believe it is in many cases placed outside the entrance, to the left and right.」 (translated; ) A seller of ritual goods answering a customer.
