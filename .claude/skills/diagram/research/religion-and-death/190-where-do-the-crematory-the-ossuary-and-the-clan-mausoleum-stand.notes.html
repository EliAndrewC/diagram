  • shinagawa-kaizoji-muentou - 「In the Edo period, when the holding jail was placed at Shinagawa, the bodies of those who died there were carried to this temple and buried; their number is said to have exceeded seven hundred between Genroku 4 (1691) and Meiwa 2 (1765).
