• jawiki-saijin - 「Ordinarily a shrine enshrines several kami; among them the one enshrined as principal is called the main kami (shushin, shusaijin), and the others are called secondary kami (haishin, haishishin, aidonoshin) and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「通常、神社では複数の神を祀っており、その中で主として祀られる神を主神（しゅしん）・主祭神（しゅさいじん）、それ以外の神を配神（はいしん）・配祀神（はいししん）・相殿神（あいどのしん）などという。」) The article flags itself as short of sources.
  • kotobank-aidono - 「Enshrining two or more kami together in the same shrine hall. Also, that hall. Also read aiden.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「同じ社殿に2柱以上の神を合わせて祭ること。また、その社殿。あいでん。」) (the Digital Daijisen entry)
  • kotobank-aidono - 「The honor paid to the aidono kami is in many cases lower than that paid to the main kami.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「相殿神に対する礼遇は主祭神よりも下る場合が多い。」) (the Nipponica entry)
  • kotobank-aidono - 「Also, a hall built by joining two bays into one, though it has two doors, is called aidono-zukuri or a two-bay shrine (nikensha).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「また、扉は二つあるが、2間を一つにあわせてつくった社殿を、相殿造または二間社（にけんしゃ）という。」) (the Nipponica entry)
  • genbu-honden-styles - 「[Aidono-zukuri] Two or more Kasuga-style or Nagare-style halls joined together and housed under a single roof.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「【相殿造】 春日造や流造を、二棟以上連結して、一つの屋根に納めたもの。」) A personal reference site with no citations.
  • tokyo-jinjacho-kamidana - 「For a three-shrine altar shelf, the best order is the Jingu talisman in the center, the ujigami's talisman to the right as you face it, and the talisman of a shrine you revere to the left.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「三社造りの場合は、中央に神宮大麻、向かって右に氏神さま、左に崇敬している神社のお神札の順がよいでしょう。」)
  • tokyo-jinjacho-kamidana - 「For a one-shrine altar shelf, the best order is the Jingu talisman frontmost, the ujigami's next, and the talisman of a shrine you revere behind it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一社造りの場合は、一番手前に神宮大麻、次に氏神さま、その後ろに崇敬している神社のお神札の順がよいでしょう。」)
  • jawiki-goshi - 「The former kind of gōshi has three types: enshrining the kami together in the main hall; moving the original shrine into the precinct as a precinct shrine; and moving it to a detached precinct as a shrine outside the precinct.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「前者の合祀には、本殿で祭神を一緒に祀る本殿合祀と、神社の境内に元の神社を移転し境内社とする境内合祀、離れた飛地境内に移転し境外社とする飛地境内合祀の3種類がある。」) The article is a stub flagged as short of sources.
