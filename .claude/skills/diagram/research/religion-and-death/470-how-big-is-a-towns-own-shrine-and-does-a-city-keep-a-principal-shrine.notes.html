• ranzan-kamagata-hachiman - 「Council of State notice: the district-shrine rules of Meiji 4 (1871), 4th day of the 7th month: "District shrines are fixed at about one shrine per household-register district. Suppose twenty villages with some thousand households. In one district there are five shrines. Each takes three or five villages as its parish. Of these five shrines, the one that should stand first - whether listed in the Engishiki, or holding an earlier rank, or the one to which natural faith returns - is to be made the district shrine. The other four are attached to the district shrine and are made village shrines."」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (quoted in the web editors' footnote to the entry, not in the register itself)
