Four mu holding 573 coffins is about 4.7 m², some 50 sq ft, for a full-body grave, taking the mu at the standard 666.7 m², which the gazetteer does not itself state. These are charity graveyards for the drowned in Jiangnan; that they were packed more tightly than a family grave sited by fengshui would be is this project's comparison, on no page read.
