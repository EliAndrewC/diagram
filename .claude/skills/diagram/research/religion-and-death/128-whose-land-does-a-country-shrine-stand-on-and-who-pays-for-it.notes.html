• jochi-jawiki - 「It refers to those lands, of the land exempted from annual tax by the shogunate and the daimyo in the Edo period, other than the vermilion-seal and black-seal lands.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代に幕府・大名より年貢を免除された土地のうち、朱印地・黒印地を除いたものを指す。」); and 「Land of special provenance, such as the precincts of temples and shrines and fields and homesteads holding a document of tax exemption」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺社の境内や、無年貢証文のある田畑・屋敷など特別な由緒のある土地で」)
  • shuinchi-jawiki - 「Even the largest, Kōyasan and Nikkō Tōshōgū, were about 10,000 koku, and the smallest a mere 1 koku」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「最も広い高野山や日光東照宮でも1万石程度、最も狭いものではわずか1石で」)
  • danka-seido-jawiki - 「A parish household belongs to a particular temple, entrusts all of its funerary and memorial rites to that temple, and pays fuse.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「檀家は特定の寺院に所属し、葬祭供養の一切をその寺に任せ、布施を払う。」)
  • mianning-village-temples - 「Some donated fields and land outright, some partly bought and partly donated, to serve as a permanent resource for the deity's incense and lamps」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「或舍田舍地，或半买半舍，以为神圣永远香灯之资」)
  • lijia-zhwiki - 「The greatest function of this lijia system lay in allocating corvée labor and collecting tax by head-count, and the like.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「此里甲制度，最大功能在于分配繇役及按丁纳税等。」) (the register was the lijia's, not the temple's)
