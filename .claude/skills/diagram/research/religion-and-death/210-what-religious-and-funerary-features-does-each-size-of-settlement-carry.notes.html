• dosojin-jawiki - 「Enshrined at spatial and social boundaries - a village's entrance and exit, a mountain pass, a crossroads, the foot of a bridge, and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村落の出入口、峠、辻、橋のたもとなど、空間的・社会的な境界に祀られ」); and 「A deity enshrined mainly in the form of a stone monument or stone image at a settlement's boundary or a village's center, at the border between inside and outside the village, at a road crossing, at a three-way junction and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「集落の境や村の中心、村内と村外の境界や道の辻、三叉路などに主に石碑や石像の形態で祀られる神で」)
  • sanko-densetsu-jawiki - 「At Mikurahana there is a dragon-god shrine near a well called Binsui-ire, and the people of Amasegawa call November 20 Dragon God Day and hold a festival」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「三倉鼻には鬢水（びんすい）入れという井戸の近くに龍神祠があって、天瀬川の人たちが11月20日を龍神の日と呼んで祭りをしている」) (a water-deity shrine rather than a wayside one)
