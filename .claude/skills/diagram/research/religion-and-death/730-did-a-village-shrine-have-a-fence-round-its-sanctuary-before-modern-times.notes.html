• kotobank-tamagaki - 「A fence set round a shrine or the imperial palace. Igaki. Mizugaki.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); its earliest cited use, 「Kojiki (712), last book, songs」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ), a song in which the word is written 多麻加岐, tamakaki; and a later one, 「modeled on the four-fold mandala, tamagaki, mizugaki, aragaki and so on, layer upon layer」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ), cited as 「the Yonezawa text of the Shasekishū (1283), book 1」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Shasekishū is a collection of Buddhist tales; the Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
  • iwashimizu-mizugaki - 「The present main-shrine building group was rebuilt by the Edo shogunate in Kan'ei 11 (1634).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); and 「the south-facing main sanctuary and the sanctuary of the Takeuchi-sha to its northwest are enclosed by the mizugaki」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • iwakiyama-mizugaki - 「The main sanctuary, the inner gate, the mizugaki, the middle gate and the rest were built, later than the tower gate and the worship hall (Ōdō), by Nobumasa, the fourth lord of Hirosaki, taking the years from Jōkyō 3 (1686) to Genroku 7 (1694)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the page's 瑞恒 here is a misprint of 瑞垣, as its title spells it); and 「The mizugaki goes from both ends of the inner gate once round the main sanctuary」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
