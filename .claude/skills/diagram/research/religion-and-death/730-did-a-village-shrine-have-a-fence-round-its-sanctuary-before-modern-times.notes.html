• kotobank-tamagaki - 「A fence set round a shrine or the imperial palace. Igaki. Mizugaki.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「神社・皇居の周囲にめぐらした垣。斎垣(いがき)。瑞垣(みずがき)。」); its earliest cited use, 「Kojiki (712), last book, songs」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「古事記（712）下・歌謡」), a song in which the word is written 多麻加岐, tamakaki; and a later one, 「modeled on the four-fold mandala, tamagaki, mizugaki, aragaki and so on, layer upon layer」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「四重の万だらをかたどりて、玉がき・水がき・あらがきなむど重々に」), cited as 「the Yonezawa text of the Shasekishū (1283), book 1」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「米沢本沙石集（1283）一」) (the Shasekishū is a collection of Buddhist tales; the Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
  • iwashimizu-mizugaki - 「The present main-shrine building group was rebuilt by the Edo shogunate in Kan'ei 11 (1634).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現在の本社社殿群は，寛永１１年（1634）に江戸幕府により造替されたものである。」); and 「the south-facing main sanctuary and the sanctuary of the Takeuchi-sha to its northwest are enclosed by the mizugaki」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南面する本殿と北西側の摂社武内社本殿を瑞籬で囲み」)
  • iwakiyama-mizugaki - 「The main sanctuary, the inner gate, the mizugaki, the middle gate and the rest were built, later than the tower gate and the worship hall (Ōdō), by Nobumasa, the fourth lord of Hirosaki, taking the years from Jōkyō 3 (1686) to Genroku 7 (1694)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「本殿をはじめ、奥門、瑞恒、それに中門などは、楼門や拝殿(大堂)より遅れて、弘前4代藩主信政が貞享3年(1686)より元禄7年(1694)までの年月を要して建立したもの」) (the page's 瑞恒 here is a misprint of 瑞垣, as its title spells it); and 「The mizugaki goes from both ends of the inner gate once round the main sanctuary」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「瑞垣は、奥門の両端から本殿を一周し」)
