• kotobank-morijio - 「In customer trades such as restaurants, assignation houses and variety halls, heaping salt small at the doorway to celebrate good fortune.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「料理屋、待合、寄席などの客商売で、縁起を祝って門口に塩を小さく盛ること。」); its earliest cited use: 「a ninjōbon, Shunshoku Magaki no Ume (about 1838-40), book 5」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「人情本・春色籬の梅（1838‐40頃）五」) (the Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
  • jawiki-morijio - 「Morijio is a custom of heaping salt into a three-sided pyramid or a cone and setting it at the entrance or inside the house.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「盛り塩（もりしお、もりじお）は、塩を三角錐型あるいは円錐型に盛り、玄関先や家の中に置く風習である。」) The article flags itself as having no verifiable sources.
  • ise-miyachu-morijio - 「When morijio is set at an entrance such as the front door, we believe it is most often placed outside the entrance, to the left and right.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「玄関等、出入口に盛り塩をされる場合は 入口外側の左右へ置いていただくことが多いように存じます。」) A seller of ritual goods answering a customer.
