  • kawazoe-2010-ryobosei - 「burial graves lie on average about 370 m away」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一方の埋め墓は，平均して 370m 前後の距離に位置している」); 「there are cases of burial graves set about 900 m away」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「900m ほど離れた距離にも埋め墓が設けられている事例があり」)
  • kawazoe-2010-ryobosei - 「burial is wanted as far from the settlement as possible, but because of practical limits such as carrying, it is compromised at a certain distance.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「集落からできるだけ遠くに埋葬したいものの，運搬などの現実的な制約から一定の距離で妥協される．」)
  • kofukuroman-sanmai - 「The sanmai stands at the edge of the hamlet, while the visiting graves are inside Hogenji (法源寺) and Eirinji (栄林寺) within the hamlet.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「サンマイは部落のはずれにあるが、参り墓は部落内の法源寺・栄林寺内にある。」) (of Takenami, in a passage the page quotes from the 1966 folklore volume 『若狭の民俗』)
  • meiji-1884-bochi-saimoku - 「Art. 2: A new graveyard is to be sited not along national roads, prefectural roads, railways or great rivers, about sixty ken or more from houses, on high, dry ground that does no harm to drinking water.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「第2条 墓地ヲ新設スルハ国道県道鉄道大川ニ沿ハス人家ヲ隔ルコト凡ソ六十間以上ニシテ土地高燥飲用水ニ障ナキ地ヲ選ムヘシ」) (the Home Ministry instruction of 1884, for new graveyards; the ken taken at about 6 ft)
  • kawazoe-2010-ryobosei - 「an up/down (kami/shimo) sense of direction created by the watercourse exists; in it, the burial grave may lie below the settlement but never above it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水系によって作り出されるカミシモの方向概念は存在している．その際に，埋め墓は集落よりも下にあることはあっても，上になることはない．」)
  • kawazoe-2010-ryobosei - 「a shrine is placed at the kami side, a temple with the visiting grave, and small shrines along roads leading out of the residential area and where roads cross watercourses.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「カミには神社，詣り墓には寺院，居住域から外部へと続く道の途中や，道と水系の交差する地点には祠がおかれる．」) (the paper's model of a two-grave settlement)
