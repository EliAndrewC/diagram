Where does the country monk keep the district's registers? In the dwelling, never in an office

Sources: terauke-seido-jawiki, shumon-ninbetsu-jawiki and jaanus-shamusho (the registers and the office a small shrine did not have)

The rule the sheet follows: the district's registers - under the terauke system the temple issued the travel certificate, the village headmen compiled the ledger - are kept, on the sheet, in a writing room or a record chest in the monk's dwelling (the sheet's own arrangement), never in an office building, because a small shrine had none: "business was frequently carried on at the private home of the administrator". Where the dwelling stands is under "Does the country monk live at the shrine?".
