What the research found. Beyond the hall, the sanctuary, the arch and the wood (the wood is under "How large was a village shrine's precinct, and how much of it was built on?"), a village shrine's ground held little, and most of what it did hold was given to it later, one piece at a time. The decision. At average wealth the sheet draws the hall, the sanctuary, the arches, the sacred tree and the basin in their clearing inside the grove, and nothing else: the guardian dogs, the lanterns and the strength stones are gifts on the sheet's wealth knob (under "What did a parish give its shrine as it grew richer?"), and the stage and the ring, real but not general, are knobs whose default is absent.
