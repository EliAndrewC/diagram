  • weilongwu-zhwiki - 「In front of the hall-and-wing house there is a rectangular heping (also called a shaiping, a drying floor), which can be used for drying grain and other crops. It can also serve as the space for activity at the turn of the year and at festivals and for weddings and funerals. The width of the heping is a careful matter, generally matching the height of the main hall.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ); and of the water before it, 「In front of the heping there is a half-moon pond, called the fengshui pond. The pond can be used to store water against drought and to raise fish and to irrigate, and it also serves somewhat to temper the air temperature around the building, and holds water for putting out fire.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ); and that the arrangement is the rule rather than one instance, 「In the middle of the half-moon inner ring's plaza stands a two-hall or three-hall ancestral shrine, and in front of it, as a matter of course, are the heping and the pond.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) This is the Hakka walled house with the ancestral hall on its axis, not a free-standing Cantonese hall.
