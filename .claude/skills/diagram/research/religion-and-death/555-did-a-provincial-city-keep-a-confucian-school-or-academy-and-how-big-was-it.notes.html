• hanko-jawiki - 「schools that the domains founded to educate the sons of their retainers」; 「Japan's first domain school was the Okayama school, founded in 1669 (Kanbun 9) by the Okayama lord Ikeda Mitsumasa」; 「In their period of growth they numbered 255 across the country, founded in almost every domain.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • hanko-jawiki - 「as with the Yanagawa and Yonezawa domains, there are also cases in which a Confucian shrine and lecture hall built around the middle of the Edo period at the house of a domain Confucian scholar were moved and enlarged in the later Edo period, domain offices for the school were set up, and it was taken under the domain as its school.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kodokan-jawiki - 「The site covers about 105,000 square meters, the largest of any domain school in the country.」; 「built inside the third bailey of Mito castle」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • taku-seibyo-jawiki - 「three bays long, three bays deep, one story with a mokoshi」; 「in 1699 (Genroku 12) he built a school (later the Togen Shosha) and placed an image of Confucius in its lecture hall, and in 1708 (Hoei 5) the worship hall was completed at the foot of Mount Shiihara」; 「after its completion it was called the Kyoanden, and the present Confucian shrine is that building」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the builder was 多久邑主の多久茂文, Taku Shigefumi, lord of Taku; a mokoshi is a skirt roof run round a building below its main roof)
  • shizutani-taiseiden-bunka - 「former Shizutani School Confucian shrine, Taiseiden」; 「mid-Edo / 1684; three bays long, three bays deep, one story, irimoya roof, hongawara tile」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shizutani-gakko-jawiki - 「a school for commoners opened by the Okayama domain」; 「the lecture hall is designated a national treasure, and 24 structures including the small study, the drinking room, the library, the Confucian shrine, the Shizutani shrine and the stone wall are designated national important cultural properties.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • yushima-seido-jawiki - 「in 1799 (Kansei 11) the great rebuilding of the Yushima Seido, long in ruin, was completed, and the site grew from 12,000 tsubo to over 16,000 tsubo, and the Taiseiden too was remade, after the Confucian shrine of Mito, as a black-lacquered building 2.5 times the scale of its first building.」; 「later it became a school under the shogunate's direct administration」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shuyuan-zhwiki - 「another educational system distinct from the official schools, an educational institution founded privately, though sometimes under official direction」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • yuelu-shuyuan-zhwiki - 「Yuelu Academy covers 21,000 square meters and is laid out as symmetrical courtyards along a central axis, several deep. The main buildings - the head gate (main gate), the second gate, the lecture hall, the Banxue studio, the Jiaoxue studio, the Baiquan pavilion, the imperial library, the Xiangshui Jiaojing hall and the Confucian temple - are gathered on the central axis」; 「dormitories, memorial shrines and the like are ranged on the two sides.」; 「the Confucian temple stands on the academy's left side」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • yuelu-shuyuan-zhwiki - 「the 23 provincial-capital academies established under the edict of Yongzheng 11」; 「Hunan, Changsha: Yuelu Academy」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
