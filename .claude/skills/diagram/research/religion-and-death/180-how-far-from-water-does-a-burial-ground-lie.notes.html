• meiji-1884-bochi-saimoku - 「Art. 2: A new graveyard is to be sited not along national roads, prefectural roads, railways or great rivers, about sixty ken or more from houses, on high, dry ground that does no harm to drinking water.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「第2条 墓地ヲ新設スルハ国道県道鉄道大川ニ沿ハス人家ヲ隔ルコト凡ソ六十間以上ニシテ土地高燥飲用水ニ障ナキ地ヲ選ムヘシ」) (the Home Ministry instruction 内務省乙第40号達 of 1884, which the page quotes in extract; it governs new graveyards, sixteen years after the shogunate fell)
  • louzeyuan-zhwiki - 「Paupers' burial grounds were generally sited on high, open, barren ground, with a fence built around them to keep out idlers.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「漏泽园一般选在高旷不毛之地，四周建有围栏，阻闲杂人等进入。」)
  • kawazoe-2010-ryobosei - 「an up/down (kami/shimo) sense of direction created by the watercourse exists; in it, the burial grave may lie below the settlement but never above it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水系によって作り出されるカミシモの方向概念は存在している．その際に，埋め墓は集落よりも下にあることはあっても，上になることはない．」) (of 22 two-grave settlements in the Shiwaku islands, Shima and Wakasa)
  • kawazoe-2010-ryobosei - 「where there is room, as in farming villages... placing the burial grave downstream on the watercourse perhaps also has the aim of preventing a hygiene problem.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「農村などの立地に余裕のあるところでは…埋め墓を水系の下流におくことで，衛生的な問題を防ぐという観点もあるだろう．」) (the author's suggestion, hedged, not a finding)
  • kawazoe-2010-ryobosei - 「the residential area and the burial grave are sometimes set on opposite banks, with the watercourse as the boundary; this is mainly in coastal fishing villages」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水系を境に居住域と埋め墓が対岸におかれることもある．これは，主に海岸部の漁村であるが」); 「seaside burial graves in particular are on unstable ground eroded by tsunami」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「特に海辺の埋め墓は，津波によって浸食される不安定な土地であり」)
  • isesaki-bochi-kijun - 「20 m or more from a river or lake.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「河川または湖沼から20メートル以上の距離があること。」) (a present-day city ordinance's siting standard for graveyards and crematoria; the same standard asks 120 m from houses and public facilities, and a place with no risk of polluting drinking water)
  • paddy-field-enwiki - 「A paddy field (or paddy) is a flooded field of arable land used for growing semiaquatic crops」 (the flooded field; the plant is semiaquatic rather than submerged, and the grave hazard and the channel exclusion are this page's reasoning)
