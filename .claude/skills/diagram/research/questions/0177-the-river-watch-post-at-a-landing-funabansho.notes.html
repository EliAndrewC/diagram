• funabansho-jawiki - 「A funabansho is an office that, in the Edo period, domains with a coastline set up at the landings of routes that boats often traveled and at other key points, to inspect passing boats and to collect taxes, among other duties.」 (translated; ) The article is a stub, flagged since 2019 as lacking sources.
  • koto-nakagawa-funabansho - 「The Nakagawa funabansho was set up on the 6th day of the 6th month of Kanbun 1 (1661) on the north bank at the Nakagawa mouth of the Onagi river. It was a checkpoint for river-boat inspection, examining the people and goods entering and leaving Edo.」 (translated; )
  • koto-nakagawa-funabansho - 「As to its size, the Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko records a little over 26 ken east-west (about 47 m) and a little over 17 ken north-south (about 31 m).」 (translated; )
  • koto-nakagawa-funabansho - 「According to the illustration in the Edo Meisho Zue, a wooden palisade ran around the station, ten spears were kept there, and a watch hut was built at the edge of the Onagi river.」 (translated; )
  • koto-nakagawa-funabansho - 「The Nakagawa watch duty was held by yoriai-rank hatamoto; the hatamoto themselves normally did not attend the station but sent their retainers.」 (translated; )
