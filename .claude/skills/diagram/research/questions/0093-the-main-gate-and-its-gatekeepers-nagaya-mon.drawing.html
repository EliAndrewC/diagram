How our maps draw the main gate and its gatekeepers (nagaya-mon)

A building plan draws its compound's main gate in one of the forms the record shows, and its gatekeepers in one of the two places they sat. Where two forms are both attested, each plan takes one at random, a knob. The gate of a Japanese magistrate's office is drawn from about 6 to 12 ft wide; a yamen's is a roofed building of three bays, drawn 18 to 24 ft wide by our guess.

  • A Japanese magistrate's office's main gate is a one-bay gate or a gate range (nagaya-mon), a knob chosen for each plan.
    Both forms survive among buildings registered as cultural properties: the one-bay gate in three examples and the nagaya-mon in one.
  • How wide is each form drawn?
    A one-bay gate is drawn about 6 to 8.5 ft wide, the frontage the three registered examples measure. A nagaya-mon's opening is drawn about 12 ft, the two ken the registered one opens in.
  • A samurai residence's gate on the town and city maps, 9 to 12 ft wide, falls between the one-bay gate and the nagaya-mon.
    That width is set at How our maps draw compound walls (neribei and tsuijibei).
  • A yamen's main gate is drawn as a roofed building of three bays.
    That is its form in the record: a building with a roof, not an opening in the wall, three bays wide by law.
  • A yamen's gate is drawn 18 to 24 ft wide, a guess.
    The width is a GUESS: no page we read gives such a gate's width.
  • The gatekeepers sit in a gatehouse beside the gate, or in a guardroom inside the gate range, a knob chosen for each plan.
    Both are attested: a gatekeepers' house separate from the gate is the Takayama jin'ya's form, the guardroom inside the gate range the Kaibara jin'ya's.
  • The gatehouse and the guardroom in the gate range are both drawn about 2 ken (~12 ft) deep.
    That depth is measured for both forms: the Kaibara jin'ya's gate range and the separate guardroom set against the gate of the temple Kita-in.
  • How long is a gatehouse of its own drawn?
    About 40 ft, a GUESS. The one separate guardroom measured, a building set against a temple gate, is about 18 ft long, and no page we read gives the size of the Takayama gatehouse; a building of 40 ft or more beside a gate is the scale of a gate range.
