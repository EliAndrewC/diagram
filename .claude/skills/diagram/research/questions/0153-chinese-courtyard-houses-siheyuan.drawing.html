How our maps choose between the courtyard house and the Japanese house

A Japanese townhouse and a Chinese courtyard house are not one house in two styles. The townhouse is one building on the street, its rooms in a row along a passage. Even a rich townsman's dwelling behind the shop was joined to it by a veranda and a garden, not built around a court. The courtyard house closes a yard with buildings on all four sides. Since wealth did not turn one form into the other, our maps do not choose the form house by house or by rank: each settlement follows one tradition or the other.

The rule the map follows:

  • Whether a settlement's houses follow the Japanese forms or the Chinese is a knob, drawn at random from the settlement's seed.
    The same choice governs the town's shops (the frontage knob at How our maps draw a town's street front (machinami)). It does not govern the town's inn, whose form is a knob of its own (How our maps draw an inn).
  • Even in a settlement of Chinese houses, the buildings inside an administrative compound are Japanese.
    The reason is given at How our maps draw a magistrate's compound.
  • In a settlement of Japanese houses, a townsman's house is one building on the street.
    Its rooms stand in a row along a passage, as How our maps draw a merchant's townhouse draws them.
  • In a settlement of Chinese houses, a house is a court closed by buildings on all four sides.
    It has one court or more, as many as three or four, by the size of its ground and of its household.
  • No courtyard house is drawn deeper than about 250 ft.
    That is the 77 m (~253 ft) of the deepest Beijing courtyard house.
  • How many courts a household gets is a GUESS.
    The number follows the ground and the household, but no page we read says which households of a Chinese town lived in a one-court house and which in more.
  • In a settlement of Chinese houses, a shop is the two-story street building of a courtyard house.
    Our maps draw every Chinese shop on the example of the old town of Qixian, where the shops of the old street mostly follow the courtyard house's plan, the building on the street mostly of two stories.
