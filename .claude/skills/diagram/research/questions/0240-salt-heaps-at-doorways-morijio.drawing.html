How our maps draw salt heaps at doorways (morijio)

Our maps draw only what is recorded before modern times, so none draws salt at the gates of an official's compound, and none draws the heap as a cone or as a pair at a door. What is recorded from then is a small heap of salt at the doorway of a trade that wanted customers, in the late Edo period; no page we read puts it at an official's gates, and no page we read gives it a cone or a pair before present-day custom.
