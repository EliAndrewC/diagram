The gate market exists for TRAFFIC, not taxes

Sources: the setting's own budget notes (tariffs at city and capital gates only; the yoriki audit), which are the setting's canon rather than historical work; the traffic drivers from guanxiang-zdic, caoshi-zhwiki, lifang-zhwiki

The why is TRAFFIC, not taxes (the GM's ruling, 2026-07-24): towns levy NO import tariffs - the setting's own budget notes put the entire tariff apparatus (the Yasuki Taka gate collection, the tariff-audit yoriki) at provincial-city and capital gates only, ~2,700 manageable collection points instead of an impossible ~14,400 town gates - and the county magistrate's jurisdiction is the whole COUNTY, so standing outside the gate crosses no tax or regulatory line at all. The honest drivers are: through-road travelers (carters, peddlers) buying services without detouring inside, the market-day chokepoint where the rural catchment trades, and late arrivals who find the gate shut at dusk - the sunset closing the record attests is a Tang WARD gate's, and that a town's own gate shut at dusk too is this page's inference.

The rule the map follows: a walled town draws a small gate market outside its gate unless it is a real exception - a purely military fort, a depopulated or suppressed gate. It scales with the gate's traffic rather than with the town's population: typically four to eight permanent premises, never fewer than three, strung along the approach road within about 420 ft of the gate and fronting the same avenue as the main street inside. Like the farmland, it is a slice of something larger. At city scale the slice shows by running off the cropped frame; a town canvas is the view and keeps its buildings clear of the frame edge, so a town's market sits whole inside the frame and the approach road running off the edge carries the "more beyond the map" signal by itself. Premises beyond the merchant dwellings are shops, which house nobody, so a larger market never disturbs the population count. The laborers and servants are never pushed outside to make room: that inverts the real pattern, in which the wall is what the commoners are inside of.
