• bunka-imado-kawarayaki - 「as the Edo meisho zue says, ... tile firing had become the specialty of Imado」 (translated; ) and 「Imado was on the west bank of the Sumida, a famous place for miyakodori.」 (translated; ) (the catalog note on Aodo Denzen's painting of tile firing at Imado, late eighteenth to early nineteenth century; the same note places Imado on the west bank of the Sumida)
  • imado-yaki-jawiki - 「Imado ware is unglazed and raku pottery fired at Imado and Hashiba and their surroundings in Taito ward, Tokyo (northeast of Asakusa).」 (translated; ) ... 「From the Edo period into the Meiji period it produced everyday wares, tea utensils, clay dolls (Imado dolls), braziers, flowerpots, roof tiles and the like.」 (translated; ) ... 「according to Kanamori Tokusui's Honcho toki kosho, at the end of the shogunate there were about fifty households producing Imado ware」 (translated; ) (the count is for all its wares, not for tiles alone; that Imado stood at the city's edge is on none of the pages read and is this record's reading of its place northeast of Asakusa)
  • liulichang-zhwiki - 「At that time the Liulichang reached north to Xiheyan on the Inner City moat and south to the present site of Beijing Normal University.」 (translated; ) and 「After the Outer City was built in the thirty-second year of Jiajing of the Ming, this became city ground, and the Liulichang, being unsuited to firing kilns inside the city, moved to Liuliqu village in the present Mentougou district」 (translated; ) (Jiajing 32 is 1553; the article elsewhere puts the move in the Qianlong years instead, and carries a banner asking for more sources; the case is a capital's, not a provincial city's)
  • higashiyamato-nariki-lime - 「For the Edo castle works, official white earth (lime) is to be fetched from Kami-Nariki and Kita-Osoki villages and Yamane. As it is urgent, relay horses are to be sent from the Mita and Kaji districts, from shogunal and private lands, and from along the road」 (translated; ) and 「In the eleventh month of Keicho 11 (1606) the shogunate, through the elders Honda Sado-no-kami Masanobu and Okubo Sagami-no-kami Tadachika, issued an order for the supply of lime to the chief intendant Okubo Iwami-no-kami Nagayasu, posted to Hachioji.」 (translated; ) and 「In the same eleventh month, Intendant Okubo passed this order on to the kiln owners who did "white-earth burning" in Kami-Nariki village of Koma district and Kita-Osoki village (Nariki 8-chome) of Tama district.」 (translated; ) and 「In this way a road was made to carry the burned lime to Edo」 (translated; ) (the Ome city page dates the order a year later)
  • higashiyamato-nariki-lime - 「from Tanashi town to Ome town, seven ri three cho, but moor road, with moor between; the moor, six ri, no houses」 (translated; ) (a road survey of 1649-50 quoted on the page; the page puts the six ri at about 23.56 km)
  • kotobank-edo - 「in 1662 (Kanbun 2) the surrounding districts that had become built up were placed under the town magistrate as town-row ground」 (translated; ) (the Nipponica entry; that these were farm villages built over is this record's reading of the entry's "had become built up")
