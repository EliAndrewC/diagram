  • imado-yaki-jawiki - 「Imado ware is unglazed and raku pottery fired at Imado and Hashiba and their surroundings in Taito ward, Tokyo (northeast of Asakusa).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「今戸焼（いまどやき）は、東京台東区の今戸や橋場とその周辺（浅草の東北）で焼かれていた素焼および楽焼の陶磁器。」) ... 「From the Edo period into the Meiji period it produced everyday wares, tea utensils, clay dolls (Imado dolls), braziers, flowerpots, roof tiles and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代から明治時代にかけて、日用雑器、茶道具、土人形（今戸人形）、火鉢、植木鉢、瓦等を生産した」) ... 「according to Kanamori Tokusui's Honcho toki kosho, at the end of the shogunate there were about fifty households producing Imado ware」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「金森得水『本朝陶器攷證』によれば、幕末期には今戸焼を生産する家が約50軒ほどあったという」) (the count is for all its wares, not for tiles alone; that Imado stood at the city's edge is on none of the pages read and is this record's reading of its place northeast of Asakusa)
  • liulichang-zhwiki - 「At that time the Liulichang reached north to Xiheyan on the Inner City moat and south to the present site of Beijing Normal University.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「当时琉璃厂的范围北至内城护城河西河沿，南至今北京师范大学位置。」) and 「After the Outer City was built in the thirty-second year of Jiajing of the Ming, this became city ground, and the Liulichang, being unsuited to firing kilns inside the city, moved to Liuliqu village in the present Mentougou district」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「到明嘉靖三十二年修建外城后，这里变为城区，琉璃厂便不宜于在城里烧窑，而迁至现在的门头沟区的琉璃渠村」) (Jiajing 32 is 1553; the article elsewhere puts the move in the Qianlong years instead, and carries a banner asking for more sources; the case is a capital's, not a provincial city's)
  • higashiyamato-nariki-lime - 「For the Edo castle works, official white earth (lime) is to be fetched from Kami-Nariki and Kita-Osoki villages and Yamane. As it is urgent, relay horses are to be sent from the Mita and Kaji districts, from shogunal and private lands, and from along the road」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸城工事のため、御用白土(石灰)を上成木・北小曽木村、山根より取寄せる。御急の事であるので、三田領・加治領、御領・私領、道中筋より助馬を出し」) and 「In the eleventh month of Keicho 11 (1606) the shogunate, through the elders Honda Sado-no-kami Masanobu and Okubo Sagami-no-kami Tadachika, issued an order for the supply of lime to the chief intendant Okubo Iwami-no-kami Nagayasu, posted to Hachioji.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「慶長11年(1606)11月、幕府から、年寄衆本多佐渡守正信と大久保相模守忠隣を通じて、八王子に配属された代官頭大久保石見守長安に石灰供出の命令書が出されています。」) and 「In this way a road was made to carry the burned lime to Edo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「こうして、焼かれた石灰を江戸へ運ぶ道ができました」) (the Ome city page dates the order a year later)
  • higashiyamato-nariki-lime - 「from Tanashi town to Ome town, seven ri three cho, but moor road, with moor between; the moor, six ri, no houses」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一、田無町より青梅町まで七里三町 但、原道 この間に原あり 一、原の間六里 家なし」) (a road survey of 1649-50 quoted on the page; the page puts the six ri at about 23.56 km)
