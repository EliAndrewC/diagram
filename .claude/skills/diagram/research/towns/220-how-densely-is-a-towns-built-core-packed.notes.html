• kotobank-gaison - 「A settlement formed of dwellings standing densely along a street, on both sides of it or on one.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「街路に沿って，その両側または片側に民家が密に立並んで形成された集落をいう。」) (the Britannica Japan entry)
  • kotobank-shukubamachi - 「At stations with heavy traffic of men and horses, such as the Tokaido and the Nakasendo, the main house was built along the highway across the full frontage of each plot」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「東海道，中山道など人馬の往来の盛んな宿場では，各敷地の街道沿いに間口いっぱいに主屋が建てられ」)
  • kotobank-shukubamachi - 「Apart from the stratum of station officials, it was usual to farm as well, and in many cases the back of the plot was a vegetable garden.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「宿役人層を除けば，農業を兼ねるのが普通で，敷地裏側は菜園になっている場合が多い。」)
  • toriimoto-jawiki - 「According to the Nakasendo Shukuson Taigaicho of 1843, the length of Toriimoto-juku from the Ono village boundary to Shimoyagura village was 13 cho (about 1.4 km) ... and the houses within Toriimoto-juku numbered 293」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「天保14年（1843年）の『中山道宿村大概帳』によれば、鳥居本宿の長さは小野村境から下矢倉村まで13町（約1.4 km）、…鳥居本宿の宿内家数は293軒」)
  • nakatsugawa-jawiki - 「the town's length, from 1663 until the end of the shogunate, was 10 cho 7 ken」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町の長さは、寛文3年(1663年)以降、幕末まで10町7間であり」) and 「(1843) Population: 928; houses: 228」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「（1843年） 人口：928人 家数：228軒」) (10 cho 7 ken is about 1,100 m)
  • itabashi-jawiki - 「of which the built street was 15 cho 49 ken (about 1.7 km)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「うち町並地は長さ15町49間（約1.7km）であり」) and 「the houses within the station 573」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「宿内家数は573軒であった」) (the survey of 1841-1844); 「the first post station counting from Nihonbashi in Edo, of the sixty-nine stations of the Nakasendo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中山道六十九次のうち江戸・日本橋から数えて1番目の宿場」) and 「prospered as one of the Four Stations of Edo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸四宿の一つとして栄えた」)
  • naraijuku-jawiki - 「The townscape of Narai-juku is about 1 km long north to south along the Narai River, and 200 m east to west.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「奈良井宿の町並みは奈良井川沿いに南北に約1km、東西に200mの長さである」)
