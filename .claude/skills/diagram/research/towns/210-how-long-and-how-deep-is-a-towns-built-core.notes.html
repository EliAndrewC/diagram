• tsumago-aisurukai - 「The length of the street within the station is given as 2 cho 30 ken (about 270 m), and it apparently did not change through the Edo period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • nakatsugawa-jawiki - 「the town's length, from 1663 until the end of the shogunate, was 10 cho 7 ken, and the road width was 3 to 4 ken」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); from the Noshu Junkoki, which 「Higuchi Yoshifuru, a retainer of the Owari domain, wrote in the Kansei era (1789-1800)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ): 「the town's length 10 cho 7 ken ... houses 175」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); and 「(1843) Population: 928; houses: 228」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (10 cho 7 ken is about 1,100 m)
  • toriimoto-jawiki - 「According to the Nakasendo Shukuson Taigaicho of 1843, the length of Toriimoto-juku, from the Ono village boundary to Shimoyagura village, was 13 cho (about 1.4 km) ... and the houses within Toriimoto-juku numbered 293」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • itabashi-jawiki - 「According to the survey by the road magistrate of 1841-1844 (the Nakasendo Shukuson Taigaicho), the station road's length was 20 cho 9 ken (about 2.2 km), of which the built street was 15 cho 49 ken (about 1.7 km)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「the houses within the station 573」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); that it was 「the first post station counting from Nihonbashi in Edo, of the sixty-nine stations of the Nakasendo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); and that it 「flourished as one of the four stations of Edo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kotobank-shukubamachi - 「A post town is characterized by stretching long along the highway, and among the large stations of the early-modern period there are some reaching a length of one ri or more.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • naraijuku-jawiki - 「The townscape of Narai-juku is about 1 km long north to south along the Narai River, and 200 m east to west.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • denken-sekijuku - 「It is the only preservation district among the Tokaido post towns, and covers almost the whole of the post town, a total length of about 1.8 km from the east fork (Higashi-oiwake) to the west fork (Nishi-oiwake) at its entrances.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • city-wall-grades-zhwiki - 「In the Ming and Qing, city walls were divided into five grades by the length of the city's circuit.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) ... 「Grade four (small cities): circuit of over 2 to 5 km. All the cities of prefectures and departments. Grade five (miniature cities): circuit under 2 km. Cities of this kind were the most numerous, such as most of the country's county seats, especially those in the western frontier regions, whose circuit was generally under 2 km.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) ... 「many county seats of the eastern coastal regions were on the contrary larger than the prefectural and departmental cities of the western frontier regions.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • visitbeijing-jimingyi - 「The Jimingyi relay town covers 225,000 square meters; the whole town is about 2,000 m round」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (225,000 square meters is about 56 acres)
  • leping-archina - 「In the forty-first year of the Ming Jiajing reign, that is 1562, the city wall was first built; the wall is 4.3 km round, about 985 m from north to south and about 1,270 m from east to west.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ); and that the county seat was 「"the crown of the six counties of Raozhou"」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
