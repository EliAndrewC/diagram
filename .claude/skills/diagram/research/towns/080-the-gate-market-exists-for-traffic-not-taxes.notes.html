• guanxiang-zdic - 「the streets outside the city gate and the area nearby」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「城门外的街道及附近区域」) (the entry gives the gate suburb its location and its name; that through-road traffic trades there without entering the walls is this record's own reading and is on no page read)
  • caoshi-zhwiki - 「It refers to the commercial districts that developed outside the compass of the city walls in each city, beginning in the Song dynasty.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「指中国宋朝开始在各城市城墙范围之外发展起来的商业区」); lifang-zhwiki - 「At sunrise, after the street drum has been struck 600 times, the ward gates are opened; at sunset, after the street drum has been struck 600 times, the ward gates are closed.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「日出敲街鼓600下後開坊門，日落敲街鼓600下後關坊門」) (the market outside the walls, descended from the rural periodic market, and gates shut at sunset - but the gates the lifang page closes are the WARD gates of a Tang city, not a city gate; that a town's gate shut at dusk too, and the late arrival stranded outside, are this page's inference)
