• guanxiang-zdic - 「the streets outside the city gate and the area nearby」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「城门外的街道及附近区域」) (the entry gives the gate suburb its location and its name; that through-road traffic trades there without entering the walls is this record's own reading and is on no page read)
  • jishi-zhwiki - 「A periodic market (jishi, xushi) is a place of periodic trade in goods located in the countryside, usually opening once every other day or every few days」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「集市、墟市（英語：Market）是位於鄉村地區的週期性商品交易地點，通數隔日或數日開市一次」) ... 「The periodic market is local: buyers and sellers are chiefly the nearby villagers」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「集市是地區性的，買賣雙方都以附近村民為主」) ... 「Many rural districts are far from a city, and for villagers to travel a long way to a city to trade is very uneconomical, and so the periodic market arose」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「好些鄉村地區遠離城市，村民要跑到老遠的城市做買賣很不經濟，集市於是應運而生」); caoshi-zhwiki - 「The forerunner of the caoshi was the rural periodic market under the Tang ward-and-market system.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「草市的前身是唐朝坊市制度下乡村的定期集市。」) (the market outside the walls descends from the market where the surrounding villagers traded; that it sits at the gate as a market-day chokepoint is this page's reading)
  • thepaper-night-gates - 「At dusk the drum sounds eight hundred times and the gates are closed」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「日暮鼓八百声而门闭」) ... 「at the palace and ward gates, the streets and the outer wall's gates, checkpoints were set up layer upon layer at night, and no one was let through」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「各宫坊里门、街道、外郭门，夜间层层设卡，禁绝行人」) (Tang Chang'an, the capital; the gates the passage reopens at dawn are the ward and market gates, and the outer wall's gates are barred by night checkpoints rather than said to shut; a county town's gate is not described)
  • caoshi-zhwiki - 「It refers to the commercial districts that developed outside the compass of the city walls in each city, beginning in China's Song dynasty.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「指中国宋朝开始在各城市城墙范围之外发展起来的商业区」); lifang-zhwiki - 「At sunrise, after the street drum has been struck 600 times, the ward gates are opened; at sunset, after the street drum has been struck 600 times, the ward gates are closed.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「日出敲街鼓600下後開坊門，日落敲街鼓600下後關坊門」) ... 「Within the city, the residential areas were divided off by the low walls of the wards (fang), to control the coming and going of their people and goods.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「城中住宅區以「坊」的短牆區隔，以管理其人員及物資的進出。」) (the city's residents live in walled wards inside it, which is where the commoners belong; that its laborers and servants are among them is this page's reading; the market outside the walls, descended from the rural periodic market, and gates shut at sunset - but the gates the lifang page closes are the WARD gates of a Tang city, not a city gate; that a town's gate shut at dusk too, and the late arrival stranded outside, are this page's inference)
