  • otabisho-jawiki - 「The otabisho is the place where, in a shrine's festival (the procession of the god), the god (generally the portable shrine carrying the god's body) rests or stays the night partway along its procession, or the destination of the procession.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) ... 「Besides those with a shrine building, such as the original shrine, a subsidiary shrine or a shrine to the god's consort, a temporary altar may be set up at the god's former seat and the like, or the god may be received at the house of the representative of the ujiko (the tonin).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kotobank-ichigami - 「The market-god festival was customarily held at the first market of the New Year」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry, by Kitami Toshio)
  • miaohui-zhwiki - 「The temple fair, also called the temple market or the festival ground, is a gathering near a temple for offerings to the gods, contests, performances and the like」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) ... 「while keeping its rites, the temple fair gradually took in market trading」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
