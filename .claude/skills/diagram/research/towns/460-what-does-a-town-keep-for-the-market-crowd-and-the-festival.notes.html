• l7r-market-day - 「Acting troupes usually make most of their money on market day, though they will frequently travel through the village districts of the county during the rest of the week to perform.」 (the setting's canon, the section "Entertainers")
  • otabisho-jawiki - 「The otabisho is the place where, in a shrine's festival (the procession of the god), the god (generally the portable shrine carrying the god's body) rests or stays the night partway along its procession, or the destination of the procession.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「御旅所（おたびしょ）とは、神社の祭礼（神幸祭）において神（一般には神体を乗せた神輿）が巡行の途中で休憩または宿泊する場所、或いは神幸の目的地をさす。」) ... 「Besides those with a shrine building, such as the original shrine, a subsidiary shrine or a shrine to the god's consort, a temporary altar may be set up at the god's former seat and the like, or the god may be received at the house of the representative of the ujiko (the tonin).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「元宮、摂末社や配偶神を祀る神社などのような社殿があるもののほか、元の鎮座地などに臨時の祭殿を設けたり、氏子の代表（頭人）の家に迎える場合などがある。」)
  • kotobank-ichigami - 「The market-god festival was customarily held at the first market of the New Year」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「市神祭は正月の初市に行われるのが通例で」) (the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry, by Kitami Toshio)
  • miaohui-zhwiki - 「The temple fair, also called the temple market or the festival ground, is a gathering near a temple for offerings to the gods, contests, performances and the like」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「庙会，又称为庙市或节场，是指在寺庙附近聚会，进行酬神、竞技和表演等的活动」) ... 「while keeping its rites, the temple fair gradually took in market trading」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「庙会就在保持祭祀活动的同时，逐渐融入集市交易活动」)
