• hutong-enwiki - 「In Beijing, hutongs are alleys formed by lines of siheyuan, traditional courtyard residences.」 ... 「Many neighbourhoods were formed by joining one siheyuan to another to form a hutong, and then joining one hutong to another.」 (lead) ; also 「Between the main hutongs, many tiny lanes ran north and south for convenient passage.」 (section "Historical hutongs")
  • desire-path-enwiki - 「A desire path ... is an unplanned small trail formed by erosion caused by human or animal traffic.」 ... 「the width and severity of its surface erosion are often indicators of the quantity of traffic it receives.」 (lead) (a path worn by the traffic along it; the paved street is not on the page)
  • desire-path-enwiki - 「The path usually represents the shortest or the most easily navigated route through a natural area or greenspace」 ... 「Desire paths typically emerge as convenient shortcuts where more deliberately constructed roads and footpaths take a longer or more circuitous route, have gaps, or are non-existent.」 (a desire path is the shortcut to where walkers are going)
