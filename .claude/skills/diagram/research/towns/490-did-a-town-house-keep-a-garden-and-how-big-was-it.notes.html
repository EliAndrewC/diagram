• niwa-jawiki - 「The tsubo-niwa means a small garden of about one to two tsubo, seen mainly in Kyoto's town houses and hermitages.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「坪庭とは、主に京都の町家や庵に見られる1～2坪ほどの小規模な庭を指す」) ... 「Because the structure of town houses standing close together gave rise to problems of daylight, airflow and ventilation, as the solution a back garden was made between the house and its neighbor, as though a part of the house had been cut away, and it was called the tsubo-niwa.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「家が密接する町家の構造は採光や通風、換気の問題が生じるため、解決方法として隣家との間に家の一部を切り取ったような裏庭を設け、坪庭と呼んだ。」) ... 「The tsubo-niwa arose in the first part of the early-modern period, when town-house building developed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「坪庭は町家建築が発達する近世前期に生まれた。」)

  • townhouse-jawiki - 「along it in a row the shop room (a workshop or selling floor), the middle room (the entrance; a reception room), the kitchen (its earth-floored part, with the stove and the sink, called the hashiri-niwa), the back room, the washroom, privy and bath, and further the tsubo-niwa and the storehouse」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「それに沿って「店（見世）の間」（工房や売り場）、「中の間（玄関）」（客間）、「台所（土間部分は竈や流しを備えた走り庭と呼ばれる）」「奥の間」「洗面所・便所・風呂」、さらに「坪庭」「蔵」が並ぶ配置である。」) (the Kyoto town house)
  • kyomachiya-jawiki - 「Many town houses have a back garden called the zashiki garden, and its planting was called senzai.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町家の多くは座敷庭とよぶ裏庭があり、植栽は前栽（せんざい）とよばれた。」) ... 「In the omoteya-zukuri form, on either side of the entrance block joining the shop block and the main house, there are the open-air genkan-niwa and the tsubo-niwa.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「表屋造りの場合、ミセ棟と主屋をつなぐ玄関棟をはさんで露天のゲンカンニワと「坪庭」がある。」) ... 「A sturdy storehouse, fireproofed, stands at the back.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「防火を施した丈夫な蔵が奥にある。」)
  • siheyuan-zhwiki - 「Flowering and fruit trees are planted in the siheyuan, for enjoyment.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「四合院中植花果树木，以供观赏。」) and 「The more common trees are jujube, persimmon and the like; the flowering trees are chiefly peony, herbaceous peony, magnolia, lilac, crabapple, wisteria, pomegranate and the like」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「比较常见的树木有枣树、柿树等，花木主要有牡丹、芍药、玉兰、丁香、海棠、紫藤、石榴等」)
