• kotobank-machikaisho - 「In Sakai, Imai, Hiranogo and Kaizuka, which developed urban self-government at the end of the middle ages, and in Kyoto, and in Osaka, which flourished greatly in the early modern period as an economic city, each separate ward within each city also had a machi-kaisho, and above them a general office for the whole, the sokaisho, was set up for each group, for each street, or for the town as a whole.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中世末に都市自治を展開させた堺・今井・平野郷・貝塚や京都，そして経済都市として近世に大いに栄えた大坂などでは，各都市内の個々の町にも町会所が設けられ，その上に全体の町会所の惣会所が各組・各筋ごとあるいは惣町に設けられた。」) (the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry)
  • kinsei-kaisho-jawiki - 「Within the ward, the machi-kaisho was used as an office and a meeting place. The ward officials were posted there; they gathered the ward's residents and passed on rules such as the machi-bure notices from the magistrate's office and elsewhere, and, the other way, did the clerical work of drawing up the papers the residents submitted.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町会内において「町会所」は事務所や集会所として用いられた。町役人が詰めて、町内の住民を集めて奉行所などからの「町触」などの規則を伝達したり、逆に住民が提出する届書を作成する事務を行ったりした。」)
  • kotobank-machikaisho - 「Its duties covered the whole of the town's government, and it is usual for them to reach, besides administration and civil justice, into policing, such as searching for and arresting criminals concerned with the townspeople. For this reason it sometimes had a white-sand court or a small jail. There were also places where it was used as lodging and reception for the domain's guests and the shogunate's officials.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その職務は町政全般にわたり，行政・民事司法に加え，町方にかかわる犯罪者の捜索や捕縛の警察面にまで及ぶことが一般である。このため白洲や小規模な牢屋を設けることもあった。また，藩の来客や幕吏の宿泊接待所に利用する所もあった。」)
  • kotobank-machikaisho - 「Among castle towns there are some, like Utsunomiya, where a kaisho was set up in each ward, but they are exceptional; on the contrary, there are places where the town officials were posted at the town magistrate's office and did their work there, and no machi-kaisho was set up.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城下町では宇都宮のように各町に会所坊が設けられたものがあるが例外的存在で，反対に町奉行の役所に町役人が詰めて執務し，町会所を設けない所もある。」) and 「Among post stations too there are cases that set up a machi-kaisho, such as Sukagawa on the Oshu-Matsumae road and Yokkaichi on the Tokaido, but they are unusual examples.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「次に宿駅の中にも奥州・松前道の須賀川や東海道の四日市のように町会所を設けた事例があるが特異な例である。」)
  • kinsei-kaisho-jawiki - 「in Edo the official residence of the ward headman doubled as the machi-kaisho」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸では町名主の役宅が町会所を兼ねた」)
  • machidoshiyori-jawiki - 「The town elders' lots were granted by the shogunate; Naraya, Taruya and Kitamura each had a corner lot facing Honcho street, in Honcho 1-chome, 2-chome and 3-chome. As with samurai, the residence doubled as the office; it was called the town elders' office」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町年寄の屋敷地は幕府から拝領されたものであり、奈良屋・樽屋・喜多村でそれぞれ本町一丁目・二丁目・三丁目の本町通りに面した角地にあった。武家と同様に住居は役宅を兼ねており、これを町年寄役所と呼び」)
  • huiguan-zhwiki - 「The huiguan, also called the gongsuo, is an industrial and commercial organization dating from China's Ming and Qing」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「会馆，又称公所，沿于中国明清的工商组织」) ... 「The earliest huiguan now known is the Wuhu Huiguan in Beijing, built in the Yongle reign of the Ming. Huiguan were set up at first only to provide fellowship, entertainment and lodging for people from the same home region; later some developed the function of uniting the merchants of one trade」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「目前所知最早的会馆是建于明朝永乐年间的北京芜湖会馆。会馆的建立起初仅为提供同乡联谊、文娱、借宿之用，后来有些发展出同行商人联合的功能」)
