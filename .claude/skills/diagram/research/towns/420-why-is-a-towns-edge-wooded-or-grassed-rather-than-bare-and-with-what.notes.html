• kotobank-zokibayashi - 「Zokibayashi, then, generally often means secondary woods of deciduous broadleaf trees, including woods for firewood and charcoal. It refers to young woods near habitation, of konara, shide, ryobu, yamahannoki and the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「したがって雑木林とは一般に薪炭用材林を含め，落葉広葉樹の二次林をいう場合が多い。コナラ，シデ，リョウブ，ヤマハンノキなど人里に近いところにある若い林をいい」) (the Heibonsha article by Tsutsumi Toshio; a modern definition, undated)
  • rinya-satoyama - 「satoyama woods, mostly of broadleaf trees such as konara and kunugi, have been used since long before the Meiji period for many purposes: fuel such as firewood and charcoal, food such as wild greens, and gathering fertilizer and materials for farming」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「コナラ、クヌギなどの広葉樹が大部分を占める里山林は、明治期以前の古くから、薪や炭などの燃料、山菜等の食糧、農業用の肥料・資材の採取など、様々な用途に利用されてきました」)
  • rinya-satoyama - 「such satoyama woods were renewed by cutting and sprouting again at intervals of about 20 to 30 years, and were woods of trees relatively thin in diameter and low in height, growing in clumps from the stump」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「こうした里山林は、20～30年程度の間隔で伐採と萌芽による更新を繰り返し、比較的直径が細く、樹高も低い株立ちした樹木からなる森林でした。」)
  • maff-musashino-giahs - 「on land thickly covered with volcanic ash and poor, trees have been planted since the Edo period to raise flatland woods, and the fallen leaves gathered and put on the fields as compost」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「火山灰土に厚く覆われ痩せた土地に、江戸時代から木々を植えて平地林を育て、落ち葉を集めて堆肥として畑に入れ」) ... 「This area had its start in the reclamation the Kawagoe domain carried out from 1654, against the food shortage that came with Edo's rapid growth in population.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「当地域は、江戸の急速な人口増加に伴う食糧不足を背景に、川越藩が1654年から行った開拓に端を発しています。」) ... 「trees were planted on grassland as far as the eye could see to make woods on the flat, and they were given several functions, such as the use of fallen leaves as compost and keeping the soil from blowing away」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「見渡す限りの草原に木々を植えて平地に林を作り出し、落ち葉の堆肥利用、土壌飛散防止など複数の機能を持たせた」) (the Musashino area of Saitama)
  • townnews-zama-magusaba - 「Magusaba were the villages' common land, where roofing material for houses and fodder for livestock and the like were cut.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「秣場とは、村々の共有地で、家屋の屋根材や家畜の飼料などを刈り取っていた場所。」)
