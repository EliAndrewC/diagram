• boso-no-mura-takei - 「The vegetable garden is on the west side of the samurai house, about 1 se (about 100 m2). Farmers from nearby came to work it. As its name, shinnomi (shiru-no-mi, 'soup-greens') field, suggests, it was planted mainly with leafy greens.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「菜園」は、武家屋敷西側に位置し、約１畝（約１００㎡）の菜園があります。菜園の耕作には近隣の農家の者が来ました。「しんのみ（汁の実）畑」と称されるように、青菜類を中心に栽培されていました。」) (the house is a museum reproduction)
  • matsushiro-bukeyashiki - 「Along with the house it has a vegetable garden, a pond, a retirement house and two dozo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「住宅とともに菜園・泉水池・隠居所・土蔵２棟などがそろっている。」) and 「The site is 3,340 m2 (1,012 tsubo), but about half of it is vegetable field, a sign of the effort at self-sufficiency.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷地は3,340㎡(1012坪)あるが、約半分は野菜畑で自給自足を図ったあとがしのばれる。」) (of the Yokota house, a 150-koku middle-rank retainer's)
  • matsushiro-bukeyashiki - 「Garden: on the south side of the building, generally with a pond」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「庭園 建物の南側にあり、一般に泉水（池）を持ち」) and 「Farm land: a work space for farm work, housework and other tasks.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「農地 農作業・家事その他の作業空間である。」) (the page's general account of how Matsushiro's samurai houses are laid out)
