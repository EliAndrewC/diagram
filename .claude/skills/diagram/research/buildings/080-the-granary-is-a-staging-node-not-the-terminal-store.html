Tax rice flowed from village granaries through the administrative compound toward central stores; the office kura held rice in transit plus a local reserve.106 Remote, high-transport-cost territory kept large on-site granary rows instead (Takayama's onkura,33 staged for all of Hida - the staging is unsourced). Which end a county sits on is readable from its settlement map - water access means through-flow, isolation means storage. The rice moved on HIRED commoner boats under the office's seals82 - the magistracy owns no hulls; its control is documentary (which is what Hayakawa's tally office at the river landing shows).
