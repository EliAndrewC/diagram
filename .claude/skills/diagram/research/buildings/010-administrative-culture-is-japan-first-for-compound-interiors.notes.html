• takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「because it is the only surviving jin'ya, a policy of preserving it as a cultural property was set out」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現存する唯一の陣屋であることから文化財として保存する方針が示された」)
  • takayama-jinya-city - 「in order, on this occasion, to preserve the historic site that is the only one surviving of the more than fifty locations nationwide」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「この機会に全国50余ヶ所のうち唯一現存する史跡を保全するため」)
  • machi-bugyo-jawiki - 「The machi-bugyo was an office of the Edo period, the post responsible for the administration and the justice of the urban districts (machikata) within the domain.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町奉行（まちぶぎょう）とは、江戸時代の職名で、領内の都市部（町方）の行政・司法を担当する役職。」) (the institutional gloss; the choice of models is this project's)
  • neixiang-yamen-zhwiki - 「In its overall layout it strictly follows the regulations for Qing-dynasty local government offices, expressing the traditional ritual-system idea of "sitting to the north and facing south, civil on the left and military on the right, court in front and sleeping quarters behind, the jail quarters located to the south".」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「它在整体布局上严格按照清代地方官署规制，表现了"坐北朝南、左文右武、前朝后寝、狱房居南"的传统礼制思想。」)
  • henan-neixiang - 「Main Court is the place where the magistrate tries criminals and holds major ceremonies. In the court, there are two kneeling stones of Ming Dynasty (1368-1644), the east for plaintiffs, while the other for defendants.」 (the source's own English)
  • henan-neixiang - 「Behind Yinbin Guesthouse is a courtyard dedicated to worshiping the God of Land and the God of Government Office.」 (the source's own English)
  • henan-neixiang - 「China's last surviving Qing Dynasty (1644-1911) prison is located right opposite the workplace mentioned above. Because the prison is located in the southwest of Major Court, it is usually called South Prison.」 (the source's own English)
  • neixiang-yamen-zhwiki - 「the county yamen's Main Hall, Second Hall and Third Hall are the three great principal buildings, of differing bulk, on the central axis」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「县衙的大堂、二堂、三堂为中轴线上体量不同的三大主体建筑」)
