• shosai-kotobank - 「A room in a private house for reading and writing.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「個人の家で、読書や書き物をするための部屋。」) (the Digital Daijisen's definition)
  • shosai-kotobank - 「The shoin or the gakumonsho too was a room or a building for the same purpose.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「書院あるいは学問所も同様の目的の部屋あるいは建物であった。」) and 「The room or building in which this tsuke-shoin was set corresponds to the study, and was called a shoin or a gakumonsho.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「この付書院が設けられた部屋あるいは建物が書斎にあたり、書院または学問所とよばれていた。」) (the Nipponica entry by Hirai Kiyoshi; the tsuke-shoin is the built-in writing desk the entry has just described)
  • kyoto-ga-sanshisuimeisho - 「a building used as Rai San'yō's study-cum-tea room」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「頼山陽の書斎兼茶室として使われた建物です。」) and 「a Confucian scholar, poet and historian active in the late Edo period」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代後期に活躍した儒学者・詩人・歴史家です。」) and 「In Bunsei 11 (1828) he newly built a study-cum-tea room」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「文政11年(1828年)には新たに書斎兼茶室を造営し」) and 「After San'yō's death Suisei-sō passed into other hands and was out of the Rai family's hands until about the middle of Meiji, and in that time the buildings other than Sanshi Suimei-sho were lost.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水西荘は山陽の死後、人手に渡り、明治の中頃まで頼家の手を離れていたため、その間に「山紫水明処」以外の建物が失われました。」) and 「there is no garden on the east side of Sanshi Suimei-sho; the garden is on the west side」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「山紫水明処の東側に庭はなく、西側に庭があります。」) (Suisei-sō is the name the page gives his estate)
  • chinesepen-shuzhai - 「In time, the study gradually separated from the living rooms and formed a function and layout of its own.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「久而久之，书斋便逐步从居室之中分离出来，形成了独立的功能和格局。」) and 「The study is generally located in a quiet part of the house compound; if there is a rear garden, it is always next to it」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「书斋一般位于宅院的僻静之处，如有后花园，必与之相邻」)
