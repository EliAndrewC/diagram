• shirobito-1717-takayama - 「The residence had rooms for the intendant or district intendant and family, a room for receiving guests, a garden, and also rooms for the yonin and maids who looked after them, an earth floor and kitchen, a well and a bath.」 (translated; )
  • boso-no-mura-takei - 「It can be divided into living space used in daily life (ima, dining room, katte (kitchen), doma) and guest space (shikidai (genkan), yoritsuki (toritsugi), zashiki (guest room)).」 (translated; ) and 「The Takei house is a single-story house in yosemune-zukuri built in the late Edo period, and the main house is made up of the doma, the katte (kitchen), the dining room, the living room, the yoritsuki (toritsugi) and the zashiki (guest room).」 (translated; ) and 「It is reproduced on the model of the Takei house, of a middle-rank samurai, in Miyakoji, Sakura City.」 (translated; )
  • honjin-jawiki - 「Used by daimyo, hatamoto, shogunate officials, imperial envoys, imperial-prince abbots and others.」 (translated; ) and 「[Honjin] had a front gate, a genkan with a shikidai, and a jodan-no-ma, which ordinary inns were not allowed to build.」 (translated; ) and, from the article's opening sentence on the honjin, 「a building in a post town, from the Edo period on, where persons of high status stayed.」 (translated; )
  • yakage-honjin - 「as honjin facilities it had guest-receiving facilities such as the o-zashiki with a jodan-no-ma and an onari-mon, and further the main house for the family's living and for running the family sake brewery, an inner storehouse, sake storehouses, a west storehouse and so on, more than ten buildings in all」 (translated; )
