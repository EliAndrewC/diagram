  • omori-daikansho-kunishitei - 「It is the site of the office that the Edo shogunate set up locally, sending an intendant, to govern the Iwami silver mine and the 150-odd villages around it, from the 17th century to the mid-19th. ... a tile-roofed, single-story front gate and the gate-rowhouse buildings attached on its left and right survive. The surviving building measures about 4 m wide and 33 m in total length.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the conversions to feet are this project's)
