• kotobank-uchigenkan - 「An entrance provided in addition to the main entrance, for household insiders such as the family to come and go.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「家族など、うちわの者が出入りするために、表玄関のほかに設けた玄関。」) (the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, whose first attested use of the word is in a joruri of 1714)
  • matsue-bukeyashiki - 「inner entrance, shikidai entrance」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「内玄関 式台玄関」) (the first two names in the page's list of the house's other features, which it does not describe)
  • kotobank-katteguchi - 「The doorway of the kitchen; also, the doorway leading from outside into the kitchen.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「台所の出入り口。また、外から台所に通じる出入り口。」)
  • homes-jin-bukeyashiki - 「And this doma is where people of lower status than the master had to come in and out, not through the genkan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そうそう、この土間は、主より身分の低い者たちは玄関からではなくここから出入りしなければなりません。」) (a floor plan the column infers for the hatamoto household of the drama JIN, set at the end of the Edo period; not a documented house)
