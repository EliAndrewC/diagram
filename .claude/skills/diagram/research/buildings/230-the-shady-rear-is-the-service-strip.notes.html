• shoinzukuri-jawiki - 「And on the south side of these connected rooms, beyond the irikawa (入り側) veranda-corridor, the garden spread out. The shoin of the jodan (上段, the raised upper seat) turned its window to the south, serving as light for reading and at the same time taking on the duty of the light that illuminated the jodan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そしてこれら連続した室の南側には、入り側を介して庭が広がっていた。上段の書院は、南側に窓を向け書見の明かり採りとするとともに上段を照らす明かり採りの用も担った。」)
  • matsue-bukeyashiki - 「The nagayamon (rowhouse gate) is one of the characteristics of a samurai residence, and was also used as the dwelling of the gatekeepers and chugen (samurai household servants).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「長屋門は、武家屋敷の特徴のひとつで、門番や中間(武家奉公人)の住居としても使われていました。」)
