• amado-kotobank - 「A board door set outside the openings of Japanese buildings; it runs on a one-groove sill and is usually stored in a tobukuro when not in use.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The amado was devised in the Momoyama period; its use at Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Jurakudai is an early example.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Heibonsha world encyclopedia entry)
  • amado-kotobank - 「Its purposes are protection against wind and rain, watchfulness at night, and keeping warm. In the 16th century it took the form of board doors sliding past each other, set between the posts at the veranda edge; in the 17th century the form with a tobukuro, set in a one-groove sill and lintel, was created.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Nipponica encyclopedia entry)
  • amado-kotobank - 「it is stored in the tobukuro in the daytime」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the Britannica entry)
