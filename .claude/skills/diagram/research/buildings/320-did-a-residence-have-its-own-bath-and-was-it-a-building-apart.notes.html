• furo-kotobank - 「In the early modern period, yudono were provided in a limited number of shoin-zukuri residences of upper-rank samurai and the like, but middle and lower samurai and ordinary people made do with gyozui or went to the public bath.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「近世には，上層の武士などの書院造の限られた邸宅には湯殿が設けられるが，中・下層の武士や一般庶民は行水ですますか，銭湯に行くことになる。」) and 「In a city, having a home bath meant the cost of digging a well and of fuel, and was a great luxury.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「都市において自家風呂を構えるには，井戸を掘る費用や燃料費がかかり，たいへんなぜいたくであった。」) and 「But in the latter half of the Edo period too, partly because coopered tubs usable as hot-water bathtubs were mass-produced, home baths gradually spread.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ただ江戸時代も後半になると，温湯浴の湯槽に使える結桶（ゆいおけ）が量産されることもあって，徐々に自家風呂が普及するようになる。」) (the Heibonsha world encyclopedia entry)
  • yokushitsu-kotobank - 「At that time nobles and warrior houses had a small bath for the family, but it was only a tub one person could get into, with a hearth built on, in a corner of the doma; because provision against the danger of fire and the high cost of fuel was inadequate, it was seldom used.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そのころ、貴族や武家は家族用の小浴室を所持していたが、土間の一隅に人が1人入れる程度の桶(おけ)にかまどを造り付けただけのもので、火を扱う危険性と高価な燃料への対応が不備であったため、めったなことでは利用されなかった。」) (the Nipponica entry; "at that time" is the late sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries of the sentence before it)
  • shirobito-1717-takayama - 「The residence had rooms for the intendant or district intendant and family, a room for receiving guests, a garden, and also rooms for the yonin and maids who looked after them, an earth floor and kitchen, a well and a bath.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「役宅には代官・郡代とその家族の部屋、客人応接の部屋、庭のほか、彼らの身の回りの世話をする用人や女中の部屋、土間や台所、井戸や風呂もありました。」)
  • kanagawa-hatamoto-kaso - 「Names such as yudono, privy and storehouse are written in red.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「赤字で湯殿や厠、倉庫など名称が記されています。」) (of the house plan of the hatamoto Okubo Kiemon house, dated Tenpo 14, 1843)
  • sayama-jinya-uematsu - 「The bath and privy were 'tsugi-oroshi', that is, premised on being added on」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「風呂と便所は「継下シ」、つまり増築を前提とし」) (of the detached houses the domain supplied to its middle-rank kyunin)
