• oshirasu-jawiki - 「The oshirasu of a magistracy either had a roof built over it, or an indoor earth floor spread with gravel was used as the oshirasu; it was not the scene of open-air white gravel seen in period dramas and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「なお、奉行所のお白洲には屋根が架けられるか、屋内の土間に砂利を敷いてお白洲として用いており、時代劇などに見られる屋外に白砂利敷の風景ではなかった。」) and 「In the oldest times the earth floor was used as it was; it was later that it became a floor of white gravel.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「もっとも古い時代には土間がそのまま用いられており、白い砂利敷となったのは時代が下る。」)
  • shirasu-imidas - 「shirasu were also set up at intendants' offices, barriers and the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「代官所や関所などにも白洲がもうけられた。」)
  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The examination room (ginmisho) and the shirasu are distinctive in being paved with guri stone (cobbles) and in being roofed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「吟味所、白州はグリ石敷で屋根のあることが特徴的である。」) and 「to preserve the historic site, the only one surviving of more than fifty across the country」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「全国50余ヶ所のうち唯一現存する史跡を保全するため」)
  • takayama-gh-shirasu - 「The province of Hida has no sea, and not enough white sand for spreading in the oshirasu could be had. So cobbles from the riverbed were spread in its place.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「飛騨の国には海がなく、お白州に敷くための白い砂が十分に手に入りません。」 and 「そのため河原のぐり石を敷いて代用したのです」) and 「as for the Takayama Jin'ya's oshirasu in particular, Hida is snow country, so for the practical reason that without a roof it would be buried in snow in winter and become unusable, the oshirasu was made inside the building.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「こと高山陣屋のお白州に関しては飛騨が雪国ですので屋根がなかったら冬場雪に埋もれてしまって使い物にならなくなってしまうという実用的な理由から、建物の中にお白州が作られました。」) (the words of the jin'ya's guide, interviewed)
  • takayama-jinya-official - 「There are two oshirasu in the jin'ya.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「御白洲は陣屋内に2ヶ所あります。」) and 「One is the place that received suits and requests from the villages, what would now be called an office's service window.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一方は、村からの訴えや願いごとを受ける、今で言うところの役所の窓口となる場所です。」) and 「At the other, those who had committed crimes were examined and judged, and it played the part of what is called a court of law.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「もう一方では罪を犯した人の取り調べや裁きが行われ、いわゆる法廷の役割を果たしました。」)
