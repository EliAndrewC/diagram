• oshirasu-jawiki - 「The oshirasu of a magistracy either had a roof built over it, or an indoor earth floor spread with gravel was used as the oshirasu; it was not the scene of open-air white gravel seen in period dramas and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「In the oldest times the earth floor was used as it was; it was later that it became a floor of white gravel.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shirasu-imidas - 「shirasu were also set up at intendants' offices, barriers and the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The examination room (ginmisho) and the shirasu are distinctive in being paved with guri stone (cobbles) and in being roofed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「to preserve the historic site, the only one surviving of more than fifty across the country」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • takayama-gh-shirasu - 「The province of Hida has no sea, and not enough white sand for spreading in the oshirasu could be had. So cobbles from the riverbed were spread in its place.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「as for the Takayama Jin'ya's oshirasu in particular, Hida is snow country, so for the practical reason that without a roof it would be buried in snow in winter and become unusable, the oshirasu was made inside the building.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the words of the jin'ya's guide, interviewed)
  • takayama-jinya-official - 「There are two oshirasu in the jin'ya.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「One is the place that received suits and requests from the villages, what would now be called an office's service window.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「At the other, those who had committed crimes were examined and judged, and it played the part of what is called a court of law.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
