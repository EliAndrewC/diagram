  • cleanup-edo-hettsui - 「The hettsui was built directly onto the doma, or, as here, also placed on the board floor. How far this helped against fire is unknown, but it certainly could be moved and was easy to clean.」 (translated; ) (of a tenement kitchen of the Edo period as the Fukagawa Edo Museum reproduces it; the hettsui is the kamado)
