• okeihan-kyomachiya - 「The plan of the machiya has changed little by little with the times, but most have as their basis the 'one row, three rooms' type, in which a long, narrow earth floor called the 'toriniwa' is lined by three rooms, the 'shop room', the 'kitchen' and the 'back room'.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町家の間取りは時代とともに少しずつ変化してきましたが、多くは「通り庭」と呼ばれる細長い土間、それに沿うように「店の間」「台所」「奥の間」の3室が並ぶ「1列3室型」が基本です。」); of the tsuboniwa, 「A garden of about one tsubo set at the back or the middle of the building. It serves both for ventilation and for light」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「建物の奥や中ほどに設けられた一坪程度の庭。風通しと採光の役割を兼ねていて」); of the back room, 「The formal zashiki, farthest in.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一番奥にある、格式の高い座敷。」) (the page speaks of Kyoto's townhouses)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「At the back of the lot were placed detached rooms, outbuildings, storehouses and the like. In Kyoto it was the rule that the storehouse was never built on the street side but set at the back of the lot, the very opposite of Edo's storehouse-built machiya. The storehouses standing at the backs of the lots made the boundary of the block and at the same time worked as a kind of firebreak.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「敷地の奥には、離れや付属屋・土蔵などが配置された。京都では土蔵が道側に建てられることはなく敷地の奥に置かれるのが通例で、江戸の土蔵造の町屋とは対極をなしていた。敷地奥に建つ土蔵は町境になると共に、一種の防火帯として機能していた。」)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「Many machiya were built in provincial castle towns, post towns, ports, temple-gate towns, country market towns and the like too, and there too machiya peculiar to the region arose. Their plans divide into Kyoto's through-earth-floor type, Edo's front earth floor made a little wider, and a fusion of the two. In regions near Kyoto the Kyoto through-earth-floor type is common, in regions farther off the front-earth-floor type and the fused type.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「地方城下町や宿場町、港町、門前町、在郷町などにも多くの町屋が建てられていたが、そこにも地方独特の町屋が成立していた。その間取りは、京都町屋の通り土間形式のもの、江戸町屋の前土間を少し広くしたもの、そしてその二つが融合したものに分かれる。京都に近い地域では京都の通り土間形式が、それより遠隔の地域は前土間形式や融合形式が多い。」)
  • kotobank-omoteyazukuri - 「One of the forms of machiya often seen in Kyoto. The shop is built on the part facing the road and the dwelling behind it as separate buildings; the two buildings are separated by an inner garden and joined by a narrow entrance building.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「京都に多く見られる町屋の形式の一つ。道路に面した部分に店舗、その奥に居住用の建物を別々の棟として建て、両棟の間は中庭で隔てられ、細い玄関棟でつながっている。」)
  • kotobank-machiya - 「many take the form with a low second story used as a storeroom」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「物置に使われる低い二階を備えている形式が多い」) (the Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry, by Suzuki Mitsuru)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「Throughout the early modern period the second floor of the Kyoto machiya did not develop much except in large merchant houses, and the mezzanine 'zushi-nikai' was usual」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「近世を通じて京都町屋の2階は大規模な商家以外あまり発達せず、中二階である「厨子二階(ずしにかい)」が一般的だった」); 「The second floor was used as a storeroom and as rooms for live-in servants」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「2階は納戸や住み込みの使用人の居室として使われた」); 「At first the second floor was built only on the street side, in the form of a 'tsushi-nikai' with low eaves」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「当初は表側にのみ2階を造り、2階の軒を低くした「つし二階」とする形式であった。」); 「As time went on it changed to a full two stories, the ground and upper floors nearly equal in area, and the second floor was used as guest rooms.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「時代が下ると、1階の面積と2階の面積がほぼ等しい総二階へと変化し、2階は客座敷として利用された。」)
  • qixian-cnr - 「The shops on both sides of the old street mostly have the northern siheyuan layout of one courtyard or several; the building on the street is mostly two stories, the ground floor used for commerce and workshops, the upper floor as a storeroom, the typical form of shop in front, workshop behind, with lodging.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「老街两侧店铺大多为北方的一进或多进四合院建筑格局，临街建筑多为两层，一层用作商业、作坊，二层为库房，为典型的前店后坊、住宿型制。」)
