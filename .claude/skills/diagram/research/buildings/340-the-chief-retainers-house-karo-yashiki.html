The chief retainer's house (karō yashiki)

A lord's chief retainer, the karō or "house elder", lived close to the lord they served, in one of two ways. At a domain lord's court the karō had mansions of their own near the lord's residence or inside the castle. At an office of the shogunate's intendants the staff lived inside the compound's walls, in small houses and long-houses.

In this setting, a county magistrate's samurai include a karō of their own.

  • A county town's magistrate has a karō of Rank 4.
    By the setting's own figures, a typical county town has 15 samurai: the magistrate, of Rank 5 and 25 koku, then the magistrate's karō and about three squad sergeants, of Rank 4 and 16 koku each.
  • At a daimyo's court, the karō and the other senior retainers often lived near the lord's residence or inside the castle.
    So says the Japanese Wikipedia article on the samurai residence.
  • The Saigo, karō to the lords of Aizu, had a mansion on a site of 2,400 tsubo, about two acres.
    The Saigo were hereditary retainers of the Matsudaira of Aizu, a house of 1,700 koku that served as karō for generations. Their site held 280 tsubo of building, about 10,000 sq ft, with 38 rooms and 328 tatami mats.

An intendancy was a smaller place, and its staff lived inside its walls.

  • An intendancy compound held its staff's small houses (koya) and long-houses (nagaya).
    The intendant's jin'ya held the intendant's official residence, the office, the rice and tax storehouses, the koya and nagaya that housed the tetsuki and tedai, and the jail, usually within high white walls, thickets, ditches or dry moats.
  • The head of the tedai was called the motojime.
    The second was called the kahan.
  • Did the motojime live apart from the rest of the staff?
    No source we read says. The one line on housing in the Japanese Wikipedia article on the tetsuki says that the tedai and tetsuki lived in nagaya or koya, and names no separate house for the motojime.
  • At Takayama the district intendant lived with the family in the residence inside the compound.
    The residence stood inside the jin'ya.
