  • tedai-jawiki - 「Tedai were chosen and appointed from among peasants, townsmen and the like who were well versed in jikata (rural administration). Formal permission from the Edo shogunate's finance office (kanjosho) was required.」 (translated; )
  • tedai-jawiki - 「Most tedai were the children of village officials and town officials. They were first hired as kakiyaku (scribes), then promoted to tedai and then to motojime tedai (chief tedai).」 (translated; )
  • neixiang-yamen - 「Going further north one enters the second rectangular courtyard, where on the east and west sides are separately set the Six Chambers, that is, personnel, revenue, rites, war, justice and works, corresponding to the court's Six Ministries; these are the county yamen's functional working organs. By the authorized establishment, one chamber was staffed with two or three men; in Ming and Qing times the establishment was frequently exceeded, the numbers even reaching more than a hundred.」 (translated; )
  • xuli-zhwiki - 「The clerks of the silver treasury are for the most part all of the surname Shi. The tax clerks of the Chongwen Gate are for the most part all of the surnames Zhang, Wang and the like, entrenched there and holding it in their grip.」 (translated; ) (entrenched hereditary clerks, and on the same page their enrichment out of the revenue - 「Officials of the capital court were often so poor they could not support themselves, while the clerks often amassed vast fortunes」 (translated; ); the rotation of magistrates over them and the Japanese tedai half are this page's)
  • mapple-takayama-jinya - 「The space next door, partitioned from the goyakusho, is the goyoba.   • mapple-takayama-jinya - of the kakiyaku-beya, 「A room only for writing, where the important documents to be submitted to the Edo shogunate and others were drawn up here.」 (translated; ) and 「The documents were bound in the chotojiba next door」 (translated; ) (the guide gives no size for either room)
  • edo-ashigaru-bugyosho - 「In this area were concentrated the functions concerned with lawsuits and examinations: the saikyosho and the shirasu, the inner sengisho and the sengisho, the duty rooms of the shacho-sen'yokata and the reikurikata, the litigants' waiting room and benches, the jail doshin's duty room and the temporary jail, and so on.」 (translated; ) (the page describes the North Town Magistracy from a plan of 1841, and says the South was built much the same)
  • jinya-kotobank - 「The daikan jin'ya was the base of the shogunate's local administration, and is the collective name for the honjin, the intendant's official residence; the office where public business was carried on; the rice kura; the levy kura; the koya and nagaya that were the official houses of the tetsuki and tedai; and the jail.」 (translated; ) (Nipponica, Murakami Tadashi)
