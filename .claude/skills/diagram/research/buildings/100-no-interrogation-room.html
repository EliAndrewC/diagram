No interrogation room

Sources: takayama-jinya-city (the examination room beside the roofed court: 「The examination room and the shirasu are distinctive in being paved with guri stone and in being roofed」, translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「吟味所、白州はグリ石敷で屋根のあることが特徴的である」); the Rokugan decision is the GM's (2026-07)

Edo justice used judicial torture as a regular step of procedure, and Takayama preserves its interrogation room (ginmisho) beside the hearing court. In Rokugan torture is unusual though not strictly forbidden, so magistracies keep no room purpose-built for it - questioning happens in the day office or the hearing court like any other business.
