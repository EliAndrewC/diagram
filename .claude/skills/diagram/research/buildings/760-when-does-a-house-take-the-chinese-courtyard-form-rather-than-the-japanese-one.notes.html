• siheyuan-zhwiki - 「Technically speaking, the siheyuan is a variant of the sanheyuan, closed by adding a further range of gate-house rooms on top of it.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「技術上而言，四合院屬於三合院的一種變體，於其上再加上一層門房的屋舍來封閉。」)
  • siheyuan-zhwiki - 「The smallest, one-court house: past the street gate you are straight in the yard, a central axis runs through it, the buildings are all single-story, and the south range, the main range and the wings enclose the yard; the north range is the main range, the buildings to east and west are the wings, and the south range's doors open to the north, which is why it is called the 'reversed-seat' range.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「最小的一进院，进了街门直接就是院子，以中轴线贯穿，房屋都是单层，由倒座房、正房、厢房围成院落，其中北房为正房，东西两个方向的房屋为厢房，南房门向北开，故称为“倒座房”。」); and 「The complete siheyuan has three courts」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「完整的四合院为三进院落」)
  • siheyuan-zhwiki - 「The siheyuan is usually lived in by a large family」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「四合院通常为大家庭所居住」); and 「The owner of the house could build according to the size of the ground and the number in the household: as small as a single court, as large as three or four courts, and it could also be built two siheyuan wide with side courts.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「房屋主人可以根据土地面积的大小、家中人数的多少来建造，小到可以只有一进，大可以到三进或四进，还可以建成两个四合院宽的带跨院的。」)
  • siheyuan-zhwiki - 「The greatest depth of a Beijing siheyuan is the distance between two hutong, about 77 m」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「北京四合院最大的进深为两个胡同之间的距离，约77米左右」)
  • qixian-cnr - 「The shops on both sides of the old street mostly have the northern plan of a single courtyard or several courtyards; the building on the street is mostly two stories, the ground floor used for commerce and workshops, the upper floor as a storeroom, the typical form of shop in front, workshop behind, with lodging.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「老街两侧店铺大多为北方的一进或多进四合院建筑格局，临街建筑多为两层，一层用作商业、作坊，二层为库房，为典型的前店后坊、住宿型制。」)
  • kotobank-machiya - 「Usually it opens a shop facing the road, fitted with shutters of the shitomido type」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「通常，道路に面して蔀戸（しとみど）形式の建具を備えた店を開き」); and 「The plan of the machiya is much alike across the country: along a long, narrow earth floor, the mise (shop), the middle room and the zashiki stand in one row or two」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町家の平面は全国的に似通っており，細長い土間に沿って〈みせ（店）〉や中の間，座敷が1列あるいは2列並んでいる」) (the Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry, by Suzuki Mitsuru)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「In the houses of upper townsmen, setting up a dwelling separate from the machiya in the middle of the block (the back garden) continued from the Kan'ei period, but that dwelling was joined to the street-front machiya by a veranda and an inner garden, and its entrance was set where one turned into an alley off the main street.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「上層町人の家では、街区の中(裏庭)に町屋と分離して住居を構えることが寛永期から続いているが、その住居は表通りの町屋とは縁と中庭でつながり、入り口は表通りから路地に入ったところに設けられていた。」)
