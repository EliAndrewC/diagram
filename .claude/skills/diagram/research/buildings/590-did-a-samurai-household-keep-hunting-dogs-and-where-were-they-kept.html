Did a samurai household keep hunting dogs, and where were they kept?

Sources: takagari-jawiki (hawking among the daimyo and its dogs and dog-handlers), inukai-kotobank (the dog-keeper; a domain lord and his hunting dog), kishuken-jawiki (boar hunting with dogs), inugoya-jawiki (the shogunate's dog houses, the first of them inside an official's compound)

Warrior households hunted with dogs. Oda Nobunaga, Toyotomi Hideyoshi and Tokugawa Ieyasu all loved hawking, and it spread widely among the other daimyo. In hawking the dogs did the flushing: many birds of prey will not go into a thicket, so dogs drove the game out of it, and the dogs were trained and kept by dog-handlers under the chief falconer. The shogun's own falconers had, besides the falconers themselves, gatekeepers for the hawk house and these handlers of the hawking dogs. Raising hunting dogs for hawking was a trade of its own, the dog-keeper's, and a place in Oita takes its name from a hunt at which the lord of Taketa domain is said to have nursed a sick hunting dog himself. Boar was hunted with dogs as well: the Kishu breed was fixed from native dogs used to hunt boar in the Kii mountains, where each hunter went into the forest with three or four of them.

One official's compound is recorded with dogs housed inside it. The shogunate's dog houses were first set up within the grounds of the jin'ya of Kitami Shigemasa, the shogun's chamberlain, at Kitami village. After the Kitami house was abolished in 1689 the site passed to the shogunate, and a record of 1693 finds about forty dogs kept there, in a care house, a nursing house and sleeping quarters for sick dogs and pups, beside the office, a gatehouse and a kitchen. That was a shelter for dogs in care, not a hunting kennel, and the shelters the shogunate built next were on a pound's scale: at Nakano in 1696, dog rooms of 25 tsubo a building, 290 of them. No page read describes a household's hunting kennel - its form, its size, or where in a samurai compound it stood.

What this means for a plan: a samurai household that hunts keeps dogs, and that is ACCURATE - the more so for a lord who hunts boar. Dogs housed inside an official's compound are attested once, at Kitami, as dogs in care. The kennel's form, its size and its seat beside the stables remain a GUESS: stables and kennel both keep the household's animals, but no source read puts the two together or gives a household kennel a size.
