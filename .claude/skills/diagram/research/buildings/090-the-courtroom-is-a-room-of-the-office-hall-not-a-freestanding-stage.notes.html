  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The examination room (ginmisho) and the shirasu (white-gravel court) are distinctive in being paved with guri stone (cobbles) and in being roofed. For the majority of criminal cases the shogunate's ruling was sought.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「吟味所、白州はグリ石敷で屋根のあることが特徴的である。事犯の大半は幕府の裁決を仰いでいた。」) (most criminal cases were referred to the shogunate for judgment)
  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The great hall of the office (goyakusho) too is of a design that places very great weight on outward form (formality).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「御役所大広間も非常に体裁を重んじた意匠となっている。」) and 「Inside, the entrance room survives just as it was rebuilt in Bunka 13 (1816), and the eye is caught by the 2.5-ken great alcove (odoko) that marks 100,000-koku rank and by the seigaiha (blue-ocean-wave) pattern that even daimyo refrained from using.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「内部は、玄関の間が文化13年改築のままでのこり、10万石格を示す2間半の大床や、大名も使用をはばかった青海波模様が目を引く。」)
