• sado-bugyosho-fires - 「The Sado bugyosho (magistracy) was built at the beginning of the Edo period, in 1603. Thereafter it repeated burning down and rebuilding five times, and after the Meiji Restoration it was used, being remodeled, as a government office and a school. In 1929 it was designated a national historic site, but in the fire of 1942 all of it burned down.」 (translated; )
