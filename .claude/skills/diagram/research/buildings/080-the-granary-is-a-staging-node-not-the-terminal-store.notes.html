  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The frame is from the Keicho era (1596 to 1615); good-quality hinoki (Japanese cypress) was used, and the finish too is by clam-blade adze (hamaguriba chona). In both age and scale it is one of the foremost grain dozo (earthen storehouses) in the country.」 (translated; )
  • gokura-jawiki - 「It was placed one per village or per several villages as a temporary storage place for carrying out agricultural produce, beginning with the tax rice.」 (translated; ) (the village granary as the chain's first link, with 「In some places it also had the role of storing crops against a bad harvest」 (translated; ); that the rice passed through the administrative compound and that the office kura held both is this page's reading)
  • takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「Surviving buildings: front gate and gatehouse; storehouses nos. 1-4 and nos. 9-12; the book storehouse.」 (translated; )
  • nishimawari-koro-jawiki - 「Zuiken reformed the shipping of rice to Edo, which had until then been handled by merchant contract, and made the cargo ships direct hires of the shogunate.」 (translated; ) (Zuiken is Kawamura Zuiken, whose reform of the tax-rice shipping this was; with 「The shipping vessels flew the red-circle "official pennant" showing they were government-service ships, and preferential measures were taken, such as priority in entering and leaving port and exemption from port-entry dues」 (translated; ) and 「he ordered the intendants to set up tachimuba stations at the ports of call, to protect the castle-rice ships and to inspect their loads strictly」 (translated; ); the county scope is this page's)
  • takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「The Takayama Jin'ya is the intendant's office - the Hida gundai's office (jin'ya) - that the Edo shogunate set up to administer Hida province as its direct territory.」 (translated; )
