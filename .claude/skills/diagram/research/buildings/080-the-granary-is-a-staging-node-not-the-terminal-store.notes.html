• takayama-jinya-city - 「The Okura is the building that had been erected as a rice storehouse in the third bailey (san-no-maru) of Takayama Castle and was moved to its present site in Genroku 8.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「御蔵は、高山城三ノ丸に米蔵として建てられていたものを、元禄8年に現在地へ移築したものである。」)
  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The frame is from the Keicho era (1596 to 1615); good-quality hinoki (Japanese cypress) was used, and the finish too is by clam-blade adze (hamaguriba chona). In both age and scale it is one of the foremost grain dozo (earthen storehouses) in the country.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「軸部は慶長年間（1596年から1615年）のもので、良質のヒノキが使われ、仕上げも蛤刃手斧であり、年代、規模共に全国有数の穀物土蔵である。」)
  • gokura-jawiki - 「It was placed one per village or per several villages as a temporary storage place for carrying out agricultural produce, beginning with the tax rice.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「年貢米を始めとする農作物を運び出す際の一時保管場所として、1か村ないし数か村ごとに1か所置かれ」) (the village granary as the chain's first link, with 「In some places it also had the role of storing crops against a bad harvest」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「場所によっては凶作時に備えて作物を蓄えておく役割もあった」); that the rice passed through the administrative compound and that the office kura held both is this page's reading)
  • takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「Surviving buildings: front gate and gatehouse; storehouses nos. 1-4 and nos. 9-12; the book storehouse.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現存建物 表門・門番所、一～四番蔵・九～十二番蔵、書物蔵。」)
  • nishimawari-koro-jawiki - 「Zuiken reformed the shipping of rice to Edo, which had until then been handled by merchant contract, and made the cargo ships direct hires of the shogunate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「瑞賢は従来の商人請負による江戸廻米を改め、積船を幕府の直雇とし」) (Zuiken is Kawamura Zuiken, whose reform of the tax-rice shipping this was; with 「The shipping vessels flew the red-circle "official pennant" showing they were government-service ships, and preferential measures were taken, such as priority in entering and leaving port and exemption from port-entry dues」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「廻漕船は御用船であることを示す朱の丸の「官幟」を掲げ、入出港においての優先権と入港税の免除などの優遇策が取られ」) and 「he ordered the intendants to set up tachimuba stations at the ports of call, to protect the castle-rice ships and to inspect their loads strictly」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「代官に命じて寄港地に立務場を置き、御城米船の保護に当らせるとともに積載量を厳密に検査させた」); the county scope is this page's)
  • takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「The Takayama Jin'ya is the intendant's office - the Hida gundai's office (jin'ya) - that the Edo shogunate set up to administer Hida province as its direct territory.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高山陣屋（たかやまじんや）は、江戸幕府が飛騨国を直轄領として管理するために設置した代官所・飛騨郡代役所（陣屋）。」)
