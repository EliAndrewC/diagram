• tenmacho-jawiki - 「Those who continued to refuse to confess were, under a ruling by the roju, urged once more to confess at the sensakusho (inquiry room), after which coercive questioning (semedoi) and torture were carried out.」 (translated; )
  • oshirasu-jawiki - 「In the oshirasu were set six torture instruments, among them the tsukubo, the sasumata, the stones of the ishidaki torture, the soroban board and thick ropes for suspension.」 (translated; ) and 「These are thought to have been used for their effect of intimidating the plaintiffs and defendants rather than for actual use, and torture was never carried out in the oshirasu」 (translated; )
  • takayama-jinya-official - 「However, the Takayama Jin'ya's torture instruments are thought to have been placed there to overawe people; the torture itself was reportedly carried out inside the jail [near present-day Kami-ichinomachi and Ebisaka].」 (translated; )
  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The examination room (ginmisho) and the shirasu (white-gravel court) are distinctive in being paved with guri stone (cobbles) and in being roofed. For the majority of criminal cases the shogunate's ruling was sought.」 (translated; )
