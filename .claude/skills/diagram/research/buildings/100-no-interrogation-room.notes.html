• tenmacho-jawiki - 「Those who continued to refuse to confess were, under a ruling by the roju, urged once more to confess at the sensakusho (inquiry room), after which coercive questioning (semedoi) and torture were carried out.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「自白を拒否し続けた者は老中決裁の下、穿鑿所にて自白を再度促した後、責問いや拷問が行われた。」)
  • oshirasu-jawiki - 「In the oshirasu were set six torture instruments, among them the tsukubo, the sasumata, the stones of the ishidaki torture, the soroban board and thick ropes for suspension.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「お白洲には突棒・刺股・拷問用の石抱きの石、十露盤、吊るし責め用の太縄などの６つの拷問器具が置かれた。」) and 「These are thought to have been used for their effect of intimidating the plaintiffs and defendants rather than for actual use, and torture was never carried out in the oshirasu」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「これらは実際の使用よりも、原告・被告に対する威嚇効果のために用いられたと考えられ、お白州で拷問が行われることはなかった」)
  • takayama-jinya-official - 「However, the Takayama Jin'ya's torture instruments are thought to have been placed there to overawe people; the torture itself was reportedly carried out inside the jail [near present-day Kami-ichinomachi and Ebisaka].」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ただし高山陣屋の拷問道具は、人々を威圧するために置かれていたと考えられており、実際は牢屋内［現在の上一之町・海老坂付近］で行われていたとのことです。」)
  • takayama-jinya-city - 「The examination room (ginmisho) and the shirasu (white-gravel court) are distinctive in being paved with guri stone (cobbles) and in being roofed. For the majority of criminal cases the shogunate's ruling was sought.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「吟味所、白州はグリ石敷で屋根のあることが特徴的である。事犯の大半は幕府の裁決を仰いでいた。」)
