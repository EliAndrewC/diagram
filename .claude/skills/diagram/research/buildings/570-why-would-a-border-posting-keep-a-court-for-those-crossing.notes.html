• kotobank-bansho - 「Each domain also set up kuchidome guard posts at the key points leading to neighboring domains or the shogun's lands, chiefly watching that the domain's products did not flow out to other territories, and sometimes taxing the goods going in and out at a fixed rate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「各藩でも隣接する藩や天領に通じる要所に口留番所を設け，主として領内の産物が他領へ流出するのを監視し，出入りする物資に一定の割合で徴税することもあった。」) and 「The domains too set kuchidome guard posts at the key points of the roads leading to other territories, and controlled the going in and out of people and goods.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「諸藩でも他領に通じる交通の要所に口留(くちどめ)番所を設置，人と荷物の出入りを取り締まった。」)
  • bansho-jawiki - 「As a guard post whose buildings of the time survive it is rare in the whole country, but the site of the Nuruyu guard post at Hanayama village, Sendai domain, is one example.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「当時の建物が現存する番所としては全国的にも珍しいが、仙台藩花山村寒湯（ぬるゆ）番所跡がその一例である。」)
  • bunka-nuruyu-bansho - 「One of the kuchidome guard posts of Sendai domain.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「仙台藩の口留番所の一つである。」) and 「a strategic point in a mountain gorge commanding the way out to Akita domain's territory」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「秋田藩領に出る口を扼する山峽の要衝であって」) and 「The front gate is built blocking the road, the inspection hall is set facing it, and the residence is to the right rear of the inspection hall as one faces it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「街道を遮って表門を構え、その正面に檢断所を置き、檢断所の向って右奥に役宅がある。」) and 「The road now runs outside the site of the guard post, but formerly it entered at the front gate and passed in front of the inspection hall.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「街道はいま番所跡敷地の外を通っているが、もと表門より門に入り檢断所の前を過っていた。」) and 「The residence is 23.19 meters along the ridge and 11.86 meters across, and takes the form of an ordinary farmhouse with the earth floor on the right and the living rooms on the left as one faces it, but it is an imposing building furnished with a shoin-style zashiki.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「役宅は桁行２３．１９メートル、梁行１１．８６メートル、向って右に土間、左に居間を配する一般民家の形式をとつているが、書院造の座敷を具えた堂々たる建物である。」) (the Agency for Cultural Affairs' designation record)
  • hakone-seki-jawiki - 「The buildings were the "front post", made up of the upper guard post, the guards' duty room, the rest room and the bath; the "opposite post", made up of the half-shift duty room, the rest room and the jail; stables, a street watch, a notice-board place and so on, and they were enclosed by a fence.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「建造物は上御番所・番士詰所・休息所・風呂場からなる「面番所」、所詰半番・休息所・牢屋からなる「向番所」、厩、辻番、高札場などが設置され、柵で囲まれていた。」)
