  • kuramai-jawiki - 「It refers to the rice received as land tax into the storehouses of the shogunate, the domains and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「幕府や藩などの蔵に年貢として収納された米のことを指す」) (with, on the same page, 「the form of disbursement in which the rice (brown rice) paid out under that system was expressed in units of bales」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その制度により支給される米（玄米）を「俵」単位で表した支給形態」); brown rice in bales as the authorities held it, the delivery form itself on no page read)
  • hatakata-men-jawiki - 「For the hatakata-men (dry-field tax rate), tax payment was often made in cash, as typified by the Kanto hata-ei system.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畑方免は関東畑永法に代表されるように金納で納税が行われることが多かった。」)
  • nta-nengu-nonyu - 「Three to four tenths of the whole land tax was nengu-kin (kokudai-no), paid in money, and its delivery too, as far as the collection office the lord designated, was the village's responsibility.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「年貢全体の３〜４割が金銭で納める年貢金（石代納）でしたが、その納入も領主に指定された受付窓口に納入するまでが村の責任でした。」) (the page names no region for the figure)
  • nagaokakyo-nengu-jono - 「The feature of the shogunal lands was that, as a principle, a tenth of the land tax was paid in soybeans (silver), three tenths in silver and the remaining six tenths in rice, but in practice there were various instructions on how to pay from year to year.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「幕府領の特徴は、年貢の１割が大豆（銀）、３割が銀、残り６割を米で納めることが原則となっていたことですが、実際にはその年ごとに、さまざまな納め方の指示がありました。」) (of the shogunal villages near Kyoto that paid the Kyoto intendant)

  • kakoimai-jawiki - 「Kakoimai is the system by which, in the Edo period, the shogunate, the domains and the towns and villages stored rice and other grain in advance in community and charity granaries against an emergency.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「囲い米（かこいまい）とは、江戸時代に江戸幕府及び諸藩・町村が米などの穀物を予め社倉、義倉に貯蔵して万が一に備えた制度。」) (the emergency reserve; and on the same page 「It has other names such as kakoimomi (reserved unhulled rice), ikoku and okigome.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「囲籾（かこいもみ）・囲穀（いこく）・置き米（おきごめ）などの異称がある。」); that unhulled rice stores longer is on no page read, ja.wikipedia 米 ranking neither form)
  • kureki-jawiki - 「Nengu kureki is the payment of split timber in lieu of the rice land tax.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「年貢榑木は年貢米の代納として榑木を納めさせるものである」) (split timber in lieu of rice in the upland Ina villages, and 「Later it became a cash payment」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「後には金納となる」); the granary shrinking toward a strongroom is this page's inference)
