• kosatsu-jawiki - 「hung high in places where traffic was heavy and people's eyes were easily caught, for example the crossroads at a city's center, entrances, the ends of bridges, barriers and the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The shogunate set up places called kosatsuba (seisatsuba) at conspicuous spots - points of heavy traffic, barriers and ports, the ends of great bridges, and further the entrances and centers of towns and villages and the like - and strictly ordered the domains to do the same.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kosatsu-jawiki - 「After the kosatsu system was abolished, laws were promulgated by posting documents before the gates of courts and prefectural offices, and in 1883 (Meiji 16) the Official Gazette was founded.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • adachi-kosatsu - 「Prohibitions, laws and the like were written in ink on wooden boards and posted to make them known to the common people; the practice reached its height in the Edo period. They were hung at kosatsuba such as bridge ends and before the gates of village officials' houses.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • gmw-tianxia-yamen - 「Whatever imperial edicts all the people needed to know were mostly hung or pasted on the splayed gate walls.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) and 「Every month the people everywhere could receive the latest edict in front of the county office. Take Zhengde 14 (1519) as an example and look at the edicts on the splayed walls」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) and 「Besides edicts, all kinds of notices were also pasted on the splayed brick walls.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) and 「Another kind was the notices or bans issued by the county's own head, such as "Notice to strictly arrest fleeing murderers and escaped criminals", "Ban on harassment by local ruffians"」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) and 「lists of tax arrears being pressed for, rulings on whether a filed complaint was accepted or not, verdicts after civil and criminal suits were concluded, and the pass lists after county-level examinations were all pasted on the splayed walls as well.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
