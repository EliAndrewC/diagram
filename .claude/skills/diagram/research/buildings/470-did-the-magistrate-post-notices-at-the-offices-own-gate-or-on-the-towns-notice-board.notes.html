  • adachi-kosatsu - 「Prohibitions, laws and the like were written in ink on wooden boards and posted to make them known to the common people; the practice reached its height in the Edo period. They were hung at kosatsuba such as bridge ends and before the gates of village officials' houses.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「禁令や法令などを板札に墨書し、庶民に周知するように掲示したもので、江戸時代に全盛を迎えました。橋詰や村役人宅の門前などの高札場に掲げられました。」)
  • gmw-tianxia-yamen - 「Whatever imperial edicts all the people needed to know were mostly hung or pasted on the splayed gate walls.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「凡有需要让天下百姓都知道的皇上“圣谕”，大多挂在或贴在八字门墙上。」) and 「Every month the people everywhere could receive the latest edict in front of the county office. Take Zhengde 14 (1519) as an example and look at the edicts on the splayed walls」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「天下老百姓们每个月都可以在县衙门前领受最新“圣谕”一道。且以正德十四年(1519)为例，看看八字墙上的“圣谕”内容」) and 「Besides edicts, all kinds of notices were also pasted on the splayed brick walls.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「“圣谕”之外，八字砖墙上还贴有各色各样的告示。」) and 「Another kind was the notices or bans issued by the county's own head, such as "Notice to strictly arrest fleeing murderers and escaped criminals", "Ban on harassment by local ruffians"」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「还有一类是本县长官发布的告示或禁令，如《严拿逃凶逸犯示》、《禁止地棍骚扰示》」) and 「lists of tax arrears being pressed for, rulings on whether a filed complaint was accepted or not, verdicts after civil and criminal suits were concluded, and the pass lists after county-level examinations were all pasted on the splayed walls as well.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「举凡催征拖欠赋税名单、具秉控告在案准或不准的批示、审结民事刑事诉讼后的判词，以及县级科举考试后的录取名单等，也都粘贴在八字墙上。」)
