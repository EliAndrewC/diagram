• takayukashiki-jawiki - 「Raised-floor storehouses have high floors to prevent damage by rats and the like (nezumi-gaeshi) and to improve ventilation against damp」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高床倉庫は、ネズミなどによる食害の防止や（ねずみ返し）、湿気対策として風通しをよくするために床を高く造り」) and 「Raised-floor storehouses were built on Amami Oshima, on Hachijojima, and among the Ainu of Hokkaido into modern times.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高床倉庫は奄美大島や八丈島、さらに北海道のアイヌ民族の間で近代まで建造されていた。」)
  • takayama-onkura-heritage - 「The onkura was built as a rice kura in the third bailey of Takayama castle and moved to its present site in 1695 (Genroku 8).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「御蔵は、高山城三ノ丸に米蔵として建てられていたものを、元禄８年（１６９５）に現在地へ移築したものである。」) and 「The onkura of the Takayama intendancy, too, is the third bailey's rice kura, moved when Takayama castle was demolished.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高山陣屋の御蔵も高山城取り壊しの際に三ノ丸の米蔵を移築したものである。」) and 「the slope of the walls called shiho-korobi, the gaps for ventilation, and so on」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「四方転びと呼ばれる壁面の傾斜や通風の隙間など」) and 「one of the country's leading grain dozo」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「全国有数の穀物土蔵である」) (the page says nothing of the floor)
  • dozo-jawiki - 「Kura are divided by contents into rice kura, miso kura, momi (unhulled grain) kura, gunpowder kura, treasure kura, sutra kura and so on, and they protect these from fire, damp, theft and the like」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「蔵は収納物によって、米蔵、味噌蔵、籾蔵、硝煙蔵、金蔵、経蔵などに分けられ、これらを火災、湿気、盗難等から守る機能をもつ」)
  • kotobank-goura - 「The goura belongs within the village settlement and is mostly built on dry-field land; where there is a vacant house lot, it is also built on a residential plot.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「郷蔵は村居の内に附、多分畑地に立る明屋館等あれば、やしきにも立る事也」) (the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, quoting the Jikata hanreiroku of 1794) and 「After the harvest, tax rice was stored in each village's goura and then sent on: in shogunal lands to the shogunate's stores at Edo and Osaka, in domain lands to the domain's stores or to market.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「年貢米は収穫後，各村の郷蔵に納められたのち，幕領では江戸・大坂の幕府蔵へ，藩領では藩庫や市場へ回送された」) (the Yamakawa dictionary of Japanese history)
