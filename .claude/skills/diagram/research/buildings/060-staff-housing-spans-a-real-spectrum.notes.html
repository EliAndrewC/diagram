• takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「On the basis of a drawn plan of 1830 (Tenpo 1), about 2 billion yen was spent and the storehouse-keepers' rowhouse, the gundai's official residence, the inner rooms and other parts were restored to nearly their Edo-period condition.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (no staff count, and no statement that staff other than the storehouse keepers lived there)
  • hatchobori-jawiki - 「However, in 1635 the many temples that stood in Hatchobori were ordered to relocate to Asakusa. Afterwards, it is said, group residences (kumi-yashiki) of the yoriki and doshin under the town magistrate came to be established on the sites where the temples had stood. It is from this that the doshin in period dramas called themselves "Hatchobori".」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • jinya-jawiki - 「one sees a composition in which a residence hall, offices, the retainers' official residences, earthen storehouses, a drill ground and the like are laid out」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (retainers' official residences inside a small domain's ramparts; 「rowhouses」 is this page's word, not the source's)
  • machi-bugyo-jawiki - 「The machi-bugyo's official residence stood inside the magistracy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the residence only; the page does not say who else lived inside)
