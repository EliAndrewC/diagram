• takayama-jinya-jawiki - 「On the basis of a drawn plan of 1830 (Tenpo 1), about 2 billion yen was spent and the storehouse-keepers' rowhouse, the gundai's official residence, the inner rooms and other parts were restored to nearly their Edo-period condition.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「1830年（天保元年）の絵図を基に約20億円を掛けて蔵番長屋、郡代役宅、奥座敷などが、ほぼ江戸時代の状態にまで復元された。」) (no staff count and no statement that the ~30 staff lived there)
  • hatchobori-jawiki - 「However, in 1635 the many temples that stood in Hatchobori were ordered to relocate to Asakusa. Afterwards, it is said, group residences (kumi-yashiki) of the yoriki and doshin under the town magistrate came to be established on the sites where the temples had stood. It is from this that the doshin in period dramas call themselves "Hatchobori".」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「しかし、1635年、八丁堀にあった多くの寺は、浅草への移転を命じられた。その後、寺のあった場所に、町奉行配下の与力、同心の組屋敷が設置されるようになったという。時代劇で同心が自分達を"八丁堀"と称したのはこれに因む。」)
  • jinya-jawiki - 「one sees a composition in which a residence hall, offices, the retainers' official residences, earthen storehouses, a drill ground and the like are laid out」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「居館・役所・家臣の役宅や土蔵、調練場などを配置する構成がみられる」) (retainers' official residences inside a small domain's ramparts; 「rowhouses」 and the ~30 figure are this page's)
