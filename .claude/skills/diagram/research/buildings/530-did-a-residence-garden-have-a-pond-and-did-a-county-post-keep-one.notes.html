• kotobank-teien - 「[1] pond gardens (池泉): the boating, the strolling and the borrowed-scenery kinds; [2] dry landscape: the Zen-temple and the pond-garden kinds; [3] roji: the grass-hut and the shoin kinds. And as the components of a garden there are (1) the pond and water, (2) the stone arrangement, (3) the artificial hills, the slopes and the planting that recreate a natural landscape, and (4) as accessories, stepping stones, paving, stone lanterns, fences and the like.」 (translated; ) and 「The dry landscape is a kind of abstract expression that expresses water without using a single drop of water」 (translated; ) (Nipponica)

  • okutono-jinya-garden - 「The Okutono Jin'ya is the jin'ya of the Okutono domain, a small domain of Mikawa province, placed in the north of Okazaki city, Aichi prefecture.」 (translated; ) and 「This is the garden of the Okutono Jin'ya drawn on an Edo-period drawing, restored from the drawing.」 (translated; ) and 「One of its features is that it is a pond garden divided into two tiers, the pond garden seen from the shoin and the pond garden of the upper ground.」 (translated; ) and 「After the Meiji restoration and the abolition of the domains, the domain office and the jin'ya were all dismantled and the ground cleared (to fields).」 (translated; )
