Guest doors feed courts, not flanks

Sources: kotobank-katteguchi; the GM's rule (2026-07)

Historical arrival was STAGED: gate, then court or garden, then the formal stepped entrance (genkan) - a visitor of rank never stepped from the street into a room or against a building's side.79 Service circulation is the deliberate inverse (the kitchen postern opens into work space).105 On the Hayakawa sheet the guests' river door opens into the guest garden, not the sliver beside the guest house's flank.
