The courtroom is a room of the office hall, not a freestanding stage

Sources: takayama-jinya-jawiki (the 1816 rebuild lists 玄関・吟味所・御役所・大広間 - genkan, ginmisho, goyakusho, ohiroma: entrance hall, examination room, office and great hall - as one block),36 takayama-jinya-city (the 吟味所 ginmisho and 白州 shirasu as one paired feature)37

At Takayama Jin'ya the office wing - reception rooms, day office (goyōba), official study - is the dominant public building,38 and the hearing court is one room of that block.39 A magistracy drawn with only a narrow ceremonial dais-pavilion has nowhere for the magistrate and samurai staff to do the daily paperwork that is most of the job; the residence's private study is the wrong side of the state/home hinge for official business. So the dais band is the FRONT of a deeper office hall (day office + official study behind it).
