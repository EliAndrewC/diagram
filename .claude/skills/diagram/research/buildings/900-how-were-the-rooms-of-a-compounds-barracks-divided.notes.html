  • mitamura-nagaya-tcpip - 「Warrior housing was built with the middle rowhouse as its standard.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「武家の住宅は、中長屋を基準として営造されたものです。」); 「what is called the middle rowhouse is, in the end, no different from nine shaku by two ken」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中長屋と申して、畢竟九尺二間に変りはない。」); 「In the middle rowhouse, one ken at the entrance was an earth floor for taking off shoes, the other ken a raised plank floor」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中長屋は、入口一間が土間の沓ぬぎ、他の一間は床あげ板」); 「The nine-by-two of the middle rowhouse: in the Warring States period there were many mountain castles, and at their feet were soldiers' rest-and-lodging houses, called negoya or 'sleeping huts'.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中長屋の九尺二間、これは戦国時代に山城が多く、その麓に根古屋とも、寝小屋とも申して、兵卒の休泊所がございました。」); 「The Dobora Gunki, writing of the chugen room in Okuwa castle, says that in a rowhouse with a beam of nine shaku and a length of two ken, when a spear with a three-ken shaft was hung up, the two ken at the blade end were inside the house and the butt end stuck outside.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「『堂洞軍記』に大桑城中の中間部屋のことを書いて、梁九尺長さ二間の長屋の中に、三間柄の鑓をかけるのに、身の方の二間は家の内に、石突きの方は外へ出ていると書いてあります。」) (the page quotes 三田村鳶魚全集 第2巻, お大名の話)
  • sakai-hanshiro-bakumatsu - 「Hanshiro came to Edo at the end of the 5th month of Man'en 1 (1860).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「伴四郎が江戸へやってきたのは、万延元年（1860年）5月末のこと。」); 「Where Hanshiro lived in Edo was the duty rowhouse on the north side of the Kishu Wakayama domain's Edo residence at Kinokunizaka, Akasaka.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「伴四郎が江戸で暮らしていたのは、赤坂紀の国坂にある紀州和歌山藩江戸藩邸の北側にあった勤番長屋。」); 「There Hanshiro lived, the three of them, with his uncle Ujita Heizo, who was also his master in the court-dress office, and his colleague Oishi Naosuke.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そこに伴四郎は叔父で衣紋方の師匠でもある宇治田平三と同僚・大石直助の３人で生活していました。」); 「Cooking went by turns, but the uncle, being his superior and master, was excused, and Hanshiro and Naosuke took it in turns.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「炊事は当番制なのですが、上司であり師匠でもある叔父は免除となり、伴四郎と直助が交代で担当しました。」)
  • kotobank-chugen - 「In the Edo period also written 仲間; a kind of warrior-household servant, below the foot soldier and above the menial. ...
