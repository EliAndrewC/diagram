Office in front, residence behind - the two-court split is universal

Sources: neixiang-yamen-zhwiki (office in front, residence behind, as regulation: 「the layout of "office in front, residence behind" fully embodies the ancient system of "court in front, sleeping quarters behind"」, translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「县衙的前衙后邸的建筑格局充分体现了古代的前朝后寝制度」); takayama-jinya-jawiki (the office block rebuilt as one range in 1816), machi-bugyo-jawiki, jinya-jawiki, yamen-enwiki, neixiang-xianya-zhwiki
