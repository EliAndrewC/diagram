Fire discipline: halls burn, kura endure

Sources: sado-bugyosho-fires (the Sado magistracy burned and was rebuilt five times)

Administrative halls were ordinary wooden buildings44 and burned repeatedly (the Sado magistracy burned and was rebuilt five times);43 only thick-walled earthen kura were fireproof,45 which is exactly why tax grain and documents got dedicated storehouse buildings instead of rooms in the hall.
