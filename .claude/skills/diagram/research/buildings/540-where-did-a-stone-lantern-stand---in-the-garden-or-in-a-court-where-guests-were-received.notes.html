• kotobank-ishidoro - 「In structure it consists, from the top, of the jewel, the cap, the fire-box, the middle platform, the post and the base.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「構造は上から宝珠，笠，火袋，中台，竿，基礎の部分から成る。」) (Britannica) and 「There are many kinds according to use, such as the Kasuga, the yukimi, the Enshū and the Oribe.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「用途によって種類が多く、春日かすが・雪見・遠州・織部などがある。」) (Daijisen)
  • kotobank-ishidoro - 「The ancient forms of China and Korea came to Japan together with Buddhism; it was originally used for offering lights to shrines and temples, and only one was stood at the center of the front.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中国，朝鮮の古代の形式が仏教とともに日本に伝わり，本来は社寺への献灯のために用いられ，正面中央に1基だけ立てた。」) and 「From the Momoyama period onward it was also used in tea gardens and ordinary gardens.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桃山時代以降，茶庭や一般庭園にも用いられた。」) (Britannica)
  • kotobank-teien - 「Later, stone lanterns came to be set as lighting for evening tea gatherings.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「のちには石灯籠が夜の茶会の照明として据えられるようになった。」) and 「It was also at this time that artificial hills were made in the roji, which had been close to a flat garden, even streams and ponds were made, and the stone lantern became an important sight.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「平庭に近かった露地に築山をもうけ，流れや池までもつくり，また石灯籠が重要な見どころとなったのもこのころである。」) (Heibonsha's World Encyclopedia)
  • kotobank-teien - 「(4) As accessories, there are stepping stones, paving, stone lanterns, fences and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「(4)付属物としての飛石、敷石、石灯籠(いしどうろう)、垣根などの類がある。」) (Nipponica)
