  • kawashiri-komegura-kunishitei - 「The Kumamoto domain Kawashiri rice storehouse site is the site of rice storehouses that were the collecting place for the Kumamoto domain's tax rice in the Edo period, and it also served as a naval port.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「熊本藩川尻米蔵跡は，江戸時代，熊本藩の年貢米の集積場となった米蔵跡であり，また軍港としても機能した。」) and 「In the Hosokawa period the domain's magistracy office, a lodge (ochaya) and storehouses were set up; normally 200,000 bales of tax rice from five districts in the domain were delivered to the Kawashiri storehouses and carried to the Osaka warehouse-estate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「細川藩時代は藩の奉行所や御茶屋(おちゃや)、御蔵が設置され、藩内の５ヶ郡から通常20万俵の年貢米が川尻の御蔵に納入され、大坂蔵屋敷へ搬送されていた。」) and 「According to drawings and other records, Kawashiri had nine buildings: the tojo-gura, the east kura and the middle kura.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「絵図等の史料によれば、川尻には外城蔵、東蔵、中蔵の９棟があった。」) and 「Also, a landing (about 150 m long) survives facing the Kase river」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「また、加勢川に面して船着場（延長約１５０メートル）が残存しており」) (the conversion to feet is this project's)
  • kuramae-jawiki - 「Its total site area was 36,646 tsubo (though Gofunai biko gives 27,900 tsubo); it was bounded by the Sumida river on the east and by moats on the other three sides, and had 67 storehouse buildings.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その総敷地面積は36646坪（ただし『御府内備考』は27900坪とする）、東を隅田川、他の南北西の三方を堀で囲み、67棟の蔵があった。」) (67 is the count at the ground's largest; the encyclopedias give 54 through most of the period)
