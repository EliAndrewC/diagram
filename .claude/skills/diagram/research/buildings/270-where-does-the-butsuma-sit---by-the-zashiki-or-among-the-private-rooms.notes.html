• butsuma-kotobank - 「A room in which Buddha images and ihai are enshrined.」 (translated; ) (Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten)
  • butsuma-kotobank - 「In the butsuma of farmhouses, there are many cases where the place for the butsudan is built projecting outside the room. Sometimes, too, a place for the butsudan is made beside the tokonoma of the zashiki and serves as the butsuma. Especially in regions of strong faith, above all regions where the Shin sect is strong, houses are often seen with a butsuma of about six mats, for placing a fine butsudan, set behind the oku-no-ma, the zashiki or the like.」 (translated; ) (Nipponica, Hirai Kiyoshi)
  • butsuma-kotobank - 「In the imperial palace the butsuma was called the kurodo, and it was the rule for it to be built as a separate building beside the tsune-goten.」 (translated; ) (Nipponica, Hirai Kiyoshi; the tsune-goten is the emperor's everyday residence hall)
  • touken-world-buke-madori - 「Entering from the entrance, the arrangement is, from the right, the "oku-no-ma", the "hikae-no-ma" for guests, the "audience room" (謁見の間, ekken-no-ma) for meeting superiors, and beyond it the "butsuma".」 (translated; ) and 「The audience room is one moved here from a house that was a shipowner of kitamaebune (the northern-route coasting ships active in Japan Sea shipping) in the Daishoji domain (today Kaga city, Ishikawa prefecture), a branch domain of the Kaga domain.」 (translated; ) and 「The residence of the Nomura family, upper samurai with a stipend of 1,200 koku, is a samurai residence with a beautiful garden on a site of about 1,000 tsubo.」 (translated; ) and 「With the dismantling of the samurai system, the residence remains preserved today are smaller than they were then, but one can experience the room layout of samurai life.」 (translated; ) and 「At the Nagamachi samurai residence site, known as a popular sightseeing spot in Kanazawa, Ishikawa prefecture, the Nomura house survives, open to the public as a samurai residence still standing today.」 (translated; ) (the Nomura house at Kanazawa)
