• butsuma-kotobank - 「A room in which Buddha images and ihai are enshrined.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「仏像や位牌を安置してある部屋。」) (Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten)
  • butsuma-kotobank - 「In the butsuma of farmhouses, there are many cases where the place for the butsudan is built projecting outside the room. Sometimes, too, a place for the butsudan is made beside the tokonoma of the zashiki and serves as the butsuma. Especially in regions of strong faith, above all regions where the Shin sect is strong, houses are often seen with a butsuma of about six mats, for placing a fine butsudan, set behind the oku-no-ma, the zashiki or the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「民家の仏間では、仏壇を置く場所を部屋の外に張り出して建てる例が多い。また、座敷の床の間のわきに仏壇を置く場所を設けて、仏間としていることもある。とくに信仰心の厚い地域、なかでも真宗の強い地域では、奥の間や座敷などの奥に、りっぱな仏壇を置くための六畳程度の仏間を設けている家がしばしばみられる。」) (Nipponica, Hirai Kiyoshi)
  • butsuma-kotobank - 「In the imperial palace the butsuma was called the kurodo, and it was the rule for it to be built as a separate building beside the tsune-goten.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「内裏(だいり)では仏間を黒戸(くろど)といい、常御殿(つねのごてん)のそばに独立した建物として建てられるのがつねであった。」) (Nipponica, Hirai Kiyoshi; the tsune-goten is the emperor's everyday residence hall)
  • touken-world-buke-madori - 「Entering from the entrance, the arrangement is, from the right, the "oku-no-ma", the "hikae-no-ma" for guests, the "audience room" (謁見の間, ekken-no-ma) for meeting superiors, and beyond it the "butsuma".」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「玄関から入ると、右から「奥の間」、来客用の「控えの間」、目上の人と対面する「謁見の間」（えっけんのま）、その奥に「仏間」という配置です。」) and 「The audience room is one moved here from a house that was a shipowner of kitamaebune (the northern-route coasting ships active in Japan Sea shipping) in the Daishoji domain (today Kaga city, Ishikawa prefecture), a branch domain of the Kaga domain.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「謁見の間は、加賀藩の支藩であった大聖寺藩（だいしょうじはん：現在の石川県加賀市）で北前船（きたまえぶね：日本海海運で活躍していた北国廻船）の船主をしていた家から移築された物。」) and 「The residence of the Nomura family, upper samurai with a stipend of 1,200 koku, is a samurai residence with a beautiful garden on a site of about 1,000 tsubo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「石高1,200石の上級武士であった野村家の邸宅は、約1,000坪の敷地面積を有する庭園が美しい武家屋敷です。」) and 「With the dismantling of the samurai system, the residence remains preserved today are smaller than they were then, but one can experience the room layout of samurai life.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「武家制度の解体によって、現在残されている屋敷跡は当時よりも小規模な物ですが、武家の暮らしの間取りを体感することができます。」) and 「At the Nagamachi samurai residence site, known as a popular sightseeing spot in Kanazawa, Ishikawa prefecture, the Nomura house survives, open to the public as a samurai residence still standing today.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「石川県金沢市で人気の観光地として知られる「長町武家屋敷跡」（ながまちぶけやしあと）には、現在も残る武家屋敷として一般公開されている「野村家」が現存。」) (the Nomura house at Kanazawa)
