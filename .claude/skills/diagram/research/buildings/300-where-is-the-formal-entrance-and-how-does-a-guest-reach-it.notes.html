• genkan-jawiki - 「In Japan it refers to the entrance of the abbot's quarters of a Zen temple, or the room or building set at a residence's formal entrance in shoin-zukuri; and in the Edo period an entrance with a shikidai was called a 'genkan'.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • bukeyashiki-wiki - 「samurai houses of the senior-retainer class had earthen walls, a nagaya-mon and a shikidai」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shirobito-1717-takayama - 「Entering by the genkan, the seigaiha of the genkan-no-ma catches the eye.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The route goes round the rooms of the goyakusho, the government office, and continues on to the residence of the intendants and district intendants.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「It is valuable as the only jin'ya in the country whose principal parts survive.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shiroishi-koseki - 「Crossing an earthen bridge and passing through a simple gabled munamon gate, one turns along the earthen wall in front and reaches the front entrance (the kitchen entrance). The earthen wall that encloses the roji in an L has simple loopholes on its front. There is no genkan with a shikidai, but from a middle gate in the wall one passes through the roji directly to the veranda of the principal zashiki.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「This site is the residence of a middle-rank retainer」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
