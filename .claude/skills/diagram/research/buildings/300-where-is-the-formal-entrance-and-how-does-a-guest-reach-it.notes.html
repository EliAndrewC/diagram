• genkan-jawiki - 「In Japan it refers to the entrance of the abbot's quarters of a Zen temple, or the room or building set at a residence's formal entrance in shoin-zukuri; and in the Edo period an entrance with a shikidai was called a 'genkan'.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本では禅寺の方丈に設けられる入口[1] や書院造で邸宅への正式の出入口に設けられる部屋や建物[1] を指し、また、江戸時代には式台を構えている出入口を「玄関」と称した[2]。」)
  • bukeyashiki-wiki - 「samurai houses of the senior-retainer class had earthen walls, a nagaya-mon and a shikidai」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「重臣クラスの侍屋敷では土塀や長屋門、式台を構え」)
  • shirobito-1717-takayama - 「Entering by the genkan, the seigaiha of the genkan-no-ma catches the eye.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「玄関から入ると玄関之間の青海波が目を引きます。」) and 「The route goes round the rooms of the goyakusho, the government office, and continues on to the residence of the intendants and district intendants.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「順路は政庁である御役所の各部屋をめぐって、代官・郡代らの役宅へと続いています。」) and 「It is valuable as the only jin'ya in the country whose principal parts survive.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「全国で唯一、主要部分が現存する陣屋として貴重な存在です。」)
  • shiroishi-koseki - 「Crossing an earthen bridge and passing through a simple gabled munamon gate, one turns along the earthen wall in front and reaches the front entrance (the kitchen entrance). The earthen wall that encloses the roji in an L has simple loopholes on its front. There is no genkan with a shikidai, but from a middle gate in the wall one passes through the roji directly to the veranda of the principal zashiki.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「土橋を渡り、簡素な切妻造の棟門をくぐり、正面の土塀を曲折して表出入口（台所口）に通ずる。L型に露地を囲んだ土塀には正面に簡素な狭間が付く。式台付き玄関はないが、塀中門から露地を経て直接に正座敷の縁側に通ずる。」) and 「This site is the residence of a middle-rank retainer」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「この地は中級家中の屋敷であり」)
