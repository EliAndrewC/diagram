• agariya-jawiki - 「the cells on the east and west were arranged, going outward, in the order kuchi-agariya, oku-agariya, the great cell (oro), the two-ken cell (nikenro)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「東西の牢は外側に向かって口揚屋・奥揚屋・大牢・二間牢の順に配列されており」; the paragraph is about 「江戸の牢屋敷」, Edo's jail compound), 「The kuchi-agariya was three rooms, each of a size of 2.5 ken (the equivalent of 15 mats), and the oku-agariya three rooms, each of a size of 3 ken (the equivalent of 18 mats); each had a privy of about half a ken.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「口揚屋は1部屋あたり広さ2間半の部屋が3部屋（15畳相当）、奥揚屋は1部屋あたり広さ3間の部屋が3部屋（18畳相当）、いずれも半間ほどの大きさの雪隠が設けられていた。」) and 「At Osaka's Matsuyamachi jail too, a "men's agariya" (6 mats) was set up in Genbun 4 (1739).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「なお、大坂の松屋町牢屋敷にも元文4年（1739年）に「男揚屋」（6畳）が設置されている。」) (the page gives mats, not the sides; the feet are this project's, at about 18 square feet a mat)
  • roya-kotobank - 「overcrowded confinement in which over a hundred were packed into the 30-mat great cell」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「30畳敷の大牢に100人以上も詰め込まれる過剰拘禁」) and 「the jail in the narrow sense, which held commoners, that is, the great cell」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「庶民を収容する狭義の牢，すなわち大牢」) (Heibonsha's World Encyclopedia, 世界大百科事典, Hayashi Yukiko)
  • roya-kotobank - 「besides these, a temporary jail to hold prisoners called to the court was set up inside the magistracy」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このほかに，法廷に呼出された囚を入れる仮牢が，奉行所内に設けられていた。」) (Britannica's concise encyclopedia)
  • edo-ashigaru-bugyosho - 「In this area were concentrated the functions concerned with lawsuits and examinations: the saikyosho and the shirasu, the inner sengisho and the sengisho, the duty rooms of the shacho-sen'yokata and the reikurikata, the litigants' waiting room and benches, the jail doshin's duty room and the temporary jail, and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このエリアには、裁許所と白洲、内詮議所、詮議所、赦帳撰要方や例繰方の詰所、公事人溜りや腰掛、牢屋同心の詰所や仮牢など、公事吟味に関する機能が集中していました。」)
  • jinya-kotobank - 「Generally, in a daikan jin'ya there were set within the grounds the honjin, the intendant's official residence, the office for public business, the subordinates' official houses, the rice kura and levy kura, the shirasu, the jail and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一般に代官陣屋では敷地内に代官の官舎である本陣，公用執務の役所，属僚の役宅，米蔵・運上蔵や白洲・牢屋などが設けられていた。」) (the Mypedia encyclopedia, マイペディア)
  • takayama-jinya-official - 「However, the Takayama Jin'ya's instruments of torture are thought to have been placed there to overawe people, and in fact [the torture] was reportedly carried out inside the jail [near present-day Kami-ichinomachi and Ebisaka].」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ただし高山陣屋の拷問道具は、人々を威圧するために置かれていたと考えられており、実際は牢屋内［現在の上一之町・海老坂付近］で行われていたとのことです。」) (the jail's site is given by the town's present street names)
  • jishinban-jawiki - 「The rule was 9 shaku by 2 ken, but in practice it was about 2 to 3 ken, with an earth floor and at the back a room laid with tatami.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「9尺2間というきまりだが、実際には2-3間ぐらいはあり、土間と奥に畳を敷いた部屋がある。」) and 「For that purpose an iron ring was fixed to a post of the board-floored room at the back of the watch house, so that the end of a captive's rope could be tied to it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そのために、番屋内部の奥の板の間の柱には鉄の環がうちつけてあり、捕らえた者の捕縄尻をそこに結わえつけられるようにしてあった」)
