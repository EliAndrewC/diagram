• tedai-jawiki - 「Tedai were chosen and appointed from among peasants, townsmen and the like who were well versed in jikata (rural administration). Formal permission from the Edo shogunate's finance office (kanjosho) was required.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「手代は、地方（じかた）に精通した百姓・町人などから選ばれて採用された。江戸幕府の勘定所の正式な許可が必要であった。」)
  • tedai-jawiki - 「Most tedai were the children of village officials and town officials. They were first hired as kakiyaku (scribes), then promoted to tedai and then to motojime tedai (chief tedai).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「手代の多くは、村役人・町役人の子弟である。まず、書役（かきやく）として採用され、手代、そして元締手代へと昇進する。」)
  • neixiang-yamen - 「Going further north one enters the second rectangular courtyard, where on the east and west sides are separately set the Six Chambers, that is, personnel, revenue, rites, war, justice and works, corresponding to the court's Six Ministries; these are the county yamen's functional working organs. By the authorized establishment, one chamber was staffed with two or three men; in Ming and Qing times the establishment was frequently exceeded, the numbers even reaching more than a hundred.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「再往北进入第二个长方形院落，东西两侧分设六房即吏、户、礼、兵、刑、工，与朝廷六部对应，是县衙职能办事机构。按编制一房设二三人，明清时常常超编，人数甚至百人以上。」)

  • xuli-zhwiki - 「The clerks of the silver treasury are for the most part all of the surname Shi. The tax clerks of the Chongwen Gate are for the most part all of the surnames Zhang, Wang and the like, entrenched there and holding it in their grip.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「銀庫書吏，大半皆系史姓。崇文門稅務書吏，大半皆系張、王等姓，盤踞把持」) (entrenched hereditary clerks, and on the same page their enrichment out of the revenue - 「Officials of the capital court were often so poor they could not support themselves, while the clerks piled up fortunes」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「京朝官多貧至不能自存，而吏人多積貲巨億」); the rotation of magistrates over them and the Japanese tedai half are this page's)
