• tedai-jawiki - 「Tedai were chosen and appointed from among peasants, townsmen and the like who were well versed in jikata (rural administration). Formal permission from the Edo shogunate's finance office (kanjosho) was required.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • tedai-jawiki - 「Most tedai were the children of village officials and town officials. They were first hired as kakiyaku (scribes), then promoted to tedai and then to motojime tedai (chief tedai).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • neixiang-yamen - 「Going further north one enters the second rectangular courtyard, where on the east and west sides are separately set the Six Chambers, that is, personnel, revenue, rites, war, justice and works, corresponding to the court's Six Ministries; these are the county yamen's functional working organs. By the authorized establishment, one chamber was staffed with two or three men; in Ming and Qing times the establishment was frequently exceeded, the numbers even reaching more than a hundred.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
