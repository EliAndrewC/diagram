• kotobank-bansho - 「Each domain also set up kuchidome guard posts at the key points leading to neighboring domains or the shogun's lands, chiefly watching that the domain's products did not flow out to other territories, and sometimes taxing the goods going in and out at a fixed rate.」 (translated; ) and 「The domains too set kuchidome guard posts at the key points of the roads leading to other territories, and controlled the going in and out of people and goods.」 (translated; )
  • bansho-jawiki - 「As a guard post whose buildings of the time survive it is rare in the whole country, but the site of the Nuruyu guard post at Hanayama village, Sendai domain, is one example.」 (translated; )
  • bunka-nuruyu-bansho - 「One of the kuchidome guard posts of Sendai domain.」 (translated; ) and 「a strategic point in a mountain gorge commanding the way out to Akita domain's territory」 (translated; ) and 「The front gate is built blocking the road, the inspection hall is set facing it, and the residence is to the right rear of the inspection hall as one faces it.」 (translated; ) and 「The road now runs outside the site of the guard post, but formerly it entered at the front gate and passed in front of the inspection hall.」 (translated; ) and 「The residence is 23.19 meters along the ridge and 11.86 meters across, and takes the form of an ordinary farmhouse with the earth floor on the right and the living rooms on the left as one faces it, but it is an imposing building furnished with a shoin-style zashiki.」 (translated; ) (the Agency for Cultural Affairs' designation record)
  • hakone-seki-jawiki - 「The buildings were the "front post", made up of the upper guard post, the guards' duty room, the rest room and the bath; the "opposite post", made up of the half-shift duty room, the rest room and the jail; stables, a street watch, a notice-board place and so on, and they were enclosed by a fence.」 (translated; ) and 「Generally, it refers to the Hakone checkpoint of the Tōkaidō, set up by the Edo shogunate on the shore of Lake Ashi at Hakone, Ashigarashimo district, Sagami province, from Genna 5 (1619) to Meiji 2 (1869).」 (translated; )
