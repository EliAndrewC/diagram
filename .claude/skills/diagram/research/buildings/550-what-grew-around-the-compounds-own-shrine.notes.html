• kotobank-yashikigami - 「A god enshrined in a corner of the residence lot, that is, inside the house plot, or in a small plot joined to it, by building a small shrine or by taking an old tree, a stone or the like as its yorishiro.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷すなわち宅地内の一隅やこれに接続した小区画に，小祠を構えあるいは古木や石などを依代（よりしろ）にするなどしてまつられている神をいう。」) (Heibonsha's World Encyclopedia)
  • jawiki-yashikigami - 「It is often enshrined in a corner of the lot the house stands on, and in that case it is often enshrined at the northwest or the northeast of the house.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷がある敷地の一角に祀られることが多く、その場合は屋敷の北西・北東に祀られることが多い。」) and 「For that reason the place of worship set up in the woods is thought at first to have been no shrine, but to have worshipped an old tree or a natural stone as the yorishiro only at the time of the rite.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そのため、山林に設けられた祭場は当初は祠などではなく、祭祀のときのみ古木や自然石を依代として祀ったものだったと考えられる。」) and 「It is thought that what had been worshipped in the woods near the house gradually drew closer to the house's buildings, becoming the form widely seen today, of building a shrine inside the lot and worshipping there.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷近くの山林に祀られていたのが、次第に屋敷の建物に近づいていって、現在広く見られるような敷地内に社を建てて祀るという形態になったと思われている。」)
  • boso-no-mura-takei - 「It is reproduced on the model of the Takei house, a middle-rank samurai's, in Miyakōji, Sakura city.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「佐倉市宮小路にある中級武士の武居家をモデルに再現しています。」) and 「Besides the main house there are an Inari, an udegi gate, a vegetable garden and so on, and the whole is enclosed by an earth bank and hedges.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「主屋の他には，稲荷，腕木門（うでぎもん）・菜園などがあり，全体が土塁と生け垣で囲まれています。」)
