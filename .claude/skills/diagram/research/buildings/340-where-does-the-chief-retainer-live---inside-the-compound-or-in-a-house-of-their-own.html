Where does the chief retainer live - inside the compound, or in a house of their own?

Sources: l7r-budgets (the magistrate's karo, in the GM's setting), bukeyashiki-wiki (where a daimyo's karo lived), aizu-saigo-karo (the size of one karo house's mansion), jinya-kotobank (what an intendancy compound held), daikan-tetsuki-jawiki (the head of an intendancy's staff, and what the staff lived in), mapple-takayama-jinya (the district intendant living inside the Takayama compound)

In the setting, a county magistrate's samurai include the magistrate's karo, of Rank 4.

History gives two places for a lord's senior retainer to live, at two scales. At a daimyo's, karo and the other senior retainers often lived near the lord's residence or inside the castle, in mansions of their own: the Saigo, karo to the Matsudaira of Aizu for generations with 1,700 koku, had a site of 2,400 tsubo (about two acres) with 280 tsubo of building and 38 rooms. At a shogunal intendancy, the office of the kind our postings follow, the staff lived inside the compound: it held the intendant's residence, the office, the rice and levy stores, the small houses (koya) and long-houses (nagaya) of the tetsuki and tedai, and the gaol, usually within high white walls, thickets, ditches or dry moats. The head of the tedai was called the motojime, but where the motojime lived, as against the staff in general, is not said. At Takayama the district intendant lived with the family in the residence inside the compound. A chief retainer's house standing inside the lord's own compound was searched for and not found.

What this means for a plan: a posting's karo, one of the fifteen or so samurai of a county town, lives at the intendancy's scale, in a small house or a long-house inside the compound as the intendancy's staff did; a mansion of their own outside the compound is the daimyo's karo's form and belongs to a castle town. That the karo's quarters are a small house of their own rather than a bay of the staff long-house is a GUESS, since no source sets the head of the staff apart from the rest.
