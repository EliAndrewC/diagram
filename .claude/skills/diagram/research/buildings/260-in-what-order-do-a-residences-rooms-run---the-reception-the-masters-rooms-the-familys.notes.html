  • shirobito-612-omote-oku - 「The living room where the castle lord did his work by day, the bedroom and so on were located behind the omote palace, and were called the "naka-oku".」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「Incidentally, the word naka-oku is not, strictly, a historical term, and in the drawings of the Edo period and so on the shogun's office and bedroom are included in the omote palace.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shirobito-612-omote-oku - 「The "oku" held, besides the castle lord's private rooms, the residences of his principal wife and his consorts.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「Because women of high status lived there, the coming and going of people was strictly limited」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • edojo-kotobank - 「The honmaru palace was divided by its use into three: the omote, the naka-oku and the ooku (the great oku).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, Murai Masuo)
  • edojo-kotobank - 「The ooku (the great oku), where the shogun's children and the women in service lived around the midaidokoro, was the shogun's private residence; it was the building on the north side of the naka-oku, but it was partitioned off by a strict wall and joined only by the Osuzu corridor, and no man but the shogun could enter the ooku.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, Murai Masuo)
  • shoinzukuri-kotobank - 「At the start of the early modern period, in the Keicho and Genna eras (1596-1624), the residences of warrior daimyo were built in many places.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The great complexes of halls were broadly divided into the omote-muki and the oku-muki, and the main buildings - the hiroma, taimenjo and goza-no-ma of the omote-muki, and the okami (the lady's palace) of the oku-muki - were alike in the developed shoin-zukuri form.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, Kawakami Mitsugu)
  • touken-world-buke-madori - 「First, from the "genkan-no-ma" there is the "hikae-no-ma", then come the "ima" and the "ni-no-ma", then the "zashiki" and the "oku-zashiki"; beside the ima is the kitchen, projecting to the east.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「The Kuchiba family was of yorigumi standing (yorigumi: a corps formed by grouping the body of retainers), an upper samurai house of about 1,000 koku.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「In the castle town of Hagi in Yamaguchi Prefecture, inscribed as a World Heritage site in 2015 (Heisei 27) as a component of the "Sites of Japan's Meiji Industrial Revolution", there are samurai residences of the Edo period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「Among them, the "Kuchiba family residence" in the Horiuchi district, which was the residential quarter of upper samurai, is a samurai residence precious even nationally, its main house and front gate surviving.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • touken-world-buke-madori - 「Looking at the plan of the main house, it is a six-room plan divided front and back, in the hojo form, a form seen of old in Zen temples.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) The page lists the rooms in this order but does not say in so many words which lies deepest; the zashiki as the deepest room is this project's reading of that order.
  • matsue-bukeyashiki - 「Master's living room: in contrast to the front of the main house, it is playfully built, with nageshi of menkawa (timber keeping the log's rounded corners) and nail covers in the shape of a bird (fukura-suzume).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「Zashiki: the room where the master receives guests, built with an eye to formality, with a shoin, straight-grained nageshi and nail covers bearing the nine-star crest.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and, among its other rooms, 「family room, wife's living room, tea room, butsuma」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
