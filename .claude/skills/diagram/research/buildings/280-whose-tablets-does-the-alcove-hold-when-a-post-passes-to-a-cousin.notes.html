• sosen-saishi-kotobank - 「As to which ancestors' tablets it venerates, Japanese ihai veneration can be sorted into two types. One is the type that in principle enshrines the successive household heads of the house and their spouses; in ihai veneration of this type the patrilineal, single-line house line is stressed, and it coincides with the house line in the inheritance of property.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本の位牌祭祀はどのような祖先の位牌を祭祀しているかについて二つの類型を設定することができる。一つは原則として家代々の家長とその配偶者をまつる型であって，この型の位牌祭祀においては父系単系的な家筋が強調され，財産相続における家筋と一致する。」) (Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, old edition, from its article on the house line; the excerpt breaks off before the second type)
