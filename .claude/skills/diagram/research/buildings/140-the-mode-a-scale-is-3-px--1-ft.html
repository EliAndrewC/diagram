Sources: takayama-jinya-city (the designated site's extent, 11,219.05 m2 - ~9,800 m2 without the front plaza65); the Jōge ~1,900 m2 figure has no readable source (see "Every administrative compound keeps a shrine")

WHY this number (GM decision, 2026-07): the real anchors bracket a county magistracy neatly - the excavated 3-man Jōge branch office was ~230×88 ft, about 1,900 m280 and Takayama Jin'ya's site ~9,800 m2 without its front plaza (11,219 m2 with it), so a 15-samurai county seat belongs between them; at the compound scale both pool manors land there (Hayakawa 247×200 ft ≈ 4,600 m2, Ochiba 267×200 ft ≈ 4,950 m2) and the feature sizes come out real (office hall 113×28 ft, hearing court 113×38 ft, cell 23×17 ft, walls 3 ft thick).104 Drawn a third smaller, the same plan would claim Takayama's gross area for a mere county post; a third larger, it pinches below what the drawn program occupies. The scale also extends the settlement ladder cleanly: a compound sheet is drawn at three times the detail of a hamlet or town map, a village at half that detail and a city at a third.
