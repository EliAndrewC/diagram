• kaogongji-wikisource - 「The cut of one ou, 1 chi wide and 1 chi deep, is called a quan. At the head of the field it is doubled: 2 chi wide and 2 chi deep is called a sui. Nine fu make a jing; between jing, 4 chi wide and 4 chi deep is called a gou. Ten li square makes a cheng; between cheng, 8 chi wide and 8 chi deep is called a xu. A hundred li square makes a tong; between tong, 2 xun wide and 2 ren deep is called a hui. These lead directly into the rivers, and each bears its own name.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) (an ou is two spades working together; a fu is one farmer's allotment; a xun is 8 chi, the span of outstretched arms)
