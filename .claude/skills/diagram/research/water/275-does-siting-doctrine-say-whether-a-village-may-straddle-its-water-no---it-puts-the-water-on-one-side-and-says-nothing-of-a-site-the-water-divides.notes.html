• jawiki-shijin-soo - 「One wants flowing water on the left; this is called the Azure Dragon. On the right there is a long road; this is called the White Tiger. In front there is a pond; this is called the Vermilion Bird. Behind there are hills; this is called the Black Tortoise.」 (translated from the page's Japanese kundoku reading by this project; original: 「左に流水有るを欲す。これを青龍と謂う。右に長道有り。これを白虎と謂う。前に洿池有り。これを朱雀と謂う。後ろに丘陵有り。これを玄武と謂う。」)
  • hu-shangantang - 「On the left it adjoins Qinglong [Azure Dragon] General Mountain, on the right it leans on Baihu [White Tiger] Ang Mountain, behind it rests on Cuiping Mountain, and in front it faces the Xiemu River - mountains on three sides, water on one side.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「左挨青龙将军山，右依白虎昂山，后靠翠屏山，前临谢沐河，三面环山，一面临水」)
  • hu-shaanxi-villages - 「There is one small stream on each of the east and west sides, flowing around and past the village, forming the "dragon-chair" fengshui configuration of "twin streams embracing".」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「东西各有一条小溪绕村而过,形成“双溪环抱”的“龙椅形”风水格局」)
  • jawiki-senjo-shuraku - 「In most cases it develops along a transport route such as a road, a river or a canal.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「多くの場合は、道路、河川、運河といった交通路に沿って発達している」) and 「In most cases, buildings are lined up on both sides of a single street.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「多くの場合は、1本の街路を挟んで両側に建物が並ぶ」)
  • ndl-yajima-shuraku - 「Houses ranged along a comparatively narrow street, on one side of it or on both, with little dependence on the street, formed chiefly as an agricultural settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「比較的狭い街路に沿って民家が、その片側または両側に並列し、街路への依存度が少なく、主として農業集落として形成されているもの」; the National Diet Library's reference database quoting 矢嶋仁吉『日本の集落』)
