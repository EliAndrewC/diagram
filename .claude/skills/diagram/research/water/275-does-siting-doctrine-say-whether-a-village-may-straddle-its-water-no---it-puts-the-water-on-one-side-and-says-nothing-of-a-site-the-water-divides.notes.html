• jawiki-shijin-soo - 「One wants flowing water on the left; this is called the Azure Dragon. On the right there is a long road; this is called the White Tiger. In front there is a pond; this is called the Vermilion Bird. Behind there are hills; this is called the Black Tortoise.」 (translated from the page's Japanese kundoku reading by this project; )
  • hu-shangantang - 「On the left it adjoins Qinglong [Azure Dragon] General Mountain, on the right it leans on Baihu [White Tiger] Ang Mountain, behind it rests on Cuiping Mountain, and in front it faces the Xiemu River - mountains on three sides, water on one side.」 (translated; )
  • hu-shaanxi-villages - 「There is one small stream on each of the east and west sides, flowing around and past the village, forming the "dragon-chair" fengshui configuration of "twin streams embracing".」 (translated; )
  • jawiki-senjo-shuraku - 「In most cases it develops along a transport route such as a road, a river or a canal.」 (translated; ) and 「In most cases, buildings are lined up on both sides of a single street.」 (translated; )
  • ndl-yajima-shuraku - 「Houses ranged along a comparatively narrow street, on one side of it or on both, with little dependence on the street, formed chiefly as an agricultural settlement.」 (translated; ; the National Diet Library's reference database quoting 矢嶋仁吉『日本の集落』)
