• harie-syozu - 「The Harie settlement lies on the left bank of the alluvial fan at the lower reaches of the Ado River, the largest river in the Kosei region of Shiga Prefecture; the settlement area, including fields and the residential zone, is about 150 ha, with somewhat over 170 households.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「針江集落は、滋賀県湖西地域で最大の安曇川下流の扇状地左岸に位置し、集落面積は田畑、居住区を含め約150ha、戸数170戸余り」)
  • imazato-1999 - 「It is a loose-cluster village sited on the river terrace of the Higashimata River, a tributary of the Togawa; as of 1998 it is a hamlet of 124 households and 408 inhabitants.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「砥川の支流，東俣川の河岸段丘に立地する疎塊村で，1998年現在，世帯数124・人口408人の部落である。」; p. 436) and 「The mountain on the north side of the settlement is called Urayama ('the back mountain'); it was privately owned and used as a source of timber and firewood. ... The mountain on the south side, across the Higashimata River, is called Mukoyama ('the facing mountain').」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「集落の北側の山をウラヤマ（裏山）といい，個人所有の山で用材や薪の供給源として利用された。…また，東俣川をはさんで南側の山はムコウヤマ（向山）と呼ばれ」; p. 439)
  • hirasawa-murazakai - 「The village boundary on the Tenryu River came, at some point, to be governed by the custom that it lay wherever the river's main current was flowing. This one boundary was a moving boundary.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「天竜川の村境は、いつの間にか川の本瀬が流れているところという仕来りができてしまいました。ここばかりは移動する村境でした。」; printed p. 20. The booklet's second half is a documented suit between Shimada village and its neighbors over the river's islands, heard in 1739, on printed pp. 22-25) and 「Shimada village was Iida domain land and the east bank was Takasu domain land, so there was nothing for it but to take the suit to Edo.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「島田村は飯田領分、川東は高須領分でしたので、江戸へ訴え出るより外ありませんでした。」; printed p. 23)
  • harie-syozu - 「The Harie Okawa flows through roughly the center of the district.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「地区のほぼ中央に針江大川が流れています。」)
  • jawiki-harie - 「The hate-ike is connected to the waterways within the settlement; water overflowing from the hate-ike goes out into the waterways and flows into the Harie Okawa, which runs through the center of the settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「端池は集落内の水路と繋がっており、端池から溢れた水は水路に出て集落の中央を流れる針江大川（はりえおおかわ）へと流れ込む。」)
  • mizu-no-bunka-60 - 「Some [kabata] mix with the channel that flows alongside the house, while in others only the overflow water goes out into the channel.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「家に沿って流れる水路と交じるものもあれば、あふれた水だけが水路に出ていくものもある。」)
  • jawiki-harie - 「The spring water (Harie's shozu) is among the Environment Ministry's selection of the 'Hundred Best Waters of the Heisei Era', and together with the adjoining Shimofuri district it has been selected as a national Important Cultural Landscape.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「湧水（針江の生水）は環境省選定の「平成の名水百選」であり、隣接する霜降（しもふり）地区とともに国の重要文化的景観に選定されている。」)
  • jawiki-shijin-soo - 「One wants flowing water on the left; this is called the Azure Dragon. On the right there is a long road; this is called the White Tiger. In front there is a pond; this is called the Vermilion Bird. Behind there are hills; this is called the Black Tortoise.」 (translated from the page's Japanese kundoku reading by this project; original: 「左に流水有るを欲す。これを青龍と謂う。右に長道有り。これを白虎と謂う。前に洿池有り。これを朱雀と謂う。後ろに丘陵有り。これを玄武と謂う。」)
  • hu-shangantang - 「On the left it adjoins Qinglong General Mountain, on the right it leans on Baihu Ang Mountain, behind it rests on Cuiping Mountain, and in front it faces the Xiemu River - mountains on three sides, water on one side.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「左挨青龙将军山，右依白虎昂山，后靠翠屏山，前临谢沐河，三面环山，一面临水」)
  • hu-shaanxi-villages - 「There is one small stream on each of the east and west sides, flowing around and past the village, forming the “dragon-chair” fengshui configuration of “twin streams embracing”.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「东西各有一条小溪绕村而过，形成“双溪环抱”的“龙椅形”风水格局」)
  • jawiki-senjo-shuraku - 「In most cases it develops along a transport route such as a road, a river or a canal.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「多くの場合は、道路、河川、運河といった交通路に沿って発達している」) and 「In most cases, buildings are lined up on both sides of a single street.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「多くの場合は、1本の街路を挟んで両側に建物が並ぶ」)
  • ndl-yajima-shuraku - 「Houses ranged along a comparatively narrow street, on one side of it or on both, with little dependence on the street, formed chiefly as an agricultural settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「比較的狭い街路に沿って民家が、その片側または両側に並列し、街路への依存度が少なく、主として農業集落として形成されているもの」; the National Diet Library's reference database quoting 矢嶋仁吉『日本の集落』)
