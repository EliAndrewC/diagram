• songshi-273-wikisource - 「If, west of Shun'an fort, the Pukou of the Yi River were opened and the water led east to pour into the sea - three hundred and more li from east to west, fifty to seventy li from north to south - then by drawing on its meres and marshes, and building dikes to store water for military farm-colonies, one could thereby check the sudden onrush of the enemy's cavalry.」 (translated from the Classical Chinese by this project; original: 「若於順安砦西開易河蒲口，導水東注於海，東西三百餘里，南北五七十里，資其陂澤，築堤貯水為屯田，可以遏敵騎之奔軼。」) (He Chengju's memorial proposing the belt: the passage grants the design and its anti-cavalry intent; that the belt was then built rests on the page's own narrative, not on this passage)
  • bitchu-takamatsu-jawiki - 「The surroundings of the castle were a belt of low wetland, and these formed a natural moat.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「城の周囲は低湿地帯でこれらが天然の堀を形成していた。」) ; 「In Tensho 10 (1582), Hashiba Hideyoshi, a retainer of Oda Nobunaga, was entrusted with the vanguard of the Chugoku campaign, and in the fourth month he set about the attack on Takamatsu Castle (the battle of Bitchu Takamatsu Castle).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「天正10年（1582年）、織田信長の家臣・羽柴秀吉は中国攻めの先鋒を任され、4月、高松城攻め（備中高松城の戦い）にかかった。」) ; 「With the coming of the 8th day of the fifth month (29 May), civil engineering works to surround the castle with an embankment dam were begun on the proposal of Kuroda Yoshitaka. The intent was thereby to submerge Takamatsu Castle, which stood on low wetland. This is what the world calls the "water attack on Takamatsu Castle".」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「5月8日（5月29日）に入り黒田孝高の献策により城を堰堤で囲む土木工事が開始された。これにより低湿地にあった高松城を水没させるというものである。世に言う『高松城水攻め』である。」) (The castle's surroundings were low wetland, which formed a natural moat; in 1582 Hashiba Hideyoshi began the attack on Takamatsu in the fourth month; from 8 May, on Kuroda Yoshitaka's proposal, earthworks enclosing the castle with a dam began, to drown the castle standing in the low wetland - the famous "water attack on Takamatsu". Note: the phrase the registry quotes, 「a swamp castle (numajiro, 沼城) standing on low wetland」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「低湿地にある沼城」), was not returned from THIS page.)
