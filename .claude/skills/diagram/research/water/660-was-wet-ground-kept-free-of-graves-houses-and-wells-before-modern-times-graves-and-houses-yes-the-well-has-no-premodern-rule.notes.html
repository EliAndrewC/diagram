  • lushi-chunqiu-juan10 - 「If the burial is shallow, foxes dig it up; if deep, it reaches the groundwater. So every burial must be on high ground, to escape the harm of foxes and the damp of the groundwater.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「葬淺則狐狸抇之，深則及於水泉。故凡葬必於高陵之上，以避狐狸之患、水泉之溼。」) (the chapter 節喪, on keeping funerals within bounds)
  • nagano-shishi-kodai-mura - 「In the middle of the Heian period, from the latter half of the ninth century into the tenth, huge central villages appear again, on the natural levees of the Chikuma River and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「九世紀後半から一〇世紀代の平安時代中ごろになると、ふたたび千曲川自然堤防上などに巨大な拠点的集落が登場する。」) ; 「These stood on slightly raised ground such as the natural levees, and are thought to have farmed paddy with the broad back marsh behind them as their field land.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「これらは自然堤防などの微高地(びこうち)上に立地し、背後の広大な後背湿地(こうはいしっち)を生産域として水田耕作を営んでいたと考えられる。」) ; 「Such a siting of sites is a traditional one seen from the Yayoi period」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「こうした遺跡の立地は弥生時代からみられる伝統的なもので」)
  • taixi-shuifa-quanlan - 「The method of digging a well has five parts. First, choosing the site. For a well, the mountain foot is best, where springs come out and shade and sun are balanced; next, sunny ground where gardens and houses stand; next after that, open country. On the mountainside, the sunny face is too hot and the shady face too cold; the well-digger judges whether spring water is present and chooses or avoids accordingly.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「鑿井之法有五 第一擇地 鑿井之處山麓為上䝉泉所出隂陽適宜園林室屋所在向陽之地次之曠野又次之山腰者居陽則太熱居隂則太寒鑿井者察泉水之有無斟酌避就之」) ; 「Western Methods of Water, six books, written in the Ming, in the renzi year of Wanli, by Xiong Sanba of the West」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「泰西水法六卷明萬厯壬子西洋熊三拔撰」) (the catalog summary on the same page; the renzi year of Wanli is 1612)
  • taixi-shuifa-quanlan - 「So in low ground and marsh country a well-digger finds a spring anywhere, regardless of veins; in other, high and dry mountain districts he must seek the water vein, and without the water vein he never reaches a spring.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「故掘井者惟下地澤國所在得泉不論脈理其他山鄉高亢必㝷水脈不得水脈終不及泉」)
