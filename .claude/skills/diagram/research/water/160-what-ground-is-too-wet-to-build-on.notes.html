• rwsshp-hand-dug-well - 「Not located in cemeteries, swampy or flood prone areas」「Top 3 metres of shaft sealed to prevent surface water entering well / Diversion ditch to stop surface water flowing near well」 (the Site Issues and Headworks rows of its table setting a traditional well against an improved one; the source's own English); wateraid-hand-dug-wells - 「Create apron or install concrete slab and drain to prevent ponding of water at well」「Top three metres of rings should be grouted with concrete to create a sanitary seal」 (the source's own English). Both are modern rural water-supply manuals for the same artifact - a hand-dug, hand-lined, bucket-drawn village well - and the reason each gives is sanitary, keeping pathogens out of the shaft, where this record's reason is practical.
  • maff-drain-shape - 「A drainage channel is a channel that gathers the runoff from the collector drains and discharges it into a river or a natural watercourse; it is placed mainly in the low-lying part, or a natural ravine or the like is used.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「排水路は、集水路からの流出水を集め、河川や自然流路に放流する水路であり、主に低位部に設置又は自然の渓流等を利用している。」); kohai-shicchi-jawiki - 「Drainage is poor, and lakes and ponds such as ox-bow lakes left unfilled are often distributed there」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「排水性が悪く、しばしば埋め残された三日月湖などの湖沼が分布する」) (the superlative and the placing of the wet ground below the collector are this page's framing)
