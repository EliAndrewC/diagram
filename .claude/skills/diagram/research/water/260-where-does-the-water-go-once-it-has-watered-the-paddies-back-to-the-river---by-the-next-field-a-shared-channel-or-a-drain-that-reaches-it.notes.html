  • maff-nogyoyosui-suiden - 「In general, when irrigation and drainage are separated and dry-field conversion advances, not only does evapotranspiration-and-percolation (the daily fall in water depth) increase, but the demand for cultivation-management water in each paddy also grows. Also, because water use becomes independent for each plot, residual water is drained directly into the drainage canal and the opportunity for repeated use decreases; further, in paddies where percolation had been suppressed by damming up and the like, percolation increases as the groundwater level falls, among other effects - so where irrigation and drainage are separated, the water requirement generally increases.」 (translated; )
