  • maoshi-zhengyi-juan7 - 「By the embankment of that marsh there are cattail and lotus. (An evocation. The embankment (bei) is the marsh's barrier. He is the lotus.」 (translated; ) ; 「The correct meaning: the marsh's barrier means the bank at the marsh's edge that holds back the water.   • chenfu-nongshu-juanshang - 「For a high field, look at the lie of its land, and where the high water gathers, reckon what it needs and dig a pond, giving up about two or three mu in every ten mu of field to store the water; at the turn of spring and summer, when the rains come in season, raise its bank high and large and dig its middle deep and broad, so that it is wide enough to hold them; on the bank plant mulberry and cudrania sparsely, to which cattle can be tethered. The cattle get cool shade and thrive, the bank is trodden by the cattle and becomes firm, the mulberry gets rich water and grows lush, in drought the water can be let out to irrigate, and in floods it does not spread over and harm the crop.」 (translated; ) (the chapter on the lie of the land, 地勢之宜篇)
