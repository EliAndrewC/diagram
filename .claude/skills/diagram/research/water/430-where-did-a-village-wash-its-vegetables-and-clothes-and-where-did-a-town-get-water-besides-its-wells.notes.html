• gujo-jsce-2017 - 「Facilities such as footings built for washing clothes and washing vegetables are called araiba when they are along a channel, and kawado when they are along the river.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) 「The board inserted into a groove built in a channel or small stream to hold water temporarily is called a segi-ita ... It is used often for daily washing, sprinkling water in summer and melting snow in winter, and is also used when splitting the flow.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) 「A mizubune is a facility in which mountain water gathering in a valley, or spring water welling from the bedrock, is led by pipe into a tank stepped in two or three tiers」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) 「the upper tier is used for drinking water, the middle for rinsing and washing food, and the lower for washing dirty things and getting the mud off vegetables.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • mlit-mizubunka-sanko - 「For some time after the war there were, in places across the city, wells and washing places called "shikko (clear water)", and the division of the tanks between drinking and washing was strictly kept.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Hirosaki); 「the ponds called kawa that reached into the kitchen, and the channels within the settlement, had rules for use at the places for washing.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Fuji, Saga)
  • harie-syozu - 「washing the soil off the vegetables picked in the fields」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the doubled 野 is the page's own typo; the passage describes the uses of the kabata)
  • nbgjz-jiangnan-minju - 「nearly every house has a small landing outside its door. The housewife does the daily washing of clothes, washing of vegetables, rinsing of rice and so on here, and passing small boats can also moor here.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • dili360-jiangnan-minju - 「even washing clothes, buying vegetables and cooking are easily taken care of at the riverside.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • mlit-mizubunka-sanko - 「prompted by the great fire of Joo 1 (1652), the domain lord Endo Tsunetomo remodeled the castle town, opening the Yanagimachi canal as a fire defense for North Town and the Kanbun channel (Shimaya canal) as irrigation water for South Town, and further spreading small channels dividing their water」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) 「even now the shared kawado are used as washing places, and in addition a cleaning rota is set for each channel」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • mizu-no-bunka-12-jokamachi - 「of the facilities with rivers as their source built in castle towns by rulers, nearly all doubled as irrigation, and ran not only domestic and fire-fighting water but also into moats and garden ponds」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • mlit-mizubunka-sanko - 「an "old-style waterworks", in which a parent well was set at the source and the water stored there was distributed by bamboo pipe to shared wells and each house's take-well, developed under the townspeople's own management」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Omihachiman)
