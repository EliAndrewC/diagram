• tameike-jawiki - 「The embankment is managed to keep its strength - among other things, cultivation on it is forbidden and mowing and burning-off are carried out to hold back the growth of grass and trees.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「堤は耕作を禁じたり、草刈りや野焼きを行ったりして草木の生育を阻む等、強度を維持するための管理が行われる。」) ; 「As a result it gets plenty of sun, and plants that like sunlight grow well there.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そのため日当りが良くなり、日光を好む植物がよく生える。」) ; 「Waremokou (great burnet), kikyou (balloon flower), rindou (gentian), ominaeshi (golden lace) and the like grew well in such places, and many of the seven autumn grasses (aki no nanakusa) too are seen in such places.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ワレモコウ、キキョウ、リンドウ、オミナエシなどはこのような場所によく生育したもので、秋の七草も多くはこのような場所に見られる。」)
  • kagawa-tameike-structure - 「It is a facility intended to prevent erosion by waves and the like, and it takes such forms of construction as riprap (suteishi, 捨石), stone pitching, and concrete-block facing.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「波等による浸食防止を図るための設備で、捨石、石張り、コンクリートブロック張などの構造があります。」) (上流法面保護工) ((The upstream slope protection) is a facility to prevent erosion by waves and the like, built as riprap, stone pitching, concrete-block facing and so on.)
