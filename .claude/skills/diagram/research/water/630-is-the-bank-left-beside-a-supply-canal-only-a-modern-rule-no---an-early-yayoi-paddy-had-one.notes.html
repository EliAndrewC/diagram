  • zhouli-zhushu-suiren - 「In laying out the countryside, between allotments there is a sui channel, and on the sui channel a jing path; for ten allotments a gou, and on the gou a zhen path; for a hundred allotments a xu, and on the xu a tu road; for a thousand allotments a kuai, and on the kuai a dao road; for ten thousand allotments a chuan, and on the chuan a lu road, reaching to the royal domain.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「凡治野，夫間有遂，遂上有徑；十夫有溝，溝上有畛；百夫有洫，洫上有塗；千夫有澮，澮上有道；萬大有川，川上有路，以達於畿。」); 「The jing, zhen, tu, dao and lu are all for carrying carts and people on foot through to the capital. The jing takes oxen and horses, the zhen a large cart, the tu one carriage track, the dao two tracks, the lu three.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「徑、畛、塗、道、路，皆所以通車徒於國都也。徑容牛馬，畛容大車，塗容乘車一軌，道容二軌，路容三軌。」); 「So the jing naturally takes no cart track, but oxen and horses and people's footpath」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「自然徑不容車軌，而容牛馬及人之步徑」) (the classic, then Zheng Xuan's Han-dynasty note, then Jia Gongyan's Tang sub-commentary; the sui channel is the smallest of the series; 萬大 is the page's slip for 萬夫)
