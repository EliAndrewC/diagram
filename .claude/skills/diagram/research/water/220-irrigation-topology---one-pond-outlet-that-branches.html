Irrigation topology - one pond outlet that branches

Sources: kagawa-tameike-structure (one intake facility, 斜樋 into 底樋)

A tameike reservoir has a single intake facility (the inclined shahi into the bottom sokohi)26 - occasionally two, in this page's reading - so all its irrigation leaves by one main channel (yosui) that then FORKS downstream to the fields, dividing at junctions;14727 it is NOT several independent pipes drilled into the pond.
