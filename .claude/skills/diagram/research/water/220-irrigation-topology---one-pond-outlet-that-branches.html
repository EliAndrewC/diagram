Irrigation topology - one pond outlet that branches

Sources: kagawa-tameike-structure (one intake facility, the inclined pipe 斜樋 (shahi) into the bottom conduit 底樋 (sokohi))

A tameike reservoir has one intake facility for its irrigation (the inclined shahi into the bottom sokohi), beside a sediment-discharge gate at the very bottom for managing the pond and drawing off its bottom water - occasionally two intakes, never several independent pipes drilled into the pond - so its irrigation leaves by one main channel (yosui) that then FORKS downstream to the fields, dividing at junctions.
