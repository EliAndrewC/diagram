• kotobank-marukibashi - 「a bridge that is just a single log laid across」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ; the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's entry) and 「a log simply laid across to serve as a bridge」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ; the Daijisen's entry)
  • kotobank-ipponbashi - 「a bridge of one log or the like laid across a brook or mountain stream」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ; the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's entry)
  • zhwiki-dumuqiao - 「A single-log bridge is a simple timber beam bridge made of a single tree trunk or board, the earliest bridge humans invented, used mainly to let walkers or livestock cross relatively narrow rivers and gorges」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • dobashi-jawiki - 「Until the Edo period the overwhelming majority of bridges over Japan's rivers were earthen bridges, and quite long bridges were also built this way. Plank-decked bridges were limited to an important few, and stone bridges were very few indeed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ; the article carries a no-sources tag dated January 2023) and 「Where logs are laid side by side without gaps to make the deck, the deck is uneven. As that is hard to walk on, earth is spread over it and trodden firm, and the earth fills the hollows and makes it flat. This is an earthen bridge.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • dobashi-jawiki - 「Many bridges span the Hikifune River. ... The structure of an earthen bridge can be seen in the bridge in the foreground. Small bridges of this kind were mostly built this way.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ; the caption to Hiroshige's print of the Koume embankment)
  • xinhua-jiahou-muqiao - 「Your grandfather found four log sections and laid them across the canal as a simple timber bridge. In our home village at that time, criss-crossed by ditches and canals, this was a common practice.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) and 「especially when it rained, the logs were rather slippery」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; ) and 「For rice to go from a single seed to a plant waiting to be harvested takes three stages.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • xinhua-jiahou-muqiao - 「Behind the house was a canal, 2-3 m wide in most places, 4-5 m at the widest.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
