• kotobank-marukibashi - 「a bridge that is just a single log laid across」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一本の丸太を渡しただけの橋。」; the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's entry) and 「a log simply laid across to serve as a bridge」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「丸木を渡しただけで橋としたもの。」; the Daijisen's entry)
  • kotobank-ipponbashi - 「a bridge of one log or the like laid across a brook or mountain stream」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「小川や渓流に丸木などを一本かけわたした橋。」; the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's entry)
  • zhwiki-dumuqiao - 「A single-log bridge is a simple timber beam bridge made of a single tree trunk or board, the earliest bridge humans invented, used mainly to let walkers or livestock cross narrow rivers and gorges」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「独木桥是由单根树干或木板制作成的简易木制梁桥[1]，是人类最早发明的桥梁，主要用来让步行者或牲畜跨越较窄的河流和峡谷[2]」)
  • dobashi-jawiki - 「Until the Edo period the overwhelming majority of bridges over Japan's rivers were earthen bridges, and quite long bridges were also built this way. Plank-decked bridges were limited to an important few, and stone bridges were very few indeed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代まで日本の川にかかる橋の圧倒的多数は土橋で、かなり長い橋もこの方式で作られた。板を橋面にした板橋は重要な少数に限られ、石橋はごく僅少であった。」; the article carries a no-sources tag dated January 2023)
  • dobashi-jawiki - 「Many bridges span the Hikifune River. ... small bridges of this kind were mostly built this way.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「曳舟川にいくつもの橋がかかっている。…こうした小規模の橋はたいていこうした造りであった。」; the caption to Hiroshige's print of the Koume embankment)
  • xinhua-jiahou-muqiao - 「Behind the house was a canal, 2-3 m wide in most places, 4-5 m at the widest.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「家的后面是一条水渠，一般地方2-3米宽，最宽的有4-5米。」) and 「Your grandfather found four log sections and laid them across the canal as a simple timber bridge. In our home village at that time, criss-crossed by ditches and canals, this was a common practice.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「你爷爷找来了四根树段，担在渠上形成了一个简易的木桥。在沟渠纵横的老家那个时候，这是一个常见的做法。」) and 「especially when it rained, the logs were slippery」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「特别是下雨的时候，树段比较滑」)
