• kotobank-marukibashi - 「a bridge that is just a single log laid across」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一本の丸太を渡しただけの橋。」; the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's entry) and 「a log simply laid across to serve as a bridge」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「丸木を渡しただけで橋としたもの。」; the Daijisen's entry)
  • kotobank-ipponbashi - 「a bridge of one log or the like laid across a brook or mountain stream」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「小川や渓流に丸木などを一本かけわたした橋。」; the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's entry)
  • zhwiki-dumuqiao - 「A single-log bridge is a simple timber beam bridge made of a single tree trunk or board, the earliest bridge humans invented, used mainly to let walkers or livestock cross narrow rivers and gorges」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「独木桥是由单根树干或木板制作成的简易木制梁桥[1]，是人类最早发明的桥梁，主要用来让步行者或牲畜跨越较窄的河流和峡谷[2]」)
  • dobashi-jawiki - 「Until the Edo period the overwhelming majority of bridges over Japan's rivers were earthen bridges, and quite long bridges were also built this way. Plank-decked bridges were limited to an important few, and stone bridges were very few indeed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代まで日本の川にかかる橋の圧倒的多数は土橋で、かなり長い橋もこの方式で作られた。板を橋面にした板橋は重要な少数に限られ、石橋はごく僅少であった。」; the article carries a no-sources tag dated January 2023) and 「Where logs are laid side by side without gaps to make the deck, the deck is uneven. As that is hard to walk on, earth is spread over it and trodden firm, and the earth fills the hollows and makes it flat. This is an earthen bridge.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「丸太を隙間なく並べて橋面を作った場合、橋面が凹凸になる。そのままでは歩きにくいので、そこに土をかけて踏み固めると、へこんだ部分に土が詰まって平らになる。これが土橋である。」)
  • dobashi-jawiki - 「Many bridges span the Hikifune River. ... The structure of an earthen bridge can be seen in the bridge in the foreground. Small bridges of this kind were mostly built this way.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「曳舟川にいくつもの橋がかかっている。…手前の橋に土橋の構造が見てとれる。こうした小規模の橋はたいていこうした造りであった。」; the caption to Hiroshige's print of the Koume embankment)
  • xinhua-jiahou-muqiao - 「Behind the house was a canal, 2-3 m wide in most places, 4-5 m at the widest.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「家的后面是一条水渠，一般地方2-3米宽，最宽的有4-5米。」) and 「Your grandfather found four log sections and laid them across the canal as a simple timber bridge. In our home village at that time, criss-crossed by ditches and canals, this was a common practice.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「你爷爷找来了四根树段，担在渠上形成了一个简易的木桥。在沟渠纵横的老家那个时候，这是一个常见的做法。」) and 「especially when it rained, the logs were slippery」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「特别是下雨的时候，树段比较滑」) and 「For rice to go from a single seed to a plant waiting to be harvested takes three stages.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「水稻从一粒种子变成一颗等待收割的水稻需要经过三个流程。」)
