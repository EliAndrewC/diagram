  • kotobank-fumiguruma - 「a human-powered water-lifting machine peculiar to Japan, used almost exclusively for paddy irrigation」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「もっぱら水田の灌漑に用いられた日本独特の人力揚水機」) 「It is not installed fixed; it can be carried to wherever it is needed and used. It was generally used for lifts of about 1 m or less from the channel to the paddy surface, but for higher paddies two or three might be used in a row.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「固定して設置するのでなく，必要なところへ随時運んで使うことができる。一般に，水路より田面までの高さが約1m以内の揚水に用いられたが，さらに高い水田へは，2台，3台と連ねて使うこともあった。」) 「whereas most dragon-bone wheels needed the labor of two people, the treadle wheel needed only one.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ほとんどの竜骨車が2人の労力を要したのに対し，踏車は1人で足りる。」) (Horio Hisashi, the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, revised edition)
