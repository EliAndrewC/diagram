  • jsslkx-002-2021 - 「Field canals should be laid out on the principle of favoring mechanized production and favoring field management; in plain polder districts they should be laid out in combination with the gridding of the fields, and in hilly and mountainous districts they should follow the hill-slope terrain: let the large bends take the lie of the land, and straighten the small bends.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「田间渠道宜考虑有利于机械化生产，有利于田间管理的原则进行布置；平原圩区宜结合格田化布置，丘陵山区宜顺山坡地形，大弯就势，小弯取直。」) (§7.4.1. The closing clause - let the large bends take the lie of the land, and straighten the small bends (original: 「大弯就势，小弯取直」) - is the alignment maxim Chinese canal design still works to, and it describes an alignment of sweeps and straights with a corner introduced nowhere. How old the maxim is, this source cannot say: what it shows is that the rule is design law in Jiangsu today. It is about setting a channel out, not about maintaining one: no page read describes a crew choosing to re-dig a bend rather than a corner)
