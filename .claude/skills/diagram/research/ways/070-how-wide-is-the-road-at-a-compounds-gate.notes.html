• ctie-michi-nazenaze - 「The road width of the Five Highways at this period was, excluding mountain roads, generally 3 to 4 ken (about 5.4 to 7.2 m), and in places close to Edo 5 ken (about 9 m) was secured.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このころの五街道の道幅は、山道を除いておおむね 3～4 間（約 5.4～7.2m）で、江戸に近いところでは 5 間（約 9m）確保されていました。」)
  • hiroshima-saigoku-kaido - 「The road was as much as roughly 2.5 ken (about 4.5 m) wide, and the setting up of milestone mounds, roadside pines and the like was the work of the Asano clan.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「道幅は概ね２間半（約４.５メートル）もあり、一里塚や街道松の設置などは浅野氏によるものでした。」) (of the Saigoku road through Hiroshima)
  • mlit-kinsei-michi - 「Houses were required to have an inu-bashiri (3 shaku, about 90 cm).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「民家には、「犬走り」（3尺・約90cm）の設置が義務付けられた。」) (a reference panel on Edo's streets, which cites no sources)
