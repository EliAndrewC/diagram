  • dobashi-jawiki - 「When logs are laid side by side with no gaps to make the bridge surface, the surface is uneven. As it is, it is hard to walk on, so earth is thrown over it and trodden down, and the earth fills the hollows and the surface becomes level.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「丸太を隙間なく並べて橋面を作った場合、橋面が凹凸になる。そのままでは歩きにくいので、そこに土をかけて踏み固めると、へこんだ部分に土が詰まって平らになる。」)
  • aze-jawiki - 「Those that also serve as a path for going round the paddy fields are called azemichi, or nawate.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水田を回る際の道としての役割も持っているもののことを、畦道（あぜみち）、畷（なわて、縄手とも）という。」) and 「What is called an azemichi is wide, and may in some cases be taken as a private bund doubling as a passage for farm work, or as a private farm road.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦道と言われるものは幅が広く、私的な「畦」兼農作業のための通路または私的な農道と考えてよい場合がある。」)
