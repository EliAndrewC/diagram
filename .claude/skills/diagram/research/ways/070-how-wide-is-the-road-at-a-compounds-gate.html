How wide is the road at a compound's gate?

Sources: ctie-michi-nazenaze (the width of the Five Highways), hiroshima-saigoku-kaido (the width of a highway through a castle town), mlit-kinsei-michi (the apron a house kept along Edo's streets)

What was read. The Five Highways were generally 3 to 4 ken wide, about 18 to 24 ft, leaving out the mountain roads, and 5 ken, about 30 ft, near Edo. The Saigoku road through Hiroshima's castle town was roughly 2.5 ken, about 15 ft. In Edo a house was required to keep an apron of about 3 ft (the inu-bashiri). No page read gives the width of the road before an official's or a samurai residence's gate, or of a lane to its side gate.

What the map draws. The road at a compound's front gate is the road the compound stands on, at that road's width - 15 to 24 ft where it is a highway, researched - and any wider ground before the gate is a guess. A lane to a side gate or cart gate is a guess too, drawn at about 6 ft where carts use it: "Could a cart use the roads here?"
