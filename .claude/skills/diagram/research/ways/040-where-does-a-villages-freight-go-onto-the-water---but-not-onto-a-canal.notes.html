• chinaknowledge-caoyun - 「Tribute grain transport (caoyun 漕運) in imperial times supplied the officialdom and the military garrisons in the capital with staple food, and the grain was also shipped to other destinations, mainly border garrisons. The grain formed part of a local tax levied in the provinces of the lower Yangtze region and was transported along a vast canal system, the Grand Canal or Imperial Canal (yunhe 運河, da yunhe 大運河).」 (the source's own English); on the Ming volumes, 「In 1472, it was decreed that the provinces of Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui, Hunan, Hubei, Jiangxi, Henan and Shandong should produce yearly 4 million shi of rice, to be transported to Tongzhou 通州 (today's Tongxian 通縣), the terminal of the Grand Canal close to the northern capital Beijing.」 (the source's own English); and on the sea route it displaced, 「Yet as demand for grain in Beijing increased, sea transport became infeasible and was ultimately fully replaced by canal transport.」
  • kashi-jawiki - 「When the Edo period began, the Edo shogunate and the domains carried out river improvement works on a large scale. At that time new kashi were set up under those lords, to carry the tax rice and other goods from the provinces to Edo, Osaka and elsewhere.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代に入ると、江戸幕府や諸藩の手により河川の改修工事が盛んに行われた。この際、年貢米やその他物品を、地方から江戸や大阪などへと運ぶために新たな河岸が、それらの領主のもとで設置された。」)
  • kashi-jawiki - 「In the Edo period merchants running wholesale businesses and their storehouses gathered at the kashi, which formed a commercial settlement of its own.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代に入ると河岸には問屋を商う商人やその蔵が集まり、一つの商業集落を形成していた。」)
  • daba-jawiki - 「There is the da, a unit of what one horse carried on its back, used in the Edo period. Under the Edo-period regulation it meant thirty-six kan (135 kg).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代に使われた馬一頭が背負う単位として駄がある。江戸時代の定めでは三十六貫(135kg)を言った」); chuma-jawiki - 「In chuma one man ordinarily led three or four horses, and carried around 100 kan of goods at one time.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中馬は普通1人で3・4頭の馬を牽引し、100貫前後の荷物を1度に運んだ。」) (the packhorse's load, and a hauler's)
