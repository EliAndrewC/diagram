• jarimichi-jawiki - 「In Japan, until modern times, roads trodden hard by people and horses were the norm, and gravel roads were limited to main roads.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本では近代に至るまで人や馬によって踏み固められた道が一般的であり、砂利道は主要道路に限られていた。」)
  • ishidatami-jawiki - 「The simplest state of a road is a strip where the grass has worn away because people and livestock walk it often, or a way made passable by felling trees in a forest or wood; that is, it is basically bare earth, and on a plain earth road rain turns it to mud, and walkers and carriages alike were put to great trouble.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「道の最も素朴な状態というのは、草地などを人や家畜などが頻繁に歩く結果として草が無くなっているところの筋が出来ている状態や、森や林の樹木を切り倒して人が歩ける状態にしたものであり、つまりは基本的に土などがむき出しの状態なのであるが、土そのままの道では雨が降るとぬかるんでしまい、歩行者も馬車も非常に苦労させられた。」) (the article's general overview, not a statement about Japan in particular)
  • mlit-tokaido-michi - 「Edo-period road policy and road types and grades were set out in the 'Ieyasu hundred articles', said to be Ieyasu's testament.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代の道路政策や道路の種類・等級が、家康の遺訓といわれる『家康百箇条』」); its table: 「field road 3 shaku (about 90 cm)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「作場道 3尺（約90cm）」)
  • gokaido-jawiki - 「Works were carried out such as spreading gravel and sand to harden the road surface and planting ginkgo trees.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「砂利や砂を敷いて路面を固めたり、銀杏木を植えたりするなどの取り組みが行われた」)
  • mlit-kinsei-michi-gokaido - 「Hardening the surface with gravel and sand ... could not be carried through; when it rained, people walked the mud roads in high geta」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「砂利と砂による路面固め…徹底できず、人々は雨が降ると高下駄を履いて泥道を歩いた」)
  • mlit-kinsei-michi-gokaido - 「In 1612 the shogunate issued an order on road maintenance as an 'oboe'. Spread and harden sand and stone where there are puddles and mud. Make water flow at the side of the road.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「1612年、幕府は「覚」として道路整備に関する命令を出した。水溜まりや泥濘の所は砂石で敷き固めよ。道の脇に水が流れるようにせよ。」)
  • mlit-tokaido-michi - 「Namely: 'hollows made by passing horses are to be filled with sand or stones and well hardened; the sides of the road are to be given a suitable amount of moisture and hardened; the turf of road embankments is not to be stripped', and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「すなわち「馬が通って生じた窪みには砂や石を敷いて良く固めること、道の傍らは適量の湿りを与えて固めること、道路堤の芝などをはいではならない」などである。」) (the same 1612 order as the note above, in this page's wording)
  • gokaido-jawiki - 「In principle the road magistrate assigned the carrying-out of highway maintenance to the post towns and villages along the road and made them responsible for it.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「原則的に道中奉行が街道の維持管理の執行を沿道の宿村に割り当てて、その執行責任を負わせた」); 「Depending on traffic, the roadside villages were sometimes not enough, and assignments extended to surrounding villages not directly on the road; each such assigned section was called a soji-choba.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「交通量によっては沿道地区村で間に合わない場合があり、沿道に直接接しない周辺の村々にも割り当てが及び、その割り当てられたそれぞれの区間を掃除丁場（そうじちょうば）といった」)
  • mlit-kinsei-michi-gokaido - 「Day-to-day maintenance was borne by roadside and nearby villages. Those bearing it were set as towns and villages within roughly 20 km of the highway.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日々の維持管理は沿道・近隣の村々が負担。負担するのは概ね街道から20ｋｍ以内の町村と規定。」)
  • ishidatami-jawiki - 「Old highways: the Edo shogunate ordered difficult stretches improved with stone paving.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧街道 難所は江戸幕府により石畳による整備が命じられた。」); 「On the old Tokaido, part of the stone paving of that time survives at Hakone and at Kikugawa-juku.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧東海道では、箱根と菊川宿に当時の石畳が一部現存している」)
  • gokaido-jawiki - 「For the 14th shogun Iemochi's journey up to Kyoto in Bunkyu 3 (1863), a record survives that the Hakone mountain road was repaired and paved with round stones.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「文久3年（1863年）の14代将軍家茂上洛の際には、箱根の山道を改修して丸石で舗装したという記録が残されており」)
  • gokaido-jawiki - 「On the Fushimi-Kyoto and Otsu-Kyoto roads, where haulage was heavy, a carriageway for ox carts was separated from the road for people and horses; thick granite cart-stones were laid in two wheel tracks, and gravel was spread level on the ox path.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「運送が盛んだった伏見 - 京都間や大津 - 京都間の街道では牛車用の車道と人馬道を歩車分離の考え方で区分し、2条の輪道に花崗岩の厚板の車石を敷き並べ、牛道には砂利を敷きならした」)
