  • guangdong-xinyu-22 - 「Jiujiang township raises fry; fry ponds are found only in Jiujiang. Elsewhere they mostly raise grown fish, and if they do raise fry, the fry do not thrive.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
  • guangdong-xinyu-22 - 「The land of Jiujiang is like a chessboard, over thirty li around; its black lines are the dikes, and the squares are the ponds. Of the pond water, seven-tenths raises fry and three-tenths raises grown fish. Fry water is turbid; grown-fish water is clear. From the color of the water one knows what kind of fish is raised.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; )
