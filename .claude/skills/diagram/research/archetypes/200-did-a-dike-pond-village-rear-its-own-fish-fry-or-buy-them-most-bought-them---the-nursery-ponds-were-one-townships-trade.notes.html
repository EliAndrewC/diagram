  • guangdong-xinyu-22 - 「The land of Jiujiang is like a chessboard, over thirty li around; its black lines are the dikes, and the squares are the ponds. Of the pond water, seven-tenths raises fry and three-tenths raises grown fish. Fry water is turbid; grown-fish water is clear. From the color of the water one knows what kind of fish is raised.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「九江之地如棋枰，周回三十餘裏，其黑脈者堤也，方方罫池塘也。池塘之水，養魚花者十之七，養大魚者十之三。養魚花水濁，養大魚水清。視其水色，則知所養為何等魚也。」)
