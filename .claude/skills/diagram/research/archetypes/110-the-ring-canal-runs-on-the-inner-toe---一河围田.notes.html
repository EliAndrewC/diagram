• weinei-he-summary - 「What is called weitian (polder field) refers to farmland formed by artificially building dikes to hold back the water, in low-lying terrain adjoining rivers, lakes and the sea.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「所谓圩田，是指在临近江河湖海的地势低洼地区，人工筑堤挡水形成的农田」) and 「Their common feature is unchanging: a dike is built at the low-lying place, enclosing the fields within and holding the water without. ... Seen from a bird's-eye view, the various polder fields look like so many chessboards, large and small.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「其共同的特征是不变的：低洼处筑堤，内以围田，外以围水。...从鸟瞰角度来看，各种圩田像是一个个大大小小的棋盘」) (a polder is farmland formed by artificially diking low-lying ground near rivers, lakes or the sea; its constant feature - a dike at the low place, fields enclosed within, water held without; from the air a polder looks like a chessboard. Contains none of the claimed 月状 / 弓状 forms or gated-ends language.)
  • cssn-jiangnan-weitian - 「Fan Zhongyan of the Northern Song described it in "Answering the Imperial Rescript, Setting Out Ten Matters" (Da shouzhao tiaochen shishi): "Jiangnan has long had polder fields. Each polder is several tens of li square, like a great city, with river channels within and gates and sluices without. In drought the sluices are opened to draw the benefit of the river's water; in flood they are shut to keep out the harm of the river's water. Neither drought nor flood reaches them, and they are a fine benefit to the farmer."」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「北宋范仲淹在《答手诏条陈十事》中描述道：“江南旧有圩田，每一圩方数十里，如大城，中有河渠，外有门闸。旱则开闸引江水之利，涝则闭闸拒江水之害。旱涝不及，为农美利。”」) (in flood they are shut to keep the river's harm out.)
