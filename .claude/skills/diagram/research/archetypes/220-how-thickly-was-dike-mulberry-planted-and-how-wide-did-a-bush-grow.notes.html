• pwsannong-gudai-zaisang - 「Row and plant spacing in old mulberry plantations differed greatly with local climate, the height of the terrain, and the tree form trained. In Jiangsu-Zhejiang, planting before the mid-Qing was fairly sparse, and in the late Qing it tended from sparse to dense. For example, south of the Qiantang River, a sericulture district newly developed in the late Qing, mid-trunk mulberry was planted, about 300 per mu.」 (translated; ) (a mu is about 7,200 sq ft, so 300 a mu is one tree to about 24 sq ft; the conversion is this page's arithmetic, and the page's mu is presumably the modern one)
  • pwsannong-gudai-zaisang - 「From the Ming, mid-trunk mulberry gradually spread in the Taihu area, and later low-trunk mulberry appeared.」 (translated; )
  • pwsannong-sangji-yutang - 「The mulberry is planted in basically the same way as in an ordinary mulberry field on flat land; in the sericulture district of the Pearl River delta 8,000 to 10,000 mulberry are planted per mu and trained as ground mulberry (root-cut)」 (translated; ) (one bush to about 0.7-0.9 sq ft, this page's arithmetic)
  • kotobank-souen - 「The number planted per 10a is 800 to 1,000 in root-cut mulberry fields and 400 to 600 in mid-cut.」 (translated; ) (ten ares is about 10,760 sq ft, so root-cut is one bush to 11-13 sq ft and mid-cut one to 18-27 sq ft, this page's arithmetic)
  • kotobank-negari - 「In root-cut, about 1,000 bushes are planted per 10a and the bushes are trained small.」 (translated; )
