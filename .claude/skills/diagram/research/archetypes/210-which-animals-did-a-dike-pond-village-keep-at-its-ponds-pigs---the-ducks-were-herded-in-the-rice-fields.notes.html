  • guangdong-xinyu-20 - 「The coastal fields of Guangzhou breed many small crabs, which each year eat the rice sprouts and harm farmers; only ducks can eat them. In the fields, ducks eat the crabs in spring and summer and the gleaned rice in autumn, and grow fat easily, so many in the villages keep ducks. Duck-keeping has landings, and each landing has an owner, chosen from people with settled property. Whenever ducks eat the rice in someone's field, the landing owner is held responsible; the owner holds the duck-keeper responsible, who pays by name, and no one dares to run wild.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「廣州瀕海之田，多產蟛蜞，歲食穀芽為農害，惟鴨能食之。鴨在田間，春夏食蟛蜞，秋食遺稻，易以肥大，故鄉落間多畜鴨。畜鴨有埠，埠有主，以民有恆產者為之。凡鴨食人田稻，責之埠主，埠主責之畜鴨民，按名以償，無有敢為暴者。」)
