The ring canal runs on the INNER toe - 一河围田

Sources: cssn-jiangnan-weitian (「channels within, sluice gates without」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「中有河渠，外有门闸」) - channels inside the dike, gates through it, opened in drought and shut in flood), weinei-he-summary (the general dike-and-canal chessboard pattern80). The 一河围田 shorthand, the inside-toe placement and the crescent 月样 / bow 弓样 forms have no readable source

What the research found: the polder's trunk distribution+collection canal rings the block on the INSIDE toe of the perimeter dike, on the FIELD side (Jiangnan shorthand 一河围田, yi he wei tian, "one river surrounds the field"; cross-section outside->in: wild water -> dike -> inner ring canal 圩内河 (weineihe) -> field ditches 浜 (bang) -> paddies). Outside the dike is the wild lake/creek the dike holds back, so NO irrigation channel runs out there; water crosses the dike ONLY through gated sluices (斗门 doumen / 水閘 shuizha / 涵洞 handong - gate, lock and culvert) cut at discrete points26 (the inlet + outfall), never as an open cut over the dike body. And the trunk line is organized-but-organic: long runs that read straight-ish but GENTLY WAVY (a surveyed dug canal wavers with terrain and repair; crescent (月样, yueyang) / bow (弓样, gongyang) trunk forms are named options in the literature113), with rounded corners rather than hard 90-degree turns - the finer laterals are visibly crookeder.128
