• kotobank-bunsan-sakuho - 「...that the land reform could not break the framework of small-scale peasant farming under the "scattered and intermixed holding system", in which the individual holdings of farmers from of old consisted of tiny pieces of cultivated land, and moreover these were scattered in various places and intermixed with other people's land...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (from the encyclopedia's entry on the land reform, whose limits it names)
  • tanada-jawiki - 「Because the sorting-out of paddy ownership carried out when the plains' fields were enlarged has not been done in the terraces, the ownership of terraces that look to an outsider like one landscape is quite intricately intermixed, and the area farmed per household is also small.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kotobank-warichi-seido - 「...a special custom of the Edo period of re-allotting the paddy, to equalize the farmers' tax burden against the good or poor quality of the soil.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ), 「It is common in places of unstable harvest, such as the new-field districts of the low wet ground along the lower reaches of great rivers and landslide districts.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「...in this system its mark is that an individual's cultivated land moves at each re-allotment of the paddy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kotobank-warichi-seido - 「The method is first to measure the true area and the fertility of the village's land (this is called jinarashi), and to divide it into several groups of fields (danchi, wari) by paddy or dry field, soil quality, lie of the land and the expected frequency of floods and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「In doing so, each group of fields is divided into the number of lots, and one of each is put together to make one lot.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • kotobank-warichi-seido - 「Also, the rule is that the re-allotment is done at fixed terms of 5, 10 or 20 years, but in practice this tended to break down...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
