• kotobank-bunsan-sakuho - 「...that the land reform could not break the framework of small-scale peasant farming under the "scattered and intermixed holding system", in which the individual holdings of farmers from of old consisted of tiny pieces of cultivated land, and moreover these were scattered in various places and intermixed with other people's land...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「…農地改革が旧来からの農民の個々の保有地が零細な耕地片から成り，しかもそれが各所に分散して他人の耕地と入り組んでいる〈分散錯圃制〉のもとでの零細農民経営の枠組みを打破しえず…」) (from the encyclopedia's entry on the land reform, whose limits it names)
  • tanada-jawiki - 「Because the sorting-out of paddy ownership carried out when the plains' fields were enlarged has not been done in the terraces, the ownership of terraces that look to an outsider like one landscape is quite intricately intermixed, and the area farmed per household is also small.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「平野の圃場大型化整備の際に行われた田圃の所有権の整理が棚田ではされていないため、外部の者にとって一体的な風景に見える棚田の所有権はかなり複雑に入り組んでおり、1世帯あたりの耕作面積も少ない。」)
  • kotobank-warichi-seido - 「...a special custom of the Edo period of re-allotting the paddy, to equalize the farmers' tax burden against the good or poor quality of the soil.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「…百姓の貢租負担と地味の良否を均等化するために田地を割り替える江戸時代の特殊な慣行。」), 「It is common in places of unstable harvest, such as the new-field districts of the low wet ground along the lower reaches of great rivers and landslide districts.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「大河川下流の低湿地の新田地帯や地すべり地帯などのような収穫の不安定地に多くみられる。」) and 「...in this system its mark is that an individual's cultivated land moves at each re-allotment of the paddy.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「…この制度では、個人の耕作地が田地の割替ごとに移動する点を特徴とする。」)
  • kotobank-warichi-seido - 「The method is first to measure the true area and the fertility of the village's land (this is called jinarashi), and to divide it into several groups of fields (danchi, wari) by paddy or dry field, soil quality, lie of the land and the expected frequency of floods and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その方法は、まず村の土地についての実面積と地力を測定し（これを地ならしという）、田畑別、地味、地形、予測される水害などの頻度などに応じていくつかの耕地グループ（団地、割）に分ける。」) and 「In doing so, each group of fields is divided into the number of lots, and one of each is put together to make one lot.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その際、各耕地グループごとに鬮の数に分け、その一つずつをあわせて鬮1本分とする。」)
  • kotobank-warichi-seido - 「Also, the rule is that the re-allotment is done at fixed terms of 5, 10 or 20 years, but in practice this tended to break down...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「また、割替は5年、10年、20年と年期を定めて行うのが原則であるが、実際は崩れがちで、…」)
