What lies between two parcels, and how wide is it?

Sources: the GM's question of 2026-07-24 and this project's decision on it; no page is cited for the ridge's width

A polder's parcels are drawn as separate shapes, so the space between two of them IS the bund - and the two directions are not the same thing. Between parcels stacked one below another down the fall there is nothing but the walking bund: a ridge of piled earth with the footpath along its top, about three feet all told (a ridge of some 20-50 cm plus the path; this rests on general reading, no source is cited).91 Along the module lines the other way a delivery ditch runs with its spoil banks either side, and that corridor is about eight feet. The distinction matters because a wide gap is a PROMISE: a reader who sees eight feet of ground between two parcels is being told that water runs there. A bay split lengthwise into strips of a single holding would otherwise give the cut between two strips of the same farm the full ditch corridor - wider than any plain walking bund, and a promise of water that is not there. The decision (2026-07-24): a gap is only as wide as what runs in it. A split within a bay gets the same plain walking bund a row edge gets, and only the module lines, where a ditch is actually drawn, keep the corridor width. The dike-pond block is unaffected: its gaps are the same in both directions already.
