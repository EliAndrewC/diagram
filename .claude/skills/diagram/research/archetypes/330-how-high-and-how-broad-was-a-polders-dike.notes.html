  • wuhurec-wanchun - 「The southeast of the polder lies on a great marsh, where wind and water drive against it; the dike cannot stay firm for long.」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「圩之东南滨于大泽，风水之所排，堤不能久坚也」) and 「This ground has no abrupt rise; what comes on slopes gently for a full hundred paces. A hundred rows of willows are set along the dike, and reeds are planted in rows below them, so that where the water bites is a hundred paces off, and the dike never meets the water. The foot of the dike is several zhang broad and its top tapers to only a few chi, so it does not set itself against the water, and the water's force cannot contend with ours.」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「此其地非有斩然崛起之势，陵迟而来者皆百步，傅堤为柳百行，其下寒苇以列艺之，则水之所齿者，在百步之外，而堤未尝与水遇，其为堤之址数丈以广，而末锐才数尺，无与水忤，使其势不得与我争。」) (the rebuilders' answer, before the work, to the objection that waves would wear the dike away)
