• cssn-sangyuanwei - 「The sayings "Minle has a dou (窦, sluice), and every household with the sound of spinning and weaving" and "a boat of silk goes out, a boat of silver comes back" are precisely the historical witness to the flourishing of the silk-weaving industry at Minle, Xiqiao, Nanhai, Foshan in those days.」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「谚语“民乐有个窦，家家纺织声”“一船蚕丝出，一船白银归”正是当时佛山南海西樵民乐丝织业发达的历史见证。」)
  • cssn-jiangnan-weitian - 「Within there are river channels, without there are gates and sluices. In drought the sluices are opened to draw the benefit of the river's water; in flood they are shut to keep out the harm of the river's water.」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「中有河渠，外有门闸。旱则开闸引江水之利，涝则闭闸拒江水之害。」)
  • fao-x6708e - 「A sluice gate consists of a protected opening in the pond dike that can be easily closed with wooden boards to regulate water level and can be screened to avoid fish losses. Whenever required, it can discharge excess water continuously.」 and 「It is best to limit the inside width of the sluice gate to 0.80 m at the most.」
  • isis-dykepond - 「Ponds are drained two or three times a year, and the mud at the bottom is dredged up to put on the dykes」; fao-x6708e: 「grooves to fix wooden boards and screen」.
