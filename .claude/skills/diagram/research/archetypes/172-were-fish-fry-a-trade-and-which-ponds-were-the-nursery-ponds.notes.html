  • cssn-sangyuanwei - 「Qu Dajun, the noted late-Ming and early-Qing Lingnan scholar, also mentioned in his Guangdong xinyu (New Discourses on Guangdong) the effect the mulberry-dike fishponds brought to the locality: "Nanhai has a village called Jiujiang ... its land is cramped and small, and fish take up half of it. The ponds are used to raise fish, the dikes to grow mulberry; the men trade in fish fry, the women feed the silkworms. Of its soil none is left over, and among its inhabitants there are no idlers - truly a beauty of custom."」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「明末清初著名岭南学者屈大钧也在其《广东新语》中提及桑基鱼塘给当地带来的影响：“南海有九江村……地狭小而鱼占其半。塘以养鱼，堤以树桑，男贩鱼花，妇女喂爱蚕。其土无余壤，人无敖民，盖风俗之美者也。”」) (the men trade fish fry, the women feed the worms. No soil goes spare and no one idles - a fine custom. (the page attributes the passage to Qu Dajun's Guangdong xinyu, and it independently supports Jiujiang in Nanhai as the fry-trading place))
