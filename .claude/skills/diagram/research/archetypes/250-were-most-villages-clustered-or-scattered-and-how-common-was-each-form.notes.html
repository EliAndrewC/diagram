  • kotobank-shuson - 「In the Kinai and the Setouchi, dense villages in which farmhouses enclosed by walls stand close beside one another are common, but in the Kanto and the Tohoku loosely clustered villages are common, in which each house lot is wide, with a homestead grove and the like, and the houses stand at wider intervals.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畿内や瀬戸内では塀(へい)で囲まれた農家が密接して隣り合う密集村が多いが，関東や東北では個々の宅地が広く屋敷林などもあって家屋間隔の広がった疎集村が多い。」)
  • kotobank-sonraku - 「Among the gathered forms there are the block village, the most general, the street village and road village in which houses stand in a line along a street, and the round village seen in Germany, Poland and elsewhere」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「集合形態では、もっとも一般的な塊村(かいそん)、街路に沿って家屋が建ち並ぶ街村や路村、ドイツ、ポーランドなどにみられる円村などがあり」)
  • kotobank-sanson - 「Of irregular dispersed villages, the Tonami plain in Toyama prefecture, the lower Oi river, the Sanuki plain, the Izumo plain and others are given as examples」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「不規則な散村では、富山県礪波(となみ)平野、大井川下流、讃岐(さぬき)平野、出雲(いずも)平野その他の例があげられているが」)
  • suido-ishizue-yachida - 「For example, in Iriki-in in Satsuma province (Kagoshima prefecture) and the Ota estate in Bingo province (Hiroshima prefecture), it has become clear, from the names (the names of the holders of land whose rights were established) recorded in documents of the time that survive today in place names and the like, that in the late Heian period the paddies were almost without exception valley paddies opened in each small valley, using spring water, rainwater and the like, and that development had not advanced on the somewhat more open alluvial lowland downstream.」 and 「Following this scattering of the paddies, the houses too were scattered, and it is thought that the landscape was one in which small villages or dispersed settlements, one house or a few farmhouses each paired with a small valley paddy, were dotted here and there up each narrow branching valley.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「たとえば、薩摩国入来院(鹿児島県)や備後国大田荘(広島県)では、平安後期には水田はほとんど例外なく小さな谷ごとに拓けた谷地田で、湧水や雨水などを利用しており、下流のやや開けた沖積低地では開発は進んでいなかったことが、現在の地名などに当時の文書に記された名(権利が確立した土地の占有者の名前)が残っているところから明らかになっている。」 and original: 「このような水田の分散状況に応じて屋敷も分散し、１戸ないし数戸の農家が狭い面積の谷地田とセットになった小村ないしは散村の形が、枝分かれした細い谷ごとにポツンポツンと散らばっているような景観を呈していたものと考えられる。」)
