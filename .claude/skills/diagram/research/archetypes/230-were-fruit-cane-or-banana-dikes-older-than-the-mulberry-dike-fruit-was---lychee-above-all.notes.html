• zhwiki-guangdong-xinyu - 「completed in the seventeenth year of Kangxi (1678), one of Qu Dajun's Five Books of Qutuo」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「成書於康熙十七年（1678年），是屈大均《屈沱五書》之一。」)
  • guangdong-xinyu-22 - 「In the villages of Guangzhou's large counties, people often give up fertile fields to make dikes, planted with fruit trees. Lychee is the most common, tea and mulberry next, mandarin and orange after them; longan is mostly planted beside houses but also on the dikes. Below the dike is a pond for fish; it is drained at year's end and in spring used to sow rice seedlings.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「廣州諸大縣村落中，往往棄肥田以為基，以樹果木。荔支最多，茶、桑次之，柑、橙次之，龍眼多樹宅旁，亦樹於基。基下為池以畜魚，歲暮涸之，至春以播稻秧。」)
  • guangdong-xinyu-25 - 「In Guangzhou, all the jiwei embankments are planted with lychee and longan, and some people give up rice fields to plant them.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「廣州凡磯圍堤岸，皆種荔支、龍眼，或有棄稻田以種者。」) (in the entry on the lychee; 磯圍 is not defined on the page and is most plausibly the diked polders' and rivers' embankments)
  • pwsannong-zhusanjiao-nongyeshi - 「Fruit-dike fishponds first arose in Nanhai and Shunde in the mid-Ming. Mulberry-dike fishponds, which replaced them in the late Ming and early Qing, grew with sericulture and in the Qing became the dominant dike-pond type」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「明中叶最先在南海、顺德兴起果基鱼塘。明末清初代之而起的桑基鱼塘伴随蚕桑业不断发展，在清代成为占优势的基塘类型」)
  • guangdong-xinyu-25 - 「In a field, each mu can take twenty-odd lychee trees, and twice that many longan. Silt is made into mounds about two chi high so that floodwater does not reach them, and they are covered over with grass so that the fierce sun does not reach them.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「田每畝，荔支可二十餘本，龍眼倍之，以淤泥為墩，高二尺許，使潦水不及，以芻草蓋覆，使烈日不及。」) (the field is the rice field given up in the sentence before; a Qing chi is about 32 cm, and a mu of 240 square bu of 5 chi about 6,600 sq ft, so twenty-odd trees is one to about 300 sq ft - this page's arithmetic)
  • pwsannong-zhusanjiao-nongyeshi - 「Besides mulberry-dike fishponds and fruit-dike fishponds, the region also had rice-dike, cane-dike and fan-palm-dike fishponds, among other dike-pond types.」 (translated from the Chinese (simplified) by this project; original: 「除了桑基鱼塘、果基鱼塘以外，该地区还有稻基鱼塘、蔗基鱼塘、葵基鱼塘等基塘生产类型。」) (the sentence gives no date; it stands in the page's section on the Ming and Qing)
  • guangdong-xinyu-27 - 「In Zengcheng's Xizhou many grow bananas; after three or four years they cut them all down to plant white cane ... and after two years of white cane, bananas again.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「增城之西洲，人多種蕉，種至三四年，即盡伐以種白蔗 ... 而白蔗種至二年，又複種蕉。」); 「cane fields are nearly equal to rice fields」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「蔗田幾與禾田等矣」)
