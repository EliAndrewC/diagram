  • aburana-zhwiki - 「In Taiwan, every year after the second-crop rice is harvested before the onset of winter, some rice farmers scatter rape seed on the paddy fields, so that the fields grow full of rape and go on to flower, and before the next year's rice transplanting it is worked into the soil as green manure.」 (translated from the Chinese (traditional) by this project; original: 「在臺灣，每年入冬前第二期水稻收割之後，有些稻農會在稻田灑油菜的種子，使稻田長滿油菜、進而開花，次年水稻插秧前再打入土壤裡做為綠肥。」) (on the zh.wikipedia 油菜 page - the second page the registry entry names as read; the passage is NOT on the ja.wikipedia アブラナ page, whose text carries no 稻 at all) (In Taiwan, after the second rice crop is cut before winter, some rice farmers scatter rape seed on the paddy; it fills the field and flowers, and is plowed in as green manure before the next year's rice transplanting. (A Taiwan green-manure practice, as the registry says - not the Yangtze oilseed rotation.))
