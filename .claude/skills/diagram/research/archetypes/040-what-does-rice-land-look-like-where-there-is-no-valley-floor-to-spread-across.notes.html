• tanada-jawiki - 「Tanada (rice terraces) are paddies made on sloping ground.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「棚田（たなだ、英：rice terraces）とは、傾斜地に作られた水田のこと。」)
  • tanada-jawiki - 「For this reason, when in the Edo period the room for new-field development on the alluvial plains, reclamation included, grew scarce, paddies were next made on steep mountain slopes to raise the kokudaka on which a domain's economy rested, and many of what are now called tanada were made.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このため、江戸時代に干拓を含めた沖積平野の新田開発の余地が乏しくなると、藩経済の基盤の石高を増やすため、今度は急傾斜の山岳斜面上に水田がつくられ、現在でいう棚田が多くつくられた。」) (the paragraph is about western Japan; of the east the page says 「For this reason, even in the Edo period there were many places that never went so far as to cut tanada into the slopes of steep mountains」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このため、江戸時代に至っても、急峻な山地の傾斜面を切り開いて棚田をつくるまでに至らなかったところが多く」))
  • suido-ishizue-tanada - 「Unlike the new-field development carried out by lords, and later by great merchants, it was not planned work needing great wealth and advanced technique, but development of small areas that could be done almost with the hoe alone.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「領主や、後に豪商が行う新田開発とは異なり、豊かな経済力と高度な技術を必要とした計画的なものではなく、ほとんど鍬だけで行えるような小面積の開発であった。」) and 「Each paddy was not only narrow but irregular in shape; its water depended largely on the favor of the rain, and it lay on a mountainside reached only by climbing narrow, winding, steep paths.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「田の1枚1枚は狭いだけでなく不整形で、水は多分に雨の恵みに左右され、曲がりくねった傾斜のきつい細い道を歩いて登らなければならない山腹にあった。」)
  • titian-zhwiki - 「China's terraces are mainly distributed in the mountain country south of the Yangtze, most of them in Guangxi and Yunnan, because these places have more rain and many mountains, and the terraces are built along the mountains; of them the terraces of the Ailao mountains in Yunnan and of Longji in Longsheng, Guangxi, are the best known.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「中国梯田主要分布在江南山岭地区，其中广西、云南居多，这是因为这些地方雨水比较多，又多山，梯田依山而建，其中以云南哀牢山和广西龙胜龙脊梯田较为出名。」)
  • suido-ishizue-yachida - 「Everywhere in Japan one sees paddies made by opening small valleys among the hills」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本のいたるところで、山あいの小さな谷を拓いてつくられた水田が見られ」) and 「From this there is also a view that in the middle ages the yachida was the most stable farmland, the standard farmland.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「こうしたことから、中世においては谷地田こそが最も安定した耕地であり、標準的な耕地であったとする説もある。」)
  • yachida-jawiki - 「The yachida is a long, narrow paddy distributed over the wet ground of a valley.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「谷地田(やちた）は谷間の谷地に分布する細長い水田[1]。」) and 「It is also called yatsuda.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「谷津田（やつだ）ともいう[2][3]」)
  • kotobank-yatoda - 「Because a yato could use its spring water for irrigation and could be opened in small pieces, the opening of valleys like the yato was pursued vigorously, above all in the middle ages, and made the representative farm landscape of the middle ages.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「谷戸は湧水を用水とすることができ，小規模な開墾が可能であったから，とくに中世には，谷戸のような谷の開田が盛んに行われ，中世の代表的な耕地景観をつくりだした。」) and 「Such valley paddies are called yatoda and yachida in the Kanto region, tanada and yamada in the Kinki region, and sakoda in the Chugoku region.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そのような谷間の田を関東地方では〈谷戸田〉〈谷地田（やちだ）〉，近畿地方では〈棚田〉〈山田〉，中国地方では〈迫田（さこだ）〉などと呼んでいる。」)
