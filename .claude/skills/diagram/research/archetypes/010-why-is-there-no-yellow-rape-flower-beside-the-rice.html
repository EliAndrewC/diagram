Why is there no yellow rape flower beside the rice?

Sources: aburana-jawiki, aburana-zhwiki (autumn sowing, March-April cutting; rape into the harvested paddy), rice-rape-calendar-summary (the rape-harvest and rice-transplanting windows). The rice months, the Jiangnan rotation calendar and the sowing window "late Sept-mid Oct" have no readable source

RAPE (油菜, Japanese aburana, Chinese youcai - the oilseed) never stands beside rice: rice and rape are the two halves of one rotation in the SAME plot (rice May-Oct (the rice harvest month is unsourced - the footnoted source gives transplanting and rape-harvest dates but no harvest month); rape sown into the drained stubble Oct-Nov, flowering Mar-Apr), so they are never both standing - mixing them at any fraction depicts two seasons at once, and the real spring picture is yellow rape against BARE stubble, not against green rice. Showing rape would mean showing a whole different season of the same field, which these maps do not do.
