• kato-1999-ittanbu-kukaku - 「It is the plot replanning that Nishikimi Chodayu carried out on about 43 chobu at Inazato, Hozumi village, Motosu district, Gifu prefecture, from the autumn of Meiji 2 (1869) until before the rice was planted the next year. ... The farm roads and the ditches are 1 ken wide, and the bunds 2 shaku wide.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「錦見長太夫が岐阜県本巣郡穂積村稲里の約43町歩について，明治2(1869)年秋から翌年の水稲植付け前にかけて行った区画整理である。…農道と水路は1間幅，畦畔は2尺幅となっている。」) (a replanned layout on the eve of modern consolidation, not a measurement of an old field)
  • shokukaku-suiden-jawiki - 「The small bunds were 50 centimeters wide or less, had no core material, and are thought to have been rebuilt every cultivation season.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「小畦畔は幅50センチメートル以下で、芯材を持たず、耕期毎に作り替えられていたと考えられている。」) and 「Small-plot paddies are paddies made up of very small divisions, built in the Japanese archipelago from the Yayoi period through the Kofun period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「小区画水田（しょうくかくすいでん）とは、弥生時代から古墳時代にかけての日本列島で造られた、非常に小さな区画からなる水田。」) (inside larger bunds 「of a width of 1 meter or more」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「幅1メートル以上の」))
