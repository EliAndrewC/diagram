pwsannong-gengzuo-jishushi

"耕作技术史" ("History of tillage technique", title translated), in the encyclopedia of agricultural history on Zhihui Sannong, the agricultural knowledge service of China Agriculture Press (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/560252.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry outlining four stages of Chinese soil tillage from the Zhou period to the Ming and Qing: the ridge-and-furrow method, ridge and flat tillage together, improved wet and dry tillage, and rotation tillage.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says paddy tillage in the south had by the Sui to Yuan era become a plow, harrow and leveler sequence, and that Ming and Qing farmers set ridge shape and sowing place by how high the land stood, sowing in the furrow on high dry land and on the ridge on low wet land. It spans some two thousand years of China with no region or date for each claim and cites works only in passing, so it cannot date a particular practice.
