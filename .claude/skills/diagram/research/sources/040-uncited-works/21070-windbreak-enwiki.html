en.wikipedia "Windbreak" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windbreak)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on windbreaks (shelterbelts): rows of trees or shrubs planted to shelter fields, homes and roads, with a section on how wind slows behind a porous barrier.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the physical rule that wind speed falls to a minimum about 3 to 5 times the windbreak's height downwind and recovers beyond that, which holds for a hedge or grove anywhere. Its examples are modern (the American Great Plains Shelterbelt, Canadian prairie programs, an East German poster), it names no Asian practice, and the article itself flags its windbreak-organizations section as unsourced.
