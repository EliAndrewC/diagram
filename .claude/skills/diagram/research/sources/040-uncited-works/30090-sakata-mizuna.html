sakata-mizuna

Sakata Seed Corporation, "ミズナ、キョウナの育て方・栽培方法" ("Mizuna, kyona no sodatekata, saibai hoho", how to grow mizuna and kyona, title translated), in the Engei Tsushin gardening site, "Shippai shinai saibai lesson (yasai)" (updated 2025-05-13) (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/lesson-vegetable/detail_19)

What it is: A seed company's home-garden growing guide for mizuna (kyona) and mibuna, leaf greens developed in Kyoto, giving spacing, ridge width (80 cm), sowing, and harvest 30-40 days after sowing in summer and about 80 days in winter.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says mizuna developed in Kyoto, was long used for simmering, and takes its name from being grown with water held between ridges, and it gives ridge width, spacing and days to harvest. Its schedules and fertilizer amounts are for modern varieties, and the page gives no historical detail beyond the name's origin.
