fukuoka-murakami-suijin-hokora

Fukuoka Mami and Murakami Tetsuo (Chubu University), "水神祠から推定する河川の古地形と生活 -庄内川・矢田川合流点付近を中心に-" ("Suijinshi kara suitei suru kasen no kochikei to seikatsu", Old river terrain and life as inferred from water-god shrines, around the Shonai and Yada river confluence, title translated), a conference abstract of 2020 on the Rikusui-Tokai database site (in Japanese; https://rikusui-tokai.sakura.ne.jp/db/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/2020_08_fukuoka.pdf)

What it is: A short conference abstract surveying every shrine on the 1:25,000 map in a 13 square kilometer flood-prone area where the Shonai and Yada rivers meet in Moriyama ward, Nagoya, and asking what the water-god shrines show of the old river and of life beside it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts 35 shrines, of which about four in ten honor a water deity (5 dragon, 2 Inari, 6 Susanoo), and finds 11 small red shrines clustered along the old canal and drainage ditches. It measures shrine stone bases under 2 m against 2.8 m for flood storehouses, and doubts the shrines served as refuges. It is a short abstract from one lowland near Nagoya, and it reads old water cults from today's shrines, so its dates are inferred, not recorded.
