en.wikipedia "History of Chinese Buddhism" (the English Wikipedia article, title as written) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Chinese_Buddhism)

What it is: A long English Wikipedia article on Buddhism in China from its arrival under the Han to the present, taking each dynasty in turn: translation, the schools, the Tang suppression of 845, the Song and Ming, and then the twentieth-century revivals in China, Taiwan and abroad.

Why it applies, and its limits: For the premodern setting it supplies dated anchors, such as the 845 order to destroy 4,600 monasteries and 40,000 temples, and 458,855 monks and nuns recorded in 1021, and the Ming account of why women entered convents. It is a history of doctrine and institutions, not of how a monastery or temple was laid out, farmed or lived in, and about half of it concerns the twentieth century and after, which does not bear on a premodern map.
