bunka-kokuzenji-kuri

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "國前寺 庫裏" ("Kokuzen-ji, the priests' quarters and kitchen," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/157645)

What it is: A national database entry for the kuri of Kokuzen-ji in Higashi ward, Hiroshima, dated about 1671, 17.7 m by 13.2 m, single-story, gabled, tile-roofed, designated an Important Cultural Property in 1993.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured dimensions and roof form for a temple dwelling of the mid-Edo period and notes a corridor to the main hall and an earthen wall at the front. The commentary field is empty, so it gives only the building's data and says nothing of who lived in it or how many.
