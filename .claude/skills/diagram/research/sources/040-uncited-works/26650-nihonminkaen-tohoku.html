nihonminkaen-tohoku

Kawasaki City Japan Open-Air Folk House Museum, "東北の村" ("Villages of Tohoku", title translated), on the museum's site (in Japanese; https://www.nihonminkaen.jp/tohoku.html)

What it is: A museum page on two farmhouses moved from the Tohoku region: the Kudo house, an Iwate headman's house of the Nanbu bent-house type with a stable wing, and the Sugawara house, a Yamagata headman's house from a heavy-snow area. It gives each one's plan dimensions and date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives plan sizes and dates for two headmen's houses: the Kudo house of about 1751-1763 (19.2 m by 11.1 m, with a 7.6 m by 6.3 m stable wing and a hearth by the earth floor) and the Sugawara house of the late 18th century (15.8 m by 9.6 m, with a snow-entry porch). Both are houses of village headmen, larger than an ordinary farmhouse, and both are single surviving examples from Iwate and Yamagata only.
