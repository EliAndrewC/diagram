kotobank-michihaba

"道幅" ("michihaba", road width), in the Concise Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (精選版 日本国語大辞典), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%81%93%E5%B9%85-2085287)

What it is: A short Kotobank dictionary entry defining michihaba as the width of a road, with one cited example from the Kanpo-period collection of shogunal edicts dated the sixth month of 1657.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its one example is an order of 1657 to leave a road six ken wide, measured in Kyoto ken, when building houses, so it records that road width was fixed by official order in the mid-seventeenth century. It does not say where the order applied, what kind of road it meant, or how the width compared with roads elsewhere.
