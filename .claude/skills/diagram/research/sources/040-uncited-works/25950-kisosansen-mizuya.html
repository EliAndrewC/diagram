kisosansen-mizuya

Kiso Sansen Park Center, "水屋（輪中の農家）" ("Mizuya (a farmhouse of the wajyu)", title translated), Kiso Sansen Park Center, Kiso Sansen National Government Park (in Japanese; https://www.kisosansenkoen.jp/~center/mkisosansenasobu_mizuya.html)

What it is: A park page on the reconstructed wajyu farmhouse at the Kiso Sansen Park Center, a mid-Meiji farm of a fairly wealthy household made of a main house, a mizuya and a barn. It explains the flood-adapted features of the delta of the Kiso, Nagara and Ibi rivers: the ring dike, the raised mizuya store, the flood boat kept at the door, the rooms left open to let water through, and the Buddhist altar hauled up to the second floor.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the flood-adapted farm of the three-river lowland: a raised plot for a store house, a boat kept at the door, and floods too frequent for the ring dike to prevent. It describes a reconstruction of a mid-Meiji farm, so the period is late for a premodern setting, and it gives no dimensions, no plan and no count of households per ring dike.
