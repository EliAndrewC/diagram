Nakajima Gakusho, "清代徽州の山林経営・紛争・宗族形成 : 祁門凌氏文書の研究" ("Shindai Kishu no sanrin keiei, funso, sozoku keisei: Kimon Ryo-shi monjo no kenkyu", Mountain-forest management, disputes and lineage formation in Qing Huizhou: a study of the Ling lineage documents of Qimen, title translated), Socio-Economic History (Shakai Keizai Shigaku) 72(1), 2006 (in Japanese, with an English abstract; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/sehs/72/1/72_KJ00005697845/_article/-char/ja)

What it is: The J-STAGE record of a journal article on one small lineage of Qimen county, Huizhou, that farmed and managed mountain forest, studied through the lineage's own documents.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says forest ownership was divided into shares (gufen) with forestry earnings split by share, and that the forest economy grew from the mid-eighteenth century and fed disputes, a lawsuit, common property and the early-nineteenth-century adoption of a lineage model. It concerns one undistinguished lineage in one hill county of southern Anhui, and the abstract gives no plot sizes or planting practice.
