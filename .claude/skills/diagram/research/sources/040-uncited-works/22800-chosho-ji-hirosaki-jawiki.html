ja.wikipedia 長勝寺 (弘前市) ("Choshoji (Hirosaki)", the Soto temple in Hirosaki, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/長勝寺_(弘前市))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Choshoji, a Soto Zen temple in Hirosaki, Aomori, founded in 1528 and moved to its present site in 1610, with its designated cultural properties and buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the temple's moves (Tanesato, Kata-Oura, Horikoshi, then Hirosaki in 1610), says it was the regional Soto office in the Edo period, and notes that the road to it is called the Zenrin district with thirty-three temples. It gives no plan, area or building dimensions, and its own banner says it lacks cited sources.
