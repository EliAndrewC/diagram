kotobank-yashikigamae-nihon-kokugo

"屋敷構" ("yashiki-gamae", the way a house plot is built), in the Shogakukan Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (精選版 日本国語大辞典), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/屋敷構)

What it is: A short dictionary entry giving two senses: the way a house plot is laid out, and a castle simplified to the form of a mansion, with an early seventeenth-century example from the Koyo Gunkan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records that a lord's stronghold could be built as little more than a fortified mansion, quoting a passage from the Koyo Gunkan about a castle in Kai. It is a definition and one quotation, with no dimensions or plan, and does not say how common such a form was.
