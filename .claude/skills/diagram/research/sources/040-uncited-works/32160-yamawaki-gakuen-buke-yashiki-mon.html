Yamawaki Gakuen, "重要文化財 武家屋敷門移設工事が完了しました" ("The relocation of the Important Cultural Property samurai-residence gate is complete", title translated), school blog post of 2016-10-05, on the Yamawaki Gakuen site (in Japanese; https://yamawaki.ed.jp/blog/4335)

What it is: A short school-news post on moving a gate from the Edo residence of the senior councillor Honda Mino-no-kami Tadatami (Okazaki domain, 50,000 koku) to the school grounds in Akasaka, Tokyo, completed in 2016.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the gate stood on the site of today's Tokyo Central Post Office in Marunouchi as the front gate of the residence of successive senior councillors, was burned in the great fire of 1862 and rebuilt at once, and that only three daimyo-residence gates survive in Japan. It gives the gate's history and no dimensions or plan of the residence, and it is about an Edo daimyo mansion in the capital, not a provincial samurai house.
