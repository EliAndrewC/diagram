bunka-kyu-yokota-ke-omoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧横田家住宅（長野県長野市松代町松代） 主屋" ("Former Yokota house, Matsushiro, Nagano; main house," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/121724)

What it is: A national database entry for the main house of a middle-ranking samurai residence of the Matsushiro domain (Sanada house), built 1794, a thatched hip-roofed two-story house 16.2 m by 8.7 m with an entrance hall and guest rooms, designated an Important Cultural Property in 1986.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the main house's size, roof, date and the statement that the gate, retirement house and outbuildings survive with little alteration, making it a model of a middle samurai compound. It gives the house's dimensions only, not the lot size or layout of the grounds, and speaks for one domain's mid-ranking retainer in a late-Edo year.
