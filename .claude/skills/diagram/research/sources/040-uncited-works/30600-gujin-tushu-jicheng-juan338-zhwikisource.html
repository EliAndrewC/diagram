"欽定古今圖書集成/方輿彙編/職方典/第0338卷 汾州府部彙考二" (Qinding gujin tushu jicheng, Fangyu huibian, Zhifang dian, volume 338, "Fenzhou prefecture, collected studies, part 2", title translated), the imperially commissioned encyclopedia, on Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/欽定古今圖書集成/方輿彙編/職方典/第0338卷)

What it is: The Wikisource text of one volume of the Qing imperial encyclopedia's geography section, on Fenzhou prefecture in Shanxi: its city and county walls and moats, its passes, bridges and ferries, its enfeoffed lords and princes, and its government offices, quoted from the prefectural gazetteer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives wall figures for a prefectural city and its six outlying counties and one department (Yongning zhou), for example Fenzhou's circuit of 9 li 13 bu, height of 3 zhang 2 chi, four gates and a moat that takes in streams, and it lists the rooms and buildings of a county office compound (gate tower, hearing hall, granaries, jail, clerks' rooms). The heights are given with their later raising (Fenzhou's from 3 zhang 2 chi to 4 zhang 8 chi in 1569), so each figure belongs to a stated rebuilding, the text covers one Shanxi prefecture to the late Kangxi years (its last dated entries are Kangxi 45 and 48, 1706 and 1709), and its punctuation was added by a program and volunteers.
