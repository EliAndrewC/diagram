yamamoto-hirao-miyauchi-2016

Yamamoto Naohiko, Hirao Kazuhiro and Miyauchi Anri, "歴史的風土特別保存地区における民家の屋敷構えに関する研究 明日香村の奥山・飛鳥・河原・野口・岡・島庄の六大字を事例として" ("Rekishiteki fudo tokubetsu hozon chiku ni okeru minka no yashiki-gamae ni kansuru kenkyu", A study of farmhouse compound layout in a special historic landscape preservation district: six sections of Asuka village, title translated), Journal of Architecture and Planning (Transactions of AIJ) 81(721), 2016 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/aija/81/721/81_675/_article/-char/ja/)

What it is: The J-STAGE record, with an English abstract, of a field study of farmhouse compounds in six village sections of Asuka, Nara, comparing clustered villages with linear villages along east-west and north-south streets.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows how main house, attached wing (tsunoya) and further outbuildings were added to a compound, and that the common development patterns follow the traditional reading of a house's position and orientation. The compounds are those standing in the 2010s and the abstract gives no dates or measurements, so it shows the rule of placement, not the Edo-period plan.
