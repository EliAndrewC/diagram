Osawa Hiroshi and Kinoshita Saho (大澤 啓志, 木下 紗帆), "西伊豆におけるランドスケープと神社の配置構造特性" ("Nishiizu ni okeru randosukepu to jinja no haichi kozo tokusei", landscape and the layout structure of shrines in West Izu, title translated), Landscape Research Japan (ランドスケープ研究) 86(5): 511-516, 2023, on J-STAGE (in Japanese; https://jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jila/86/5/86_511/_article/-char/ja)

What it is: The J-STAGE record page of a 2023 paper, giving its bibliographic data and English abstract; the full text is a separate PDF that this page does not carry. The paper surveys 167 shrines in the Nishiizu area of Shizuoka and reports where they stand against the terrain, the settlements and hazard maps.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its abstract says shrines at the foot of mountains were the most frequent (51.5%) and that shrines tended to stand at the boundary between inhabited land and the mountains, which bears on where a village shrine sits. It is a coastal, mountain-and-cove district surveyed in the present day, with hazard maps and priest interviews as its method; the page gives no dates for the shrines and none of the paper's body.
