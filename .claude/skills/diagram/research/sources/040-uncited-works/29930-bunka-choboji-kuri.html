bunka-choboji-kuri

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "長母寺庫裡" ("Chobo-ji, the priests' quarters and kitchen," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/140848)

What it is: A national database entry for the kuri (kitchen and living quarters) of the Chobo-ji temple in Higashi ward, Nagoya, built 1828, single-story, tiled, 361 square meters, registered in 1999.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the building as nine bays by five with a gabled entrance end, joined to the main hall by a corridor and guest room with a tea room at one corner, and says the master carpenter and date are known from the ridge tablet. It gives one temple's dwelling, which the note calls grand in scale (雄大な規模) with heavy beam framing, so it is a large kuri rather than a typical one; it gives no comparison to other temples and no number of residents.
