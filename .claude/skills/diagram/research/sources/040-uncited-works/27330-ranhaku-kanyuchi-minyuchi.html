ranhaku-kanyuchi-minyuchi

Ranzan Town (嵐山町), "用語解説：官有地・民有地の区分" ("Glossary: the division into state land and private land", title translated), in the Ranzan Town Web Museum, vol. 6 (嵐山町web博物誌), on the Ranzan town site (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09terminology.html)

What it is: A one-page glossary that accompanies the town's Hiki County temple register pages. It lists the Meiji land categories, state land of four classes and private land of three, quoting the 1874 proclamation (Dajokan proclamation no. 120) that set them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It explains the label on each temple's precinct: shrine grounds are state land of the first class, while temples, schools, preaching halls and hospitals that are not private land fall in the fourth class; private land of the second class is land held by several people, a village or several villages with proof of title, such as schools, village storehouses (gogura), pastures, mowing grounds and shrines and temples not on state land. That tells a reader that the register's precinct areas record a tax and title category, not a measured plan. The page quotes the proclamation only in outline, dates from 1874, and says nothing of how the categories were applied before then.
