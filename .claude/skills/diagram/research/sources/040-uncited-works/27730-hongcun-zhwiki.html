zh.wikipedia 宏村村 ("Hongcun cun", Hongcun village, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%AE%8F%E6%9D%91)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Hongcun, a World Heritage village in Yi county, Anhui, founded in the Southern Song, describing its water channel, ox-shaped plan and surviving Ming and Qing houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the village was founded in 1131 by the Wang clan, laid out afresh with a fengshui master's advice in the Yongle years, and brought a channel nearly 1 meter wide from the west river through its lanes to a half-moon pond and a south lake. It gives a village area of about 280,000 square meters, 1 Ming, 102 Qing and 34 Republic-era buildings, and the 1855 house of a salt merchant at about 2,100 square meters with over 60 rooms around nine light wells. It describes a wealthy Huizhou merchant-clan village kept as a showpiece, the counts are of survivors, and the page is flagged for few sources.
