kotobank-sakuhosei

"錯圃制" ("sakuhosei", the scattered-plot system, title translated), quoted in the entry on the monwari system in the older Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/錯圃制-1323949)

What it is: A Kotobank stub for sakuhosei, with no entry of its own and only one excerpt, from the older Heibonsha encyclopedia's article on the monwari land system of Satsuma domain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in Satsuma each member of a mon group was given about equal land, scattered across the whole village, and that plots were reallotted every few years, which the excerpt says spread typhoon losses evenly and checked class divisions among farmers. The evidence is one Satsuma domain's system, a southern typhoon region, and the page does not say it was general.
