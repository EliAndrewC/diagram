sengoku-oki-tama-bukeyashiki

Yamagata Okitama Tourism Council, "武家屋敷" ("bukeyashiki", samurai residences), page on the Sengoku kanko Yamagata site (in Japanese; https://sengoku.oki-tama.jp/?p=log&l=92615)

What it is: A short tourism page on the lower-samurai residences (harakata-shu) of Yonezawa, which stand in the southern districts of the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Uesugi placed lower samurai outside the castle town after moving from Aizu because the town could not hold every retainer, that they lived half as farmers and half as warriors and opened wasteland, and that a stone wall kept out Matsukawa floods while an ukogi (うこぎ) hedge kept out intruders, so it ties a lower-samurai house to a hedge and a flood wall. The explanations are the page's, with no source, and the wall and hedge are said to be 400 years old from Naoe Kanetsugu, which the page does not support.
