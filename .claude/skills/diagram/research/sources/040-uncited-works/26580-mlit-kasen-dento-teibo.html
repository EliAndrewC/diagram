mlit-kasen-dento-teibo

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, "河川伝統技術データベース：分類別リスト【堤防】" ("Traditional river techniques database: classified list, levees", title translated), on the ministry's river bureau site (in Japanese; https://www.mlit.go.jp/river/pamphlet_jirei/kasen/rekishibunka/kasengijutsu07-01.html)

What it is: A national ministry's table of more than a hundred named levees, listed by river and regional bureau, each with a period label and a short account of what it was, who built it and why. It covers Japanese levee forms from the Heian period to the mid-20th century, among them the discontinuous kasumi levee, the village-encircling levee, the wajuu ring levee, the Shingen levee and levees backed by planted pines, bamboo or cedar.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated, named examples of premodern levee forms and a few dimensions, such as the Naoe stone levee of the 1600s at about 5.4 m across the top and 1.5 to 1.8 m high, and the Ogakoi levee of 1608-1609 along the Kiso River at 48 km. The entries are uneven: some are 19th and 20th century works, several say the record is lost or the builder cannot be confirmed (the Satsuma levee), and most give no dimensions, so each entry must be read for its own period before a size or form is used.
