bunka-yokota-jutaku-omoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧横田家住宅（長野県長野市松代町松代） 主屋" ("Kyu Yokota-ke jutaku (Nagano-ken Nagano-shi Matsushiro-machi Matsushiro) omoya", the former Yokota residence, main house, title translated), on Bunka Isan Online (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/121724)

What it is: A national cultural-heritage database entry for the main house of the former Yokota residence in Matsushiro, Nagano, an Important Cultural Property designated in 1986. It gives the date, dimensions and roof of the house and a short note on the estate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured main house of a mid-rank samurai of the Matsushiro domain: built in 1794, 16.2 m by 8.7 m, hipped and thatched, single-storied except for a two-story projection on the south side, with an entrance platform and guest room, and a front gate, retired-parent's house and other outbuildings still standing. It gives only the main house's outer size, not its room plan or the lot's size, and describes one house of one domain in the late Edo period.
