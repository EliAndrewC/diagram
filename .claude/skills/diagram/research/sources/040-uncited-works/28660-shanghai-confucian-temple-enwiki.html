en.wikipedia "Confucian Temple of Shanghai" (上海文廟, Shanghai Wenmiao), on English Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucian_Temple_of_Shanghai)

What it is: A short English Wikipedia stub on the Confucian temple in the old city of Shanghai, giving its founding in the Yuan dynasty when Shanghai became a county seat, its destruction in the fighting of 1851-1855, its rebuilding and its present use.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that Shanghai's Confucian temple was founded when the place was raised to a county seat, that a Confucian temple was typically a combination of temple and school and the county's most prestigious learning institution, and that it is similar to the temples at Qufu and Beijing but on a smaller scale. It gives no dimensions, plan or buildings for the temple, and the present site is a rebuilding after the mid-nineteenth-century destruction, so the page does not show the layout of a premodern county temple-school; after that destruction it was rebuilt at what the page calls the present site, and the page's only two references are a Rough Guide and a Time Out listing.
