kubota-rice-straw-in-housing

Kubota Corporation, "【稲と住】 屋根や壁など生活のさまざまな場面で使われた藁" ("Rice and housing: straw used for roofs, walls and many other parts of life", title translated), from 稲と衣食住, on Kubota no Tanbo (in Japanese; https://www.kubota.co.jp/kubotatanbo/history/life/living.html)

What it is: A company educational page listing the uses of rice straw, hulls and bran in a farm household: thatch, wall plaster, bedding, fertilizer, fuel for the hearth, fodder, bales, rope, sacred straw rope and carrying baskets.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a stone straw-beating block was an essential item of a farmhouse's earthen-floor room, that straw was mixed into wall earth, and that hulls and straw served as bedding for cattle, horses and chickens before going to the compost, which shows what a farm yard held. It is undated and general, gives no figures, and does not say which uses were old, which were regional, or how much straw a household needed.
