yokohama-kanagawa-kosatsuba

Kanagawa Ward, City of Yokohama (横浜市神奈川区), "高札場（こうさつば）" ("kosatsuba", the notice-board site, title translated), in the Kanagawa ward's history spots (神奈川区の歴史 スポット紹介), last updated 12 March 2021 (in Japanese; https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kanagawa/shokai/rekishi/spot/kosatuba.html)

What it is: A ward page on the notice-board place (kosatsuba) of Kanagawa post town, which stood west of the present police station. A reconstruction in front of the Kanagawa district center is described at about 5 m wide, 3.5 m high and 1.5 m deep.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the board existed to make the shogunate's laws and rules known to ordinary people, that it was an important facility of a post town, and that it disappeared in the Meiji era as other means of communication arrived. It gives the size of a reconstruction of the Kanagawa board taken from a pamphlet, which is one post town on a major highway, and it says nothing of village notice boards or how they were sited.
