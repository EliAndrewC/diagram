bunka-jizoin-hondo-shoro

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "地蔵院　本堂　鐘楼" ("Jizo-in, main hall and bell tower," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/133558)

What it is: A national database entry for a temple's main hall (1700) and bell tower (1644) in Sekicho, Kameyama, Mie, with their bay counts and roof forms, designated an Important Cultural Property in 1988.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the main hall at five bays by four with a single-bay entrance porch and the bell tower at one bay square, a pair of dated measured temple buildings of a roadside town. The explanatory text of the entry is empty, so it gives only the structural data, no bay lengths in meters, and no account of how the buildings were used.
