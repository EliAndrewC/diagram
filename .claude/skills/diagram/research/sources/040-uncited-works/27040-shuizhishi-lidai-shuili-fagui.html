Knowledge Service Assistant (知识服务助手) of the China Water & Power Press, "中国历代水利法规" ("Water-conservancy laws and regulations of Chinese dynasties", title translated), a water-conservancy encyclopedia entry (in Chinese; https://www.shuizhishi.cn/c/2020-04-09/524112.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry surveying the laws and regulations on water in China from the Zhou through the Qing, dynasty by dynasty. It covers the Qin farm-field statute, Han, Tang and Song provisions, and the Ming and Qing codes, with duties of officials and how works were paid for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the legal setting for water works: the Qin statute fixing the months for clearing channels and mending embankments and ponds, the Tang rule that a prefect or magistrate inspects embankments and, where repair is needed, has it done after the autumn harvest, with a flogging for an official who neglects them, and the Qing division of works into official and popular ones, the popular split by whether the state aided or oversaw them or the people or merchants built them alone. It states rules as written in codes and gives no account of how well any was enforced in a village. It also moves from the Zhou to the Qing in one list, so a rule must be dated by the dynasty it is listed under.
