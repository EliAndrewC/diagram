What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Kamigamo Hondori, the east-west street in Kita Ward, Kyoto, that runs to Kamigamo Shrine, known for the walled houses of the shrine families (shake) along the Myojin stream.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the shake townscape of stone-faced earthen walls, tile-roofed gates and small bridges over the Myojin stream, a surviving house whose garden draws water from the stream through a pond and back, and a camphor tree of about 500 years at a small shrine on the stream. It is a present-day account of a preserved street (designated a traditional building district in 1988), the street was joined and extended in the 1950s, and it gives no date for the houses or any dimension for a plot.
