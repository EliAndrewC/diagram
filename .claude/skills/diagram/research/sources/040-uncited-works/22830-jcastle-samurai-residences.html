Jcastle, "Samurai Residences" (武家屋敷), an introductory article on Jcastle.info (https://jcastle.info/view/Samurai_Residences)

What it is: A signed introductory article on a castle-enthusiast website, describing the parts of a surviving samurai residence (gate, main house, garden, storehouses, stable, vegetable garden) with the Yokota residence at Matsushiro as its example, and comparing castle towns.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says only samurai were allowed walled homes and gates, that nagayamon gates were common, and that plot size varied by castle town, with Hagi and Kanazawa compact and Kakunodate and Matsushiro larger. The author states that it rests on his own impressions from visits and that he found no good Japanese book on the subject; it describes surviving houses, some rebuilt or reduced, and gives no measurements.
