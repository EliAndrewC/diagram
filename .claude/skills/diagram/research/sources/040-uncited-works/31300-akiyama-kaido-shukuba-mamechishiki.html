akiyama-kaido-shukuba-mamechishiki

Akiyama Yutaka (秋山 豊), "改訂 街道と宿場の豆知識" ("Revised: Small facts about the highways and the post towns", title translated), a ten-page handout, on the author's personal site (in Japanese; https://akiyama.my.coocan.jp/kaiteikaidoumame.pdf)

What it is: A signed ten-page handout surveying Japan's highways and their post towns from antiquity to the Edo period, centered on the Tokaido: the facilities of the road (barriers, mile mounds, roadside trees, signposts) and of the post town (the headquarters, the honjin and waki-honjin inns, inns, teahouses, gates and the bent entrance, the notice board), with sizes for several of them. It ends with the travelers who used the road, roadside stone monuments and a short reading list.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives drawn-from-the-page dimensions for road furniture and post-town buildings: a mile mound of five ken (about 9 m) square and one jo (about 3 m) high, road classes from 6 ken down to 3 shaku wide, honjin floors mostly 220-300 tsubo (Narumi 600, Kumagaya 700) and inns of 10-50 tsubo. It states the road widths as set out in the "Ieyasu one hundred articles" and gives no source for the other figures, and it describes the highways under the Tokugawa shogunate, so a local lord's road or a village lane is outside it.
