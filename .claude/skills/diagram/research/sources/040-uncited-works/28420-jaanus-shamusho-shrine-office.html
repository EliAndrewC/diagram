jaanus-shamusho-shrine-office

Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System (JAANUS), "shamusho 社務所" ("shrine office", title translated), entry in the online dictionary of Japanese architecture and art (https://aisf.or.jp/~jaanus/deta/s/shamusho.htm)

What it is: A dictionary entry defining the shamusho, the group of administrative and sometimes commercial buildings at a Shinto shrine, with what they held and how large shrines named their offices.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says each ancient shrine ran its own affairs, often at the administrator's own house at small shrines, and that the shamusho became a recognized element from the later 19th century, when shrines were ranked, with offices, quarters for visiting officials, servants and a storeroom, and at the largest shrines rooms for ceremonies and the sale of amulets. That means the building as defined is largely a Meiji-era arrangement: for earlier or small shrines the entry supports only that business was done at a private house, and it gives no sizes or plans.
