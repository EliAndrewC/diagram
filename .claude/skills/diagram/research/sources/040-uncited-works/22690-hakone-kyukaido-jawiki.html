ja.wikipedia 箱根旧街道 ("Hakone kyukaido", the old Hakone highway, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/箱根旧街道)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the stretch of the old Tokaido across Hakone and Mishima, a national historic site, listing the dates of its designation and the parts included.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms that the designated stretch includes stone paving (ishidatami), roadside trees and mile mounds (ichirizuka), among them the Nishikida mound built on a 1604 order at 28 ri from Nihonbashi and pairs of mounds at 23 and 26 ri. The article is almost wholly the designation history of 1922 to 2009 and gives no dimensions, paving date or road width, so it shows what survives on a mountain section of the highway and nothing of how it was laid.
