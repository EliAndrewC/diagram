info-toyama-aimoto-bridge

Toyama Tourism Organization, "愛本橋" ("Aimoto Bridge", title translated), in the Toyama travel guide Info Toyama (in Japanese; https://info-toyama.com/attractions/51015)

What it is: A tourist-guide entry on the Aimoto Bridge over the Kurobe River in Kurobe, Toyama, a red arch bridge, with the history of the earlier bridges on the site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the present bridge is the twelfth, replacing one lost in the 1969 floods, and that the earlier one was a 63 m hanebashi (a cantilevered bridge) built in the early Edo period, used by the Kaga, Daishoji and Toyama domains' processions to Edo on the upper Hokkoku highway. It gives the length and the date of building only, nothing of its structure, its cost or its upkeep, and the surviving form is the modern arch, not the Edo bridge.
