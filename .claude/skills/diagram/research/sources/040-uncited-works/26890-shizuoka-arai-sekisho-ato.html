shizuoka-arai-sekisho-ato

Shizuoka Prefecture, "新居関跡 現存する日本唯一の関所建物" ("The Arai checkpoint site, the only surviving checkpoint building in Japan", title translated), in the "My Shizuoka Japan No. 1 100" series on the Shizuoka Prefecture official website (in Japanese; https://www.pref.shizuoka.jp/kensei/information/myshizuoka/1002263/1040950/1011195.html)

What it is: A prefectural page on the Arai checkpoint (Imagire checkpoint) beside Lake Hamana, set up in 1600 and moved twice after earthquakes and tsunami. It lists the buildings of the Edo-period station and which survive.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the parts of a checkpoint (the inspection hall, the audience room, rest rooms for officials and foot soldiers, a women's inspection long house) and says women passing were examined by an inspector who was the mother of a foot soldier and lived in that long house. The standing buildings date from a rebuilding of 1855-1858 after the 1854 earthquake and were repaired in 1971, and it gives no dimensions or plan.
