zh-yue.wikipedia 風水林 ("fengshui lin", fengshui woods; Cantonese-language article, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh-yue.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%A2%A8%E6%B0%B4%E6%9E%97)

What it is: A short Cantonese Wikipedia article on the fengshui wood, a grove kept or planted around a village to improve its setting, with its Lingnan origin and its Korean and Ryukyuan counterparts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the wood is kept or planted by custom, usually on the hill behind a south-facing Lingnan village, with a stream in front; villagers do not fell it, and plant trees useful in daily life; it breaks wind, slows a fast stream that runs straight at the houses and holds the soil against landslides. It names the Korean bibo-rim and Ryukyuan hogo-rin as kin, but gives no sizes, ratios or dates, and speaks of Lingnan (the far south) first; the books it lists are on Hong Kong's woods.
