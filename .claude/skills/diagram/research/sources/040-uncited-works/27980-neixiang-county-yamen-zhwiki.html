zh.wikipedia 内乡县衙 ("the Neixiang county yamen", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%86%85%E4%B9%A1%E5%8E%BF%E8%A1%99)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the county yamen of Neixiang, Henan, which it calls the best preserved Qing county office in China, giving its rebuilding history, its axial plan and its jail.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the Qing county yamen's layout rule (faces south, civil to the left and military to the right, offices in front and residence behind, jail to the south) and its gate sequence of five gates, with three main halls on the axis, 117 rooms on 6,700 square meters, and the jail in the southwest corner. The standing buildings date from the rebuilding of 1894 under a magistrate from Shaoxing who, the article says, mixed northern and southern styles, only the central axis survives, and the article itself carries a notice that some of its citations may be inapplicable or misread.
