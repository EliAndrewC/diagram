hyogo-koukohaku-koinumaru

Hyogo Prefectural Museum of Archaeology, "小犬丸遺跡出土品一括（41点）（平成12年度指定）" ("Koinumaru site finds, a set of 41 items, designated 2000", title translated), in its Main Collection pages (in Japanese; https://www.hyogo-koukohaku.jp/modules/collection/index.php?action=PageView&page_id=28)

What it is: A museum collection page on the 41 finds from the Koinumaru site in Tatsuno, designated as a prefectural important cultural property, and on why the site is held to be the Fuse post station.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says tile finds, the place-name and the supposed Sanyodo route first pointed to the 'Fuse station' of the Engishiki, and that a 1985 dig 200 m east turned up pottery and tablets inscribed 'station' and 'Fuse', a well and part of the Sanyodo. The finds are listed as 3 wooden tablets, 16 ink-inscribed pottery pieces, 1 incised pottery piece and 21 wooden items; the page gives no dimensions or building plan of the post station, though it says later digs by the Tatsuno city board of education are bringing the station's central buildings to light and cites Hyogo cultural-property survey report no. 66, the closer sources for a plan.
