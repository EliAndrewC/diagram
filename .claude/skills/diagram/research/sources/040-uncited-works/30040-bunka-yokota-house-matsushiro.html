bunka-yokota-house-matsushiro

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧横田家住宅" ("Kyu Yokota-ke jutaku", the former Yokota residence, title translated), in the Museum List of Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/museums/detail/12678)

What it is: A museum listing for the former house of the Yokota family in Matsushiro, Nagano, a mid-ranking samurai family of the Matsushiro domain with a 150-koku stipend, whose thatched main house, retirement house, gate, two storehouses, pond garden and large vegetable plot survive.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a mid-ranking domain samurai's house as a compound: three thatched buildings, a gate, two storehouses, a garden with a pond, and a wide vegetable garden. The page is a visitor listing with opening hours and no dimensions, and it does not say which of these parts date from the Edo period rather than from later repair.
