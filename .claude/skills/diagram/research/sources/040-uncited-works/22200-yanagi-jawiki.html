yanagi-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "ヤナギ" (willow, the genus Salix, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ヤナギ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on willows (genus Salix), covering their form, species, riverbank habitat, uses, medicine and cultural associations in Japan, China and Europe.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says willows are planted along rivers and ponds in Japan as flood protection, that cuttings root easily and are used as stakes for slope and bank work, and that Europe wove willow shoots into baskets and fences from prehistory; it also names the Chang'an custom of giving a willow twig to a departing traveler. The page carries a notice that it cites no sources, its Japanese farming content is brief and undated, and its detailed craft material (pollarded willows, withy fields) is about the Low Countries, not East Asia.
