yangyujing-agri-history

"养鱼经" ("Yang yu jing", the Classic of Fish Raising, title translated), on the Agricultural History site of the Institute for the History of Natural Sciences, Chinese Academy of Sciences (in Chinese; http://agri-history.ihns.ac.cn/books/onfishing.htm)

What it is: The Chinese text of a short fish-raising passage presented as a dialogue in which Fan Li (范蠡, named in the opening line) answers how he made his fortune, and it describes how to stock and run a carp pond.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a premodern Chinese pond design: a pond of six mu with nine islets in it, stocked with carp in the second month, with soft-shelled turtles added so the fish do not leave, and a stated yearly return. The page prints no date, edition or translation, and it is an ideal recipe with round figures, so it shows what the text prescribed, not what a working pond measured.
