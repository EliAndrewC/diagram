kotobank-doboita

"溝板" ("doboita" or "mizoita", the ditch board), entries in the Digital Daijisen and the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/溝板-642208)

What it is: A short Kotobank page of two dictionary entries defining doboita, the board laid over a ditch or drain, with Edo-period quotations.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms that boards covering a town ditch were an ordinary thing by the 1760s, citing a book of 1766 and one of 1763 to 1769, the latter showing a person stepping through one. It gives no dimensions, material or placement rules, and the entries are one-line definitions.
