shosai-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 書斎 ("shosai", the study, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9B%B8%E6%96%8E)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the shosai, a room in a scholar's or writer's house set apart for reading and writing, with its Chinese names and a section on the Western study from the fifteenth to the nineteenth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the room is called shufang or wenfang in Chinese and that it holds a desk and writing tools as well as books, unlike a plain book store, and that the Japanese study until the early Showa era usually had a low desk on tatami. Most of the text is the European history of the study, taken from a single 1987 essay, and the page gives no plan, size or date for a Japanese or Chinese study, so it supports only that such a room existed and what it held.
