ja.wikipedia 柵 ("saku", the fence; the shigarami section on the weir-fence of stakes and crosspieces, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/しがらみ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the fence (saku), reached from the redirect for shigarami, a stake-and-crosspiece weir. Most of it is about modern fences by use and material; one section covers the shigarami, which it says turned a river's flow and was used in intakes and reinforced pond banks.

Why it applies, and its limits: The shigarami section says that excavations show it was also a buried foundation structure, that it was built into river intakes once paddy irrigation spread, and later used as the frame for earthen pond and canal banks, with traces regularly found in Edo's reclaimed ground. The section is a few sentences with no citation of its own, gives no dates or dimensions, and the rest of the page (airport, rail, road and playground fences, aluminum and mesh) is modern engineering with nothing for the setting.
