zh.wikipedia "荆州城墙" ("The Jingzhou city wall", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8D%86%E5%B7%9E%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the city wall of Jingzhou in Hubei, a nationally protected monument, with its rebuilding history from the Han earth wall to the Qing brick wall and its measured dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the surviving wall of a Qing prefectural seat in figures: a perimeter of 11.821 km around an irregular rectangle 3.75 km by 1.2 km enclosing 4.50 square km, a height of 8.83 m, a top 3 to 5 m wide and a thickness of about 10 m, with 4,567 crenels, 26 gun platforms and 6 drains, and it says the wall seen today is the 1646 rebuild. These are the dimensions of the wall as it stands now, which the page says gained three new gates after 1970, and it does not say which of them are original.
