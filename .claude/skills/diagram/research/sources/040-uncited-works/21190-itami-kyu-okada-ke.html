itami-kyu-okada-ke

Itami City cultural properties office, "旧岡田家" ("The former Okada house", title translated), Itami cultural property list (in Japanese; https://itami-bunbora.main.jp/bunkazaiitiran/10.okadake.html)

What it is: A city page on the former Okada house and sake brewery in Itami, Hyogo: a townhouse shop built in 1674, a brewery added about 1715, and a wash area and boiler house beside it, designated an Important Cultural Property in 1992.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a surviving merchant shop to 1674 from its ridge-beam tablet and the brewery to about 1715, and describes their form (a tiled gabled shop with a low second floor and a long earthen-floor passage, a brewery with a pressing rig whose base posts were found in excavation). It is one prosperous sake-brewing house in one town, it records no sizes, and the buildings were taken down and repaired after the 1995 earthquake, with a later brick stove used until the business closed in 1984.
