kurihara-kanyu-bansho-ato

Kurihara City, "仙台藩花山村寒湯番所跡" ("The Kanyu guardhouse site, Hanayama village, Sendai domain", title translated), on the city's history pages (in Japanese; https://www.kuriharacity.jp/w038/010/010/010/020/5404.html)

What it is: A municipal page on a nationally designated historic site, the Kanyu guardhouse on the Hanayama pass into Akita, which checked travelers and goods for over 200 years; it names the surviving four-legged gate and office, rebuilt in the early Ansei era, with visiting hours.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the guardhouse was a border checkpoint of the Sendai domain on the Akita road, that it began in the Keicho era after Date Masamune entered Iwadeyama, and that it checked people and goods, which bears on a domain's border barrier. It is a visitor page that describes only the surviving mid-nineteenth-century gate and office, not the staffing, layout or size of the original barrier.
