kagoshima-fumoto-outer-castle

Japan Heritage Satsuma Samurai Town promotion council, "薩摩藩独自の外城制度" ("The Satsuma domain's own outer-castle system", title translated), on the Japan Heritage site "Satsuma samurai town" (in Japanese; https://kagoshima-fumoto.jp/outer-castle/)

What it is: A Japan Heritage page explaining Satsuma's outer-castle system, in which samurai lived in fumoto districts at the foot of old castles across the domain instead of in one castle town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says samurai were about a quarter of the Satsuma population, that there were as many as 120 fumoto at the end of the Edo period, and that each had an office (kariya) at its center and wide drill roads (baba) around which the houses were laid out. It is the domain's account of why it differed, and it does not say how the other domains laid out their districts or give house counts per fumoto.
