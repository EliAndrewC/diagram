bunka-fukui-iwano-heizaburo-dozo

"岩野平三郎製紙所土蔵及び繋ぎ屋" ("Iwano Heizaburo paper mill: storehouse and connecting building", title translated), Cultural Assets of Fukui (福井の文化財), Fukui Prefecture Board of Education (in Japanese; https://bunkazai.pref.fukui.lg.jp/search_category/content?detail_id=935-0)

What it is: A prefectural catalog entry for a nationally registered building: a two-story plastered storehouse and the connecting building that joins it to the dwelling, at a paper mill in the Echizen washi district (the former Imadate town), with the building area (footprint) of the two together (125 square meters).

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a storehouse of a papermaking workshop sited at the north end of the lot, facing south, with its plaster walls carried up to the eaves and a lower wall of overlapping boards, and a covered connecting building to the house that holds a bath. It dates the building to the late Taisho period (the 1920s), so it is evidence for how a storehouse was joined to a house in the twentieth century, not in the Edo period, and it gives no dimensions beyond that combined footprint, so the storehouse's own size is not stated.
