Luoyang Museum of Ancient Tombs, "北宋砖室墓" ("a Northern Song brick-chamber tomb", title translated), in the museum's exhibit pages, 5 July 2022 (in Chinese; https://www.gumumuseum.com/relic/207)

What it is: A museum exhibit page on a Northern Song brick-chamber tomb found on Shachang North Road in Luoyang, with a short note on peony designs in Luoyang's Song tombs. It describes the tomb's passage, door and round chamber and its carved imitation of a timber building.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the chamber's size (a round plan 2.5 m across and 3.4 m high) and says it imitated a timber building, with pillars, bracket sets, tiled eaves and false doors and windows carved in brick, and a coffin bed with peony and goat reliefs. It is one tomb in Luoyang and says nothing of how many were found or of the house or village its builders lived in, so it speaks for tomb form and Song decoration, not for ordinary buildings.
