chikugai-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 竹害 ("chikugai", bamboo damage, title translated), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/竹害)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on bamboo-grove blight, the spread of neglected moso and madake groves into surrounding woodland, with its history, its effects on plants, soil water and landslides, wild boar, and methods of removal and of putting the bamboo to use.

Why it applies, and its limits: A single sentence says that until the 1950s the moso bamboo, introduced to Japan in the early modern period, was grown in managed groves for timber and shoots, and that a dry ditch about 1 m deep was often dug around a grove; the article tags that sentence as needing a citation. The rest describes neglect after the war and modern control (buried barriers about 50 cm deep, repeated cutting, grazing), so its figures are not a premodern grove's.
