kotobank-inseishokubutsu

"陰生植物" ("inseishokubutsu", shade plants, title translated), in the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, the Nipponica and the Britannica concise encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/陰生植物)

What it is: A Kotobank page defining shade plants, those that grow well in low light, with the Nipponica and Britannica entries giving the plants' leaf form, the light level at which they can live and example species.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names Japanese shade plants (aoki, yabutsubaki, kanaoi, dokudami) and gives the Britannica compensation points of 100 to 500 lux for shade plants against over 1000 lux for sun plants, which supports what may grow under a grove canopy in Japan. The figures are general plant physiology with no site, and the lists name plants of shaded woodland, mostly evergreens, without saying which grew near villages.
