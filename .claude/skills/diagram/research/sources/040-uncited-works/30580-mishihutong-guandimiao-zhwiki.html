zh.wikipedia 米市胡同關帝廟 ("Mishi hutong Guandi miao", the Guandi temple of Mishi Lane, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/米市胡同关帝庙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on a small Guandi temple in a lane of Beijing's Xicheng district, built in 1625 and repaired in 1839 by a monk, later made the ancestral hall of Pan Zuyin, torn down in 2008 and rebuilt at a street corner.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a late-Ming city-lane temple to the Tianqi years and says it faced east with an irregular rectangular plan, its main buildings on one axis. It gives no dimensions, it is one temple of the capital and not a village shrine, and the buildings now standing are a post-2008 rebuild (the article calls it a destructive demolition and new construction), so only the dates and the plan description speak for the original.
