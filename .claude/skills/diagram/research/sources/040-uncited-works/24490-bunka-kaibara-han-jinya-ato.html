bunka-kaibara-han-jinya-ato

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "柏原藩陣屋跡" ("Kaibara domain jinya site", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/205913)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of the designated historic site of the Kaibara domain's administrative headquarters in Tamba, Hyogo, giving the Oda house's 20,000-koku domain and the history of its residence, built in 1714, burnt, rebuilt and made a school in 1873.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the Oda house held the domain at 20,000 koku until the Restoration, and describes what is left of the residence: an entrance and reception rooms, plus a nagaya gate 13.5 bays long and 2 bays deep, said to be original. It is the only daimyo jinya still on its original site, per the page, so it is a rare survivor of a small domain's seat and not a typical one. What survives is only the entrance and reception wing of the residence rebuilt after the fire of 1818 (or 1816). The rest was demolished or replaced by 1914 while it served as a school, and the page gives no plan or area of the grounds.
