nta-edo-yasai-zei-quiz

National Tax Agency (国税庁), National Tax College, "江戸時代の野菜の税" ("Taxes on vegetables in the Edo period", title translated), a quiz in its tax-history series (税の歴史クイズ) (in Japanese; https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/quiz/1102/index.htm)

What it is: A quiz page of the Japanese tax agency's college on how an Edo-period village in the Kanto region was taxed on five crops, with the answer and a short explanation. It sets the regular land tax (nengu) against the miscellaneous levies (komononari), and shows that the lotus root grown in a marsh was taxed differently from the field vegetables.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that paddies and fields entered in the survey registers paid the land tax, that Kanto field tax was a fixed cash payment under the Kanto hatae rule, and that a marsh lay outside the registers and paid instead a lotus-root levy (renkon unjo) buying the right to gather there for a term of about 3 to 7 years. That backs treating a lotus pond as ground outside the assessed fields, but the page covers one Kanto village as its example, gives no rates, and says nothing about other regions or about when the practice began.
