nihonminkaen-shinetsu-mura

Kawasaki City, "信越の村" ("Shinetsu no mura", the Shin'etsu village), page of the Nihon Minkaen open-air farmhouse museum in Ikuta Ryokuchi park (in Japanese; https://nihonminkaen.jp/shinetsu.html)

What it is: A page of a Kawasaki city-managed open-air museum of relocated old houses, listing five buildings of its Shin'etsu section (a water mill, a village headman's house and four gassho-zukuri farmhouses) with each one's former site, roof form, ridge and beam lengths in meters, and date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured plan sizes and building dates for buildings moved from Nagano, Toyama and Gifu, for example the Sasaki headman's house at 24.1 m by 7.3 m (1731) and the Yamashita gassho house at 18.2 m by 10.9 m (early 19th century), and notes what rooms were for, such as the guest bathing room with no tub. The sizes are of the surviving buildings as moved, the page gives only these short notes with no study cited, and it covers mountain and highland houses of the Shin'etsu and Gokayama region, not lowland villages.
