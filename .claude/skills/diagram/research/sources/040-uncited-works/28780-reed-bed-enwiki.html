en.wikipedia "Reed bed" (reedbed, the marsh habitat, title translated) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_bed)

What it is: A short English Wikipedia article on reedbeds, the natural habitat of floodplains, waterlogged depressions and estuaries, covering their types, their plants and animals, and their use as constructed wetlands for treating sewage.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a reedbed is and how it ages: reed swamps hold 20 cm or more of surface water in summer, reed fens have water at or below the surface, and the litter of an old bed rises above the water until shrubs and trees move in. Its plant and animal lists are European (common reed, bittern, otter), and its one use is modern sewage treatment, so it says nothing of East Asian reed marsh or of reed as a harvested crop. The page itself carries a banner asking for more citations.
