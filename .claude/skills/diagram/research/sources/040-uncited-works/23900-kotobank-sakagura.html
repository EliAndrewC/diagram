kotobank-sakagura

"酒蔵" ("sakagura", sake storehouse), in the Digital Daijisen, the Concise Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%85%92%E8%94%B5)

What it is: A Kotobank page gathering dictionary definitions of the sakagura, a storehouse for brewing or storing sake, with a Muromachi-to-early-modern citation and an encyclopedia excerpt on storehouse types.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the sake storehouse as a place for brewing or storing sake and its encyclopedia excerpt names it among storehouses classed by use, adding that from the late sixteenth century thick earth plastering became common for fire protection. It gives no dimensions, plan or place within a town or farm, and the plastering remark is about storehouses in general, not sake storehouses.
