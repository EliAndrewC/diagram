kunishitei-kaibara-han-jinya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "柏原藩陣屋跡" ("the site of the Kaibara domain's jinya", title translated), in the National Database of Nationally Designated Cultural Properties (国指定文化財等データベース) (in Japanese; https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/heritage/detail/401/1889)

What it is: The national database entry for the designated historic site of the Kaibara domain's jinya (lord's headquarters) in Tamba City, Hyogo, a 20,000-koku domain of the Oda house, with its surviving entrance hall and audience rooms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the domain's residence was first built in 1714, burned around 1818, was rebuilt and in 1873 became a primary school, that only the entrance, the shikidai and the shoin rooms with their corridor survive, and that the long gate of 13.5 ken by 2 ken is said to be original. It says it is the only daimyo jinya left standing in its original place, so it is an unusual example, and it gives no plan or size of the whole compound.
