bunka-sakamoto-satobo

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "大津市坂本" ("Sakamoto, Otsu City", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/125164)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of the Sakamoto Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings in Otsu, Shiga, designated in 1997 (28.7 hectares), a gate town of Enryakuji and Hiyoshi Taisha with many satobo, the retirement houses of mountain monks.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a satobo compound looks like: a gate on the street, a stone wall of the Anou-style masonry or a hedge, halls inside, and a clear stream or channel running through. It gives the district's area but no dates for the compounds and no count of them, and it describes the present preserved town.
