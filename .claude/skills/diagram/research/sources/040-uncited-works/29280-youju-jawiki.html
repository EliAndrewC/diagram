ja.wikipedia 陽樹 ("yoju", sun-demanding tree, title translated; reached by redirect from 陽性植物) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/陽性植物)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article defining the sun tree, a tree that needs much light, and describing its role as a pioneer in forest succession, with Japanese examples such as black pine, red pine, alder and Japanese angelica tree.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the point that sun trees cannot grow in the shade of an established forest and take over cleared ground and canopy gaps, naming black pine and red pine among them. That is a statement about how trees grow, and the page gives no light levels, growth rates or spacing, and names no use of the trees in a village or field.
