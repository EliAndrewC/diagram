ja.wikipedia 新居関所 ("Arai sekisho", the Arai checkpoint, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/新居関所)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Arai (Imagire) checkpoint on the Tokaido at the mouth of Lake Hamana, a designated special historic site. It covers the checkpoint's founding in 1600, its moves after storm and earthquake damage, what its officials inspected, and its one surviving building.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the checkpoint as an Edo institution: a post on the busiest of the five highways that checked incoming guns and outgoing women, run first by a shogunal magistrate and from 1702 by the lord of Yoshida, with its inspection rules dated 1667. The page's table ranks it among the highest-grade checkpoints, so it is not an ordinary one, and the standing building dates from 1855 after a rebuild, and its shore and site have since been changed by landfill. It gives no dimensions of the compound beyond naming its parts (inspection hall, boat office, women's inspection row, gate, landing).
