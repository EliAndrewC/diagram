ja.wikipedia ダイズ ("soybean", title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%80%E3%82%A4%E3%82%BA)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the soybean, covering its botany, its origin and spread from East Asia, world production, uses, processed foods, and health effects.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the soybean is a poor restorer of the soil, not a fertilizing crop: it must be rotated with paddy rice, it is not very tolerant of waterlogging in a paddy rotation, and growers answer that by heaping the ridges high. It also dates the crop in Japan to the Jomon period and says it was a food crop confined to East Asia until the early twentieth century. Most of the page is twentieth- and twenty-first-century production figures, nutrition and health findings, which say nothing about a premodern field.
