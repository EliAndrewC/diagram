Ehime Prefectural Lifelong Learning Center, "愛媛県史 地誌Ⅰ（総論）第四章 第二節 二 農業水利 3 井堰灌漑 一文字堰" ("History of Ehime Prefecture, Geography I, chapter 4, section 2: farm water, weir irrigation, the straight weir", title translated), in the database Memories of Ehime (in Japanese; https://www.i-manabi.jp/system/regionals/regionals/ecode:2/31/view/4363)

What it is: A passage of the prefectural history (1983) on the three forms of irrigation weir, straight across the river, slanted (mino-te) and bag-shaped, with Ehime river examples of each.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a weir across the river suits large rivers while a slanted weir suits small ones, because on a large river a slanted weir silts up the intake, and a straight weir on a small river diverts little; the bag form suits sandy streams and is rare in the prefecture. It also says the Uwajima domain had 1,676 weirs recorded in 1872 and that Matsuyama's Ishite river weirs date from reworks by Adachi Shigenobu (1563-1631). It gives no weir dimensions, and the 1872 count is just after the setting's cut-off.
