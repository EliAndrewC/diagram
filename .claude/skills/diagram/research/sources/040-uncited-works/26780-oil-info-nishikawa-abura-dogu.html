oil-info-nishikawa-abura-dogu

"植物油information" ("Vegetable oil information", title translated), an article on the pressing tools kept by the Nishikawa oil shop of Kyoto, on oil.or.jp (in Japanese; https://www.oil.or.jp/info/88/page03.html)

What it is: A magazine-style visit to a Kyoto oil dealer, showing the tools of rapeseed oil making and selling that the shop kept: a scroll of the pressing steps, a wedge press and its mortar, a hair cloth bag, a paulownia carrying tub, the shop's measuring vat, an early-Showa price list and old cash boxes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the steps of rapeseed oil: harvest near the hills, threshing, drying, grinding, heating, wedge pressing and barreling, and says oil was carried through the streets in tubs on a pole and sold by measure, a way the page says changed little until the end of the Edo period. The press is shown as a model beside a used wedge and mortar, the tools are not dated, the hair bag was not the shop's own, the price list is from early Showa, the scroll is of unknown date and place, and the page says the shop had stopped pressing and bought its oil from a refiner.
