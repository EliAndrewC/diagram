ja.wikipedia "満濃池" ("Mannoike", the Manno reservoir, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%BA%80%E6%BF%83%E6%B1%A0)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Mannoike, the large irrigation reservoir in Mannou, Kagawa, with a dated history of its building, breaches and repairs from about 701-704 to the present, and tables of its dimensions at several repairs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a premodern reservoir's size at two dated repairs: in 821 a dam 22 m high, a circuit of about 10.58 km and about 81 ha, and in 1631 a dam of 12.5 ken and a length of 45 ken, with a command area of 35,814 koku. It also records that after a breach in 1184 the pond bed was not restored until 1631 and was settled and farmed as a village in the meantime. Its present-day dam (32 m, 138.5 ha, 3,239 ha irrigated) comes from raisings of the dam in the twentieth century and cannot stand for the old figures, and the page cites one local history for most of its numbers.
