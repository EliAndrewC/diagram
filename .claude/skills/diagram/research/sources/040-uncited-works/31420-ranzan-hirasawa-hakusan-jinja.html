Ranzan Furusato Juku / Team Ranzan, "神社明細帳 白山神社 菅谷村(現・嵐山町)平澤 ルビ・注" ("Jinja meisaicho: Hakusan Jinja, Sugaya-mura (now Ranzan-machi) Hirasawa", The shrine register: Hakusan Shrine at Hirasawa in Sugaya village, with readings and notes, title translated), in the blog GO! GO! 嵐山 3, 2012-02-14 (in Japanese; https://ranzan.blog.jp/archives/1456392.html)

What it is: A local-history blog post transcribing, with readings and notes, the official register entry of a village shrine, Hakusan Shrine at Hirasawa in Hiki County, Saitama (now Ranzan town). It gives the gods, history, hall, grounds and parishioner households.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records one village shrine's size: grounds of 55 tsubo confirmed in 1948 and 1949, a main hall only, and 46 parishioner households. It also dates its rebuilding to 1839 and repair to 1870, and the merging of a small shrine into it in 1912. The register entry is a twentieth-century form (with a 1946 incorporation note) and covers a single Kanto village shrine, so it shows a late and small case, and the page does not say how far the 1839 form matched the later grounds.
