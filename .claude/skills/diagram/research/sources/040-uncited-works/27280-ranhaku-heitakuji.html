ranhaku-heitakuji

Ranzan Town (嵐山町), "天台宗 平沢寺 菅谷村大字平沢" ("Tendai sect, Heitaku-ji, Hirasawa in Sugaya Village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register (比企郡寺院明細帳), in the Ranzan Town Web Museum, vol. 6 (嵐山町web博物誌), on the Ranzan town site (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter11.html)

What it is: A town-hosted transcription, from a 1963 file of the Saitama prefecture school affairs section, of the Hiki County temple register entry for Heitaku-ji, a Tendai temple in Hirasawa village, Musashi, with the town's footnotes. It gives the temple's rebuilding history, hall size, precinct, outlying land and parishioner count.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a village temple's losses and rebuilding: its founding year is unknown, it was rebuilt in the Tensho era (1573-1592) by a priest of Jiko-ji, burned in 1838, had its hall rebuilt in 1839, and was repaired in 1880 with parishioners' donations. The hall is 6 ken by 3 ken, the precinct 294 tsubo (revised in 1949 to 457.77, with 284 also noted), and there are 41 parishioners (counted in persons, not households). The register gives no reason for the fire or the rebuilding, and the page covers one temple.
