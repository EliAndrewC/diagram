kotobank-urasaku

"裏作" ("urasaku", the second crop, title translated), entries from the Digital Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, Nipponica, Britannica and Yamakawa, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%A3%8F%E4%BD%9C-35292)

What it is: A page of dictionary and encyclopedia entries defining urasaku, the winter crop taken from the same field after the main summer crop, usually barley or rapeseed after rice, with a short history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the second crop on a paddy needs a well-drained dry paddy, that it appears in Heian sources and began in earnest in the early modern period, with barley, rapeseed and vegetables as crops, and that nearly half of paddies carried it during World War II. It gives no yields or dates by region, and a Fukuoka example (Kuroda Josui, 1600) shows it was not universal.
