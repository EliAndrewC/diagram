bunka-umayo-kaibaoke

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "馬用飼葉桶" ("horse fodder trough", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/12824)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of an item of folk material culture from Kannari, Miyagi: a 47-centimeter wooden fodder trough for horses, made by a local cooper.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a width for a horse's trough and says cattle troughs were larger than horses', and that horses there were kept for farm work. It is one object, undated, from Tohoku, and gives only its width, not its other dimensions or its age.
