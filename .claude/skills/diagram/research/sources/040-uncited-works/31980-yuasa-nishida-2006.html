yuasa-nishida-2006

Yuasa Yasuo (湯浅安夫) and Nishida Motoyasu (西田素康), "藍住町の屋敷神について" ("On the house-ground deities of Aizumi town", title translated), Journal of the Awa Society (阿波学会紀要) no. 52, July 2006, pp. 115-120 (in Japanese; https://library.bunmori.tokushima.jp/digital/webkiyou/52/115_120.pdf)

What it is: A paper on the small shrines (yashikigami) within house grounds in Aizumi, Tokushima, from interviews at 37 houses in former indigo-growing villages, with a table of each shrine's place, offerings and form.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts where the shrine stands in the lot: the northwest corner in 17 of 37 houses (the direction ancestors' spirits are thought to come from) and the northeast in 11, and lists the names, festival days (most at New Year) and offerings. The survey is of 2000s households in an indigo-merchant district and cannot say how old the placements are; some shrines have been moved at rebuilding, and many are relatively new cement.
