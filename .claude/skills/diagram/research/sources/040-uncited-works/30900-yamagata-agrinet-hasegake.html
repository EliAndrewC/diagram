yamagata-agrinet-hasegake

Yamagata Agrinet (Yamagata Prefecture Agricultural Technology and Environment Division), "庄内平野では、稲を干すとき、棒にまくように干していますが ... 稲の干し方が違うのはなぜですか" ("Why is rice dried differently in different places?", title shortened and translated), in the Rice-growing Q&A, posted 16 February 2022 (in Japanese; https://agrin.jp/crop/suito/q_and_a/hosikata.html)

What it is: A prefectural farm-information answer explaining why Shonai Plain farmers hang rice on stakes (kui-gake) while other regions use a horizontal rack (hase-gake), and how drying methods changed over time.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives sizes and reasons: stakes about 5 cm thick and about 2 m long suit windy plains, racks suit sheltered hill country, and a one-tier rack for a 10-are (10 m by 100 m) field needs 100 m of rack. It also dates the change: older practice stood sheaves on the ground, then on the paddy bank, and stake drying began in the Shonai hills before the Meiji Restoration and on its plains around 1881-82. It is about Yamagata, and its dates come from the page without a cited source.
