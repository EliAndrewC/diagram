wujunzhi-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 吳郡志 ("the Wujun zhi, Gazetteer of Wu Commandery", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/吳郡志)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the Wujun zhi, the Southern Song gazetteer of Pingjiang prefecture (Suzhou) by Fan Chengda, in fifty chapters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the gazetteer holds in its 39 sections: population and taxes, tribute goods, customs, city walls, schools, buildings, waterworks, temples and local figures, with the city described in detail and the waterworks section preserving material on the Lake Tai basin. It first dates from 1192 or 1193 and was expanded from 1229 to 1256, and the article says the later additions are mixed in as text or notes in a confused order; it quotes no figures from the gazetteer, so it only points to the primary source.
