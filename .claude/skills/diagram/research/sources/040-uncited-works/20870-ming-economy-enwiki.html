en.wikipedia "Economy of the Ming dynasty" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_the_Ming_dynasty)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the economy of Ming China (1368-1644): its regions, villages, farming, taxes, crafts, trade and money, drawn largely from the Cambridge History of China and Russian and Western surveys. It gives allotment and farm sizes in mu, the lijia household grouping and the grain-to-silver conversion of the land tax.

Why it applies, and its limits: It could carry how a Ming county's households were grouped and taxed (110 households to a li, ten jia of ten households each, rotating duties), what a state allotment was (15 mu a peasant in the north, 16 in the south, 10 mu then equal to 0.58 hectares) and what size of farm it calls usual: 10 mu for a family with a buffalo in Jiangnan, 100-300 mu in the north. It is Ming and not Song or Tang, covers the whole empire at once, and mixes figures from many periods and authors, so a number such as the 70,000 mu southern estate or the silver conversion rates cannot be tied to one place or date from this page.
