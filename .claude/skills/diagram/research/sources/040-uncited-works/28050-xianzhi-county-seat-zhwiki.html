zh.wikipedia 县治 ("xianzhi, the county seat", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%8E%BF%E6%B2%BB)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the county seat in China, tracing whether county seats were walled from the Han law of 201 BCE through the Sui, Song, Ming and Qing, and describing the modern county seat.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports that county seats were walled in the Han, that the Song discouraged new walls inland, and that wall building peaked twice in the Ming, so by late Ming an estimated two thirds or more of county seats had walls and by the late Qing nearly all prefecture, subprefecture and county seats did. The estimates are those of the researchers it cites, from one 2009 paper, the article is flagged as needing more sources, and its second half is about modern administration and county seats outside any wall.
