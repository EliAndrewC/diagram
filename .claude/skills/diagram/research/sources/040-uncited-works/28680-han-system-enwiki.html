en.wikipedia "Han system" (the English Wikipedia article on the Edo-period domain, han 藩, title as written) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_system)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the han, the daimyo's domain of Edo-period Japan (1603-1868), and its abolition in the 1870s. It covers its Chinese origin of the character, its Kamakura-to-Edo history, the valuation of a domain in koku, and its end in 1871.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a daimyo was a lord of a han assessed at 10,000 koku or more, that a domain was defined by projected rice income rather than area, and that a han could overlap several provinces; it gives the Kaga domain as the richest in 1690, at slightly over 1 million koku. It is a short political summary with no figures on a domain's villages, castle town or land use, and its Kamakura-era account of han is stated in a single paragraph without a source for the claim.
