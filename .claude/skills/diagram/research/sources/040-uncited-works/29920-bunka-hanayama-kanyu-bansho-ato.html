bunka-hanayama-kanyu-bansho-ato

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "仙台藩花山村寒湯番所跡" ("Site of the Kanyu checkpoint of Hanayama village, Sendai domain," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/137867)

What it is: A national database entry for a Sendai-domain border checkpoint (kuchidome bansho) in Kurihara, Miyagi, on the upper Ichihasama river toward Akita territory, designated a historic site in 1963.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the layout: a front gate across the highway, a judging office facing it and the official residence behind to the right, the residence 23.19 m by 11.86 m in the form of a common farmhouse with earth floor on one side and living room on the other yet with a shoin-style reception room. It says the standing buildings date to the Ansei years and that the judging office survives only as a base, and says checkpoints served border guarding and economic control, so it speaks for one domain's mountain checkpoint, not for a barrier on a main highway.
