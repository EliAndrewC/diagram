jakago-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 蛇籠 ("jakago", the snake basket or gabion, title translated), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/蛇籠)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the jakago, a long woven basket of bamboo or wire filled with broken stone and used to line riverbanks and steady slopes, with its name, types, uses, construction and modern problems.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in Japan the jakago was originally a cylindrical basket of bamboo, was a standard flood-control structure in the Edo period (shown at Koishikawa Korakuen), and that Korakuen still lines its pond with bamboo ones, replaced about every two years as the weave loosens. It gives no basket length or width, and most of its types, parts and wire standards belong to modern engineering; the page also notes it lacks footnotes.
