osaka-info-nippombashi

Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau, "大阪・日本橋の観光ガイド｜歴史ある橋と電気街のルーツを辿る" ("Osaka Nippombashi sightseeing guide: tracing the roots of a historic bridge and the electric town", title translated), on the Osaka Info site (in Japanese; https://osaka-info.jp/spot/nippombashi-bridge/)

What it is: A tourism-bureau page on Nippombashi, the Edo-period bridge where the Sakaisuji (Kishu Road) crosses the Dotonbori canal in Osaka's Chuo Ward, and the district around it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the Edo-period bridge was a wooden one about 40 meters long and 7 meters wide, that the bridge road was the long-established post town called Nagamachi, that a landing stood at the bridgehead, and that the shogunate's notice board stood west of the south end, where the road turned toward Dotonbori. The page hedges the figures with 'reportedly' and names no source; the rest of the page is about the later bookstore and electronics district.
