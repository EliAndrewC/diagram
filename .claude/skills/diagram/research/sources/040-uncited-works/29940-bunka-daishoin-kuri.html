bunka-daishoin-kuri

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "大照院 庫裏" ("Daisho-in, the priests' quarters and kitchen," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/147428)

What it is: A national database entry for the kuri of Daisho-in, the Rinzai temple of the Hagi domain's lords in Yamaguchi, dated about 1750, 18.1 m by 18.0 m, with a commentary on the whole temple compound.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the kuri's size and roof, and says the temple was re-founded by the second Hagi lord by 1656, holds the graves of the first and every even-numbered lord, and was rebuilt after a fire in 1747 with hall, kuri, study and bell gate in 1750. It is a daimyo family temple, so its scale is the upper end for a provincial temple, and the commentary describes the rebuilt compound rather than the earlier one.
