sangji-yutang-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 桑基鱼塘 ("the mulberry dike and fish pond", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E6%A1%91%E5%9F%BA%E9%B1%BC%E5%A1%98)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article defining the mulberry dike and fish pond, the system of fish in the pond and mulberry on the pond bank, with its recycling of silkworm waste and pond mud, and its survival in the Pearl River delta and at Huzhou.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the form - a deepened pond with a raised bank planted in mulberry, silkworm waste fed to the fish and pond mud spread back on the trees - and says the best surviving Pearl River delta area is at Qixing village in Xiqiao, with most of the delta's ponds lost to industrialization. It gives no date for the system's origin, no dimensions of pond or bank and is flagged as needing more sources, so it supports that the form exists but not how large or how old it was.
