joetsu-takada-rekishi

Joetsu City, Cultural Promotion Division, "城下町高田の歴史・文化" ("The history and culture of the castle town of Takada", title translated), on the city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.joetsu.niigata.jp/soshiki/bunka/takada-rekishi-bunka.html)

What it is: A city page on Takada, the castle town founded in 1614 in present-day Joetsu, Niigata, describing its planned layout of warrior quarters, townsmen's streets and a temple town, its narrow-fronted machiya houses and its snow-country covered arcades (gangi).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a planned castle town's structure: the districts named for their direction from the castle, about 40 individual machi, temples gathered on the west and at the road entrances, and a machiya plan with an earthen passage about 1 m wide running to the rear. It also says that frontage set the tax and that the arcade types date from after the town was set up in the time of Matsudaira Mitsunaga or from Meiji on. The house plan is quoted from a modern guide book and describes the machiya standing today, some since renovated, with no date given for the plan; and the page gives no dimensions for the districts or for lots.
