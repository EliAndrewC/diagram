zh.wikipedia 西递村 (Xidi village, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/西递村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Xidi, a Hu-family village in Yi County, Anhui, a World Heritage site, with its history, street pattern and main buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the village was founded in the Song, that the Hu family turned to trade from 1465, that it peaked in the 18th and 19th centuries with about 600 houses, and that a main street with two parallel ones runs along a stream, with small squares before the halls; 124 old houses and 3 ancestral halls survive. The counts are from a tourism company and the village's later heritage listing, and the page gives no street widths or plot sizes.
