ja.wikipedia 愛本橋 ("Aimoto-bashi", Aimoto Bridge, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/愛本橋)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Aimoto Bridge over the Kurobe River in Kurobe, Toyama, covering its successive bridges from the Kaga domain's cantilever bridge (hanebashi) of 1663 to the present steel bridge of 1972, with a timeline, the old bridge's dimensions and its flood history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the old hanebashi as 61.42 m long and 3.62 m wide (33 ken by 10 shaku as built), a cantilevered timber bridge of the same form as Saruhashi, built because piers would not last in the flood-prone river, and says the bridge was rebuilt eight times between 1663 and 1862. Most of the page is about the twentieth-century truss and Nielsen-Lohse bridges and modern restoration plans, which do not bear on a premodern bridge, and it describes one large bridge on a Kaga highroad, not an ordinary village crossing.
