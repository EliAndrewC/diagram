historist-karishiki

Yamakawa Nihonshi Shojiten (山川 日本史小辞典, revised edition, Yamakawa Shuppansha, 2016), "刈敷" ("karishiki", green manure), on Historist (in Japanese; https://www.historist.jp/word_j_ka/entry/030769/)

What it is: A short dictionary entry on karishiki, green manure made from grass and young shoots cut in early spring and plowed into fields, reproduced on Yamakawa's Historist site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says karishiki was gathered from mountains, field banks and common land and plowed under, that grass fertilizer was widespread from antiquity and an important self-supplied fertilizer in Edo-period farming, and so required wide grass-cutting land around the paddies. It gives no area per field or region, and says nothing about how the cutting land was managed.
