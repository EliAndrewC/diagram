huozhou-shu-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 霍州署 (the Huozhou prefectural office, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/霍州署)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Huozhou prefectural office in Shanxi, a nationally protected site whose Yuan great hall survives, with the Ming plan from the 1558 gazetteer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says most of the office was destroyed at the end of the Yuan, leaving only the great hall, three bays wide and eight purlins deep, and that the Ming and Qing rebuilt the screen gate and the admonition pavilion; the drum tower is modern. It gives no overall dimensions or room counts, and the 1558 plan is shown only as a picture.
