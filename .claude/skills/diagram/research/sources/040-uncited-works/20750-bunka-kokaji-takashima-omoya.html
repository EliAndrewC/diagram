bunka-kokaji-takashima-omoya

"小鍛冶屋（旧高島家住宅）主屋" ("Kokajiya (the former Takashima house), main house", title translated), Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/575652)

What it is: A catalog entry in the national Cultural Heritage Online database for a registered tangible cultural property: the main house of a townhouse-type dwelling at Iwamuro hot spring, Niigata, built 1830-1868, with its floor area (247 square meters) and a short description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the house stands at the north end of the Iwamuro hot-spring settlement as a two-story gabled townhouse, and that a front garden (zensai) lies between the old house's front and the highway. That gives one dated Edo-period example of a roadside house with a planted strip before it, in Niigata. It describes a single house that has been added to and rebuilt repeatedly, so its plan is not the original, and it gives no lot size or dimensions for the garden.
