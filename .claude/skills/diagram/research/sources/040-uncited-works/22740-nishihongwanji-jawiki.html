ja.wikipedia 西本願寺 ("Nishi Hongan-ji", the Western Hongan-ji temple in Kyoto, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/西本願寺)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Nishi Hongan-ji, the head temple of the Jodo Shinshu Hongan-ji branch in Kyoto, giving its history from 1272, its move to Horikawa Rokujo in 1591, and a building-by-building account of its precinct with dimensions for its two main halls.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the layout of a large Edo-period temple precinct: the founder's hall (62 by 48 m) built larger than the main hall (45 by 42 m) to hold sermons for large congregations, the gates, the audience-hall and reception compound, the moat-like Horikawa on the east side, and a gate-town outside. Most surviving buildings date from rebuilding after the fire of 1617 and the 1760 reconstruction, and the precinct is a wealthy head temple of one sect, so its sizes are an upper bound, not a village or town temple.
