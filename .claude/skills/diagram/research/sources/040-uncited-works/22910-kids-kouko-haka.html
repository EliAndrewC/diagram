Zenkoku Kodomo Koukogaku Kyoushitsu (National Children's Archaeology Classroom), "2021 調べてみよう ～生活文化～「墓」" ("2021 Let's find out: daily life and culture, graves", title translated) (in Japanese; https://kids-kouko.com/prayer/)

What it is: A children's archaeology page surveying Japanese graves from the Paleolithic through the Kofun period, with pit graves, jar burials, coffin types, burial goods and the great tombs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that Jomon burials at Sannai Maruyama lay in rows beside the road and in the village plaza, with children's jars beside the houses, and that Yayoi graves were marked by ditches and mounds. The whole page ends before the Heian period, so it says nothing of how a medieval or Edo village placed its graves.
