zhang-lvxiang-bu-nongshu

Zhang Lüxiang, "补农书" ("Bu nongshu", Supplement to the Agricultural Treatise), with the "沈氏农书" (Shen's Agricultural Treatise) it supplements, in a plain-text copy in the Daizhige corpus on GitHub (in Chinese; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/garychowcmu/daizhigev20/master/%E5%AD%90%E8%97%8F/%E5%86%9C%E5%AE%B6/%E8%A1%A5%E5%86%9C%E4%B9%A6.txt)

What it is: The full text of a Ming-Qing farming treatise from the Huzhou country of Zhejiang, as far as this file carries it: a month-by-month list of field, mulberry and household tasks, the rules for plowing, planting and weeding paddy, mulberry and wheat, and the account of the great floods and droughts of 1640 to 1642. The file ends at "to be continued" and holds only part of the work.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a farmer's own account of how a Jiangnan paddy, mulberry and fishpond household worked its year: tools, manure bought in town, spacing of mulberry (seven feet each way, about two hundred trees to the mu), and the timing of drainage. It speaks for the Huzhou lowlands in the seventeenth century, not for northern or upland farms, and the file is a transcription with some characters lost (marked in the text by placeholders), cut off before the work ends.
