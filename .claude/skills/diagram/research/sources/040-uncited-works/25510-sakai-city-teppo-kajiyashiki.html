sakai-city-teppo-kajiyashiki

Sakai City, "鉄炮鍛冶屋敷（堺市立町家歴史館 井上関右衛門家住宅）" ("Teppo kaji yashiki (Sakai City Machiya History Museum, the Inoue Sekiemon house)", the gunsmith's compound, title translated), in "堺市の文化財" ("Cultural properties of Sakai City", title translated), Sakai City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kanko/rekishi/bunkazai/bunkazai/teppoukaji.html)

What it is: A city page on the Inoue Sekiemon house in Sakai, an Edo-period gunsmith's workshop and dwelling opened as a museum in March 2024 after conservation work. It lists the designated buildings (main house, reception wing, tool storehouse, rice-bale storehouse, annex) and says the compound held several gardens and tea rooms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a craftsman's property combined workshop and home, with a shop room and a forge room reconstructed for display, that over 20,000 old documents rewrote the history of Japanese gun production, and that estate plans let researchers trace how the lot and its buildings were altered. It gives no dimensions or plan, and the displays are reconstructions in a building restored in 2020-2024, so the page shows what the museum presents, not the workshop as first built.
