bunka-motohoma-sakagura-kamaya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧本間酒造酒蔵及び釜屋" ("kyu Honma shuzo sakagura oyobi kamaya", the former Honma brewery's sake storehouse and boiler house, title translated), on Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/298899)

What it is: A Cultural Heritage Online record of the former Honma sake brewery's storehouse and boiler house in Maebashi, Gunma, a Taisho-era (1912-1926) building registered as a tangible cultural property, with its floor area and a short description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes how a brewhouse was laid out: a two-story earthen-walled storehouse used from rice washing through brewing, a boiler house attached on the east and a wide lean-to called oroshi on the south, 524 square meters in all. The building is Taisho, not Edo, and the page gives no earlier form of the brewery.
