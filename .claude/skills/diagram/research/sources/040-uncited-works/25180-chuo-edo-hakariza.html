chuo-edo-hakariza

Chuo City, "江戸秤座跡" ("Site of the Edo Hakariza", title translated), in the Chuo City cultural properties pages (in Japanese; https://www.city.chuo.lg.jp/a0052/bunkakankou/rekishi/bunkazai/kuminbunkazai/edohakarizaato.html)

What it is: A municipal page on the site of the Edo Hakariza, the licensed office that held the monopoly on making, selling, mending and inspecting scales in eastern Japan, from a ward newsletter column of 2013 by a named cultural properties adviser.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the monopoly to 1653, when the shogunate gave the scale privilege to two houses, the Morizui of Edo for 33 eastern provinces and the Kamiya of Kyoto for 33 western provinces with Iki and Tsushima, and says weights were set by the Goto family's standard weights, so it fixes who regulated weights and scales and where the Edo office stood until 1875. It is about an office and a regime of licenses, not about the scales or weights themselves; it gives no dimensions of the office or a scale-maker's shop and no price.
