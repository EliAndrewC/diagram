Osaka Bunkazai Navi, "The Fujino Residence" (藤野家住宅), the English page of the Osaka heritage guide run by Osaka architects and heritage owners' associations (https://osaka-bunkazainavi.org/en/bunkazai/藤野家住宅)

What it is: An English page on the Fujino residence in Fujiidera, Osaka, a registered cultural property of 1893 and the house of a family that served as village headman (shoya daikan) through the Edo period, describing the main house, gates and its mud walls.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the layout of a headman's compound: main house at the center, annexes and mud walls around the whole premises, a front gate on the road to the temple gate, and a central garden much larger than the entry and working yards. The building listed dates from 1893, after the Edo period, and the page says the family held the office but gives no dimensions.
