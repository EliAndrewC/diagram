hirosaki-zenrin-gai

Hirosaki Tourism and Convention Bureau (弘前観光コンベンション協会), "禅林街(禅林三十三ヶ寺)" ("Zenrin-gai, the thirty-three Zen temples", title translated), on the Hirosaki tourism site (in Japanese; https://hirosaki-kanko.or.jp/details.html?id=cnt00403281540414065)

What it is: A short tourism entry on the temple street southwest of Hirosaki Castle, Aomori, where 33 Soto temples stand along one approach lined with cedar trees, with its address and transport.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the street lies southwest of the castle, in the unlucky ura-kimon direction in fengshui terms, with the Tsugaru family's temple Chosho-ji at the far end and 33 Soto temples gathered from all over the Tsugaru country and set in order along a straight approach, and that the arrangement is a nationally designated historic site. It gives no dates, no sizes and no distances, and does not say who laid the street out or why.
