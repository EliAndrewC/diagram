hidatakayama-takayama-jinya

Hida-Takayama Tourism and Convention Association, "高山陣屋" ("Takayama Jinya", title translated), in the Hida Takayama travel guide, Spots (in Japanese; https://www.hidatakayama.or.jp/spot/detail_1106.html)

What it is: A tourist-guide page on Takayama Jinya in Hida, the shogunate's magistrate's office for Hida province, listing its rooms and buildings (reception and office rooms, trial room, kitchen, officials' living quarters, garden, rice storehouse) with visiting information.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that Takayama Jinya is the only surviving gundai or daikansho (shogunal magistrate's office), out of some sixty such offices at the end of the shogunate, that the post was raised to gundai in 1777, that the compound holds forty rooms with tatami and edging that differ by use, and that its rice storehouse, moved from the castle's third bailey, is among the oldest and largest surviving. It gives no dimensions or plan, and a shogunal office in a timber-rich direct-rule district is not a domain's own administration.
