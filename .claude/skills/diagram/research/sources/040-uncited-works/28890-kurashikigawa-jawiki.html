kurashikigawa-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 倉敷川 ("Kurashiki-gawa", the Kurashiki River, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/倉敷川)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kurashiki River in Okayama Prefecture, a 13.6 km class-two river that runs from the Kurashiki Bikan historical quarter to Lake Kojima. It gives its origin as a tidal canal, its use by boats until the 1950s, and its later revival.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Kurashiki was a port on a tidal flat at the mouth of the Takahashi River, that new-field reclamation from the Edo period left a remnant inlet that became a canal worked by the tide, and that goods came by boat until the early 1950s. The article carries a notice that it has no or too few sources, the origin is given as a saying ("is said to be"), and most of its text is on the 1889 cotton mill, the 1959 dike and the present park, so it supports the shape of the place only loosely.
