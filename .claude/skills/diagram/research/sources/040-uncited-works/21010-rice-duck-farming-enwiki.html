en.wikipedia "Rice-duck farming" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice-duck_farming)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on raising ducks and rice on the same paddy, with its systems in China, Malaysia, Korea, Vietnam and Indonesia, and its advantages and disadvantages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says ducks in the paddy eat weeds and pests and manure the rice, and, in a sentence that cites no source, that the practice has existed in Asia for centuries, including China, which supports ducks as a paddy-side animal. Its integrated systems use hybrid ducks such as Aigamo and its dated evidence is from the 1990s to the 2010s, and it gives no premodern record or Japanese date, so it does not show how common the practice was in the setting's era.
