ja.wikipedia 垣根 ("kakine", the hedge or fence, title translated; the URL is the redirect "ikegaki", living hedge) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/生垣)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kakine, a fence marking off a plot or garden, whether of woven bamboo or of planted trees (the ikegaki), with a section on English hedgerows and a list of named Japanese bamboo fence types.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists what Japanese house boundaries and garden dividers are made of (earthen walls, board fences, stone walls, bamboo fences, living hedges) and names the bamboo types that block the view and those that do not. Its European section says a hawthorn hedge was planted with a drainage ditch beside it and trimmed to shoulder height; the page does not date any Japanese type, gives no dimensions or planting species for a village hedge, and most of the named types are garden fences of temples and villas, not farm boundaries.
