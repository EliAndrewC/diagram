kunishitei-asakura-ke-omoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧朝倉家住宅 主屋" ("the former Asakura house, main building", title translated), in the National Database of Nationally Designated Cultural Properties (国指定文化財等データベース) (in Japanese; https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/bsys/maindetails/102/00003889)

What it is: The national database entry for the main house of the former Asakura residence in Sarugakucho, Shibuya, Tokyo, an Important Cultural Property built in 1919 for a landowning family who made their money milling rice with a water wheel.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the plot and the house size: a lot of 5,419.81 square meters, a main house of 573.76 square meters, the main house at the center facing south, a storehouse west, a garden gate and outbuilding east and a stroll garden south and southwest. It records a wealthy rural landowner's house of the Taisho era on the edge of a growing city and not an Edo-period farmhouse, though it says the family were local landowners and mill operators from the end of the Edo period.
