worldagroforestry-alnus-japonica

Orwa C, A Mutua, Kindt R, Jamnadass R and S Anthony, "Alnus japonica (Thunb.) Steud.", in the Agroforestree Database 4.0, World Agroforestry (ICRAF), 2009 (in English; https://apps.worldagroforestry.org/treedb/aftpdfs/alnus_japonica.pdf)

What it is: A five-page species sheet from the World Agroforestry database on the Japanese alder (hannoki), Alnus japonica: its botanical description, native range (Japan, China, Korea, Taiwan, Siberia), habitat, uses and management.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what kind of ground the tree takes in Japan - wet soils along streams and in swamps, marsh or swamp forest with a high water table - and that it is a pioneer, coppices easily and yields poles and firewood in 5 years on fertile sites; these are facts of the tree, not of any period. Its uses and spacing are those of present-day agroforestry (shade for coffee at 15 m by 15 m in the Philippines, pruned to 3-5 m), and it says nothing of the alder in Japanese paddy-field edges or drying racks.
