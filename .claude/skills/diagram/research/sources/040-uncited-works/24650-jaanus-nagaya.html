jaanus-nagaya

Mary Neighbour Parent (compiler), "nagaya 長屋" (long house or row house, title translated), in JAANUS, the on-line Dictionary of Japanese Architectural and Art Historical Terminology, Media Center for Art History, Columbia University (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/nagaya)

What it is: A dictionary entry on the nagaya, a long house or row house of several residences under one ridge, with its use around Edo-period warrior mansions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in the Edo period nagaya were built around a warrior's mansion for lower-ranking samurai to live in, cites the former Asa Mori clan's Hagi residence nagaya as an Important Cultural Property, and defines the long house gate (nagayamon) as one with a gateway in one section. It gives no number of units, size or region beyond the Hagi example.
