senglusi-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia "僧錄司" ("The Office of Buddhist Registry", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/僧录司)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the Senglusi, the central office that governed Buddhist monks in China and Korea, with a Ming six-ministries navigation table beside it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the office began in the Song under the Court of State Ceremonial, with a left and a right street office keeping the registers of monks and nuns and appointing monastic officials, and that the Ming kept it under the Ministry of Rites; its table gives the ranks of the Ming officers (a sixth-rank head and a lesser deputy). It is five sentences citing no source, with no numbers of monks or offices and nothing on its seat or buildings, and the Korean abolition in 1424 is outside the setting's model.
