zh.wikipedia 唐长安城 ("Tang Chang'an city", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%94%90%E9%95%BF%E5%AE%89%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Sui-Tang capital Chang'an, covering its founding and fall, its outer city, palace city and imperial city, its 110 wards, two markets and canals, and the protection of its site at Xi'an.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured figures for the capital's plan: an outer wall 9,721 m by 8,652 m (83.1 square km) of rammed earth about 12 m wide, 110 walled wards of 500-838 m by 550-1,125 m with gates guarded and a curfew, a central avenue 150-155 m wide, and two markets of about 1,025 m by 927 m each. It describes an imperial capital, the largest city of its day, so its scale is an upper bound and not a county or provincial town, and the article's own population figures vary widely (250,000 to 600,000 for the Sui, up to 2 million for the Tang).
