Sakura City Cultural Affairs Division, "佐倉武家屋敷" ("Sakura Samurai Residences: the former Kawahara, Tajima and Takei houses", title translated), on the Sakura city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.sakura.lg.jp/soshiki/bunkaka/bunkazai/sisetsu/buke/3032.html)

What it is: A municipal visitor page for three preserved samurai houses of the Sakura domain in Sakura, Chiba, standing on a street lined with an earthen embankment and hedges. It names each house, says all three date to the late Edo period, and gives opening hours and access.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports a retainer street of the castle town with its embankment-and-hedge frontage and tells which of the houses stood on its present site (the Tajima house) and which was moved (the Takei house, whose move brought excavated materials to light). It gives no plan, no lot or building size and no rank or stipend for the households, and most of the page is admission and access information.
