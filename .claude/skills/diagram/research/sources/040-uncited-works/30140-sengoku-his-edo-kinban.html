sengoku-his-edo-kinban

"江戸勤番はツライよ…浅葱裏と馬鹿にされ、寂しく蕎麦をすする" ("Edo kinban wa tsurai yo...", the hardships of duty in Edo, mocked as asagiura, eating soba alone, title translated), on the history site Sengoku History (戦国ヒストリー), 2023-08-28 (in Japanese; https://sengoku-his.com/1638)

What it is: A popular article on retainers who accompanied their lords to Edo under sankin kotai, describing the kinban's idleness, the three kinds of daimyo residence in Edo, the plain barracks in the lower residence, and the rules limiting their outings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the three tiers of a domain's Edo residences (upper, middle, lower, plus warehouse residences mostly in Osaka), where kinban lived in the lower residence's barracks ranked by rank, and the rules of Kishu and Kaga. It is a retelling for a general reader, citing two books of 2013 and 2022, and gives no figures for the barracks' size; its picture is of the late Edo period.
