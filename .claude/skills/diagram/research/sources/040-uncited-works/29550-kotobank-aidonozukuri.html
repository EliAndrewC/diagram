kotobank-aidonozukuri

"相殿造" ("aidono-zukuri, the shared-hall shrine form", title translated), a headword compiled on Kotobank from several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/相殿造-2000173)

What it is: A short Kotobank entry from the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten for aidono-zukuri, the form of shrine main hall in which two or more shrines for the deities enshrined together are joined in one building, also called nikenyashiro.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the form and cites a 1699 Shinto glossary, so it shows the term and the building type were current by then. It is a two-line definition with no size, no date of origin, no place and no count of how common the form was.
