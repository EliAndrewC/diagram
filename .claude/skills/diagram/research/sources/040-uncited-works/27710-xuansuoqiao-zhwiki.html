xuansuoqiao-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 悬索桥 ("xuansuoqiao", the suspension bridge, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%90%8A%E6%A1%A5)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on suspension bridges, mostly their modern structure and construction, with a history section listing early bamboo and iron-chain bridges in China.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its history section says the earliest suspension bridges are Chinese, of woven bamboo cable, gives the Jihong chain bridge of Yunnan (first built 58-68 AD) as 115 meters long, 3.8 wide with a 56.2-meter clear span of 18 chains, the Luding bridge of 1706 at about 110 meters, and a level-deck bridge of 1420 in the southwest. Those dates and sizes are the whole premodern content and rest on one cited book; the rest of the page is steel-cable engineering, and it is flagged for missing sources.
