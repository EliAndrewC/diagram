bunka-tsuchiya-dozo

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧土屋家住宅土蔵" ("Kyu Tsuchiya-ke jutaku dozo", the former Tsuchiya residence earthen storehouse, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/404091)

What it is: A database record for a two-story earthen-walled grain storehouse of 1890 in Sado, Niigata, registered as a tangible cultural property, with its floor area (34 square meters) and a short description of its construction.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the footprint and form of a farmhouse grain store, which the page calls an indispensable farm outbuilding, with a granary room on the ground floor. It dates from 1890 and was moved from another part of Sado, so it is a late-Edo form recorded in the Meiji era and is not shown on its original site.
