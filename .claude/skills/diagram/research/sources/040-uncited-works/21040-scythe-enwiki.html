en.wikipedia "Scythe" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scythe)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the scythe, the long-bladed hand tool for mowing grass and reaping grain, with its parts, technique, sharpening, history and place in several European cultures.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives blade lengths of 60 to 100 cm for grass, wheat and rough work, and describes team mowing, early-morning haymaking, and peening and honing in the field, which can show how a mowing party worked. The article is built on European and North American practice, and its only East Asian passage is an uncited remark that a Japanese man judged a large scythe would not work for rice in Japan, so it does not show how Japanese or Chinese farmers reaped; it lists the kama, a Japanese hand tool used in farming, only under See also, and the mowing team it describes belongs to a European meadow, not the setting's fields. Its blade lengths and technique rest on a 2016 scything handbook and modern hobby sites.
