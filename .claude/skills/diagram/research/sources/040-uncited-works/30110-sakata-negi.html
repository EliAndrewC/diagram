sakata-negi

Sakata Seed Corporation, "ネギの育て方・栽培方法" ("Negi no sodatekata, saibai hoho", how to grow the Welsh onion, title translated), in the Engei Tsushin gardening site, "Shippai shinai saibai lesson (yasai)" (updated 2025-05-13) (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/lesson-vegetable/detail_21)

What it is: A seed company's home-garden growing guide for the Welsh onion, giving its spacing (5-8 cm), the trench of 20-25 cm for root-deep negi, hilling-up three to four times, and the split between root-deep negi in the Kanto and north and leaf negi from the Kansai west.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the regional split between kinds of negi, the trench depth, spacing and hilling schedule, and that the plant tolerates cold to minus 8 degrees C. Its fertilizer amounts and plastic tunnels are modern, and the page does not say how old the regional split is.
