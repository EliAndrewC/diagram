kakegawa-otemon-banshosho

Kakegawa City, "大手門・大手門番所" ("The Otemon gate and the Otemon guardhouse", title translated), on the city's tourism site (in Japanese; https://www.city.kakegawa.shizuoka.jp/kanko/kana/spot-list/otemon.html)

What it is: A city tourism page on the main gate of Kakegawa Castle (Shizuoka), a two-story turret gate rebuilt in 1995, and on its guardhouse, which dates from the 1859 rebuilding after the 1854 earthquake.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the gate's frontage was 7 ken (about 12.7 m) by 3 ken (about 5.4 m) deep, and that the guardhouse was the only one linking the post town and the castle, where everyone entering the castle was checked. The gate is a 1995 reconstruction standing about 50 m from the original site, so its size is the rebuilders' reading, and the guardhouse is a late-Edo building of one small castle; the page gives no dimensions for the guardhouse.
