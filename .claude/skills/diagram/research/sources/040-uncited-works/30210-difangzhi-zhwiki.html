zh.wikipedia 地方志 ("difangzhi", local gazetteers, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/地方志)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the local gazetteer, the Chinese genre of books recording a place's geography, history, people and trades, with its history from the Zhou onward, its classification, the offices that compile it today, and examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a gazetteer held and how it was used under the Ming and Qing: a newly posted prefect or county magistrate looked up the local tax and corvee registers and the gazetteer to learn the area, its household count, property registration, taxes and customs, and the compilers were local scholars paid mostly by local subscription. It names the Song gazetteers and the Ming and Qing compilers who drew on them. It lists titles rather than quoting their contents, and its sections on administration describe the Republic and the People's Republic, which is no evidence for the setting.
