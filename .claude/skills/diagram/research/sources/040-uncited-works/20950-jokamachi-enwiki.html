en.wikipedia "Jōkamachi" (castle town) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jōkamachi)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the jokamachi, the Japanese castle town, covering its history from the Kamakura period, its defensive and commercial layout, its districts for samurai, ashigaru, townspeople and temples, and its population range.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places samurai compounds nearest the castle by rank, ashigaru at the outer rim of the townspeople's districts and temples on the outer edge, and gives town sizes of about 4,000 to 120,000 with about 10,000 common, which can check a castle town's zoning and scale. It gives no source for the sizes and describes the Edo castle town in general, not any one domain.
