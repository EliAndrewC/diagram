bunka-shinmeisha-inari-torii

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "神明社稲荷鳥居" ("Shinmei shrine, Inari torii", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/170002)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of a registered stone torii of 1810 at the Shinmei shrine in Yurihonjo, Akita: 4.1 meters high and 3.0 meters wide, of the Inari type, standing at the top of a stair at the approach's entrance.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dimensions of a dated Edo-period stone torii of a village-level shrine: a height of about 4 meters, a 3-meter span between pillars, and pillars that lean inward 25 centimeters over a 3.4-meter height. It is one Tohoku shrine's gate, in stone, and says nothing about whether wooden gates were larger or smaller.
