bunka-takeda-dozo

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "武田家住宅土蔵" ("Takeda-ke jutaku dozo", the Takeda residence earthen storehouse, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/615380)

What it is: A database record for a thatched earthen-walled storehouse of 1830-1868 in Kashiwazaki, Niigata (60 square meters), standing at the front corner of the farmstead, with a short description of its unusual covering of board siding.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the storehouse to the last decades of the Edo period and places it in the front of the farmstead, among fields, with the house grove, which supports a storehouse near the front of a Niigata farm. It covers one building in one village and says nothing about how common the arrangement was.
