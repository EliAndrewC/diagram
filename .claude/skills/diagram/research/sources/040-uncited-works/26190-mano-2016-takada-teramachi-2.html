Mano Toshikazu, "近世城下町における寺町と寺院 越後国高田寺町の場合" ("Kinsei jokamachi ni okeru teramachi to jiin", Temple quarters and temples in an early-modern castle town: the case of Takada in Echigo, title translated), Bulletin of the Kubikino Local History Archive 1, 2016 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kfa/1/1/1_1/_article/-char/ja/)

What it is: The J-STAGE record of a research-bulletin article on the temple quarter (teramachi) of Takada, Echigo, a castle town built in the early seventeenth century on open land.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Takada's 162 temples, counting main temples and sub-temples, stood in one quarter at the town's west, that the town was divided into a warrior district, a townspeople's district and the temple quarter, and that the author reads the quarter's role as worship, entertainment and social welfare. The abstract says it is not about the quarter's layout or founding, and the count is an estimate for one point in time at one unusually large planned town.
