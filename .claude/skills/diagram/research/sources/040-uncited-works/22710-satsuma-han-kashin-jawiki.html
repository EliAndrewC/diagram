ja.wikipedia 薩摩藩家臣 ("Satsuma-han kashin", the retainers of the Satsuma domain, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/薩摩藩家臣)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the retainer ranks of the Satsuma domain, with approximate house counts per rank, and on the great branch families, listing each family's fief size in koku and its line of heads.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one large domain's ranking of its samurai into ten grades (four Goichimon houses, about 30 Ishodokoro, about 760 Komban, about 3,000 Ko-shogumi), with the castle-town samurai set apart from the Tojo samurai in rural districts, and 43,119 samurai households at the Meiji Restoration. It says that figure is conspicuously high among domains because the domain held three provinces and absorbed local warriors in conquering Kyushu, and its fief sizes run to 35,000 koku, so Satsuma is an outlier among domains, and its ranks were fixed by 1711 and are set out as a list, not a village-level account.
