kotobank-waku

"枠" ("waku, the frame", title translated), a headword compiled on Kotobank from several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/枠)

What it is: A Kotobank headword for the kokuji (Japan-made) character waku, gathering Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, the Heibonsha encyclopedia and Jitsu (Zutsu), on its senses of a frame, a boundary, a spool for winding thread, and, in the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, a stone-filled timber frame used as an embankment's water-breaker.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its sense 7 in the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten defines a levee water-protection work built as a wooden frame packed with stones, with a citation from the Jikata Hanreiroku (1794). The entry is a short definition: it gives no dimensions, no placement and no region, and most of the page concerns other senses of the word.
