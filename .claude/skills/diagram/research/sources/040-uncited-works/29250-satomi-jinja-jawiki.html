ja.wikipedia 郷見神社 ("Satomi jinja", the Satomi Shrine, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/郷見神社)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on a village-rank shrine in Shimo-Satomi-machi, Takasaki, Gunma Prefecture, formerly the Suwa Shrine, with its precinct area, hall size, a merger of local shrines in 1910, and the legend of a burial mound on the hill behind it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured precinct of 251 tsubo (about 830 square meters) and a main hall two ken wide and three deep, a concrete size for one rural shrine ground. It adds that four older shrines were merged into it in 1910 and that their halls, dated early to mid Tokugawa, were kept. The foundation date is stated as unknown, the precinct and halls are those of a single Kanto-area village shrine, and the burial-mound tale rests on lost documents the article says it cannot prove.
