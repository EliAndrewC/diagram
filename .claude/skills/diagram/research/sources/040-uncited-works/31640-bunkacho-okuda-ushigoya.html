bunkacho-okuda-ushigoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "奥田家住宅牛小屋" ("Okuda-ke jutaku ushigoya", the cattle shed of the Okuda house, title translated), in the Cultural Heritage Database (文化遺産データベース) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/db/heritages/detail/582125)

What it is: The registry entry for a Meiji-era cattle shed in Akitakata, Hiroshima, registered as a tangible cultural property in November 2025. It gives the shed's construction, floor area and interior plan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a cattle shed of 1898-1912 of a wealthy farmer: a single-story timber building with a tiled gable roof and 161 square meters of floor, an open front at the middle of the west face, and an earth-floor interior divided into six rooms, with a workshop at the south, a storehouse and cattle stalls at the north and storage above, called a very long shed that conveys a rich farmer's livelihood. It is one building of the late Meiji period belonging to a rich household, so it shows the form of a large farm's shed and gives no stall size or the housing of an ordinary farmer's ox.
