ja.wikipedia 武家屋敷 ("buke yashiki", warrior residences, title translated), reached from the redirect 足軽屋敷 ("ashigaru yashiki", foot-soldier residences) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/足軽屋敷)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on warrior residences in Edo-period Japan, covering daimyo, hatamoto and samurai residences, the samurai quarter of a castle town, and a short section on the housing of ashigaru foot soldiers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how a castle town was zoned: the higher a retainer's rank, the nearer the lord's seat, with ashigaru housing in rows (nagaya) on the outer edge, often at the town entrances for defense. It also notes that residences were granted by the lord and changed with rank or transfer. The ashigaru section is a few sentences and gives no sizes or counts, and two of the article's sections carry a notice that they lack sources.
