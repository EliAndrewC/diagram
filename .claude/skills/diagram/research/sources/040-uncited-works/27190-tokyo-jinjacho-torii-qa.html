tokyo-jinjacho-torii-qa

Tokyo Association of Shrines, "神社の施設（1）" ("Shrine facilities (1)", title translated), in 神社Q&A (in Japanese; http://www.tokyo-jinjacho.or.jp/qa/jinja_shisetsu/01/)

What it is: A question-and-answer page explaining the torii: its place as a gate between the sacred ground and the human world, how several torii are numbered from the outside, its parts, materials and named types.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the torii's parts as two pillars, a top lintel (kasagi) and a tie beam (nuki) beneath it, says the earliest ones were of cypress or cedar with stone, bronze and concrete later, and that the types fall into two families, the Shinmei and the Myojin, the latter being the more common. It gives no dimensions or dates for any type, and its account of the origin is a legend.
