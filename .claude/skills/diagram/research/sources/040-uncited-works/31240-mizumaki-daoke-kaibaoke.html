mizumaki-daoke-kaibaoke

Mizumaki Town Historical Museum, "駄桶・飼葉桶" ("Dao-ke, kaiba-oke", the feed bucket for pack animals, title translated), in its collection of folk materials (in Japanese; https://www.town.mizumaki.lg.jp/museum/040/020/050/280/823.html)

What it is: A museum collection record for one wooden feed trough with iron fittings, used for fodder for cattle and horses, in Mizumaki, Onga district, Fukuoka, with its dimensions and date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes how a horse's feed was prepared and given: chopped grass, grain and straw mixed in the trough, which was hung at a fixed place so the horse could put its mouth into it, and it gives the size, 55 by 53 by 31 centimeters. The piece is dated Showa, after the war, so it shows a form of the feed trough in a hand-farming village but not an Edo one, and it is a single object from northern Kyushu.
