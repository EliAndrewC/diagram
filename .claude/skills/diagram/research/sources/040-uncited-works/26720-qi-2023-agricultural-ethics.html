qi-2023-agricultural-ethics

Qi Wentao (齊文濤), "中國古代的農業倫理思想" ("Agricultural ethical thought of ancient China", title translated), in 中國社會科學報 (Chinese Social Sciences Today), National Social Science Fund supplement, on the site of the National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences, 1 August 2023 (in Chinese; http://www.nopss.gov.cn/BIG5/n1/2023/0801/c448883-40048252.html)

What it is: A short essay by a Heilongjiang University philosophy professor summarizing the rules of conduct found in Chinese farm books, on planting, raising animals, taking wild plants and animals, and the size of a farm. It quotes from the Qimin Yaoshu, Wang Zhen's Nongshu, the Zhiben Tigang and earlier classics.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that farm books preferred grazing to penning (cattle out most days of the year, sheep penned only in hard cold), that the Qimin Yaoshu's design gave each chicken a little over one square meter, and that the ideal farm was about 100 mu, which it equates to 27 to 29 present-day mu. These are the essay's summaries of ideals set down in texts, not counts of real farms, and it gives no region or date for the figures beyond naming the books.
