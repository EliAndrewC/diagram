Vincent Goossaert, "Counting the Monks. The 1736-1739 Census of the Chinese Clergy", Late Imperial China 21 (2), 2000, pp. 40-85, abstract page on HAL SHS (https://shs.hal.science/halshs-00106193v1)

What it is: The HAL SHS repository record, with an English abstract and keywords, of a journal article analyzing archival data from the Qing census of Buddhist and Daoist clerics taken in 1736-1739, which counted about 340,000 clerics. The page carries the abstract and metadata, with the full text offered as a separate PDF.

Why it applies, and its limits: The abstract says the article treats both the political control behind the census and its results, including the geography of the clergy in maps and tables, so the full paper could carry numbers on how many monks and priests China held and where in the eighteenth century. This page holds only the abstract, not the figures, and its evidence is Qing, not the setting's period, and says nothing of Japan.
