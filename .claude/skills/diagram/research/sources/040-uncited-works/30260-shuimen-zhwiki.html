shuimen-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 水門 ("Water gate", title translated; the article is titled 水门) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/水门)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article defining the shuimen (water gate), the opening in an old city wall where a river enters or leaves, with a photograph of the Song-dynasty Kaifeng model showing the Guangli water gate and a picture of the combined water-and-land gate at Shaoxing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the form: the wall is carried across the river on stone footings joined by arches over the water, so boats still pass and the crossing is defended, with a sluice in the gateway to release flood water and regulate the level, or a closed water pass where no boat traffic is needed. It is a one-paragraph definition resting on one newspaper article about an excavated Song water gate at Yangzhou, and gives no dimensions, dates or count of gates for any city.
