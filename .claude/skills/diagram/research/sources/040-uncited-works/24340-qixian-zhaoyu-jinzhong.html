Jinzhong Evening News (晋中晚报), "昭馀古城，传奇千年" ("The old city of Zhaoyu, a thousand years of legend", title translated), reposted on Tencent News (in Chinese; https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20231124A00HMT00)

What it is: A newspaper feature on the old walled county town of Qixian (Zhaoyu), Shanxi: its layout, its Qing merchant trade and two great merchant compounds: the Qu compound on the town's east street, and the Qiao compound in Qiaojiabao village, 12 km northeast of the town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the town's rectangle of about 850 by 700 m, a wall circuit of about 3 km, four main streets 6 to 7 m wide with 28 lanes, two-storey shopfronts of five bays of 2.5 to 3 m, and over 1,000 shops in the whole county in 1910 (a county count, not the town's), with an undated 214 shops in six trades inside the town. It describes a rich merchant-banking town of the Ming and Qing, not an ordinary county seat, with a layout read as it stands, round numbers, and a count of lanes that differs between paragraphs (28 and over 30).
