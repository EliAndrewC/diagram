Touken World (刀剣ワールド), "日本庭園の構成要素「石灯篭」" ("A constituent of the Japanese garden: the stone lantern", title translated), in the basics of the Japanese garden (日本庭園の基礎知識), on Touken World, the sword site of the Nagoya Token Museum (in Japanese; https://touken-world.jp/tips/127831)

What it is: A short popular article on the stone lantern as part of a Japanese garden, naming its parts, its roles as lighting, garden emblem and tea-garden object, and three common types: Kasuga, Yukimi and Oribe.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists a lantern's parts (roof, jewel finial, fire box, middle platform, shaft, base), says lanterns were first used to light the night, and ties the garden lantern to the tea garden (roji) that arose in the Azuchi-Momoyama period. It gives no date for the lantern's first use and no sizes, and outside gardens it names only Kasuga Taisha, whose corridors, halls and approach hold many of the Kasuga type named for it; so it supports the lantern's form, its tea-garden setting and the lanterns of a great shrine, not lanterns in villages.
