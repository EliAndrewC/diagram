kasuga-baba-chimei

Terasaki Naotoshi (Kasuga City Local History Society), "馬場" ("baba", riding ground, title translated), in Kasuga Fudoki: place names of Kasuga, on the Kasuga City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.kasuga.fukuoka.jp/miryoku/history/1010879/1010921/1010926.html)

What it is: A short local-history column, printed in the city bulletin in 2015 and posted on the city website, on the place name Baba (riding ground) and its use for training horses, with the one Baba in Kasuga City and its distance from a medieval moated manor site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that horses and oxen supplied transport and plowing power in every farm village until the early Showa years and that Chikuzen alone has about 70 places named Baba. It reads the place name as a riding ground used by a local warrior who lived in a moated manor 300 m away; that reading is the author's inference from the layout, and the page says only that the manor was found, not that a riding ground was.
