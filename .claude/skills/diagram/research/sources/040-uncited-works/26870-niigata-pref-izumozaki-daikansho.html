niigata-pref-izumozaki-daikansho

Niigata Prefecture, Nagaoka Regional Development Bureau, "20 代官所跡" ("20. Site of the intendant's office", title translated), in its Ryokan-visiting road guide of 88 sites (in Japanese; https://www.pref.niigata.lg.jp/sec/nagaoka_kikaku/1356812169117.html)

What it is: A short prefectural page on the site of the shogunal intendant's office at Izumozaki, Niigata, the town's moves from 1616 to 1808 (the Akita residence, Amase's Inari-machi, Haguro-machi, then Amase again), with the poet Ryokan's brother's failed attempt to bring it back.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names four sites of the intendant's office in two centuries, 1616, 1624, 1724 and 1763, and a last move in 1808 within Amase after landslides, which shows that an intendant's office could be moved and was placed in the town. It says nothing of the office's buildings, size, staff or layout, and is a roadside guide to one town on the Hokkoku highway.
