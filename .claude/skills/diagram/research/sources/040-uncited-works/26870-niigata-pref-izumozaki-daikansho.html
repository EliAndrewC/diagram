niigata-pref-izumozaki-daikansho

Niigata Prefecture, Nagaoka Regional Development Bureau, "20 代官所跡" ("20. Site of the intendant's office", title translated), in its Ryokan-visiting road guide of 88 sites (in Japanese; https://www.pref.niigata.lg.jp/sec/nagaoka_kikaku/1356812169117.html)

What it is: A short prefectural page on the site of the shogunal intendant's office at Izumozaki, Niigata, the town's moves from 1616 to 1808 (the Akita residence, Amase's Inari-machi, Haguro-machi, then Amase again), with the poet Ryokan's brother's failed attempt to bring it back.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the office's moves over two centuries: in 1616 it was placed at the Akita residence, the Uesugi-era headquarters in the town's center; in 1624 it moved to Inari-machi in Amase; in 1724 to Haguro-machi after landslide damage; in 1763 back to Inari-machi; and in 1808, after a landslide, to its last site within Amase, which shows that an intendant's office could be moved and was placed in the town. It says nothing of the office's buildings, size, staff or layout, and is a roadside guide to one town on the Hokkoku highway.
