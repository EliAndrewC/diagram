bunka-kunibanzai-minami-shikomigura

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "國萬歳酒造南仕込蔵" ("Kunibanzai brewery, south brewing storehouse", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/195899)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of a registered brewing storehouse of 1908 in Akita: a plastered single-story storehouse of 251 square meters, 18 bays by 4, where the mash ferments, with a king-post truss roof.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of a sake brewery's fermenting storehouse, 18 bays by 4 bays in a gabled, gable-entered building. It is a 1908 building with a Western-style king-post truss, so it is later than the premodern brewery it follows.
