tokyo-naiso-fusuma-gaikan

Tokyo Interior Material Cooperative (東京内装材料協同組合), "襖の外観" ("The outward form of fusuma", title translated), in its Fusuma Dictionary (襖辞典), on the Japan interior materials dictionary site (in Japanese; https://naisouzairyou-annai.jp/fusuma/knowledge/exterior.html)

What it is: A trade cooperative's dictionary page naming the kinds of fusuma (sliding paper doors) by height, by the number that fit between two posts, by how they open, by frame width and by how they are made.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the standard heights in shaku and sun (5 shaku 7 sun is about 171 cm, 5 shaku 8 sun about 174 cm), the two-panel and four-panel sets that fit between posts, and says wide-framed fusuma are used mainly in the Tohoku and Hokuriku regions. It describes the fusuma trade's current products and gives no dates, so the sizes are modern standards, not a record of what an Edo room used.
