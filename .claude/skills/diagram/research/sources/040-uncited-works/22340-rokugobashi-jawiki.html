ja.wikipedia 六郷橋 ("Rokugo Bridge", title translated), with its history of the Rokugo ferry (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/六郷の渡し)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Rokugo Bridge over the Tama River between Tokyo and Kawasaki, covering the Tokaido's Edo-period bridge and then ferry at Rokugo and each later bridge up to the present one.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Tokugawa Ieyasu had a bridge built where the Tokaido crossed the Tama River in 1600, that it was rebuilt several times and gives the 1662 bridge as 107 ken (about 194.5 m) long and about 8 m wide, and that after a flood in 1688 it was not rebuilt and a ferry served until the Meiji era. Most of the page is about 19th and 20th century bridges and the modern road bridge, and it is the record of one major crossing on the main highway, not of an ordinary river crossing.
