Yanlin Wang, "5. Residential Buildings", in Chinese Culture, open textbook on Pressbooks (https://raider.pressbooks.pub/chineseculture/chapter/5-residential-buildings/)

What it is: A textbook chapter introducing four Chinese dwelling types: the Beijing siheyuan courtyard house, the Loess Plateau yaodong cave house, and the Fujian tulou, with a general note on north and south house orientation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the siheyuan's layout (gate at the southeast, screen walls, front court with rooms facing north, inner court, rear row) and says northern houses face north-south around open courts while southern ones have smaller courts and more openings, so it supplies a plan vocabulary for a northern courtyard house. It is a survey for students, gives yaodong sizes in feet and tulou dates of the 15th to 20th centuries without sources, and the siheyuan it describes is the Beijing type, not a county or village house.
