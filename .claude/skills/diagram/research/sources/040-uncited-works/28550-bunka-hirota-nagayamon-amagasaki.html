bunka-hirota-nagayamon-amagasaki

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "廣田家住宅長屋門" (the gate-house of the Hirota house, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka4.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka4.nii.ac.jp/index.php/heritages/detail/542598)

What it is: A database entry for a gate-house (nagayamon) in Amagasaki, Hyogo, a registered tangible cultural property dated 1898-1912 (Meiji), one story, 30 square meters, 3.5 ken by 2.5 ken. The note describes the gate opening in the west half and a storeroom in the east half, on the south edge of the compound.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and layout of a small gate-house of an old family's compound: a gabled tiled roof, a double plank door with a side wicket, a wall with a plank lower band and black plaster above, and a storeroom beside the gate. It dates from the late Meiji period, so it records a Meiji gate-house (the page does not say whether it follows an earlier one), and it is a single gate-house of a family the note calls an old house of standing, not a typical one.
