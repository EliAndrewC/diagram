ja.wikipedia 桐生織 ("Kiryu-ori", Kiryu silk weaving, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%A1%90%E7%94%9F%E7%B9%94)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the silk textiles of Kiryu in Gunma, tracing the craft from the Nara period through Edo-period market growth to the 20th-century factory era.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries dated particulars for a silk-weaving town: a market held six times a month, on the days ending in 5 and 9, which grew out of the market at the Tenjinsha shrine, the high loom brought from Kyoto in 1738, and a specialized preparation trade by the late Edo period. Its Edo material is a summary of a local history, and much of its length is Meiji to Showa factory and export history, which does not apply to the setting.
