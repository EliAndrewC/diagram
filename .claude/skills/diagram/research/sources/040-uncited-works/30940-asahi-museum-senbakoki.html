asahi-museum-senbakoki

Asahi Town Museum of History (Asahi-machi Rekishi Hakubutsukan), "千歯扱き" ("Senbakoki", the iron-toothed threshing comb, title translated), in the museum's Digital Museum collection database (in Japanese; https://asahitown-museum.com/post-1839.html)

What it is: A museum collection record for one senbakoki, an iron-toothed comb for threshing rice and wheat, giving its use and the period it served.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the tool was invented in the Edo period, stripped grain from the stalk in one pass, and was in wide use for about 200 years until the Meiji and Taisho periods; this example is said to have been used in the early Taisho era. It is a short catalog entry with no dimensions, and it dates the type but not any village's adoption of it.
