kitamoto-shishi-mugi-dakkoku

Kitamoto City, "畑作の過程 (一)麦 脱穀・調製" ("The stages of dry-field farming: barley threshing and preparation", title translated), in the History of Kitamoto City, folklore volume, ch. 3 sec. 1, on the Kitamoto Digital Archives (in Japanese; https://kdas.jp/detail_display.php?t_cd=1&acc_cd=3&aclc_cd=0&chap=3&hp_page=18&bc_cd=1)

What it is: A section of a city history's folklore volume on how barley and wheat were threshed and bagged in Kitamoto, Saitama, from informants' accounts, with the tools, their measurements and the work of the yard.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the threshing yard was bare earth kept for the work, roped off in the rain and strewn with straw in frost, that barley was beaten with flail poles or a stone roller drawn by an ox or horse, and that a household had two or three combs. The informants speak of the Meiji to early Showa years, and the page names the tools by their measurements, but gives no size for the yard and speaks only for one Saitama town.
