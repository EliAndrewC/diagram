bunka-shibata-ashigaru-nagaya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧新発田藩足軽長屋" ("Kyu Shibata-han ashigaru nagaya", the former foot-soldier row house of Shibata domain, title translated), on Bunka Isan Online (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/144338)

What it is: A national cultural-heritage database entry for a surviving row house of low-ranking foot soldiers of Shibata domain in Niigata, an Important Cultural Property designated in 1969. It gives the date, size and roof and a short description of the units.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured row house of the lowest warrior rank: eight units in one building of 1842, 43.6 m by 7.3 m, hipped and thatched, each unit with an entrance, two six-mat rooms, a three-mat room and a kitchen, which the entry calls quite cramped. It covers one building of one northern domain in the late Edo period and says almost no others of its kind survive, so it fixes the unit's size but not how common this plan was.
