ise-yamada-bugyosho-kinenkan

Ise City, "施設案内 山田奉行所記念館" ("Facility guide: the Yamada Magistrate's Office Memorial Hall", title translated), on the Ise City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.ise.mie.jp/cul_spo_edu/culture/shisetsu/yamadabugyousho/index.html)

What it is: A municipal facility page, updated in 2026, for the reconstructed part of the Yamada magistrate's office in Ise, which gives the building's floor areas, the office's founding and move, its duties and its staff.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a shogunate magistracy's staff (two magistrates, three yonin, three kyunin, four unit heads, six yoriki, twenty doshin, forty sailors), its four duties (guarding the two Ise shrines, justice over Ise and Shima, defense, and inspecting ships at Toba), its 1603 founding at Arataki and its move in 1635 to Kobayashi village. The reconstruction is the formal part only: a main house of 81.3 tsubo and a gate row of 19.6 tsubo, about one sixth of the old office's building area, so it is not a full plan of the office; the duties and roster are quoted from older books the page names, and the page is mostly a visitors' guide with its fees and hours.
