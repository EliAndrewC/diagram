minamiawaji-kitamoto-ke

Minamiawaji City, "旧北本家住宅並びに付属家" ("The former Kitamoto house and its outbuilding", title translated), Minamiawaji City cultural heritage pages (in Japanese; https://www.city.minamiawaji.hyogo.jp/bunkaisan/bunkazai/r-28.html)

What it is: A city heritage entry for a prefecture-designated folk-culture property on Awaji Island, Hyogo: the main house and outbuilding of a middling fishing household of the Meiji period, moved and restored in 1973, with the main house's frame size and roof form.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the main house's size (7.88 m by 6.89 m, with a ridge-board dated 1906) and the outbuilding's (6.9 m by 3.94 m), a stepped tile roof popular on Awaji, the attic used as a second floor reached by a ladder from the okuma room, and a hearth with a hanging pothook; that is a dimensioned fisher's house of a coastal island. It is a Meiji house, a fisher's rather than a farmer's, was moved from Ama to Kashu in 1973, and the page says the house is now dismantled and stored because of damage.
