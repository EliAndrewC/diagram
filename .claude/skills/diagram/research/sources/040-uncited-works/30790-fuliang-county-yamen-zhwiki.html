zh.wikipedia 浮梁县衙 ("Fuliang xian ya", the Fuliang county office, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/浮梁县衙)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the Fuliang county yamen in Fuliang, Jiangxi, built in the Daoguang reign of the Qing, listing its six surviving parts and its ground area, with photographs of its gate, screen wall and halls.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists what a county office held in a surviving example, the middle gate, the courtyard, the main hall, the second hall, the inner residence and the kitchen, on about 1,200 square meters, and says it is one of only four old county yamen left in China. It is a Qing building after 1820 and gives no plan, hall sizes or staff, and its stated area is of the surviving part and not of the whole compound as it stood, and one of its photographs is of a rebuilt stone arch.
