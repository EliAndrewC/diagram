ja.wikipedia 国衙 ("Kokuga", the provincial government compound, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/国庁)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kokuga, the compound where the governor of a province under the ritsuryo codes carried out government, with its usual layout, its history to the Muromachi period and a regional list of excavated sites.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the layout of the provincial office as excavated: a walled compound often tens to about 100 m on a side (300 by 200 m for the Musashi site), a central hall with side halls on an east-west axis, a gate to the south, and workshops, kitchens, the governor's residence and granaries around it, and it records measurements for sites such as Hoki (an outer compound 273 by 227 m), Izumo (167 m square) and Hizen (77.2 by 104.5 m). It is the ancient Nara and Heian state's governing compound, which the page says declined by the early Muromachi period, and it itself notes that the plans differ more than they agree between provinces.
