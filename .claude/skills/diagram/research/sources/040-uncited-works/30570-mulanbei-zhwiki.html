mulanbei-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 木兰陂 ("Mulan pi", the Mulan weir, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/木兰陂)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Mulan weir on the Mulan stream at Putian, Fujian, a Northern Song irrigation and tide-barring work, with its dimensions, its canal system and the three attempts to build it between 1064 and 1083.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that a Song-dynasty river weir was a masonry work of a size beyond a village's means: 219.13 m long, with 32 sluice gates and 33 piers, a main canal over 120 km with more than 300 sluices and culverts, and flood banks over 500 m, and it also served as a tide barrier where Xinghua Bay's tide reached. It describes the surviving structure and does not say how much of the present stone is original, and the three attempts it narrates (the first two failed to a flood and the tide) rest on short citations to local culture pages.
