yamaoka-chizumame-mitomi-shinden

Yamaoka Mitsuharu (Office Chizumame), "「地図豆」の地図を広げて街歩き: 16 屋敷森と雑木林をもとめて三富新田を歩く" ("Walking the town with a Chizumame map: 16, walking Mitomi Shinden in search of house groves and coppice woods", title translated), a map-walk leaflet (PDF) on the Chizumame site (in Japanese; https://www.ripro.co.jp/yamaoka/mapsuv200/meguri/16-1chizusanpo2.pdf)

What it is: A walking-guide leaflet by a map writer on the Mitomi Shinden new-field district in Miyoshi town and Tokorozawa, Saitama, with a route of about 9 km and a short account of how the district was laid out in the Genroku era under Yanagisawa Yoshiyasu. It reads the district's present and pre-1945 topographic maps for its strip plots of house ground, field and coppice wood along 6-ken roads.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the layout a settlement map can draw: 6-ken (about 11 m) roads cut both ways, with each farm's house plot, then field, then coppice wood in one strip behind it, about 375 ken (562 m) from road to wood edge and 40 ken (about 72 m) of frontage, no irrigation channel, and tea planted on the boundaries and near the houses. Its evidence is a planned Musashino upland field of 1694-1696 on loam, read through maps of 1921 to 1948 and a present-day walk, so it is a Kanto dry-field form, not a rice-paddy one, and it gives no sources for its figures.
