ja.wikipedia 寺町寺院群 ("Teramachi jiingun", the Teramachi temple cluster of Kanazawa, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/寺町寺院群)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the temple district of Teramachi and Nomachi in Kanazawa, Ishikawa, listing its nearly 70 temples and noting its 22.0-hectare designation as a preservation district in 2012.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Kaga domain gathered temples on the Sai river side as a defense against the Ikko-ikki, with similar groups at Utatsuyama and Kodatsuno, so it shows a castle town placing temples together on its margin. The article gives no map or dates for the placement, its list is mostly temple names and lord associations, and the district as it stands is the 2012 survivor.
