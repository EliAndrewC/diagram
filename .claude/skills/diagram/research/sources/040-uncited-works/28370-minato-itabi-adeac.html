minato-itabi-adeac

Ito Hiroyuki, "板碑の出土と伝来" ("the unearthing and transmission of itabi", title translated), in 『港区史』 (history of Minato Ward), chapter 3 "Medieval temples of Minato Ward", section 3 "Medieval itabi", on the Adeac digital archive (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/minato-city/texthtml/d110010/mp100010-110010/ht002100)

What it is: A signed section of a ward history of Minato, Tokyo, on the itabi (medieval stone memorial slabs) found in the ward, how Edo-period writers recorded them, and where they were dug up or brought in.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says about thirty slabs, more than seventy percent of the ward's, are known from Zenpukuji, and reasons from slabs found there, at Chodenji and in a site north of Zenpukuji that a medieval cemetery stood along the southern edge of the plateau, so it supports a graveyard beside a temple on high ground. The cemetery is inferred ("may have been", "is presumed") from the slabs, not excavated as a cemetery, and the section is about the slabs' discovery and movement, not about the temples' layout.
