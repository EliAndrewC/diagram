bunka-hinomisaki-tamagaki

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "日御碕神社 神の宮(上の宮)玉垣" (the jewel fence of the upper shrine of Hinomisaki Shrine, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/207926)

What it is: A national important-cultural-property entry for the jewel fence (tamagaki) of the Kami no Miya upper shrine of Hinomisaki Shrine in Izumo, Shimane, a cypress-bark-roofed fence twenty-five ken long with bends, dated 1644, with a note on the rebuilding of the whole shrine in 1634-1644.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a shrine fence to 1644 and gives its length, and notes that the buildings are set on the uneven ground of a hill's foot, using the terrain, so it supports a fence enclosing a shrine's main buildings and a precinct fitted to a slope. It is a major shrine's fence of the early Edo period, in a refined Momoyama manner the entry praises, and says nothing of a village shrine's grounds or the fence's height.
