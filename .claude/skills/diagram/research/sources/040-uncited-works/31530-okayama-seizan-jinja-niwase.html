Okayama City, "⓬ 清山神社（庭瀬城址）" ("No. 12, Seizan Shrine (the site of Niwase Castle)", title translated), in "吉備・陵南にある石碑を訪ねて 第一編 神社の石碑" ("Visiting the stone monuments of Kibi and Ryonan, part 1: shrine monuments", title translated), pp. 107-115, on the city's Kibi area community site (in Japanese; https://townweb.e-okayamacity.jp/kibi-r/robo/assets/img/shrine/1-12.pdf)

What it is: A nine-page illustrated guide to Seizan Shrine, the shrine to the founding lords of the Itakura house of Niwase domain in Okayama, built on the grounds of the domain's castle. It has a plan of the grounds with the monuments, lantern, gate and fence marked and measured, their dates and donors, a donor tablet of more than 500 names, the family tree, and a list of the shrine's treasures.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports a domain lord's ancestor shrine set in the former lord's residence grounds: founded in 1793 in the southeast garden of the estate, moved in the early Meiji years, with a fence given by the headmen of three counties in 1793, a stone torii and paired lanterns dated 1793, and a donor tablet naming retainers, townspeople and villagers. It is a small castle-town domain's shrine, not a village shrine, and what stands now is its Meiji-period site with 20th-century additions (a 1991 renovation, a 1993 monument); the sizes are printed on the plan in millimeters but the saved text scatters them from the objects they measure, so read none of them from this text without the page's plan.
