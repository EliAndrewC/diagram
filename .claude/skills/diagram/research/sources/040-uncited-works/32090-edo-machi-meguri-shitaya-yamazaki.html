Edo machi meguri (江戸町巡り), "【下谷②】下谷山崎町" ("[Shitaya 2] Shitaya Yamazaki-cho", title translated), blog post of 23 March 2018 (in Japanese; https://edo.amebaownd.com/posts/3911689)

What it is: A blog entry in a series on Edo's town districts, giving the name, reading, dates, later names and a short history of Shitaya Yamazaki-cho in Edo (now Taito ward), with a house count.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the district was reclaimed from a reed marsh in 1616 as a grant to the kurokuwa-mono (low-rank laborers), became a townsmen's district under the town magistrate in 1699, had 611 houses in 1828, and was one of Edo's three great slums; it also lists who lived on each block. It is a hobbyist's compilation citing a few old maps and records by name, concerns one part of the capital in the Edo period, and gives no figure for plot or block sizes.
