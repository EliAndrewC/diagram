bunka-fujimoto-nishi-naya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "藤本家住宅西納屋" ("Fujimoto house, west shed", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/118774)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of a registered building in Fujiidera, Osaka: a Meiji-period wooden single-story shed of 30 square meters, four by two bays, in the yard of a house.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and plan of a small outbuilding on a house lot (four bays by two, split into two rooms, tied to a storehouse and another shed) and how outbuildings frame the west and south edges of the yard. It is Meiji (1868-1911), with no closer date, and from the Kinai, and does not say what the shed held. The house it belongs to has north and south rice storehouses, a clothing storehouse, a tool storehouse and front and back gates (the page's list of related buildings), so this is a large house's yard with many storehouses, not a small farm's; the page does not say who the family were.
