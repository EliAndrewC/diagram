bunka-nishie-umagoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "西江家住宅旧馬小屋及び男部屋" ("Nishie house, former stable and men's quarters", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/116594)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of a registered tangible cultural property in Narihacho, Takahashi, Okayama: a Meiji-period earthen-walled single-story building of 198 square meters at a house's gate, holding a row of horse stalls and a room with a loft.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a stable built as a row of stalls on about two thirds of the building's length beside a gate and the main house, with a hipped-gable front, which gives a layout for a stable; its 198 square meters is the whole building, the men's room and earthen floor included, with the stalls on about two thirds of its length. It is dated only to the Meiji period (1868-1911) and is a wealthy house's plastered building, so it is later and more substantial than a premodern farm stable.
