zh.wikipedia 苏州城 (Suzhou city, redirecting from the city wall, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/苏州城墙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Suzhou's city and walls, reached by its redirect from 苏州城墙 (Suzhou city wall), giving the wall's history from the Wu kings to the present.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records that the Ming-Qing prefectural city had a wall 45 li long in 1662, that the Wu Yue ruler rebuilt it in brick and stone in 922 as the earliest known brick city, and that only about 2 kilometers survive. It gives no height, thickness or gate sizes, and the ancient wall's location is disputed.
