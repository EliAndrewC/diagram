pwsannong-zhongyujing

"《种鱼经》" ("The Fish-Raising Classic", title translated), in the encyclopedia of agricultural history on Zhihui Sannong, the agricultural knowledge service of China Agriculture Press (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/560774.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry on the Zhongyujing, a Ming treatise by Huang Shengzeng (1490-1540) on raising freshwater fish, about 3,000 characters in three parts. It summarizes the sections on fry, pond building and fish disease.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the book advises keeping two ponds together, a shallow pond with the north side dug deeper, an artificial island in the pond, and banana, trees and hibiscus planted around it, and feeding at fixed times and places. It is an encyclopedia's summary of a Ming book written before 1540 by a man of Wu county, which also records mullet farming on the Songjiang coast, and the page gives no pond sizes or depths.
