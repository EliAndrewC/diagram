kubota-polishing-rice-mortar

Kubota Corporation, "玄米をついて糠(ぬか)を取り除く「精米」" ("Milling: pounding brown rice to remove the bran", title translated), from 稲作の歴史とそれを支えた伝統農具, on Kubota no Tanbo (in Japanese; https://www.kubota.co.jp/kubotatanbo/history/tools/milling.html)

What it is: A short company educational page on milling brown rice by pounding in a mortar with a pestle, by foot treadle or by water wheel, with the sizes of a buried foot-mortar, a water wheel and a hand hook.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the foot-driven mortar sunk into the ground (570 mm long, 560 mm high and 2,200 mm deep, as the page gives the one piece it measures) and the water-wheel mill that lifts the pestle by gears, which supports both as milling tools of a village. It gives no date for either, no region, and no count of households that used a water mill, so it cannot say which was usual.
