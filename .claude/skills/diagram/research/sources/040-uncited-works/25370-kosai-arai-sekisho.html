kosai-arai-sekisho

Kosai City, "新居関所・史料館【特別史跡新居関跡】" ("Arai Checkpoint and Museum [Arai Checkpoint Site, Special Historic Site]", title translated), Kosai City tourism pages (in Japanese; https://www.city.kosai.shizuoka.jp/kanko_bunka_sports/kankospot/9675.html)

What it is: A city tourism page on the Arai checkpoint (also called Imagire) on the Tokaido beside the Imagire ferry landing in Shizuoka, with visitor information and the text of the site's signboard on its founding in 1600, its two moves, its staffing and its surviving guardhouse.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the checkpoint to 1600, says it moved after a storm in 1699 and after the 1707 earthquake and tsunami, that the shogunate ran it until 1702 and the Yoshida domain afterward, and that dozens of officials served in shifts inspecting travelers and guns bound for Edo; that supports the staffing and function of a major road barrier. The surviving guardhouse was rebuilt in 1855-1858, so it shows a late-Edo building, and the page gives no plan, dimensions or layout of the grounds.
