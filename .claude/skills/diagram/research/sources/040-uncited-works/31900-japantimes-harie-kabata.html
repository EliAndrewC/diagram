Joanna Kawecki, "How a single current keeps Shiga's 'village of water' in concert", The Japan Times, July 26, 2025 (https://japantimes.co.jp/life/2025/07/26/travel/harie-village-of-water-kyoto-travel)

What it is: A travel article on Harie, a district of Takashima, Shiga, where groundwater from an alluvial fan feeds household spring basins called kabata, with quotes from the local water-village committee's head and a farmer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says about 100 of Harie's 140 households have a kabata of three basins in sequence (one for drinking and cooking, one for washing vegetables and cooling food, one where carp clean dirty pots), that the system began at the village temple in the latter half of the Edo period by the account of a resident, and that the whole village trims the stream grass four times a year. The Edo dating is one resident's account quoted in a newspaper travel piece whose trip was paid for by the Biwako Visitors Bureau, and the households, the population of 420 and the washing with eco-friendly soap after the carp are as of 2025.
