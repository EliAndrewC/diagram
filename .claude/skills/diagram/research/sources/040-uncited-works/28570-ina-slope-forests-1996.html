四方 圭一郎, "長野県伊那市を事例とした農村集落における段丘崖斜面樹林の保全" ("Study on the Conservation of Slope Forests of Terrace Scarps in Rural Community in the Area of Ina City, Nagano Prefecture", as the page gives its English title), 『ランドスケープ研究』 (Landscape Research) 59 (5), 85-88, 29 March 1996, Japanese Institute of Landscape Architecture, catalog record on CiNii Research (in Japanese; https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390282680646806784)

What it is: The CiNii library record of a four-page journal paper on the slope woods of terrace and fault scarps in the Ina valley of Nagano, where many farming settlements lie along the scarps and use most of the slope as woodland. It carries the paper's abstract, its citation links and its reference list, not the paper's text.

Why it applies, and its limits: The abstract says the slope woods of the settlements studied in Ina City are of three types: house groves (yashikirin), shrine groves (shasorin) and production woods, and that differences in structure and in who owns the slope decide how well each is kept. It is the abstract of a 1996 survey of how the woods are held and cared for in the present, and says nothing of their size, how they were used or what the form was in the Edo period; the paper itself was not read.
