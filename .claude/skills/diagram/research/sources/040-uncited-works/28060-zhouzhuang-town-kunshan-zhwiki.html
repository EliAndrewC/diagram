zh.wikipedia 周庄镇 (昆山市) ("Zhouzhuang town, Kunshan", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%91%A8%E5%BA%84%E9%95%87_(%E6%98%86%E5%B1%B1%E5%B8%82))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Zhouzhuang, a water town in Kunshan, Jiangsu, amid lakes and canals, giving its geography, its growth as a market town, its history from the Song, its protected buildings and its rise as a tourist site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes how the town spread from the east bank of the market river in the Yuan toward the west in the mid-Ming and by the early Qing centered on one bridge with two T-shaped main streets, and quotes the 1991 town gazetteer for eight shop streets and boat traffic on a grid of waterways. The growth account is brief, partly flagged "source requested", and the page's other material (legends of Shen Wansan, the 2010 administrative and population figures, tourism from 1986) concerns the twentieth century and later, so it gives a sequence for a water town's growth but no dimensions.
