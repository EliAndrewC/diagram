komonjokan-shokunin-sho

Kinsei Komonjokan (近世こもんじょ館), "嘉永二年三月改御支配帳 諸職人抄" ("Kaei ninen sangatsu aratame goshihaicho, shoshokunin sho", the craftsmen's extract of the register of supervised retainers revised in March 1849, title translated), original title "天保御支配帳", in the Kirokukan decipherment section of the Komonjokan site (in Japanese; https://komonjokan.net/cgi-bin/komon/kirokukan/kirokukan_view.cgi?mode=details&code_no=80017&class_id=17)

What it is: A transcription, on a site of deciphered early modern documents, of a register of a domain's craftsmen listed under the offices that supervised them (arms, the wardrobe, the works office, the finance magistrate), dated to the third month of Kaei 2 (1849). Each line gives the stipend (in rice-stipend units, koku of rice, horse-loads or ryo) and the trade, such as master carpenter, smith, tatami-maker, plasterer, tub-maker, roofer and sword polisher, with the craftsman's name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows which building and metal trades a domain kept on stipend in the late Edo period, how they were ranked (master, deputy, apprentice) and that some lived in other towns (Hanamaki, Nobeji, Edo are named). It is a bare list: the page gives no commentary and does not itself name the domain, and it records only craftsmen on the domain's books, not the trades of ordinary townspeople or the sizes of their shops or yards.
