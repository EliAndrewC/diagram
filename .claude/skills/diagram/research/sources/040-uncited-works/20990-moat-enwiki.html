en.wikipedia "Moat" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moat)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the moat, the deep ditch around a castle or town, with its historical use in the ancient world, medieval and later Europe, Africa, Asia, Australia and North America, and its modern uses.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its Asia section says Japanese castles had up to three concentric moats, that most feudal-era ones were dry (karabori) in contrast to the water-filled mizubori of modern castles, and that the outer moat protected support buildings and served as a waterway for the city. It also names the Forbidden City and Xi'an moats in China, but those and most of the Asian passage carry no citation, and the dimensions it gives are for European or African works, not East Asian ones.
