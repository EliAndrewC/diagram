Agency for Cultural Affairs, "掛川城御殿" ("The Kakegawa castle palace," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/188993)

What it is: A national database entry for the residence and office building in the second bailey of the 50,000-koku Ota domain's Kakegawa castle, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1854, with a hall and study part of 1855, a small study part of 1861 and an offices part of 1860, designated in 1980.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the rooms of the reception hall, study, council room and the offices, such as the chamberlain's room, the censor's room and the kitchen steward's room, as one of few surviving castle palaces and notes that nearly all of it survives. It lists room names only, without sizes, and shows a small domain's palace at its final rebuilding in the 1850s.
