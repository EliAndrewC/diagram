en.wikipedia "Hedge" (the English Wikipedia article on hedges and hedgerows, reached from "Hedgerow") (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedgerow)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on hedges and hedgerows as field boundaries and live fences. It covers their history, species, hedgerow trees, laying and trimming, the Devon and Cornish earth-bank hedges, and the Qing Willow Palisade.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives hedgerow tree spacing (no closer than 10 meters apart), a hedge's width (usually no more than about one meter), and a short paragraph on the Willow Palisade of southern Manchuria, two dikes with a moat between them, topped with willows tied together, built in the 17th century to control movement and tax ginseng and timber. Nearly all the rest is about Britain, Ireland and the Low Countries, with species and management from European farms, so apart from the palisade it says nothing of East Asian field boundaries, and the palisade paragraph gives no length or dimension.
