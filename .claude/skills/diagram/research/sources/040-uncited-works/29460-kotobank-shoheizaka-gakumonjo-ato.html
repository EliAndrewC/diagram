kotobank-shoheizaka-gakumonjo-ato

"昌平坂学問所跡" ("Shoheizaka Gakumonjo ato, site of the Shoheizaka Academy", title translated), a headword compiled on Kotobank from several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/昌平坂学問所跡-3043825)

What it is: A Kotobank excerpt of the Heibonsha Nihon Rekishi Chimei Taikei (Historical Place-Name Encyclopedia of Japan) entry for the site of the shogunate's Shoheizaka academy beside the Yushima Seido in Bunkyo ward, Tokyo; the page says it prints only part of the text.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the school was attached to the Yushima Seido and made directly shogunal in 1790 as part of the Kansei reforms, with Neo-Confucianism as the orthodox learning, and that instructors' residences and lecture halls were finished in 1792. The excerpt gives no site area or layout, and this is the shogunate's own school in Edo, not a domain or provincial one.
