en.wikipedia "Kam Tin" (the English Wikipedia article on Kam Tin, 錦田, a district of the New Territories, Hong Kong) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kam_Tin)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Kam Tin, an area on an alluvial plain in Yuen Long District, Hong Kong, home of the Tang clan. It lists its villages and notes the walled village of Kat Hing Wai, study halls, ancestral halls and a martyrs' grave of 1936.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the Tang clan's settlement at Kam Tin from 973, says the walled village Kat Hing Wai is compact, of narrow row-houses and temples separated by small alleys, and that its wall was raised against pirates and bandits. The article is short, its own banner says it rests on a single source, and it gives no dimensions, population or layout beyond that one sentence, and the walls and halls described are those standing today.
