zh.wikipedia 猪厕 (zhuce, the pig toilet, title translated), simplified-character edition (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/猪厕)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the pig toilet, a latrine built over or beside a pigsty so that pigs eat the waste, covering China, Korea and the Ryukyus, its forms, and its benefits and harms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the form in China from Han tomb models and the Guoyu, describes three types (loft, ground-level, stilt) and says Han houses commonly joined latrine and sty, and it says the Ryukyu type came from Fujian settlers and was banned after the Second World War. Its dates for Korea are loose, it gives almost no dimensions beyond a Korean pit 1.5 m deep, and the 13 to 15 percent measly-pork figure it cites comes from a 2010 survey of mainland sties, so the figure is not premodern evidence.
