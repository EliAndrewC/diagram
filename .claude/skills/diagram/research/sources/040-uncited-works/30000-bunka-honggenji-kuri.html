bunka-honggenji-kuri

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "本源寺 庫裏" ("Honggenji kuri", the kitchen-and-living hall of Honggenji temple, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/229698)

What it is: A national database record for the kuri (temple kitchen and priests' quarters) of Honggenji, a Rinzai temple in Tsuyama, Okayama, listing its dimensions (20.0 m by 12.0 m, one story with a partial second floor, gabled, tiled) and a short commentary on the temple precinct.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured plan size for a kuri built about 1673-1681 and says how the precinct is laid out: the main hall in the middle, the kuri beside it to the east, the Mori family graves to the northwest. It speaks for one temple of the Tsuyama domain's lords, a daimyo family temple the page itself calls an early surviving example, so it is not a village temple or a typical size.
