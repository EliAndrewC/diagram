miura-2003

Miura Osamu, "屋敷林調査ノート -2001.9.10広戸風体験記-" ("notes on a house-grove survey: an account of experiencing the Hirodo wind, 10 September 2001", title translated), 季刊地理学 (Quarterly Journal of Geography) 55(1), 2003, pp. 35-39, on J-STAGE (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tga1992/55/1/55_1_35/_pdf)

What it is: A short signed forum note by a geographer, written while surveying the windbreak groves called kose near Mount Nagi in northern Okayama, recounting what he saw of the local Hirodo wind during typhoons in August and September 2001 and measuring the wind against the nearby AMeDAS records.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the kose as a windbreak raised on a bank (tsukiji) behind the house and planted with trees, shows a madake-dominated yard grove beside lodged rice, and describes the pollarding of the trunks to a little above the main roof ridge; the author judged the wind at over 10 and then over 15 m/s by its force (force 6 and 7), while the Nagi AMeDAS station recorded at most 5 m/s that day; the station did record 13 to 16 m/s in the stronger Hirodo wind of 21 August 2001, and the wind's damage zone is cited as about 4 km north-south by 10 km east-west. The note is mostly about the wind, not the grove: the kose get a few sentences, with no counts or dimensions, and the evidence is one observer's 2001 trip.
