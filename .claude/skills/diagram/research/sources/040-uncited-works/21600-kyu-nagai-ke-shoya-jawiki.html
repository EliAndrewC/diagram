ja.wikipedia 旧永井家庄屋屋敷 ("kyu Nagai-ke shoya yashiki", the former Nagai village headman's residence, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%97%A7%E6%B0%B8%E4%BA%95%E5%AE%B6%E5%BA%84%E5%B1%8B%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on a house built in 1791 in Tsurugi town, Tokushima, listing its site area and its buildings: main house, storehouse, an indigo drying-and-composting building, a well and a garden.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the compound of a village headman's household in an indigo-growing district, about 550 tsubo with a main house, storehouse, indigo building and a garden, which fits a headman's holding in a premodern setting. It is a three-paragraph stub with no plan or dimensions of the buildings, it says the house was restored in 1997, and its infobox types the house as a samurai residence even though its title and name call it a headman's.
