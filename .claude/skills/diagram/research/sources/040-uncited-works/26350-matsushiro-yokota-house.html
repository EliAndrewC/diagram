matsushiro-yokota-house

Shinshu Matsushiro Tourist Association, "旧横田家住宅" ("The former Yokota residence", title translated), under Tourist Attractions (in Japanese; https://www.matsushiro-kankou.com/spot/spot-419/)

What it is: A tourist-association page on the former residence of the Yokota family, middle-rank retainers of the Matsushiro domain in Nagano, a nationally designated Important Cultural Property.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the family held 150 koku, moved to this plot in the late 18th century, that such a house was a kind of official housing, and that the plot holds a front gate, the main house behind it, a retirement house east of the main house and a storehouse to its southwest, preserved almost fully in its late Edo positions. It gives the arrangement of buildings in words only, with no dimensions, and the page's remainder is visitor information and family biography.
