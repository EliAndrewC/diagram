en.wikipedia "Buddhist monasticism" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist_monasticism)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Buddhist monasticism across Asia: the origin of the monastic order, the Vinaya traditions, ordination stages, celibacy, diet, hierarchy, and regional sections on Tibet, East Asia, Japan and Southeast Asia.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its East Asia section says monks in China, Korea, Vietnam and much of Japan generally did not beg, so monasteries took bulk donations of rice and funds and many monks farmed their own food after Baizhang Huaihai, which bears on how a temple was supplied; it also says East Asian ordination is under the Dharmaguptaka Vinaya, with 250 rules for monks, and that Confucian views discouraged begging. It gives no dates for these practices, no counts of monks or temple grounds, and says Japan's clergy were mostly non-celibate priests rather than Vinaya monks, so it does not describe a monastery's size or layout; three of its sections carry the article's own banner that they lack citations.
