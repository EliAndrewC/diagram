"欽定八旗通志 (四庫全書本)/卷068 土田志七" (Qinding baqi tongzhi, Siku quanshu edition, volume 68, "Treatise on land, part 7", title translated), the imperially commissioned "Comprehensive Gazetteer of the Eight Banners", on Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/欽定八旗通志_(四庫全書本)/卷068)

What it is: The Wikisource text of volume 68 of the Eight Banners gazetteer: the land figures for the imperial household's estates (large and half estates, gardens, orchards, honey, reed and cotton-indigo households, grain estates) year by year from the Shunzhi reign to the Qianlong, and the estate and garden lands of the imperial clansmen by banner.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how a state-run estate near the capital was sized and taxed: a grain estate of 18 qing with 10 to 15 laborers and a headman, a half estate of 9 qing, one "cord" of land as 42 mu, a vegetable-garden keeper given 1 qing 80 mu, and a tax of 3 fen of silver and a bundle of fodder per mu. These are the early Qing court's quotas for bannermen's and bondservants' land in Zhili and Fengtian, not a village's holdings, and the clan estates are given in a unit (shang) the page does not define.
