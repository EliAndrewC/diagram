eic-net-kunugi-konara-forest

Environmental Innovation Information Organization (EIC Net), "クヌギ・コナラ林" ("kunugi-konara forest", the sawtooth oak and konara oak wood, title translated), in its environmental glossary (in Japanese; https://www.eic.or.jp/ecoterm/?act=view&serial=672)

What it is: A glossary entry defining the woods dominated by kunugi and konara oaks, which grow from the Tohoku region south to Kyushu, and how people managed them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says these are a typical secondary wood of the satoyama, mostly used as fuel wood and kept up by human management such as cutting, undergrowth clearing and leaf raking, and that the mix of species varies with that management. It gives no dates, sizes or rotation lengths, and its note that bamboo and sasa now invade is about the woods since they stopped being used for fuel.
