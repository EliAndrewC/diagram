gakugei-terakoya-orai

Tan Kazuhiro, "寺子屋の学習と往来物" ("Terakoya learning and the orai-mono textbooks", title translated), electronic exhibition, Open Educational Contents Archive of Tokyo Gakugei University (東京学芸大学教育コンテンツアーカイブ) (in Japanese; https://d-archive.u-gakugei.ac.jp/exhibition/orai)

What it is: A university library's online exhibition, reworked from the catalog of a 1993 exhibit, on the textbooks (orai-mono) used in Edo terakoya schools and the order in which a pupil learned: the syllabary, numerals, single characters, then compounds, letters and moral texts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports what a terakoya taught and in what order, with titles and dates of the textbooks (for example the Shobai Orai of 1732 and the Jitsugokyo and Doji-kyo of 1690), and calls the terakoya's teacher's central work handwriting, with arithmetic taught in cities and their outskirts. It gives no pupil counts, building sizes or school layouts, and it notes that a pupil's course varied by region, class, sex and the teacher's ability, and that few pupils reached the legal texts or Chinese classics.
