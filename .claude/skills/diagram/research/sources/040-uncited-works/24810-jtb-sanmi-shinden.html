jtb-sanmi-shinden

Japan Travel Bureau Foundation (公益財団法人日本交通公社), "三富新田" ("Sanmi Shinden", the Sanmi new fields), in the National Tourism Resource Ledger (全国観光資源台帳), as of April 2020 (in Japanese; https://tabi.jtb.or.jp/res/110426-)

What it is: A tourism-resource ledger entry on the Sanmi (Kamitomi, Nakatomi, Shimotomi) new-field villages in Saitama, a dry-field settlement of about 1,400 ha opened in 1694-96 under the lord of Kawagoe, Yanagisawa Yoshiyasu.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a planned layout with figures: 5 cho (about 5 ha) per household in a long strip 40 ken (about 72 m) wide and 375 ken (about 675 m) deep, along a village road 6 ken (about 10.8 m) wide, with house, field and coppice woodland (yama) in that order on each side, and 11 deep wells (about 22 m) shared by several households. It notes the woodland supplied fuel and compost and that the large grant was because the land was not very productive. It describes the original scheme on dry upland, not paddy, and says nothing of how the strips changed after the 1690s.
