National Agricultural Exhibition Center (China Agricultural Museum), "浙江湖州南浔桑基鱼塘系统" ("The Nanxun mulberry-dike fish-pond system, Huzhou, Zhejiang", title translated) (in Chinese; https://www.ciae.com.cn/detail/zh/16186.html)

What it is: A museum web page, dated 2020, on the mulberry-dike and fish-pond system of Nanxun district, Huzhou, Zhejiang, which states its extent of about 120,000 mu, its claimed age of about 2,500 years, and its 2017 recognition by the FAO as a globally important agricultural heritage system.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the cycle of mulberry on the dike, leaves to silkworms, silkworm droppings to the fish, and pond mud back to the mulberry, names the checkerboard drainage of the lowland (a long channel every five to seven li, a cross channel every seven to ten) and the four carps stocked together, and says the low flood-prone Linghu land suits mulberry rather than rice. The figures describe the system as it stands (120,000 mu, 50,000 mu in the core), not a premodern survey; its 2,500-year age is a claim the page does not support, and it gives no pond or dike dimensions.
