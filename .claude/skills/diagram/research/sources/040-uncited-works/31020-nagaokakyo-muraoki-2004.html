nagaokakyo-muraoki-2004

Nagaokakyo City Lifelong Learning Division, Cultural Properties section (長岡京市生涯学習課文化財係), "展示コーナーだより 第16号" ("Exhibition corner news, no. 16", title translated), April 2004 (in Japanese; https://city.nagaokakyo.lg.jp/cmsfiles/contents/0000005/5235/hurusatofile16.pdf)

What it is: A one-sheet exhibition leaflet on the village rules (muraoki) that survive in Nagaokakyo, Kyoto, from 1642 to 1840. It describes the rules against theft and gambling, the rules for mountain use and thrift, the youth association's rules, and the penalties.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated examples from villages near Kyoto: the oldest local rule, Tomooka village 1642, with three articles on stealing mountain bamboo and wood, banning waterfowl hunting, and banning gambling; a 1683 Chohoji village rule banishing crop thieves and fining a gambling host one kan and players 500 mon; and an 1840 youth-group rule. It reports a handful of surviving documents from one county, and says nothing about how often the penalties were applied.
