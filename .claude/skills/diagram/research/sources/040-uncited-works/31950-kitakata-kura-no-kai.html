kitakata-kura-no-kai

Kitakata Kura no Kai (喜多方 蔵の会), "蔵の歴史と風土" ("The history and climate of the kura", title translated), quoting Suma Akira, Kura o shiru mini hyakka (蔵を知るミニ百科) (in Japanese; https://kuranokai.jp/know/know2)

What it is: A page by a Kitakata, Fukushima preservation society on the kinds and number of earthen-walled storehouses (kura) of the town, the 70 m Shimashin goods storehouse, and why the town built so many.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists kinds of kura (for brewing, shops, goods, sitting rooms, retirement, privies, boundary walls), counts about 2,600 in the old town and over 4,000 after the municipal mergers from the tax register, and says the 70 m 'thirty-eight ken' kura finished in 1882 held a year's goods made by farmers in winter. Its explanations for the kura (Nakae Toju's teaching of equality, rivalry with Aizu-Wakamatsu) are the author's opinion and are not documented, and the counts are of the present day.
