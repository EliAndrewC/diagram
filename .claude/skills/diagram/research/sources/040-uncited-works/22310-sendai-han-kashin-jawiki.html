ja.wikipedia 仙台藩家臣 ("Retainers of the Sendai domain", title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/仙台藩家臣)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article listing the senior retainer houses (monbatsu) of the Date lords of the Sendai domain by rank, each with its stipend in koku, its castle or residence and its line of heads.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rank ladder of one large domain's vassal houses (ichimon, ikka, junikka, ichizoku, shukuro, chakuza, tachiage, meshidashi) with stipends from a few hundred to about 30,000 koku, and the seat each held, which shows how a domain's retainers were graded and spread over its districts. It covers only the upper ranks of Sendai, with ranks and sums as they stood late in the Edo period and no account of what a stipend bought, so it does not carry over to other domains or to rank-and-file samurai.
