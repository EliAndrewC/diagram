ja.wikipedia 仙台藩家臣 ("Retainers of the Sendai domain", title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/仙台藩家臣)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article listing the senior retainer houses (monbatsu) of the Date lords of the Sendai domain by rank, each with its stipend in koku, its castle or residence and its line of heads.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rank ladder of one large domain's vassal houses (ichimon, ikka, junikka, ichizoku, shukuro, chakuza, tachiage, meshidashi) with stipends from 50 koku to about 24,000 among houses that lasted (30,000 for houses that died out early or became lords in their own right), and the seat each held, which shows how a domain's retainers were graded and spread over its districts. It covers only the upper ranks of Sendai (the meshidashi by family name only), gives each stipend without a date though the list shows houses promoted, demoted and moved during the period, and gives no account of what a stipend bought, so it does not carry over to other domains or to rank-and-file samurai.
