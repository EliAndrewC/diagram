bunka-hakusan-jinja-haiden

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Japan, "白山神社拝殿" ("Hakusan jinja haiden", the worship hall of Hakusan Shrine, title translated), a building record in the National Designated Cultural Properties database (国指定文化財等データベース) (in Japanese; https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/heritage/detail/102/1951)

What it is: A government database record for one Important Cultural Property, the worship hall of Hakusan Shrine in Uji, Kyoto Prefecture, giving its date, structure, designation number and owner.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a surviving shrine hall to 1277 (late Kamakura) and gives its form: three bays by three bays, a single story, a hipped roof thatched with reed, designated in 1910. The commentary, history and inscription fields are blank, so the record gives no floor area, no account of the shrine's village or precinct, and no basis for judging whether a hall of this size was typical of village shrines.
