nippon-com-tamagaki

nippon.com, "神社空間を読み解く⑧玉垣" ("Reading the shrine space, 8: the tamagaki", title translated), in the series "神社に行こう！" ("Let's go to a shrine!", title translated), 21 June 2016 (in Japanese; https://www.nippon.com/ja/views/b05208/)

What it is: A short magazine article on the tamagaki, the fence around a shrine's main hall that separates the sacred inside from the world outside. It names the inner and outer fences and the sakaki tree that the oldest fences were made of.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the fence around the honden marks the sacred ground inside from the ordinary world outside, that the innermost is called the mizugaki, and that Ise's inner shrine has four fences, which supports a shrine of rank having more than one enclosing fence. It also says the oldest form was a hedge of living trees, the sakaki, and that the shrine wood was once evergreen broadleaf forest. It gives no measures or dates, and the account of the oldest form is the article's own statement.
