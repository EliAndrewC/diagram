zh.wikipedia 王祯农书 (Wang Zhen's Book of Agriculture, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/王禎農書)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Yuan-dynasty farming treatise by Wang Zhen, with its three parts and its count of volumes and illustrations.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the book holds: a first part on farming and sericulture, a second on grains, vegetables and bamboo, and a third of illustrated farm implements, in 22 volumes and 281 pictures, and that historians have used it to reconstruct lost machines. It is only a summary of the contents, quotes none of them, and gives no figure about any field, water or building.
