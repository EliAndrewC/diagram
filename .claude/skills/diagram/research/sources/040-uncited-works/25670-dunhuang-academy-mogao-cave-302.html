dunhuang-academy-mogao-cave-302

Dunhuang Academy, "莫高窟第302窟" ("Mogao Cave 302", title translated), in its cultural-topics series on the Dunhuang grottoes (in Chinese; https://www.dha.ac.cn/info/1425/3681.htm)

What it is: A page of the Dunhuang Academy describing Mogao Cave 302, a cave dug around 584 in the early Sui, its central pillar and its wall and ceiling paintings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a mural of the Futian jingbian (the sutra of the field of merit) on the cave roof, showing works of merit such as planting orchards, digging wells on long roads, building bridges and putting up small lodges beside roads, and says the scenes drew daily-life images, including merchants on the Silk Road. It is a Sui painting of a Buddhist text, so it shows an ideal of charity as painted, not a surveyed road or well, and the page gives no sizes or layouts.
