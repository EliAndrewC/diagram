ja.wikipedia "甲府代官所" ("Kofu daikansho", the Kofu intendant's office, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%94%B2%E5%BA%9C%E4%BB%A3%E5%AE%98%E6%89%80)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kofu daikansho, the shogunate intendant's office for Kai province from 1724 to 1868, with its site beside Kofu castle, how it was run, its staff and its list of 29 intendants.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the office on the samurai-quarter ground outside the castle's inner enclosure, with the barracks and jail of its lower officials to its west and the merchant town to its south. It also says the office held about 100,000 koku and, in the year of the Tenpo uprising, an intendant with 15 clerks. It describes one office in one province late in the Edo period, after the area passed to direct shogunate rule, and gives no plan or dimensions of the compound.
