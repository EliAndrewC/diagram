fukumoto-jinya-ato-kamikawa

Japan Heritage (Silver Carriage Road) tourism site, Kamikawa area, "福本藩陣屋敷跡" ("the site of the Fukumoto domain's jin'ya residence", title translated) (in Japanese; https://wadachi73.jp/spot/30)

What it is: A tourism page on the remains of the Fukumoto domain's administrative residence (jin'ya) in Kamikawa, Hyogo, built in 1663, with the extent of its layout and the garden that survives.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the layout of a small domain's jin'ya: a precinct about 600 m north-south and 450 m east-west, a highway through its west with gates and checkpoints at north and south, townhouses along the road, and the samurai quarter separated from them by a bamboo thicket and bamboo palisade running 500 m north-south, crossed by two cuttings with guardhouses. It is one domain's seat, and what survives is only part of its garden, kept since a shrine was moved onto the site in 1913.
