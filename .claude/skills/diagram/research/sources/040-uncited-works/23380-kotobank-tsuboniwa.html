kotobank-tsuboniwa

"坪庭" ("tsuboniwa", a small enclosed courtyard garden, title translated), in the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and two housing dictionaries, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%9D%AA%E5%BA%AD)

What it is: A short Kotobank page defining the tsuboniwa, a small garden in a house's grounds between buildings or enclosed by walls and fences. One housing dictionary adds that it was first the garden between the main house and the rear building of a machiya.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports a small garden between a town house's front and rear buildings, serving to bring light and air into the interior. It gives no size and no date: the earliest quotation is from 1910, and the machiya origin is stated by a modern housing glossary without a source, so it cannot show how early or how common the form was.
