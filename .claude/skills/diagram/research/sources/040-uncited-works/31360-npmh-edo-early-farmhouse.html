npmh-edo-early-farmhouse

"江戸時代前期中農農家" ("A middle farmer's house of the early Edo period", title translated), exhibit sheet 3 of a learning series of the Nagano Prefectural Museum of History (長野県立歴史館), Chikuma, Nagano (in Japanese; https://npmh.net/school/2020/03/19/manabi_3.pdf)

What it is: A one-page exhibit sheet for a farmhouse about 300 years old, moved from Sanzui village (now Iizuna) in Kamiminochi county, Shinano, and shown furnished, with a lettered key to fifteen items: the privy pit, hoe, earth floor, stable, straw-beating stone, hearth, loom, the dirt-floor living room and the cooking pot, and a note on how farm life changed from the early to the late Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a middle farmer's house held in northern Shinano: a stable inside the house, an earth-floored living room with straw mats laid on the ground, boards and tatami only in the guest room, hemp clothing and no cotton, and a pit privy for human waste used as fertilizer for barley and wheat from the first half of the 18th century. The two back rooms were not restored in the museum, and the sheet is for children, so it gives no dimensions and no plan of the farmyard.
