weblio-shigarami

Gakken, "しがらみ【柵】" ("shigarami", a brushwood weir, title translated), in the Gakken Zenyaku Kogo Jiten, on Weblio (in Japanese; https://kobun.weblio.jp/content/%E3%81%97%E3%81%8C%E3%82%89%E3%81%BF)

What it is: A classical-Japanese dictionary entry on shigarami, a barrier set across a stream, made by driving stakes across it and weaving bamboo and brushwood between them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the structure as stakes driven across the flow with bamboo and brushwood woven through, used to hold back the water, and cites a Kokinshu autumn poem for the word. The entry gives only the definition and the poem, so no size, spacing or date for the construction, and it does not say whether farmers used it to raise water or only to check it.
