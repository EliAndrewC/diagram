yamen-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 衙門 (the yamen, the government office, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/衙门)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the yamen, reached by its redirect from 衙门, covering the word's origin in the military standard gate and the six-leaf gate of the office.

Why it applies, and its limits: It traces the word from a general's tooth-flag gate, attested by the Northern and Southern dynasties, to the office of a local official who tried cases, and says the great gate often had six leaves to show dignity. It is a short word-history with a 1803 Shaoxing plan as its only picture, and it gives no layout, size or staffing.
