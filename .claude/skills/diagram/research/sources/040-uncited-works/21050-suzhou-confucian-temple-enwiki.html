en.wikipedia "Suzhou Confucian Temple" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzhou_Confucian_Temple)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Confucian temple and prefectural school of Suzhou, Jiangsu, founded by Fan Zhongyan in 1035, with its history, buildings and the four Song stone inscriptions kept there, among them the Pingjiang map.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a Song-era prefectural school's scale (213 rooms in 1241, with classrooms, dormitories, exam rooms, canteens, a garden and the temple hall) and says the school once covered about six times the 17,800 square meters that remain. Its dates are scattered across Song, Ming and Qing rebuildings, the 213-room count rests on a Song local record the article only names, and the surviving buildings are mostly of 1864.
