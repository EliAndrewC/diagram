ja.wikipedia 諸国御林帳 ("Shokoku ohayashi-cho", the register of the shogunate's forests in the provinces, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%AB%B8%E5%9B%BD%E5%BE%A1%E6%9E%97%E5%B8%B3)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the ohayashi-cho, the ledger the Edo shogunate kept of the forests (ohayashi) it owned in its own lands, and the similar ledgers of the domains.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists what such a ledger recorded for each forest: its position and area, the number of standing trees by species, the stand and ground, the felling, and the distance to the market or shipping port, with a forest map attached and the contents sometimes copied into village registers. It is a few lines from a single reference-book source, gives no figures for any forest, and says nothing about how a domain or village managed its own woodland beyond that such ledgers existed.
