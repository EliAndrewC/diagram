kotobank-tsukurizakaya

"造酒屋" ("tsukurizakaya", the brewing sake house), in the Concise Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (精選版 日本国語大辞典), with a Heibonsha World Encyclopedia mention, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%80%A0%E9%85%92%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: A Kotobank dictionary entry defining the tsukurizakaya, a shop that brews sake and sells it wholesale, with an Edo-period first citation (1814-46), plus an excerpt from the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia's sake-shop article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It separates the brewer from the retail sake shop and says that in a broad sense a sake shop may include brewers who also retail, wholesalers and pure retailers, which supports drawing brewing and selling as distinct trades. The encyclopedia excerpt dates its Nada and Edo-shipment remarks to the late Edo period and the entry gives no size, layout or place for a brewery.
