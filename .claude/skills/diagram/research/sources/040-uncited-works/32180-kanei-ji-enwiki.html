What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Kan'ei-ji, the Tendai temple founded in 1625 north-east of Edo Castle as a Tokugawa funeral temple, covering its founding, its wealth, its abbots and its destruction in the Battle of Ueno in 1868.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the scale of a great shogunal temple precinct in Edo: the whole heights north and east of Shinobazu Pond, over 30 buildings, on land taken from three daimyo suburban residences, with a pond island built in imitation of Chikubushima. It is the site of the shogun's own house temple, so it is an upper bound and no ordinary temple, and it gives no site area or plan.
