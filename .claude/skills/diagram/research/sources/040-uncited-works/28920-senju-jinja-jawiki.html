ja.wikipedia 千住神社 ("Senju Jinja", the Senju Shrine in Adachi, Tokyo, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/千住神社)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on Senju Shrine in Adachi Ward, Tokyo, formerly Nishinomori Shrine and Senzaki Inari Shrine. It gives the shrine's founding tradition, its mergers, its precinct shrines and its access.

Why it applies, and its limits: It tells how a settlement shrine grew by layers: an Inari shrine said to be founded in 926 to guard the land and pray for the five grains, a Hikawa shrine added in 1279, both made village shrines in 1872 and merged in 1873, with many small shrines in the precinct. The founding dates are tradition, the 'woods' of the shrine's old names (Futatsumori, Nishi no mori) are named without a size or layout, and the article is a stub from a few cited local publications, so it supports the form of a shrine cluster in a post-town, not its ground.
