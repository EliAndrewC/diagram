bunka-ito-ushigoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "伊藤家住宅牛小屋" (the cowshed of the Ito residence, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/379887)

What it is: A registered-cultural-property entry for the cowshed of the Ito residence in Kashiwa, Chiba, built 1868-1882: single-storied, thatched, hipped, 28 square meters, standing northeast of the main house, with earthen walls, an open vent high on the wall and a stall on each side inside.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a thatched farm cowshed with paired stalls and a ventilation gap, set on the northeast of a farmhouse as part of a traditional farm lot, which supports how a cowshed was built and placed. It is dated to the first years of Meiji, so it records the form at the very end of the premodern period, and it is one farm in Kanto with no word on how many cattle it held.
