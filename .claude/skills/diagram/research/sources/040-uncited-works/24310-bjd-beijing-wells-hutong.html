Jing Genr (京根儿), "对于北京而言，井与胡同的渊源颇深！井也在北京有了特殊的意义" ("For Beijing, wells and hutong are deeply bound; wells took on a special meaning in Beijing", title translated), Beijing Evening News, on Beijing Daily's website (in Chinese; https://news.bjd.com.cn/2022/09/20/10163783.shtml)

What it is: A newspaper feature on Beijing's wells, the hutong named for them, and the water-carrying trade, quoting Yuan to Qing sources and legends.

Why it applies, and its limits: It reports that the Qing gazetteer Jingshi fangxiang zhi gao counted over a thousand wells inside and outside the city's walls, that Beijing wells were sweet or bitter, that official wells were run by banner-camp cooks who also carried water under licence, and describes a village well with a windlass and a stone trough that fed water carts. It covers only Beijing and gives no count of its own; the figures are the gazetteer's as the writer quotes it, and much of the piece is anecdote and legend.
