banta-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "番太" ("banta", the town watchman, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%95%AA%E5%A4%AA)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the banta, the Edo-period men who kept the night watch, policed vagrants and did jail and execution chores in towns, living in a watch hut beside one of the kido, the wooden gates that closed each ward's street at night, mostly of hinin status.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms that the watch was kept from a small hut beside the kido (the gates of the wards inside a city, not gates in a town wall) and that its men were mostly of the hinin outcaste class, which bears on who lived at a ward gate. Beyond that the page is mainly about 1874, when some bantaro were taken into the new police and samurai officers resigned over it, and its only figure, about 500 of some 6,000 taken into the police, is of that year; it gives no count of watch huts and no dates for the earlier practice.
