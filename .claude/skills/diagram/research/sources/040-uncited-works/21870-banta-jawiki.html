banta-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "番太" ("banta", the town watchman, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%95%AA%E5%A4%AA)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the banta, the Edo-period men who kept the night watch, policed vagrants and did jail and execution chores in towns, living in a watch hut at a town gate, mostly of hinin status.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms that the watch was kept from a small hut beside the town's gates and that its men were mostly of the hinin outcaste class, which bears on who lived at a town gate. Beyond that the page is mainly about 1874, when some bantaro were taken into the new police and samurai officers resigned over it, and it gives no numbers for Edo or any other town and no dates for the earlier practice.
