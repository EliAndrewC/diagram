ja.wikipedia 藩 ("han", the domain of a feudal lord, title translated), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/藩)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the han, the territory and government of a feudal lord, covering the Chinese origin of the word, the Edo-period daimyo domain, its Meiji adoption as an official name and abolition in 1871, how domains were named, and main and branch domains.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the word han was a Confucian scholars' label for a daimyo's holding rather than an official Edo name (the shogunate said ryobun and kachu), that a domain gathered its samurai in the castle town and drew rice tax from villages registered by yield, and that some small domains held scattered lands. It speaks of the institution, not of any place's layout or size, and its Chinese section does not describe a Chinese domain's form.
