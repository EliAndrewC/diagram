en.wikipedia "Levee" (the raised ridge beside a river, article reached as "Natural levee", title translated) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_levee)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article "Levee", which the address Natural levee redirects to, covering artificial and natural levees: how natural levees form on floodplains, how artificial ones are built, their word history, notable systems worldwide, and how they fail.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its section on natural levees explains that coarse sediment settles beside the channel during floods and builds ridges that hold the river, and that a channel bed can rise above the plain, as the page says of the Yellow River near the sea. That is the physical process behind a raised riverside ridge, with no date or place tied to it. Most of the page's examples are Mississippi, Dutch, Egyptian and North American systems, with China given only a few lines, so it supports how a levee forms and fails, not how one was built or used in East Asia.
