kotobank-yoseishokubutsu

"陽生植物" ("yoseishokubutsu", sun plants, title translated), in the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, the Nipponica and the Britannica concise encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/陽生植物-654226)

What it is: A Kotobank page defining sun plants, those that grow in full light and fail in shade, with the Nipponica and Britannica entries giving traits and example species.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says most annuals, field crops and weeds of cultivated land are sun plants, naming red pine (akamatsu), silver birch, susuki and dandelion, and gives the compensation point of over 1000 lux against 100 to 500 for shade plants. These are general physiology facts for Japanese flora, with no site and no dating.
