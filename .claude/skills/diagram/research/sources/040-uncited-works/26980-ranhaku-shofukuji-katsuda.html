ranhaku-shofukuji-katsuda

"新義真言宗 勝福寺 七郷村大字勝田" ("Shofukuji, Shingi Shingon school, Katsuda, Shichigo village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register, in Ranzan Town Web Natural and Local History, volume 6 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter06.html)

What it is: A transcription of one temple's entry in the Hiki County register of temples: the Shingi Shingon temple Shofukuji in Katsuda, founded in 1658. It gives its halls, precinct, the fields, woods and house plot it owns outside the precinct, and its parishioners.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a main hall 5 ken 3 shaku by 4 ken 3 shaku, a priest's residence 6 ken 3 shaku by 2 ken 3 shaku, a precinct of 442 tsubo and 34 parishioners, and a note that the Edo gazetteer Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko spells the temple name differently and gives the founder's death in 1658 rather than a building date. It is a Meiji-era register of a small Shingon temple in one Musashi county, so the 1658 date is stated, not shown.
