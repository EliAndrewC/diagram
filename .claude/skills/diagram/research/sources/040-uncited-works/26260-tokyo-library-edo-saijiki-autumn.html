Tokyo Metropolitan Library, "秋（7月～9月）" ("Autumn (July to September)", title translated), in "江戸の歳時記" ("Edo's seasonal calendar", title translated), Edo-Tokyo Digital Museum (in Japanese; https://www.library.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/portals/0/edo/tokyo_library/saijiki/autumn.html)

What it is: A page of the Tokyo Metropolitan Library's Edo-Tokyo Digital Museum site that lists the annual events of the seventh to ninth months in Edo, with captions for woodblock prints. It covers the Bon festival, the Hassaku holiday of the shogunate, the Chrysanthemum festival of the ninth day of the ninth month and the Kanda Myojin festival.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names dated observances of an Edo townsman's year: grass markets (kusaichi) set up from the 12th of the seventh month in places such as Fukagawa to sell Bon goods, the Yabuiri holiday on the 16th when merchant-house workers could go home or to theaters and temples, and Hassaku on the first of the eighth month, kept as the shogunate's most important holiday since Ieyasu entered Edo in 1590. It describes Edo, a city of the Tokugawa shogunate, in a few sentences per event, so it gives no counts, sizes or places within the city beyond Fukagawa and Asakusa, and says nothing of villages.
