kotobank-warazuka-daijisen

"藁塚" ("warazuka", the straw stack, title translated), entries from the Digital Daijisen and Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%97%81%E5%A1%9A-665655)

What it is: A short page defining the warazuka, threshed rice straw piled in a cylinder on a cut paddy or on the bund, with an autumn seasonal-word note and a 1905 quotation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports that straw was stacked in the field or on the paddy bund after threshing, the placement a map would show. It gives no size, spacing or purpose for the stacks, and its earliest quotation is a 1905 novel, so the page does not itself date the practice before the Meiji period.
