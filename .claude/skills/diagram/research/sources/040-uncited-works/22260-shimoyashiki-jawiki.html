shimoyashiki-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "下屋敷" (the lower residence, redirected to "江戸藩邸" (Edo domain residences), title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/下屋敷)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Edo residences of the daimyo (the page a search for shimoyashiki leads to), covering the upper, middle, lower and warehouse residences, how land was granted and held, and what the sites became after 1868.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a shimoyashiki was mainly a garden villa, lay mostly in the outskirts, was usually larger than the upper or middle residence, served as a refuge in Edo's great fires and was sometimes used for storage or vegetable plots; it gives the 1738 size standard (2,500 tsubo for 10,000 to 20,000 koku, 7,000 for 100,000 to 150,000) and 3,195 residents in the Tosa domain's Edo residences in 1684. It concerns daimyo in Edo only, calls its own word "han-tei" a later coinage, and carries a notice that its footnotes are insufficient.
