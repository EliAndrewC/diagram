en.wikipedia "Chaozhou" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaozhou)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Chaozhou, a prefecture-level city in eastern Guangdong, giving its history from the Qin and Sui, its geography and rivers, 2020 census population, dialect, culture, tourist sites and overseas communities.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the Han River running through the city, hills covering 65% of the land, and rice, sweet potato and other crops on the river banks, and lists a 2.6-kilometer Ming city wall, the Southern Song Guangji Bridge and Kaiyuan Temple, which place one southern Chinese walled prefectural seat in its setting. Its population, area and density figures are from the 2020 census and describe the modern prefecture-level city, not the Ming or Song town, and it gives no figures for the walled town, its streets or its fields.
