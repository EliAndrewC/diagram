kunimine-okazaki-2022-ibaraki-machiya

Kunimine Taiki and Okazaki Atsuyuki, "茨城県における町屋群の残存概況及び棟向きの分布 インターネット地図機能を用いた基礎的広域調査" ("Ibaraki-ken ni okeru machiya-gun no zonzon gaikyo oyobi muneki no bunpu", The surviving townhouse groups of Ibaraki and the distribution of ridge directions: a wide-area survey using online maps, title translated), Reports of the City Planning Institute of Japan 20(4), 2022 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/reportscpij/20/4/20_470/_article/-char/ja/)

What it is: The J-STAGE record of a two-page report counting surviving machiya (townhouses) in the main districts of Ibaraki, from old maps and provincial maps checked against online map imagery.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counted 517 machiya in 36 settlements and found the roof-ridge direction similar across the prefecture, with many having a shop storehouse (mise-gura) or shutter boxes at both ends of the upper window. It is a desktop count of buildings standing in the 2020s, and the abstract gives no ridge percentages or dates.
