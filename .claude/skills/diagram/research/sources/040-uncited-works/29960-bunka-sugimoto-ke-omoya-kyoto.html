bunka-sugimoto-ke-omoya-kyoto

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "杉本家住宅 主屋" ("Sugimoto house, main house," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/179065)

What it is: A national database entry for the main house of a large Kyoto machiya in Shimogyo ward, dated 1870, with a shop part of 116.79 square meters and a living part of 187.03 square meters, both two stories, designated an Important Cultural Property in 2010.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes an omote-zukuri house, a shop at the front and living rooms behind joined by an entrance, with three storehouses behind the main house, a high wall around the lot and tea rooms added from Meiji to early Showa. Its date of 1870 is just after the Restoration, and it describes one large merchant house of central Kyoto, not a typical townhouse.
