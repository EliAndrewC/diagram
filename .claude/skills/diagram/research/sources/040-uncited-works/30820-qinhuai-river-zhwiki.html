zh.wikipedia 秦淮河 ("Qinhuai he", the Qinhuai River, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/秦淮河)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Qinhuai River of Nanjing, a Yangtze tributary 110 km long, with its two sources, its inner and outer branches around the city, the stretch through the old city known as the ten-li Qinhuai, and its pollution cleanup.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the river's course and its split at Nanjing into an inner branch through the city and an outer branch that it says was dug as the moat of the Five Dynasties city, and it quotes two late-Ming and early-Qing writers on the crowded riverside houses with balconies and the lantern boats. The quotations describe a famed city pleasure district and not a working canal, the page carries a banner asking for more sources, and its flow figure is a modern gauge reading.
