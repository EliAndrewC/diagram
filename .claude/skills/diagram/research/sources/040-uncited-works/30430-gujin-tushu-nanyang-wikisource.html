"欽定古今圖書集成/方輿彙編/職方典/第0451卷" ("Imperially Authorized Complete Collection of Illustrations and Writings, Past and Present: Geography Section, Gazetteer Statutes, volume 451", title translated), on Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/欽定古今圖書集成/方輿彙編/職方典/第0451卷)

What it is: The transcribed text of one volume of the Qing imperial encyclopedia's gazetteer section, on Chinese Wikisource, covering Nanyang Prefecture (Henan): its list of enfeoffed lords from the earliest dynasties to the Ming, and its register of government offices, with each county and department (zhou) seat's halls, gates, granaries, shrines and other buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists, county by county, what an official seat of the Qing prefecture held and in what order the gates and halls stood (great hall, front gate, jail, granaries, pavilions), with dates of repair under the Hongwu to Kangxi reigns, so it carries a layout of a Chinese county or prefecture office compound. It describes one prefecture in Henan, speaks of the Qing office plans as rebuilt after the Ming collapse, gives no dimensions, and the page's punctuation is a later machine-and-volunteer addition to an unpunctuated original.
