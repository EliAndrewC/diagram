ja.wikipedia 大鎌 ("ogama", the scythe, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/大鎌)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the long-handled scythe (ogama, scythe) as a mowing tool, with its parts, the mowing stroke, its place in myth and its use as a weapon, with Wikipedia's warnings of missing sources and possible original research.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tool's dimensions (a snath of about 170 cm and a blade of 60-90 cm) and how the blade is peened and sharpened, so it fits a question about how a grass-mowing tool is built and worked. It is about the European-pattern scythe in its examples and its military uses, and it never says whether or when the tool was used in Japan or China, so it cannot show it for the setting.
