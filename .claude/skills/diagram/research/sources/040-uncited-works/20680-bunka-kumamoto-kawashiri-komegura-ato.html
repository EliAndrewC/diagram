bunka-kumamoto-kawashiri-komegura-ato

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "熊本藩川尻米蔵跡" (the site of the Kawashiri rice storehouses of Kumamoto domain, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/147327)

What it is: A national historic-site entry for the ruins of Kumamoto domain's Kawashiri rice storehouses (Kumamoto), where the domain's tax rice was gathered in the Edo period, with a note on the designation of a ferry landing used by the boatmen who lived across the river.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that a domain collected its tax rice at a riverside depot, that the depot doubled as a military port, and that the boatmen served from the opposite bank by ferry, which supports a domain's rice store standing at a landing. The entry is a short designation note giving no dimensions, layout or dates beyond the Edo period, and it describes one large domain in Kyushu.
