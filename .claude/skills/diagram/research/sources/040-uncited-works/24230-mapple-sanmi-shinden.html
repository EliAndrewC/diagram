MAPPLE, Inc., "地図の雑学 三富新田(埼玉県)~筆から江戸時代のエコロジーな生活が見えてくる~" ("Map trivia: Sanmi Shinden, Saitama: Edo-period ecological life visible in the parcels", title translated), column no. 10 of the MAPPLE Land Registry Map Viewer series, 2024-02-09 (in Japanese; https://mapple.com/column/20240209/)

What it is: A short company column on the Sanmi Shinden, the new-field villages of Tokorozawa and Miyoshi in Saitama set up under Yanagisawa Yoshiyasu, showing how each long plot combines house ground, field and woodland.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says each plot held house ground, fields and a coppice in one strip, with bamboo, oak and zelkova planted at the house, windbreak shrubs and tea at the field edge, and oak and sawtooth oak woodland whose fallen leaves were compost and branches fuel. It says the current land-registry parcels still show this layout, but gives no dimensions, dates or sources, and its description is a summary of the system. It describes one planned new-field settlement on poor, water-short upland with no paddy, so its strip of house, field and woodland belongs to dry-field upland reclamation and not to an ordinary paddy village.
