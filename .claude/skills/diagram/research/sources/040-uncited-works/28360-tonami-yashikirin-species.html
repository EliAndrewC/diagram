tonami-yashikirin-species

Tonami City Board of Education, "8 これからの屋敷林" ("8 the house grove of the future", title translated), from 『砺波平野の屋敷林』 ("House Groves of the Tonami Plain", Tonami Dispersed-Settlement Research Institute, 1996, excerpted), on the Tonami Shoso site (in Japanese; https://1073shoso.jp/www/sankyo/detail.jsp?id=18763)

What it is: A city web page excerpting a 1996 research-institute book, setting out which trees suit a new house grove on the Tonami plain, grouped by height, shade tolerance, wind-breaking root habit and fruit or flower, followed by reflections on life in a grove.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the trees grouped for windbreak use (cedar, red and black pine, ginkgo, horse chestnut and zelkova as deep-rooted wind trees) and those that reach more than 20 m, which shows what a present-day planner of this country's groves counts as the tall and windproof layer. It is advice for planting now, with modern ornamentals such as hydrangea and hibiscus among its choices, and it gives no dates, so it does not say which trees an old farmstead actually held.
