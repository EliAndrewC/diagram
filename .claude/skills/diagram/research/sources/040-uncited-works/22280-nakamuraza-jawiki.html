nakamuraza-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "中村座" (the Nakamura-za, an Edo kabuki theater, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/中村座)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Nakamura-za, one of Edo's three licensed kabuki theaters, covering its founding in 1624, its moves, its fire of 1841, its curtain and the thief Nezumi Kozo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the theater's sites in order (Nakabashi in 1624, Negicho in 1632, Sakaicho in 1651, then Saruwakacho in Asakusa after the fire of 1841, where the Tenpo reforms gathered five theaters) and says the shogunate allowed it a white stripe in its curtain. It gives places and dates only, with no size, plan or number of seats, and part of the page concerns a thief said to be a theater hand's son.
