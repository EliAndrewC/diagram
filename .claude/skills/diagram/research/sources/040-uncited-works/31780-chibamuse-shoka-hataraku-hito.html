chibamuse-shoka-hataraku-hito

Chiba-muse digital museum (chiba-muse.or.jp), "商家で働く人" ("People who worked in a merchant house", title translated), townscape section (machinami), in the digital museum under MURA (in Japanese; https://www.chiba-muse.or.jp/MURA/digitalmuseum/machinami/all_9.html)

What it is: A short museum page on the staff of a merchant house, which was divided into the shop and the private quarters behind: male clerks and apprentices in the shop, female servants in the kitchen. It explains the ranks of apprentice (detchi), clerk (tedai) and head clerk (banto) and how one rose.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ladder of an apprentice taken in after elementary school, unpaid apart from two yearly gifts, who after a term became a clerk and then a head clerk, and it says that few were allowed to set up their own shop (noren-wake). Its apprentices enter after primary school and end their term at the conscription examination, so the picture is of the modern merchant house, and it gives no counts of staff or figures for the shop.
