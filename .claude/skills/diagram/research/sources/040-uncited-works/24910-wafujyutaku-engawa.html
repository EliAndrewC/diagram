wafujyutaku-engawa

Shinken Press (上質な日本のすまい), "縁側の起源と機能" ("The origin and function of the engawa", title translated), in its basic knowledge of Japanese-style houses, on wafujyutaku.jp (in Japanese; https://wafujyutaku.jp/outside-and-inside/327)

What it is: A short explainer from a Japanese-style housing guide on the engawa, the veranda strip along a house: its supposed origin, its functions, its two kinds (the open nureen outside the shutters and the enclosed irigawa-en inside them), how its boards are laid and what woods and bamboo are used.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the engawa's origin as the hisashi (eave) rooms of the Heian shinden-style mansion, separates the open wet veranda under the eaves from the covered one inside the shutters, and says glass doors on it came only in the Meiji era. It is written for people building a new Japanese-style house, mentions the sukiya and shoin styles of the elite, and gives no region, no dimensions and nothing on how common the strip was in a farmhouse.
