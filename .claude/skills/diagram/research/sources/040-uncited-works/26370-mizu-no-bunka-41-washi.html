mizu-no-bunka-41-washi

Sugihara Yoshinao (Sugihara Shoten, washi paper wholesaler), interviewed in "和紙の今昔物語" ("The past and present of washi", title translated), no. 41, "Expressions of washi", of the magazine Mizu no Bunka, Mizkan Water Culture Center (in Japanese; https://www.mizu.gr.jp/kikanshi/no41/01.html)

What it is: A 2012 magazine interview with a tenth-generation washi wholesaler of Echizen, Fukui, on what washi is, the early history of Japanese papermaking, Echizen's role and the trade's decline.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says paper-making reached Japan in the early 7th century, that by 737 and 774 records name 5 and then 19 provinces as paper producers, that kozo fibers run about 10 mm and gampi and mitsumata about 5 mm, that gampi cannot be farmed (about 7 years to grow) and is gathered wild, and that Echizen made official paper for samurai documents in the Edo period. It is one dealer's oral account: the festival-count date of 1,290 years is his own arithmetic, the Edo-period part is a few lines, and it describes the trade of 2012 more than any village's papermaking.
