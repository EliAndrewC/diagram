ja.wikipedia 坊守 ("bomori", the temple keeper, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9D%8A%E5%AE%88)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the bomori, originally a temple's caretaker and in Jodo Shinshu the title of the head priest's wife, with the title used for the wife of the head of the school.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Jodo Shinshu use of the word is attested in a 1343 register, which places a priest's wife as a recognized temple role well before the Edo period. It describes only the Jodo Shinshu school's custom, a married priesthood that other schools did not share, and says nothing of the bomori's duties, her quarters or the temple's layout. It is a stub, and its note on 2000s rulings about female priests concerns the present day.
