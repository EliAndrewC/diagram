en.wikipedia "Qinhuai River" (秦淮河, the river through Nanjing, title translated) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qinhuai_River)

What it is: A short English Wikipedia article on the Qinhuai River, a 110 km Yangtze tributary flowing through Nanjing, Jiangsu, covering its legendary naming under the First Emperor, its history as a district of noble families, scholars and courtesans, and its present-day scenic belt around the Confucius Temple.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a prosperous riverside quarter of a walled city, with the Confucius Temple at its center, and says the banks were a gathering place for noble families and scholars and, in the Ming, a famed pleasure district. The history it gives is mostly of the Ming and Qing and of the modern restoration of 1985, with the Qin origin given only as a legend, and it has no figures for the river's width, banks or buildings.
