ja.wikipedia 秣 ("magusa", fodder, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%92%AD)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia stub on magusa, the grass fed to horses and cattle, with its writing and the old fodder levies.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that before chemical fertilizer the dung of horses and cattle was a valuable manure, that the fodder itself could be trampled into the field as green manure (karishiki), and that villages therefore kept fodder fields and meadows (magusada, magusano); it also cites the Engishiki's rule that provinces send rice and soybeans as fodder levy. It is a stub with two reference-book sources and gives no acreage, ratio of meadow to field, or region.
