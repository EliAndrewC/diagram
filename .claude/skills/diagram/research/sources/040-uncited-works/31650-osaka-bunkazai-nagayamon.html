osaka-bunkazai-nagayamon

Osaka Cultural Property Navi (大阪文化財ナビ), "長屋門" ("nagayamon", the row-house gate, title translated), in its glossary of old Japanese architecture (in Japanese; https://osaka-bunkazainavi.org/glossary/長屋門)

What it is: A glossary entry defining the nagayamon, a gate form of early modern samurai and village-headman houses, on the cultural property guide of Osaka's architects' associations.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the nagayamon as a gate form of the early modern period for samurai and village-headman (shoya) houses: a long building passable near its middle, the gate part framed by a lintel and two main posts with two doors and a wicket, and the rooms beside the doors used as quarters for retainers and servants and as storage. It is a definition only, with no dimensions, region or date, so it says what the form is and who owned one but not how large or how common it was.
