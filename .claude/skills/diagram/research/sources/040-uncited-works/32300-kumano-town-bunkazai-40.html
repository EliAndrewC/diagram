kumano-town-bunkazai-40

Kumano Town, Hiroshima (熊野町総務部産業観光課), "文化財40選" ("40 selected cultural properties", title translated, as it reads on the page), visitor guide with local legends, dated 2024.12 (in Japanese; https://www.town.kumano.lg.jp/material/files/group/6/anke-to.pdf)

What it is: A town visitor guide to 40 shrines, temples, stones and other cultural properties of Kumano in Aki County, Hiroshima Prefecture, each with a short account of its enshrined deity or image and its founding, plus a front page of local folklore (otters, foxes, the nue, a well that did not dry up).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives founding traditions for village shrines and halls from 1181 to the 1800s (for example an Inari shrine and a shrine to the ox god where about 80 oxen once gathered at the autumn equinox), and says an old well at the Ochiya house kept flowing through the drought of 1924, so it shows what a Hiroshima hill-country town's shrines were dedicated to and how old they are said to be. Almost every date is introduced as a tradition ("it is said"), the guide gives sites and deities but no ground plans or sizes except a few single items, and the folklore is told as legend.
