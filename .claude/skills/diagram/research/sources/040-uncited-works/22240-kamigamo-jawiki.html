Japanese Wikipedia, "上賀茂" (Kamigamo, a district of Kyoto, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/上賀茂)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Kamigamo, the northernmost district of Kyoto and the former Kamigamo village of Atago District, covering its history, sights, local products, festivals and community groups.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the six villages of the Kamo estate were long the lands of Kamo Wakeikazuchi (Kamigamo) Shrine, that the Kamigamo farmhouses grew the sukina turnip, which was a gift of the shrine families and was barred from being taken to other villages by an order of 1804, and that the village was merged into Kyoto in 1931. Its population figures (3,677 in 1920, 12,289 in 2020) are modern, and its festivals and community groups are described as they run today, with no earlier date given.
