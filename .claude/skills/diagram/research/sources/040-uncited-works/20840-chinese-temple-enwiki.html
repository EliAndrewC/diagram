"Chinese temple", English Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_temple)

What it is: An English Wikipedia article on Chinese temples, listing the Chinese terms for their kinds (temples to gods, ancestral halls, Taoist and Buddhist monasteries, Confucian temples, City God temples). It describes how local village and lineage groups run folk temples without professional priests, and has a history section on the campaigns from 1898 onward to convert or abolish temples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the Chinese words for the kinds of temple and says folk temples are administered by local managers, village communities and lineage congregations and are usually small. Its only dated history begins in 1898, with the confiscation of temples for schools and the Republican and Communist campaigns after it, so it says nothing of how a temple stood in a premodern settlement. Its overview section, which holds the claims on folk temples used here, carries a banner asking for more citations and cites none of those claims; the history section is marked only as needing expansion.
