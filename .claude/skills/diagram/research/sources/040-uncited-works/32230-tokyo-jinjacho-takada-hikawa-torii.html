tokyo-jinjacho-takada-hikawa-torii

Tokyo Jinjacho (Association of Shinto Shrines of Tokyo), "石造鳥居" ("the stone torii") of Takada Sochinju Hikawa Shrine, in its Goshaho meguri (shrine treasures) series, Toshima ward (in Japanese; https://tokyo-jinjacho.or.jp/goshahou/sekizoutorii)

What it is: A page of the Tokyo shrine association on the stone torii of Takada Sochinju Hikawa Shrine in Toshima ward, a ward-designated cultural property, with a short history of the shrine.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a stone torii at a village shrine to 1790, dedicated by the lord of Toba domain, whose lower residence stood in Edo, and says the shrine fronts the old Kamakura road with the torii on its east side. It is one shrine near Edo given by a daimyo, and it says nothing of the torii's size or of how common stone torii were at village shrines.
