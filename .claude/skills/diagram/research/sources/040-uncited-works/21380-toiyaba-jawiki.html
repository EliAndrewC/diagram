toiyaba-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 問屋場 ("toiyaba", the post-station forwarding office, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%95%8F%E5%B1%8B%E5%A0%B4)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the toiyaba, the office in an Edo-period highway post town that arranged relays of porters and horses and the levy of auxiliary labor.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the posts staffed there (the toiya who ran it, his assistant, a clerk who entered traffic and fares, and a horse assigner), says all of them turned out for a daimyo's procession, and gives the 1868 and 1872 changes that ended the office. It gives no size, floor plan or position within the town, and it is a stub the page itself calls unfinished, so it supports who worked there and what they did, not how the building was drawn.
