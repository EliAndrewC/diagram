otsuki-saruhashi

Otsuki City, "日本三奇橋 猿橋" ("Saruhashi, one of Japan's three marvelous bridges", title translated), Otsuki City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.otsuki.yamanashi.jp/15/05/)

What it is: A short city page on Saruhashi, a timber cantilever bridge over a gorge in Otsuki, Yamanashi, on the Koshu road, with its size, its structure, a founding legend, a 1478 traveler's account and its military and road history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the bridge as 31 m long and 3.3 m wide over a gorge 31 m deep, held by four tiers of cantilevered beams since piers cannot be set in the gorge; it quotes the 1478 account of the monk Doko and says the Takeda burned it down in wartime to stop an enemy advance and that it stood on the Koshu road, one of the five main Edo roads. It does not say how the present timbers relate to the earlier bridge, and it is one famous gorge bridge, not a typical crossing.
