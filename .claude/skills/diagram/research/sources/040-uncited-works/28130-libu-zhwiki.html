libu-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 吏部 (libu, the Ministry of Personnel, title translated), traditional-character edition (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hant/%E5%90%8F%E9%83%A8)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Ministry of Personnel, first of the Six Ministries, covering its Han to Tang origins, its Ming and Qing staffing and ranks, and its counterparts in Korea and Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the Ming ministry's posts (a minister of rank 2a, two vice-ministers of 3a, four bureaus each with a director of 5a) and the Qing changes in rank, and notes the Joseon yijo and Japan's Ministry of Ceremonial (shikibu-sho). It is about officials and ranks only, gives no buildings or number of clerks, and its Tang and Song passages say the ministry's power was often taken by other offices, so it does not show what a post actually did.
