kashihara-jingu-sando-torii

Kashihara Jingu (橿原神宮), "参道・鳥居" ("Approaches and torii", title translated), from the shrine's sights page (in Japanese; https://kashiharajingu.or.jp/point/8277.html)

What it is: The shrine's own page on the approach paths and four torii of Kashihara Jingu in Nara: an approach lined with oaks, two more approaches through woods, and the gates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the main approach runs straight about 300 m from the first torii, lined both sides with oaks, past a bridge and the largest of four torii before the south gate, and that the four torii are unpainted plain-wood myojin type built in 1940 of Taiwanese cypress and repaired in 2020. It describes a large shrine established in 1890 for the imperial founder-myth, with its torii of 1940, so it gives the dimensions of an imperial shrine's approach and not of a village shrine's.
