zh.wikipedia 理坑村 (Likeng village, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/理坑村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Likeng, a village of the Yu family in Wuyuan County, Jiangxi, with its heritage listings and a table of its Ming and Qing buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the village to the late Yuan or early Ming and says over fifty Ming and Qing buildings survive, naming halls, ancestral halls, a gatehouse, wells and about nine bridges, so it shows what a Huizhou lineage village kept. It lists buildings by name and date only: no plan, spacing or measurement.
