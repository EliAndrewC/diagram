adachi-2012-matsue-buke-yashiki

Adachi Masatomo (足立正智), "「武家屋敷」創建時の姿を探る" ("Searching for the original form of the 'samurai residence'", title translated), a paper from the Shimane University repository (in Japanese; https://coc.lib.shimane-u.ac.jp/files/attach/1/1504/SMC002-07.pdf)

What it is: A short survey paper on the preserved samurai residence on Shiomi Nawate in Matsue, Shimane, the house of a mid-rank retainer of the Matsue domain, reporting a 2012 partial survey of its main house and gate-range, its lot size and its alterations, with plans and sections.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the lot of a mid-rank domain retainer's house beside the castle moat: six lots in the row, about 500 tsubo each by an even division, about 23 ken of frontage and 18 ken of depth, raised above the road on a stone wall, with the row's houses held by retainers of 120 to 550 koku (this house's own Ujiie family at 550, its neighbors at 120 to 450). The paper says the house's date is uncertain (a pamphlet claims rebuilding after the 1733 fire; the author estimates under 200 years old), that the main house and gate-range were repeatedly altered and restored in 1969, and that the lot figure is the author's own simple division, not a surveyed boundary. Citing Wada's 1992 study, the paper puts this lot a little above the average samurai lot of the Uchinakabara-machi area, so it is one row in one castle town, not a typical retainer's lot.
