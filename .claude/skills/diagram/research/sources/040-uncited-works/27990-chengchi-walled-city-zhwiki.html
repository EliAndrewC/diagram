zh.wikipedia 城池 ("chengchi, the walled city", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%9F%8E%E6%B1%A0)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the walled city of ancient China and neighboring East Asia, covering its history, its ranks by administrative level, its walls, gates and required temples, and a table of walled areas in the 1890s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a standard walled city of the imperial period needed a City God temple, Confucian temple, martial temple, fire god temple and wealth god temple, that a prefecture city usually had eight gates and a county city four, and it tabulates the walled area of twenty Chinese cities in the 1890s, from Beijing at 6,320 hectares down to Guiyang at 225. The gate counts are given as usual practice, not a rule, the article is flagged as needing more sources, and the table lists provincial and prefectural capitals only, so it gives no figure for a county seat.
