Ishioka City, "石岡の陣屋門" ("The Ishioka jinya gate", title translated), prefecture-designated cultural property page, official city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.ishioka.lg.jp/page/page000870.html)

What it is: A municipal page on the jinya gate of the Fuchu domain in Ishioka, Ibaraki, a prefecture-designated cultural property, with its date, materials and moves.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Fuchu domain, a branch of the Mito house, had a jinya, an office for governing locally while its lord stayed in Edo, and that the gate of 1828 was built from timber left over from rebuilding the lord's Edo mansion, mainly zelkova (keyaki) with some camphor. It gives no dimensions or plan of the jinya, the gate was moved to a school in 1969 and to its present site in 2014, and the page describes only the gate, not the compound.
