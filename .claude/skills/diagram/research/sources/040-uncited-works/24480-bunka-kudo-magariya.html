bunka-kudo-magariya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧工藤家住宅(旧所在 岩手県紫波郡紫波町)" ("former Kudo house (formerly in Shiwa, Iwate)", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/199529)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of the Important Cultural Property Kudo house, a thatched Nanbu magariya farmhouse of about 1751-1763 moved to the Nihon Minkaen open-air museum in Kawasaki: 19.2 by 11.1 meters, with a 7.6 by 6.3 meter wing for the earthen-floor kitchen and the horse stall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of an Iwate farmhouse whose stable projects forward in an L, and says that magariya appeared in Iwate from the mid-eighteenth century and this one was built so from the start, among the oldest in the prefecture. It is one surviving house of a northeastern form, and it has been moved and reassembled.
