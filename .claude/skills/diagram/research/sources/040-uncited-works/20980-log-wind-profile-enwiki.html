en.wikipedia "Log wind profile" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log_wind_profile)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the logarithmic relationship between mean wind speed and height in the lowest part of the atmosphere, with its equation, its terms and its limits.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the equation, a displacement height of 2/3 to 3/4 of the obstacle height, and ranges for the roughness length, 0.1-0.25 m for cropland and 0.5-1.0 m for brush and forest, which can estimate how a broad cover of trees or crops slows the wind near the ground. It describes wind over a uniform surface, not the sheltered strip behind a single windbreak or tree belt, which is reckoned in multiples of the belt's height and needs a windbreak study; its roughness ranges come from a modern handbook on wind loads on buildings. The profile holds only in about the lowest 100 m and is reliable for neutral conditions, and the article itself says the roughness values are subjective and ranges, not definitive numbers.
