ja.wikipedia 水天宮 (久留米市) ("Suitengu, Kurume", the head shrine of the Suitengu family, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E5%A4%A9%E5%AE%AE_(%E4%B9%85%E7%95%99%E7%B1%B3%E5%B8%82))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kurume Suitengu in Fukuoka, the head shrine of the family, giving its deities, its founding tradition, its moves, its precinct and its festivals.

Why it applies, and its limits: It traces a local river-god shrine on the Chikugo river (an older worship of a river deity and a guardian of oxen and horses, from a 1670 domain survey) to a shrine moved to its present site in 1650 by the Kurume lord, who with later lords was its patron. It gives an example of a river shrine promoted by a domain, but its founding story is a shrine tradition, and its precinct list describes the present grounds, with no sizes.
