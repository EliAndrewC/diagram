ranhaku-hoyakuji-ochihata

"曹洞宗 宝薬寺 七郷村大字越畑" ("Hoyakuji, Soto school, Ochihata, Shichigo village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register, in Ranzan Town Web Natural and Local History, volume 6 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter05.html)

What it is: A transcription of one temple's entry in the Hiki County register of temples: the Soto temple Hoyakuji in Ochihata, founded in 1645. It gives its halls, precinct, and the paddy, field, woodland, pond and graveyard land it holds outside the precinct.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a main hall 7 ken by 5 ken 3 shaku, a priest's residence 3 by 5 ken, a precinct of 383 tsubo and 86 parishioners, and lists the temple's own paddies, fields, woods, a reservoir pond of 3 se 1 bu and a graveyard of 2 se 28 bu. It is a Meiji-era register of the temple of the same village as the 1693 report (Ochihata), so the 1645 founding is only stated, and one woodland value is lost from the page.
