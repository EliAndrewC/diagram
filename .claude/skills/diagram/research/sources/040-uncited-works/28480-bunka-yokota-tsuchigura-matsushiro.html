bunka-yokota-tsuchigura-matsushiro

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "旧横田家住宅(長野県長野市松代町松代) 土蔵" (the former Yokota house, Matsushiro, Nagano: storehouse, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/121927)

What it is: A database entry for one earthen-walled storehouse (dozo) of the former Yokota house in Matsushiro, Nagano City, an Important Cultural Property designated in 1986, dated to the late Edo period (1751-1829). It gives the storehouse's size (5.5 m by 4.5 m, two stories, gabled, tiled roof with an eaves lean-to on the east) and a short note on the whole house.

Why it applies, and its limits: The note calls the house the residence of a middle-ranking samurai of the Sanada domain of Matsushiro, with a thatched main house, front gate and retirement house, and a leading example of Matsushiro samurai housing that preserves its compound layout, one of few in the country. The storehouse entry itself gives only outer dimensions, no plan or use, and says nothing of how this compound compares with others in size.
