kagoshima-fumoto-chiran

Minamikyushu City, "知覧麓（南九州市）" ("Chiran Fumoto, Minamikyushu City", title translated), on the Japan Heritage site "Satsuma samurai town" (薩摩の武士が生きた町) (in Japanese; https://kagoshima-fumoto.jp/minamikyushu/)

What it is: A Japan Heritage page on Chiran Fumoto, a surviving Satsuma samurai district, describing a district of stone walls and hedges along a bent old road with samurai gates, and listing its component sites.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that straight ahead inside each samurai gate stands a cut-stone screen, which the page says was for defense and that each house has a garden borrowing a mountain view, and it invites visitors to step back into the mid-Edo period, a visitor's phrase rather than a dating of the houses. It is short and gives no plot sizes or house counts, and it describes a Satsuma district, a form the same council's page on the outer-castle system (kagoshima-fumoto-outer-castle) presents as particular to that domain.
