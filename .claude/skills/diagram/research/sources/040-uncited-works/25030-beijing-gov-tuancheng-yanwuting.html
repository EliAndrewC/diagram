Li Qiyao (text) and Wu Yibin (photographs), "香山团城演武厅史料首次公开" ("Xiangshan Tuancheng Yanwuting shiliao shouci gongkai", the Tuancheng Parade Hall at Xiangshan opens its historical materials for the first time, title translated), originally in Beijing Evening News, on the Beijing Municipal People's Government portal (in Chinese; https://www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/whrl/rdtj/202211/t20221115_2859061.html)

What it is: A news story from 15 November 2022, carried on Beijing's municipal government portal, on the reopening of the Tuancheng Yanwuting, the Qing Jianrui (Elite Scout) Battalion's drill hall and parade ground at Xiangshan, with its exhibition.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a Qing garrison drill complex founded in 1749: a walled circular ground of 1,800 square meters with north and south gate towers, a hall for reviews, a parade ground of over 300 mu to its south, a command tower, a horse-release enclosure and a stele pavilion, with the battalion's banner quarters said to hold 68 watchtowers and over 9,000 barracks rooms. It is one imperial Manchu banner unit's site, not a county or provincial garrison, and the page reports the museum's reconstruction and exhibit rather than surveyed dimensions.
