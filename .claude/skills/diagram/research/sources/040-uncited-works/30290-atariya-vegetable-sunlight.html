atariya-vegetable-sunlight

"野菜の日照条件" ("Sunlight conditions for vegetables", title translated), atariya.net, archived copy (in Japanese; https://web.archive.org/web/2024id_/https://www.atariya.net/kiso/nisho.htm)

What it is: A short home-gardening page that sorts vegetables into sun-loving, half-shade and shade-tolerant plants and gives the daily hours of direct sun each class needs, followed by a table of common vegetables.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the thresholds as approximate hours of direct sun: about 6 hours or more for sun-loving plants, about 3 to 4 for half-shade plants, and 1 to 2 for shade plants, which is a usable yardstick for how much sun a garden bed needs. The saved text loses the table's columns, so which vegetable falls in which class cannot be read from it; the table lists many crops of New World origin (tomato, pepper, potato, corn, sweet potato) that a premodern East Asian setting would not grow, and the page states no source for its hours.
