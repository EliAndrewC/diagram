bunka-hikone-tenshu

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "彦根城天守、附櫓及び多聞櫓 天守" (the Hikone Castle keep with its attached turret and long barracks, keep entry, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/174632)

What it is: A national-treasure entry for the keep of Hikone Castle (Shiga): three stories and three floors, tile-roofed, with a basement stair room and entrance, completed by 1606 as the seat of the Ii house since 1603.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a keep from inscriptions and documents to at least 1606 and describes its finish, with plastered walls, eaves and gables and gilded fittings and black lacquer, so it supports the form of a castle keep at the start of the Edo period. It describes one of the few surviving original keeps, a lavish one by the entry's own word, and gives no floor areas or heights.
