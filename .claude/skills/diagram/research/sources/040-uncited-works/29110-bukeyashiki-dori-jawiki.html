ja.wikipedia 武家屋敷通り (南九州市) ("Bukeyashiki-dori", the Samurai Residence Street, Minamikyushu; the page also covers the Chiran samurai residence gardens, title translated; the URL is the redirect "Chiran bukeyashiki teien") (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/知覧武家屋敷庭園)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Samurai Residence Street of Chiran, Minamikyushu, Kagoshima, a 0.8 km road through a preserved samurai district, with the history of the Satsuma domain's outer-castle districts, its designations and the seven designated gardens.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Satsuma domain divided its land into 113 districts called togo, each with a samurai settlement called a fumoto around the lord's or steward's residence, and that Chiran's surviving residences, from the time of its 18th lord, line a bent road with stone walls and heavily clipped hedges, the hedges named as podocarp (inumaki); the district designated for preservation is 18.6 ha and the gardens are dated to the 1700s and early 1800s. Satsuma's scattered warrior settlements were unusual among domains, the gardens are dry-landscape gardens with a borrowed mountain view, and the present street has been repaved and had its utilities buried for preservation.
