muenbotoke-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "無縁仏" ("muenbotoke", the dead with no one to tend them, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%84%A1%E7%B8%81%E4%BB%8F)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on muenbotoke, the dead who have no kin to make offerings for them, with the folk belief about them, the mound (muenzuka) built for the unidentified dead of disaster, famine or plague, and the modern unclaimed-grave problem in Japan and Korea.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in folk belief the dead without offerings cannot become ancestral spirits, that such dead include those who died unseen or on the road, and that local observances such as Jizo-bon, insect-sending and plague-sending are sometimes tied to tending them. Most of the page is about the modern grave system (a 1999 rule change on removing unclaimed graves, Korean grave terms of 30 to 60 years), which does not describe a premodern village, and it gives no date or region for the older custom.
