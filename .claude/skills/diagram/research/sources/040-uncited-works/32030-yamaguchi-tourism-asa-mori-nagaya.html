Yamaguchi Prefectural Tourism Federation, "旧厚狭毛利家萩屋敷長屋" ("the former Asa Mori family's Hagi residence row house", title translated), on the Oidemase Yamaguchi prefectural tourism site (in Japanese; https://yamaguchi-tourism.jp/spot/detail_14031.html)

What it is: A short spot entry for the surviving row house (nagaya) of the Asa Mori family's residence in Hagi, Yamaguchi, giving its length, roof and fittings, and visiting details.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one measured figure that matters for a samurai compound: a row house 51 m long, a single hip-and-gable tile roof, with a formal entrance step (shikidai), a veranda and a room for servants, standing in the castle town of Hagi. The Asa Mori were a branch of the Choshu lords' Mori family, so this is the compound of a high-ranking house, not an ordinary retainer's, and the page says nothing about the plot's size, the main house or the age of the building.
