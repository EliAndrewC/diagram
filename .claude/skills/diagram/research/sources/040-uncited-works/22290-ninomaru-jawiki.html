ninomaru-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "曲輪" (kuruwa, a castle's walled ward; the page "二の丸" (the second ward) redirects here, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/二の丸)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kuruwa, a castle's enclosed ward, covering the layout of wards, their names by position (honmaru, ninomaru, sannomaru), direction and shape, and types such as the horse-out (umadashi) and the pleasure ward.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the ninomaru is the ward next to the honmaru and may also have halls, that the sannomaru was where retainers' residences sometimes stood, and that these names are Edo-period; it also warns that the Edo military theory behind the standard layouts was never tested in war. The page gives no dimensions, and it is about castle layout, not the walled-in samurai quarters of a town below.
