kadoma-kawachi-renkon

Kadoma City, Industry Promotion Division, "河内れんこんが「なにわの伝統野菜」に認証されました" ("Kawachi lotus root certified as a Naniwa traditional vegetable", title translated), on the city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.kadoma.osaka.jp/soshiki/shiminbunkabu/6/3/6/32867.html)

What it is: A short 2024 city notice that Kawachi lotus root, grown mainly in Kadoma, was certified as an Osaka traditional vegetable, with a few lines on its history and where it is sold.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in the low, poorly drained old Ibaraki County (茨田郡), where Kadoma and Moriguchi now stand, lotus was grown in place of paddy rice, and that a native wild lotus is recorded in an Nara-period book. This supports lotus as the crop of wet ground too poor for rice, but the page says the present Kawachi lotus came from Kaga and Bitchu varieties introduced in the Taisho era, and it gives no yields, plot sizes or dates of the shift from rice.
