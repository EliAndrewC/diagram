zh.wikipedia "明清北京城" ("Ming and Qing Beijing", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/北京内城)

What it is: A long Chinese Wikipedia article on the walled Ming and Qing capital of Beijing, with its building history, its four nested walls (palace, imperial city, inner city, outer city), its gates, moats and defenses, and its demolition after 1949.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measures of a capital's walls: the inner wall at 45 li after the Zhengtong work of 1436 to 1445, the outer wall of 1553 at 28 li (14.409 km) with a base of 11 to 15 m, and the Qing practice of moving Han residents out of the inner city, which became a Manchu banner quarter. The outer wall's height is given twice with different figures (7.5 to 8 m in one place and 6 to 7 m in another), and the page describes the wall as it was surveyed before demolition, in a capital with no counterpart in a provincial city.
