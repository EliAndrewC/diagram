bunka-tsuchiya-ke-dozo-makabe

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "土谷家住宅土蔵" ("Tsuchiya house, storehouse," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/194369)

What it is: A national database entry for a two-story earthen-walled storehouse of the Tsuchiya house in Makabe, Sakuragawa, Ibaraki, built between 1830 and 1867, 29 square meters, registered in 2003.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the storehouse was built as relief work after the typhoon disaster of 1833 and describes its stone-laid base, sea-cucumber-pattern wall base, heavy double doors and plastered eaves. It is a single small storehouse of a Kanto town, its date is given only as a range, and the relief-work account concerns one town's storehouse.
