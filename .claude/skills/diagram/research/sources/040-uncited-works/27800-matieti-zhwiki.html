zh.wikipedia "马蹄铁" ("The horseshoe", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%A9%AC%E8%B9%84%E9%93%81)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the horseshoe, a partial translation of the English one, covering how the hoof wears, the Roman and medieval iron shoe, the hipposandal and a short paragraph on China.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that a hoof's hard horn is only 2 to 3 cm thick and wears fast on the ground, that nailed iron shoes were common in the Roman and medieval West, and that many animals were still never shod. Its only Chinese evidence is one paragraph: the leather horse shoe of the Han Yantie lun, the Uighurs shoeing horses in the Five Dynasties, and a Dunhuang mural of a shoeing scene; the page itself carries a banner calling its translation poor.
