kaf2-chusei-no-haka

Kanagawa Archaeological Foundation, "中世の墓" ("Chusei no haka", Medieval graves, title translated), in the permanent exhibition of its archaeology museum (in Japanese; https://www.kaf2.org/josetsu/chusei/chisei_iko/chusei_grave)

What it is: A short museum exhibition page on medieval burial types in Kanagawa: the yagura, a rock-cut tomb of the Kamakura area, and the plain earth pit grave.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says yagura are cave tombs cut into hillside slopes around Kamakura, apparently for people of high rank, holding mostly cremated bone, and that medieval pit graves were usually square pits with the body buried on its side with legs bent, sometimes with six-ri coins, with mass burials found in Kamakura's beach dunes. It covers the Kanagawa region's medieval finds only, gives no dates within the period, and treats graves as excavated features, not as a village's burial ground.
