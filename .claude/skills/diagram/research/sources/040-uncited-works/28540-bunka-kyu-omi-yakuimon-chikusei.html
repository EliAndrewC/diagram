bunka-kyu-omi-yakuimon-chikusei

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "旧尾見家住宅薬医門" (the yakui gate of the former Omi house, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/292830)

What it is: A database entry for a small thatched gate (yakuimon) in Chikusei, Ibaraki, a registered tangible cultural property dated 1883-1897 (Meiji), 2.5 m wide, one bay with one door, standing south of the main house. The note describes its simple construction and calls it a rare surviving thatched gate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the form of a one-bay thatched gate of a farmhouse compound in the Kanto plain: forked-rafter roof frame, plank door, no metal fittings, built after the Restoration but kept as the entry says to show the old compound layout. It is dated to the Meiji period, so it carries the traditional form but not the date of the setting, and it gives only the width of the gate.
