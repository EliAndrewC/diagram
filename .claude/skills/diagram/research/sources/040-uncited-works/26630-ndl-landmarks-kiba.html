ndl-landmarks-kiba

National Diet Library, "木場" ("Kiba", title translated), in "錦絵と写真でめぐる日本の名所" ("Famous places of Japan in color prints and photographs", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.ndl.go.jp/landmarks/details/detail365.html)

What it is: A brief entry in the National Diet Library's electronic exhibition on Kiba, in Fukagawa, Edo, with a list of prints and pictures of it. It says the land was granted in 1701 to 15 lumber wholesalers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the 15 wholesalers built embankments on all four sides and dug moats around the ground, and that Kiba became Edo's lumber market, which gives the date and the form of an embanked, moated timber-storage district. The entry is a few lines long, with no area, no lot sizes and no plan, and it cites no source: the prints and the 1834 Edo meisho zue illustration it lists are pictures of the place, not a survey.
