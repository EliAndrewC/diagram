mulberry-history-agri-history

"桑树栽培史" ("Sangshu zaipei shi", history of mulberry cultivation, title translated), on the Agricultural History site of the Institute for the History of Natural Sciences, Chinese Academy of Sciences (in Chinese; https://agri-history.ihns.ac.cn/agrobiology/mulberry.htm)

What it is: An unsigned survey article on how mulberry was grown in China from the Classic of Poetry to the Ming, covering regions and varieties, propagation, manuring, tilling, pruning and tree forms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the old farm books on what a mulberry grove was fed and cut with, for example pond and canal mud as the main fertilizer in the Lake Tai region, tilling twice a year, and the winter-to-early-spring pruning season. It is a summary that quotes the treatises rather than giving field measurements, it dwells on Jiangnan, and it gives no spacing or yield figures.
