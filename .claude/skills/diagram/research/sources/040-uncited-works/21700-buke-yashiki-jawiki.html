buke-yashiki-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 武家屋敷 ("buke yashiki", the warrior house or estate, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A6%E5%AE%B6%E5%9C%B0)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on warrior residences, covering daimyo, hatamoto and ordinary samurai houses, the samurai quarters of castle towns, and the separate housing of ashigaru.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the placing rule of a castle town: higher ranks near the castle or lord, lower ranks farther out, ashigaru housing at the town's entrances, and Edo as about half warrior estates. It also separates the daimyo's upper, middle, lower and storehouse estates by function. Three sections (hatamoto houses, warrior quarters, samurai houses) carry the page's own banner saying they cite no sources. Those are where the rank-by-distance rule and the figure of about half of Edo stand, though the sourced lead also puts ashigaru row houses on the outer edge. It gives no plot sizes.
