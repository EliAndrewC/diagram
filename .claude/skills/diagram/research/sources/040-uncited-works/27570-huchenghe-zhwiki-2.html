zh.wikipedia 護城河 (moat, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/護城河)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the city moat, a dug channel around a city, palace or temple, with examples in China, Taiwan, Japan and Europe.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names Xi'an, the Forbidden City, Matsumoto Castle and the Imperial Palace at Edo among moated sites, notes drawbridges in Europe, and says the Ming chose Xuanwu Lake as Nanjing's moat. It gives no width, depth or water-supply figure, and a short list of gallery photographs is its only detail.
