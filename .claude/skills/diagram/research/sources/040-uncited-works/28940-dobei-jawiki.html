dobei-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 土塀 ("dobei", earthen wall, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/土塀)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the dobei, the traditional clay wall, giving its materials, the steps of building one in courses of clay balls under a tiled cap, and the Arita brick wall variant.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the recipe for a village or compound wall made of local clay, lime, funori (seaweed glue), straw and rapeseed oil, laid as outer and inner rows of clay balls on a foundation with stones packed between the rows, and says builders took the clay from nearby ground. It names no wall height, thickness or date, says the wall was once common everywhere in Japan without a period, and carries Wikipedia's warning that it cites no sources.
