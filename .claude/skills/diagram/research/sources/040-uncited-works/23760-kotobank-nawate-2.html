kotobank-nawate-2

"畷" ("nawate", a path between paddies, title translated), in the Heibonsha Jitsu character dictionary, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%B7)

What it is: A character-dictionary entry on 畷, read nawate or aze, glossed as the field path, with its Shuowen definition and a Liji gloss.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries the Shuowen definition of the character as the joined paths between fields "six feet wide" and a commentary on the Liji glossing it as the place among the fields where the farmers of the well-field were overseen, that is, a road between fields, so it shows what the word meant in Han-era Chinese usage. It is a philological gloss on a character, not a description of any Japanese field road, and its six-foot width is the Shuowen's, in the Han foot of about 23 cm (about 1.4 m, not 1.8 m in the Japanese shaku), and is a definition, not a measurement.
