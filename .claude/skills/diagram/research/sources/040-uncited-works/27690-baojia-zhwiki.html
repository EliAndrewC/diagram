zh.wikipedia 保甲制度 ("baojia", the bao-jia household-grouping system, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BF%9D%E7%94%B2%E5%88%B6%E5%BA%A6)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the baojia system of grouping households for policing and registration, with a short account of its origin and dynasties and longer sections on Macau, Taiwan, and the Republic of China and early People's Republic periods.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the basic form of ten households to a jia and ten jia to a bao, traces it to Wang Anshi's Song reform (abandoned in the Xuanhe years), says it recurred under later dynasties under other names, that the Ming founder's version was the strictest, and that it was applied from 1684 under the Qing until 1912. Most of the page is twentieth-century, with the Shanghai 1949 counts, and its premodern part is a few lines under a notice that it needs expansion, with no detail on village practice.
