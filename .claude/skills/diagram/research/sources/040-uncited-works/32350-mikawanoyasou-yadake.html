mikawanoyasou-yadake

Flora of Mikawa (三河の植物観察), "ヤダケ" ("yadake", arrow bamboo, title translated), plant page on mikawanoyasou.org (in Japanese; https://mikawanoyasou.org/data/yadake.htm)

What it is: A botanical page on the arrow bamboo (Pseudosasa japonica), giving its culm length, habitat and distribution, with a genus description from the Flora of China and notes on other Pseudosasa species.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the plant is named for its use as arrow shafts, that it is also used for fishing rods and brush handles, and that it has long been cultivated across Japan with an unknown origin, which supports arrow bamboo as an old cultivated Japanese bamboo. It gives the culm as 1-3 (to 5) m tall and 5-15 mm across, so it describes a small slender bamboo, and it gives no planting layout or date for any use.
