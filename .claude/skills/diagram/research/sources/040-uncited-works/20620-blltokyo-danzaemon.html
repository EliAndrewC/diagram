blltokyo-danzaemon

Buraku Liberation League Tokyo Federation, "東京の部落史と現状・浅草弾左衛門" ("Tokyo no buraku-shi to genjo: Asakusa Danzaemon", the history and present of Tokyo's burakumin: Danzaemon of Asakusa, title translated), section 2 of "東京の被差別部落の歴史と現状" (in Japanese; https://blltokyo.net/tkburaku/history/index.html)

What it is: A page by a Tokyo burakumin liberation organization on Danzaemon, the hereditary head of the outcaste groups of the Kanto region under the Edo shogunate. It covers his rank, the groups he governed, his monopolies in leather and other goods, his duties in policing and executions, his money lending and his house tradition of origin.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how Edo ruled its outcaste communities: Danzaemon answered to the Edo town magistrate, lived at the level of a bannerman, and ran guard, police and execution duty, while still treated as outcaste by samurai and townsmen. The page itself judges the family's account of its origins before 1590 to be contrived to justify the post and says the full system was set up only in the middle Edo period; it is an advocacy page of an Edo-region case, not a study of other regions.
