kaido-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 街道 ("kaido", highways, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/街道)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kaido, Japan's old overland roads, with a history from the ancient period to the present and a list of the principal ones.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says most road names date from the Meiji era's road administration, that in the Edo period the five main highways and branch roads were under the Dochu bugyo (set up in 1659) and the more important side roads (the Ise, Chugoku and Sado roads) under the Kanjo bugyo, that post towns and branch roads were built nationwide, and that most surviving mile mounds, distance stones and signposts are Edo-period. It gives no widths, surfaces or spacing, and the page carries a notice that it lacks sources, with only one footnote, so its statements are unsupported beyond that.
