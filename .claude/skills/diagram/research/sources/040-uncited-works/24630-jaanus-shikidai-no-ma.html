jaanus-shikidai-no-ma

Mary Neighbour Parent (compiler), "shikidai-no-ma 式台の間" (the shikidai room, title translated), in JAANUS, the on-line Dictionary of Japanese Architectural and Art Historical Terminology, Media Center for Art History, Columbia University (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/node/1318)

What it is: A dictionary entry on the shikidai-no-ma, a room at the lower end of a leading warrior's reception hall in the 16th-17th centuries, with the term's source and an example.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the room was joined to the main hall and the guard house (tozamurai), began as a retainers' waiting area and later became the formal entry where visitors were greeted, sometimes with a genkan porch, and names the Kaitokukan at Kochi Castle (1747-9) as an example. It speaks of the residences of leading warriors, not of lower retainers' or village houses, and gives no dimensions.
