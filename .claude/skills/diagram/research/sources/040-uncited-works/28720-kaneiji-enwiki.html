en.wikipedia "Kan'ei-ji" (the English Wikipedia article on Kan'ei-ji, 寛永寺, the Tendai temple of Ueno, Tokyo) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan'ei-ji)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Kan'ei-ji, the Tendai temple founded in Edo in 1625 as a Tokugawa funeral temple, with its history from the grant of the land to its destruction in the Battle of Ueno in 1868.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the temple was sited northeast of Edo Castle to ward off evil spirits, that the land was taken from the suburban residences of three daimyo, that it once held over 30 buildings across the heights north and east of Shinobazu Pond, and that the shogunal burials alternated with Zojo-ji. It is the record of one great shogunal temple, the largest kind of precinct there was, so its scale does not stand for a town or village temple, and it gives no measured area.
