nishikanbara-nosagyo

Nishikanbara Land Improvement District, "昔の農作業風景" ("Farm work scenes of the past", title translated), on the district's site (in Japanese; http://www.nishikan.or.jp/about/nosagyo.html)

What it is: A page by a land improvement district in Niigata, with photographs, describing rice farming before machines: flooding the field, puddling, pulling seedlings, reaping, drying the sheaves on hasagi trees and threshing with a senbakoki.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that before dryers, farmers dried the cut rice by planting straight, tall trees in rows and laying poles across them alternately, and that threshing used the iron-toothed comb. It also says puddling was done by hand with hoes or with a plow drawn by cattle or horses in some places, and seedlings were carried by field boat where the water was high. The page is a few lines for each step, gives no tree species, spacing or dates, and describes the age before machines in general, not a century.
