adachi-mon-yogo

Adachi City, "用語解説（門）" ("Glossary of terms: gates", title translated), in the cultural properties pages of the Adachi City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.adachi.tokyo.jp/bunka/chiikibunka/bunkazai_yougo_mon.html)

What it is: A short glossary page from a Tokyo ward's cultural properties section defining four gate terms: the four-legged gate (shikyakumon), the physician's-gate type (yakuimon), and the one-bay, one-door and three-bay, one-door gate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the plain structure of the two gate types (a four-legged gate has two main posts and four supporting posts; a yakuimon has two main posts with two supporting posts behind) and the bay unit, one ken of about 1.8 m, so it can fix what a given gate name means and how wide its bays run. It is a glossary written for visitors to the ward's designated gates, so it gives no dates and no sizes of any particular gate, and the page does not say which gates a village headman or a samurai house would have had.
