yamamoto-awa-koie

Yamamoto Tomio, "近世の村落生活に於ける家の関係 - 阿波藩の場合 -" ("The relation of houses in early modern village life: the case of the Awa domain", title translated), Tokushima Chireki Gakkai, in the Awa Gakkai Research Bulletin (阿波学会研究紀要), on the Tokushima Prefectural Library's digital archive (in Japanese; https://library.bunmori.tokushima.jp/digital/webkiyou/02/0202.htm)

What it is: The full text of a mid-twentieth-century paper on the Awa domain (Tokushima), whose villages were groups of main houses (壱家) and their dependent branch houses (koya), with counts from household registers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives counts from the household registers: in a village of 1674 it counts 53 main houses to 146 branch houses, in 1811 87 to 376, and in another of 1722 12 to 27, mostly kin branches, and it says the Awa domain had almost no five-household neighbor groups. It reads a few Awa villages' registers and says itself that two places cannot stand for Awa or Japan as a whole, it closes by saying it gave its conclusions without setting out all its evidence, and it records a domain of Shikoku and not a home-county rice village.
