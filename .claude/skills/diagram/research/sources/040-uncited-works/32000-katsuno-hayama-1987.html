katsuno-hayama-1987

Katsuno Takehiko and Hayama Yoshikazu, "都市近郊農業地域の屋敷林構造とその保全に関する研究" ("a study of the structure and conservation of house groves in a suburban farming district", title translated), 造園雑誌 (Journal of the Japanese Institute of Landscape Architecture) 50(5), 1987, pp. 191-196, on J-STAGE (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jila1934/50/5/50_5_191/_pdf)

What it is: A signed journal paper mapping 93 house groves (yashikirin) and 5.8 km of hedges, with a tree-by-tree survey of 14 of the groves in the Misono district of Urawa, Saitama, on the Omiya upland, with a count of grove layouts by which sides of the house they stand on, species, heights and crown spreads.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured counts for one dispersed-farm district: 37 of 93 groves (40%) stand on the north, east and west sides, the paper gives that type and the north-and-west type together as 82 (68%), though its own counts of 37 and 25 make 62, and the stated purpose is to block the winter north and northwest winds; shirakashi is 35.2% of 770 surveyed trees and groves of 20 m or more are named. It is a 1980s survey of a suburban Kanto district whose groves the authors say have been changed and thinned by road works and changed ways of living, they treat the three-sided form as the original, and they note that it is one district only, a district that grows garden trees and nursery stock, where many hedges line nursery plots.
