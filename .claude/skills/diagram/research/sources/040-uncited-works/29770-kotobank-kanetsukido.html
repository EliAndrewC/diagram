kotobank-kanetsukido

"鐘撞き堂" ("kanetsukido", the bell-striking hall, title translated), in the Digital Daijisen and the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/鐘撞き堂)

What it is: A Kotobank dictionary page defining kanetsukido as a hall in which a temple bell is hung to be struck, a bell tower (shoro), with the Edo place nickname of the same name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms the building type, a hall holding a hanging bell, with a Juzo-sho (1252) passage placing one beside the abbot's house. It gives no size, siting rule or number, and its second sense is a nickname for Honjo Irie-cho in Edo, named from the nearby bell house that struck the hours.
