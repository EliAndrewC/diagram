Quan Jie (Guangzhou Daily, Xinhuacheng), "树说广州 | 村前古榕遮风雨" ("Trees of Guangzhou: the old banyan before the village shelters from wind and rain", title translated), 29 April 2022, on the Huacheng site of Guangzhou Daily (in Chinese; https://huacheng.gz-cmc.com/pages/2022/04/29/287620eb29da43c89aa9a4d5db42fb91.html)

What it is: A short newspaper piece with a data card on one small-leaved banyan beside the old Beidi temple in Datian village, Jianggao town, Baiyun district, Guangzhou: a third-grade old tree, estimated 130 years old, 13.5 meters high, 520 cm around the trunk, with an average crown spread of 24.5 meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records, on the word of village elders, that a village banyan beside a temple sheltered people from sun and cold wind, that villagers sat and talked in its shade, and that it was treated as a feng shui and wishing tree. The tree is one specimen, its age is an estimate, and at about 130 years it was planted near the end of the setting's period, so it shows the custom as the elders remember it rather than an earlier village.
