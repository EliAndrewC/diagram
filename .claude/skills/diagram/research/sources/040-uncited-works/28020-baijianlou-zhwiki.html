baijianlou-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 百间楼 ("the Hundred-Room Houses", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E7%99%BE%E9%97%B4%E6%A5%BC)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on Baijianlou, two old streets of houses along both banks of the Baijianlou river in Nanxun, Huzhou, Zhejiang, giving their length, house count and building features.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a canal street built up on both banks for about 400 m with roughly 180 house numbers (97 on the east bank, 84 on the west), continuous covered eaves along the water and fire gables between the houses with arched passages beneath them, said to have been built by a Ming minister of rites. The founding is tradition ("it is said"), the west bank was bombed in the 1940s and altered when repaired, and the article says nothing of house sizes or lot widths.
