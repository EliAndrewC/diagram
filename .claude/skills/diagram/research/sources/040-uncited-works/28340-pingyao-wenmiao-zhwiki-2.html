zh.wikipedia 平遥文庙 ("the Pingyao Confucian temple", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/平遥文庙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Confucian temple in Pingyao, Shanxi, a nationally protected site, with its founding, its Jin-dynasty main hall and the academy attached to it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the temple's founding to the early Zhenguan years of the Tang and its main hall (Dacheng hall) to a rebuilding in 1163 under the Jin, and says it is the only Jin building among China's Confucian temples, so it anchors where one county-town temple stood and how old its hall is. The article gives no plan, dimensions or area, and what it dates is the hall, not the temple's grounds as a whole.
