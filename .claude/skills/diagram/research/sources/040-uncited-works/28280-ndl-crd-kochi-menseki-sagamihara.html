Sagamihara City Sagamiono Library (相模原市立相模大野図書館), "江戸時代と明治初期の農地（水田・畑）の面積を知りたい。" ("I want to know the area of farmland (paddy and dry fields) in the Edo period and early Meiji", title translated), reference case 相大-R1-014, in the Reference Collaborative Database (レファレンス協同データベース) of the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?page=ref_view&id=1000261399)

What it is: A library reference answer, registered 2019, that gathers published figures for Japan's total cultivated area from the 8th century to 1905 from six books. They include 2,065 thousand cho in 1600, 2,991 thousand in 1750, and 3,234 thousand at the 1873 land-tax reform.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives national totals at several dates, such as about 2.0 million cho at the end of the 16th century and about 2.97 million in 1716-1748, which is a check on the scale of Edo farmland. The sources disagree (early Meiji figures run from about 3.2 to 4.2 million cho), the totals cover the whole country, and the librarian found no Edo source that separates paddy from dry field.
