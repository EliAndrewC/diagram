tokyo-archives-gofunai-bikou

Tokyo Metropolitan Archives, "所蔵資料解説 府内備考" ("Explanation of holdings: Gofunai bikou", title translated), in 東京都公文書館の所蔵資料紹介 (in Japanese; https://www.soumu.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/01soumu/archives/03022funaibikou.htm)

What it is: An archives page describing the Gofunai bikou, the source collection compiled in 1826-1829 for the shogunate's gazetteer of Edo, and its temple and shrine continuation of 1829 in 147 volumes, with the headings listed for each temple.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists what each temple returned to the shogunate: parent temple, name and precinct area, founding and founder, temple lands, main hall and image, precinct facilities, old documents and a precinct plan, so it shows what an Edo survey of temples counted. It describes the compilation and does not give any area or count itself, and the page says its copy has never been printed in transcription.
