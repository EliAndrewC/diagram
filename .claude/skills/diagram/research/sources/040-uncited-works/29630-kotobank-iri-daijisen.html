kotobank-iri-daijisen

"圦" ("iri", the sluice), in the Shogakukan Digital Daijisen (デジタル大辞泉) and the Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (精選版 日本国語大辞典), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/圦)

What it is: Two short dictionary entries for the character 圦: read iri, a sluice, which is a pipe or trough buried under a bank to regulate water coming in and out; and read kure, a turf cut with its soil and planted on a thatched roof in eastern Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the sluice as a culvert set into the bank of a pond or river embankment, with a dictionary citation of 1717, and so names the part of a bank where water was let in or out. It gives no dimensions, no region for the sluice, and no description of its working; the kure sense is a roofing practice with only a 1717 citation.
