ja.wikipedia 三道具 ("the three tools", the three polearms used to seize a suspect, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E9%81%93%E5%85%B7)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the mitsudogu, the sasumata, tsukubo and sodegarami, three pole weapons used in the Edo period to capture a criminal suspect.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes how a party of catchers used the three tools together to pin, strike and pull down a suspect without killing him: barbs along the shaft and metal plates at the head so a sword could not cut it. It gives no dates, no counts of catchers and no place, and the article is marked as a stub with almost nothing beyond this one paragraph of description.
