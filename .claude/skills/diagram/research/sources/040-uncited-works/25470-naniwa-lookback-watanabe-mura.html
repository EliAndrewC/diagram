Osaka City Naniwa Ward Office, "浪速区制100周年プレ企画 ルックバック浪速区（第5回浪速地域）" ("Pre-event for the Naniwa Ward centennial, Look Back Naniwa Ward, part 5: the Naniwa district", title translated), Naniwa Ward pages on the Osaka City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/naniwa/page/0000634484.html)

What it is: A long ward-office page in two parts: a 2025 roundtable of local residents, then a history of the Naniwa district, which began as Watanabe village, a leather-working village at Osaka, with its moves, hide trade, drum making, school, wartime loss and rebuilding, a timeline and a list of sites.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the village settled in 1706 on land of Kizu village as its branch village, grew to over 5,000 people at its peak, and spread outward though a river hemmed it in; that twelve hide wholesalers were licensed in 1615-1624, over 100,000 hides a year came to it from western Japan, and it made most of the 73,000 drums made in Osaka in about 1881. That gives a dated, concrete account of a premodern leather district. The page says it is based on local storytellers and not historically verified, the roundtable is postwar memory, and its later parts (postwar land readjustment, shoemaking) are modern.
