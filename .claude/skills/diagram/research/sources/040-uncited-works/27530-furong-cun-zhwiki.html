zh.wikipedia 芙蓉村 (Furong village, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/芙蓉村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Furong, a Chen-family village in Yongjia County, Zhejiang, on the Nanxi River, a nationally protected group of old buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the village is laid out on a "seven stars and eight dippers" scheme, with the lamps as stars and the wells as dippers, narrow east to west and long north to south, and keeps a stockade wall and gate, an ancestral hall, an academy and nearly fifty old buildings. It does not date the layout: it gives the village's founding only as a tradition of the late Tang, and its heritage listing dates the buildings Ming to Qing. It gives no dimensions, and the seven-stars scheme rests on a single cited book (Chen Zhihua and Li Qiuxiang, 2010).
