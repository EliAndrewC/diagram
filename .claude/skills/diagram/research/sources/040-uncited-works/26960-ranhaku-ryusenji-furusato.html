ranhaku-ryusenji-furusato

"天台宗 龍泉寺 古里村" ("Ryusenji, Tendai school, Furusato village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register, in Ranzan Town Web Natural and Local History, volume 6 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter02.html)

What it is: A transcription of one temple's entry in the Hiki County register of temples: the small Tendai temple Ryusenji in Furusato village, a branch of Sensoji in Asakusa. It gives its hall size, precinct, a Yakushi hall in its grounds and its parishioners.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a main hall 5 ken square, a precinct of 439 tsubo, a Yakushi hall of 2 by 2 ken 5 shaku and only 8 parishioners, so it is a measured example of a very small village temple with a subsidiary hall. It is a register entry of the Meiji period or later, its origin is stated as unknown, and it says nothing of any building outside the halls.
