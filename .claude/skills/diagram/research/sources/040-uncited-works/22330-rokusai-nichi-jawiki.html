ja.wikipedia 六斎日 ("Rokusai-nichi", the six Buddhist fast days, title translated), with its section on the rokusai-ichi market (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/六斎市)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the six monthly fast days of Buddhism, with a section on the rokusai-ichi, the periodic market held six times a month, and on the three-times-a-month sansai-ichi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the six-times-monthly market to about the Muromachi period, gives an example of the schedule (the 2nd and 8th of each ten days), and says that after the Onin War peasants and estate officials sold produce at these markets for cash, which moved tribute goods into trade. Its present-day examples from Nakatsugawa and Chiba's Isumi area describe modern revivals and survivals, and it gives no market sizes, places or numbers for the period.
