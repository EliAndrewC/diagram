Nanjing City Culture and Tourism Bureau (from the Nanjing City Wall Research Society), "【遗产大观】寿县城墙" ("Shouxian city wall", title translated), World Heritage nomination research page, on wlj.nanjing.gov.cn (in Chinese; https://wlj.nanjing.gov.cn/ztzl/mcq/syyj/202309/t20230905_4003568.html)

What it is: A heritage page on the walls of Shou County (Shouxian), Anhui, a Ming and Qing prefecture-rank walled town, with its dimensions, gates and flood-resisting features.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a roughly square wall of 7.147 km circuit, 8.33 m high, 18-22 m at the base and 4-10 m at the top, rammed earth faced with brick, four gates with semicircular barbican yards turned off axis, a moat and a street grid from the four gates, so it is a measured example of a provincial walled town. The form is Ming and Qing, mostly reshaped for floods, and the page is a nomination write-up that cites its source only as a later book.
