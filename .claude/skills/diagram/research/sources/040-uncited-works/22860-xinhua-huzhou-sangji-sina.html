Xinhua News Agency, "湖州“桑基鱼塘”的前世今生" ("The past and present of Huzhou's mulberry-dike fish ponds", title translated), 18 January 2019, on Sina News (in Chinese; https://k.sina.cn/article_213815211_0cbe8fab02000iswr.html)

What it is: A Xinhua news feature, reposted on Sina, on the mulberry-dike and fish-pond system of Huzhou in Zhejiang, covering its claimed origin, its present extent, a farming household's year, and the silk and fish customs of the area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the cycle of pond, mulberry on the dike, silkworm waste feeding fish and pond mud fed back to the mulberry, and gives a household's calendar from a farmer with 3 mu of mulberry and 5 mu of pond. Its household calendar is that of one present-day couple of 2019 who keep a few sheets of silkworms, its figures (60,000 mu of mulberry, 150,000 mu of ponds, 0.046 hectare per person) are the heritage area's extent at the time of writing, and its 2,500-year origin is stated without a source.
