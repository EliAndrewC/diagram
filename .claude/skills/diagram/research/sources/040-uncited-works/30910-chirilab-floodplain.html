chirilab-floodplain

Chiri Labo (地理ラボ), "氾濫原に広がる微地形（自然堤防・後背湿地など、河川の下流部の地形）" ("Microlandforms of the floodplain: natural levees, back marshes and other lower-river landforms", title translated), on 地理ラボ 詳しすぎる高校地理 (a high-school geography site) (in Japanese; https://alivevulnerable.com/basic/floodplain)

What it is: An illustrated high-school geography article defining the floodplain and its landforms (natural levee, back marsh, oxbow lake, raised-bed river) with Japanese examples from the Ishikari, Iwaki, Shinano and Ishikari river plains.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a natural levee stands from tens of centimeters to a few meters above its surroundings, that old villages and highways sat on the levee, and that the poorly drained back marsh was mainly paddy; its Iwaki and Shinano River examples show that pattern. Its photographs and maps are of the present day with modern embankments, and it gives no premodern dates.
