What it is: A stub Japanese Wikipedia article on the Nuruyu checkpoint, a Sendai domain border post in present-day Kurihara, Miyagi, preserved as a national historic site with its gate and the official's residence.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the post guarded the Akita-side road out of the domain, checked travelers and goods for about two hundred years from the Keicho era, and had a gate built across the road with an inspection hall in front of it, the hall now gone. It names the surviving thatched gate (rebuilt in the early Ansei years) and a two-storey residence. It gives no dimensions, no staff count and no layout of the yard, and the visitor details on the page describe the site as it is open today.
