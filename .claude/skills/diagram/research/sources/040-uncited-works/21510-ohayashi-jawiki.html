ohayashi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "御林" ("ohayashi", the lord's forest, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BE%A1%E6%9E%97)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the ohayashi, the forests held under the shogunate or a domain lord in the Edo period, giving the names each domain used, why they were set aside and how they were managed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says such forests were set aside either for timber or for erosion, flood, wind and sand control, that from the 1660s (Kanbun era) a lord's officials managed them, with planting from the 1700s, and that villagers could gather underbrush for a fee while cutting a single tree could draw the death penalty. It names no area or yield, and as a stub it cites only dictionary entries, so it says how a lord's forest was run but not how large one was.
