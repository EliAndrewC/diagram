moe-tonami-plain-sankyoson

Ministry of the Environment (Japan), "砺波平野散居村（屋敷林に囲まれた農家住宅と水田が織りなす日本の原風景）" ("The scattered-settlement villages of the Tonami Plain: the original landscape of Japan, farmhouses within house-woods among paddies", title translated), entry 16-4 in its list of Important Satoyama (in Japanese; https://www.env.go.jp/nature/satoyama/16_toyama/no16-4.html)

What it is: A ministry entry selecting the dispersed farmstead villages of the Tonami Plain in Toyama (Tonami and Nanto cities) as an Important Satoyama, with the reasons and the local conservation work.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says farmhouses ringed by house-woods called kainyo lie scattered over the Sho River fan, the woods mainly cedar with fruit trees, among paddies, and that fireflies live in the waters around them. It records the landscape as maintained now, with no dates, plot or grove sizes or counts, and it is a selection for nature conservation, not a study of how the settlement was laid out.
