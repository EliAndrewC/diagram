matsue-bukeyashiki-facility

Matsue City designated cultural property Bukeyashiki (samurai residence), "施設の概要" ("Outline of the facility", title translated), on the residence's own site (in Japanese; https://www.matsue-bukeyashiki.jp/about/index.html)

What it is: The visitor page of a surviving retainer's house on Shiomi Nawate in Matsue, Shimane, describing the main house, gate-house and wall, its history and its 2016-2018 restoration.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the house was lived in by domain retainers of about 500 to 1,000 koku who changed places by reassignment, was rebuilt after the great fire of 1733, has a main house of about 67 tsubo, and separates the formal front (the entrance and rooms for guests) from the private rear in both build and materials. The restoration followed a Meiji-era drawing, so what stands may differ from the Edo house, and the page gives no lot size or plan for the grounds.
