seika-kinsei-04-05

Seika Town (Kyoto), "近世 4" and "近世 5" ("kinsei", the early modern period), pages of a town-history booklet on seikasya.town.seika.kyoto.jp (in Japanese; https://seikasya.town.seika.kyoto.jp/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/15_seika-print-kinsei04_05.pdf)

What it is: Booklet pages on early modern villages of Seika, Kyoto: irrigation ponds and mountain commons, water and mountain disputes, and the local deputies (zaichi daikan) of bannerman lords.

Why it applies, and its limits: It tabulates Edo-period pond counts per village (Hishida with 25, 13 village-held and 12 private, a count that includes five ditches (hori) used as ponds and ponds as small as 3 bu) and gives the 1784 estimate for one pond, about 48 ares for 8,900 labor days, and describes a village hill of about 1.7 ha used only for brush and undergrowth, so it is a source for village ponds, shared hills and water rights. The pond counts come from 1679 to 1843 registers and a 1880 map for a few Yamashiro villages, and it covers one district near Kyoto.
