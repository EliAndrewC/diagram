kushiro-env-alder-wetland

Ministry of the Environment, Kushiro Nature Office, "湿原に生息する植物：ハンノキ" ("Plants of the wetland: the alder", title translated), Kushiro Wetland Data Center (in Japanese; https://kushirodata-center.env.go.jp/wetland/wetland_article2_10.html)

What it is: A government page on the alder (hannoki) in the Kushiro mire in Hokkaido: that it is one of the few trees that grows tall in a wetland, how far its woods spread by 1998, and the suspected causes of their spread.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the alder grows in lowland wet ground all over Japan and has existed in the Kushiro mire as natural vegetation, which supports it as a tree of wet ground. The spread it records (about 30 percent of the mire by 1998; one district's alder woods from 735,337 to 865,165 square meters between 1993 and 2000) is put down to modern development around the mire (silt washed in after river works, drying, a changed water table and nutrient inflow), so it is no measure of premodern stands, and the page itself says the causes are not yet scientifically settled.
