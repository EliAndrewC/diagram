shiga-jori-jiwari

Shiga Prefecture, "3 いまでも残る条里地割" ("Imademo nokoru jori jiwari", the jori land division that still remains, title translated), pp. 91-92 of an attached prefectural PDF on Takashima (in Japanese; https://pref.shiga.lg.jp/file/attachment/4040488.pdf)

What it is: Two pages describing the surviving jori grid of the Ado River basin in Takashima, Shiga: the six-cho square block (about 654 m) split into 36 tsubo of about 109 m, the Meiji-Taisho land readjustment at Kita-Funaki and the 1970s field consolidation at Aiba, with before-and-after plot sizes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of the ancient grid (a block 654 m a side, tsubo about 109 m) and the plot sizes before consolidation (at most about 1 are, enlarged to about 10 ares at Kita-Funaki), which shows what the old field pattern was where the grid survives. The page says the Kita-Funaki readjustment lies at 17-18 degrees east of north against 16 degrees for the ancient grid, and that Aiba's grid ran at 6 degrees; its figures for the modern plots (54 m by 18 m, 100 m by 30 m) describe consolidated fields, not the old ones.
