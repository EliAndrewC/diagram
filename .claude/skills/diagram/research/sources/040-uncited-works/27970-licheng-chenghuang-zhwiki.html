zh.wikipedia 黎城城隍庙 ("the Licheng City God Temple", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/黎城城隍庙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the City God temple of Licheng county town in Changzhi, Shanxi, a nationally protected site, giving its building history, plan and dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the footprint of a county town's City God temple - 36.23 m east to west and 52.65 m north to south, 1,892 square meters, one courtyard with a gate and a main hall on the axis, flanked by side gates - and a founding in 1023-1031 with rebuilding in 1369. The standing gate is Ming and the hall Qing, so the plan is a Ming-Qing one and may not match the Song original, and the article is a short stub that describes only the surviving gate and hall, not the rest of a temple precinct.
