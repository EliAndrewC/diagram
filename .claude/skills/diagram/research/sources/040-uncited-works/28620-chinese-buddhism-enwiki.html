en.wikipedia "Chinese Buddhism" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Buddhism)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Han Chinese Buddhism, covering its history from the Han dynasty to the present, its doctrine, practices, deities, temples, monasticism, the thirteen traditions and the festival calendar.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes Mario Poceski that Chinese Buddhist temples are halls and courtyards arranged symmetrically on a north-south axis with the main hall central, and says monasteries ranged from city and country to deep-mountain ones, large ones holding thousands of monastics and the smallest standing in small villages, supported by rich elites in the case of the most prestigious. It gives no sizes or ground plans and no dates for the layout or for monastery sizes, and most of its text covers doctrine, schools and the twentieth century rather than the building or the village temple.
