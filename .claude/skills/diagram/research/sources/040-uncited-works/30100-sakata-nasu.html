sakata-nasu

Sakata Seed Corporation, "ナスの育て方・栽培方法" ("Nasu no sodatekata, saibai hoho", how to grow eggplant, title translated), in the Engei Tsushin gardening site, "Shippai shinai saibai lesson (yasai)" (updated 2025-05-13) (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/lesson-vegetable/detail_2)

What it is: A seed company's home-garden growing guide for eggplant, giving its spacing (about 50 cm), ridge width (about 70 cm), seedling raising for 60-80 days, pruning to three stems, and feeding every three weeks.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how far apart eggplants stand, that they are a hot-season crop needing much water and sun, and that seedlings take 60-80 days to reach planting size. Its heated seedling boxes, black plastic mulch and chemical fertilizer are modern methods, so its schedule is for today's cultivation and not for the Edo period.
