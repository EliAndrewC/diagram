suiron-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 水論 ("suiron", the water dispute, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E8%AB%96)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the suiron, a dispute over dividing irrigation water among fields or villages, with its causes, how it was settled and a note on water disputes outside Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists what villages quarreled over - a weir's or sluice's form, dredging, the fairness of a division works, turns at taking water, moving an intake upstream, and new paddies upsetting the balance - and says disputes were violent in the medieval period but by the Edo period went to lawsuits and mediation. It says shares of cost often followed assessed yield, and that later arrivals ranked lower. It is general and cites two books, with no case worked in detail.
