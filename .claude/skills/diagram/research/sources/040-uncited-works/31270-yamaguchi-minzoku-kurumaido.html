yamaguchi-minzoku-kurumaido

山口民俗資料 (Yamaguchi folk materials), "車井戸" ("kuruma-ido", the pulley well, title translated), exhibit no. 26, on the Yamaguchi folk museum site (in Japanese; https://yamagutiminzoku.sakura.ne.jp/3zyuuseikatu/bunnruizyuuseikatu/zyuuseikatu26.html)

What it is: A museum exhibit entry for the pulley well, in the museum's housing-and-living category under well tools, describing how water was drawn and noting the shallow groundwater of the Yamaguchi district.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the pulley well: a small bucket (tsurube) on a thin rope run over a pulley on a frame above the well, and as other forms the sweep well with a weight on one end of a bamboo pole and a long bamboo pole with the bucket fixed directly on it. It adds that the Yamaguchi district's groundwater is generally shallow, so wells were not dug deep. The entry gives no date for the objects and does not name the prefecture, and it says nothing of how many households shared a well or where wells stood.
