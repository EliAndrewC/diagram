kotobank-tokaido-shukuson-taigaicho

"東海道宿村大概帳" ("Tōkaidō shuku-son taigai-chō", the summary register of the Tokaido's post stations and villages, title translated), in the Heibonsha Historical Place-Name Encyclopedia of Japan (日本歴史地名大系), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9D%B1%E6%B5%B7%E9%81%93%E5%AE%BF%E6%9D%91%E5%A4%A7%E6%A6%82%E5%B8%B3-3064454)

What it is: A Kotobank page giving the Historical Place-Name Encyclopedia's description of the Tokaido shukuson taigaicho, a 31-volume manuscript compiled by the shogunate's road magistrate in the late Edo period and held by the Communications Museum.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists what the register records for each post station: assessed yield, townscape, population and households, the size and number of honjin and waki-honjin, inn counts, porter and horse fees, plus roadside trees, milestone mounds, bridges, ditches and rest stops on the road to the next station and the livelihoods of villages along it. It is only a description of the register's contents, giving none of its figures, and Kotobank shows just part of the entry (the rest is behind a paid service).
