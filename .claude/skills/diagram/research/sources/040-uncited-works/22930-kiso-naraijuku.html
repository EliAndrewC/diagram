kiso-naraijuku

Kiso Wide-area Tourism Association, "奈良井宿" ("Narai post town", title translated), on Kisodani Trip (in Japanese; https://kiso-nagano.ne.jp/naraijuku/)

What it is: A regional tourism page on Narai post town on the Nakasendo in Nagano, giving its distances, its length, its founding and its present preservation district.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the town's length as 8 cho 5 ken, about twice Niekawa's, a post function by the mid-16th century (a quoted document has the Kiso lord setting post stations in 1533) and a formal post station in 1601, with the honjin and the toiya (wholesale house) still standing. It gives nothing about the street's width or the house plots, and its picture is of a preserved district that missed the Meiji road rebuilding.
