en.wikipedia "Ancestor veneration in China" (page as last edited 15 September 2026) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancestor_veneration_in_China)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Chinese ancestor veneration: its definition as the worship of deified ancestors in lineage shrines, its history, and its practices of mourning, funeral rites, burial and household altars.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says ancestor veneration was strongest in south China where lineages were strong and communal deities were prominent in the north, that lineage organizations were the most powerful local body before 1949, and that burial was delayed by rank (seven months for a king, five for magnates, three for other officers, one for commoners). It is a general summary with no place or date for most of its practices, its mourning and household-altar passages carry "citation needed" tags, and it covers China as a whole, not a county or a village.
