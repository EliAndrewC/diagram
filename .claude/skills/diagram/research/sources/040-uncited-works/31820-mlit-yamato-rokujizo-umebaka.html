mlit-yamato-rokujizo-umebaka

Yamato River Office, Kinki Regional Development Bureau, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (国土交通省近畿地方整備局 大和川河川事務所), "六地蔵(埋墓)" ("The six Jizo (burial grave)", title translated), in the Yamato River walking guide (大和川散策ガイド) (in Japanese; https://www.kkr.mlit.go.jp/yamato/river/past/guidemap/other/other_02.html)

What it is: A short river-office walking-guide entry on the six Jizo statues beneath a large hackberry on the Saho River bank at Hieda in Yamatokoriyama, Nara, beside the village's burial grave.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a burial grave (umebaka) is the ground where the body is buried, with no gravestone but a small mound with a wooden stupa, and that the grave visited on ordinary days is a separate visiting grave (mairibaka). It names one village on one river bank, and gives no date, no size and no word on how common the double grave was, so it shows the form where it survives and not its range.
