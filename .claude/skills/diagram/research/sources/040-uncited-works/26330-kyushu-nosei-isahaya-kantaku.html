kyushu-nosei-isahaya-kantaku

Kyushu Regional Agricultural Administration Office, MAFF, "干拓の歴史" ("The history of reclamation", title translated), in its Isahaya Bay reclamation project pages (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/kyusyu/seibibu/isahaya/rekishi.html)

What it is: A project page of the Kyushu agricultural bureau on the history of tidal-flat reclamation in the Ariake Sea and Isahaya Bay in Nagasaki and Saga.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says reclaimed fields have been added to the Ariake coast since before the Edo period, in embanked plots lined up toward the sea like scales, for over 260 square km in all; that the oldest is said to date to the Suiko reign (593-629); that mud builds up as much as 5 cm a year; and that Isahaya Bay's reclamation began about 450 years ago and made a plain of about 3,500 ha. The 260 square km counts every period up to the present with no split for the Edo period, and the page is the bureau's own account of a project it ran, so it says little of how the early embanked fields were laid out.
