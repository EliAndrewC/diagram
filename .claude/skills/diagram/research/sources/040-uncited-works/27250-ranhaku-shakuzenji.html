ranhaku-shakuzenji

Ranzan Town (嵐山町), "天台宗 積善寺 七郷村大字杉山" ("Tendai sect, Shakuzen-ji, Sugiyama in Shichigo Village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register (比企郡寺院明細帳), in the Ranzan Town Web Museum, vol. 6 (嵐山町web博物誌), on the Ranzan town site (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter08.html)

What it is: A town-hosted transcription of one entry of the Meiji-era temple register for Hiki County, Musashi (now in Saitama): a Tendai temple in Sugiyama village, with the town's footnotes. It gives the temple's founding account, hall and kitchen-building sizes, precinct area, land holdings and parishioner count.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts hard numbers on one small village temple: a main hall of 7 ken 3 shaku by 6 ken, a priest's residence (kuri) of 2 ken 3 shaku by 3 ken 3 shaku, a precinct of 287 tsubo registered as state land of the fourth class, 39 parishioner households, and a list of paddy, field, forest and waste plots outside the precinct with their land-tax values. The founding (1525, by the monk Yugen) is the register's own account and the page offers no other evidence; the sizes and tax values are those of the Meiji land system, not of an earlier century, and the page covers this one temple only.
