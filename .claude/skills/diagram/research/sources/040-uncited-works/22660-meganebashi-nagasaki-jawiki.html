ja.wikipedia 眼鏡橋 (長崎市) ("Meganebashi, Nagasaki", the Spectacles Bridge, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/眼鏡橋_(長崎市))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the two-arch stone bridge over the Nakashima River in Nagasaki, built in 1634 by the Chinese monk Mokusu Nyojo, with its dimensions, flood damage and repairs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a surviving seventeenth-century stone bridge's size (22 m long, 3.65 m wide, 5.46 m above the river), says it was built to replace bridges swept away by the river's floods, was repaired after a flood in 1648, and had its steps removed about 1873 for rickshaws. Much of the article is the 1982 flood and the bypass channels and house removals that followed, which are twentieth-century work, and the bridge as it stands was half destroyed in 1982 and rebuilt with its recovered stones, its end steps restored from traces then found. The article calls it Japan's first stone arch bridge (Ryukyu's Tennyobashi aside), built with masons brought from China, and the only two-arch bridge on its river, so its form and size do not stand for an ordinary village bridge.
