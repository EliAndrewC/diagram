matsushiro-bunbu-gakko-adeac

Nagano City history, "二 文武学校" ("2 the Bunbu school", title translated), in 第一一章 "Warrior and commoner life and culture", section 5 "Terakoya and the Bunbu school", on the Adeac digital archive (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/nagano-city/texthtml/d100040/ct00000004/ht001850)

What it is: A section of the Nagano city history on the domain school of Matsushiro, opened provisionally in 1855, with its size, its plan and the long line of lecture-hall and teaching arrangements that led to it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the school's floor area as just over 460 tsubo (about 1,518 square meters), and says that its plan has no Confucian shrine or seat, which the domain schools of Ueda, Takato and Takashima had, so it supports both the scale of a mid-size domain school and that a shrine to Confucius was not universal. It is a late school (preparations from 1851, opened 1855), and the page calls it a typical comprehensive military-and-letters school, so it does not speak for older or smaller schools.
