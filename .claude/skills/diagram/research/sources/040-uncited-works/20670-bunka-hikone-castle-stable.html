bunka-hikone-castle-stable

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "彦根城馬屋" (the Hikone Castle stable, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/145098)

What it is: A database entry in Japan's national register of cultural properties for the stable inside the Sawaguchi gate of Hikone Castle's second bailey (Shiga), dated about 1688-1703, with its two wings' dimensions (25.2 m and 31.1 m long, 5.9 m deep, single-storied) and a well house attached.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and form of a surviving castle stable and states that it holds nineteen horses and that no other surviving example exists, so it shows how a domain lord's stable was housed and where it stood against the gate. It is one castle's stable of the middle Edo period, called unique by the entry itself, so it is the upper end of a stable, not a typical one, and the entry says nothing about stalls or fittings beyond the horse count.
