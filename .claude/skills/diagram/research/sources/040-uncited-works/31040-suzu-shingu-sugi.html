suzu-shingu-sugi

Suzu City (珠洲市), "78. 新宮神社のスギ" ("No. 78: the cedar of Shingu Shrine", title translated), entry in its list of designated cultural properties, p. 114 (in Japanese; https://city.suzu.lg.jp/uploaded/attachment/2772.pdf)

What it is: A one-page cultural-property entry for a designated natural monument, the sacred cedar in the precinct of Shingu Shrine in Wakayama-machi, Suzu, Ishikawa. It gives the designation date (5 June 1973), the height of 31.0 m, the trunk girth of 6.54 m at breast height, and describes the crown.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies measured dimensions of a shrine's sacred cedar on the Noto coast, and says that the tree stands in the middle of the precinct, that locals revere it as the great cedar, that its branches spread broom-like from about 5 m up (a branching the page calls peculiar, and it says whether the tree is the rare muresugi variety is undecided, so the crown is not a typical cedar's), and that it ranks 18th among the prefecture's big cedars in a 1982 survey. The measurements are present-day, the page gives no age for the tree, and it says the dying lower branches are being crowded out by the surrounding trees.
