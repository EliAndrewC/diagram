zh.wikipedia 无锡城墙 (Wuxi chengqiang, the city wall of Wuxi, title translated), simplified-character edition (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%97%A0%E9%94%A1%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the city wall of Wuxi in Jiangsu, giving its building and repair history from the Western Han until its demolition in 1950-1951.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a dated sequence for one Jiangnan county city's wall: an inner and an outer city in the Han, the inner city rebuilt in 1022, brick facing begun in 1357, and a 1554 repair with a stone-strip base and large brick facing. It gives no length, height or gate count, and the page itself carries a notice asking for more sources, so it supports the sequence of rebuilds only.
