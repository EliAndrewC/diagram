en.wikipedia "Mu (land)" (the Chinese unit of land area; reached from "Mu (unit)") (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_(unit))

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the mu, the traditional Chinese unit of land area, with its values on the mainland, in Macau, Hong Kong and Taiwan and its history from the Han dynasty.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Han mu as 240 square bu, about 461 square meters by the article's own working, against the older 100 bu mu, and the Qing-based mu of 1915 as 614.4 square meters, which lets a Chinese field size be converted. The 666.67 square meter mu is the modern market unit of 1930 and 1959, and the Han figure is the article's calculation from a third-century quotation, so each period's mu needs its own value.
