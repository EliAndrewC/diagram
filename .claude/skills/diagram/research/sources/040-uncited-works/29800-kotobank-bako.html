kotobank-bako

"馬耕" ("bako", plowing with a horse, title translated), in the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the older Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/馬耕)

What it is: A Kotobank dictionary page defining bako, plowing fields with a horse-drawn plow, with a Meiji gazette quotation and an excerpt from the older Heibonsha encyclopedia's horse article.

Why it applies, and its limits: The excerpt says it takes about 3 days to plow 1 ha with a horse against 5 with an ox, and that a horse works 5 to 10 times as fast as a person. The oldest quoted use is an 1883 gazette that praises horse plowing over hand work, so the page does not show how common it was before Meiji, and the encyclopedia figures are undated.
