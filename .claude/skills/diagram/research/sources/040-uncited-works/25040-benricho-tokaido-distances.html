benricho-tokaido-distances

Minna no Chishiki Chotto Benricho, "『東海道五十三次』宿場毎の距離一覧 -「宿村大概帳」版" ("Tokaido gojusan tsugi shukuba goto no kyori ichiran, Shukuson taigaicho edition", list of the distances between the post stations of the Fifty-three Stations of the Tokaido, title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.benricho.org/map_53tsugi/kyori.html)

What it is: A hobbyist reference site's table of the distances between the 53 Tokaido post stations from Edo's Nihonbashi to Kyoto's Sanjo Bridge, in ri, cho and ken as the Edo shogunate's Shukuson taigaicho gives them, with metric conversions and a second column from the 1810 Ryoko yojinshu.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Edo-period road distances station by station, with the shogunate's total of 126 ri 6 cho 1 ken (495.6 km) against the 1810 travelers' guide's 125 ri 26 cho (493.8 km), and says the Shukuson taigaicho dates to about 1843. It is a distance table only: it records nothing of a station's size or layout, the conversions are the site's own (1 ri taken as 3.92727 km), and the page itself notes some distances differ from modern measurements.
