ja.wikipedia 武家屋敷通り (仙北市) ("Buke yashiki dori", the samurai-residence street of Semboku, reached from the redirect "角館武家屋敷"; title translated), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/角館武家屋敷)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the street of former samurai residences in the Kakunodate district of Semboku, Akita, with its history from the 1590 castle and the 1620 relocated town, its preserved 6.9-hectare district, the houses open to visitors, and nearby sights.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the castle town was moved in 1620 to the south of Kogai-yama after river floods, laid out as a long north-south town with the samurai quarter (the inner town) to the north, and that a crank-shaped bend (masugata) at mid-street applies castle-building practice to the town plan. It records that mid- and lower-ranking samurai lived here behind black board fences and under thatch, but gives no lot sizes or plan, and what stands today is a preserved district, its famous weeping cherries brought later from Kyoto.
