chiyoda-kita-machibugyosho

Chiyoda City Tourism Association, "北町奉行所跡" ("Site of the Kita (North) Town Magistrate's Office", title translated), spot guide on the Visit Chiyoda site (in Japanese; https://visit-chiyoda.tokyo/app/spot/detail/639)

What it is: A short spot guide to the site of Edo's North Town Magistrate's Office, near the Nihonbashi exit of Tokyo Station in Chiyoda, with its address. It says the office moved often and sat here from 1805, and that Toyama no Kin-san served as magistrate here for three years.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the North office was moved repeatedly before settling at this site in 1805 (Bunka 2), and that excavation found the office's water supply and wells, with a stretch of the Edo castle outer moat's stone wall nearby. It gives no plan, size or building list, and its one famous magistrate is a popular-fiction figure, so it supports only that a magistrate's office had its own water supply and wells, not its layout.
