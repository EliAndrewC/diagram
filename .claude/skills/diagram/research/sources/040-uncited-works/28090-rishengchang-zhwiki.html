zh.wikipedia 日昇昌 (Rishengchang, the Rishengchang draft bank, title translated), simplified-character edition (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%97%A5%E6%98%87%E6%98%8C)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Rishengchang, the Qing draft bank (piaohao) of Pingyao, Shanxi, covering its origin in a dye shop, its branches, its partners, its coded drafts and its closing in 1932.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows how a Qing merchant firm ran remittance across the empire: a dye shop on Pingyao's West Street (a 2,324 square meter site, a figure the article gives no reference for) turned to drafts in place of shipping silver, with branches in many cities, a settlement period cut from six years to four, and a cipher on drafts. Most of its dates fall in the nineteenth century (the firm closed in 1932), it says the founding year is uncertain (between 1819 and 1838), and it is about one firm's finance and not about how a town or shop was laid out.
