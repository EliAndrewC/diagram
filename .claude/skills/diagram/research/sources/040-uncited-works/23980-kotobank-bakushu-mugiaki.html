kotobank-bakushu-mugiaki

"麦秋" ("bakushu" or "mugiaki", the wheat autumn), in the Digital Daijisen, the Concise Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Jitsu and Heibonsha reference works, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%BA%A6%E7%A7%8B-139987)

What it is: A Kotobank page gathering dictionary entries on bakushu or mugiaki, the early-summer season when wheat and barley ripen and are cut, also an old name for the fourth lunar month and a summer season word in haiku, with a 1951 film of the same name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the word means the ripening and cutting of the wheat-family crop in early summer, around the fourth lunar month, and that the Jitsu entry quotes an ancient commentary that grains count their first growth as spring and their ripening as autumn. It gives the season of the cut but no yields, acreage or region, and part of the page concerns the film.
