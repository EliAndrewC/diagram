en.wikipedia "Headworks" (the civil engineering term, title as written) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headworks)

What it is: A stub on the English Wikipedia defining headworks as any structure at the head or diversion point of a waterway, smaller than a barrage, that diverts water from a river into a canal or from a canal into a smaller one.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the plain definition and the historical sense of the word, the 'head of the works' of an irrigation network, which names the structure at an intake. It rests on one 1896 irrigation manual, offers a Welsh canal as its only example, and says nothing of East Asian intakes, sizes or construction.
