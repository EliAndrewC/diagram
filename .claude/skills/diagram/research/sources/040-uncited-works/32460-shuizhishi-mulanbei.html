Water Resources Encyclopedia (水利百科), "木兰陂" ("Mulan Bei", the Mulan weir, title translated), on shuizhishi.cn (in Chinese; https://shuizhishi.cn/c/2020-04-09/521732.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry on the Mulan weir on the Mulan River at Putian, Fujian, a tide-blocking, fresh-water-storing irrigation work built in the Northern Song.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the work to 1067-1083 after two failed builds, and gives 35 zhang long, 2.5 zhang high with 32 sluices, irrigating about 10,000 qing and yielding 37,000 hu of army grain a year in the Southern Song, so it supports a premodern coastal weir with a tide gate and divided canals. The area figures for the Qing and for today are modern, and it describes one Fujian estuary scheme and gives no figures for a village-scale work.
