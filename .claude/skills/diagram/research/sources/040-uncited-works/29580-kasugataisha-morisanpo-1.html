kasugataisha-morisanpo-1

Kasuga Taisha, "春日の杜散歩 1 一之鳥居から二之鳥居へ" ("A walk in the Kasuga forest, 1: from the first torii to the second torii", title translated), in the shrine's guide to its grounds (境内のご案内), on the shrine's own site (in Japanese; https://kasugataisha.or.jp/guidance/morisanpo)

What it is: The Nara shrine's visitor guide to the first stretch of its approach: the straight road east from the first torii that was also used as a horse track, the tower sites, the Tobihino meadow where deer graze, and the stream and trees along the way.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the approach of a great shrine as a straight road that doubled as a horse track and ended at a bridge, a first torii with an iron ring where a sakaki branch was set up, and that the torii marks the line between the everyday world and the sacred place. It is a great Nara shrine of the Fujiwara nobles, not a village shrine, it gives no dimensions, and it is a present-day walking guide that mixes old history with modern items such as a bus stop, a camphor tree planted after 1908 and fenced-off borders.
