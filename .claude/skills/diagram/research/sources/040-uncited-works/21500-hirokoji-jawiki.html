hirokoji-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "広小路" ("hirokoji", the wide lane, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BA%83%E5%B0%8F%E8%B7%AF)

What it is: A Japanese Wikipedia disambiguation page for hirokoji, wide streets set out after the Edo period began, with a short opening definition and lists of streets, districts and stations bearing the name.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its opening says the hirokoji began as a kind of fire-break laid out by the shogunate after the 1657 Meireki fire at places such as Ueno and Ryogoku, followed in 1660 by Nagoya, and that a bank (hiyokedote) ran along it, which supports a wide street as a deliberate fire-break. It gives no width, and the page itself is flagged as lacking sources.
