ja.wikipedia 道頓堀 ("Dotonbori", a district and canal of Osaka, title translated), reached from the redirect 道頓堀川 ("Dotonbori-gawa", the Dotonbori canal) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/道頓堀川)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Dotonbori, the entertainment district of Osaka's Minami area, and on the Dotonbori canal (about 2.7 km long, 30 to 50 m wide), covering its dig from 1612 to 1615, the theater quarter on its south bank, its water quality and its population.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a dated example of a canal dug by a private contractor and a lord's order, with riverside blocks named for the farmers who developed them and a theater district moved to the south bank, which supports a canal-side entertainment quarter in a large premodern commercial city. Most of the page is about the modern district (neon signs, the 2019 population of 431, water-quality figures, riverwalk), and Osaka's scale is far beyond a provincial town.
