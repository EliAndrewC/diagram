chiba-pref-kitagata-kantaku

Chiba Prefecture, Kaiso Agricultural Office, "干潟干拓の歴史" ("History of the Kitagata reclamation", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.pref.chiba.lg.jp/ap-kaisou/kaisou/kibanseibi/higata.html)

What it is: A prefectural page on the drained lakebed of Kitagata in Chiba, the 5,100 ha of fields called "Kitagata 80,000 koku" reclaimed from a lake in 1670, with the new channel, the drainage ditches and the encircling combined ditch, the water shortages that followed, and the Taisho and 1935 irrigation works.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a case of a large lake drained in the 1670s in the Boso area: the area (about 5,100 ha), the date (1670), the new river cut by two undertakers, and the ring ditch and ponds, and it says that the new fields did not get enough water, with groundwater lowered for the higher paddies upstream. It is a short summary, with no yields or settlement sizes, and the later Tone River pumping and tunnel works it describes (from 1935) are modern.
