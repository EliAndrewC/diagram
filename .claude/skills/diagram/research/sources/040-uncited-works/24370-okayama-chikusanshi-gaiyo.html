okayama-chikusanshi-gaiyo

Okayama Prefecture Livestock History (岡山県畜産史), Part 1, Chapter 1, Section 3, "家畜およ畜産物の利用" ("the uses of livestock and livestock products", title translated), on the Okayama prefectural livestock library site (in Japanese; https://okayama.lin.gr.jp/tosyo/history/1-1-3.htm)

What it is: A chapter section of a prefectural history of livestock raising, put online, on how cattle and horses were used in Japan and Okayama up to the domain period: draft work, transport, manure, milk, meat, hides and ritual. It cites older works by name and gives dates and some figures, such as Bizen cattle and horse counts from 1649 to 1677.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies Okayama-specific evidence for the Edo period: cattle ploughing was widespread in western Japan and rare in the east, horses were the draft animal of dry-field country and cattle of paddy country, and a cattle-keeping village such as Sone had only 14 cattle among 113 households in 1840. It reaches back to the Nara period for milk, meat and hides, and its figures are quoted from other authors' works (Saito 1965, the Okayama City history) rather than the author's own counts, so a number needs its original checked.
