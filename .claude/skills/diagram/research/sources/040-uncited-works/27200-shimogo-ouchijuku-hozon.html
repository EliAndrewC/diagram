shimogo-ouchijuku-hozon

Shimogo Town Board of Education, "下郷町大内宿伝統的建造物群保存地区" ("Shimogo Town's Ouchi-juku preservation district for groups of traditional buildings", title translated), on the Shimogo town site, updated 2023-12-27 (in Japanese; https://www.town.shimogo.fukushima.jp/organization/kyouiku/4/150.html)

What it is: A town page on Ouchi-juku, a post station of the Aizu domain's Shimotsuke road in Fukushima selected as a preservation district in 1981, with its extent, plot pattern and house form and the work done to keep it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a half-post, half-farm station about 500 m long and 200 m wide with 44 main houses on both sides of the road, on nearly equal plots 6 to 7 ken wide and 30 to 33 ken deep, thatched with the gable end to the road and two parlors side by side facing it. It is a surviving village as it stands now, after reconstruction of the honjin, removal of utility poles and asphalt, and re-thatching, and it describes one mountain station.
