kotobank-uranagaya

"裏長屋" ("uranagaya", the back-alley row house, title translated), in the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (abridged edition) and the older Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/裏長屋)

What it is: A Kotobank dictionary page defining the uranagaya, a row house on a back street or alley, with quoted passages from the older Heibonsha encyclopedia on the Edo and Osaka rental houses of that name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the uranagaya in the townsman districts of Edo and Osaka, rented to craftsmen, peddlers and day laborers, and quotes the Edo block plan of 60 ken square with a 20 ken square common yard in the middle. It is about big-city lower-class rentals only and says nothing of a provincial town or a village.
