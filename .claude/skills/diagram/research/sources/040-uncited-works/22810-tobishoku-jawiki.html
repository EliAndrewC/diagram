tobishoku-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 鳶職 ("tobishoku", the high-work builders' trade, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/鳶職)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tobi trade, the construction workers of high work, covering its modern divisions and its history as the workers who raised frames, moved buildings, pulled down houses and fought fires in Edo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Edo firefighting was destructive, pulling down houses in the path of a fire, and that the town fire brigades were drawn mainly from the tobi, who also sang the timber-hauling songs at shrine and building rites. Most of the article describes the present-day trade and its qualifications, and it gives no figures for Edo crews or for how many tobi a ward kept.
