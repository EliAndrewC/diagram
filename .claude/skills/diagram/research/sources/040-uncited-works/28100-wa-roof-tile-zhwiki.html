wa-roof-tile-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 瓦 (wa, the roof tile, title translated), simplified-character edition (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%93%A6)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the roof tile, tracing finds in China from the Longshan period through the Shang and Zhou, noting its spread to Japan and Europe, and giving its uses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the tile in China from archaeological finds (Longshan sites, Erlitou, Zhengzhou Shang city, Sanxingdui), says tile was still rare until the early Western Zhou and common in tiled houses by the Spring and Autumn period, and gives one account that the tile reached Japan from Baekje in 588. It gives no size, weight, price or cost compared with thatch, and says nothing of who in later periods could afford tile, so it is evidence for when and where tile began, not for how common it was on a given building.
