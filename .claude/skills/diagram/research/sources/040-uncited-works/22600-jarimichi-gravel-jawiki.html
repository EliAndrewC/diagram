ja.wikipedia 砂利道 ("jarimichi", the gravel road, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/砂利道)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the gravel road, a road whose surface is mainly gravel, with its construction and its history in Japan and abroad.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in Japan until modern times the common road was earth trodden down by people and horses and that gravel was limited to main roads, with gravel found on a 7th-century Musashi road of the Tosando found at Kokubunji, Ieyasu's maxim to fill dips with gravel, and the five highways largely gravelled, and that shrine approaches are laid with gravel to purify them. Its 30-40 cm base and 10 cm wearing course are modern engineering practice, undated on the page, and not a premodern figure.
