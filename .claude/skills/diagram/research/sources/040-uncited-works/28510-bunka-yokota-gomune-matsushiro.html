bunka-yokota-gomune-matsushiro

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "旧横田家住宅 主屋 表門 隠居屋 土蔵2棟" (the former Yokota house: main house, front gate, retirement house and two storehouses, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/182500)

What it is: A database entry for the five designated buildings of the former Yokota house in Matsushiro, Nagano City, with the dates and sizes of each: the main house (1794, 17.2 m by 9.5 m, thatched), the gate-house (1842, 16.4 m by 3.7 m), the retirement house (1820, 7.7 m by 4.6 m, thatched) and two storehouses (late Edo).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the footprints and dates of each building of a compound of a house in a castle-town district, and shows a gate-house (nagayamon) as long as the main house. The note field of this page is empty, so it says nothing about the owner's rank, the compound's boundary or how the buildings stood in relation to one another; those come only from the Agency's other entries for the same house. It gives the main house as 17.2 m by 9.5 m, while the Agency's entry for the main house alone (bunka-yokota-jutaku-omoya) gives 16.2 m by 8.7 m with the same attachments listed, so the two entries disagree by about a meter each way.
