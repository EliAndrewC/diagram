ja.wikipedia "畝" ("une", a ridge in a field, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%95%9D)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the une, the raised strip of soil on which a dry-field crop is sown or planted, with its kinds by height, the work of making and tending it, and its figurative uses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a ridge is for (drainage, aeration, warmer soil), that low ridges are under 10 cm or 20 cm by different books and high ridges above that, and that high ridges suit wet clay or high-water-table fields. Its sources are modern gardening and farming-guide books of 2012 to 2022 and its examples are modern vegetables, so it carries no premodern ridge width or spacing.
