yamaron-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "山論" ("yamaron", the mountain dispute, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%B1%B1%E8%AB%96)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia stub on the yamaron, the disputes over mountain forest and wasteland that were frequent in the Edo period, with two named examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It explains why villages quarreled over hills: mountains gave timber and hunting income to villages short of arable land, the land survey converted mountain earnings into rice-value taxes, and shared-use (iriai) rights made boundary disputes common, usually settled by the shogunate or the lord with a ruling and a map. It gives no area, date beyond the Edo period or count of cases, and its two examples (Hagi and Tokuyama; Hirosaki and Morioka) are domain-level disputes, not village ones.
