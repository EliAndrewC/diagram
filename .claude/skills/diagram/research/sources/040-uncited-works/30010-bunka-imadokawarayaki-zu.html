bunka-imadokawarayaki-zu

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "今戸瓦焼図" ("Imado kawarayaki zu", picture of roof-tile firing at Imado, title translated), by Aodo Denzen, in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/377137)

What it is: A database record for an oil-on-silk painting by Aodo Denzen (1748-1822), held by the Kobe City Museum, with a short commentary saying Imado, on the west bank of the Sumida River, was known for its tile kilns and quoting the Edo meisho zue's description of tile-making there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places tile firing at Imado on the Sumida River in the late 1700s to early 1800s and quotes the Edo meisho zue, so it supports tile-making as an Edo-edge trade. The page is a short catalog note on one painting: it gives no kiln sizes or output, and what the picture shows is not described in the text.
