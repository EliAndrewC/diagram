Toyonaka City Library (豊中市立図書館), "丹波国篠山藩ほか、江戸幕府が指定した城々に有事の備荒米（城詰米）がたくわえられていたが、その制度や運用の仕組みについてくわしく調べたい。" ("Castles designated by the shogunate, such as Sasayama in Tanba, stored emergency famine rice (shirotsume-mai); I want to study the system and how it was run", title translated), reference case 6000008041, in the Reference Collaborative Database (レファレンス協同データベース) of the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?page=ref_view&id=1000107294)

What it is: A library reference answer, registered 2012, on the shirotsume-mai system by which the shogunate had rice stored in the castles of designated domains. It summarizes a chapter and three papers by Yanagiya Keiko and gives the passage on Sasayama, whose domain held 3,000 koku of this rice in a granary inside the castle.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes how castle rice was stored, restocked each year, shipped to Edo or Osaka in need, and paid for, with the domain bearing the cost of granaries and staff, so it shows that a castle held a shogunal rice granary beside the domain's own. It is a librarian's summary of other scholars' work, and the librarian could not tell whether the "rice granary" marked on the Sasayama plan was the one holding this rice.
