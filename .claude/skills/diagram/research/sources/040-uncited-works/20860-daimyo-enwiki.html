"Daimyo", English Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimyo)

What it is: An English Wikipedia article on the daimyo, the territorial lords of Japan from the Muromachi period to the Meiji Restoration, in sections on the shugo-daimyo, the Sengoku daimyo, the Edo period and the end of the daimyo in 1871.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Edo-period daimyo were those heading a han assessed at 10,000 koku or more, sorted by their tie to the Tokugawa into shinpan, fudai and tozama, and required to keep residences in Edo and alternate years there and in their domains (sankin-kotai). It also gives example assessments, such as 1,000,000 koku for Kaga. It is Japanese history, so the Rokugan daimyo are a different institution, and the article's own sections on the shugo-daimyo and the Edo period carry banners or tags for missing citations.
