Nanyang City Party Branch Secretary Academy (南阳市党支部书记学院), "内乡县衙" ("Neixiang xianya", the Neixiang county yamen, title translated), on its website (in Chinese; https://nydzbsjxy.com/productinfo/3060383.html)

What it is: A short page on the Neixiang county government compound in Henan, a surviving Chinese county office open as a museum. It gives the compound's size and says what the compound is held to be.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of a county yamen as a whole: over 47,000 square meters, 18 courtyards in sequence and more than 260 rooms, laid out on the model of the Forbidden City, and calls it the best preserved county-level office of the feudal era in China. The page gives no date for the buildings or any rebuilding, and its remaining text is a political quotation, so it supports the scale of one famously complete yamen, not a typical one.
