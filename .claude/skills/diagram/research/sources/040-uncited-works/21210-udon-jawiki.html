udon-jawiki

ja.wikipedia うどん ("udon", wheat noodles, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%81%86%E3%81%A9%E3%82%93)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on udon, covering its disputed origins, regional types, preparation, dishes, the east-west noodle divide, and consumption statistics by prefecture.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its history section lists competing origin theories and dates the word "udon" to late Nanboku-cho texts and says the modern form was in nationwide use by the early Edo period; its culture section says the old buckwheat-in-the-east, udon-in-the-west rule is not well founded and that wheat is grown by double cropping in Musashino and Gunma. Most of the article is modern (JAS labeling, 2014 household survey, 2005 wheat acreage, instant noodles), so only its history and culture sections speak to the premodern period, and the origin theories are left unresolved.
