hefang-yilan-qinwei-shu

Chang Jujing, "查理沁卫二河疏" ("Memorial on examining and managing the Qin and Wei rivers", title translated) and the memorial "河工大举疏" ("on the great river works", title translated), in the 河防一览 ("Survey of river defense", title translated; Pan Jixun's compilation of 1590), chapter 47 of the copy on the QQ reading site (in Chinese; https://m.iwrite.qq.com/read/1000785572/47)

What it is: The text of two late Ming memorials in a river-works compilation, set in Henan and the Wei and Qin rivers: whether to divert the Qin into the Wei for the grain canal, and then rules for the Yellow River dike works, covering stores, wages, guard huts and storage yards.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives working rules of the Yellow River dikes: a guard hut every 2 li on the dikes, each with two dike workers and an elder for every five huts, a larger three-room hut every 2 li at the works for brush bundles, and labor paid at 3.33 fen of silver per unit of a zhang square by five cun high, the old rate, which the memorial calls too low to live on in a year of dear rice and proposes raising to 4 fen for that year's workers only, the old rate to return in good harvest years. It is a Ming memorial written after 1585, the latest year the page names, it argues for official policy and not the ordinary village, and is a character-only web copy with no edition information.
