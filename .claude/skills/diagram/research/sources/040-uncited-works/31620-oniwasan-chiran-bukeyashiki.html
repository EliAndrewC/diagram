oniwasan-chiran-bukeyashiki

Ito Masatoshi, "知覧武家屋敷庭園" ("Chiran buke-yashiki teien", the Chiran samurai residence gardens, title translated), on Oniwasan, the garden magazine run by Karenfuji (in Japanese; https://oniwa.garden/chiran-samurai-residence-gardens)

What it is: A garden-magazine page on the samurai quarter of Chiran in Minamikyushu, Kagoshima, a nationally designated preservation district. It describes the quarter's history and its streetscape of clipped hedges, beside links to its seven designated gardens.

Why it applies, and its limits: It explains the Satsuma domain's outer-castle system, which settled samurai in more than 100 small castle-town-like villages called fumoto (113, by the page's count) instead of gathering them in the capital, and says Chiran's present street was shaped in the middle of the Edo period, with about 1 km of trimmed hedges. The page gives two competing guesses for where the hedge style came from (Kyoto or the Ryukyus) and cites no source, the writer visited in 2013, 2017 and 2022, and the outer-castle system was a Satsuma arrangement, not a general one.
