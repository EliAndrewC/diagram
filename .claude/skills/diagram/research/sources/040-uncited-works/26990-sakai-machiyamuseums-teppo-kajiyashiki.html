Sakai Machiya History Museums, "堺市指定有形文化財 鉄炮鍛冶屋敷" ("Sakai City designated tangible cultural property: the gunsmith's house", title translated), on the Sakai Machiya Museums site (in Japanese; https://www.sakai-machiyamuseums.com/teppoukaji/)

What it is: A visitor page for the Inoue Sekiemon house in Sakai, a gunsmith's dwelling, workshop and shop from the Edo period that the city has designated a cultural property. It describes the plot, its buildings and the family papers found in them, with the family's history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the layout of an Edo craftsman's compound: a plot running from one street to another, with the main house and a guest-room wing beside it and a tool store and a rice-bale store along the west side. It records that the house appears on a Sakai map of 1689 and that the family drew a rice stipend from the Kato lords of Ozu. It is one trade in one port city, and the page gives no dimensions for the plot or the buildings; it says the house is the only surviving gunsmith's workshop-residence in Japan, so it is a rare survivor, not a typical case.
