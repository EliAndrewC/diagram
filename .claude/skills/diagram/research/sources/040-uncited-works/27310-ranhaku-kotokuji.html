ranhaku-kotokuji

Ranzan Town (嵐山町), "時宗 向徳寺 菅谷村大字大蔵" ("Ji sect, Kotoku-ji, Okura in Sugaya Village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register (比企郡寺院明細帳), in the Ranzan Town Web Museum, vol. 6 (嵐山町web博物誌), on the Ranzan town site (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter17.html)

What it is: A town-hosted transcription of the Meiji-era Hiki County temple register entry for Kotoku-ji, a Ji-sect temple in Okura village, Musashi, with the town's footnotes. It lists the buildings, precinct, a long schedule of outlying plots and the parishioner count.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the landholding of a mid-sized village temple in unusual detail: dozens of separate paddy, field and forest plots, some of them in neighboring villages (Kamikaraco and Shogunzawa), set against a precinct of 1,207 tsubo of state land, a main hall of 9 ken by 7, a bell tower, a gate of the kabuki-mon type, and 250 parishioner households. It also shows the register being amended: a priest's residence rebuilt with permission in 1927, a notice revised in 1908, and precinct areas restated in 1948-49. The temple's origin is given as unknown, and the page covers one temple.
