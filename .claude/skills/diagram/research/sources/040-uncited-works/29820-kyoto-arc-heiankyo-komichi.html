kyoto-arc-heiankyo-komichi

Kobiyama Kazuyoshi (Kyoto City Archaeological Research Institute), "平安京の小径" (The alleys of Heian-kyo, title translated), Leaflet Kyoto no. 319 (August 2015) (in Japanese; https://kyoto-arc.or.jp/news/leaflet/319.pdf)

What it is: A four-page leaflet from Kyoto's municipal archaeology institute reporting excavated alleys (shoko) inside the blocks of Heian-kyo, with a site plan and a map of six dig locations.

Why it applies, and its limits: It reports paired ditches 0.9 to 2.3 m wide, 3.3 to 4.6 m apart, framing alleys of about 3.0 m and 3.3 to 4.6 m, set against the 15-shaku width the Engishiki prescribes, and an average alley width of about 2.3 m across the six sites. It finds alleys at many positions in a block and in numbers that differ from the law (two in one block where the law allows one), the main site's ditches holding mid-to-late ninth-century finds. It concerns the capital's blocks, not a village or a provincial town.
