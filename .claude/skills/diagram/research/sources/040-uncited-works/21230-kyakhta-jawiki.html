ja.wikipedia キャフタ ("Kyakhta", town on the Russia-Mongolia border, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%82%AD%E3%83%A3%E3%83%95%E3%82%BF)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia stub on Kyakhta, a town in Buryatia, Russia, founded as the Russian-Chinese border trading post after the Treaty of Kyakhta of 1727.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the trade there was Russian woolens, furs and cloth against Chinese tea, at first by barter because Russia banned the export of coin, and that Chinese merchants inspected goods in Russian merchants' warehouses; it says the post declined in the later 19th century. It is a stub of a few paragraphs resting on one cited book, and it describes a Russian border town of the 18th and 19th centuries, not a Chinese or Japanese settlement.
