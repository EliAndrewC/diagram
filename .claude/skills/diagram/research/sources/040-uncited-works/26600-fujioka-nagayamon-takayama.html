fujioka-nagayamon-takayama

Fujioka City Cultural Properties Protection Division (for the Japan Tourism Agency), "長屋門の建築の特徴" ("Special Architectural Characteristics of Nagayamon Gate", title translated), in the Regional Tourism Resources Multilingual Explanation Database, 2019 (in Japanese; https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/R1-00073.html)

What it is: A short signboard text, in Japanese and English, on the nagayamon gate of the Takayama family in Fujioka, Gunma. It describes the rooms on either side of the gate doors, the woods used and the metalwork.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the gate has a dirt-floored storeroom on one side, a gatekeeper's room on the other and a raised-floor storeroom beyond, and that the zelkova doors show the family's wealth because commoners were forbidden zelkova in the Edo period. It describes one family's gate in Gunma, gives no dimensions or building date, and its claim about the zelkova ban is the text's own, without a cited law.
