tokorozawa-mitomi-shinden

Tokorozawa City Urban Planning Division, "三富新田" ("The Three-Tomi New Fields", title translated), in the 2011 Tokotoko Landscape Award, on the Tokorozawa city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.tokorozawa.saitama.jp/kurashi/jutaku/keikan/mijikanakekan/tokotokokeikansyo/h23tokotokokeikansyou/toskei20190218151754504.html)

What it is: A city landscape-award page on the Mitomi new fields of Tokorozawa, Saitama, a settlement opened in the Genroku years (about 300 years before the page), in which each household's land was a long strip 40 ken (about 72 m) wide and 375 ken (about 682 m) deep. It says the original house plots, fields and woodlots are still preserved in the present landscape.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the dimensions of one household's holding in a planned upland new-field settlement, and says that house plot, field and coppice woods were laid out within it and survive. It is two sentences with no map, no order of the three uses along the strip and no area for each, and the strip's 40 by 375 ken is the page's own figure with no source named.
