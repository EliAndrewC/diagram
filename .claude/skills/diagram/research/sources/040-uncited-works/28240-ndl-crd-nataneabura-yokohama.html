Yokohama Central Library (横浜市中央図書館), "江戸時代に菜種からどのように油を精製していたか知りたい。" ("I want to know how oil was refined from rapeseed in the Edo period", title translated), reference case 横浜市中央2537, in the Reference Collaborative Database (レファレンス協同データベース) of the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000250527&page=ref_view)

What it is: A library reference answer, registered 2019, that points the asker to Okura Nagatsune's Seiyuroku (1836), which shows rapeseed oil pressing with drawings of the work and the tools. It lists editions of the text, books that summarize it, and related works such as Nagatsune's Yusairoku on growing the rape plant.

Why it applies, and its limits: It tells a reader which primary treatise describes Edo rapeseed pressing, that the treatise records a difference between Kanto and Kansai in how the seed was roasted, and where its editions and drawings can be read, including a free J-STAGE copy. The page describes no process itself, and it warns that the rapeseed-oil chapter of the 1912 Nihon shokubutsu yushi describes Meiji methods, not Edo ones.
