shinmeisya-shinpen-musashi-intro

Iizuka Mitsuru, head priest of Shinmeisha, introduction to his furigana edition of the Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko for the Hodogaya and Asahi wards, "神明社：新編武蔵風土記稿" ("Shinmeisha: the Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko", title translated), on the Shinmeisha site (in Japanese; https://www.shinmeisya.or.jp/rekisi/musasi02.html)

What it is: The introduction and editorial notes of a shrine priest's annotated PDF edition of the Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko, the Edo shogunate's gazetteer of Musashi province, for the parts covering Hodogaya and Asahi wards in Yokohama. The page itself carries the download links and the editor's notes, not the gazetteer's text.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the gazetteer, quoting a preface: begun in 1810 and finished in 1828, presented to the shogunate in 1830, compiled by about forty staff, in 265 volumes. It quotes another preface calling the work exact and detailed, especially on temple and shrine treasures and old documents. It shows where to find the gazetteer's text for Hodogaya and Asahi, but the page does not itself contain any village entry, so it supports what the gazetteer is and when it was made, not a village's form.
