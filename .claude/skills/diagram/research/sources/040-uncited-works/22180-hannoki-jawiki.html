hannoki-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "ハンノキ" (the Japanese alder, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ハンノキ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Japanese alder (Alnus japonica), a deciduous tree of wet lowlands and paddy bunds, covering its form, habitat, range from Hokkaido to Kyushu and its uses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the alder is planted on paddy bunds for rice-drying racks, and that alder and willow grow well in paddy country where other trees do not, so they were prized there for firewood, charcoal and woodwork; it adds that in Hokuriku the wood was used as cremation fuel and the leaves as green manure. The page gives no date or region for most of these uses (it says only "formerly" for the charcoal cutting), and the tree facts it states (height 4 to 20 m, a nitrogen-fixing root partner) are botany, not village practice.
