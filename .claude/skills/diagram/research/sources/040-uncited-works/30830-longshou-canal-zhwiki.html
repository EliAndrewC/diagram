zh.wikipedia 龍首渠 ("Longshou qu", the Dragon Head Canal, title translated; the address 龙首渠 redirects to it) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/龙首渠)

What it is: A very short Chinese Wikipedia article on two canals of one name: a Western Han irrigation canal on the Luo River in Shaanxi, and the Sui and Tang canal that brought Chan River water to Chang'an.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Han canal was dug in the Yuanshuo years under Zhuang Xiongpi, took ten years, ran from the Luo River at Chengcheng to the Luo at Dali, irrigated the lower east bank and was abandoned by the Tang, and that the Sui canal of 583 divided at Changle slope to circle the city's northeast. It gives no length, width, area irrigated or labor, and the two canals are only joined by their name, so it cannot size a ditch or a farm's water.
