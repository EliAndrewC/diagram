ja.wikipedia 稲荷神社 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/稲荷神社)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Inari shrines, the shrines of the rice and prosperity deity Inari: a short article on their torii and fox statues, their number, and a list of shrines by region.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says vermilion lacquer on shrine buildings and torii has come to symbolize Inari, which is why a vermilion shrine reads as an Inari one. It is a tertiary encyclopedia summary with no period or place attached to the color, and the sentence rests on Smyers, The Fox and the Jewel (1999), a study of present-day worship, so nothing on the page shows the association held before Meiji. Vermilion is not Inari's alone: Fushimi Inari Taisha's own FAQ says it was widely used on ancient palaces, shrines and temples, so it is only a partial marker (the article's stronger one is the pair of fox statues). It mentions small field and lane shrines only in its count, and gives no color for them.

Used for: vermilion as the mark of an Inari shrine (government)
