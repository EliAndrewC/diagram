ja.wikipedia 稲荷神社 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/稲荷神社)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Inari shrines, the shrines of the rice and prosperity deity Inari: their history, their spread and their buildings, torii among them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says vermilion lacquer on shrine buildings and torii has come to symbolize Inari, which is why a vermilion shrine reads as an Inari one. It is a tertiary encyclopedia summary with no period or place attached to the color, and it speaks of shrine buildings and torii, never of a small wayside shed.

Used for: vermilion as the mark of an Inari shrine (government)
