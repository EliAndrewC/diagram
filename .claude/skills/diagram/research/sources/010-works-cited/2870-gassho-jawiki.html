What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the gassho-zukuri farmhouse of the Sho river valleys: its steep thatched roof of 45 to 60 degrees, the king-post-free loft of two to four levels it yields, and the snow and the silkworms behind both.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a steep thatched roof yields a large loft, the loft these maps' farmhouses carry. A tertiary summary of one regional house type; its reasons for the pitch are snow and rain rather than thatch, and it does not call the loft dark.

Used for: the steep roof and the large loft it gives
