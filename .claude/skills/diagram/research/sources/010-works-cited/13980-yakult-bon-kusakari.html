"『お盆』前に草刈りをするって知ってた！？" ("Did you know the grass is cut before Bon?", title translated from the Japanese by this project), Mama Yell column, Yakult (2020, updated 2026) (https://yakult-lady.jp/mama-yell/4247)

What it is: A company's lifestyle column on Bon customs, among them cutting the grass round graves and houses before the festival.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that says the grass round the graves was cut before Bon, and that in some places it was cut so the spirits could pass - the clearing of a grave's surroundings as a seasonal act. It is a present-day popular column with no sources, describing regional custom as it survives, so it attests the practice and nothing of its extent or antiquity.

Used for: cutting the grass round graves before Bon (religion and death)
