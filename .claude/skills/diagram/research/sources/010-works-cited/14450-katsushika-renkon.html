katsushika-renkon

Katsushika ward, 葛飾区史 第5章 暮らしの移り変わり ("Katsushika ward history, chapter 5: how life changed", title translated from the Japanese by this project), section "Lotus root" (in Japanese; https://www.city.katsushika.lg.jp/history/history/5-2-5-305.html)

What it is: A section of the ward's official history on lotus growing in the low ground east of Tokyo, citing the 1872 Tokyo prefectural survey and a 1917 village record for when and how much lotus was grown.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates lotus in these villages from documents and gives one area figure. Its dates are all modern, after the Edo period this setting models. The 1872 survey lists no lotus in these villages, only by leaving it out. The 1917 village record gives 7 chō 6 tan (about 7.5 hectares) across four villages. The growing spread fast only after the 1923 earthquake, into the 1930s, as a crop for the Tokyo market. So it shows lotus coming late as a market crop on the edge of a modern city, not when lotus reached premodern villages. The 1917 figure gives no total paddy to set it against, so it is not a share.

Used for: lotus arriving late in the villages east of Tokyo (archetypes)
