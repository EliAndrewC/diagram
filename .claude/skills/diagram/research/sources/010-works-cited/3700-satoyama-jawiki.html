satoyama-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 里山 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/里山)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the satoyama, the managed woodland belt between village and mountain, its cutting cycle and its products; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that leaf litter and undergrowth were carried off as fertilizer and fodder on a 10 to 20 year cutting cycle, which is the worked floor this page draws; a general article with no stand densities.

Used for: the worked woodland floor (vegetation)
