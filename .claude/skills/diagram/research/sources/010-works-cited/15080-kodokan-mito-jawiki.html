ja.wikipedia 弘道館 ("Kodokan", the Mito domain school) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BC%98%E9%81%93%E9%A4%A8)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kodokan, the domain school Tokugawa Nariaki founded at Mito in 1841, with its history and the layout of its grounds.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lays out the school's martial wing - three halls by art and a bout ground in the south garden - the plan of a large domain's state martial institution. The Kodokan's grounds, about 105,000 square meters, were the largest of any domain school in the country, so it is the top of the range, not the typical case; its martial halls burned in 1868 and the article gives no sizes for them. It is an encyclopedia article, a tertiary summary: the paragraph laying out its wings ends on a citation of Suzuki Eiichi's short history of the Mito Kodokan, and it carries the arrangement of the martial wing, not any measure of it.

Used for: a large domain school's martial wing of three halls and a bout ground (buildings)
