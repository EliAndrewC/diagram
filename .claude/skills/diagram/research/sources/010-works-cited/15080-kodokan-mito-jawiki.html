ja.wikipedia 弘道館 ("Kodokan", the Mito domain school) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BC%98%E9%81%93%E9%A4%A8)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kodokan, the domain school Tokugawa Nariaki founded at Mito in 1841, with its history and the layout of its grounds.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lays out the school's martial wing - three halls by art and a bout ground in the south garden - the plan of a large domain's state martial institution. The Kodokan was the largest domain school of its day, so it is the top of the range, not the typical case; its martial halls burned in 1868 and the article describes them from the record, without their sizes.

Used for: a large domain school's martial wing of three halls and a bout ground (buildings)
