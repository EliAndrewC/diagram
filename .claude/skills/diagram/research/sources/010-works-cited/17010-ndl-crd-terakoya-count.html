ndl-crd-terakoya-count

National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, "江戸時代の寺子屋の数が知りたい。" ("I want to know the number of terakoya in the Edo period", title translated), answered by the Okayama Prefectural Library, 2019 (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000276571&page=ref_view)

What it is: A reference answer by the Okayama Prefectural Library summarizing six works: three books giving the national count of terakoya from the Meiji Ministry of Education survey (Nihon kyōikushi shiryō), with the regions that survey missed; one book that maps the counts by province in bands; and two works (one of them a 1970 reprint of the survey itself) cited only for the village schools (gōgaku), not for terakoya.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets the several published counts side by side with their books, which is what shows the figure is uncertain. Its limits are that it is a librarian's summary of secondary books, not the survey itself; that the survey was gathered after the fact in the Meiji era with many regions unreported, so every count is a floor; that its figures (10,287, 10,296, 15,560) count schools opened over four centuries (Bunmei to Keiō, or on into Meiji), most of them after 1800, not the number teaching at any one time; and that its regional and provincial breakdowns are only described, not given, so the page fixes no count for a single village or town.

Used for: the national count of terakoya and the survey's gaps (urban-features)
