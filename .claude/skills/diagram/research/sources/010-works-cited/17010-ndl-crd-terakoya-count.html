ndl-crd-terakoya-count

National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, "江戸時代の寺子屋の数が知りたい。" ("I want to know the number of terakoya in the Edo period", title translated from the Japanese by this project), answered by the Okayama Prefectural Library, 2019 (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000276571&page=ref_view)

What it is: A librarian's answer summarizing four books on the national count of terakoya from the Meiji survey, with the regions the survey missed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets the several published counts side by side with their books, which is what shows the figure is uncertain. Its limits are that it is a summary of secondary books, not the survey, and that it gives national counts only.

Used for: the national count of terakoya and the survey's gaps (urban-features)
