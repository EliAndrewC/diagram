takayama-onkura-heritage

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Japan Heritage portal, 高山陣屋（御蔵） (The Takayama Jin'ya: the rice storehouse), text by the Takayama city board of education (in Japanese; https://japan-heritage.bunka.go.jp/ja/culturalproperties/result/2060/)

What it is: The Japan Heritage portal's page on the rice storehouse of the Takayama Jin'ya, written by the city's cultural-properties office: where it was first built, when it was moved, the age of its frame and how it was built.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the one surviving grain store of an Edo intendancy, an earth-walled storehouse with sloping walls and ventilation gaps, which is the model a magistracy's granary is drawn from. It says nothing of the floor, and it is one building, first built for a castle and later moved.

Used for: the Takayama rice storehouse as an earth-walled grain storehouse with ventilation gaps, moved from the castle in 1695 (buildings)
