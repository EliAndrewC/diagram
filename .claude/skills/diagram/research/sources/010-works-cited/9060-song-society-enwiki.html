What it is: The English Wikipedia survey of Song society, which records that the curfew was abolished in 1063 and the marketplaces of Kaifeng were then open every hour, against the strict dusk curfew over the two official markets of Tang Chang'an, and that Kaifeng's houses stood along the streets rather than inside walled and gated wards.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the institution our record says had lapsed before the period these cities are modeled on, which is what disposes of the dusk-curfew explanation for lodging outside a gate. Its limits are that it is tertiary and that its own footnotes into the Song urban-history literature would be the better citation; and that a single date for a change that came unevenly across a large empire is a simplification.

Used for: the ward curfew as a Tang institution abolished in 1063, well before the period these cities are modeled on
