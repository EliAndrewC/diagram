What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the komainu, the guardian lion-dogs, including their history as commoners' stone donations from the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the stone guardian pair at ordinary shrines to Edo-period commoner donation. Its limits: tertiary, though a rated good article whose Edo-donation claim is footnoted to specialist works (Uesugi, Fujikura); its one count, about 59% of shrines nationally having a pair, is a present-day figure, not a premodern or village-level one, and it gives no size.

Used for: stone guardian dogs as Edo-period donations (religion-and-death.html).
