ishizue-waju

水土の礎 (the Japanese Society of Irrigation, Drainage and Rural Engineering's history site), 水から土地を守る、輪中 ("Guarding the land from water: the waju", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://suido-ishizue.jp/daichi/part2/03/06.html)

What it is: A short page in the society's history of land development in Japan on the ring-diked communities of the Nobi plain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that farmers long put their villages and fields on the natural levees and other slight rises of the plain, and that an intake cut through a ring levee so often caused a breach that ring-diked villages found it hard to draw river water and dug wells instead. It is a popular summary of one region.

Used for: villages on natural levees; wells rather than intakes inside a ring levee (water)
