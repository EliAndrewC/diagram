Yokkaichi City Museum, exhibition guide 3371, "The sweep bucket" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「跳ね釣瓶（つるべ）」) (in Japanese; http://guidance.city.yokkaichi.mie.jp/hakubutsukan/jp/3371.html)

What it is: An exhibition-guide entry of a municipal museum in Mie prefecture, beside its reconstruction of a village well, contrasting the ordinary well's rope and pulley with the sweep, a pole worked like a seesaw with a weight on the far end.

Why it applies, and its limits: A short museum text for visitors that names the pulley as the ordinary gear and the sweep as the other; it gives no period, place or dimension, and its "ordinary" is the museum's general statement rather than a count.

Used for: the pulley as the ordinary well gear and the sweep as the other (urban-features)
