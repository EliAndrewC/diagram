yokkaichi-hanetsurube

Yokkaichi City Museum, exhibition guide 3371, "The sweep bucket" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「跳ね釣瓶（つるべ）」) (in Japanese; http://guidance.city.yokkaichi.mie.jp/hakubutsukan/jp/3371.html)

What it is: An exhibition-guide entry of a municipal museum in Mie prefecture, beside a well in its full-scale reconstruction of historical Yokkaichi (the page does not say which scene; the gallery's list places its "well, field" exhibit in the medieval market scene, 四日の市), contrasting the ordinary well's rope and pulley with the sweep, a pole worked like a seesaw with a weight on the far end.

Why it applies, and its limits: A short museum text for visitors that names the pulley as the ordinary gear and the sweep as the other; it gives no period, place or dimension, and its "ordinary" is the museum's general statement rather than a count. That statement is about Japanese wells, written from today's point of view (the page notes that ordinary wells are now rarely seen). It says nothing of China, where deep wells were commonly worked by a windlass rather than a pulley, so it can carry "ordinary" only for the Japanese half of the setting.

Used for: the pulley as the ordinary well gear and the sweep as the other (urban-features)
