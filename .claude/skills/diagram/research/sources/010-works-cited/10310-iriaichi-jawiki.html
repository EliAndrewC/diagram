ja.wikipedia 入会地 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%85%A5%E4%BC%9A%E5%9C%B0)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on iriaichi, land a village held or used in common, with its regional names and its fate under the Meiji land laws.

Why it applies, and its limits: It divides the commons into the two kinds a map draws differently - forest taken for fuel, timber and leaf litter, and open grass and riverbank land cut for fodder and thatch. It is a tertiary encyclopedia article, citing one paper on place-names, and gives no sizes or locations.

Used for: the two kinds of commons, forest and grass land (vegetation)
