Chinese Wikipedia, "徐光啟" (Xu Guangqi; in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hant/%E5%BE%90%E5%85%89%E5%95%9F)

What it is: The encyclopedia article on Xu Guangqi, the late-Ming scholar and official who compiled the Nongzheng quanshu.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used only for where he came from - Shanghai county, in Songjiang prefecture - and for his sobriquet Xuanhu, which identifies him as the "Master Xuanhu" whose remarks the compendium quotes; it says nothing about the pond practice he advised.

Used for: Xu Guangqi's home in Shanghai and his sobriquet Xuanhu (archetypes)
