matsushiro-kankou-higuchi

Matsushiro tourism association, 「The former Higuchi residence」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧樋口家住宅」) (in Japanese; https://www.matsushiro-kankou.com/spot/spot-620/)

What it is: The Matsushiro (Nagano) tourism association's page on the former Higuchi residence, the preserved house of a Sanada-domain retainer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what stood on each side of a samurai residence's grounds: the wood, bamboo and garden to the south of the watercourse, and the main house, the storehouse, the rowhouse, the household shrine and the front gate to the north. Its limits are that it is one house of one domain's retainers, an inspector's household of upper rank, described as restored today on a page for visitors, with no plan, no date and no sizes; and that it places things by compass side of the watercourse, not front and back, with the storehouse on the same (north) side as the main house and the front gate, so that "behind" is our reading from the gate's side, not its word.

Used for: what stood behind a residence (buildings.html).
