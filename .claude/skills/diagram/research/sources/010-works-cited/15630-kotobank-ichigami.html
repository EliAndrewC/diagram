kotobank-ichigami

Kotobank, 市神 ("ichigami, the market god", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the entries of the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, the Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica) and others (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/市神)

What it is: The Kotobank page gathering Japanese reference works' entries on the market god, by the folklorists Kitami Toshio and Kamata Hisako among others.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the market god's shrine or stone at a village boundary, a landing, a bridge foot or a crossroads, and says a market is inferred from where one stands; it dates the market-god festival to the year's first market. It is folklore scholarship on what survives, not a survey of any one town, and it gives no size for the shrine or the stone.

Used for: where the market-god stone stands, and when its festival was held (towns)
