l7r-stable-horse-culture

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Stable Horse Culture" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the section "Stable Horse Culture": how many horses a domain keeps, what they cost the daimyo, and what becomes of the surplus.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: that a daimyo's surplus horses reach the farms as draft animals through the village headman is the setting's own rule, and these maps follow it. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; they do not say how many farms such horses reach, nor in which provinces.

Used for: the daimyo's surplus horses given out to farms as draft animals (homesteads)
