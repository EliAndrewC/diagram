nippon-com-terakoya

nippon.com, "寺子屋：驚くべき江戸時代の教育力" ("Terakoya: the remarkable educational power of the Edo period", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.nippon.com/ja/japan-topics/g01005/)

What it is: A feature article on the terakoya, with a worked example of one village school's curriculum in Kozuke, saying a school needed only a master and one room.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states plainly that the school was a room, not a building, which is the point the map turns on. Its limits are that it is a general-audience article, and that its "about as many as villages" is a saying it reports, not a count.
