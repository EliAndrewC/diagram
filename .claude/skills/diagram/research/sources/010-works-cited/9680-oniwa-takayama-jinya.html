oniwa-takayama-jinya

Oniwa-san, 高山陣屋庭園 (the garden of the Takayama Jin'ya), Ito Masatoshi (in Japanese; https://oniwa.garden/takayama-jinya/)

What it is: A page on a garden-visiting site by its author, from a visit in 2021, describing the garden of the Takayama Jin'ya, the shogunate's intendancy office for Hida: which rooms look onto it, the tea room and stepping stones, and the site pamphlet's dating of the garden's remaking.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable account found of how the garden of a surviving intendancy office sat against the intendant's own rooms - the kind of posting our compounds draw. Its limits are that it is a personal site, not the keeper's; that its dating is the author passing on the site pamphlet; and that, as the page itself says, most of the buildings standing today are late-twentieth-century reconstructions.

Used for: one garden seen from both the great hall and the intendant's living room, with a tea room and stepping stones from each (buildings)
