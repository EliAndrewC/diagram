吴钩, 1000年前的小区围墙是怎样被推倒的, 儒家网 (https://www.rujiazg.com/article/7581)

What it is: An essay of 2016-02-24 by Wu Gou, a published writer on Song history, on the Confucian-studies portal 儒家网, tracing how the walled wards of the Tang city broke down and the ward walls came down in the Song, with the officials who argued over it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Song end of the ward wall in a historian's narrative beside the encyclopedia summary. Its limits are that it is a popular essay whose author writes to a thesis about the openness of Song cities, and that it names the period texts it reads in its prose without footnotes, so it cannot be checked passage by passage.

Used for: the ward walls torn down by the Song
