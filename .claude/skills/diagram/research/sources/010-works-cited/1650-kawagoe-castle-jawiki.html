What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Kawagoe Castle: the lord's residence and the armaments office in the ninomaru until the fire of 1846, and the honmaru palace built in 1848 to replace them, which is the palace that survives.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a domain seat whose lord and offices sat in the second bailey, the arrangement this page uses for where a domain's council met. A tertiary summary used for that. The article does not say the domain's business was transacted in the second bailey, only that the lord's residence and the armaments office stood there; a 170,000-koku domain is a large one, and the arrangement is the one a fire ended in 1846.

Used for: Kawagoe's residence and armaments office in the ninomaru
