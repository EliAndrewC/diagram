西安城墙档案 ("The Xi'an city wall file", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 华商网 (Huashang), republished on Sohu, 2025-02-14 (https://www.sohu.com/a/858946075_119659)

What it is: A Xi'an news site's fact sheet on the Ming wall of Xi'an - its dimensions, gates, barbicans, towers, parapets and moat - and the five lines of defense at its gates, from the moat and the drawbridge inward.

Why it applies, and its limits: It explains how the drawbridge worked and who worked it, and measures a barbican court. It is a news summary without references, of one of the largest walls in China, rebuilt in part in modern times, so its sizes are a ceiling for a provincial city, not a norm; some of its figures differ from the encyclopedia's (a moat 8 m deep where Wikipedia gives 10).

Used for: the drawbridge and the tower that raised it; the south barbican 70 by 50 m; the parapets on the wall's top (defenses)
