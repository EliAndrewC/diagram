genkan-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, 玄関 (Genkan) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%8E%84%E9%96%A2)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the genkan, the entrance of a Japanese building, from its origin as a Zen term and the entrance of a Zen temple's abbot's quarters to the entrance of a modern house.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the historical sense of the word: in the shoin style the room or building at a residence's formal entrance, and in the Edo period an entrance with a shikidai. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary, most of it about the modern house, and that the historical sentence rests on two architectural dictionaries it cites rather than on a study of Edo houses.

Used for: the genkan as the Edo-period entrance with a shikidai (buildings)
