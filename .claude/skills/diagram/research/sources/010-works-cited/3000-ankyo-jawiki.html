What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the culvert, the covered watercourse, and the covering of urban streams as cities grew.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that watercourses inside a growing city disappear from the surface under building, the reason no relic of a diverted stream's old bed is drawn through a city. A tertiary summary of a modern process.

Used for: urban watercourses vanishing under buildup
