en.wikipedia Fengshui woodland (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fengshui_woodland) - the concept ("A Fengshui woodland, Fungshui woodland, or Fengshui forest, known in Chinese as 風水林"), regional totals and the 57-village survey mentioned;

What it is: The English Wikipedia article "Fengshui woodland", a short encyclopedia entry on the patches of woodland kept behind and around villages in southern China and Hong Kong for geomantic reasons, giving the Chinese term 風水林 and some regional totals.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one thing only - that the village grove is a recognized, named class of woodland rather than a decoration we invented. It is a tertiary summary whose figures we take only where its own references support them, and it carries no count of groves per village and no grove area at all, so the sizes and numbers on our maps come from the survey papers and, where those run out, are marked as guesses.

Used for: the fengshui forest as a named village-woodland class (vegetation.html); not for its count or size.
