刀剣ワールド (Token World), 江戸時代の住宅（家）とは (https://www.touken-world.jp/tips/51221/)

What it is: An article on a commercial Japanese reference site about swords and castles on Edo-period housing by status, giving the band of hatamoto residence sizes graded by stipend.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size band of a hatamoto's residence, against which this page classes a compound. Its limit is real: a commercial hobby reference without citations, used for a band.

Used for: hatamoto residences of 500 to 2,300 tsubo by stipend
