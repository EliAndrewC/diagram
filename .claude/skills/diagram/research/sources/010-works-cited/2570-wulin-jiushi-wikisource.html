周密 (Zhou Mi), 武林舊事 (Wulin jiushi), 卷六 瓦子勾欄, Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A6%E6%9E%97%E8%88%8A%E4%BA%8B/%E5%8D%B7%E5%85%AD)

What it is: The late thirteenth-century memoir of Southern Song Hangzhou, whose sixth chapter lists the city's pleasure precincts (wazi) inside and outside the wall by name, twenty-three of them, and the goulan of the most flourishing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the primary list of a Song capital's pleasure precincts, the count this page sets against a provincial city's touring players. A primary text read from a crowd-transcribed wiki copy; it gives a goulan count for one precinct only, and the other Hangzhou memoir counts the precincts differently.

Used for: Hangzhou's twenty-three wazi and the North Wa's thirteen goulan
