南健斗・水田瑛人・米澤千夏・大風翼 (Minami Kento, Mizuta Eito, Yonezawa Chinatsu and Okaze Tsubasa), on the windbreak effect of homestead groves set in rows across the prevailing wind, 風工学研究論文集 28 (2024), J-STAGE PDF (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/windengresearch/28/0/28_J14/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A peer-reviewed wind-engineering paper on the windbreak effect of homestead groves set in rows across the prevailing wind in an Osaki settlement, with a survey of the groves' composition and heights.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the surveyed height band of a settlement's igune, 9 to 16 m with tall trees and 3 to 6 m without, the band this page's grove heights are drawn within. A modern engineering study of a present-day settlement; its groves are what survives, not the period's.

Used for: the surveyed height band of Osaki's igune
