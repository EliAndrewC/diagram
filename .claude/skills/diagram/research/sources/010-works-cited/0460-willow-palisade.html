Qing Willow Palisade statute (border embankment) (https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Willow%20Palisade/1480401)

What it is: The English rendering of the Baidu Baike encyclopedia entry on the Willow Palisade (柳条边), the Qing dynasty's willow-planted border embankment across Manchuria, including the statute that laid down how the willows along it were to be set.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a planting specification for a willow-planted earthwork in numbers - three cuttings at each five-chi interval, of stated girth and length - which is what fixes the spacing of willows along an embankment on our maps, as an analog: a Qing frontier barrier standing in for a field dike, which the record labels as such. Its limits are that it is a collaborative encyclopedia entry rather than the statute it repeats; that we read it through the site's machine-assisted English rendering, so the wording of a Qing regulation reaches us at two removes through a translation nobody edited; and that the analog is a frontier work, not a farm one.

Used for: willow whips every 5 chi (~1.67 m) in rows on an embankment. ANALOG only - no polder-dike-specific statute was found
