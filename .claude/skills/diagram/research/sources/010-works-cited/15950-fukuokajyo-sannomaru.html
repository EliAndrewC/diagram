Fukuoka Castle and Korokan official site (【公式】福岡城・鴻臚館), 福岡城内の足跡(表示石)を訪ねて（その１）～三の丸跡･侍屋敷跡～ ("Visiting the marker stones inside Fukuoka Castle, part 1: the third-bailey site and the samurai-estate site", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2022-01-05 (https://fukuokajyo.com/19252/)

What it is: A post on the official site of the Fukuoka castle ruins describing its third bailey: the wall that split it into an eastern and a western part, the great retainers' estates in the eastern part from the early Edo period to the end of the shogunate, the western part's size and its first use for middle and lower retainers' houses, and a later lord's residence built there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the castle's own keepers stating that great retainers lived INSIDE the castle's outer bailey, the form that makes the lineage compounds' siting a knob. It speaks of one very large castle of a great western domain, in a short visitor-facing post that cites no documents, and it notes that the estates' layout changed over time.

Used for: great retainers' estates in the eastern part of Fukuoka's third bailey through the Edo period (cities/capitals)
