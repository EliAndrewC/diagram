fukuoka-museum-ido

Fukuoka City Museum, leaflet No. 395, 井戸 - 遺跡編 ("Wells: the archaeological sites", title translated), for an exhibition of 2011 (in Japanese; https://museum.city.fukuoka.jp/archives/leaflet/395/index.html)

What it is: A museum leaflet tracing the wells excavated in Fukuoka from the Yayoi period to the Edo period by their linings: unlined pits, timber and bent-wood linings, stave-tub linings, stone linings from about the twelfth century, and tile rings in the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a museum's summary of excavated premodern wells in one city. It says that the curb above ground never survives in an excavated well, which is why archaeology cannot size the curb; it measures only the early pits, and speaks for Fukuoka alone.

Used for: that no excavated well keeps its curb (urban-features); that Japan's earliest wells were dug at lowland sites (water)
