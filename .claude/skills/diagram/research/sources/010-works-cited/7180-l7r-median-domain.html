l7r-median-domain

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "The Median Domain" and "Place Names" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the sections "The Median Domain" and "Place Names". They set out how many hamlets and villages a typical domain holds, how many inhabitants live at each tier, the size of a household, where a hamlet's headman lives, and how a village and its district come to share a name.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: where the historical record is silent, contradictory or simply messier than a map can show, the setting's own notes decide, and every settlement count and tier on these maps follows from them. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim.

Used for: the settlement-tier counts a median domain holds (~1,296 hamlets, ~216 villages, ~40% of inhabitants in a hamlet), five to a household, a hamlet's 50-100 for 10-20 households, that a hamlet's headman lives in the main village, and that a village and its district share a name.
