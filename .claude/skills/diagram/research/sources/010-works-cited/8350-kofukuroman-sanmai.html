kofukuroman-sanmai

福井県伝承浪漫, 県内各地サンマイ写真（火葬場・土葬地）【福井県の葬送Ⅳ】 (https://kofukuroman.com/sousou4-sanmai/)

What it is: A page of 福井県伝承浪漫, one enthusiast's folklore website about Fukui prefecture, on the sanmai - the burial and cremation ground of a Fukui hamlet - with field photographs, the local names, and the observation that the sanmai stands at the edge of the settlement while the visiting-grave is at the temple within it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is our readable witness that ordinary hamlets buried their own dead at their own fringe, which is what sets a burial ground at the edge of a settlement on our maps, and it describes the cremation site as a dug pit with no building, which is what keeps our drawn cremation ground bare. Its limits are considerable and worth naming: it is one person's compilation with photographs rather than a survey, written and published without editorial review, so nothing on it has been checked by anyone but its author; it gives no areas or distances at all; the grounds it photographs are twentieth-century survivals rather than sites dated to any premodern century, so they witness a custom that lasted into living memory rather than one we can date; and it speaks for one prefecture.

Used for: a worked instance of the two-grave system: the sanmai standing at the edge of the hamlet while the visiting graves stand at temples inside it
