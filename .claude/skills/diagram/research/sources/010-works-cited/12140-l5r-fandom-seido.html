L5R Wiki (Fandom), "Seidō" (https://l5r.fandom.com/wiki/Seid%C5%8D)

What it is: The fan wiki of the Legend of the Five Rings tabletop game, its page on the Rokugani shrine, summarizing the game's rulebooks and citing, for the passages used here, Emerald Empire: The Essential Guide to Rokugan, p. 143.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the game's own setting as its fans summarize it - that no village was without some manner of shrine, and that priests were the shrine's administrators and caretakers. It is a fan summary rather than the rulebook, it is the published game's Rokugan rather than the GM's, and the GM's own campaign notes govern where the two differ. Its line that no village was without a shrine cannot say how common shrines were in real Chinese or Japanese villages.

Used for: the published setting's village shrine and its keepers (Religion and the dead).
