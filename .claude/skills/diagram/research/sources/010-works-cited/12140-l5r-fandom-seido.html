L5R Wiki (Fandom), "Seidō" (https://l5r.fandom.com/wiki/Seid%C5%8D)

What it is: The fan wiki of the Legend of the Five Rings tabletop game, its page on the Rokugani shrine, summarizing the game's rulebooks and citing Emerald Empire: The Essential Guide to Rokugan (Fantasy Flight Games, 5th edition), p. 143.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the game's own setting as its fans summarize it - that no village was without some manner of shrine, and that priests were the shrine's administrators and caretakers. It is a fan summary rather than the rulebook, it is the published game's Rokugan rather than the GM's, and the GM's own campaign notes govern where the two differ.

Used for: the published setting's village shrine and its keepers (religion-and-death.html).
