Matsue Buke Yashiki (Shiomi Nawate), Buke yashiki ni tsuite (About the samurai residence), the residence's own account of the house and its households (https://www.matsue-bukeyashiki.jp/about/)

What it is: The about page of the preserved samurai residence on Matsue's Shiomi Nawate, run by the San-in Chuo Shimpo newspaper company, whose credit the page carries: the house's size in tsubo, its rebuilding after the fire of 1733, the division of its front and private halves, and the rank of the retainers who lived in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured size of a surviving domain-seat samurai residence and the stipend band of its households, the anchor this page checks its detached samurai house against. A custodian's visitor page rather than a survey report; its households were retainers of 500 to 1,000 koku, an upper case beside the poor samurai our terrace houses.

Used for: the residence's 67 tsubo and the 500-to-1,000-koku band of its households
