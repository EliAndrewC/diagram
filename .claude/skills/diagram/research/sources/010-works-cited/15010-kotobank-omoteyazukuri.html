kotobank-omoteyazukuri

Kotobank, 表屋造り ("front-building construction", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - the entry from Kodansha's Ie to Interia no Yogo ga Wakaru Jiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%A1%A8%E5%B1%8B%E9%80%A0%E3%82%8A-796071)

What it is: A two-sentence entry in a Kodansha dictionary of house and interior terms, defining the omoteya-zukuri, a townhouse form common in Kyoto with the shop and the dwelling built as separate buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the form a wealthier merchant's house took: the shop on the street, the dwelling behind, an inner garden between and a narrow entrance wing joining them. It is a dictionary definition, not a study: it gives no date, no size and no share of houses built this way, and it speaks of Kyoto, not of small provincial towns.

Used for: the merchant house built as shop in front, garden between and dwelling behind (buildings)
