iwasaki-kitakata

Iwasaki Electric, "Kitakata Kura no Sato" in the travel series of its lighting pages (in Japanese, title translated by this project; https://www.iwasaki.co.jp/lighting/eyeroom/eye/23_fukushima/02.html)

What it is: A lighting manufacturer's travel page, written in its mascot's voice, on the storehouse town of Kitakata in Fukushima and its museum of relocated storehouses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page found that gives storehouses as a ratio to households, the figure a town-wide count needs. It is a promotional page with no source, it reports the figure as hearsay, the count is a modern one, of a stock that grew after the town's great fire of 1880 (the page itself gives that fire as one reason for the storehouses, and shows brick ones among them), so it describes a Meiji-to-Showa town more than an Edo one, and Kitakata is famous precisely for having unusually many; another page counts about 4,000.

Used for: one storehouse to every four households, as a ceiling (cities/fabric.html)
