kanagawa-hatamoto-kaso

Kanagawa Prefectural Museum of Cultural History, 旗本の家 (A hatamoto's house), "this month's treasure", December 2024 (in Japanese; https://ch.kanagawa-museum.jp/monthly_choice/2024_12)

What it is: A curator's article on a house plan (kaso-zu, a plan drawn for a geomantic reading) of the Edo house of the hatamoto Okubo Kiemon, dated 1843 and held by the museum on deposit, describing the rooms, the names written in red on it, and a long-house added by the front gate afterwards.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a museum's reading of a real plan of a samurai house, and it names a yudono and a privy among what the plan draws. Its limits are that it is one house, in Edo, of a 350-koku hatamoto on a 650-tsubo site; that the article does not say where on the plan the yudono lies or whether it stood apart from the house; and that its suggestion that the long-house was added as the household's retainers grew is offered as a possibility.

Used for: the yudono and privy named on a hatamoto's house plan (buildings)
