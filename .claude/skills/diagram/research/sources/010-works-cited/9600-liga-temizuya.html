liga-temizuya

note.com (LIGA), an article on the history of the temizuya (https://note.com/liga/n/na05eabb89521)

What it is: A popular article on the purification basin's history: the rite is ancient, the roofed pavilion much newer, its earliest example the shogun's shrine at Nikko.

Why it applies, and its limits: It separates the old rite from the newer pavilion. Its limits: a popular article without sources, silent on whether small shrines had the pavilion or only a basin.

Used for: the basin's rite and the pavilion's later date (religion-and-death.html).
