yusuichi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 遊水地 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%81%8A%E6%B0%B4%E5%9C%B0)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the yusuichi, the retarding basin that holds a river's flood water for the sake of the ground downstream.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the flood basin as purposed wet ground, one of the bounded forms wetness took near a city on these maps. A tertiary summary used for the definition.

Used for: the flood basin as purposed wet ground
