changan-zhitu-jingqu

Li Haowen 李好文, 長安志圖 ("Maps for the Gazetteer of Chang'an", title translated), Siku Quanshu text on Chinese Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E9%95%B7%E5%AE%89%E5%BF%97%E5%9C%96_(%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC)/%E5%85%A8%E8%A6%BD)

What it is: A Yuan-dynasty work on the Chang'an region, its preface dated 1342, whose last chapter sets out the Jing canal irrigation of Shaanxi: its weir, its works and its water rules, citing an order of 1274.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a premodern rulebook for how a canal's field sluices were run: opened on a permit, run at the appointed time, shut when the watering was done, with a season of use and a season of repair. It is one great state canal in the dry north, watering wheat, hemp and millet rather than rice, so its months do not transfer to a paddy landscape; only the shape of the calendar does.

Used for: the permit, turn and season of a field sluice on a Yuan-dynasty canal (cities/capitals)
