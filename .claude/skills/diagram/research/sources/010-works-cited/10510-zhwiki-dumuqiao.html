zhwiki-dumuqiao

zh.wikipedia 独木桥 (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/独木桥)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the single-log bridge, a short tertiary entry defining it as a simple timber beam bridge of one trunk or one board, the earliest kind of bridge, for walkers and livestock crossing narrow water. It is written in Chinese but describes the form worldwide, with no Chinese example: its references are a 1915 United States national parks conference and a 2000 British bridge-engineering manual, and its pictures are from Papua New Guinea, the United States, Slovakia and France.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Chinese name and definition of the simplest crossing, though not Chinese evidence for it, since its sources are American and British. Its wording counts a single board as the same object as a single trunk, which matters for how our plank glyph is read, but that reading is the editor's: the 1915 source it cites speaks only of logs "in natural condition or hewn". It is a short encyclopedia article with no period, place or dimensions, and it speaks of narrow rivers and gorges rather than farm ditches.

Used for: that the single-log bridge, of one trunk or one board, carried walkers and livestock over narrow water (water)
