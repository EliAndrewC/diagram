zh.wikipedia 独木桥 (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/独木桥)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the single-log bridge, a short tertiary entry defining it as a simple timber beam bridge of one trunk or one board, the earliest kind of bridge, for walkers and livestock crossing narrow water.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Chinese side of the same simplest crossing, and it counts a single board as the same object as a single trunk, which matters for how our plank glyph is read. It is a short encyclopedia article with no period, place or dimensions, and it speaks of narrow rivers and gorges rather than farm ditches.

Used for: that the single-log bridge, of one trunk or one board, carried walkers and livestock over narrow water (water)
