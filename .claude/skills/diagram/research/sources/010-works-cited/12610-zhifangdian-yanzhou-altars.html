zhifangdian-yanzhou-altars

Gujin Tushu Jicheng (欽定古今圖書集成), 方輿彙編, 職方典, volume 232, "Yanzhou prefecture: shrines and temples, part 2" (in Chinese; Wikisource transcription; https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/欽定古今圖書集成/方輿彙編/職方典/第0232卷)

What it is: A volume of the imperial encyclopedia compiled in the early eighteenth century, gathering from the prefecture's and counties' own gazetteers the altars, shrines and temples of each county of Yanzhou prefecture, Shandong, with where each stood and, often, when and by whom it was built.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a primary gazetteer compilation for real Ming-Qing county seats, giving the state altars' and the City God temple's places against the wall, the gates and the yamen. It covers one prefecture of northern China; it gives places, not sizes; and the counties do not all follow one pattern - some put the altar of soil and grain north or southwest of the seat rather than west.

Used for: where a county seat's state altars and City God temple stood (religion-and-death)
