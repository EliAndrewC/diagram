asakusa-danzaemon-1000ya

Matsuoka Seigow, "1284夜 『浅草弾左衛門』 塩見鮮一郎" ("Night 1284: Shiomi Sen'ichiro, Asakusa Danzaemon", title translated from the Japanese by this project), Senya Sensatsu (in Japanese; https://1000ya.isis.ne.jp/1284.html)

What it is: A long essay-review in a well-known Japanese book-review series, on Shiomi Sen'ichiro's book about Danzaemon, the head of the Edo outcastes, summarizing the book with its figure captions: his enclosure at Asakusa, its houses, its shrine and bathhouse, and how his residence was moved outward.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that gives the house count of the Asakusa enclosure (232 houses in 1800) and names its Hakusan shrine. It is a reviewer's summary of a popular history, not the book or a primary record, it describes the seat of the Kanto head, which was far larger than any town's quarter, and its remark that Danzaemon spread the Hakusan shrines is the book's view as the reviewer reports it.

Used for: the Asakusa enclosure's house count, its lot, pond, shrine and bathhouse, and the Edo head's move outward (urban-features)
