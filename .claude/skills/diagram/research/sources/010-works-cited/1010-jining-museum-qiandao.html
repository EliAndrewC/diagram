济宁博物馆 (Jining Museum), 浙东运河古纤道 - - 秋水长堤 通古鉴今 (https://www.jiningmuseum.com/list/article_details.do?articleId=1932)

What it is: A feature article of 2018-11-09 from the Jining Museum, a municipal museum on the Grand Canal in Shandong, on the ancient towpath of the Eastern Zhejiang Canal at Shaoxing: its Tang origin under the commissioner Meng Jian in 815, and its two forms, along the bank and out in the water.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the towpath and describes its two forms from a canal museum's own account, which is what our river capital's hauling path is drawn from. Its limits are that it is a museum feature rather than the gazetteer it draws on, and that it gives no dimensions.

Used for: the 815 date and the towpath's two forms
