Himeji Dokkyo University, report of the lecture "Ancient state road: the Sanyodo and its stations" in the course Harima Studies II, 2014 (in Japanese; https://www.himeji-du.ac.jp/single/2014/11/2032/)

What it is: A university's report of a public lecture on the ancient Sanyodo highway in Harima province and its relay stations, summarizing the road's width and course, the stations' spacing and horses, and the excavated station at Koinumaru.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one excavated station's walled compound and its buildings, the footprint the setting's waystation is measured against. Its limits are that it is a lecture report, not the excavation report; that it spells the station's name differently from other sources; and that the Sanyodo was the grandest of the ancient roads, its stations built to receive foreign envoys.

Used for: one station every 16 km with 20 horses on the Sanyodo; the Koinumaru compound about 80 m a side behind a rammed-earth wall, with seven or more tile-roofed buildings (towns)
