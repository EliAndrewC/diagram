en.wikipedia "Environmental impact of irrigation" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_impact_of_irrigation)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the environmental effects of irrigation schemes: the deep percolation that recharges groundwater, the rising water table, waterlogging and salinity.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a scheme's percolation losses recharge the groundwater and waterlog land, which is the mechanism behind the wet ground below a ditch on these maps. A modern engineering summary standing in for a physical relation.

Used for: irrigation seepage and waterlogging
