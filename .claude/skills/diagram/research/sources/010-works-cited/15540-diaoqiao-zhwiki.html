What it is: A stub Chinese Wikipedia article on the drawbridge, a movable bridge over a moat, with one sentence on how a Ming drawbridge was built, cited to the Ming military encyclopedia Wubei zhi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a Ming drawbridge was made of and how it hung. It is a two-sentence stub flagged as needing sources, resting on one line of a 1621 treatise; it gives no size.

Used for: a Ming drawbridge of juniper or pagoda-tree wood hung by iron rings, chains and hemp rope (defenses)
