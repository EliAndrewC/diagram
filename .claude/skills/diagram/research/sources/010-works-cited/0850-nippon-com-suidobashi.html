nippon-com-suidobashi

Nippon.com, "Carp Over the Kanda River: 'Suidō Bridge and Surugadai'" (guide to Japan) (https://www.nippon.com/en/guide-to-japan/gu004006/)

What it is: A feature of 2018-05-01 on Nippon.com, the Nippon Communications Foundation's public-interest site, pairing Hiroshige's print of Suidō Bridge and Surugadai from his One Hundred Famous Views of Edo with the place today, and explaining the bridge's name from the wooden water pipe it once carried.

Why it applies, and its limits: It ties the name of a real Edo bridge to the aqueduct that crossed at it and confirms which waterway the print shows, which is what our flume-bridge glyph and its caption rest on. Its limit is that it is a popular feature that says the name is said to derive from the pipe, not a documentary history of the bridge.

Used for: the name Suidōbashi from the conduit the bridge carried, and the Kanda Jōsui in Hiroshige's print of it
