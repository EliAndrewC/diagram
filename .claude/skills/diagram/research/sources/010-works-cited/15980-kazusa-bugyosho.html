To KAZUSA (a local-history blog), 南町奉行所と大岡越前 ("The South Town Magistracy and Ooka Echizen", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kazusa.jpn.org/b/archives/2883)

What it is: A local-history blog post on the Edo South Town Magistracy and its best-known magistrate, Ooka Tadasuke, with the site and building sizes of the two Edo magistracies.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one readable figure found for the size of an Edo magistracy's ground, which is the anchor our Imperial Magistrate's compound is calibrated to. It is an enthusiast's post that hedges the figure with "it seems" and does not name the plan it comes from (a search summary attributes a 2,617-tsubo figure to a plan of Ooka's official residence, not read); the magistracies were Edo's, the largest of their kind.

Used for: the two Edo magistracies each on about 2,500 to 2,600 tsubo with a mansion of over 1,000 tsubo (cities/capitals)
