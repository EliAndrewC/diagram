jikata-chigyo-jawiki

ja.wikipedia, 地方知行 (jikata chigyō, a fief held as land) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/地方知行)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Edo-period form of stipend in which a retainer was granted villages and their yield rather than rice from the lord's granary, and on where such a retainer had to live.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says even the retainers who held land were obliged, except where duty required, to live in the castle town, with Sendai domain's retainers moving between the castle town and their fief as the named exception - the historical counterpart of the setting's samurai who hold rural land but keep a country estate beside it. The article cites no source at all and carries the encyclopedia's own notice saying so, and it gives no count of how many retainers held land or kept a house on it.

Used for: landholding retainers bound to live in the castle town, Sendai's alternation as the exception (fields)
