What it is: A travel and heritage feature on Nippon.com on Matsue, the castle town between Lake Shinji and Nakaumi whose waterways spread out from the castle moat through the town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places Matsue's channel network on its lakeside ground and names it as the castle moat's own spread. Its limit is that it is a travel feature.

Used for: Matsue's waterways spreading from the castle moat, between two lakes
