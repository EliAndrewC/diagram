"Kokudaka", en.wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kokudaka)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on kokudaka, the rice-denominated assessment by which Japanese domains, fiefs and stipends were rated.

Why it applies, and its limits: This one is cited for a caution rather than a fact: the article's own account of the assessment converting other crops and produce into their rice equivalent is what stops us from reading a domain's rice rating as evidence that its land was mostly wet paddy, which would put paddy on maps that should carry dry field. It is a tertiary summary, used here only for the definition of a fiscal convention, which is the kind of thing an encyclopedia states plainly, and for no measurement at all.

Used for: what it does NOT establish. Kokudaka's rice denomination is a FISCAL convention - "the value of other crops and produce converted to their equivalent value in terms of rice" - covering dry field as well as paddy, so it is not evidence of wet-rice land-use dominance.
