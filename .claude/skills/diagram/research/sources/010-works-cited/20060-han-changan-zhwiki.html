Chinese Wikipedia, 汉长安城 ("Han Chang'an", translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B1%89%E9%95%BF%E5%AE%89%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Western Han capital at Chang'an, whose wall was begun in the first year of Emperor Hui (194 BCE); it gives the wall's height and base and the moat outside it as found by excavation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives an excavated moat of a Chinese capital some fifteen centuries before the setting's model period, a premodern figure where the other great-city moats read are measured on surviving and restored moats. It disagrees with the English encyclopedia's figures for the same moat (8 m wide against 45 m) and cites no excavation report for its own, so it bounds the question rather than settling it; and an imperial capital's moat is not a provincial seat's.

Used for: the excavated moat of the Han capital (cities/defenses)
