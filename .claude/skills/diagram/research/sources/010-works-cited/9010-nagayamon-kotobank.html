nagayamon-kotobank

Kotobank, 長屋門 - 世界大百科事典, 日本大百科全書 (the entry signed 工藤圭章) and ブリタニカ国際大百科事典 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%95%B7%E5%B1%8B%E9%96%80)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the nagayamon, the gatehouse with a range of rooms to either side of the doorway, carrying three published encyclopedias: what the gate expressed, how its form was fixed by the house rank of the daimyo or bannerman who held it, which commoners were permitted one, and what the rooms flanking a commoner's version held.

Why it applies, and its limits: Our setting makes a merchant's walled and gated compound a granted right rather than a purchase, and this is the historical arrangement it is modeled on, stated by three independent works in the language of permission and tied to rank and to office. Its limits are that these are tertiary encyclopedia entries; that they describe Tokugawa Japan while the walls on our city maps take their form from the Chinese side of the setting's ancestry; and that they regulate the GATE and say nothing about a compound wall.

Used for: the gate whose form followed its holder's house rank, permitted outside the warrior class to village officials and town elders; the stables and sheds in a commoner gate range
