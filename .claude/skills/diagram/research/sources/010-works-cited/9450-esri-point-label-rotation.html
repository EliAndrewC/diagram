esri-point-label-rotation

Esri, ArcGIS Pro documentation, "Set label rotation using a numeric field" (https://pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/help/mapping/text/set-label-rotation-using-a-numeric-field.htm)

What it is: A help page of Esri's ArcGIS Pro on rotating a point's label by an angle stored with the feature, and on how that rotation overrides the ordinary placement positions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the documented practice behind turning a label to a rotated feature's own angle: when a point carries a direction, its label takes that angle and is placed by it. It is one engine's documentation; it does not say when a mapmaker should prefer this over horizontal text, which the GM's ruling decides for these maps.

Used for: a rotated feature's label taking the feature's angle (presentation)
