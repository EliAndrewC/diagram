satologue-yashikigami

satologue.com, 屋敷神様 (https://satologue.com/living/758) - 「In the small shrine (hokora) standing in a corner of the plot there dwells the homestead deity (yashikigami-sama).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「敷地の一角に佇む小さな祠には、屋敷神様がいます」)

What it is: A page on satologue.com, a present-day Japanese site that writes about country living and rural custom, describing the yashikigami, the deity of a farm household's own ground, and the small hokora shrine that stands in a corner of the plot to house it.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for two plain facts of a custom that is still visible on farms today and that we draw on every homestead: the shrine is tiny, and it sits at a corner of the grounds rather than at the center. It is a lifestyle site and not a survey or a historical document, so it is good for what the thing looks like and not for how common it was or how old the practice is, and because the page names no compass direction, which corner we put it in is our own choice.

Used for: the shrine's small size and its corner-of-the-grounds placement.
