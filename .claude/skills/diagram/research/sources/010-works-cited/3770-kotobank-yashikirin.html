kotobank-yashikirin

Digital Daijisen (Shogakukan) and Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho, s.v. 屋敷林, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/屋敷林)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the yashikirin, the grove planted round a homestead against wind and fire.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the grove as standing round the homestead itself, which is the proximity this page draws; dictionary lines with no distances.

Used for: the grove round the house (vegetation)
