江戸雑記録 (Edo miscellany), 町奉行役宅 (The town magistrate's official residence), a personal blog (in Japanese; http://edo.ashigaru.net/?eid=23)

What it is: A blog post describing the Edo North Town Magistracy from a plan of 1841 as both an office and a residence, and listing what stood in its quarter for lawsuits and examinations: the court, the examination rooms, the record sections' duty rooms, the litigants' waiting room, and the jail doshin's duty room with a temporary jail.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts the record-keeping staff's rooms and a temporary cell in the same quarter as a magistracy's court, which is how our office hall groups its clerks, court and cell. Its limits are that it is an unsigned personal blog without references, that it describes the capital's town magistracy rather than a country office, and that it gives no sizes.

Used for: the record sections' duty rooms and the temporary cell in the magistracy's court quarter (buildings)
