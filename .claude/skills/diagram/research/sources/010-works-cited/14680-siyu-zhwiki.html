siyu-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 司獄 ("jail warden", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/司獄)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the siyu, the Ming and Qing officer in charge of a jail, and the jail office he headed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says every prefecture, department and county kept a jail, and that from the prefecture up the jail had its own office - the tier at which a jail becomes an institution with a staff. It is a stub article, and says nothing of the jail's size or place.

Used for: that a regional seat's jail had an office of its own (cities/government)
