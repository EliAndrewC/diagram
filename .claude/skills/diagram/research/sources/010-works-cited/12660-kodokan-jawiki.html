ja.wikipedia 弘道館 ("the Kodokan", the Mito domain school, title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/弘道館)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kodokan, the domain school of Mito founded by its ninth lord inside the castle's third bailey, with its site area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the upper bound of a domain school's ground and says where one stood against the castle. The article itself calls it the largest in Japan, so it is not a typical school, and it was founded late, in the nineteenth century.

Used for: how large a domain school could be and where it stood (religion-and-death)
