kagawa-tameike-data

Kagawa Prefecture, Land Improvement Division, ため池 データ ("Irrigation ponds: data", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.pref.kagawa.lg.jp/tochikai/about_tameike/deta.html)

What it is: The prefectural government's data page on its irrigation ponds: how many there are, how their density ranks nationally, how many are small, and how much of the farm water depended on them.

Why it applies, and its limits: Kagawa, on the dry Sanuki plain, is the country's densest pond landscape, so it bounds how common a pond among the paddy could be. The counts are of today's ponds (a 2021 census), long after the Edo-period building peak, and the page gives no count per village or per field.

Used for: the pond count, its national density rank and the share of tiny ponds, as the upper end of how often paddy land kept ponds (fields)
