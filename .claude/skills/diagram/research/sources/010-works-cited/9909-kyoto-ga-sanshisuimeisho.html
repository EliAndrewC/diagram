kyoto-ga-sanshisuimeisho

Kyoto City Greenery Association, "山紫水明処（頼山陽書斎）の庭" (the garden of Sanshi Suimei-sho, Rai San'yō's study), in its series on Kyoto's gardens (in Japanese; https://www.kyoto-ga.jp/kyononiwa/2009/09/teien002.html)

What it is: A page by Kyoto's municipal greenery foundation on Sanshi Suimei-sho, the study-cum-tea room the Confucian scholar Rai San'yō built on his Kyoto estate in 1828, a national historic site, with its history, its garden and the works it draws on.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests a study built as a building of its own on an Edo-period estate, standing apart from the house, with its garden beside it. It is one scholar's town estate in Kyoto, not an official's or a samurai compound, and the page gives the site's area but not the building's size.

Used for: a detached study-cum-tea room built in 1828, the one building of the estate that survives (buildings)
