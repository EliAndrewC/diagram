What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Imado ware, the unglazed and raku pottery fired at Imado and Hashiba northeast of Asakusa in Edo, with the count of some fifty producing households at the end of the shogunate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of an Edo kiln quarter in households, which is what makes a kiln a quarter rather than a shop on our capital. A tertiary summary used for that count, which it attributes to a period source.

Used for: some fifty kiln households at Imado
