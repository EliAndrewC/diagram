shinke-nagayamon-kanazawa

Kanazawa City Tourism Association, 新家邸長屋門 (Araie-tei nagayamon, "the gate range of the Araie residence" - the page reads 新家 as あらいえ, Araie; title translated), Kanazawa tabi monogatari (in Japanese; https://www.kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp/spot/detail_50533.html)

What it is: The Kanazawa tourism association's page on a gate range still standing in the Nagamachi samurai quarter of Kanazawa, the castle town of the Kaga domain, at a residence where the Kuwashima family lived in the domain period and of which, the page says, only a detached building now stands besides the gate range; it describes the gateway, the chugen room on one side of it and the stable on the other.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a standing gate range of an Edo-period samurai residence room by room, and says how its servants' room was entered and divided: one entrance with a lattice sliding door, and two rooms of six mats behind it. It is a short tourist page with no date for the building and no plan; it is one residence in one domain, and it does not say how many servants the room held, or who they were - single hired menservants or families - so it shows one servants' room behind one entrance and cannot say whether a rowhouse of servant households had a door for each household. It names the room's one entrance and no other door, but it does not say there was no other: the earth-floored space with a sink behind the two rooms may have had its own door to the yard, and the page does not say. The building has also been altered since the domain period (its roof of boards weighted with stones was replaced with tile in 1928), and the page does not say whether the lattice door is original.

Used for: a chugen room of two six-mat rooms behind one entrance, in a samurai's gate range (buildings)
