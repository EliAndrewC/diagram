What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the gokura, the Edo-period village granary that held the tax rice awaiting shipment; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the village granary as a temporary store for the tax rice and, in places, a famine reserve, which is the first link of the chain this page draws; it says nothing of the office's own storehouse; the article rests on a single reference, gives no size or builder, and hedges the famine role to some places.

Used for: the tax rice's passage from village to store (buildings)
