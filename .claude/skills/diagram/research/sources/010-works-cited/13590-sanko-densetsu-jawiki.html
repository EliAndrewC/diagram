ja.wikipedia 三湖伝説 ("the legend of the three lakes", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E6%B9%96%E4%BC%9D%E8%AA%AC)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the northern Tōhoku legend of Hachirōtarō and the three lakes, with the local shrines and festivals tied to it.

Why it applies, and its limits: In passing it places a small dragon-god shrine (a 祠) beside a named well, the Binsui-ire, at Mikura-hana, where the people of Amasegawa kept a festival on 20 November, which is a readable instance of a small shrine at a well. Its limits: it is an encyclopedia article, and this passage repeats a twentieth-century folklore study (Takaya Shigeo, 雨の神, 1984), so the festival is as that study recorded it, not a present-day observation; the shrine's founding date is not given, so nothing here shows it is premodern; it is one shrine, mentioned in a legend article rather than a survey; and it is a water-deity shrine tied to rain-prayer rather than a wayside one.

Used for: a small dragon-god shrine beside a well (religion-and-death)
