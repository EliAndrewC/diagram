zh.wikipedia 六畜 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/六畜)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article 六畜 (liuchu, "the six domestic animals"), a short tertiary encyclopedia entry on the classical Chinese grouping of livestock - horse, ox, sheep, pig, dog and chicken - that Chinese writing has treated as a set since antiquity.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one small thing, that the chicken belongs to that classical set, which is what puts a hen-house on a farmstead in a setting built on imperial China and Japan; the article is in Chinese because the category is a Chinese literary one. It is a tertiary summary of a term rather than evidence of how many birds any particular farm kept, so it settles that chickens belong on a farm and nothing at all about their numbers.

Used for: the chicken as one of the six livestock.
