hattori-site-yayoiken

服部遺跡 (the Hattori site, Moriyama, Shiga), "縄文晩期〜弥生初期（水田と集落）" ("From the end of the Jomon to the start of the Yayoi: paddies and settlement", title translated), a page of a site on the Hattori excavation (in Japanese; https://hattori-iseki.yayoiken.jp/h-zenki.html)

What it is: A page describing the paddies found in the lowest layer of the Hattori site, about 260 plots of the earliest Yayoi period, with the widths and heights of their large and small bunds and of the central canal and the drain, its plans credited to the site's excavation report and one illustration to the Moriyama city history; the measurements in its text carry no citation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured widths for an excavated paddy net - large bunds 80-150 cm running along the central canal, small bunds 20-60 cm, a canal 1.4-2 m wide and a drain 0.5-1 m - with water openings set in the bunds, all two thousand years before modern consolidation. It is one early site on the Lake Biwa plain, far older than the setting's period, and a popular page that names no publisher; its figures most likely come from the excavation report, which was not read. It is also a different scale of paddy: its plots run from about 10 to 180 square metres, far smaller than the paddies of later periods, and its one canal and one drain serve an excavated net of about 2 hectares (a few times that, the page guesses, beyond the dug area), so its small-bund widths belong to a net of tiny plots worked with stone tools, and its canal and drain widths to a single small net rather than a village's. The use of the large bunds as paths it gives only as likely.

Used for: an early paddy's canal, drain and bund widths, the large bund along the canal and the water openings in the bunds (Water); the drain's 0.5 m narrow end beside the width a gathering drain begins at, and the widths and heights of the small and large bunds as the one premodern measure of a bund's height (Fields)
