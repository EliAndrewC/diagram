hattori-site-yayoiken

服部遺跡 (the Hattori site, Moriyama, Shiga), "縄文晩期〜弥生初期（水田と集落）" ("From the end of the Jomon to the start of the Yayoi: paddies and settlement", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a page of a site on the Hattori excavation (in Japanese; https://hattori-iseki.yayoiken.jp/h-zenki.html)

What it is: A page describing the paddies found in the lowest layer of the Hattori site, about 260 plots of the earliest Yayoi period, with the widths and heights of their large and small bunds and of the central canal and the drain, taken from the site's excavation report and the Moriyama city history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured widths for an excavated paddy net - large bunds 80-150 cm running along the central canal, small bunds 20-60 cm, a canal 1.4-2 m wide and a drain 0.5-1 m - with water openings set in the bunds, all two thousand years before modern consolidation. It is one early site on the Lake Biwa plain, far older than the setting's period, and a popular page that names no publisher; its figures come from the excavation report, which was not read. The use of the large bunds as paths it gives only as likely.

Used for: an early paddy's canal, drain and bund widths, the large bund along the canal and the water openings in the bunds (water)
