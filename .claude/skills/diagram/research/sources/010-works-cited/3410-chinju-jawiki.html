What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the chinju, the tutelary deity guarding a building or a tract of land, and its shrine; a short encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the tutelary this page sets beside the Chinese earth god; it treats village enshrinement and shows the shrine grove, but carries the encyclopedia's own banner for lacking sources and attaches no era, and the comparison is this page's.

Used for: the tutelary chinju in its grove (religion and death)
