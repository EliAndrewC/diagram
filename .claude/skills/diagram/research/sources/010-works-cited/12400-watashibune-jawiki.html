ja.wikipedia 渡し船 ("ferry boat", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B8%A1%E3%81%97%E8%88%B9)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the river and harbor ferry in Japan: its history, the Edo-period ferries, and a long list of ferries past and present.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a tertiary source, written by anonymous editors, and serves for the shape of the practice rather than as authority for it: it says where Edo ferries ran - where a bridge was hard to build or not permitted - in a sentence it gives no citation for, and records a shogunate-designated ferry run by farmers (the Yagiri ferry) on the word of two tourist-board pages. Its Edo section is short; its boat figures for the Banyu ferry say "up to 20" where the ministry page it cites says "about 20", and most of the article is about modern ferries.

Used for: where ferries ran, and a ferry farmers kept (ways)
