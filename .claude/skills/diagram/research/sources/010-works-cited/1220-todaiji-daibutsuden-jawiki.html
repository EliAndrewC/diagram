What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Great Buddha Hall of Todaiji in Nara, formally the temple's kondo: the dimensions of the standing eighteenth-century hall and of the far wider eighth-century original it replaced.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the two measurements that bound the largest Japanese temple hall, standing and lost, which is how this page keeps its hall-size ceiling honest about which it means. A tertiary summary used for the dimensions.
