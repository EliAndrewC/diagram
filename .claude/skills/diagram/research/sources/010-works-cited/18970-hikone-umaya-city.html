hikone-umaya-city

Hikone City, "Cultural properties within Hikone Castle" (彦根城内の文化財) (in Japanese; https://www.city.hikone.lg.jp/kakuka/kanko_bunka/8/2_1/3/4581.html)

What it is: The city's page on the designated buildings of Hikone Castle, including the stable that kept the lord's horses, with its 21 standing and tying places and a small room at its east end "for managing the horses" (馬を管理する小部屋); the page does not say who used it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the stall count inside the building the national entry measures, and the one room set aside for managing the horses. Its count (21 places) differs from the national entry's nineteen horses, and it gives no dimensions or date; it describes a castle stable, not a commoner's. The page itself calls it the only large stable surviving inside an early-modern castle anywhere in Japan, so it is one surviving example and cannot show what a typical stable was like.

Used for: the Hikone stable's 21 places and its room for managing the horses (towns)
