hikone-umaya-city

Hikone City, "Cultural properties within Hikone Castle" (彦根城内の文化財) (in Japanese; https://www.city.hikone.lg.jp/kakuka/kanko_bunka/8/2_1/3/4581.html)

What it is: The city's page on the designated buildings of Hikone Castle, including the stable that kept the lord's horses, with its 21 standing and tying places and a small room at its east end for the men who managed the horses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the stall count inside the building the national entry measures, and the one room given to the keepers. Its count (21 places) differs from the national entry's nineteen horses, and it gives no dimensions or date; it describes a castle stable, not a commoner's.

Used for: the Hikone stable's 21 places and its keepers' room (towns)
