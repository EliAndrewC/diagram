Aizu Bukeyashiki, 壮大な『家老屋敷間取図』 (The grand plan of the karo mansion) (in Japanese; https://bukeyashiki.com/pickup/3729.html)

What it is: A short page of the Aizu Bukeyashiki, a private museum at Aizu-Wakamatsu, on its reconstruction of the mansion of the Saigo, hereditary karo of the Aizu domain, giving the house's stipend, the site and building areas and the number of rooms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the scale of a karo's own mansion at a great domain, which is what a posting's chief retainer's house is not. Its limits are that it is a museum's promotional page without references, describing a reconstruction: the late-Edo house burned in 1868, and the museum rebuilt it on its own grounds in 1975 from a bird's-eye drawing found after the war, not from a measured plan. So its areas and room count are the reconstruction's figures, and the page does not say whether the 2,400-tsubo site is the original lot or the museum's (a regional tourism page gives 2,300 tsubo). A further limit is that the Saigo held 1,700 koku in a domain of over 200,000, the top of the scale, far above a magistrate's karo.

Used for: the site, building area and rooms of a great domain's karo mansion (buildings)
