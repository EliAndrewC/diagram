yashio-kenchi

れきナビ - やしお歴史事典 (Yashio City's online history encyclopedia), 検地 ("Land survey", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; http://yashio-rekinavi.com/reki-navi/index.php?title=%E6%A4%9C%E5%9C%B0)

What it is: An entry in the Yashio city history encyclopedia (Saitama prefecture) on the early modern land survey and its registers, with a worked example from the 1684 register of Kami-Baba village and a table of the surveys of the Hachijo district.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its example line records one parcel of middle-grade paddy, 16 by 10.5 ken, 5 se 18 bu, and it explains that a parcel's sides were found by converting it to a rectangle and that a parcel is one line of the register - a dated, measured premodern paddy parcel. Its limits: one parcel of one village, held by a temple; a register parcel is a unit of assessment, and the entry does not say whether it was one bunded sheet or several; and the rectangle is a surveyor's conversion, so the parcel's real shape is unknown.

Used for: the size of a 17th-century paddy parcel in a survey register, and how a parcel was measured (fields)
