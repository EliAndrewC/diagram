zh.wikipedia 南阳知府衙门 ("the Nanyang prefect's yamen", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/南阳府衙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the prefectural yamen of Nanyang in Henan, the seat of the prefects of the Yuan, Ming and Qing, a protected monument: its history and its surviving extent.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one fully preserved prefect's yamen in China, the tier between a county and a province, so its dimensions are the direct measure of a regional governor's compound. It is an encyclopedia article, gives the site's outer dimensions without saying how they were taken, and describes the compound as rebuilt through 1901; another readable page gives the same site twice the area.

Used for: the size of a prefectural yamen (cities/government)
