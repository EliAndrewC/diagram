kigosai-azenuri

NPO Kigosai (Kigo to Saijiki no Kai), "Azenuri", Kigosai saijiki (in Japanese; https://kigosai.sub.jp/001/archives/9893)

What it is: An online almanac of haiku season words kept by a non-profit society, with its entry for azenuri: filed under late spring, a short definition, and three example verses with their poets and anthologies.

Why it applies, and its limits: A season word records a task farmers did at a set time of year. The entry places azenuri before rice planting, and its verses show the task in poetry. It is a volunteer-run almanac with no date. It does not date its verses, and it notes that the work is now done by machine.

Used for: that azenuri was a late-spring task, before rice planting (fields)
