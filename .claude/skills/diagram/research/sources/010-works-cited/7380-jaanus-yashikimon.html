jaanus-yashikimon

JAANUS (Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System), "yashikimon" 屋敷門 (http://www.aisf.or.jp/~jaanus/deta/y/yashikimon.htm) - "The front gate of a warrior's manor house"; the illustrated example is the former Tōjin yashiki gate at Kōfukuji, Nagasaki, 1740.

What it is: The entry for yashikimon 屋敷門 in JAANUS, the Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System, an online dictionary of Japanese art and architectural terms. It defines the word as the front gate of a warrior's manor house and illustrates it with the former Tojin yashiki gate of 1740, now at Kofukuji in Nagasaki.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for vocabulary - what the formal front gate of a walled residence is called and what one looks like - when naming and drawing the gates on our compound plans. Its limits: it is a specialist dictionary, which is to say a compact summary rather than a survey, and its illustrated example is a single eighteenth-century gate; and the term as JAANUS defines it belongs to a warrior's residence, so nothing about a farmstead's gate or about what such a gate meant for a farming household's standing can be sourced to it.
