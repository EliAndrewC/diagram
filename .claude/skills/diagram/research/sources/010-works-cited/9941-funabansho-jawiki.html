Japanese Wikipedia, "船番所" (funabansho, the boat watch post) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%88%B9%E7%95%AA%E6%89%80)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia stub defining the boat watch post of the Edo period: an office that domains set up at busy landings and other key points to inspect passing boats and collect taxes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states what such a post was for and where it stood, which is the question a river watch on the map raises. It is a tertiary stub of two sentences, flagged since 2019 as giving no sources, speaks of coastal domains and names only posts at the mouths of two bays in Nagasaki; it gives no size, staffing or plan.

Used for: what a boat watch post was for and where it stood (river cities)
