kotobank-machidojo

Digital Daijisen and Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten, s.v. 町道場, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/町道場-137003)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the reference entries on the machi-dojo, the private commercial martial-arts hall of the town and its Bakumatsu boom.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the hall as in town, privately run and fee-paying and dates its flourishing to the Bakumatsu, which is the urban institution this page draws; reference entries with no plan; the private and fee-paying definition is the modern trade's, with the Bakumatsu flourishing named as history.

Used for: the private martial-arts hall (government)
