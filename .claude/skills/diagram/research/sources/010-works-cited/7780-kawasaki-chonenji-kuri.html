kawasaki-chonenji-kuri

Kawasaki City, 長念寺庫裏 (a designated kuri) (https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/880/page/0000000260.html) - 「The main block is 9 ken along the ridge and 5 ken across the beams, with eaves of dashigeta construction」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「主屋は桁行9間、梁行5間の規模で、軒を出桁造とし」)

What it is: A municipal cultural-property page on a late-Edo parish temple's kuri, with its dimensions and the rooms it held.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for what a priest's dwelling held - the priest's living room, the kitchen with its earthen floor, a reception room, a study - and for its size, 9 by 5 ken at a temple of some standing. Its limit is that standing: a village kuri was smaller, and this page bounds the dwelling from above.

Used for: a kuri of 9 by 5 ken and the rooms of a priest's dwelling (religion-and-death.html).
