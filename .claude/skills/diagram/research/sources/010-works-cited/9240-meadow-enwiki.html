en.wikipedia "Meadow" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meadow)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on meadows, the open grassland kept by mowing or grazing; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the general process behind a cut margin: cleared land that is no longer cut or grazed goes over to scrub and woody plants and on toward woodland. The process is ecological and holds anywhere, but the page's examples are European and North American; its only Asian ones are Himalayan and Tibetan alpine meadows, which last without cutting and say nothing about lowland East Asian farmland. It describes meadows, never a field's margin or a hedgerow, so applying it to a field's edge is this record's inference, and it gives no distance from that edge.

Used for: why cutting kept scrub off a field's margin (vegetation)
