en.wikipedia "Meadow" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meadow)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on meadows, the open grassland kept by mowing or grazing; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the general process behind a cut margin: cleared land that is no longer cut or grazed goes over to scrub and woody plants and on toward woodland. The process is ecological and holds anywhere, but the page's examples are European and North American, it says nothing about East Asia, and it gives no distance from a field's edge.

Used for: why cutting kept scrub off a field's margin (vegetation)
