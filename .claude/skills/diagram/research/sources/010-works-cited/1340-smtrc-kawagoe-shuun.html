三井住友トラスト不動産 (Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Realty), このまちアーカイブス 3：川と舟運 ～ 川越 (https://smtrc.jp/town-archives/city/kawagoe/p03.html)

What it is: A sourced local-history page in a real-estate company's town archive series on Kawagoe's rivers and boat transport: the town's earlier outer port on the Irima river, the wet ground between the landings and the castle town, and the town's growth as a wholesale center.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the landings as an outer port separated from the castle town by low ground, which is the relation our river capital's wharf has to its wall. Its limit is that it is a company's popular archive rather than the town history it summarizes. It describes the same town as the encyclopedia article cited beside it, so the two are not independent corroboration.

Used for: the outer port and the wet ground between the landings and the town
