ja.wikipedia 高田馬場 ("Takadanobaba", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%AB%98%E7%94%B0%E9%A6%AC%E5%A0%B4)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Tokyo district named after the Takada riding ground, which the third shogun laid out in 1636 for his bannermen's riding and mounted archery; it gives the ground's length and breadth.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures a real riding ground, the largest end of the range. It was the shogun's ground in Edo, far larger than a domain's, so it bounds the size from above rather than setting it; the article is mostly about the modern district.

Used for: the upper bound of a riding ground's length (cities/government)
