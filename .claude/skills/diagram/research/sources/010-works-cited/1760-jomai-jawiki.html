What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on jōmai, castle rice: originally the tribute rice of the shogunal lands, later the rice the shogunate and the fudai domains stockpiled in their castles as military provision.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the rice stock kept inside a castle against siege, one of the two granaries our capital draws. A tertiary summary used for that. The article carries a notice that it shows no verifiable references, and the siege-store claim rests on it alone.

Used for: jōmai as military provision stockpiled in castles
