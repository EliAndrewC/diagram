ja.wikipedia 正保城絵図 ("the Shoho castle plans", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A3%E4%BF%9D%E5%9F%8E%E7%B5%B5%E5%9B%B3)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the castle plans the shogunate ordered from every castle-holding daimyo in 1644: their purpose as thematic maps of the military works, their format, the drawing standards the shogunate set (as recorded on the Saga domain's side), and the 63 that survive in the National Archives.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out what a period map of a castle and its town was required to show - the works, the keep and turrets, every street - which is the precedent for our castle drawn as works without rooms. It is a tertiary summary with references; the standards are one domain's record of the order, and the page lists what the plans had to show and says their written notes were in principle confined to militarily important matters (citing Senda 2001), but it says nothing of what the drawings left out, so that they drew no residences is our reading; the plans themselves, viewable in the National Archives of Japan digital archive, would show it directly.

Used for: the 1644 order and the plans' military theme; the drawing standards: walls, earthworks and moats measured, the keep, turrets and walls drawn, every street shown (cities/capitals)
