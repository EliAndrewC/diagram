goka-mizuka

Goka Town, 水塚（みずづか） ("Mizuka", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.town.goka.lg.jp/kurashi-machi-shigoto/gokanabi/youkoso/town-profile/town-history/page000014.html)

What it is: A page of the town history of Goka, Ibaraki, a farming town between the Tone and Edo rivers, on its flood-refuge mounds.

Why it applies, and its limits: It adds what stood beside a mizuka on a working farm - a stone for tethering the horse, since the plow animals had to be saved too, and a small boat hung under the eaves. It is one town's page for residents.

Used for: the horse-tethering stone and the boat under the eaves (water)
