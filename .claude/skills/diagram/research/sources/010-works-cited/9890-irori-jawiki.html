irori-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 囲炉裏 (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9B%B2%E7%82%89%E8%A3%8F)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the irori, the square hearth cut into the floor of the traditional house: its uses, the seating around it, and where in the house it was set.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where a common house put its sunken hearth, in the floor of a room beside the earth-floored doma or kitchen or in the doma itself, which is what a plan's irori is checked against. It is tertiary, and it describes the minka, the house of farmers and townspeople, not the samurai house.

Used for: where a common house set its irori (buildings)
