kotobank-hinoban

Kotobank, 火の番 ("Hi no ban, the fire watch", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%81%AB%E3%81%AE%E7%95%AA-612078)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the fire watch, carrying the Digital Daijisen and the Sekai daihyakka jiten: the shogunate's fire watches in Edo, the ward watch houses and gate watchmen, and who fought fire in the villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one reference found that says who fought fire in a village. Its limits are that it is a tertiary encyclopedia entry, and that its village sentence is one line naming the groups without describing any apparatus.

Used for: village firefighting by the young men's association and the five-household groups (urban-features)
