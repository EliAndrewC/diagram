en.wikipedia "Alluvial fan" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alluvial_fan)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the alluvial fan, the cone of sediment that spreads out from a canyon mouth; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: Read for the landform's shape alone - narrowest at its apex and widening outward - which is the opposite of the drawn irrigation fan this page describes and keeps the two from being confused. It says nothing of paddy or canals.

Used for: the direction an alluvial fan widens (fields)
