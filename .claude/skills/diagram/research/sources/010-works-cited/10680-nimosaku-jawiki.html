nimosaku-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 二毛作 (nimosaku, double cropping) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BA%8C%E6%AF%9B%E4%BD%9C)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on double cropping, with a section on its history in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives two reasons a paddy did not carry a winter crop - no fertilizer to spare away from the crowded districts, and a wet paddy that barley cannot stand - which is what makes the winter crop a per-settlement matter. A tertiary summary; the fertilizer sentence is set in Japan's medieval period and rests on one cited book (Yokoi and Amino, 2003); the wet-paddy sentence cites nothing, so that reason stands on the encyclopedia alone until a period or scholarly source carries it; and the page gives no Edo figures.

Used for: no winter barley where fertilizer was short, nor on a wet paddy (fields)
