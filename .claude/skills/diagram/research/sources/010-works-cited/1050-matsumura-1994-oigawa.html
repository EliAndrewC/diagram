松村博 (Hiroshi Matsumura), 大井川徒渉制を中心とする近世・東海道の渡河形態について, 土木史研究 14: 125-, June 1994 (refereed) (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/journalhs1990/14/0/14_0_125/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A refereed civil-engineering-history paper on how the Tokaido crossed its rivers in the Edo period, centered on the Oi river, which travelers had to wade or be carried across because neither a bridge nor a ferry was allowed; it weighs the defensive, political, technical and economic explanations for that rule.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms the prohibition from the engineering-history literature and, unlike the popular account, sets the defensive explanation beside the others and doubts it, which is why our page states the motive as argued rather than settled. Its limit is that it is a paper on crossing forms, not on roads following rivers, which it does not address.

Used for: the Oi river's bridge and ferry prohibition and the competing explanations for it
