kanra-ashigaru-nagaya

甘楽町教育委員会 (Kanra Town Board of Education), 旧小幡藩武家屋敷足軽長屋 (https://www.town.kanra.lg.jp/kyouiku/bunkazai/bunkazai/20200722123543.html)

What it is: A municipal cultural-property page from Kanra in Gunma on a surviving Edo-period ashigaru row house of the former Obata domain: a terrace of identical minimal units, each two ken wide and three and a half deep, built for the domain's low-ranking retainers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a measured surviving example of the uniform terrace for a domain's lowest retainers, the form this page weighs and declines for the capital. Its limit is that it is one small domain's terrace. The late-Edo date is the page's presumption, five of an estimated ten units survive, and the page names no survey for its dimensions.

Used for: a surviving ashigaru terrace of identical two-ken units
