kawasaki-chonenji-hondo

Kawasaki City, 長念寺本堂 ("Chōnen-ji Hondō, the main hall of Chōnen-ji temple", title translated) (https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/880/page/0000000259.html) - 「Ridge span 7 ken (9 at the rear), beam span 7 ken」 (translated; original: 「桁行7間（背面9間）、梁行7間」)

What it is: A municipal cultural-property page on the same temple's main hall of 1824.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for a parish hondō of 57.3 by 55.5 shaku, well above a village hall - an upper bound only.

Used for: a parish hondō of 57.3 by 55.5 shaku (Religion and the dead).
