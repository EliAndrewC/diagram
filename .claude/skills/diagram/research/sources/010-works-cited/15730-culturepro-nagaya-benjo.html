Culture Pro, 長屋の便所とごみ溜 ("The tenement privy and the rubbish heap", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2022 (in Japanese; https://www.culture-pro.co.jp/2022/06/17/%E9%95%B7%E5%B1%8B%E3%81%AE%E4%BE%BF%E6%89%80%E3%81%A8%E3%81%94%E3%81%BF%E6%BA%9C/)

What it is: A short history column by an educational publishing house on how Edo disposed of its night soil and its rubbish, set against Paris.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the back tenement's shared privy (sogoka), says the landlord held the right to its contents and sold them, and says the collectors were at first farmers dealing directly with the houses and later specialist dealers. It is a popular column without notes, about Edo only; its figures are not used, only these plain statements, which the Japanese Wikipedia article on night soil also makes.

Used for: who owned and who carried off a tenement's night soil (towns)
