en.wikipedia, Yamen (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamen)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the yamen, the office and residence compound of a Chinese official: its typical parts - gate, courtyard, hall of judgment, offices, cells and stores - and the residence of the official and his family within it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states plainly that the official lived inside the compound he worked in, which is why the Chinese seat on our maps has no separate magistrate's house. It is a tertiary summary, and the sentence we rest on sits in a section the article itself marks as citing no sources, so it carries the discount of any uncited encyclopedia line; the Columbia teaching page beside it corroborates the rotation and avoidance rules that made the official an outsider, not the residence itself.

Used for: the official's residence inside the yamen
