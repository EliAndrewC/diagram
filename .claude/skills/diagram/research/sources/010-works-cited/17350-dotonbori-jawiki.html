What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Dotonbori, the canal and district of Osaka, including its five theaters licensed in 1653 and their later history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the licensed theater count of the second great city of the Edo period: five licenses (芝居名代) granted in 1653. It also shows the theaters standing together on the south side of one street along the canal's south bank, with their teahouses across the street between them and the water. The five is a count of licenses in one district, not of the city's playhouses. The same district also held puppet theaters the article does not count (Takemoto-za from 1684, Toyotake-za from 1703), so five is a floor for a great city's playhouses, not a ceiling. The 1653 sentence carries no footnote of its own, the five theaters' names are later ones, and it gives no theater's measures.
