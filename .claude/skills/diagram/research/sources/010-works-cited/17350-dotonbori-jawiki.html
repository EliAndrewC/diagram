ja.wikipedia 道頓堀 ("Dotonbori", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/道頓堀)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Dotonbori, the canal and district of Osaka, including its five theaters licensed in 1653 and their later history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the licensed theater count of the second great city of the Edo period and shows its theaters standing together along one bank, which sets the upper end of a capital's playhouse count. A tertiary summary about a district's whole history into the present; it gives no theater's measures.

Used for: Osaka's five licensed theaters (urban-features)
