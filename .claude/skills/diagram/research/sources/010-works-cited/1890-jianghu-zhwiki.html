jianghu-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 匠戶 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8C%A0%E6%88%B6)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the jianghu, the hereditary registered artisan households of the Yuan and Ming, their separate register and their obligation to serve the government workshops.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that artisans were a registered hereditary class bound to the government's works, which is the organization this page's kiln quarter rests on. A tertiary summary used for the institution; it names no kilns or potters.

Used for: the registered hereditary artisan household
