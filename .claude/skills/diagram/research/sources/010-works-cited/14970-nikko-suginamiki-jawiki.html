ja.wikipedia 日光杉並木 ("the Nikko cedar avenue", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/日光杉並木)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the cedar avenue lining three highways to Nikko for about 35 km, planted by one retainer of the Tokugawa over some twenty years from 1625, and the surviving example of the avenues of pine and cedar the shogunate kept along its main highways.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the shogunate established and kept tree avenues along the Five Highways and other main roads, which bears on what lined a trunk road near a town. The Nikko avenue itself is an exceptional memorial planting; the article does not say how continuous the ordinary highway avenues were, or how far from a town they ran.

Used for: that avenues of pine and cedar lined Japan's main highways (ways)
