ja.wikipedia 通し矢 ("toshiya", the long-shot archery contest, title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%80%9A%E3%81%97%E7%9F%A2)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the contest of shooting the length of the Sanjusangendo temple's veranda in Kyoto, with a section on how the domains practiced for it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the domains built practice structures for the contest and names five that had a full practice hall, which shows archery practice as a built place in a castle town. It hedges the list as hearsay, and the contest was a specialty of the great domains.

Used for: that domains built archery practice halls (cities/government)
