What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Dongting Lake, the Yangtze flood-basin lake whose shores were poldered; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the lake's seasonal range of level and area and names the polders made in its basin, which is the fluctuating water outside this page's polder; it says nothing of what the exposed bed carries; its figures are twentieth-century measurements of one exceptional flood basin, transformed by reclamation and upstream regulation.
