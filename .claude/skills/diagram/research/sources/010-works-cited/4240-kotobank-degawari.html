kotobank-degawari

Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten, s.v. 出替り, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/出替り)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the reference entries on degawari, the changeover day of live-in servants and its dates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the changeover on the fifth of the third and ninth months on the fifth of the third and ninth months. They are dictionary entries; the decree is the shogunate's for Edo, and the two works date it a year apart (1668 or 1669).

Used for: the servants' changeover day (government)
