kotobank-degawari

Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten, s.v. 出替り ("servant hiring-change day", title translated from the Japanese by this project), on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/出替り)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the reference entries on degawari, the changeover day of live-in servants and its dates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the changeover on the fifth of the third and ninth months. They are dictionary entries; the decree is the shogunate's for Edo, and the page's entries do not agree on when it took hold (the two cited works say 1668 or 1669, the Nipponica entry on the same page places it after the Meireki fire of 1657). It is not one fixed day: the older second and eighth months' changeover long coexisted with it, even in Edo, the date varied by region, and farm villages hiring servants by the year changed them over early in the twelfth month - so a village or hamlet map should not take the city date as its own.

Used for: the servants' changeover day (government)
