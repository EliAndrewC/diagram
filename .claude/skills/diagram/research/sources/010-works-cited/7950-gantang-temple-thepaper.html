gantang-temple-thepaper

Zhao Chunlan (赵春兰), a fieldwork essay on 甘棠庙, The Paper (澎湃新闻), 2019-11-07 (https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_4855063) - 「The temple compound covers about 2.5 mu.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「庙院占地2.5亩左右。」)

What it is: An ethnographic fieldwork essay by Zhao Chunlan of Fudan University's Department of Sociology, published on The Paper on 2019-11-07, on the village temple Gantang Miao in northern Zhejiang as a public space under rural consolidation - a temple its villagers rebuilt between 1999 and 2012.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the one compound area any readable source gives, about 2.5 mu, and for the villagers' rotating upkeep. Its limits: the compound described was built between 1999 and 2012, on ground reshaped by contemporary village consolidation, and the essay does not establish that the rebuilt yard continues any premodern predecessor; it is one village; and it is the only compound area in this record, so a drawn precinct resting on it is a guess anchored on a modern rebuild, never an attested area.

Used for: a village temple compound of about 2.5 mu, a modern rebuild (religion-and-death.html).
