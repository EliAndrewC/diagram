kotobank-banta

Kotobank, 番太 ("Banta, the hired watchman", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%AA%E5%A4%AA-118241)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the watchman hired by towns and villages, carrying the Digital Daijisen, the Seisen-ban Nihon kokugo daijiten, Nipponica and Britannica Japan: his duties in town and village, his status, his hut and his pay.

Why it applies, and its limits: Four reference works agree on the watchman's duties and status and separate the village's from the town's. Their limits are that they are tertiary; that they say his status varied by place (commoners in Edo, mostly hinin elsewhere); and that they give no count of villages with one and no size or siting of his hut.

Used for: the village watchman's duties, status, hut and pay (urban-features)
