Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), Kanto Regional Bureau, Yokohama office, "東海道と宿場の施設: 旅籠・茶屋" (the Tokaido and its post-town facilities: inns and teahouses) (in Japanese; https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/03_sisetu/0801.htm)

What it is: One page of the road ministry's regional series on the old Tokaido through Kanagawa, on inns and teahouses: the inns counted per post town, the teahouses' places, and how a teahouse was built.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says teahouses were many between post towns and describes the teahouse building - an open earth-floored room divided only by fittings, kept distinct from an inn. It is a modern government office's short summary of one stretch, Kanagawa, of the busiest highway in the late Edo period. Its inn counts come from the shogunate's mid-1800s survey of the post towns (東海道宿村大概帳) and its teahouse places from a period road guide, but it states the teahouse building as a rule (原則) without naming a source, so it gives the layout and not a survey of real buildings. Its teahouses cluster at named rest stops (立場), and on the Tokaido's traffic they were more numerous than on a lesser road. It gives no floor size for a teahouse.

Used for: the teahouses between post towns and how a teahouse was built (ways)
