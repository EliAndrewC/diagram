Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one site's plot count over its area (about 250 plots on some 4 ha at Terasaka), from which the small cell is this page's arithmetic, and the stone-walled banks that hold the steps, which is the terrace form this page draws; it does not say what sets the cell's size.
