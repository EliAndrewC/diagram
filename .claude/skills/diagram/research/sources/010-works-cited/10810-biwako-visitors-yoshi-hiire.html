Biwako Visitors Bureau, 「The spring tradition of burning the reeds」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「春の風物詩「ヨシの火入れ」」) (https://www.biwako-visitors.jp/event/detail/29890/)

What it is: An event listing on Shiga prefecture's official tourism site for the spring burning of the reed beds at Takashima on Lake Biwa.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states in one sentence that the reed was long a part of daily life and that the beds were kept healthy by cutting in winter and burning in spring. It is a tourism notice, not history, and it speaks of Lake Biwa's reed beds only.

Used for: the winter cutting and spring burning that kept a reed bed (vegetation)
