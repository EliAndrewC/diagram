doyoboshi-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "土用干し" (Doyoboshi, drying at the doyo), revision of 2020-02-14 (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9C%9F%E7%94%A8%E5%B9%B2%E3%81%97)

What it is: A short encyclopedia article on the summer doyoboshi, the airing done around the summer doyo: clothes and books, pickled plums, and, in farming, draining the paddy.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the drain's other name (nakaboshi), its season and its purpose, and the usual length of about a week; it does not say which name is older. It is a stub whose farming paragraph rests on one prefectural extension answer (Yamagata). It describes present practice and gives no date for the custom: the week and the purposes it gives (air to the roots, holding back useless stems) are modern extension advice. The drain is older than that - Edo-period farming manuals describe it - but before the war it was done only in some districts with plenty of water and fields that could be drained, and it became general only after the war, with drainage works and chemical fertilizer (Horikawa, Yoshida and Masumoto, 2011). So for our setting it is a local practice, not a universal one, and the week is a modern figure.

Used for: the doyoboshi, the drain at the summer doyo, and its length (fields)
