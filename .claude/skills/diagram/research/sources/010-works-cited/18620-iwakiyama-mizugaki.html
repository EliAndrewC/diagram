iwakiyama-mizugaki

Aomori Prefecture, Cultural Properties Protection Division, "岩木山神社本殿、奥門、瑞垣、中門" ("Iwakiyama Shrine: sanctuary, inner gate, mizugaki and middle gate", title translated), updated 2023-03-28 (in Japanese; https://www.pref.aomori.lg.jp/soshiki/kyoiku/e-bunka/jubun_kenzoubutu_4.html)

What it is: The prefecture's page on four buildings of Iwakiyama Shrine designated Important Cultural Properties, with their dates and a short description of each, the fence round the sanctuary among them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a fence running right round a shrine's sanctuary to 1686-1694, a premodern example of the form. Its limits: the fence was built, with the sanctuary and gates it encloses, by the fourth lord of the Hirosaki domain, and its panels are painted with birds and beasts beside a black-lacquered, gilded sanctuary, so it shows what a lord's patronage built, not what a village gave its shrine; the 1686-1694 date is that of the whole group, not of the fence alone; it gives no height, material or build for the fence beyond its windows and painted panels; and it describes one shrine.

Used for: a fence round a shrine's sanctuary built 1686-1694 by a domain lord (religion-and-death)
