jta-aoyagi-kakunodate

Japan Tourism Agency, multilingual commentary database, "Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings (Aoyagi Samurai Residence)" (in English and Japanese; https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/R1-02053.html)

What it is: The national tourism agency's commissioned description of the Aoyagi house in Kakunodate's samurai district: its fence, its gate and what a visitor meets inside it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names what stood inside a samurai house's gate - a roofed well and a rice storehouse - in a surviving house. It is written for visitors, describes one house as preserved today, and Kakunodate was a small castle town.

Used for: the well and storehouse inside a samurai house's gate (cities/government)
