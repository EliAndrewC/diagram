Donald Wagner, Iron production in three Ming texts (https://donwagner.dk/MingFe/MingFe.html)

What it is: "Iron production in three Ming texts," a page by Donald B. Wagner presenting translations and commentary on Ming-dynasty Chinese accounts of the iron industry, among them Song Yingxing's description of the chao fining hearth and a passage numbering the workforce of one ironworks.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a translated and annotated set of Ming texts on ironmaking by the leading historian of Chinese ferrous metallurgy, cited for the fining hearth as Song Yingxing describes it and for the size of a large iron enterprise's workforce - core setting material, not a stand-in. Its limits are that it is a conference paper on the author's own site rather than a journal article; that the workforce figures come from Qu Dajun's account of about 1680 looking back at the late Ming; and that Wagner's own commentary warns that the passage describes a firm running several ironworks, which Qu Dajun seems to have misread as one, so the figures are an enterprise's and not one works'.

Used for: the chao fining hearth as Song Yingxing describes it; the 200 charcoal producers / 200 furnace tenders / 300 miners
