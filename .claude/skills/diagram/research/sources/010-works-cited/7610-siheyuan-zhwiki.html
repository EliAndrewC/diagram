zh.wikipedia 四合院 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9B%9B%E5%90%88%E9%99%A2)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the siheyuan, the courtyard house of northern China: a tertiary entry describing the plan - the main range on the north, the side wings east and west, the gate and the screen - and what each part was used for.

Why it applies, and its limits: One sentence says where a courtyard household kept its animals and why: in north China the east wing takes the western sun in summer and the cold northwesterly in winter, so it is unfit to live in, and wealthy households used it to store grain or as a kitchen or a horse stable. That is the branch of this project’s byre knob where a household owning its team stables it inside its own yard, and it gives the map a NAMED side instead of "one wing". Its limits are kind and provenance: an encyclopedia article is a tertiary summary, and this article’s own reference for the sentence is a folklore dictionary page surviving only as a 2007 capture, so no scholarly survey stands behind it. A Shaanxi or Hebei vernacular-architecture survey would be the better footing.

Used for: the east wing of a northern courtyard house taking the stable, the kitchen and the grain store
