Shirobito, [高山陣屋] (Takayama Jin'ya), a visitor's write-up on the castle page of the Japan Castle Association's site (in Japanese; https://shirobito.jp/castle/1717)

What it is: The Takayama Jin'ya page on Shirobito, a site endorsed by the Japan Castle Association, carrying a visitor's account of the shogunate's intendancy office at Takayama: the genkan, the route through the office's rooms to the intendants' residence, and what the residence held.

Why it applies, and its limits: Takayama is the one intendancy office whose main buildings survive, the nearest real thing to a posting, and the page says where its formal entrance is and what rooms its residence had, a guest room and a bath among them. Its limits are that it is a visitor's write-up rather than the Association's own text or a survey, that it gives a route rather than a plan, and that the office and residence it walks through were restored after the Showa era, so it describes the compound as rebuilt.

Used for: the genkan of the office and the route from it to the residence; the residence's guest room, well and bath (buildings)
