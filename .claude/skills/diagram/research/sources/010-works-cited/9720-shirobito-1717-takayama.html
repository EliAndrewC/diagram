Shirobito, [高山陣屋] (Takayama Jin'ya), a visitor's review on the castle page of a site endorsed by the Japan Castle Association (in Japanese; https://shirobito.jp/castle/1717)

What it is: The Takayama Jin'ya page on Shirobito, a site endorsed by the Japan Castle Association, carrying visitors' reviews, of which the one used here (posted 2026-06-09 by the user イオ, of a visit on 2025-11-16) is an account of the shogunate's intendancy office at Takayama: the genkan, the route through the office's rooms to the intendants' residence, and what the residence held.

Why it applies, and its limits: Takayama is the one intendancy office whose main buildings survive, the nearest real thing to a posting, and the page says where its formal entrance is and what rooms its residence had, a guest room and a bath among them. Its limits are that it is a visitor's write-up rather than the Association's own text or a survey, that it gives a route rather than a plan, and that route is the museum's modern visitors' route, not evidence of how officials or guests passed through the compound in the period; that the office and residence it walks through had been lost and were restored from the Showa era on, so it describes the compound as rebuilt; and that Takayama was the seat of a district intendant (gundai) governing all of Hida, larger than most intendancy offices, so it is the upper end of what a posting would have.

Used for: the genkan of the office and the route from it to the residence; the residence's guest room, well and bath (buildings)
