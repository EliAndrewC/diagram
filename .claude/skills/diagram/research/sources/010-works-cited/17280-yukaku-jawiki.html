yukaku-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 遊廓 ("pleasure quarter", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/遊廓)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the yukaku, the licensed and enclosed pleasure quarter of Japan, from its Edo-period form through the modern licensed districts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a licensed quarter was, how few there were in the Edo period, and that most pleasure districts grew up without a wall - which is what keeps a walled quarter off all but a great city. A tertiary summary; its account of the gates confuses the Yoshiwara with Osaka's Shinmachi (the Shinmachi article sets it right), and its passage on quarters being moved to the outskirts is about the modern period.

Used for: what a licensed quarter was, the twenty-odd of them, the unenclosed majority of pleasure districts and the unlicensed places beside them (urban-features)
