Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Kanto Regional Development Bureau, Yokohama office, "東海道への誘い: Q15 旅籠屋と木賃宿はどう違うのでしょうか？" ("An invitation to the Tokaido: Q15, how did the hatago and the kichin-yado differ?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/04_qa/index2/a0215.htm)

What it is: One answer in a question-and-answer series on the old Tokaido highway published by a regional office of Japan's Ministry of Land, explaining the difference between the inn with meals and the cheap firewood-fee inn, what each cost in the 1830s, and where the cheap inns stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the cheap inn at the edge of the post town and prices it against the inn with meals, which is what decides where a map puts its cheapest lodging. It is a short popular answer by a road agency, with no sources given, and it speaks of the Tokaido's post-stations, not of a provincial seat.

Used for: the cheap inn at the edge of the post town; a hatago costing more than five times a kichin-yado in the Tenpo era (cities/fabric)
