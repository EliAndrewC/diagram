What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the torii, the Shinto gate, its forms, names and uses; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the naming of ranked gates along an approach (ichi-, ni-, san-no-torii) and the donor's name on each of Fushimi's gates, which are the two regimes this page draws; it gives no spacing figure and does not date the votive custom.

Used for: ranked torii and donated torii (religion and death)
