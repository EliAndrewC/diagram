tehaishi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 手配師 ("labor broker", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%89%8B%E9%85%8D%E5%B8%AB)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tehaishi, the broker who finds day laborers work, tracing the trade back to the Edo-period placement broker who stood guarantor for work-seekers in the cities, lodged them and found them work.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that says the Edo broker's house was also a lodging for the men placed, which is what makes a labor boss's house larger than a laborer's. It is weak: the article is mostly about the modern trade, and it carries the encyclopedia's own warnings that it cites no sources, may hold original research and has had its accuracy disputed; it gives no size or plan for the lodging.

Used for: the broker's lodging for the men placed (towns)
