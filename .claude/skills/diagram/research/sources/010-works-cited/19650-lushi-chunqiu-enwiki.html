What it is: The encyclopedia's article on the Lushi chunqiu, a Chinese classic compiled under the Qin chancellor Lü Buwei: its date, contents and scholarship.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the compilation to about 239 BC, which places the text's rule on burial far before modern times.

Used for: the date of the Lushi chunqiu (Water)
