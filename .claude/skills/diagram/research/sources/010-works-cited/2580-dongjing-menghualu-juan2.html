孟元老 (Meng Yuanlao), 東京夢華錄 (Dongjing meng Hua lu), 卷二, the chapter on the streets east of the corner tower (Dongjiaolou jiexiang), Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%9D%B1%E4%BA%AC%E5%A4%A2%E8%8F%AF%E9%8C%84/%E5%8D%B7%E4%BA%8C)

What it is: The second chapter of the memoir of Northern Song Kaifeng written about 1147, on the streets east of the Eastern Corner Tower: the three pleasure precincts there, the fifty-odd goulan within them and the great sheds that held thousands.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the primary count of a Song capital's theater sheds, the figure this page sets against a provincial city's touring players. A primary text read from a crowd-transcribed wiki copy; it describes the imperial capital, a ceiling rather than a norm, and it counts one district's precincts.

Used for: Kaifeng's fifty-odd goulan and the largest shed holding several thousand
