Wikipedia, Nagare-zukuri (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagare-zukuri) - 「The most common sizes are 1 and 3 ken.」

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the flowing-roof sanctuary style, the commonest form of a Shinto honden.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the sizes a honden of this style takes - one to eleven bays, one and three the commonest - which grounds the one-bay sanctuary. A tertiary summary of an architectural type; it says nothing about which villages had which.

Used for: the commonest honden sizes, 1 and 3 ken (religion-and-death.html).
