ja.wikipedia 水塚 ("Mizuka, the flood-refuge mound", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E5%A1%9A)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the mizuka, the storehouse raised on a mound as a flood refuge, with its regional names.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one number a map needs - the mound stood some tens of centimeters to 3 m above the main house - and the regional names (mizuya on the Kiso rivers, dangura on the Yodo, mizukura on the Shinano), and what was stored on each floor. It is a stub with few citations, and it describes the eastern river basins' form.

Used for: the height of the refuge mound; its regional names and stores (water)
