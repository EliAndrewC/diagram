Wikipedia (English), "Qimin Yaoshu" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qimin_Yaoshu)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Qimin Yaoshu, the Chinese agricultural treatise by Jia Sixie, giving its author, its date and an outline of its contents.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a modern encyclopedia summary, used only to date the treatise, and its sentence giving the date cites no reference of its own (the article's four references sit on the sentence before it, about the author), so the date rests on the article alone; it gives two accounts of the completion date (544, or between 533 and 544), both in the sixth century, and says nothing of how widely its methods were practiced.

Used for: the Qimin Yaoshu finished in the sixth century (archetypes)
