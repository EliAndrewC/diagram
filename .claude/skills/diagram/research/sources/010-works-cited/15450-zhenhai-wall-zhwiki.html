zhenhai-wall-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 镇海城墙 ("the Zhenhai city wall", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E9%95%87%E6%B5%B7%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the wall of Zhenhai, a county town at the mouth of the Yong river in Zhejiang: a wooden stockade in 1368, a stone wall from 1374, enlarged after a garrison was set there in 1387, with its height, its thickness at base and top, its gates, their barbicans and drawbridges, and its moat side by side, quoting the Ming prefectural gazetteer for the figures.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is one of the few public pages that measures a county wall in section and its moat on each side, and says every gate had a barbican and a drawbridge. It is a tertiary summary of one coastal town that was also a garrison seat, so its wall and gates may be heavier than an inland county's; its metric figures are the article's own conversions of the gazetteer's zhang and chi.

Used for: a county wall's height and its thickness at base and top; a barbican and a drawbridge at every gate; the moat's width side by side and its absence on the sea side (defenses)
