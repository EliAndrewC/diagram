bujutsukarate-tategi

武術空手研究帳・増補（１０） 大間違いの鍛錬法 (A martial-arts and karate notebook, supplement 10: a badly mistaken training method) (in Japanese; http://bujutsukarate.com/zouho/10.htm)

What it is: An essay on a personal site by an unnamed writer on the old martial arts, criticizing a sword school's training method, which sets the standing timber of a "main line" against the knee-high bundle of branches of a "branch line" of the same name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that gives the standing timber a size - a log of a little over 2 m set about 70 cm into the ground - and the height it is struck at. Its limits are that it is an anonymous personal essay, arguing a case, not a reference work; that it deliberately does not name the two schools, which the tategi-uchi name and the bundle mark as the Satsuma schools of Jigen-ryū and its relatives only by inference; and that its measurements are the writer's own, without a source.

Used for: the standing timber about 2 m long, set about 70 cm into the ground and struck from shoulder to stomach height; the knee-high bundle of branches of the branch line (buildings)
