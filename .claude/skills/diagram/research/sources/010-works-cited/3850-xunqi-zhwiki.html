zh.wikipedia 汛期 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/汛期)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the flood season, the yearly rise of a river's level; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that a river's level rises seasonally and predictably, which is the hydrology behind this page's stepped quay; it gives no magnitude.

Used for: the seasonal rise of a river (river cities)
