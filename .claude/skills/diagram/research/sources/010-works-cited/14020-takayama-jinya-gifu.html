takayama-jinya-gifu

Gifu Prefecture, 第９章 高山陣屋管理事務所 ("Chapter 9: the Takayama Jin'ya management office", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a chapter of the prefecture's annual report on its facilities (in Japanese; https://www.pref.gifu.lg.jp/uploaded/attachment/39687.pdf)

What it is: A two-page chapter from a Gifu prefectural report of about 2012 on the Takayama Jin'ya, the shogunate's intendancy office for Hida province, giving its site, its restoration phases from 1970 to 1996, and a table of every building's floor area with its date of building or restoration.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read that measures the buildings of one surviving intendant's office side by side - office, residence, staff rowhouse, rice storehouse, book storehouse - so it is the anchor for their sizes and their ranking. Its limits: it is one office, and an unusually large one (a whole province's intendancy); the residence and the rowhouse are 1990s restorations from old drawings rather than surviving buildings; and it gives floor areas only, never a length or a depth.

Used for: the floor areas of the Takayama office, residence, rowhouse, rice storehouse and book storehouse (buildings)
