l7r-market-day

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Entertainers" and "Demographics" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the sections "Entertainers" and "Demographics". They say the county town holds a market day about once a week, when the county's farmers come in to buy, sell and barter, because the town is the lowest tier with merchants, and that acting troupes make most of their money on that day.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: how often the county town holds its market is the setting's to decide, and these maps follow it. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; they say nothing of where in the town the market stands.

Used for: the county town's weekly market day (towns)
