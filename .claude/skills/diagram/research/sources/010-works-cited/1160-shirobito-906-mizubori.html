加藤理文 (Kato Masafumi), 【理文先生のお城がっこう】城歩き編 第18回 水堀と空堀, 城びと (Shirobito) (https://shirobito.jp/article/906)

What it is: An installment of the castle researcher Kato Masafumi's educational column, written as a teacher and a director of the Japan Castle Association, on the castle web magazine Shirobito, on wet and dry moats: where the water of a wet moat came from - groundwater, castle wastewater, rain, or a river, lake or sea it was deliberately drawn from - and why flatland castles so often have wet moats.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the sources a moat's water actually came from, from a specialist rather than a guidebook, which is what our capital's moat-filling rule is drawn from. Its limit is that it is a popular column and ranks none of the sources.

Used for: the sources of a wet moat's water
