nagano-shishi-kodai-mura

Nagano City, Nagano Shishi (the Nagano city history), vol. 2, history, prehistory to the middle ages, part 1 ch. 3 section 4, 「The ancient village as archaeology sees it」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「考古学からみた古代のムラ」), pp. 427-430, on the ADEAC digital archive (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/nagano-city/texthtml/d100020/ct00000002/ht001860)

What it is: The Nagano city history's account, from excavation, of how the villages of the Nagano basin moved and changed from the late Kofun period to the 12th century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the siting of villages on the dry natural levees, with the wet ground behind them farmed as paddy, to the mid-Heian period, and calls that siting a tradition seen from the Yayoi period on. It is one basin's archaeology; the paddy use of the wet ground is given as what the villagers are thought to have done, not as a record; and it says nothing about wells, graves or shrines.

Used for: Heian villages on the natural levees, the wet ground behind them farmed, not built on (water)
