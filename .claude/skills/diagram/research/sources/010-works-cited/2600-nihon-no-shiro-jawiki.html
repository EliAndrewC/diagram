ja.wikipedia 日本の城 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%97%A5%E6%9C%AC%E3%81%AE%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia survey article on the castles of Japan: their history, their parts and their layouts, including the gate-mouths and which way the front of an early-modern castle usually faced.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the main gate of an early-modern castle stood at the front, usually the south, with the rear gate opposite, which is the pair and the aspect our capital's castle draws. A tertiary summary used for the convention; it gives no reason for the aspect and nothing on divination.

Used for: the ote-mon at the front, usually south, the karamete-mon at the rear
