吴钩, 宋朝的土葬与火葬之争, 儒家网 (https://www.rujiazg.com/article/4066)

What it is: An essay by Wu Gou, a published writer on Song history, on the Confucian-studies portal 儒家网, on the Song dispute between burial and cremation: the edicts against cremation, the dozens of crematoria of the Southern Song capital and the order restoring fourteen of them, and the monks who kept the state's paupers' grounds under stipend.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the Song administrative record on how many crematoria the capital had. Its limits are that it is a popular essay with a thesis, and that it quotes the record rather than being it. The essay names no edition or archive for the edict it quotes, so the quotation cannot be checked against a text.

Used for: Lin'an's several dozen crematoria and the edict restoring fourteen
