コトバンク 深田 and ja.wikipedia ヨシ (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B7%B1%E7%94%B0-123763)

What it is: The Kotobank entry for 深田 (fukada, "deep paddy"), a Japanese online reference that reprints entries from published dictionaries and encyclopedias; here it defines the word as a paddy whose mud is deep, a swamp paddy (沼田, numata). It is cited together with the Japanese Wikipedia article on ヨシ (yoshi, the common reed), which covers reed beds and their cutting.

Why it applies, and its limits: Japanese dictionaries are good evidence that the deep-mud wet paddy we draw was a distinct and familiar kind of field, common enough to have its own everyday word. The limits are that a dictionary defines a word rather than measures a field, so there is no depth, no extent and no date here, and that the encyclopedia and Wikipedia entries beside it are tertiary summaries; anything more than the term and its sense comes from other entries in the record.

Used for: 深田 「A paddy whose mud is deep. A marsh paddy (numata).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「どろの深い田。沼田」) - the deep-mud paddy; reed cutting after the rice harvest as a seasonal custom; the Meiji-drainage persistence and the iriai framing not on either page
