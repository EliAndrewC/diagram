zh.wikipedia 无尽藏 ("The inexhaustible treasury", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/無盡藏)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the inexhaustible treasury, the store of donated wealth kept inside temples of the Three Stages sect in the Sui and Tang, its best-known house in Chang'an, the imperial order that destroyed it, and how the scheme spread as temple lending.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries the treasury, its lending at a profit and the 713 order, which the record uses for a Chinese temple as an economic house. It is a stub resting on one Japanese article, and it gives no reason for the 713 order.

Used for: the inexhaustible treasuries lending donated wealth at a profit; the Chang'an treasury destroyed on Xuanzong's order in 713 (religion-and-death)
