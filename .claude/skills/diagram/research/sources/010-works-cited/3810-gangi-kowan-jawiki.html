ja.wikipedia 雁木 (港湾) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/雁木_(港湾))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the gangi as a harbor structure, the stepped landing of pre-modern boat landings; a short encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the stepped landing and gives its reason - the water surface rising and falling with tide and river flow against a moored hull, a gangplank laid to the step rather than to a quay wall - which is this page's hydrology; its examples are sea-facing.

Used for: the stepped landing and the moving water level (river cities)
