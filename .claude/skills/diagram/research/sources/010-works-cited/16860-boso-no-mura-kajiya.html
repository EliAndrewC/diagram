boso-no-mura-kajiya

Boso no Mura (Chiba Prefectural Museum), digital museum, "鍛冶屋" ("The smithy", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.chiba-muse.or.jp/MURA/digitalmuseum/machinami/shoka/kajiya.html)

What it is: The open-air museum's page on its rebuilt smithy, the Isumiya: what a village smith (nokaji) made and how the trade was run, the building's size and floor plan, and its fittings.

Why it applies, and its limits: The building was modeled on a surveyed village smith's workshop that stood at Arami in Narita, so its size and plan are one real workshop's, and the page describes the trade in general terms for the Kanto countryside. Its limits are that it is one reconstruction, undated beyond "once", in a museum street of shops rather than in a village, and that it says nothing of how common such workshops were or where in a village they stood.

Used for: the village smith's small family trade, and one smithy's size and plan (urban-features)
