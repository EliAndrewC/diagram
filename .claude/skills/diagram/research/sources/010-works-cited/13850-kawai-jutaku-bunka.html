kawai-jutaku-bunka

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 河合家住宅主屋 ("The main house of the Kawai residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/181523)

What it is: The national heritage database's entry for a registered tangible cultural property in Gujo, Gifu prefecture: the main house, built in 1805, of a family that served as the district's shoya, with its dimensions and building area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is one measured headman's house of the Edo period - about 12.5 by 6.5 ken, 291 sq m of building area, two stories - which gives a real size to set against the one this record draws. Its limit is that it is a single house, in a mountain town of central Japan, two-storied and tile-roofed, and it gives no ordinary farmhouse of the same place to compare it with.

Used for: the measured size of one Edo-period headman's house (homesteads)
