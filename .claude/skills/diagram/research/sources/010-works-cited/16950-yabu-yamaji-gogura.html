yabu-yamaji-gogura

Yabu City, "まちの文化財（85） 山路の郷蔵" ("The town's cultural properties (85): the Yamaji gogura", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.yabu.hyogo.jp/soshiki/kyoikuiinkai/shakaikyoiku/1/1/1992.html)

What it is: The city's page on a surviving village storehouse at Yamaji in Hyogo: the shogunate's order to build reserve storehouses, and the building standing alone with its door to the old road.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a real storehouse where it still stands, which is the one siting observation found. Its limits are that it is one building on one road, that it dates the shogunate's order a year later than the dictionaries do, and that it gives no size.

Used for: a village storehouse standing alone, door to the road (urban-features)
