gunmaibun-jori

Gunma Archaeological Research Foundation, 条里制ってなんだろう？ ("What is the jori system?", title translated) (https://gunmaibun.org/faq/faq_09)

What it is: A question-and-answer page of the Gunma Archaeological Research Foundation, the prefecture's excavating body, comparing the paddies it excavated at one site, Kamitaki-Enokimachi-kita, in the middle of the Kofun period and at the end of the Heian period, and relating the later ones to the jori land division.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an excavator's own account that Heian paddies were laid out inside large bunds and waterways whose large bunds faced east-west and north-south, so the right-angled frame is a dug and dated fact at one place, not only a land-register idea. It shows one site; the relation to the jori system is given as the accepted view, not proved; and it says the small plots inside the frame were often out of shape and did not follow the grid in detail. It gives a direction for the large bunds only, not for the waterways, and no distance between the large bunds (only the small plots' sides, 5 to 20 m). So it cannot show that the frame kept the jori's 1-cho (about 109 m) interval, or that the channels themselves ran at right angles.

Used for: excavated Heian paddies inside large bunds and waterways, the large bunds facing east-west and north-south, the small plots inside not all regular (fields)
