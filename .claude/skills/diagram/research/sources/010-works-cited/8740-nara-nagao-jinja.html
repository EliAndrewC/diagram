Nara Prefecture, cultural resources database, 長尾神社 (Nagao Shrine, Katsuragi) (https://www.pref.nara.lg.jp/ikasu-nara/bunkashigen/main00275.html)

What it is: The Nara prefectural government's cultural-resources page for Nagao Shrine in Katsuragi, an Engishiki-listed shrine, describing its approach and the horseback archery once performed along it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures the gap between two ranked gates and the way between them: from the first torii to the second at the precinct entrance is about 300 meters, and the approach is a little over 7 meters (4 ken) wide, on an approach whose horseback archery the page dates to the Kamakura period. Its limits are that it is a prefectural heritage page citing no survey; that one shrine is one instance rather than a distribution; and that what the page dates to the Kamakura period is the PRACTICE and the place name - the 300 m and the 7 m describe the approach as it now stands, and nothing on the page says either is unchanged.

Used for: about 300 m between Nagao Shrine's first and second torii, on an approach a little over 7 m (4 ken) wide. This supersedes the 200 m the record carried for the same pair
