What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kanda aqueduct, Edo's first waterworks: its course from Inokashira pond, its wooden mains under the streets, the draw-wells it fed in the tenements, and the order in which it served the city's districts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the tenements drew from shared aqueduct wells and that the samurai districts were supplied first with the remainder going to the townspeople, which is the shared-draw pattern and the status distinction this page's wells follow. A tertiary summary; it describes the shogunal capital, the largest system in the country.

Used for: tenement draw-wells shared in the alley; samurai districts served first
