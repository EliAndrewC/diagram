ja.wikipedia 後背湿地 and 自然堤防 (with GSI notes) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/後背湿地)

What it is: Japanese Wikipedia's article on 後背湿地 (kohai shicchi, the backswamp) read together with its companion article on 自然堤防 (the natural levee), explaining how a river builds raised banks along its own channel and leaves poorly drained, fine-grained lowland behind them, and how that lowland was farmed.

Why it applies, and its limits: The landform does not change with the century, so a modern description of it serves a premodern map directly, and we use it for what lies behind a river's bank - wet ground, traditionally worked as paddy, extended over time by draining marsh - which decides where our fields and our houses go on a floodplain. It is a tertiary summary, so we take the description of the landform and its traditional use and no number from it.

Used for: backswamp formation behind the natural levee and its land use; and, at the city tier, the badly drained low ground that becomes paddy rather than building ground
