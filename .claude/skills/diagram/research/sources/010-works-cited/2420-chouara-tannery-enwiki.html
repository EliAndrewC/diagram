What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Chouara tannery of Fez, the medieval tannery still worked by hand in stone vats of lime, urine and pigeon dung.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows one other tradition soaking hides in standing vats rather than a current, the pit-soaking form a ditch-fed yard on these maps falls back to. A tertiary summary of one Moroccan case; it does not speak for every other tradition.

Used for: the standing-vat soak at Fez
