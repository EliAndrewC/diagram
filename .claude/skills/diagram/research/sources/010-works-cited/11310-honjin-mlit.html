honjin-mlit

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Kanto Regional Development Bureau, Yokohama office, "The Tokaido and the facilities of its post stations: the honjin" (in Japanese; https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/03_sisetu/0701.htm)

What it is: A page of a Japanese government roads office's guide to the old Tokaido in Kanagawa prefecture, on the honjin: its origin, how the office of honjin keeper was granted, and its building, illustrated with floor plans of two Kanagawa honjin.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the honjin's building - single-story in principle, with its rooms, gate, entrance, raised room and garden - which is what a map would draw. It is a popular guide by a roads office, not a history, it gives no dimensions in its text, and its examples are Kanagawa's stations.

Used for: the honjin as a single-story building with many rooms, an imposing gate, a wide entrance, a raised room and a garden (towns)
