mlit-tokaido-bojikui

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), Kanto Regional Bureau, Yokohama office, "東海道と宿場の施設: 傍示杭" (the Tokaido and its post-town facilities: the boundary posts) (in Japanese; https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/03_sisetu/0901.htm)

What it is: One page of the road ministry's regional series on the old Tokaido through Kanagawa, on the boundary posts along the road, counted from the late-Edo register of the highway's post towns and villages and seen in Hiroshige's prints.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the posts on one stretch of the Tokaido - at both ends of most post towns and at most village boundaries - which is what places a post where a highway crosses a village's line. The count is for Kanagawa's Tokaido alone; no post survives, so the page hedges on what was written on them and judges their height from prints.

Used for: the boundary posts at a post town's ends and at the village boundaries along the highway (ways)
