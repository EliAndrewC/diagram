bunka-adachi-yakuimon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 安達家住宅薬医門 (The yakuimon of the Adachi house) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/294790)

What it is: The national database entry for a registered gate at Sakuragawa in Ibaraki prefecture, dated 1830 to 1868: a one-bay, one-doorway yakuimon, the formal gate of the house's reception rooms, opening onto the street, with its frontage in meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a surveyed width for the one-bay gate form. Its limits: the entry does not say whose house it was, so it cannot show that a samurai or an official used a gate of this size; it is one late-Edo example.

Used for: a one-bay yakuimon 2.6 m across its frontage (buildings)
