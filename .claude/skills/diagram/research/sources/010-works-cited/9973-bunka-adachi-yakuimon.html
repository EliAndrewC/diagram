bunka-adachi-yakuimon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 安達家住宅薬医門 (The yakuimon of the Adachi house) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/294790)

What it is: The national database entry for a registered gate at Sakuragawa in Ibaraki prefecture, dated 1830 to 1868: a one-bay, one-doorway yakuimon, the formal gate of the house's reception rooms, opening onto the street, with its frontage in meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a surveyed width for the one-bay gate form. Its limits: the gate entry does not name the household. The same database's entry for the house at the same address (安達家住宅見世蔵及び主屋, online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/245828) describes a merchant's shop-house, with a five-bay shop storehouse and an account area. So this is a merchant's gate in the country market town of Makabe, and it cannot show that a samurai or an official used a gate of this size. It is one late-Edo example, and the entry calls its full-tile front roof unlike anything else in the area.

Used for: a one-bay yakuimon 2.6 m across its frontage (buildings)
