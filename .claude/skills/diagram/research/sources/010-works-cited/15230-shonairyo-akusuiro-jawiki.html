ja.wikipedia 庄内領悪水路 ("the Shonairyo akusuiro", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BA%84%E5%86%85%E9%A0%98%E6%82%AA%E6%B0%B4%E8%B7%AF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on a drainage channel in Kasukabe, Saitama prefecture: a short account of how it was laid out in the Edo period as the drain of a district watered by the Nakajima canal and its branches, with its route and the river it joins.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a named, dated example of a farm drain (an akusui channel) built beside an irrigation system to carry the fields' spent water to a river. It is one channel on the Kanto lowland, not a survey, and a short encyclopedia article; it says nothing of how a single field's drain reached it, nor of drains in hill valleys.

Used for: that a farm drain carries the fields' water to a river (water, fields)
