ja.wikipedia 庄内領悪水路 ("the Shonairyo akusuiro", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BA%84%E5%86%85%E9%A0%98%E6%82%AA%E6%B0%B4%E8%B7%AF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on a drainage channel in Kasukabe, Saitama prefecture: a short account saying that in the Edo period the district was watered by the Nakajima canal and its branches, and that this channel was made the drain of those canals' fields, with the present-day channel's route and the river it joins (the Naka). It does not date the drain itself.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a named example of a farm drain (an akusui channel) serving an Edo-period irrigation system and carrying the fields' spent water to a river. The article does not say when the drain was dug, and the route it gives is today's channel: a city-managed minor river joined by a modern trunk drain, with a pumping station planned. It is one channel on the Kanto lowland, not a survey, and a short encyclopedia article; it says nothing of how a single field's drain reached it, nor of drains in hill valleys.

Used for: that a farm drain carries the fields' water to a river (water, fields)
