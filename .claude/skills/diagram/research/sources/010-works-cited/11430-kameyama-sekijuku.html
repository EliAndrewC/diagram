kameyama-sekijuku

Kameyama City, Cultural Affairs Division, 町の成り立ちと宿場の働き ("How the town was formed and how the post station worked", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.kameyama.mie.jp/docs/2014112311846/)

What it is: A page of the Kameyama city townscape-preservation office on Seki, a Tokaido post town in Mie prefecture, on how the town grew in the late sixteenth century and on its size in the 1843 survey: houses, inhabitants, the lords' inns and the ordinary inns.

Why it applies, and its limits: Seki survives nearly whole as a preservation district, so its 1843 house count can be set against its measured length, which is how this record derives a post town's density. It is one station, a prosperous one at a road junction, and the page gives the counts only; the length comes from the preservation-district page.

Used for: Seki's 632 houses and 42 inns in 1843 (towns)
