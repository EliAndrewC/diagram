kotobank-kurayashiki

Kotobank, 蔵屋敷 - 日本大百科全書 and 世界大百科事典 entries (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%94%B5%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7-58201)

What it is: The kotobank reference page for the kurayashiki, the domains' warehouse compounds at Osaka, carrying the entries of two Japanese reference encyclopedias: their concentration on Nakanoshima and the Tosabori and Edobori rivers for the convenience of water transport, and the sale of their rice through the Dojima brokers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the waterside siting of the rice warehouses from standard reference works, which is why our granaries stand at the wharf. Its limit is the encyclopedia's summary form. The kurayashiki was an institution of the commercial capital, where domains sold their rice; that a domain seat's own granaries stood at its wharf in the same way is this page's inference.

Used for: the warehouse compounds concentrated on the rivers for water transport
