kanzaki-hamlet-shrines

Kushida-gu, 神埼町 周辺 (the hamlet shrines of Kanzaki), quoting the 1915 Kanzaki district shrine register (http://www5b.biglobe.ne.jp/~kusidagu/siryou/chinju.html)

What it is: A page written in September 2006 by Shigyō Takenori, then head priest of Kushida-gu in Saga, on the hamlet shrines under his care, quoting for many of them the district shrine register (entries of about 1880 and of 1915) with its precinct area, and for several the Saga domain's temple-and-shrine returns of 1663-1859, which give the Edo site area in se and bu.

Why it applies, and its limits: Nine unranked hamlet shrines give the small end of the band. Its limits: registers of about 1880 and 1915, made after the Meiji survey of 1877 had cut precincts to their core and taken the rest as state land (one shrine's former grounds of 7 tan 5 se kept 1 tan), so these areas can understate the Edo grounds; a flat Kyushu farming district; nine entries picked from one priest's jurisdiction. The same page's Edo domain figures (mostly 2 to 8 se, about 60 to 240 tsubo, a few 1 tan or more) are the closer check on the band.

Used for: hamlet-shrine precinct areas, Saga (religion-and-death.html).
