kanzaki-hamlet-shrines

Kushida-gu, 神埼町 周辺 (the hamlet shrines of Kanzaki), quoting the 1915 Kanzaki district shrine register (http://www5b.biglobe.ne.jp/~kusidagu/siryou/chinju.html)

What it is: A page written in 2006 by the head priest of Kushida-gu in Saga on the hamlet shrines under his care, quoting each one's 1915 register entry, precinct area included.

Why it applies, and its limits: Nine unranked hamlet shrines give the small end of the band. Its limits: a register of 1915, a flat Kyushu farming district, and nine entries chosen by one priest's jurisdiction.

Used for: hamlet-shrine precinct areas, Saga (religion-and-death.html).
