ehime-kenshi-joka-shokunin

Ehime Prefecture, Ehime kenshi: kinsei ge (1987), chapter 6, section 3, part 2, "城下と職人町の形成" ("The castle town and the forming of the craftsmen's streets", title translated from the Japanese by this project), in the database Ehime no kioku (in Japanese; https://www.i-manabi.jp/system/regionals/regionals/ecode:2/63/view/7977)

What it is: The prefectural history's account of how the castle towns of Iyo gathered craftsmen, gave them house plots and set them in streets by trade, with the domains' registers of their retained craftsmen.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a scholarly prefectural history drawing on the domains' own registers, so its account of how craftsmen were settled in a castle town is well grounded. Its limits are that it describes the domains of one province, Iyo, and that its counts are of craftsmen retained by the lord rather than of every craftsman in the town.

Used for: castle towns gathering their craftsmen into streets by trade (urban-features)
