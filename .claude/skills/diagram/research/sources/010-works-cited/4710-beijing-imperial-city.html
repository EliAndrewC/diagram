Imperial City, Beijing (Wikipedia) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_City,_Beijing)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article "Imperial City, Beijing", on the walled precinct that enclosed the Forbidden City and the imperial services around it. The passage we take describes the Corridor of a Thousand Steps south of Chengtianmen, with the offices of the government ministries lining either side of it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It answers a layout question for a capital map: the bureaucracy sits on the ceremonial avenue outside the palace wall rather than inside it, which decides where the ministry compounds are drawn. That particular sentence is flagged in the article as lacking a citation, so we treat the arrangement as a plausible shape to draw rather than as a documented fact.

Used for: the Six Ministries' offices flanking the Corridor of a Thousand Steps outside Chengtianmen - the bureaucracy on the ceremonial avenue OUTSIDE the palace walls
