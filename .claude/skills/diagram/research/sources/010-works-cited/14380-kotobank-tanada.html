kotobank-tanada

Kotobank, 棚田 ("terraced paddy", title translated from the Japanese by this project), entries from the Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica) and the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%A3%9A%E7%94%B0-93872)

What it is: The Japanese reference-site entries for the terraced paddy from two national encyclopedias: the narrow strip fields and the high stone-kept walls between them on steep ground, the old register notation for how many paddies made up a tan, and the terraces' share of Japan's paddy.

Why it applies, and its limits: Two signed encyclopedia entries describe the form and give a period notation for the size of a terrace paddy. They give no wall height in meters, and the notation is one example, not a count; the share of paddy land is a modern national figure.

Used for: the terrace's narrow strips and high walls, and four paddies to a tan (archetypes)
