ja.wikipedia 旧金毘羅大芝居 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/旧金毘羅大芝居)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kanamaruza at Kotohira, the playhouse of 1835 in the town before the Kotohira shrine's gate, the oldest surviving playhouse in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a permanent roofed playhouse standing in a shrine town, the case nearest to the provincial-town theater this page draws. A tertiary summary; the article does not say the house stood on the shrine's own ground.

Used for: the Kanamaruza of 1835 in Kotohira's gate-front town
