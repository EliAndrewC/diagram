ja.wikipedia 旧金毘羅大芝居 ("Old Konpira Grand Theater", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/旧金毘羅大芝居)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kanamaruza at Kotohira, the playhouse of 1835 in the town before the Kotohira shrine's gate, the oldest surviving playhouse in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a permanent roofed playhouse standing in a shrine town, the case nearest to the provincial-town theater this page draws. A tertiary summary; the article does not say the house stood on the shrine's own ground. It gives the building's measures as built, the one Edo-period playhouse whose size this record has read; it was modeled on an Osaka theater by one account, which is the ground for taking its size for a city house, and a pilgrimage town's playhouse may have been smaller or larger than a capital's.

Used for: the Kanamaruza of 1835 in Kotohira's gate-front town; its measures, its Osaka model and the temporary huts before it (urban-features)
