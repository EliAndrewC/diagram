kamigamo-shake-plan

Kyoto City, 上賀茂伝統的建造物群保存地区保存計画 ("Preservation plan for the Kamigamo preservation district for groups of traditional buildings") (in Japanese; https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/tokei/page/0000281306.html)

What it is: The Kyoto city office's preservation plan for the shake-machi of Kamigamo, the quarter beside the Kamigamo shrine where its priest families lived from the Muromachi period, describing the quarter's history and its buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows where a shrine's priest families lived and that their houses were a style of their own, walled and gated, distinct from the townhouses. Its limits: a shrine's priests, not a temple's clergy; one exceptional quarter, the only one designated under that name; the buildings are those standing today, most of the Edo period or later.

Used for: where clergy families lived outside a precinct and what their houses looked like (religion-and-death.html).
