l7r-geisha-houses

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Justifiable Homicide Cases" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#justifiable-homicide-cases) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master: a set of court cases for a city magistrate, one of which turns on a young clerk employed at the largest geisha house in a city's district, where gambling, opium and prostitution are abundant.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: where the setting's prostitution goes on is the setting's to decide, and these maps follow it. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; they do not say where in a city the geisha houses stand or how large they are.

Used for: the geisha houses as where a city's trade goes on, behind why no map draws a walled pleasure quarter (Trades and services)
