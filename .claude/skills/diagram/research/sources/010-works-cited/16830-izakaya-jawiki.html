ja.wikipedia 居酒屋 ("izakaya, drinking house", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%B1%85%E9%85%92%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the drinking house, mostly on the modern trade, with a section on how the Edo drinking house grew out of the sake retailer who let customers drink on the spot.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Edo more than 1,800 drinking houses in the 1820s and says the countryside was slow to get them, not until the later eighteenth century - the contrast between a great city and a village. A tertiary summary; the Edo count is reported, not sourced on the page to a named survey.

Used for: Edo's drinking houses in the 1820s and their late arrival in the villages (urban-features)
