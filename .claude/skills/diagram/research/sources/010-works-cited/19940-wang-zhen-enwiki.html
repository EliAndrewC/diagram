Wikipedia (English), "Wang Zhen (inventor)" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Zhen_(inventor))

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Wang Zhen, the Yuan-dynasty official who wrote the Nong Shu, giving the book's date and an outline of the technologies it describes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a modern encyclopedia summary, used only to date the Nong Shu to 1313; it says nothing of the book's manure chapter or of how widely its methods were practiced, and its interest is mostly in the book's machines and printing.

Used for: the Nong Shu dated to 1313 (archetypes)
