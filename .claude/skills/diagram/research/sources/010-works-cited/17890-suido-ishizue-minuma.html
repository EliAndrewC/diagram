suido-ishizue-minuma

Agricultural and Rural Development Information Center (ARIC), Suido no Ishizue, "The fruit of the early-modern irrigation and drainage system: the Minuma substitute canal" (in Japanese; https://www.suido-ishizue.jp/daichi/part2/05/07.html)

What it is: A page of an agricultural-engineering association's illustrated history of Japanese land and water works, on the Minuma canal dug in 1727-28 under the shogun Yoshimune by Izawa Yasobei of the Kishu school, and the reclamation of the Minuma reservoir that went with it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates to 1728 a reclamation laid out with supply canals along the edge of the former reservoir and a drain down its center, which is the separated layout in an Edo-period work. It is a short popular history by an industry body, describing one large shogunal project on the Kanto plain rather than a village's own ditches, and it says nothing of how the plots inside were fed.

Used for: that supply canals along the high edge and a drain on the low line were laid out in 1728 (water)
