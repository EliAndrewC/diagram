tatebayashi-nagayamon

Tatebayashi City, 長屋門 ("row-house gate", title translated from the Japanese by this project), on the site of the Takajomachi samurai residence "Butakan" (in Japanese; https://www.city.tatebayashi.gunma.jp/sp007/030/20210106180858.html)

What it is: A short page by the city of Tatebayashi (Gunma prefecture) on the gate range at its preserved samurai-house site, opening with a sentence on what a nagayamon was.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its general sentence says the nagayamon was one form of an early-modern samurai residence's front gate and also served as a storeroom and as servants' quarters. The gate it describes is not historical evidence: it was probably built in the Taisho period (1912-1926), and the page says no Edo-period gate range survives in the old castle town; only the general sentence is used.

Used for: the gate range as storeroom and servants' quarters (government)
