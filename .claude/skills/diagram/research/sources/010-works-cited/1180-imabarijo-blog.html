今治城 スタッフブログ (Imabari Castle staff blog), post of 2014-08-30 (http://imabarijo.blogspot.com/2014/08/blog-post_30.html)

What it is: The staff blog of Imabari Castle, run by the city museum that keeps the castle, describing the moat's exchange of seawater with the harbor and how its level rises and falls with the tide.

Why it applies, and its limits: The castle's own custodians on their own moat, for the tidal exchange our seaside capital's moat could show. Its limits are that it is a blog post describing the moat as it exchanges water with the modern port today, so that the Edo arrangement was the same is an inference, and that it says nothing about springs.

Used for: Imabari's moat exchanging tidewater with the sea
