shosai-kotobank

Kotobank, 書斎 (shosai, the study) - the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the Nipponica entry by Hirai Kiyoshi (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9B%B8%E6%96%8E-534509)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the study, with dictionary definitions and a Nipponica encyclopedia entry by the architectural historian Hirai Kiyoshi tracing it from the medieval writing desk built into a monk's room to the shoin and the gakumonsho of the court.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in so many words that a study could be a room or a building, which is the question a detached study raises. It is a tertiary summary; its historical examples are temples and the imperial court, not warrior houses, and it gives no size for a detached study.

Used for: a study as a room in a private house; a shoin or gakumonsho as a room or a building (buildings)
