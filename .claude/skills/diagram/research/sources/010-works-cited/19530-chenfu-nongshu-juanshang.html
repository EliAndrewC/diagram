chenfu-nongshu-juanshang

陳旉 農書 卷上 ("Treatise on Agriculture, Book One", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (Chen Fu, Nongshu, completed 1149), Chinese Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%BE%B2%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7%E4%B8%8A)

What it is: The first of the three books of Chen Fu's farming manual of the Song dynasty, its fourteen chapters on farming, in a Wikisource transcription whose editor is not named; among them the siting of fields by the lie of the land (地勢之宜篇), weeding (薅耘之宜篇) and the seedbed (善其根苗篇).

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a primary text on the wet-rice farming of the lower Yangtze, written centuries before the setting's period, and gives a pond sized to the field it waters, the draining of paddies one plot at a time for weeding, and a seedbed's water kept shallow or deep by the weather. It is about China, not Japan; its pond of two or three mu in ten is a rule of thumb for high fields, not a survey, and its advice on water depth is for a seedbed just sown and gives no figure.

Used for: the pond given two or three mu in every ten of the field it waters; the paddies drained plot by plot for weeding and the seedbed's shallow, sun-warmed water (fields)
