What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the karausu or fumi-usu, the treadle mortar in which a foot-worked pestle pounds grain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the treadle pestle this page puts in a rice dealer's shop. A tertiary summary used for the tool; it does not say where the tool stood. The article is a stub carrying the encyclopedia's own notice that it needs sources, and it dates and locates the tool nowhere.
