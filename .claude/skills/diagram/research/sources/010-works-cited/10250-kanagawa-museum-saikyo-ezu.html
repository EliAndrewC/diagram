Kanagawa Prefectural Museum of Cultural History, 「September 2021's featured object: the ruling map for the field boundary and mountain dispute between Nanazawa and Susugaya villages」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「七沢村と煤ヶ谷村田畑境并山論裁許絵図」) (https://ch.kanagawa-museum.jp/monthly_choice/2021_09)

What it is: A curator's page on a map of 1649 in the museum's collection, drawn when inspectors sent by the shogunate ruled on a boundary dispute between two villages of Sagami province, and on what such ruling maps were.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes one dated ruling that fixed a village boundary through fields and hill land as a drawn line, with the land on either side assigned. It is one map, which the page itself calls unusual in its paper and its colored line.

Used for: that a disputed village boundary was fixed as a ruled line on a map (vegetation)
