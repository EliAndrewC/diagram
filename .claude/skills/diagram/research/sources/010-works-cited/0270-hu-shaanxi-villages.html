Zhai Zhouyan et al., "Identification of landscape genes in the cultural heritage of Shaanxi traditional villages", Progress in Geography 36(9): 1067 (2017) (https://www.geog.com.cn/article/2017/1007-6301/1007-6301-36-9-1067.shtml)

What it is: A peer-reviewed survey identifying landscape genes across traditional villages in Shaanxi, with worked cases - Shenquan, with a stream to the west of the village, and Wanfu, with a stream on each side flowing around and past it.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its recurring verbs are the evidence: streams that flow AROUND AND PAST a village, on a named side. The two worked cases differ in strength - Wanfu has a stream on each side flowing around and past it, while Shenquan's runs west of the village and through the whole of its DOMAIN before reaching the river below, which says less than the first. Its limits: Shaanxi is a different region from the Japan these maps model first, the analysis is of ideal configurations as much as surveyed fact, and the paper states no case of a village divided by its own stream - an absence, which is weaker than a statement. Two more limits travel with it: its sample spans three regions and building traditions, from northern Shaanxi's cliff cave-dwellings to southern Shaanxi's courtyard and stone-slab houses (Wanfu, the village quoted here, is in the south); and the 35 villages are heritage-listed survivors chosen for typicality, so they are a selection rather than a distribution.

Used for: streams flowing around and past a village on a named side
