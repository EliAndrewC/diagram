ja.wikipedia 舟橋 ("pontoon bridge", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%88%9F%E6%A9%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the pontoon bridge, with Japanese examples: the temporary bridges laid for the shogun over the Kiso rivers, and the permanent bridge at Toyama.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that a bridge of boats was the exception - laid for great processions, or kept at one castle town - while the rivers were ordinarily ferried. The article carries a banner saying its footnotes are insufficient, and its figures for the Toyama bridge are its own, unreferenced.

Used for: the pontoon bridge and how rare it was (ways)
