toyokawa-akasaka-juku

Toyokawa City, 赤坂宿 ("Akasaka-juku", the post station) (in Japanese; https://www.city.toyokawa.lg.jp/kanko_bunka_sports/rekishi/2/2/11746.html)

What it is: Toyokawa city's page on Akasaka, a Tokaido post station, describing its relay office, its lords' lodgings, its inns and the surviving inn Ohashiya, with counts from a 1733 record.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the rooms an inn was made of - bath and privy among them - says the road front was usually two stories, and gives the frontage and depth of one large inn and of a lord's lodging. It is one station on the busiest highway, written by the city for visitors and citing no documents beyond the 1733 count; the sizes are given as approximate.

Used for: the rooms of a post-station inn and one large inn's frontage and depth (buildings)
