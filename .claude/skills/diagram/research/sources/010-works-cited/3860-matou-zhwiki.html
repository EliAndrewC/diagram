matou-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 碼頭 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/碼頭)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the matou, the wharf or landing, in its two forms; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the projecting mole and the flight of steps as the two forms of a landing, which this page draws; it names no material and ranks neither form; a general modern definition that dates neither form, so the premodern attestation of the step rests on the Hiroshima gangi page.

Used for: the two forms of a Chinese landing (river cities)
