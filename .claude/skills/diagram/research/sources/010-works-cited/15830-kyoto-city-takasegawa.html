kyoto-city-takasegawa

Kyoto City, 都市史22 高瀬川 ("Urban history 22: the Takase river", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www2.city.kyoto.lg.jp/somu/rekishi/fm/nenpyou/htmlsheet/toshi22.html)

What it is: A page of Kyoto city's own urban-history series on the Takase canal: its route and length, the takasebune (their average size and the number licensed around 1710), and the count of boat-entry basins along it over the Edo period, from the old maps.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the city's historical office describing a real Edo city canal with figures drawn from period records, the best-grounded account of the canal read. It is one canal in the imperial capital and gives the boats' size rather than the channel's width.

Used for: the canal's length, the boats that worked it, and how many boat-entry basins lined its city stretch (cities/river-cities)
