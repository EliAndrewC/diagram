水田用水の番水技術の実態と論理, 農業農村工学会誌 (Journal of the Japanese Society of Irrigation, Drainage and Rural Engineering) 77(7): 559- (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjsidre/77/7/77_559/_article/-char/ja)

What it is: A four-page article by Tajima Masahiro, Ishii Atsushi and Miwa Isao in the 2009 volume of the journal of the Japanese Society of Irrigation, Drainage and Rural Engineering, on bansui, the rotation of paddy water: what it is, how blocks, intervals and order are set, and when it is used.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states from the engineering literature that rotation is the regime switched to in drought while normal supply is continuous. Its limits are that it describes modern irrigation districts, that only its abstract was read, and that the journal carries commissioned pieces beside refereed ones and the landing page does not say which this is.

Used for: rotation as the drought regime of paddy water supply
