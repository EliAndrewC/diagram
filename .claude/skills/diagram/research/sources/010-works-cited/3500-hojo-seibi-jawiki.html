What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on hojo seibi, the postwar consolidation of paddy plots into machine-sized rectangles; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the standard plot sizes of the mechanization era and the rectangle as the basic shape, which is the modern look this page tells the reader to avoid; it carries no dates for the program's standards.

Used for: the machine-era plot as an anachronism (archetypes)
