itami-okada-sakagura

Itami City, Culture Promotion Division, "旧岡田家住宅" ("The former Okada residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.itami.lg.jp/SOSIKI/TOSHIKATSURYOKU/BUNKA/bunnkazai/SINAI_BUNKAZAI/KUNI_SITEI/1386841790741.html)

What it is: The city's page on a nationally designated Important Cultural Property in the old brewing town of Itami: a shop built in 1674 by its ridge tag and a sake brewery built behind it about 1715, with the owners' history, each building's dimensions and structure, and the restoration.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured size of a surviving brewery hall of the early eighteenth century, 15.7 by 14.8 m with a two-story main body, and says later owners added a second brewery and, in 1804-30, a larger one, so it is a dated Edo-period anchor for how big a brewer's hall was. Its limits: it is one brewer's premises in Itami, one of the two great export-brewing towns of the period, so it may run large for a provincial seat; the later halls are named but not measured; and the buildings were altered and restored, the page reporting the survey's reading of the original form.

Used for: the size of a brewery hall of about 1715 (urban-features)
