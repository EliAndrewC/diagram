ja.wikipedia 多久聖廟 ("the Taku Confucian shrine", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/多久聖廟)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Confucian shrine of Taku, in Hizen, completed in 1708 at the foot of Mt. Shiibara by Taku Shigefumi, lord of Taku, whose school (founded 1699, later the Tōgen Shōsha) had until then kept the Confucius image in its lecture hall; it gives the hall's size in bays.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a small lord's school and Confucian hall that survive, sized in bays. Taku was a sub-fief of the Saga domain, not a domain seat, and the article gives bays, not feet. The article is a short stub with no references of its own: its bay count is the national designation's building formula, whose primary is the Agency for Cultural Affairs' database of designated properties, which the article links. The hall was reroofed in 1907, tile to copper sheet, so its roof is not as built.

Used for: how big a domain school's Confucian hall was (religion-and-death)
