ja.wikipedia 多久聖廟 ("the Taku Confucian shrine", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/多久聖廟)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Confucian shrine of Taku, in Hizen, built in 1708 by the lord of Taku beside the school he had founded in 1699, with its size in bays.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a small lord's school and Confucian hall that survive, sized in bays. Taku was a sub-fief of the Saga domain, not a domain seat, and the article gives bays, not feet.

Used for: how big a domain school's Confucian hall was (religion-and-death)
