ja.wikipedia 石畳 ("stone paving", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%9F%B3%E7%95%B3)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on stone paving: why roads were paved at all (bare earth turns to mud), with a short section on Japan naming the old highways' paved stretches.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the shogunate ordered the highways' difficult stretches paved with stone and names where that paving survives, which bounds how much of a Japanese road was stone. Its general account of the bare-earth road is not about Japan in particular, and the article carries a banner saying it lacks sources; it says nothing of village lanes.

Used for: the bare-earth road and its mud, and stone paving only on the highways' difficult stretches (ways)
