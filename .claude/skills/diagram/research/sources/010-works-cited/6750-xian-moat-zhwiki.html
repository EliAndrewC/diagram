zh.wikipedia 西安城墙 (moat section) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article 西安城墙 (the city wall of Xi'an), in the section on its moat - a tertiary encyclopedia entry on the Ming dynasty wall that still rings the old city, and on the water ditch outside it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dimensions of a great city's moat - 20 m wide, 10 m deep, water all year - which is the top of the range our moats are measured against. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary; that this is a former imperial capital, the top of the scale and not a provincial town's ditch; and that the figures describe the moat as it stands today, dredged and landscaped in the modern era - the article dates the wall's construction and rebuilds but gives no period source for the moat's size, so the number is a modern measurement of a much-repaired work rather than a Ming dimension.

Used for: 「On the outer side of the city wall there is a moat, 20 meters wide and 10 meters deep, holding water all year round, which together with the wall forms a complete defensive system.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「城墙的外侧有护城河，宽20米，深10米，终年积水，和城墙共同构成完整的防御体系」)
