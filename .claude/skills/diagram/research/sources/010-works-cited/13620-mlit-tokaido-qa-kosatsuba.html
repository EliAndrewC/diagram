Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Kanto Regional Development Bureau, Yokohama National Highway Office, Tokaido Q&A 22, "What is a kosatsuba?" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「高札場」（こうさつば）って、なんのこと？」) (in Japanese; https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/04_qa/index2/a0222.htm)

What it is: A short question-and-answer page in the national highway office's public guide to the Tokaido, giving the usual height and frontage of the notice boards of the nine Kanagawa post towns and describing the reconstructed board at Kanagawa: a stone base, a fence, the boards in two tiers and a roof.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that describes a board's whole form - base, fence and roof together - but it describes a modern reconstruction of one post town's board, written for a general audience, and gives ranges rather than measurements. It says nothing of village boards.

Used for: the notice board's stone base, fence and roof, and its usual height and frontage (urban-features)
