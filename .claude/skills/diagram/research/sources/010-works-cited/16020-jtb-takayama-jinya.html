jtb-takayama-jinya

Japan Travel Bureau Foundation (公益財団法人日本交通公社), 全国観光資源台帳 (National Register of Tourism Resources), 高山陣屋 ("Takayama Jin'ya", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://tabi.jtb.or.jp/res/210022-)

What it is: The foundation's register entry for the Takayama Jin'ya, the shogunate's intendant's office for Hida province, with a short description of the site: the office, the intendant's residence, and the rice storehouse moved there from the third bailey of the former Takayama castle.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that an intendant's office held its rice storehouse inside its own grounds, which is one of the two attested sitings for our Emperor's granaries. It is a brief tourism-register description with no references, of one office in a mountain province that inherited a castle's storehouse rather than building its own.

Used for: the rice storehouse inside the intendant's office grounds, moved from the castle's third bailey (cities/capitals)
