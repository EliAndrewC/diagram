esri-prevent-label-overlap

Esri, ArcGIS Pro documentation, "Prevent labels from overlapping certain features" (https://pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/help/mapping/text/prevent-labels-from-overlapping-certain-features.htm)

What it is: A help page of Esri's ArcGIS Pro on keeping labels off chosen features, including how the Maplex engine moves a label that cannot avoid a road so that it crosses as few roads as possible.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one readable rule found for a label that must cross a line: cross one road rather than several. It is software documentation for modern road maps; no source found gives the older cartographic advice to cross a line at a right angle.

Used for: a label that must cross a way crosses as few as possible (presentation)
