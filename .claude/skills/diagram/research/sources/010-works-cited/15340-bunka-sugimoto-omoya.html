bunka-sugimoto-omoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 杉本家住宅 主屋 ("Sugimoto family residence, main house", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/179065)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record of the main house of the Sugimoto residence in Kyoto, an Important Cultural Property designated in 2010: its building areas, its form, and the storehouses and wall of the estate.

Why it applies, and its limits: The state's own description of a large surviving merchant town house of Kyoto, for the arrangement of a great merchant's estate. The main house was built in 1870, after the Edo period, though its storehouses are older; it is one house in Kyoto, the imperial capital and one of the three great cities of the Edo period (Edo itself was larger), so it shows the form rather than how common it was; and the record describes the arrangement in words and gives the building areas of the shop and living parts, not a drawn plan or the size of the lot.

Used for: a great merchant's shop-house, its storehouses behind and its wall round the estate (cities/fabric.html)
