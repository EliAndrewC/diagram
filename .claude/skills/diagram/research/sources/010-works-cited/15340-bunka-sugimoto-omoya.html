bunka-sugimoto-omoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 杉本家住宅 主屋 ("Sugimoto family residence, main house", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/179065)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record of the main house of the Sugimoto residence in Kyoto, an Important Cultural Property designated in 2010: its building areas, its form, and the storehouses and wall of the estate.

Why it applies, and its limits: The state's own description of one of the largest surviving merchant town houses of Kyoto, for the plan of a great merchant's estate. The main house was built in 1870, after the Edo period, though its storehouses are older; it is one house in the largest city of its day, so it shows the form rather than how common it was.

Used for: a great merchant's shop-house, its storehouses behind and its wall round the estate (cities/fabric.html)
