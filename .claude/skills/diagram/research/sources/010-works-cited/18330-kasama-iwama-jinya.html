kasama-iwama-jinya

Kasama City, 「Exploring Kasama's history」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「笠間の歴史探訪」) (in Japanese; https://www.city.kasama.lg.jp/sp/page/page006384.html)

What it is: A page of Kasama City's (Ibaraki) local-history series, on the Iwama jin'ya, the local administrative seat a domain kept in the district: its grounds, gate, wall and what stood inside.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one official compound read that keeps a vegetable garden inside its enclosure - about two tan of it, worked, it is said, by the jin'ya's own officials. Its limits are that it reports a tradition ("it is said"), not a plan; that it does not say where in the grounds the garden lay; and that it is one jin'ya.

Used for: whether an official compound kept a vegetable garden inside its walls (buildings.html).
