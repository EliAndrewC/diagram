What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on foxtail millet: the plant's height and ears, its varieties, and its sowing and harvest months in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: Foxtail millet is one of the oldest grains of Japan and China, and the plant's height and ripening color are botany rather than practice. A tertiary encyclopedia article. Its height (about 150 cm) and its months come from modern popular books on grains (Hayashi 1998, Taira 2017), and the yellow drooping ears have no citation at all. The months (sown May to June, harvested late September to October) are the general modern Japanese ones for one growing pattern. The article itself separates a spring millet for cold regions from a summer millet for warm ones, and notes millet grown between rows of wheat or after it, so a place modeled on north China may sow and harvest on a different calendar. It gives no field layout.

Used for: foxtail millet's height, its yellow drooping ears, and its season (fields)
