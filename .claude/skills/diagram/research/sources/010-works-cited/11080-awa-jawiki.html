What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on foxtail millet: the plant's height and ears, its varieties, and its sowing and harvest months in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: Foxtail millet is one of the oldest grains of Japan and China, and the plant's height and ripening color are botany rather than practice. A tertiary encyclopedia article; the months it gives are the general modern Japanese ones, and it gives no field layout.

Used for: foxtail millet's height, its yellow drooping ears, and its season (fields)
