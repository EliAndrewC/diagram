shangxinhe-gazetteer

Jiangsu provincial gazetteer site on Shangxinhe; Wikipedia/Baidu, Shangxinhe (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%8A%E6%96%B0%E6%B2%B3%E9%95%87)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on 上新河镇 (Shangxinhe), the timber town that grew on a Ming-built side channel off the Yangtze at Nanjing, with references to the Qing gazetteer of the two counties. It describes the rafts moored in mass along the banks at the channel's mouth, the timber market that became a town, and the trade's Qing peak; it gives no measurement of the channel.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a real case of a timber town made by its moorings - rafts along the bank, a market on the shore, a town behind it - which is the arrangement our river timber towns copy. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary standing on gazetteer material; that it is one Chinese river port applied to our timber-town features; and that it carries no measurement of any kind, so a channel length is ours and unsourced.

Used for: Nanjing's side-channel timber market (no length is on the page); rafts moored in mass along the banks; the Qing-peak "constantly sufficient through all seasons" trade
