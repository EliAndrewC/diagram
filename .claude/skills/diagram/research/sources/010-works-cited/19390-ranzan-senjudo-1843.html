ranzan-senjudo-1843

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 村差出明細帳下書 天保14年：千手堂 関根関太郎家文書 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/01_10.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription, from the town history of 1968, of a draft of the account of itself that Senjudō village (Hiki district, Musashi) submitted in 1843: its yield, its fields, its 33 households and two monks, its two temples, and the halls and shrines with the tax-exempt land each held.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an Edo record of the same village whose two temples, Senjuin and Kōshōji, appear in the district's post-1868 temple register, so it sets Edo figures beside the register's. Its limits: it gives the exempt land held for a hall, fields included, not the precinct's area, and no main hall's size; it is a draft kept by one house; and Senjudō is a small hill village, below the scale of a town.

Used for: the Edo exempt holdings of two of the register's temples and a hall's size in 1843 (religion-and-death.html).
