seitai-kobo-kaibori-2017

Hayashi Norio (author), Yagi Ai and Kataoka Tomomi (eds.), 『よみがえれ！私たちの池 かいぼりがわかる本』 ("Come back to life, our pond! A book for understanding kaibori", title translated from the Japanese by this project), Seitai Kobo (認定NPO法人 生態工房), first edition 10 February 2017 (in Japanese; https://eco-works.gr.jp/pdf/kaibori_booklet_2.pdf)

What it is: An illustrated booklet for the general public by a Tokyo conservation NPO, written by a curator of the Chiba Prefectural Central Museum, explaining kaibori - draining a pond to dredge its bottom and catch its fish - and why the NPO revives it at ponds such as Inokashira in Tokyo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in plain words what the traditional draining of a pond yielded to the farmers who used it - the dredged mud as fertilizer, the fish as food. It is a modern conservation booklet, not a historical study: it says "in the past" without a date or a place, and its case ponds are urban park ponds, not village tameike.

Used for: what a pond's draining yielded to the farmers who used it (water)
