yamaguchi-ouchi-ido

Yamaguchi City, 大内時代の井戸 ("A well of the Ouchi era", title translated), in the city's register of historical and cultural resources (in Japanese; https://www.city.yamaguchi.lg.jp/rs/rekibunshigen/r450.html)

What it is: A city register entry for a stone-lined well of the Muromachi period, excavated about 100 m southeast of the Ouchi residence site and preserved under a school, with its inner diameter (about 1 m) and depth.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures a premodern Japanese well shaft from the excavated thing itself. It is one well, in a lord's town rather than a village, and it measures the shaft below ground, not the curb that stood over it.

Used for: the size of a premodern well shaft (urban-features)
