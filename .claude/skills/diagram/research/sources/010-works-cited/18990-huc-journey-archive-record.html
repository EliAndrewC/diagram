huc-journey-archive-record

Internet Archive, catalog record for Huc, A journey through the Chinese empire, vol. II (https://archive.org/details/journeythroughch02huceiala)

What it is: The Internet Archive's catalog page for the scanned volume, giving its author, publisher (New York: Harper) and publication date, 1855.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the edition whose scanned text this record's Huc citations quote, which the scanned text itself does not show. The 1855 is the date of this English edition by Harper, not of the account itself: the French original, L'Empire chinois, appeared in Paris in 1854. Huc's travels through China were in 1844-1846, so this date cannot date anything he describes. The scanner's own note on the page calls the exact publication date unknown; the 1855 is the catalog's.

Used for: the year of the English edition the record quotes, 1855 (Towns)
