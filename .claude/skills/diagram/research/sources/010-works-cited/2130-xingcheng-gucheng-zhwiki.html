What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the old walled city of Xingcheng in Liaoning, the best-preserved Ming garrison city, with its walls, gates and the bell-and-drum tower at its central crossroads.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tower's 21 m square platform pierced by a cross vault and the city's character as a Ming garrison, which is the street-arch tower type this page keeps off a small county seat. A tertiary summary used for the dimension and the type. The dimensions carry no citation on the page, whose one reference is the heritage listing, and they describe the platform after its repair of 1777.

Used for: Xingcheng's 21 m tower over the crossroads in a garrison city
