biwa-jawiki

ja.wikipedia ビワ ("loquat", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ビワ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the loquat, covering the tree, its cultivation and its uses, with a section on folk belief.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records the folk belief that a loquat planted in a garden or a homestead brings unending sickness, which bears on whether a farmstead kept one. It gives no date or region for the belief and does not say how widely it was followed.

Used for: the folk belief that kept loquat out of the homestead (vegetation)
