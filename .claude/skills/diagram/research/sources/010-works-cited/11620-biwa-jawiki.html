ja.wikipedia ビワ ("loquat", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ビワ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the loquat (a tertiary, community-edited encyclopedia article, revision of 2026-08-25), covering the tree, its cultivation in modern Japan and its uses, with a section on folk belief.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records the folk belief that a loquat planted in a garden or a homestead brings unending sickness, which bears on whether a farmstead kept one. It is a tertiary summary. It carries the belief only through two footnotes, both 20th-century Japanese folklore records: an article in the folklore journal Kyōdo Kenkyū (1916) and the Miyagi prefectural history's folklore volume (1956). The Miyagi one is from a cold northern prefecture near the edge of the loquat's range. Neither shows the belief in the Edo period, and nothing here gives a Chinese counterpart. It does not say how widely the belief was followed. The same article says Edo-period temples kept many loquats for leaf medicine and that garden loquats are common south of Chiba. So it shows only that the belief was recorded, not that farmsteads went without the tree.

Used for: the recorded folk belief that a loquat planted at the house kept illness in it (vegetation)
