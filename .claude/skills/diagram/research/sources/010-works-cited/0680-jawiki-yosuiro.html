jawiki-yosuiro

ja.wikipedia 用水路 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/用水路)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the yosuiro, the supply channel: a channel built to draw water for irrigation, drinking or industry, taking its water by a weir, dam, wheel or pump or by natural inflow, and distinguished from a channel whose purpose is drainage alone.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the plain distinction between a channel that brings water and one that takes it away, which is the distinction the map's two ditch classes draw. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary with no history of when the two were separated and nothing on where drainage went, so those rest on other sources; and that the page itself says a supply channel today often carries rainwater and household waste water too, so the clean two-way split is the older form rather than the present one.

Used for: the supply channel defined, and set apart from a channel for drainage alone
