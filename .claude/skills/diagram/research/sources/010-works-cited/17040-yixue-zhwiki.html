What it is: A stub article on the Qing charity schools, quoting the Kangxi order of 1713 to set up many in the provinces, prefectures and counties, and giving the pupils' ages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Chinese state's elementary school below the capital. Its limits are that it is a stub with a banner saying it cites no sources, and that it says nothing of the school's building or where it stood.
