yixue-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia, 义学 (学校) ("Yixue, the charity school", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%BE%A9%E5%AD%B8_(%E5%AD%B8%E6%A0%A1))

What it is: A stub article on the Qing charity schools, quoting the Kangxi order of 1713 (the page's date; the 51st year of Kangxi is 1712) to set up many in the provinces, prefectures and counties, and giving the pupils' ages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Qing local elementary school for orphaned and poor children, which a court order told every province, prefecture and county to set up, and which was founded by officials, by local donation or by one private person. Its limits are that it is a tertiary stub with a banner saying it cites no sources, so its dates and the pupils' ages (six to eleven) are unchecked and serve for the shape of the school, not as figures; that it covers only the Qing, from 1702; and that it says nothing of the school's building or where it stood.

Used for: the Qing county charity school (urban-features)
