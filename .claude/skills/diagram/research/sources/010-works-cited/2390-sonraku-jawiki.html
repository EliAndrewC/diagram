What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the village as a settlement form, with its classification of village shapes and the ground each was sited on.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that line villages follow natural levees and the spring line at the foot of the hills, the easy-water ground this page's dispersed households sit on. A tertiary summary used for the siting. The passage dates nothing and its examples are present-day villages, so it establishes the landform logic rather than that premodern villages were sited so.

Used for: villages strung along levees and spring lines
