Hakone Town Tourist Association, 箱根関所・箱根関所資料館 ("The Hakone barrier and the Hakone barrier museum", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.hakone.or.jp/676)

What it is: The Hakone tourist association's page on the rebuilt Hakone barrier and its museum: what each building was for, and how the barrier was rebuilt.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the barrier was rebuilt in 2007 from the detailed records of its large repair at the end of the Edo period, which is what lets the rebuilt plan stand for the historical one, and that the main guard house was where travelers were examined. It is a promotional page, and it gives no plan, no measurements and no account of where each building stands.

Used for: that the rebuilt barrier follows its Edo-period repair records, and the main guard house as the place of inspection (defenses)
