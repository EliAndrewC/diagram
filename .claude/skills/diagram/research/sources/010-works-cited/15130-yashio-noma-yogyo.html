Yashio City, Rekinavi - Yashio rekishi jiten, "農間余業" ("Work in the intervals of farming", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; http://yashio-rekinavi.com/reki-navi/index.php?title=%E8%BE%B2%E9%96%93%E4%BD%99%E6%A5%AD)

What it is: An entry in the Yashio city history encyclopedia, a wiki of the city's local history, summarizing the late-Edo surveys of the trades kept by the households of its nineteen villages: a table of 1829 giving each village's inhabitants, households, full-time farmers, traders and craftsmen, taverns, hairdressers, bathhouses and pawnshops, and lists of 1794 to 1836 naming each trading household's goods.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a transcription of domain surveys, the survey of 1829 named with its document and its printed edition, so its counts are primary figures for one district of villages near Edo, with Hachijo, a market village of 856, as its largest place. Its limits are that it is one district of the Kanto plain in the last decades of the Edo period, close to the great city and richer in trade than a remote one; that its places are villages, the largest smaller than a county seat; and that the page is a local-history summary, not the survey itself.

Used for: how many shops, taverns, pawnshops and bathhouses a market village and its district kept, that the trades were kept by farming households, a brewer and an oil presser in the market village (urban-features)
