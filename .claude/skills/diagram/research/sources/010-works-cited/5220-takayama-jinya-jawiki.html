ja.wikipedia 高山陣屋 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/高山陣屋)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article 高山陣屋 (Takayama Jinya), on the shogunal intendancy office of Hida province at Takayama, held under direct shogunal rule from 1692 until the Meiji Restoration - the only such office to survive - citing one work, Ueshima Zenji's 1992 excavation report on the intendant's residence site (Gifu Prefectural Cultural Properties Protection Center), and linking the national cultural-property register. It records the 1816 rebuild of the office (entrance, inquiry room, office, great hall), the numbered storehouses (一～四番蔵・九～十二番蔵) and their removal from Takayama castle in 1695, the compound's origin as the Kanamori lords' lower residence, the historic-site designation of 1929, and the 1996 restoration from an 1830 drawing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the buildings of a provincial office compound as it survived - the office, the numbered storehouses, the front gate and gatehouse - which our magistracy plans draw on. It carries no plan, arrangement or dimensions: how those buildings are laid out (whether the office is one range, whether the storehouses stand in rows, whether there is a front court) must come from the 1992 report or the site's own plan, not from this article. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary; that this is one atypically preserved compound in a mountain province under direct shogunal rule, a province-level office (a gundai's from 1777, with branch offices in Mino and Echizen) larger than a district magistracy; that it was not built as an office but adapted from the Kanamori lords' lower residence after 1692, and its storehouses are Keichō-era castle storehouses moved there in 1695; that its plank roofs are, by the article's own account, a snow-country choice; and that the buildings it lists describe the post-1996 state, in which the storehouse-keepers' rowhouse, the intendant's residence and the inner rooms are a reconstruction from a drawing rather than standing Edo fabric.

Used for: the 1816 rebuild of the office - 玄関・吟味所・御役所・大広間 (that these form one range is not stated on this page); the numbered storehouse rows (一～四番蔵・九～十二番蔵・書物蔵); the surviving front gate and gatehouse (表門・門番所); the 25 intendants over 177 years are on the city's page, not this one
