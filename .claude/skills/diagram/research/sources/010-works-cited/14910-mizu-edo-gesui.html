mizu-edo-gesui

Mizkan Water Culture Center, 水の文化 no. 18, 江戸から東京へ流れる排水の歴史 ("The history of drainage flowing from Edo to Tokyo", title translated from the Japanese by this project), an interview with a former Tokyo sewerage bureau official (https://www.mizu.gr.jp/kikanshi/no18/02.html)

What it is: A magazine interview with a retired official of the Tokyo sewerage bureau who wrote a book on Edo's drains, describing them from the smallest up: the ditch under the eaves along the front street, the ditch before each house small enough to step over, the drains at lot and ward boundaries, and the wide channels dug down the middle of roads on low ground.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the kinds and widths of the drains of an Edo commoner district, the one early-modern Japanese town whose drainage is described at the scale of a single house front. It is about Edo, far larger than any provincial town; much of its evidence is the comic verse of the period, read by the interviewee, rather than surveys.

Used for: the eaves-drip ditch along a town's front street, the width of the ditch before a house, and where a back tenement's ditch drained (ways)
