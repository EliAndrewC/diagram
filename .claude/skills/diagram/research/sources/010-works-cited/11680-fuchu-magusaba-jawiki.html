ja.wikipedia 府中秣場騒動 ("the Fuchu fodder-ground riot", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/府中秣場騒動)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on a 1715 clash between villages near Fuchu, west of Edo, over the limited fodder ground of the Musashino upland, with its background and aftermath.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its background section says what the fodder ground supplied - fodder, green manure and fuel - and that it ran short as paddies spread, which is the evidence that the meadow mattered enough to fight over. It is a short, incomplete encyclopedia account of one dispute on one upland common, resting on published histories (Oishi Manabu 1996 and 2006, Takei Hirokazu 2015). The shortage it describes belongs to the early Edo boom in new rice fields, so it shows what a fodder ground was worth when reclamation pressed on it, not that fodder was always short.

Used for: the three things a fodder ground supplied, and its scarcity as fields spread (vegetation)
