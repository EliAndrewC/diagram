What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kawagoe Five Kashi, the five landings on the upper Shingashi river that served as the castle town's outer port, with the counts of their wholesale houses and boats.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a castle town's river landings were its outer port, the arrangement this page draws for a capital on a river. A tertiary summary used for that. It describes the same town as the company archive cited beside it, so the two are not independent corroboration, and the form is generalized to any river capital from one case.

Used for: the five landings as Kawagoe's outer port
