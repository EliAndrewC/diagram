Liu Tong and Yu Yizheng, 帝京景物略 (Dijing jingwulue, "Sketches of the sights of the imperial capital", title translated), book 2, the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%B8%9D%E4%BA%AC%E6%99%AF%E7%89%A9%E7%95%A5/2)

What it is: The second book of a Ming description of Beijing and its customs, its preface dated 1635, whose section on the spring grounds describes the Qingming visit to the family graves.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a premodern eyewitness account of what families did at a grave at Qingming, weeding it and adding earth among the rest. Its limits: it describes the capital's families and their suburban graves in the late Ming, not a village; it says the grave itself was weeded and topped up, and gives no width of ground cleared around it; the text is a volunteer transcription, not a scholarly edition.

Used for: weeding the grave and adding earth to it at Qingming in 1630s Beijing (Religion and the dead)
