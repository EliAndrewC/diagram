kotobank-katanakake

Kotobank, 刀掛 (the sword rack), entries from Shogakukan's Digital Daijisen and Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%88%80%E6%8E%9B-463571)

What it is: The Kotobank page defining the katanakake, a stand or shelf on which swords rest lying level on forked supports, usually in two tiers, with the word's attestation in the Japanese-Portuguese dictionary of 1603-04 and a use in a Mori Ōgai story of 1914.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rack's form in a standard dictionary. Its limits are that it is a dictionary definition, giving no size; that the rack it describes is an indoor furnishing for a sword or pair of swords; and that its one example of use, a guardroom's rack, comes from a story written in 1914 about the 1830s.

Used for: a sword rack of forked supports at equal height, in two tiers; a guardroom's sword rack (buildings)
