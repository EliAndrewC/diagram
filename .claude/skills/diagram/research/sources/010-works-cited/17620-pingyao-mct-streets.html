Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the People's Republic of China, "平遥古城：2800岁正青春" ("Pingyao old city: 2,800 years old and still young", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://www.mct.gov.cn/wlbphone/wlbydd/xxfb/qglb/sx/202305/t20230515_943747.html)

What it is: A 2023 news article on the website of China's Ministry of Culture and Tourism about the walled old city of Pingyao in Shanxi, a World Heritage site, describing its conservation, its street net and its present population.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the streets and lanes of a surviving Ming and Qing walled seat, the kind of city our county seats are modeled on, against the number of house compounds they serve. It is a government news piece, not a survey; its figures are round; the population it gives is today's; and it gives the streets' count, not their width or the share of the ground they take.

Used for: Pingyao's four main streets, eight small streets and seventy-two lanes, and the 4,000 house compounds they tie together (cities/fabric)
