What it is: A news feature by Zhu Meng and Guo Zhiqing from China Culture Daily (中国文化报), reposted on the website of China's Ministry of Culture and Tourism on 15 May 2023, about the walled old city of Pingyao in Shanxi, a World Heritage site. It is mostly about the town's tourism and festivals since it was listed in 1997, and gives its street net, its house compounds and its present population in a single sentence.

Why it applies, and its limits: It repeats the local saying for the streets and lanes of a surviving Ming and Qing walled seat, the kind of city our county seats are modeled on, against the number of house compounds they serve. "Four main streets, eight small streets and seventy-two lanes" is the town's traditional saying, not a count: seventy-two is a conventional number, and other accounts give eighty-four streets and lanes, or hundreds if dead ends and newer streets are included. The 4,000 compounds and the population are both counted today, not in the Ming or Qing; and it gives the streets' count, not their width or the share of the ground they take.

Used for: Pingyao's four main streets, eight small streets and seventy-two lanes, and the 4,000 house compounds they tie together (cities/fabric)
