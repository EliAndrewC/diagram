What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on irrigation-water disputes in Japan: their causes - weirs, conduits, the fairness of division works, the order and timing of intake - and their shift from violence to litigation under village self-government.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that irrigation water was held and allocated by village associations under customary practice and that the order of intake was itself fought over in the courts, which is why this page will not give a kawata quarter a headwater tap. A tertiary summary used for the institution; it is Japanese only. The article is a stub marked for expansion.

Used for: village associations holding and litigating irrigation intake
