Touken World, 間取りで見る武家屋敷 ("samurai residences seen through their floor plans", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.touken-world.jp/tips/105023/)

What it is: A popular article on the website of a sword museum's foundation that walks through the plans of several surviving samurai residences, among them the Nomura house in Kanazawa and the Kuchiba house in Hagi, with their stipends, plot sizes and room layouts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives plot sizes and a kitchen's place for named upper-samurai houses of about 1,000 to 1,200 koku that still stand, which anchors the top of a castle town's samurai ladder. It is a general-audience article that cites no documents, describes only well-known surviving houses of rich retainers, and says nothing of the ordinary retainer's house.

Used for: the plot of a 1,200-koku retainer and where a senior house's kitchen stood (cities/government)
