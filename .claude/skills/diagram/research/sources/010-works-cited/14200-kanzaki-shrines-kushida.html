kanzaki-shrines-kushida

執行武典 (Shigyo Takenori), the former chief priest of Kushida-gu, notes on the shrines of Kanzaki town and its districts, 2006 (in Japanese; http://www5b.biglobe.ne.jp/~kusidagu/siryou/chinju.html)

What it is: A shrine priest's record of the small shrines of Kanzaki, a town in Saga prefecture, and of its surrounding hamlets, giving for each its registered ground in square meters, often its buildings in ken, and extracts from the old registers of the ground each held, one of them from 1459.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ground and the halls of dozens of small shrines, one of them a town ward's, which is the size of a small shrine in figures. The areas are the registered ones of 2006, most of the shrines are hamlet shrines rather than town shrines, and the old register figures are quoted by the priest, not reproduced.

Used for: small shrines of some 250 to 500 m²; a sanctuary 1 ken square before a worship hall 3 by 2 ken; a town ward's shrine of about 100 tsubo that lost its ground (religion-and-death)
