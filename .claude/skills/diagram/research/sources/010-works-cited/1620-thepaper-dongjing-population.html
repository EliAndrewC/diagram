What it is: A history feature in The Paper's private-history section on the population of Northern Song Kaifeng, drawing on Li Hequn's doctoral study of the city's layout: the nine administrative wards outside the wall by 1021 and the population they held.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the extent of settlement outside the Song capital's wall in the historian's own figures, the case behind this page's reading of a capital's suburbs. Its limit is that it is a news feature reporting a thesis, and it speaks of population, not trade. Kaifeng was the empire's capital, a city of about a million, so extramural settlement on this scale is a ceiling and not a model for a provincial capital.

Used for: the nine wards outside Kaifeng's wall and their population
