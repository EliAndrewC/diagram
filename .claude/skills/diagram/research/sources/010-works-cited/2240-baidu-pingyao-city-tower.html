Baidu Baike, "Pingyao City Tower" (https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Pingyao%20City%20Tower/84025)

What it is: The English-language Baidu Baike encyclopedia entry on Pingyao's Market Tower, the pavilion straddling South Street at the center of the walled county seat: its height, its position and its standing as the town's one tall building.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tower's height and its singularity in the town, which is the county-tier anchor this page's tower glyph is sized to. A tertiary summary from a commercial encyclopedia used for those two facts. Pingyao was an unusually rich merchant town, so its tower is a generous county anchor; the height is the same figure the Chinese Wikipedia entry carries, so the two do not corroborate one another; and the English page is a machine rendering of the Chinese.

Used for: the Market Tower's 18.5 m and its being the only tall building
