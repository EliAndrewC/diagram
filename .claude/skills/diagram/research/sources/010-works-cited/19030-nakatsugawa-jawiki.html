ja.wikipedia 中津川宿 ("Nakatsugawa-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%AD%E6%B4%A5%E5%B7%9D%E5%AE%BF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Nakatsugawa, a post town on the Nakasendo in Mino (today Gifu), giving the town's length and road width from 1663 to the end of the shogunate after a local family's papers, a quotation from an Owari domain retainer's survey of the 1790s with its length and houses, and the houses and population of 1843.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its length and its 1790s house count come from Edo-period documents, and the 1843 count from the same period, so a post town's length and houses can be set against each other without a modern extent. It is an encyclopedia article; the 1843 figures carry no named source on the page, and neither count says how many houses fronted the road.

Used for: one post town's length, road width and houses in the 1790s and in 1843 (towns)
