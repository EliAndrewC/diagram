kotobank-marukibashi

Dictionary entries s.v. 丸木橋 (maruki-bashi), aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/丸木橋)

What it is: The entries for the log bridge in two standard Japanese dictionaries, the Daijisen and the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, as kotobank reproduces them, with the dictionary's earliest attestations of the word in medieval poetry.

Why it applies, and its limits: It fixes what the simplest Japanese crossing of small water was: a single log laid across and nothing more. It is a definition only - it gives no size, no place, no date beyond the word's first appearance in writing, and nothing on who laid one or over what kind of water.

Used for: that the simplest crossing was a single log laid across the water (water)
