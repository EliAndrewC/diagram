What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the River Law, the statute of 1896 and its replacement of 1964 that put whole river systems under unified state and prefectural management.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the two statutes under which Japan's rivers were named and managed as single systems, the modern one-name-per-river this page sets against the old per-reach names. A tertiary summary used for the dates.

Used for: the River Laws of 1896 and 1964
