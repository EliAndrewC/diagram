What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Juan weirs, the seventeenth-century irrigation canals drawn from the Isawa river that opened the Isawa fan to paddy.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the canals' lengths and the ground they watered, the weir-canal irrigation of a fan this page describes. A tertiary summary; it does not say where on the fan the intake stood.

Used for: the Juan weir canals of the Isawa fan
