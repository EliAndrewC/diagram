udworks-igune

Urban Design Works (都市デザインワークス), 「What is an igune?」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「居久根とは？」) (https://www.udworks.net/igune/about-igune)

What it is: An explanatory page by a Sendai non-profit that runs a project on the homestead groves (igune) of southern Tohoku, with residents' recollections of the groves from the early twentieth century to the present.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the four tall trees that form an igune's frame - sugi, zelkova, alder and black pine, reaching over 20 m - on the homestead's north-west, with many middle and low species brought in by birds, which is a statement of how mixed the grove was. It is a local group writing for the public about the Sendai area, with no survey method or counts, and its recollections go back only to the early Showa period.

Used for: the igune's tall frame of four species and its many lesser ones (vegetation)
