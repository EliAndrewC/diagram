chion-in-mieido

浄土宗総本山知恩院 (Chion-in), 第6回 御影堂の建築様式 (1) ("Episode 6: The Architectural Style of the Mieido Hall (1)", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 国宝・御影堂ものがたり ("National Treasure: The Story of the Mieido Hall", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.chion-in.or.jp/mieido/story/story_06.php)

What it is: The temple's own account of its National Treasure Mieido hall at Chion-in in Kyoto: its dimensions, 45 m across and 35 m deep, and its standing among the largest halls in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: A temple's own measurement of one of the largest standing Buddhist halls, the lower anchor of the hall-size band our temple glyphs are checked against. Its limit is that it is the temple describing itself.

Used for: the Mieido's 45 by 35 m
