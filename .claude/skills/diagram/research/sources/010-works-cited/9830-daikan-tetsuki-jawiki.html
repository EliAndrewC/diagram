daikan-tetsuki-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, 代官手附 (Daikan tetsuki) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BB%A3%E5%AE%98%E6%89%8B%E9%99%84)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tetsuki, the shogunal intendant's staff officers created in the 1790s beside the older tedai, with their ranks, numbers and duties and where they lived.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the head of an intendancy's staff, the motojime, and says what the staff lived in, which is the nearest history offers to a posting's chief retainer. Its limits are that it is an encyclopedia article (tagged a stub, though it cites standard references such as the Kokushi daijiten), so its one sentence on housing rests on books we have not read, and it gives the names nagaya and koya but no size or layout; that the motojime tetsuki exists only from the 1790s on, the motojime tedai before; that the motojime was a clerk of low rank - the tedai were hired commoners and the tetsuki minor housemen - so he stands in for a chief retainer by his office and not by his rank; that about half of an intendancy's staff served at its Edo office rather than at the posting; and that it says nothing of where the motojime lived as against the other staff.

Used for: the motojime as the head of the tedai; the tedai and tetsuki housed in nagaya or koya (buildings)
