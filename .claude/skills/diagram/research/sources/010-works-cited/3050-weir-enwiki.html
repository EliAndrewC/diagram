en.wikipedia "Weir" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weir)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the weir, the low dam across a river that impounds water and lets it flow over its crest, with its uses in flood control, measurement and milling.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a weir impounds water, slows the river above it and passes the flow over its crest, the ponding and overflow behavior this page's sluice-fed reaches and set-level moats rest on. A tertiary summary; it does not describe a head-race's width or say in so many words that a weir holds a level.

Used for: water impounded and slowed above a weir, passing over the crest
