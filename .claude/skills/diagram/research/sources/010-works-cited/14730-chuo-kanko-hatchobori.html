chuo-kanko-hatchobori

Chuo City Tourism Association correspondents' blog, 八丁堀組屋敷 ("The Hatchobori group residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://tokuhain.chuo-kanko.or.jp/detail.php?id=4676)

What it is: A 2024 post on the Chuo ward tourism association's volunteer-correspondent blog on the Hatchobori group residence of the town magistracies' yoriki and doshin, drawing on a published 1:1600 plan of it in 1853.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the residence ground's extent and how it was divided - one block granted to the whole corps, split into plots for fifty yoriki and two hundred doshin - which is the size of the quarter a city's constables lived in. It is a volunteer's post summarizing a published plan, not the plan itself.

Used for: the extent and division of the constables' residence quarter (cities/government)
