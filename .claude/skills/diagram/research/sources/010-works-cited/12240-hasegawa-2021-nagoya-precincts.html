hasegawa-2021-nagoya-precincts

Hasegawa Yasuhiro 2021, 「The present state of tree planting and removal, and types of tree planting, at urban Shinto shrines」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「都市の神社境内地における植樹と樹木伐採の実態及び植樹の類型」), Journal of the Japanese Institute of Landscape Architecture (ランドスケープ研究) 84(5), 671-676 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jila/84/5/84_671/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A peer-reviewed questionnaire survey of the shrines of Nagoya city registered with the Aichi prefectural shrine agency, 92 of which answered, on how their groves are planted and felled.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the precinct areas of the 92, in five bands, a present-day spread of ordinary shrines' grounds to set beside the village registers. It is a city's shrines today, after a century of urban growth and the Meiji land orders, answering by mail, and it does not split a precinct into buildings, grove and open ground.

Used for: the present-day spread of shrine precinct areas (religion-and-death.html).
