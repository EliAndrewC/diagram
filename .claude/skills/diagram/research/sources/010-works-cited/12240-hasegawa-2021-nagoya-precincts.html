hasegawa-2021-nagoya-precincts

Hasegawa Yasuhiro 2021, 「The present state of tree planting and removal types of tree planting activities at urban shinto shrines」 (the paper's own English title; original: 「都市の神社境内地における植樹と樹木伐採の実態及び植樹の類型」), Journal of the Japanese Institute of Landscape Architecture (ランドスケープ研究) 84(5), 671-676 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jila/84/5/84_671/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A peer-reviewed questionnaire survey of the shrines of Nagoya city registered with the Aichi prefectural shrine agency, 92 of which answered, on how their groves are planted and felled.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the precinct areas of the 92, in five bands, a present-day spread of ordinary shrines' grounds to set beside the village registers. It is one city's shrines in 2018, after a century of urban growth and the Meiji land orders. Only 92 of the 290 shrines the questionnaire reached answered it (31.7%; 146 more letters came back undelivered), and each manager reported the area themselves, so the smallest and least-kept shrines are the likeliest to be missing. The areas are so skewed (mean 5,003 m², standard deviation 19,833 m²) that only the bands, not the mean, can be used, and it does not split a precinct into buildings, grove and open ground.

Used for: the present-day spread of shrine precinct areas (religion-and-death.html).
