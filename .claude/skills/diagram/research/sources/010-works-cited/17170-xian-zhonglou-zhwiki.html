zh.wikipedia 西安钟楼 ("the Xi'an Bell Tower", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E9%92%9F%E6%A5%BC)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the bell tower of Xi'an, built in 1384 opposite the drum tower and moved to the city's central crossing in 1582.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the pair's first arrangement - the bell tower standing opposite the drum tower about 1,000 m from where it now stands - and says the two buildings reported the hours by drum and by bell. It is one Ming provincial capital, much larger than the seats this project draws.

Used for: the paired drum and bell towers of a Chinese capital (cities/capitals)
