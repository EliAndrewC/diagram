zh.wikipedia 西安钟楼 ("the Xi'an Bell Tower", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E9%92%9F%E6%A5%BC)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the bell tower of Xi'an, built in 1384 opposite the drum tower and moved to the city's central crossing in 1582.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the pair's first arrangement - the bell tower standing opposite the drum tower about 1,000 m from where it now stands - and says the bell tower served to tell the hours and that it and the drum tower to its west together preserve the Ming and Qing city's system of telling the hours; it does not say how the two divided that work. It is a tertiary source, and its two references cover only the 1939 bombing and the 1996 heritage listing, so the 1384 founding, the first site opposite the drum tower and the 1,000 m distance rest on no cited authority; it serves for the arrangement, not the figure. It describes one city, a Ming provincial capital rather than the seat of the throne.

Used for: the paired drum and bell towers of a Chinese capital (cities/capitals)
