ryosenji-shoro

Ryosenji (霊山寺), Nara, 鐘楼(重文) ("the bell tower, an important cultural property", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.ryosenji.jp/treasureDetail15.html)

What it is: The temple's own page on its bell tower, an early-Muromachi building, giving its size in bays, its roof and the date of its bell.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a surviving temple bell tower sized in bays. It gives no feet, and Ryosenji is a head temple of its sect, not a town temple.

Used for: how big a temple bell tower was (religion-and-death)
