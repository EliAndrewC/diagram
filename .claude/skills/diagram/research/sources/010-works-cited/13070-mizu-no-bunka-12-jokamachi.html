mizu-no-bunka-12-jokamachi

Mizkan Water Culture Center, 水の文化 no. 12, 近世城下町に見る水道の知恵 ("The wisdom of the water systems of early modern castle towns", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.mizu.gr.jp/kikanshi/no12/02.html)

What it is: An interview in the Mizkan Water Culture Center's magazine with a historian of Japanese waterworks on the town water systems of the Edo-period castle towns, with Omihachiman's townspeople's well associations as the worked example.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the river-fed water systems rulers built into castle towns nearly all doubled as irrigation and fed moats and garden ponds too, and describes the other kind: a parent well at the source, bamboo pipes to take-wells, run by a well association of its users. It is an interview, and it speaks of castle towns and one merchant town, not of how many towns had either.

Used for: castle-town water systems doubling as irrigation and moat supply; piped water to take-wells (water)
