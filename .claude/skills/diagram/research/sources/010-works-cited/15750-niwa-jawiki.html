ja.wikipedia 庭 ("the garden", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the section on the tsubo-niwa (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/庭)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the garden, a tertiary encyclopedia article flagged by its editors for unclear Japanese, whose section on the tsubo-niwa describes the small garden inside a town house's lot; every claim in that section rests on one cited work, Mori Takao (ed.), Jū no minzoku jiten (a folklore dictionary of dwellings), 2019, pp. 283-286.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tsubo-niwa's size, about one to two tsubo, says it is found chiefly in Kyoto's town houses, and says why it was made - for light and air in a house hemmed in by its neighbors - and when, with the town house of the early modern period. It speaks of Kyoto's town houses and hermitages; it does not say how common the garden was in a provincial market town, or whether Chinese town houses had any counterpart. It is an encyclopedia's summary of a single source, the 2019 folklore dictionary it cites, which we did not read; the size and the reason are that dictionary's, passed on here, and the dictionary would be the better source to cite.

Used for: the size and the reason of a town house's small garden (towns)
