顺德图书馆, 发现顺德·经济篇 - 桑基鱼塘 (https://www.sdlib.com.cn/home/article/detail/id/741.html)

What it is: An article by the journalist 李健明 (Li Jianming), published in the 珠江商报 (Zhujiang Business Daily) in July 2005 and republished by the Shunde district library in its local-history series, on the dike-pond landscape of Shunde in the Pearl River delta: the dike built from the pond's own spoil and planted, the crops it carried, and the spread of the system from the Song polder through the Ming silk boom to its Qing peak.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the making of the dike-and-pond block in plain words - dug from the pond, raised from the spoil, planted - and a chronology of the landscape's spread, which is what our delta maps draw and when. Its limits are that it is a newspaper's local history, written to celebrate a local heritage rather than as scholarship; that it quotes period gazetteer phrases without naming which gazetteer, so they cannot be checked at this remove; and that it is subtropical southern China, not Japan.

Used for: the dike built from the pond's own spoil (「The mud is taken out and heaped over the four sides to make the dike; the hollow left sunken in the middle becomes the pond.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「取泥覆四周为基，中凹下为塘」)), planted with mulberry ("基种桑"), hemp, soybeans, peanuts and melon trellises; the Song 桑园围, the Jiajing spread, the Xianfeng-Tongzhi peak
