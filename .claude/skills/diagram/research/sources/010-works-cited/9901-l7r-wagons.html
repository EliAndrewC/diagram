l7r-wagons

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Caravan Hooks", "The Fox Road footpads" and "Hay" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master: adventure hooks with a caravan's wagons crossing a bridge, a bounty for footpads who took a cartload of charcoal on a road, and a note on hay hauled in wagons behind teams of oxen.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: whether wagons and carts use the roads between towns is the setting's to decide, and here they do, where Edo Japan barred carts from its highways in principle, a rule that varied by place and period. There is no historical limitation to state; the notes give no cart sizes or road widths.

Used for: wagons and carts on the roads between towns, behind the cart gates, cart yards and cart lanes of compounds (ways)
