seika-mingu-dakkoku

せいか舎, みんなの民具: 脱穀 ("Seika-sha, Everyone's Folk Implements: Threshing", title translated), Seika town, Kyoto prefecture (https://seikasya.town.seika.kyoto.jp/mingu/rice-farming/dakkoku)

What it is: A page of Seika town's online catalog of the farm and household tools in its collection, written for schoolchildren: the threshing comb, the treadle thresher, the rake for turning drying grain, and the earthen husking mill, each with what it was used for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an independent account, from the Kinki region, of the threshed grain being dried on straw mats in the sun, agreeing with the Kanto record. Its limits: a short museum caption, undated, that describes the practice in general and not any one yard.

Used for: the threshed grain spread on mats and dried in the sun
