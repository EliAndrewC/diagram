Banshizhai zhuren (半是斋主人), "Shaiguping de qianshi jinsheng" (The drying floor, past and present), a blog post on Wenxuecity, 2023 (in Chinese; https://www.wenxuecity.com/blog/202304/80401/6455.html)

What it is: A personal memoir by a writer from a terraced rice valley at the foot of Mount Heng, in Hunan, of the drying floors of the village of their childhood: each household's floor before its house, how it was made, and how the collective era changed it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an eyewitness account of a south-central Chinese rice village in which the drying floor belonged to the household, which is one of the two forms the record finds. It is a memoir of the twentieth century, of one village, without references, and what it says of earlier centuries is the writer's own belief; it is used only for the household floor and how it was surfaced.

Used for: the household drying floor before the house, and its surface (homesteads)
