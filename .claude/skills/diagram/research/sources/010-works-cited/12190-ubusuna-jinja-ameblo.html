ubusuna-jinja-ameblo

「At shrines with no temizuya」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「手水舎が無い神社では」), a post on the blog 幸せを呼び込む神社 神さまからの後押しを頂く参拝とは, on Ameba Blog (in Japanese; https://ameblo.jp/ubusuna-jinja/entry-11582938316.html)

What it is: A personal blog post, undated on the page, on how to purify oneself at a shrine that has no roofed water pavilion.

Why it applies, and its limits: It observes that many shrines have no pavilion at all and that unstaffed shrines are especially many in the provinces, which bears on whether a village shrine's basin stood unroofed. It is one writer's observation of shrines today, not a survey, and says nothing of the period before 1868.

Used for: the basin with no pavilion (religion-and-death.html).
