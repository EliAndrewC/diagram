ehime-kenshi-mura-shokunin

Ehime Prefecture, Ehime kenshi: kinsei ge (1987), chapter 6, section 3, part 3, "在町・郷村の職人" ("Craftsmen of the country towns and villages", title translated from the Japanese by this project), in the database Ehime no kioku (in Japanese; https://www.i-manabi.jp/system/regionals/regionals/ecode:2/63/view/7978)

What it is: The prefectural history's account of village craftsmen in Iyo: the early rule that villages make only for themselves and buy their tools in town, the later licensing of carpenters, coopers and smiths in villages, the itinerant craftsmen, and village registers counting craftsmen.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a scholarly prefectural history that cites the village registers behind its counts, and it gives both forms, villages with no craftsmen and villages with licensed ones. Its limits are that it is one province; that its Kawanoe figures are for a large coastal village that its own authors note differs sharply from the district's table; and that it gives no workshop's size or siting.

Used for: whether a village kept craftsmen, a smith among them, and how many a large village held (urban-features)
