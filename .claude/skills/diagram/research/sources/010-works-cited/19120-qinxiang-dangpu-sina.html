Zhui Qing (blog), "无锡洛社秦巷当铺" ("The Qinxiang pawnshop at Luoshe, Wuxi", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 2023, quoting the Wuxi media article "秦巷当铺：两百多年的岁月故事" (in Chinese; https://blog.sina.com.cn/s/blog_4eebd24b0102z7hq.html)

What it is: A personal travel blog's account of a visit to a restored pawnshop on the old street of Qinxiang, a rural market town north of Wuxi in Jiangsu, followed by a passage the blog says it excerpts from a local news article on the building's history.

Why it applies, and its limits: The excerpted article dates the pawnshop to about the mid-Qing and gives its original size and form - 18 m wide and 20 m deep, five bays, three courts, two stories, a street gable 8 m high and fire walls 5 m high on the other sides - so it attests a large, storied, high-walled pawnshop on a market-town street before 1912. Its limits: it is a blog repeating a newspaper, which cites unnamed "records" for the figures, and the date is approximate ("about the mid-Qing"); the page does not say which part of the building held the pledges, and the building was renovated, with older footings found under it.

Used for: a mid-Qing market-town pawnshop's size, stories and walls (urban-features)
