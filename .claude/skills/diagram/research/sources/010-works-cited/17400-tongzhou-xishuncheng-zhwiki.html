zh.wikipedia 西顺城街 (北京市通州区) ("West Shuncheng Street (Tongzhou district, Beijing)", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/西顺城街_(北京市通州区))

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on a street in Tongzhou, the old walled town east of Beijing on the Grand Canal, which ran along the inside of the town's west wall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the street had formed by the Ming as a patrol road along the wall, called the West Horse Road, which is the one page read that names the wall-side street's use. It is a stub about one street of one town, it gives no width and no date closer than "the Ming", and it does not say whether building on the road was barred.

Used for: the wall-side street as a Ming patrol road (defenses)
