zh.wikipedia 西顺城街 (北京市通州区) ("West Shuncheng Street (Tongzhou district, Beijing)", title translated) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/西顺城街_(北京市通州区))

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on a street in Tongzhou, the old walled town east of Beijing on the Grand Canal, an east-west street running from South Street west to Station Road. It is the western half of Shuncheng Street, the street along the town's south wall (the page gives only the street's course; Baidu Baike's article on East Shuncheng Street places it inside the south wall).

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the street had formed by the Ming as a patrol road along the wall, called the West Horse Road, which is the one page read that names the wall-side street's use. It is a stub about one street of one town, whose only reference is a 1992 place-name gazetteer of Tong county (北京市通县地名志, p. 32), not a record from the period; it gives no width and no date closer than "the Ming", and it does not say whether building on the road was barred.

Used for: the wall-side street as a Ming patrol road (defenses)
