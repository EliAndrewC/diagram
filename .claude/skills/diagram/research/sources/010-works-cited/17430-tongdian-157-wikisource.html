Du You 杜佑, 通典 卷157 ("Tongdian, book 157", title translated), on zh.wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/通典/卷157)

What it is: A book of the military section of the Tongdian, the Tang encyclopedia of institutions finished in 801, preserving the drill for drawing up and fighting a Tang army, largely from the lost military writings of the general Li Jing. The classical text as transcribed on Wikisource.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ranges at which a Tang army's crossbowmen and archers began to shoot, 150 and 60 paces, the one range for a bow read in any source. It is a field-battle drill rather than a siege manual, it gives the range at which men open fire rather than one at which an arrow kills, and its paces must be converted with a foot length from elsewhere.

Used for: the archers' 60 paces and the crossbowmen's 150 (defenses)
