pfaf-kaki

Plants For A Future, "Diospyros kaki - Thunb." (https://pfaf.org/user/plant.aspx?latinname=Diospyros+kaki)

What it is: The entry for the Japanese persimmon in the Plants For A Future database, a charity's online reference on useful plants, giving the tree's habit, its size at maturity, its uses and how it is grown.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a mature persimmon's height and spread together, which the Japanese pages read give only as a height. Its limits are that it is a modern horticultural reference, compiled from other works by a British charity, describing the species in cultivation, not an old farmyard tree in Japan. Its 12 m by 7 m is the size the tree grows to, a maximum rather than a typical tree, and carries no reference of its own; and the same page, in a general passage on persimmons, gives a lower full height of 4.5 to 7.6 m, so we take the spread as an upper bound.

Used for: the persimmon's crown spread (homesteads)
