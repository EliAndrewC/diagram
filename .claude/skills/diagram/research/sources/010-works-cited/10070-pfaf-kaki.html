pfaf-kaki

Plants For A Future, "Diospyros kaki - L.f." (https://pfaf.org/user/plant.aspx?latinname=Diospyros+kaki)

What it is: The entry for the Japanese persimmon in the Plants For A Future database, a charity's online reference on useful plants, giving the tree's habit, its size at maturity, its uses and how it is grown.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a mature persimmon's height and spread together, which the Japanese pages read give only as a height. Its limits are that it is a modern horticultural reference describing the species in cultivation, not an old farmyard tree in Japan, and it gives one typical figure rather than a range.

Used for: the persimmon's crown spread (homesteads)
