What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia disambiguation page for the qiaolou, whose lead line defines it as the drum tower of an official compound where the drums and horns for announcing the hours and receiving guests were kept.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the form as the official compound's own timekeeping building, the variant this page names beside the freestanding tower. A tertiary summary used for the definition. The page carries no references at all and fixes no period.
