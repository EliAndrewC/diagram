zh.wikipedia 西安鼓楼 ("the Xi'an Drum Tower", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E9%BC%93%E6%A5%BC)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the drum tower of Xi'an, built in 1380, which stands west of the city's separate bell tower.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows one Ming provincial capital, Xi'an, in which drum and bell are two buildings, each reporting the hours by its own instrument; the article calls the pair the timekeeping system of Ming and Qing Xi'an. It is one city's example: the article does not say how many other capitals built the pair, so it shows that the form existed, not that it was typical. Xi'an was far larger than any seat this project draws, and the article is an encyclopedia summary with a single listed reference and no inline citations.

Used for: the paired drum and bell towers of a Chinese capital (cities/capitals)
