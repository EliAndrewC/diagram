hirosakipark-tenshu

弘前公園 (Hirosaki Park official site), 弘前城 天守／御三階櫓 (https://www.hirosakipark.jp/707.html)

What it is: The official Hirosaki Park site's page on the castle's keep, formally its three-story turret: its first-floor plan of five by six ken and its ridge height.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured footprint of the keep, from the castle's own custodians on their own building. It is a visitor page rather than a survey report, and the standing building is the three-story turret of 1810, a deliberately modest late-Edo structure, not the five-story keep of 1611 that burned in 1627.

Used for: the keep's 5 by 6 ken plan
