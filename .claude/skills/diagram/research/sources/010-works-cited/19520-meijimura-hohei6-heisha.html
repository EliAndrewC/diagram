Museum Meiji-mura, 歩兵第六聯隊兵舎 ("barracks of the 6th Infantry Regiment", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.meijimura.com/sight/%E6%AD%A9%E5%85%B5%E7%AC%AC%E5%85%AD%E8%81%AF%E9%9A%8A%E5%85%B5%E8%88%8E/)

What it is: The open-air architecture museum's page on an army barracks built at Nagoya castle in 1873 and moved to the museum in 1965, a registered cultural property.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a barracks whose squad rooms are furnished with wooden beds to the first years of the modern army, the earliest barracks with beds found. It is a museum page for visitors; the beds are a recreation of the rooms, and the page does not say when beds first came into a Japanese barracks.

Used for: an 1873 army barracks with beds, marking the bunk room as modern (buildings)
