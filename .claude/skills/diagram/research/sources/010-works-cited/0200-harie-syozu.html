harie-syozu

Harie Shozu-no-Sato, the Harie district's own site, "What the kabata are" (https://harie-syozu.jp/about/)

What it is: The official site of the Harie district in Takashima, Shiga - the community's own account of its spring-fed water system: where the settlement sits, the channels through it, and the kabata, the household water-huts built over the springs.

Why it applies, and its limits: Harie is a working Japanese farming settlement whose water arrangement is a nationally designated Important Cultural Landscape, and this page states both facts our question needs in the community's own words - the settlement on one bank of the river, its own stream through the middle of the district. Its limits: it is a community and tourism page rather than scholarship, with no date for the arrangement it describes, so it is evidence of the FORM and not of its age; and the springs that make Harie what it is are a local hydrology, not the general case.

Used for: a settlement on the left bank of its river; its own channel running through the middle of the district
