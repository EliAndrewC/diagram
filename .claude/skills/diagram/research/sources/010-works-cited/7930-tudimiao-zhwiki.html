zh.wikipedia 土地庙 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%9C%9F%E5%9C%B0%E5%BA%99) - 「In height from under two meters to that of an ordinary house; in area from a few square meters at the small end to over ten at the large end.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「高度从不到2米至正常房屋高度；面积大小小则几平方米，大则十几平方米」)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the earth-god shrine across China, Taiwan and Southeast Asia, much of it on the post-1980 revival of the form.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the size of the ordinary earth-god shrine - a few square meters to over ten - to show that nobody lived in one; the form is old and the conclusion survives the discounts. Its limits: tertiary and undated; the shrines behind its size band are largely the rebuilds and factory-made concrete units of the post-1980 revival, a modern measurement of a premodern form; and its reach runs past the Chinese core into Taiwan and Southeast Asia.

Used for: the earth-god shrine's size (religion-and-death.html).
