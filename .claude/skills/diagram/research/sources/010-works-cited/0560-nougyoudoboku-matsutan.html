nougyoudoboku-matsutan

のうぎょうとぼく, 「Terminal irrigation channel / small irrigation channel within a paddy field」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水田圃場内の「末端用水路・小用水路」」) and 「terminal drainage channel / small drainage channel」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「末端排水路・小排水路」) (https://nougyoudoboku.com/terminal-irrigation-canal/)

What it is: A page on the terminal irrigation canal - the last channel before the paddy plot - from のうぎょうとぼく, a self-published site on agricultural civil engineering written by a credentialed agricultural engineer and farmer, a former public servant in the field, posted in 2022 and updated in 2024. It sets out the three-tier supply hierarchy of a consolidated paddy district, the 300 to 600 m spacing of branch canals, and the height of the terminal channel's bed against the field surface; the matching terminal drain is the subject of a separate article on the same site, which this page links but does not contain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the gravity logic of a paddy's water supply - the order of main, branch and terminal channel and how the last one sits against the field - which wet rice has not changed and which is what our canal trees are drawn from. Its limits are that it is present-day engineering guidance, self-published by a practitioner rather than a ministry standard or a peer-reviewed work; that it assumes consolidated, machine-worked paddy with precast concrete channels, so its 300 to 600 m branch spacing is the dimension of a modern land-readjustment block, the very thing a premodern field lacked, and only the order and the gravity transfer; and that the drainage side - the terminal drain's depth, the drain hierarchy, the sizing from catchment - is not on this page but on the linked companion, so the drainage claims taken from this page rest on nothing readable at its link.

Used for: the three-tier supply hierarchy 幹線 -> 支線 -> 末端用水路; branch spacing 300-600 m (a consolidation-block dimension); the terminal channel's bed set -5 to +10 cm against the paddy surface (the drainage mirror, the terminal drain's depth and the sizing from 集水面積 are on the site's separate drainage article, which this key does not link)
