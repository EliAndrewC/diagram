ja.wikipedia 文武学校 ("Bunbu Gakko", the Matsushiro domain school, title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%96%87%E6%AD%A6%E5%AD%A6%E6%A0%A1)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Bunbu Gakko of the Matsushiro domain (Shinano), opened in 1855 and one of the few domain schools whose ground and buildings survive, with its site area and a list of its buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the smallest whole domain school read for this record - about 3,089 square meters - and its building list (a hall of letters, classrooms, a book store, sword, jujutsu, spear and archery halls, an office and a guardhouse) names no Confucian shrine, so it is the evidence that a school could go without one. It is one domain building late, on the ground of a converted official residence in the town. It is an encyclopedia article without inline citations, a tertiary summary of local histories. The missing shrine was unusual: the Nagano city history (長野市誌 第四巻, in its section on the founding of the school, 文武学校の設立) names it as what set this school apart from the Ueda, Takato and Takashima domain schools, which had one. So it shows a school could do without a shrine, not that many did. Matsushiro was a domain of 100,000 koku, so its ground is the smallest read here, not the smallest a domain school could be.

Used for: the small end of a domain school's ground and a program without a Confucian shrine (cities/capitals)
