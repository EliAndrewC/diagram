ja.wikipedia 大手門 ("the ote gate", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%A4%A7%E6%89%8B%E9%96%80)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the ote-mon, the main gate of a Japanese castle: how it was built, what bridge usually reached it, and a list of surviving examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states what a castle's main gate usually was - a masugata with a large yaguramon, reached by an earthen causeway. A tertiary summary speaking of castles in general, with no dimensions and no count behind its "often".

Used for: the main gate usually a masugata, and usually reached by an earthen causeway (defenses)
