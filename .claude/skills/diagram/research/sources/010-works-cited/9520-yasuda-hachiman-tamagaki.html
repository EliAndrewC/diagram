yasuda-hachiman-tamagaki

Agency for Cultural Affairs, national cultural-property database, 安田八幡宮玉垣 (https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/heritage/detail/101/00006725)

What it is: The national heritage database's entry on the late-Edo stone fence along the approach of Yasuda Hachiman Shrine, with its dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: The one Edo-dated stone fence found, and it lines the approach rather than ringing the precinct. Its limits: a Hachiman shrine of some local standing, one example, and a listing chosen for survival.

Used for: a late-Edo stone fence along an approach (religion-and-death.html).
