meiji-jingu-access

Meiji Jingu, official access page (https://www.meijijingu.or.jp/access/)

What it is: The access page of Meiji Jingu's own site, giving the shrine's entrances, the approaches that lead in from them and how long the walk from each takes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a walking measure of a ranked-torii approach: the shrine buildings stand at roughly the center of the precinct, and it takes about ten minutes on foot from each entrance to the main hall. That is the span the first, second and third torii cover, and it is why a ranked pair of gates is off the edge of every settlement sheet this project draws. Its limits are that it is a visitor page giving a walking time rather than a distance, so any figure in meters beside it is this project's own arithmetic, and that Meiji Jingu was founded in 1920, so its precinct is a modern great-shrine layout rather than a premodern one, and an exceptionally large one at that - a forest planted for a national memorial - so ten minutes is a ceiling rather than a typical span. For a premodern approach measured in meters the record has Nagao Shrine, registered beside it.

Used for: the ~10-minute walk from each entrance to the main hall, which the three ranked torii span
