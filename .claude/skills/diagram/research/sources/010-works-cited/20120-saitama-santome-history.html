saitama-santome-history

Saitama Prefecture, 三富地域の歴史 ("The history of the Santome area", title translated from the Japanese by this project), on the prefecture's page for the green Santome area (in Japanese; https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/a0108/908-20091216-188.html)

What it is: The prefectural government's short history of the new-field colonies of the Musashino plateau: the Nobidome villages of 1661 along the Kawagoe road, and Santome Shinden of 1694-1696 with its frontage, depth, road width and the arrangement of house lot, field and woodland on both sides of the road, citing the founding headman's own account of 1699.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the strip row's figures for two colonies, and says the strips lay on both sides of the road, from a public body that names its Edo source. It is an unsigned summary for the public rather than a study, and both colonies are planned dry-field settlements on one plateau, not paddy villages that grew on their own.

Used for: the strip row on both sides of a road at Santome (40 by 375 ken, a road of 6 ken) and at Nobidome (strips about 400 ken deep) (homesteads)
