2026 年度广州市从化区高标准农田改造提升建设项目初步设计报告（评审稿） (Preliminary design report for the 2026 high-standard farmland improvement and upgrading construction project, Conghua District, Guangzhou; client the Conghua District Bureau of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, designer 中联合创设计有限公司), June 2026 (http://nyncj.gz.gov.cn/attachment/8/8037/8037125/10854794.pdf)

What it is: The review-draft preliminary design report for a district farmland improvement scheme in Guangzhou, published by the district's own agriculture bureau. Among its works schedules and design clauses it gives the built cross-section of each grade of canal in the scheme, in metres, tier by tier.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one openly readable document this project has found that gives canal widths by grade as BUILT rather than as a minimum: the lateral (斗渠) at 1.0 and 1.2 m and the farm canal (农渠) at 0.4 and 0.6 m, which puts a measured figure under the roughly 1 m distribution lateral in our width ladder and brackets our 0.3 m field ditch from above. Its limits begin with the quantity itself: the width of a lined rectangular section's floor is not the width of a hand-dug earthen ditch carrying the same water, which lies back on its side slopes and runs wider and shallower, so the RATIO between two lined widths is not straightforwardly the ratio between two dug ones. It is also a modern works schedule, dated 2026, for rectangular mortared brick and stone sections laid to a 1/2000 grade, so what it can fix is the LADDER'S PROPORTIONS - roughly three to one between successive grades - and never the material, the construction or the true width of a hand-dug premodern channel; it is one district's design report rather than a national standard, so its sections are what one designer specified for one scheme on one terrain; and it does not build the finest grade at all, the 毛渠, which is the tier our 0.3 m field ditch sits on. It cites GB 50288-2018 by number for its own scour and siltation velocities, so like the Jiangsu guideline it is a readable application of a standard we cannot read.

Used for: the built widths of the Chinese canal grades - lateral 1.0 and 1.2 m, farm canal 0.4 and 0.6 m - behind the distribution lateral in the water-width ladder
