orange-hatchobori-yoriki

A personal Edo-history page, 南北町奉行所跡 ("The sites of the South and North town magistracies", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (http://orange.zero.jp/kkubota.bird/machibugyousho.htm)

What it is: A personal web page on the sites of the Edo town magistracies and the Hatchobori district where their staff lived, quoting two reference works - the Edo-Tokyo encyclopedia (江戸東京学事典) and a dictionary of Edo policing (江戸の司法警察事典) - for the plot sizes of the magistracies' officers and constables.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the plot sizes that show a magistracy's staff lived outside its compound on ground of their own, one of the two forms of our Imperial Magistrate's staff housing. It is a personal page quoting its sources without page numbers, and only the constables' plots are placed at Hatchobori by name.

Used for: the magistracy officers' granted plots of about 200 to 300 tsubo, and the constables' plots of somewhat over 100 tsubo in group housing at Hatchobori (cities/capitals)
