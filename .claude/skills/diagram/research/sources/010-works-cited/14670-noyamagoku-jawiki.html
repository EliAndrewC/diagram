ja.wikipedia 野山獄 ("Noyama jail", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/野山獄)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Noyama jail, the Chōshū domain's jail in its castle town of Hagi, and the Iwakura jail across the road from it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a domain's jail described as a place: two separate compounds on confiscated retainers' residence plots, one for samurai and one for everyone else, with twelve cells in two facing rows. It is one domain's jail and an encyclopedia article; it gives no area.

Used for: a castle town's jail as a compound apart from the magistracy (cities/government)
