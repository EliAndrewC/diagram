ja.wikipedia 野山獄 ("Noyama jail", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/野山獄)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Noyama jail, the Chōshū domain's jail in its castle town of Hagi, and the Iwakura jail across the road from it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a domain's jail described as a place: two separate compounds on confiscated retainers' residence plots, one for samurai (the Noyama jail, twelve cells in two facing rows of six, with an inspection hut and an execution ground) and one for everyone else (the Iwakura jail). It is one domain's jail, and a stub encyclopedia article: its one cited source, a Kyōhō-era account of Hagi printed in 1891, supports only how the two jails came to stand on the two confiscated plots, and the cell layout, inspection hut and execution ground are uncited. The jails stood apart because two residences were confiscated after one assault in 1645, not by any stated rule, and the article does not say where the magistracy stood; that the jail stood apart from it is our inference. It gives no area.

Used for: a castle town's jail as a compound apart from the magistracy (cities/government)
