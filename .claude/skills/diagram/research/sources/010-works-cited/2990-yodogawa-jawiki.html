What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Yodo river system, from Lake Biwa to Osaka bay, with the names its reaches carry.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the river's three names and where each begins - at a border and at a confluence - the per-reach renaming this page's rivers follow. A tertiary summary used for the names.

Used for: the Seta, Uji and Yodo names and where they change
