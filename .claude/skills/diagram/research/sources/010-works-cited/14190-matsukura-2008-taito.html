matsukura-2008-taito

Matsukura et al. 2008, a study of the temple districts of Taito ward, Tokyo, and the formation of their roads, blocks and temples, 都市計画論文集 (Journal of the City Planning Institute of Japan) 43.2 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/journalcpij/43.2/0/43.2_1/_pdf)

What it is: A peer-reviewed city-planning paper that dates the roads, blocks and temples of Taito ward, which holds much of old Edo's temple quarters, from Edo-period temple records and modern surveys, and compares the size of the blocks formed in each period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of the Edo-period blocks that survive in the old temple quarters of Yanaka and Negishi-Shitaya, which bounds how many precincts a block held. The block areas are the authors' approximations for comparison, and the table of each temple's precinct in tsubo that the paper compiled is not printed.

Used for: surviving Edo-period blocks of about 7,300 to 9,000 m² in the old temple quarters (religion-and-death)
