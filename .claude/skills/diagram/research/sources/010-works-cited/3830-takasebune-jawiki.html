ja.wikipedia 高瀬舟 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/高瀬舟)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the takasebune, the flat-bottomed river cargo boat, with Heian and Edo records of its dimensions; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the boat's recorded lengths and beam and its cargoes, which are the barge this page moors at its quay; the figures are records of particular boats, not a fleet mean; the two records are nine centuries apart (884 and 1817) and differ by more than double in length.

Used for: the size of a river barge (river cities)
