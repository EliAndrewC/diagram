zh.wikipedia 周庄镇 (昆山市) ("Zhouzhuang, Kunshan", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/周庄镇_(昆山市))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Zhouzhuang, a canal market town of the lower Yangzi near Suzhou, with its growth in the Ming and Qing, its two main streets meeting in a T around a central bridge, and its surviving stone arch bridges.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a real Chinese market town - the tier the setting's towns sit at - with its street plan and its bridges, in the wet rice south. It is one town, a water town in which the canals do much of what streets do elsewhere, and a tertiary article; it gives no bridge count or dimensions.

Used for: a Chinese market town's two main streets meeting in a T, and its stone arch bridges (ways)
