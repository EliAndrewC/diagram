maff-waju-mizuya

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, 輪中の水屋 ("The mizuya of the waju", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.maff.go.jp/j/nousin/sekkei/museum/m_kakuti/32_mizuya/)

What it is: A page of the farm ministry's online museum of rural engineering on the flood-refuge buildings of the ring-diked villages of the Kiso rivers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the order of defenses at the house: the plot raised on fill first, then, because that was not enough, a still higher mound inside the plot with the mizuya on it, ranging from a hut to a spare dwelling, every one of them formerly used as a store. It is a short government page on one region, the Kaizu lowland in Gifu. It names no period and gives no date for the mounds or the buildings, only describing the landscape that survives, and it gives no heights or sizes. So it supports the order of the defenses, not their date or any figure.

Used for: the raised house plot and the mizuya on a higher mound (water)
