yaotsu-sanron

Yaotsu town information site, 「The mountain dispute over the village boundary」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村境をめぐる 山論」), with articles by members of the Yaotsu town cultural properties council (https://yaotsu-mall.com/category/history/sanron/)

What it is: A local-history series on a town promotion site in Gifu prefecture, written by members of the town's cultural properties council, on the dispute between Kutami village (Owari domain) and Fukuchi and eight other villages (Naegi domain) over their shared hill land, which flared in 1813 and was ruled on in 1823.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in plain words why hill-land boundaries between villages were unclear, and what the hill land was used for, from one well-documented dispute. It is a popular local history on a commercial site, about one pair of domains in the Kiso valley, and cites no documents by name.

Used for: that boundaries within hill forest were vague because of the terrain (vegetation)
