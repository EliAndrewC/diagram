ja.wikipedia 紺屋 ("dyer", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%B4%BA%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kon'ya, the Edo period's dyer: the dyers' guilds of Osaka, Edo and Kyoto, the four kinds of dyer by dyestuff, how indigo was prepared, and the sayings the trade left behind.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used for the saying that a dyer's promised day could not be trusted because dyeing depended on the weather, which is why a dyer needs open ground for drying. A tertiary summary with few citations.

Used for: dyeing that waited on the weather (urban-features)
