omori-daikansho-kunishitei

Agency for Cultural Affairs, 国指定文化財等データベース, 代官所跡 ("Database of Nationally Designated Cultural Properties: the intendant's office site", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a component of the Iwami silver mine historic site (in Japanese; https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/bsys/maindetails/911/64)

What it is: The national cultural-property database's description of the site of the intendant's office at Omori in Iwami, which governed the silver mine and some 150 villages around it; it gives the site area and the size of the surviving front gate with its gate rowhouses, built in 1815.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures a rowhouse that stood in an intendant's office compound, the building type a magistracy's staff range is drawn from. Its limits: it gives only the overall width and length of the gate-and-rowhouse range, not the rooms, not who lived there, and not how many; and a mining intendancy was a large posting.

Used for: the size of the gate rowhouses of an intendant's office (buildings)
