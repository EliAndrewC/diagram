kotobank-ryorijaya

kotobank 料理茶屋 ("restaurant teahouse", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%96%99%E7%90%86%E8%8C%B6%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entry and the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry on the ryorijaya, the Edo-period restaurant, from the Nara-chameshi house of Asakusa after 1657 to the famous restaurants of Edo, Kyoto and Osaka.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names where Kyoto's restaurants stood - before the gate of the Gion shrine and along the Takase canal - an example of eating houses gathering at a gate and on the water. A signed reference entry on the great cities' fine restaurants, not a small town's eating house.

Used for: Kyoto's restaurants at the Gion shrine's gate and along the Takase canal (urban-features)
