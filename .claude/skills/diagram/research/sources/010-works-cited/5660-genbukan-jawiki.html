What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article 玄武館 (Genbukan), on the swordsmanship hall founded in 1822 at Nihonbashi Shinagawacho in Edo by Chiba Shusaku, later moved to Kanda Otamagaike, which the article ranks among the three great dojos of late-Edo Edo. It records the school's founding, its moves and the Chiba family's service as instructors to the Mito domain.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for a metropolitan dojo with a date and an address, so that one can be put on a street, and for the point that a big city had large private martial schools; it is not used for a count of disciples. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary, and that this was one exceptional institution, so it is a ceiling and not a typical dojo. The page says nothing about fees or commercial operation - the Chiba family ran the hall while serving a domain as instructors - so whether such a hall was a paying business rests on nothing readable here.
