miyoshi-ikegami-house

Miyoshi Town Museum of History and Folklore, 施設案内 ("Guide to the facilities", title translated), on the former Ikegami house, Miyoshi, Saitama (http://www.jade.dti.ne.jp/~miyoshir/sisetu/sisetu.html)

What it is: The town museum's page on its grounds, with the survey findings on the former Ikegami house it moved there in 1987-88: its date, its size and its plan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the farmhouse whose doors and walls the museum's columns describe, and says how large a house it was. The survey itself calls the house large for its district and its nine-room plan (three rows of three) very rare, so it speaks for a well-to-do household's house in an upland village of dry fields cleared in the Edo period, not for an ordinary farmhouse or a paddy village. That its builders were rich is the museum's inference from the house's size, not a record of what the family owned. The date is the survey's estimate from prayer tablets in the roof, the spacing of the posts and the nails, since no ridge tablet was found, so it is the end of the Edo period or the first years of Meiji, not one year.

Used for: the date and size of the Ikegami farmhouse (Estates and other compounds; Homesteads)
