Chinese Wikipedia, "Yeolha ilgi" (熱河日記) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/%E7%83%AD%E6%B2%B3%E6%97%A5%E8%AE%B0)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Park Ji-won's travel diary, giving the embassy of 1780 to the Qianlong Emperor's seventieth birthday and the diary's structure.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the journey the diary records, which the Wikisource text of the chapter does not state.

Used for: the year of Park Ji-won's journey, 1780 (towns)
