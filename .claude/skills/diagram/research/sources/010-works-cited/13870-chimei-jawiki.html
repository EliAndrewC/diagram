ja.wikipedia 地名 ("place names", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9C%B0%E5%90%8D)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on place names: how they are made, what they are made from, and how far their origins can be known.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that naming a place from its terrain is a common way of naming, most of all for small-scale terrain and old names, and that many names' origins are disputed or unknown - the ground and the limit of reading a village's name for where it stands. Its limits: it is a general encyclopedia article that gives no count of how many names are topographic, and its examples are modern and Japanese.

Used for: place names made from the terrain, and how often a name's origin is uncertain (homesteads)
