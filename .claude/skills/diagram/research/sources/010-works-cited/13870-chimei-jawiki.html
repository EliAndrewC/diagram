ja.wikipedia 地名 ("place names", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9C%B0%E5%90%8D)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on place names: how they are made, what they are made from, and how far their origins can be known.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that naming a place from its terrain is a common way of naming, most of all for small-scale terrain and old names, and that many names' origins are disputed or unknown - the ground and the limit of reading a village's name for where it stands. Its limits: it is a general encyclopedia article, a tertiary summary, that gives no count of how many names are topographic. Its terrain passage rests on a single citation to Sugimura's 1978 book on place names, for which the article itself asks a page number. Its sentence that many origins are disputed or unknown cites nothing, and the article carries maintenance tags asking for citations. Its terrain examples are old generic Japanese terms, not modern ones, and it also treats Chinese and Korean naming, but only in passages we do not cite. A closer public source exists that we have not read: Yanagita Kunio's study of Japanese small place names, 地名の研究 (1936), on Aozora Bunko.

Used for: place names made from the terrain, and how often a name's origin is uncertain (homesteads)
