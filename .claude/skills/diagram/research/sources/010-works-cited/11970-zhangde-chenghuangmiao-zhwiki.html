Chinese Wikipedia, 彰德府城隍庙 ("Zhangde Prefecture City God Temple") (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%BD%B0%E5%BE%B7%E5%BA%9C%E5%9F%8E%E9%9A%8D%E5%BA%99)

What it is: The encyclopedia article on the City God temple of Zhangde prefecture in Anyang, Henan, rebuilt in the Ming and restored in 1982, with its site and built areas.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a prefectural city's City God temple, one rank above a county seat's. Its limits: one temple as restored in 1982, now a museum, and its areas are the present compound's, not a surveyed Ming or Qing precinct; the article gives areas but no layout; a stub encyclopedia article whose areas rest on a 2017 local newspaper (the Anyang Daily), not a survey or a period record.

Used for: the size of a prefectural city's City God temple (religion-and-death.html).
