ehime-mushiroboshi

愛媛県史 民俗 上 ("History of Ehime Prefecture, Folklore, vol. 1", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 1983, the rice harvest, in the prefecture's database えひめの記憶 (https://www.i-manabi.jp/system/regionals/regionals/ecode:2/49/view/6481)

What it is: The harvest section of the folklore volume of Ehime prefecture's official history, published in 1983 and put online by the prefecture's lifelong-learning center: reaping, the ways the sheaves were dried, threshing and husking, with local names and the older farmers' accounts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a third region, in Shikoku, recording the threshed grain dried on mats, and it names the permanent tall racks of the mountain villages. Its limits: remembered practice of the late nineteenth and twentieth centuries, and one prefecture's customs.

Used for: the grain mat-dried for three to six days after threshing; tall permanent racks of six or seven tiers in mountain villages, some roofed
