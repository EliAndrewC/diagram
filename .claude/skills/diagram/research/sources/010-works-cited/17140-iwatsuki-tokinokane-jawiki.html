What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the time bell of Iwatsuki, a castle town north of Edo: when the domain placed it, who rang it and how often.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a domain's own time bell described as the castle's bell, placed at one spot in the town, rung twelve times a day by a handful of low-ranking men - the single time bell of an ordinary castle town. It is one small domain; the tower that survives is a later rebuilding.

Used for: how many time bells a castle town kept, and who rang it (cities/capitals)
