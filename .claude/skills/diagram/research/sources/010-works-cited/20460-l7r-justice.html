l7r-justice

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "The Ministry of Justice" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#the-ministry-of-justice) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the section "The Ministry of Justice": that the ministry oversees street policing, bandit patrols, magistrates, courts and jails, and which magistrate - a Family's, a Clan's or the Emperor's - hears a dispute between samurai of which houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: which office polices a city in this setting is the setting's to decide. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; they name no town magistrate and do not say where a city's constables live, which the government page takes from Edo's town magistracies as an analogue.

Used for: which office polices a city, and the Clan and Family magistrates, behind the town magistracy and its constables (The government quarter)
