The Japanese Society of Irrigation, Drainage and Rural Engineering (農業農村工学会), 「Improving farmland: land consolidation」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「農地を改良する，耕地整理」) (in Japanese; https://www.jsidre.or.jp/tabata3-c/)

What it is: A page of the engineering society's popular history of Japan's farmland, on the land consolidation of the Meiji period and after: what it replaced, what it reorganized the plots into, and where in Japan it was and was not carried out.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says, in the words of the society whose members did the work, that the plots consolidation replaced were narrow and irregular and had come down from the early-modern period, and that western Japan was the exception, holding regular plots from the ancient grid. It is a short popular summary with no figure for the old plots' size, and it describes them from the consolidators' side, as the problem to be fixed.

Used for: the narrow, irregular early-modern paddy plots that land consolidation replaced, and western Japan's older regular ones (towns)
