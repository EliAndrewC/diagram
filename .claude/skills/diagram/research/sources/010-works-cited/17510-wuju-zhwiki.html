wuju-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 武舉 ("military examination", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/%E6%AD%A6%E4%B8%BE)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the wuju, the imperial military examination, from its Tang origins through the Ming and Qing, with a section on Korea's.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out the levels of the Ming and Qing military examination - provincial, metropolitan and palace, the last held in the Hall of Supreme Harmony before the emperor - which places the examination's stages in the provincial capitals and the imperial capital rather than the county seat. A tertiary summary; it says nothing of where candidates trained or whether a county seat had private martial schools.

Used for: the levels of the Ming and Qing military examination (government)
