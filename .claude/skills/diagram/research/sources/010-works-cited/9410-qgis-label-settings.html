qgis-label-settings

QGIS project, QGIS 3.44 User Manual, "Setting a label" (https://docs.qgis.org/3.44/en/docs/user_manual/style_library/label_settings.html)

What it is: The user manual of QGIS, the free and open-source geographic information system, for its labeling engine: how a label is placed around a point (its ranked position priority and its Cartographic mode), how far it may stand from the feature, how it treats obstacles and overlaps, and its callouts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the most complete public statement of the ranked positions around a point, and it says where the order comes from: the guidelines of Krygier and Wood's cartography textbook and others. It is software documentation for modern digital maps, not a cartographic treatise, so it describes what the engine does and what it defaults to rather than arguing why; it gives no fixed distance, leaving the gap to the map's author. Its order is also one of several: the Ordnance Survey's guide to cartography, after Imhof (1975), ranks bottom right second where QGIS ranks top left, and puts directly right or left last where QGIS puts them fifth and sixth, so the order this project uses follows one reading of a convention the cartographic literature does not fully agree on.

Used for: the ranked positions around a point and their order; the gap measured from the symbol's bounds; the maximum distance; callouts (leader lines) for displaced labels (presentation)
