nwcg-spotting

National Wildfire Coordinating Group, "Crown Fire: Spotting Fire Behavior", Fire Behavior Field Reference Guide (PMS 437) (https://www.nwcg.gov/publications/pms437/crown-fire/spotting-fire-behavior)

What it is: The spotting chapter of the US interagency Fire Behavior Field Reference Guide: how firebrands are lofted by a crown fire's convection column and carried beyond the fire, and which fires generate them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the standards body's own statement that long-range spotting comes from crown fires, which is why a small pyre's brands cannot carry a city block. Its limit is that it is written for wildland fire in North America. The guide says nothing of small or contained fires and sets no minimum fire size, so that a pyre's brands cannot carry a city block is our inference from its silence.

Used for: long-range spotting from a crown fire's convection column
