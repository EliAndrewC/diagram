inukai-kotobank

Kotobank, 犬飼 (inukai) - the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the Nipponica entry by Kaneko Shun'ichi (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%8A%AC%E9%A3%BC-31874)

What it is: The Kotobank page for inukai, collecting two dictionary definitions of the word as the keeper who raised hunting dogs for falconry and a Nipponica encyclopedia entry on the former town of Inukai in Oita prefecture, whose name it traces to an Edo-period hunt held by Taketa domain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows raising dogs for hawking as a recognized trade, and a domain lord hunting with dogs and caring for one himself. The definitions are brief tertiary entries that say nothing of where the dogs were kept, and the Taketa story is a local place-name tradition, told as hearsay.

Used for: the dog-keeper who raised hawking dogs; a domain lord nursing his own hunting dog (buildings)
