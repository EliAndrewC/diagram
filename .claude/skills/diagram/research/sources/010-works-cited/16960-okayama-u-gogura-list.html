okayama-u-gogura-list

Baba (Okayama University), "郷倉" ("Gogura", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a ranked list of surviving village storehouses by prefecture (in Japanese; https://www.cc.okayama-u.ac.jp/~baba/ranking/49_goukura.html)

What it is: A personal page on a university server listing surviving gogura across Japan, each with its form, date, designation and a note from its source: how many once stood on Sado, one built per hamlet section at Hataya, eight built jointly in the Nikko shrine estate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gathers many surviving storehouses in one place, and its notes show the unit a storehouse served varying from place to place. Its limits are that it is a hobbyist compilation of other pages rather than research, and that each note is one line from its source.

Used for: how many storehouses a district had and what unit each served (urban-features)
