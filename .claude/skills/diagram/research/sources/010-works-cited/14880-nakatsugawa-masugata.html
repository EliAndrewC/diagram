nakatsugawa-masugata

Nakatsugawa City, Nakasendo History Museum, ｟中津川宿12｠枡形(ますがた) ("Nakatsugawa post town 12: the masugata", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.nakatsugawa.lg.jp/museum/n/nakatsugawa_juku/4829.html)

What it is: A short page in a city museum's series on Nakatsugawa, a post town of the Nakasendo highway, explaining the masugata at its entrance: the highway bent twice at right angles, and the route it still takes through the town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the general purpose of a post town's masugata - defense, making the town hard for an enemy to enter - and shows one surviving example on the ground. It is a local museum's popular page, citing no sources, and it describes a single town; how many post towns had one is not on it.

Used for: that a post town's entrance bent the highway twice at right angles, and why (ways)
