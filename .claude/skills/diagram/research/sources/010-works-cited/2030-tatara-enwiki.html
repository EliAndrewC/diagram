What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the tatara, the Japanese ironsand smelting furnace: its construction, the three-day firing, the tonnages of sand and charcoal consumed and of steel produced.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the charcoal consumed against the steel produced, which is the fuel hunger this page argues from. A tertiary summary used for the tonnages; it ties the product to swords and says nothing of hooves. The tonnages are given with no date and no attribution to a period smelt; they describe the standardized run of the kind carried out today for swordsmiths, not an Edo ledger.

Used for: the tonnages of ironsand and charcoal per firing
