National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, a question on Fushimi Inari's Senbon Torii (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?page=ref_view&id=1000130073)

What it is: A library reference-service record (Kyoto City Library, 2013) on Fushimi Inari's rows of torii, saying they began to be erected from the Edo into the Meiji period through worshippers' donations.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the row custom. Its limits: a librarian's summary of the shrine's own published histories, not a dating from period records; it speaks of Fushimi only, not of when or whether rows reached village shrines.

Used for: the Edo-to-Meiji dating of donation rows (Religion and the dead).
