National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, a question on Fushimi Inari's Senbon Torii (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?page=ref_view&id=1000130073)

What it is: A library reference-service record on Fushimi Inari's rows of torii, saying they began to be erected from the Edo into the Meiji period through worshippers' donations.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the row custom. Its limits: it speaks of Fushimi only, not of when or whether rows reached village shrines.

Used for: the Edo-to-Meiji dating of donation rows (religion-and-death.html).
