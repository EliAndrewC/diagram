What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on water rights in Japan, including the customary rights the modern River Act recognizes for intakes that predate it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that intakes drawn since before 1896 hold customary right under the modern law, which is the durability of a village's water right this page rests on. A tertiary summary used for that continuity; it is about Japanese law and says nothing of China. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. The inference backward from a modern statute's recognition of old custom to the durability of a premodern village's right is this page's own.

Used for: customary water rights surviving into modern law
