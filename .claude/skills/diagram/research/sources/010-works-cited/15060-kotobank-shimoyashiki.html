kotobank-shimoyashiki

Kotobank, 下屋敷 ("lower residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - entries from the Digital Daijisen, the Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Nihon Daihyakka Zensho, the Yamakawa Nihonshi Shojiten and others (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B8%8B%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7-75422)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the shimo-yashiki, the Edo-period secondary residence of a daimyo or rich merchant, carrying several dictionary entries; the Yamakawa entry says what one held.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a lord's suburban estate was for and what it held - gardens for leisure, vegetable gardens and bamboo groves supplying the main residence - and that lords and upper retainers kept such houses in other castle towns too. The entries are short and give no size; they describe daimyo estates at Edo, far larger households than a single samurai's, and name the castle-town case only as something found.

Used for: what a lord's suburban estate held: gardens, vegetable plots and bamboo (buildings)
