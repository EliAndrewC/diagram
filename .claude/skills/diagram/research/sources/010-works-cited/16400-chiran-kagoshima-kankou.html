chiran-kagoshima-kankou

Kagoshima Prefectural Visitors Bureau, Discover Kagoshima, "Chiran Samurai Residence Complex" (in English; https://www.kagoshima-kankou.com/for/attractions/11053)

What it is: The prefecture's tourism page on the samurai district of Chiran, a Satsuma outpost town, with its stone-walled lanes, its hedges and its seven listed gardens.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a third form of the samurai house's enclosure, a stone wall with a clipped hedge behind it. It is a visitor page, and Chiran was one of Satsuma's rural samurai settlements rather than a castle town, so its form is regional.

Used for: the stone wall and hedge as a samurai house's enclosure (cities/government)
