What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the sodo, the communal hall of a Zen temple where the monks meditate, eat and sleep; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that celibate clergy lived communally in a hall that is one of the compound's own buildings, which is the housing this page implies within the wall; a Japanese Zen institution, not a Chinese urban monastery, whose one figure is the allowance of a single tatami per monk in the hall, and whose frame includes today's training monasteries.

Used for: monks housed inside the temple wall (religion and death)
