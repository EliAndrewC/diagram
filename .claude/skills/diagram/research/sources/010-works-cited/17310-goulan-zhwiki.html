goulan-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 勾欄 ("goulan", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/勾栏)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the goulan, the Song performance hall inside the entertainment precincts, and on the word's later sense of a brothel.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how many halls a Song precinct could hold and how large an audience one seated, and it dates the word's brothel sense to the Ming, which is why the Song precinct is not read as a pleasure quarter. A tertiary summary; its sentence on the hall's box-like look rests on a quotation that compares a cart's box to a railing, and its count of thirteen halls in one precinct is from a Southern Song Hangzhou source; it gives no hall's measures.

Used for: the Song halls' audiences and their number in a precinct, and the Ming change in the word's meaning (urban-features)
