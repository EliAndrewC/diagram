賈思勰 齊民要術 卷第二 ("Essential Techniques for the Common People, Scroll Two", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (Jia Sixie, Qimin Yaoshu, c. 540), Chinese Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E9%BD%8A%E6%B0%91%E8%A6%81%E8%A1%93/%E5%8D%B7%E7%AC%AC%E4%BA%8C)

What it is: Book 2 of the sixth-century Chinese agricultural treatise, with its chapters on grains, pulses, hemp and rice, in a Wikisource transcription; the rice chapter (水稻第十一) sets out the season of a flooded rice field from sowing to harvest.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a primary text from before the setting's period giving the stages of a paddy's water: weeds cut under the water, the water let out after weeding so the roots are sunned firm, watering by the season's weather, and the water taken off again as the rice ripens. It describes North China in the sixth century and says nothing of Japan, and it gives no depth of water.

Used for: the paddy's water let out and let in again through the season (fields)
