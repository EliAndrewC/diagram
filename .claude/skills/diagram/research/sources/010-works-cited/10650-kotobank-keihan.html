kotobank-keihan

Kotobank, 畦畔 (keihan, the bund), reprinting the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries' glossary of agricultural terms, with an excerpt from Heibonsha's Sekai Daihyakka Jiten (old edition) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%A6%E7%95%94-489174)

What it is: Kotobank's page of short entries on the keihan, the heaped-earth bank round a paddy that keeps its water in, including the farm ministry's own glossary entry.

Why it applies, and its limits: The ministry's entry names what else a bund is used for - a way to walk for weeding and manuring, and a place to rest - which is why a bund is where a farmer's path runs. It is a present-day government glossary that also mentions concrete and plastic bunds, so it describes the bund's uses as they are now; that the uses are old is carried by the other sources on the question, not by this one.
