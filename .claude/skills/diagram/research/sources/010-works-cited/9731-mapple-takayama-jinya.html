Mapple Web, 高山陣屋の見どころナビ (A guide to the sights of the Takayama Jin'ya), Mapple Magazine editorial staff, updated 2024-04-19 (in Japanese; https://www.mapple.net/article/48327/)

What it is: A travel guide's article on the Takayama Jin'ya by the editors of the Mapple guidebooks, walking through the office, the residence where the district intendant lived, its living room, kitchen and doma, and the storehouse.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in so many words that the district intendant lived with the family in the residence inside the compound, which is the arrangement our postings follow. Its limits are that it is a tourist guide without references, written about the buildings as they stand today, and the residence it walks through is not the Edo building but a reconstruction finished in 1996 from an 1830 plan of the compound, so its rooms, its kitchen and its three stories are a modern rebuilding of one building's last Edo layout; who lived there is a matter of the record, not of the rebuilding. Takayama was also an unusually large office, a gundai's office from 1777 rather than an ordinary daikan's.

Used for: the district intendant living with the family in the residence inside the compound; the goyoba beside the goyakusho and the writing room, as rooms of the office (buildings)
