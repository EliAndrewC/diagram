What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kido-ban, the keeper of a town's block gate: the gates installed in Edo and the castle towns, closed at the fourth hour of night, and the wicket by which those with business passed after inspection.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that block gates were the castle towns' night closure, not only Edo's, which is what lets this page say the monzen was barred like any block. A tertiary summary used for that.

Used for: block gates closed at night in Edo and the castle towns
