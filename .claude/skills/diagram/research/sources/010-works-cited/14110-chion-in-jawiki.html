ja.wikipedia 知恩院 ("Chion-in", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/知恩院)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Chion-in, the Jodo head temple in Kyoto, whose great halls were built with the shogunate's support in the early Edo period, describing how its precinct is laid out and the size of its main hall and its gate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a great temple on a hillside laid out in three terraces, gate and sub-temples below, main halls in the middle, the founder's mausoleum above, with measured sizes of the main hall and gate. It is a tertiary summary, and a head temple in the old capital is at the top of the range.

Used for: a great temple's three terraces; a main hall 44.8 by 34.5 m; a gate 24 m tall (religion-and-death)
