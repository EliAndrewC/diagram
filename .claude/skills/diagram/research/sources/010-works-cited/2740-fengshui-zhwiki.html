What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on fengshui, the geomancy of siting, with the classic formula of the good seat - mountains behind, the sun before, water in front.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the seat principle a dwelling was sited by, the hill behind and water before that this page's clusters answer to. A tertiary summary; its water is a watercourse or pond, not paddy.

Used for: the fengshui seat: mountains behind, water before
