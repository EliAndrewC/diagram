Japan Product Heritage Association, "Commentary on the Fifty-three Stations of the Tokaido, 51: Minakuchi" (東海道五十三次の解説 51 水口) (in Japanese; https://www.n-heritage.org/2024/05/%E6%9D%B1%E6%B5%B7%E9%81%93%E4%BA%94%E5%8D%81%E4%B8%89%E6%AC%A1%E3%81%AE%E8%A7%A3%E8%AA%AC%E3%80%8051%E3%80%80%E6%B0%B4%E5%8F%A3/)

What it is: A short commentary by an association of enthusiasts on Hiroshige's prints of the Minakuchi post station, noting that the Gyosho edition shows a thatched house with "kichin-yado" written large on its paper doors, and that a Hokusai print has the same composition.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one description read of a kichin-yado as a period artist drew it: a thatched house like any other, marked only by the words on its doors. It is an amateur commentary, gives no date for either print and says nothing of the building's size or rooms, and a print is a picture, not a survey. The page itself says Hokusai's print has the same composition, so the scene may be a stock image one artist took from the other rather than one building seen at Minakuchi.

Used for: what a kichin-yado looked like from the road in an Edo print (towns)
