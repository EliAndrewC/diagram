What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the City God temple, the shrine of a walled city's tutelary deity, with the Ming edict of 1369 that ranked the City Gods by the administrative rank of their cities and set off temple-building across the empire.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the ranking of City God temples down to the county, which is why a county seat on these maps carries one beside its yamen. A tertiary summary used for the edict and its date.

Used for: the 1369 edict ranking City God temples to the county
