What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Sue ware, the gray stoneware of ancient Japan: its kilns, its high reducing firing, and the tile-and-ware kiln sites of the Nara period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that ware kilns were built beside tile kilns at the same sites, which is why these maps draw one works for both. A tertiary summary used for that pairing; it is ancient, not Edo, and speaks of kilns side by side rather than one kiln firing both.

Used for: tile kilns and ware kilns built together
