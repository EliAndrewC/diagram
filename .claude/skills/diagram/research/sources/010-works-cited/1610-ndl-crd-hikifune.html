National Diet Library of Japan, Collaborative Reference Database, 江戸時代、川の下流から上流へどのように舟を動かしていたか知りたい。 (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000279632&page=ref_view)

What it is: A librarian's sourced answer in the National Diet Library's Collaborative Reference Database to the question of how Edo-period river boats were moved upstream: by sail with a following wind, and otherwise by men hauling on a long rope from the bank.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states from the library's cited works that river transport depended on hauling from the bank, which is why our river capital draws a towpath. Its limit is that it is a reference answer summarizing published histories rather than the histories themselves.

Used for: upstream river boats hauled by rope from the bank
