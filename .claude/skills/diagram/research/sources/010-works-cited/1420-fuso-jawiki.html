What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on exposure burial, the leaving of the dead in the open, naming the grounds outside Heian-kyō where it was practiced from the Heian period: Katabiragatsuji, Adashino, Toribeno and Rendaino.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests exposure at both of Kyoto's grounds this page discusses, the first of the three jobs a funerary field did. A tertiary summary used for that.

Used for: exposure burial at Toribeno and Rendaino
