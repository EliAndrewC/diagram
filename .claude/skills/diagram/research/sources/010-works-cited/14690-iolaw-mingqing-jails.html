iolaw-mingqing-jails

Institute of Law, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences (中国法学网), 明清州县的监狱 ("The jails of Ming and Qing departments and counties", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (http://iolaw.cssn.cn/gdfls/200501/t20050112_4594856.shtml)

What it is: A legal-history paper on the jails of Ming and Qing local government, published on the website of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences' Institute of Law: the statutes, the staff and the parts of a jail.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the jail's internal parts by statute - an inner jail for the condemned, an outer one for lesser convicts, a walled women's jail beside it - which is what a drawn jail compound divides into. It is about the county and department tier, and says the four-layer form held in some counties, not all.

Used for: the parts of a Chinese jail (cities/government)
