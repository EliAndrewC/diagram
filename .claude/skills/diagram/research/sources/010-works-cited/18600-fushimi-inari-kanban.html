fushimi-inari-kanban

伏見稲荷大社 ("Fushimi Inari Taisha, the Fushimi Inari shrine", title translated), "伏見稲荷大社の豆知識" ("Fushimi Inari Taisha trivia"), the shrine's page of short notes on its sanctuary, foxes, gates and mountain (in Japanese; https://inari.jp/kanban/index_01.html)

What it is: A page of the shrine's own website giving short explanations of its sights, among them the vermilion gates and the Senbon Torii, and when the custom of donating gates began there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the shrine's own dating of its donated gates to around the late Edo period, which is the latest and narrowest of the dates the shrine gives. Its limits: the institution describing itself, hedged as what "is said", with no document named; it speaks of Fushimi, the head shrine of the cult, and says nothing of any village shrine; and it gives no count of the gates in the Edo period.

Used for: the donated-gate custom at Fushimi dated to around the late Edo period (religion-and-death)
