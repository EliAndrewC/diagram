ja.wikipedia 脇本陣 ("waki-honjin", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%84%87%E6%9C%AC%E9%99%A3)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the waki-honjin, the second-rank lodging for lords at an Edo post station, with a list of surviving and rebuilt examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what set the waki-honjin apart from the honjin and from the inns. It is a tertiary summary and gives no sizes.

Used for: the waki-honjin as smaller than the honjin but furnished to its standard, taking ordinary guests when no lord used it (towns)
