hiroshima-saigoku-kaido

Hiroshima Prefecture, "江戸の世のひろしま探訪" (exploring Hiroshima in the Edo period), "解説集 - 町並み" (commentaries: townscapes) (in Japanese; https://hiroshima-history.com/townscape/)

What it is: A page of the Hiroshima prefectural government's history portal on the townscapes of the Edo period in the prefecture, among them the Saigoku road that ran through Hiroshima's castle town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the width of a highway where it passed through a castle town, the kind of road a compound's front gate might open onto. It is a general-audience summary without references, the figure is rounded ("roughly"), and it is one road in one domain. The page ranks the Saigoku road as the most important side highway after the Five Highways, a main trunk route from Kyoto to Shimonoseki, so its width is a top figure for the road a compound faces, not the width of an ordinary town street. The page also does not say whether the width is the road's width inside the town or along its whole route.

Used for: the Saigoku road at about 2.5 ken (about 15 ft) through Hiroshima (ways)
