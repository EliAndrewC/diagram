金沢市 (City of Kanazawa), 城下町金沢学術研究1 第1分冊 日本の城下町と金沢城下町 - 発展過程と空間類型 (https://www4.city.kanazawa.lg.jp/material/files/group/22/kanazawajouka1-1-s.pdf)

What it is: A city-commissioned academic report, the first volume of Kanazawa's castle-town research series, comparing Japanese castle towns and typologizing their space, and tracing how Kanazawa's quarters were arranged around the castle as the city was rebuilt.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states where a government's offices stand - inside the castle, with the ruling house's own mansion, at the apex of a hierarchy the whole samurai quarter is arranged around - which is our rule that offices cluster on the grandest compound rather than floating mid-block. Its limits are that it describes one very large castle town of one of the greatest domains, so a county seat's handful of offices is a much smaller thing; that it is Japanese; and that it works in quarters and hierarchies rather than dimensions, so it sizes nothing on our sheet.

Used for: government offices standing with the ruling house's compound at the center of the plan
