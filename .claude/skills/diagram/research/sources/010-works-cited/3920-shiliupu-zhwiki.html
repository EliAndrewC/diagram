zh.wikipedia 十六铺 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/十六铺)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Shiliupu, the wharf district of old Shanghai between the county wall and the Huangpu; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests a working riverfront of moorings and wholesale trade outside the water-side gate, which is this page's wharf outside the wall; its counts are Republican-era; the district as named dates from the 1860s and much of the article describes the treaty-port and steam-era port, so only the pre-treaty riverfront is taken.

Used for: the wharf outside the water gate (river cities)
