kotobank-kawazarae

kotobank 川浚 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%B7%9D%E6%B5%9A)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entries on kawazarae, the periodic dredging of a river bed, with Edo-period usages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the sarae this page names as a moat's maintenance and attests the practice in the Edo period, for rivers. A dictionary reference used for the term; it says nothing of moats.

Used for: the word sarae for periodic dredging
