chongqing-chengqiang-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia, 重庆城墙 (the Chongqing city wall) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/重庆城墙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the walls of Chongqing, a city on a peninsula between the Yangtze and the Jialing, tracing them from the Qin wall of 316 BC and the Shu Han wall of 226 through the Southern Song rebuilding of 1239-1240 and the Ming reconstruction of 1373 to their demolition in the twentieth century and the surveys and excavations since, gate by gate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for a walled Chinese city that took the rivers along its flanks as its moat - the Song layout "relying on the rivers as the moat", the Qianlong-era county gazetteer's "the encircling rivers serving as its moat" - with a single landward side. It is a tertiary summary that quotes the gazetteer through a 2015 newspaper report and the layout phrase through a site-survey report, and Chongqing is a hill city whose walls stand on river cliffs, so it shows the river standing in for the moat, not how a lowland river city's dug moat met its river; it nowhere compares the river's defensive worth with a dug moat's.

Used for: the river as a city's moat on the flank it runs along (cities/river-cities)
