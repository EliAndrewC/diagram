What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on bunkotsu, the division of one person's cremated remains between two or more places of interment, and the law around it; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the practice and its procedure, which is what the divided remains on this page rest on; it is written for the present day, cannot date the practice at all, and says nothing of a portion going to a head temple.

Used for: divided remains (religion and death)
