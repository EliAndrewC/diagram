ihns-bunongshu

Zhang Lüxiang, 补农书 (Bu nongshu, "Supplement to the Farm Manual", title translated), postface dated 1658, full text on the agricultural-history site of the Institute for the History of Natural Sciences (in Chinese; http://agri-history.ihns.ac.cn/books/bns.htm)

What it is: A full transcription of the farm book of Zhang Lüxiang (1611-1674), a scholar who farmed his family's land in Tongxiang county, Zhejiang, with the Shen family's farm manual printed before it and a modern editor's notes after each part.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the household garden of a rice-and-silk farm of the Lower Yangzi in south China: fenced with a thorny hedge, beside the house, and a mu of it feeding ten people their vegetables all year. Its limits: it is one well-off scholar-farmer's advice on how a farm should be kept, not a survey of how farms were; it names the mu without giving its size; and the online text has no page references and gaps where characters could not be typed.

Used for: the fenced garden beside a south Chinese farmhouse, its thorny hedge, and one mu of garden to a household of ten (Homesteads)
