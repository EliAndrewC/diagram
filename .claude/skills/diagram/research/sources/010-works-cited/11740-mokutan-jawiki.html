ja.wikipedia 木炭 ("charcoal", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/木炭)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on charcoal, with a history of how it was made in Japan period by period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the soft black charcoal, cooled inside a closed kiln, to the late Muromachi and Edo periods - the kind of kiln a farming village worked. It is an encyclopedia summary, and it says itself that the firm distinction between black and white charcoal is modern.

Used for: black charcoal burned in a closed kiln in the Edo period (vegetation)
