Sakai Tourism and Convention Bureau, "祝・開館！鉄炮鍛冶屋敷 見学レポート" ("The Gunsmith's House opens: a visit report", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.sakai-tcb.or.jp/feature/detail/84)

What it is: A tourism bureau's report on the opening of the Inoue gunsmith's house in Sakai as a museum, walking through its rooms and the family's history from its documents.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the street-facing earth floor as the finishing room, where guns were assembled and adjusted, and says the forge now shown was newly built from a Meiji-period drawing, so it separates what in the house is period fabric from what is a reconstruction. Its limits: it is a promotional visit report, not a survey; its heading calls the rebuilt forge an Edo one while its text rests it on a Meiji picture; and it gives no size or place for the forge building.

Used for: that a town gunsmith's street room was a finishing room and his forge a rebuilt separate room known from a Meiji drawing (urban-features)
