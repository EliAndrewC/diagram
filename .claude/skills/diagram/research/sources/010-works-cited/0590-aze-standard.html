aze-standard

Japanese land-improvement standard designs for 畦畔 (paddy bunds) - Aomori prefecture ほ場整備 standard drawing set and 大和川流域水田貯留技術基準 (https://www.pref.aomori.lg.jp/soshiki/nourin/noson/files/H2904_nnzusyu_H2911syuusei.pdf)

What it is: Aomori Prefecture's standard drawings for consolidated paddy (2017 revision), a public-works design standard: the outer bund drawn as a trapezoid 30 cm high above the higher field, 30 cm wide at the crest, with side slopes of 1:1 to 1:1.2, in three types by the difference in level between the fields it separates, up to 4 m, with berms on the tallest.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives an engineered cross-section for a paddy bund in numbers, which calibrates the most repeated line on a paddy map against the one to two feet the period sources give. Its limits are that it is a post-war land-consolidation standard for machine-formed embankments between fields whose surfaces may differ by meters, a different object from a hand-piled ridge between two level plots, so its figures are a modern engineered minimum and not a measurement of a premodern bund; that a hand-piled bund would have been less regular; and that an enlarged cold-region bund of about 50 by 40 cm is not on the Aomori sheet - the berms on it are for tall bunds, and its only frost-heave provision concerns a flume's subgrade - and the other standard the entry names is neither linked nor quoted, so that figure stands as a guess.

Used for: the standard outer bund as a trapezoid of 30 cm top width, 30 cm high, 1:1 side slopes (so ~90 cm at the base); the enlarged cold-region bund of ~50 cm x 40 cm is not on the Aomori sheet and the Yamato standard is unlinked, so it stands as a guess
