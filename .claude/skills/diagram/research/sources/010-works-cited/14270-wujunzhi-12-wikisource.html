范成大 (Fan Chengda), 吳郡志 ("Gazetteer of Wu Commandery"), 卷十二 ("Chapter 12", title translated from the Chinese by this project), completed 1192, Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/吳郡志/卷12)

What it is: A chapter of the Southern Song gazetteer of Suzhou that opens its section on the shrines and temples of the cults, entering each by name with its history.

Why it applies, and its limits: With chapters 31 and 32 it shows that the gazetteer filed the cults' shrines apart from the Buddhist temples. One gazetteer of one great city; it is not evidence of how every gazetteer filed them.

Used for: the cults' shrines filed under their own heading, one of them moved from outside a gate into the city (religion-and-death)
