范成大 (Fan Chengda), 吳郡志 ("Gazetteer of Wu Commandery"), 卷十二 ("Chapter 12", title translated from the Chinese by this project), completed 1192, Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/吳郡志/卷12)

What it is: A chapter of the Southern Song gazetteer of Suzhou. Its first half tells of the officials of past dynasties (官吏); its second half opens the section on the shrines and temples of the cults (祠廟) and enters each by name with its history.

Why it applies, and its limits: With chapters 31 and 32 it shows that the gazetteer filed the cults' shrines apart from the Buddhist temples. One gazetteer of one great city; it is not evidence of how every gazetteer filed them. The line between the two was not clean even here: a hall of the Heavenly King, a Buddhist guardian, is filed among the cults' shrines. Read in a Wikisource transcription that the page marks as not fully proofread.

Used for: the cults' shrines filed under their own heading, one of them moved from outside a gate into the city (religion-and-death)
