tsuijimatsu

tsuijimatsu.com (https://tsuijimatsu.com/)

What it is: The preservation website for the tsuijimatsu, the tall clipped pine windbreak hedges of the Izumo Plain, run by the city of Izumo in Shimane Prefecture through its architecture and housing division. Besides documenting the hedges and the dispersed-settlement landscape they belong to, the site runs pruning workshops and tree-health programs, and carries a page comparing Izumo's hedges with the homestead groves of other regions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the species of a homestead grove and what each was cut for - the cedar core, the ash, elm, oak, bamboo, persimmon and chestnut around it, leaves and dead wood for fuel, bamboo for the household's gear - which is what our grove is planted with. Its limits are that it is a conservation council's site rather than a survey; that it describes living groves kept under a modern conservation program; and that the passage we quote is on its regional-comparison page and describes another prefecture's groves - the kainyo of Tonami in Toyama - so it is one municipal body's secondhand description of another region, which happens to agree with the Toyama sources cited beside it.

Used for: bamboo in the homestead setting
