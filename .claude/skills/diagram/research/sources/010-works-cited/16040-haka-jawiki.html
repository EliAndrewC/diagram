What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the grave, with a section on graves in the modern period.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one fact: until the Second World War many graves were built on a corner of a household's own land or kept by a neighborhood group, so a burial place beside the settlement, not a central one, was ordinary. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary, gives no source for the sentence, no period earlier than the twentieth century and no share of households, and does not say how far from the houses such graves lay.

Used for: graves on a household's own land or kept by a neighborhood group, before the Second World War
