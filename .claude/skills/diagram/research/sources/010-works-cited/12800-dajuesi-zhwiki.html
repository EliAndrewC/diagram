zh.wikipedia 大觉寺 (北京) ("Dajue Temple, Beijing", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/大觉寺_(北京))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Dajue Temple in Beijing's western hills, with the buildings of its middle axis and its total site area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the order of a Chinese temple's axis with its bell and drum towers. It is a hillside temple outside the city, not a town temple, and the article's site area of 6,000 m² disagrees with the far larger figure the Beijing cultural heritage bureau is reported to give, which could not be read.

Used for: the order of buildings on a Chinese temple's axis (religion-and-death)
