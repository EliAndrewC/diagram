Shirahata Shrine (Fujisawa), 境内案内 - 石玉垣 (https://www.shirahata-jinja.jp/blog/%E5%A2%83%E5%86%85%E6%A1%88%E5%86%85%EF%BD%9E%E7%9F%B3%E7%8E%89%E5%9E%A3%EF%BD%9E/)

What it is: The shrine's own blog post on its stone fence, carved with a date of 1928 and raised by subscription to mark the Showa emperor's enthronement.

Why it applies, and its limits: A dated fence around the shrine building, raised by a later donation. Its limits: a town shrine with many donor neighborhoods, not a hamlet's, and a twentieth-century fence; it dates the form, not the premodern norm.

Used for: a stone fence of 1928 around the shrine building (religion-and-death.html).
