ranzan-temple-register

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 第9節：寺院・神社・堂庵明細帳, index of the Hiki district temple register (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09_00.html)

What it is: The index page of the Ranzan town office's local-history site for its transcription of the Hiki district temple, shrine and hall registers made by the Saitama prefectural school affairs section in 1963, listing the area's nineteen temples by village and linking one page per temple.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how many temples one rural district of Saitama kept and in which of its old villages, and each linked page gives a temple's precinct in tsubo and its main hall in ken. Its limits: one district of the Kantō plain, rural rather than a town; a register begun in the Meiji era and carried into a 1963 file, so the figures are not Edo figures and some pages add later surveys; a transcription with editors' footnotes; the counts, ranges and medians drawn from it are this project's arithmetic.

Used for: the temple count per village and the band of a temple's precinct and hall (religion-and-death.html).
