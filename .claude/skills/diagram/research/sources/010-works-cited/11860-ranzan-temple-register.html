ranzan-temple-register

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 第9節：寺院・神社・堂庵明細帳, index of the Hiki district temple register (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09_00.html)

What it is: The index page of the Ranzan town office's local-history site for its extract of the main entries of the Hiki district temple, shrine and hall registers held in the Saitama prefectural school affairs section's 1963 files, covering only the present Ranzan town area (the Meiji villages of Nanago and Sugaya, not the whole district): it lists that area's nineteen temples by village and links one page per temple, and lists its seventeen shrines and twelve halls and hermitages separately.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how many temples one rural part of a Saitama district kept and in which of its old villages, and each linked page gives a temple's precinct in tsubo and its main hall in ken. Its limits: some twenty old villages making up one corner of one Kantō-plain district, rural rather than a town; a register begun in the Meiji era and carried into a 1963 file, so the figures are not Edo figures and some pages add later surveys; the precincts are those left after the Meiji confiscations of temple land (1871, 1875), which cut many precincts back to the ground the rites needed, so a precinct in tsubo likely understates the same temple's Edo precinct; the area's twelve halls and hermitages are registered apart from the temples and are not in the count of nineteen, though many would have been counted as small temples in the Edo period, so the count is a floor; an extract of the register's main entries rather than a full transcription, with editors' footnotes; the counts, ranges and medians drawn from it are this project's arithmetic.

Used for: the temple count per village and the band of a temple's precinct and hall (religion-and-death.html).
