tabiiro-takayama-jinya

旅色 (Tabiiro, a travel guide), 高山陣屋 ("Takayama Jin'ya", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://tabiiro.jp/leisure/s/200247-takayama-takayamajinya/)

What it is: A travel-guide listing for the Takayama Jin'ya, the shogunate's surviving intendant's office in Hida, with a short description of the grounds and the buildings a visitor sees.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of the grounds of the one intendant's office whose main buildings survive, our second anchor for the Imperial Magistrate's compound. It is a commercial travel listing (its text is MAPPLE's guidebook copy), not a survey, and gives the size only as "exceeding 3,000 tsubo"; the grounds are the restored site of one mountain province's office. Gifu Prefecture's own report from the Takayama Jin'ya management office gives the grounds as 9,807.42 m² (about 2,967 tsubo, just under 3,000) and the designated historic site as 11,219.05 m² including the square in front, so the guidebook's round figure is roughly right but is not the office's measurement.

Used for: the Takayama Jin'ya's grounds exceeding 3,000 tsubo (cities/capitals)
