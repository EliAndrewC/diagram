kamigamo-plan-kyoto

京都市 (City of Kyoto), 上賀茂伝統的建造物群保存地区保存計画 ("Preservation plan for the Kamigamo preservation district for groups of traditional buildings", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/tokei/page/0000281306.html)

What it is: Kyoto city's official preservation plan for Kamigamo (published by city notice in 1988), the quarter of the houses of the Kamigamo shrine's priest families, formed from the Muromachi period, describing how the priest families' houses and the town houses beside them were built.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets a hereditary priest family's house against a town house of the same street, which says whether clergy households could be told from their neighbors. It describes one quarter of one great shrine as preserved today, and its priests are shrine priests, not the temple families of a Buddhist house.

Used for: priest families' houses one-story, gabled and walled with a gate, unlike the town houses beside them (religion-and-death)
