dechi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia, 丁稚 ("dechi, a shop apprentice", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%81%E7%A8%9A)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the dechi, the boy apprentice of a merchant house, especially numerous in the Edo period: his age on entering service, his live-in life in the shop, his pay and his rise to clerk.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the apprentice lived in the shop he served from about the age of ten, which is why a town's merchant servants are not drawn as houses of their own. It is a tertiary encyclopedia article, written of the merchant houses of the great cities as much as of a county town's, and it says nothing of the building beyond that the apprentice lived in it.

Used for: a merchant's apprentices living in the shop from about the age of ten (towns)
