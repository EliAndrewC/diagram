ja.wikipedia 本堂 ("hondo, a temple's main hall", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/本堂)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the hondo, the hall of a Buddhist temple that houses its principal image.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the hall at a temple's center that houses the principal image, and it says that hall goes by other names depending on era and sect: kondo at temples founded from the Asuka to the early Heian period, chudo in Tendai, butsuden in Zen, daiyu hoden in Obaku. Some temples have both a kondo and a hondo, and the article says no clear line can be drawn between the names. It is a Wikipedia stub, a tertiary summary whose two listed references are not tied to any statement, so it carries the definition and not a detail. It covers Japan only and says nothing of Chinese temples, which our setting is equally modeled on. It gives no sizes.
