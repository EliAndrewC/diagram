ja.wikipedia 口留番所 ("kuchidome bansho", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8F%A3%E7%95%99%E7%95%AA%E6%89%80)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kuchidome bansho, the barrier posts the Edo-period domains set on their borders and at key points of traffic in place of the barrier stations the shogunate forbade them: what they watched, the duty they levied and their hours.

Why it applies, and its limits: It separates the domain's border post from a town's own watch, which is what keeps a border post off a town map. A tertiary summary with few inline citations, one of them a television program; it describes the institution, not any post's buildings.

Used for: the domain's barrier posts on its borders, watching travelers and goods, levying a duty, open sunrise to sunset (defenses)
