What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the aze, the paddy bund of heaped mud that divides the fields and holds their water, with the named bund along an open channel.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the bund's purpose - that the water not leak out - and names the bund on the ditch side, which is why a basin on these maps is sealed on every side. A tertiary summary used for the definitions.

Used for: the bund holding the paddy's water on every side
