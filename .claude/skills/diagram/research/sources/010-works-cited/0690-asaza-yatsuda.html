asaza-yatsuda

NPO Asaza Fund, "The water cycle of the yatsuda" (谷津田の水の循環), a public education page (https://www.asaza.jp/activity/yatsuda2/)

What it is: A page from a Japanese conservation group working on the Lake Kasumigaura catchment, explaining for a general reader how a valley-head paddy is watered: rain stored in the upland woods seeps out as springs, a pond is made where the water issues, the paddies lie below it, and the used water runs on to the river and the lake.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the small valley paddy's water from source to river in one page, and it is the one source read that says in so many words that the water, once used, goes on to the river. Its limits are that it is a present-day outreach page, not scholarship, describing one region's valley paddies, and that it says nothing about a stream running beside the paddies, so it neither confirms nor denies a brook surviving its intake.

Used for: the spring impounded where it issues, the paddies below, the used water flowing on to the river
