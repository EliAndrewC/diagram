chinaknowledge-tang-econ

chinaknowledge.de, Tang-period economy (http://www.chinaknowledge.de/History/Tang/tang-econ.html)

What it is: Ulrich Theobald's survey of the Tang-period economy on ChinaKnowledge.de, a single-author sinological reference site, covering land tenure, the great estates and the place of the monasteries in them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries in one sentence all three things this record asserts about monastic wealth - that manors were owned by monasteries among others, that they held oil mills, spinneries and breweries, and that the labor on them was slaves, craftsmen and tenant farmers - which is what puts productive enterprise inside a temple's economy on these maps rather than leaving a precinct as a place of worship only. Its limits are that it is a serious single-author reference rather than primary or peer-reviewed scholarship, and that its own authorities for this material, Gernet and Twitchett, could not be read.

Used for: monastic manors carrying oil mills, spinneries and breweries, worked by dependent labor; monastic estates outside the state's tax reach
