hanamoku-enoki

Hanamoku Sanpo, エノキ ("enoki", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a tree guide page (in Japanese; https://hanamokusanpo.jp/book/2129)

What it is: A popular tree-guide page on the enoki (Japanese hackberry), whose section on the tree's place in human life quotes a Chiba prefecture series on the prefecture's great old trees.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that an enoki was planted near a village's entrance beside its boundary god or small shrine, and why that is thought to have been - the one readable statement the record found of a village's entrance tree. It is a secondary guide quoting a prefectural leaflet; the reasons it gives are hedged ("is thought to have been"), and it gives no date or count.

Used for: the enoki at a village's entrance beside its boundary shrine (vegetation)
