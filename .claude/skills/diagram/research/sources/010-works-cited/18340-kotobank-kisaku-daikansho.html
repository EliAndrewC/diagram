kotobank-kisaku-daikansho

「The Kisaku lodge and Kisaku daikan office site」 (translated; original: 「木作御仮屋・木作代官所跡」), in Nihon rekishi chimei taikei (日本歴史地名大系, Heibonsha), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9C%A8%E4%BD%9C%E5%BE%A1%E4%BB%AE%E5%B1%8B%E6%9C%A8%E4%BD%9C%E4%BB%A3%E5%AE%98%E6%89%80%E8%B7%A1-3022613)

What it is: The entry, in Japan's standard historical gazetteer of place names, for the site of a daikan's office of the Tsugaru domain in Kisaku (Aomori), reproduced on Kotobank.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives an official compound's vegetable ground in measure, from the land survey of 1736: beside the residence lot of 70 by 60 ken, a vegetable field of 30 by 20 ken, two tan. Its limits are that the gazetteer, summarizing the register in one sentence, gives the field as its own measured parcel beside the residence lot and does not say whether it lay inside the same enclosure or where; that Kotobank shows only part of the entry (the rest is on JapanKnowledge, which was not read), so the rest may say more; and that it is one office of one northern domain.

Used for: an official compound's vegetable field, its size, and that it could be a parcel of its own (buildings.html).
