What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the ashigaru, the foot soldiers of the warrior houses: their rise in the wars and their Edo-period standing, strictly set apart from full samurai status and often reckoned with the household servants.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that Edo ashigaru stood below samurai status. A tertiary summary used for that standing. The Edo-period section the quoted sentence sits in carries the encyclopedia's own insufficient-sources notice.

Used for: ashigaru set apart from samurai status in the Edo period
