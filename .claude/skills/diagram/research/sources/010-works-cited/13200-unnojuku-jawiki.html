ja.wikipedia 海野宿 ("Unno-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B5%B7%E9%87%8E%E5%AE%BF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Unno, a post station of the Hokkoku Kaido in Nagano and a preservation district, describing about a hundred historic buildings along 650 m of the old road, with lattice fronts and fire walls (udatsu) on the roofs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a surviving road town whose houses were built close enough to need fire walls between them. It describes one preserved town as it stands, with buildings of several periods including later sericulture houses, and does not date the fire walls or say how many towns had them.

Used for: a post town's fire walls on its roofs (towns)
