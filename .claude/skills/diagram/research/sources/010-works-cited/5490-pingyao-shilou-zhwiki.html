What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Pingyao Shilou (平遥市楼), the market tower that straddles the middle of South Street at the center of the walled county town of Pingyao in Shanxi, giving its plan, its footprint, its height and the date of the rebuilding that produced the present structure.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the plan, footprint and height of a real market tower at the center of a county town - three bays square, about 133 square meters, 18.5 m high, standing in the middle of the main street - which is what sizes the tower on our county-seat maps. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary and the figures we take are its whole use, while its only reference is an archived page on a commercial site, so the numbers rest on thin support; that the present tower is a Qing rebuild of 1688 rather than an earlier one; and that Pingyao was an unusually rich merchant town whose tower is unusually well preserved.

Used for: 「The ground story is three bays across the front and three bays deep, occupies 133.4 square meters, and is square in plan.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「底层面阔、进深各三间，占地133.4平方米，平面呈方形」), 18.5 m high, rebuilt 1688
