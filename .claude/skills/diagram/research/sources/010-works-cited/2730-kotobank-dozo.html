kotobank-dozo

kotobank 土蔵 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%9C%9F%E8%94%B5)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the reference-encyclopedia entries on the dozo, the plastered earthen storehouse: its construction and what townhouses and farmhouses kept in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the labor the walls took and what a farmhouse stored, the construction behind this page's reading of the kura as a wealth marker. A tertiary summary of published reference works; it does not price the building.

Used for: the dozo's construction and contents
