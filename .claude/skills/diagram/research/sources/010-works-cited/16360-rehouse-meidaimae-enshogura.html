rehouse-meidaimae-enshogura

Mitsui no Rehouse, 明大前で江戸時代の面影をたどってみよう ("tracing the Edo period at Meidaimae", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2019 (in Japanese; https://www.rehouse.co.jp/relifemode/column/burarehouse/meidaimae/)

What it is: A neighborhood-history column on a real-estate firm's site about the Meidaimae district of Tokyo, where the shogunate kept a gunpowder magazine on the Koshu highway from the 1750s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the magazine's place outside the city on a highway, its ground and its guard. It is a promotional column written for a real-estate site on a local historian's walk, and it cites nothing. Its account of the guard does not match the Suginami ward's site-marker text: the column has the magazine's officials guarding it day and night, while the marker says three officials lived there in yearly turns and the guarding was a labour levy on 16 nearby villages. It never says why the magazine stood outside the city; it ties the site to the highway's military role, so "outside the city" is our reading of its location. And the magazine was the shogunate's in Edo, not a domain's.

Used for: a gunpowder magazine kept outside the city (cities/government)
