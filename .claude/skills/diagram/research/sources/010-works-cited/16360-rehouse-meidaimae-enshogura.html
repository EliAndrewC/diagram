rehouse-meidaimae-enshogura

Mitsui no Rehouse, 明大前で江戸時代の面影をたどってみよう ("tracing the Edo period at Meidaimae", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2019 (in Japanese; https://www.rehouse.co.jp/relifemode/column/burarehouse/meidaimae/)

What it is: A neighborhood-history column on a real-estate firm's site about the Meidaimae district of Tokyo, where the shogunate kept a gunpowder magazine on the Koshu highway from the 1750s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the magazine's place outside the city on a highway, its ground and its guard. It is a popular column that cites nothing, and the magazine was the shogunate's in Edo, not a domain's.

Used for: a gunpowder magazine kept outside the city (cities/government)
