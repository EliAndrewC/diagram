en.wikipedia, Beijing city fortifications (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_city_fortifications)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the walls, gates and moats of Beijing from the Yuan through the Ming and Qing, giving the perimeter of each ring - the Inner city, the Outer city and the imperial and palace cities - and the history of their demolition in the twentieth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Ming inner city's wall perimeter, the size of the city the Beijing drum tower served. It is a tertiary summary and gives the inner city as a perimeter, not as side lengths, so any east-west and north-south figures are read off a map rather than taken from this page.

Used for: the extent of Beijing's inner city, as the area one drum tower timed (the bell-and-drum tower)
