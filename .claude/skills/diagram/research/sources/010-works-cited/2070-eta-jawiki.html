What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the eta, the hereditary outcaste status of pre-Meiji Japan: its occupations, its administration under the chori heads, and where its communities stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states where the communities stood - the village edge, beside a river - and what work the status carried: disposing of fallen cattle and horses, hide work, leather goods, sandals, lamp wicks, and the executioner's, constable's and watchman's duties. A tertiary summary used for the siting and the occupations; it is a national generalization and names no particular castle town. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources.

Used for: the outcaste quarter at the edge and by water; the caste's occupations
