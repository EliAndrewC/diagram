kotobank-bansho

Kotobank, 番所 (the guard post), entries from Shogakukan's Digital Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Encyclopedia Nipponica, and others (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%AA%E6%89%80-118119)

What it is: The Kotobank page gathering dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the bansho, the guard post of the Edo period: the shogunate's posts at ports and rivers, the domains' kuchidome posts at their borders, and the watch posts of the towns.

Why it applies, and its limits: Several standard references agree that the domains set guard posts at their borders to watch people and goods, and sometimes to tax goods. Their limits are that they are short entries, giving the posts' purpose and not their buildings, and that they describe the Edo period's borders between domains, which our setting's clan borders only resemble.

Used for: the domains' kuchidome guard posts at their borders, watching and sometimes taxing people and goods (buildings)
