l7r-tariffs

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "The Ministry of Revenue" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the section on the Ministry of Revenue, which set out that tariffs are charged where goods are sold, not where they pass through.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: whether a river watch takes a toll from passing boats is the setting's to decide, and the maps follow it. There is no historical limitation to state; the notes say nothing about river watch posts themselves.

Used for: a river watch that inspects and takes no toll from boats passing by (river cities)
