攻城団ブログ (Kojodan blog), 【お城の基礎講座】53. 水堀（みずぼり）, 2020-09-25 (https://blog.kojodan.jp/entry/2020/09/25/180000)

What it is: A numbered lesson in the castle-visiting community Kojodan's basics course, on the wet moat: how flatland castles drew water from rivers and how a dug moat often filled from below on its own.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the groundwater case in plain terms, the source our inner moat is written to hold. Its limit is that it is a hobbyist community's explainer.

Used for: water welling up in a dug moat
