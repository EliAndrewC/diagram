shukubamachi-kotobank

kotobank 宿場町 ("post town", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the dictionary and encyclopedia entries gathered on one page (https://kotobank.jp/word/宿場町-528292)

What it is: The kotobank page for the Edo-period post town, gathering its Japanese dictionary and encyclopedia entries: how the town was laid out along its highway, how wide the highway ran through it, where the transport office and the lodgings for lords stood, and what marked its two ends.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a reference-work summary of the Japanese post town of the seventeenth to nineteenth centuries - the one planned small town of the period whose street, lots and ends are described in figures - so it speaks directly to a town on a trunk road. It generalizes across hundreds of post towns and names no one town; its figures are ranges, and its contrast between busy highways and byways is hedged as what was often the case.

Used for: the width of the street through a post town and the watercourse down it, the strip lots along it, the order of its buildings from the middle outward, and the barrier banks at its ends (ways)
