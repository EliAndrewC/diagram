sayama-jinya-uematsu

Uematsu Kiyoshi, 絵図を読む② 大阪狭山藩陣屋 (Reading the plans, 2: the Osaka-Sayama domain jin'ya), note.com, 2022-02-24 (in Japanese; https://note.com/kuematu0404/n/n7cd277b4e3f3)

What it is: An illustrated essay in a local-history series by Uematsu Kiyoshi, an architectural historian of early-modern Osaka (a doctorate from Osaka City University, and a first-class architect), reading the domain's own plans and records of the jin'ya of the small Sayama domain (Osaka prefecture): the land granted to upper retainers, the fences and hedges, the council hall's rooms, the detached houses the domain supplied to its middle-rank retainers and the long-houses of the lower ones.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is one of very few readable accounts of how a small domain's seat housed its retainers room by room, and it gives the upper privies of an official building with a shikidai genkan, and the bath and privy of middle-rank houses as additions. Its limits are that it is an essay on a blogging platform, not a reviewed paper, though written by an architectural historian reading named domain records; that the council hall is an official building of 1775 whose plan the author only surmises the upper houses shared; and that Sayama was a domain of about ten thousand koku, a small seat rather than a typical one.

Used for: the council hall's two upper privies with board verandas; the bath and privy added on to the middle-rank houses (buildings)
