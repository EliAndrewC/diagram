kotobank-shuson

Kotobank, 集村 ("clustered village", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the entry from Heibonsha's Hyakka jiten Mypedia (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%9B%86%E6%9D%91-77146)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the clustered village, gathering dictionary and encyclopedia entries; the Mypedia entry says the clustered village is the general form in Japan and describes its dense and loose regional variants.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the plainest statement read of which settlement form was usual in Japan, and of how it differed between the west and the east. It is a short modern encyclopedia entry describing the countryside as geographers know it, not a count; "general" and "common" are its words, with no figure and no period.

Used for: the clustered village as the general form in Japan, dense in the Kinai and Setouchi and loose in the Kanto and Tohoku (archetypes)
