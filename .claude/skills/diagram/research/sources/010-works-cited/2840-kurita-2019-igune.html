栗田英治・重岡徹・武元将忠 (Kurita Hideharu, Shigeoka Toru and Takemoto Masatada), on conserving the igune landscape of the Osaki Koudo world agricultural heritage area, 農業農村工学会誌 87(10) (2019), 825-828, J-STAGE PDF (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjsidre/87/10/87_825/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A report in the irrigation and rural engineering society's journal on conserving the homestead-grove landscape of the Osaki agricultural heritage area in Miyagi, with heights of six groves measured on the ground and from the air.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured heights of Tohoku homestead groves, the anchor for the tall end of this page's grove band. A present-day survey of surviving groves in one district.

Used for: measured igune heights at six points in Osaki
