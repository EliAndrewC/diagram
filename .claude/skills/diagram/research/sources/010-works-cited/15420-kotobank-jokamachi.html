Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten (Heibonsha, the passage used by Fukai Jinzo), Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho / Nipponica (Shogakukan) and other works, s.v. 城下町 ("castle town", translated from the Japanese by this project), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%9F%8E%E4%B8%8B%E7%94%BA-78941)

What it is: The kotobank page for the Japanese castle town, carrying several encyclopedia entries on how the towns were founded, who was gathered into them and how they were zoned.

Why it applies, and its limits: Signed encyclopedia entries, one of which qualifies the separation of farmer from townsman: in small provincial castle towns some townsmen farmed alongside their trade. It says nothing of where they farmed - inside a town's defenses or outside them - and a castle town is not a walled Chinese-style city.

Used for: townsmen who farmed beside their trade (cities/fabric.html, settlements.html)
