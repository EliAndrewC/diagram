冷鹏 (Leng Peng), "生存需求、政策引导、经济转型与技术革新：明清苏南生态农业的发展动因探析" ("Subsistence, policy, economic change and technical innovation: the drivers of ecological agriculture in southern Jiangsu in the Ming and Qing", title translated), Journal of Nanjing Agricultural University (Social Sciences Edition) 20(6), 2020, 105-114 (in Chinese; https://html.rhhz.net/njnydxxbskb/20200611.htm)

What it is: A peer-reviewed paper by a doctoral student of Nanjing Agricultural University's institute of Chinese agricultural history, on why intensive, recycling farming (the mulberry-dike fishpond, multiple cropping, water vegetables) grew up south of the Yangzi in the Ming and Qing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a modern historian's reading of the Ming-Qing Lake Tai region, the region the record's polders come from; its water-vegetable paragraph rests on a 2012 doctoral thesis (Cao Ying) rather than a period text quoted on the page, and it names districts, not areas or shares.

Used for: Wu county the main lotus-root district among Suzhou prefecture's water-vegetable districts in the Ming and Qing (archetypes)
