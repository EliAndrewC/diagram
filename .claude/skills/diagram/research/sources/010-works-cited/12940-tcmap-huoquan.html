tcmap-huoquan

tcmap.com.cn, 霍泉 ("Huo Spring", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (http://www.tcmap.com.cn/landscape/10/huoquan.html)

What it is: A short Chinese gazetteer-style tourist entry on Huo Spring in Hongdong county, Shanxi, whose spring pool, division pavilion and stele pavilion are the head of an old irrigation district shared between two counties.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a Chinese built division work: under the pavilion iron pillars part ten openings, three to the south county and seven to the north, and a stele records the split with a diagram. It is a tourist listing, it gives no date for the pillars, and the split is between two counties, far above a village ditch.

Used for: a Chinese split set by counted openings between iron pillars (water)
