shinagawa-kaizoji-muentou

品川区 (Shinagawa City, Tokyo), designated cultural property 海蔵寺無縁塔群 (the group of muen stupas at Kaizoji) (https://www.city.shinagawa.tokyo.jp/PC/sangyo/sangyo-bunkazai/sangyo-bunkazai-shitebunkazai/shina/20240108180212.html)

What it is: A ward's listing for a designated cultural property: the group of stupas for the unconnected dead at Kaizoji in Shinagawa, the temple that took the dead of the Shinagawa holding jail, the victims of the Tenpo famine and those drowned by a tsunami.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries every element of the pauper ossuary our city maps draw, on dated Edo evidence: more than seven hundred prisoners dead of the holding jail between 1691 and 1765, their bones gathered up and reinterred in 1708 under a raised burial mound with a single Kannon image set on top, and stupas for the unconnected dead raised beside it in 1865 and 1873, the later one enshrining 1,268 of them. Its limits are that it is a municipal heritage listing for one temple rather than a survey; that the temple stands in the Shinagawa post town and not outside a wall beside a crematory, so it supports the FORM of the ossuary and not its position; and that it gives no dimension for the mound.

Used for: the pauper ossuary as a mound of gathered bones with one image on top and muen stupas beside it, dated 1708 onward
