What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the shake, the hereditary priestly families of a shrine; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the office passed within a family for generations and that the families' houses stood near the shrine, clustering into a named quarter, which is the householder clergy this page relies on; it gives no generation count and speaks of Japanese shrines, not Chinese temples.

Used for: hereditary shrine priesthoods and where they lived (religion and death)
