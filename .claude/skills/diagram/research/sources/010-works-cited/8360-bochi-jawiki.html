What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the graveyard as a category, which distinguishes the kinds - among them the village graveyard, held in common and managed by the inhabitants of a settlement - and notes that new graveyards of that kind are no longer permitted under present-day Japanese law.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one fact: the village graveyard held in common by its own inhabitants is a recognized kind of place, which is what justifies drawing one ground for a settlement rather than scattered family plots. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary largely concerned with modern regulation, that it gives no size, no siting rule and no period for the village form, and that it gives no household threshold and nothing on the disused grounds left standing.

Used for: the village graveyard held in common and managed by the settlement's own inhabitants
