en.wikipedia, Keep (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keep)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the keep, the great tower of the European castle: its square and round forms, the vulnerability of a square tower's corners to mining and to the dead ground they create, and how little that weakness mattered before the siege engines and crossbows of the twelfth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the European comparison this page draws - the corner as the weak point of a curtain - and, read fully, it corrects the comparison by calling the weakness theoretical for most of the period. A tertiary summary, and about Europe rather than East Asia.

Used for: the theoretical weakness of a square tower's corners and the date after which it mattered
