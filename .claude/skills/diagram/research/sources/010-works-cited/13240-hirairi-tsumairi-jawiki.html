ja.wikipedia 平入と妻入 ("hirairi and tsumairi", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%B9%B3%E5%85%A5%E3%81%A8%E5%A6%BB%E5%85%A5)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article defining hirairi and tsumairi, the two names for a building by which face holds its main entrance: the long side under the ridge, or the gable end.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the definition that ties a house's roof orientation to where its door is, so either orientation puts a town house's door on the street. It is a short definitional article, marked by the encyclopedia as lacking sources; it is used only for the definition.

Used for: the entrance on the ridge side or the gable end (towns)
