zhwiki-chengkan

zh.wikipedia 呈坎村 (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/呈坎村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Chengkan, a historic village in the Huizhou district of Huangshan, Anhui, listed among China's famous historic villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the village's river on its east side, flowing past it - a one-bank siting, and a check on travel pages that describe the same river as running through the village. It is a short tertiary article, and the river is a 19 km watercourse, larger than a hamlet's brook.

Used for: that the Zongchuan river flows past the east side of Chengkan (water)
