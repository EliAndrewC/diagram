zhwiki-chengkan

zh.wikipedia 呈坎村 (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/呈坎村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Chengkan, a historic village in the Huizhou district of Huangshan, Anhui, listed among China's famous historic villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the village's river on its east side, flowing past it - a one-bank siting, and a check on travel pages that describe the same river as running through the village. It is a short tertiary article, and the sentence on the river carries no footnote of its own. It describes the present-day village, whose water has been changed since (a lake was dug in 2007). It is silent on the two channels (水圳) that other accounts say carry the river's water through the village's streets, so it shows the river on one side of the village, not a village that draws nothing from it. The river is a 19 km watercourse, larger than a hamlet's brook.

Used for: that the Zongchuan river flows past the east side of Chengkan (water)
