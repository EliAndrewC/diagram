yakage-honjin

Yakage Town, 旧矢掛本陣石井家 (The former Yakage honjin, the Ishii house) (in Japanese; http://www.town.yakage.okayama.jp/machi/annai/honjin.html)

What it is: The town of Yakage's (Okayama prefecture) page on the Ishii house, the honjin of the Yakage post town, an Important Cultural Property, describing its site of about 1,000 tsubo and its buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists, at a surviving honjin, the guest facilities (the o-zashiki with its jodan-no-ma and the gate for a lord's arrival) apart from the main house where the family lived and brewed sake. Its limits are that it is a short municipal page; that it lists rather than plans, so whether the o-zashiki is a building of its own is not said; and that a honjin was a commoner family's licensed lodging house, not a samurai residence.

Used for: the honjin's guest facilities listed apart from the family's main house (buildings)
