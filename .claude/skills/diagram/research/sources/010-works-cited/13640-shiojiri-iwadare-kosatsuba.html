shiojiri-iwadare-kosatsuba

Shiojiri City, Cultural Properties, "Kosatsuba" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高札場（こうさつば）」) (in Japanese; https://www.city.shiojiri.lg.jp/site/bunkazaihouhou/29756.html)

What it is: The Shiojiri city cultural-properties page on the surviving notice board of Iwadare, a farming village in Nagano prefecture, a city-designated historic site: its stone base, its roof and its seat between the houses of the village's two headmen.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a VILLAGE board still standing, which the post-town tables are not, and it says where in the village the board was set. The board is thought to have been repaired between the 1820s and 1850s, and the page calls it the only one in the city that keeps its old form, so it is one survivor, perhaps a large one; the page gives no dimensions, and the equidistant seat answers a village split between two lords, which was not the ordinary case.

Used for: a village board's stone base and roof, and its seat between the headmen's houses (urban-features)
