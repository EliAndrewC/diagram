shiojiri-iwadare-kosatsuba

Shiojiri City, Cultural Properties, "Kosatsuba" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高札場（こうさつば）」) (in Japanese; https://www.city.shiojiri.lg.jp/site/bunkazaihouhou/29756.html)

What it is: The Shiojiri city cultural-properties page on the surviving notice board of Iwadare, a village (岩垂村) in the Seba area of Shiojiri, Nagano prefecture, a city-designated historic site: its stone base, its roof and its seat between the houses of the village's two headmen.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a VILLAGE board still standing, which the post-town tables are not, and it says where in the village the board was set. The board is thought to have been repaired between 1818 and 1854 (the Bunsei to Kaei eras), and the page calls it the only one in the city that keeps its old form, so it is one late-Edo survivor, and the page calls it large and fine, not an ordinary village's board. The page is a short, unsigned municipal summary that cites no record for its dating or for its reading of the board's placement, and it gives no dimensions. The equidistant seat answers a village split in 1691 between the shogunate's own land and the Takatō domain, which was not the ordinary case.

Used for: a village board's stone base and roof, and its seat between the headmen's houses (urban-features)
