kotobank-kyodo-bochi

Digital Daijisen (Shogakukan), Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (Shogakukan) and the old edition of the Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten (Heibonsha), s.v. 共同墓地, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/共同墓地)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the dictionary and encyclopedia entries reached under the communal graveyard, the burial ground a settlement holds in common; the passage this record quotes is from the encyclopedia's entry on the grave (墳墓) rather than from an entry on the communal graveyard itself.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a settled community forms its graveyard apart from its dwellings, which is the siting this page relies on; the statement is a universal one about settled as against nomadic life, undated and with no East Asian, period or scale content, so it bears on these maps only as a general rule; dictionary entries of a few lines, with no figures and nothing on how many foundations draw on one ground.

Used for: the communal graveyard outside the built area (religion and death)
