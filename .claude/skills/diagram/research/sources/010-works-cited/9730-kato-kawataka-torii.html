kato-kawataka-torii

Kato City, 河高住吉神社の石鳥居 (the stone torii of Kawataka Sumiyoshi Shrine), a prefecture-designated cultural property (https://www.city.kato.lg.jp/kakukanogoannai/kyouikushinkoubu/shogaigakushuka/bunkazai/siteibunkazai/kensiteibunkazai/1510384600965.html)

What it is: Kato City's (Hyogo) page on a single stone torii at an ordinary shrine, 2.5 m between the posts, dated to the late Muromachi or early Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a single stone arch at an ordinary shrine early in the period. Its limits: one arch, dated by style, and a single gate, not a row.

Used for: a single early stone arch at an ordinary shrine (religion-and-death.html).
