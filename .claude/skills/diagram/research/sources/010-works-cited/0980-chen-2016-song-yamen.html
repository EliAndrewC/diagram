陈凌 (Chen Ling), 建筑空间与礼制文化：宋代地方衙署建筑象征性功能诠释, 西南大学学报（社会科学版） 42(5): 182-187, September 2016 (https://xbgjxt.swu.edu.cn/data/article/preview-pdf?doi=10.13718/j.cnki.xdsk.2016.05.023)

What it is: A peer-reviewed article in the journal of Southwest University on the Song local yamen as a symbolic building: how its central axis and its front-hall, rear-residence layout expressed the ritual order of rank, from an author at Nanyang Normal University's college of humanities.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the axial and hierarchical plan of the Chinese local government compound from the architectural-history literature, which is why the yamen on our city maps is drawn axial and symmetrical. Its limits are period - the Song, some centuries before the Qing yamen that survive - and that it argues from ritual meaning rather than from surveyed plans.

Used for: the central axis and front-hall, rear-residence layout of a local yamen
