弘前市 (City of Hirosaki), 弘前市歴史的風致維持向上計画 第4章 ("Hirosaki plan for maintaining and improving its historic character, chapter 4", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.hirosaki.aomori.jp/jouhou/keikaku/files/rekimati4shou.pdf)

What it is: A chapter of Hirosaki city's official plan for its historic townscape, describing the castle town's districts, among them the two temple streets the Tsugaru lords laid out in the seventeenth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says why and when the temples were gathered - for the castle town's defense and the control of its population - and that a second temple street was laid out after a fire, sorted by sect. It is a city planning document summarizing the history in a few sentences, with no plot sizes.

Used for: temples gathered in 1610 for defense and control, the castle-town temple quarter as a defensive arrangement; a Soto temple street and, after the 1649 fire, a street for the other sects (religion-and-death)
