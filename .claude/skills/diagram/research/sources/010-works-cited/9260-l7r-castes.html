l7r-castes

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Castes" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the section "Castes". They set out the Celestial Order's castes - samurai, the half-people farmers and merchants, the non-people laborers, servants and entertainers - with the burakumin as the lowest of the non-people, doing the ritually unclean work of morticians and tanners, and monks standing outside the Celestial Order altogether.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: who handles the dead, and whether doing so pollutes, is the setting's to decide, and these maps follow it. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; how the monks and the burakumin divide the work at a pyre is not in them.

Used for: the burakumin as the setting's morticians and the monks' order standing outside the caste system, behind who serves at the crematory (religion and death)
