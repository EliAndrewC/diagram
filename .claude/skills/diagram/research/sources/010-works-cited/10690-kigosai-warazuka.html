kigosai-warazuka

Kigosai (the Society for Season Words and Almanacs, an NPO), "藁塚 (warazuka), late autumn", in its online almanac of haiku season words (in Japanese; https://kigosai.sub.jp/001/archives/2187)

What it is: The online haiku almanac's entry on the straw rick as a season word of late autumn, with its other names, a short explanation of how it is built and what its straw is for, and one example verse.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what shape a rick took and what the straw went to, compost and straw goods, which is why it stood through the winter to be drawn on. A present-day almanac written for poets; it gives no date, place or measurement, and filing the rick under late autumn says when the rick is seen, not how long it stood.

Used for: the rick built round a central pole; its straw chopped for compost or made into bales and rope; a late-autumn season word (fields)
