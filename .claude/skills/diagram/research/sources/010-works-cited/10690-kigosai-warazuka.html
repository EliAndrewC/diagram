kigosai-warazuka

Kigosai (the Society for Season Words and Almanacs, an NPO), "藁塚 (warazuka), late autumn", in its online almanac of haiku season words (in Japanese; https://kigosai.sub.jp/001/archives/2187)

What it is: The online haiku almanac's entry on the straw rick as a season word of late autumn, with its other names, a short explanation of how it is built and what its straw is for, and one example verse.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one form of the rick, straw stacked in a cylinder round a central pole, and says the shape varied from region to region; and it says what the straw went to, compost and straw goods (rice bags and rope), which is our reason for drawing the rick still standing into the winter - the entry itself does not say how long it stood. A present-day almanac written for poets by volunteers, citing nothing; it gives no date, place or measurement, the pole-centered cylinder is one regional form among several, and its one example verse is of the twentieth century (Matsumoto Takashi, 1906-1956), so nothing here dates the rick to the Edo period or earlier, and filing the rick under late autumn says when the rick is seen, not how long it stood.

Used for: the rick built round a central pole; its straw chopped for compost or made into rice bags (tawara) and rope; a late-autumn season word (fields)
