en.wikipedia, Chinese units of measurement (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_units_of_measurement)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on traditional Chinese units, with a table giving, dynasty by dynasty, the length in meters of the foot (chi), of the pace (bu) at five and at six feet, and of the li.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read that turns the paces of a sixth-century treatise into meters, and it gives the two reckonings of the pace side by side. It is a tertiary summary; its table has a Wei-Sui row (220-266 and 581-618) and nothing for the centuries between, so a foot for the 540s is carried across a gap the table leaves, and the article itself notes that it omits the several kinds of foot in use in some periods.

Used for: the pace as five or six feet, and the Wei-Sui foot, to put the Qimin yaoshu bed in feet (cities/hinterland)
