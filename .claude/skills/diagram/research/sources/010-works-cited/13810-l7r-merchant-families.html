l7r-merchant-families

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Merchant Families" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the section "Merchant Families": what the merchant families of the setting do, and that besides their main business they draw much of their revenue from renting farmland, because most farmers are tenants and most landlords are merchants.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: who holds a village's land in the setting is the setting's to decide, and it makes the tenant household the ordinary one and the rich farming landlord the exception. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; they do not say where a tenant lives or how big a tenant's house is.

Used for: most farmers being tenants and most landlords merchants (homesteads)
