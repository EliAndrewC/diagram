en.wikipedia "Temple of Confucius" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Confucius)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Confucian temple, the school-temple every prefectural and county school carried; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Tang decree of 630 that put a Confucian temple in every province's and county's school and the temple's siting at the school, which is the state character of the school-temple this page relies on; it is a tertiary summary and dates the requirement to the Tang, not the Ming.

Used for: the Confucian school-temple as a state foundation (religion and death)
