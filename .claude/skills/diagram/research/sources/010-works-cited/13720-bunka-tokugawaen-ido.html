bunka-tokugawaen-ido

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Bunka Isan Online, "Tokugawa-en bucket well" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「徳川園釣瓶井戸」) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/234271)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's entry for a registered tangible cultural property in Nagoya: a stone bucket well of about 1900 on the former Owari Tokugawa estate, with its cut-granite curb, its posts, its gabled roof and its iron pulley.

Why it applies, and its limits: An official description of a surviving well with a well house and its footprint. It is a Meiji-era well on a great house's estate, built with care over its details, so it shows the form at its most finished, not a village's common well.

Used for: a curb of cut stone, a roof on posts and a hung pulley, on about 1.6 square meters (urban-features)
