What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Hirosaki Castle: its original extent of 612 by 947 m and 385,200 square meters, and its surviving three-tier keep, the lowest of the extant three-tier keeps at about 14.4 m.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the castle's original area, against which the keep's footprint is measured on this page. A tertiary summary used for that figure. A 47,000-koku domain is a modest one, which makes it a good anchor; but the English article on the same castle, registered separately, quotes the same extent, so the two keys are one datum in two languages and do not corroborate one another.
