sukumo-shishi-kenchi

Sukumo City, 『宿毛市史』 ("History of Sukumo City", title translated from the Japanese by this project), early modern part, "the organization and life of farming villages: the land system", as published on the city's website (https://www.city.sukumo.kochi.jp/sisi/058401.html)

What it is: A section of the municipal history of Sukumo, in Kochi prefecture (the old Tosa domain), on the land surveys of 1589-1590 and after, and on the cultivators the survey registers list with no house plot of their own.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that many registered cultivators held no house plot, most of them kin, retainers or dependents of a powerful farmer living in a separate building or hut on that farmer's plot, and counts 120 plot-holders among 360 registered cultivators in one village - the evidence that many households lived on another's plot. Its limits: the survey is of the very start of the Edo period, when such dependents were most common; the count is one village's; and the history itself offers other readings of the plotless names (exempt officials, cultivators from other villages), so the proportion is not a village's households.

Used for: the cultivators with no house plot, living in huts and separate buildings on a powerful farmer's plot (homesteads)
