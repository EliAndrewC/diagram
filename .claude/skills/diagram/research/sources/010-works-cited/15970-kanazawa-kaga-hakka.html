kanazawa-kaga-hakka

Kanazawa City (Cultural Properties Protection Division), 加賀八家屋敷跡 ("The estate sites of the Kaga Eight Houses", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www4.city.kanazawa.lg.jp/soshikikarasagasu/bunkazaihogoka/gyomuannai/3/1/1/bunkazai_tagengo/kagahakka/index.html)

What it is: A short page of the city's multilingual heritage series introducing the eight elder houses of the Kaga domain and the sites of their estates: which houses they were, that each inherited a stipend of 10,000 koku or more, and a table of each house's late-period stipend (Honda 50,000 koku down to the Naoyuki-line Maeda at 11,000); it names no author or date and cites no records.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the holding of the house whose estate is our upper anchor, so the anchor can be set against the holdings of the shogunate's scale of estates. It is a brief introductory page for visitors about one exceptional domain, and it gives no areas itself. Its stipends are rounded late-period figures (it gives the senior Okumura house 17,000 koku where other city pages give 17,450). The page says nothing about how a house's holding relates to the size of its estate, or whether Kaga followed the shogunate's scale for its own retainers in its own castle town, so setting our anchor against that scale is our inference, not the page's.

Used for: the eight elder houses each holding 10,000 koku or more (cities/capitals)
