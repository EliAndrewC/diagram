kanazawa-kaga-hakka

Kanazawa City (Cultural Properties Protection Division), 加賀八家屋敷跡 ("The estate sites of the Kaga Eight Houses", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www4.city.kanazawa.lg.jp/soshikikarasagasu/bunkazaihogoka/gyomuannai/3/1/1/bunkazai_tagengo/kagahakka/index.html)

What it is: A short page of the city's multilingual heritage series introducing the eight elder houses of the Kaga domain and the sites of their estates: which houses they were and that each inherited a stipend of 10,000 koku or more.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the holding of the house whose estate is our upper anchor, so the anchor can be set against the holdings of the shogunate's scale of estates. It is a brief introductory page for visitors about one exceptional domain, and it gives no areas itself.

Used for: the eight elder houses each holding 10,000 koku or more (cities/capitals)
