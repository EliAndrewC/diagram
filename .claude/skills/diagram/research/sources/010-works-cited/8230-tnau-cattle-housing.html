tnau-cattle-housing

TNAU Agritech Portal, Animal Husbandry, housing space for cattle (https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/animal_husbandry/animhus_cb_housing_space.html)

What it is: A page of the Agritech Portal of Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, an Indian agricultural extension resource, tabulating the floor space livestock housing should allow by class of animal - for adult cattle, 3.5 square meters under cover and 7.0 square meters of open area, with a manger 60 to 75 cm.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a second modern figure for the room one adult beast needs, and it is what we check the FAO manual against: the two come out on the same order of size, which tells us neither is idiosyncratic. Its limits are the manual's and two more. The agreement is NOT independent corroboration of anything premodern - both pages are normative housing standards saying what a keeper ought to provide for modern stock, so both may be generous for the same reason, and two such standards agreeing says they share a convention rather than that an old byre measured what they recommend. The page is also unsigned and carries no references, so no figure on it can be traced back to a study. And it is a recommendation rather than a survey of what exists, written for southern Indian husbandry, so it stands in for East Asian practice on the strength of the animal rather than the place.

Used for: a second modern standard for the same question: the covered and open space an adult beast is given, and the width of its manger
