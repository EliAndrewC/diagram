"Getting close to the history of irrigation in ancient China", The Paper (government account), 2024 (in Chinese; https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_27710049)

What it is: A popular article on China's heritage irrigation works, published on The Paper by a government account, describing among others the Mulan weir in Putian, Fujian, begun in 1064.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the one means of shutting a water opening read in this pass: boards slotted into cut grooves, added or removed to set the water held. The Mulan weir is a large river work, not a village intake, and the article is a modern popular summary.

Used for: that a flow was regulated by gate boards slotted into grooves and added or removed (water)
