guangdong-xinyu-27

Qu Dajun (屈大均), Guangdong xinyu (廣東新語, New Sayings of Guangdong), chapter 27, "Grass talk" (草語), the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E5%BB%A3%E6%9D%B1%E6%96%B0%E8%AA%9E/%E5%8D%B727)

What it is: The chapter on grasses and herbaceous plants of a late-seventeenth-century description of Guangdong, with entries on the banana and on sugar cane: where they were grown, and how banana and white cane were rotated on the same ground.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the primary account nearest the dike-pond hamlet's period of where banana and cane grew in the delta, and it places both in fields, not on pond dikes. That is an absence in two entries of one book, not proof that no pond dike carried either; and its rotation is described for one district, Zengcheng, not for the delta as a whole.

Used for: that banana and cane were field crops in the late seventeenth century, rotated on the same ground in Zengcheng, with nothing placing either on a pond dike (archetypes)
