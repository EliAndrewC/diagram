zh.wikipedia 龍骨水車 ("Dragon-bone water wheel", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hant/%E9%BE%8D%E9%AA%A8%E6%B0%B4%E8%BB%8A)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the longgu shuiche, the square-pallet chain pump, with its history from the Eastern Han through its spread in the Song and its working principle.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the pump spread further in the Song, suits the plains, and lifts water about 1 to 2 meters from a river or canal to fields by human or animal power - the Chinese counterpart of the Japanese devices, in the Song period the setting draws on. It is a tertiary article resting partly on popular web sources.

Used for: the Chinese chain pump's lift and its spread in the Song (water)
