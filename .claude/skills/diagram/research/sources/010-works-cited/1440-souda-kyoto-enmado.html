そうだ 京都、行こう。 (JR Tokai's Kyoto campaign site), 千本ゑんま堂［引接寺］ (https://souda-kyoto.jp/guide/spot/senbonenmado.html)

What it is: A temple guide page on JR Tokai's Kyoto tourism site for the Senbon Enmadō, Injō-ji: its position at the entrance of the Rendaino funerary ground and its standing as the hall where the spirits of the dead are welcomed back.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries the gate-temple's own tradition of standing where the living pass to the other world, which is what this page uses the temple for. Its limit is that it is a tourism page, and it does not mention the Odoi rampart.

Used for: Injō-ji at the entrance of Rendaino as the place leading to the other world
