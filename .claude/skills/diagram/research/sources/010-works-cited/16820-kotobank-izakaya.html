kotobank-izakaya

kotobank 居酒屋 ("drinking house", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%B1%85%E9%85%92%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entries and the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry on the drinking house in Japan (by Suzuki Shin'ichi), in China (by Terada Takanobu) and in Europe.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives both halves of the setting's frame: the Japanese drinking house of the later Edo period, found everywhere and in Osaka even on the bridges, and the Chinese wine shop, which spread with the towns and market towns of the Song and stood across the country under the Yuan, Ming and Qing, marked by a blue flag. Signed reference entries: tertiary summaries that cite no sources. The Osaka bridges are hedged as hearsay ("it is said") and describe a great city, not a small town. The entry states the blue flag in its paragraph on Tang Chang'an, as the common usage, and does not repeat it for the Song-to-Qing town and market-town shop. The entries give no counts for a small town.

Used for: Osaka's drinking houses even on the bridges, and the Chinese wine shop in towns and market towns under its flag (urban-features)
