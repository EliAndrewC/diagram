伏見稲荷大社, the Senbon Torii page of the shrine's own precinct map (https://inari.jp/sp/map/spot_07/)

What it is: The Senbon Torii entry in Fushimi Inari Taisha's own precinct map: what the tunnel of gates along the approach to the Myobu-sha is, and when the custom of dedicating them began.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the shrine's own dating of the donated-gate custom to the Edo period, which is what lets a premodern map draw a votive approach at all rather than a modern tourist one. Its limits are the same as its sibling's: the institution describing itself rather than a survey, and the most famous shrine of its kind, so it bounds the top of the range and never the middle.

Used for: the dedication of torii along the Myobu-sha approach as an Edo-period devotion
