What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Jiangning imperial weaving office at Nanjing, one of the three Jiangnan weaving offices that supplied the Qing court, with the wares each of the three was assigned.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the court's weaving was confined to three cities, which is why our capital gets a purveyor and not an imperial works. A tertiary summary used for the three cities and their wares; it gives no headcount, and the arrangement is the QING court's, so the negative drawn from it - no imperial works at a provincial seat - is one dynasty's organization of court supply carried across the period.

Used for: the three Jiangnan weaving offices and their assigned wares
