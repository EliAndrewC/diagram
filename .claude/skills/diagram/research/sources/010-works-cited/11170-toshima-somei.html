toshima-somei

Toshima City, 豊島区が発祥の美しい桜 ソメイヨシノのふるさと ("The beautiful cherry born in Toshima: the home of the Somei-Yoshino", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/132/miryoku/meguru/someiyoshino.html)

What it is: The ward government's page on Somei village at the northern edge of Edo, whose nurserymen bred and sold the Somei-Yoshino cherry, citing the 1824 gazetteer that named Somei a source of primroses, azaleas and chrysanthemums.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a village on a city's edge given over to growing plants, chrysanthemums among them, for the city's market. A municipal page written to promote local history; it gives no areas or plot sizes, and Somei's nurserymen grew trees and potted plants rather than field crops of flowers.

Used for: Somei, a nursery village on Edo's edge, as a producer of chrysanthemums for the city (fields)
