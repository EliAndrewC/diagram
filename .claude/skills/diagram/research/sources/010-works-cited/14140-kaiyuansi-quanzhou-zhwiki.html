zh.wikipedia 开元寺 (泉州) ("Kaiyuan Temple (Quanzhou)", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/開元寺_(泉州))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Kaiyuan temple of Quanzhou, a prefectural city of Fujian, founded under the Tang and rebuilt under the Ming, with its ground, its buildings on and beside its axis and the size of its main hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: A great temple of a prefectural city rather than a capital, laid out building by building with a measured main hall. The ground it gives is the temple's today, the halls are Ming and later rebuildings, and a great port city is still well above the setting's provincial city.

Used for: a prefectural city's great temple of 78,000 m², its axis from screen wall to sutra library, twin pagodas east and west, a main hall of nine by six bays (religion-and-death)
