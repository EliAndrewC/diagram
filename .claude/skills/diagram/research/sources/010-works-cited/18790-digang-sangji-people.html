"Zhejiang Huzhou mulberry-dike fishpond system: the wisdom of water control that makes farming thrive," People's Daily Zhejiang edition, 2023-11-26 (in Chinese; http://zj.people.com.cn/n2/2023/1126/c186327-40655074.html)

What it is: A regional People's Daily report from Digang village, on the south shore of Lake Tai, the core of the Huzhou mulberry-dike fishpond system listed as an agricultural heritage site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the fish ponds as a reporter sees them today, from the air and on foot, and dates the water works only as "over a thousand years" old; it places no layout of the ponds before the present day, so it is evidence of the modern look and of nothing earlier.

Used for: that the chessboard look of the ponds is the present-day view from the air (archetypes)
