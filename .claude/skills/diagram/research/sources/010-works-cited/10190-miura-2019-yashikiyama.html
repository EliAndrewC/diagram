miura-2019-yashikiyama

Miura Osamu (三浦 修), 「The coining of the term yashikirin and its spread into geography」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「用語「屋敷林」の造語過程と地理学への普及」), Quarterly Journal of Geography (季刊地理学) 71:3 (2019), pp. 120-127, short report (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tga/71/3/71_120/_pdf)

What it is: A short report in the journal of the Tohoku Geographical Association tracing how the word yashikirin (homestead grove) was coined by forestry writers in the early twentieth century, and quoting the older words for the same thing - among them an entry from a 1684 Mito-domain register of village woods, and a 1910 forestry paper's description of the Musashino landscape.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes a seventeenth-century register that gives three named households' homestead woods with their dimensions and areas, the only period measurement of such a wood this record has read, and a 1910 description of how the woods and fields were arranged around a group of farmhouses. It is a study of a word, not a survey of groves: the three register entries are examples the author picked, from one Mito-domain village, and one of them (9 by 9 ken, stated as 5 se 21 bu) does not multiply out as printed; the 1910 landscape is the Musashino upland near Edo, as a forester saw it.

Used for: the size of a homestead's own wood in a 1684 register, and the order of woods and fields around a farmhouse group (vegetation)
