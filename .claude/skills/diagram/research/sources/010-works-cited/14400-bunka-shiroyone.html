bunka-shiroyone

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 白米の千枚田 ("The Shiroyone Senmaida", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/173825)

What it is: The national cultural-properties database's description of the Shiroyone terraces at Wajima (Ishikawa prefecture), designated a scenic place in 2001: the slope and its fall, the number and average size of the paddies, and their earth bank faces.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the government's own designation text, with a paddy count and an average size and the fact that the bank faces are earth. Shiroyone is the extreme of smallness on a landslide slope, largely rebuilt in the nineteenth century after a landslide of 1684, so it bounds a terrace paddy from below rather than typifies one; the prefecture's page counts over a thousand paddies where this one counts about eight hundred.

Used for: the smallest terrace paddies and their earth bank faces (archetypes)
