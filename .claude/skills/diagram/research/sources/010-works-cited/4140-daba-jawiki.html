ja.wikipedia 駄馬 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/駄馬)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the pack horse and its regulated load; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the pack horse's load, 150 kin (about 90 kg) by a decree of 739 and 36 kan (about 135 kg) by the Edo regulation, both legal ceilings rather than measured loads, which is the ceiling this page puts on land carriage; a tertiary summary.

Used for: the packhorse's load (ways)
