agematsu-shinden-rokujizo

Hachijuni Culture Foundation, "六地蔵（新田墓地）" ("Six jizo (Shinden graveyard)", title translated), in its catalog of Nagano's cultural properties (in Japanese; https://www.82bunka.or.jp/bunkazai/detail.php?no=2036&seq=0)

What it is: A catalog entry for a set of six stone jizo at the Shinden graveyard in Agematsu, Kiso district (Nagano), a town-designated tangible cultural property, giving its period as Edo and its date as 1678 (Enpo 6).

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a set of six jizo in a graveyard in the Edo period, in a mountain post town. Its limits: a catalog line, not a study; it does not say whether the date is inscribed, nor where in the graveyard the figures stand (at its entrance or elsewhere); it dates the figures, not where they stood - its title names the graveyard they are in now, and it does not say they have stood there since 1678 rather than been moved there later; it is one site, and says nothing of a cremation ground.

Used for: a dated Edo-period six jizo at a graveyard (religion-and-death)
