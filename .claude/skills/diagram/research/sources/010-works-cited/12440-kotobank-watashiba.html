kotobank-watashiba

「Ferry landing」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「渡し場」), in the Digital Daijisen (Shogakukan), with an excerpt from the Heibonsha Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten entry on the crossing, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B8%A1%E3%81%97%E5%A0%B4-665240)

What it is: A dictionary definition of the ferry landing, beside a short excerpt of an encyclopedia entry listing the Edo highways' river crossings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the landing and names which Tokaido rivers were forded and which ferried. The encyclopedia text is an excerpt cut off mid-list, so it names examples, not every crossing.

Used for: the ferry landing, and which Tokaido rivers were forded and which ferried (ways)
