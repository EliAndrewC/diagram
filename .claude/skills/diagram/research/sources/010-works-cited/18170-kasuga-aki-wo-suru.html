kasuga-aki-wo-suru

平田善積, 秋をする ("Doing the Autumn", title translated), 春日風土記, Kasuga city, Fukuoka prefecture (https://www.city.kasuga.fukuoka.jp/miryoku/history/1010879/1012451/1013121.html)

What it is: A short piece by a member of the Kasuga local-history society, first printed in the city bulletin in 2021, on the old rice harvest in northern Kyushu: reaping, the sheaf stacks in the field, threshing, and drying the grain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a fourth region, Kyushu, recording the threshed grain dried on mats every day. Its limits: a popular local-history column, recalling practice before combine harvesters without a date.

Used for: the grain dried on mats every day after threshing
