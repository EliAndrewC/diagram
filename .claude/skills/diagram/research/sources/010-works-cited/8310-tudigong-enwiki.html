en.wikipedia, Tudigong (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tudigong)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Tudigong, the Chinese earth god, and the small shrines kept for him - in villages, in the countryside, and inside larger temples and houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the shrine's PLACE: the article shows such a shrine standing within a village's entrance gate, which is what puts the village shrine at the way in on our maps rather than at its center. Its limits are sharp: the instance it shows is a photograph caption of one walled village in Hong Kong, not a general statement; the article's own PROSE gives a different typical placement, describing such shrines as commonly located beneath the main altar of a temple or below the door of a house, so a reader of the page would come away with the household and temple shrine rather than the village gate; and nothing on it connects the earth-god shrine to the water-mouth specifically, so the join our record makes between the two is our own reading and is labeled as such.

Used for: the earth-god shrine standing inside a walled village's entrance gate
