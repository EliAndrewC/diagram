sanheyuan-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 三合院 ("three-sided courtyard house", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E4%B8%89%E5%90%88%E9%99%A2)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the sanheyuan, the Han Chinese house of a main range with a wing on each side around a court, with its plan, the use of each part and the smaller forms beside it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the three-sided house as a basic Han house form and names the simpler single-range and L-shaped forms beside it, so a Chinese-leaning village has more than one plan to draw. Its limits: a short tertiary article whose examples are Fujian and Taiwan and whose account of who lived in which room is the ideal, not a survey; it gives no sizes and no share of households in each form.

Used for: the three-sided, L-shaped and single-range plans of a Chinese farmhouse (homesteads)
