en.wikipedia "Cangue" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cangue)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the cangue, the wooden collar worn as a public punishment in China and its neighbors, with its use in the Qing code and its later religious survivals.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the collar was worn continuously for up to several months and shows the magistrate's seal on the boards, which is the public half of the punishment this page draws. A tertiary summary used for the duration and the seal; it gives no statutory table of terms. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. The seal cited is read off a photograph of about 1870, a late-Qing object rather than a statute.

Used for: the cangue worn for months under the magistrate's seal
