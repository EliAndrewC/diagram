Kotobank, 門前町 - 改訂新版 世界大百科事典, 日本大百科全書 and ブリタニカ国際大百科事典 entries (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%96%80%E5%89%8D%E7%94%BA-143199)

What it is: The kotobank reference page for the monzen-machi, the town before a great shrine or temple's gate, carrying the entries of three Japanese reference works: a street-village of inns, shops and craft workshops along both sides of the pilgrimage road, contrasted with the jinaicho, the temple town ringed by moat and rampart.

Why it applies, and its limits: Standard reference works on the open commercial form of the gate-front town, and on the enclosed temple town as a distinct type, which is why our capital's monzen is drawn as open machi. Its limit is the encyclopedia's summary form.

Used for: the monzen as an open street-village of inns and shops, and the moated jinaicho as a different type
