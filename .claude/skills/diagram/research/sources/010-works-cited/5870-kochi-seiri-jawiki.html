ja.wikipedia 耕地整理 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%80%95%E5%9C%B0%E6%95%B4%E7%90%86)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on 耕地整理 (kochi seiri, arable-land consolidation), the rearrangement of farm plots into regular rectangles squared to the compass, covering the Shizuoka method first carried out in 1872 and the national consolidation law of 1899.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for a date rather than a measurement: it places the neat rectangular grid of Japanese paddy in the Meiji era and after, which is why the fields on our maps keep the irregular shapes the ground gave them. It is a tertiary summary whose figures we take only where its own references support them, and here what we take is an institution and its statute, which is the kind of thing such an article states plainly.

Used for: rectangular plot consolidation as a Meiji institution - the Shizuoka method of 1872, the 耕地整理法 of 1899/1900, 「the regularization of plot shape and its alignment to the cardinal directions」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「区画の整形化と正方位化」)
