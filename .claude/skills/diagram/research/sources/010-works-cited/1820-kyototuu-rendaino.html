kyototuu-rendaino

京都通百科事典 (Kyoto-tsu encyclopedia), 蓮台野 (https://www.kyototuu.jp/Geography/AreaRenndaino.html)

What it is: A privately compiled Kyoto topographical encyclopedia's entry on Rendaino, the valley from Funaoka-yama to the Kamiya river that was the old capital's northern funerary ground, and the road of a thousand stupa-tablets that led to it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the ground at the head of the road that became Senbon-dōri, corroborating the Wikipedia article on the street. Its limit is that it is an amateur reference.

Used for: Rendaino's extent and its road of tablets
