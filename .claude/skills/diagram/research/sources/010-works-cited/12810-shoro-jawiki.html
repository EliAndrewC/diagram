shoro-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 鐘楼 ("shoro, a bell tower", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/鐘楼)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the bell tower of a temple, where its bell hangs, and how its place in the temple plan changed from the Nara period to the Muromachi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the building is and where it stood in the plan. It gives no sizes and says nothing of how many temples kept one.

Used for: what a temple bell tower is and where it stood (religion-and-death)
