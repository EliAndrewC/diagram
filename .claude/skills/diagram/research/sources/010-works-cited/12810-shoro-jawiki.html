shoro-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 鐘楼 ("shoro, a bell tower", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/鐘楼)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article (an encyclopedia summary by anonymous editors, revised 2025) on the bell tower of a temple, where its bell hangs, and how its place in the temple plan changed from the Nara period to the Muromachi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the building is and where it stood in the plan. It gives no sizes and says nothing of how many temples kept one. It is a tertiary source: its account of the plan rests on one 1986 general handbook (Mae Hisao, Kokenchiku no kiso chishiki), and its examples are great temples such as Hōryū-ji and Tōdai-ji, not the small temple of a village or town. Its short section on China covers palace and city bell towers, not temple ones.

Used for: what a temple bell tower is and where it stood (religion-and-death)
