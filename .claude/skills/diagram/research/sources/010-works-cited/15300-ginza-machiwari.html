ginza-machiwari

Ginza Design Council, "The machiwari Ieyasu made" (in Japanese, title translated by this project; https://www.ginza-machidukuri.jp/column/29/)

What it is: A column by Ginza's district design council on how the townsmen's quarter from Nihonbashi to Kyobashi was laid out when the shogunate was founded in 1603: the square block, the lots along its streets, the open ground in its middle, and the shops and tenement rows on each lot.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Edo block in figures, the Japanese early-modern counterpart of the ancient capital's block. It is a short popular page by a modern district body, with no citations; it describes the one quarter it is about rather than all of Edo, and it says itself that the use of the open middle is unknown.

Used for: the Edo block of 60 ken, its 20-ken lots and open middle (cities/fabric.html)
