What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Tonami plain, the alluvial fan in Toyama whose dispersed farmsteads are Japan's best-known case, with the Kaga domain's reclamation policy held to explain the scattering.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the tenure arrangement thought to be a major cause of the dispersed pattern, the reason this page gives for its dispersed hamlets. A tertiary summary; the arrangement is a cultivation right rather than a land grant, and the reclamation runs from the late Sengoku into mid-Edo.

Used for: the Kaga tenure arrangement behind the scattered farmsteads
