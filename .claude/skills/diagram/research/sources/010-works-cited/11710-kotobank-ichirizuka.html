Digital Daijisen, Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and other entries, s.v. 一里塚 ("milestone mound", title translated from the Japanese by this project), on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/一里塚-31425)

What it is: Several Japanese dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the milestone mound, the largest of which says the mounds faced each other across the road and that their tree was, as a rule, an enoki.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms the pairing and the tree from a standard dictionary. The entries differ in how firmly they put it - one says "as a rule", others "mostly", and one notes pine and cedar too.

Used for: the enoki as the milestone mound's usual tree (vegetation)
