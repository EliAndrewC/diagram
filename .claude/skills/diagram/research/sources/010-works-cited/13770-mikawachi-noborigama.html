mikawachi-noborigama

Mikawachi Ware cooperative, "The climbing kiln" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「登り窯」) (in Japanese; https://www.mikawachiware.or.jp/learn/climbingkiln/)

What it is: A page of the Mikawachi ware (Nagasaki prefecture) producers' site on the two great climbing kilns the village once fired, with their lengths and working dates, and on the small kiln the potters rebuilt in 1996, with its dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates and measures two long-lived communal kilns of a domain porcelain village. It is the producers' own page, written for visitors, and gives no source for the lengths; the rebuilt kiln is a modern teaching kiln and is not used here.

Used for: two village kilns of 100 m and 120 m, fired from the late 17th and early 18th centuries (urban-features)
