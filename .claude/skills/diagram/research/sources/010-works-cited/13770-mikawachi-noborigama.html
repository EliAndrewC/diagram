mikawachi-noborigama

Mikawachi Ware cooperative, "The climbing kiln" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「登り窯」) (in Japanese; https://www.mikawachiware.or.jp/learn/climbingkiln/)

What it is: A page of the Mikawachi ware (Nagasaki prefecture) producers' site on the two great climbing kilns the village once fired, with their lengths and working dates, and on the small kiln the potters rebuilt in 1996, with its dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates and measures the two long multi-chamber climbing kilns of Mikawachi's Sarayama district, a porcelain village of the setting's own era and region. It is the producers' own page, written for visitors, and gives no source for the lengths. It gives one undated length for each kiln, though both fired on until 1937 and 1942, so the 120 m and 100 m may be their twentieth-century size rather than their Edo one. It does not say who owned or shared the old kilns; the "shared kiln" it describes is the modern one. The small kiln the potters built in 1996 is a modern reconstruction and is not used here.

Used for: two village kilns of 100 m and 120 m, fired from the late 17th and early 18th centuries (urban-features)
