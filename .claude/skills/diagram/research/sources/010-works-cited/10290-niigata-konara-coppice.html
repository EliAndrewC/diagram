Niigata Prefecture Forest Research Institute, 「A coppicing method that keeps aged konara woods from thinning out」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高齢化したコナラ林の低密度化を抑制する萌芽更新方法」), Ringyō Niigata, December 2024 (https://www.pref.niigata.lg.jp/site/shinrin/rin-nii-202412.html)

What it is: A short article by the prefectural forest research institute on a trial of two-stage felling in a 60-year-old konara wood at Murakami, to keep old stumps alive when the wood is returned to coppicing.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its opening sentence states the cutting cycle of the old konara fuel woods. The article is about a modern trial; the old practice is one line of background.

Used for: the cutting cycle of a konara fuel wood (vegetation)
