kotobank-hori

Digital Daijisen (Shogakukan), s.v. 堀, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/堀)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the dictionary entry on the hori, the moat or cut channel.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the water moat by the standing water it holds, which is the stage this page says a moat wants; its first sense is a channel that passes water, so the entry cannot say a moat never does.

Used for: the moat as held water (river cities)
