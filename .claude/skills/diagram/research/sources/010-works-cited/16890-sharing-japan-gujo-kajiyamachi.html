sharing-japan-gujo-kajiyamachi

Sharing Japan, "城下町の家並み 職人町、鍛冶屋町" ("The castle town's houses: Shokunin-machi and Kajiya-machi", title translated) (in Japanese; https://sharing-japan.co.jp/sight/4041/)

What it is: A travel site's page on the craftsmen's streets of Gujo Hachiman, a preserved castle-town district in Gifu, giving the trades on the two streets from a castle-town house register of 1692 and the width of the smallest houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that counts a small castle town's smiths against its other trades from a dated register. Its limits are that it counts two streets rather than the town - the craftsmen's quarter, one street named for its smiths, so smiths are concentrated there by design - that it names only the leading trades (8 smiths, 5 physicians, 3 coopers, 2 horse doctors, "and so on") for 18 of the 50 houses, so it carries smiths as the most numerous trade on those streets and not a full or town-wide count of trades, and that its 2-ken frontage is the small end of a range, not every craftsman's house.

Used for: a small castle town's smith count against its other trades, and the width of a craftsman's house (Trades and services)
