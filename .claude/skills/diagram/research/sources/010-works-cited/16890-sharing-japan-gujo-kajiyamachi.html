sharing-japan-gujo-kajiyamachi

Sharing Japan, "城下町の家並み 職人町、鍛冶屋町" ("The castle town's houses: Shokunin-machi and Kajiya-machi", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://sharing-japan.co.jp/sight/4041/)

What it is: A travel site's page on the craftsmen's streets of Gujo Hachiman, a preserved castle-town district in Gifu, giving the trades on the two streets from a castle-town house register of 1692 and the width of the smallest houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that counts a small castle town's smiths against its other trades from a dated register. Its limits are that it is a commercial tourism page with no author or citation beyond naming the register, that it counts two streets rather than the town, and that its 2-ken frontage is the small end of a range, not every craftsman's house.

Used for: a small castle town's smith count against its other trades, and the width of a craftsman's house (urban-features)
