en.wikipedia "Alfred Weber" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Weber)

What it is: The English Wikipedia biography of Alfred Weber, carrying his least-cost theory of industrial location and its material index.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rule by which heavy, cheap goods are made near their market, which this page applies to storage jars. A modern economic theory standing in for a transport constraint that does not change with the century; the application to ceramics is this page's own.

Used for: the material-index rule of location
