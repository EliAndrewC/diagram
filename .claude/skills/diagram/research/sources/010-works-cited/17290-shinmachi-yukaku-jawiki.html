ja.wikipedia 新町遊廓 ("the Shinmachi pleasure quarter", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/新町遊廓)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Shinmachi, Osaka's one licensed pleasure quarter, laid out in 1627 west of the castle town; it compares its enclosure and gates with Kyoto's Shimabara and Edo's Yoshiwara.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its comparison of the three great quarters - inside the town with a fence on one side, at the edge with an earthen wall, cut off by a wall and fields - gives the three forms a walled quarter could take, and it places Osaka's on new ground at the town's west. A tertiary summary; it gives the quarter's blocks and its courtesans but not its area, and the three quarters it compares are those of the three greatest cities.

Used for: Osaka's quarter on new ground west of the castle town, its blocks and courtesans, and the three great quarters' enclosures compared (urban-features)
