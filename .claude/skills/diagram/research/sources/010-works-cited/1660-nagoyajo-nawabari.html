名古屋城公式ウェブサイト (Nagoya Castle official website, City of Nagoya), 縄張 (https://www.nagoyajo.city.nagoya.jp/learn/construction/nawabari/)

What it is: The City of Nagoya's official castle site on the castle's layout: its rectangular, straight-lined enclosures set out in the teikaku manner, and how simple that is beside the Sengoku layouts built to be hard to storm.

Why it applies, and its limits: The castle's own keepers on the plan of the plains castle our capital most resembles, which is why its baileys are drawn as clean rectangles. Its limit is that it speaks of Nagoya alone. Nagoya was one of the largest castles in the country, laid out at one stroke on flat ground under a shogunate-ordered construction levy, which is why its plan is so regular; a smaller seat, or one grown on a hill, is a different case.

Used for: Nagoya's rectangular, straight-lined enclosures
