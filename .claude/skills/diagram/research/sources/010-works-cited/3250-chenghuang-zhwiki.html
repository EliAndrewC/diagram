zh.wikipedia 城隍 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/城隍)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the City God, the tutelary deity of a walled city and its official cult; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Ming founder's enfeoffment of city gods at the capital, prefecture, department and county levels with the local official leading the sacrifice, which is the state character of the City God temple this page relies on; a tertiary summary, and it does not order the foundations of a county seat.

Used for: the City God temple as a state foundation (religion and death)
