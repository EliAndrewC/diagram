kokugakuin-eos-torii

Kokugakuin University, Encyclopedia of Shinto, "Torii" (https://d-museum.kokugakuin.ac.jp/eos/detail/?id=9635)

What it is: The Kokugakuin University Encyclopedia of Shinto entry on the torii, a scholarly reference work in English.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the first, largest arch at the approach to the grounds and others within the precinct. Its limits: normative and general, with no distances or village examples.

Used for: the first arch at the entry to the grounds (religion-and-death.html).
