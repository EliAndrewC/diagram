Saitama City, 観音寺観音堂 (a designated Kannon hall) (https://www.city.saitama.lg.jp/004/005/006/001/017/010/003/p000210.html) - 「Both the ridge span and the beam span are 6.540 meters.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桁行・梁間とも6.540メートル。」)

What it is: A municipal cultural-property page on a thatched, three-bay-square Kannon hall of 1778 with its measured dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the measured size of a small village hall - 6.540 m on each side, three bays square - the low end of the hall band. One designated hall, described as a Kannon hall rather than a parish temple's main hall.

Used for: a village hall 6.540 m square, 1778 (religion-and-death.html).
