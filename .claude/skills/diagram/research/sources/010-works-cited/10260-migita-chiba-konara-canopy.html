migita-chiba-konara-canopy

Migita Chiharu and Chiba Yukihiro (Forestry and Forest Products Research Institute), 「The spatial distribution of branches in a konara canopy」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「コナラ林冠における枝の空間分布」), abstract of a talk at the Japanese Forest Society meeting, Nihon Shinrin Gakkai Taikai Happyō Dētabēsu 121 (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jfsc/121/0/121_0_108/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A one-page conference abstract by two researchers of Japan's national forest research institute, measuring the branches of konara oaks in a 29-year-old planted konara stand at Tsukuba, and stating the stand's density, mean height and mean trunk diameter.

Why it applies, and its limits: Konara is the chief tree of the Japanese fuel wood, and the abstract gives a measured stems-per-hectare figure for a stand of about coppice age. It is a modern planted stand, not a worked coppice regrown from stumps, measured at 29 years, at the long end of a coppice cycle, and it gives no crown width.

Used for: the stems per hectare of a konara stand of about coppice age (vegetation)
