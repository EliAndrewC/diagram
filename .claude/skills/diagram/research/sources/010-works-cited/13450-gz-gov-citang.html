Guangzhou Municipal People's Government, 广州有约1300座古祠堂 最早可追溯至宋代 ("Guangzhou has about 1,300 old ancestral halls, the earliest dating back to the Song", title translated from the Chinese by this project), reposting a Guangzhou Daily article (https://www.gz.gov.cn/zlgz/whgz/content/post_8754576.html)

What it is: A newspaper feature reposted on the city government's portal on Guangzhou's surviving ancestral halls: how many there are, the periods of hall building, and several halls described.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the halls and says those built in the city's second great period of hall building were mostly small and middling halls three bays wide and three halls deep, which confirms the common size. Its limits: a popular newspaper feature; it counts the halls of a whole modern city, town and country together, and its size statement is for one period's new halls, not all of them.

Used for: the number of old halls in Guangzhou and the usual size of a hall of the later period (homesteads)
