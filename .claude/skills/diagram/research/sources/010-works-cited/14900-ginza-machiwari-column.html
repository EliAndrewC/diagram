銀座街づくり会議・銀座デザイン協議会 (Ginza Machizukuri Council and Ginza Design Council), 家康がつくった町割 ("The town plan Ieyasu made", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.ginza-machidukuri.jp/column/29/)

What it is: A column by the Ginza street-planning councils on the Edo-period plan of the townsmen's land around Ginza: blocks 60 ken square, an open lot in the middle, town lots 20 ken deep along the streets, and the small alleys running between the shops to the row tenements behind.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the block and lot dimensions of a planned Edo commoner district, the measure against which a town block's depth can be read. It describes the shogun's capital, not a provincial town, and one district of it; and its picture of alleys between shops 3 to 5 ken wide is offered as a scene to imagine, not as a measured record.

Used for: the depth of a planned Edo town block and its street-front lots, and the alley between shops leading to the tenements behind (ways)
