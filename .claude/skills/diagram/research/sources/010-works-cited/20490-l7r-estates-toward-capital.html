l7r-estates-toward-capital

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "The First Toshi Ranbo Campaign" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#the-first-toshi-ranbo-campaign) and "Hitachi" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#hitachi) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, on the country around the city of Toshi Ranbo: the capital lies to its southeast, so southeast is the most prestigious direction, and the wealthiest samurai place their country estates there; many nobles keep country estates in the province to the city's southeast for the same reason.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: where the setting's samurai put their country estates is the setting's to decide. The notes describe one city, whose capital happens to lie southeast; that every city's wealthiest estates face its own capital is this project's generalization, and the notes give no distance, count or size for an estate.

Used for: the side of a city its country estates are drawn on (rendering/cities/hinterland)
