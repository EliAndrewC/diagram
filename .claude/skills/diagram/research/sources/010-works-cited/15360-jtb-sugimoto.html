jtb-sugimoto

Japan Travel Bureau Foundation, National Tourism Resources Register, 杉本家住宅・庭園 ("Sugimoto residence and garden", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://tabi.jtb.or.jp/res/260203-)

What it is: The foundation's register entry for the Sugimoto house and its gardens, current as of 2025: the site's frontage and depth, the house's plan, and the storehouses behind it across the garden.

Why it applies, and its limits: A second, independent statement of the site's size and the storehouses' place. It is a tourism register rather than a survey, and its 30 by 52 m does not agree with the 1,200 square meters given elsewhere; which is right is not settled here.

Used for: the Sugimoto site's frontage and depth, and its storehouses across the garden (cities/fabric.html)
