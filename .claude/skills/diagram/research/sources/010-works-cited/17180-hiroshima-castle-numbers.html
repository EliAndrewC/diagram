hiroshima-castle-numbers

Hiroshima Castle, 探検！史跡広島城 - 数字でみる広島城 ("Exploring the Hiroshima Castle site: Hiroshima Castle in numbers", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://hiroshimacastle.jp/about/tanken/)

What it is: The Hiroshima Castle museum's page of figures for the castle as it was and as it is: its extent, what its baileys held, the widths of its moats, the area of the honmaru and its palace, and the measurements of the 1958 reconstructed keep.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives, for one castle, the sizes this record needs side by side - a castle about 1 km across, an upper honmaru tier of about 21,700 square meters (the honmaru also had a lower tier, with a riding ground and storehouses, that this figure leaves out) with a palace ground of about 16,500, moats of 20 to 104 m (worked out from an Edo-period plan of the castle), and a keep with a ground floor of about 354 square meters (1,359 over its five floors) that is 26.6 m tall. Hiroshima was the seat of one of the great domains, so every figure is at the large end, and the keep figures are of the 1958 reinforced-concrete reconstruction. Its outside was built from prewar drawings, but it lacks the two three-storey small keeps that were joined to the keep until the Meiji era.

Used for: the size of a large domain's castle, honmaru, palace ground, moats and keep (cities/capitals)
