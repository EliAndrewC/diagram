ranzan-senjuin

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡寺院明細帳, 曹洞宗 千手院 菅谷村大字千手堂 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter13.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription of the Hiki district temple register entry (filed in Saitama prefecture's 1963 papers) for Senjuin, a Sōtō Zen temple of Senjudō, with its history, its main hall of 7 by 6 ken and a precinct of 813 tsubo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is one of the register's nineteen temples and one of the two for which an Edo village record survives, so it lets the register's precinct be set beside an Edo holding. Its limits: the register is post-1868 (land classes of the 1874 decree, a note of 1948 giving a second area of 887.64 tsubo), its history says the temple's exempt land was surrendered after the Restoration and its halls burned in 1876 and were rebuilt, so its hall is a Meiji building; and it is one village temple.

Used for: a register precinct and a post-1876 hall set beside the temple's Edo holding (religion-and-death.html).
