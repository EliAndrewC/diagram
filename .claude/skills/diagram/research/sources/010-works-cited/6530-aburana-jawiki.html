ja.wikipedia アブラナ (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/アブラナ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article アブラナ on rape (oilseed rape, the yellow-flowered brassica grown for oil and as a green vegetable). The Japanese page's calendar - seedlings transplanted before winter, the crop cut in March and April - is that of nabana, the flowering-greens vegetable grown as a market crop; the Chinese page describes rape sown into the harvested paddy and plowed in before the next transplanting, a present-day Taiwanese double-crop practice.

Why it applies, and its limits: Between them they give the shape of the rice-and-rape year - rape in the paddy over winter, cut in spring before the rice goes in - which is what lets a plot on our maps carry two crops. Their limits are that they are tertiary summaries; that the calendar we quote is a modern market-garden regime for the vegetable, transplanted at spacing, not the broadcast oilseed stand our maps draw, so its dates are taken as plausible for oilseed rape rather than shown for it; and that the green-manure practice is an explicitly modern Taiwanese one, not an attested premodern rotation.

Used for: rape sown in autumn, overwintering, cut in March-April (the food crop's calendar); rape sown into the harvested paddy and plowed in before the next transplanting (a Taiwan double-crop practice) - neither page gives the rice months
