What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the idobata kaigi, the well-side gossip circle, as the gathering of tenement women at a communal well and as the idiom it became.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the circle was a tenement institution around a shared well, which is why this page counts the dense well pattern as urban rather than village density. A tertiary summary used for that setting; it gives no households-per-well figure. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. It dates the practice only as former.

Used for: the well-side circle as an urban tenement institution
