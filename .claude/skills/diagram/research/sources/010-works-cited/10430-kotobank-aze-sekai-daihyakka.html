kotobank-aze-sekai-daihyakka

Tada Atsushi, "Aze", Kaitei shinpan Sekai daihyakka jiten, Heibonsha, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%A6-424980)

What it is: A general encyclopedia's entry on the paddy bund, reproduced on Kotobank beside two dictionary entries. It gives the bund's uses (holding water, the path of farm work, the boundary of a holding, grass for manure and fodder, beans grown on it) and describes azenuri, the plastering of the bund before transplanting.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a signed encyclopedia entry covering both the bund's role and its yearly upkeep, which this record needs. It gives no width. It is a modern tertiary entry, written from agricultural engineering about Japan's paddies today: "from of old" is its only reach into the past, and it gives no period of its own. Some of its detail is modern and is not drawn: it asks the bund to carry "people and work machines", treats seepage through the bund as a loss of irrigation water, and names concrete and vinyl bunds. The uses and the azenuri it describes are long-standing hand practice, and we use it for those alone.

Used for: the bund as path and boundary, its grass and beans, and what azenuri does (fields)
