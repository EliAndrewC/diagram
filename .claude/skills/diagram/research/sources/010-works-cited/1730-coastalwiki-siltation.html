Coastal Wiki (Flanders Marine Institute), "Siltation in harbors and fairways" (https://www.coastalwiki.org/wiki/Siltation_in_harbors_and_fairways)

What it is: A technical reference article in Coastal Wiki, the coastal-engineering encyclopedia maintained by the Flanders Marine Institute for the European coastal-science community, on why harbor basins and fairways silt up and what their maintenance costs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the mechanism by which a still basin cut off a moving channel fills with sediment, which is why our river capital keeps its wharf on the bank. Its limit is that it is present-day harbor engineering, written for tidal ports rather than premodern river towns.

Used for: basin siltation from sediment exchange, and the dredging it forces
