mizumaki-goemonburo

Mizumaki town historical museum, 五右衛門風呂 (https://www.town.mizumaki.lg.jp/museum/040/020/050/550/1258.html)

What it is: A page from the historical museum of the town of Mizumaki on the goemonburo (五右衛門風呂), the iron-bottomed bath tub heated by a fire lit directly underneath it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It tells us in a museum's own caption that the cauldron bath was widely used in self-sufficient farming villages, which is what lets an ordinary farmstead on our maps have a bath. Its limits are that it is one object's caption from one town museum in Fukuoka, northern Kyushu, with no survey and no proportion behind "widely"; and that the object it describes is dated by the museum to the postwar Showa years, so the page attests the practice in living memory and reaches our period only as a continuity argument.

Used for: the goemon-buro "used widely in self-sufficient farm villages".
