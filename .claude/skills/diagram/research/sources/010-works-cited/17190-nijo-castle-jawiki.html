ja.wikipedia 二条城 ("Nijo Castle", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BA%8C%E6%9D%A1%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Nijo Castle in Kyoto, the shogun's residence in the imperial capital, with the dimensions of its enclosures and the floor area of each building of its surviving ninomaru palace.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures a whole castle (about 500 by 400 m), its honmaru (about 150 m square) and each hall of a palace - the entrance guardroom, the formal porch, the great audience hall and the two shoin - which the article calls the only castle palace of its kind still standing (smaller domain palaces survive in part at Kochi, Kakegawa and Kawagoe). The figures are for the castle as enlarged in the Kan'ei rebuilding of 1624-26, measured today; Ieyasu's castle of 1601 was only the eastern half. They are a Wikipedia article's figures, and since May 2024 the article has carried notices that its sources are insufficient and that it may contain original research, so they are used for size and not checked against a survey. It is the shogun's, not a daimyo's: the ceremonial halls are larger than a domain seat's would be.

Used for: the size of a castle, its honmaru and its palace halls (cities/capitals)
