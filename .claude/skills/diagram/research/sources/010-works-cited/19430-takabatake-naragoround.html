takabatake-naragoround

Nara City Tourism Association (奈良市観光協会), 奈良Go Round 高畑 (in Japanese; https://narashikanko.or.jp/naragoround/takabatake/)

What it is: The city tourism association's walking guide to Takabatake in Nara, which it describes as the Kasuga shrine's priests' quarter, surviving until the Meiji era, with the paths the priests walked from it to the shrine.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a shrine's hereditary priest families in a quarter of their own beside the shrine until 1868. Its limits: it is tourist writing; it gives no date for the quarter's beginning, no count of houses and no plan, and it concerns a shrine's priests, not a temple's.

Used for: a shrine's priest families living in their own quarter before modern times (religion-and-death.html).
