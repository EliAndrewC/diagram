旧时楼台月 (a NetEase Hao account), 近代时期，在东北风靡一时的"大车店"，是怎样的一个店？ ("what was the 'big cart inn' that swept the Northeast in modern times?", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 2025 (in Chinese; https://www.163.com/dy/article/KC620JJE0543ANG9.html)

What it is: A self-published post on NetEase's user platform, by an unnamed account, describing the cart inns on the roads of Northeast China in the late Qing and Republican periods: where they stood, their yard and stable, and their shared sleeping platforms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable account of a Chinese cart inn's parts - a fenced yard, mud-brick lodging, a large stable, hay in the corners, rooms of long heated platforms. It is weak: self-media with no author and no sources, the platform disclaiming it, and it describes one frontier region in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, not an older or a southern inn. It is used only for the parts of such an inn, never for a size.

Used for: the yard, stable and sleeping rooms of a Chinese cart inn (buildings)
