zhwiki-ganchen

Chinese Wikipedia, "杆秤" (gancheng, the steelyard) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%A1%BF%E7%A7%A4)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the steelyard: its other names, its parts - a wooden or bamboo beam, a hook and a sliding weight - how it is read, and the sayings built on it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It establishes the steelyard as China's traditional weighing tool and describes its working. It is a brief tertiary article, flagged since 2014 as listing no sources, speaks of market use in the present tense and dates nothing: it gives no origin or history for the steelyard and no scale for heavy ones, so its use in imperial China rests only on its word "traditional" (传统) and its note that steelyard-making was listed as Shanghai intangible heritage in 2019, not on any period evidence.

Used for: the steelyard as China's traditional weighing tool, and how it is built and read (urban features)
