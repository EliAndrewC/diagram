What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Kitsuki Castle in Oita, built on the Daiyama promontory at the mouth of the Yasaka river, with the Takayama river to its north and Morie Bay to its east.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the castle on its promontory between the rivers and the bay, the flank position with water on two sides that this page cites it for. A tertiary summary used for the siting.

Used for: Kitsuki's promontory at the Yasaka mouth, the Takayama and Morie Bay
