whw-huangling

朱虹 江西婺源篁岭——草民的皇宫 ("Huangling in Wuyuan, Jiangxi - the 'Palace' of Common Folk", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (Zhu Hong), 文化网 (whw.kim), 7 April 2021 (https://www.whw.kim/?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2319)

What it is: A travel essay, a reader's submission to a Chinese culture site, on Huangling, a hill village of Wuyuan county in the old Huizhou region; it describes the village's water-mouth grove, its houses and its terraces.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the trees of one water-mouth grove as it stands today and gives their ages: over a hundred trees, most over five hundred years old, which puts a grove of that size at the village mouth well before 1912. It is one grove in one village, described by an essayist rather than surveyed; it gives no area, does not say how the ages were found, and says nothing of trees lost or planted since.

Used for: the tree count of one water-mouth grove whose trees predate the modern period (vegetation)
