tokyo-ja-kasai-renkon

JA Tokyo Central Union, 葛西蓮根 ("Kasai lotus root", title translated from the Japanese by this project), from its series on Tokyo's farming history (in Japanese; https://www.tokyo-ja.or.jp/farm/edomap/tokyo17.php)

What it is: The Tokyo agricultural cooperatives' page, written from a marker at a Kasai shrine, on the lotus-root district east of Edo: grown only a little from the mid-Edo period, then spreading fast after the mid-Meiji because it paid better than rice.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a lotus district's growth and names the conditions it needed - deep paddies, rich soil, a great city's night soil. It is a short cooperative page hedged in its own words ("it seems"), with no area figures, and it describes a district on the edge of the largest city in the country.

Used for: a lotus district as a late market crop on deep paddies near a city (archetypes)
