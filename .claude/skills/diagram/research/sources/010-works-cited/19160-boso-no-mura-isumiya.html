boso-no-mura-isumiya

Boso no Mura (Chiba Prefectural Museum), facility guide, "鍛冶屋（夷隅屋）" ("The smithy (Isumiya)", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.chiba-muse.or.jp/MURA/facility/page-1521181423063/page-1519140291665/)

What it is: The open-air museum's visitor guide to its smithy in the street of shops, saying how the building was made and what is shown and demonstrated in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the building was put up on the basis of a survey of a working smith's workshop that made farm and household tools, so it tells how far the reconstruction reaches back: to a workshop surveyed in modern times. Its limit is exactly that: it gives no date for the surveyed workshop, and nothing on it places the building's form before 1868.

Used for: that the museum's smithy was built from a modern survey of a working smithy, undated (urban-features)
