boso-no-mura-isumiya

Boso no Mura (Chiba Prefectural Museum), facility guide, "鍛冶屋（夷隅屋）" ("The smithy (Isumiya)", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.chiba-muse.or.jp/MURA/facility/page-1521181423063/page-1519140291665/)

What it is: The open-air museum's visitor guide to its smithy in the street of shops, saying how the building was made and what is shown and demonstrated in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the building was put up on the basis of a survey of a smith's workshop that made farm tools and household implements, so it tells how far the reconstruction reaches back: to a surveyed workshop, not to a period plan. Its limits: it dates neither the survey nor the workshop, and does not say where the workshop stood or that the smith was still working (Isumiya, after a Boso district, is a shop name like its neighbors', not a provenance). That the survey was made in modern times is this project's inference from the museum's opening in 1986, not the page's words, and nothing on it places the building's form before 1868.

Used for: that the museum's smithy was built from a survey of a smith's workshop, undated and unplaced (modern by inference from the museum's founding) (urban-features)
