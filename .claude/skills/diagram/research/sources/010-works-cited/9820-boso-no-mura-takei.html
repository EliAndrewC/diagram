Chiba Prefectural Boso-no-mura, 武家屋敷 (The samurai residence) (in Japanese; https://www.chiba-muse.or.jp/MURA/facility/page-1519175606779/)

What it is: The open-air museum's page on its samurai house, a reproduction modeled on the Takei house, the late Edo house of a retainer of the Sakura domain, describing how its rooms divide into living space and guest space.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says plainly that a middle-rank samurai's guest rooms, the shikidai genkan, the announcing room and the zashiki, were part of the one main house. Its limits are that the house shown is a museum reproduction rather than the building itself; that it is one house, of a retainer of about 90 koku; and that the detached building now on the site is a modern museum facility, not part of the house.

Used for: the guest space of a middle-rank house within its one main house (buildings)
