zh.wikipedia 風水林 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/風水林)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the fengshui wood, the village grove kept for geomantic reasons in south China; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the grove as the screen at the village's rear, the back-hill 龍座林 planted against wind and wash, and the useful trees planted in it; a tertiary summary whose detail is Hong Kong's, giving a grove area of about 600 sq m to one hectare below 100 m elevation, to be read beside the survey this page's grove sizes rest on.

Used for: the back grove as the village's screen (vegetation)
