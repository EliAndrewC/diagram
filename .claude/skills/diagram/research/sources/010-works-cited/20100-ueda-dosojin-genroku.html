ueda-dosojin-genroku

Ueda City, "Paired-figure dosojin" (双体道祖神), a city-designated cultural property at Takeshi Toya, on the city's cultural-properties site (in Japanese; https://museum.umic.jp/bunkazai/document/dot102.php)

What it is: The Ueda city (Nagano) cultural-properties catalog entry for one stone dosojin, designated in 1994: its kind, place and date, with a short description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a wayside stone at a settlement's entrance before modern times: the stone carries the year Genroku 8 (1695) cut into it, and the page says it was set up at the entrance of a settlement on an old road beside a river weir. Its limits: the date is the stone's inscription, but the entrance siting is the city's present-tense description, so the stone may have been moved to where it stands; it is one stone of Shinano, and the page itself calls a dosojin of the Genroku years very rare in the prefecture, so it attests the form's age, not how common it was in 1695.

Used for: a wayside stone at a hamlet's entrance dated 1695 (religion and death)
