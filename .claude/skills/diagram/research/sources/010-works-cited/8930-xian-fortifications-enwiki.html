en.wikipedia Fortifications of Xi'an (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortifications_of_Xi%27an)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Ming walls of Xi'an, covering the circuit, the four corner watch towers and the ditch outside the wall, whose width and depth it gives in meters and feet.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the one measured moat width this record could read for a Chinese provincial-capital enceinte, which is what the drawn figure is checked against. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary whose own citation for the figure was not followed; that Xi'an is among the largest walled circuits ever built, so its ditch is a ceiling and not an average; and that the measurement is of the ditch as it stands today after conservation.

Used for: the measured width and depth of a Chinese provincial-capital moat, against which the drawn 66 ft is judged
