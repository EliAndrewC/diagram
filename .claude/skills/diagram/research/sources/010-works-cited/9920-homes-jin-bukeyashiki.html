LIFULL HOME'S, 【間取り探偵㊹】JIN～仁～の武家屋敷を解説！ (Floor-plan detective 44: the samurai house of JIN) (in Japanese; https://www.homes.co.jp/cont/living/living_00374/)

What it is: A column on a Japanese real-estate site that infers a floor plan for the house of the Tachibana, the hatamoto family of the manga and television drama JIN, set at the end of the Edo period, and walks through who used which room and door.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states who came in by the doma rather than by the entrance, which bears on who used a residence's kitchen door. It is the weakest kind of source: a popular column's inferred plan of a fictional household's house, not a documented building, so it can support a practice only beside a dictionary or a surviving house.

Used for: those of lower status than the master coming and going by the doma (buildings)
