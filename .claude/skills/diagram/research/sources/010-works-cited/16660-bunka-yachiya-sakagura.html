bunka-yachiya-sakagura

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, "やちや酒造酒蔵" ("Yachiya Brewery sake store", title translated from the Japanese by this project), registered tangible cultural property, Kanazawa, Ishikawa (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/193919)

What it is: The national database's entry for the sake store of an old brewery north of Kanazawa castle: a two-story earthen storehouse built between 1751 and 1829, long and narrow, about 19 m on its long side, with a building area of 132 square meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an official, measured record of a brewery building of the Edo period in a castle town, standing in the middle of the brewer's long plot beside his tool store and near his house. Its limits are that it is one building of one brewery, in a great domain's castle town rather than a county seat, and gives neither the tanks inside nor the whole compound's area.

Used for: an Edo-period castle-town brewery store of about 1,400 sq ft (urban-features)
