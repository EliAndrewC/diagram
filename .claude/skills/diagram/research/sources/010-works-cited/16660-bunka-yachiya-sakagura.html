bunka-yachiya-sakagura

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, "やちや酒造酒蔵" ("Yachiya Brewery sake store", title translated from the Japanese by this project), registered tangible cultural property, Kanazawa, Ishikawa (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/193919)

What it is: The national database's entry for the sake store of an old brewery north of Kanazawa castle: a two-story earthen storehouse of the later Edo period (the database's bracket is 1751-1829, and the entry dates it only as thought to be about as old as the brewery's main house), long and narrow, about 19 m on its long side, with a building area of 132 square meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an official, measured record of a brewery building of the Edo period in a castle town, standing just north of the tool store that stands in the middle of the brewer's long north-south plot. Its limits are that its date is an estimate by association with the main house rather than a documented one, that it is one building of one brewery, in a great domain's castle town rather than a county seat, and gives neither the tanks inside nor the whole compound's area.

Used for: an Edo-period castle-town brewery store of about 1,400 sq ft (urban-features)
