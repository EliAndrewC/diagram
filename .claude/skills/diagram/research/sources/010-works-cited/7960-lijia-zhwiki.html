zh.wikipedia 里甲 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E9%87%8C%E7%94%B2) - 「The greatest function of this lijia system lay in allocating corvée labor and collecting tax by head-count.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「此里甲制度，最大功能在于分配繇役及按丁纳税等。」)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the lijia system of household registration for corvée and tax, instituted at the Yuan-Ming transition; it rests on a single cited paper.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for where a Chinese village's population was registered - the lijia, not the temple. Its limits: tertiary, resting on a single cited paper; and the lijia is a Ming institution that decayed under the Qing as fiscal reform and the baojia displaced it, so the registers were the state's under either name and never the temple's.

Used for: population registered by the lijia (religion-and-death.html).
