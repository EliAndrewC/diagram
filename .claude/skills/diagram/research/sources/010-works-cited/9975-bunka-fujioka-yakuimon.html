bunka-fujioka-yakuimon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 藤岡家住宅薬医門 (The yakuimon of the Fujioka house) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/185253)

What it is: The national database entry for a registered gate at Gojō in Nara prefecture, of the Meiji period: a one-bay yakuimon built entirely of zelkova under a tiled roof, said to give a large estate its stately look, with its frontage in meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the narrowest of the surveyed one-bay gates read. Its limits: it was built in the Meiji period (1868-1911), after the Edo period, though in the traditional form; it is the gate of a large estate of a well-off family (the entry names only the Fujioka house - Gojō's own pages say the Fujioka were village headmen from the Edo period on), with elaborate bracketing, so it shows a headman's gate and not a common household's; the entry gives two frontages that disagree - 2.4 m in its data line and 1.8 m for the one-bay gate in its description, the figure we use; and it is one example.

Used for: a one-bay yakuimon 1.8 m across its frontage (buildings)
