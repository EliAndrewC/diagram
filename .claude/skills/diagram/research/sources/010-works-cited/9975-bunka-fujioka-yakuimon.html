bunka-fujioka-yakuimon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 藤岡家住宅薬医門 (The yakuimon of the Fujioka house) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/185253)

What it is: The national database entry for a registered gate at Gojō in Nara prefecture, of the Meiji period: a one-bay yakuimon built entirely of zelkova under a tiled roof, said to give a large estate its stately look, with its frontage in meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the narrowest of the surveyed one-bay gates read. Its limits: it was built after the Edo period, the entry does not say whose house it was, and it is one example.

Used for: a one-bay yakuimon 1.8 m across its frontage (buildings)
