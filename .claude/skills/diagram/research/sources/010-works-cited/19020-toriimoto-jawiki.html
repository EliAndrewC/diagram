ja.wikipedia 鳥居本宿 ("Toriimoto-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%B3%A5%E5%B1%85%E6%9C%AC%E5%AE%BF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Toriimoto, a small post town on the Nakasendo in Omi (today Hikone, Shiga), giving its length, houses, inns and population from the shogunate's 1843 survey of the Nakasendo's post towns.

Why it applies, and its limits: The length and the house count are both the survey's, of one year, so they measure a post town as it stood in the Edo period rather than a preserved townscape today. It is an encyclopedia article repeating the survey's figures, one small station only; the length runs between two neighboring villages' boundaries, and the count does not say how many of the houses fronted the road.

Used for: one small post town's length and houses in 1843 (towns)
