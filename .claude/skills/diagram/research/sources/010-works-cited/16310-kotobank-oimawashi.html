kotobank-oimawashi

Kotobank, 追廻 ("oimawashi", title translated from the Japanese by this project), gathering Heibonsha's Historical Place Names of Japan entries (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%BF%BD%E5%BB%BB-3024743)

What it is: A dictionary aggregator's page gathering gazetteer entries for places named Oimawashi, the name of a castle town's riding ground, at Kumamoto and Sendai.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places two real domain riding grounds - Kumamoto's running south from the lord's Hanabata residence, banked on one side, and Sendai's below the castle - and says what was practiced on them. The entries are excerpts behind a paywall's teaser, and give no size.

Used for: where two domains' riding grounds stood and what they were for (cities/government)
