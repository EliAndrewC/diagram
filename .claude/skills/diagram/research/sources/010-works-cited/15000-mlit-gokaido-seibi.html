Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), 道路：道の歴史：近世の道 ("Roads: the history of roads: early modern roads", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/michi-re/3-2.htm)

What it is: A page of the national roads ministry's short public history of Japanese roads, on the Five Highways: the 1605 order setting their standard width, a mound at every ri and roadside trees, and how the roads and Edo's own streets were kept up, with the complaint of a contemporary that Edo's streets turned to mud after a little rain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the official standard for the width and furniture of the trunk highways that ran through Japanese post towns, and a contemporary word on the surface of a town's streets. It is a brief outline by a government office, citing only the one period text it quotes; another page dates the milestone order a year earlier, to 1604.

Used for: the standard width of the Edo highways, their milestone mounds and roadside trees, and the mud of Edo's streets (ways)
