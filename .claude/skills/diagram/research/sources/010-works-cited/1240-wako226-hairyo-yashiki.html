大江戸歴史散歩を楽しむ会 (an Edo history walking group), 大名の拝領江戸屋敷 (https://wako226.exblog.jp/239926652/)

What it is: A post on the blog of an Edo-history walking group setting out the shogunate's table of granted residence sizes for daimyo by stipend, from 2,500 tsubo for the smallest domains to 7,200 for the largest, and noting that the plots actually held ran larger.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page that gives the whole table, which sizes the daimyo compounds on our capital. Its limits are real: it is an amateur group's summary with no citation of the regulation, so the table is used as a band. And the institution is a different one: the table is of plots the SHOGUNATE granted daimyo in Edo under alternate attendance, while a domain seat's compound was the lord's own ground, so the band transfers as a scale of what a house of a given stipend kept, not as a rule that applied there.

Used for: the bakufu's table of granted residence sizes by stipend
