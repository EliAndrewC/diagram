esri-offset-point-labels

Esri, ArcGIS Pro documentation, "Offset point labels" (https://pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/help/mapping/text/offset-point-labels.htm)

What it is: A help page of Esri's ArcGIS Pro, the most widely used commercial GIS, for its Maplex label engine: how far a point's label stands from the symbol, the preferred offset and the maximum offset, and the leader line that lets a label use the maximum offset without ambiguity.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the offset rule in measurable terms: the gap is measured from the symbol's boundary to the label's edge, a preferred distance may be relaxed to a maximum given as a percentage of it, and a leader line removes the ambiguity of a label set further out. It is the documentation of one commercial engine's defaults, not a published standard, and it leaves both numbers to the map's author: it names no preferred distance (its 5 points is an illustration), and its default maximum is 100 percent of the preferred, no relaxation at all, with 200 percent given only as an example, so the multiple this project uses is its own.

Used for: the offset measured edge to edge; the maximum offset as a multiple of the preferred; the leader line for a label beyond its normal offset (presentation)
