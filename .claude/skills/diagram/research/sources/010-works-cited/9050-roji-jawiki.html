roji-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 路地 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%B7%AF%E5%9C%B0)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the roji, the narrow passage caught between one building and the next: its width between one back-lane tenement and another, that almost nobody but those connected with the adjoining buildings used it, and the widening at the back of the merchant house or the middle of the lane where a shared well or water supply stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured width our drawn lane is judged against, and the widening at the shared well that the map draws, both for exactly the kind of lane the map is packing. Its limits are that it is tertiary, that the width is a range for Edo back-lane tenements rather than a survey figure, and that the article covers the modern sense of the word alongside the historical one.

Used for: the roji at three to six shaku between tenement buildings, who used it, and the widening at the shared well
