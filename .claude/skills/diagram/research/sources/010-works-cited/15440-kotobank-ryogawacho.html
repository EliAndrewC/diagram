kotobank-ryogawacho

Sekai Daihyakka Jiten (old edition, Heibonsha), passages mentioning 両側町 ("both-sides ward", translated from the Japanese by this project), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B8%A1%E5%81%B4%E7%94%BA-1437236)

What it is: The kotobank page gathering the encyclopedia's passages on the ryogawa-cho, the ward made of the houses on both sides of one street, which grew up in Kyoto from the late middle ages and became the townsmen's unit of life.

Why it applies, and its limits: An encyclopedia's account of how the Japanese town ward took its form, which fixes that the ward was the street and its two facing rows of houses rather than a block. It is about Kyoto from the fourteenth to the sixteenth century; it gives no ward sizes and no counts, and says nothing of Edo directly.

Used for: the ward as the street community (cities/fabric.html)
