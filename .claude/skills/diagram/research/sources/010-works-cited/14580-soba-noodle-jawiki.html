ja.wikipedia 蕎麦 ("Soba", the noodle, title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%95%8E%E9%BA%A6)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on soba, the buckwheat noodle, its history and its regional styles, among them the soba of the shops on the approach to Zenkoji in Nagano.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the name for Zenkoji's gate soba seems to have settled from the density of the soba shops and from worshippers looking for a resting place and a meibutsu, a place's famous specialty - the word used of what a worshipper seeks at a temple's gate. It is a tertiary source, an encyclopedia article that Wikipedia itself flags as insufficiently sourced, and the sentence rests on footnotes this project has not read. It speaks of the present-day street in a modern writer's words, hedges the account ("seems to have"), and gives no date for when the word was first used there - so it shows meibutsu is the word used today for what a worshipper seeks at a temple's gate, not that the word was used so in the Edo period.

Used for: meibutsu as the word for what worshippers seek at a temple's gate (religion and death)
