ja.wikipedia 蕎麦 ("Soba", the noodle, title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%95%8E%E9%BA%A6)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on soba, the buckwheat noodle, its history and its regional styles, among them the soba of the shops on the approach to Zenkoji in Nagano.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the name for Zenkoji's gate soba seems to have settled from the density of the soba shops and from worshippers looking for a resting place and a meibutsu, a place's famous specialty - the word used of what a worshipper seeks at a temple's gate. It speaks of the present-day street, hedges the account, and gives no date for when the word was first used there.

Used for: meibutsu as the word for what worshippers seek at a temple's gate (religion and death)
