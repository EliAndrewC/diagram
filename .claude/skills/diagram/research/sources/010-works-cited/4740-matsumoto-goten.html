matsumoto-goten

Matsumoto Castle official site, Honmaru/Ninomaru Goten pages (https://www.matsumoto-castle.jp/eng/about/palace)

What it is: The pages of the official Matsumoto Castle site describing the Honmaru and Ninomaru palaces that stood inside the castle's enclosures.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one Edo-period case of a castle's inner enclosure proving too small for its government: after the 1727 fire the functions moved to the second bailey, and because that was smaller the county office, the town office and the hall where the lord met the village headmen were moved out into the town - the warrant for putting some of a castle town's official buildings outside the walls on our maps. Its limits are that it is the castle's own visitor page, unsigned, undated and without citations; that it is a single case; and that its dates want care, since the page carries at least one internal error in them.

Used for: the county office and town office being moved OUT of the castle into Rokku town when the ninomaru proved too small, and the daimyo/headman conference hall to Agetsuchi town
