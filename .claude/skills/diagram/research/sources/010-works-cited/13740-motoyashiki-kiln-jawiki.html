What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Motoyashiki kiln site at Toki (Gifu prefecture), a national historic site with the oldest multi-chamber climbing kiln in Mino, of the early 17th century: its length, width and chambers, and the workshops found beside the kilns.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives an excavated climbing kiln's measured length, width and chamber count, and records that the ground of a disused kiln became the workshop of the next. It is one early kiln in the principal pottery region, summarized by an encyclopedia from the excavation; later kilns elsewhere grew much longer.

Used for: a climbing kiln about 24 m long and 2.2 m wide with fourteen chambers, and the workshop beside it (urban-features)
