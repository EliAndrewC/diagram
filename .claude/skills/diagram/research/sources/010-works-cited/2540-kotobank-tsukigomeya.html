kotobank-tsukigomeya

kotobank 搗米屋 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%90%97%E7%B1%B3%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the reference-encyclopedia entries on the tsukigomeya, the Edo rice-hulling retailer that bought brown rice from the brokers, polished it and sold it white to consumers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the hulling and polishing was the shop's own work, which is why the rice dealer draws as an ordinary shop with no separate mill. A tertiary summary of published reference works, used for that one fact.

Used for: the rice dealer polishing in-shop
