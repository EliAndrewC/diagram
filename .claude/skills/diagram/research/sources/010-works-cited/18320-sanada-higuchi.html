Sanada Treasure Museum, 「The former Higuchi residence」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧樋口家住宅」) (in Japanese; https://www.sanadahoumotsukan.com/facility_detail.php?n=9)

What it is: The page of the Sanada Treasure Museum, the public museum that keeps the preserved samurai houses of Matsushiro (Nagano), on the former Higuchi residence, the house of a Sanada-domain retainer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where a samurai residence's vegetable field lay: the woods, bamboo and garden south of the pond, in the middle of the grounds, were used as a field in the Edo period, and the grounds once fed the household. That is a working plot on the sunny south side, in the zone the formal garden holds. Its limits are that it is one house, of one domain's retainers, not an official compound; that it gives no size and no date within the period; and that it describes the ground as it was used, not a plan of beds.

Used for: where a walled residence kept its vegetable ground (buildings.html).
