What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Tokoname ware, the stoneware of the Chita peninsula, with its medieval kiln-site counts and its later history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the scale of a medieval kiln district in kiln sites, which is the real size this page's one drawn works stands down from. A tertiary summary used for the count; it does not give distribution or workers' residence.

Used for: the thousands of medieval Tokoname kiln sites
