kyototuu-unagi

京都通百科事典, 鰻の寝床 (https://www.kyototuu.jp/Life/ProverbUnaginoNedoko.html)

What it is: A page from 京都通百科事典, a Japanese web encyclopedia about Kyoto, on the saying 鰻の寝床 ("an eel's bed") for the long narrow townhouse, giving typical dimensions of about two ken of frontage (roughly 3.6 m) and ten to twelve ken of depth (roughly 18 to 22 m).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the proportions of the eel's-nest townhouse lot - about two ken of frontage to ten or twelve of depth - which is what our merchant plots are drawn to, and the frontage-tax story as a foil. Its limits are that it is a popular regional web encyclopedia written for tourism and a local-knowledge examination, with no citations of any kind; that it is one city's building tradition; and that both the tax story and the dimensions themselves are given as hearsay - "it is said" - with no source behind either.

Used for: 「a frontage of about 2 ken (about 3.6 m), a depth of 10 to 12 ken (about 18 to 22 m)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「間口２間（約３.６m）前後、奥行１０〜１２間（約１８〜２２m）」); carries the popular frontage-tax explanation that kyomachiya-jawiki disputes
