jaanus-honden

JAANUS, honden (Columbia mirror) (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/honden) - 「The honden is located at the rear of shrine complex and is never entered by anyone other than a priest or, in case of major shrines, the Emperor.」 (the source's own English)

What it is: The Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System (JAANUS) entry on the honden, the main sanctuary of a Shinto shrine. JAANUS is an online dictionary of Japanese architectural and art-historical terms compiled by Mary Neighbour Parent, first built by the Atsumi International Scholarship Foundation and now hosted by Columbia University's Media Center for Art History.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the one placement it states - the sanctuary at the rear of the shrine's ground - which, with the worship hall on the same axis (the same glossary's haiden entry), puts the sanctuary at the back of the precinct behind the hall. A glossary entry, undated, describing the type in general, not any one village's shrine.

Used for: the sanctuary at the back of the precinct (religion-and-death.html).
