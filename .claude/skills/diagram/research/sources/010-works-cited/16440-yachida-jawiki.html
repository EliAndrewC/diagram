ja.wikipedia 谷地田 ("valley-bottom paddy", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%B0%B7%E5%9C%B0%E7%94%B0)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the yachida, the long, narrow paddy lying on the wet floor of a valley, also called yatsuda, often a permanently wet paddy of low yield.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one fact the valley-chain form needs from it: that the valley paddy is long and narrow. It is a stub citing dictionaries and an NGO page, used for that definition only; it gives no period and no dimension.

Used for: the valley paddy is long and narrow (archetypes)
