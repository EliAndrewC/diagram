What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the moat: its types, the natural moat made of an existing river, the sogamae moat around a castle town, boats on river-fed moats, and the sectioning of a wet moat with earthen weirs into pools at different levels.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the vocabulary and the practices our moats are drawn with - the natural moat, the sectioned moat, the town moat. A tertiary summary used for those definitions.

Used for: the sectioned moat (mitochigai), the natural moat, and the town's sogamae moat
