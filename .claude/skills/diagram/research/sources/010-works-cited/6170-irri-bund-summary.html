irri-bund-summary

International Rice Research Institute, Rice Knowledge Bank, "How to construct bunds" (http://www.knowledgebank.irri.org/step-by-step-production/pre-planting/land-preparation/how-to-construct-bunds)

What it is: A four-line how-to page in the Rice Knowledge Bank, the International Rice Research Institute's extension library for rice growers, on building the earth bunds that hold the water in a paddy: how large to make them, how well to compact and seal them, what spillway height to set for the depth of water wanted, and a heavier section where rats are the problem.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives an upper bound on the ordinary field bund - no wider and taller than 50 cm by 30 cm - which is one of the three attested figures the drawn bund rests on, and it says what a bund has to be to do its work: compacted and sealed, so the plot does not leak through it. Its limits are that it is present-day extension advice written for smallholder rice growers anywhere in the tropics rather than a measurement of an East Asian field; that it states a maximum and not a typical size; and that it is four sentences of practical guidance with no survey behind them.

Used for: the ordinary field bund's upper bound of 50 cm by 30 cm, and the requirement that a bund be compacted and sealed against seepage
