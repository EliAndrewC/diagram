tsuijibei-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 築地塀 ("rammed-earth wall", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/築地塀)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tsuijibei, the rammed-earth wall with its own tiled roof; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the wall's construction and the roof set on top of it, which is why this page draws a compound wall as a roofed thickness; it states neither the roof's purpose nor the wall's cost; the page carries the encyclopedia's banner for lacking sources, and every example it names is a palace, great-temple or castle wall, so an ordinary compound's wall is an extrapolation downward.

Used for: the roofed earthen compound wall (Estates and other compounds); the striped wall as a temple's rank (Religion and the dead)
