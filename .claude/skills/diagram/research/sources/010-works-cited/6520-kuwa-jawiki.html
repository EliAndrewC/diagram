ja.wikipedia クワ (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/クワ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on クワ, the mulberry (genus Morus): its botany, its species, and its cultivation for silkworm rearing, including the note that although a mulberry can grow into a tree of ten meters or more, most are bushes and cultivated ones are usually trained low.

Why it applies, and its limits: It tells us that cultivated mulberry is trained as a low shrub rather than let grow into the tree it can become, which is why a mulberry plot on our maps is waist-high bushes and not an orchard. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary; that it gives no planting density and no cultivated height in figures, so our height and spacing are guesses; and that it states the low-training habit as a present practice without dating it, so its use for a premodern plot is an assumption the page does not itself carry.

Used for: cultivated mulberry 「training as a low shrub is common」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「低木仕立てが多い」) - kept as a low shrub; the height and density figures are unsupported by any readable page (search syntheses: low-trunk mulberry under 70 cm, 1.2 m an optimal feeding height; 800-1,000 plants per mu in Jiangsu/Zhejiang, 5,000-6,000 in Guangdong)
