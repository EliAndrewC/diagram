集落町並みWalker, 青梅新町（東京都青梅市） ("Ome Shinmachi, Ome, Tokyo", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a private survey site of historic townscapes (in Japanese; http://www.shurakumachinami.natsu.gs/03datebase-page/tokyo_data/oumeshinmachi/oumeshinmachi_file.html)

What it is: A private enthusiast's page on Ome Shinmachi, a new village opened on the Ome road early in the Edo period: the road through its middle, the house lots on each side, their frontage and count, and why the front yards were wide.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a second planned row's frontage - far narrower than Santome's - and says the narrowness went with a plan to hold a market and become a post town, which marks where a farming row ends and a street village begins. It is an amateur page that names one published guide as its reference and gives no date for the figures, so its numbers are a secondary report of a single village.

Used for: a planned row with 9 ken of frontage and 33 lots each side, meant to grow into a market and post town (homesteads)
