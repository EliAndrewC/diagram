What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the towpath of the Eastern Zhejiang Canal at Shaoxing: its Tang reconstruction under Meng Jian in 815, its total length of nearly a hundred li, the 7.5 km protected stretch, and its two forms along the bank and out in the water.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the towpath's overall length and its forms, which set the scale and shape of the hauling path our river capital draws. A tertiary summary; it carries no dimensions of the deck, and its length and forms describe the towpath as it survives, whose fabric is later than the Tang institution.

Used for: the towpath's length and its two forms
