rice-rape-calendar-summary

Yangtze rice-rape rotation calendar - unsupported by any readable page: rape sown late September to mid-October, harvested early April (southern Jiangxi) to early May (Jiujiang); rice transplanted mid-April to early May (https://www.ecoagri.ac.cn/article/doi/10.12357/cjea.20240340?viewType=HTML)

What it is: A paper in the Chinese Journal of Eco-Agriculture (中国生态农业学报) by Liu Dan, Tian Jun and Duan Licheng on how a warming climate has shifted rice planting within the rice and rapeseed rotation of Jiangxi Province, built on weather records from 85 stations and concentrating on the years 2011 to 2022. It prints the district-by-district calendar: rapeseed harvested in early April in the south of the province and early May at Jiujiang, with rice transplanted from mid-April to early May.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated points in a plot's year for a rice-and-rapeseed rotation - rape harvested in early April in southern Jiangxi and early May at Jiujiang, rice transplanted from mid-April to early May - which is what decides what a plot on the map is carrying in a given month. Its limits are that it is a modern calendar, and the paper's own subject is that warming has moved these dates, so an older calendar sits later than the one it prints; that one southern Chinese province stands in for our rice country; and that it carries only the spring transitions - it gives neither the rice harvest month nor the rapeseed sowing window, which the record marks as unsourced.

Used for: the spring transitions of a rice-and-rape plot - rape harvested early April (southern Jiangxi) to early May (Jiujiang), rice transplanted mid-April to early May; the rice harvest month and the rapeseed sowing window are not on the page
