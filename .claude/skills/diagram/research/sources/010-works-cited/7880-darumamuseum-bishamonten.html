darumamuseum-bishamonten

Daruma Museum (Gabi Greve), Bishamonten festival and iconography (https://darumamuseum.blogspot.com/2007/04/bishamonten-festival.html) - 「Stone tigers like Komainu in front of Bishamon temples.」 (the source's own English)

What it is: A long-running self-published blog, in English with German passages, on Japanese Buddhist and folk iconography, its Bishamonten page of 2007: the deity's form, his tiger and centipede associations, the votive objects sold at his temples, and a scatter of named Bishamon temples and shrines.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it to corroborate the tiger pair as the general form at Bishamon temples, which it states as a rule rather than for one temple. Its limits: a hobbyist compilation, secondary, quoting other sites; the sentence we rest on is a bare photo caption that cites nothing and names no temple, no period and no count, so it cannot date the custom or say how widespread it was; and it names a village Bishamon shrine in Iwate but describes nothing of its precinct.

Used for: tigers in place of lion-dogs at a Bishamon dedication (religion-and-death.html).
