ja.wikipedia 瑞龍寺 (高岡市) ("Zuiryuji (Takaoka)", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/瑞龍寺_(高岡市))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Zuiryuji, the Soto Zen temple the Maeda lords built in the castle town of Takaoka in the early seventeenth century to mourn the lord who had built its castle, with its plan, its buildings and the mausolea behind it.

Why it applies, and its limits: A lord's great temple in a provincial castle town, which is nearer the setting's provincial city than any capital temple, and its plan is given building by building. It gives no area for the precinct; it is a tertiary summary; and part of the plan it describes was restored in the twentieth century.

Used for: a provincial great temple's plan: gate, Buddha hall and dharma hall on one line inside a cloister, the kuri, monks' hall, bath and privy to the sides, the bell tower on the cloister, the lords' mausolea outside it (religion-and-death)
