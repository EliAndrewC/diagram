kurayashiki-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 蔵屋敷 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%94%B5%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kurayashiki, the warehouse compounds the domains kept in Osaka and other cities to store and sell the tax rice and produce shipped in from their territories, with the counts of such compounds at Osaka in the 1670s and the 1840s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the concentration of the domains' rice trade at Osaka with two counts, which is the attested fact behind our capital's wharf-side granary district. It is a tertiary summary used for those counts only; the article carries a notice that it lacks sufficient sources and states the counts as what is said to have been, uncited - the discount that matters most where two numbers are taken - and it says nothing about how much of any domain's rice went there.

Used for: eighty domain kurayashiki at Osaka in the 1670s and 125 in the 1840s
