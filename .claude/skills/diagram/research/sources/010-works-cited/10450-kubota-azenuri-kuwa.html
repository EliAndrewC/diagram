kubota-azenuri-kuwa

Kubota Corporation, "Samazama na kuwa o tsukatta aze nuri" (Azenuri with various hoes), Kubota no tanbo, "The history of rice farming and the traditional tools behind it" (in Japanese; https://www.kubota.co.jp/kubotatanbo/history/tools/border-coating.html)

What it is: A page on a farm-machinery maker's educational site about traditional rice tools. It says azenuri was done with the hoe, quotes the early modern farm book Nogu benriron on how hoes differed from province to province, and classes the hoes by use.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says azenuri was done by hand with the hoe, before machines, but gives no date for that practice; the Nogu benriron line it quotes is early modern but secondhand, and speaks of hoes in general, not of azenuri. So it shows the hoe as the tool of traditional Japanese azenuri, not that the practice as described is Edo-period. It is a company's popular page with no date or author, and it contradicts itself on the pulling hoe, calling it once a hoe for moving soil and once one for deep tillage. It is cited only for the hoe and the Nogu benriron line.

Used for: that azenuri was done by hand with the hoe (fields)
