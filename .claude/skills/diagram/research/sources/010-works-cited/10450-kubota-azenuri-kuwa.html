kubota-azenuri-kuwa

Kubota Corporation, "Samazama na kuwa o tsukatta aze nuri" (Azenuri with various hoes), Kubota no tanbo, "The history of rice farming and the traditional tools behind it" (in Japanese; https://www.kubota.co.jp/kubotatanbo/history/tools/border-coating.html)

What it is: A page on a farm-machinery maker's educational site about traditional rice tools. It says azenuri was done with the hoe, quotes the early modern farm book Nogu benriron on how hoes differed from province to province, and classes the hoes by use.

Why it applies, and its limits: It ties azenuri to the hand hoe of the early modern farm books, before machines. It is a company's popular page with no date or author, and it contradicts itself on the pulling hoe, calling it once a hoe for moving soil and once one for deep tillage. It is cited only for the hoe and the Nogu benriron line.

Used for: that azenuri was done by hand with the hoe (fields)
