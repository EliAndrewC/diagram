What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article 評定所 (Hyojosho), on the Tokugawa shogunate's high council of judicature - the body made up of the town magistrate, the temple-and-shrine magistrate, the finance magistrate and one member of the shogun's senior council, which heard important matters of government and the suits that crossed several magistrates' jurisdictions.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for a single fact of placement: the Hyojosho stood at Tatsunokuchi outside Edo Castle, on ground that is now Marunouchi, and not within the castle walls - which is why a capital on our maps puts its high court outside the lord's enceinte. Its limits: it is a tertiary summary, taken only where it states a plain checkable fact of location and composition; it describes one specific Tokugawa institution in one city, which our fictional capitals resemble rather than copy; and it is in Japanese, quoted here in our own translation.

Used for: the Hyōjōsho standing 「Tatsunokuchi, outside Edo Castle」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸城外の辰ノ口」) - OUTSIDE Edo castle at Tatsunokuchi (present Marunouchi 1-chome)
