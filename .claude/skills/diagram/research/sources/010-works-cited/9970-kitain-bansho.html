Kawagoe Daishi Kita-in, 番所 (The guardroom) (in Japanese; https://kitain.net/history/cultural-asset/spot08/)

What it is: The temple's own page on its guardroom, a Saitama prefectural cultural property standing against its main gate: its size, roof and date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one measured guardroom standing as a building of its own beside a gate, the size a gatehouse is checked against. It is a temple's guardroom, not a samurai compound's, and it was moved to the gate after 1841, when a plan of the precinct shows it inside, behind the gate.

Used for: a guardroom of 3 by 2 ken built against a gate (buildings)
