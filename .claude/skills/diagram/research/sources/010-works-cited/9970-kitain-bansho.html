kitain-bansho

Kawagoe Daishi Kita-in, 番所 (The guardroom) (in Japanese; https://kitain.net/history/cultural-asset/spot08/)

What it is: The temple's own page on its guardroom, a Saitama prefectural cultural property standing against its main gate: its size, roof and date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one measured guardroom we read that stands as a building of its own beside a gate (3 by 2 ken, mid-to-late Edo), the size a gatehouse is checked against. It is a temple's guardroom, not a samurai compound's (Edo Castle's freestanding guardhouses are warrior-side examples we did not read), and the page is two unsigned sentences. Its place against the gate is not of its building: an 1841 plan of the precinct shows it inside, behind the gate, and the page only believes it was moved afterward, without a date - the move may be Meiji or later, so the size carries for our period and the placement against the gate does not by itself.

Used for: a guardroom of 3 by 2 ken, a building of its own, now standing against a gate (buildings)
