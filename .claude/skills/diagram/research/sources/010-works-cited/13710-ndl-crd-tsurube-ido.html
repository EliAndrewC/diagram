National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, Saitama Prefectural Kuki Library, "I want to know the structure of a bucket well: how big it was, what the rope was made of" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「つるべ井戸の構造について知りたい。どのくらいの大きさか、ひもがどのような素材か等。」) (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000346279&page=ref_view)

What it is: A public library's recorded answer to a reader's question, listing books that show a bucket well and quoting the caption of one book's photograph, which gives the well's width and depth and the bucket's size.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one measured wellhead the record has read. It is a librarian quoting a caption from a 2013 children's picture book of old implements (Showa-era objects), so it measures one preserved well of the 20th century, not a survey of old wells.

Used for: a bucket well's head at 118 cm by 118 cm (urban-features)
