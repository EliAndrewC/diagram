National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, Saitama Prefectural Kuki Library, "I want to know the structure of a bucket well: how big it was, what the rope was made of" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「つるべ井戸の構造について知りたい。どのくらいの大きさか、ひもがどのような素材か等。」) (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000346279&page=ref_view)

What it is: A public library's recorded answer to a reader's question, listing books that show a bucket well and quoting the caption of one book's photograph, which gives the well's width and depth and the bucket's size.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one measured wellhead the record has read. It is a librarian quoting a photograph's caption in a 2013 children's picture book of implements used in the Meiji, Taisho and Showa periods (1868-1989), so it measures one well of unstated place and date from after the Edo period, not a survey of old wells: the bucket-and-pulley form is older than the specimen, but the figure is not an Edo one. The caption gives 118 cm by 118 cm for the "well" without saying which part it measures (that it is the wellhead is this project's reading). The page also lists archaeological studies of wells (Kanekata 2003; Akita 2010) that would bear more closely on premodern wells, but none of them could be read online.

Used for: a bucket well's head at 118 cm by 118 cm (urban-features)
