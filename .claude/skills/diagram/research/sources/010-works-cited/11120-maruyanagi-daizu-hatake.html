maruyanagi-daizu-hatake

Maruyanagi, 大豆を収穫するまで：北海道の大豆畑の成長記録 ("Until the soybeans are harvested: a growth record of a Hokkaido soybean field", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.maruyanagi.co.jp/mushimame/daizu-hatake/)

What it is: A food company's photo diary of one contract soybean field in Hokkaido through a season, from May sowing to October harvest.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records what a soybean field looks like month by month - how tall the plants stand and when the field turns from green to yellow-brown. It is one modern, machine-sown field of a few hectares in a cold northern region, so its months are late and its plant heights are those of a modern variety; only the sequence of colors is carried to the maps.

Used for: the height a soybean field reaches and its turn from green to yellow-brown before harvest (fields)
