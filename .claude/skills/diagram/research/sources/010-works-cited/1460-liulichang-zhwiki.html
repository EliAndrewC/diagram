What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Liulichang in Beijing, the glazed-tile works of the Yuan and Ming that gave the later book and antiques street its name: its place among the Ministry of Works' five great works, its enlargement for the palaces, and its extent inside the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives an official works standing at the fringe of the capital, enlarged to serve the palace building, the case behind our capital's tile works. A tertiary summary used for that; it gives no distance from the palace. And it describes the imperial capital, whose Ministry-of-Works establishments and gate-signal apparatus a provincial seat had no counterpart of, so the form is stepped down two tiers by us.

Used for: the Liulichang as one of the five great works, enlarged for the palaces, and its extent
