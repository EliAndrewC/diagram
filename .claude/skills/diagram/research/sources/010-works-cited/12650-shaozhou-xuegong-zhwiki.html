zh.wikipedia 韶州府学宫 ("the Shaozhou prefectural school", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/韶州府学宫)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the school-temple of Shaozhou prefecture, Guangdong, rebuilt after a fire of 1602, listing the buildings it once held and measuring its surviving main hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a prefectural school-temple's full set of buildings and one measured hall. Only the hall survives, so the list is of what the complex held, not a plan with sizes.

Used for: what a prefectural school-temple held and how big its hall was (religion-and-death)
