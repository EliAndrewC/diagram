shaozhou-xuegong-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 韶州府学宫 ("the Shaozhou prefectural school", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/韶州府学宫)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the school-temple of Shaozhou prefecture, Guangdong, rebuilt after a fire of 1602, listing the buildings it once held and measuring its surviving main hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a prefectural school-temple's full set of buildings and one measured hall (five bays by three, 374 square metres of floor inside, 16.4 metres high). It is an encyclopedia article: both the list and the measurements rest on the paper it cites, Bi Xiaofang and Cheng Jianjun's analysis of the hall in Southern Architecture (南方建筑), 2015. Only the hall survives, so the list is of what the complex held at some undated time before the exams ended in 1905, not a plan with sizes. The hall is the rebuilding after the 1602 fire, repaired four times under the Qing and used as an auditorium and a dance hall after 1949. It is a far-southern (Guangdong) building whose frame mixes the southern pierced-post system with the beam-lifting one, so its construction is regional, and a county's school-temple would be smaller.

Used for: what a prefectural school-temple held and how big its hall was (religion-and-death)
