kuramai-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 蔵米 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/蔵米)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kuramai, the rice received as land tax and held in the authorities' storehouses, and the stipends reckoned from it; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that the tax rice the authorities held was brown rice reckoned in bales, which is the form this page's granary carries; it describes the stipend's reckoning rather than the villages' delivery, so the delivery form itself is on no page read; the article carries the encyclopedia's own banner for insufficient sources.

Used for: the tax rice as brown rice in bales (buildings)
