ja.wikipedia 薬屋 ("pharmacy", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%96%AC%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the pharmacy, mostly on the modern trade and its law, with a list of related pages that includes the Edo streets where drug sellers stood together.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used for one line: that drug shops stood eave to eave in Nihonbashi Honcho from the Edo period, an example of a trade street in the great city. A tertiary summary whose history is a single uncited line in a list of related pages (a gloss on the article on Nihonbashi Honcho, which also cites nothing and names the trade there as wholesale druggists); it gives no count of shops, no date within the Edo period, and says nothing of a small town.

Used for: Edo's drug shops standing eave to eave in one street (urban-features)
