What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the pharmacy, mostly on the modern trade and its law, with a list of related pages that includes the Edo streets where drug sellers stood together.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used for one line: that drug shops stood eave to eave in Nihonbashi Honcho from the Edo period, an example of a trade street in the great city. A tertiary summary whose history is a single line in a list of related pages; it says nothing of a small town.
