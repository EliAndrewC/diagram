zh.wikipedia 宁波府城隍庙 ("the Ningbo prefectural City God temple", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/宁波府城隍庙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the City God temple of Ningbo prefecture, Zhejiang, giving its buildings along the axis and its site and built areas.

Why it applies, and its limits: A prefectural City God temple that survives, measured, is a size anchor for the temple a Chinese prefectural seat kept. The figures (about 4,700 square metres of site, 4,200 of buildings) are for the surviving complex as restored in 2018-2020. Its buildings are the 1884 rebuild, on the layout the article dates to a 1727 repair. They are not a historical survey, and they come from an encyclopedia article whose references are local newspaper columns (2007) and a 1995 city gazetteer. The article calls this one of the largest City God temples in China, so it is an upper example of a prefectural temple, not a typical one. It is also one example from one Zhejiang prefecture, in a late-Qing official style mixed with east-Zhejiang features such as the opera stage joined to the ceremonial gate.
