zh.wikipedia 宁波府城隍庙 ("the Ningbo prefectural City God temple", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/宁波府城隍庙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the City God temple of Ningbo prefecture, Zhejiang, giving its buildings along the axis and its site and built areas.

Why it applies, and its limits: A prefectural City God temple that survives, measured, is a size anchor for the temple a Chinese prefectural seat kept. The figures are for the surviving complex as restored, not a dated historical survey, and one prefecture's temple is one example.

Used for: how big a prefectural City God temple was and what stood on its axis (religion-and-death)
