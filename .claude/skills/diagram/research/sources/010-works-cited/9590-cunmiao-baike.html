Baidu Baike, 村庙 (village temple) (https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E6%9D%91%E5%BA%99/3494869)

What it is: The Baidu encyclopedia entry on the Chinese village temple, comparing the areas of village temples in southern and north-eastern Fujian.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives China's village temple as a single hall of about 100 to 400 m². Its limits: an open encyclopedia with no date for its figures, which describe present-day Fujian temples, many rebuilt, and may be building areas rather than compounds.

Used for: China's village temple size (religion-and-death.html).
