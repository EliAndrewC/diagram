Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, こどもそうだん ("Children's questions"), イネの苗を何cm間隔で植えたらいいですか ("How many cm apart should rice seedlings be planted?", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/j/heya/kodomo_sodan/0405/01.html)

What it is: The ministry's short answer, written for children and updated in 2023, on how far apart to set transplanted rice, citing a children's book on growing rice.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives today's advice - rows and hills about 30 cm apart, closed to about 20-25 cm between hills for short seedlings - one modern statement (itself quoted from a children's book, 『イネを育てる』) of the familiar 20-30 cm figure, and so shows that figure to be modern practice. It says nothing about older or pre-Meiji planting, and its 20-25 cm is hedged as "it is said".

Used for: today's hill spacing, as the modern figure (Fields)
