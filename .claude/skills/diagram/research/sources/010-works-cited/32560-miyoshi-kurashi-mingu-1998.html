miyoshi-kurashi-mingu-1998

Miyoshi Town Museum of History and Folklore, くらしの民具（１９９８年度分） ("Tools of everyday life, the 1998 series", title translated), a monthly column from the town bulletin, Miyoshi, Saitama (http://www.jade.dti.ne.jp/~miyoshir/mingu/mingu98.html)

What it is: A year of short columns by the town's museum on the parts of the old Ikegami house, a large thatched farmhouse moved onto the museum's grounds: its roof, its three doorways, its earth walls, its veranda, its main post, its sills and its earth floor.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how this one farmhouse's big door and veranda were opened by day and closed at night, and how its inner earth walls were built. It describes one large, rich house of the Musashino upland, built at the end of the Edo period or a little after, as its museum shows it; how the household used it is told from the house's fittings and a local resident's memory, not from a period document.

Used for: the big door shut and barred at night and the wicket used then; the veranda's storm shutters and paper screens (Estates and other compounds); a farmhouse's earth walls on lath (Homesteads)
