denken-kakunodate

National Council of Preservation Districts for Groups of Traditional Buildings (Denkenkyo), "仙北市角館 伝建地区詳細" [Kakunodate, Semboku city: district details] (in Japanese; https://www.denken.gr.jp/archive/senboku-kakunodate/index.html)

What it is: The national council of preservation districts' short description of Kakunodate's samurai district, the lots of its upper and middle samurai.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the district's lot division to the end of the Edo period, so the lots a visitor sees are late-Edo lots. It gives no lot's area, covers only the upper and middle samurai's ground, and gives no date of its own.

Used for: that Kakunodate keeps the lot division of the end of the Edo period (cities/government)
