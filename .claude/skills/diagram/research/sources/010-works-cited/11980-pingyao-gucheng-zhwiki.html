What it is: The encyclopedia article on the walled Ming and Qing county seat of Pingyao, Shanxi, a World Heritage site, including a sentence setting out where its City God temple, county office, Confucian and Martial temples and its Daoist and Buddhist temples stand about the main street.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a county seat's temples inside its walls by the town's plan. Its limits: one well-preserved city, perhaps more regular than most; the sentence states the arrangement without a date or a source of its own.
