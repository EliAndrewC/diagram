Xinhua News client, "Tracing China: a home-town story for the children - the simple timber bridge behind the house", 2025-03-21 (in Chinese; https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=ce1dee5cf05a86c9fed794df0333c1ae)

What it is: A memoir published by the Xinhua news agency's app, in which the writer tells a child how the family crossed the irrigation canal behind their house in a rice-growing village: the grandfather laid four log sections across it, and the logs were later replaced by a concrete slab.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one account read in which a household itself lays timber over a farm canal, and it says this was common in a village criss-crossed by ditches. Its limits are severe: it is MODERN (the clues - hybrid rice just being promoted, walking tractors, a pump station - place it in the late twentieth century), it names no province, it is one family's memory, and the canal it describes is 2-3 m wide, several times the width of the ditches our maps plank.

Used for: a modern instance of a household laying logs over a farm canal (water)
