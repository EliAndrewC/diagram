What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Jōkan-ji, the temple near the Yoshiwara quarter known as the throw-in temple for the courtesans buried there after the 1855 earthquake.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the temple's name, its origin and its position beside the quarter it served, the case this page uses for a burial ground standing near the wall. A tertiary summary; the distance is our own measurement. The page attests the temple's nearness to the pleasure quarter, not to any wall - Edo had none, so the wall is our frame - and the throw-in burials are one disaster episode of 1855 at the end of the period, not a standing practice.

Used for: the throw-in temple's name and its nearness to Yoshiwara
