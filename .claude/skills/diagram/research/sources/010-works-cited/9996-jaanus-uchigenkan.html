jaanus-uchigenkan

JAANUS (Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System), "uchigenkan" 内玄関 (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/uchigenkan)

What it is: The entry for uchigenkan 内玄関, the inner or secondary entrance, in JAANUS, an online English-language dictionary of Japanese art and architectural terms, setting it against the main genkan with its wide low step.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says who came in by the main entrance and who by the inner one, which decides which door a guest's path leads to. Its limits: it is a specialist dictionary's compact definition, compiled by Mary Neighbour Parent, with no references of its own; it dates the term only to the latter half of the 18th century onward (Edo Japan); and it describes only houses grand enough to have a formal genkan - warrior residences, machiya and the grandest minka - so the rule holds for a house of that rank and says nothing of an ordinary house's single entrance.

Used for: the main genkan kept for the head of the household and honored guests (buildings)
