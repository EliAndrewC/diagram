Japan Travel Bureau Foundation, National Tourism Resources Register, 喜多方蔵のまち並み ("Kitakata's storehouse townscape", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://tabi.jtb.or.jp/res/070048-)

What it is: The foundation's register entry for Kitakata's storehouse streets, current as of 2023: how many storehouses stand in the city, what they were for, and when and why they spread.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the spread of the earthen storehouse in one town and gives the reasons, which says how far that town's count stands above the ordinary. Its count is for the whole modern city, rural settlements included, and is given as what is said; most of the spread it describes is late Edo and Meiji.

Used for: when and why Kitakata's storehouses multiplied (cities/fabric.html)
