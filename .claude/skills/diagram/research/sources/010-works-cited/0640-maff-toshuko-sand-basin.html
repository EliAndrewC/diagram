maff-toshuko-sand-basin

MAFF (Japan, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries), land-improvement design standard, technical appendix on head works (土地改良事業計画設計基準 設計 頭首工 付録技術書), chapter 20, the general design of settling basins (https://www.maff.go.jp/j/nousin/attach/pdf/tousyukou-79.pdf)

What it is: The chapter on the general design of settling basins from the same technical appendix to the Ministry of Agriculture's head-works design standard: where the basin that drops a canal's sand stands relative to the intake mouth, and how its transitions are shaped.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the settling basin - immediately after the intake mouth, at the start of the canal - which tells us what the first stretch of a head race is for. Its limits are that it is modern design guidance with no historical content, that its shapes and angles belong to concrete works, and that it gives no distance from the intake to anything, so the length of a head race rests on nothing here.

Used for: the settling basin at the canal's start, immediately following the intake mouth
