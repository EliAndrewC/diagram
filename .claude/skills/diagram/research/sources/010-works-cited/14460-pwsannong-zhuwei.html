智汇三农 (the China Agriculture Press knowledge service), 古代筑圩 ("Polder building in antiquity", title translated from the Chinese by this project), an entry of its encyclopedia of Chinese water-conservancy history (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/548127.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry on how polders were planned and built in imperial China: the regional names, the range of sizes and shapes, and the three measures - dike, dredged channel, sluice - set down in the Northern Song.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size range of polders, from a few tens of mu to over a hundred thousand or even several hundred thousand, which places the great polders whose dike figures survive against the village polders these maps draw. It is a modern encyclopedia summary with no author, no date and no references. Its range covers the Tang to the Qing without tying any figure to an era or a place, so it gives the shape of the range but not a figure to build on. It names the period treatises that would carry the figures (Chen Hu's Zhu wei shuo, Ming; Sun Jun's Zhu wei tu shuo, Qing), and it describes China only.

Used for: the range of polder sizes and shapes (archetypes)
