kotobank-warazuka

kotobank 藁塚 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%97%81%E5%A1%9A)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entries on the warazuka, the straw rick piled in the reaped field after threshing, an autumn season-word.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states what the rick is and when it stood, the seasonal feature these maps leave undrawn. A dictionary reference; it gives no standing duration and does not use the Toyama word waraguro.

Used for: the straw rick after the harvest
