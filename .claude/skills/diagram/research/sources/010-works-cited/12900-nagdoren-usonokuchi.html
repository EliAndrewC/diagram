nagdoren-usonokuchi

Nagano Federation of Land Improvement Associations, いにしえの慣わしを今に伝える 鷽ノ口円形分水 ("The Usonokuchi circular divider, carrying an old custom into the present", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.nag-doren.or.jp/canallist/canallist-985/)

What it is: A page in the Nagano prefectural land-improvement federation's series on the province's historic irrigation canals, describing an Edo-period canal in Sakuho town shared by seven villages (the old Nakahata village and five others, and the old Kami village) and the "wisteria-vine" division its users practiced until a concrete divider replaced it in 1953.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes, step by step, how two quarreling villages set their split each spring - measuring sticks cut to the length of two sealed vines, and water mouths built of sandbags to those lengths at a pool on the channel - which is a village-built division work with no permanent structure. It is one mountain canal as its present managers tell its history, for a general audience, with no dates for when the custom began.

Used for: the wisteria-vine division, a split set yearly with sandbags to agreed widths (water)
