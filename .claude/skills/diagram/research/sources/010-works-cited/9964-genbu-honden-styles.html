genbu-honden-styles

genbu.net, "神社建築：本殿様式・その他" (shrine architecture: main hall styles, other) (in Japanese; https://genbu.net/tisiki/sonota.htm)

What it is: A page of a long-running personal website on Japanese shrines, describing the less common styles of main hall with a photograph of an example of each.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the aidono-zukuri form, two or more small halls joined under one roof, which is one of the ways a hall housed several kami. It is an amateur's reference site with no author credentials and no citations, so it is used only for what a style looks like, never for how common it was, and it dates nothing.

Used for: that aidono-zukuri joins two or more small halls under a single roof (religion-and-death).
