genbu-honden-styles

genbu.net, "神社建築：本殿様式・その他" (shrine architecture: main hall styles, other) (in Japanese; https://genbu.net/tisiki/sonota.htm)

What it is: A page of a long-running personal website on Japanese shrines, describing the less common styles of main hall with a photograph of an example of each.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the aidono-zukuri form, two or more small halls joined under one roof, and shows a photograph of an extant example. It describes only the building's shape. It does not say why the halls are joined, so the reading that the form housed several kami comes from the style's name and from the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's definition (相殿の神をまつるために), not from this page. It is an amateur's reference site, not updated since 2015, with no author credentials and no citations on the page, and it covers present-day Japanese buildings, including modern concrete ones. So it is used only for what a style looks like, never for how common the style was or when it arose, and it dates nothing.

Used for: that aidono-zukuri joins two or more small halls under a single roof (religion-and-death).
