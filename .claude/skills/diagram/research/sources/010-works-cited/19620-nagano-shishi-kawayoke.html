nagano-shishi-kawayoke

Nagano City, Nagano Shishi (the Nagano city history), vol. 4, history, early modern 2, ch. 9 section 4, 「The flood-control measures of the Matsushiro domain: how river-protection works were built」 (translated; original: 「松代藩の治水対策 川除け普請の方法」), pp. 252-256, on the ADEAC digital archive (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/nagano-city/texthtml/d100040/ct00000004/ht000860)

What it is: The Nagano city history's account of how the Matsushiro domain built and protected its river embankments in the Edo period: what the embankments were called, how their slopes were faced, and the groynes that shielded them. The groynes and the damage it lists are drawn from domain documents of 1790 to 1861.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the grass facing of an embankment's slopes to the Edo period: the slopes were turfed to stop the soil washing away, or faced with layered reed-grass, brushwood and pine needles. It is about river embankments in one inland domain, not the bank of a farm pond; it says nothing about mowing or burning the bank; and it is a modern municipal history's summary, not the documents themselves. Its sentence on turfing is the historian's general statement about the domain's embankments. It names no document and no date, so it dates the practice only to the Edo period at large.

Used for: an Edo embankment's slopes turfed to hold the soil (water)
