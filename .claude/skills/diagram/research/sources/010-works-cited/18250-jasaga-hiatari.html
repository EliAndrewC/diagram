jasaga-hiatari

JA Saga (Saga Prefecture Agricultural Cooperative), 「Sunlight - the basics of the home vegetable garden」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日当たり｜家庭菜園の基礎」) (in Japanese; http://jasaga.or.jp/kateisaien/yasai/kiso/sun)

What it is: A page of the Saga agricultural cooperative's guide to the home vegetable garden, listing which vegetables like full sun, which do with half a day of sun, and which do even in shade.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a second, independent sorting of the same crops by their need for sun, from a farm cooperative rather than a seed seller, and it agrees with the seed company's: daikon, eggplant, cucumber and the beans like full sun; negi, komatsuna, taro and ginger do with half a day; mitsuba and myoga do in shade. Its limits are that it gives no hours - only full sun, half a day and shade - and that it is modern advice for modern cultivars in a southern prefecture.

Used for: the hours of direct sun a kitchen bed needs (homesteads.html).
