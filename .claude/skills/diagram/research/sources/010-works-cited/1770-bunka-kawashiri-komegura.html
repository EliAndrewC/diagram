bunka-kawashiri-komegura

文化遺産オンライン (Cultural Heritage Online, Agency for Cultural Affairs), 熊本藩川尻米蔵跡 (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/147327)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record for the Kumamoto domain's Kawashiri rice-granary site, a historic site: the river-port granary where the domain's tribute rice was collected, which also served as a naval port.

Why it applies, and its limits: An official record of a domain's tribute-rice granary standing at its river port, the wharf granary our capital draws. Its limit is that the record says nothing of stipends.

Used for: the domain's tribute-rice granary at its river port
