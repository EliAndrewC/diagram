ja.wikipedia 道場 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/道場)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the dojo, the martial-arts practice hall, with its history from private and outdoor practice to the built hall; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests practice at a private residence, outdoors or on an earthen floor before the mid-Edo boarded hall, and the great enrolled dojos of Edo, which is the ladder this page's county and city tiers rest on; it frames the change by period, not by town and country, and gives no floor areas.

Used for: where samurai trained (buildings)
