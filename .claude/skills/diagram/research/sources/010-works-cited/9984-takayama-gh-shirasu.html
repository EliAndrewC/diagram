Hida no Tabarubako, "高山陣屋"の七不思議（後編） (The seven mysteries of the Takayama Jin'ya, part 2), an interview with a Takayama Jin'ya guide (in Japanese; https://www.takayama-gh.com/tabaru/article/jinya_mystery02/)

What it is: A Takayama tourist site's article in which a guide at the Takayama Jin'ya answers questions about its buildings, among them why its court is not white and why it is roofed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the reasons for the one surviving intendant's court's form - river cobbles for want of white sand, and a roof because of the snow - which is what lets a map choose the court's surface by its place. Its limits are that it is an interview on a tourist site without references, that its reasons are the guide's account rather than a cited record, and that the roof over the accused is stated in the interviewer's question, the guide saying the court was made inside the building.

Used for: why Takayama's court is of river cobbles and under a roof (buildings)
