ranzan-kinsenji

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡寺院明細帳, 曹洞宗 金泉寺 七郷村大字越畑 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter05.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription of the Hiki district temple register entry for Kinsenji, a Sōtō temple of Oppata village, Saitama, with its buildings, precinct and land held outside the precinct, and the site editors' glosses of the terms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists a village temple's kuri and monks' dormitory, glosses the kuri as the house of the head priest and his family, and enters the temple's graveyard among its land outside the precinct at another place in the village. Its limits: one rural temple; a Meiji-era register carried into 1963, not an Edo record; the glosses are the web editors', not the register's, and describe the modern Japanese usage.

Used for: who lives inside a temple's precinct, and a graveyard held apart from it (religion-and-death.html).
