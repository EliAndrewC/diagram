kotobank-daihachiguruma

kotobank, "大八車" (daihachiguruma, the Edo hand cart) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%A4%A7%E5%85%AB%E8%BB%8A-558136)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting several Japanese reference works' entries on the daihachiguruma, the large two-wheeled hand cart of Edo: its origin after the fire of 1657, its size, where it was used, and the regulation of it.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its entries are signed reference-work articles, one of them quoting an 1815 report from Edo, and they give the cart's size and its spread through the towns. They disagree with each other on Osaka - the 1815 report says the cart was used there, the other entries that Osaka used a different cart - and they say nothing of carts on the highways or of the width of the lanes a cart used.

Used for: the Edo hand cart's spread through the castle towns and its bed of about 8 by 2.5 ft (ways)
