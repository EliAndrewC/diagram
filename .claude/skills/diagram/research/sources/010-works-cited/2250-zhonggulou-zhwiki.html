zh.wikipedia 鐘鼓樓 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%90%98%E9%BC%93%E6%A8%93)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the bell-and-drum towers of Chinese cities: the paired arrangement facing across an axis, the single combined building, and the morning-bell, evening-drum institution of announcing the hours.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the paired form as the usual arrangement, the combined single tower as a variant, and the towers' duty of announcing the time, which is the county-seat tower these maps draw. A tertiary summary; it gives no rule about how many towers a county seat had. The article is a stub carrying the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks references.

Used for: the paired and the combined tower forms; morning bell, evening drum
