Saga TV (サガテレビ), an article on Yutoku Inari Shrine's torii (https://www.sagatv.co.jp/kachiplus/media/archives/903653)

What it is: A Saga television station's web article counting Yutoku Inari's torii - just under 300 in the precinct, 223 on the path to the inner shrine - and saying they stand almost 10 cm apart.

Why it applies, and its limits: The one page-stated pitch for a donation row. Its limits: a great Inari shrine, a modern count, and a pitch for a packed stretch rather than the whole path.
