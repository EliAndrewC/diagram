yutoku-inari-sagatv

Saga TV (サガテレビ), an article on Yutoku Inari Shrine's torii (https://www.sagatv.co.jp/kachiplus/media/archives/903653)

What it is: A Saga television station's web article counting Yutoku Inari's torii - just under 300 in the precinct, 223 on the path to the inner shrine - and saying, by the reporter's eye, that they seem to stand about 10 cm apart (2021).

Why it applies, and its limits: The one page-stated pitch for a donation row. Its limits: a great Inari shrine; a modern count that includes arches offered as recently as 2020; the 10 cm is a reporter's impression by eye, and the page's word may mean the gap between arches rather than post-to-post pitch; and it holds for a packed stretch, not the whole 300 m path, over which 223 arches average about 1.3 m apiece.

Used for: a donation row's pitch (religion-and-death.html).
