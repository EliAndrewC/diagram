kotobank-asakusa-okura

Kotobank, s.v. 浅草御蔵 ("the Asakusa Okura, the shogunate's rice granary at Asakusa", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - the entries of the 改訂新版 世界大百科事典 (Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, revised edition; the entry by Kitahara Susumu), 日本大百科全書 (Encyclopedia Nipponica) and 百科事典マイペディア (MyPedia) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B5%85%E8%8D%89%E5%BE%A1%E8%94%B5-24993)

What it is: Three Japanese encyclopedias' entries on the shogunate's granary at Asakusa in Edo, founded about 1620: how it was built on filled ground on the Sumida with eight boat channels, its size, that its rice was paid out as stipends to the shogunate's retainers, and the Kuramae block before it where the brokers and rice wholesalers kept their houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is standard reference work on the one granary our capital's stipend granary and brokers' row are modeled on, and it states plainly both its waterside siting and the merchant street in front of it. Its limits are the summary form, and that this was the shogunate's central granary for Edo, many times the size of anything a domain seat built, so its form transfers and its scale does not.

Used for: the granary's stipend-paying job and its riverside site with boat channels; the brokers' and wholesalers' houses on its town side, and the firebreak in the Kuramae block (cities/capitals)
