ja.wikipedia 国分寺 ("kokubunji, the provincial temples", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/国分寺)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kokubunji, the monastery and nunnery Emperor Shomu ordered set up in every province in the 740s, each monastery with a seven-story pagoda.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one case in which a pagoda was required of a temple in each province, the nearest history comes to a provincial city's temple keeping a pagoda by rule. The order is of the eighth century, and it says nothing of whether the provinces' later temples kept pagodas.

Used for: the provincial temple's pagoda (religion-and-death)
