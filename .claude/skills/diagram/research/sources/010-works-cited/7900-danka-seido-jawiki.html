ja.wikipedia 檀家制度 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AA%80%E5%AE%B6%E5%88%B6%E5%BA%A6) - 「A parish household belongs to a particular temple, entrusts all of its funerary and memorial rites to that temple, and pays fuse.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「檀家は特定の寺院に所属し、葬祭供養の一切をその寺に任せ、布施を払う。」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the danka system, the parish household's bond to its temple.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the parish economy - the household entrusting its funerary rites to its temple and paying fuse - which is the "gifts and fees" of the campaign notes. Tertiary; it describes the system, not one temple's income.

Used for: the household's fuse to its temple (religion-and-death.html).
