Inomata Seiya and Sasaki Yo, 郡上八幡における水利用施設の管理実態にみられる多様性と主体性について ("On the diversity and initiative seen in the management of water-use facilities in Gujo Hachiman", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 土木計画学研究発表会・講演集 55, Japan Society of Civil Engineers, 2017 (http://library.jsce.or.jp/jsce/open/00039/201706_no55/55-59-08.pdf)

What it is: A conference paper by two civil-engineering planners surveying how the residents of Gujo Hachiman, a small castle town in Gifu laced with channels, keep their shared washing places, spring tanks and channel boards.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the washing places a reader would see - araiba on a channel, kawado on the river, each a footing for washing clothes and vegetables - and the boards slotted into a channel to pond water for washing or split the flow, and the three-tier spring tank used top to bottom from drinking to muddy vegetables. It surveys one town today; the facilities are old in form but the paper does not date each one.

Used for: the araiba and kawado; the channel board; the tiered washing tank (water)
