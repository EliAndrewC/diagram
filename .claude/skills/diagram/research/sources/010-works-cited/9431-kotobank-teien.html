kotobank-teien

Kotobank, 庭園 (the garden), entries from Heibonsha's World Encyclopedia (revised edition) and Shogakukan's Encyclopedia Nipponica (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%BA%AD%E5%9C%92-99810)

What it is: The Kotobank page gathering several Japanese encyclopedia entries on the garden, the longest being the history of the Japanese garden from the ancient capitals through the Zen, tea and daimyo gardens, and a classification of garden kinds and their components.

Why it applies, and its limits: Two standard encyclopedias, signed by their authors, give the kinds of garden (pond, dry landscape, tea garden), the parts a garden was made from, the stone lantern's arrival in the tea garden, and the size of one shoin garden. Their limits are that they tell the history through famous temple, palace and daimyo gardens, not the gardens of ordinary samurai houses or offices; and that the one garden size used, the Daisen-in's, is a Zen temple's.

Used for: the three kinds of garden and a garden's components; the Daisen-in's shoin garden of about 100 m2, viewed from the room and veranda; the stone lantern in the tea garden (buildings)
