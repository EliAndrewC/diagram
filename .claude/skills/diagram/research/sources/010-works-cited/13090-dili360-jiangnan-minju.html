Wu Hua, 江南民居 临河人家的水乡梦 ("Jiangnan houses: the water-town dream of the riverside household", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 博物 2009 no. 1, on the Chinese National Geography site (https://www.dili360.com/index.php/nh/article/p5350c3d8e3a2a85.htm)

What it is: The opening of a 2009 article in 博物, the popular natural-history magazine of the Chinese National Geography group, on the houses of the Jiangnan water towns; the site shows only the first paragraphs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in the dense canal country washing, shopping and cooking were all done at the riverside, and that the ideal house had one door on the street and one on the water. Only a teaser is readable. It describes the houses of southern Jiangsu and Zhejiang generally, as the water towns stand today. It says the riverside life goes back "since ancient times" (自古以来), but a popular magazine piece with no references cannot prove that, so it carries the practice and no detail or number.

Used for: washing done at the riverside in Jiangnan (water)
