民间风水：农村住宅风水的正确布局 ("Folk fengshui: the right layout for a rural house", title translated from the Chinese by this project), a reposted article on Sohu (搜狐), credited to the account 悟空看非遗 (in Chinese; https://www.sohu.com/a/481439988_121124711)

What it is: A popular fengshui article, reposted by a self-publishing account on a Chinese web portal, giving folk rules for the layout of a rural house, among them the pond before it and why its best shape is a half circle.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that states the meaning given to the half circle - a half-full moon, because a full moon wanes - and names the Hakka enclosed house's front pond among its examples. Its limits are severe: it is an anonymous modern popular text, carries no references and no date for the belief, has visible typos, and speaks for present-day folk fengshui rather than for any period's builders; it shows that the reading is current, not that the ponds were dug for it.

Used for: the folk reading of the half-moon pond's shape as a moon that has not yet waned (homesteads)
