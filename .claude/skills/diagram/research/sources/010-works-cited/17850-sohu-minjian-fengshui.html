民间风水：农村住宅风水的正确布局 ("Folk fengshui: the right layout for a rural house", title translated), a reposted article on Sohu (搜狐), credited to the account 悟空看非遗 (in Chinese; https://www.sohu.com/a/481439988_121124711)

What it is: A popular fengshui article dated 4 August 2021, posted on a Chinese web portal's self-publishing platform by an account that describes itself as a micro-media outlet of China's hongmu (hardwood furniture) trade. It gives folk rules for the yard of a present-day self-built rural house: its enclosing wall, and its pond, including why the pond's best shape is a half circle.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that states the meaning given to the half circle - a half-full moon, because a full moon wanes - and names the Hakka enclosed house's front pond among its examples. Its limits are severe: it is an anonymous text, carries no references and no date for the belief, has visible typos. Its advice is written for modern self-built and luxury houses and gives the same rule for fountains and swimming pools, and it comes from a furniture-trade media account, not from anyone who studies vernacular building; it shows that the reading is current, not that the ponds were dug for it.

Used for: the folk reading of the half-moon pond's shape as a moon that has not yet waned (Homesteads)
