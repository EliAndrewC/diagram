iwamuro-kenchicho

Keio University Old Documents Room, exhibition 2013, item 5: "Survey register of Iwamuro village, Koka district, Omi" (in Japanese; https://kmj.flet.keio.ac.jp/exhibition/2013/05.html)

What it is: An exhibition page of Keio University's old-documents room showing a village survey register of 1602 (Keicho 7) from Omi province, with a transcription of a dozen of its entries, each a plot with its grade, area and holder.

Why it applies, and its limits: A primary register from the start of the Edo period, so its plot sizes are premodern Japanese fields as surveyed. The page transcribes only a dozen entries of one village of Omi, not a polder, and gives no count or average, so it shows a range of plot sizes, not a typical one.

Used for: that single paddy plots in 1602 ran from 2 se to 1 tan 8 se (archetypes)
