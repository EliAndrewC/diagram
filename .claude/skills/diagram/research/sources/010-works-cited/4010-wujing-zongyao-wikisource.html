Zeng Gongliang and Ding Du, Wujing Zongyao (1044), first collection, juan 12, on Chinese Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/武經總要/前集/卷十二)

What it is: The Northern Song imperial military compendium's chapter on building and defending a walled city, in the Wikisource transcription; a primary text.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prescribes four gates with two kept shut, three on awkward ground, and the enemy tower's bays, which are the counted openings and the tower this page draws; a prescription for the Song state's cities, not a survey of what was built.

Used for: the compendium's rules for gates and towers (defenses)
