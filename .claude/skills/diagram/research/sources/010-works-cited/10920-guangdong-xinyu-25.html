guangdong-xinyu-25

Qu Dajun (屈大均), Guangdong xinyu (廣東新語, New Sayings of Guangdong), chapter 25, "Tree talk" (木語), the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E5%BB%A3%E6%9D%B1%E6%96%B0%E8%AA%9E/%E5%8D%B725)

What it is: The chapter on trees of a late-seventeenth-century description of Guangdong, with entries on the lychee and the longan and on how they were planted in the delta's fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how thickly lychee and longan stood when planted on raised mounds in a field, the nearest figure read for the fruit trees of a fruit dike. The mounds are in fields, not on pond dikes, so the spacing is a comparison, not a measurement of a dike.

Used for: twenty-odd lychee, or twice as many longan, to a mu of field, on silt mounds about two feet high (archetypes)
