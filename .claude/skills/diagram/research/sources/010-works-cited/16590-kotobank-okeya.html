kotobank 桶屋 ("cooper", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%A1%B6%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the cooper, among them the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry by Endo Motoo on how the trade grew from medieval guilds into the early-modern cooper who made and sold at home.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the early-modern cooper both made and sold in his own house, that coopers gathered in cooper streets in castle towns, and that some went round towns and villages mending hoops. A signed reference entry on Japan; it gives no workshop size.

Used for: the cooper working and selling at home, clustering in castle towns, and mending on the road (urban-features)
