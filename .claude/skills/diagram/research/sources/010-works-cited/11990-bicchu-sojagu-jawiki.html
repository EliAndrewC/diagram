What it is: The encyclopedia article on the sōja of Bitchū province in Sōja, Okayama, explaining why provinces set up a sōja near their provincial capital and how the town grew around it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a province keeping one principal shrine at its seat of government, with a town grown around it. Its limits: the sōja is an institution of the ancient provincial government, older than the castle towns; one shrine; no precinct area is given.

Used for: a provincial capital's principal shrine and where it stood (religion-and-death.html).
