hagi-meirinkan-guide

Hagi City, "Guide to the former Hagi domain school Meirinkan" (in Japanese; https://www.city.hagi.lg.jp/site/meiringakusha/annaizu.html)

What it is: Hagi city's guide to the surviving buildings of the Hagi (Choshu) domain school, among them the Yubikan, the school's fencing and spear hall, a nationally designated historic site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured size, floors and division of a surviving domain martial hall, the one attested form of a state dojo in this record. It is one hall of one large domain, built in the late Edo period as a school's martial wing, so it shows the state hall, not a private dojo or a small domain's hall; the guide is brief and names its size and rooms, not how the hall was used day to day.

Used for: the size and plan of a domain school's fencing and spear hall (buildings)
