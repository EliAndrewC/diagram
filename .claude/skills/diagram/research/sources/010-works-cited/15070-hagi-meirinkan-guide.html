hagi-meirinkan-guide

Hagi City, "Guide to the former Hagi domain school Meirinkan" (in Japanese; https://www.city.hagi.lg.jp/site/meiringakusha/annaizu.html)

What it is: Hagi city's guide to the surviving buildings of the Hagi (Choshu) domain school, among them the Yubikan, the school's fencing and spear hall, a nationally designated historic site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured size, floors and division of a surviving domain martial hall, the one attested form of a state dojo in this record. It is one hall of one large domain, the school's old fencing and spear halls moved and enlarged when the school moved to a new site in 1849, so it shows the state hall of the late Edo period, not a private dojo or a small domain's hall. It was also the domain's official hall for bouts with visiting swordsmen and spearmen from other domains, with viewing rooms for the lord, so it is likely larger and more formal than a hall for the retainers' own practice alone. The guide is brief: it gives the size, rooms and those two uses, not the daily routine.

Used for: the size and plan of a domain school's fencing and spear hall (buildings)
