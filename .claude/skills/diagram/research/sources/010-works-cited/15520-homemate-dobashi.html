homemate-dobashi

Homemate Research, 土橋 ("dobashi", title translated from the Japanese by this project), castle glossary (https://www.homemate-research-castle.com/useful/glossary/castle/2134101/)

What it is: A glossary entry on a commercial castle-information site defining the castle dobashi, the causeway left across a moat before an entrance, and naming examples at Edo and Osaka.

Why it applies, and its limits: It adds one thing the encyclopedia does not: that a causeway across a wet moat could hold the water at a different level on each side. A popular glossary with no references and no measurements.

Used for: the water held at two levels either side of a causeway (defenses)
