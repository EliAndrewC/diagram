kotobank-bunsui

kotobank 分水 ("Water division", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%88%86%E6%B0%B4-128545)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the reference-encyclopedia entries on 分水, among them the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia's entry and an excerpt from that encyclopedia's older edition, under its entry 分木 (bunboku), defining the graduated division stake.

Why it applies, and its limits: The bunboku excerpt says what the one fixed marker at a two-way village split was: a graduated stake at the division point, by whose height the water was divided, with a Shiga example. It is an excerpt of a dictionary entry, so it gives the device and one place, not how common it was or what it looked like beyond a stake.

Used for: the bunboku, a graduated stake at a two-way split (water)
