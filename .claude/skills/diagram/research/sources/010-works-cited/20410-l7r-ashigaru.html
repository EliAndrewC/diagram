l7r-ashigaru

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Ashigaru" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the section "Ashigaru": how many of the countryside's farmers are ashigaru - about a tenth - and so how much of the countryside has martial training.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: in the setting the ashigaru are peasants of the villages, a deliberate departure from historical Japan, where they were the lowest retainers of a domain. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; they do not say where an ashigaru lives, and the record reads from them only that the setting's capital has no foot soldiers' quarter to draw.

Used for: the setting's ashigaru as a share of its farmers, behind why a capital has no foot soldiers' quarter (cities/capitals)
