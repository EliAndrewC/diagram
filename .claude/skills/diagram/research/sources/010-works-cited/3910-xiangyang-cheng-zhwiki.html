What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the walled city of Xiangyang, Hubei, on the Han River's south bank; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the city's siting with water on three sides and the moat's 250 m at its widest point, which is the one-bank form this page draws; it does not say the moat was dug or fed from the river.

Used for: a walled city on one bank of its river (river cities)
