semboku-bukeyashiki

Semboku City, 武家屋敷 石黒家 ("the Ishiguro samurai residence", with the district's other open houses, title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.semboku.akita.jp/sightseeing/travel/details/01-bukeyashiki.html)

What it is: The city's tourism page on the samurai houses of Kakunodate open to visitors, one short paragraph each: their gates, fences, wells, gardens and the families that held them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says which surviving houses have their own well and what their fences and gates are, including the Iwahashi house, a mid-rank samurai's. It is a visitor page for one small castle town, describing houses as preserved today.

Used for: samurai houses' own wells and their fences (cities/government)
