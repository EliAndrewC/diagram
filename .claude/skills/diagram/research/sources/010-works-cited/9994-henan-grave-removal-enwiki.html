en.wikipedia Henan Grave Removal Project (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Grave_Removal_Project)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the 2012-2013 campaign in Henan province, on the north China plain, to level the graves scattered across its farmland and turn the ground back into fields, and on the protest that partly reversed it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the mid-field grave as a living custom of a Chinese farming plain on a vast scale - more than two million graves leveled - which is independent support for graves standing inside working fields. Its limits: it is a modern event reported in a tertiary summary; the two million is the provincial government's own announced total in a political campaign, not an independent count, and a total for a whole province, so it gives no density of graves in a field or a village's land and no size for a single grave; Henan is a dry-farming plain of wheat, not rice country; and it says nothing of how the graves were placed before the modern era.

Used for: that graves stood scattered across the farmland of the north China plain, in numbers large enough that the province leveled more than two million of them (fields)
