kotobank-kuchimai

Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten (Heibonsha, the entry by Oguchi Yujiro), Hyakka Jiten Mypedia (Heibonsha), Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (Shogakukan) and other works, s.v. 口米 ("kuchimai, the rice surcharge", translated), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%8F%A3%E7%B1%B3-484198)

What it is: The kotobank page for kuchimai, the small rice surcharge collected with the Edo land tax, carrying several dictionary and encyclopedia entries on what it was, its rates and what it paid for.

Why it applies, and its limits: The entries are modern reference works that cite no sources. They agree that the surcharge paid for the shogunal intendants' offices until 1725, when the shogunate took it and paid the offices' costs itself. They disagree on why: the one signed entry (Oguchi Yujiro's, in the Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten) blames the intendants' misuse of public money; the unsigned Mypedia entry says, in a single sentence, that the costs ran below the surcharge; the others give no reason, and none gives a figure for any office's costs. The 1725 order itself, which the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten names from the Tokugawa Kinreiko, has not been read here. It concerns the shogunate's own intendancies, not a daimyo's county offices.

Used for: what the surcharge paid for, the 1725 change, and the entry that says the costs ran below it (buildings)
