en.wikipedia "Mill race" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mill_race)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the mill race, the channel that leads water from a pond or weir to a wheel and away again.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the race is narrow and swift against the broad pond, the distinction this page draws between the ponded reach above a sluice and the race below it. It says so in one sentence with no citation; its examples are English and European watermills and its one substantial reference is a popular book on the Industrial Revolution, so it carries the physics of a race, not any East Asian mill or irrigation channel - applying it to a sluice on a paddy channel is this record's inference.

Used for: the race as the narrow swift member beside the broad pond
