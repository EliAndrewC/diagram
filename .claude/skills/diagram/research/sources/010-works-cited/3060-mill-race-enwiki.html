What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the mill race, the channel that leads water from a pond or weir to a wheel and away again.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the race is narrow and swift against the broad pond, the distinction this page draws between the ponded reach above a sluice and the race below it. A tertiary summary.

Used for: the race as the narrow swift member beside the broad pond
