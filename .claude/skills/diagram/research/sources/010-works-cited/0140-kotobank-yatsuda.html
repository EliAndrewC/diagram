What it is: The encyclopedia entry for 谷津田 / 谷地田 (yatsuda, the valley-bottom paddy) on kotobank, from Heibonsha's Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten, describing paddy lying in the low ground of valleys cut into a plateau: wetland, opened up long ago, and in many cases dependent on rainfall because such valleys are not well supplied with water sources.

Why it applies, and its limits: Where our maps run fields up a valley floor, this is the field type they are, and the entry gives both the setting and its character - old, wet, and often rain-fed rather than fed by a channel, which bears on whether we draw a supply ditch to it. It is a tertiary summary describing a landform-and-field type rather than measuring one, so we take the character of the ground from it and no dimension.
