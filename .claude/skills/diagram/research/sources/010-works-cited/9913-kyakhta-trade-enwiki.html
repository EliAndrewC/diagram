English Wikipedia, "Kyakhta trade" (in English; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyakhta_trade)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the trade between Russia and Qing China through Kyakhta, on the Mongolian border, from the Treaty of Kyakhta of 1727, including the founding of the Russian post and the Qing fort of Maimaicheng facing it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the other historical form of regular dealing across a land border: a post on each side of the line, a few hundred feet apart, with officials of both handling diplomacy and disputes. It is a tertiary article, and a weakly cited one: Wikipedia tags it as lacking inline citations and needing factual verification, it says it is a summary of Clifford M. Foust's monograph Muscovite and Mandarin (1969), and its passage on the two posts a few hundred feet apart, with officials on both sides, carries no citation, so that distance is the article's and not a checked figure (Foust is the closer source); the parties are the Russian and Qing empires in the eighteenth century, not domains or clans, and it describes no building where the two met.

Used for: Kyakhta and Maimaicheng as a post on each side of the border (buildings)
