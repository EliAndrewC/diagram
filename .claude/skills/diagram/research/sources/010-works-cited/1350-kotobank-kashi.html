kotobank-kashi

Kotobank, 河岸（かし） - 日本大百科全書 and other reference entries (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B2%B3%E5%B2%B8-459508)

What it is: The kotobank reference page for the kashi, the Edo-period river landing, carrying the entries of Japanese reference encyclopedias: the bank where cargo was landed, the markets that formed there, its standing as a town or village, and the kashi wholesaler who ran the landing for fees.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that the landing was a place of business run by its wholesalers rather than a public quay, which is how this page describes it. Its limit is the encyclopedia's summary form.

Used for: the kashi wholesaler's loading, storage and fees
