What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the hokora, the small wayside god-shelf below the shrine tier; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a hokora has no torii or only a very small one and stands inside the human sphere - at a settlement's entrance, a crossroads, the fields - which is the tier below the shrine on this page; it gives no count against shrines.

Used for: the hokora tier without a torii (religion and death)
