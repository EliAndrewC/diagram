What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the kosatsu, the Edo-period official notice board, its contents and where it stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the boards stood where traffic was frequent - at post stations and barriers - which is the traffic siting this page's board follows. A tertiary summary used for that siting. The article is a few paragraphs on two references, one of them another wiki, and it says what the Japanese article cited beside it says, so the two are not independent.
