qiaokou-hanzheng-street

硚口区人民政府 (Qiaokou District People's Government, Wuhan), 两江淌出一条街, 2017-08-28 (https://www.qiaokou.gov.cn/qkgk/hghh/201708/t20170828_117732.shtml)

What it is: A local-history article on the Wuhan district government's site on how Hanzheng Street came to be: Hankou's first street the river street along the wharves, then the streets stepping inland from it, with merchant boats moored stern to bow along the bank.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a Chinese city on a live river keeping its wharf on the bank and growing its commercial district up from it, which is the arrangement our river capital draws instead of a boat basin. Its limit is that it is a municipal history page rather than an urban-history study. Hankou was one of the empire's great river entrepots, not an ordinary provincial seat, so it shows the extreme of bankside commercial growth.

Used for: the river street first, the wharves in rows along the bank, and the streets stepping inland
