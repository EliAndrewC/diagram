ja.wikipedia 関宿 ("Seki-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%96%A2%E5%AE%BF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Seki, a post station of the Tokaido in Mie and a preservation district, describing its surviving townscape of plastered and exposed-post machiya of two stories, a half-story and one story.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that one street of a busy highway town mixed heights rather than keeping one. It is a tertiary encyclopedia article (a stub), and its sentence on the mix of heights cites a 2010 guidebook. It describes the townscape preserved today rather than a dated Edo-period survey: most of Seki's houses were built from the late Edo period through Meiji, and only about half of the district's buildings are Edo or Meiji, the rest reaching into the prewar Showa era. So the mix includes rebuilding after the Edo period and is not a picture of an Edo street. It covers one town only, so it says nothing about the share of each height elsewhere.

Used for: one post town's mix of two-story, half-story and one-story houses (towns)
