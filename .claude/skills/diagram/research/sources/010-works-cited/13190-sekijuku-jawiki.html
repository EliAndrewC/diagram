ja.wikipedia 関宿 ("Seki-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%96%A2%E5%AE%BF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Seki, a post station of the Tokaido in Mie and a preservation district, describing its surviving townscape of plastered and exposed-post machiya of two stories, a half-story and one story.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that one street of a busy highway town mixed heights rather than keeping one. It describes the townscape preserved today rather than a dated Edo-period survey, and one town only, so it says nothing about the share of each height elsewhere.

Used for: one post town's mix of two-story, half-story and one-story houses (towns)
