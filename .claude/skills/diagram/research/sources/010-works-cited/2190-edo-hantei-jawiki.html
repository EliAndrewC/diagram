What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Edo domain residences, the upper, middle and lower estates each daimyo kept in the shogunal capital under alternate attendance: their siting relative to the castle, their sizes and their uses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the lower residences were large garden estates in the outskirts used as villas and fire refuges while the working residence stood nearest the castle, which is the pattern of roomier estates outside this page draws. A tertiary summary; it describes daimyo under alternate attendance in Edo, an institution a domain seat's own samurai did not have. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources.
