kotobank-yaba

Kotobank, 矢場 ("archery ground", title translated from the Japanese by this project), gathering entries from Shogakukan's Nipponica encyclopedia and its dictionaries (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%9F%A2%E5%A0%B4-648895)

What it is: A dictionary aggregator's page for the word; the Nipponica encyclopedia's entry gives the samurai archery ground's fixed length and breadth, its target mound, and where such grounds were set.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that sizes a samurai archery ground and says where it stood. The figures are the standard a samurai house set, not a survey of real ranges; the entry is short and names no town.

Used for: the archery ground's size and its places (cities/government)
