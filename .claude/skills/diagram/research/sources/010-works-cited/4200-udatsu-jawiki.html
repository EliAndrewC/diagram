udatsu-jawiki

ja.wikipedia うだつ (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/うだつ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the udatsu, the raised firewall at the roof boundary between adjoining townhouses; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests machiya built adjoining and continuously with each house's gable raised into a firewall against its neighbor, which is the continuous frontage this page draws; it makes the units separate houses, not a shared wall.

Used for: adjoining machiya and the firewall between them (fabric)
