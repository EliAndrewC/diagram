Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Kanto Regional Development Bureau, Yokohama National Highway Office, Tokaido Q&A, 宿場とはどういうところだったのでしょう？ ("What kind of place was a post station?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/04_qa/index2/a0201.htm)

What it is: A question-and-answer page in a national highway office's public guide to the Tokaido, explaining what a post station was for and counting one: Oiso in 1803, its houses, how many lined the highway on strip lots, and its inns, transport offices and trades.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a real count for one post town on the main highway - how much of the town stood on the road and how much did not - from an 1803 survey. It is one town, on the coast, where fishermen and farmers made up most of the households; a town inland or off the Tokaido would differ, and the page does not say where the houses off the highway stood.

Used for: how many of a post town's houses lined its highway, and its count of inns (ways)
