What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Himeji domain: its founding in 1600 when Ikeda Terumasa was granted the whole of Harima at 520,000 koku, and its later reduction to 150,000.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the assessed yield of the domain whose castle is one of this page's scale anchors, and it dates that figure to the Ikeda enfeoffment so the anchor is not read as the domain's later size. A tertiary summary used for the figure.
