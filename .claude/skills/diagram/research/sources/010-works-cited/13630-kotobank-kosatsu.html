kotobank-kosatsu

Kotobank, 高札 ("kosatsu, the notice board", title translated from the Japanese by this project), quoting Heibonsha's Sekai Daihyakka Jiten (old edition) among other dictionaries (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%AB%98%E6%9C%AD-64337)

What it is: The Kotobank dictionary page for 高札, which gathers the entries of several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias; the Heibonsha encyclopedia's entry says where the boards were hung and how their sites were enclosed and guarded.

Why it applies, and its limits: A signed encyclopedia entry by a historian, general across Edo-period Japan. It names the enclosures - a palisade, a stone wall, a turf bank - as alternatives without saying which was usual or giving any size.

Used for: the enclosure of a notice-board site (urban-features)
