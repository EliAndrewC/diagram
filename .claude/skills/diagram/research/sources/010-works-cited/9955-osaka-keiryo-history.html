osaka-keiryo-history

Osaka Prefecture, "計量の歴史" (the history of weighing and measuring), a page of the prefecture's measurement-verification office (in Japanese; https://www.pref.osaka.lg.jp/o110120/keiryo/rekisi2023/index.html)

What it is: A prefectural government's general-audience history of weights and measures, from the balance of the ancient world to the steelyard and the scales of Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the balance and the steelyard were each used for in Japan - the balance for valuables, the steelyard for food and daily goods - from which we infer what a weighing floor for charcoal used; the page never names charcoal, and gives no period for the two uses within Japan. It is a popular summary without references; its account of the balance's weight is set in the ancient world, and its account of the two tools' uses is hedged ("it seems"). Updated 2024.

Used for: the balance's precision and weight, and the balance for valuables against the steelyard for everyday goods (urban features)
