jawiki-goshi

Japanese Wikipedia, "合祀" (gōshi, joint enshrinement) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%90%88%E7%A5%80)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on gōshi, the moving of one shrine's kami into another shrine, and the three ways it was done.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the choice this record needs - a second kami taken into the main hall, or given its own small shrine in the precinct - but it is a stub flagged as short of sources, its three-way division resting on a library reference-desk answer, and much of what it says concerns the forced shrine mergers of the Meiji era, long after the period the maps draw.

Used for: that a second kami could be enshrined in the main hall or given its own shrine in the precinct (religion-and-death).
