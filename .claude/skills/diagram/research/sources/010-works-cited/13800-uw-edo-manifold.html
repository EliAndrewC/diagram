University of Washington, Manifold edition of "Edo" (an open textbook section on the city of Edo) (https://uw.manifoldapp.org/read/edo/section/4ccada16-da7e-4863-94cd-62966d83567e)

What it is: A copy, on the University of Washington's Manifold platform and with no author, date or credit shown, of the English Wikipedia article "Edo" (its sections and wording match the article's), describing the layout of Edo - its districts, the daimyo residences and the city's edges - including the path and canal along the northern edge from the Sumida to the Yoshiwara, near which Danzaemon, head of the eta, lived.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the outcaste head's seat at the city's northern edge beside the river, in English. It is a tertiary source: Wikipedia's article, copied, and the passage cites nothing even on Wikipedia, so it carries the general layout and not a checked fact. It puts Danzaemon only "nearby" the path and canal to the Yoshiwara (the path starts at the Sumida bank), not by the river itself. It describes one city, the capital, and gives no reason for the placing.

Used for: Edo's outcaste head living at the northern edge of the city by the river (urban-features)
