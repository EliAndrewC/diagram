University of Washington, Manifold edition of "Edo" (an open textbook section on the city of Edo) (https://uw.manifoldapp.org/read/edo/section/4ccada16-da7e-4863-94cd-62966d83567e)

What it is: A section of an open university text describing the layout of Edo - its districts, the daimyo residences and the city's edges - including the path and canal along the northern edge from the Sumida to the Yoshiwara, near which Danzaemon, head of the eta, lived.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the outcaste head's seat at the city's northern edge beside the river, in English. It is a teaching text, drawn from secondary scholarship and without citations at the passage; it describes one city, the capital, and gives no reason for the placing.

Used for: Edo's outcaste head living at the northern edge of the city by the river (urban-features)
