北京晚报 (Beijing Evening News), 明清各大"部委"都在哪儿办公? ("Where did the great ministries of the Ming and Qing have their offices?", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s5def7f86e4b0fe93cc1ed226.html)

What it is: A 2019 local-history feature by Yang Zheng in the Beijing Evening News on the Ming and Qing ministries' compounds outside Tiananmen: when each was built, the corridor that lined the approach, and what became of them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the capital's ministry compounds and says the Ministry of Rites was built first on the model of its Nanjing predecessor, with a storehouse of its own. It is a newspaper feature; it gives no dimensions for any compound.

Used for: the capital's ministries as compounds of their own, built one by one either side of the approach (cities/government)
