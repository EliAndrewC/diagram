zh.wikipedia 寿县城墙 ("the Shou county wall", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%AF%BF%E5%8E%BF%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the surviving wall of Shou county in Anhui, rebuilt in the Southern Song and kept up against the Huai river's floods, naming its four gates and saying their barbicans survive.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests a walled town with four gates, each with a barbican. It is a stub resting on two tourism pages; it gives no dimensions and does not date the barbicans. The town was no ordinary county seat: the article calls it an important military stronghold, on the Huai, which was the Song-Jin frontier when the wall was rebuilt, and the page's own list of Anhui walls files it as the wall of Shouzhou, a department seat under Fengyang prefecture in the Qing. Its four barbicans therefore speak for a fortified frontier seat above county rank, and for an ordinary county seat's wall only loosely.

Used for: four gates, and a barbican at each (defenses)
