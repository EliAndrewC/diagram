zh.wikipedia 寿县城墙 ("the Shou county wall", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%AF%BF%E5%8E%BF%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the surviving wall of Shou county in Anhui, rebuilt in the Southern Song and kept up against the Huai river's floods, naming its four gates and saying their barbicans survive.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests a walled town with four gates, each with a barbican. It is a stub resting on two tourism pages; it gives no dimensions, and it does not say what rank of seat the town was when its wall and barbicans were built, so it speaks for a county-scale wall only loosely.

Used for: four gates, and a barbican at each (defenses)
