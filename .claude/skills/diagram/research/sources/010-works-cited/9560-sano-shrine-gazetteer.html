sano-shrine-gazetteer

kyonsight.com, 安蘇郡 (Sano) shrine compilation, citing 『下野神社沿革誌』 (1902) and 『栃木県神社誌』 (1964) (https://kyonsight.com/jt/sano/0sano.html)

What it is: A private compilation transcribing, entry by entry, two prefectural shrine records for the Sano district of Tochigi, with each shrine's buildings and precinct area.

Why it applies, and its limits: Fifty village shrines give a second district's band, and two entries give buildings and precinct together. Its limits: an amateur transcription of records of 1902 and 1964, not premodern; a wooded district whose precincts run larger; the medians and built shares are this project's arithmetic.

Used for: village-shrine precinct areas and built share, Tochigi (religion-and-death.html).
