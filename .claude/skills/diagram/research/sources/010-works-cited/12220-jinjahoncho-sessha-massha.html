jinjahoncho-sessha-massha

Association of Shinto Shrines (神社本庁), 「Sessha and massha, the subsidiary shrines」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「摂社・末社」) (in Japanese; https://www.jinjahoncho.or.jp/omairi/smallkeidai/)

What it is: A page of the Association of Shinto Shrines, the national association to which most (not all) Shinto shrines belong, explaining the small shrines found in a shrine's precinct.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says one sometimes sees small shrines in a precinct besides the main hall, and that the formal standard dividing the two classes of them was set at the high-ranking government shrines before the war. That standard belongs to the Meiji government's shrine ranking (1871-1945), which came after the Edo period this setting is modeled on, so the formal division is a modern rule; the page's only older anchor is Ise, whose classes it takes from the Engishiki shrine register (905) and the Enryaku ritual records (804). It is a present-day explainer that cites nothing; it does not say how often an ordinary or village shrine had one, nor how old the custom of small precinct shrines is.

Used for: subsidiary shrines in a precinct (religion-and-death.html).
