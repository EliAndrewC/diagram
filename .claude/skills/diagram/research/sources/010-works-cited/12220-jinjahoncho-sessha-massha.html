jinjahoncho-sessha-massha

Association of Shinto Shrines (神社本庁), 「Sessha and massha, the subsidiary shrines」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「摂社・末社」) (in Japanese; https://www.jinjahoncho.or.jp/omairi/smallkeidai/)

What it is: A page of the national body of Shinto shrines explaining the small shrines found in a shrine's precinct.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says one sometimes sees small shrines in a precinct besides the main hall, and that the formal standard dividing the two classes of them was set at the high-ranking government shrines before the war. It is a present-day explainer; it does not say how often an ordinary or village shrine had one.

Used for: subsidiary shrines in a precinct (religion-and-death.html).
