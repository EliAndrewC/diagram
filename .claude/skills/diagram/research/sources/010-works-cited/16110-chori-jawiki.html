chori-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 長吏 (賎民) ("chori, the outcaste status", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%95%B7%E5%90%8F_(%E8%B3%8E%E6%B0%91))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on chori, the name the outcaste status went by in eastern Japan, with a list of what the word meant region by region, including the castle towns of Shinano and of the Shonai domain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page that places named castle towns' quarters on the ground - Matsumoto's at the castle town's entrances in 1588, Ueda's likewise in 1649 - and gives their local name, machi-hanare. It is an encyclopedia article that cites no page numbers for these lines, it speaks of central and northern Honshu only, and it gives no household counts.

Used for: the quarters of the Shinano castle towns at their entrances, their duties, and the name machi-hanare (urban-features, cities/fabric)
