en.wikipedia "Danka system" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danka_system)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the danka system, the Edo-period affiliation of every household to a Buddhist temple that buries it and that it supports; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the relation between a temple and its patron households and the sects a household might be registered to, which is the patronage this page's temple count rests on; it says nothing of how many temples a place has or how the number scales; Japan only, with nothing on the Chinese half of the setting's ancestry, and the bond it describes was made compulsory in 1638 as a Tokugawa registration apparatus rather than being a general premodern arrangement.

Used for: each temple as a separate foundation with its own patrons (religion and death)
