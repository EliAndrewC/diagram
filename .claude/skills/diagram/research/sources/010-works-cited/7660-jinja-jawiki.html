ja.wikipedia 神社 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%A5%9E%E7%A4%BE) - 「Traditionally, small shrines often had no dedicated priest; the parishioners managed them in rotation, and the rites were performed by the parishioners themselves or by a priest called in from outside.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「従来、小規模な神社には専属の神職がいない場合が多く、氏子が交替で管理し、氏子自らないし外部から神職を呼んで祭祀を行った。」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Shinto shrine: its precinct, its buildings and its priesthood.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for two things - that small shrines traditionally had no dedicated priest and were kept by the parishioners in rotation, and the parts of a shrine precinct (the torii marking the sacred ground, the temizuya beside the approach, the shamusho). A tertiary summary; "traditionally" is its own word and carries no date.

Used for: small shrines kept by parishioners in rotation; the parts of a precinct (religion-and-death.html).
