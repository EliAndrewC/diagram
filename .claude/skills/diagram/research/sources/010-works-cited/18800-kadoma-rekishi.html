kadoma-rekishi

Kadoma City, Kadoma History Museum, "History" (in Japanese; https://www.city.kadoma.osaka.jp/soshiki/shiminbunkabu/shogaigakushuka/4/2/3/2828.html)

What it is: The city of Kadoma's short history of its own area (Osaka prefecture), written by its history museum for a general audience, running from the prehistoric sites through the Mamuta embankment, the medieval estates and the Edo-period villages to the modern city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a local museum's modern summary, not a period record, and dates the growth of lotus-root growing only to "the late Edo period", with no area, no village and no source named. It is useful as the local historians' own dating of lotus-root growing becoming active on low wet ground near Osaka before the Meiji era, named there as one late-Edo cash crop beside rapeseed and cotton. The same page puts the peak of lotus growing, and the fame of "Kawachi lotus root", in the Taishō period (1912-26), after the Kaga and Bitchū lotus varieties were brought in. So the lotus district known today is a 20th-century one, and the late-Edo growing was an earlier, smaller stage; how much of the villages' land the lotus took in either period is not on it. Updated 2019.

Used for: lotus-root growing becoming active at Kadoma in the late Edo period, in water-rich farm villages on low wet ground (Field archetypes)
