minka-en-yashikigami

minka-en.com, 屋敷神 (https://minka-en.com/shisetsu/shisetsu_7/) - 「In one corner of the homestead, and especially at the kimon (鬼門, the northeast corner), there is a small shrine (hokora, 祠) of stone or of wood or with a straw-thatched roof, and ...」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷の一隅、特に鬼門（きもん）（北東隅）に、石や木、わら屋根の祠（ほこら）があり」)

What it is: The official site of the Fukushima City Minka-en, a municipal open-air museum of relocated folk houses; the page is the museum's own guide entry for its yashikigami exhibit, describing the household shrine as it was kept - a small hokora of stone, wood or thatch at a corner of the plot, the northeast corner especially.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the form and the customary corner of the household shrine in a museum's own words, which is what the shrine glyph on our homesteads and its corner placement are drawn from. Its limits are that it is a museum caption about its own exhibited example, not a survey, with no frequency; and that the museum is in Fukushima, so it speaks for Tohoku practice - one more northern source on a page that leans on them.

Used for: the household shrine in a corner of the yard, especially the NE (鬼門) corner, as a small stone or wooden shrine.
