en.wikipedia "Mu (land)" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_(land))

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the mu, the Chinese unit of land area, with its statutory values by period; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the 1915 mu of 614.4 m2 fixed on the Qing definitions and the 1930 metric mu of 666 2/3 m2 (0.164 acre), which convert this page's figures. It gives no Ming value and nothing about parcel sizes, so it supports nothing about holdings; the two values it gives are twentieth-century republican statutes, and the mu varied by period and locality, which bears on every conversion made with it.

Used for: the size of a mu (fields)
