大和郡山市観光協会 (Yamatokoriyama Tourism Association), 箱本館紺屋 (https://www.yk-kankou.jp/spotDetail51.html)

What it is: The Yamatokoriyama tourism association's page on the Hakomotokan museum in the town's Konya-machi, the dyers' street of the castle town, with the channel down the middle of the street fed from the castle moat in which the dyed cloth and thread were rinsed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a castle town's dyers' ward standing on water led from the castle moat, the siting our capital's dye works copy. Its limit is that it is a tourism page and gives no founding date.

Used for: the dyers' street on a channel off the castle moat
