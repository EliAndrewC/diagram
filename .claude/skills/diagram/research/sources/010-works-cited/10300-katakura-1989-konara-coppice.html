katakura-1989-konara-coppice

Katakura Masayuki and Okumura Shunsuke, 「Coppice regeneration of konara secondary forest and fertilizing of mature stands」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「コナラ二次林の萌芽更新と成木林肥培」), Bulletin of the Nagano Prefectural Forestry Research Center 5 (1989) (https://www.pref.nagano.lg.jp/ringyosogo/seika/kenkyu/ikurin/documents/iku-05-1.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural forestry research report on felling trials in konara secondary woods at two sites in Nagano, made to regrow shiitake logs faster, counting and measuring the shoots that rose from each cut stump each autumn for three years after felling; its second half is a fertilizer trial on mature stands.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures how many stems a konara stump sends up after cutting, which is why a worked fuel wood stood as clumps of thin stems. It is a modern trial (felled 1982, counted 1983-85) on plots of about 0.05 ha, ten stumps to a plot, and some plots were thinned to a set number of shoots, weeded with herbicide or given chemical fertilizer; only its untreated plots stand for a worked wood. The woods it felled were old charcoal and fuel woods left uncut since the fuel change of the 1960s and grown to 26-31 years, older than a worked wood was cut. Its count runs only three years after felling (about 33 shoots a stump the first autumn, about 14 by the third), so it does not show how many stems a stump still carried when it was cut again. Its stand table gives the density of those overgrown woods, not of a worked one, so no stand density is taken from it.

Used for: the number of shoots a cut konara stump sends up (vegetation)
