katakura-1989-konara-coppice

Katakura Masayuki and Okumura Shunsuke, 「Coppice regeneration of konara secondary forest and fertilizing of mature stands」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「コナラ二次林の萌芽更新と成木林肥培」), Bulletin of the Nagano Prefectural Forestry Research Center 5 (1989) (https://www.pref.nagano.lg.jp/ringyosogo/seika/kenkyu/ikurin/documents/iku-05-1.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural research report on felling trials in konara woods in Nagano, counting the shoots that rose from each cut stump over the following years.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures how many stems a konara stump sends up after cutting, which is why a worked fuel wood stood as clumps of thin stems. It is a modern trial on a few plots, and its stand tables do not read cleanly in the published file, so no stand density is taken from it.

Used for: the number of shoots a cut konara stump sends up (vegetation)
