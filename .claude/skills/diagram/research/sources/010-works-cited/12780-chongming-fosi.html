chongming-fosi

Chongming District People's Government, Shanghai, 崇明的佛寺 ("Chongming's Buddhist temples", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://www.shcm.gov.cn/zjcm/006008/20210423/3b8c208d-d6cd-4fe5-ae35-e724fd758922.html)

What it is: A local-government history of the Buddhist temples of Chongming island, drawn from the old county gazetteers, giving for several temples their founding, their place against the county seat and their size.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sizes a late-Ming temple inside a real county seat - its rooms, floor area and site - which is the tier and the kind of temple the maps draw. It is a modern summary of the gazetteers rather than the gazetteer itself, it converts one reign year to the wrong Western year (1625 for Tianqi 4, which is 1624), and Chongming is one island county.

Used for: how big a county seat's Buddhist temple was and what it held (religion-and-death)
