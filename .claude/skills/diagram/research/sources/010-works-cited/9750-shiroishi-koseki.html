shiroishi-koseki

Shiroishi City Culture and Sports Promotion Foundation, 武家屋敷 (The samurai residence), 2024-07-30 (in Japanese; https://www.shiro-f.jp/shiroishijo/residence)

What it is: The page of the foundation that runs Shiroishi castle on the old Koseki house, a middle-rank retainer's house of the castle town of Shiroishi (Miyagi prefecture), a prefectural cultural property restored after 1991, describing its gate, walls, approach and rooms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out, from a surviving house, the approach of a house with no genkan: the street gate leading along a wall to the kitchen entrance, and a middle gate in the wall leading guests through a walled garden path to the zashiki's veranda. Its limits are that it is one house, of a retainer of the Katakura, the lords of Shiroishi under the Date, rather than of a daimyo's own vassal; that it is thatched and was, in the page's own words, a transitional form built on a farmhouse base; and that its approach is that of a private house of middle rank, not of an office or a posting.

Used for: a middle-rank house with no shikidai genkan, entered by a middle gate and a walled garden path to the zashiki's veranda (buildings)
