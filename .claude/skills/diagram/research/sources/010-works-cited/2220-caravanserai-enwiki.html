en.wikipedia "Caravanserai" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravanserai)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the caravanserai, the roadside inn of the Silk Road and the Islamic world, with its courtyard, its well or fountain, and its stabling for pack animals.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that whole trains were lodged and watered from a courtyard well or fountain, the relay-watering pattern this page's livery yard follows. A tertiary summary of a different region's tradition standing in for the pattern; it makes no comparison with multiplying basins.

Used for: watering a whole train from one courtyard well
