ja.wikipedia 榑木 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/榑木)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kureki, split timber, and the Edo-period upland villages that paid their land tax in it; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests a mountain district's tax arriving as a local good in place of rice and later as cash, which is the upland tax this page describes; it draws no consequence for the storehouse; one upland district of Shinano, whose shift to cash had a local cause in exhausted hills and falling demand.

Used for: the upland tax in timber and cash (buildings)
