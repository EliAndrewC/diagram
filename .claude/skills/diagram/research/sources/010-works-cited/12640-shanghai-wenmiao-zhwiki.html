zh.wikipedia 上海文庙 ("the Shanghai Confucian temple", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/上海文庙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Confucian temple of Shanghai county, rebuilt in 1855 inside the county seat's west gate, with its two axes of temple and school and the size of its main hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a county-tier Confucian temple with a measured main hall and a stated place inside the wall. The complex is a late-Qing rebuilding, later restored, and one county's temple is one example.

Used for: where a county's Confucian temple stood and how big its hall was (religion-and-death)
