sento-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 銭湯 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%8A%AD%E6%B9%AF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the 銭湯, the public bathhouse, covering the institution and how many of them a city carried, including more than 600 in Edo during the Bunka era of 1804 to 1818, when its population stood at roughly one to 1.2 million.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one number the record calls its cleanest case for scaling a service by population - more than six hundred bathhouses in Edo in the early nineteenth century - which is what sets how many bathhouses a city of a given size carries. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary, and the sentence we take carries no citation in the article and is written as reported tradition ("it is said there were") rather than as a sourced count; and that Edo was the largest city in Japan at its height, so the step down to a provincial city is ours.

Used for: 「In the Bunka era (1804-1818), across the whole of Edo, whose population was about 1,000,000 to 1,200,000, there were more than 600 bathhouses (sento).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「文化年間（1804〜1818年）には、人口約100万〜120万人の江戸全体で600軒以上の銭湯があった」) - the record's "523 in 1810" is a search-summary figure
