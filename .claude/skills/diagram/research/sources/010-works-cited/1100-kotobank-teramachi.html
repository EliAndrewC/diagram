Kotobank, 寺町 - 改訂新版 世界大百科事典, デジタル大辞泉 and ブリタニカ国際大百科事典 entries (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%AF%BA%E7%94%BA-101789)

What it is: The kotobank reference page for the teramachi, carrying side by side the entries of seven Japanese reference works, three of which are quoted here, on the temple district: its collective, continuous placement at the key points of a castle town's perimeter, and the military value of its large buildings and wide precincts in an emergency.

Why it applies, and its limits: Standard reference works on why a castle town's temples stood together at its edge, the rule our temple rim follows. Its limit is the encyclopedia's: it summarizes the planning intent without citing the town plans it rests on.

Used for: the collective placement of temples at the perimeter's key points for their military value
