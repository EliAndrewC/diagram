"The inhabitants who keep the kabata life", Mizu no Bunka (Water Culture) no. 60, Mizkan Water Culture Center (https://www.mizu.gr.jp/kikanshi/no60/06.html)

What it is: A magazine feature from a corporate water-culture research center, reporting from Harie on how households live with the channels and their kabata.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one source read that puts the channel physically against the dwellings - the channel that flows alongside the house - which is the relation our question is about. It is REPORTAGE, not scholarship: a journalist's visit, undated as to the arrangement's age, and it should carry no weight beyond describing what a visitor sees today.

Used for: the channel running alongside the house
