bunka-kakunodate

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, "仙北市角館" [Kakunodate, Semboku city], Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/196970)

What it is: The national cultural heritage database's entry for Kakunodate's samurai district, selected as a preservation district in 1976, with a short description of it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the district follows the land division of the domain era and that its houses stand on vast lots, so the roominess of Kakunodate's lots is a premodern one. It gives no area for any lot and no date within the Edo period. "Vast" is the heritage agency's own word today, not a measurement, and it does not say what the lots are vast compared with. The 6.9-hectare district is the part of the samurai quarter that survived and was chosen for preservation, so it does not show that every samurai lot in the town was as roomy. And Kakunodate was a small northern castle town of a branch of the Satake house.

Used for: that a small castle town's samurai lots were vast in the Edo period (The government quarter)
