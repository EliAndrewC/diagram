What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Odawara Castle, the Hojo seat whose outer enclosure, the sogamae, took in the whole town within nine kilometers of rampart and dry moat running from Mount Hachiman to the sea.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the length and extent of the largest town enclosure of its age, the exemplar behind our capital's single rampart around the whole population. It is a tertiary summary used for those two facts, and the article carries a notice asking for its sources; the nine-kilometer sentence itself is uncited, which matters because a map is scaled against it.

Used for: the nine-kilometer sogamae from Mount Hachiman to the sea
