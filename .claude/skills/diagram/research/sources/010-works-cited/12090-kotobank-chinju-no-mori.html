kotobank-chinju-no-mori

Tsutsui Michio and Hashimoto Yoshiyuki, "鎮守の森" ("chinju no mori", the tutelary shrine's wood), in the revised edition of the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia (改訂新版 世界大百科事典), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%8E%AE%E5%AE%88%E3%81%AE%E6%A3%AE-569966)

What it is: A signed entry in Japan's standard general encyclopedia, reproduced on the Kotobank dictionary site, defining the wood that stands in a tutelary shrine's precinct.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in general terms where a Japanese village's tutelary shrine stands - within the village or at its edge - and that the shrine ground is where the village gathers for its main observances. It is a modern encyclopedia's summary, gives no date or region, and names both sites without saying which was commoner or why a village chose one.

Used for: where a Japanese village's tutelary shrine stands (religion-and-death.html).
