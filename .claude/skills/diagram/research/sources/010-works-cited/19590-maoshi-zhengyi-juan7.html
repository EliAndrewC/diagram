毛詩正義 卷七 ("The Correct Meaning of the Mao Odes, Book Seven", title translated from the Chinese by this project), Chinese Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E6%AF%9B%E8%A9%A9%E6%AD%A3%E7%BE%A9/%E5%8D%B7%E4%B8%83)

What it is: A Wikisource transcription of the seventh book of the Maoshi zhengyi, which prints the poems of the Airs of Chen and neighboring states from the Book of Songs with the Mao commentary, Zheng Xuan's notes and a subcommentary headed 正義 (the correct meaning); among them the poem 澤陂, "The marsh's embankment", on the cattail and lotus by the bank of a marsh.

Why it applies, and its limits: The poem places cattail and lotus at the embankment of a marsh, and the commentary glosses the embankment as the bank that holds back the marsh's water and says the two plants grow inside it, not on the bank itself - an old Chinese reading of where water plants stand against a bank. The page names no author or date for the subcommentary (it is traditionally Kong Yingda's, of the Tang dynasty, which the page does not say); the water is a marsh with a bank, not a farm reservoir; the poem is a love song whose plants are images, not a description of a place; and it says nothing about Japan.

Used for: cattail and lotus inside a marsh's embankment, not on the bank itself (water)
