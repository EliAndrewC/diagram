ja.wikipedia 御旅所 ("otabisho, the resting place of a shrine's procession", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/御旅所)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia stub on the otabisho, the place where a shrine's portable shrine rests or stays the night during its festival procession, or which the procession sets out to reach.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the one piece of ground a Japanese shrine festival keeps outside the shrine, and says what it may be - a building of its own, a temporary altar, or a parish representative's house. It is a short article flagged as needing sources, with no dates and no size.

Used for: what a festival keeps on the ground in a town (towns)
