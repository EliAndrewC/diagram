Matsusaka City, 旧長谷川治郎兵衛家 ("The former house of Hasegawa Jirobei", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.matsusaka.mie.jp/site/kanko/kyuhasegawajirobeeke.html)

What it is: The city's visitor page for the Hasegawa house, walking the reader from the front storehouse through the house to the four storehouses at the back, the drain behind them, and the garden beyond.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places each storehouse on the lot and names the lot's back edge as the ward boundary, which the designation record does not. It is a visitor's guide describing the house as it stands today, with buildings of several periods up to the Taisho era; it gives no date for any storehouse beyond calling the Ōkura the oldest. It describes one of Matsusaka's wealthiest merchant houses, whose grounds the page says were built up by repeatedly buying neighbouring lots, so where its storehouses stand shows one large merged lot and not an ordinary town lot. The garden beyond the drain lies across the town boundary, on the site of a Kishū domain magistrate's office that the family bought in the early Meiji years, so the house reaching past the drain is a Meiji change and not the Edo lot.

Used for: where the Hasegawa storehouses stand, and the drain at the lot's back (cities/fabric.html)
