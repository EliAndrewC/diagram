What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on irrigation, its methods and history in Japan, including plot-to-plot watering.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines plot-to-plot irrigation, the cascade from paddy to paddy that lets interior plots on these maps stand without a ditch of their own. A tertiary summary used for the term.
