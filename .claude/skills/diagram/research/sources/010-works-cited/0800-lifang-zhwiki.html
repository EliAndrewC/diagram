zh.wikipedia 里坊制 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%87%8C%E5%9D%8A%E5%88%B6)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the lifang system, the walled and gated residential wards of the Chinese city from the Han to the Tang: how the wards were closed at night, how the Song did away with the wards, the markets and the curfew together, and that the system did not return after the Song and Yuan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the end of the walled ward, which is why the Chinese half of our block-gate argument turns on palings across streets rather than ward walls. It is a tertiary summary used for the dating, with the essayist Wu Gou's account of the same change beside it.

Used for: the Song's abolition of the walled ward and the night curfew, and the system's disappearance after the Song and Yuan
