miyashiro-kumehara-1698

宮代町 (Miyashiro town, Saitama), 文化財調査報告書『岡安家文書』関係史料集 ("Source collection for the cultural-property survey report on the Okayasu family papers", title translated from the Japanese by this project), chapter 3, document 1: 元禄十一年 久米原村年貢割付状 (the tax assessment of Kumehara village for 1698) (in Japanese; https://www.town.miyashiro.lg.jp/cmsfiles/contents/0000002/2922/okayasukebunsyo.pdf)

What it is: A town's printed transcription of a village headman's family papers; its first document is the lord's tax assessment of 1698 for Kumehara (signed 細六郎兵; a later note in the same collection names the village the fief of the bannerman Hosoi, not shogunal land under an intendant), a village of the Hyakken district of Saitama county, listing each grade of paddy, dry field and house land by area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the land of a small village of the Saitama plain grade by grade - about 8 cho of paddy against about 11 of dry field and 0.6 of house land - and the assessments printed with it deduct for flood loss, so its fields took water. Its limits: it is one village (the collection's assessments for 1699-1702 repeat the same areas, so they are the register's standing figures, not one year's measure); the page names no landform, and calling the village lowland is our inference - the town's own page puts Miyashiro on the northeast edge of the Omiya terrace (8-11 m) with back marsh and a natural levee beside it, so its dry fields may be terrace fields rather than dry land within a lowland; it says nothing of a row or of where the dry fields lay; the Kanto plain carried unusually large shares of dry field, so this is one Kanto village's share, not a rice village's norm in western Japan or southern China; the areas are the tax register's; and the transcription prints vertical text that a reader must follow column by column.

Used for: the share of dry field in a lowland paddy village (homesteads)
