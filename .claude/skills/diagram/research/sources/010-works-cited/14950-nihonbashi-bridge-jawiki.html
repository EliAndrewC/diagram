ja.wikipedia 日本橋 (東京都中央区の橋) ("Nihonbashi, the bridge in Chuo ward, Tokyo", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/日本橋_(東京都中央区の橋))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Nihonbashi, the bridge in Edo from which the Five Highways were measured, with its history from the first timber bridge of 1603 to the present stone arch, quoting an early seventeenth-century account of the timber bridge's length and width.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dimensions of the most important timber bridge of the shogun's capital, which sets the upper bound for a bridge a town might carry. It is the exceptional bridge of the largest city, not a town's; the figure is one early account quoted in the article.

Used for: the length and width of Edo's chief timber bridge, as an upper bound (ways)
