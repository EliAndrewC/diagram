What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Senju-juku, the first post station north of Edo on the Nikko and Oshu highways: its distance from Nihonbashi, its bridge over the Arakawa, and the Kozukappara execution ground in the town just south of it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the northern execution ground about two ri, five miles, out along the highway, which is the distance behind this page's rule that a ground hugs a road rather than a gate. A tertiary summary used for the distance and the placement. The two ri is the page's distance to the post town; that the ground in the town just south of it stood about as far out is this page's reading of two sentences.

Used for: Kozukappara two ri out along the highway
