What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kokucho time bell, Edo's first and principal time bell: its founding, its public funding and the bell fee levied on the blocks within earshot, and the list of the city's other time bells.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the bell was run at public expense on a fee levied from the roughly four hundred blocks that could hear it, and lists the city's other time bells, all but this one at temples, which is the earshot anchor and the temple-bell pattern this page uses. A tertiary summary used for the fee district and the list; the fee is per ken of frontage, not per household.

Used for: the Kokucho bell's fee district of about 400 blocks; Edo's other time bells at temples
