What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article 江戸三座 (the three theaters of Edo), on the playhouses licensed to stage kabuki by the Edo town magistrate's office from the middle of the period onward. It records that the city once had a great many playhouses, which were consolidated to four and finally to three.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives us the institution we need for a town map - that a theater is a licensed establishment, that the magistracy decided how many there were, and that the number ended up very small, capped because a big timber hall in a townhouse block was a fire the city could not fight - which is why a settlement gets one playhouse or none rather than a row of them. Its limits are that an encyclopedia is a tertiary summary, and this article carries the encyclopedia's own warning that its sources are unclear; that the licensing was an Edo city policy under one government, so we take the arrangement rather than the number itself; and that the count is dated - four houses by the 1670s, three only from 1714 - so "three" is not true of the whole period.

Used for: the three playhouses licensed by the town magistracy after consolidation from many, to four, to three
