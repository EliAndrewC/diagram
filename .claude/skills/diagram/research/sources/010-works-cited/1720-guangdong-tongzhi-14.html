guangdong-tongzhi-14

『廣東通志』卷十四 城池 (the Guangdong provincial gazetteer, Siku Quanshu recension), Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/廣東通志_(四庫全書本)/卷14)

What it is: The walls-and-moats chapter of the Guangdong provincial gazetteer in its Siku Quanshu recension, a primary administrative record listing each city's walls and moats and the officials who built, repaired and dredged them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a primary record of a Chinese city moat silting up and being dredged again and again by successive prefects, the maintenance cycle our moat's drawdown state stands for. Its limit is that it records the fact of each dredging and not the interval between them. It is read from a crowd-transcribed wiki copy rather than a critical edition, so a disputed character would have to be settled elsewhere, and the quoted case is Guangzhou, a great provincial capital and port, not an ordinary county seat.

Used for: Guangzhou's moat silting and being dredged again under successive officials
