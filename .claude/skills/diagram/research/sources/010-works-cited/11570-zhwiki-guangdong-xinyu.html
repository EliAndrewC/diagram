Chinese Wikipedia, "廣東新語" (Guangdong xinyu; in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hant/%E5%BB%A3%E6%9D%B1%E6%96%B0%E8%AA%9E)

What it is: The encyclopedia article on Qu Dajun's description of Guangdong: when it was completed and printed, and what it covers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used only for the book's date, which the chapters the record quotes do not state: completed in 1678, so a late-seventeenth-century work. The article is an encyclopedia summary whose dates cite no source. Its printing date is uncertain: it gives a 1687 printing but also calls the 1700 Mutiange edition the original one, and other listings give 1700 as the first printing. It says nothing about the delta's fish ponds or ducks.

Used for: dating Qu Dajun's Guangdong xinyu to the late seventeenth century (archetypes)
