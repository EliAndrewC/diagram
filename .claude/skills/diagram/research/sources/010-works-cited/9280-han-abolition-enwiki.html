en.wikipedia Abolition of the han system (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abolition_of_the_han_system)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Meiji government's replacement of the domains (han) by prefectures, in 1869-1871.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the domains that survived to the 1871 edict, 261, which is the nearest readable count to the end of the Edo period. It is a tertiary summary, the count is taken just after the period rather than in it, and other pages give other figures (274 daimyo in 1869, "just under 300" domains), so it is a round order of magnitude, not an exact census.

Used for: the number of domains at the close of the Edo period (government)
