Shiojiri City Library, answer to the reference question "Every year we clean the graves on 7 August - does it mean anything?" (in Japanese), Collaborative Reference Database, National Diet Library (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000329772&page=ref_view)

What it is: A public library's reference answer, registered 2023, that quotes the relevant line of each of seven books on Japan's annual observances, among them the Zenkoku nenchu gyoji jiten and a Shiojiri ward history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries the statement that on nanuka-bon the graves AND the paths to the graves were cleaned, and a local record of the kin of one surname gathering to clean their ancestors' graves. It is a librarian's digest of printed books, one line each, and the books are modern works on custom as still observed; how far back each practice goes, and how far from the grave the cleaning reached, it does not say.

Used for: the cleaning of graves and of the paths to them at the start of Bon (religion and death)
