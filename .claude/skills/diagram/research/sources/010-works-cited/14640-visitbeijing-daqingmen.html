Visit Beijing (北京旅游网), 文章详情 (an article on the Daqing gate and the offices either side of it; title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://lyzz.visitbeijing.com.cn/news/detail?id=24260)

What it is: An article on Beijing's official tourism site about the Daqing gate south of Tiananmen and the central offices that lined the streets either side of it in the Ming and Qing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names, street by street, which ministries stood east of the gate and which west, which is how the record can say that five ministries stood together and Justice stood apart with the courts. It is a tourism article, not a plan; it gives no sizes.

Used for: where each of the six ministries stood in the Ming-Qing capital (cities/government)
