en.wikipedia, Japanese castle (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_castle)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article surveying the Japanese castle: its baileys and their arrangements, its walls, moats and gates, and the yagura turrets set at the corners of the walls and over the gates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the corner turret as a standard element, one of the three things this page says make a castle read as one. A tertiary summary used for that element.

Used for: yagura at the corners of the walls
