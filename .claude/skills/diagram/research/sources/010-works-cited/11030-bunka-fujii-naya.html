bunka-fujii-naya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 藤井家住宅納屋 ("Fujii house, barn", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/292940)

What it is: The national register's entry for the barn of the Fujii house in Higashiosaka, built in the Taisho years (1912-1926): where it stands, its length and depth, its roof, its earth-floored inside.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and siting of a small, plainly built barn. Its limits: a twentieth-century building of a household with a detached room and a rice storehouse, near Osaka; one example.

Used for: the size and siting of a small farm barn (homesteads)
