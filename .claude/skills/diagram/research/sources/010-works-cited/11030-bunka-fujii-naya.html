bunka-fujii-naya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 藤井家住宅納屋 ("Fujii house, barn", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/292940)

What it is: The national register's entry for the barn of the Fujii house in Higashiosaka, built in the Taisho years (1912-1926) and repaired in 2001: where it stands, its length and depth, its earthen storehouse-style walls and tiled gable roof, its earth-floored inside.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and siting of a small barn, plain inside (an earth floor, a roof frame of unsquared logs). Its limits: a twentieth-century building, repaired in 2001, of a well-to-do household (the register also lists its main house, detached room, rice storehouse and dry storehouse), built with earthen storehouse-style walls under a tiled roof, so a more solid barn than an ordinary farm family's; near Osaka; one example, and its siting is only its place among its own household's buildings.

Used for: the size and siting of a small farm barn (homesteads)
