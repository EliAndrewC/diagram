Wikipedia (English), "Testament of Ieyasu" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Testament_of_Ieyasu)

What it is: A short English Wikipedia article on the maxim handed down as Tokugawa Ieyasu's testament. It separates that maxim from the longer text in a hundred articles, and lists the three English translations of the longer text.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that says what the hundred articles are: a longer text on how the shogunate was to be run, at least partly drafted by Ieyasu's successors. It is an encyclopedia summary that cites the translations and gives no argument of its own.

Used for: the hundred articles as a text at least partly drafted after Ieyasu (ways)
