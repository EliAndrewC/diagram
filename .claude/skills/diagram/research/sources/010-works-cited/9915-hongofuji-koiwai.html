hongofuji-koiwai

Hongō Fujiyama Park (Yokohama), provisional site, "古民家ミニ博物館" (the old farmhouse mini-museum) (in Japanese; https://hongofuji.stars.ne.jp/guidance/cultural-property/museum/)

What it is: The park's page explaining the parts of the old Koiwai farmhouse it preserves - its entrances, doors, wicket, gate and storehouse - written for the park's visitors.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives, in plain words, the everyday entrance of a farm or fishing-village house as a one-ken sliding door with a wicket, the wicket being the door in daily use. It is a visitor's page for one preserved farmhouse near Yokohama, not a survey; it speaks of commoners' houses, not warrior residences, and the same page at the park's main address could not be fetched, so the provisional site is the one cited.

Used for: a farmhouse's one-ken main door with a wicket; the wicket as a low, narrow doorway (buildings)
