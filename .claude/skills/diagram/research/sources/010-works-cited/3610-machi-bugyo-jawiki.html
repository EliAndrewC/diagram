machi-bugyo-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 町奉行 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/町奉行)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the machi-bugyo, the Edo-period urban magistrate, his office and its jurisdiction; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the office as the administration and justice of the urban districts and puts the magistrate's residence inside the magistracy, which is the co-location this page's compound rests on; it gives no layout; the office described is Edo's, the largest of its kind, so a provincial magistracy is reached by extrapolation.

Used for: the urban magistracy as a model (buildings)
