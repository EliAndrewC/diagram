ja.wikipedia 鍛冶町 ("Kajimachi", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/鍛冶町)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia disambiguation page for the place name Kajimachi, the smiths' ward, with a one-line account of its origin and a list of the towns that keep the name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the name often marks a castle town's ward of smiths under settlement by trade, and its list shows the ward in provincial castle towns, not only the great cities. A disambiguation page, not an article: the list is of present-day place names, and it does not date any ward or say how many smiths lived in one.

Used for: the smiths' wards of provincial castle towns (urban-features)
