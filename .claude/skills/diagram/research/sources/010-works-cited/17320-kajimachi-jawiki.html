ja.wikipedia 鍛冶町 ("Kajimachi", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/鍛冶町)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia disambiguation page for the place name Kajimachi, the smiths' ward, with a one-line account of its origin and a list of the towns that keep the name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the name often marks a castle town's ward of smiths under settlement by trade, and its list shows the ward in provincial castle towns, not only the great cities. A disambiguation page, not an article: its origin line cites no source, and the list is of present-day place names. It does not say which towns were castle towns (that reading is ours, from the town names, and some entries, such as Seki, were smithing towns rather than castle-town wards), and it does not date any ward or say how many smiths lived in one.

Used for: the smiths' wards of provincial castle towns (urban-features)
