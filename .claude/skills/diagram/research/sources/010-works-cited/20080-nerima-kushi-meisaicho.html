nerima-kushi-meisaicho

Nerima Ward, 「Nerima Ward History, History volume: part 7, historical documents」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「練馬区史 歴史編 第七部 歴史資料編」) (in Japanese; https://www2.i-repository.net/contents/myc/text_2rekishihen/rekishihen_honcho7.xhtml)

What it is: The documents part of Nerima ward's official history, published online as text: transcriptions of the Edo-period detailed returns (murameisaichō) of Kami-Nerima, Doshida and other villages now in the ward, from 1756 to the 1850s, with extracts from the shogunate's gazetteer of Musashi and from early Meiji village records.

Why it applies, and its limits: The returns are primary Edo records, filed by each village's headman with its intendant, listing every shrine and temple with its exempt land in tan, se and bu, and some halls' sizes. They are two villages of one district near Edo; an exempt-land figure can include fields held for a shrine, so it is the shrine's precinct or more, not the precinct itself; and the history prints the returns' columns as running text, so a figure is not always plainly tied to one shrine. The page gives no publication date.

Used for: Edo village shrines' exempt lands, 1804 to 1831, as a check on the precinct band (religion-and-death.html).
