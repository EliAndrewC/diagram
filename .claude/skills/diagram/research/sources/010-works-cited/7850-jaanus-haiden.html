JAANUS, haiden (Columbia mirror) (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/haiden) - 「A worship hall at a Shinto shrine, usually placed on the same axis as the offering hall, heiden 幣殿, and the main sanctuary, honden 本殿.」 (the source's own English; the characters are the page's own gloss)

What it is: The Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System glossary entry on the haiden, the worship hall of a Shinto shrine.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the one arrangement it states - the worship hall on one axis with the offering hall and the sanctuary - which is what puts the sanctuary behind the hall on the approach axis. A glossary entry, undated, describing the type in general.

Used for: the hall and sanctuary on one axis (religion-and-death.html).
