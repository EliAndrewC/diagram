ja.wikipedia 伏越 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/伏越)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on 伏越 (fusekoshi), the inverted siphon - a canal carried in a closed conduit beneath a river or another watercourse instead of over it. The article describes the technique and names historical examples, among them the Shibayama fusekoshi on the Minuma-dai irrigation canal and the Ugamori fusekoshi conduit in the Ogaki ring-dike country.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one narrow thing: that carrying one channel under another was a real method in old Japanese waterways, and not a modern trick we invented for a map. It is a tertiary encyclopedia summary, so we take from it only the existence of the technique and the named historical works it points to, never a dimension, a gradient or a construction detail - and the examples it gives are large public irrigation projects, which is a bigger class of work than a village would undertake on its own.

Used for: the inverted siphon as a method "historically seen in old waterways" - 見沼代用水の柴山伏越, 大垣輪中の鵜森伏越樋
