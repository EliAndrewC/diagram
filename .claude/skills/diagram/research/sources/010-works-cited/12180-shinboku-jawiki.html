ja.wikipedia 神木 ("shinboku, the sacred tree", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%A5%9E%E6%9C%A8)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the sacred tree, as the seat of a kami and as an object of worship.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says most of Japan's shrines were first built where such a tree or grove already stood as a divine seat. The article carries the encyclopedia's own banner for lacking sources and possibly containing original research, so the claim is its unsourced summary, and it gives no count of shrines that kept a tree.

Used for: the shrine built where the sacred tree stood (religion-and-death.html).
