What it is: A short popular article on the Beijing Daily's contributor platform about the drainage of old Beijing: a Ming temple's courtyards, the excavated water gate of the Jin capital Zhongdu, and the Ming-Qing drains under the central axis.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that at the Jin capital (twelfth century) the water gathered inside the city in the flood season ran out through a water gate into the moat south of the city - the moat as the city's drain. It is a user-contributed piece compiled from other outlets and carries the platform's disclaimer that it speaks only for its author; it describes a capital, not a provincial seat; and it gives no measurements of the moat or the drains.

Used for: a city's storm water led through a water gate into its moat (cities/hinterland)
