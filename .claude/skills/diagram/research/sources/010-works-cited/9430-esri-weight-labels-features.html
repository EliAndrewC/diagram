esri-weight-labels-features

Esri, ArcGIS Pro documentation, "Weight labels and features" (https://pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/help/mapping/text/weight-labels-and-features.htm)

What it is: A help page of Esri's ArcGIS Pro on feature and label weights: a scale from 0 (the feature is free space a label may cover) to 1,000 (an obstacle a label must not cover), and how the Maplex engine uses them to choose between free space and the least important thing to cover.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the plainest public statement of the rule that decides whether a label should cover something or move: free space first, and when none remains, the location with the lowest total weight. It is one engine's documentation; which features deserve which weight is left to the map's author, so the classification this project uses is its own.

Used for: free space first, then the least total weight covered; the 0 to 1,000 scale (presentation)
