What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the ujidera, the clan temple: the Buddhist temples that powerful clans built from the Asuka period onward for the repose and benefit of their house, in place of the tombs they had raised before.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the clan temple to the Asuka period, which is what makes it older than the castle towns on this page. A tertiary summary used for the dating. The article carries a no-reliable-sources notice, cites only a dictionary, stops at the medieval period and says nothing of clan temples in castle towns, which is the comparison this page draws.

Used for: the clan temple from the Asuka period onward
