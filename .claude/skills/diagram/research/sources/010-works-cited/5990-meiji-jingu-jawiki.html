ja.wikipedia 明治神宮 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%98%8E%E6%B2%BB%E7%A5%9E%E5%AE%AE)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Meiji Jingu, the large Shinto shrine in Tokyo, covering its grounds, its approach and its buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: The article counts eight torii along the approach and gives no distances between them, so no spacing figure on our maps comes from it. Beyond being a tertiary summary whose figures we take only where its own references support them, it describes a modern shrine of exceptional scale, which makes it a poor model for the small country shrines our settlements mostly carry.

Used for: the shrine's torii - the page counts 8, not 3; no spacing figures
