kotobank-omotedana

Yamakawa Nihonshi Shōjiten, Digital Daijisen and Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, s.v. 表店, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/表店-1194792)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the reference entries on the omotedana, the street-fronting shop premises of the early-modern town and the stratum that held them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives who held the frontage against who lived behind, which is the sorting this page's zoning rests on; it sorts by wealth and status, not by a trade's need for a street front.

Used for: who held the street frontage (towns)
