zh.wikipedia 武學 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/武學)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the wuxue, the state military school of the Song through Ming; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives military students enrolled in prefectural and county schools, the state institution this page sets against the private dojo; it describes only state schools; the page names the school's shooting ground (射圃) for archery on the new and full moon, and its county-level evidence is late, military-student places in every county school coming only in 1637.

Used for: the state military school (government)
