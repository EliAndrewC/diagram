ja.wikipedia 住職 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BD%8F%E8%81%B7) - 「There are also temples where only a monk without the qualification of head priest resides, or where no one resides and a head priest from far away commutes.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「住職資格がない僧しか居住していない、だれも居住しておらず遠方の住職が通う寺院もある。」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the jūshoku, the resident head priest of a Buddhist temple.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the disclosure that not every temple had a resident: some held only an unqualified monk, and some had no one, a distant head priest commuting. A tertiary summary describing present-day Japan as much as the Edo period; it names the pattern and gives no share.

Used for: unstaffed temples with a commuting priest (religion-and-death.html).
