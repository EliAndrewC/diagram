Hiratsuka City Museum, "Hiratsuka rekishi kiko 4: the facilities of Hiratsuka station - hatago" (in Japanese; https://www.hirahaku.jp/web_yomimono/hirareki/hirareki04.html)

What it is: A short article by the city museum of Hiratsuka, a Tokaido post station, on its inns: their number in the 1843 shogunal survey of the Tokaido's stations, how inns were graded large, middle and small by their street frontage, the Tokaido-wide averages per grade from Kodama Kota's handbook on post stations, and one inn measured in an 1862 register of the station's houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read that sizes a hatago, and it works from named primary registers. Its limits are that the grading by frontage is reported with "it is said" and is Mishima's scheme, not a rule for every station; that the one measured inn is one building; and that it is about the Tokaido, the busiest highway, in the last decades of the Edo period.

Used for: the three frontage classes, the Tokaido average of 55 inns by class, and one two-story inn of 5.5 ken and 57.25 tsubo (towns)
