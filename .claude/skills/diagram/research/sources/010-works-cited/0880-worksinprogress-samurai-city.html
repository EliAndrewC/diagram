worksinprogress-samurai-city

Samuel Hughes, "Samurai city", Works in Progress, 2026-06-02 (https://worksinprogress.co/issue/samurai-city/)

What it is: A long-form essay in the magazine Works in Progress on how Edo was governed and laid out: the walled daimyo estates of the High City, the gated commoner blocks and alleys of the Low City, and the night closing of the whole mesh of internal gates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states in plain English two things the Japanese reference pages state separately - that each samurai estate was its own walled enclosure with one or two gates, and that the commoner city was cut up by thousands of gates that closed at night - and it gives the scale of the block system. Its limits are that it is a magazine essay rather than scholarship, written for a general reader, and that its estates are Edo's greatest, not a provincial castle town's.

Used for: the per-estate wall and gate of a samurai compound, and the night closing of Edo's internal gates
