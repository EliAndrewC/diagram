kotobank-hyakusho-denki

Kotobank, "Hyakusho Denki" (in Japanese; entries from the Nipponica encyclopedia, the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia and the Yamakawa dictionary of Japanese history, https://kotobank.jp/word/百姓伝記-368304)

What it is: Three dictionary entries on the Hyakusho Denki, a farm manual in 15 volumes, giving its date, its region and the contents of each volume.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the manual (1680-1682 in the Nipponica entry, the Tenna years 1681-1684 in Heibonsha's, 1673-1684 in Yamakawa's), which is what places what the manual says before 1868. Its limits: it summarizes the manual rather than quoting it. Each date is the editors' estimate, not a year the manual gives: the manual names no author and no year of writing, and it survived only in manuscript copies (Nipponica). So the three ranges differ, but all fall between 1673 and 1684, well before 1868. The manual describes farming in Mikawa and Totomi (today's Aichi and Shizuoka), one region of Japan, not the whole country.

Used for: the date of the Hyakusho Denki (homesteads)
