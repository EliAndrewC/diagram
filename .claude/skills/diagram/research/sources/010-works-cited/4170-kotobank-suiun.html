Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten and Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho, s.v. 水運, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/水運)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the encyclopedia entries on water transport and its efficiency against land carriage for heavy and bulky goods.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the efficiency of water over land for heavy goods, which is why this page sends a village's freight onto the water; a general statement rather than a Japanese case.

Used for: heavy freight by water (ways)
