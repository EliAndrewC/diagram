fujita-2007-shaso-slopes

Fujita Naoko 2007, 「A study on the evaluation of shrine-grove space as green space in cities (2): an analysis of shrine groves in urban green space by spatial scale」 (translated; original: 「都市における緑地としての社叢空間の評価に関する研究（２）―空間スケール別にみる都市緑地における社叢の分析―」), Bulletin of the University of Tokyo Forests (東京大学農学部演習林報告) 117, 21-64 (in Japanese; https://repository.dl.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/record/25282/files/esrh117003.pdf)

What it is: The second half of a University of Tokyo thesis on shrine groves as urban green space, which classifies the shrines standing on slopes inside Tokyo's Yamanote loop by where their grove stands relative to the hall and the slope, read from aerial photographs of 2003 laid over the shrines' own site plans and checked by a field survey with interviews of the shrines' keepers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one survey read that sorts shrine groves by which sides of the hall they stand on, and it names the forms: a grove behind the hall, behind and at the sides, at the sides only, and - on a hill-shaped rise - all around it; and it ties each to where the hall sits on its slope. Its limits are that its named examples (Hie, Nezu, Kanda, Atago) are great city shrines, not a village's tutelary shrine; that it sorts the groves that survived, not the groves that were planted - the paper itself says many of these groves burned in the war and were replanted after it, and that the grounds have shrunk and been built on (halls, fire stores, parks), so a grove at the sides only may be what the city left rather than a form anyone chose; that its population is small (27 slope-sited shrines with groves, of 122 listed); that it never states what its percentages are shares of, and they do not come out as whole counts of 27, so they give the forms' order of commonness and are not used as rates; and that its English summary glosses the figures differently from its Japanese text, which is the one cited.

Used for: the forms a shrine's grove takes on sloping ground, and which is commonest (Religion and the dead).
