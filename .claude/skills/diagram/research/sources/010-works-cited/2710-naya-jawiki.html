ja.wikipedia 納屋 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%B4%8D%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the naya, the separate storage shed of a farm or a fishing village.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the shed a farm kept its crop and tools in, the commoner outbuilding these maps draw beside the rarer storehouse. A tertiary summary used for the definition.

Used for: the naya as the farm's crop and tool shed
