kyototuu-mimizuka

京都通百科事典, 耳塚 (the Mimizuka, Kyoto) (https://www.kyototuu.jp/Sightseeing/HistorySpotMimizuka.html)

What it is: The entry on the Mimizuka, the ear mound raised in Kyoto over the trophies of the Korean campaigns, in Kyoto-tsu Hyakka Jiten, a regional encyclopedia site about the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the mound's base beside its height - about 49 m (30 ken) across, 7.2 m (4 ken) high, under a five-ring stone pagoda about 9 m tall - and that base is the anchor for how wide a monumental bone mound is drawn, which nothing else read supplies. Its limits are that it is a regional encyclopedia site rather than a primary record or a scholarly work and names no source for its figures; that the mound is a monumental war memorial rather than an ordinary funerary feature, so it bounds the top of the range and not the middle of it; that the figures are of the mound as it now stands, after later repair and its stone fence; and that the national historic-site designation file, which would carry surveyed dimensions, could not be opened.

Used for: the ear mound's 30 ken (about 49 m) width across, its 4 ken height and its 5 ken stone pagoda
