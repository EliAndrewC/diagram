saruhashi-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, s.v. 猿橋 (Saruhashi) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%8C%BF%E6%A9%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Saruhashi, the cantilever bridge over the Katsura River at Otsuki (Yamanashi prefecture), with a section on how a hanebashi is built and a history of the bridge's rebuildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the hanebashi as an Edo-period form whose timbers are set into holes in the bank rock, and dates the Saruhashi's rebuilding records from 1676 and its present form from at least 1756. Its limits: it is an encyclopedia article whose general description of the form is not tied to one dated source; the bridge standing today is a restoration of the 1851 bridge with its members changed to steel; and it concerns a gorge crossing, not the low river or ditch crossings our maps mostly draw, so it attests a crossing carried back into its banks and gives no landing length.

Used for: the hanebashi, a premodern bridge set into its banks, and the Saruhashi's size and date (ways)
