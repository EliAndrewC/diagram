nisira-komedawara

Nishira rice shop (にしら米穀店), "米俵" (rice bales), a catalog page (in Japanese; https://www.nisira.com/4komedawara.htm)

What it is: A rice shop's catalog of straw rice bales made today, sold hollow for display, listing each size's capacity and its length and diameter.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured size of a bale of the 60 kg (4-to) standard, which no historical page read gave. It is a seller's page for present-day display bales, hollow unless filled to order, so its figure is a modern bale's size at the Meiji-era standard; an Edo-period bale, holding anywhere from 2 to 5 to, ran smaller or larger.

Used for: the size of a 60 kg rice bale, about 2.5 by 1.5 ft (urban features)
