sugiura-1977-tohoku

Sugiura Tadashi, 「The establishment of the farm building composition system in the Tohoku district: the cases of central Iwate prefecture, the Aizu district and the Sendai plain」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「東北地方における農家建物構成方式の成立 - 岩手県中部,会津地方及び仙台平野の事例から」), Jinbun Chiri (Human Geography) 29(5): 451-482, 1977 (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjhg1948/29/5/29_5_451/_pdf)

What it is: A peer-reviewed geography article by the author of the 1973 outbuilding count, tracing when and why the farmsteads of three parts of the Tohoku region came to have the outbuildings they do. It reconstructs the prewar outbuildings of several villages from town-office records and interviews, and cites a prewar housing-improvement report for where the bath and privy stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives ownership rates of the outdoor privy and bath shed that differ sharply from village to village, and the one statement found of where on the plot the bath and privy were usually put. Its limits are that it is the cold northeast only; that the prewar rates are a reconstruction for the 1930s-40s which the author warns may count some buildings twice; and that the front-yard statement is the author passing on a 1941 report, for northern Miyagi only.

Used for: the bath and privy in the front yard in northern Miyagi; prewar outdoor privy and bath-shed rates by village; the roofed firewood stack at Yahaba (homesteads)
