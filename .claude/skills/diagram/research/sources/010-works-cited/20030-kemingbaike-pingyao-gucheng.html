kemingbaike-pingyao-gucheng

Kemingbaike, 平遥古城 ("Pingyao old city", translated from the Chinese by this project), an online encyclopedia entry (https://www.kemingbaike.com/baike/2024121325051.html)

What it is: A Chinese online encyclopedia's long entry on the walled county town of Pingyao in Shanxi, with a year-by-year chronicle of the wall's building and repairs from the Western Zhou to the Qing, giving the rebuilt wall of 1370 in the traditional measures (circuit in li, height and moat in zhang).

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that gives a county seat's moat in the measures of its own time, one zhang wide and deep in 1370, which puts a period figure under the lesser end of the moat band. It is an anonymous compilation that names no gazetteer for the figure, of the kind that copies older encyclopedia text, so the figure is as good as the county records it was presumably taken from and no better; and it describes one county town.

Used for: the width and depth of a county town's moat in the Ming (cities/defenses)
