kemingbaike-pingyao-gucheng

Kemingbaike, 平遥古城 ("Pingyao old city", translated), an online encyclopedia entry (https://www.kemingbaike.com/baike/2024121325051.html)

What it is: A Chinese online encyclopedia's long entry on the walled county town of Pingyao in Shanxi, with a year-by-year chronicle of the wall's building and repairs from the Western Zhou to the wall repairs of 2022 (written and edited by the site's contributors, last updated 2024-12-05), giving the rebuilt wall of 1370 in the traditional measures (circuit in li, height and moat in zhang).

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found that gives a county seat's moat in the measures of its own time, one zhang wide and deep in 1370, which puts a period figure under the lesser end of the moat band. It is an anonymous compilation that names no gazetteer for the figure, of the kind that copies older encyclopedia text, so the figure is as good as the county records it was presumably taken from and no better; and it describes one county town, in semi-arid Shanxi in the north, whose moat need not match a wetter southern town's. The figure is the moat as dug in 1370: the same chronicle records the moat widened in 1850 and dredged in 1873 and 1880, so it is the early-Ming size, not the moat of the later centuries.

Used for: the width and depth of a county town's moat in the Ming (City defenses)
