engawa-kotobank

Kotobank, 縁側 (engawa) - Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica), Hirai Kiyoshi; Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, Suzuki Mitsuru (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%B8%81%E5%81%B4-447425)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the engawa, the veranda of a Japanese house, carrying several dictionaries; the two used here are the Nipponica encyclopedia entry by the architectural historian Hirai Kiyoshi, on the veranda inside the building line and the one outside it, and the Heibonsha world encyclopedia entry by Suzuki Mitsuru, on the wide veranda between the main room and the garden and on when farmhouses took it up.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a scholar-written reference account of the veranda of the warrior household's shoin-style house, which is the house our residences draw. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary, that it gives the width of the floor boards but not of the veranda itself, and that its dating of the farmhouse veranda is hedged by the entry's own "though it differed by region".

Used for: the inner irikawa and the outer nure-en a step below; the wide veranda between the main room and the garden; the farmhouse veranda outside the zashiki and then along the front of the main rooms (buildings)
