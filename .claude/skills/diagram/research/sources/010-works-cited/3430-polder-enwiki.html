en.wikipedia "Polder" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polder)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the polder, the diked tract below the surrounding water level; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives full enclosure by dikes, the floor below the surrounding water some or all of the time, exchange through sluices and the permanent flood risk, which is the enclosure rule this page states; a general article, Netherlands-weighted but carrying the Chinese case (Kunshan's polders, the Yangtze delta's built from the tenth to thirteenth centuries); its drainage sentence presumes pumps, so only the sluice half is premodern.

Used for: why a polder is enclosed on every side (archetypes)
