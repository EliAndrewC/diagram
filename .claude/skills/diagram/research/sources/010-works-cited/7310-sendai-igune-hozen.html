sendai-igune-hozen

Sendai City, the igune conservation page (https://www.city.sendai.jp/ryokuchihozen/kurashi/shizen/midori/hyakunen/hozen.html)

What it is: The page the city of Sendai keeps about conserving igune, the farm windbreak groves of its plain: what the word covers, how long it has been in use, what a grove does for the farmstead it stands around, and what help the city offers an owner who keeps one.

Why it applies, and its limits: The record cites it for one sentence, its definition of the word - trees planted so as to encircle the perimeter of the homestead - which is the picture a reader is likeliest to meet and the one the surveyed form does not bear out, since the grove stands on one or two windward sides. Quoting it is how the record shows that the encircling reading is a gloss on the TERM rather than a description of what was built, and the same page's dating of the name to the middle of the Edo period is what makes the word old enough to matter for a setting pitched before Meiji - though the page hedges that itself, saying the name SEEMS to have become established by then, and gives no source for it. Its limits are that it is a municipal information page with no survey behind it, that its definition is a working one written for owners rather than a finding, and that it speaks of Miyagi alone.

Used for: the city's encircling definition of the word igune, quoted against the two-sided form the scholarly record gives, and its dating of the name to the middle of the Edo period
