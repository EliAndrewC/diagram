zh.wikipedia 驿站 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%A9%BF%E7%AB%99)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the yizhan, the imperial post-relay stations of China, with the Tang network's count and staffing and the Han's thirty-li spacing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Tang network's 1,639 stations and the Han's spacing of a station every thirty li, the comparison figure for the setting's own denser courier network. A tertiary summary used for those two numbers; the setting's road mileage and courier counts are the GM's canon. The count and the spacing come from different dynasties, the count cited to the 大唐六典 and the spacing given for the Han.

Used for: the Tang post network's count and the Han spacing
