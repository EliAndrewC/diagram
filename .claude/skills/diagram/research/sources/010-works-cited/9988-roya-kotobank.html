roya-kotobank

Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten (Heibonsha; Hayashi Yukiko) and the Britannica concise encyclopedia, s.v. 牢屋, aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%89%A2%E5%B1%8B-152554)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the jail, carrying encyclopedia entries on Japanese jails from the ancient period to the Edo jail compound at Tenmacho - its kinds of cell by status, the overcrowding of its great cell - and on Chinese jails, with the Britannica entry's note that a magistracy kept a temporary cell for prisoners called to its court.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is what says a magistracy held a cell of its own for those awaiting its court, which is the cell a county office draws, and it gives the great cell's size. Its limits are that the entries are tertiary, that the sizes are the capital's jail's, and that it gives no size or form for the temporary cell itself.

Used for: the 30-mat great cell; the temporary cell inside the magistracy (buildings)
