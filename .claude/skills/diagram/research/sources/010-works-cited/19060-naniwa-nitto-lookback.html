naniwa-nitto-lookback

Naniwa Ward, Osaka City, "Naniwa Ward centenary pre-project: Look back Naniwa Ward (8th installment, the Nitto district)" (in Japanese; https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/naniwa/page/0000640110.html)

What it is: A 2025 page of the Naniwa ward office of Osaka City: a round-table of residents, followed by a short history of the Nitto district, the old Nagamachi along Nipponbashi-suji, from the Edo period to today, with a chronology.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a local government's popular history, not a study, and it gives no sources; it says Nagamachi, where the road to Sakai and Kii left Edo-period Osaka, was lined with inns and kichin-yado, and that migrant laborers and the destitute gathered in its back streets. The motive it gives for the renaming of 1792 is hedged as "it is also said", and the page gives no number of houses or lodgers; the round-table half is remembered modern testimony and is not relied on.

Used for: that a city's cheap flophouses stood in an Edo-period poor quarter on the road in (urban-features)
