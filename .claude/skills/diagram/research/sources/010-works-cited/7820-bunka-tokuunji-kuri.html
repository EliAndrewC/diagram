bunka-tokuunji-kuri

Cultural Heritage Online, 徳運寺庫裏 (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/262292) - 「At 12 ken along the ridge by 8 ken across the beams it is large in scale」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桁行一二間、梁間八間と大規模になる」)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database entry on a large late-Edo Zen temple's kuri.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one sentence: that the kuri's form resembles the region's honmune-zukuri farmhouse - the attestation behind drawing the monk's dwelling as a farmhouse. Its limit: the building it describes is large, so the resemblance is of form, not of size.

Used for: a kuri resembling the region's farmhouse (religion-and-death.html).
