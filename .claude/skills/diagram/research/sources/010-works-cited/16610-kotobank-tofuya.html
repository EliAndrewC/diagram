kotobank-tofuya

kotobank 豆腐屋 ("tofu shop", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%B1%86%E8%85%90%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entry and the Nipponica encyclopedia's entry by Endo Motoo on the tofu shop: street sale from the fifteenth century, shop sale from the seventeenth, and tofu makers in the villages around the towns by the eighteenth.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the tofu maker's shop, says it spread from the towns to nearby villages, and says it sold both over the counter and from peddlers' tubs, which is why the trade draws as an ordinary shop. A short reference entry on Japan; it gives no premises or water supply.

Used for: the tofu shop selling from its front and by peddling, in towns and nearby villages (urban-features)
