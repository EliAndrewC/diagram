mianning-village-temples

Long Sheng (龙圣), 清代冕宁的村庙组织、村治实践与村落内生秩序, Folklore Studies (民俗研究) 2020 no. 5, read on its 163.com syndication (https://www.163.com/dy/article/FR2AA2DV05438Q4K.html) - 「The village temples generally engaged monks and Daoists to reside and keep watch, responsible for guarding the property, cleaning, and managing the temple's incense offerings」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「各村庙宇普遍请有僧道住守，负责看护财物、打扫卫生以及经营庙内香火」)

What it is: A peer-reviewed historical study by Long Sheng of Shandong University, published in the journal Folklore Studies in 2020 and read on a news-portal syndication that prints it without its notes, on the village-temple organizations of Qing-era Mianning county in Sichuan: their elected heads, their land, their resident monks, worked from contracts, stelae and local documents.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the resident monk or Daoist the village temples generally engaged to live at and keep them, and for the temple land given by donation - the Chinese side of the residence question. Its limits: one county in Qing Sichuan - and a southwestern frontier county in what is now Liangshan, a Han-Yi borderland rather than a core agricultural county; read on a news-portal syndication because the journal's own page refuses fetches, and the article's own sources are not visible from it.

Used for: village temples with a resident monk; temple land by donation (religion-and-death.html).
