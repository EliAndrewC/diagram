kotobank-bansui

コトバンク 番水 ("Kotobank: bansui", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%AA%E6%B0%B4-118160)

What it is: The Kotobank page gathering Japanese dictionary and encyclopedia entries on bansui, the delivery of irrigation water in turns: the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and signed articles of the Sekai Daihyakka encyclopedia.

Why it applies, and its limits: The dictionaries date the practice from the medieval period, as a drought regime, and the encyclopedia says its rules were being written down by the late medieval period, so it places turn-by-turn delivery before 1868. They are reference summaries, not the village documents themselves, and they give no season, no hours and no gate.

Used for: that delivering irrigation water in turns in a drought is medieval and early modern Japanese practice (cities/capitals)
