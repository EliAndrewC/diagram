和樂web (Waraku web, Shogakukan), これは、350年前に繋がる石段。超高給だが命がけ…"長崎奉行所"の知られざる世界 ("These stone steps lead back 350 years. Lavishly paid but at the risk of one's life: the unknown world of the Nagasaki magistracy", title translated), 2025-02-12 (https://intojapanwaraku.com/rock/travel-rock/263663/)

What it is: A feature of 2025 by the freelance writer Dyson Naoko on Waraku web, the site of the publisher Shogakukan's Japanese culture magazine, drawing on interviews with Yoshida Shinya, a curator of the Nagasaki Museum of History and Culture, on the Nagasaki magistracy's Tateyama office: its move to Tateyama, finished in 1673, its duties, and the part rebuilt at the Nagasaki Museum of History and Culture. It reproduces a map of Nagasaki of 1802 from the National Diet Library.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Tateyama office's area in tsubo and its size against the West office, which sets a shogunal magistracy outside Edo beside Edo's own. It is a popular feature that does not attribute its area figure in the text or say what year it describes; among its references is the Nagasaki Prefecture Board of Education's excavation report on the site (長崎県文化財調査報告書146, 1998), the likely origin and the source to read for the number. The figure is the whole compound - the magistrate's residence, longhouses, storehouses and a riding ground as well as the offices - and one of the magistracy's two offices, the West office standing as well at about half its area; its "about 10,000 square meters" rounds down, since 3,278.5 tsubo is about 10,800. The Nagasaki magistrate's office also handled foreign trade and diplomacy, so its size reflects duties no domain office had.

Used for: the area of the Nagasaki magistrate's Tateyama office, 3,278.5 tsubo, about twice its West office (cities/government)
