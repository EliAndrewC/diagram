和樂web (Waraku web, Shogakukan), これは、350年前に繋がる石段。超高給だが命がけ…"長崎奉行所"の知られざる世界 ("These stone steps lead back 350 years. Lavishly paid but at the risk of one's life: the unknown world of the Nagasaki magistracy", title translated), 2025-02-12 (https://intojapanwaraku.com/rock/travel-rock/263663/)

What it is: A feature of 2025 on Waraku web, the site of the publisher Shogakukan's Japanese culture magazine, on the Nagasaki magistracy's Tateyama office: its move to Tateyama, finished in 1673, its duties, and the part rebuilt at the Nagasaki Museum of History and Culture. It reproduces a map of Nagasaki of 1802 from the National Diet Library.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Tateyama office's area in tsubo and its size against the West office, which sets a shogunal magistracy outside Edo beside Edo's own. It is a popular feature that does not say where its area figure comes from, and the Nagasaki magistrate's office also handled foreign trade and diplomacy, so its size reflects duties no domain office had.

Used for: the area of the Nagasaki magistrate's Tateyama office, 3,278.5 tsubo, about twice its West office (cities/government)
