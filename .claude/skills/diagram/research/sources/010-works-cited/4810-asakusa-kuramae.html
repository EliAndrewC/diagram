asakusa-kuramae

Asakusa / Kuramae district histories; Rice broker (Wikipedia) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_broker)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article "Rice broker", on the fudasashi of Edo - the licensed brokers of Kuramae in Asakusa, "before the storehouses", who warehoused the stipend rice of the shogunate's retainers, converted it to coin and lent against it, and whose wealth fed the neighboring entertainment district.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the institution our capitals draw beside the government granaries - brokers who turned tax rice into money, clustered before the storehouses - which is what puts a brokers' street next to the granary on our maps. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary carrying the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks inline citations, so nothing in it can be checked against a reference; that it does not say where the shogunate's storehouses stood - the riverside site on the Sumida our maps assume is not on this page and rests on other sources; and that it describes the capital's brokers, whose scale no provincial city approaches.

Used for: Kuramae (蔵前) "before the storehouses" as the brokers' district; fudasashi warehousing, converting and lending against stipend rice, and their wealth seeding the neighboring entertainment district (that the shogunate's granaries stood on the Sumida riverside is not on the page)
