ranzan-hakusan-jinja

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡神社明細帳, Hakusan Shrine, Hirasawa (http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09jin11.html)

What it is: One shrine's page of the Ranzan town office's transcription of the Hiki district shrine register (the register entered as ranzan-shrine-register): Hakusan Shrine, a village shrine at Hirasawa, with its precinct's area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the smallest of the fourteen Saitama village precincts, the low end of the band. Its limits are the register's: a Meiji-to-Showa register filed in 1963, its area recorded in 1948, after the Meiji taking of shrine lands, so not an Edo precinct; one shrine.

Used for: the low end of the Saitama village-precinct band (religion-and-death.html).
