ohmi-yoshi

公益財団法人淡海環境保全財団, ヨシとは (https://www.ohmi.or.jp/yoshi/about/whats/)

What it is: A public information page, ヨシとは ("What the reed is"), from the Ohmi Environmental Conservation Foundation (公益財団法人淡海環境保全財団), a public interest foundation in Shiga prefecture whose work is the environment of Lake Biwa. It describes the common reed and the long standing practice of cutting the winter-withered stems for use.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is our evidence that a reed bed is a harvested resource rather than scenery - cut in winter for yoshizu blinds, thatch, summer shoji and standing screens, and left to deteriorate when nobody cuts it - which is what a reader is told when they click the reeds on a map. Two honest limits: it is a conservation foundation writing for the public, not scholarship, and everything it describes is a lake or river reed bed on Lake Biwa, so we cite it for lake and river reeds only; nothing we could read puts that harvest economy on the margins of a farm pond, and our record says so rather than stretching this page to cover it.

Used for: the reed-harvest economy - winter cutting for yoshizu screens, thatch and summer shoji, and an uncut bed going 荒れる - and, just as importantly, for the SCOPE LIMIT recorded against it: this foundation is named for Lake Biwa and every example on the page is a lake or river reed bed. It is cited as evidence about lake and river reed beds ONLY. Nothing found puts that economy on tameike margins, and the research entry says so rather than extending it.
