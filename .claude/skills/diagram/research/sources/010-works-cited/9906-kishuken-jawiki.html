Japanese Wikipedia, "紀州犬" (Kishu dog) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%B4%80%E5%B7%9E%E7%8A%AC)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kishu, a Japanese dog breed fixed from the native dogs of the Kii mountains, with sections on its use in boar hunting and on the local legend of its founding dog.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests boar hunting with packs of native dogs in the Kii mountains, the kind of hunt a lord who hunts boar would keep dogs for. It is a breed article: its account of traditional hunting describes hunters with guns and is undated, and the story of a castle lord's hunt is a legend.

Used for: boar hunted with native dogs, three or four to a hunter (buildings)
