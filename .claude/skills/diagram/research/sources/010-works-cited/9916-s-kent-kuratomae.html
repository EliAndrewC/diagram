Suzuki Kento Kogyo, "左官一服噺 部位○蔵戸前（くらとまえ）" (a plasterer's notes: the storehouse door front), 2016 (in Japanese; https://s-kent.jp/2016/10/26/%E5%B7%A6%E5%AE%98%E4%B8%80%E6%9C%8D%E5%99%BA-%E3%80%80%E9%83%A8%E4%BD%8D%E2%97%8B%E8%94%B5%E6%88%B8%E5%89%8D%EF%BC%88%E3%81%8F%E3%82%89%E3%81%A8%E3%81%BE%E3%81%88%EF%BC%89%EF%BC%88godown-door-front/)

What it is: A short note in a Tokyo plastering firm's series on its craft, describing the doorway of a plastered storehouse: its three doors, the grading of its stepped surround, and the standard size of its hinged door leaves.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a working plasterer's statement of the standard leaf size of a storehouse door, the figure the drawn storehouse doors are set against. It is a modern craftsman's note, not a historical study; it gives the leaf, not the opening, and does not say from what period or region the standard comes.

Used for: a storehouse's double hinged doors, each leaf 3.5 by 6 shaku (buildings)
