What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Fujian tulou: the round and square earthen communal houses of the mountain districts of Longyan and Zhangzhou, their function as dwelling and defense in one, and which forms are the most common.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the square tulou is the most widespread form, the fact this page uses to show that the round fortress-house is the exception even where it is famous. A tertiary summary used for that ranking.

Used for: the square tulou as the most widespread form, and dwelling and defense combined
