en.wikipedia "Cultivator" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultivator)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the cultivator, the implement drawn between crop rows to break the soil and kill weeds, described from the ancient hoe through the horse-drawn and tractor-mounted machines of the last century; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the row-crop cultivator's purpose - weed control between the rows - and that hoe weeding between rows is ancient, which is why ruled rows paid on dry land. The row-crop cultivator whose purpose it states is a modern machine, horse- or tractor-drawn from the early 1900s, and only its one general sentence on hoe weeding reaches back before that; it names no region and no East Asian practice, and its one mention of paddy is of cage-wheeled machines in rice-growing areas, not of premodern paddy.
