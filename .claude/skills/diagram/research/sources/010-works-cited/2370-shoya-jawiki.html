What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the shoya, the Edo-period village headman (nanushi, kimoiri): the duties of the office, the documents it signed and relayed, and the literacy it required.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the headman copied and relayed the lord's circulars and needed reading, writing and the abacus for the office, which is the one reader per settlement this page's notice board depends on. A tertiary summary used for the duties. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources.

Used for: the headman relaying circulars and required to be literate
