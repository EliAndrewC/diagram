Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 森家住宅納屋 ("Mori house, barn", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/545121)

What it is: The national register's entry for the barn of the Mori house, a farm of a village on the Koto river in the Sanuki plain, Kagawa, built in 1907: where it stands on the plot, its floor area, its build.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the siting and size of a real farm barn. Its limits: a large barn of an old household grown rich on rice and barley milling, built fireproof in 1907; one example.

Used for: the siting and size of a farm barn (homesteads)
