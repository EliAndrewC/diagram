kotobank-warichi-seido

Kotobank, 割地制度 ("the warichi system", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica) entry by Yoshitake Kaichiro (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%89%B2%E5%9C%B0%E5%88%B6%E5%BA%A6-880122)

What it is: A signed national-encyclopedia entry on warichi, the Edo-period custom by which a village periodically re-divided its fields among its landholders by lot: where it was found, why it arose, and how the land was graded, parcelled and drawn.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the standard short account of the custom, citing the monograph on it, and it describes the method step by step. It says nothing about how the redistributed shares looked on the ground - whether they were cut as equal strips or marked - and it describes Japan only.

Used for: warichi's periodic redistribution of fields by lot and how the shares were made up (archetypes)
