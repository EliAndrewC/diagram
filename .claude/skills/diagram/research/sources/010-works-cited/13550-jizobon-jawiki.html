ja.wikipedia 地蔵盆 ("Jizō-bon, the street jizo's festival", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9C%B0%E8%94%B5%E7%9B%86)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the late-summer festival a town block holds for its street-corner jizo, mostly in the Kinki region.

Why it applies, and its limits: It qualifies the block-by-block count - a block with no jizo of its own borrows one from a temple - and says that east of the Kinki the dōsojin does the street jizo's work, which is why a map carries one kind of stone or the other. Its limits: a tertiary article describing a custom as kept today, with no counts.

Used for: a town block without its own jizo; the dōsojin in the street jizo's place in the east (religion-and-death)
