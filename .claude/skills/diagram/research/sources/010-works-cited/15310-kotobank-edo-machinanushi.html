kotobank-edo-machinanushi

Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho / Nipponica (Shogakukan, the entry by Yoshiwara Ken'ichiro), s.v. 江戸町名主 ("Edo town headman", translated from the Japanese by this project), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B1%9F%E6%88%B8%E7%94%BA%E5%90%8D%E4%B8%BB-36954)

What it is: The kotobank page for the town headmen of Edo, carrying a dictionary entry and an encyclopedia entry: how many wards one headman governed, how many there were, where he lived and worked, and what he was paid.

Why it applies, and its limits: Published reference works on the office that stood over the ward, which is the unit the map divides a city's commoner ground into. It is about Edo, the largest city of its time, not a provincial castle town, and its count of headmen is given as what is said rather than as a tally.

Used for: the headman over several wards, living in one of them (cities/fabric.html)
