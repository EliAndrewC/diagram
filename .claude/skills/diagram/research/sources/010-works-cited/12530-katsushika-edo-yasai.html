Katsushika City, 子ども葛飾区史 第2章 第6節 江戸時代: 江戸の暮らしを支えた葛飾の農作物 ("Katsushika history for children, chapter 2, section 6, the Edo period: the crops of Katsushika that supported life in Edo", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.katsushika.lg.jp/history/child/2-6-1-45.html)

What it is: A page of the ward's history written for children, on the vegetables Katsushika, east of Edo, grew for the city in the Edo period and the city's night soil that came back by boat to feed them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the reason a city's vegetables were grown close to it - fresh vegetables could not be kept long - and the return flow of night soil, in a local government's own words. It is a children's summary of one district of the largest city, with no figures, and its sentence on the night-soil landings runs on into the 1960s, so the landings are not dated to the Edo period by it.

Used for: vegetables grown near Edo because they could not be kept fresh, and the city's night soil bought and brought by boat (towns)
