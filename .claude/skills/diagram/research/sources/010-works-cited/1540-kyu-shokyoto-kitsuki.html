kyu-shokyoto-kitsuki

九州の小京都 (Kyushu Shokyoto, the Kyushu little-Kyoto towns' federation), 城下町のご案内（杵築） (https://www.kyu-shokyoto.org/kitsuki/jyouka/)

What it is: A regional tourism federation's description of the castle town of Kitsuki in Oita: the samurai quarters on the high ground north and south of the castle and the merchants' town in the valley between them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Kitsuki's sandwich plan - warriors on the heights, merchants below - which is the arrangement this page cites the town for. Its limit is that it is a tourism federation's page. The page itself calls the arrangement unique to Kitsuki among castle towns, so the form is cited as one attested case and not as a norm.

Used for: the samurai quarters on the heights and the merchants' town in the valley
