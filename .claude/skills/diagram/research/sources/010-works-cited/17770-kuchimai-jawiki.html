kuchimai-jawiki

ja.wikipedia, 口米 ("kuchimai, the rice surcharge", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8F%A3%E7%B1%B3)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kuchimai, the surcharge collected with the land tax from the medieval estates to the Edo period, with what it paid for, the problems it raised and its reform in 1725.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that at intendant offices where the surcharge yielded little and could not grow, it became one cause of spending the main tax on the office and of bribery, and that income differed between offices because the rates differed by region. It is a tertiary summary; it names no office and gives no count of how many ran short, and it concerns the shogunate's own intendancies, not a daimyo's county offices.

Used for: the offices where the surcharge fell short (buildings)
