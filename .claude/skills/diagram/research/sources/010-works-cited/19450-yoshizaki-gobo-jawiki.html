ja.wikipedia 吉崎御坊 ("the Yoshizaki temple", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%90%89%E5%B4%8E%E5%BE%A1%E5%9D%8A)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the temple Rennyo founded at Yoshizaki, Echizen, in 1471; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the ground round the temple was lined with priests' quarters and lodgings for believers and formed a temple town, which places the clergy's houses round a temple in fifteenth-century Japan. Its limits: an encyclopedia summary, not a period record, and its sentence on the temple town has no footnote (the article's only printed reference is a 2018 popular book about prefectural borders); Yoshizaki was the seat of the sect's head and a pilgrimage center, not an ordinary temple; it gives no count, size or plan of the houses.

Used for: the priests' quarters round the Yoshizaki temple (religion-and-death.html).
