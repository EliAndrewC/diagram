Tokyo Fire Department, 望楼のことあれこれ ("All about the watchtower", title translated from the Japanese by this project), quoting the Shin shobo zatsugaku jiten, 2nd revised edition, Tokyo Rengo Boka Kyokai, 2001 (in Japanese; https://www.tfd.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/libr/qa/qa_71.htm)

What it is: A page of the Tokyo Fire Department's online library, reprinting a fire-service reference book's short history of the fire-watch tower from the Edo period to the 1970s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the height of the first towers and the rules that kept every other tower lower, black, open-legged and without a watchman, which is what a town's tower was. It is a fire service's popular history rather than a historian's, it speaks of Edo only, it dates the first tower to 1650 where the encyclopedias give 1658, and it gives no footprint.

Used for: the first tower's height and the rules on the others (urban-features)
