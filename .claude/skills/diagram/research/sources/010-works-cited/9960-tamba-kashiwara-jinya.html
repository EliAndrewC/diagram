tamba-kashiwara-jinya

Tamba City, 柏原藩陣屋跡 (The site of the Kashiwara domain's jin'ya), updated 2024 (in Japanese; https://www.city.tamba.lg.jp/soshiki/shakaikyoikubunkazaika/gyomuannai/7/1/2441.html)

What it is: The city of Tamba's page on the national historic site of the Kashiwara domain's seat, the residence and government office of a 20,000-koku domain from 1714, with the designation's text and a description of the surviving gate range and part of the front hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures a surviving jin'ya gate range and says what its rooms were, the guardroom among them, which is one of the two forms a gatehouse is drawn in. It is a domain seat, larger than a county post, the gate is thought (the designation's word) to be of the first building of 1714, and the page notes that the guardroom end's posts and wall are not original and that the range once ran on further north, cut off by a later alteration, so the length it gives is the surviving length and not the length as built; it gives the length two ways, 13.5 ken and 13 ken 1 shaku 2 sun.

Used for: the gate range of 13.5 by 2 ken with its guardroom beside the gate; the same range as a jin'ya's main gate, whose doorway width it does not give (buildings)
