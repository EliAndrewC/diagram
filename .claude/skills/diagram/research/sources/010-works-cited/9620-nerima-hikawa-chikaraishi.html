Nerima City, 氷川神社の力石・氷川神社の旧拝殿 (https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kankomoyoshi/annai/rekishiwoshiru/rekishibunkazai/bunkazai/b025.html)

What it is: Nerima ward's cultural-property page on the eight strength stones beside the approach of a Hikawa Shrine, used by young men for trials of strength from late Edo into Meiji.

Why it applies, and its limits: A dated example of strength stones and where they stood. Its limits: one shrine in the Edo hinterland, and the stones date from late Edo on.

Used for: strength stones beside the approach (religion-and-death.html).
