myoji-taito-kotobank

Kotobank, 苗字帯刀 - 日本大百科全書 (the entry signed 北原章男), 改訂新版 世界大百科事典 (signed 水林彪), 百科事典マイペディア and 山川 日本史小辞典 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%8B%97%E5%AD%97%E5%B8%AF%E5%88%80)

What it is: The Kotobank page for myoji-taito, the privilege of a surname and the wearing of swords, carrying four published reference works: who among commoners was granted it and in what forms, that a domain could grant it only within its own territory, that licensing it was a means by which the early modern state reached into the localities, and that from the middle of the period the domains issued it for money.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries both halves this record needs from one page - the privilege as a lord's grant tied to office and to merit, which is the setting's own arrangement almost word for word, and the later practice that contradicts the setting's rule that money cannot buy it, which is what lets the deviation be stated precisely rather than assumed. Its limits are that these are tertiary summaries; that they describe Tokugawa Japan only; and that none of them says a wall or a gate could be obtained this way, so the departure is recorded for the surname privilege and read across to the compound by us.

Used for: the surname granted to purveyor merchants and town elders, in perpetuity or for one generation, and limited to the domain; the grant as an instrument of rule; the domains issuing it for monetary contributions in the later period
