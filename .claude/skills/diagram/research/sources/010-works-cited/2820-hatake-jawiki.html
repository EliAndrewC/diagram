What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the hatake, the dry field, and the grains, vegetables, beans, tubers and fruit grown on it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines where a household's bulk vegetables and dry crops were grown, which is why the dooryard bed on these maps is small. A tertiary summary used for the definition; it draws no line between a field and a kitchen garden.

Used for: the dry field as the place of bulk vegetables and dry crops
