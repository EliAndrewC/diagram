bukeyashiki-wiki

Japanese Wikipedia, 武家屋敷 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A6%E5%AE%B6%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the buke-yashiki (武家屋敷), the samurai residence of an early modern castle town. It sets out the zoning of such a town: upper samurai in hedged and walled plots inside the middle moat near the castle, and the row houses and small plots of ashigaru and other low-ranking retainers arranged outside them, often massed at the town's entrances for defense.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is the source for the rule our samurai wards follow, that rank sets distance from the castle and that ranks of small uniform housing belong on the outer fringe rather than inside a great house's fence. It is a tertiary summary and gives no plot dimensions, setbacks or counts, so it decides the pattern while the measurements come from elsewhere, and it describes the Edo-period castle town specifically rather than samurai housing in every era.

Used for: the buke-yashiki plot program and the perimeter nagaya; setback and enclosure conventions
