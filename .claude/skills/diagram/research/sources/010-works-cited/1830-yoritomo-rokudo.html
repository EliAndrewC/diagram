yoritomo-rokudo

鎌倉手帳 (yoritomo-japan.com), 六道の辻 (https://www.yoritomo-japan.com/nara-kyoto/rokudonotuji.html)

What it is: A privately maintained Japanese history-sites reference's page on the Rokudō crossroads in Kyoto, on the road to the Toribeno funerary ground, where the dead were sent off.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the crossroads on the way to Toribeno, the siting this page uses; the temple's own article beside it carries the same. Its limit is that it is an amateur reference.

Used for: the Rokudō crossroads on the road to Toribeno
