kotobank-shaso

Kotobank, 社倉 ("Shaso, the relief granary", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%A4%BE%E5%80%89-76023)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the relief granary, carrying the Digital Daijisen, the Seisen-ban Nihon kokugo daijiten and the Sekai daihyakka jiten: how grain was collected and lent, its Chinese origin in the Sui charity granary set in each village community, and its adoption in Aizu in 1655.

Why it applies, and its limits: Standard reference dictionaries give both the Japanese and the Chinese institution on one page. Their limits are that they are tertiary summaries; that the Aizu date is given as "it is said"; and that they describe an institution, not the building or where it stood.

Used for: the relief granary in Japan and the Chinese village granary's start (urban-features)
