en.wikipedia, Tamagawa Aqueduct (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamagawa_Aqueduct)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Tamagawa Jōsui, the aqueduct dug in 1653 to 1654 to bring the Tama river's water 43 km into Edo: its route from Hamura to the Yotsuya gate, its open earth cut across the Musashino plateau, and the buried wooden and stone conduits that carried the water on under the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the length, the route and the two forms of the channel - open cut outside the city, buried pipe within - which is exactly the division our capital's water supply draws. It is a tertiary summary and is used only for facts its own references and the Edo-Tokyo Museum's exhibition page state as well.

Used for: the Tamagawa Jōsui's 43 km, its open earth cut from Hamura to the Yotsuya gate, and its buried conduits inside Edo
