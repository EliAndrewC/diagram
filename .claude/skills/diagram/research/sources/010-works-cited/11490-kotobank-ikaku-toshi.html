Kotobank, 囲郭都市 ("enclosed town", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten and Hyakka Jiten Mypedia (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%9B%B2%E9%83%AD%E9%83%BD%E5%B8%82-201610)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the enclosed or walled town, with an encyclopedia article on why towns were walled and where, saying that in Japan town areas bounded by moats or rivers are found but whole-town walls were rare apart from Kyoto's earth rampart, and a shorter entry describing China as a society of enclosures, every county and provincial town walled.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states both halves of the contrast, China's walled county towns and Japan's rare town walls, in one reference page. The two entries are tertiary summaries with no counts, and the Chinese statement is a sweeping one that the dynasty-by-dynasty history qualifies.

Used for: walled towns common in China and rare in Japan (towns)
