en.wikipedia "Riparian zone" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riparian_zone)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the riparian zone, the vegetated interface between land and a stream; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that a natural stream's margins carry their own vegetation to the water, which is why this page gives a brook no scrub margin; it says nothing of maintained banks on an engineered channel; it says riparian zones may be natural or engineered, but nothing of a bank kept clear by maintenance.

Used for: the natural stream bank vegetated to the edge (vegetation)
