What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Pingyao, the walled county town in Shanxi: its position on the east bank of the Fen river in the Taiyuan basin, its Ming wall, and the 4 m moat outside the wall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the town on its river. A tertiary summary used for the siting and the moat, and its 4 m moat is a measurement of the restored World Heritage site as it stands today, with no period source given for it.

Used for: Pingyao on the Fen's east bank, and the moat outside its wall
