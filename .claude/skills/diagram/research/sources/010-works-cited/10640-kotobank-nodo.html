kotobank-nodo

Kotobank, 農道 (farm road), reprinting the Digital Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia and others (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%BE%B2%E9%81%93)

What it is: Kotobank's page of dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the farm road, the way made for farm work between the farmhouses and the fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its one-line definition says what a way to the fields is for and what it joins, which is what a map's field path is. The definition is from an undated present-day dictionary; the encyclopedia entries on the same page describe modern planned farm roads (road widths for machines, a road within 100 m of every point) and are not used for anything older; the dictionary's earliest example, from an 1877 travel report, may describe a foreign country and is not used either.

Used for: the farm road as the way between the farmhouses and the fields (fields)
