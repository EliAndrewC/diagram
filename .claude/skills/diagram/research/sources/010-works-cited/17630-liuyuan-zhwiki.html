zh.wikipedia 留园 ("Lingering Garden", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%95%99%E5%9B%AD)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Lingering Garden (Liuyuan) of Suzhou, a private garden outside the Changmen gate, with its history from its founding in 1593 as the East Garden of the official Xu Taishi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places one named Ming scholar-official's private garden just outside a city gate, which is the near-city retreat this record asks about. It is one garden, in Suzhou, the richest garden city of late imperial China, and a tertiary summary; it says the garden was his but not whether he lived there or in town, gives no distance from the wall, and says nothing about how common such a garden was.

Used for: a Ming official's private garden just outside a city gate (cities/hinterland)
