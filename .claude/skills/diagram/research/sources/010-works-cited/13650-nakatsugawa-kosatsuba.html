nakatsugawa-kosatsuba

Nakatsugawa City Nakasendo History Museum, "Nakatsugawa-juku 3: the kosatsuba site" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「｟中津川宿3｠高札場跡」) (in Japanese; https://www.city.nakatsugawa.lg.jp/museum/n/nakatsugawa_juku/4846.html)

What it is: A municipal museum's page on the notice board of Nakatsugawa, a post town on the Nakasendo in Gifu prefecture: what a board was, which boards it carried, and where it originally stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records one board's original seat - at the crossroads with the Hida road, a short way up the slope from where the reconstruction stands - and the reason given, that more people would see it there. One post town, described by a museum for visitors; it gives no date for the siting and no source for the reason.

Used for: a board set at a crossroads of two roads so that more people would see it (urban-features)
