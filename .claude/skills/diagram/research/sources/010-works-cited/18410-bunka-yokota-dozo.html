bunka-yokota-dozo

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 「The former Yokota residence, storehouse」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧横田家住宅 土蔵」) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/144618)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record of the smaller of the two storehouses of the former Yokota residence at Matsushiro, a middle-rank Sanada-domain retainer's house designated an Important Cultural Property.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a samurai household's own storehouse measured: earth-walled, 2.7 by 3.6 m, a gable roof. Its limits are that it is one storehouse of one middle-rank house, of the late Edo period (1751-1829); that it is the smaller of that household's two storehouses, the other (a separate record of the same designation, `bunka-yokota-dozo-2`) being two-storeyed and 5.5 by 4.5 m, so a household of this rank could keep more than one and a larger one; and that the record gives no position on the grounds.

Used for: how big a household storehouse was (buildings.html).
