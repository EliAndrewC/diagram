bunka-yokota-dozo

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 「The former Yokota residence, storehouse」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「旧横田家住宅 土蔵」) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/144618)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record of the storehouse of the former Yokota residence at Matsushiro, a 150-koku Sanada-domain retainer's house designated an Important Cultural Property.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a samurai household's own storehouse measured: earth-walled, 2.7 by 3.6 m, a gable roof. Its limits are that it is one storehouse of one middle-rank house, the smaller of the size range, and that the record gives no position on the grounds.

Used for: how big a household storehouse was (buildings.html).
