ranzan-koshoji-senjudo

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡寺院明細帳, 日蓮宗 光照寺 菅谷村大字千手堂 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter14.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription of the Meiji-era Hiki district temple register entry (filed in Saitama prefecture's 1963 papers) for Kōshōji (光照寺), a Nichiren temple of Senjudō, Saitama, with its main hall of 4 by 4 ken 2 shaku and a precinct of 453 tsubo.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its main hall is the narrowest of the nineteen Ranzan-area temples the town transcribes from the district register, the bottom of the band. Its limits: one village temple of sixty-five parishioners, below the scale of a town monastery, so it bounds the band from beneath rather than typifying it; and the register is a Meiji one (land values in yen, a precinct classed as government land, woodland surrendered after the Restoration, a note of 1942) that the prefecture filed in 1963, so its hall is the one standing after 1868 - most likely the one rebuilt after the fire of the Enkyō era (1744-1748), but not proven to be Edo.

Used for: the narrowest main hall in the band (religion-and-death.html).
