Nagoya Castle, the English Wikipedia article (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_Castle)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Nagoya Castle, the great Tokugawa castle of Owari domain, describing its enclosures and buildings - the keep in the inner bailey, the Ninomaru palace that served as both the lord's residence and the domain's supreme government office, and the third bailey holding the auxiliary mansions of the lords and the houses of senior retainers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the arrangement our castle plans follow: the palace at the core as both residence and the working seat of domain government, the keep a separate defensive tower, and the third bailey given over to the mansions of the senior retainers. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary, and that Nagoya was one of the largest castles in the country, so it stands for the arrangement of a castle's parts and never for their size at a provincial seat.

Used for: the Sannomaru Oyakata mansions in the third bailey; the goten as the administrative and residential center as distinct from the defensive tenshu
