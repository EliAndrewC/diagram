watariroka-kotobank

Kotobank, 渡廊下 (watari-roka) - Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B8%A1%E5%BB%8A%E4%B8%8B-2094270)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the watari-roka, carrying the Shogakukan unabridged Japanese dictionary's definition of it as a corridor between two buildings that joins them, with its other names and a first attestation from 1631.

Why it applies, and its limits: It fixes what the word means on our residence plans. It is a dictionary definition only: it says nothing of which houses had one or how long it ran.

Used for: the watari-roka as a corridor joining two buildings (buildings)
