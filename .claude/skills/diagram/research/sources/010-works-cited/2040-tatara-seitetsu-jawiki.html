What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on tatara ironmaking: the furnace, the iron sand and charcoal it consumed, the products it yielded and the two-stage refining that followed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states what the iron was made into and how the refining hearth worked - pig iron reheated and its carbon burned down under the bellows - which is what this page's account of a smelting valley's works rests on. A tertiary summary used for the products and the refining sequence.

Used for: the products of tatara iron; the refining hearth's decarburizing
