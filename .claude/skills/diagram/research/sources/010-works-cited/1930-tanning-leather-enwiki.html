What it is: The English Wikipedia article on tanning, the making of leather from hides: the ancient methods of soaking, liming, bating with dung and tanning in vats, and the trade's banishment to the edge of town for its smell.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the water soak, the unhairing with lime or urine, the bating with dog or pigeon dung, the salt curing of raw hides and the trade's place at the outskirts, which are the process and the siting this page's tanning yard draws. A tertiary summary of a general and largely Western history, used for the mechanism; it says nothing of Japan.
