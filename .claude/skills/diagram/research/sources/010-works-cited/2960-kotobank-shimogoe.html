What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the reference-encyclopedia entries on shimogoe, night soil as fertilizer, and its use by the farm villages around the early-modern cities.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the villages supplying a city's greens took its night soil in bulk, the suburban vegetable belt this page draws around a town. A tertiary summary of published reference works.

Used for: the night-soil-fed vegetable belt around a city
