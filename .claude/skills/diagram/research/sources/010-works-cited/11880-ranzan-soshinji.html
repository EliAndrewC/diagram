ranzan-soshinji

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡寺院明細帳, 曹洞宗 宗心寺 七郷村大字吉田 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter03.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription of the Hiki district temple register entry for Sōshinji, a Sōtō temple of Yoshida, Saitama, listing its main hall, entrance, bell tower, kuri and earthen storehouse with their dimensions and its precinct of 1,665 tsubo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives what stood in a rural temple's precinct beside the main hall, with sizes. Its limits: one rural temple; a Meiji-era register carried into 1963, so the buildings are those standing then, not necessarily an Edo set.

Used for: a temple's bell tower and storehouse (religion-and-death.html).
