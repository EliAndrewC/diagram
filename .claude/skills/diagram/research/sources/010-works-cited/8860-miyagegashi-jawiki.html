ja.wikipedia 土産菓子 (souvenir confectionery) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9C%9F%E7%94%A3%E8%8F%93%E5%AD%90)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on souvenir confectionery: how the sweets sold to travelers became the named products of the places that sold them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts the sweet trade where the record can support it. Once the Edo period came in and traffic on the highways increased, the sweets sold at the teahouses along the way developed in their own directions out of what travelers said of them, and travelers spreading word of those sweets was the greatest occasion for confectionery developing in the provinces. Its limits are that it is a tertiary article; that it places the teahouse on the HIGHWAY rather than at a temple gate, so it supports sweets on the pilgrim road and not a sweet-seller at the gate; and that it is a general statement about the period with no one place or date attached.

Used for: the sweets sold at the highway teahouses, and the traveler's word of mouth that made them regional specialties
