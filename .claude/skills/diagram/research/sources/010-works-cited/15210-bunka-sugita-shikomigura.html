bunka-sugita-shikomigura

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, "杉田酒造仕込蔵" ("Sugita Brewery fermenting store", title translated from the Japanese by this project), registered tangible cultural property, Oyama, Tochigi (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/206114)

What it is: The national database's entry for a sake brewery's fermenting store: a two-story earthen storehouse of the late Edo period (1830-1868), moved to its present site around 1883-1897, 18.2 m by 9.14 m, with a building area of 167 square meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an official, measured record of a surviving brewery hall of the Edo period in a Kanto country district, the size of the building the brewery glyph stands for. Its limits are that it is one building of one brewery, moved from where it was built, and gives neither the tanks inside nor the whole compound.

Used for: a late-Edo brewery hall of about 60 by 30 ft (urban-features)
