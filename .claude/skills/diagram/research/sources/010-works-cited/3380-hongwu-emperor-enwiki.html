en.wikipedia "Hongwu Emperor" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongwu_Emperor)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Hongwu Emperor, founder of the Ming; an encyclopedia article, read for its sentence on the restriction of monasteries under the Hongwu reign.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its statement that each county was limited to one monastery with a maximum of two monks is the one readable figure for the size of an ordinary Ming temple, and it points to a very small median; it is a tertiary summary of one edict, for which the page gives no year, placing it only under its heading for the 1370s, and describes as a one-moment suppression order with nothing on its enforcement or duration, so reading it as the dynasty's ordinary or median temple is this record's own inference; and it says nothing of the great urban monasteries.

Used for: the size of the median Ming temple (religion and death)
