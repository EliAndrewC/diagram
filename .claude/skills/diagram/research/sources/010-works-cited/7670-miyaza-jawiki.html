ja.wikipedia 宮座 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%AE%AE%E5%BA%A7) - 「It keeps no full-time priest; the members of the miyaza take the priest's role in yearly rotation, the tōya ("duty household") system.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「専任の神職を持たず、宮座の構成員が年番で神主役を務める当家（とうや）制を取る。」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the miyaza, the closed association of village households that ran a village shrine's rites.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the tōya system - no full-time priest, the members serving as priest by yearly turn - which is the pure-Shinto village arrangement the setting does not draw. Tertiary, regional in its examples (the Kinki), undated in this sentence.

Used for: the yearly rota in place of a priest (religion-and-death.html).
