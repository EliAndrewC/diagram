kiyosu-haisui-schedule

清須市 (Kiyosu City, Aichi), 令和8年度 水田の配水スケジュールについて (https://www.city.kiyosu.aichi.jp/kurashi_joho/seikatsu_kankyo/nogyo/tsusui_R7.html)

What it is: A municipal notice from Kiyosu in Aichi giving the year's paddy water-distribution schedule: the date supply opens in April, the days each block receives water through the summer, and the date it is shut off at the end of September.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a real irrigation calendar, which is the shape our field gates follow. Its limits are large and stated: it is present-day district practice in a machine-farmed landscape whose water comes by pressurized pipeline rather than by open ditch and field gate, not a premodern village's regime, and it is one district's year; only the season's SHAPE - an opening in spring, a shutoff before harvest - transfers, never a date.

Used for: a paddy water calendar from April to the end of September, delivered by block
