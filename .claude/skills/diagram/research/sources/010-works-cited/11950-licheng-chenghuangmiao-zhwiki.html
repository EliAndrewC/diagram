Chinese Wikipedia, 黎城城隍庙 ("Licheng City God Temple") (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E9%BB%8E%E5%9F%8E%E5%9F%8E%E9%9A%8D%E5%BA%99)

What it is: The encyclopedia article on the City God temple of Licheng county town, Shanxi, a national protected site, with its history from the Song, its layout and its dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a small county seat's City God temple, one courtyard with a gate and a main hall, and its area. Its limits: one temple in one Shanxi county, as it survives - the article names the gate and the main hall as what REMAINS (遗有), so its plan is the least the temple held, not its full Ming-Qing layout; its gate is Ming and its hall Qing, repaired as late as 1911; its area is a modern measurement of the protected site; and it is a stub encyclopedia article whose figures come from the Licheng county government's 2023 notice on its historic buildings.

Used for: the size of a county seat's City God temple (religion-and-death.html).
