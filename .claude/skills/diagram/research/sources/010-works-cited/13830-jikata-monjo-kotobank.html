jikata-monjo-kotobank

Kotobank, 地方文書 ("village documents", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%9C%B0%E6%96%B9%E6%96%87%E6%9B%B8-72435)

What it is: A dictionary aggregator's page carrying Japanese reference entries on jikata monjo, the administrative records Edo-period villages produced - land registers, tax records and the like.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where those records were kept and survive: in the houses of the families that served as village officials, nanushi and shoya. Its limit is that it speaks of where the papers survive today and does not say in which building of the house they were kept, so it does not by itself put them in a storehouse; it is Japanese only.

Used for: the village's registers kept in the headman's house (homesteads)
