気ままに江戸♪ 散歩・味・読書の記録 (an Edo-topography blog), 浅草御蔵（新江戸百景めぐり㊶） (https://wheatbaku.exblog.jp/30482848/)

What it is: A post on an Edo-topography enthusiast's blog on the Asakusa Okura, the shogunate's rice granary at Kuramae: its eight boat-entry channels for unloading the rice shipped from the shogunal lands, and the storehouses set on both sides of them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states plainly what the channels were for and where the storehouses stood, which is the arrangement our wharf granaries copy. Its limits are that it is an enthusiast's post, used beside the encyclopedia article it agrees with, and that its subject is the shogunate's central granary of sixty-seven storehouses, from which only the form and not the scale transfers to a provincial seat.

Used for: the boat-entry channels for unloading rice, with storehouses on both sides
