Ōtawara City, 大雄寺 (https://www.city.ohtawara.tochigi.jp/docs/2013082764829/) - 「Ridge span 25.5 meters, beam span 16.3 meters, single story, hipped roof, thatched (partly a temporary copper-plate roof)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桁行（けたゆき）25.5メートル、梁間16.3メートル、一重、寄棟造、茅葺（一部銅板仮葺）」)

What it is: A municipal page on a lordly Sōtō temple's thatched main hall with its dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it as a contrast only: a lord's family temple at 25.5 by 16.3 m shows how far above a village hall the great halls stood. Not a village example.

Used for: a lordly hondō of 25.5 by 16.3 m (religion-and-death.html).
