aric-sato

Association of Rural Infrastructure and Community (ARIC), "里の章: 我が国の農村の歴史について" (the chapter on the village: the history of our country's farming villages) (in Japanese; https://www.aric.or.jp/kiseki/jp/sato/index.html)

What it is: A chapter of an agricultural engineering association's online history of Japanese farming villages, from prehistory to postwar rural planning.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the village partition of the early Edo period fixed village boundaries with irrigation water and rights of common in mind, which is why the line was a matter of water and commons rather than of a mark on the ground. It is a broad institutional summary without references, written from the side of land improvement; it says nothing of how the line was marked.

Used for: what the village partition weighed in fixing a village's boundary (ways)
