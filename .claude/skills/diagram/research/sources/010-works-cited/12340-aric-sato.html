aric-sato

Agricultural and Rural Development Information Center (ARIC; 一般社団法人 農業農村整備情報総合センター), "里の章: 我が国の農村の歴史について" (the chapter on the village: the history of our country's farming villages) (in Japanese; https://www.aric.or.jp/kiseki/jp/sato/index.html)

What it is: A chapter of an agricultural-engineering information center's online history of Japanese farming villages, from prehistory to present-day rural policy, re-edited for the web from the center's own public-relations magazine (ARIC情報 Nos. 135-138); no author is named, and an English version is on the same site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the village partition of the early Edo period fixed village boundaries with irrigation water and rights of common in mind, in one sentence that names them as examples among the factors weighed (「such factors as irrigation water and rights of common to hill and field」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「用水・山野入会権などの諸要素」)), not as the only ones. It is an unsigned, broad institutional summary written from the side of land improvement, with a short list of main references at its end, none of them for this passage. It says nothing of how the line was marked, so that the line was a matter of water and commons rather than of a mark on the ground is our inference, not its statement.
