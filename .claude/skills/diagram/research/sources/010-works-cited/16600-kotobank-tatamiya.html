kotobank-tatamiya

kotobank 畳屋 ("tatami maker", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%B3%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entry and the Nipponica encyclopedia's entry by Endo Motoo on the tatami maker: when the trade arose, what the work was and what tools it used.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the tatami maker worked mostly at home and sometimes out on the job, which is why the trade fits an ordinary house-shop. A short reference entry on Japan; it says nothing of the workshop's size or where in a town it stood.

Used for: the tatami maker working mostly at home (urban-features)
