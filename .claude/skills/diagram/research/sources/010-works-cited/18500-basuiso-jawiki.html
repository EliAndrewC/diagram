basuiso-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, 馬水槽 ("basuiso, the horse-watering trough", title translated from the Japanese by this project), version of 2025-06-03 (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%A6%AC%E6%B0%B4%E6%A7%BD)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the troughs set by roadsides and in stables for draft animals to drink from, with the history of the surviving Japanese examples: the granite trough the London Metropolitan Drinking Fountain and Cattle Trough Association gave to Tokyo City, set up in front of the city hall in 1906, and later ones of 1917 and 1928.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the arrival in Japan of the free-standing public watering trough, and a newspaper headline it cites calls the 1906 one the first. It is a tertiary article; that no earlier trough is named is the article's silence, not a finding that none existed, and it says nothing of how horses were watered before 1906.

Used for: that the London-type watering trough reached Japan in 1906 (urban-features)
