umakato-noborigama

Umakato (Saga ceramics portal), "Let's walk the climbing kilns" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「登り窯を散策しよう」) (in Japanese; https://www.umakato.jp/waza/noborigama/index.html)

What it is: A page of a Saga prefecture ceramics portal explaining the climbing kiln - the stoking chamber at the foot, the chambers up the slope - and how the Edo-period kilns of Arita grew, the largest over 100 m and shared by several kiln houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the size the great porcelain kilns reached and that they were shared, which bounds the kiln's length from above. It is a promotional portal written for visitors, about Arita's porcelain industry, the largest in the country, and gives no source for its figures.

Used for: the largest climbing kilns, over 100 m and shared by several kiln houses (urban-features)
