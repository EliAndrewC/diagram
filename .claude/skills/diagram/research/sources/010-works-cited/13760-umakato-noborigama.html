umakato-noborigama

Umakato (Saga ceramics portal), "Let's walk the climbing kilns" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「登り窯を散策しよう」) (in Japanese; https://www.umakato.jp/waza/noborigama/index.html)

What it is: A page of Umakato, a Saga ceramics portal published by a private printing firm (Fukuhaku Printing Co., Ltd., copyright 2002), explaining the climbing kiln - the stoking chamber at the foot, the chambers up the slope - and how the Edo-period kilns of Arita grew, the largest over 100 m and shared by several kiln houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the size the great porcelain kilns reached and that they were shared, which bounds the kiln's length from above. It is a printing firm's promotional portal written for visitors. It describes Arita, a specialist porcelain-making district of Edo Japan, not an ordinary town's kiln. It names no kiln site and gives no source for its "100 m or more". The excavated Hizen kilns recorded on public heritage pages (Hasami among them) would carry the figure better; none has been read for this record.
