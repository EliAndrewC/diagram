citang-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 祠堂 ("citang, the ancestral hall", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%A5%A0%E5%A0%82)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the ancestral hall: what it was for, its kinds, the history of who was allowed to build one, and where halls survive.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a hall served for a lineage's meetings and feasts as well as its rites, that commoners were widely allowed to build them only from the Ming's Jiajing reign, and that they survive mostly in the south - why a south-China village has one and where it met. Its limits: a tertiary article whose statements rest on web pages, some archived; it gives no size or siting for a hall.

Used for: the hall as a lineage's meeting place, when commoners could build one, and where halls survive (homesteads)
