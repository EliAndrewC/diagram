Wikipedia, Shinbutsu bunri (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinbutsu_bunri) - 「A first order issued by the Jinguji Muka in April 1868 ordered the defrocking of shasō and bettō (shrine monks performing Buddhist rites at Shinto shrines).」

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the 1868 separation of Shinto and Buddhism.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the order that defrocked the shasō and bettō and sent them back to their shrines as kannushi - the clearest statement that a monk had been serving the shrine. Tertiary; the policy, not the village.

Used for: the 1868 order defrocking the shrine monks (religion-and-death.html).
