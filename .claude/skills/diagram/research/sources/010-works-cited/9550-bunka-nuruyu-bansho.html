bunka-nuruyu-bansho

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 仙台藩花山村寒湯番所跡 (the site of the Nuruyu guard post, Hanayama village, Sendai domain) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/137867)

What it is: The national designation record of the Nuruyu guard post, a kuchidome border post of Sendai domain on the pass into Akita domain, designated a historic site in 1963: its setting, its layout, and its surviving gate and residence.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the official description of one of the very few border guard posts whose buildings survive, and it gives the arrangement of gate, inspection hall and residence and the residence's size. Its limits are that the inspection hall is gone and only its base remains; that the buildings standing date from the Ansei era (1850s), the post's scale having changed over time; and that it is one post, on a mountain road, and speaks of watching the border and controlling trade, not of receiving officials.

Used for: the gate across the road, the inspection hall facing it and the road passing before it; the residence's size and its shoin-style reception room (buildings)
