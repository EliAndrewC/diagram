kotobank-debata

kotobank 出機 ("putting-out weaving", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%87%BA%E6%A9%9F)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entries and the Nipponica encyclopedia's entry by Tsunoyama Yukihiro on debata, weaving put out by a clothier to other households, set against uchibata, weaving done in the clothier's own house.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says put-out weaving was a rural industry, done by farm households as a side job in the slack season, and that the clothier's own weaving was a household industry - which is why weaving draws as houses and not as a works. A signed reference entry on Japan that says the arrangements are still a question for textile history; it gives no dates or numbers.

Used for: weaving put out to farm households and done in the clothier's own house (urban-features)
