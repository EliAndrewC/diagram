nonoichi-keihanritsu

Nonoichi City (Ishikawa prefecture), 「On the bund ratio」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦畔（けいはん）率について」) (https://www.city.nonoichi.lg.jp/soshiki/29/30267.html)

What it is: A city notice defining the bund ratio - the share of a registered paddy that is levee and slope face and cannot be cropped - and setting it for the city's subsidy calculations for 2025-2029.

Why it applies, and its limits: Nonoichi lies on the flat Kanazawa plain, so its 2.8% is a figure for how little of the paddy on a flat plain is bund, the counterpart to the hill figure. The city did not survey it: it is the average, over 2020-2024, of the farm ministry's statistics for the city's roughly 230-250 ha of paddy, a small and shrinking suburban remnant, and it is a share of paddy area only, not counting farm roads or ditches. It is a modern administrative number for consolidated fields, larger than old paddies, so the old share on the plain was likely higher; it is used only for the contrast between flat ground and hill ground.

Used for: the bund share of a flat paddy district (vegetation)
