kotobank-nagarezukuri

"流造" ("nagare-zukuri", the flowing-roof sanctuary), Kotobank's collected dictionary entries, among them Heibonsha's Encyclopedia Mypedia (百科事典マイペディア) and the older edition of the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia (世界大百科事典（旧版）) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B5%81%E3%82%8C%E9%80%A0%E3%82%8A-3140056)

What it is: Kotobank's page gathering several Japanese encyclopedias' entries on the nagare-zukuri, the commonest style of shrine sanctuary.

Why it applies, and its limits: Two of its encyclopedias count the style among the sanctuaries designated National Treasures or Important Cultural Properties - more than half, more than 55 per cent. The count is of designated buildings, which are the old and notable ones rather than a sample of village shrines; a further sentence on one-bay sanctuaries being the majority is about Kamakura-period survivals only, and is not used.

Used for: how common the flowing-roof sanctuary is (religion-and-death.html).
