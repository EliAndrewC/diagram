mlit-tokaido-mitsuke

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), Kanto Regional Bureau, Yokohama office, "東海道と宿場の施設: 見付" (the Tokaido and its post-town facilities: the mitsuke) (in Japanese; https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/03_sisetu/0401.htm)

What it is: One page of the road ministry's regional series on the old Tokaido through Kanagawa, on the gate-mounds at a post town's ends, read from a shogunate road survey map, a Hiroshige print and a late-Edo plan of Kanagawa's entrance.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the form of the mounds - banks either side of the road, fenced on top - and the measurements of one of them, from a period plan. The measurements are of a single post town's mound, and the page itself hedges on how much the mounds still mattered late in the period; it describes post towns on the main highway only.

Used for: the post town's gate-mounds, their form and their size (ways)
