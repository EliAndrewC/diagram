ja.wikipedia 長屋門 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/長屋門)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the nagayamon, the gate cut through a perimeter row house; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the perimeter nagaya round a residence and the gate opened in it, which is the range this page draws as the plot wall; no dimensions; Japan only, and its commoner holders are dated after Meiji, except rural samurai and wealthy farmers by permission.

Used for: the perimeter row house and its gate (government)
