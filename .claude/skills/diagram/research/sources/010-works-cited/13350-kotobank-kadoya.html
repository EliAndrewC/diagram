kotobank-kadoya

Kotobank, 門屋 ("kadoya, the gate-hut and its dependent household", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - the Nihon Daihyakka Zensho entry by Baba Akira, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, the Yamakawa Nihonshi Shojiten, and excerpts of the Sekai Daihyakka Jiten (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%96%80%E5%B1%8B-464959)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the kadoya, carrying several published Japanese encyclopedias and dictionaries: the dependent farmers named for the gate-hut on their master's plot where they lived, their regional names, and an excerpt on the huts and retirement houses inside a registered house plot's enclosure.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is our evidence that dependent households lived in gate-huts and huts inside a larger farm's enclosure, counted with the master's household as one hearth. Its limits: tertiary entries generalizing over the whole Edo period, with the arrangement strongest early in it; they give no size for the huts and no count of how many plots held them.

Used for: the dependents housed in gate-huts and huts inside a master's enclosure (homesteads)
