kakegawa-goten-bunka

Cultural Heritage Online (Agency for Cultural Affairs), 掛川城御殿 ("The Kakegawa Castle palace", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/188993)

What it is: The national cultural-properties database entry for the palace of Kakegawa Castle, an Important Cultural Property: its dates, the rooms of each of its three parts, and the Agency's note on why it matters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the room list of a median domain's palace - the ninomaru palace of the Ota family's 50,000-koku castle - divided into a hall-and-shoin part (with a consultation room and the karo's goyobeya), a small shoin for the lord, and an offices part, and it says the survival of the whole, offices included, is rare. It is one palace, rebuilt 1855-1861 after an earthquake, and gives rooms, not dimensions.

Used for: the plan of a domain's palace and where its council met (cities/capitals)
