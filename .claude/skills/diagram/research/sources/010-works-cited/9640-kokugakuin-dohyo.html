kokugakuin-dohyo

Kokugakuin University, an article on the dohyo rite (https://www.kokugakuin.ac.jp/article/50375)

What it is: A Kokugakuin University article on the consecration rite of the sumo ring, noting the same rite at sumo offered at shrines across the country.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the offered sumo and its ring at ordinary shrines. Its limits: no date for the village practice and no size for a village ring.

Used for: the sumo ring as a knob (Religion and the dead).
