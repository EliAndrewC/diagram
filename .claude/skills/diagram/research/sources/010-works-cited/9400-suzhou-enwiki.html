What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the city of Suzhou in Jiangsu, covering its history, its hydrology and its canals and historic districts, and describing the old city as a network of rivers and canals that, into the early twentieth century, linked it to the surrounding countryside.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for Suzhou as a premodern Chinese city laid over an engineered water network, the example given of a city whose water ran into canals rather than marsh. It is a tertiary summary of a modern city, its description of the canals is general and undated beyond "as late as the early 20th century", and it says nothing of where the fields' drainage went - that part of the claim it is cited beside rests on no page read.

Used for: Suzhou's network of canals, as the Chinese instance of a city draining into engineered water (water)
