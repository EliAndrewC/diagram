zh.wikipedia 班房 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/班房)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the banfang, the runners' duty room of a yamen and its later senses; a short encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the runners' station inside the yamen and its use as a holding room, which bears on where this page houses the runners; it says nothing of its form or of where the runners lived; a short entry that dates nothing.

Used for: the runners' duty room (government)
