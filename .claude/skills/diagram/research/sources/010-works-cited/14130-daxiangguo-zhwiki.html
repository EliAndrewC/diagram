zh.wikipedia 大相国寺 ("Daxiangguo Temple", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/大相国寺)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Daxiangguo temple in Kaifeng, the Northern Song capital, with its size at its height and a Song writer's description of the market held in its courts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ground, the number of cloisters and the population of the greatest Buddhist house of a Chinese capital, the upper end of a city temple. It is a tertiary summary; the figures are round ones, and the mu it measures in is not said.

Used for: a capital temple of 540 mu with 64 cloisters and several thousand monks, its courts used as a market (religion-and-death)
