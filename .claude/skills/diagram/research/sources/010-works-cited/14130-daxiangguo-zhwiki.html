zh.wikipedia 大相国寺 ("Daxiangguo Temple", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/大相国寺)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Daxiangguo temple in Kaifeng, the Northern Song capital, with its size at its height and two Song writers' descriptions of the market held in its courts (Wang Yong's Yanyi yimou lu and Meng Yuanlao's Dongjing meng hua lu, quoted in the original).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ground, the number of cloisters and the population of the greatest Buddhist house of a Chinese capital, the upper end of a city temple. It is a tertiary summary, and the sentence giving the ground, the cloisters and the monks carries no citation in the article, so those figures cannot be traced to a Song record; they are round ones, and the mu it measures in (a Song mu or a modern one) is not said. The market in its courts is firmer: it rests on the two Song texts the article quotes.

Used for: a capital temple of 540 mu with 64 cloisters and several thousand monks, its courts used as a market (religion-and-death)
