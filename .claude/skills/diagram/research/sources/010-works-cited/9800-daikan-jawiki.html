daikan-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 代官 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/代官)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the daikan, the intendant who administered land on a lord's behalf, chiefly the Edo shogunate's intendants of its own lands, with their staff, their offices and a list of intendancies.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for the size of an intendant's staff: about ten shogunal retainers (tetsuki) and a few locally hired clerks (tedai), and at one late-Edo intendancy a single intendant, three tetsuki and ten tedai. It is an encyclopedia article flagged for missing citations, and its figures are for the shogunate's intendancies, which varied in size.

Used for: the small staff of a daikansho, against the Chinese county's clerks (buildings)
