en.wikipedia "Burakumin" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burakumin)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the burakumin, Japan's outcaste communities, their historical statuses (eta, hinin, kawaramono), their occupations in butchery and leatherwork, and where they lived.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the communities stood on riverbanks because tanning needed the water and that they were registered and settled apart, as separate villages or as parts of towns, which is the siting this page's tanning yard and outcaste quarter follow. A tertiary summary used for the siting and the segregation; it gives no siege rationale and no distance.

Used for: the outcaste communities on riverbanks for the tanning, settled apart
