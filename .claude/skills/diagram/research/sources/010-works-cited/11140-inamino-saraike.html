inamino-saraike

いなみ野ため池ミュージアム ("Inamino Tameike Museum", translated from the Japanese by this project), さまざまなため池 ("Many kinds of pond", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.inamino-tameike-museum.com/what-is-a-pond/pond-various.html)

What it is: A page by the body that documents the pond cluster of the Inami plateau in Hyogo, describing the plains "dish pond", the valley pond and the stepped chain of valley ponds.

Why it applies, and its limits: It distinguishes the pond built on flat farmland from the one dammed in a valley, which is the distinction a map draws. It describes one region's ponds as they stand today, for a general audience, and gives no date or era for any of them: it says only that the ponds "were made", so it carries the forms and not their age, which must come from another source. Its only figures are that a valley chain runs to three or four ponds and that one plains pond, Kako-Oike, covers 49 ha; it is an explanatory page, used for the shape of each kind and not for measurement.

Used for: the shallow, flat-bottomed plains pond against the valley pond and the stepped valley chain (fields)
