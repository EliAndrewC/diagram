zh.wikipedia 吴门桥 ("Wumen Bridge", title translated) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%90%B4%E9%97%A8%E6%A1%A5)

What it is: The short Chinese Wikipedia article on the Wumen Bridge, the single-arch stone bridge over the Grand Canal south of Suzhou that carries the main way in and out of Pan Gate, a provincial protected monument.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a large bridge on the main way in and out of Pan Gate, near the gate tower. The page gives that nearness only as a scenic grouping with the tower and the Ruiguang Pagoda, not as a measured position. It never mentions a water gate, so the fact that Pan Gate pairs a land gate with a water gate, and so the bridge's adjacency to that water gate, has to come from another source. It dates the first bridge only to the Northern Song and the standing one to an 1872 rebuilding, so the present span, 66 m long and about 10 m high, is a late Qing structure and not evidence for an earlier bridge's size. It describes a bridge outside the wall over the moat-canal, and says nothing about roads or bridges inside the wall.

Used for: a bridge carrying the main way to a Suzhou gate stands beside its water gate (Ways)
