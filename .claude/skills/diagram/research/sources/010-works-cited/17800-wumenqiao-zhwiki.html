zh.wikipedia 吴门桥 ("Wumen Bridge", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%90%B4%E9%97%A8%E6%A1%A5)

What it is: The short Chinese Wikipedia article on the Wumen Bridge, the single-arch stone bridge over the Grand Canal south of Suzhou that carries the main way in and out of Pan Gate, a provincial protected monument.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a large bridge at a paired land-and-water gate of a canal city, beside the gate tower, which is the adjacency of bridge and water gate a city map draws. It is a tertiary stub with no inline citations; it dates the first bridge only to the Northern Song and the standing one to an 1872 rebuilding, so the present span, 66 m long and about 10 m high, is a late Qing structure and not evidence for an earlier bridge's size. It describes a bridge outside the wall over the moat-canal, and says nothing about roads or bridges inside the wall.

Used for: a bridge carrying the main way to a Suzhou gate stands beside its water gate (ways)
