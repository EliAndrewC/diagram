garan-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 伽藍 ("garan, a temple's buildings", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/伽藍)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the garan, the set of buildings of a Buddhist temple, giving the older seven-hall list from a Kamakura-period text and the Zen seven-hall list.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states which halls a full Japanese temple was held to have, old and Zen. The article itself says the lists vary by sect and era and that the name was used loosely of any large temple; it gives no sizes, and nothing on what an ordinary small temple held.

Used for: which buildings a full temple precinct held (religion-and-death)
