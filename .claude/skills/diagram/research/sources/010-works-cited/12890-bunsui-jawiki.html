bunsui-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 分水 ("Water division", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%88%86%E6%B0%B4)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on dividing water from one source or channel among its users, with a short history from the shrine-kept division points of antiquity through the village water disputes of the medieval and Edo periods; it lists three Japanese historical dictionaries as references but ties no sentence to them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that management of division passed to leading farmers and village organizations in the medieval period, that rotation rules (bansui) fixed channel widths and gate-opening times by locality, and that the upstream-downstream quarrel was the standing one - the frame for how a ditch was split between villages. It is a short tertiary article without footnotes, so it carries the general pattern and no single village's practice.

Used for: water division passing to village organizations; bansui fixing channel widths and opening times; the upstream-downstream quarrel (water)
