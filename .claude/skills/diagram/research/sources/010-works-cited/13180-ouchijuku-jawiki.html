ja.wikipedia 大内宿 ("Ouchi-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%A4%A7%E5%86%85%E5%AE%BF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Ouchi, a post station of the Aizu Nishi Kaido in Fukushima and a preservation district, describing it as a "half farming, half station" stop whose thatched houses still line the old road amid the fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a surviving instance of the quiet road town the record sets against the busy highway's, thatched and farmhouse-like. It is one mountain station in the snowy north-east, and what survives was kept by later preservation; the "half farming" character is hedged on the page itself as what it is thought to have been, and it does not say how common such a town was.

Used for: a half-farming station's thatched houses along its road (towns)
