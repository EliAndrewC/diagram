ja.wikipedia 在地領主 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/在地領主)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the resident local lord of medieval Japan's estate order and his moated seat on its own fields; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the lord's residence with its directly worked fields, storehouse, servants' huts, stable and ramparts, ringed by a moat that also irrigated, which is the individually fortified country seat this page draws; medieval Japan, earlier than the record's Edo and Ming-Qing anchor.

Used for: the fortified country seat on its own land (hinterland)
