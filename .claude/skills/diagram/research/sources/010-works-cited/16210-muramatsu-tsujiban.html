muramatsu-tsujiban

Watanabe Yoshiaki, 村松藩の辻番所 ("The Muramatsu domain's guard box", title translated from the Japanese by this project), on the author's site (in Japanese; http://www.ywatanabe.net/ronbun16.html)

What it is: An article by a member of a local history society of Muramatsu (Niigata) on the Edo guard box kept by the Muramatsu domain at a crossing in Kanda, with the institution's history, numbers, plan and staffing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a guard box's usual plan and roof and a count of the boxes in Edo. It is a local historian's article, its plan is hedged ("it seems"), its hut areas are the author's reading of an 1883-84 survey map, and its count of 899 does not match its own sub-counts, which add to 910.

Used for: the guard box's plan and size (urban-features)
