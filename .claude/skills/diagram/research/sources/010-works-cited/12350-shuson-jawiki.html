ja.wikipedia 集村 ("nucleated village", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%9B%86%E6%9D%91)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the nucleated village in settlement geography, with sections on the row village (roson) and the street village (gaison).

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out the two forms a village takes along a road - houses on both sides with little trade, or packed close with shops and lodging - and says they look alike in plan. The categories are those of modern settlement geography, borrowed from German usage, not Edo terms, and the article gives no share of villages in either form.

Used for: the row village and the street village along a highway (ways)
