Bakumatsu Guide (幕末ガイド), 幕末のピリピリした江戸に単身赴任！なのに緊張感ゼロな武士の日常系日記が面白い【酒井伴四郎日記】 ("posted alone to tense late-Edo Edo, yet a warrior's everyday diary with zero tension is fun: the Sakai Hanshiro diary", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2019 (in Japanese; https://bakumatsu.org/blog/2019/12/sakai_hanshiro)

What it is: A popular history blog's retelling of the diary kept in 1860-1861 by Sakai Hanshiro, a junior retainer of the Wakayama domain on duty in Edo, covering his lodging, meals and outings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says who shared his duty rowhouse unit and how they split the cooking, the one account read of how many unmarried retainers lived in one unit. It is a blog, not scholarship, and paraphrases the diary rather than quoting it; it gives no plan or size of the unit; and it describes one domain's Edo residence, not a country office.

Used for: three retainers sharing one duty rowhouse unit in 1860 (buildings)
