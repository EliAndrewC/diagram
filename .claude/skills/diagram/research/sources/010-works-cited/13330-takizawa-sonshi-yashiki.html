takizawa-sonshi-yashiki

Takizawa City, 『滝沢村誌』 ("Takizawa village history", title translated from the Japanese by this project), part 3, chapter 3, 屋敷 ("house plots"), and chapter 5, 本家と分家 ("head houses and branch houses"), as published on the city's website (https://www.city.takizawa.iwate.jp/about-takizawa/gaiyo/bunkazai-history/sonshi/sonshi-sonrakukozo/p20241125122940)

What it is: A chapter of the municipal history of Takizawa village, in Iwate prefecture, in the lands of the old Nanbu domain, on the siting of houses, the domain's rules for house plots, and the customs of branch households, published by the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Nanbu domain's survey rule of 1737 on how big a house plot a farmer was allowed (three to five se), the rule that a dependent nago got no plot, the order of 1715 that sent new branch houses to vacant plots, the field edge and the hill-foot roads, and a district's least allowance for a branch house - main house, stable and privy. Its limits: one northern domain's regulations, read through a local history that names its documents but is itself secondary; what a domain allowed is not what was built; and the chapter says nothing of a headman's plot.

Used for: the house plot allowed an ordinary farmer, the nago's lack of one, and where a new branch house was allowed to go (homesteads)
