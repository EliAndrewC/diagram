Chiba Prefectural Boso no Mura, 上総の農家, the museum's page on its Kazusa farmhouse (https://www.chiba-muse.or.jp/MURA/facility/page-1521181891660/page-1521294570333/)

What it is: The open-air museum's own page on its reconstructed Kazusa farmhouse, a village-headman-class compound of eight buildings rebuilt by traditional methods from nineteenth-century models.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the eight buildings of a headman's compound, the top of the farmstead ladder these maps draw the typical plot below. A custodian's page on a reconstruction; it describes one class of farmhouse in one province.
