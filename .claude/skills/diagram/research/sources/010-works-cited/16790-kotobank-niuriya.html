kotobank-niuriya

kotobank 煮売屋 ("cooked-food shop", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%85%AE%E5%A3%B2%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary entry and the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry by Suzuki Shin'ichi on the niuriya, the Edo shop that sold simple meals with tea or sake, and on how it multiplied after the great fire of 1657.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the everyday eating house of an Edo town, a shop selling cooked food with drink to working men, and it shows such shops crowding a busy place by the river. A signed reference entry centered on Edo and Osaka, the largest cities, and it gives no count for a small town.

Used for: the cooked-food shop and its crowding at Ryogoku (urban-features)
