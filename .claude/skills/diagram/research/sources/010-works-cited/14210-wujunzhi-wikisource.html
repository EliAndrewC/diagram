范成大 (Fan Chengda), 吳郡志 ("Gazetteer of Wu Commandery"), 卷三十一 ("Chapter 31", title translated from the Chinese by this project), completed 1192, Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/吳郡志/卷31)

What it is: A chapter of the Southern Song gazetteer of Suzhou, which enters the Daoist abbeys of the commandery (three in the city, the rest outside its gates and in the hills of Lake Tai) and then, under its own heading, the Buddhist temples of the prefectural city, each with its site and history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows how a Song gazetteer filed a walled city's temples and gives the count of Buddhist houses inside one great city. Suzhou was among the largest cities of the empire, so its count is an upper figure; the entries are the foundations the compiler chose to enter, not every chapel. Of the thirty entries, one (Qianyuan si) is identified by the compiler himself with another entry, the North Chan cloister (Daci si), and one (Nanchan si) is already lost, so they are twenty-nine houses. Among them are a sub-cloister of another entry and the city lodge of a mountain monastery. The Wikisource transcription is only partly proofread.

Used for: the Buddhist houses of the prefectural city filed under their own heading, thirty of them (religion-and-death)
