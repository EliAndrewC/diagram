koto-nakagawa-funabansho

Koto City (Tokyo), "中川船番所跡" (the site of the Nakagawa boat watch post), a page of the ward's list of historic sites (in Japanese; https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/103020/bunkasports/bunka/bunkazaisiseki/shiseki/16120.html)

What it is: The Koto ward government's note on its designated historic site of the Nakagawa boat watch post, the shogunate's checkpoint for river boats entering and leaving Edo from 1661 to 1869: who manned it, its size and its furniture.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the best-described river watch post found, with its size from the 1830 gazetteer Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko and its palisade, spears and river-edge hut from the illustration in the Edo Meisho Zue (1830s). It is one post, and an exceptional one - the checkpoint on the approach to the shogun's capital - so its size is no guide to a watch kept at a provincial office's landing. Updated 2025.

Used for: one river watch post's purpose, size, palisade, arms and river-edge hut, and who manned it (river cities)
