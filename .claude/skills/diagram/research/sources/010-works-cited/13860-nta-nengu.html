National Tax Agency, National Tax College, 平成14年度特別展示 ("2002 special exhibition", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 1. 年貢の時代 ("1. The age of the annual tax", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/tokubetsu/h14shiryoukan/01.htm)

What it is: A page of the Japanese National Tax College's 2002 exhibition of tax documents, explaining in outline how an Edo-period village paid its land tax in rice.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the order the rice moved in - to the headman first, bagged by the village, held for a time in the gogura, then sent to the lord's storehouse - which is why the headman's house and a village store belong together. Its limits: it is a short museum-style summary of the shogunal norm, with no regional variation, no dates within the period and nothing on China. It is a different page from the same college's 2018 exhibition page on paying the tax, which the record also cites.

Used for: the tax delivered to the headman and held in the gogura (homesteads)
