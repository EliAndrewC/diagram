What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kawaramono, the medieval and early-modern outcaste group of Kyoto named for the riverbeds they lived on, their trades in slaughter and leather, and the two explanations offered for the riverbed - untaxed ground, or the water the leather work needed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the name, the trades and the riverbed siting with both reasons, which is the riverside outcaste quarter this page draws. A tertiary summary; the name is one they were called, not one they gave themselves. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. Its subject is medieval Kyoto, earlier than most of what these maps draw from.
