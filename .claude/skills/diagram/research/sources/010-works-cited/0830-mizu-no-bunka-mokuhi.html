mizu-no-bunka-mokuhi

ミツカン 水の文化センター (Mizkan Water Culture Center), 木で作られた水道管 ～江戸時代のインフラを支えた上水道のかたち～, seminar report, 2022-12-26 (https://www.mizu.gr.jp/news/221226_report.html)

What it is: A report of a public seminar held in 2022 by the Mizkan Water Culture Center, a corporate foundation for the study of water in Japanese life, with speakers from the Tokyo Waterworks Historical Museum and the Tokyo Metropolitan Archaeological Center, on the wooden water mains of Edo: how the pipes were made and laid, and the lengths of open channel and buried conduit of the Kanda and Tamagawa systems, which it takes from the Meiji-era survey 東京通志.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the two systems' conduit lengths separately, which is what shows that a figure often quoted for Edo belongs to the Kanda system alone; our drawn capital's buried mains are sized from the pair. Its limits are that it is a seminar report rather than the survey it cites, that the Meiji survey it rests on measured the systems at the end of their life, and that Edo was the largest city in the country, so its conduit lengths are an upper bound no provincial capital approaches.

Used for: the Kanda system's 67 km and the Tamagawa's 84.7 km of stone and wooden conduit
