kotobank 元禄国絵図 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%85%83%E7%A6%84%E5%9B%BD%E7%B5%B5%E5%9B%B3)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the reference-encyclopedia entry on the Genroku provincial maps, the shogunate's 1697 survey of every province: its policy for drawing boundaries and recording village yields, and how disputes and variances from the older maps were settled.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the district boundaries within a province - not domain borders - were to be drawn clearly and variances settled by inquiry before the map was made up, which is the authenticity this page claims for a drawn line. A tertiary summary of a published reference work, used for the survey's policy.

Used for: the Genroku survey's boundary policy
