ja.wikipedia 出雲国造 ("Izumo no kuni no miyatsuko", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/出雲国造)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the hereditary office of the Izumo kokuso, the chief priesthood of the Izumo shrine, held in the Senge and Kitajima lines, with the list of its holders by generation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It numbers the generations of one hereditary shrine priesthood down to the present holder, which is the length such an office could run. The early generations are traditional rather than historical, and the page numbers the recent holders inconsistently, calling the present holder's father both the 83rd and the 84th.

Used for: a shrine priesthood counted past eighty generations, passed from father to son (religion-and-death)
