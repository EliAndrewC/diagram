kojodan-higuchi

Kojodan (castle-visit site), notes on the former Higuchi residence, Matsushiro (in Japanese; https://kojodan.jp/castle/61/memo/478.html)

What it is: A guide note (updated 2019) on Kojodan, a castle-visiting community site, on the former Higuchi residence at Matsushiro, the house of a 230-koku Sanada-domain retainer in the upper-rank samurai quarter, with photographs from the Sanada Treasure Museum.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where the house's storehouse stood: behind the main house lies a garden, and the storehouse stands beyond it. Its limits are that it is a site's guide note, partly hearsay, not a survey; one house, of an upper-rank retainer whose grounds were larger than a lesser samurai's; the ground behind the house is a garden, with a pond; the grounds as restored and landscaped today; no sizes.

Used for: where a residence's storehouse stood (buildings.html).
