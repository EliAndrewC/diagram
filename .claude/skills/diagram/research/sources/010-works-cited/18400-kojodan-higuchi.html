kojodan-higuchi

Kojodan (castle-visit site), notes on the former Higuchi residence, Matsushiro (in Japanese; https://kojodan.jp/castle/61/memo/478.html)

What it is: A visitor's note on a castle-and-heritage site, on the former Higuchi residence at Matsushiro, a Sanada-domain retainer's house.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where the house's storehouse stood: behind the main house lies a yard, and the storehouse stands beyond it. Its limits are that it is a visitor's note, not a survey; one house; the grounds as restored today; no sizes.

Used for: where a residence's storehouse stood (buildings.html).
