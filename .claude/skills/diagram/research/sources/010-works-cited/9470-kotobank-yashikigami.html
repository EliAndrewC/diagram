kotobank-yashikigami

Kotobank, 屋敷神 (the household god), entries from Heibonsha's World Encyclopedia (revised edition), Shogakukan's Encyclopedia Nipponica and others (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7%E7%A5%9E-143651)

What it is: The Kotobank page gathering dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the yashikigami, the god of a household's own plot: where it is kept, in what form, and its Korean counterpart.

Why it applies, and its limits: A standard encyclopedia, signed by a folklorist, says the god was kept in a corner of the lot or a plot joined to it, in a small shrine or at an old tree or stone. Its limits are that it describes a folk custom recorded by twentieth-century folklore, largely among farming households, and does not speak of samurai houses or offices.

Used for: the household god in a corner of the lot, in a small shrine or at an old tree or stone (buildings)
