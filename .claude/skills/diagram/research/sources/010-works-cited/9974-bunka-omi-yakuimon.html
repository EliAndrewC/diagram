bunka-omi-yakuimon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 旧尾見家住宅薬医門 (The yakuimon of the former Omi house) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/292830)

What it is: The national database entry for a registered gate standing south of the former Omi house's main building: a one-bay, one-doorway yakuimon with a thatched roof on a sasu truss, with its frontage in meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a second surveyed width for the one-bay gate form. Its limits: the entry does not say whose house it was, and the thatch and truss are a rural house's, not a samurai's or an office's.

Used for: a one-bay yakuimon 2.5 m across its frontage (buildings)
