bunka-omi-yakuimon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 旧尾見家住宅薬医門 (The yakuimon of the former Omi house) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/292830)

What it is: The national database entry for a registered gate built in the Meiji period (1883-1897), standing south of the former Omi house's main building in Chikusei, Ibaraki: a one-bay, one-doorway yakuimon with a thatched roof on a sasu truss, with its frontage in meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a second surveyed width for the one-bay gate form. Its limits: the gate was built between 1883 and 1897, after the Edo period, so its 2.5 m was measured on a late gate built the old way, with no metal fittings, rather than on an Edo-period gate; it is one gate in one Kanto prefecture; the entry does not say whose house it was; and the thatch on a sasu truss is how farmhouses were built, not how a samurai's house or an office was.

Used for: a one-bay yakuimon 2.5 m across its frontage (buildings)
