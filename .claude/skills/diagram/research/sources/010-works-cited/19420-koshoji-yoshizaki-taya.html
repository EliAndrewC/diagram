koshoji-yoshizaki-taya

Kōshōji head temple (本山興正寺), 興正寺史話 55, 蓮如上人の行実 その二 ～吉崎での教化～ (2019) (in Japanese; https://koshoji.or.jp/shiwa_55.html)

What it is: One of a Shin Buddhist head temple's short history talks, on Rennyo's years at Yoshizaki from 1471, where a town grew up round his temple and lodging houses (taya) for the priests stationed there were built.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the priests' lodging houses round a temple to the 1470s. Its limits: it is the sect's own popular history; it does not count the houses, lay out where they stood or say who lived in them besides the priests and visiting believers - the priests' wives are in the encyclopedia article on bōmori in Kotobank.

Used for: the priests' houses built round the Yoshizaki temple in the fifteenth century (religion-and-death.html).
