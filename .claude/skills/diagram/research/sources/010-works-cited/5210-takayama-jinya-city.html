Takayama City, 高山陣屋跡 (https://www.city.takayama.lg.jp/kurashi/1000021/1000119/1000847/1000954/1000956.html)

What it is: The city of Takayama's official page for 高山陣屋跡 (the site of the Takayama Jin'ya), the only one still standing of the more than fifty offices from which the Tokugawa shogunate's intendants (代官, later 郡代) governed its directly held lands, published by the city's board of education; the site itself is owned by Gifu Prefecture (the front plaza by the city) and managed by the prefecture's board of education. It lists the buildings and how they are built, notes the one structure moved in from the castle and when (the rice storehouse, 御蔵, in 1695), and gives the extent of the protected ground.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the strongest source in the record for what a provincial office contained: an intact compound described by the body that keeps it - the examination hall and its court, both paved with coarse cobble (グリ石) and roofed, the rice storehouse moved from the castle, the office range of 1816 - and its designated area. Its limits are that this is the only survivor of more than fifty such offices, in a mountain province under direct shogunal rule; that the compound served as a modern government office from Meiji until 1969, so what stands is the product of a 1970-1983 restoration that returned it only "almost" (ほぼ) to its Edo form, and the intendant's residence is a 1992-1996 reconstruction guided by a 1991 excavation (other accounts, not this page, add an 1830 drawing); and that the area figure, 11,219 square meters, is an exact sum of ten surveyed parcels under a modern legal boundary accumulated across four designations between 1929 and 1989, not a measured Edo footprint - so the compound area our maps derive from it, the total less the front plaza, carries the hedge rather than the source.

Used for: the 吟味所 standing with the 白州 (「The ginmisho (examination hall) and the shirasu are characteristically laid with guri stone (coarse cobble ballast) and roofed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「吟味所、白州はグリ石敷で屋根のあることが特徴的である」)); the 御蔵 moved from the castle's third bailey in 1695; the gate (御門) and the gatekeepers' house (門番所), both of 1832, listed separately; the designated site figure 「Number/quantity: 11,219.05 square meters」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「員数 11,219.05平方メートル」) (員数 is the register's standard quantity field; the page lists the ten parcels, which sum exactly to it)
