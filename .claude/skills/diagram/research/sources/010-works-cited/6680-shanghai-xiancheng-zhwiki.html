zh.wikipedia 上海县城 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%8A%E6%B5%B7%E8%80%81%E5%9F%8E%E5%8E%A2)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on 上海县城, the old walled county town of Shanghai, describing among other things the wall first thrown up around the town in 1553 against pirates coming up the river, and its roughly circular rather than square outline.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for two things about a walled town: that a Chinese county seat could go unwalled for a long time and then be walled in a hurry under a specific threat, and that such a wall could take a round outline rather than the textbook square. It is a tertiary summary taken where its references support it, about one town in the Yangtze delta, and while the record likes the idea of a wall raised very fast, the article does not put a season on it, so the speed we imply is ours rather than the source's.

Used for: 「In 1553, in order to fend off the wokou (Japanese pirates) coming up the river, a city wall encircling the town was built for the first time.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「1553年为抵御溯江而上的倭寇第一次修建了环城的城墙」) and its 圆形轮廓; "one season" not stated
