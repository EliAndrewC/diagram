kanazawa-honda-kamiyashiki

Kanazawa City (Cultural Properties Protection Division), 本多家上屋敷西面門跡及び塀跡 ("The west gate site and wall site of the Honda family's upper estate", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www4.city.kanazawa.lg.jp/soshikikarasagasu/bunkazaihogoka/gyomuannai/3/1/1/siteibunkazai/5/5812.html)

What it is: The city's register entry for a municipal historic site, the remains of the west gate and wall of the upper estate of the Honda house, one of the eight elder houses of the Kaga domain, designated in 2013: what the estate was, its size, and when the house received it.

Why it applies, and its limits: A city's own heritage office gives the size of a great retainer house's estate in a castle town and says it was also where the house's retainers served and ran its affairs, which is our upper anchor for a lineage compound and a witness that such an estate held the house's own people. It is one house of the largest domain in Japan, far above a mid-sized domain's elders, so it bounds the range from above rather than setting a typical size; the entry is brief and gives the area only as "over 10,000 tsubo".

Used for: a great retainer house's estate of over 10,000 tsubo, where the house's retainers served and ran its affairs (cities/capitals)
