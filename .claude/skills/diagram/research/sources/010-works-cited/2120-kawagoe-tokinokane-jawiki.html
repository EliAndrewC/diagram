What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Kawagoe's time-bell tower, the three-story wooden bell tower that has rung the hours over the old castle town since the seventeenth century and stands rebuilt after the fire of 1893.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tower's height and its three stories, the one Japanese time-bell tower this page sizes against. A tertiary summary; it gives no base dimension and the town's population is not on it. The 16 m is the height of the 1894 rebuilding after the town fire, the fourth tower on the site, not a measured Edo tower.

Used for: the Kawagoe bell tower's 16 m and three stories
