"Harie district", Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/針江区)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the Harie district: its spring water, the kabata basins, the channels within the settlement and the Important Cultural Landscape designation it shares with the neighboring Shimofuri district.

Why it applies, and its limits: It corroborates the district's own page on the one point the record turns on - channels INSIDE the settlement, feeding a named stream down its center. Its limits are the ordinary ones for an encyclopedia: it is tertiary, it cites the designation rather than a survey, it is used here only for what the community's own site already says, and its one date for the arrangement is a hearsay line that kabata already existed in the Yayoi period - which dates the water-huts rather than the layout of the channels, so like the district's own page it is evidence of the FORM and not of its age.

Used for: the waterways within the settlement, and the stream through its center
