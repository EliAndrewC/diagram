murakami-castle-population

Murakami Castle visual reconstruction project, "大都市だった？ 村上城下（２）" ("Was it a great city? Murakami castle town (2)", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://vr-murakamicastle.jp/nazo/nazo8-2.html)

What it is: A page of an independent website reconstructing Murakami castle (it names no author or institution; first written 2007, last updated 2017), comparing the size and population of Echigo's towns around 1700 - Takada, Murakami, Niigata, Nagaoka and others - and, in an aside, quoting the national population ranking of 1886 from the Meiji-Taisho statistical compendium 明治大正国勢要覧.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Takada's population, from a named statistical compendium, which the bathhouse count of the castle town can be set against. Its limits are that the figure is of 1886, almost two decades after the Edo period, and counts the town after the domains were abolished (1871), when many former samurai households had left the castle towns, so it may understate the Edo-period town (the page itself notes Takada's population was small for its area even around 1700); that the figure is second-hand, copied by the site from the compendium, which we have not read; and that the site is an anonymous individual's reconstruction project, neither academic nor institutional.
