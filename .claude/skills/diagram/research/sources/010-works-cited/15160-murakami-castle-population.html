murakami-castle-population

Murakami Castle visual reconstruction project, "大都市だった？ 村上城下（２）" ("Was it a great city? Murakami castle town (2)", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://vr-murakamicastle.jp/nazo/nazo8-2.html)

What it is: A page of a website reconstructing Murakami castle, comparing the populations of the castle towns of Echigo - Takada, Murakami, Niigata, Nagaoka and others - and citing the national population ranking of 1886.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Takada's population, from a named statistical compendium, which the bathhouse count of the castle town can be set against. Its limits are that the figure is of 1886, almost two decades after the Edo period, and counts a town whose samurai had by then lost their status; and that the site is a local heritage project, not an academic one.

Used for: Takada's population in 1886 (urban-features)
