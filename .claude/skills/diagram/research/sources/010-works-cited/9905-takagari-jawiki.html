takagari-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "鷹狩" (takagari, falconry) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%B7%B9%E7%8B%A9)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on falconry, covering its history in Japan from antiquity through the Edo shogunate's falconry establishment, the birds used, and a section on the hawking dogs that flushed game and the dog-handlers who trained them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the plainest readable account that the warrior elite hunted with hawks and dogs, and of who kept the dogs in the shogun's service. It is a tertiary, encyclopedic summary; its detail on staffing is the shogun's establishment, not a provincial household's, and it describes no kennel building.

Used for: hawking among the daimyo; the hawking dogs and their handlers under the chief falconer (buildings)
