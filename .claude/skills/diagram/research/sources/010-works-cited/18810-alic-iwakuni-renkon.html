alic-iwakuni-renkon

Agriculture and Livestock Industries Corporation (ALIC), "Producing district report: Yamaguchi prefecture, JA Iwakuni-shi", Yasai Joho monthly, February 2015 (in Japanese; https://vegetable.alic.go.jp/yasaijoho/santi/1502_santi1.html)

What it is: A report in the vegetable-market monthly of a government agricultural corporation, describing the Iwakuni lotus-root district (Yamaguchi prefecture): its origin, its growth, today's planted area and shipments, and its marketing.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its history is a modern retelling that hedges the 1796 start as what "is said", and it does not date the growth it credits to the domain's successive lords; the Iwakuni city tourism page gives a farmer, not the lord, as the one who brought the seed lotus. Its area figures (331 hectares in 1972, lotus about six times the rice today) are modern, the first after incentives to convert from rice, so they measure a modern district, not a premodern village.

Used for: Iwakuni lotus said to begin in 1796 and spread by the domain's lords on reclaimed land fit for no other crop; the one lotus area share read, a modern one (archetypes)
