nagaokakyo-take-takenoko

Nagaokakyo City Lifelong Learning Division, Cultural Properties Section, "Exhibition corner news no. 34: bamboo and bamboo shoots, from the Edo period to the Meiji period" (in Japanese; https://www.city.nagaokakyo.lg.jp/cmsfiles/contents/0000005/5235/hurusatofile34.pdf)

What it is: A two-page exhibition leaflet of 2008 from the city of Nagaokakyo, southwest of Kyoto, on the bamboo and bamboo-shoot trade of the Otokuni district from the Edo period into the Meiji period, from village documents it names and dates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out, from dated documents, the bamboo tax (agetake) the district's villages owed in the Edo period, the bamboo-grove management that grew in those villages, and the clearing of hill forest to make moso thickets in the 1830s, so it places managed bamboo stands in villages before 1868. It is one bamboo-specialist district near a great city, so it says little of an ordinary village's thicket; it gives no size or place for a grove.

Used for: managed bamboo groves in Edo-period villages (vegetation)
