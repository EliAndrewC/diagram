mlit-tokaido-michi

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), Kanto Regional Bureau, Yokohama office, "東海道と宿場の施設: 道" (the Tokaido and its post-town facilities: the road) (in Japanese; https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/03_sisetu/1101.htm)

What it is: One page of the road ministry's regional series on the old Tokaido through Kanagawa, on the road itself: the road works of 1604, a table of road classes and widths, and the shogunate's orders on road upkeep.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a width for the field road, the narrowest class, and a second wording of the 1612 order on filling ruts and hardening the road. The road classes come from a text the page itself calls "said to be" Ieyasu's testament, a later compilation of doubtful authorship, so the widths are a traditional scheme rather than a measured survey; the page misprints one width (mm for m).

Used for: the field road's 3-shaku width, and the 1612 order in a second wording (ways)
