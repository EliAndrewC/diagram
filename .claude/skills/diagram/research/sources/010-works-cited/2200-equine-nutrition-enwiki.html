en.wikipedia "Equine nutrition" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equine_nutrition)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the feeding and watering of horses, with the daily water intake of an average horse and its increase under work, heat and dry forage.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a horse's daily water and the multiplier under hard work, which sizes the watering a courier or wagon yard must supply. A modern husbandry summary standing in for a physical requirement that does not change with the century. The figure is keyed to a modern horse of 450 kg; the horses of a premodern East Asian post road were far smaller, so the intake carried onto these maps is an upper figure.

Used for: a horse's daily water intake and its increase under work
