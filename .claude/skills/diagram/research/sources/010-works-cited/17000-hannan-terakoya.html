hannan-terakoya

Hannan City, "寺子屋 ～江戸時代の教育～" ("Terakoya: education in the Edo period", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.hannan.lg.jp/kakuka/syogai/syogai_s/bunkazai_shokai/bunkazai_arekore/1494826741043.html)

What it is: The city's cultural-properties page on the terakoya of its villages at the end of the Edo period, counted from the Nihon kyoikushi shiryo, with two more known from their masters' gravestones.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one village-by-village count found, and it shows the typical village with one school. Its limits are that it covers a few villages in one district, and that the survey it counts from missed some schools, as its own gravestone finds show.

Used for: how many schools a village kept (urban-features)
