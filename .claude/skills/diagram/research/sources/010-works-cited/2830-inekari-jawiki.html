What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the rice harvest: cutting in autumn, rack-drying in the field and threshing once the ears have dried.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that rice is cut in autumn and dried on racks in the field before threshing, which is why a paddy household's yard needs less threshing floor than a dry-grain one and why its season is the autumn. A tertiary summary; it gives no drying time and names no month for threshing.

Used for: rice cut in autumn and field-dried on racks before threshing
