What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kaison, the clustered village, the commonest form a village took of itself, with the ground it arose on and the row village it is set against.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states where a clustered village arose - the foot of a hill, a river terrace, a crossroads - and describes the houses gathered in an irregular lump, the knoll cluster these maps draw. A tertiary summary used for the siting and the form.

Used for: the clustered village on narrow dry ground where water could be got
