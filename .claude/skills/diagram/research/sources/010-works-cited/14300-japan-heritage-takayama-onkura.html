Agency for Cultural Affairs, Japan Heritage Portal, 高山陣屋（御蔵） ("Takayama Jin'ya (the onkura)", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://japan-heritage.bunka.go.jp/ja/culturalproperties/result/2060/)

What it is: The national Agency for Cultural Affairs' portal entry on the storehouse (御蔵) of the Takayama Jin'ya, a component of a designated Japan Heritage story; its summary says the storehouse was built as a rice storehouse in the third bailey of Takayama castle and moved to the office in 1695.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a government heritage body's short summary of the storehouse of Takayama, the one intendancy office whose main buildings survive (a fact from other sources, not from this page), so it settles what the building was for; it is a summary, not a survey, and gives no dimensions or capacity; it describes one mountain-province compound that inherited a castle's storehouse, with a frame from 1596-1615, rather than building its own; and the page itself calls it among the oldest and largest grain storehouses in the country, so it is an exceptional example and not a guide to the usual size of one.

Used for: the onkura was built as a rice storehouse (米蔵)
