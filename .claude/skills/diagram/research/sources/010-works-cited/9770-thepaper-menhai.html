The Paper (澎湃新闻), 故宫里的大水缸到底有多少口，这些大水缸的作用又是什么 (https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_4257673)

What it is: A short popular article, posted to The Paper by a government account, on the great water vats standing before the halls of the Forbidden City in Beijing: what they held, what they were called and how they were kept filled.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for the vats' popular name, men-hai, "the sea before the gate", beside the names taiping-gang and jixiang-gang. It is a modern popular piece about the imperial palace, not a county compound, cites no source for the name and gives it only as "it is said", so the name is recorded with that hedge.

Used for: the fire-water vats' popular name men-hai (buildings)
