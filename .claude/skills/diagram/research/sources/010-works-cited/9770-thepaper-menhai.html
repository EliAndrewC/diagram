What it is: A short popular article with no named author, posted to The Paper by a government account on 2019-08-26 and credited at its foot to the popular-history site 中国历史网 (China History Net), on the great fire-water vats standing before the halls of the Forbidden City in Beijing: what they held, what they were called, how they were kept from freezing in winter, and their number, metals and cost.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for the vats' popular name, men-hai, "the sea before the gate", beside the names taiping-gang and jixiang-gang. It is a modern popular piece about the imperial palace, not a county compound, reposted without an author from a popular-history website. It cites no source for the name and gives it only as "it is said", so the name is recorded with that hedge.
