en.wikipedia "Anagama kiln" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anagama_kiln)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the anagama, the single-chamber tunnel kiln of East Asia built into a slope: its form, its firing of days stoked round the clock, and the cooling that follows.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the firing's length and its round-the-clock stoking, which is why this page keeps the potters living at the kiln, and the sloped tunnel form the kiln glyph draws. A tertiary summary used for the firing and the form; it does not name the noborigama. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. Its account of the firing blends historic Asian kilns with modern studio practice, so the days-long firing is a modern potter's as much as a premodern one's.

Used for: the days-long firing stoked round the clock; the sloped tunnel form
