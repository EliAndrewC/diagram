NPO Jinken Center Nagano, 部落史概要 ("An outline of buraku history", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://jinkennagano.com/sirutasikameru/467/)

What it is: An outline history of the outcaste communities of Nagano prefecture (the old Shinano), from the medieval period to the present, published by a Nagano human-rights organization.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the kinds of place the Shinano domains settled the kawata in - their castle towns, the post towns and road checkpoints, beside an intendant's office - and says many were later invited into villages as guards. It is a short advocacy organization's summary without footnotes, it covers one province, and it gives no counts or plans.

Used for: the post towns, checkpoints and villages the quarters were also settled in, and the village guards (urban-features)
