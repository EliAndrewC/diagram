What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the daikon radish, its varieties and its sowing and harvest seasons.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the autumn-sown daikon's season, standing in the ground from early autumn into winter, which puts the kitchen bed's binding season in the same month as the rice drying. A tertiary summary used for the season.

Used for: the daikon in the ground through the autumn
