zh.wikipedia 治所同城 ("seats sharing one city", reached as 附郭, title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/附郭)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on counties whose seat lay inside the city of the prefecture above them, so that one walled city held a prefectural and a county yamen.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the Chinese answer to whether a regional seat kept a separate office for its own town: it did, a county yamen beside the prefect's, and a large city two or three. It is an encyclopedia article on late imperial practice and gives no plans or sizes.

Used for: a city holding a county office beside the governor's (cities/government)
