ja.wikipedia 紺屋町 ("Kon'ya-machi, dyers' town", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%B4%BA%E5%B1%8B%E7%94%BA)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia list of places named Kon'ya-machi or Kon'ya-cho across Japan, from Hirosaki to Hitoyoshi, with a one-line account of the name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the name most often marks the ward of an early-modern castle town where the dyers were settled together when the town was laid out by trade, and the list shows the name was widespread across Japan. A tertiary list page: its account of the name carries no citation (its four footnotes are about single wards, three of them from a hobby blog), and the list is of place names, some of them former names. It says itself that the name also occurs outside castle towns, so it is no count of castle towns with a dyers' ward. It says nothing of any one ward's size or of water.

Used for: the dyers' ward of a castle town (urban-features)
