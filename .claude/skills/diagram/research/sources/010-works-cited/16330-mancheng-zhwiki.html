zh.wikipedia 滿城 ("Manchu city", the Qing banner garrison quarter, title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%85%AB%E6%97%97%E9%A9%BB%E9%98%B2%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the separate quarters the Qing built for the banner garrisons and their families in cities across China, with how they were sited and what they held.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that describes a standing garrison living in a city as a place: a walled corner of a provincial capital laid out as barracks, with its own archery lanes, a firearms store, stables and a drill ground. It is the Qing's arrangement for a conquering army kept apart from the people it held. The quarters were built from the 1640s to the 1790s, the same centuries as the Edo castle towns, but for a different purpose than housing a daimyo's retainers. The article is an unsigned encyclopedia summary with no measured plans or sizes, and its list of the quarter's buildings has no citation of its own (its references are three Chinese journal articles and the Qing gazetteer of the Jingzhou garrison, none of which was read). So it is used for what stood in such a quarter and where, never for a size or a count.

Used for: the garrison quarter, its place in the city and what stood in it (cities/government)
