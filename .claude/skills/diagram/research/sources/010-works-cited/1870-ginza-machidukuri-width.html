銀座街づくり会議・銀座デザイン協議会 (Ginza Machizukuri Council and Ginza Design Council), 銀座通りの幅はなぜ27mなのか (https://www.ginza-machidukuri.jp/column/26/)

What it is: A column by the Ginza street-planning councils on why Ginza-dori is 27 m wide, tracing the Meiji street back to the Edo-period Tokaido's 7 to 8 ken and showing the modern carriageway is exactly that width.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the width of the shogun's capital's grandest through-street in the Edo period, the ceiling this page sets a castle town's avenue under. Its limit is that it is a planning council's column, and one street. The column is unsigned and undated and cites nothing for its Edo figure, which it gives as background, so the number is a modern planning body's retrospective assertion about the busiest highway in the shogunal capital.

Used for: the Edo highway width of 7 to 8 ken, Ginza-dori included
