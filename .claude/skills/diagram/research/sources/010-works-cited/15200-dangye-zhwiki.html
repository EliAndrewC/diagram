zh.wikipedia 典當業 ("The pawn trade", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hk/%E5%85%B8%E7%95%B6%E6%A5%AD)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the pawn trade, with a section on the trade in mainland China from its early history, the pawnshop's counter and its screen board, and the pawnshop as a multi-story building for storing pledges.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the Chinese pawnshop's building form, the other half of the Japanese storehouse. Its limits are that it is tertiary; and that the sentence on the building, though it gives no period itself, rests on a single footnote to the Macau Museum's page on the Tak Seng On pawnshop (德成按, opened 1917), so the storied pawnshop it describes is the south-China (Macau and Guangdong) pawn tower of the late Qing and Republican trade - one twentieth-century building standing for the type - and it cannot carry the form of a Song, Yuan or Ming pawnshop, which needs another source.

Used for: the Chinese pawnshop as a storied building for its pledges (urban-features)
