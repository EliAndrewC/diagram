What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the mulberry genus, with its cultivation for silkworm leaf; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the yearly pruning of leaf mulberry to 1.5 to 1.8 m, the bush height this page draws; the regime it describes is a modern plantation's, pruned once a year in the monsoon season and planted at 1.8 or 2.4 m each way, and it says nothing of crown width or of fruiting under leaf harvest.

Used for: the height of coppiced silkworm mulberry (archetypes)
