Yoshioka Yumi, Ito Maho, Nakamura Kimihito, Takimoto Hiroshi and Tsuchihara Takeo, "River water - groundwater interaction and groundwater recharge sources of the Tedori River alluvial fan seen from oxygen and hydrogen stable isotope ratios" (in Japanese), Journal of Groundwater Hydrology 60(2), 2018, pp. 205-221 (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jagh/60/2/60_205/_article/-char/ja/)

What it is: A 2018 peer-reviewed hydrology paper that traces where the groundwater of the Tedori River fan in Ishikawa comes from, using the oxygen and hydrogen isotope ratios of the fan's ground and surface water (river and paddy). The study was prompted by a 2015 landslide in the river's headwaters that muddied the river while the fan's water table fell.

Why it applies, and its limits: The Tedori fan is a Japanese fan under paddy, the landform the record's comb fans depict, and its abstract shows the water standing on the paddies recharging the mid-fan's groundwater beside the river's water. It also measures a disturbed state: after a 2015 headwater landslide, with the river's flow down, when the paddy water's share of the recharge rose because the river's fell. So it is cited for the physical process only (paddy water recharges the fan's groundwater), not for any share or amount, nor for any premodern practice, and only its abstract is cited.

Used for: paddy water as a recharge source of a fan's groundwater, beside the river (water)
