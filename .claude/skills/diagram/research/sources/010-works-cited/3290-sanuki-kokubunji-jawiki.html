What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Sanuki provincial temple, which takes its precinct dimensions from a 2011 guidebook published by the site museum's friends' association; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the excavated Nara-period precinct as 240 by 220 m, the one instance this page's ~218 m figure rests on, and it is the small end of the range the other provincial temples read give; the figure's own provenance is that guidebook rather than an excavation report, the site is an eighth-century state foundation, centuries before the period these maps take, and it is one site, with nothing on shrines.

Used for: the size of a provincial temple precinct (religion and death)
