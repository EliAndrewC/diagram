matsushiro-bukeyashiki

Matsushiro Bunkazai Volunteer no Kai, 松代の武家屋敷 (The samurai houses of Matsushiro), 2023 (in Japanese; https://ma-vol.jp/rekibun/%E6%9D%BE%E4%BB%A3%E3%81%AE%E6%AD%A6%E5%AE%B6%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7/)

What it is: A page by the volunteer guides of Matsushiro (Nagano), the castle town of the Sanada domain, on its surviving samurai houses: their gates, how their grounds were laid out, and the Yokota house, an Important Cultural Property, with its dates and sizes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a middle-rank retainer's house measured, the main house of about 49 tsubo on grounds of 1,012 tsubo about half in vegetable field, and names his stipend and post, which is the scale a posting's residence and garden are checked against. It is a local guides' page rather than a survey report, it describes one domain's town, and its account of the garden to the south and the farm ground is a general description of Matsushiro houses, not of one plan. The page itself names the pond gardens as the thing that sets Matsushiro apart (it calls the town a "garden city"), so a garden south of the house is one town's particular and not the pattern of every samurai house. The half in vegetable field is the grounds as they stand today; that the field fed the household in the Edo period is the guides' inference, not a record.

Used for: the Yokota house's 49-tsubo main house, its 150-koku owner, and its grounds half in vegetable field; the Matsushiro garden to the south of the house (buildings)
