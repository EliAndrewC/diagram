jawiki-torii

Japanese Wikipedia, "鳥居" (torii) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%B3%A5%E5%B1%85)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the torii: its purpose, its origins, its forms, the naming of several gates on one approach and a list of noted examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an overview of mixed footing, some passages footnoted to books on the torii and others tagged as needing a citation. It names the gates of one approach first, second and onward from the outermost, and lists a subsidiary shrine within a larger precinct (Tomouji-sha within Kitano Tenmangu) that has its own stone torii. It is a tertiary summary. The sentence on counting the gates carries no footnote and no date and states the convention as present usage, so it shows the naming but not that the naming is premodern. The subsidiary shrine is named only in a list of Kyoto's three curious torii, a section the article flags as possibly original research, with no source and no description: an oddity listed, not an ordinary case described. Its examples are famous shrines rather than ordinary ones, and it gives no distance from any torii to the hall it serves.

Used for: the counting of several gates on one approach from the outermost, and a subsidiary shrine with its own torii inside a larger precinct (religion-and-death).
