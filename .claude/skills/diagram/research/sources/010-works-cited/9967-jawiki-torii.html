jawiki-torii

Japanese Wikipedia, "鳥居" (torii) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%B3%A5%E5%B1%85)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the torii: its purpose, its origins, its forms, the naming of several gates on one approach and a list of noted examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a well-footnoted overview that names the first, second and third gates of one approach and lists a subsidiary shrine within a larger precinct that has its own stone torii. It is a tertiary summary; its examples are famous shrines rather than ordinary ones, and it gives no distance from any torii to the hall it serves.

Used for: the counting of several gates on one approach from the outermost, and a subsidiary shrine with its own torii inside a larger precinct (religion-and-death).
