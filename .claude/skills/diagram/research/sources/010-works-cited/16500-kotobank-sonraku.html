kotobank-sonraku

Kotobank, 村落 ("rural settlement", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the entry by Nakata Eiichi from Shogakukan's Nihon daihyakka zensho (Nipponica) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9D%91%E8%90%BD-90694)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the rural settlement; the Nipponica entry classifies villages by plan (gathered and dispersed, regular and irregular), by livelihood and by origin, and by where they sit.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the block village as the most general of the gathered forms, which ranks the clustered forms against each other. The ranking is a general one of settlement geography, not stated for Japan alone, and the entry gives no count or period.

Used for: the block village as the most general gathered form (archetypes)
