zakkoku-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 雑穀 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/雑穀)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on zakkoku, the coarse grains other than rice and wheat (millets, barnyard millet, sorghum and the like), their uses round the world and their history in Japan; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in Japan coarse grain was once an important staple, eaten as the main grain everywhere but Edo, until rice output rose in the Showa era. It gives no shares of the diet and no dates before the modern era, so it supports that coarse grain filled part of a village's diet, not how large a part.

Used for: that coarse grain filled part of the diet, behind the acreage-from-population rule (fields)
