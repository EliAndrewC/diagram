Visit Beijing (Beijing Municipal Culture and Tourism Bureau), 明代迁都北京时曾设'大五厂'，现如今都在哪儿？ (https://www.visitbeijing.com.cn/article/47yYgUFzCMj)

What it is: A popular history article on the Beijing tourism bureau's portal on the five great works the Ming set up when the capital moved to Beijing - the timber yards, the black-tile kiln, the glazed-tile works and the foundation-materials yard - and where each stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the five works and the Liulichang's job of firing tile and vessels for the court, which is what our capital's fringe tile works is modeled on. Its limit is that it is a tourism bureau's feature rather than the Ming administrative records. And it describes the imperial capital, whose Ministry-of-Works establishments and gate-signal apparatus a provincial seat had no counterpart of, so the form is stepped down two tiers by us. Its period phrase is quoted with no source named.

Used for: the five great works and the Liulichang's function
