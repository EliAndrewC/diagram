What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Willow Palisade, the Qing border earthwork of Manchuria planted with willow rows; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rows of willow planted on the levees and the levees' dimensions, which is the row-on-embankment form this page borrows as an analog; a border work, not a polder dike, and it carries no in-row spacing; the 5 chi in-row spacing the record carries under willow-palisade rests on the machine-rendered Baidu page alone and is not on this one.

Used for: willow rows on an embankment (archetypes)
