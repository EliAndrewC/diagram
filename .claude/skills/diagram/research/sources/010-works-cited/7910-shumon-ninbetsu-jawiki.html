ja.wikipedia 宗門人別改帳 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%AE%97%E9%96%80%E4%BA%BA%E5%88%A5%E6%94%B9%E5%B8%B3) - 「The compiling of the register was to be done every year, town or village by town or village, by the nanushi or shōya headmen and the town elders」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「改帳の作成は、町村毎に名主や庄屋、町年寄が毎年行うこととされていた」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the sect-and-population registers of the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for who compiled the register - the village headmen, yearly - beside the temple's certification, which is why the country monk's records need a writing room and not a building. Tertiary, and the quoted sentence is normative - what was to be done - rather than a description of observed practice.

Used for: the register compiled by village headmen (religion-and-death.html).
