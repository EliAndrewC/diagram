koden-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "荒田" (Koden, abandoned paddy), revision of 2021-10-30 (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8D%92%E7%94%B0)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on koden, ancient and medieval paddy that went out of cultivation. It gives the causes (poor soil, flood damage called kawanari, the difficulty of keeping up water works), the ancient code's split between nenko and joko, and how the code handled their re-opening.

Why it applies, and its limits: It separates paddy lost for good from a plot resting between crops, and its note on kataarashi is the one dissent read from the alternate-year definition. It is an anonymous encyclopedia article. That note gives no source of its own and is weighed as an opinion, not a finding. The article covers the ancient and medieval periods only.

Used for: the causes of abandoned paddy, and the dispute over whether kataarashi rested in alternate years (fields)
