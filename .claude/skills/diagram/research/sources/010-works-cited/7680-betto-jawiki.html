ja.wikipedia 別当 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%88%A5%E5%BD%93) - 「Further, some of the jingūji in which the bettō resided were also called bettō-ji, and the word sometimes refers to those.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「更に別当が居住した神宮寺の一部を別当寺とも称し、それを指す場合もあった。」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the office of bettō, the head of a temple attached to a shrine under the fusion of kami and Buddhas.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the sentence that the jingūji where the bettō resided was itself called a bettō-ji - the monk lived at the shrine's temple. A tertiary article on an office with many senses; only its shrine-temple sense is used.

Used for: the bettō resident at the shrine's temple (religion-and-death.html).
