zh.wikipedia 南校場 ("the south drill ground", Fuzhou, title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%8D%97%E6%A0%A1%E5%A0%B4)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the drill ground outside Fuzhou's south gate, the provincial capital's ground for military exercises and reviews from the Ming, with its later buildings and a footnote quoting a Ming gazetteer of the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a provincial capital's drill ground at a gate and gives its size, and its gazetteer footnote says barracks were built at its sides. It is a tertiary encyclopedia article: of its size figures only the Ming gazetteer's "broad four li" is carried by a reference, while the four-li round (given in the modern li of 500 m) and the "over thirty mu" are uncited, and the two do not agree (a four-li round is far more than thirty mu). The barracks line is only in the footnote, and those barracks were raised in the early Wanli years to house troops brought in from elsewhere against the wokou raids, an emergency measure rather than a standing arrangement; and it is one provincial capital.

Used for: a provincial capital's drill ground, its place and size, and barracks beside it (cities/government)
