matsusaka-hasegawa-bunka

Matsusaka City, Cultural Properties section, 旧長谷川家住宅（三重県松阪市魚町) 主屋 大正座敷 大蔵 新蔵 米蔵 西蔵 表蔵 離れ ("Former Hasegawa family residence: main house, Taisho room, storehouses and detached room", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.matsusaka.mie.jp/site/culture-info/kyuhasegawakejyutaku.html)

What it is: The city of Matsusaka's own page on the designated Hasegawa residence, listing the buildings and attached items the designation covers and giving the estate's frontage at the end of the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: The owner's statement of how wide a great merchant's frontage grew and of what stood on the lot. It is one exceptionally rich house; the walls and the inner gate it lists are attached items without dates or sizes.

Used for: the Hasegawa frontage of about 34 ken, and its storehouses and walls (cities/fabric.html)
