kusawake-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 草分け ("kusawake, the founding house", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8D%89%E5%88%86%E3%81%91)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kusawake, the person or house held to have first cleared the land and founded a village, and the standing such houses kept.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that founding houses, historical or legendary, held the village offices such as headman generation after generation and held first place at the places of worship and in the shared land and water - the link between the headman and the village's oldest house. Its limits: a short stub article resting on two encyclopedia entries it names in its bibliography, with no footnotes to individual statements, no period or region narrower than "before modern times", and nothing on where a founding house stood in the village.

Used for: the founding houses holding the headman's office and first place at worship and in the shared land and water (homesteads)
