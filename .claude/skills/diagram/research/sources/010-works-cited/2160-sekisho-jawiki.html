What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the sekisho, the barrier stations of Japanese roads from antiquity through the Edo period: their military, policing and toll-levying functions and their siting at passes and river crossings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the inspection-and-toll function and where the stations stood, which is the institution behind the tariff posts these maps place at the gates by the setting's canon. A tertiary summary; the historical stations stood on the roads rather than on a city's gates, a difference this page states.

Used for: barrier stations levying tolls, at passes and river crossings
