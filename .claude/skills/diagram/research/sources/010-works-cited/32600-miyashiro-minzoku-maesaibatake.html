miyashiro-minzoku-maesaibatake

宮代町史 民俗編 ("History of Miyashiro Town, Folklore", title translated), chapter 6, section 3, "屋敷構え" ("The layout of the homestead", title translated), the page "前菜畑" ("The front vegetable field", title translated), p. 564, in the Miyashiro town library's digital local archive (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/miyashiro-lib/texthtml/d100020/mp100020-100020/ht061560)

What it is: One short page of a Saitama town's folklore volume on the vegetable plot kept in front of a farmhouse: what grew in it, why it was kept near the house, and that it was given to mulberries while silk farming flourished.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the plot's everyday crops and gives the villagers' own reason for keeping it close. Its limits: it is undated folk memory of one town on the Kanto plain, written partly in the present tense, so its crop list is a modern one and not a list for an earlier age, and it gives no size for the plot.

Used for: the front vegetable field's everyday crops and why it was kept near the house (Homesteads)
