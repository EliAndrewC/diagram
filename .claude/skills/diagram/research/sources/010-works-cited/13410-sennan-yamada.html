sennan-yamada

Sennan City Tourism Association, 恋するせんなん ("Koisuru Sennan"), 旧庄屋・豪農 山田家住宅 ("The Yamada house, former shoya and rich farmers", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://welcome-sennan.com/tourist-spots/yamadake)

What it is: The tourism association's page on the Yamada house in Sennan, Osaka prefecture: a family of rich farmers who held the headman's office for generations, and its eight registered buildings of the middle to late Edo period on a plot of about 840 tsubo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists what one headman's homestead held - main house with entrance and kitchen wings, two storehouses, a front gate, a gatehouse and an earthen wall - on a stated plot, which is what a headman's or rich farmer's homestead is drawn from. Its limits: a tourism page, not a survey; one family in the Kinki region whose village was assessed at 1,600 koku, well above an ordinary village; and it lists the buildings without their sizes or arrangement.

Used for: a hereditary headman's plot and its eight buildings, gate and wall among them (homesteads)
