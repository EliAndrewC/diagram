kotobank-mizunomi

Kotobank, 水呑百姓 ("mizunomi-byakusho, the landless villager", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - the Nihon Daihyakka Zensho entry by Uesugi Masahiko and the Britannica International entry (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B0%B4%E5%91%91%E7%99%BE%E5%A7%93-3172144)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the mizunomi, the Edo-period villager holding no assessed land, carrying two published encyclopedias on who they were, their standing in the village and how they lived.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the landless households whose housing the record asks about: not full villagers, with no part in village government, living by tenancy, labor or a craft. Its limits: tertiary entries on Tokugawa Japan in general; they say nothing of where in the village the landless lived or how big their houses were.

Used for: who the landless villagers were and how they lived (homesteads)
