What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the horseshoe, with a section on Japan: the straw umagutsu used from ancient times, the systematic adoption of Western metal shoes from Meiji, and straw sandals lingering in farming districts into Taisho.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that Japan protected hooves with straw until Meiji, which is why these maps give a courier station a sandal supply rather than a shoeing forge. A tertiary summary used for that one fact; it does not say nails were unknown before Meiji. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources.
