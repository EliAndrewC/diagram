amagasaki-teramachi

Amagasaki City, cultural properties page on the Teramachi of Amagasaki (in Japanese; https://www.city.amagasaki.hyogo.jp/manabu/bunkazai_0/1028308/1028344/1028717.html)

What it is: The Amagasaki City History Museum's heritage-walk page (no references given) on the temple quarter of the former castle town of Amagasaki, Hyōgo, giving its temple count on a 1635 map and today, its place in the town and the reasons thought to lie behind it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a castle town's temple count and where its temples were gathered. Its limits: Amagasaki was a castle town, larger than the setting's county town; the quarter was laid out in 1617 by the lord's order, gathering temples from the castle site, the older town, the surrounding district and the lord's own connections, so its 20 temples on the 1635 map are a planned concentration rather than what the town grew on its own, and they are the quarter's count, not every temple in the town; its placement at the northwest corner by the samurai houses is a Japanese castle-town device with no counterpart in a Chinese county seat; the page is written for visitors, and it hedges the purpose it gives ("it is also thought").

Used for: a castle town's temple count and the temple quarter at its edge (religion-and-death.html).
