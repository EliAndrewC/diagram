amagasaki-teramachi

Amagasaki City, cultural properties page on the Teramachi of Amagasaki (in Japanese; https://www.city.amagasaki.hyogo.jp/manabu/bunkazai_0/1028308/1028344/1028717.html)

What it is: The Amagasaki city office's page on the temple quarter of the former castle town of Amagasaki, Hyōgo, giving its temple count on a 1635 map and today, its place in the town and the reasons thought to lie behind it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a castle town's temple count and where its temples were gathered. Its limits: Amagasaki was a castle town, larger than the setting's county town; the page is written for visitors, and it hedges the purpose it gives ("it is also thought").

Used for: a castle town's temple count and the temple quarter at its edge (religion-and-death.html).
