bunka-imado-kawarayaki

Cultural Heritage Online (Agency for Cultural Affairs), 今戸瓦焼図 ("Tile firing at Imado", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a painting by Aodo Denzen (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/377137)

What it is: The national heritage database's entry for Aodo Denzen's oil painting of the tile kilns at Imado on the Sumida river in Edo (late eighteenth to early nineteenth century, Kobe City Museum), with a note quoting the Edo meisho zue on tile making there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a city's roof-tile kilns on the river bank at Imado and says, quoting the period guidebook, that tile firing had become the place's specialty. It is a catalog note on a painting, not a study of the industry; it gives no count of kilns and does not say where Imado stood relative to the built city.

Used for: tile firing as the specialty of Imado on the west bank of the Sumida (towns)
