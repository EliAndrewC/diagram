Kawasaki City Board of Education, 「The water basin of Nagao Shrine」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「長尾神社の手水鉢」) (in Japanese; https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/880/page/0000146792.html)

What it is: The Kawasaki City Board of Education's short entry for a water basin at Nagao Shrine in Tama ward. The basin is listed under the city's local-cultural-property recognition scheme (created 2017 for objects not designated under the protection laws) as a tangible folk cultural property. The entry gives its three carved inscriptions but not its material or size.

Why it applies, and its limits: The basin is dated 1828 and inscribed as given by the whole body of the shrine's parishioners, a dated case of a parish giving its shrine a basin before 1868. The page says the basin stands outdoors today and says nothing of a pavilion over it then, nor of the shrine's rank.

Used for: a parish's dated gift of a basin (religion-and-death.html).
