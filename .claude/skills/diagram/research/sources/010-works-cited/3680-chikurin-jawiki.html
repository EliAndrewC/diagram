What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the bamboo grove, its near-monospecific growth, its place between the flat fields and the satoyama and its harvest; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the dense, nearly single-species community and the grove's standing at the village edge as a harvested resource, which is the stand this page draws; it says nothing of a hard edge.

Used for: the bamboo grove's form and place (vegetation)
