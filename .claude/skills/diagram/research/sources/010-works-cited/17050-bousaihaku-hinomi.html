bousaihaku-hinomi

Fire and Disaster Management Museum (online), Kazuo Tsuchiya, "火の見櫓は何を語るか" ("What do fire-watch towers tell us?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.bousaihaku.com/firetower/4293/)

What it is: An essay by a lecturer in design who, with a group, surveyed nearly a thousand fire-watch towers in Shizuoka, on their forms and dates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the towers standing today to the postwar decades, which is why a surviving village tower is no evidence for an Edo-period one. Its limits are that it is one prefecture's survey, and that the essay does not say what a village had before the towers.

Used for: the modern date of the fire-watch towers standing today (urban-features)
