en.wikipedia "Endorheic basin" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endorheic_basin)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the endorheic basin, the closed drainage whose water leaves only by evaporation and seepage, and where such basins occur.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a closed water body is characteristically a desert arrangement, which is why a terminal moat with no outlet belongs to an arid site and not to rice country. A tertiary summary of a physical-geography concept.

Used for: the closed basin as a desert form
