kawashiri-komegura-kunishitei

Agency for Cultural Affairs, 国指定文化財等データベース, 熊本藩川尻米蔵跡 ("Database of Nationally Designated Cultural Properties: the Kumamoto domain's Kawashiri rice storehouse site", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/heritage/detail/401/00003685)

What it is: The national cultural-property database's designation text for the Kawashiri rice storehouse site, the river port south of Kumamoto where the domain gathered its tax rice; it says what stood there, how much rice came in and where it went, how many storehouse buildings the old drawings show, and how long the surviving landing is.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an official account of a large domain's tax-rice store standing at a river port rather than in the castle, with its buildings counted and its intake given, which is what a provincial city's granary is drawn from. Its limits: Kumamoto was one of the largest domains, so its figures are the top of the range; the text gives no building's size; and the two surviving storehouses are only thought to belong to the old set.

Used for: a domain's tax-rice store at a river port: its nine buildings, its intake and its landing (buildings)
