zojoji-keidai

Zojoji (増上寺), 境内散歩 ("A walk through the precinct", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the temple's own site (in Japanese; https://www.zojoji.or.jp/keidai/)

What it is: The temple's own guide to its precinct and buildings, with the distances along its approach and the size of its sutra repository.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the distances from the outer gate to the main gate and from the main gate to the main hall, the spacing of a great temple's approach. The page ties both distances to Buddhist numbers (108 passions, 48 vows), so they are as much symbol as survey, and they describe the precinct as rebuilt rather than as it stood in the Edo period.

Used for: the approach of a great temple, about 108 ken from outer gate to main gate and 48 ken on to the main hall; a sutra repository 9.5 ken square (religion-and-death)
