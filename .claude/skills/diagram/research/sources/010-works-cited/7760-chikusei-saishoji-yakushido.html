Chikusei City, 最勝寺薬師堂 (a designated Yakushi hall) (https://www.city.chikusei.lg.jp/kyouiku-bunka-sports/bunkazai/city-designated/page000317.html) - 「Ridge span 3.83 m, beam span 3.83 m」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桁行3.83ｍ　梁間3.83ｍ」)

What it is: A municipal cultural-property page on a small Yakushi hall rebuilt in 1685, with its dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the smallest measured hall the pass found, 3.83 m square - a subsidiary hall on a temple's grounds, not a village's main hall, which is why it bounds nothing and only shows how small a hall could be.

Used for: a 3.83 m square hall of 1685 (religion-and-death.html).
