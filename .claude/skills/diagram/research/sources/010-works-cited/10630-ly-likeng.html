"Old villages of China, 54: Likeng, small bridges, flowing water, homes", travel diary by 瑞木鸿鸟 on the LY.com (Tongcheng) platform, 2020-09-11 (in Chinese; https://www.ly.com/travels/1125748.html)

What it is: A visitor's travel diary on a Chinese travel-booking platform describing Likeng, a village in Wuyuan, Jiangxi, founded in the Northern Song, whose houses line a small stream crossed by many bridges.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one account read of a village whose houses face each other across a small stream, the two banks joined by bridges. It is the weakest kind of source: one visitor's impressions of the village as it stands today, with no date for the layout; the Chinese Wikipedia article on Likeng confirms only that bridges are among what survives.

Used for: that Likeng's houses face each other across its stream, the two banks linked by a dozen or more bridges (water)
