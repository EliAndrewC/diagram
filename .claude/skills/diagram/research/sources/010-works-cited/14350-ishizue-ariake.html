ishizue-ariake

水土の礎 (the history site of land and water run by the Agricultural and Rural Integrated Information Center, ARIC), 干潟から沃野へ、有明海干拓 ("From tidal flat to fertile plain: the Ariake reclamations", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://suido-ishizue.jp/daichi/part2/03/07.html)

What it is: A short, unsigned page in ARIC's popular history of land development, on the reclamation of the Ariake Sea's tidal flats in Kyushu, stage by stage, behind successive sea dikes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the problem a sea polder's drainage met - the tide's range, the flats growing until the older land lost its outlet - and where its settlements stood, on the previous stage's sea dike. It is a popular summary without dates for most of what it says or sources for its figures, and it does not describe the outlets themselves or say how they were worked.

Used for: a sea polder's drainage problem and its settlements on the old sea dikes (archetypes)
