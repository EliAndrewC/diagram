What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on rape, the oilseed brassica, including what is grown after the second rice crop in Taiwan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rotation this record needs from the Chinese side - rape sown into the paddy after the year's rice is off, so the two never stand together. Its limits are that it is a tertiary encyclopedia and that its worked case is Taiwan, one island of the setting's Chinese half.

Used for: rape sown after the second rice crop in Taiwan
