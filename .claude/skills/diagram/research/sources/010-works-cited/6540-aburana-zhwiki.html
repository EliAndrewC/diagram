What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on rape, the oilseed brassica, including what is grown after the second rice crop in Taiwan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rotation this record needs - rape sown into the paddy after the year's rice is off, so the two never stand together. Its one worked case is Taiwan, where rape is broadcast after the second of two rice crops a year and ploughed in before the next transplanting as green manure, not grown for its seed; that case rests on Taiwanese green-manure guides and a 2013 newspaper item, in a short article flagged since 2010 as needing expert attention, and it does not say how old the practice is.

Used for: rape sown after the second rice crop in Taiwan
