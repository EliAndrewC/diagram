What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the doma, the earth-floored part of a Japanese house, and what a farmhouse did in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the doma's uses - meals after work, drying the crop, weaving sandals, tending tools - the working floor this page gives a household without a shed. A tertiary summary; it does not call the doma a store.

Used for: the doma as the farmhouse's working floor
