宋史 (History of Song), 卷二七三, the biography of He Jiyun with He Chengju appended, Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%AE%8B%E5%8F%B2/%E5%8D%B7273)

What it is: The Yuan-compiled official history of the Song, whose chapter 273 carries He Chengju's memorial proposing the dike-and-marsh belt across the Hebei frontier to stop the Khitan cavalry.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the primary record of a state deliberately making wet ground as anti-cavalry terrain, the defensive inundation this page names. A primary text read from a crowd-transcribed wiki copy; the passage carries no date.

Used for: He Chengju's proposal of the Hebei marsh belt against cavalry
