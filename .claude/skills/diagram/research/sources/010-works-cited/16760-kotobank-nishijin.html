kotobank-nishijin

kotobank 西陣織 ("Nishijin weaving", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%A5%BF%E9%99%A3%E7%B9%94)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary and encyclopedia entries on Nishijin, the weaving quarter of Kyoto that grew up on the site of the western army's camp after the Onin war and wove the country's finest silks on the draw-loom.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the example of a city quarter of weaving houses bound in a guild of draw-loom weavers, the one place a weaving district belongs on a map. It describes the imperial capital's luxury trade, a ceiling rather than a provincial norm, and gives no house or loom counts.

Used for: Kyoto's weaving quarter and its draw-loom weavers' guild (urban-features)
