kotobank-yakushuya

kotobank 薬種屋 ("apothecary", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%96%AC%E7%A8%AE%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting two dictionary entries on the yakushuya, the house that compounded crude drugs and sold Chinese-style medicine, with an illustration cited from the Edo Meisho Zue.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the apothecary as a house that made up and sold its medicines itself, the kind of trade the generic shop glyph stands for. A dictionary definition only; it gives no premises.

Used for: the apothecary compounding and selling at home (urban-features)
