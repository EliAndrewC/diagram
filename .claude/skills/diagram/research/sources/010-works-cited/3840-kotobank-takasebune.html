kotobank-takasebune

Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho and Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, s.v. 高瀬舟, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/高瀬舟)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the encyclopedia and dictionary entries on the takasebune, the shallow-draft river cargo boat, from the fifteen-koku Kyoto canal boat to the two-hundred-koku Tone barge.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the type's range and the large Tone boat's length, which set the frontage this page moors barges along; reference entries with no drawings; the 27 m Tone boat is the extreme of the type, an order above the 15-koku canal boat on the same page.

Used for: the size of a river barge (river cities)
