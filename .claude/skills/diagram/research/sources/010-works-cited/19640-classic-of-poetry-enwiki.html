"Classic of Poetry", English Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classic_of_Poetry)

What it is: The encyclopedia's article on the Book of Songs (Shijing), the oldest collection of Chinese poetry: its 305 poems, their dates, sections and transmission.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the collection's poems to the 11th to 7th centuries BC, which places a poem of it far before modern times. It is a general encyclopedia article, used only for that date; it does not date the commentaries printed with the poems.

Used for: the date of the Book of Songs' poems (water)
