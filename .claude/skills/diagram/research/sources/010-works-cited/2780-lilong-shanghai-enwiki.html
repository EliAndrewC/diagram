What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Shanghai's lilong lane housing, the dense terraces of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries whose lanes became the residents' shared living space.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the lanes were the public space all the houses faced and used, the socially live lane this page draws in a village. A tertiary summary of a modern urban form standing in for a village's; the scale and the century both differ.
