njg-horita

Morioka Hiroshi, 輪中と堀田 ("The waju and the horita", title translated from the Japanese by this project), Nippon Jitsugyo Publishing column, 2025 (in Japanese; https://www.njg.co.jp/column/column-41456/)

What it is: A publisher's column by a writer on Japanese surnames, explaining the surname Horita from the horita fields of the Nobi waju: the paddy half dug out, the mud piled on the rest, the dug part a channel for field boats.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the form clearly and says what the dug channels were used for. It is a popular column whose subject is a name, it gives no dates or figures, and it draws on the waju museum rather than on records.

Used for: the horita's dug channels traveled by field boat (archetypes)
