eisi-yashikibaka

Ikebuchi Sekizai Kogyo, "屋敷墓〜関東地方のお墓の一類型" ("House-plot graves: a type of grave in the Kanto region", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the company's information page, 30 August 2016 (https://www.e-isi.co.jp/cgi-bin/info/index.cgi?no=45)

What it is: A Nara stonemason's blog post describing the household graves the writer saw on a trip to Fukaya in northern Saitama.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes where such graves stand today: on part of the household's own land, in the middle of a field along the road or in the front garden. It is a present-day tradesman's impression from one trip to one town, not a survey; that such graves belonged mostly to long-rooted, influential households is the writer's guess, marked as one on the page.

Used for: household graves in a field or a front garden (religion and death)
