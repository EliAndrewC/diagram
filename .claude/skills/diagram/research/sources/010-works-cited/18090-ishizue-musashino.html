ishizue-musashino

水土の礎 (the history site of land and water run by the Agricultural and Rural Integrated Information Center, ARIC), 新田村落の典型、武蔵野 ("Musashino, the model new-field village", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://suido-ishizue.jp/daichi/part2/02/03.html)

What it is: A short, unsigned page in ARIC's popular history of land development on the row villages the Edo-period new-field development founded on the Musashino plateau west of Edo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a planned Edo reclamation's layout: houses in a row along a road, a long narrow strip of field and woodland behind each, fields allotted nearly equally in many villages at the start, and the rows running the way of the canals dug along the roads. Its limits: the land is dry field and woodland on a waterless plateau, not paddy, so it shows an Edo planned layout but not a paddy one; it is a popular summary without sources; and the "checkerboard" in its section title is the series' heading, not a claim about these villages.

Used for: the equal strips behind a row of houses on the Musashino new fields, and the rows following the canals (fields)
