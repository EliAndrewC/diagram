en.wikipedia, Five Punishments (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Punishments)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Five Punishments (五刑), the collective name for the physical penalties of the dynastic Chinese legal system, tracing them from the ancient mutilations to the Sui-to-Qing set of beating, penal servitude, exile and death, with a brief survey of the Qing forms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the two bamboo beatings (笞 and 杖) as statutory punishments of the Qing code, which is what the justice works' "beating" is. It is a tertiary summary; it defines each punishment and says nothing of where or before whom a beating was carried out, so the yamen courtyard as its place rests on other pages or on our reading.

Used for: the light and heavy bamboo as the Qing code's beatings (the punishment ground in town)
