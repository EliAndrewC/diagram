chinagate-tongxiang

China.org.cn (中国网), 中国特色产品推介 - 桐乡杭白菊 ("Chinese specialty products: Tongxiang's Hang white chrysanthemum", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (http://cn.chinagate.cn/product/2013-04/09/content_28487282.htm)

What it is: A 2013 product-promotion page on a state news portal about the white chrysanthemum grown as a medicinal and tea flower in Tongxiang, Zhejiang, with a short account of its origins.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a Chinese instance of chrysanthemums grown as a field crop for sale, which the Japanese sources lack, but the crop it describes is the present-day one (one of the "Zhejiang eight" medicinal herbs, sold nationwide), not a premodern one. A promotional page with no footnotes. Its origin story is a tradition it repeats, not a documented history: Southern Song officials settled at Tongxiang and "built gardens and planted chrysanthemums" (建园栽菊), which is gardens, not fields. The Song text it quotes (a "Zhifu quanji" 致富全集) cannot be identified as a Song work, and the passage is about flower displays at Lin'an, not Tongxiang. It gives no field sizes, and its modern output figures (1 billion yuan a year; "10,000,000 tonnes" a year, plainly an error) must not be used.

Used for: the chrysanthemum as a dried medicinal flower crop in Tongxiang, and the tradition of its planting there since the Southern Song (fields)
