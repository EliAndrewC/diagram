范成大 (Fan Chengda), 吳郡志 ("Gazetteer of Wu Commandery"), 卷三十二 ("Chapter 32", title translated from the Chinese by this project), completed 1192, Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/吳郡志/卷32)

What it is: A chapter of the Southern Song gazetteer of Suzhou that opens its entries on the temples outside the city wall, beginning with the temple on Tiger Hill.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the gazetteer filing the temples outside the wall under their own heading, 郭外寺 ("temples outside the outer wall"). The matching heading for the temples inside the wall, 府郭寺, opens the chapter before this one (chapter 31), which this entry does not cite. So this chapter alone shows the "outside" half of the division. One gazetteer of one great city. The Wikisource transcription is marked 25% and miscopies characters (憚 for 禪 throughout), so it carries headings and the order of entries, not exact wording.

Used for: the temples outside the wall filed under their own heading (religion-and-death)
