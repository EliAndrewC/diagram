chikuma-obasute

Chikuma city, 姨捨の棚田とは? ("What are the Obasute terraces?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.chikuma.lg.jp/soshiki/norin/kanko/3/1659.html)

What it is: The city of Chikuma's (Nagano prefecture) page on the Obasute terraced paddies, a designated scenic place: the slope they lie on, their altitude, and how many paddies lie in how many hectares.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a slope and a paddy count over a stated area for one famous terrace landscape, which is what an average paddy size is worked out from. The figures are rounded tourism-page summaries ("about"), with no survey method given, and they count the terraces as they are now, kept by preservation groups under a paddy-rental scheme. The page gives two areas that disagree: about 1,800 paddies in 75 ha overall, and about 1,500 in some 40 ha of the protected area. So the 75 ha takes in walls, banks and other ground that is not paddy, and an average paddy size worked out from it is gross, not net: about 420 square meters against about 270. It is one steep mountain landscape (a 1/7 slope), dated to a 1578 mention and developed from the Edo period on, and it gives no wall height and no date for the paddies' present size.

Used for: a terrace's slope and the number of paddies to its area (archetypes)
