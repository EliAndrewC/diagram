chikuma-obasute

Chikuma city, 姨捨の棚田とは? ("What are the Obasute terraces?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.chikuma.lg.jp/soshiki/norin/kanko/3/1659.html)

What it is: The city of Chikuma's (Nagano prefecture) page on the Obasute terraced paddies, a designated scenic place: the slope they lie on, their altitude, and how many paddies lie in how many hectares.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a slope and a paddy count over a stated area for one famous terrace landscape, which is what an average paddy size is worked out from. The count is of the terraces as they are now, after some reconstruction, and it gives no wall height and no date for the paddies' present size.

Used for: a terrace's slope and the number of paddies to its area (archetypes)
