孙红梅、张冬冬、修小娟 (Sun Hongmei and Xiu Xiaojuan of the Guangdong Zhongshan State-owned Forest Resource Protection Center, 广东省中山市国有森林资源保护中心; Zhang Dongdong, the corresponding author, of the Fuxin City Forestry Bureau, Liaoning, 辽宁省阜新市林业局), 「The medicinal plant resources of the fengshui groves of Zhongshan City」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「中山市风水林的药用植物资源」), 热带生物学报 (Journal of Tropical Biology) 7(3) (2016) 368-372 (https://rdswxb.hainanu.edu.cn/cn/article/doi/10.15886/j.cnki.rdswxb.2016.03.016; DOI 10.15886/j.cnki.rdswxb.2016.03.016)

What it is: A peer-reviewed survey by Sun Hongmei, Zhang Dongdong and Xiu Xiaojuan, "The medicinal plant resources of the fengshui groves of Zhongshan City" (中山市风水林的药用植物资源), published in 热带生物学报 (Journal of Tropical Biology) in 2016; the authors work for the state forest resource protection center of Zhongshan in Guangdong and for the forestry bureau of Fuxin in Liaoning, and the fieldwork behind it covers that one municipality's fengshui groves.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it not for its botany but for the count it had to make first: 66 groves in 66 natural villages totaling 269.85 hectares, which works out at about four hectares a village and gives us a typical grove to draw against the very large outliers other studies report. Its limits are place and era - the Pearl River Delta is subtropical southern China, warmer and wetter than the setting's Japanese model, and the groves were measured as they stand now, so the figure describes the survivors of a century of clearing around them rather than what a village once held.

Used for: a Pearl River Delta municipal census of fengshui groves - 66 groves in 66 natural villages, 269.85 ha in total (about 4.1 ha each, the record's arithmetic)
