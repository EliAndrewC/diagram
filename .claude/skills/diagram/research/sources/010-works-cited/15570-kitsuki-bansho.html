kitsuki-bansho

Kitsuki City, 杵築城下町のスポット解説 番所 ("Kitsuki castle town spot guide: the bansho", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.kitsuki.lg.jp/soshiki/1/syoukou_kankou/kanko/1/kitsukispot_kuchiya.html)

What it is: A city tourism page on the six guard posts at the entrances of Kitsuki, a small castle town in Oita, describing what they watched, their night closing and where each stood, from old maps.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that shows a small Japanese town's entry posts on the ground. It is a local tourism page without references, and Kitsuki was a castle town, the seat of a small domain, so its posts may be more than an ordinary market town kept; it gives no building sizes.

Used for: six entry posts that watched people and goods and shut at night; no houses outside the main one; a post at the end of an earthen causeway with a small gate opened at night (defenses)
