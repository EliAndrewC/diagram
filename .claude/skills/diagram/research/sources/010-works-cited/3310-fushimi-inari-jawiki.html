What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Fushimi Inari Taisha, the head shrine of the Inari cult at Kyoto, with its Senbon Torii; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the custom of dedicating torii to the Edo period, puts the donated gates at about ten thousand on the mountain and describes the dense rows; a tertiary summary of one shrine, from which this page generalizes the donation row, and the ten thousand is today's accumulation on the mountain rather than a period density.

Used for: the Inari donation row (religion and death)
