plos-transition-zone-drought

"Drought characteristics and their impact on vegetation net primary productivity in the climate-sensitive transition zone", PLOS ONE (2026), doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0343746 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12935246/)

What it is: An open-access remote-sensing study of drought and vegetation productivity in the climate-sensitive transition zone of northern China, whose description of its study area defines the region and states its rainfall.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use one sentence of it: northern China's 400 to 800 mm of annual rain, concentrated in summer showers, which is the rainfall that leaves a dry field there chronically drought-exposed and makes a well worth sinking in the middle of one. Its limits are that this is a modern instrumental climatology of recent decades rather than a historical one; that its region is defined by isotherms and isohyets and takes in both the north-China plain and the loess provinces, so it fits our sentence's own conflation of the two better than it fits either strictly; and that everything else in the paper - the drought indices, the satellite productivity data, the trends - is about the present day and is used for nothing here. The sentence we do take is the paper's own uncited description of where it worked, not a result it measured.

Used for: 400-800 mm of annual rain concentrated in the summer, for a named northern-China region
