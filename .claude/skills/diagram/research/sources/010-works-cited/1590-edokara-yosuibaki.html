edokara-yosuibaki

edo→tokyo (edokara.tokyo), 玉川上水の遺構-余水吐跡 (https://edokara.tokyo/conts/2020/06/12/1485)

What it is: A page on a site documenting Edo's surviving waterworks remains, on the spillway of the Tamagawa aqueduct at the Yotsuya gate: the facility that discharged surplus water to regulate the flow, and the valley and river it discharged into.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that a real aqueduct carried a purpose-built spillway, which is why our capital's aqueduct is drawn with one. Its limit is that it is an enthusiast site, and its case discharged to a valley rather than a moat. The page gives no date for the spillway itself, so its Edo-period existence rests on the page's framing rather than on a dated statement.

Used for: the Tamagawa aqueduct's spillway and where its surplus went
