ja.wikipedia 五重塔 ("five-story pagoda", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/五重塔)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on five-story pagodas, with a list of the surviving national-treasure pagodas and their heights.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the range of heights of real five-story pagodas, lowest to tallest. Every pagoda on the list stands at a great or famous temple, and the list says nothing of how common pagodas were.

Used for: how tall a five-story pagoda was (religion-and-death)
