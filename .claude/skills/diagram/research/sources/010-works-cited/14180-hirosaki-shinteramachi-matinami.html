日本の古い町並み (Old townscapes of Japan), 弘前市新寺町 ("Shin-teramachi, Hirosaki", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://matinami.o.oo7.jp/hokkaidou-tohoku/hirosaki-sinteramati.html)

What it is: An amateur site's page on the Shin-teramachi quarter of Hirosaki, summarizing its history from the standard place-name dictionaries: when it was laid out, and what the town's lot surveys and an early Meiji gazetteer counted in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the town houses laid out among the temples of a temple street, from the domain's own surveys, which says a temple quarter was not temples alone. It is a hobbyist's page drawn from reference books it names rather than from the surveys themselves.

Used for: a temple street laid out in 1650 with 45 town houses beside its temples in 1678, and 14 temples among 234 houses in early Meiji (religion-and-death)
