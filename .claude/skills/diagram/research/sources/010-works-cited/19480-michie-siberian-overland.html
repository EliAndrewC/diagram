michie-siberian-overland

Alexander Michie, The Siberian Overland Route from Peking to Petersburg, London: John Murray, 1864 (Project Gutenberg; https://www.gutenberg.org/files/45167/45167-h/45167-h.htm)

What it is: A British merchant's account of his journey in 1863 from Beijing across Mongolia and Siberia to St Petersburg, beginning with the road from Tianjin to Beijing and on through the Nankou pass.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an eyewitness of north China's road inns in the Qing, describing their courtyards crowded with travelers' animals and gear and their common dormitories. It is one foreigner's passing impressions, written with a traveler's disdain, of a few inns on one road in the north; it gives no plan and no size, and one line of the text misprints the year as 1861.

Used for: the inn yard holding travelers' animals and the common dormitory, in 1863 (buildings)
