katsushika-horikiri

Katsushika City, 子ども葛飾区史 第3章 第9節 小菅（小菅・堀切・東堀切） ("Katsushika history for children, ch. 3 sec. 9: Kosuge, Horikiri and Higashi-Horikiri", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.katsushika.lg.jp/history/child/3-9-169.html)

What it is: The ward's local-history page for young readers on the Horikiri iris garden, founded on low wet ground outside Edo at the end of the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a dated case of a flower garden laid out on a village's farmland near a great city, and of the same ground going back to paddy. Written for children by the ward; it gives no area for the Edo-period garden, and the plant count it gives is today's park.

Used for: the iris gardens of Horikiri, and the garden that became paddy again in 1942 (fields)
