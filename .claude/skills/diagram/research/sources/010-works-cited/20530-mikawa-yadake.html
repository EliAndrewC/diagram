mikawa-yadake

三河の植物観察 (Mikawa plant observation, a regional plant survey site), 「Yadake, Pseudosasa japonica」 (translated; original: 「ヤダケ Pseudosasa japonica」) (https://mikawanoyasou.org/data/yadake.htm)

What it is: A plant-survey page on yadake, the arrow bamboo, giving its life form as a bamboo grass (sasa) and quoting Nakai Takenoshin's account of its culms - 2 to 5 m tall, hollow, 5 to 15 mm across - beside the Flora of China's.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the height of the one bamboo the Tonami grove held that is not a timber bamboo. Its limits are that the page's own header and the Flora of China give a shorter range (1 to 3, to 5 m) than the Nakai passage cited, and that, beyond noting it has long been planted throughout Japan for arrows, fishing rods and brush handles, it says nothing of how or where it was grown on a farmstead.

Used for: yadake's height and its being a bamboo grass
