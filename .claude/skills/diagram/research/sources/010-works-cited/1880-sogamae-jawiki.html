What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the sōgamae, the outermost enclosure of a Japanese castle that took in the whole castle town within moats, stone walls and earthen ramparts, with Odawara's nine-kilometer circuit as the exemplar.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the form our single-rampart capital follows and gives its largest example. A tertiary summary used for the definition and the scale. The form is a late-Sengoku and Momoyama answer to a besieging army, systematized after the siege of Odawara, not an Edo-period peacetime norm - which bears on whether a capital of ours draws one rampart around its whole population.

Used for: the sōgamae enclosing the whole castle town, and Odawara's 9 km
