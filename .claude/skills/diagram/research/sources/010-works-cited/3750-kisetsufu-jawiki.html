ja.wikipedia 季節風 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/季節風)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the monsoon winds, with Japan's summer southeasterly and winter northwesterly; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the regional default this page's windbreaks face - the Siberian High's northwesterly in winter; a tertiary summary, and the continental article gives a different winter direction for China.

Used for: the winter wind's default direction (vegetation)
