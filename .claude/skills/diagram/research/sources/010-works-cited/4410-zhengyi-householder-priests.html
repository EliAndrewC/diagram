Patheos Religion Library, "Taoism: Ethics and Community - Leadership" (https://www.patheos.com/library/taoism/ethics-morality-community/leadershipclergy)

What it is: The leadership-and-clergy article of the Patheos Religion Library’s Taoism section, a general-audience reference on the world religions, describing who Daoist clergy are and how the Zhengyi and Quanzhen traditions differ in how they live.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the statement behind this project’s householder clergy: a priest of the Zhengyi tradition marries, marriage is required at the highest rank, and the role is often passed down in a family - which is what puts families rather than celibates inside a temple precinct on these maps. Its limits are that it is a tertiary religion-portal article, written in the present tense about clergy in China and Taiwan TODAY rather than about any premodern century, and it names no sources of its own.

Used for: married priests, marriage required at the highest rank, the role passed down in a family
