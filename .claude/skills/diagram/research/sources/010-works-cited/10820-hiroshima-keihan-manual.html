hiroshima-keihan-manual

Hiroshima Prefecture agricultural extension offices, 「Bund management manual」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦畔管理マニュアル」), version 1.1, June 2010 (https://www.pref.hiroshima.lg.jp/uploaded/attachment/230222.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural extension manual for farm corporations on keeping the grass of paddy bunds and their slope faces down with less labor, with a survey of how the corporations mow.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures how much of a paddy landscape is bund and bank: the bund share of cultivated land in hilly Hiroshima averages 9.5%, and in mountain paddies the slope faces can exceed the flooded area; and it records that the bunds are mown some 3.4 times a year. It is modern and mechanized - the cutting is by brush cutter, and the manual's purpose is to cut less - so it is used for the lie of the land around a paddy, which terracing fixed long before, not for how often a pre-modern farmer cut it.

Used for: how much of a hill paddy landscape is bund and slope face (vegetation)
