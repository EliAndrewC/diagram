hiroshima-keihan-manual

Hiroshima Prefecture Western, Eastern and Northern Agricultural Technology Guidance Offices, 「Labor-saving bund management manual」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦畔管理省力化マニュアル」), version 1.1, June 2010 (https://www.pref.hiroshima.lg.jp/uploaded/attachment/230222.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural extension manual for farm corporations on keeping the grass of paddy bunds and their slope faces down with less labor, with a survey of how the corporations mow.

Why it applies, and its limits: It reports how much of a paddy landscape is bund and bank: in 2007 bunds took 9.5% of Hiroshima's paddy land, a prefecture-wide average over flat and steep fields alike (fourth in Japan, against a national 5.7%), and it notes, without a figure, that some mountain paddies have more slope face than flooded area; and it records that the bunds are mown some 3.4 times a year. It is modern and mechanized - the cutting is by brush cutter, and the manual's purpose is to cut less - and the land it measures is modern too: the manual says the long bund slopes were built by postwar field consolidation (圃場整備), which re-graded hill paddies into larger fields behind taller banks. So the 9.5% is used only as the order of magnitude of how much of a hill paddy landscape is bund and bank, not as the share on a pre-modern terrace, which consolidation changed. It is not used for how often a pre-modern farmer cut the bunds.

Used for: how much of a hill paddy landscape is bund and slope face (vegetation)
