kyototuu-sugimoto

Kyoto-tsu Encyclopedia, 杉本家 ("The Sugimoto family", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.kyototuu.jp/Life/MachiyaSugimotoke.html)

What it is: A Kyoto enthusiasts' encyclopedia page on the Sugimoto house, giving its site area, floor area and frontage, and the building area and date of each of its three storehouses, copied without citation from the national designation record.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the great merchant's site area and frontage, which the official record does not; its storehouse areas repeat the national designation record, which Bunka-cho's Cultural Heritage Online publishes and which is the better citation for them. The storehouses were built before 1822, but the main house whose frontage and floor area it gives was raised in 1870, just after the Edo period, rebuilt after the 1864 fire in the old form. It is an unsigned private site with no citations; its site area of about 1,200 square meters disagrees with another page's 30 by 52 m, and it reports the storehouses' surviving the 1864 fire as what is said.

Used for: the Sugimoto site, frontage and storehouse sizes (cities/fabric.html)
