kyototuu-sugimoto

Kyoto-tsu Encyclopedia, 杉本家 ("The Sugimoto family", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.kyototuu.jp/Life/MachiyaSugimotoke.html)

What it is: A Kyoto enthusiasts' encyclopedia page on the Sugimoto house, giving its site area, floor area and frontage, and the building area and date of each of its three storehouses from the designation records.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts the great merchant's estate and its storehouses in figures the official record does not give. It is an unsigned private site with no citations; its site area of about 1,200 square meters disagrees with another page's 30 by 52 m, and it reports the storehouses' surviving the 1864 fire as what is said.

Used for: the Sugimoto site, frontage and storehouse sizes (cities/fabric.html)
