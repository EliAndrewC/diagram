mlit-mizubunka-sanko

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, reference tables of local water culture (水の郷 materials), 参考 ("Reference", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.mlit.go.jp/tochimizushigen/mizsei/mizusato/files/mizubunsanko2.pdf)

What it is: A table compiled by the national land ministry's water-resources department of local water customs and facilities reported by municipalities across Japan - washing places, water wheels, old waterworks - one short paragraph a place, each credited to its municipality.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its rows show the shared washing place as a common thing in town and village alike - Gujo Hachiman's castle-town channels of the 1650s and their kawado, Hirosaki's spring-fed washing places with drinking and washing tanks kept apart, washing spots with rules on the channels of a village in Saga, and Omihachiman's piped town water from a parent well. It is a list written by each municipality for the ministry, uneven and undated row by row, and what it describes is what survives.

Used for: shared washing places in towns and villages; Gujo's castle-town channels; Omihachiman's piped water (water)
