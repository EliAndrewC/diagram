What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tamagaki, the fence around a shrine or sacred ground, with its synonyms and Ise Jingu's four concentric fences.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the word and for Ise's rings as the great-shrine case. Its limits: a tertiary summary, undated, whose one worked example is the imperial shrine; it does not say what an ordinary village shrine had.

Used for: what a shrine fence is called, and Ise's four rings (religion-and-death.html).
