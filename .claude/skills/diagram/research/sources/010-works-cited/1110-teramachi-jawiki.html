What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the teramachi, the temple district of a Japanese town: how castle towns gathered their temples and graveyards at the town's edge as a defensive line, and the surviving examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the defensive intent behind the temple district, which is why our capital draws its temple row at the rim. A tertiary summary used for that intent, beside the reference encyclopedias that say the same; the sentence quoted speaks of the time before the Edo period, which is earlier than most of what our capitals are drawn from.
