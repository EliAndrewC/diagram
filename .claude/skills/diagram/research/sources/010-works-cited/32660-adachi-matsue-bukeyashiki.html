adachi-matsue-bukeyashiki

足立正智 (Adachi Masatomo), "'Buke yashiki' soken-ji no sugata o saguru" (Seeking the samurai house as first built), Shimane University (in Japanese; https://coc.lib.shimane-u.ac.jp/files/attach/1/1504/SMC002-07.pdf)

What it is: An architectural survey of the Matsue samurai house on the Shiomi Nawate street, a middle-ranking retainer's house: its site and buildings, the restorations of 1969 and later, and what the measured drawings show of the house as first built.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a measured survey that says which buildings on the site are old and which later, so it can place the well beside the main house and set aside the privy now in the grounds as a later building. It is one house in one castle town, and it dates the well's roof as comparatively recent without dating the well itself.

Used for: the well on the east side of a samurai house, and the later date of the privy in its grounds (Estates and other compounds)
