kotobank-washi

kotobank 和紙 ("Japanese paper", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%92%8C%E7%B4%99)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the dictionary and encyclopedia entries on handmade Japanese paper, among them the Nipponica entry by Machida Seishi on how it is made, from the fiber to the sheet dried on a board in the sun, and the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry on its history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used for the last step of the making, the wet sheets brushed one by one onto drying boards and set in the sun, which is the paper maker's visible yard. A signed reference entry describing the craft as practiced into modern times; it gives no board size or count.

Used for: paper dried on boards in the sun (urban-features)
