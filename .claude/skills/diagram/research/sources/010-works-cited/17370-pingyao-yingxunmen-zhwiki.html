zh.wikipedia 迎熏门 (平遥) ("Yingxun Gate (Pingyao)", title translated) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/迎熏门_(平遥))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the south and principal gate of Pingyao's city wall, a short encyclopedia stub giving the history of its tower and of the four great gate towers the county rebuilt in 1703.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures the gate towers of a county seat - five bays, 14 m wide and 10 m deep - which is the smallest main gate tower read. The towers were all lost by 1948 and the present ones are reconstructions of 1997-2002, so the figures are those the article gives for the 1703 towers, drawn from a tourism office's page rather than a survey.

Used for: a county seat's gate tower, 46 by 33 ft (defenses)
