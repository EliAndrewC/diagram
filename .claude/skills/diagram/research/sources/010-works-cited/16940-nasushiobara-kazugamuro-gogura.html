nasushiobara-kazugamuro-gogura

Nasushiobara City, cultural properties, "数ヶ室の郷蔵" ("The Kazugamuro gogura", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.nasushiobara.tochigi.jp/soshikikarasagasu/shogaigakushuka/bunkazai/2/2/2549.html)

What it is: The city's page on a designated surviving village storehouse, one of those the Kurobane domain built in its villages in 1769 against famine, graded by the village's population and holding millet.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a municipal cultural-properties page on a real surviving building and dates the domain's program. Its limits are that it is one domain's program, that its per-household figure is hedged ("about", "it seems"), and that it gives no dimensions or siting.

Used for: a domain building reserve storehouses in its villages, graded by population (urban-features)
