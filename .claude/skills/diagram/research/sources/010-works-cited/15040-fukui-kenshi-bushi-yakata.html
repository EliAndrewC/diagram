Fukui Prefecture, Fukui Kenshi (history of Fukui Prefecture), general history vol. 2, the Middle Ages, ch. 5 sec. 3, "Warriors' residences and the medieval town" (in Japanese; https://www.library-archives.pref.fukui.lg.jp/fukui/07/kenshi/T2/T2-5-01-03-02-02.htm)

What it is: A section of the prefecture's official history, put online by the prefectural archives, on the residences of medieval warriors in Echizen and Wakasa and the towns that grew from them, drawing on surviving deeds.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the usual size of a medieval warrior's moated residence, the fields its servants worked before its gate, and one deed transferring a residence with its house site and paddy - what a samurai house among its own fields held. It is a scholarly regional history, but its figures are general statements and single deeds from the Kamakura and Muromachi periods, not measurements of Edo-period country houses; it says the local documents are few.

Used for: the size of a medieval warrior's residence, its fields at the gate, and a deed's house site and paddy (buildings)
