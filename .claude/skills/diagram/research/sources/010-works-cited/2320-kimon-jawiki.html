What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kimon, the northeast as the unlucky direction of Japanese geomancy, with the temples set to guard it in Kyoto and Edo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the direction and Edo's guardian temple in it, which is the bearing this page reads the city's northern institutions against. A tertiary summary used for the direction; the alignment of execution ground, pleasure quarter and outcaste head's compound to it is this page's reading.
