kotobank-bukeyashiki

Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten, s.v. 武家屋敷, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/武家屋敷)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the encyclopedia entry on the buke-yashiki, the samurai residence in Edo and in the provincial castle towns.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the provincial plot's enclosures - earthen wall, board fence or hedge - which is where this page's rank ladder takes its materials; it attaches none of them to a rank.

Used for: the enclosures of a samurai plot (government)
