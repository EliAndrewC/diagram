bunka-sado-tsuchiya-dozo

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 旧土屋家住宅土蔵 ("former Tsuchiya house, storehouse", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/404091)

What it is: The national register's entry for a grain storehouse of 1890 moved from the southern part of the central plain of Sado island: its construction, its floor area and a line of description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and build of one real farm grain kura and calls it an ancillary building a farmhouse could not do without. Its limits: one building, Meiji rather than Edo, and a registered survivor; "could not do without" is the register's comment on this kind of farm, not a count of how many farms had one.

Used for: the size and build of a farm grain kura (homesteads)
