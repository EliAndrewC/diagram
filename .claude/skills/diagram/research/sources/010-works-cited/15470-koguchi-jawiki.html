ja.wikipedia 虎口 ("koguchi, the fortified entrance", title translated from the Japanese by this project; reached at 枡形) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9E%A1%E5%BD%A2)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the koguchi, the entrance of a Japanese castle or enclosure, and its forms - the plain entrance, the staggered entrance, the masugata and the umadashi - with how each makes an attacker turn under fire.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the masugata, its inner and outer forms and its two gates, which is what a Japanese-model gate court is drawn from. A tertiary summary; it gives no dimensions, and its account of where each form developed is marked as wanting a source.

Used for: the masugata: a square court with two gates, inner and outer forms, a koraimon in front and a yaguramon behind (defenses)
