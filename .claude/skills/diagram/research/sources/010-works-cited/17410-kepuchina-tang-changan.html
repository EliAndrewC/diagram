科普中国 (Kepu China, the China Association for Science and Technology's popular-science site), 唐长安城 ("Tang Chang'an", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 2019 (https://www.kepuchina.cn/wiki/ct/201903/t20190323_1029448.shtml)

What it is: A popular-science encyclopedia entry on Tang Chang'an that summarizes the archaeological survey of the city: its outer wall, its avenues measured one by one, its 110 wards and its two markets.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the wall-side streets on all four sides inside the outer wall, 20 to 25 m wide, which is both the one measured patrol road read and the evidence that Chang'an's wards stood back from the rampart. It is a secondary summary without references of its own, of a capital planned on a grid in the 580s, not of a provincial seat, and it does not describe what stood on the wall-side street.

Used for: the wall-side street on all four sides of Chang'an, 20-25 m, and the roads dividing the city into wards (defenses)
