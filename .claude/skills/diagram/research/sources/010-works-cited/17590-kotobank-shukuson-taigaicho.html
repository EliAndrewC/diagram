kotobank-shukuson-taigaicho

コトバンク 宿村大概帳 ("general register of post-stations and villages", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%AE%BF%E6%9D%91%E5%A4%A7%E6%A6%82%E5%B8%B3-841874)

What it is: A page on kotobank, a Japanese reference site that reprints several dictionaries' entries for one word, here the Shukuson Taigaicho, the survey the shogunate's highway commissioners compiled late in the Edo period of every post-station on the five highways and some side roads, and of the villages along them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the survey recorded for each station - its households, its number of inns, its headquarters inn, its shrines and temples - which shows that inns with meals were counted and what else was. It is a dictionary summary of the survey, not the survey itself, so it gives no station's figures; and it covers post-stations on the shogunate's highways, not the provincial seat that is not a station.

Used for: that the survey recorded each station's households and number of hatago, and names no count of the cheap firewood-fee inns (cities/fabric)
