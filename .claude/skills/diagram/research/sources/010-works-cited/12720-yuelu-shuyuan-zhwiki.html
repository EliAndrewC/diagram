zh.wikipedia 岳麓书院 ("Yuelu Academy", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/岳麓书院)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Yuelu Academy at Changsha, the provincial capital of Hunan, with its site area and the buildings on its axis.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a famous provincial academy that survives, and the article gives its site area. It is an encyclopedia article, a tertiary summary: its 21,000 square metres has no footnote of its own (the paragraph's one reference is a 2007 web page on the academy's buildings), and it does not say what the figure takes in - the Ming axis ran on past the compound to the Xiang River and the mountain top. It is one of the four great academies of China, made Hunan's provincial academy by an edict of 1733, and the article calls it the largest surviving academy in China, so it bounds an academy's size from above rather than showing a typical one. Its layout is Ming and Qing, but much of what stands was rebuilt: after Japanese bombing in 1938 and 1941 and near-ruin in the Cultural Revolution it reopened in 1986 after five years' repair, the Chongsheng shrine and Minglun hall were rebuilt in 2003-04, and the dormitory ranges date from 1903, when it became a modern school. And the article places the academy's Confucian temple on the axis in one passage and to one side of it in another.
