zh.wikipedia 岳麓书院 ("Yuelu Academy", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/岳麓书院)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Yuelu Academy at Changsha, the provincial capital of Hunan, with its site area and the buildings on its axis.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a famous provincial academy that survives, measured. It is one of the most celebrated academies in China and so larger than most; its buildings are Qing and later restorations; and the article places the academy's Confucian temple on the axis in one passage and to one side of it in another.

Used for: how big a provincial Chinese academy was and what it held (religion-and-death)
