leping-archina

ARCHINA, 以城市故事为载体的历史城区修补探析 ("An exploration of repairing a historic city district through the city's stories", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://archina.com/index.php?a=show&g=portal&id=4563&m=index)

What it is: A 2020 planning article by the design firm 中工武大 (Zhonggong Wuda), posted on the architecture trade portal ARCHINA, about the old town of Leping, a county seat of the old prefecture of Raozhou, which gives the Ming wall of 1562 (its circuit and its two spans) and describes the old town's cross street, main commercial street and grid of lanes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured wall and a described street plan for one real county seat, which is rarer on a readable page than a capital's. It is a modern planning article written to guide a conservation plan, not a scholarly paper: a design firm's piece on a trade portal that says its content is partly supplied by third parties, and it cites no source for its wall figures. Its 14-by-9 grid of lanes is not dated and is described from the lanes that survive today, so it shows the late-imperial town at best, not a plan of 1562. It is also about one prosperous county seat that the article itself calls the first of its prefecture's six, so it stands at the large end of county seats.

Used for: one county seat's wall size and cross-street plan (towns)
