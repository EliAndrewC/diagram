leping-archina

ARCHINA, 以城市故事为载体的历史城区修补探析 ("An exploration of repairing a historic city district through the city's stories", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://archina.com/index.php?a=show&g=portal&id=4563&m=index)

What it is: A planning article on the architecture site ARCHINA about the old town of Leping, a county seat of the old prefecture of Raozhou, which gives the Ming wall of 1562 (its circuit and its two spans) and describes the old town's cross street, main commercial street and grid of lanes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured wall and a described street plan for one real county seat, which is rarer on a readable page than a capital's. It is a modern planning paper written to guide conservation, about one prosperous county seat that the article itself calls the first of its prefecture's six, so it stands at the large end of county seats.

Used for: one county seat's wall size and cross-street plan (towns)
