kotobank-tanagari

Kotobank, 店借 ("house tenant", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - dictionary entries with excerpts from the older Sekai Daihyakka Jiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%BA%97%E5%80%9F-1183490)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the tanagari, the townsman who rented his house in an early-modern Japanese city, with excerpts from the encyclopedia's entries on tenancy, on the house-rental guarantee and on the house manager, giving the share of tenants among city residents in Edo and Osaka and by region.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that says how many of a city's residents rented, and that the share differed widely by region, which is what sets a town's back tenements apart from a great city's. The figures are estimates quoted in short excerpts, not a survey the page shows; the regional shares are of all urban residents, not of laborers, and it does not say which towns were counted or when.

Used for: the share of tenants in Edo and Osaka and by region (towns)
