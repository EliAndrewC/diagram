kotobank-idoyakata

Digital Daijisen and Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, "井戸屋形", via kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/井戸屋形-435033)

What it is: The Digital Daijisen and Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entries for 井戸屋形 (idoyakata, the well house), gathered on kotobank, each defining it as a simple structure of posts set beside a well with a roof on top of them and nothing more.

Why it applies, and its limits: Our well glyph puts a small roof over the well head, and these entries confirm that the roofed well of Japanese practice really was that plain - posts and a roof, not a shed. A dictionary gives the form of a thing and not how common it was, so how many of a settlement's wells are drawn roofed is not decided by this source.

Used for: the well house is posts beside the well with a roof on them and nothing more - the form the glyph's roof square depicts; its prevalence is not stated
