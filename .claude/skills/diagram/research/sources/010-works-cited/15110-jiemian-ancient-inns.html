Jiemian News, 从《清明上河图》中，看大侠与诗人都爱住的古代"民宿" ("the old-time guesthouses of heroes and poets, seen in Along the River During the Qingming Festival", title translated from the Chinese by this project), reprinted from 宿志, 2018 (in Chinese; https://www.jiemian.com/article/1952375.html)

What it is: A popular article, reprinted on a news site, surveying Chinese inns and relay stations from the Qin to the Qing through paintings and literature.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the two kinds of Ming inn a reader would meet - the eating house with lodgers behind, and the cheap mule-and-cart inn in the open country where carters cooked for themselves. It is popular writing that cites no scholarship, gives no plan and no size, and runs over two thousand years in a few pages.

Used for: the Ming eating-house inn and the carters' inn in the open country (buildings)
