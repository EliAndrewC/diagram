Sohu (古建家园), 河南古建筑 - - 衙署的功能与建筑特色 ("Henan's historic architecture: the functions and features of the yamen", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://www.sohu.com/a/740571173_99935361)

What it is: A short 2023 post by a heritage-architecture trade platform on Henan's yamen, taking the Nanyang prefectural yamen as its example: its dimensions and the halls on its central axis.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a second, larger figure for the same prefectural yamen, which is what shows the site's extent is reported differently. It is a light popular post with loose wording (it calls the prefectural compound a county yamen in one sentence, and the six rooms "six boards"), so it is used only for the figure it states beside the encyclopedia's.

Used for: the larger reported size of the Nanyang prefectural yamen (cities/government)
