ja.wikipedia 草津宿本陣 ("the Kusatsu-juku honjin", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8D%89%E6%B4%A5%E5%AE%BF%E6%9C%AC%E9%99%A3)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the surviving honjin of Kusatsu, a national historic site, with its site and floor areas and its history as a lodging for lords.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures one real honjin, site and floor. Its limits are that the page calls it among the largest that survive, so it is the top of the range, not the ordinary house; that it is a tertiary summary of the city's own survey reports (the site area is footnoted to the city board of education's 1985 preservation-management plan; the floor area has no citation of its own); and that both are a modern measurement of what survives, which the page calls most, not all, of the site and buildings, rebuilt after the 1718 fire and restored in 1989-1996, so they may understate the Edo-period premises.

Used for: a site of about 4,719 square meters and a floor of about 1,547 square meters (towns)
