nta-nengu-nonyu

National Tax Agency, National Tax College, 租税史料特別展示, 3 年貢の納入 (https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/tokubetsu/h30shiryoukan/03.htm)

What it is: A page of the Japanese National Tax College's 2018 special exhibition of tax documents, explaining how Edo-period villages delivered their land tax to the lord's storehouse or collection office, with receipts and shipping records from shogunal lands.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for the share of the Edo land tax paid in money, which it gives as three to four tenths of the whole. It names no region or period for that figure, and its own district example (a shogunal district ordered to pay nearly six tenths in money) shows the share varied widely, so the figure is a general proportion, not a rule for any one county.

Used for: the share of the Edo land tax paid in coin (buildings)
