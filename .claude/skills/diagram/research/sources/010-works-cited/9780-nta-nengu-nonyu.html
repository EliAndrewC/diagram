National Tax Agency, National Tax College, 租税史料特別展示 ("Special Exhibition of Historical Tax Materials", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 3 年貢の納入 ("3. Paying the Annual Tax", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/tokubetsu/h30shiryoukan/03.htm)

What it is: A page of the Japanese National Tax College's 2018 special exhibition of tax documents, explaining how Edo-period villages delivered their land tax to the lord's storehouse or collection office, with documents from villages of three domains (Imabari, Okudono, Matsumae), a Meiji 4 (1871) prefectural notice, and an account of one shogunal territory's shipments of tax rice to Edo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for the share of the Edo land tax paid in money, which it gives as three to four tenths of the whole. It is a modern exhibition text, unsigned, that gives no source for that figure and no region or period. Its own example is a single village of the Okudono domain. That village paid a third of its tax in money by old custom and was then ordered to pay more in money, so that nearly six tenths of its 1841 tax was paid in money. The share therefore varied widely by lord and by year, and the figure is a general proportion for Edo Japan, not a rule for any one county.

Used for: the share of the Edo land tax paid in coin (buildings)
