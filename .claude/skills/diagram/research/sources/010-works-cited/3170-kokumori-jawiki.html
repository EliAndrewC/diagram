What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kokumori, the assessed standard yield per tan set for each grade of paddy, dry field and house plot in the early-modern cadastral surveys; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Taiko-survey ladder (upper paddy 1.5 koku, middle 1.3, lower 1.1 per tan; upper dry field 1.2) and its derivation from an assumed harvest at a five-tenths milling. These are tax-assessment rates for land grades, not measured yields, which is the limit stated beside the figure.

Used for: the 1.3 koku/tan figure and the paddy-over-dry-field value ladder (fields)
