yokushitsu-kotobank

Kotobank, 浴室 (Yokushitsu) - Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica), Nakamura Hitoshi (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B5%B4%E5%AE%A4-653701)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the bath room, carrying the Nipponica entry by Nakamura Hitoshi on the bath room abroad and in Japan, from the temple bath to the modern unit bath.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the family bath of noble and warrior houses around 1600 as a one-person tub with a built-on hearth in a corner of the doma, which is where a residence's bath sat and how small it was. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary; that the sentence is dated by the one before it to the late sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries, so it describes the early Edo house rather than the later one; and that it gives no dimensions.

Used for: the family bath of warrior houses around 1600 in a corner of the doma, and why it was seldom used (buildings)
