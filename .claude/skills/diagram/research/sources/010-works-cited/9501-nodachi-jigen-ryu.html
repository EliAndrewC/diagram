nodachi-jigen-ryu

野太刀自顕流 薬丸自顕流, 野太刀自顕流の特徴 (the features of Nodachi Jigen-ryū) (in Japanese; https://nodachi.jimdofree.com/%E9%87%8E%E5%A4%AA%E5%88%80%E8%87%AA%E9%A1%95%E6%B5%81%E3%81%A8%E3%81%AF/)

What it is: A short page on a practitioners' site for Nodachi (Yakumaru) Jigen-ryū, a Satsuma school that the page insists is distinct from Tōgō Shigekata's Jigen-ryū (示現流). It describes the school's stance, its training and its practice ground as they are today.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the practice ground is open-air and practice is barefoot and mostly tategi-uchi, which is the setting a practice ground in the open needs. Its limits are that it is a practitioners' page describing today's practice without dates or sources, that the school is Nodachi Jigen-ryū, which the page says is a different school from Jigen-ryū (示現流); and that an open-air, barefoot ground for tategi-uchi is one Satsuma school's own practice, so it supports an open practice ground and not the form of a roofed training hall elsewhere in Japan.

Used for: an open-air, barefoot practice ground where tategi-uchi is repeated (buildings)
