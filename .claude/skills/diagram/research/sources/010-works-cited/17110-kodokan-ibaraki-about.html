Ibaraki Prefecture Kodokan Office, 弘道館について ("About the Kodokan", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kodokan-ibaraki.jp/about/)

What it is: The official site of the Kodokan, the Mito domain school, run by the prefectural office that manages it: its history, the buildings and grounds it held, and a comparison of the largest domain-school grounds in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the whole program of the largest domain school - main hall, halls of letters and of martial arts, medical school, observatory, Confucian shrine and Kashima shrine, riding, drill, archery and gunnery grounds - and ranks the next three grounds by size (Fukuyama, Kanazawa, Hagi). It is the site's own account of an exceptional school, the biggest in the country, so its program is the upper bound, not the median.

Used for: the largest domain-school grounds and the full program of one (cities/capitals)
