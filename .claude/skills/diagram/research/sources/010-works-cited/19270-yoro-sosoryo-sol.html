官制大観, 喪葬令 全17条 現代語訳 養老律令 ("The Funeral Ordinance, all 17 articles: the Yoro code in modern Japanese", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (http://www.sol.dti.ne.jp/hiromi/kansei/yoro26.html)

What it is: A private reference site on the offices of Japan's ancient law codes, giving the funeral ordinance of the Yoro code article by article in a modern Japanese rendering.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its ninth article forbids burial near the capital or beside roads, which is a premodern siting rule for a burial ground. It is a hobbyist's modern translation, not the original text, it gives no date for the code, and the article sets no distance.

Used for: that the ancient code kept burial away from the capital and the roads, without a distance (cities/capitals)
