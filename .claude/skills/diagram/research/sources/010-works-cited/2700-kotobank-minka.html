kotobank-minka

kotobank 民家 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B0%91%E5%AE%B6)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the Nipponica encyclopedia's entry on the minka, the Japanese folk house, with its plan types - the three-room hall type, the four-room type - and how the complex plans grew from them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a complex farmhouse plan is the standard type with rooms added, the growth by addition these maps' varied houses follow. A tertiary summary of a published reference work; it gives no proportions and no direction of growth.

Used for: the minka plan growing by added rooms from a standard type
