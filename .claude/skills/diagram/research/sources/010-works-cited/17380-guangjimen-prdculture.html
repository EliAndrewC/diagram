guangjimen-prdculture

Guangdong Provincial Cultural Relics Bureau, 广济门城楼 ("The Guangji Gate tower", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 2015, on the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao cultural information site (https://www.prdculture.org.cn/prdculture/wzwhyc/201506/137bfd2ccb3140be94bd87255e15db39.shtml)

What it is: A short heritage notice by the Guangdong provincial cultural relics bureau on the tower over the east gate of Chaozhou, a prefectural city, first built in 1370 and facing the Guangji bridge.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures a prefectural seat's gate tower - a ground story 25.8 m wide and 13 m deep, five bays - which is the largest provincial gate tower read. The tower was taken down and rebuilt in 2002 to its original structure, so the figure is the rebuilt tower's; the notice gives no source for its numbers, and a south-coast city's tower may differ from a northern one's. It is also a single tower, over the gate the notice calls the city's chief point of defense and traffic, facing the river and its bridge, so it shows how large a prefectural seat's main gate tower could be, not what a typical one was; the city's lesser gates were likely smaller.

Used for: a prefectural seat's gate tower, 85 by 43 ft (defenses)
