ja.wikipedia 茶園 ("tea garden", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/茶園)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tea garden, which describes, beside the planted tea field, the bund tea (keihan-cha): tea set in one row along the banks between fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the form and what it was for - a boundary mark, a windbreak, a check on soil washing off - and says a household sold what it did not drink. It is an encyclopedia article whose sentence on bund tea rests on a local history museum's page and a personal site, and it gives no date, region or count for the practice.

Used for: tea planted in a row along the field bunds as a boundary and windbreak (vegetation)
