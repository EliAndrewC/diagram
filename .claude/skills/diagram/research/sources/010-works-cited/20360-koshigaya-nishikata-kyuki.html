koshigaya-nishikata-kyuki

越谷市デジタルアーカイブ (Koshigaya City Digital Archive), [西方村旧記五] ("Old records of Nishikata village, part 5", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a transcription of a village's own record book of the Bunsei era (1818-1830), from the Koshigaya city history's source volumes (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/koshigaya-city-digital-archives/texthtml/d000040/mp000080-100010/ht200020)

What it is: The village officials' copies of their dealings with the shogunate's intendant over the rice tax from 1818 to the late 1820s, among them the fixed-rate tax agreed for the five years from 1825, which states the village's assessed yield and area split into paddy and dry field.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives, in the village's own figures, how much of a Saitama lowland village's land was paddy and how much dry field - about 118 cho of paddy against 31 of dry field - and the same book counts some 44 cho of its paddy as waterlogged ground, so the village stood on low, wet ground. Its limits: it is one village of one year's assessment; the page does not name the landform, say whether the village stood in a row, or say where the dry fields lay; and the areas are tax areas, the registered measure of the fields, not a survey of the ground.

Used for: the share of dry field in a lowland paddy village (homesteads)
