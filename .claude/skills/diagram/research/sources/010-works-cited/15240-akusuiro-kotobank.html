akusuiro-kotobank

Kotobank, 悪水路 (akusuiro, a drainage channel), the entry of the 精選版 日本国語大辞典 (Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten) with its cross-reference to 悪水堀 (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%82%AA%E6%B0%B4%E8%B7%AF-195115)

What it is: A dictionary entry from the abridged edition of the largest historical dictionary of Japanese, glossing akusuiro as the same word as akusuibori and defining that as a channel that carries away unneeded water.

Why it applies, and its limits: It fixes what the word the record uses for a field's drain means in Japanese. It is a definition and nothing more: it gives no size, layout or date for such a channel. The definition is general, not about fields: it defines the word as any channel for unneeded water and also as a sewer (下水道). Reading it as a field's drain comes from the record's context, not from this entry. The abridged entry also quotes no dated usage, so it does not show the word was in use before Meiji.

Used for: what an akusui channel is (fields)
