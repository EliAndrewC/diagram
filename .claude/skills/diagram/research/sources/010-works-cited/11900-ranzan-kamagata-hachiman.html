ranzan-kamagata-hachiman

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡神社明細帳, 八幡神社 菅谷村大字鎌形 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09jin14.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription of the Hiki district shrine register entry for the Hachiman Shrine of Kamagata, Saitama, a district shrine from 1927, with its precinct, parishioners and history, and an editors' footnote quoting the Council of State's district-shrine rules of 1871.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one district shrine's precinct and the 1871 rule that set one district shrine over some twenty villages. Its limits: the shrine rose to district rank only in 1927 and its precinct had absorbed moved shrines in 1908, so it is not an Edo town shrine; the 1871 rule is quoted in the editors' footnote, not in the register; one district.

Used for: what a district shrine was and how large one could be (religion-and-death.html).
