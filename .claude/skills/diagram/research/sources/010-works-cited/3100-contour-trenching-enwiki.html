en.wikipedia "Contour trenching" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contour_trenching)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on contour trenching, the soil-conservation technique of digging trenches along the contour to catch runoff.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a trench dug along the contour catches the water flowing down the slope, the interceptor form this page's field-toe ditch takes. A modern conservation technique; its purpose is infiltration rather than protection.

Used for: a trench along the contour catching downhill water
