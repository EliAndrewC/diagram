ja.wikipedia 流造 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B5%81%E9%80%A0) - 「It is the most common form of shrine honden in the whole country.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「全国で最も多い神社本殿形式である。」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on nagare-zukuri, the flowing-roof honden style.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for two statements: that it is the most common honden form in the country, and that a one-bay front makes it an ikkensha. Tertiary and undated, and "most common" is a statement about the surviving national stock rather than a premodern census.

Used for: the commonest honden form; the one-bay definition (religion-and-death.html).
