What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the jori system, the ancient square land division whose grid of about 109 m survives across Japan's old plains; an encyclopedia article listing the plains and basins where its remains are found.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the surviving regular grid on long-settled plains and that western Japan's jori parcels were regular enough to need no later readjustment. It describes land division, not how a village's paddies read at village scale.

Used for: gridded paddies in ancient core provinces (fields)
