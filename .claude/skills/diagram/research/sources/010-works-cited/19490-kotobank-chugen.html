kotobank-chugen

Kotobank, 中間 ("chugen", title translated from the Japanese by this project), entries from several Japanese dictionaries (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B8%AD%E9%96%93-97157)

What it is: Kotobank's page gathering dictionary entries on the chugen, the attendants of a warrior household ranked below the foot soldiers, among them the Yamakawa concise dictionary of Japanese history.

Why it applies, and its limits: The Yamakawa entry, a reference work by historians, says the Edo-period chugen lived together in large rooms in their lord's residence. It is a dictionary summary: it names the room but gives no plan, size or count of men, and speaks of the lords' residences rather than a country office.

Used for: servants living together in large common rooms in a lord's residence (buildings)
