en.wikipedia "Coppicing" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppicing)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on coppicing, the cutting of broadleaf trees to the stool on a rotation; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the floor vegetation that light after cutting produces and the shaded, bare floor of an overstood coppice, which is the worked wood's floor this page draws; British examples; the floor plants it names are European (bluebell, anemone, primrose), so only the light-driven pattern transfers, never the species.

Used for: the floor of a worked coppice (vegetation)
