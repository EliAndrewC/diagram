kotobank-tamagaki

Kotobank, "玉垣" ("tamagaki", title translated from the Japanese by this project), gathering the entries of the Digital Daijisen and the Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%8E%89%E5%9E%A3-562692)

What it is: A dictionary aggregator's page for the word tamagaki, a shrine's fence, carrying the historical dictionary of Japanese with its dated earliest uses.

Why it applies, and its limits: The historical dictionary quotes the word from the Kojiki of 712 and a shrine's layered fences from the Shasekishu of 1283, which dates the word and the fence at a shrine long before modern times. Its limits: a dictionary dates words, not buildings; its quotations do not say which shrines had fences or how common they were, and the Shasekishu passage speaks of layered fences modeled on a mandala, the pattern of the great shrines.

Used for: the shrine fence as a word and a form in the Kojiki (712) and the Shasekishu (1283) (religion-and-death)
