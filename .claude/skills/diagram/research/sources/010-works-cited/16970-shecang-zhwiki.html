shecang-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia, 社仓 ("Shecang, the community granary", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%A4%BE%E5%80%89)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the community relief granary: set mostly in the rural community (the 社, the village association), mobilized and overseen by officials but run mostly by local people from grain that wealthy and ordinary households gave; its history from the Sui through Zhu Xi's Southern Song model and the Yuan and Ming village granaries (a Ming 社 of twenty or thirty households with elected heads) to the Qing edicts of 1679 (社仓 granaries in villages, 义仓 granaries in market towns) and 1715.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the Chinese village granary in the village, which is the Chinese half of the setting's ancestry. Its limits are that it is a short tertiary article whose own references are two modern Chinese historical dictionaries (1990, 2000) and one line of the Sui history; that it spans many dynasties in a few lines; that by its own account the village siting was often an aim rather than the practice (the Tang granaries stood mostly in prefecture and county seats, and Zhu Xi's were run by rich households who abused them); and that it says nothing of the building.

Used for: the Chinese village relief granary and who ran it (urban-features)
