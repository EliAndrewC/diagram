shecang-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia, 社仓 ("Shecang, the community granary", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%A4%BE%E5%80%89)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the community relief granary: set mostly in the rural community, run mostly by its inhabitants from grain they gave, its history from the Sui to Zhu Xi's Southern Song model.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the Chinese village granary in the village, which is the Chinese half of the setting's ancestry. Its limits are that it is a short tertiary article, that it spans many dynasties in a few lines, and that it says nothing of the building.

Used for: the Chinese village relief granary and who ran it (urban-features)
