「Water fixtures: a record of the Showa era, 4: the bath」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「水回り昭和の記録 ４，お風呂」), a memoir on 21water.jp (in Japanese; https://www.21water.jp/k1/omoide/mukasi/ando/ando5.html)

What it is: A personal memoir, one of a series recording household water fixtures in the Showa period, recalling the public bathhouses of the town and the separate bath and privy huts of the writer's mother's farm family around the late 1940s and 1950s. The author is not named on the page.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a first-hand memory of a thatched farmhouse with neither bath nor privy inside, each in its own hut, and of other farm families that had both indoors. Its limits are that it is one family's memory from the mid-twentieth century, the farm's region is not stated, and its line that every Edo farm household had a bath is the writer's own unsourced impression, hedged as such.

Used for: bath and privy in their own huts on one farm, both inside the house on others (homesteads)
