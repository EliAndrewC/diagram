kotobank-funadama

kotobank, "船霊" (funadama) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%88%B9%E9%9C%8A-125522)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting several Japanese reference encyclopedias' entries on the funadama, among them the Nipponica and the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its entries are signed reference-work articles by folklorists, stronger than an open encyclopedia, and they say where in the boat the guardian is kept. The entries disagree on that point - one puts it in the bow, another in the middle of the boat - and they describe folk practice recorded in modern fieldwork, mostly among fishermen and sea sailors rather than river boatmen.

Used for: where in the boat the guardian is kept, and the talisman used in its place (river cities)
