Asia for Educators, Columbia University, "The Grandeur of the Qing" - the Qing state (https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/qing/state.html)

What it is: A university teaching page from Columbia's Asia for Educators program, written with the Qing historian Madeleine Zelin as consultant, describing the Qing state: the examination-recruited bureaucracy, the rule that an official never served in or beside his home province, and the short three-year postings after which he rotated on.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the rotation and avoidance rules that made the Chinese local official a short-term outsider living in the yamen, the institutional fact behind the Chinese seat's form on our maps. Its limit is that it is a teaching summary for schools, not the administrative statutes.

Used for: three-year postings and the rule of avoidance for Qing officials
