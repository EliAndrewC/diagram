zh.wikipedia 户部 (官署) ("the Ministry of Revenue (government office)", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/户部_(官署))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Ministry of Revenue, one of the Six Ministries, from its origins to 1906: its internal bureaus and the offices under it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows what a ministry held inside - fourteen bureaus by province - and which of its functions (the treasuries, the granaries, the mint) were separate offices of their own. It is a tertiary encyclopedia article, and a thinly sourced one: its only references are the 1989 Cihai (for the Tang renaming) and the Yoro code (for Japan), so the bureaus and the separate offices it lists carry no citation of their own. It runs the Ming and the Qing together - the fourteen provincial bureaus are the Qing count (its own Ming table lists thirteen, with no Jiangnan bureau), and the mint board and the three treasuries are named in their Qing forms. The bureaus sat in the capital and divided the empire's accounts by province; a provincial office was a different body, reached here only by scaling down.

Used for: what a ministry holds inside, and what it keeps elsewhere (cities/government)
