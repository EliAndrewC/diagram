zh.wikipedia 户部 (官署) ("the Ministry of Revenue (government office)", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/户部_(官署))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Ministry of Revenue, one of the Six Ministries, from its origins to 1906: its internal bureaus and the offices under it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows what a ministry held inside - fourteen bureaus by province - and which of its functions (the treasuries, the granaries, the mint) were separate offices of their own. It is an encyclopedia article on the capital ministry, so a provincial office is reached by scaling down.

Used for: what a ministry holds inside, and what it keeps elsewhere (cities/government)
