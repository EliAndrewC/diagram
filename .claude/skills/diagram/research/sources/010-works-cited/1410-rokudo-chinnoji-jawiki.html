What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Rokudō Chinnō-ji, the temple at the Rokudō crossroads in Kyoto held to stand at the entrance to the Toribeno cremation ground and at the boundary between this world and the next.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the gate-temple at the mouth of Toribeno and the tradition attached to it, the form our funerary complex's entrance follows. A tertiary summary used for that.

Used for: the Rokudō crossroads as Toribeno's entrance and the boundary of the worlds
