nagaokakyo-nengu-jono

Nagaokakyo City Library, 展示コーナーだより 第13号 (2003), 江戸時代の年貢上納 (https://www.city.nagaokakyo.lg.jp/cmsfiles/contents/0000005/5235/hurusatofile13.pdf)

What it is: A two-page exhibition leaflet from the Nagaokakyo city library, near Kyoto, describing how the villages of the district paid their Edo-period land tax, from local village documents such as a 1734 tax assessment.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read for soybeans as a recurring share of the land tax: in the shogunal lands paying the Kyoto intendant, a tenth of the tax was soybeans, three tenths silver and six tenths rice. It speaks of one area near Kyoto, calls the proportions only the principle and says that in practice each year's orders set the payment differently, and marks the soybean share as paid in silver, so it does not show soybeans delivered in kind and gives no reason for the soybean share.

Used for: the soybean share of the land tax (buildings)
