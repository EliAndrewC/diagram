hinomi-yagura-bousaihaku

Fire and Disaster Management Museum (消防防災博物館), (3)消防資機材等 ("(3) Firefighting equipment and materials", title translated from the Japanese by this project), in its history of firefighting (in Japanese; https://www.bousaihaku.com/ffhistory/11305/)

What it is: A page of the online museum of the Institute of Scientific Approaches for Fire and Disaster, on the fire-watch tower from the Edo period and the hand and steam pumps of the Meiji era.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the first tower's height (5 jo, about 15 m, against the 3 jo of the encyclopedias), the rule that every other tower was lower and a townsmen's tower open-legged, and the liter figures of the Meiji pumps from which a koku's volume is read. It is an institute's popular history about Edo and Tokyo, without citations, and gives no footprint.

Used for: the first tower's height, the open legs of a townsmen's tower, and a koku in liters (urban-features, cities/fabric)
