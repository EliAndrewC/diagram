chiran-bukeyashiki-gardens

Chiran Samurai Residence Gardens LLP, 知覧武家屋敷庭園 (the gardens of the Chiran samurai residences), official site (in Japanese; https://chiran-bukeyashiki.com/)

What it is: The home page of the partnership that keeps the gardens of the Chiran samurai houses in Minamikyushu, Kagoshima, a preserved district of the Satsuma domain's samurai settlements, describing the district's origin and its seven gardens designated as places of scenic beauty.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the kinds of garden kept at one group of rural samurai houses: of seven, one pond garden and six dry landscapes. Its limits are that it is a keeper's page written for visitors; that Chiran was a Satsuma settlement of rural samurai, a regional form whose gardens the page itself links to those of Ryukyu; that the seven are the gardens that survived to be designated, not a count of all the houses, and they lean to the settlement's leading households (three are Sata houses, a family of the old lords' line that held Chiran's highest offices), so the count describes its better houses more than its ordinary ones; and that it dates only the street layout, to about 280 years ago, not each garden.

Used for: one pond garden and six dry-landscape gardens among the seven designated samurai-house gardens of Chiran (buildings)
