fukui-kuzuryu-edo-dikes

Fukui River and National Highway Office (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism), 4．江戸時代の治水 ("4. Flood control in the Edo period", title translated from the Japanese by this project), from the Kuzuryu river basin history (in Japanese; https://www-2.kkr.mlit.go.jp/fukui/kasen/siryou/hen2/2syou2/4edo1.html)

What it is: A short page of the river office's history of the Kuzuryu basin in Echizen, on the continuous dikes the Fukui domain built in the early seventeenth century, giving their usual base, crown and height in ken and meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that gives an Edo-period earth dike's cross-section in figures, from a government office drawing on local histories. The dikes are river dikes guarding a castle town and its plain, not a village's ring dike round its own fields, so they bound a village polder's dike from above rather than measure it; the page says the size varied from place to place and gives no source for each figure.

Used for: an early Edo earth dike's base, crown and height (archetypes)
