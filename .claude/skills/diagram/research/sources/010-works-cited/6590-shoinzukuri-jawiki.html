shoinzukuri-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 書院造 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/書院造)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article 書院造 (shoin-zukuri), on the formal residential style of the warrior and priestly household that began late in the medieval period and was completed early in the Edo period: the study with its alcove and shelves, the formal rooms in a row, the veranda and the garden they open onto.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the orientation rule our compounds follow - the garden on the south of the formal rooms, beyond the veranda - which fixes the show face of every compound we draw. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary describing an ideal type rather than a surveyed house; that the article carries its editors' own notice that it lacks adequate citations, worth saying when a rule that fixes every compound's face is taken from it; and that it is silent about the north service side, so the kitchen and the servants' quarters rest on other sources.

Used for: 「On the south side of these consecutive rooms, a garden spread out beyond the irikawa (the outer aisle inside the eaves).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「これら連続した室の南側には、入り側を介して庭が広がっていた」) - the garden on the south of the formal rooms; nothing on the north service side
