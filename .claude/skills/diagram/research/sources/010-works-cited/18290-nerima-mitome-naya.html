nerima-mitome-naya

Nerima City, "旧見留家納屋" ("Former Mitome house barn", title translated), a page on the ward's registered cultural properties (in Japanese; https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kankomoyoshi/annai/rekishiwoshiru/rekishibunkazai/bunkazai/kyumitomeke.html)

What it is: The Nerima ward office's page on a farm naya from Oizumi, taken down in 1991 and rebuilt in a farm park in 1993: its build, its hipped roof (thatched with straw before it was copper-clad), its length, depth and building area, its board-floored and earth-floored halves, and its date as judged from its roof frame and posts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures one farm barn from Oizumi in Nerima, Tokyo, on the Musashino upland west of Edo, dated by the ward to the end of the Edo period or the Meiji years. Its limits: its date is an inference from its build, and spans the end of the Edo period and the Meiji years, so it may be later than 1868; it has been moved and rebuilt; and it is one building, which the ward keeps because it is among the oldest farm barns still standing in Nerima, not because it is typical; the page gives no plan, says nothing of what the barn held or of the farming around it, and cites no sources.

Used for: the size and roof of a farm shed of the end of the Edo period or the Meiji years (Homesteads)
