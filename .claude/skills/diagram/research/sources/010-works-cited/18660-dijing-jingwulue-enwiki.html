"Dijing Jingwulue", English Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dijing_Jingwulue)

What it is: The English encyclopedia's short article on the Ming book describing Beijing, naming its author and his assistants and the date of its preface.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the book whose Qingming passage the record quotes, through its preface of 1635. Its limits: a short tertiary article flagged for needing citations, for possibly containing original research, and for lacking inline citations - its one footnote supports its Jingwan claim, so the preface date and the authorship rest only on its uncited references (Carpenter 1988, Ledderose 2004); and it names Liu Tong as author while saying Yu Yizheng, a Beijing native, compiled the book and Liu "may have just polished the prose", so the book is better credited to Liu Tong and Yu Yizheng together. It is used only for the book's authorship and the date of its preface.

Used for: the date of the Dijing jingwulue's preface, 1635, and its author (religion-and-death)
