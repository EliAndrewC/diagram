What it is: The English encyclopedia's short article on the Ming book describing Beijing, naming its author and his assistants and the date of its preface.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the book whose Qingming passage the record quotes, through its preface of 1635. Its limits: a short article flagged for needing citations and possibly containing original research; it is used only for the book's author and the date of its preface.

Used for: the date of the Dijing jingwulue's preface, 1635, and its author (religion-and-death)
