kotobank-uchigenkan

Digital Daijisen and Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, s.v. 内玄関 (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%86%85%E7%8E%84%E9%96%A2-434837)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the dictionary entries on the uchigenkan, the inner entrance a house kept beside its front entrance.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the household's own entrance, apart from the formal one, which is the door a residence's family used instead of the kitchen's. Dictionary lines with no plan and no rank attached; its earliest example is a joruri of 1714, the date of the word's first recorded use, not of any building.

Used for: the inner entrance for the family beside the formal entrance (buildings)
