江戸雑記録 (Edo zakkiroku), 町奉行役宅 ("The town magistrate's official residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (http://edo.ashigaru.net/?eid=23)

What it is: A 2018 post on a hobbyist site about the officials of late-Edo Tokyo, walking through the plan of the North town magistracy of 1841 area by area, with its plan drawing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the one city magistracy whose plan survives in detail - office on one side, residence on the other, the court with its temporary jail in the widest quarter - and says the South office was built nearly alike. It is an amateur's reading of the plan, not a survey; the plan itself is linked from the page.

Used for: the plan of an urban magistracy (cities/government)
