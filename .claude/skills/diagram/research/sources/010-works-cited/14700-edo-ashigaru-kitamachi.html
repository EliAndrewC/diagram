江戸雑記録 (Edo zakkiroku), 町奉行役宅 ("The town magistrate's official residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (http://edo.ashigaru.net/?eid=23)

What it is: A 2018 post on a hobbyist site about the shogunate's officials in the city of Edo from about 1804 to 1860, walking through the plan of the North town magistracy of 1841 area by area, with its plan drawing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the plan of Edo's North town magistracy as it stood in 1841 - office on one side, residence on the other, the court with its temporary jail in the widest quarter - and says the South office was built nearly alike. It is an amateur's reading of the plan, not a survey, and the post does not say which archive or book its plan drawing comes from; that plan, and one of the South office, are linked from the page. It is the magistracy of the shogun's capital, a city of about a million, so it shows the office at its largest and does not give the size of a provincial town's.

Used for: the plan of an urban magistracy (cities/government)
