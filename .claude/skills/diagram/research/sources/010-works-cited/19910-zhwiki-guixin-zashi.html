zhwiki-guixin-zashi

Chinese Wikipedia, "癸辛雜識" (Guixin zashi; in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hant/%E7%99%B8%E8%BE%9B%E9%9B%9C%E8%AD%98)

What it is: A short encyclopedia article on Zhou Mi's notebook: its author, his dates, and when and why it was written.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used only for the notebook's author and date - Zhou Mi (1232-1298), writing after the fall of the Song - which the transcribed chapter does not state. The article is a stub that cites no source for these dates, and it files the book both as a Yuan-era work and among Song-dynasty notebooks; either way it is late thirteenth century. It says nothing about fish.

Used for: dating Zhou Mi's Guixin zashi to the late thirteenth century (Field archetypes)
