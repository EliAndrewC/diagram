FAO, Irrigation water management: introduction to irrigation, training manual 1, chapter 5, Irrigation canals (https://www.fao.org/4/r4082e/r4082e06.htm)

What it is: A chapter of the FAO's introductory irrigation training manual on canal cross-sections, side slopes, freeboard and earthen construction.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the dug earthen trapezoidal section and the convention for its side slope and freeboard, the shape this page's ditch widths are computed from. A modern extension manual standing in for a physical form; it gives no slope for a particular soil.

Used for: the dug earthen canal's trapezoidal section
