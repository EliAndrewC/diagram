ja.wikipedia 農業全書 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/農業全書) - 「an agricultural manual published in Genroku 10 (1697)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「元禄10年（1697年）刊行された農書」).

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Nogyo Zensho (農業全書), the farming manual published in 1697, the tenth year of Genroku - the standard general treatise on Japanese agriculture of its day.

Why it applies, and its limits: It identifies and dates the manual named in our note on why night soil was aged before it went on a field, and a period farming manual is the right kind of authority for what a farmer was told to do. The limit is exact: this encyclopedia page describes the book and gives its year but says nothing about night soil or manuring, so it supports the manual's identity and date and not the practice - the passages about letting dung rot to ripeness before use are quoted on the encyclopedia's separate page for night soil.

Used for: the farming manual named in the night-soil withdrawal note (homesteads.html); the year and the night-soil attribution are flagged for the GM.
