shichiya-kotobank

Kotobank, 質屋 - 世界大百科事典 and 日本大百科全書 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%B3%AA%E5%B1%8B-73884)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the pawnbroker, carrying two published encyclopedias on the Edo-period trade as short-term lending on movable property, with counts: 2,700 pawnshops in eighteenth-century Edo, 2,731 in the Edo pawnbrokers' guild in 1723, 628 in Kyoto in 1699, and about 2,400 in Osaka at the end of the shogunate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the count a capital's amenity ratio is built from, given twice by two independent works, which is what turns a per-head figure from an assertion into arithmetic a reader can follow. Its limits are that these are tertiary summaries; that a guild roll counts members rather than premises; and that the page gives no population, so the one-per-so-many ratio this record quotes is our own division against a figure taken from elsewhere.

Used for: the Edo pawnshop count, at 2,700 in the eighteenth century and 2,731 in the 1723 guild, with Kyoto and Osaka beside it
