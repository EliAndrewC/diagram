ja.wikipedia 揚屋 (牢獄) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8F%9A%E5%B1%8B_(%E7%89%A2%E7%8D%84))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the agariya, the cells of Edo's Tenmacho jail kept for prisoners of higher status than commoners, with the number and size of their rooms and a later cell of the kind at Osaka's jail.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that sizes single cells, in mats, which is what a holding cell's size is calibrated against. Its limits are that it is a short tertiary article, that its cells are the great city jails', not the temporary cell of a country office, and that it gives each room's floor in mats and one side, not both sides.

Used for: the sizes of the Tenmacho and Osaka agariya cells (buildings)
