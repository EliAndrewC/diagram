What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Hanzheng Street in Wuhan, the ten-li commercial street that grew along the Han river's north bank in the Ming as the wharves of the new town of Hankou joined up into one street.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the growth of a Chinese river town's commercial street straight out of its line of wharves, the form our river capital's bankside district copies. A tertiary summary used for that. Hankou was one of the empire's great river entrepots, not an ordinary provincial seat, and most of the article's body is about the twentieth century; only its Ming paragraph is ours.

Used for: Hankou's wharves joining into a ten-li bankside street
