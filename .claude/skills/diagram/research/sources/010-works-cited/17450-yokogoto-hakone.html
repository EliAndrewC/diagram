週末大人旅 ("Weekend trips for grown-ups", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 【箱根関所跡】箱根観光で外せない『箱根関所』は、日本で唯一完全再現された関所。見どころ解説 ("The Hakone barrier, the one barrier in Japan fully rebuilt: a guide to what to see", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2020, updated 2023 (https://www.yokogoto.net/travel/20121.html)

What it is: A personal travel blog's illustrated walk through the rebuilt Hakone barrier, building by building, with photographs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the foot soldiers' guard house, with its jail, across from the main guard house, which is the arrangement a map's two gate stations follow. It is a visitor's account, on a travel blog that carries affiliate advertising, of the reconstruction completed in 2007, not a historical study. It describes one barrier, a shogunate checkpoint on a highway rather than the gate of a walled town, so a town gate's two stations borrow its arrangement rather than being shown by it. The reconstruction shows the barrier as the records of its late-Edo repair describe it; that it is faithful rests here on the blogger's own statement, and the town's site on the reconstruction (hakonesekisyo.jp) is the closer source.

Used for: the two guard houses of the Hakone barrier standing opposite each other (defenses)
