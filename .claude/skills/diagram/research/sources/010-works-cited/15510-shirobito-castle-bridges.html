shirobito-castle-bridges

Shirobito (Japan Castle Foundation), 超入門！お城セミナー 第62回お城は橋を戦術的に使い分けていた？ ("Castle seminar 62: did castles use their bridges tactically?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://shirobito.jp/article/755)

What it is: An introductory article of 2019 on the website of the Japan Castle Foundation, on the two kinds of castle bridge - the earthen causeway and the timber bridge - and which kind of gate each tended to serve, in medieval mountain castles and early-modern castles.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where each crossing tended to go and how broadly an early-modern causeway was built, which is what a map's gate crossings are chosen from. It is a popular article with no references, hedged in its own words ("seems to have tended"), and it gives no measurements.

Used for: timber bridges at rear gates, causeways at sortie and main gates; early-modern causeways stone-faced and very wide (defenses)
