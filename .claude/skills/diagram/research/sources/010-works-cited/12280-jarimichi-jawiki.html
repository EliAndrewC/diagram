ja.wikipedia 砂利道 ("gravel road", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%A0%82%E5%88%A9%E9%81%93)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the gravel road: its structure, its history, and a section on Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its section on Japan says that until modern times the ordinary road was earth trodden hard by people and horses, and gravel was confined to main roads - the one readable statement found of what a lesser road was surfaced with. It is a short tertiary summary, and it gives no date or region for the rule.

Used for: trodden earth as the Japanese norm, with gravel only on main roads (ways)
