karamete-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 搦手 and 大手門 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%90%A6%E6%89%8B%E9%96%80)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia articles on the karamete (搦手) and the ote-mon (大手門), the terms for a Japanese castle's rear approach and its main front gate.

Why it applies, and its limits: A castle in our maps needs a back way out as well as a ceremonial front, and these articles establish the plain vocabulary and the plain geometry - the karamete-mon stands opposite the ote-mon. That is all they establish: they are tertiary summaries of a generic form, they name no particular castle, and they say nothing about the divination or orientation reasoning that might have decided which side was which, so any such reasoning on our maps is ours and not theirs.

Used for: the karamete-mon as the rear gate opposite the ōte-mon (generic; Edo not named; no divination reasoning)
