en.wikipedia "Soil salinity control" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soil_salinity_control)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on controlling soil salinity in irrigated land, with the percolation losses that raise the water table and waterlog the ground.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that irrigation water the aquifer cannot carry away raises the water table and waterlogs the land, the seepage-below-a-command relationship this page draws. A modern engineering summary; it does not localize the waterlogging below the command.

Used for: percolation losses raising the water table to waterlogging
