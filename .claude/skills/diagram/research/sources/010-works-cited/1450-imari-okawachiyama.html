imari-okawachiyama

伊万里鍋島焼協同組合 (Imari Nabeshima-yaki Cooperative Association), 大川内山の歴史 (https://imari-ookawachiyama.com/history/)

What it is: The kiln village's own trade association's history page for Okawachiyama, where the Nabeshima domain moved its kiln in 1675 and guarded it with a barrier so that its techniques and craftsmen could not leave.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one plain case of a domain kiln set apart and guarded, the form our record keeps off-map as a prestige-ware kiln. Its limit is that it is the kiln village describing its own history, and it gives no distance from the castle town.

Used for: the Nabeshima kiln's move to Okawachiyama in 1675 and its barrier
