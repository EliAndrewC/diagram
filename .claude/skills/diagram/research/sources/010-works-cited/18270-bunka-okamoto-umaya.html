bunka-okamoto-umaya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 岡本家住宅馬屋 ("Okamoto house, stable", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/166622)

What it is: The national register's entry for the stable of the Okamoto house at Ozu, Kumamoto prefecture, built about 1840 and altered between 1926 and 1988: its period, its timber build and tiled roof, its building area, and where it stands on the plot.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the building area and siting of a freestanding stable of the Edo period. Its limits: it gives no length or depth, only an area; the building was altered in the twentieth century (its upper story cut down in the early Showa years, the page says), so the area may not be the original; it belongs to a substantial household with several storehouses and gates; and it is one example, in Kyushu.

Used for: the size and siting of an Edo farm stable (homesteads)
