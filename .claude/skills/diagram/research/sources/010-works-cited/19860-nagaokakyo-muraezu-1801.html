nagaokakyo-muraezu-1801

Nagaokakyo City Lifelong Learning Division, Cultural Properties Section, "Exhibition corner news no. 49" (in Japanese; https://www.city.nagaokakyo.lg.jp/cmsfiles/contents/0000005/5235/hurusatofile49.pdf)

What it is: A two-page exhibition leaflet of 2012 from the city of Nagaokakyo, southwest of Kyoto, on old roads and sites of the district, which describes a village map of 1801 drawn when two villages settled a boundary dispute.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the 1801 map draws a local lord's house surrounded by a thicket (yabu), with houses along the road, so it shows a map before modern times drawing a thicket as a feature of its own. It does not say the thicket was bamboo, nor how the map drew it; it is one map, described, not reproduced large enough to read.

Used for: a thicket drawn on a village map of 1801 (vegetation)
