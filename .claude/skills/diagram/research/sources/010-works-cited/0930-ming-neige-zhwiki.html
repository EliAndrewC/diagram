What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Ming Grand Secretariat, the body of grand secretaries who drafted the emperor's decisions: its origin, its standing, and where its offices stood inside the Forbidden City.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the emperor's secretariat inside the palace, which is the Chinese half of the rule this page draws - the ruler's council beside the ruler, the executive bureaus outside. A tertiary summary used for that placement, and it also warns that the body was never a first-rank organ of state.

Used for: the Grand Secretariat's offices east of the Meridian Gate, inside the palace
