上下水道情報plus, 連載水道の話いろいろ(7) 江戸明治の上水 (https://ktjplus.jp/org/masukosuidou01/masukosuidou07/)

What it is: An installment, dated 2024-10-25, of a serialized column on the history of Japanese water supply by Masuko Atsushi, a former head of the Tokyo Waterworks Bureau, published on a water-industry information site, covering the Edo and Meiji water systems and giving a count of the draw-wells that the Edo aqueducts fed across the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page that gives a city-wide count of aqueduct wells, which shows that a smaller figure often quoted belongs to one system alone. Its author ran the successor utility, which is more authority than a trade column usually carries; its limits are that it is a column without its own citations, and that its count spans Edo and early Tokyo together.

Used for: the 8,000 aqueduct wells across Edo-Tokyo
