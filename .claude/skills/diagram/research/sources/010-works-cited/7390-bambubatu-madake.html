Bambu Batu, Phyllostachys bambusoides (https://bambubatu.com/phyllostachys-bambusoides-japanese-timber-bamboo/) - "Cold hardiness: Approximately -5 to -10°F (-20 to -23°C)"

What it is: A page on madake from Bambu Batu, an educational bamboo blog carrying affiliate links for tools and bedding, giving the species' cold tolerance in a quick-facts box - about -5 to -10 F, -20 to -23 C - and a line on frost damage to the culms.

Why it applies, and its limits: A plant's cold tolerance is a constant of the species, so a present-day figure for the temperature at which madake is damaged bears on a premodern grove, and this page sits at the tolerant end of the spread the record keeps open. Its limits are that the figure is an unsourced quick-facts entry on a blog, with no method behind it, not a measurement; and that it describes cultivated plantings today, chiefly outside East Asia.
