What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tauchiguruma, the hand-pushed rotary paddy weeder of the Meiji period; a short encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that paddy weeding before the weeder was by hand or by hand claw, crouching, and that the weeder is walked between the rice hills - the between-rows tool for mud this page's argument turns on. It gives no dates for the tool's spread.

Used for: hand weeding before the rotary weeder (fields)
