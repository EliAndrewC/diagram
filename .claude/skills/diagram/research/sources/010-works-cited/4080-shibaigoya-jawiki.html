ja.wikipedia 芝居小屋 ("playhouse", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/芝居小屋)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the shibai-goya, the Japanese playhouse; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the roofed, seated kabuki theater to the late Edo period, arising "centered on" Edo, Kyoto and Osaka, and says such houses spread to every city only after the Meiji Restoration; that supports the permanent house as an urban form, not a village one. Its limits: it is a four-sentence encyclopedia stub whose history rests on a single 1997 paper about the playhouses' twentieth-century decline, so its Edo dating is a background summary and only approximate; it says "centered on", not "only", and its own list includes the Kanamaru-za (1835) in the shrine town of Kotohira, an Edo-period permanent playhouse outside the three great cities; and it covers Japan alone, saying nothing of Chinese permanent stages.

Used for: the permanent theater's tier (towns); roofed playhouses mostly in the three great cities before Meiji (urban-features)
