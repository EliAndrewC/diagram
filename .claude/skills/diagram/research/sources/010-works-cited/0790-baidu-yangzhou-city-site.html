Baidu Baike (English edition), Yangzhou City Site (https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Yangzhou%20City%20Site/934310)

What it is: The English edition of Baidu Baike's entry on the Yangzhou city site, the archaeological remains of the Tang city: a zicheng of offices on the old Sui palace ground and a luocheng below Shu hill holding the commercial, craft and residential quarters, enlarged in the mid Tang.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the two-ring form of a Tang prefectural city and which ring held what, the case our capital's citadel-and-town pairing is drawn from. Its limits are serious and stated: Baidu Baike is an open-edit encyclopedia and its English edition a machine rendering of the Chinese, so it is cited only for a statement the scholarly literature makes too, and the monograph that makes it, Heng Chye Kiang's Cities of Aristocrats and Bureaucrats, is in print only.

Used for: Tang Yangzhou's zicheng of offices and luocheng of commerce and housing
