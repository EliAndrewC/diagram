shanghai-tudi-zhengli

DG/TJ08-2079-2010 (J11790-2010), 土地开发整理工程建设技术标准 ("Standard for land consolidation engineering"), a Shanghai municipal engineering construction standard, printed 2011 and served by the city's housing and urban-rural development commission (https://zjw.sh.gov.cn/shsd/userfiles/255%E5%9C%9F%E5%9C%B0%E5%BC%80%E5%8F%91%E6%95%B4%E7%90%86%E5%B7%A5%E7%A8%8B%E5%BB%BA%E8%AE%BE%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E6%A0%87%E5%87%86.pdf)

What it is: A municipal technical standard for land development and consolidation works, published by the Shanghai city government's housing and urban-rural development commission. Its irrigation section names the grades of an open-channel canal system and says how each is laid out and sized.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the five fixed grades of the Chinese irrigation ladder in order - main, branch and lateral as the canal system proper, farm and field as the in-field distribution - and puts the maoqu at the bottom of it, laid out to the size of the individual basin and, usefully for a map of a hamlet, dug by the farmers themselves. That is what tells us where the drawn net honestly stops and what lies below the last channel we can draw. Its limits: a municipal standard of 2010 for consolidated, machine-worked land, so the ladder it names is the one a present-day designer works to rather than a premodern one, and it gives no dimensions we use; and its PDF's embedded font inserts spaces between characters and a filler glyph at some clause starts, so a machine reading it and a person reading it see different strings.

Used for: the five fixed canal grades named in order, and the maoqu as the lowest of them - sized to the individual basin and dug by the farmer
