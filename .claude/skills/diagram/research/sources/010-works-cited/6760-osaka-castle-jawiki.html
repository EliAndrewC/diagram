What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Osaka Castle (大坂城), a tertiary encyclopedia summary of the castle's site, its successive constructions and its history, including the plateau it stands on and the rivers running below it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one geographic statement our river-and-castle maps use - the Yodo and its tributaries below the northern plateau serving as natural moat and as the water drawn into the castle's moats. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary, and the sentence we quote carries no reference in the article, so we take it as well-known geography and nothing more; and that Osaka is the largest castle in Japan, exceptional even for a capital-tier map, so the arrangement transfers and the scale does not.

Used for: the Yodo and its tributaries below the northern plateau 「It served the function of a natural moat and was at the same time used to draw water into the moats inside the castle.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「天然の堀の機能を果たすとともに、城内の堀へと水を引き込むのに利用された」)
