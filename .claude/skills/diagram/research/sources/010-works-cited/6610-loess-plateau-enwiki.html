en.wikipedia Loess Plateau (replaces the Grokipedia History of agriculture in China) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loess_Plateau)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Loess Plateau of northern China, covering the region's wind-blown soils, its erosion, its agricultural history and its climate, including the concentration of the year's rain into June, July and August.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for that one climatic fact, because a summer-concentrated rainfall is what makes storage ponds, bunds and flood channels worth drawing in a dry-looking landscape. It is a tertiary summary whose figures we take only where its own references support them, and it covers one region of northern China, not the whole of the country our setting draws on.

Used for: "40-60% of the precipitation concentrate in June to August" - the summer concentration; the 400-800 mm range is not on the page
