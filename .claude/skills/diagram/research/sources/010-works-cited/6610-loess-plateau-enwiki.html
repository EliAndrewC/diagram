en.wikipedia Loess Plateau (replaces the Grokipedia History of agriculture in China) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loess_Plateau)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Loess Plateau of northern China, covering the region's wind-blown soils, its erosion, its climate and its modern restoration projects, including the concentration of the year's rain into June, July and August.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for that one climatic fact, because a summer-concentrated rainfall is what makes storage ponds, bunds and flood channels worth drawing in a dry-looking landscape. It covers one region of northern China, semi-arid in the north and sub-humid in the south, not the whole of the country our setting draws on. The figure sits in the article's table of recent climate and environmental change, from a single cited study, in a cell headed "Uniform distribution" that contradicts its own number: the summer concentration of the monsoon is old, but the 40-60% is a modern measurement summarized loosely.

Used for: "40-60% of the precipitation concentrate in June to August" - the summer concentration; the 400-800 mm range is not on the page
