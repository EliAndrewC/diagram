kotobank-katteguchi

Digital Daijisen, Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho and a house-terminology dictionary, s.v. 勝手口 ("kitchen door", title translated from the Japanese by this project), on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/勝手口)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the dictionary entry on the katteguchi, the kitchen door of a Japanese house and the host's door of a tea room.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the kitchen door as a named entrance set against the guests', which is the service circulation this page inverts; dictionary lines of the present-day language, giving the door as the way from outside into the kitchen and apart from the formal entrance. The Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's dated examples put the word in the kitchen sense in 1666, which dates the door, not its use; who uses the door (tradesmen, neighbors, children) is given only in the present tense, of houses today.

Used for: the kitchen door as the service entrance, the word's first attested use, and who uses the door today (buildings)
