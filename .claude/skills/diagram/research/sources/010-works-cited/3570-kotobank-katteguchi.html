kotobank-katteguchi

Digital Daijisen, Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho and a house-terminology dictionary, s.v. 勝手口, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/勝手口)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the dictionary entry on the katteguchi, the kitchen door of a Japanese house and the host's door of a tea room.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the kitchen door as a named entrance set against the guests', which is the service circulation this page inverts; dictionary lines of the present-day language, giving the door as the way from outside into the kitchen and apart from the formal entrance, with no era attached.

Used for: the kitchen door as the service entrance (buildings)
