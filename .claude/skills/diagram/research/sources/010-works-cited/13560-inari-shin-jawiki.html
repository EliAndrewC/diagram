ja.wikipedia 稲荷神 ("Inari, the deity", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%A8%B2%E8%8D%B7%E7%A5%9E)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Inari, the deity of rice and later of trade, and on the spread of its worship.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records the Edo saying that Inari shrines were among the three things one met everywhere in the city, which is the one statement read on how thick small street shrines stood in a Japanese town. Its limits: a saying is not a count, and it is about Edo, the largest city in the country, rather than a county town.

Used for: small Inari shrines everywhere in Edo (religion-and-death)
