homemate-sannomaru

Homemate Research (城・日本の城・城郭用語辞典, a castle glossary site), 三の丸 ("the third bailey", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.homemate-research-castle.com/useful/glossary/castle/2128201/)

What it is: A short glossary entry on the sannomaru, a castle's third bailey: its defensive role guarding the second bailey, and what stood in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists in one sentence what a castle's outer bailey held - senior retainers' residences, places for keeping horses, storehouses - which is what our inventory of the castle's contents and the siting of the lineage compounds rest on. It is a commercial site's general-audience glossary with no references and no named castle, so it states the typical arrangement and not how common it was; the record pairs it with one named castle's own page.

Used for: what the third bailey held: senior retainers' residences, horse-keeping, storehouses (cities/capitals)
