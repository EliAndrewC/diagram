Wenhui Bao, Zhao Zhengnan, "Why is the character 井 written this way? The first well found in China was square - Ruan Yisan on well culture" (title translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「井字为何这样写？我国发现的第一口井是方的！听阮仪三谈井文化」), 28 February 2018 (in Chinese; https://www.whb.cn/zhuzhan/jjl/20180228/190600.html)

What it is: A Shanghai newspaper interview with Ruan Yisan, director of Tongji University's research center for historic cities, on the old wells of Chinese towns: the curb with several openings, the platform with its separate clean-water and waste-water ditches, and the curb stone of each dynasty.

Why it applies, and its limits: A leading conservation scholar describing surviving town wells in China, from Song to Qing. It is a newspaper interview without measurements, and it speaks of towns, not villages. Its stone for each dynasty's curb (basalt for Song and Yuan, a grey sedimentary stone for Ming, granite for Qing) is read from surviving wells, and a curb's stone follows the nearby quarries, so the sequence dates a curb in the region those wells stand in; it is no rule for a well elsewhere in China.

Used for: the Chinese town well's curb and its platform with drains (urban-features)
