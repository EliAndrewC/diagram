Uekipedia, クロマツ（黒松） (the Japanese black pine), garden-tree encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://www.uekipedia.jp/%E5%B8%B8%E7%B7%91%E9%87%9D%E8%91%89%E6%A8%B9/%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AD%E3%83%9E%E3%83%84/)

What it is: The page on the Japanese black pine in a Japanese encyclopedia of garden trees written for gardeners: its height, its use as the lead tree of the Japanese garden, its trained forms, and the pruning it needs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the species' height and says plainly that a garden pine is a pruned one. Its limits are that it is a present-day gardening reference, not a history, so its pruning is today's practice; and that it gives the tree's untended height only, not the size a garden pine is kept to.

Used for: the black pine's height of 15-40 m; the pine as the garden's lead tree, kept by pruning twice a year (vegetation)
