ja.wikipedia 小石川養生所 ("the Koishikawa Yojosho", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/小石川養生所)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Koishikawa Yojosho, the free infirmary for the poor that the shogunate opened in 1722 inside its herb garden in Edo, with its building, its beds, its staff and its patients by year.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one Edo-period public infirmary this record has read, and it says what it was - one longhouse of forty beds in the herb garden - which sets the Japanese form a relief house takes on a capital's map. A tertiary summary; it describes the shogun's own city, gives the building's type but not its measures, and says nothing of such houses in the domains.
