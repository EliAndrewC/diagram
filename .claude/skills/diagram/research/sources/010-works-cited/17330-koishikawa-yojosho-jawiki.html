ja.wikipedia 小石川養生所 ("the Koishikawa Yojosho", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/小石川養生所)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Koishikawa Yojosho, the free infirmary for the poor that the shogunate opened in 1722 inside its herb garden in Edo, with its building, its beds, its staff and its patients by year.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one Edo-period public infirmary this record has read, and it says what it was - one longhouse of forty beds in the herb garden - which sets the Japanese form a relief house takes on a capital's map. It describes the shogun's own city, gives the building's type (a shingle-roofed longhouse) but not its measures, and says nothing of such houses in the domains. The forty is the capacity at the opening in 1722, given without a footnote; the article says the number of places was raised from time to time after 1723 without saying to what, so forty is the house at its founding, not the house of every later decade.
