National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, a question on the origin of shrine fences (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000040750&page=ref_view)

What it is: A library reference-service record: a patron asks about shrine fences because their neighborhood shrine has only a torii, and the librarian points to a book on fences.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a plain witness that an ordinary shrine with an arch and no fence was unremarkable. Its limits: modern, one unnamed shrine, and the absence is incidental to the question; it is not a survey.

Used for: a neighborhood shrine with only an arch (religion-and-death.html).
