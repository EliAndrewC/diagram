What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Himeji castle, with the lengths and widths of its three moat circuits.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the moat widths of the great provincial castle these maps take as their provincial-city moat anchor. A tertiary summary; the widths are the moats as they survive, and no average is stated.

Used for: the three moats' widths
