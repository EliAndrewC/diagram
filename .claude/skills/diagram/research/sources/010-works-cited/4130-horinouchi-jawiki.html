horinouchi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 堀の内 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/堀の内)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the horinouchi, the moated residence of a medieval warrior or resident lord; a short encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the moated and banked seat as the usual form, which is the individually fortified estate this page disperses across the district; medieval Japan; the page gives the seat's size as 1 to 2 chō square (about 100 to 200 m) up to several tens of chō, and says the form declined in the Sengoku as daimyo drew local warriors into their castle towns, so an Edo-modeled district scatters a form its own era had consolidated away.

Used for: the moated warrior residence (hinterland)
