Kotobank, 俸禄制 - 日本大百科全書 and 世界大百科事典 entries (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%BF%B8%E7%A6%84%E5%88%B6-1414874)

What it is: The kotobank reference page for the stipend system of the Edo domains, carrying the entries of two Japanese reference encyclopedias: retainers paid in warehouse rice out of the tax rice the domain collected into its own granary.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the domain granary's two-way job - tax rice in, stipends out - which is the whole function of the granary district on our capital. Its limit is the encyclopedia's summary form.

Used for: stipends paid from the domain granary out of the tax rice it collected
