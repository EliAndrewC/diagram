psu-geog486-point-labels

Penn State, GEOG 486 Cartography and Visualization, Cary Anderson and Fritz Kessler, Lesson 2, "Label Placement" (section "Point labels") (https://courses.ems.psu.edu/geog486/node/557)

What it is: A lesson of Penn State's online cartography course, openly licensed, on labeling points, lines and areas: legibility and association as the two concerns for a point label, the preferred positions, spacing, line breaks, labels along lines, and leader lines.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a university teaching text that states the textbook rules plainly and says where authorities differ on the exact ranking of positions. It is course material rather than a primary study, and it gives no number for how close a label should stand: it says the choice depends on the map and that consistency matters most.

Used for: legibility and association as the point label's two concerns; consistency of spacing; labels above lines and never upside down (presentation)
