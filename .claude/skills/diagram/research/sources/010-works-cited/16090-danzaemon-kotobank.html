danzaemon-kotobank

Kotobank, 弾左衛門 ("Danzaemon", title translated from the Japanese by this project), gathering the entries of several Japanese encyclopedias, among them Shogakukan's Nipponica and Heibonsha's Sekai Daihyakka Jiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%BC%BE%E5%B7%A6%E8%A1%9B%E9%96%80-95031)

What it is: The published encyclopedias' entries on Danzaemon, the hereditary head of the outcastes of the Kanto in the Edo period: his jurisdiction, his residence at Asakusa and the count of the households under him.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the count of eta, hinin and monkey-trainers' households under the Kanto head in a return of about 1800, which is the scale of a whole region's outcaste population, not of one town's quarter. The encyclopedias on the page differ in small things - one dates the count to 1800 and counts the hinin in households, another to the Kansei era and counts them in huts - and it describes Edo, the Kanto and the parts of Izu, Kai, Suruga and Mutsu under the same head only. The encyclopedias are tertiary summaries: the count is the head's own return to the shogunate (a kakiage, as the Nipponica entry calls it), repeated secondhand, and it takes in the households under him directly and indirectly, so it is the figure a head gave for his whole jurisdiction, not a census of the settlements in it.

Used for: the household count of the Kanto head's jurisdiction (urban-features)
