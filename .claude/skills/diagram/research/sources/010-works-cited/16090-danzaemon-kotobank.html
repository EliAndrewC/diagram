danzaemon-kotobank

Kotobank, 弾左衛門 ("Danzaemon", title translated from the Japanese by this project), gathering the entries of several Japanese encyclopedias, among them Shogakukan's Nipponica and Heibonsha's Sekai Daihyakka Jiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%BC%BE%E5%B7%A6%E8%A1%9B%E9%96%80-95031)

What it is: The published encyclopedias' entries on Danzaemon, the hereditary head of the outcastes of the Kanto in the Edo period: his jurisdiction, his residence at Asakusa and the count of the households under him.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the count of eta, hinin and monkey-trainers' households under the Kanto head in a return of about 1800, which is the scale of a whole region's outcaste population, not of one town's quarter. The encyclopedias on the page differ in small things - one dates the count to 1800 and counts the hinin in households, another to the Kansei era and counts them in huts - and it describes Edo and the Kanto only.

Used for: the household count of the Kanto head's jurisdiction (urban-features)
