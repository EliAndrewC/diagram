agechi-rei-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 上知令 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%8A%E7%9F%A5%E4%BB%A4)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the agechi-rei, orders recalling a holder's granted lands. It is mostly about the Edo shogunate's orders (1764, 1799-1855, the Tenpō order of 1843), with a two-sentence section on the Meiji government's orders of 1871 and 1875 that confiscated the domains (寺社領) temples and shrines had held in the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the taking of shrine lands that stands between an Edo precinct and every Meiji-or-later register figure. Its limits: tertiary, and its Meiji section is two sentences resting on one forestry-encyclopedia citation (Shimada 1984). It dates the orders and says they took the shrines' granted domains, but it says nothing of the precinct itself: it does not say that the 1871 order took the land outside the precinct, or that the 1875 rules cut the precinct down to what the rites needed. Nor does it say what either took from an ordinary village shrine, which seldom held a granted domain. For those steps it carries only the dates.

Used for: the Meiji taking of shrine lands behind the precinct registers (religion-and-death.html).
