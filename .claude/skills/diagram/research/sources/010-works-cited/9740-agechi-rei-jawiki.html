What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the orders of surrender (agechi-rei), including the Meiji government's orders of 1871 and 1875 that confiscated the domains temples and shrines had held in the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the taking of shrine lands that stands between an Edo precinct and every Meiji-or-later register figure. Its limits: tertiary; it gives the orders' dates and not what each took from an ordinary village shrine, which was often only its own ground.

Used for: the Meiji taking of shrine lands behind the precinct registers (religion-and-death.html).
