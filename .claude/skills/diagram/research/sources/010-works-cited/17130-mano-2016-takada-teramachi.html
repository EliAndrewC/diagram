mano-2016-takada-teramachi

Mano Toshikazu, 近世城下町における寺町と寺院 越後国高田寺町の場合 ("Temple quarters and temples in the early modern castle town: the case of the Takada teramachi in Echigo", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 頸城野郷土資料室学術研究部研究紀要 1(1), 2016, pp. 1-25 (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kfa/1/1/1_1/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: An open-access research paper by a folklorist on the temple quarter of the castle town of Takada (Echigo), asking what the quarter did for the town: faith and pilgrimage, entertainment, and social welfare.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the defensive reading of the temple quarter is the near-settled view, names the works that hold it and the one that has objected, and gives the author's own doubt for Takada, whose plan left the highway nearly undefended. The doubt is argued for one town and the author says it is too little to generalize, so it makes the defensive purpose contested rather than refuted.

Used for: whether the temple quarter was placed for defense (cities/capitals)
