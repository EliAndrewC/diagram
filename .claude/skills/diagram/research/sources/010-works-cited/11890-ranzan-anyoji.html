ranzan-anyoji

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡寺院明細帳, 天台宗 安養寺 菅谷村大字大蔵 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter18.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription of the Hiki district temple register entry for Anyōji, a Tendai temple of Ōkura, Saitama, with its main hall and a precinct of 285 tsubo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the smallest main precinct figure among the nineteen temples of this register that the Ranzan town museum transcribes (those within present Ranzan town, not the whole Hiki district), the bottom of the band. Its limits: one rural temple; a Meiji-era register (land valued in yen, the precinct classed 民有地第一種) kept in a 1963 prefectural file, drawn up after the land-tax reform and the Meiji seizures of temple land; so the 285 tsubo is the Meiji precinct, possibly smaller than the Edo one, and is a floor rather than an Edo measurement.

Used for: the smallest temple precinct in the band (religion-and-death.html).
