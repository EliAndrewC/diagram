mboso-hana

Minamiboso City, 南房総いいとこどり, 花栽培の歴史 ("The history of flower growing", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.mboso-etoko.jp/manabu/sangyo/hana/)

What it is: The city's tourism page on the history of flower farming in southern Boso, from the Edo-period narcissus sent to Edo to the Taisho-era growers who shipped cut flowers to wholesalers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records flowers sold from the country into a city's houses in the Edo period, and when growing flowers as a farm crop began there. A promotional page that relies on a growers' association anniversary book and on legend. For the Edo-period sales it says only that a record exists, without naming it; the one period text it quotes, Matsudaira Sadanobu's 1792 travel diary, says narcissus bloomed in great numbers near Hota; the page reads that as showing they were already grown there, but it shows no sale. So the sale into Edo's houses is the page's claim, not a record we have seen; it contradicts itself on when market growing began, since its history section names Meiji-era lilies and peonies before the Taisho start its headline gives.

Used for: Edo-period narcissus sold into Edo's houses from Boshu; flower farming as a later development (fields)
