maff-nogyoyosui-suiden

MAFF (Japan, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries), land-improvement planning standard, technical volume on agricultural water for paddy (土地改良事業計画設計基準 計画 農業用水 水田 技術書, the new-old comparison table), section 1.1 on Edo-period paddy and water development and section 7.3 on separating irrigation from drainage (https://www.maff.go.jp/j/nousin/noukan/tyotei/kizyun/attach/pdf/nougyouyousui_suiden-1.pdf)

What it is: The technical volume of the Japanese Ministry of Agriculture's planning standard for paddy irrigation water, in its published old-against-new comparison form. Its opening history describes Edo-period development - in a drought every drop of a mountain catchment's runoff and every district's return flow was taken up downstream - and its figure of irrigation types names the three layouts: field to field, one combined channel for supply and drainage, and separate supply and drainage channels, the last the standard of postwar field consolidation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the ministry's own account of how paddy water was used before modern works, and it is the source for two things on our maps: that a village's drainage went on to be used again downstream rather than being wasted, and that a separate drainage channel is the modern layout while the older ones ran drainage field to field or back into a shared channel. Its limits are that it is a planning standard whose history is a preface; that the page linked is an amendment's comparison table, in which every unamended passage is elided, so a reader following the link finds fragments of the standard rather than the whole; that its Edo passage is about the whole basin in drought rather than one brook; and that it gives the separated layout a modern date without dating the older ones.

Used for: in an Edo drought all mountain runoff and all return flow taken up basin-wide; the three irrigation layouts (field to field, combined, separated); separation as the field-consolidation standard; separation raising the water requirement because return flow is no longer reused
