What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the willow genus, its habitat, its uses in basketry and its planting on stream banks; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives willow's wet-ground habit, its interlacing roots protecting a bank against the water and the withies grown in pollards, which is the bank-planting mechanism this page's dike rows rest on; a general article with nothing on Chinese dikes or dates.
