honjin-kotobank

Kotobank, s.v. 本陣 (honjin; the Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica), among others; in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9C%AC%E9%99%A3-133353)

What it is: The Kotobank dictionary page gathering Japanese reference works' entries on the honjin, the lodging house at a post station where daimyo and other persons of rank stayed: who used it, how many a station had, and what its building had.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the count per station and the honjin's guests. It is a set of dictionary entries, general across all highways, and gives no sizes.

Used for: who lodged at a honjin; one or two to a station on average, none at some, five or six at others (towns)
