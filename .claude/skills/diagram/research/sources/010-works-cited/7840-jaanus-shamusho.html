jaanus-shamusho

JAANUS, shamusho (Columbia mirror) (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/shamusho) - 「No distinction was made between public and private affairs and business was frequently carried on at the private home of the administrator, keieisha 経宮者, especially at small shrines.」 (the source's own English; the characters are the page's own gloss)

What it is: The Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System glossary entry on the shamusho, a shrine's office building.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the absence of an office at small shrines, whose business was done at the administrator's private house - which is why the program has no office building. A glossary entry, undated.

Used for: no office at small shrines (religion-and-death.html).
