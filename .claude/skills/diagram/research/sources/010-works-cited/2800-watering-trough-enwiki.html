en.wikipedia "Watering trough" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watering_trough)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the watering trough, the basin set at a rural or urban watering place for animals and, as the abreuvoir, for humans too.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a community's watering place served its beasts and its humans alike, which is why a village well on these maps may have a trough beside it. A tertiary summary of a largely Western institution; it names springs, not wells, as where troughs stood.

Used for: the shared watering place for humans and animals
