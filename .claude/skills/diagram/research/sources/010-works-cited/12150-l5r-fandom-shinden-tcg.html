What it is: The fan wiki of the Legend of the Five Rings game, its page on the Rokugani temple, summarizing the rulebooks; the paragraph quoted here closes on a reference to Emerald Empire: The Essential Guide to Rokugan (Fantasy Flight Games, 5th edition), pp. 176-178.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the smallest temples relied on visiting clergy or volunteers but most had at least one resident monk. It is a fan summary of a fictional game's setting and says nothing about how temples in historical China or Japan were staffed; the sentence carries no reference of its own, it speaks of temples rather than village shrines, and the GM's campaign notes govern.
