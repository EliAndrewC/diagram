zh.wikipedia 驛道 ("post road", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/驛道)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the official post road of imperial China, illustrated by the surviving stone-slab stretch at Yunnanyi in Yunnan, a Ming and Qing post station, and the width of about one zhang (roughly 3 m) it says the road was built to.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the surface and width of a surviving stretch of Chinese official road where it ran through a post station, the Chinese counterpart of an Imperial road through a town. It is a stub, tagged as listing no sources, and describes one remote station in the southwest; it is not evidence for the streets of towns in general. It does not say when the surviving slabs were laid, so the surface seen today may be a later relaying or restoration rather than the Ming or Qing one. Its "about one zhang" width and its "national standard" are the article's own unsourced statements, so the width is an approximation, not a documented rule.

Used for: the stone-slab surface and about one-zhang width of a Chinese official road through a post station (ways)
