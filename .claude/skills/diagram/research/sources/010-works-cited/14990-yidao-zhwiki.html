zh.wikipedia 驛道 ("post road", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/驛道)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the official post road of imperial China, illustrated by the surviving stone-slab stretch at Yunnanyi in Yunnan, a Ming and Qing post station, with the standard width it was built to.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the surface and width of a surviving stretch of Chinese official road where it ran through a post station, the Chinese counterpart of an Imperial road through a town. It is a stub, tagged as listing no sources, and describes one remote station in the southwest; it is not evidence for the streets of towns in general.

Used for: the stone-slab surface and one-zhang width of a Chinese official road through a post station (ways)
