What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the pig toilet, the privy set over a pigsty, once common in rural China and attested from Han-dynasty tomb models.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the privy-and-pigsty grouping and its antiquity, the manure economy behind this page's siting of the animal facilities with the latrine. A tertiary summary; it speaks of pigsties, not of cattle sheds or chicken houses.
