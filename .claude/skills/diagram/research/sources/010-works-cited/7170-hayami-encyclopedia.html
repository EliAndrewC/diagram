hayami-encyclopedia

"Hayami, Akira", encyclopedia.com (https://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/hayami-akira)

What it is: A reference-work biography of the Japanese historical demographer Akira Hayami, written by the historical demographer Osamu Saito for the Encyclopedia of Population and republished on encyclopedia.com. It describes how Hayami brought the methods of European historical demography to the Japanese 宗門改帳 (shumon aratame cho), the annual religious-investigation registers of households and population, and summarizes what those registers showed about marriage, fertility, mortality and household size in Tokugawa Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: Household size is the number that turns a population into a count of houses on a map, and this entry vouches for the source those numbers come from and for the direction of travel - households in the regions studied got smaller over the period. It is a tertiary summary of somebody else's scholarship rather than the scholarship itself, and it gives no absolute household figure at all, so we use it only to confirm the method and the trend and take actual numbers from elsewhere.

Used for: confirming the shumon aratame cho register methodology and a downward household-size trend in the regions studied. Recorded as giving NO absolute figure
