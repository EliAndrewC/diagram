What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the waju, the ring-diked polder settlements of the Nobi plain; an encyclopedia article citing prefectural and academic works.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the continuous ring dike and the homestead's own windbreak and flood groves sited by the winter wind and the flood direction, which is this page's evidence that a polder village kept its house groves; it says nothing of planting on the dikes themselves.
