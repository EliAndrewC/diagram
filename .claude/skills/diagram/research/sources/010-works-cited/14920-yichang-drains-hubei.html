Hubei Provincial Department of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, 《东湖县志》之《县沟衢图》宜昌古城排水系统图解与述往 ("The county drains-and-streets map of the Donghu county gazetteer: the drainage system of old Yichang, illustrated and explained", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (http://zjt.hubei.gov.cn/bmdt/ztzl/hbcjda/dayd/201910/t20191028_82099.shtml)

What it is: A provincial archives feature on a map of the drains and streets of the walled city of Yichang in the county gazetteer of 1864, describing the city's size, its forty-odd streets and lanes, and its five main covered drains and their construction.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the drains of a Chinese walled county-level city of the late Qing from a map drawn by its own magistrate, the kind of seat the setting's larger towns and provincial cities resemble. It is one city, on the Yangzi at the mouth of the gorges, and a late date; how common covered drains were in smaller towns is not on it.

Used for: that a Chinese walled city's drains ran covered, with stone lids a cart could cross (ways)
