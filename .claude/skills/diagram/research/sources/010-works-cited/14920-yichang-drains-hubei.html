Hubei Provincial Department of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, 《东湖县志》之《县沟衢图》宜昌古城排水系统图解与述往 ("The county drains-and-streets map of the Donghu county gazetteer: the drainage system of old Yichang, illustrated and explained", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (http://zjt.hubei.gov.cn/bmdt/ztzl/hbcjda/dayd/201910/t20191028_82099.shtml)

What it is: An article from the China Archives News (中国档案报, 30 March 2018), reprinted in the archives column of the Hubei housing department's site, on a map of the drains and streets of the walled city of Yichang in the county gazetteer of 1864 (the original held by the Yichang Municipal Archives), describing the city's size, its forty-odd streets and lanes, and its five main covered drains and their construction.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the drains of a Chinese walled city of the late Qing - the seat of Yichang prefecture and of its attached Donghu county - from a map drawn by the county magistrate, the kind of seat the setting's larger towns and provincial cities resemble. It is one city, on the Yangzi at the mouth of the gorges, and a late date; how common covered drains were in smaller towns is not on it. It is a modern newspaper's summary, not the gazetteer's text: that the drains were brick- or stone-lined under stone lids that carts and horses could cross is the article's own sentence, not a passage it quotes from the gazetteer.

Used for: that a Chinese walled city's drains ran covered, with stone lids a cart could cross (ways)
