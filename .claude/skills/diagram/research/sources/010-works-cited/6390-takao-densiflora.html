takao-densiflora

Takao 599 Museum, Pinus densiflora (https://www.takao599museum.jp/treasures/selected/1419/?lang=en)

What it is: The Japanese red pine's species page at the Takao 599 Museum, the natural history museum at the foot of Mount Takao outside Tokyo: what the tree is, where it stands and what it is planted for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page that says a red pine is often planted for gardens and windbreaks, which is the human use a map draws rather than the botany. Its limits are that it is a museum's public species page rather than a flora or a survey, and that it describes the tree in one Japanese locality.

Used for: the red pine planted for gardens and windbreaks
