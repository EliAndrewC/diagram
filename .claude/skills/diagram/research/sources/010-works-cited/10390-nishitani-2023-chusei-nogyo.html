nishitani-2023-chusei-nogyo

Nishitani Masahiro, "Chusei no nogyo to tochi seido" (Medieval agriculture and the land system), in "Nihon chusei keizaishi no kaiko to tenbo", Shakai keizai shigaku (Socio-Economic History) 89(3), November 2023, pp. 217ff. (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/sehs/89/3/89_217/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A review article by an economic historian at Fukuoka University, surveying research on medieval Japanese agriculture and land tenure for the Socio-Economic History Society's journal. Among much else it gives the cropped share of Heian paddy, the worked and resting paddy of Oyama estate in 1102, the yearly re-choosing of plots on unstable land, and the stabilizing of land and the nucleation of villages from the Kamakura period on.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a recent, peer-reviewed survey of the field, and it carries the figures and the sequence behind this record's two forms of paddy, the unstable and the settled. It is a review, so each figure is another scholar's (Takashima 2017, Toda 1967, Kinda 1985) and is read here at second hand. It says nothing of which plots in a village rested. The one estate figure is a whole estate's in 1102, not a hamlet's.

Used for: how much paddy lay out of crop in the early medieval period, the yearly re-choosing of plots, and how the unstable land was overcome (fields)
