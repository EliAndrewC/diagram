nihonminkaen-kanto

Kawasaki Municipal Nihon Minka-en (open-air museum of Japanese folk houses), "Kanto village" (in Japanese; https://www.nihonminkaen.jp/kantou.html)

What it is: The museum's page on the houses of its Kanto section, giving for each house moved there its former site, its type (farmhouse, headman's house, net-owner's house), its roof form and material, its length and depth in meters, and its date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured dimensions and roof forms of real seventeenth- and eighteenth-century farmhouses of eastern Japan, the kind these maps draw. Its limit is who survives: a house is preserved because it was old, large and well built, so the houses here lean to headmen and well-off households and do not show an ordinary smallholder's house; it covers one region only.

Used for: the measured length, depth and roof form of Kanto farmhouses, and the two-roof (bunto) houses (homesteads)
