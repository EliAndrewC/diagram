huipai-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 徽派建筑 ("Huizhou-style architecture", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BE%BD%E6%B4%BE%E5%BB%BA%E7%AD%91)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the building style of Huizhou in southern Anhui - its houses, ancestral halls and memorial arches, its courtyard and skywell plan, its white walls and dark tile.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the look of a south Chinese house from outside and above - white walls under dark tile - in one well-known region. Its limits: the page itself carries a neutrality warning; Huizhou was a merchant region whose showpiece villages are not typical of poor farmers; and it gives no figures. It is also a tertiary source: its "white walls, black tile" line (白墙黑瓦) has no footnote, and most of its references are pages from a provincial tourism bureau and news sites, so it is good for the look and not for anything more exact.

Used for: dark tile as the roof of a south Chinese house in one region (homesteads)
