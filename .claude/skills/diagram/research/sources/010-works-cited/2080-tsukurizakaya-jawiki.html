ja.wikipedia 造り酒屋 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%80%A0%E3%82%8A%E9%85%92%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the sake-brewing house as a business and a social institution: its origins among families of wealth, its shop at the town center, its household of clerks, apprentices and guards.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the brewer's standing as local elite with a shop at the center of town, which is why this page keeps the brewery in town rather than at its edge. A tertiary summary used for that standing; it gives no count of brewers per town and no building sizes. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. The article runs down to the modern trade, so the shop at the center is read as the early-modern form.

Used for: brewers as town elite with a shop at the center
