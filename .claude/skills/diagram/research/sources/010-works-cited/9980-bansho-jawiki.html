bansho-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 番所 (in Japanese; https://ja.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%95%AA%E6%89%80)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the bansho, the guard post: the checkpoints and harbor posts of the Edo period, the guardrooms at Edo castle's gates, the ward and bridge watches, and the few surviving buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the guardroom at a gate as an institution of Edo castle and points to a surviving pair beside the Akamon, which supports a gatehouse set beside a gate. It is tertiary and short, it gives no sizes, and its gate examples are the shogun's castle and a daimyo's mansion gate, far above a county post.

Used for: the guardrooms at Edo castle's gates and either side of the Akamon (buildings)
