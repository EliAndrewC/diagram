ja.wikipedia 庫裏 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BA%AB%E8%A3%8F) - 「The place where a temple's monks live.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺院の僧侶の居住する場所」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kuri (庫裏), the building of a Buddhist temple that holds its kitchen and, in ordinary usage, the quarters where its monks live.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one plain fact: that the kuri is where a temple's monks live, which is what puts the country monk's dwelling on the shrine's own precinct. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary with no measurements, and that it describes the institution in general rather than the village temple in particular.

Used for: the kuri as the monks' living place (religion-and-death.html).
