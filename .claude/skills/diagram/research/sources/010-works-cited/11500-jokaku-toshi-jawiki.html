What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on walled cities, with a section on Japan: the ancient capitals' gates without defensive walls, Kamakura's natural defenses, the self-governing towns and temple towns ringed by earth banks and moats, the civil-war castle towns with an outer ring, and Kyoto's earth rampart.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the specific Japanese towns that were enclosed and what enclosed them, which gives this record's rare moat-and-bank form its cases. It is an encyclopedia article whose Japanese section is a survey of exceptions, so it says little about how common enclosure was.

Used for: the Japanese towns enclosed by earth banks and moats (towns)
