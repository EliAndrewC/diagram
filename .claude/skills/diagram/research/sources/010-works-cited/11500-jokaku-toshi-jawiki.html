ja.wikipedia 城郭都市 ("walled city", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9F%8E%E9%83%AD%E9%83%BD%E5%B8%82)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on walled cities, with a section on Japan: the ancient capitals' gates without defensive walls, the contested reading of Kamakura as a city defended by its terrain, the self-governing towns and temple towns ringed by earth banks and moats, the civil-war castle towns with an outer ring, and Kyoto's earth rampart.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the specific Japanese towns that were enclosed and what enclosed them, which gives this record's rare moat-and-bank form its cases. It is an encyclopedia article, flagged by Wikipedia since September 2015 as lacking sources, and its passage naming the moated towns (Hakata, Sakai, Hirano, Imai, the temple towns) carries no footnote, so it gives the cases' names and not evidence for them; its Japanese section is a survey of exceptions, so it says little about how common enclosure was.

Used for: the Japanese towns enclosed by earth banks and moats (towns)
