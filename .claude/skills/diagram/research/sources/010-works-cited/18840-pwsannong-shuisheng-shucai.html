"Aquatic vegetable cultivation" (水生蔬菜栽培), an encyclopedia entry on the Zhihui Sannong agricultural knowledge service (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/560567.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry on the history of China's cultivated water vegetables - lotus, wild-rice stem, water caltrop, water chestnut and others - tracing each through the old texts, with the lotus's forms (root lotus, seed lotus, flower lotus), its districts and its planting methods by dynasty.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a modern historian's summary that dates each form, which is what the question needs, but it names its old texts only in passing - neither the deep-water lotus before the Song nor the Hunan switch names the text it rests on, and the first is dated by where shallow-water lotus is first recorded (Song texts), so it tells what the surviving record shows, not what every pre-Song pond grew - and it gives output, never area: the Hunan seed-lotus districts and the switch from rice after the Daoguang reign are qualitative, and seed lotus is a different crop from the root lotus the maps draw. Its author is not named. The page gives China Agriculture Press as its publisher and places the entry in that press's agricultural encyclopedia, under the history of Chinese agricultural science and technology (中国农业科学技术史 > 蔬菜栽培技术史), but it does not name the printed volume the entry comes from.

Used for: deep-water lotus as the form grown before the Song; Qing Hunan paddies given up for seed lotus (archetypes)
