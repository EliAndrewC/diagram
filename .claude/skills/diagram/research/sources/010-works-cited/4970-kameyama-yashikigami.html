kameyama-yashikigami

亀山市史 民俗編, 屋敷神 (kameyamarekihaku.jp) - (https://kameyamarekihaku.jp/sisi/MinzokuHP/jirei/bunrui3/data3-4/index3_4_1.htm)

What it is: A page from the folklore volume of the 亀山市史 (the official city history of Kameyama, Mie prefecture), published online by the city's historical museum, collecting recorded examples of yashikigami - the small household deity shrines kept within a farmstead's grounds - and where on the grounds each stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives real cases of where a household shrine stood - at a corner of the homestead grounds, in a stone wall, south of the main house - which is what confirms that the shrine on our maps belongs at a corner rather than in the open yard. Its limits are that it is twentieth-century folklore fieldwork on a much older practice; that it is one district of Ise; and that it records six individual cases and no tally, so no corner weighting can come from it - the cases it gives put the shrine at the southwest and northwest more often than the northeast - and the corner weights our maps use are a guess.

Used for: a yashikigami hokora at a corner of the homestead grounds - six named cases (SW, NW, SW, south of the main house, NW in a stone wall, SE); the page carries no tally, so no corner weighting comes from it
