Don't be fooled by film and television", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 2025-02-28 (https://www.163.com/dy/article/JPG2U7P10541TVHQ.html)

What it is: A popular article on a news platform's self-publishing channel setting the ceremonial avenues of the ancient capitals against the ordinary streets of Chinese cities, and giving round figures for the main streets and lanes of Ming and Qing cities.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found giving a width for the ordinary main street and lane of a Chinese city of the late imperial period, which the setting's towns and cities follow. It is a self-published article, carrying the platform's disclaimer for user uploads, and it cites no source for its figures, so they are an informed popular account, not a measurement.

Used for: the width of an ordinary Ming and Qing city's main street and its lanes (ways)
