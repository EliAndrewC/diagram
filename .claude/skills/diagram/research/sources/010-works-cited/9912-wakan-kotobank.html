wakan-kotobank

Kotobank, 倭館 (wakan, the Japan House in Korea) - the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia entry by Yazawa Kōsuke, with Nipponica, Mypedia and other entries (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%80%AD%E9%A4%A8-153933)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the Japan House, the compound on Korea's southern coast where Japanese, and in the Edo period the officers of Tsushima domain, lived and conducted diplomacy and trade with Korea, collecting several encyclopedia entries on its history and layout.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a readable account of how two neighboring powers actually did business across a frontier: in a compound one side set aside for the other, with the host's banquet hall and lodgings adjoining it. It is a tertiary summary; the frontier is a sea crossing, not a land border between clans, and it says nothing of a meeting on a line.

Used for: the Edo-period Japan House at Choryang, its trade hall and the adjoining Korean banquet hall (buildings)
