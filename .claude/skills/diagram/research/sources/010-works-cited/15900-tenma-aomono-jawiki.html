ja.wikipedia 天満青物市場 ("The Tenma produce market", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/天満青物市場)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Tenma produce market, Osaka's licensed wholesale market for vegetables and fruit, which stood along the Okawa river from 1653 until 1931.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a real Edo-period city's produce market on its riverbank, along the Okawa, and names it one of Osaka's three great markets with the Dojima rice market and the Zakoba fish market; it does not put the fish market beside it (Zakoba stood on the Hyakkenbori canal in the west of the city). It is a short tertiary article with no references of its own, mostly on the market's privileges and quarrels; it says nothing of its buildings, and of why it moved to the river it says only that its previous site was inconvenient. It was the licensed monopoly market of one of Japan's three great cities, so its riverbank site speaks for a large river city, not a town.

Used for: a city's produce market on its riverbank (cities/river-cities)
