What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on dageng, the night watch of Chinese towns: the five watches from seven in the evening, the watchmen who walked the streets striking clappers and gongs to call the hour, and the warnings against fire and theft they cried.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the watches were relayed through the streets by walking watchmen, which is how one tower's signal reached a whole city and why this page gives a capital no second tower. A tertiary summary used for the relay and the five watches. Much of the article documents modern survivals and regions outside the setting's Chinese core, so the imperial-era dating of the relayed watch rests on its general framing.

Used for: night watchmen relaying the hours through the streets
