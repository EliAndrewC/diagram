What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Oi river, the Tokaido's most feared crossing, where in the Edo period neither bridge nor ferry was allowed and travelers were carried over: the crossing system, and the explanations given for the ban.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out the competing explanations for the ban - the defense of Edo and Sunpu, the river's own violence, the profits of the crossing towns - so that this page can state the motive as argued rather than certain. A tertiary summary used for that.

Used for: the Oi river crossing and the explanations for its bridge and ferry ban
