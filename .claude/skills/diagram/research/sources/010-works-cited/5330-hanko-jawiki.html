hanko-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 藩校 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/藩校)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article 藩校 (hanko), on the domain schools of the Edo period. It describes their founding, their curriculum in the Chinese classics and their compulsory enrollment of retainers' sons, gives the count of 255 schools from a published reference work, and lists the schools domain by domain - a list in which some entries name a drill hall (演武場) among the school's buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It tells us that a domain seat of consequence had a school, which is what puts one on a capital's map. Its limits are that it is a tertiary encyclopedia summary; that the count of 255 is a late-Edo peak, so near-universal coverage is an end state rather than a constant; and, the one that bears on the drawing, that the article never says a drill hall was a standard part of a school - the drill halls are inferred by us from the names of a few particular schools in its list, so a drill hall drawn on every school compound is our generalization and not the source's.

Used for: 255 domain schools at the peak, in nearly every domain; martial arts encouraged and 演武場 among their facilities
