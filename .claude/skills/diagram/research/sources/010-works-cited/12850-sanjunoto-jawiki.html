ja.wikipedia 三重塔 ("three-story pagoda", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/三重塔)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on three-story pagodas, with a table of the surviving designated ones and their heights.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the heights of real three-story pagodas. Like the five-story list, it covers designated pagodas at notable temples and says nothing of frequency.

Used for: how tall a three-story pagoda was (religion-and-death)
