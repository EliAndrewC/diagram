kotobank-bangoya

Kotobank, 番小屋 ("Bangoya, the watch hut", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%AA%E5%B0%8F%E5%B1%8B-606389)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the watch hut, carrying the Digital Daijisen and the Seisen-ban Nihon kokugo daijiten, with an early example quoted from Minkan seiyo (1721) of a watch hut built at a village's entrance.

Why it applies, and its limits: The quotation is the one period statement found of where a village's watch hut stood. Its limits are that it is one line, that it concerns a watch named for the hawks rather than the ordinary village watchman, and that the dictionary gives no size.

Used for: a village's watch hut standing at its entrance (urban-features)
