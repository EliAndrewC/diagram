zh.wikipedia 会馆 ("huiguan, the guild or native-place hall", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E4%BC%9A%E9%A6%86)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia stub on the huiguan (also called gongsuo), the hall of a Ming-Qing association of fellow-provincials or of merchants of one trade.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the institution (the earliest known, in Beijing, from the Yongle reign of the early fifteenth century) and says what it began as and became - a place of fellowship and lodging for people from one home region, then for some a merchants' association. It is a short article on a city institution; it says nothing of county towns, of the building, or of where it stood.

Used for: the Chinese counterpart of a townspeople's hall (towns)
