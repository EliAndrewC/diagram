higashiueta-sanmai

Higashi-Ueta Community (Takamatsu, Kagawa), "杣尾の三昧" ("The sanmai of Somao", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.higashiueta.com/places/6.php)

What it is: A page of a Takamatsu neighborhood association's website on a local landmark, the site of the settlement's old cremation ground with its hall and six stone jizo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what stood at a local cremation ground - six jizo, which it says were always set there - which is what a map may draw beside the fire bed. Its limits: a community page without sources, describing one site as it stands today; its "always" is the page's own generalization; and it gives no size or siting.

Used for: the six jizo at a cremation ground (religion-and-death)
