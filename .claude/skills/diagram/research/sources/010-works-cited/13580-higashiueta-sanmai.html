higashiueta-sanmai

Higashi-Ueta Community (Takamatsu, Kagawa), "杣尾の三昧" ("The sanmai of Somao", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.higashiueta.com/places/6.php)

What it is: A page of the website of the Higashi-Ueta Community, a community group for a rural district of Takamatsu, on a local landmark: the site of the district's old cremation ground, which the page says only was used "formerly", with its jizo hall, six jizo (shown in photographs) and a Konpira lantern.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what stood at a local cremation ground - six jizo, which it says were always set there - which is what a map may draw beside the fire bed. Its limits: a community page without sources, describing one site as it stands today; its "always" is the page's own generalization; it never dates when the ground was in use, so it cannot show that the six jizo stood there before the modern era rather than in the Meiji-to-Shōwa village practice from before municipal crematoria; it gives no size; and its only siting is general and unsourced: six jizo were set "at the entrance of graveyards" (a graveyard, not specifically a cremation ground).

Used for: the six jizo at a cremation ground (religion-and-death)
