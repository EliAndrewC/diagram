fan-2003-forest-floods

Fan Baomin, Dong Yuan, Zhang Juncheng and Yin Jiayou, 「The effects on flood and drought of the destruction of forests in Chinese history - a discussion of the climatic and hydrological effects of forest」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「中国历史上森林破坏对水旱灾害的影响——试论森林的气候和水文效应」), 『林业科学』 (Scientia Silvae Sinicae) 39(3), 2003, 136-142 (https://html.rhhz.net/linyekexue/html/20030322.htm)

What it is: A paper in China's main forestry journal surveying the destruction of China's forests over its history, basin by basin, and its effect on floods and droughts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the causes of forest loss in China's past - fire clearing and fire hunting, farming, war, building and fuel - and says that in the Qing a fast-growing population sent great numbers of 「shed people」 into the mountain forests of central China to clear and plant the slopes. It is a synthesis from written history rather than a measurement of any village's hills, it argues a case about floods, and it says how the hills were stripped, not how bare a particular hill became.

Used for: the historical stripping of China's hill forests for fuel and farming (vegetation)
