FAO/NACA, Integrated Fish Farming in China, ch. 4 "Rearing of fry and fingerlings" (https://www.fao.org/4/ac264e/AC264E04.htm)

What it is: Chapter 4 of the FAO and Network of Aquaculture Centers in Asia manual on Chinese integrated fish farming, on the nursery chain from fry to fingerling to grow-out pond.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the sizes of fry and fingerling ponds and the stocking chain, which is this page's warrant for small nursery parcels in a pond block; a twentieth-century design manual, so its figures are prescriptions rather than a premodern survey.

Used for: nursery ponds in a dike-pond block (archetypes)
