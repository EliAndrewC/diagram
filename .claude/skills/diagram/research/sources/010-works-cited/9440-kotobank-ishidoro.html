kotobank-ishidoro

Kotobank, 石灯籠 (the stone lantern), entries from Shogakukan's Digital Daijisen and Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the Britannica International Encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%9F%B3%E7%81%AF%E7%B1%A0-30587)

What it is: The Kotobank page gathering dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the stone lantern: its parts, its kinds, its first attestations in documents of the 1390s-1420s, and its history from votive light at shrines and temples to a garden furnishing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives, in standard reference works, the lantern's parts, its named kinds, and that it entered tea gardens and ordinary gardens from the Momoyama period. Its limits are that the entries are short dictionary definitions; that the history of the single lantern at the front of a shrine or temple is the Britannica entry's in a sentence; and that nothing on it speaks of a residence's court.

Used for: the stone lantern's parts and kinds; one lantern at the center front of a shrine or temple; its use in tea and ordinary gardens from the Momoyama period (buildings)
