fuchu-joge-jinya

Fuchu City (Hiroshima), 上下陣屋（代官所）のようす ("What the Joge jin'ya (intendant's office) was like", title translated from the Japanese by this project), a pamphlet page (in Japanese; https://www.city.fuchu.hiroshima.jp/material/files/group/23/daikanpanfu2.pdf)

What it is: A children's pamphlet page from Fuchu city on the Joge jin'ya, a branch office of a shogunal intendancy, redrawing its plan from a drawing of 1869 (by way of the Joge town history of 2003) and giving the size of its central building and the number of officials sent there at the end of the shogunate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read that sizes the main building of a small outlying office, the scale of a county magistracy. Its limits: it is a popular pamphlet, it gives one rounded figure ("about 80 tsubo"), and the plan is a simplified redrawing of a late drawing.

Used for: the size of a branch intendancy's central building and its handful of officials (buildings)
