What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kakoimai, the Edo-period reserve of grain stored against famine by the shogunate, domains and villages; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the reserve and its names, one of which is reserved unhulled rice, which is the corner of unhulled grain this page keeps; it does not compare the keeping qualities of hulled and unhulled rice; a stub on one reference work.

Used for: the famine reserve of unhulled rice (buildings)
