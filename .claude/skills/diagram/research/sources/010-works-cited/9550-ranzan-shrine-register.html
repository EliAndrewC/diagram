ranzan-shrine-register

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡神社明細帳, one page per shrine (http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09jin01.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's local-history site transcribing, shrine by shrine, the Hiki district shrine register as filed by the Saitama prefectural education office in 1963 (a register begun in the Meiji era and amended into the Showa era), with each precinct's area in tsubo.

Why it applies, and its limits: Fourteen village-rank shrines of one district give the band of a village precinct's area. Its limits: one district; a register carried from the Meiji era into a 1963 file, so an area may be a Meiji, Taisho or post-war figure (one entry records a 1949 re-survey); precincts were cut by the Meiji taking of shrine lands in the 1870s, and some were later enlarged by returned land and the 1907 mergers (one entry adds 1 tan 5 se of returned forest in 1903), so these are not Edo precincts and may run smaller or larger; precincts may also have moved since the Edo period; a transcription rather than the register itself; the medians are this project's arithmetic.

Used for: village-shrine precinct areas, Saitama (religion-and-death.html).
