ranzan-shrine-register

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡神社明細帳, one page per shrine (http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09jin01.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's local-history site transcribing, shrine by shrine, the Meiji-era shrine register of Hiki district, Saitama, with each precinct's area in tsubo.

Why it applies, and its limits: Fourteen village-rank shrines of one district give the band of a village precinct's area. Its limits: one district, a Meiji register of precincts that may have moved since the Edo period, and a transcription rather than the register itself; the medians are this project's arithmetic.

Used for: village-shrine precinct areas, Saitama (religion-and-death.html).
