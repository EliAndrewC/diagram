bunka-hasegawa-omoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 旧長谷川家住宅（三重県松阪市魚町） 主屋 ("Former Hasegawa family residence, Uomachi, Matsusaka, Mie: main house", translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/293090)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record of the main house of the Hasegawa residence, an Important Cultural Property designated in 2016: its dimensions, and how the estate grew from the middle of the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: The state's own account of a great merchant house in a castle town rather than a great city, and of how it grew by buying the neighboring lots. The family's fortune was made in Edo, where it kept its wholesale house, so the Matsusaka house is the home of an exceptionally rich family, not a typical town merchant.

Used for: the Hasegawa main house's size and the estate's growth (cities/fabric.html)
