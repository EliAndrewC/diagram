What it is: The English Wikipedia article on kegare, the Shinto concept of ritual defilement; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that death is the primary source of defilement, that the kami are kept from it and that priests must avoid it, which is the real-world rule this setting's shrines depart from; a tertiary summary; a stub whose examples are present-day practice.

Used for: Shinto's avoidance of death pollution (religion and death)
