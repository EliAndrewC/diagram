chuoku-nihonbashi-kashi

中央区まちかど展示館 (Chuo City machikado exhibition halls), 日本橋エリア特集 Part.5 (https://www.chuoku-machikadotenjikan.jp/feature/special09_nihonbashi05.html)

What it is: A local-history feature from Tokyo's Chuo ward on the Edo-period landings of the Nihonbashi district (the three named kashi stood on the Nishi-Horidome river), named by the cargo each handled - rice, small boats, salt.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the cargo-specialized landings of one Edo river, the pattern our wharf district's specialized quays follow. Its limit is that it is a ward's heritage feature. And it is Edo, the largest city in the country, whose quay specialization a provincial seat would not match.

Used for: landings named by cargo along the Nihonbashi river
