ja.wikipedia 久地円筒分水 ("The Kuji cylinder divider", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B9%85%E5%9C%B0%E5%86%86%E7%AD%92%E5%88%86%E6%B0%B4)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the 1941 cylinder divider on the Nikaryo canal in Kawasaki, with a section on the timber proportional flume (the Kuji bunryo-hi) that stood there from the mid-Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the Edo-period precedent for a built structure at a split: a flume dividing one canal four ways in proportion to each district's cultivated area, which did not end the disputes. It says nothing of the flume's size or form, and nothing of it survives.

Used for: the proportional flume, a built division work of the Edo period (water)
