闽南文化生态保护区 (Minnan Culture Ecological Protection Zone, Fujian), 府志里的泉州古城垣成长记 《泉州府志》城垣研究成果新近推出, 2026-04-17 (https://www.mnwhstq.com/whdt_347/202604/t20260417_220969.htm)

What it is: A news account on a Fujian provincial cultural-heritage portal of newly published research on the walls of Quanzhou, the prefectural seat of southern Fujian, drawn from the successive editions of the prefecture's own gazetteer, the 泉州府志: when each ring was built, how many gates each carried, and how the circuit grew to thirty li by 1352.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page that states the nested rings of a Chinese prefectural seat in the gazetteer's own terms - the yacheng of the offices inside the zicheng inside the luocheng - which is the pattern our capital's walled citadel follows. Its limits are that it is a portal's summary of scholarship rather than the scholarship, and that it describes one city; Quanzhou was a great port and grew a fourth ring, so it is an upper case rather than a typical one.

Used for: the yacheng, zicheng, luocheng nesting of a prefectural seat, with the gate counts and the 1352 circuit
