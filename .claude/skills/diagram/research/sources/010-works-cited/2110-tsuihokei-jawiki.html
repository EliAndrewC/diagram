What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on banishment as a punishment in Japan, from the ancient codes through the Edo period, including the banishments a village imposed under its own law.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a village punished breaches of its own code by banishment, which is the ceiling this page gives village authority. A tertiary summary used for that one institution; it does not say that nothing heavier was ever available to a village. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources.

Used for: the village's own banishment, murabarai and tokorobarai
