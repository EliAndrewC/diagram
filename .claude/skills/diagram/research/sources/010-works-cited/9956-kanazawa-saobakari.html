kanazawa-saobakari

Kanazawa Museum of Local Life (金沢くらしの博物館), "仕事～計る・運ぶ" (work: weighing and carrying), a teaching sheet in its series "昔のくらし" (life in former times) (in Japanese; https://www.kanazawa-museum.jp/minzoku/teachers/data_detail08.html)

What it is: A city folk museum's sheet for schoolteachers on the weighing and carrying tools in its collection, giving each tool's period of use and, for the steelyards, the loads they were made for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the steelyard's use from the Edo period and the platform scale's from the Meiji period, and shows steelyards made for loads of 16 kan, which is the range a bale needs. It is a short teaching sheet about objects in one museum, most of them probably of the late 19th and 20th centuries; the rice-bale use of the platform scale comes from an image caption.

Used for: the steelyard in use from the Edo period, in sizes to 16 kan, and the platform scale only from the Meiji period (urban features)
