zh.wikipedia 闽南传统建筑 ("traditional architecture of southern Fujian", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%97%BD%E5%8D%97%E4%BC%A0%E7%BB%9F%E5%BB%BA%E7%AD%91)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the building style of southern Fujian: its swallowtail ridges, its ceramic-shard ridge carving, its three-sided courtyard houses and its arcades.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the three-sided courtyard house is found across China, not in one province, and that the showy swallowtail ridge belongs to temples, mansions and ancestral halls rather than to ordinary houses. Its limits: a short tertiary article about one region's style, with no figures.

Used for: the three-sided courtyard house as a form found across China, and the swallowtail ridge as a mark of the rich (homesteads)
