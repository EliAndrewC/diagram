zh.wikipedia 闽南传统建筑 ("traditional architecture of southern Fujian", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%97%BD%E5%8D%97%E4%BC%A0%E7%BB%9F%E5%BB%BA%E7%AD%91)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the building style of southern Fujian: its swallowtail ridges, its ceramic-shard ridge carving, its three-sided courtyard houses and its arcades.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the three-sided courtyard house is found across China, not in one province, and that the showy swallowtail ridge is commonly seen on temples, mansions and ancestral halls; it does not say ordinary houses lack it, so "a mark of the rich" is our reading, not its statement. Its limits: a short tertiary article about one region's style (southern Fujian and Taiwan, as surviving buildings show it), with no figures; its "found across China" is a single passing sentence whose one footnote is a web page on Taiwan's Minnan houses, so it carries the shape of the claim, not proof of how widespread the form was.

Used for: the three-sided courtyard house as a form found across China, and the swallowtail ridge as a mark of the rich (homesteads)
