zh.wikipedia 梯田 ("terraced fields", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E6%A2%AF%E7%94%B0)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on terraced fields: a short history from the Western Zhou and Han texts, and where China's terraces lie, with the best-known sites.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places China's terraces in the southern mountains, Guangxi and Yunnan above all, which grounds the Chinese half of the hill-terrace form. The article carries its own 2023 notices that it is regionally biased and short of sources, so it is used for distribution only, and it gives no dimensions or dates for the named sites beyond Longji's founding in the Yuan.

Used for: where China's terraces lie (archetypes)
