ja.wikipedia 円筒分水 ("Cylinder divider", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%86%E7%AD%92%E5%88%86%E6%B0%B4)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the cylinder divider, the round overflow tank that splits a canal's water in fixed proportions, with its invention and early examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the device: devised from the Taisho era, the first built in 1914 in Gifu - which is what makes the round divider an anachronism on a premodern map. It is used for that date only. It is a tertiary encyclopedia article, fit for a date that nothing on the map rests on; the date itself rests on the article's own references. It covers Japan only: it says when the device was invented there, not that nothing like it existed in premodern China. That no such device stood in premodern China is this project's reading, not the page's.

Used for: the cylinder divider dated to 1914 and after (water)
