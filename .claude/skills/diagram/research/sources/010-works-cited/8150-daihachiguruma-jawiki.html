ja.wikipedia 大八車 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/大八車)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the daihachiguruma, the large two-wheeled hand cart of Edo-period Japan - a tertiary encyclopedia entry covering what the cart was, who was allowed to use it, where it was allowed, and the smaller beka-guruma of the Kamigata region.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one thing only, the legal confinement of the freight hand cart to Edo, Owari and Sunpu, which is what makes a cart the exception rather than the norm on a country lane, and for the article's note that freight carts were not permitted on the Tokaido until the Meiji period. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary rather than a document or a study; that it speaks for Japan alone, one half of this setting; and that it is about the regulated cities and highways, so it tells us what was forbidden outside them and nothing about what an ordinary village lane looked like.

Used for: the Edo hand cart, and the three places the shogunate allowed it: Edo, Owari and Sunpu
