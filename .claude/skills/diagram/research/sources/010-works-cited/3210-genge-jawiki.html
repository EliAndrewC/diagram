What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on genge (Chinese milk vetch, Astragalus sinicus), the legume sown among the standing rice in autumn and turned in as green manure in spring; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the winter green-manure practice in the paddy itself, its root-nodule nitrogen fixation with a quantity, and its disappearance under chemical fertilizer and earlier planting. It says nothing of night soil, ash or oil cake and does not describe the plowing-under; it cannot date the practice, the regime it describes is the prewar one that vanished after the war, and its nitrogen figure is a modern agronomic measurement.

Used for: renge as the paddy's winter green manure (fields)
