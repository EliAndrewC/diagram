Fei Xiaotong, Kaixiangong / the Xichang polder layout (https://archive.org/details/PeasantLifeInChina)

What it is: Fei Xiaotong's 1939 study of the village of Kaixiangong, read here for its plan of the farm layout in the Xichang polder - the map and passages showing houses standing on the raised polder margin along the interior streams, with mulberry on the same high strip.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is our worked example of how a settlement sits inside deep-water, diked ground: not on a hill but on the only raised line there is, the dike itself. The limits are the same as for the book at large - one delta village observed in the 1930s, in China - and it is read here only for the polder layout, not for the wider ethnography.

Used for: houses inside the polder along its interior streams (the deep-water settlement case)
