meshimori-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 飯盛女 ("meshimori onna", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/飯盛女)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the meshimori onna, the serving women of the Edo-period post-town inns who were in fact unlicensed prostitutes, with the shogunate's caps of 1772 and the case of the three post towns under Mount Asama.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where the trade of a post town went on - in its inns, under the name of inn servants - which is what decides that an inn town draws nothing more for it. A short encyclopedia article marked by the encyclopedia as a stub; its caps are for four post towns only, and its population figures are for the three Asama post towns, not post towns in general.

Used for: the serving women of the post-town inns, their tolerated status and the 1772 caps (urban-features)
