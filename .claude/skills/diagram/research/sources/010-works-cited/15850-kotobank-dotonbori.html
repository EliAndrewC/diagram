kotobank-dotonbori

Kotobank, 道頓堀川 ("Dotonbori river", title translated from the Japanese by this project), the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Daijisen entries (https://kotobank.jp/word/道頓堀川-103981)

What it is: The dictionary entries on the Dotonbori canal gathered on Kotobank, one of which gives the canal's Edo-period dimensions from a 1787 document held at Kyoto University: its east-west length and its width at each end in ken.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one read figure for a city canal's width in the Edo period itself, cited to a period document. It is a dictionary's one-line quotation of that document, for one Osaka canal; Osaka's canals were among the widest in Japan, so it marks the wide end of the range.

Used for: a city canal's Edo-period width (cities/river-cities, water)
