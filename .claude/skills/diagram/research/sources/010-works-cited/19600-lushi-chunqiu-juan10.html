呂氏春秋 卷十 ("The Lushi chunqiu, Book Ten", title translated from the Chinese by this project), Chinese Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%91%82%E6%B0%8F%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B/%E5%8D%B7%E5%8D%81)

What it is: A Wikisource transcription of the tenth book of the Lushi chunqiu, the chapters for the first month of winter: 孟冬紀, 節喪 (on keeping funerals within bounds), 安死, 異寶 and 異用.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its chapter 節喪 says a burial must go on high ground, to keep it from foxes and from the damp of groundwater, which dates the rule that a grave keeps off wet ground to the late Warring States. It is a Chinese philosophical text arguing for modest funerals, not a survey of where graves were actually dug; it speaks of high ground in general, not of a marsh or of a village graveyard; the page gives no date; and it says nothing about Japan.

Used for: a burial placed on high ground, away from the damp of groundwater (water)
