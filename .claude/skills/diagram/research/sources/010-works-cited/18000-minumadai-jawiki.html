Japanese Wikipedia, 見沼代用水 (the Minuma-dai canal) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%A6%8B%E6%B2%BC%E4%BB%A3%E7%94%A8%E6%B0%B4)

What it is: The encyclopedia article on the Minuma-dai canal of the Kanto plain, cut from September 1727 to February 1728 under Izawa Yasobei, with its survey, its intake and its course.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives two Edo-period dimensions: the intake, a wooden trough about 2 ken (3.6 m) wide and 24 ken long, and the new channel cut below the split from the Hoshi River, about 6 ken wide. That is one of the great shogunal canals, watering a whole plain, so it marks the top of the main-canal band rather than a village's main; it is an encyclopedia article whose sources were not read.

Used for: the width of a great Edo main canal (water)
