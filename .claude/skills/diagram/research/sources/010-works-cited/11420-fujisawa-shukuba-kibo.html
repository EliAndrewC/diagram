fujisawa-shukuba-kibo

Fujisawa City, Local History Section, 藤沢宿と県内各宿場の規模について ("On the size of Fujisawa-juku and the post stations in the prefecture", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.fujisawa.kanagawa.jp/bunkazai/kyoiku/bunka/kyodoshi/kibo.html)

What it is: A short page by the Fujisawa city local-history office giving, from the shogunate's 1843 survey of the Tokaido stations, the population, house count and inn count of Fujisawa and of the eight other stations in Kanagawa prefecture.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a primary survey's figures, republished by a city office, for nine consecutive stations of one highway, which gives the real spread of post-town sizes from a mountain station to a castle town. It covers one stretch of the busiest road in one year, so it is the top of the range for Japanese road towns rather than a typical county town, and it gives no lengths or areas.

Used for: the household and inn counts of nine Tokaido stations in 1843 (towns)
