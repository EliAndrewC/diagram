sagami-2012-gogurasho

Sagami Chikao (相模誓雄), 近世期の越後国における郷蔵所の空間構成に関する研究 ("Spatial structures of gogura-syo located in the Echigo province in the Edo period"), Journal of Architecture and Planning (Transactions of AIJ) vol. 77 no. 675, May 2012, pp. 1179-1186 (in Japanese with an English abstract; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/aija/77/675/77_1179/_pdf)

What it is: An architectural-history paper on the storehouse sites (gogura-sho) of Echigo, where a group of villages kept the tax rice of the shogunate's estates through the winter (the 1769 site itself was then in the Inaba family's Yodo domain, passing to the shogunate in 1786); it analyzes six old plans, among them a drawing of 1769 that gives the sizes of each storehouse on one site in ken and shaku.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures the storehouses where the tax rice of several villages waited before it went on, the scale just below a county office's grain store. Its limits: it is one snowy province, the sizes come from drawings rather than surviving buildings (and the paper calls the 1769 drawing sketchy, so whether its storehouses had the lean-to roofs the paper treats as typical of Echigo's storehouse sites, and so their full footprint, is not known), and the measured site served only three villages, where a storehouse group could run from two or three villages to several tens.

Used for: the sizes of the tax-rice storehouses on a three-village store site of 1769 (buildings)
