bunka-inshu-ikeda-gate

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 旧因州池田屋敷表門 ("front gate of the former Inshu Ikeda residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/193210)

What it is: The national heritage database's record of the "Black Gate", the front gate of the Tottori domain's Edo residence, now in the grounds of the Tokyo National Museum and an Important Cultural Property since 1951.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its registration line describes a surviving late-Edo samurai gate range from the official record: a nagayamon of one story, hip-and-gable roofed, with a guard room projecting at each side under a cusped gable. It is one daimyo's gate in Edo, the grandest form a samurai gate took, not a provincial retainer's; and the record gives no dimensions.

Used for: that a surviving samurai gate range is single-story, with projecting guard rooms (government)
