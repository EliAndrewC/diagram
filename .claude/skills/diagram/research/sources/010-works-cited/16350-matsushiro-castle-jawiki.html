ja.wikipedia 松代城 ("Matsushiro Castle", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9D%BE%E4%BB%A3%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the castle of the Sanada domain at Matsushiro, with its history and a description of each bailey.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts a domain's gunpowder store inside the castle, in its second bailey, which is where the record keeps the armory. It hedges the store's place as apparent, and it is one castle.

Used for: a domain's gunpowder store inside its castle (cities/government)
