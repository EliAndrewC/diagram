ja.wikipedia 松代城 ("Matsushiro Castle", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9D%BE%E4%BB%A3%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the castle of the Sanada domain at Matsushiro, with its history and a description of each bailey.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts a domain's gunpowder store inside the castle, in its second bailey, which is where the record keeps the armory. It is a tertiary encyclopedia article, which Wikipedia itself tags as a stub whose sources lack page numbers, and it cites nothing for this passage, so it carries the idea (a stone-built powder store, 煙硝蔵, in the western part of an Edo-period domain castle's second bailey) and not a rule. It hedges the store's place as apparent, it does not date the store, and it is one castle. The same article puts the arms magistrate's office (武具奉行役所) in the third bailey, not beside the powder store, so on its own it does not support an armory in the second bailey.

Used for: a domain's gunpowder store inside its castle (cities/government)
