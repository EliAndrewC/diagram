osaka-city-dotonbori

Osaka City, 道頓堀川について ("About the Dotonbori river", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/kensetsu/page/0000010856.html)

What it is: The city's construction bureau page on the Dotonbori, the canal cut across Osaka from 1612 to 1615: its length, its catchment and its present width in its eastern and western halves, with the modern flood walls.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured width for a real Edo-period city canal that is still open. The width is today's, after twentieth-century flood works, so it is read beside the Edo-period figure from kotobank-dotonbori, not alone.

Used for: the wide end of a city canal's width (cities/river-cities)
