yanagawa-jogo-mizuochi

Yanagawa City, 掘割、城堀の水落ち ("The canals, and draining the castle moat", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.city.yanagawa.fukuoka.jp/kurashi/dorosuiro/suiro/suiro_horiwari.html)

What it is: A page of the Yanagawa city government (Fukuoka) on the yearly drying of the canals and the draining of the moat ringing the old castle town: how the moat is filled and emptied, and what the dredged mud was used for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a castle-town moat that is filled from a river through a sluice and holds stored water, and canal mud spread on paddies and fields as fertilizer - the moat as reservoir and its link to the fields. It is one town on a low delta whose whole ground is laced with canals, so its moat is part of a drainage and water-supply network unlike a hill castle's; the text is adapted from a local-history book and describes the practice as it stood up to the late Meiji period and today; and the fertilizer and the after-harvest timing are said of canal-drying in general, not of the castle moat.

Used for: a castle-town moat holding stored water, and canal mud spread on the fields (cities/hinterland)
