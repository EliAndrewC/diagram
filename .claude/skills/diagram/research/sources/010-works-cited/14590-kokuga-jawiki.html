ja.wikipedia 国衙 ("provincial government office", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/国衙)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kokuga, the provincial government office of the ancient imperial state (8th-10th centuries), with a list of excavated provincial offices and the size and plan the excavations found at each.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one Japanese tradition of a governor's seat standing in a planned provincial town, and it gives measured plans: a main hall facing south with side halls east and west, districts from about 100 m square to 300 by 200 m. Its limits are that these are archaeological reconstructions of 8th-century sites, several hedged as estimates, an encyclopedia summary rather than the excavation reports, and that the article itself says the plans differ more than they agree from province to province.

Used for: the size and plan of a Japanese provincial office, and the avenue running south from its gate (cities/government)
