What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Bianjing Lou, the Ming drum tower of Dai county in Shanxi, standing at the central crossroads of the county town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places a county town's drum tower at the exact center of its crossroads, which is the siting these maps give the tower. A tertiary summary used for the siting.

Used for: a county drum tower at the central crossroads
