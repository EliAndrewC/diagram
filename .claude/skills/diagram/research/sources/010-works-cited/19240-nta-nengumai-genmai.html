National Tax Agency, National Tax College, 税の歴史クイズ 年貢米（答え） ("Tax history quiz: tax rice (answer)", title translated from the Japanese by this project), by Funahashi Akihiro (https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/quiz/1504/answer.htm)

What it is: The answer page of a quiz by a research officer of the Japanese National Tax College, on whether Edo-period tax rice was paid in the hull or as brown rice.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that Edo tax rice, and the rice farmers shipped for sale, was threshed and hulled brown rice, and that paying tax in brown rice had replaced paying in the hull over several centuries after the eighth. It is a short popular page by a tax historian with no references, and it speaks for Japan as a whole and for Japan only: the page itself calls paying tax in brown rice a form very unusual in the world, so it cannot carry the practice for the setting's Chinese side; a library reference answer notes that some lords took their tax in the hull and that famine reserves were stored that way.

Used for: that Edo farmers hulled their rice before it left the village, as tax or for sale (urban-features)
