What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Reihendorf, the German row village of farmsteads along a street with each holding's land adjacent to it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the row form and its reason - the farmland beside the dwelling saves transport - which is the linear settlement category this page places its strung-out hamlets in. A tertiary summary of a German form used as the documented type; the setting's villages are not German.
