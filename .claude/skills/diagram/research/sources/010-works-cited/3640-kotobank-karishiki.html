kotobank-karishiki

Yamakawa Nihonshi Shojiten and Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten, s.v. 刈敷, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/刈敷)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the reference entries on karishiki, the green manure cut from hills, fields and bunds and plowed into the paddy.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the bund grass cut for green manure, one of the uses this page names; the entries say nothing of fodder or thatch.

Used for: the bund grass cut for green manure (vegetation)
