National Diet Library Collaborative Reference Database, entry on hongo/oyamura and edago/komura (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000166660&page=ref_view)

What it is: An entry in the National Diet Library's Collaborative Reference Database, where Japanese librarians publish researched answers to reference questions with the works they consulted. This one explains the parent village (hongo or oyamura) and its branch settlement (edago or komura): the cadastral register and the tax notice stood in the parent's name, the branch could pay its land tax only through the parent, and even a petition to the lord needed the parent's consent.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the fiscal mechanism of a branch hamlet's dependence on its parent village in plain words, from the right country and the right era, which is what the record needed for the parent-and-branch relationship on our maps. Its limits are that it is a librarian's compiled answer from reference works rather than original research, a chain of dictionaries and a city history; and that the answering library and every work it cites are Amagasaki-local, so one district's reference literature stands in for the general Tokugawa rule.

Used for: the fiscal mechanism of the dependency - land registers and tax quotas in the PARENT village's name, the branch able to pay only through it - which is the reason a branch hamlet had no headman of its own standing
