edogawa-watashi

Edogawa City (Tokyo), 「Edo-period ferries」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代の渡し」), explanatory sheet no. 3-9, updated 2014 (in Japanese; https://www.city.edogawa.tokyo.jp/documents/9204/3-09.pdf)

What it is: A one-page local-history sheet from Edogawa ward on the Edo-period ferries across the Naka and Edo rivers on the roads through the ward, illustrated from the Edo Meisho Zue and Hiroshige.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says no bridge was permitted over those two rivers, so ferries joined the roads, and that one landing stood by a barrier. It is written for schoolchildren and residents, without references, and covers one ward near Edo, where the shogunate's defense of the city shaped the rule.

Used for: the ferries that joined the roads where no bridge was allowed (ways)
