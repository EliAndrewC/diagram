ja.wikipedia 湯島聖堂 ("Yushima Seido", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/湯島聖堂)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Confucian shrine at Yushima in Edo, which became the shogunate's own school, with its site area before and after its 1799 rebuilding.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the top of the Japanese scale - the shogun's school, not a domain's - and records that its hall was rebuilt on the model of the Mito domain's Confucian shrine. It is an encyclopedia article, flagged since 2018 as lacking verifiable sources, and its site areas (12,000, then some 16,000 tsubo) and the hall's 2.5-fold enlargement carry no citation of their own, so we use them for the order of size, not as exact figures. It says nothing of an ordinary domain school's size.

Used for: the scale of the shogunate's Confucian school, above any domain's (religion-and-death)
