What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the walls and moats of Beijing: the moats of the palace city, the inner city and the Jiajing outer city, their widths, and the water system that fed them from the Jade Spring hills and drained them to the Tonghui river.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ringed moats of the imperial capital and where their water came from and went, the Chinese anchor for our capital's engineered moat ring. A tertiary summary used for that. Beijing was the imperial capital, so its moat ring is a ceiling and not a norm, and the dimensions mix the period specification with a 1953 survey of a much-altered work.

Used for: Beijing's ringed moats, fed from the Jade Spring hills and draining to the Tonghui
