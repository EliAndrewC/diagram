bunka-akasaka-bukeyashikimon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 武家屋敷門 ("samurai residence gate", title translated from the Japanese by this project), Akasaka, Minato ward, Tokyo (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/145691)

What it is: The national heritage database's record of a samurai residence's gate built 1862-1864 and designated an Important Cultural Property: a two-storied long-house gate with its measured length and depth and an attached guardroom.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures a real long-house gate, which gives the upper bound of the range a great house put on its street. It is one gate in Edo, rebuilt after the fire of 1862 in the last years of the period, and by its owner school's account it was the front gate of a daimyo's official residence (the senior councillor Honda Tadatami's, in Marunouchi), one of three such gates left. So it is the top of the range: a senior retainer's gate, let alone a provincial one, was smaller. It has been moved twice and now stands in Akasaka, so it no longer shows the street it faced, and the record gives no plot or house behind it.

Used for: the size of a long-house gate (cities/government)
