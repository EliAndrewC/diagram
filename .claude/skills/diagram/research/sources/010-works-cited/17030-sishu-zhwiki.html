sishu-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia, 私塾 ("Sishu, the private school", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%A7%81%E5%A1%BE)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the traditional private school: its curriculum, and the lineage's ancestral hall serving as the school in ordinary times.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Chinese village school's usual seat, a building that already stands for another purpose. Its limits are that it is a short tertiary article, and that the ancestral-hall passage is not tied to a period or region.

Used for: the Chinese lineage hall as a school (urban-features)
