sishu-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia, 私塾 ("Sishu, the private school", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%A7%81%E5%A1%BE)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the traditional private school: its curriculum, and the lineage's ancestral hall serving as the school in ordinary times.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one seat of the Chinese village school, a building that already stands for another purpose: the lineage's ancestral hall, used for rites in spring and autumn and as the school the rest of the year. Its limits are that the ancestral-hall passage is not tied to a period or region, and its only citation is the transcript of a modern Buddhist lecture series (淨土大經解演義, archived 2013), a recollection rather than a historical study; and that the same article says most such schools were kept in the teacher's own home, so the hall is one seat among several, not the usual one. A closer scholarly source, linked from the article, is Liu Hsiang-kwang's study of Huizhou village teachers and elementary schooling, 1100-1800.

Used for: the Chinese lineage hall as a school (urban-features)
