kamigamo-shake-okeihan

Keihan group, 京都ツウのススメ 第152回 京の社家 ("Tips for Kyoto connoisseurs, no. 152: Kyoto's priest families", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.okeihan.net/navi/kyoto_tsu/tsu202103.php)

What it is: A railway company's popular guide to the priest families' quarter at Kamigamo in Kyoto: when it formed, how many houses it held, and what a priest family's house looked like from the street.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in so many words that a priest family's house was walled with a gate, and dates the quarter. It is a tourist guide, not a survey, and speaks of one quarter.

Used for: a priest family's house enclosed by an earthen wall with a gate at its entrance (religion-and-death)
