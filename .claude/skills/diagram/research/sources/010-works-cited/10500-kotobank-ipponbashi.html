kotobank-ipponbashi

Dictionary and gazetteer entries s.v. 一本橋 (ippon-bashi), aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/一本橋)

What it is: The Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's entry for the one-log bridge, with an attestation in a haikai collection of 1722, and a gazetteer entry (Heibonsha's Nihon Rekishi Chimei Taikei) on a bridge of that name in Tottori that began as a single log.

Why it applies, and its limits: The dictionary names the water such a bridge crossed - a brook or a mountain stream - which is the scale of water our hamlet maps draw. It says nothing of paddy ditches, gives no size, and the gazetteer's account of the Tottori bridge's first form is hedged ("it is said") and concerns a river crossing, not a field one.

Used for: that a one-log bridge was laid across a brook or mountain stream (water)
