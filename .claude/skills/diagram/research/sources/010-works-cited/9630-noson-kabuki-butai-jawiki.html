What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on farmers' kabuki stages, with the national count of 1,777 and their usual siting on shrine ground.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the stage real and shrine-sited but far from universal. Its limits: tertiary; the count is of surviving or recorded stages, a modern census, not a premodern prevalence.
