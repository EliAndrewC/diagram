sugamo-kikumatsuri

Sugamo Jizo-dori shopping street association, すがも中山道 菊まつり ("The Sugamo Nakasendo chrysanthemum festival", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://sugamo.or.jp/kikumatsuri/)

What it is: The local shopping street's page for its modern chrysanthemum festival, with a short history of Sugamo's Edo-period chrysanthemum growing and shaped displays.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records the scale of Edo's chrysanthemum trade on the city's edge - dozens of nurseries and a hundred stalls. A promotional page for a modern festival (held since 1993). It takes the figure from records it does not name, and it misdates the one period text it quotes, the Edo meisho hana-goyomi, to 1847 (Bunsei 10 is 1827). The count is the peak of one of three passing crazes in the mid-to-late Edo period, not a standing number. It is also the nursery belt of a capital of about a million people, more than a town's edge would carry. It says nothing about fields or their size.

Used for: the number of nurseries in Sugamo's Edo-period chrysanthemum booms (fields)
