shizutani-taiseiden-bunka

Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン), "旧閑谷学校聖廟 大成殿" ("the Confucian hall of the former Shizutani school", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/201969)

What it is: The national cultural-property database's entry for the Confucian hall of the Shizutani school in Okayama, giving its date and its size in bays.

Why it applies, and its limits: A government register's measured description of a surviving domain-built Confucian hall. It gives bays, not feet, and describes one building.

Used for: how big a domain school's Confucian hall was (religion-and-death)
