shizutani-taiseiden-bunka

Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン), "旧閑谷学校聖廟 大成殿" ("the Confucian hall of the former Shizutani school", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/201969)

What it is: The national cultural-property database's entry for the Confucian hall of the Shizutani school in Okayama, giving its date and its size in bays.

Why it applies, and its limits: The Agency for Cultural Affairs' register entry for a surviving Confucian hall that Okayama domain built in 1684: three bays by three, single storey, hip-and-gable, tiled. It gives bays, not feet, so a size in feet rests on a bay width taken from elsewhere, and it describes one building. Shizutani was a rural school the domain founded for commoners, not the domain's own school in the castle town, so the hall stands for a domain-founded school's hall rather than the domain school's.

Used for: how big a domain school's Confucian hall was (religion-and-death)
