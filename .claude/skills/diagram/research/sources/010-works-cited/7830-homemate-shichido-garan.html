Homemate Research, 七堂伽藍 (the seven halls of a temple) (https://www.homemate-research-religious-building.com/useful/23125_religious_025/) - 「The full seven-hall arrangement is found only at large temples where many monks live; in general, small temples with only a "main hall" and a "bell tower" are by far the most common.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「七堂伽藍が整っているのは大勢の僧侶が住む大きなお寺のみで、一般的には「本堂」と「鐘楼」だけといった小さな寺の方がほとんど」)

What it is: A commercial reference page on the seven-hall temple layout and what ordinary small temples actually had.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the statement that the full seven halls belonged only to large temples with many monks and that most small temples had only a main hall and a bell tower - the floor of the program and the reason the bell is a knob. Its limits: a commercial tertiary page with no sources of its own, and its sentence is undated and in the present tense - a statement about the small temples that stand today, read back onto a premodern program; the bell knob's presence rule is labeled a guess for that reason.

Used for: the minimal temple: a main hall and a bell tower (religion-and-death.html).
