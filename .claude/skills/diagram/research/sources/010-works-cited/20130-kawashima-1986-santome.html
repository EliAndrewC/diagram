kawashima-1986-santome

川嶋雅章 (Kawashima Masaaki), 三富新田集落の現況と土地利用秩序保全上の問題 ("The present state of the Santome Shinden settlements and the problems of conserving their land-use order", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 農村計画学会誌 (Journal of Rural Planning) 6(1), June 1987, pp. 44-47, a paper from the 1986 rural planning seminar (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/arp1982/6/1/6_1_44/_pdf)

What it is: A rural-planning paper by a Meiji University engineer on Santome Shinden as it stood in the 1980s: its founding figures (the strip of 40 by 375 ken, the households at the opening), the directions its strips run, the one-sided row of Shimotome, its woods, and how a homestead is used and planted.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one read source that says where the grove stands on a row-village homestead, on the road side, and that names a row with houses on one side of the road only. Its limits: it is a survey of the 1980s, not of the Edo period, so the trees it describes were standing then, not dated to the founding; it is one planned dry-field colony; and its founding figures are summarized without the page of the register they come from.

Used for: the road-side grove and south-facing house of a row-village homestead, the one-sided row at Shimotome, and the households at the opening (homesteads)
