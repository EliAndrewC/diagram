dorui-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 土塁 ("earthwork rampart", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9C%9F%E5%A1%81)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the dorui, the earth bank raised around residences, moated settlements, camps, castles and temples in Japan from antiquity to the Edo period: how it is built, its height, the names of its top and base, and how its faces were planted or footed in stone.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Japanese earth bank's height and parts, which is what a Japanese-model town enclosure is drawn from. A tertiary summary drawing on popular castle handbooks; its 2 to 3 m height is a general range for castle banks, not a measured town bank, and it gives no base width.

Used for: the earth bank's height, its top and base, the fence on it, and its planting (defenses)
