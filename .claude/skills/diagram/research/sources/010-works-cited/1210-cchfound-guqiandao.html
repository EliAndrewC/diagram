cchfound-guqiandao

中国华夏文化遗产基金会 (China Huaxia Cultural Heritage Foundation), 古纤道 (https://www.cchfound.com/product/2934.html)

What it is: A heritage-item page from a Chinese cultural-heritage foundation on the ancient towpath at Shaoxing: its Tang date, its 7.5 km protected extent, and the dimensions of the stone-pier form - the deck's width, the piers' span, the slabs and the height above the water.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page giving the towpath's measurements, which size the hauling path on our river capital. Its limits are that it is a foundation's summary page without its own citations, and that its figures describe the preserved central stretch as it stands today, whose fabric is later than the Tang institution it dates.

Used for: the towpath's 1.5 m width and its stone deck
