National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, 江戸時代の籾蔵の大きさ（寸法や容量）、１坪当たりに収蔵できる俵の数を知りたい ("I want to know the size of an Edo-period grain-reserve storehouse and how many bales a tsubo held", title translated), 2024 (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/detail?page=ref_view&id=1000360040)

What it is: A National Diet Library reference answer, marked unresolved, pointing a reader to the literature on the Edo-period storehouses for unhulled rice and on how much a bale held.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its research notes cite a dictionary of historical units for the Edo bale's capacity - bales of 3 to 3 sho 3 go, of 4 to and of 5 to, which bale a domain used differing by domain, unified in the Meiji period at 4 to and 60 kg (a weight of hulled rice, not of the unhulled grain these storehouses held); the answer itself adds a 1616 order fixing the bale at 3 to 7 sho, a bakufu and Kanto bale of 3 to 5 sho, and a second dictionary's range of 2 to 5 sho up to 5 to - and it quotes storehouse sizes and counts of bales per building. It is a librarian's pointer to printed works, not a study; it gives no length or diameter of a bale anywhere. But the question asked for storehouse sizes and a bale's capacity and weight, not its size, so that silence shows only that these works were not searched for one; it cannot show that no bale dimension is on record.

Used for: the Edo bale's capacities by domain, and that this answer, which was not asked for one, gives no bale dimension (urban-features)
