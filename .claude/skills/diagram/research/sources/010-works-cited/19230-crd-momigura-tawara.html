National Diet Library, Collaborative Reference Database, 江戸時代の籾蔵の大きさ（寸法や容量）、１坪当たりに収蔵できる俵の数を知りたい ("I want to know the size of an Edo-period grain-reserve storehouse and how many bales a tsubo held", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2024 (https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/detail?page=ref_view&id=1000360040)

What it is: A National Diet Library reference answer, marked unresolved, pointing a reader to the literature on the Edo-period storehouses for unhulled rice and on how much a bale held.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its research notes cite a dictionary of historical units for the Edo bale's capacity - bales of 3 to 3 sho 3 go, of 4 to and of 5 to, which bale a domain used differing by domain, unified in the Meiji period at 4 to and 60 kg - and it quotes storehouse sizes and counts of bales per building. It is a librarian's pointer to printed works, not a study; it gives no length or diameter of a bale anywhere, which is itself the finding it is cited for beside the capacities.

Used for: the Edo bale's capacities by domain, and that no bale dimension is on record (urban-features)
