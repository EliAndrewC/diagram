kotobank-jobosei

Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten (Heibonsha, the entry by Kishi Toshio) and Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho / Nipponica (Shogakukan), s.v. 条坊制 ("the jobo grid system", translated from the Japanese by this project), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9D%A1%E5%9D%8A%E5%88%B6-294594)

What it is: The kotobank page for the jobo system, the grid plan of the ancient Japanese capitals on the Chinese model, carrying the entries of two published encyclopedias: the ward and block sizes of Fujiwara-kyo and Heijo-kyo, and the ward heads the ritsuryo code set over them.

Why it applies, and its limits: Signed encyclopedia entries by specialists, for the block that stayed the same while the ward grew and for who headed a ward. It concerns the capitals of the seventh and eighth centuries, not a provincial town; the Fujiwara plan is, in its own words, the one inferred from the Taiho code.

Used for: the older capitals' wards cut into constant blocks, and their ward heads (cities/fabric.html)
