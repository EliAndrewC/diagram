What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kuruwa, the baileys of a Japanese castle: what a bailey is, the names of the principal ones, and the classification of layouts by how the baileys are arranged - concentric, in a row along a ridge, backed against a natural obstacle, circular, spiral.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the standard layout taxonomy our castle's bailey arrangement is drawn from, and it says which forms follow the terrain. It is a tertiary summary used for the definitions, and it is also the page that shows one of those forms is circular, which keeps this record from calling them all angular.

Used for: the rinkaku, renkaku and teikaku layouts and the terrain the renkaku form follows
