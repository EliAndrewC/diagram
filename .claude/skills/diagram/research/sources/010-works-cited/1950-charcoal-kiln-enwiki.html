en.wikipedia "Charcoal kiln" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charcoal_kiln)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the charcoal kiln, from the traditional earth-covered pile to the masonry and steel kilns of later industry.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the traditional charcoal burn as a hemispherical pile sealed with earth, which is the low mound shape this page contrasts with the potter's tunnel kiln. A tertiary summary used for the form of the traditional pile. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. And the article is European throughout, German in its terms and examples; the earth-covered pile is a form East Asia also knew, but the page does not say so.

Used for: the charcoal burn as a low earth-sealed mound
