yamagata-kashiwakura

Yamagata Prefecture, 未来に伝える山形の宝 ("Yamagata treasures to hand down"), 最上川が運んだ文化と黒塀の豪農屋敷群 ("The culture the Mogami River carried and the black-walled houses of the rich farmers", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.yamagata-takara.com/takara/important/gounouyashiki)

What it is: A page of the prefecture's heritage portal on the Kashiwakura houses of Oka, in Nakayama town: a family that became grand headman over a group of villages, its main house's plot and buildings, a branch house's, and the black board walls that ring the kin's houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a rich farming family's compound in figures - about 2,300 tsubo of plot, a gatehouse, four storehouses and granaries - and a kin group's houses walled side by side, the top of the range a headman's or rich farmer's homestead reaches. Its limits: a promotional heritage page; the family was a grand headman over 6,700 koku, a landlord and the largest grower of safflower in the whole Murayama region, far above a village headman; the buildings are of several dates, the branch's main house from 1920-1922; the 2,300 tsubo is the site inside and outside the residence (邸内・外), not the walled compound alone, and the page dates none of the main house's buildings, so the figures are the compound as it stands, not as it stood in the Edo period; and the walls are described as they stand today.

Used for: a grand headman's plot and storehouses, and a rich family's walled houses (homesteads)
