ja.wikipedia 朱印地・黒印地 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9C%B1%E5%8D%B0%E5%9C%B0) - 「Even the largest, Kōyasan and Nikkō Tōshōgū, were about 10,000 koku, and the smallest a mere 1 koku」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「最も広い高野山や日光東照宮でも1万石程度、最も狭いものではわずか1石で」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on shuinchi and kokuinchi, the temple and shrine lands confirmed by shogunal and daimyo seal.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the range of such grants, down to a single koku, which shows the small end existed. Tertiary; the smallest grants are not tied to village temples in the text.

Used for: seal-land grants as small as one koku (religion-and-death.html).
