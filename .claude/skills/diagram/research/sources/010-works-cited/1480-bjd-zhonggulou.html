北京日报 (Beijing Daily), 北京钟鼓楼的奇妙数字 (https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s650bf36be4b0d5bd075f4be1.html)

What it is: A local-history feature in the Beijing Daily on the Bell and Drum Towers of Beijing: the order of drum and bell at the setting and lightening of the watch, and how the nine city gates closed and opened on their signal.

Why it applies, and its limits: It documents the job our bell-and-drum tower is drawn for - sounding the hour the gates shut and open. Its limit is that it is a newspaper feature and speaks of the city gates, not the lane palings. And it describes the imperial capital, whose Ministry-of-Works establishments and gate-signal apparatus a provincial seat had no counterpart of, so the form is stepped down two tiers by us.

Used for: the towers' signal for closing and opening the city gates
