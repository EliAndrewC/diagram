FAO, Farm structures in tropical climates, chapter 14, Rural water supply and sanitation (https://www.fao.org/4/s1250e/s1250e1d.htm)

What it is: A chapter of the FAO and SIDA manual on farm buildings for the tropics, on siting and protecting a rural well against the pollution of animal yards and privies.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the modern sanitary rule that a well stands uphill and some twenty meters from an animal yard, the contamination this page's byre-and-well spacing answers. A modern engineering manual for the tropics standing in for a physical hazard that does not change with the century.

Used for: the well kept twenty meters up grade from animal yards
