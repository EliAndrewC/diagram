matsushiro-shoro

Sanada Treasure Museum (Nagano city), 旧松代藩鐘楼 ("The former Matsushiro domain bell tower", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.sanadahoumotsukan.com/facility_detail.php?n=11)

What it is: The Nagano city museum's page on the bell tower of the Matsushiro domain, a city-designated cultural property: what a domain's time bell did and when this one was set up.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a domain had its great bell struck every toki (the page's own gloss is "about two hours"; the Edo hour was unequal and changed with the season, so it is not a fixed clock interval), day and night, that retainers and townspeople told the time by it and that it gave the alarm for fire, and that Matsushiro's first lord set it up in 1624 together with a fire lookout. It is a museum's visitor page for one domain; the present tower is a rebuilding of 1801.

Used for: a domain's time bell and its duties (cities/capitals)
