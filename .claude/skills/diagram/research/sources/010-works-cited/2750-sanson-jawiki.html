What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the dispersed village, sanson, the settlement form of farmhouses scattered among their fields, with the Toyama name sankyoson for the Tonami case.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the dispersed form and gives both its names and readings, the form and the words this page uses for its dispersed hamlets. A tertiary summary; it does not say how a steading was reached.

Used for: the dispersed village and its two names
