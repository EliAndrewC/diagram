kotobank-yatoda

Kotobank, 谷戸田 ("yatoda, the valley paddy", title translated from the Japanese by this project), excerpts from Heibonsha's Sekai daihyakka jiten (old edition), entries 棚田 and 谷戸 by Kuroda Hideo (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%B0%B7%E6%88%B8%E7%94%B0-1429507)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the word yatoda, which quotes two signed encyclopedia entries by a historian of medieval Japan: on the terrace-type paddies opened from ancient times on, and on the yato valley whose spring-fed paddies made the typical medieval farm landscape, with the regional names for such fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: A signed entry by a specialist placing the valley paddy at the center of the medieval landscape and giving its regional names, which the valley-chain form rests on. It is a pair of excerpts, not the whole entries, and it describes the middle ages, before the setting's period; it gives no dimensions.

Used for: the valley paddy's regional names, and valley fields as the representative medieval farm landscape (archetypes)
