itabashi-kotobank

Encyclopedia entry s.v. 板橋 ("plank bridge", translated from the Japanese by this project), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/板橋)

What it is: The dictionary and encyclopedia entry for 板橋 (itabashi) at kotobank, which aggregates the major Japanese reference works, giving the word's plain sense: a bridge made of boards.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it to fix the name of the small crossing our maps draw over a farm ditch, so that the glyph and the word a reader clicks mean the same object - a planked deck and not an earthen one. Its limits are that it is a dictionary definition and nothing more: it gives no construction, no size and no picture of how such a bridge was used, so everything else our entry says about the plank comes from elsewhere. Its one dating is the dictionary's earliest example of the word, a poem in a collection of about 1310, and the place-name dictionary's note that a camp was pitched at "the Itabashi" in 1180; neither says what the plank crossed.

Used for: what an itabashi is: a bridge made of boards; that the word was in use in Japan by about 1310
