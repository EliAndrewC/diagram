zh.wikipedia 弘化寺 ("Honghua Temple", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%BC%98%E5%8C%96%E5%AF%BA)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Honghua Temple at old Hezhou (now Minhe county, Qinghai), a Tibetan Buddhist monastery of the Gelug school founded by Ming imperial order, quoting the Qing gazetteers of the district.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes a gazetteer giving the temple, founded in the seventh year of Zhengtong (1442), fifty-five official monks, which is a Ming establishment count for one named house. It is a frontier monastery of a different Buddhist tradition founded by decree with lands granted for its keep, not an urban Chinese temple, the figure is a staffing decree rather than a census, and another gazetteer gives fifty-seven lamas.

Used for: a Ming establishment count of monks for one temple (religion and death)
