What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the packhorse and its use where roads are absent; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the packhorse as the carrier where wheels cannot go, which is the traffic this page pictures on a lane; a general article that gives no load figure and no width for a track or a bridge.
