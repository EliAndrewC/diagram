zh.wikipedia 秋审 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/秋审)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the qiushen (秋审), the Qing dynasty's autumn assize, in which the court decided each autumn which death sentences would be carried out. It describes the route a case took: the Three Judicial Offices - the Board of Punishments, the Court of Judicial Review and the Censorate - heard the provincial cases jointly, and the prisoners waiting in provincial jails were held over until the review returned.

Why it applies, and its limits: A magistracy on our maps has a jail and an office, and this gives the shape of what happens in them: a capital case climbs from a provincial cell to the capital and the confirmed sentence comes back down, which is why the jail holds prisoners for months rather than days. Its limits are that an encyclopedia article is a tertiary summary, so we take its procedure only where its own references support it, and that this is Qing Chinese law standing for the setting's justice generally, our setting drawing its officialdom from the Chinese pattern as well as the Japanese one.

Used for: the Qing autumn review of death sentences through 刑部, 大理寺, 都察院 and the emperor - the capital case climbs and the confirmed sentence comes back down
