What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Osaka's seven graveyards, the Edo-period burial grounds on the city's rim and the excavations of the Umeda ground in 2017 and 2019-2020; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the consolidation of the city's scattered graveyards onto its outer rim for military reasons and the excavations' counts of skeletons and ossuaries, over 200 skeletons in 2017 and over 1,500 with more than 350 ossuaries in 2019-2020; it says nothing of how the graves lay, so it carries the density of use and not the plan.

Used for: Edo-period urban burial at Osaka (religion and death)
