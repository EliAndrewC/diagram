ja.wikipedia 立場 ("tateba, the rest stop", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%AB%8B%E5%A0%B4)

What it is: The short Japanese Wikipedia article on the tateba, the rest stop between post towns on the Edo highways.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where rest stops were set, what stood there and who set them up. It is a brief unreferenced summary; its remark that a rest stop grown into an in-between station had inns is loose: it notes only that the shogunate strictly limited such places, while the article on the in-between station says lodging there was forbidden in principle and allowed only when the post towns overflowed.

Used for: what a rest stop was, where it was set, and the grown rest stop (ways)
