sakai-inoue-kajiyashiki

Sakai City, "井上関右衛門家住宅 主屋・座敷棟・道具蔵・俵倉・附属棟" ("The Inoue Sekiemon house: main house, parlor wing, tool store, bale store and annex", title translated from the Japanese by this project), updated 2024-08-27 (in Japanese; https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kanko/rekishi/bunkazai/bunkazai/shokai/bunya/kenzobutu/inouesekiemonke.html)

What it is: The city's description of a designated Tangible Cultural Property: the workshop and dwelling of a Sakai gunsmith family, on its site by the map of 1689, with the frontage, date and plan of each block of the main house and of the parlor wing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the core of a town smith's house to the early Edo period and gives its frontage, three and a half ken, with a through earth floor and a board-floored strip where bellows and gun-making tools are now shown, so it is a period anchor for how wide a town smith's house stood on its street. Its limits: the smith was a gunsmith in a great commercial city, not a tool smith of a county town; the page gives frontages but not depths; and it says where the tools are shown today, not where the forge stood in the period.

Used for: an early-Edo town smith's house frontage (urban-features)
