The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "The First Toshi Ranbo Campaign" and "Abuse of Authority Cases" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, on the city of Toshi Ranbo: a captain who keeps a small home next to one of the city's district barracks, and a garrison that outgrew its barracks and was quartered in merchants' homes until more barracks were built.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: that the setting's cities house their garrisons in barracks, spread by district, is the setting's to decide. The notes describe one city, a contested one with a large garrison, and give no barracks' size, form or number.

Used for: a city's garrison living in district barracks (cities/government)
