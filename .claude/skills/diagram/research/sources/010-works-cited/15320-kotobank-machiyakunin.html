kotobank-machiyakunin

Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho / Nipponica (Shogakukan, the entry by Yoshiwara Ken'ichiro) and other works, s.v. 町役人 ("town officials", translated from the Japanese by this project), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%94%BA%E5%BD%B9%E4%BA%BA-136420)

What it is: The kotobank page for the townsmen who governed a Japanese city and its wards: the city-wide town elders, the headmen, the monthly duty-holders, and the ward levy that paid for it all.

Why it applies, and its limits: Dictionary and encyclopedia entries on how a ward was run and what it paid for, gathered by kotobank; only the Nipponica and Heibonsha entries are signed, both by Yoshiwara Ken'ichiro, a historian of Edo's town officials, and the rest are short unsigned definitions. Most of what it says is of Edo, the shogun's capital and the largest city of the period, with Osaka beside it. Its list of the ward's costs is one ward's, Minami-Denmacho in the Kansei era (1789-1801), and it says the items differed somewhat from ward to ward. It names the officials of Kyoto, Sakai, Kubota and Sendai mostly to say that their titles and terms differed (Sendai's hereditary, Kubota's two to a ward on short terms); it describes the monthly duty's work and the ward's costs only for Edo.

Used for: the monthly duty, the ward's costs, and Edo's town elders (cities/fabric.html)
