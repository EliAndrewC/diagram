What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the shukuba, the post station of the Edo highways: its transport office, its inns for officials and for ordinary travelers, and the porters and horses it kept on hand for the relay.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a post station kept horses and porters permanently for the official relay beside its inns, which is the wayside facility with stabling this page names. A tertiary summary used for the institution; it says nothing of pasture.

Used for: the post station's inns and standing horses
