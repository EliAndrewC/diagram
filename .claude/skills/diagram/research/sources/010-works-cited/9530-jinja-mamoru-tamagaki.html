What it is: A blog series on note.com about keeping shrines, explaining the tamagaki and saying the older fences were wooden rails or hedges and stone ones spread later.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the order wood-then-stone. Its limits: a popular blog with no dates or sources for the change, so it gives the order and no chronology.
