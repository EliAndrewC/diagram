ja.wikipedia 檀家 ("parish household", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AA%80%E5%AE%B6)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the danka, the household attached to a parish temple, and the Edo registration system that bound it.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one fact: under the Edo temple registration, making one's grave at one's parish temple was half an obligation - the one pull toward a grave away from the household's own settlement. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary; the sentence gives no date of its own (the Edo period comes from the surrounding passage); it does not say whether the grave meant the body's or the visited one; and it says nothing of how far the parish temple lay.

Used for: the grave at the parish temple, half an obligation under the Edo temple registration
