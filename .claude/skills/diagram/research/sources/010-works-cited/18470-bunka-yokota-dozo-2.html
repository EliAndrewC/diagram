bunka-yokota-dozo-2

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, the former Yokota residence's second storehouse (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/121927)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record of the larger of the two storehouses of the former Yokota residence at Matsushiro, a middle-rank Sanada-domain retainer's house designated an Important Cultural Property (1986).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the larger of one samurai household's two storehouses measured: earth-walled, two stories, 5.5 by 4.5 m, a gable roof. Its limits are that it is one storehouse of one middle-rank house, of the late Edo period; that the record gives no position on the grounds; and that the pair's two sizes bound a household's storehouses only for this one house.

Used for: how big a household storehouse was (Estates and other compounds).
