ja.wikipedia オオムギ ("Barley", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%82%AA%E3%82%AA%E3%83%A0%E3%82%AE)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on barley: its kinds, its autumn sowing and early-summer harvest, its place as the winter crop after rice, and the season word 麦秋 for the ripening field.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the look of a ripening barley field and its place in the rice year from Kanto to Kyushu, which is what a map in early summer shows. A tertiary encyclopedia article, and the passage we use - barley sown after the rice harvest and cut before transplanting, the pale-brown field, 麦秋 - carries no footnote of its own; its ripening dates (late May to early June) are given in the Gregorian calendar, as the crop falls now. The cropping pattern it describes is the Japanese one (China appears only as barley overtaken by wheat and rice), and its one line on when the winter crop spread - double cropping in the medieval period - cites a modern paper on barley flour, so that account is general.

Used for: barley's autumn sowing and early-summer harvest as the winter crop after rice, and the pale-brown ripening field (fields)
