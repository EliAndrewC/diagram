Wikipedia (English), "Baler" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baler)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the baler, the farm machine that compresses cut hay or straw into bound rectangular or round bales, with the history of the hay press and the baling machine.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the machine that makes a bale: mechanical cutting devices of the 1860s, from which mowers and balers came, and a knotter that bound hay into bundles invented in 1872 and sold from 1874 - all in the United States and Europe, after the setting's period. It is a modern encyclopedia article on farm machinery, written from the North American record; it does not say when balers reached Japan or China, and it says nothing of how hay was kept before the machine.

Used for: the rectangular hay bale a product of the baling machine of the late nineteenth century (towns)
