kotobank-negari

Kotobank, "Negari" (根刈, root-cutting), the entry in the Britannica kokusai daihyakka jiten shokomoku jiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%A0%B9%E5%88%88-111166)

What it is: A short encyclopedia entry on root-cut mulberry, the training form in which the trunk is kept within half a meter of the ground and the bushes are small.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms the planting number for root-cut mulberry and that the bushes are kept small, and says the form made up most of Japan's mulberry fields. It is a modern encyclopedia describing twentieth-century practice, undated beyond that, in fields rather than on dikes, and it gives no crown width.

Used for: about a thousand root-cut bushes to ten ares, kept small (archetypes)
