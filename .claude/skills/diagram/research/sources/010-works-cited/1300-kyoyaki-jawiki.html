What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Kyoto ware: the kilns of Higashiyama with the kiln houses concentrated at Sanjo Awataguchi, the other wares of the eastern hills, and the lesser kilns scattered in the east and north of the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the Kyoto kilns clustered on the city's eastern side, the pattern our capital's works district follows, and it also shows some scattered, which keeps this page from saying never. A tertiary summary used for that.

Used for: the kiln houses concentrated at Awataguchi, and the kilns scattered elsewhere
