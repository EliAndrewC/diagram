komonjyo-kenchi

古文書ネット (komonjyo.net), 検地とは 検地帳を通して知る百姓の権利義務 ("What was the land survey: a farmer's rights and duties read through the survey register", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://komonjyo.net/kenchitoha.html)

What it is: A page on a site teaching the reading of old documents, transcribing lines of the 1678 survey register of Yaho village (Tama district, Musashi) and explaining what each line records.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its lines give single fields with their sides and areas, from 11 by 10 ken (3 se 20 bu) to 41 by 10 ken (1 tan 3 se 20 bu), and it reads a line as one field - dated, measured premodern plots. Its limits: the fields it transcribes are dry fields, not paddy; the lines are an excerpt of one village's register; it ties "one field" to its first example only; and it is an educational site, not a published edition.

Used for: the sizes of single dry fields in a 1678 survey register (fields)
