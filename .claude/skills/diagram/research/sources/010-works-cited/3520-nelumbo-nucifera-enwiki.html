What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the sacred lotus, with a section on its cultivation as a root crop; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the species' water depth, the deep and the shallow (adlittoral) cultivar classes and the rotation of lotus with rice in one field, which is the agronomy this page's lotus overlay rests on; it is a general article, not a description of any premodern village; the cultivation passages describe present-day Chinese production.
