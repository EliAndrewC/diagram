wazi-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 瓦肆 ("wazi", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/瓦舍)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the wazi (also washe), the entertainment precincts of Song cities, with their performance halls and shops.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a Song entertainment precinct held - railed-off stages among shops, teahouses and wine shops - which is the Song form of a capital's entertainment district. A short tertiary summary resting on three Song memoirs of the two capitals; it says nothing of provincial cities or of the precincts' size.

Used for: the Song entertainment precinct and what stood in it (urban-features)
