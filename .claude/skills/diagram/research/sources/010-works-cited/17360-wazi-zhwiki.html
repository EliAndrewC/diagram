wazi-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 瓦肆 ("wasi", also called wazi, title translated) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/瓦舍)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the wazi (also washe), the entertainment precincts of Song cities, with their performance halls and shops.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a Song entertainment precinct held - railed-off stages among shops, teahouses and wine shops - which is the Song form of a capital's entertainment district. Its references are three Song memoirs of the two capitals (Kaifeng and Lin'an), a historical dictionary and a modern popular guide to Song travel, but its footnotes attach only to the origin of the name and to its dating. What it says stood in a precinct is uncited here, so any detail we rely on should be checked against the memoirs themselves. It says nothing of provincial cities or of the precincts' size.

Used for: the Song entertainment precinct and what stood in it (urban-features)
