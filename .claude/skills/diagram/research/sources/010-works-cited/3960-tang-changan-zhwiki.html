zh.wikipedia 唐长安城 ("Chang'an, the walled capital of the Tang dynasty", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/唐长安城)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Tang Chang'an, its wards and their gates; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ward enclosed on all four sides with guarded gates shut at curfew, which is the sealed ward this page's fence stands for; a capital of the Tang, and it does not describe where a ward wall met the city wall; the walled, curfewed ward itself lapsed at the Tang-Song transition, so for a Ming-modeled seat it is an earlier form rather than a contemporary one. It is a tertiary summary, and its paragraph on the wards carries no reference of its own. It gives four gates only to a large ward; a small ward opened just two, east and west, so a fence drawn with four gates is the large-ward case.

Used for: the walled ward and its gates (defenses)
