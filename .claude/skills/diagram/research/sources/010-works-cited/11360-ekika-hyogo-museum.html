Hyogo Prefectural Museum of Archaeology, "The ancient relay stations (umaya)" (in Japanese; https://www.hyogo-koukohaku.jp/modules/guidance/index.php?action=PageView&parent_category_id=3&language=ja&number=28)

What it is: A short explanatory page by a prefectural archaeology museum on the relay stations of the ancient highways and what excavations at two Hyogo sites found of their buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a museum's summary of excavated evidence for what a relay station looked like. It is brief and popular, gives no dimensions, and its "first in the country" refers to the buildings found, at two sites together.

Used for: the tile-roofed, white-walled, red-pillared buildings found at Koinumaru and Ochiji (towns)
