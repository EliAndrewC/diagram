rokusha-jinja-fuchu

六社神社 (Rokusha Shrine, Fuchu, Hiroshima), a local shrine page (http://mimegumi.chu.jp/rokusyazinnzya.html)

What it is: A local page on Rokusha Shrine, ranked a village shrine in 1872, giving its worship hall, office, arches and precinct area.

Why it applies, and its limits: The one entry found giving a village shrine's building areas and its precinct together, from which the built share is computed. Its limits: a Meiji-and-later record on a private page, one shrine; its office is a Meiji-era building type; and its 285-tsubo precinct is the post-Meiji core, with 2,162 tsubo of outlying shrine land listed apart, so a built share over the precinct alone may overstate the share of the grounds an Edo shrine held.

Used for: the built share of a village precinct (religion-and-death.html).
