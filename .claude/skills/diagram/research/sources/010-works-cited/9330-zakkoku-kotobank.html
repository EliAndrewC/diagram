zakkoku-kotobank

kotobank 雑穀 (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%9B%91%E7%A9%80-68803)

What it is: The kotobank page gathering several Japanese dictionaries' entries on zakkoku, coarse grain; the entry cited is the Yamakawa Nihonshi Shojiten (revised edition), a short dictionary of Japanese history.

Why it applies, and its limits: The history dictionary says coarse grain was historically outside the land tax and was grown as the farmers' own food and as a famine crop, which is the reason a village's rice need not feed it alone. It is a two-line dictionary entry with no period, region or quantity, so it supports the practice and not a figure.

Used for: that coarse grain filled part of the diet, behind the acreage-from-population rule (fields)
