植木ペディア カキ (https://www.uekipedia.jp/落葉広葉樹-カ行/カキ/)

What it is: The entry for kaki (persimmon) in Uekipedia (植木ペディア), a Japanese online tree encyclopedia maintained by a person working in the gardening trade, which catalogs ornamental and useful trees by flower, fruit, leaf, growing conditions and landscape use for gardeners and landscapers choosing what to plant.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for one measurement, the persimmon's mature height of 3 to 20 m, which sets how large a dooryard fruit tree may be drawn. It is a horticultural reference site rather than a peer-reviewed one, and it is describing the tree as planted in Japan today; height is a species trait that changes little between then and now, but the entry gives no crown width, so the spread of the tree on our maps rests on other grounds.

Used for: persimmon height 3-20 m. No crown width.
