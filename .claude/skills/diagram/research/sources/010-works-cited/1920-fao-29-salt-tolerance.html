fao-29-salt-tolerance

R. S. Ayers and D. W. Westcot, Water Quality for Agriculture, FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 29 Rev. 1, chapter 2 (https://www.fao.org/4/t0234e/T0234E03.htm)

What it is: The FAO's standard reference on irrigation water quality, whose second chapter tabulates the salt tolerance of crops as the soil and water salinity at which yield begins to fall, and groups the crops from sensitive to tolerant.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives rice's threshold and classes it moderately sensitive, which is the measured fact behind this page's rule that a tannery's brine must not reach the paddy. A modern agronomic table standing in for a physical bound that does not change with the century; its limits are that it is a soil-salinity table and does not draw the field-water distinction this page draws.

Used for: rice's salt tolerance threshold and class
