China National Radio travel channel, 祁县昭馀古城：现实版的清明上河图 ("The old town of Zhaoyu in Qixian: a real-life Along the River During the Qingming Festival", title translated from the Chinese by this project), reprinted from the magazine 古村落, 2012-09-12 (in Chinese; http://travel.cnr.cn/2011lvpd/gny/201209/t20120912_510898266_1.shtml)

What it is: A travel feature on the old county seat of Qixian in central Shanxi, a home town of the Shanxi merchants, describing its streets of shops: courtyard plans behind the street, a two-story street building with the shop below and a storeroom above, frontages of about five bays of 2.5 to 3 m, deep buildings, and small gray tile.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that gives a Chinese county seat's shop frontage and height in figures. It is popular writing, not a survey; it describes the surviving Ming and Qing town of one unusually rich merchant county, so its five-bay shop is likely at the wide end, and it does not say how many shops were smaller.

Used for: a Chinese county seat's shop frontage, depth, height and roof, and its close-set street (towns, urban-features)
