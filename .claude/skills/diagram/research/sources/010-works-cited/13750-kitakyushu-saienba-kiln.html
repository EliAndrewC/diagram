kitakyushu-saienba-kiln

Kitakyushu City, Kokurakita Ward, "The Saienba kiln site" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「菜園場窯跡」) (in Japanese; https://www.city.kitakyushu.lg.jp/kokurakita/file_0090.html)

What it is: A ward page on the excavated kiln of the early Edo lord of Kokura, a climbing kiln of a stoke mouth and four chambers, with its measured length.

Why it applies, and its limits: An excavated climbing kiln of the early 17th century with its whole length measured, the smallest in the record. It was a lord's kiln fired for his own pleasure rather than a trade works, so it may be smaller than a working potters' kiln.

Used for: a four-chamber climbing kiln 16.6 m long (urban-features)
