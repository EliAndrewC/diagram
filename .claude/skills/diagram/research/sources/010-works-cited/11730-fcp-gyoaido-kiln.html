fcp-gyoaido-kiln

Nakano Kodai, 「On the charcoal kilns of the Gyoaido B site」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「行合道B遺跡の木炭窯について」), research column no. 53, Fukushima Cultural Foundation, archaeological survey department (in Japanese; https://www.fcp.or.jp/iseki/column/359)

What it is: An excavator's column on four charcoal kilns dug at a site on a hill slope in Date city, Fukushima, giving each kiln's measurements, its structure (dug into the slope, with a chimney running up it and a work area before its mouth) and a radiocarbon date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a measured charcoal kiln of the right age - late 16th to early 17th century - which is what sizes a kiln on a map. It is one site in one district; the measured figure is the firing chamber, not the whole works; and the excavator leaves open whether some kilns were fully or half sunk into the ground.

Used for: the size, siting and form of an early-modern charcoal kiln (vegetation)
