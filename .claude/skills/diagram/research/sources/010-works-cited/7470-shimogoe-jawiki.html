ja.wikipedia 下肥 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/下肥) - 「Fresh night soil is harmful to crops, so when it is used as fertilizer it is stored for a set period and left to rot down and mature.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「新鮮な屎尿は作物にとって有害であり、肥料として用いる際には、一定期間貯蔵して腐熟させる」); the storage vessel is the 肥溜め

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on 下肥 (shimogoe), human night soil used as fertilizer, which explains that fresh night soil harms crops and so is stored for a set period to rot down and mature, in a pit called the 肥溜め.

Why it applies, and its limits: It answers why a farmstead has a manure pit at all and why the stuff sits there instead of going straight onto the fields, which is what justifies drawing a separate pit on the plot rather than treating it as part of the privy. It is a tertiary summary whose statements we take where its own references support them, and it gives no storage times or pit dimensions, so the size we draw the pit at is our own decision.

Used for: night soil stored to ripen in the manure pit and used on the fields.
