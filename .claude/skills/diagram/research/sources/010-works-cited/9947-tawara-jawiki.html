tawara-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "俵" (tawara, the straw bale) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BF%B5)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the straw bale: what it is woven from, what was shipped in it, and the square charcoal bales of one Hokkaido district.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a bale is and that charcoal went in them, the objects the map draws on its barges and in its charcoal yards. It is a brief tertiary article; it gives no dimensions, only capacities of four or six to depending on the product. Its square charcoal bales are from Hokkaido's Iburi district, and its only source for them is a 2014 oral history of the Showa era (1926-1989). They are a twentieth-century practice, not evidence that premodern charcoal was packed square.

Used for: what a bale is and what went in one, and square charcoal bales (urban features)
