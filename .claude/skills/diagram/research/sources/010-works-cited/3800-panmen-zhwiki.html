zh.wikipedia 盘门 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/盘门)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Pan Gate at Suzhou, the paired land and water gate whose surviving fabric dates to 1351 under the Yuan, its barbican added in 1356; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the water gate's two layers, its sluice and grating and its depth, which is the gated canal entry this page draws; it names no arch and no basin; one exceptional surviving gate, restored four times through 1986, so its depth and grating are the monument's as it stands.

Used for: the water gate's sluice and grating (river cities)
