inugoya-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "犬小屋 (江戸幕府)" (the Edo shogunate's dog houses) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%8A%AC%E5%B0%8F%E5%B1%8B_(%E6%B1%9F%E6%88%B8%E5%B9%95%E5%BA%9C))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the dog shelters the shogunate built in the 1680s and 1690s - first within the jin'ya of Kitami Shigemasa, then at Yotsuya, Ōkubo and Nakano - taking their buildings, counts and costs from modern historians, chiefly Nezaki Mitsuo's books, who read the period account books (among them the 1696 Nakano repair accounts that Ōta Nanpo copied into Takehashi yohitsu in 1800).

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable record found of dogs housed inside an official's compound, and it gives the shelters' buildings and sizes from the account books. The shelters were for sick and ownerless dogs under the shogunate's policy of the time, not hunting kennels, and the Nakano buildings were on a pound's scale, so neither their form nor their size stands for a household's kennel. It is an encyclopedia article: its figures are the account books' figures at second hand, through the historians it cites, and were not read from the books themselves.

Used for: dogs housed within the Kitami jin'ya, later a shogunate office; the Nakano shelters' 25-tsubo dog rooms (buildings)
