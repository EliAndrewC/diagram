Ji Liping (嵇立平), Beijing Daily, reprinted by China Taiwan Net (Taiwan.cn), 老北京是如何处理垃圾的 ("How old Beijing dealt with its rubbish", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 2020 (in Chinese; https://culture.taiwan.cn/cul/202006/t20200612_12281184.htm)

What it is: A popular history article by Ji Liping (嵇立平), first printed in the Beijing Daily (北京日报) and reprinted by China Taiwan Net, on how late-Qing and Republican Beijing cleared its rubbish and night soil, with a section on the night-soil men and their yards.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the Chinese collection as a trade: night-soil men collecting daily from households and dry latrines, some cleaning chamber pots, each belonging to a night-soil yard with its own fixed routes. It describes the capital from the late Qing into the Republican years, in a popular newspaper article without notes; it says nothing of a county town or of earlier centuries.

Used for: who carried off a Chinese city's night soil, and where it went (towns)
