takamatsu-my-kagawa

うどん県旅ネット (Kagawa Prefecture Tourism Association), 城舟体験（史跡高松城跡 玉藻公園） (https://www.my-kagawa.jp/point/2840/)

What it is: The Kagawa prefectural tourism association's page on the castle-boat ride in the moat of Takamatsu Castle, the seaside castle whose moat is filled with the Inland Sea's own water and swum by sea fish.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the readable statement of the one plain case of a castle moat filled from the sea, which this page uses to show how wide the range of moat sources ran. Its limit is that it is a tourism page about the moat as it is today.

Used for: Takamatsu's moat of seawater and sea bream
