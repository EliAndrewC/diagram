icid-diversion-weirs

International Commission on Irrigation and Drainage, technical term list, "Diversion Weirs" (https://icid-ciid.org/Knowledge/basic_term/0/Diversion%20Weirs/2247)

What it is: The entry for "diversion weir" in the ICID's list of basic irrigation and drainage terms: a low dam or wall across a stream for diverting part or all of its water into a canal.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the profession's own definition, and it settles one point the record was otherwise silent on: that taking all of a stream's water is within the ordinary meaning of a diversion weir. Its limits are that it is a one-line definition with no case, no condition and no period, so it establishes that whole diversion is a possibility and nothing about whether a village ever did it.

Used for: a diversion weir diverts "part or all" of a stream's water
