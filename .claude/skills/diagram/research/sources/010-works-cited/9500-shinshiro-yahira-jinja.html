Shinshiro City, 八平神社 (Yahira Shrine), a registered cultural property (https://www.city.shinshiro.lg.jp/kanko/minzokugeino/yahirajinjya.html)

What it is: Shinshiro City's (Aichi) official page on Yahira Shrine's registered buildings: a nagare-zukuri sanctuary of 1700 and a roofed board fence of 1918 around it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a dated, measured-by-listing example of a rural shrine's fence ringing the sanctuary alone, added two centuries after the building. Its limits: one shrine, registered because it is notable, so it shows the form's existence and date, not how common it was.

Used for: a sanctuary-only fence of 1918 (religion-and-death.html).
