What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on tamahagane, the steel of the tatara: how the kera lump was broken and sorted from the 1750s, and how its fractions were finished into steel or into wrought iron at the smithy.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the crushing and sorting of the kera and the finishing of part of it into wrought iron, the sequence this page's smelting valley follows. A tertiary summary used for that sequence. The crushing and sorting is dated to the 1750s, while the finishing of fractions at the smithy into wrought iron sits in the article's account of the modern Yasukuni tatara, so the sequence's seventeenth-century date rests on the paper cited beside it.

Used for: the kera crushed, sorted and part refined to wrought iron
