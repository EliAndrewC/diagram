ja.wikipedia 禅林街 ("Zenrin-gai", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/禅林街)

What it is: The short Japanese Wikipedia article on Zenrin-gai in Hirosaki, the tree-lined street of Soto Zen temples leading to the Tsugaru lords' family temple.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the number of temples in one planned temple quarter of an early Edo castle town: 33 Soto temples, Chōshō-ji among them, standing along the tree-lined avenue that leads to Chōshō-ji, which the second Tsugaru lord moved to Hirosaki in 1610 as the castle was built. It lists the temples but does not map them, so it does not show whether all 33 face that one avenue or some stand on streets off it. The article itself calls so many temples of one sect in one place rare nationwide, so this is an upper bound set by a domain's deliberate policy, not a typical temple quarter. It is a short tertiary article resting on city and tourist-guide pages, and it gives no sizes.
