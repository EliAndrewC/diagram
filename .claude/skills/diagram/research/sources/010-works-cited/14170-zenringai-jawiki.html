ja.wikipedia 禅林街 ("Zenrin-gai", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/禅林街)

What it is: The short Japanese Wikipedia article on Zenrin-gai in Hirosaki, the tree-lined street of Soto Zen temples leading to the Tsugaru lords' family temple.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the number of temples on one temple street, how many precincts a single street of a temple quarter could line. It is a short tertiary article with no sizes or dates.

Used for: 33 temples along one avenue (religion-and-death)
