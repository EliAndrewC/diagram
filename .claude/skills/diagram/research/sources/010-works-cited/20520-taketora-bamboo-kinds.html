taketora-bamboo-kinds

竹虎 (Taketora, the bamboo-craft maker of Susaki, Kochi), 「Bamboo, its kinds」 (translated; original: 「竹について（竹の種類）」) (https://www.taketora.co.jp/c/special/bamboo)

What it is: A bamboo-craft company's page describing the kinds of bamboo found in Japan - madake, hachiku, moso and a dozen smaller bamboos and bamboo grasses - each with the height of its culms and their diameter.

Why it applies, and its limits: Bamboo's height is the plant's, not a period's, so a present-day maker's figures stand for the bamboos of a premodern farmstead. Its limits are that it is a commercial page, not a botanical survey, giving each kind one range without saying how it was measured or where; and that it gives culm heights of grown stands, not of a stand kept short by cutting.

Used for: the heights of madake, hachiku and moso
