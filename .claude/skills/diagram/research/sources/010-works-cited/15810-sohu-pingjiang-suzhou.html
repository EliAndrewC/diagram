sohu-pingjiang-suzhou

风浮说星座 (a Sohu contributor), 从宋《平江图》追寻苏州古城的全貌 ("Tracing the whole of Suzhou's old city from the Song Pingjiang tu", title translated from the Chinese by this project), Sohu, 2023-02-28 (https://www.sohu.com/a/647521251_121607363)

What it is: A popular article on the 1229 stone plan of Suzhou and the city it shows: the moat, the "three across and four down" rivers, the streets paired with rivers, the stone-faced banks and the houses fronting a street and backing on the water, with stone steps down to it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the canal city that the record's river cities draw their in-city canals from, and states the bank form - stone revetment, railings in places, stepped house landings - in plain words. It is a contributor's post on a news portal, not scholarship, and it describes the city's canals from the plan and from what survives without dating the revetments or measuring anything; it is used for the bank form and for how the moat met the city's canals, never for a number. It posts under an account named for astrology, with no named author, so it is corroborated where it can be (the paired land-and-water gates by pingjiang-tu, the moat-canal junction at Pan Gate by pan-gate-enwiki).

Used for: what faces and lines a Chinese city canal's banks; the moat joined to the city's canals through the water gates (cities/river-cities)
