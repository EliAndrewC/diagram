東京都水道局 (Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Waterworks), 玉川上水の歴史 (https://www.waterworks.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kouhou/pr/tamagawa/rekishishiritai/rekishi)

What it is: The Tokyo waterworks bureau's own history page for the Tamagawa aqueduct: its digging as an open cut from Hamura to the Yotsuya gate and the stone and wooden mains laid under the city's streets the following year.

Why it applies, and its limits: The successor utility's own account of the aqueduct's two forms, open cut outside and buried conduit inside, which is the vocabulary our capital's water supply is drawn in. Its limit is that it is one system's history. And it is the shogun's own work in the largest city in the country, an upper bound a provincial seat would never match.

Used for: the buried stone and wooden mains under Edo
