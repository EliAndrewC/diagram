ja.wikipedia 水車 ("Water wheel", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E8%BB%8A)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the water wheel, with a section on its history in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the full use of power water wheels is held to date from the Edo period, for polishing rice and milling grain as white rice spread, which dates the village mill. It hedges the claim ("is said to") and cites no source for it.

Used for: the power water wheel dated to the Edo period (water)
