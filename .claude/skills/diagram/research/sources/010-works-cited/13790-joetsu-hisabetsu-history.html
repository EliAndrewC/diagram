Joetsu City, Human Rights, "Toward a bright society without discrimination" (title translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「差別のない明るい社会の実現に向けて」) (in Japanese; https://www.city.joetsu.niigata.jp/soshiki/jinken/sabetunonai.html)

What it is: A city human-rights page for Joetsu (Niigata prefecture) with a short history of its outcaste community: when the castle town of Takada was laid out in 1614, the people charged with guard and cleaning duties were moved from Naoetsu to a block at the town's northwest edge along the Kaga road.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records the founding siting of one castle town's outcaste quarter - at the edge and on the highway out - by the city itself. One town, told briefly for civic education without a source; it names the duties, not a tannery, and gives no reason for the northwest.

Used for: the outcaste quarter set at a castle town's edge along a highway (urban-features)
