pwsannong-sangji-yutang

"Sangji yutang" (桑基鱼塘, the mulberry-dike fishpond), an entry in the sericulture section of the encyclopedia on Zhihui Sannong (智汇三农), a Chinese agricultural knowledge database (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/564807.shtml)

What it is: A short Chinese agricultural encyclopedia entry on the mulberry-dike fishpond: how the dikes are built from pond mud, how the mulberry on them is planted and trained in the Pearl River delta and in Jiangsu and Zhejiang, and how the system arose in Guangdong from the fruit-dike ponds after the sixteenth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that gives how thickly the dike mulberry of the Pearl River delta was planted and how it was trained, and it describes the system the map's dike-pond hamlet is drawn from. It is a modern reference work describing the practice as it stood in the twentieth century, with no dates and no sources of its own, so its planting density is evidence for the later form of the system, not a measured pre-modern figure; it names no crown width.

Used for: how densely dike mulberry was planted in the Pearl River delta, and that it was trained as root-cut ground mulberry (archetypes)
