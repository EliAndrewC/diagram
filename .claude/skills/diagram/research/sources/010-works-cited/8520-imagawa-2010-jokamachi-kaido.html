今川朱美 and 小田雄司, "城下町の形成と街道網の関係 - 広島を事例として -" (The relation between the formation of a castle town and the highway network, with Hiroshima as the case), 広島工業大学紀要研究編 44 (2010) 41-45 (https://libwww.cc.it-hiroshima.ac.jp/library/pdf/library_research44_10.pdf)

What it is: A university bulletin paper that types castle towns by how their streets meet the highway network, distinguishing the town whose main street runs to the castle's great gate from the town where the two cross, with Hiroshima as its worked case.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the second half of our siting rule in a scholar's words: the principal street is the one that runs from the main gate, the front entrances of townhouse and samurai plots range along it, and rank falls off with distance outward. Its limits are that it is a short bulletin paper about Japanese castle towns of one period with a single case; that it is about street types rather than buildings, so it gives no frontage width and no plot size; and that it says nothing about Chinese practice, where our offices rule also claims to apply.

Used for: offices and residences fronting the avenue that runs from the main gate, with rank falling off outward from it
