SUUMO Journal, 「Can you still build a kura today?」 (title paraphrased and translated from the Japanese by this project), 2013-09-20 (in Japanese; https://suumo.jp/journal/2013/09/20/52128/)

What it is: A property-portal magazine article of 2013 on building a traditional storehouse today, with a short account of the old ones.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the common size of a household storehouse, about 2 by 3 ken (3.64 by 5.46 m), in the words of a plasterer who builds them. Its limits are that it is a modern popular article quoting one craftsman, not a survey; it gives no source, period or class for the figure; and it speaks of storehouses in general, not a samurai household's.

Used for: how big a household storehouse was (buildings.html).
