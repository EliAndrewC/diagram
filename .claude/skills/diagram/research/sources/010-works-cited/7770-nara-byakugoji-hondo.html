Nara City, 白毫寺本堂 (https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/site/bunkazai/8394.html) - 「Ridge span 5 ken, beam span 5 ken, hipped roof, a one-bay worship porch, formal tile roof, pantile at the rear」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桁行5間、梁間5間、寄棟造、向拝1間、本瓦葺、背面桟瓦葺」)

What it is: A municipal cultural-property page on an early-17th-century temple main hall in Nara, five bays by five.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for a hondō of 5 by 5 ken, the top of the hall band. A Nara temple rather than a village's, so an upper bound, not a village example.

Used for: a hondō of 5 by 5 ken (religion-and-death.html).
