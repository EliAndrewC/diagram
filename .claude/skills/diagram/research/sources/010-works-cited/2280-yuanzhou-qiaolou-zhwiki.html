What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Yuanzhou qiaolou in Jiangxi, the drum tower of a prefectural office founded in 944 under the Southern Tang and fitted out in 1219 as an observatory for measuring, keeping and announcing the time.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the qiaolou was part of the prefectural office compound, which is why this page calls it the compound's own tower rather than a poor seat's substitute. A tertiary summary; it does not say the tower stood over the compound's gate. The building is of the tenth to thirteenth centuries, several centuries before the Ming and Edo anchors these maps take.

Used for: the qiaolou as part of a prefectural office compound
