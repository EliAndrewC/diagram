tokinokane-list-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 時の鐘 ("toki no kane, the time bells", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/時の鐘)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia list of the surviving time bells of Edo-period towns, with where each hangs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that a town's time bell often hung at a temple. It is a list of survivors, with no count of how many towns kept one or where the rest hung.

Used for: the town time bell hung at a temple (religion-and-death)
