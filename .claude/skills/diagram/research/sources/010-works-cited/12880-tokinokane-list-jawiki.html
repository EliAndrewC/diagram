ja.wikipedia 時の鐘一覧 ("list of time bells", title translated from the Japanese by this project; the address 時の鐘 redirects to it) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/時の鐘)

What it is: A Japanese Wikipedia disambiguation page, with no sources, that points to the articles on thirteen Japanese time bells. It gives each bell's present-day address and sometimes the temple, shrine or castle it stands at, and marks one as no longer existing. It gives no dates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that a town's time bell could hang at a temple: three of the thirteen bells it names are at temples (Tenryū-ji in Shinjuku, Sensō-ji in Asakusa, Nion-ji in Kaizu). One more is at a shrine, and the rest are at a castle, a town street or a present-day park. It is a tertiary page with no sources, it says it is incomplete, and it gives no dates, no count of how many towns kept a bell, and nothing on who rang it. So it supports "sometimes at a temple", not "often". The ja.wikipedia article 石町時の鐘, which cites a 1979 history of Chūō Ward, lists Edo's nine authorized bells, most of them at temples.
