tsumago-aisurukai

Tsumago wo Aisuru Kai (the Society for Loving Tsumago), 妻籠宿の歴史 ("The history of Tsumago-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.tumagowoaisurukai.jp/abouttsumago/)

What it is: The local preservation society's history of Tsumago, a small post town on the Nakasendo in the Kiso valley, covering its Edo-period street, its inns, its village's houses in 1642 and the preservation movement of the twentieth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the station's street length in the period's own units and says it did not change through the Edo period, the smallest such length found. It is a society's page for visitors with no source or date for that figure, and it gives no house count for the station alone, only the whole village's in 1642.

Used for: one small post town's street length in the Edo period (towns)
