mlit-kanazawa-dobei

Japan Tourism Agency, Multilingual Commentary Database, 長町武家屋敷跡界隈 武家屋敷の土塀・石垣 (The Nagamachi samurai quarter: the earth walls and stone bases of samurai residences) (in English, with a Japanese translation beside it; https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/H30-00648.html)

What it is: The text of a 2018 sign in Kanazawa's Nagamachi samurai quarter, written in English by a writer the Japan Tourism Agency sent to the region, with the Kanazawa City Tourism Association as the regional council (the Japanese beside it is a translation of the English): how its earth walls were built by ramming, which ranks could build them, and the stone bases they stand on.

Why it applies, and its limits: It ties the earth wall to rank, which is what decides whether a compound draws a wall at all. Its limits: it is a sign for visitors in one domain's castle town, with no references, so its rank rule is Kaga's and not Japan's. It gives no date for that rule and describes the walls as they are kept today. It names only two ranks, foot soldiers with hedges and heishi and above with earth walls, and says nothing of the ranks between. It gives no thickness.

Used for: Kanazawa's rammed-earth samurai walls, foot soldiers barred from walls, heishi and above walled (buildings)
