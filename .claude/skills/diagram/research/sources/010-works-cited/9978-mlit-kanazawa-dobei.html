Japan Tourism Agency, Multilingual Commentary Database, 長町武家屋敷跡界隈 武家屋敷の土塀・石垣 (The Nagamachi samurai quarter: the earth walls and stone bases of samurai residences) (in Japanese; https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/H30-00648.html)

What it is: The text of a 2018 sign in Kanazawa's Nagamachi samurai quarter, written by the Kanazawa City Tourism Association for the national commentary database: how its earth walls were built by ramming, which ranks could build them, and the stone bases they stand on.

Why it applies, and its limits: It ties the earth wall to rank, which is what decides whether a compound draws a wall at all. Its limits: it is a sign for visitors in one domain's castle town, with no references, and it gives no thickness.

Used for: Kanazawa's rammed-earth samurai walls, foot soldiers barred from walls, hirazamurai and above walled (buildings)
