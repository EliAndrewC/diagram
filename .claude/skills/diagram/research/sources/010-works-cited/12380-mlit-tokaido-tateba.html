mlit-tokaido-tateba

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), Kanto Regional Bureau, Yokohama office, "東海道と宿場の施設: 立場" (the Tokaido and its post-town facilities: the rest stops) (in Japanese; https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/03_sisetu/1001.htm)

What it is: One page of the road ministry's regional series on the old Tokaido through Kanagawa, on the rest stops: their count and places from the late-Edo register of the highway's post towns and villages, and where their teahouses stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts the post towns, the villages between them and the rest stops on one stretch of highway, and says where a rest stop's teahouses clustered - the figures behind the share of highway villages that kept one. The counts are for Kanagawa's Tokaido, the busiest highway, and may overstate a quieter road; the page gives no count of teahouses per rest stop, and one village name in its text disagrees with its own table.

Used for: how many villages kept a rest stop, where its teahouses stood, and how many post towns and villages lay on the road (ways)
