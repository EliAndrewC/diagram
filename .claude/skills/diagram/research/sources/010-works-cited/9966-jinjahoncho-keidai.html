jinjahoncho-keidai

Jinja Honcho (the Association of Shinto Shrines), "境内について" (about the shrine precinct) (in Japanese; https://www.jinjahoncho.or.jp/omairi/keidai/)

What it is: The national association of Shinto shrines' own guide for visitors to what stands in a shrine precinct, from the torii and the approach to the small subsidiary shrines.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where a torii stands - at the boundary between the shrine and the outside - and that small subsidiary shrines stand inside the precinct besides the main hall, which is what decides whether one arch serves more than one shrine. It is a general guide for present-day visitors, with no dates, no measurements and nothing on a shrine inside a residence.

Used for: that the torii marks the precinct's boundary and that subsidiary shrines stand within the precinct (religion-and-death).
