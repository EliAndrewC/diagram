Wikipedia, "Soybean", Description (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article "Soybean", and in particular its Description section - a tertiary encyclopedia entry on the plant, its habit and its cultivation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the two facts a bean plot on a map turns on - that the plant is an annual, replanted every year, and how tall it stands - and the first is a constant of the species. Its limits are that it is a tertiary summary; and that the height it gives, 50 to 125 cm, describes the crop as bred and grown now - the article gives a separate modern-cultivar figure and says nothing about landraces - so the drawn height is a modern-crop range used because no premodern measurement of the plant could be read.
