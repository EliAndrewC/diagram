What it is: The Kotobank page quoting the World Encyclopedia's entry on Jigen-ryū where it mentions tategi-uchi: the school's basic technique of practicing against a standing timber, its Satsuma character, and its founder.

Why it applies, and its limits: A standard encyclopedia names practice against a standing timber as the school's basic technique and dates its founder. Its limits are that it is an excerpt of a few lines from an older edition, and gives no size, count or setting for the post.

Used for: tategi-uchi as Jigen-ryū's basic technique, a Satsuma form; its founder Tōgō Shigetaka (1561-1643) (buildings)
