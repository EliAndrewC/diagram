What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Six Ancient Kilns, the six medieval Japanese ceramic centers - Seto, Tokoname, Echizen, Shigaraki, Tamba and Bizen - and the wares named for them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the districts each known for one ware, which is this page's evidence that fine stoneware was a district's specialty rather than a county seat's. A tertiary summary used for the names; it does not say what an ordinary county seat did or did not fire. The grouping of six is a twentieth-century scholar's construct, devised after the war by Koyama Fujio; the districts are medieval, the canon is modern.

Used for: the districts each named for their one ware
