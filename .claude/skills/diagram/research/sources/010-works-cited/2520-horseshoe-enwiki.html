What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the horseshoe: its history from wrapped hooves in ancient Asia through the nailed iron shoe of medieval Europe and the iron shoes of Yuan China.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that iron shoes became common in China only from the Yuan and that earlier hoof protection was wrapped or strapped, which is why this page does not rest its argument on Chinese practice. A tertiary summary; it does not say whether the Yuan shoes were nailed.

Used for: hoof protection before and after the Yuan
