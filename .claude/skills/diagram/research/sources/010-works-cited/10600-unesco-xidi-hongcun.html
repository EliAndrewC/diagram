unesco-xidi-hongcun

UNESCO, World Heritage List, "Ancient Villages in Southern Anhui - Xidi and Hongcun" (https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1002/)

What it is: The World Heritage listing, inscribed in 2000, of two villages in the Huizhou region of Anhui whose Ming and Qing layout survives, with the committee's description of how each sits on its water.

Why it applies, and its limits: In one authoritative page it shows both answers to our question side by side: Xidi built along and between its streams, and Hongcun next to its stream. Its limits: both are wealthy merchant-lineage villages in one region of China, larger and richer than a farming hamlet, and the page describes them as they survive rather than dating the layout.

Used for: that Xidi was built along and between three streams and Hongcun next to a stream (water)
