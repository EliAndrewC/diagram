xuli-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 胥吏 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/胥吏)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the xuli, the sub-official clerks of imperial Chinese government, quoting Song to Qing memoirs and memorials; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the clerks as entrenched local staff, the official's dependence on their knowledge and their enrichment out of the revenue they handled, which is the clerk this page's county runs on; a Chinese picture, and it says nothing of the Japanese tedai.

Used for: the permanent local clerk (buildings)
