What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Hie Shrine in Kurihara, Miyagi prefecture, a shrine ranked as a village shrine in 1874 whose sanctuary and worship hall are prefectural cultural properties.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the bay counts of an ordinary village shrine's two buildings, both Edo in date - the sanctuary 2 bays by 1, dated by its style to the early Edo period at the latest, and the worship hall 3 bays by 2, thought to be late Edo. It gives bay counts and no metric sizes, it dates the worship hall only by estimate, and it is one shrine in the northeast, picked out because its buildings were designated.

Used for: the size of a village shrine's own halls (religion-and-death.html).
