What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Hie Shrine in Kurihara, Miyagi prefecture, a shrine ranked as a village shrine in 1874 whose sanctuary and worship hall are prefectural cultural properties.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the bay counts of the two buildings of a shrine ranked a village shrine only in 1874 (a Meiji rank; before the separation of Shinto and Buddhism it was a Sannō shrine, and the article does not give its Edo standing), both Edo in date - the sanctuary 2 bays by 1, dated by its style to the early Edo period at the latest, and the worship hall 3 bays by 2, thought to be late Edo. It gives bay counts and no metric sizes, it dates the worship hall only by estimate, and it is one shrine in the northeast, picked out because its buildings were designated - a sanctuary with Momoyama-style carving and fittings, likely finer than an ordinary village shrine's. It is a tertiary article restating Miyagi prefecture's designation text (https://www.pref.miyagi.jp/soshiki/bunkazai/25hie.html), which gives the same counts and is the closer source for them.

Used for: the size of a village shrine's own halls (religion-and-death.html).
