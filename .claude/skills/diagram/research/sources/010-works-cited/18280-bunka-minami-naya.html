bunka-minami-naya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 南家住宅納屋 ("Minami house, barn", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/195999)

What it is: The national register's entry for the barn of the Minami house at Hannan, Osaka prefecture, dated 1830-1867: its period, its length and depth in ken, its tiled gable roof, its two rooms, its building area, and its place in the farm work area of the plot.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a farm work shed of the Edo period with a readable size and siting, the premodern anchor for a naya's size where the other measured barns were built after 1868. Its limits: one building, near Osaka, of a household whose register also lists several storehouses, so not an ordinary smallholder's; it is dated only to a span of nearly forty years; it says nothing about how common such a shed was; and its figures are a 2005 registration's account of the building as it survives, not a period measure: the 28 m² building area takes in the half-ken lean-to along the west and around the south, so the 3 by 2 ken body alone is about 20 m² at a 6-shaku ken, and the entry does not say how long its ken is.

Used for: the size and siting of an Edo farm shed (homesteads)
