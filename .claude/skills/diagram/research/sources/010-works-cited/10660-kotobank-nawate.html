kotobank-nawate

Kotobank, 縄手 (nawate), reprinting the Digital Daijisen and the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%B8%84%E6%89%8B)

What it is: Kotobank's page of dictionary entries on the nawate, the path between paddies, with the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's dated earliest use of that sense.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the word for the path between the paddies to the ten-volume Wamyosho of about 934, so the bund walked as a path is not a modern arrangement. A dictionary gives the word and its first attestation, not how often or how widely such paths ran, and says nothing of how a path met the paddies.

Used for: the nawate, the path between the paddies, attested by about 934 (fields)
