What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the gangi of Hiroshima, the stone stepped landings and revetments of the delta city's rivers; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the stepped landing as itself a revetment, in timber and then granite, built where boats berthed and cargo was landed, and the step against the floating pier for a moving water level; a delta port on a 4 m tide, not a river's annual stage.

Used for: the stepped quay as revetment (river cities)
