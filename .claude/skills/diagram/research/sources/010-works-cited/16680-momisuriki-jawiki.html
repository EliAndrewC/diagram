ja.wikipedia 籾すり機 ("rice huller", title translated from the Japanese by this project; reached from 籾摺り) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%B1%BE%E6%91%BA%E3%82%8A)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the rice huller, the machine that strips the husk from paddy to leave brown rice, with a short history from the pestle and mortar through the wooden and clay hulling mills of the Edo period to the rubber-roll machine of the 1930s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the clay hulling mill broke many grains and that the farm household ate the broken rice because it could not be shipped, which puts the hulling on the farm, before the rice left it. A tertiary summary centered on modern machinery; its history is a paragraph, and it does not say in so many words who hulled.

Used for: hulling done on the farm before the rice was shipped (urban-features)
