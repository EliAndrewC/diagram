matsue-bukeyashiki

Matsue Buke Yashiki (Shiomi Nawate), museum building documentation (https://www.matsue-bukeyashiki.jp/about/equipment.html)

What it is: The building documentation published by the museum that operates the Matsue Buke Yashiki, a preserved middle-rank samurai residence standing on the Shiomi Nawate street in the castle town of Matsue, describing the house room by room and the gate on its street front.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a surviving building described by the people who keep it, which is why we use it for the nagayamon standing on the street and doubling as the quarters of the gatekeeper and the household servants, and for a room program that includes the servants' service rooms - both things our residence plans have to place. It is one house in one castle town, presented for visitors rather than as a study, and a restored and interpreted one, so it tells us what a middle-rank residence in Matsue is like and not what a typical residence anywhere was.

Used for: a middle-rank residence with its nagayamon on the street; room program including servants' service rooms
