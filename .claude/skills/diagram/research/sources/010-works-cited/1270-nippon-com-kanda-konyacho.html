nippon-com-kanda-konyacho

nippon.com, 『神田紺屋町』：浮世写真家 喜千也の名所江戸百景第20回 (https://www.nippon.com/ja/guide-to-japan/gu004020/)

What it is: An installment of a Nippon.com series pairing each of Hiroshige's One Hundred Famous Views of Edo with a photograph of the place today, here Kanda Konya-cho, the dyers' town: the indigo dyers who gave it its name and the bolts of cloth drying in rows that made the sight famous.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Edo's dyers' quarter and the drying as its visible signature, which is what our dye-works row is drawn to show. Its limit is that it is a photographic feature rather than a trade history.

Used for: the Kanda dyers eave to eave, and the drying bolts ranged like banners
