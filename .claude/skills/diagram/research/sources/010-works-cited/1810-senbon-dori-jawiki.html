ja.wikipedia 千本通 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/千本通)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Senbon-dōri, the Kyoto street named for the thousand stupa-tablets once raised along the road to the funerary ground at the western foot of Funaoka-yama.

Why it applies, and its limits: It ties the street's name and course to the funerary ground at its head, which is how this page locates Rendaino. A tertiary summary used for that. The article gives two competing origins for the name, and the one this page uses carries no citation and no date while the other does.

Used for: Senbon-dōri as the road of tablets to the Funaoka-yama burial ground
