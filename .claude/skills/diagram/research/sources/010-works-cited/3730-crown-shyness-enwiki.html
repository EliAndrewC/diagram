en.wikipedia "Crown shyness" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_shyness)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on crown shyness, the gaps that fully stocked tree crowns keep between one another; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that stocked crowns do not touch; it describes rainforest and same-species stands, not a temperate coppice.

Used for: crown gaps in a stocked stand (vegetation)
