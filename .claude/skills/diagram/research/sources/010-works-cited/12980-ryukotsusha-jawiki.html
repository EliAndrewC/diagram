ja.wikipedia 竜骨車 ("Dragon-bone wheel", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%AB%9C%E9%AA%A8%E8%BB%8A)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the ryukotsusha, the Chinese chain pump as used in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the device came from China, was trodden by two people facing each other, was used mainly around the Kinki region in the early Edo period, and was driven out by the treadle wheel. It is a tertiary source: a stub whose "Kinki, early Edo" claim rests on one footnote to a 2017 popular reference (Heibonsha's 日本史モノ事典), the rest on a 1977 annotated edition of Ōkura Nagatsune's 農具便利論 (1822). It records that its sources disagree on how widely the device spread. Ōkura says it was in general use in every province before the treadle wheel; Aoki Kunio says it broke easily and never spread widely. So it carries the device's form and the order in which the treadle wheel replaced it, not how common the chain pump was.

Used for: the chain pump in Japan: two treaders, Kinki, displaced by the treadle wheel (water)
