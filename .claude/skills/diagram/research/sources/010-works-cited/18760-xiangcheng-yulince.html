xiangcheng-yulince

Ren Yafeng, "Exploring Xiangcheng's fish-scale registers," Suzhou archives website ("archive stories" column), 2022 (in Chinese; http://www.daj.suzhou.gov.cn/detail/135849.html)

What it is: A short popular article by Ren Yafeng in the "archive stories" column of the Suzhou archives' website, describing nine fish-scale registers compiled in 1897 for the polders of Taipingqiao town in Xiangcheng - five donated to the Xiangcheng District Archives in 2022, four kept at the Taiping subdistrict's local-history hall - with a rough count of their parcels and acreage.

Why it applies, and its limits: A late-Qing land register of polder country on the Lake Tai plain, where the register is arranged polder by polder, so it speaks to the rice polders the maps draw. The totals are the article's rough count over nineteen polders (it says elsewhere the registers cover twenty), taken from registers this project has not seen, the acreage mixes good rice land with marsh and waste land and is the register's own mu, which may be a tax figure; the page gives no average, so a mean parcel worked from its totals is this project's arithmetic and good only to its order of magnitude. It is one town near the end of the period.

Used for: about three mu a parcel in the polders of 1897 (archetypes)
