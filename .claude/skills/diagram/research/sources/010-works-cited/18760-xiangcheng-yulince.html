xiangcheng-yulince

Ren Yafeng, "Exploring Xiangcheng's fish-scale registers," Xiangcheng District Archives, Suzhou, 2022 (in Chinese; http://www.daj.suzhou.gov.cn/detail/135849.html)

What it is: A page by the district archives of Xiangcheng, in Suzhou, describing nine fish-scale registers compiled in 1897 for the polders of Taipingqiao town, donated to the archives in 2022, with a rough count of their parcels and acreage.

Why it applies, and its limits: A late-Qing land register of polder country on the Lake Tai plain, where the register is arranged polder by polder, so it speaks to the rice polders the maps draw. The totals are the archivist's rough count over nineteen polders, the acreage mixes good rice land with marsh and waste land and is the register's own mu, which may be a tax figure; the page gives no average, so a mean parcel worked from its totals is this project's arithmetic and good only to its order of magnitude. It is one town near the end of the period.

Used for: about three mu a parcel in the polders of 1897 (archetypes)
