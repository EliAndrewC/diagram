kotobank-hitoyado

Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten and Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten, s.v. 人宿, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/人宿)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the reference entries on hitoyado, the Edo-period placement brokers for servants, with excerpts from the older encyclopedia's entries on brokers and on servants.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the brokers grew up with the cities, that samurai households were most of their clients, and that samurai-household service came to be done by peasants and townsmen hired through them. It is written about Edo above all, the servants it names are mostly attendants and footmen rather than house staff, and it never says where in a town the brokers kept their premises.

Used for: how a samurai household hired its servants (government)
