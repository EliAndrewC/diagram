What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Ming city wall of Nanjing, its gates and the water gates and conduits by which the Qinhuai and the lake water passed the wall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that water crossed the wall at gates and through conduits laid in the wall's foundation, the culvert-through-the-wall this page draws for a city drain. A tertiary summary of the imperial capital's wall.

Used for: water passing a city wall by gate and conduit
