kotobank-sobaya

kotobank 蕎麦屋 ("noodle shop", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%95%8E%E9%BA%A6%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting two dictionary entries on the soba shop, the shop that makes noodles and serves or sells them, with its earliest attested uses around 1800.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the noodle shop as one that both makes and serves in the same place, which is the shop-front-and-kitchen form the generic shop glyph stands for. A dictionary definition only; it gives no size, no count and no siting.

Used for: the noodle shop making and serving in one place (urban-features)
