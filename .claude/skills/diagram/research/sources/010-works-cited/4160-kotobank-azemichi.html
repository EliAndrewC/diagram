kotobank-azemichi

Digital Daijisen (Shogakukan), s.v. 畦道, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/畦道)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the dictionary entry on the azemichi, the path along a paddy bund.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the bund path, which is where this page has farmers walk past the field track's end; a dictionary line with no width; an undated present-day dictionary, so it carries the term and not a premodern path.

Used for: the bund as a walked path (ways)
