Joetsu City Public Records Center, exhibition sheet for "資料から探る江戸時代の風呂事情" ("Bathing in the Edo period, explored from the documents", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2017 (in Japanese; https://www.city.joetsu.niigata.jp/uploaded/attachment/135605.pdf)

What it is: The printed explanation sheet of the Joetsu archive's exhibition on Edo-period bathing: the bathhouse guilds of Takada castle town (19 houses from 1769) and Naoetsu-imamachi (8), mixed bathing and the domain's orders against it, opening hours and the strong-wind closure, bath fees, and the home baths shared in turn in the countryside.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is written from named domain and family documents held in the Takada library, so its counts and the orders it quotes are primary evidence for one provincial castle town and its neighbor. Its limits are that it is one domain; that it says why separate tubs were avoided (space, firewood, water) but gives no building size; and that it is an exhibition sheet, not a study.

Used for: the castle town's and Naoetsu-imamachi's bathhouse counts, home baths shared in the countryside, firewood and water as a bathhouse's costs, and the strong-wind closure (urban-features)
