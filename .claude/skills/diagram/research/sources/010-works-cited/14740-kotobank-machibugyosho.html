kotobank-machibugyosho

Kotobank, 町奉行所 ("town magistracy", title translated from the Japanese by this project), from Heibonsha's 日本歴史地名大系 (Japanese historical place names) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%94%BA%E5%A5%89%E8%A1%8C%E6%89%80-1419532)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the Edo town magistracy, carrying the place-name dictionary's account of where the offices stood and when they moved, and the world encyclopedia's note on the yoriki.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sites the town magistracies inside the gates of the castle's outer moat, facing the commoners' town - the answer to where a city's own magistracy stood. It is a reference-work extract (the full entry is behind a paywall) about Edo alone.

Used for: where Edo's town magistracies stood (cities/government)
