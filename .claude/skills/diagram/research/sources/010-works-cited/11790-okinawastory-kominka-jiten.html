okinawastory-kominka-jiten

Okinawa Convention and Visitors Bureau, Okinawa Monogatari, "Okinawa kominka mini jiten" (A small dictionary of Okinawa's old houses) (in Japanese; https://www.okinawastory.jp/feature/kominka/kominka_jiten)

What it is: A glossary page on the prefecture's official tourism site explaining the parts of a traditional Okinawan house and plot - the stone wall, the screen wall (hinpun) behind the gate, the pigsty, the roof - for visitors. Dated 2017.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states plainly that the traditional Okinawan house was generally walled in coral and limestone against the typhoons, which is one attested form of a farmstead boundary; the screening and the line between settlement and plot it gives to the hinpun, not to the wall. It is a tourism page, not a survey, it gives no date for the form, and it describes the Ryukyu islands, whose climate and history differ from the Japanese main islands, so it supports the stone-walled plot only for a warm, storm-exposed, stony coast.

Used for: a plot walled in coral and limestone (homesteads)
