ja.wikipedia 東海道五十三次 ("the fifty-three stations of the Tokaido", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9D%B1%E6%B5%B7%E9%81%93%E4%BA%94%E5%8D%81%E4%B8%89%E6%AC%A1)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the fifty-three post stations of the Tokaido, with the distances between them and a paragraph on how many inns the stations held.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the spread of inn counts across one highway's stations, from Miya's 247 down to Fujisawa's 45. It is a tertiary summary; the total of nearly three thousand is hedged "is said", and the counts are the Tokaido's, the busiest highway, so they are an upper range.

Used for: the inn counts of the Tokaido's stations (towns)
