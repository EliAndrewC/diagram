hasegawa-2018-toyama-konara

Hasegawa Mikio, Nakajima Haruki, Ōmiya Tōru and Zushi Kōtarō, 「Species composition and stratification of konara-dominated secondary forests in Toyama prefecture」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「富山県におけるコナラを主とする二次林の種組成と階層構造」), Bulletin of the Toyama Prefectural Agricultural, Forestry and Fisheries Research Center, Forestry Research Institute 10 (2018), pp. 1-10 (https://agriknowledge.affrc.go.jp/RN/2010925171.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural research bulletin reporting 72 plots in konara-dominated woods across Toyama prefecture - woods cut on a cycle for fuel until the mid-twentieth century and left uncut since - with their standing timber and the species in each layer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets the timber in today's uncut woods against what the same woods held when they were cut every twenty years, which says how much thinner a worked fuel wood was. The twenty-year figure is quoted from another study, and the woods measured are abandoned ones, 50 to 60 years old.

Used for: how much less timber a worked fuel wood held than the same wood left uncut (vegetation)
