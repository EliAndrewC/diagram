sadler-1937-legacy-of-ieyasu

A. L. Sadler, The Maker of Modern Japan: The Life of Tokugawa Ieyasu (1937), chapter XLIV, "The Legacy Of Ieyasu" (https://gwern.net/doc/japan/history/1937-sadler-themakerofmodernjapan-thelegacyofieyasu.pdf)

What it is: The chapter of Sadler's 1937 English biography of Ieyasu that introduces and translates the "legacy", the testament in a hundred articles. Sadler was a scholar of Japanese at the University of Sydney.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how far the testament can be trusted: it exists in several versions and was plainly added to after Ieyasu's death, though Sadler holds that much of it reflects his principles. It is one scholar's 1937 view and the generous one, since others have doubted that Ieyasu wrote the text at all. His translation leaves out what he judged "obviously later" without listing it and carries no road article, so it cannot show whether that article is a later addition; Gubbins's 1919 translation with the variant readings (Transactions of the Japan Society, London, XVII) is where that would be settled.

Used for: the testament's standing - several versions, added to after Ieyasu's death (ways)
