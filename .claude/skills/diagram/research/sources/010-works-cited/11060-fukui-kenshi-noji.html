fukui-kenshi-noji

Fukui Prefectural Archives, 『福井県史』通史編4 近世二 (History of Fukui Prefecture, general history vol. 4, early modern 2), the section on the farm year (in Japanese; https://www.library-archives.pref.fukui.lg.jp/fukui/07/kenshi/T4/T4-2-01-03-02-03.htm)

What it is: The prefecture's official history, published online by its archives, here describing the Edo-period farm year in Echizen and Wakasa from local farming manuals and diaries, among them the calendar called Kosaku Makitane Reijiroku (耕作蒔種令時録).

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a scholarly regional history of the period these maps draw on, and it dates the bund work and the dry-crop sowing by the solar terms farmers used. It describes one region on the Sea of Japan side; other regions' calendars differed with climate, and it summarizes its manuals rather than quoting them at length.

Used for: the yearly shaving and re-plastering of the bunds before transplanting; the dry crops sown and harvested between the rice tasks (fields)
