kotobank-hinomi-yagura

Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho, Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten and Digital Daijisen, s.v. 火の見櫓, on kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/火の見櫓-120180)

What it is: Kotobank's reproduction of the encyclopedia and dictionary entries on the hinomi-yagura, the fire-watch tower, its bell code and its Edo distribution.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tower's height and bell and the one-tower-per-ten-blocks rule beside the block posts' ladders, which is the split between state and neighborhood watch; reference entries with no plan; the one-tower-per-ten-blocks rule is the Kyōhō era's (1716-36) in the shogunal capital, not a general ratio.

Used for: the fire-watch tower and who kept it (towns)
