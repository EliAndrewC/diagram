zh.wikipedia, 常平仓 ("Changping cang, the ever-normal granary", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%B8%B8%E5%B9%B3%E4%BB%93)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the state granary for steadying grain prices and for relief, from the Han to the Qing's system reaching over 1,300 counties, mostly in the county seat under the magistrate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the state granary in the county seat, against the village's own store. Its limits are that it is a short tertiary article citing secondary books, and that its siting sentence follows a Qing sentence without being tied to a period itself.

Used for: the Chinese state granary standing in the county seat (urban-features)
