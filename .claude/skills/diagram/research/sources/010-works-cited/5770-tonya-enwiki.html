en.wikipedia Ton'ya (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ton%27ya)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on ton'ya, the trade brokers of early modern Japan who acted as wholesalers, warehouse managers and shipment managers, and who became central to the economy of the Edo period (1603-1867).

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it only for what a ton'ya was and what work it did, which is enough to justify drawing a merchant's warehouse-and-office block in a town's commercial quarter and to name it correctly. It is a tertiary summary, so we take its figures and dates only where its own references support them, and it describes an institution rather than a building - nothing about the size, plan or placement of a ton'ya on our maps comes from it.
