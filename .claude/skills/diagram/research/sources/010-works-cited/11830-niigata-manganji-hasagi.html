niigata-manganji-hasagi

Niigata Visitors and Convention Bureau, Niigata Kanko Navi, "Manganji hasagi namiki" (The Manganji row of rice-drying trees) (in Japanese; https://niigata-kankou.or.jp/spot/5569)

What it is: The prefectural tourism site's page on a row of trees along a farm road in Niigata city on which rice was hung to dry, with the story of how the row was made.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the best-known row of drying trees along a farm road to a land consolidation of the 1940s, when the farmers moved their trees there, which shows that a long row of racks beside a straight road is a modern arrangement. It is a tourism page, and it does not say where the trees had stood before they were moved.

Used for: the row of drying trees along a road as a 1940s arrangement (homesteads)
