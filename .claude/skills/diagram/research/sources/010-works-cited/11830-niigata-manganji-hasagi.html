niigata-manganji-hasagi

Niigata Prefectural Tourist Association, Niigata Kanko Navi, "Manganji hasagi namiki" (The Manganji row of rice-drying trees) (in Japanese; https://niigata-kankou.or.jp/spot/5569)

What it is: The prefectural tourism site's page on a row of trees along a farm road in Niigata city on which rice was hung to dry, with the story of how the row was made.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the best-known row of drying trees along a farm road to a land consolidation of the 1940s, when the farmers moved their trees there, so this straight farm road, and the row along it, are an arrangement of the 1940s. It is a tourism page about one row in one district. It says only that the trees were brought "from various places" in the district, not how they had stood there. So it dates this row, but it cannot show how drying trees stood before the consolidation or in our setting. And the row survives because the district was kept for air-dried seed rice after machine drying ended drying trees elsewhere, so it is a preserved exception, not a typical landscape.

Used for: the row of drying trees along a road as a 1940s arrangement (homesteads)
