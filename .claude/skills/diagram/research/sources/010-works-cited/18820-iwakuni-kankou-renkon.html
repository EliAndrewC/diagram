iwakuni-kankou-renkon

Iwakuni City Tourism Promotion Division, "Iwakuni lotus root" (in Japanese; https://kankou.iwakuni-city.net/iwakunirenkon.html)

What it is: The Iwakuni city tourism page on its lotus root (posted 2015), giving the story of the crop's arrival from Bitchū, the domain's enterprise from 1811, the legend of its nine holes matching the Kikkawa crest, and the root's texture.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a promotional page written today, naming no source for its dates, and its account of the start (a Muronoki farmer's initiative) differs from the ALIC report's (the lord's order). It is used only for its date of 1811 for the domain's own lotus enterprise, which places a lotus district under a domain before the Meiji era; it gives no area.

Used for: Iwakuni lotus a domain-run enterprise from 1811 (archetypes)
