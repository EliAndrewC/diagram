kotobank-gono

Kotobank, 豪農 ("gōnō, the rich farmer", title translated from the Japanese by this project) - the Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, revised edition, entry by Sasaki Junnosuke among others (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%B1%AA%E8%BE%B2-170491)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the gōnō, carrying published dictionaries and the Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry on the rich farmer-landlords of the later Edo period: when they arose, from what, and what they did.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the rich farmer of the question - a village landlord of the mid-eighteenth century on, out of older landlords or out of trade - so the record can say who such a homestead belonged to. Its limits: an economic historian's definition that says nothing about houses, plots or buildings, and describes Tokugawa Japan's later commercial economy rather than any one village.

Used for: who the rich farmer-landlords were (homesteads)
