Narumi Kunitada (鳴海 邦匡), 「A proposed definition and classification of early modern mountain-dispute maps, from the southern foot of the Hokusetsu hills」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「近世山論絵図の定義と分類試論 - 北摂山地南麓地域を事例として -」), Historical Geography (歴史地理学) 44:3 (209), 2002, pp. 1-21 (http://hist-geo.jp/img/archive/209_001.pdf)

What it is: A peer-reviewed paper in the journal of the Association of Historical Geographers in Japan that catalogs 57 maps drawn for disputes over hill land and village boundaries in the hills north of Osaka between 1612 and 1859, and classes them by who made them and how they show the ground and the boundary.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes how a disputed hill-land boundary was actually fixed and shown - by drawn marker rocks, and by a ruled line with seals at its ends and bends - from the maps themselves. It covers one region's surviving maps, and says how boundaries were drawn on paper, not how they ran on the ground.

Used for: how a ruled hill-land boundary was marked and drawn (vegetation)
