zh.wikipedia 敌台 (城墙) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/敌台_(城墙))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the ditai, the bastion projecting from a city wall, with the Wubei Zhi's dictum on it; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the bastion's purpose - flanking fire from both sides - and shows square, round and semicircular forms in its images, which bears on this page's shape rule; a tertiary summary that gives no spacing figure, and whose round and semicircular exemplars are European walls (Ávila, Krak des Chevaliers), its Chinese example rectangular.

Used for: the wall bastion's purpose and forms (defenses)
