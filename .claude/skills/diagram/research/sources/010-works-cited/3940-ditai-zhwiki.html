zh.wikipedia 敌台 (城墙) ("defensive wall tower", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/敌台_(城墙))

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the ditai, the bastion projecting from a city wall, with the Wubei Zhi's dictum on it; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the bastion's purpose - flanking fire from both sides - and shows square, round and semicircular forms in its images, which bears on this page's shape rule; a tertiary summary that gives no spacing figure, and whose round and semicircular exemplars are non-Chinese walls (Ávila in Spain, Krak des Chevaliers in Syria), its Chinese examples (Jinshanling, Xi'an, Linhai, Dazhuangke) squared towers.

Used for: the wall bastion's purpose and forms (defenses)
