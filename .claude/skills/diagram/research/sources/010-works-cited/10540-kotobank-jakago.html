kotobank-jakago

Dictionary and encyclopedia entries s.v. 蛇籠 (jakago), aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/蛇籠)

What it is: The entries for the gabion - a long basket of woven bamboo packed with stones - in the Daijisen, the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (which cites the Japanese-Portuguese dictionary of 1603-04) and the Nipponica encyclopedia, which gives the usual size of the basket.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one dimension the record holds for a piece of a small traditional water-work: the basket's diameter. The size is the encyclopedia's present-day statement of what is common, not a measured pre-modern figure, and the entries describe gabions as bank protection and flow control in general, not as a village weir.

Used for: what a gabion is and how thick one basket is (water)
