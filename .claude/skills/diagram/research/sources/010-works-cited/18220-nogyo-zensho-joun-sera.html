JK, 農業者ネットせら (the Sera farmers' network blog), J.Kの書斎：農業全書『鋤芸』抄訳（1） ("JK's study: a partial translation of the Nogyo Zensho's section on hoeing and weeding, part 1", title translated from the Japanese by this project), 2019 (in Japanese; http://3d.txt-nifty.com/blog/2019/07/post-8cc77c.html)

What it is: A blog post by a writer signed only JK rendering into modern Japanese part of the Nogyo Zensho, the farming manual Miyazaki Yasusada published in 1697, from its general section on hoeing and weeding.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lets a reader see what Japan's standard Edo-period farming manual says of a dry field: that it had ridges, that weeds were spread on the ridge tops, and that millet was hoed before its seedlings rose to the ridge tops. It is an amateur's translation of an excerpt, it does not say which edition it worked from, and the manual drew on the Chinese Nongzheng quanshu as well as Japanese practice, so it states what a learned manual advised rather than what every village did.

Used for: that an Edo dry field was worked in ridges, with millet sown between them (fields)
