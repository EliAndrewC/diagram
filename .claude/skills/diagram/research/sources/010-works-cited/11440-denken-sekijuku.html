denken-sekijuku

National Council of Preservation Districts for Groups of Traditional Buildings (Denkenkyo), 亀山市関宿 伝建地区詳細 ("Seki-juku, Kameyama: preservation district details", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://www.denken.gr.jp/archive/kameyama-sekijuku/index.html)

What it is: The national preservation-district council's archive page for Seki, stating that the district covers almost the whole former post town, about 1.8 km from the fork at its east entrance to the fork at its west, with its houses running continuously along the road.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the length of a surviving Edo-period post town between the two forks at its entrances (Edo-period landmarks), from the council of the municipalities that hold the preservation districts; the figure is a rounded "about 1.8 km", and the page does not say how it was measured. It is one town's length as preserved today, a short page with no plan or lot dimensions.

Used for: Seki's length along its road (towns)
