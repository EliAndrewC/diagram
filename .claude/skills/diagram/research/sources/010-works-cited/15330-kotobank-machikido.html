kotobank-machikido

Digital Daijisen and Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (Shogakukan), s.v. 町木戸 ("ward gate", translated from the Japanese by this project), aggregated at kotobank (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%94%BA%E6%9C%A8%E6%88%B8-634599)

What it is: The kotobank page for the machi-kido, the wooden gate set in each of Edo's wards for security and shut at night, from two Japanese dictionaries, one with an 1818-45 attestation.

Why it applies, and its limits: A dictionary definition, which fixes that the gate belonged to the ward and was closed at night. It is two lines long and about Edo; it gives no hours, no size and no count.

Used for: the ward's own night gate (cities/fabric.html)
