zh.wikipedia 文庙 ("Confucian temple", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/文庙)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Confucian temples, including how a local Confucian temple was joined to the official school of its prefecture or county and the layouts that joining took.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the general arrangement of temple and school in one sentence. It is an encyclopedia summary, not a survey, and gives no sizes or counts.

Used for: how a Chinese seat's Confucian temple and school were laid out together (religion-and-death)
