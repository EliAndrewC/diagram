maff-suitou-mizu

MAFF (Japan, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries), 水稲栽培のポイント (https://www.maff.go.jp/j/seisan/gijutsuhasshin/techinfo/attach/pdf/suitou-2.pdf)

What it is: An extension leaflet from the Japanese Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, 水稲栽培のポイント ("points for paddy rice cultivation"), setting out how deep the water is held in a paddy at each stage of the season: somewhat deep until the transplanted seedlings root, shallower afterward to warm the water and encourage tillering, then the mid-season drain until the mud cracks and shows a footprint, with deep water held only as a guard against a cold snap.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the staged water depths of a rice year - a few centimeters until rooting, less after, the field drained in midsummer until it cracks and takes a footprint, intermittent watering after, deep water only against cold - which is the calendar our paddies are drawn to. Its limits are that it is present-day extension advice for modern cultivars in a machine-transplanted, herbicide-using system, its figures averaged from twenty-first-century demonstration plots; and that the mid-season drying in particular is documented here as modern practice - it appears in Edo-period farming texts only in places with water enough to re-flood, and spread generally in the twentieth century - so a drained, cracked field in midsummer on our maps is a guess resting on a modern document until a period source carries it.

Used for: the STAGED water depth, verbatim - 田植後活着するまでは水深3~4cmのやや深水 (3-4 cm until rooting), 活着後は水深2~3cmのやや浅水 (2-3 cm after rooting, to warm the water and promote tillering), the 中干し mid-season drain to 田面に小さなヒビが入り、軽く足跡がつく程度 (until small cracks appear and a footprint shows), and the 10 cm / 20 cm deep-water states as an explicit COLD CONTINGENCY - 気温が下がる恐れがある場合は - not a maintained depth. Modern extension guidance, labeled as such wherever cited
