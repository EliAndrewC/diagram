ja.wikipedia ソバ ("Buckwheat", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%82%BD%E3%83%90)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on buckwheat, the plant and the crop: its height, flowers and stems, and its summer and autumn sowings in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: The plant's size and colors are botany, the same in any century, and those are all it is used for. A tertiary encyclopedia article; its cultivation figures describe modern Japanese varieties and farming, and it says nothing of how a pre-modern field was sown.

Used for: buckwheat's height, the color of its flowers and stems, and its short season (fields)
