ja.wikipedia 小区画水田 ("small-plot paddy fields", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%B0%8F%E5%8C%BA%E7%94%BB%E6%B0%B4%E7%94%B0)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tiny paddy plots of the Yayoi and Kofun periods known from excavation: large bunds a meter or more wide enclosing blocks divided by small bunds into plots of a few square meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one dimension of a hand-built dividing bund read from the ground itself - half a meter or less, with no core, rebuilt each season - which bounds the ridge between two parcels. It describes paddies of two thousand years ago, not of the setting's period, and the rebuilding each season is the article's "thought to have been"; it is an encyclopedia summary of archaeological reports.

Used for: the small dividing bund of the earliest paddies, half a meter wide or less (archetypes)
