ja.wikipedia 納戸 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/納戸)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the nando, the storeroom of a Japanese house that also served as a sleeping room; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the room's use for sleeping, which is where this page puts a small household's servants; it does not say who slept there; the sleeping use it attests is the nurigome's, the closed room of the aristocratic house the nando descends from, not a farmhouse's or a samurai household's nando.

Used for: the nando as a sleeping room (government)
