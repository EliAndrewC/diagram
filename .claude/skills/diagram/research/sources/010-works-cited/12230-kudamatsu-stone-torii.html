kudamatsu-stone-torii

Kudamatsu City, 下松市史異説 (Kudamatsu city history, variant accounts), section 「Stone torii」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「石鳥居」), on the ADEAC local-history archive (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/kudamatsu-city/texthtml/d100060/mp100060-100060/ht000720)

What it is: A chapter of Kudamatsu's (Yamaguchi prefecture) municipal history on the city's stone monuments, listing its stone torii by their inscribed dates and transcribing the inscriptions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It counts 64 stone arches in the city, lists those dated up to the Kansei era year by year from 1679, and shows the earliest to have been built by the shrine's parishioners - each a single gate at an ordinary shrine. It is one city's inventory, it lists the arches rather than their spacing, and it describes no row.

Used for: dated single stone arches, one built by a parish (religion-and-death.html).
