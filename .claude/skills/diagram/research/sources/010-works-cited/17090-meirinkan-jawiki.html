ja.wikipedia 明倫館 ("Meirinkan", the Choshu domain school, title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%98%8E%E5%80%AB%E9%A4%A8)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Meirinkan, the domain school of Choshu (Hagi), founded in 1718 in the castle's third bailey and moved in 1849 to a far larger ground in the castle town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one domain school's ground twice - 940 tsubo at its founding, laid out around a Confucian hall with the martial halls as its outer ring, and 15,184 tsubo with a drill ground after the move - so it shows both the small and the large end of the band and both the in-castle and the in-town siting. It is one large domain, the figures are unsourced in the article, and it says nothing of how typical either ground was.

Used for: a domain school's ground, its Confucian hall and its siting (cities/capitals)
