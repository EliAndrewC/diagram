Wikipedia contributors, "Wind gradient" (English Wikipedia; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_gradient)

What it is: An English Wikipedia article on how wind speed changes with height above the ground, and on how the roughness of the surface below sets how much the wind is slowed near it.

Why it applies, and its limits: The physics is the same in any age and country: a smooth surface such as open water drags on the wind less than land does, so the wind near it stays stronger. Its figures are for the sea, large open water and ice, not for a sheet of small ponds divided by planted dikes, so it supports the direction of the effect over the ponds, not how strong the wind is at any one dike.

Used for: that wind crossing open water is slowed less than wind crossing land (archetypes)
