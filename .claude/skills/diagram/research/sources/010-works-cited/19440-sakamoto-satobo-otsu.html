sakamoto-satobo-otsu

Ōtsu City, 大津市坂本伝統的建造物群保存地区 (the city's signboard text for the Sakamoto preservation district, updated 2024) (in Japanese; https://www.city.otsu.lg.jp/bz/a/03/15/60746.html)

What it is: The city's cultural properties office's description of Sakamoto, the town at the foot of Mount Hiei, where in the Edo period monks who had grown old in training on the mountain were granted retirement residences by the head of the Tendai school.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates to the Edo period an old monk's own residence standing apart from the monastery, in the town below it. Its limits: signboard text for visitors; it concerns celibate monks of one great monastery, not families, and gives no count or size.

Used for: the retired monk's residence apart from its temple (religion-and-death.html).
