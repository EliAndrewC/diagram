en.wikipedia "Water gate" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_gate)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the water gate, the fortified gate opening from a wall onto water, with surviving examples; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the form with premodern examples on a tidal river, which is the river-face gate this page allows; European examples; a stub with no East Asian example, giving the generic form only, while the setting's own form rests on the Suzhou Pan Gate pair (panmen-zhwiki, suzhou-panmen-gov).

Used for: a water gate on the river face (river cities)
