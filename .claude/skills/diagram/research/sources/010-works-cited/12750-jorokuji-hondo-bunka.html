jorokuji-hondo-bunka

Agency for Cultural Affairs, national designated cultural properties database, "丈六寺本堂（元方丈）" ("Jorokuji main hall, formerly the abbot's quarters", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/heritage/detail/102/3244)

What it is: The national register's entry for the main hall of Jorokuji in Tokushima, built in 1629, with its measured length and depth, its roof and its tiling.

Why it applies, and its limits: A dated, measured early-Edo main hall of a provincial temple is a size anchor for the largest building a temple precinct held. Jorokuji was a leading temple of its province, and the hall was built as the abbot's quarters, so it stands for a substantial temple rather than a small town one.

Used for: how big a temple's main hall was (religion-and-death)
