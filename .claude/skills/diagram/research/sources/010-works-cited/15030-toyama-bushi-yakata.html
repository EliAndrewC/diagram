toyama-bushi-yakata

Toyama Prefecture, 武士の館 ("the warrior's residence", title translated from the Japanese by this project), in the prefecture's pages on its archaeological sites (in Japanese; https://www.pref.toyama.jp/3009/miryokukankou/bunka/bunkazai/mb002-1/mb002-34.html)

What it is: A short page by Toyama Prefecture on medieval warriors' residences excavated in the prefecture, giving the measured moat of one fourteenth-century residence and the size of a fortified enclosure.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured sizes for a rural warrior's moated residence from excavation, the physical anchor for a samurai house standing among its own fields. It covers a few sites in one prefecture, medieval rather than Edo-period, and says little of what stood inside; the second size is hedged as an estimate. Updated 2021.

Used for: the measured moat of a medieval country warrior's residence (buildings)
