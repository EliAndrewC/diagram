miyazu-teramachi

Miyazu City, 宮津城下町を歩く (10) 寺町の成り立ち ("Walking the Miyazu castle town, 10: how the temple quarter came to be") (in Japanese; https://www.city.miyazu.kyoto.jp/site/citypro/5091.html)

What it is: A Miyazu city office page on the temple quarter of the former castle town of Miyazu, Kyoto prefecture, giving its twelve temples by sect, their place in the town and the view that the quarter was planned with the castle town.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a small castle town's temple count and where they stood. Its limits: a castle town, not a county seat; a short page for visitors, which hedges the planning ("it is thought"); no population is given.

Used for: a small castle town's temple count and its temple quarter (religion-and-death.html).
