shuyuan-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 书院 ("academy", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/书院)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the shuyuan, the academies of China and its neighbors, and how they stood apart from the official schools.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines what an academy was against a prefectural or county school. It is a general summary with no sizes or counts of academies per city.
