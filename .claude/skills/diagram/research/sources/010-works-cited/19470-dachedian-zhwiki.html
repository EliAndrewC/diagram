zh.wikipedia 大车店 ("cart inn", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%A4%A7%E8%BD%A6%E5%BA%97)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the cart inn (dachedian) of Northeast China: when it arose, why, its sleeping platforms, and its passing with the motor road.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the cart inn to the end of the Qing, which places the form before 1912, and says its rooms held long heated platforms sleeping about twenty. It is a short tertiary article resting on a 2001 popular history and two provincial newspapers; it admits the name's first use cannot be dated and puts it only roughly in the late Qing and early Republic; and it speaks of one frontier region only.

Used for: the date of the Northeast's cart inn and its heated sleeping platforms (buildings)
