zh.wikipedia 大车店 ("cart inn", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%A4%A7%E8%BD%A6%E5%BA%97)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the cart inn (dachedian) of Northeast China: when it arose, why, its sleeping platforms, and its passing with the motor road.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the cart inn to the end of the Qing, which places the form before 1912, and says its rooms held long heated platforms sleeping about twenty. It is a short tertiary article resting on a 2001 popular history, two provincial newspaper pieces and one untitled reference; it admits the name's first use cannot be dated and puts it only roughly in the late Qing and early Republic; it speaks of one frontier region only; and it gives the form an industrial-era cause - the late-Qing opening of the Changbai mines and animal carts fitted with rubber tires borrowed from the motor car - so the dense string of inns about forty li apart that it describes belongs to the 1900s, not to a premodern road. Its figure of about twenty sleepers to a platform carries no footnote.

Used for: the date of the Northeast's cart inn and its heated sleeping platforms (buildings)
