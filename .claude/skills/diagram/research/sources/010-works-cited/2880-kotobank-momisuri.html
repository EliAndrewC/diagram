kotobank 籾摺り (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%B1%BE%E6%91%BA%E3%82%8A)

What it is: The Kotobank page reprinting the Nipponica encyclopedia's entry on hulling, the removal of the husk from paddy rice, with the hulling ratio by weight and by volume.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the hulling yield, 80% by weight, the conversion between paddy and brown rice this page's yard sizing runs through. A tertiary summary of a published reference work.

Used for: the hulling ratio of paddy to brown rice
