Wikipedia (English), "Water Margin" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_Margin)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Water Margin, the Chinese novel of the outlaws of Mount Liang, giving its setting, its authorship and the history of its editions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a modern encyclopedia summary, used only to date the novel: it calls it a novel of the Ming dynasty, set in the Northern Song, and names a printed revision of the reign of Emperor Shizong (1522-1566) as the ancestor of the most widely read versions. It leaves the date of the first writing open between the late Yuan and the mid-Ming, so it dates the novel only to the Ming.

Used for: Water Margin a Ming novel set in the Northern Song (towns)
