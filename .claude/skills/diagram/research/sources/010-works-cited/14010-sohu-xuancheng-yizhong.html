sohu-xuancheng-yizhong

"宣城'漏泽园'历史概述" ("An outline history of Xuancheng's louzeyuan", title translated from the Chinese by this project), a local-history article reposted on Sohu (https://www.sohu.com/a/375277815_99949262)

What it is: A local-history article on the pauper and charity burial grounds of Xuancheng county in Anhui, from the Song louzeyuan to the Qing charity graves, quoting the county gazetteer's list of them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the gazetteer's sizes for a county's charity graves - several separate grounds of one to three mu each, and a later purchase of twenty-two mu - the one set of areas read for a county's pauper grounds. The list is Qing, not Song, it is one county, the article elides the gazetteer around the figures it quotes, and it is a repost by an unnamed local writer.

Used for: the number and size of a county's charity burial grounds (religion and death)
