ja.wikipedia 桜田門 ("the Sakurada gate", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%A1%9C%E7%94%B0%E9%96%80)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Sakurada gate of Edo castle, a surviving masugata gate of about 1663 made of a koraimon and a yaguramon, with the size of its court and the history of its name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one measured masugata court and records the gate's early name as an earthen causeway. It is a tertiary summary of the shogun's own castle. Its 27 by 38 m carries no footnote on the page; the footnoted figure is the court's 320 tsubo (about 1,060 square metres), which agrees with it. The page calls this court unusually large, built so to guard the western enclosure (Nishinomaru), and the widest of Edo castle's surviving masugata, so its size is a ceiling, not a typical gate.

Used for: a masugata court about 27 by 38 m; the gate's early name as the Sakurada causeway (defenses)
