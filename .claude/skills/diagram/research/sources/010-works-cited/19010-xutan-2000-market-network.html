xutan-2000-market-network

Xu Tan (许檀), 明清时期城乡市场网络体系的形成及其历史意义 ("The formation of the urban-rural market network in the Ming and Qing and its historical significance", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 中国社会科学 (Social Sciences in China) 2000 no. 3, reposted in full on Aisixiang (in Chinese; https://www.aisixiang.com/data/21367.html)

What it is: An economic historian's survey of how China's trading cities, regional centers and rural periodic markets formed one network between the Ming and the late Qing, with national estimates of the number of rural markets and of their density and reach in the mid-Qing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates its market figures to the mid-Qing, the eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries, so it places the rural market's catchment before modernity rather than in twentieth-century fieldwork. Its figures are province-wide averages synthesized from other scholars' provincial studies and from the author's own 1997 paper, not a measurement of any one market, and the page gives the ranges without the tables behind them.

Used for: the mid-Qing rural market's area and walking radius, on the plains and in the mountains (towns)
