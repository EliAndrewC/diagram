en.wikipedia "Standard step method" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_step_method)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the standard step method for computing a channel's water-surface profile, with a worked example of the backwater above a sluice gate returning to normal depth upstream.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that an impoundment lifts the surface upstream only for a finite reach before the channel returns to its own depth, which is why raising a moat cannot lift its whole feeder. A modern hydraulics text on a canal gate, standing in for the physics.

Used for: the finite backwater above a gate
