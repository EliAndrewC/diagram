bunka-kuchiba

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 「The Kuchiba house (Horiuchi, Hagi, Yamaguchi prefecture): main house」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「口羽家住宅(山口県萩市堀内) 主屋」) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/146928)

What it is: The national cultural heritage database's entry on the main house of the Kuchiba house at Hagi: its period, its dimensions and wing, its roof, and a three-sentence description of the household and why the house matters.

Why it applies, and its limits: The national register's own measure of a surviving house of a retainer of about 1,000 koku from before 1868. It gives the period only as "late Edo / 1801-1900", no plan and no rooms, and its dimensions (12.9 by 10.0 m with an east wing of 2.0 by 3.9 m) are much smaller than the prefecture's for the same house, without saying why; which of the two measures the whole house is not known.

Used for: the Kuchiba house's rank and its size by the national register's measure (buildings)
