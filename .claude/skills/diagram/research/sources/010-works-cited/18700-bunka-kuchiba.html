bunka-kuchiba

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 「The Kuchiba house (Horiuchi, Hagi, Yamaguchi prefecture): main house」 (translated; original: 「口羽家住宅(山口県萩市堀内) 主屋」) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/146928)

What it is: The national cultural heritage database's entry on the main house of the Kuchiba house at Hagi: its period, its dimensions and wing, its roof, and a three-sentence description of the household and why the house matters.

Why it applies, and its limits: The national register's own measure of a surviving house of a retainer of about 1,000 koku from before 1868. It gives the period only as "late Edo / 1801-1900", no plan and no rooms, and its dimensions (12.9 by 10.0 m with an east wing of 2.0 by 3.9 m) are much smaller than the prefecture's for the same house. The entry lists pent-roofed aisles (hisashi) on the south, east and north sides without measuring them, so its figures are likely of the main body alone, which may account for part of the gap; it does not say so, and which of the two measures the whole house is not known. It is the house of a senior retainer in the castle town's quarter of high-ranking samurai, and the entry calls it one of relatively few surviving samurai houses, so it stands for that rank and not for an ordinary samurai's or a villager's house.

Used for: the Kuchiba house's rank and its size by the national register's measure (buildings)
