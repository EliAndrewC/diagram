宋史 (History of Song), 卷一七八, the treatise on food and money (食貨志), Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷178)

What it is: The Yuan-compiled official history of the Song; this chapter of its economic treatise records the state's relief institutions, the poorhouses, relief clinics and public pauper cemeteries set up under Cai Jing from 1102 to 1104.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the primary record of where the Song ordered its pauper cemeteries placed - in each county, and in any lesser town of a thousand households with an official - which is the one-to-a-seat rule this page draws. It records the order, not how fully it was carried out, and it is read from a crowd-transcribed wiki copy.

Used for: the louzeyuan set up in 1104 and ordered for every county and every sizable town
