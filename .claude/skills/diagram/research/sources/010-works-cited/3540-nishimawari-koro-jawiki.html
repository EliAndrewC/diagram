ja.wikipedia 西廻り航路 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/西廻り航路)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the western sea route, the Edo-period shipping route round Japan and Kawamura Zuiken's reform of the tax-rice shipping on it; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that the tax rice moved on commoner hulls the shogunate contracted and then hired directly, under officials, pennants and inspection rather than ownership, which is the documentary control this page describes; the actor is the bakufu's route administration, not a county office.

Used for: hired hulls for the tax rice (buildings)
