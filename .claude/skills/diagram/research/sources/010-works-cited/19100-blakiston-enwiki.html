Wikipedia, "Thomas Blakiston" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Blakiston)

What it is: The English Wikipedia's biography of Thomas Blakiston, a British army officer, explorer and naturalist of the nineteenth century who traveled up the Yangtze and later lived in Hokkaido.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the year of his Yangtze journey, 1861, which dates the observation of shod cattle in north China that the horseshoe page quotes from him. It is a stub that Wikipedia marks as lacking reliable references and that rests mainly on the 1901 Dictionary of National Biography, and it says nothing of shoeing itself. It dates only his journey up the Yangtze, which went through central and western China, not the north. That the shod cattle were seen in north China in 1861 comes from the quoted passage, not from this page, which can only place the report in the mid-nineteenth-century Qing.

Used for: the date of a Qing-period report of shod horses and cattle in north China (urban-features)
