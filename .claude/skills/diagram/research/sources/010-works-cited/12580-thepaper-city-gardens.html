The Paper (澎湃新闻), 私家历史 column, 城市里的田园：都市为何离不开农村 ("Fields in the city: why cities could not do without the countryside", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (in Chinese; https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1477443)

What it is: A popular-history essay on the farmland and vegetable gardens inside and around Chinese cities, quoting the sixth-century farm manual Qimin yaoshu, the twelfth-century memoir of Kaifeng, and nineteenth- and twentieth-century observers of Hankou, Beijing and Shanghai.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries, in their original words, two old passages on the vegetable ground round a Chinese city: the manual's advice to grow melons and vegetables on land near a city wall, and the memoir's Kaifeng ringed with gardens. It is a newspaper essay, not a study; most of it concerns farmland inside the walls and in the twentieth century, and the two old passages are quoted from it rather than read in their own editions.

Used for: the manual's advice to grow vegetables near a city wall and sell the surplus, and the gardens round Kaifeng at the end of the Northern Song (towns)
