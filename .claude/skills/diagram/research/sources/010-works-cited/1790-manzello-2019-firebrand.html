manzello-2019-firebrand

Hedayati, Bahrani, Zhou, Quarles and Gorham, "A Framework to Facilitate Firebrand Characterization", Frontiers in Mechanical Engineering 5:43, 2019 (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/mechanical-engineering/articles/10.3389/fmech.2019.00043/full)

What it is: A peer-reviewed open-access review in Frontiers in Mechanical Engineering of how firebrands are generated, carried and start new fires, with the distance beyond which only a burning brand can do so.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the physics behind this page's judgment that a cremation ground's fire risk ends at its wall: the sixty-meter figure from the fire-science literature. Its limit is that it is a wildland and structure fire review, not a study of pyres. The 60 m figure is the paper's introductory framing of the wildland-fire literature rather than a result it measured, and the paper says nothing of pyres or of small contained fires.

Used for: the 60 m beyond which only burning firebrands start new fires
