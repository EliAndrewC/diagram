What it is: A popular history article carried on the Sina news portal describing the Neixiang county yamen in Henan, the most complete surviving county government compound, room by room: the six boards' offices as side halls east and west of the courtyard before the great hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states where the six offices stood in a county yamen, which is why our county-scale seat draws them as rooms on the court rather than as buildings. Its limit is that it is a news portal's feature about one surviving yamen. The article has no named author and no citations, and the standing buildings are a late-Qing rebuild.
