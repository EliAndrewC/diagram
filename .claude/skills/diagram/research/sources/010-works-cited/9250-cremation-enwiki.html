en.wikipedia "Cremation" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cremation)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on cremation: its history, the modern process, what remains afterward and why families choose it; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: The weight of what a cremation leaves is a physical fact that holds for any period or place, which is what the record uses it for. Its figures come from modern crematories and are of remains ground by machine, though it notes that in most Asian countries the bones are kept unground; it gives weight and never volume, and its sentence on cemetery space carries no reference of its own.

Used for: how little a cremation leaves to bury - about 2.4 kg for an adult, roughly 3.5% of body mass - and its economy of cemetery space (religion and death)
