ja.wikipedia 草津宿 ("Kusatsu-juku", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8D%89%E6%B4%A5%E5%AE%BF)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Kusatsu, the post station where the Tokaido and the Nakasendo met, with its 1843 counts of houses, lodgings and inhabitants and a list of its facilities.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets one station's inns against its houses and population, which shows how much of a post town's street was inn. It is a tertiary summary; Kusatsu, a junction of two highways, is busier than most stations, and the page itself gives a higher inn count at another time.

Used for: Kusatsu's 586 houses, 2,351 inhabitants and 72 inns in 1843, one house in five or six (towns)
