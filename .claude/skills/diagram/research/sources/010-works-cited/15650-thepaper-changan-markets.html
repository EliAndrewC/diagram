The Paper (Pengpai), 趣历史 | 长安城的东市与西市 ("Chang'an's East Market and West Market", title translated from the Chinese by this project), 2019, from the Xi'an local gazetteer office (in Chinese; https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_3641516)

What it is: A popular history article from the Xi'an city gazetteer office, published on The Paper's government-account channel, on the markets of Han and Tang Chang'an.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the walled Chinese market as a built thing: each Han market a square with its own walls and gates, a market tower at the center from which officials oversaw it, and storehouses behind the shops against the market wall; the Tang capital's two markets each took two wards. It describes the capital only, and it is a popular article without notes; it says nothing of a county town's market. Its Han market (each a square of 266 bu, with a five-story tower) follows later literary texts rather than excavation, and its own figures disagree: it gives the Han East and West markets unequal areas, and the Tang East Market an east-west width of 24 meters beside an area of 0.92 square kilometres. It is used for the shape of a market, not for any of its measurements.

Used for: what a walled Chinese market looked like, in the capital (towns)
