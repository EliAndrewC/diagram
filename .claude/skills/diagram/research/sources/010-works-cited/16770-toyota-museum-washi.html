toyota-museum-washi

豊田市博物館 (Toyota City Museum), 農閑期の副業 和紙づくり ("Papermaking, a side job of the farming off-season", title translated from the Japanese by this project), exhibit guide (in Japanese; https://hakubutsukan.city.toyota.aichi.jp/guide/labo/scene/7)

What it is: An exhibit note of the city museum of Toyota (Aichi) on the papermaking of its hill district, named among the area's famous products in a gazetteer of 1836 and carried on at small scale from the Edo period into modern times.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says papermaking there was a side job of the farming off-season, which puts the paper maker in a village, not a town. It is one district's short museum note, and it gives nothing of the premises.

Used for: papermaking as a farm household's off-season side job (urban-features)
