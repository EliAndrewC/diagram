kotobank-kido

Kotobank, 木戸 - 改訂新版 世界大百科事典 (Heibonsha) and 山川 日本史小辞典 entries (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9C%A8%E6%88%B8-51154)

What it is: The kotobank reference page for the kido, the wooden gate that closed an Edo street block at night, carrying side by side the entries of five Japanese reference works, of which two are quoted here: the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, revised edition, and the Yamakawa Concise Dictionary of Japanese History.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the block gates were for and who paid for them, from standard reference works rather than a hobby page, and it gives the machi-kido's hours. Its limits are those of an encyclopedia: it summarizes without citing the ordinances it summarizes, and its hours for the town gate differ from another page this record cites, which the note at that sentence discloses.

Used for: the block gate's role in public order, its upkeep from the block's own levy, and its hours
