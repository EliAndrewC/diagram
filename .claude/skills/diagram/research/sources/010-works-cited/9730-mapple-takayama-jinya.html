Mapple Web, 高山陣屋の見どころナビ (A guide to the sights of the Takayama Jin'ya), Mapple Magazine editorial staff, updated 2024-04-19 (in Japanese; https://www.mapple.net/article/48327/)

What it is: A travel guide's article on the Takayama Jin'ya by the editors of the Mapple guidebooks, walking through the office, the residence where the district intendant lived, its living room, kitchen and doma, and the storehouse.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in so many words that the district intendant lived with the family in the residence inside the compound, which is the arrangement our postings follow. Its limits are that it is a tourist guide without references, written about the buildings as they stand today, and that Takayama was an unusually large office; its remark that the residence was three stories high is a peculiarity of this one building.

Used for: the district intendant living with the family in the residence inside the compound (buildings)
