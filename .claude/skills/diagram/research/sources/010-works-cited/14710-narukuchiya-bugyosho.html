のんびり氣まま放浪記 (a personal travel blog), 江戸散歩 南町奉行所〜北町奉行所〜八丁堀与力・同心組屋敷跡 ("An Edo walk: the South and North magistracies and the yoriki and doshin residences at Hatchobori", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://narukuchiya.com/edo3po-bugyo/)

What it is: A personal blog post (2022, author unnamed) about a walk to the sites of Edo's two town magistracies and the constables' residence ground at Hatchobori. It gives each magistracy's area in tsubo without saying where the figures come from; it quotes no site marker and cites no book.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found giving both magistracies' areas in tsubo. It is a blog that names no source for its figures, so the areas are used as reported approximations, and the record says so where it uses them. The offices it measures served the shogun's capital, a city of about a million, so their areas are an upper bound for a governor's or magistrate's compound, not a typical figure for a provincial town or city.

Used for: the areas of Edo's two town magistracies (cities/government)
