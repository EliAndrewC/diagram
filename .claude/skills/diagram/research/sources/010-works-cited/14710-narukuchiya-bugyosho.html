narukuchiya-bugyosho

のんびり氣まま放浪記 (a personal travel blog), 江戸散歩 南町奉行所〜北町奉行所〜八丁堀与力・同心組屋敷跡 ("An Edo walk: the South and North magistracies and the yoriki and doshin residences at Hatchobori", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://narukuchiya.com/edo3po-bugyo/)

What it is: A personal blog's walk to the sites of Edo's two town magistracies and the constables' residence ground, repeating the figures on the site markers and in popular histories.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable page found giving both magistracies' areas in tsubo. It is a blog that names no source for its figures, so the areas are used as reported approximations, and the record says so where it uses them.

Used for: the areas of Edo's two town magistracies (cities/government)
