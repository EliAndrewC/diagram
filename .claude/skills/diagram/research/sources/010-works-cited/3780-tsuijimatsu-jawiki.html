tsuijimatsu-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 築地松 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/築地松)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tsuijimatsu, the clipped black-pine screen round the farmsteads of the Izumo plain; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the screen enclosing the whole house before Meiji and the north and west after, and its level clipping, which is the tight homestead planting this page draws; one region.

Used for: the Izumo pine screen round the house (vegetation)
