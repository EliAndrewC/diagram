en.wikipedia "Demographic transition" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographic_transition)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the demographic transition, the model of mortality and fertility before and after industrialization; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its band for pre-transition death rates brackets the rate this page's sizing arithmetic uses; its worked examples are colonial North America, and it names no figure for premodern China or Japan.

Used for: the crude death rate behind the burial-ground sizing (religion and death)
