ja.wikipedia 牓示 ("boji, boundary marker", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%89%93%E7%A4%BA)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the boji: first a posted notice of the ancient court, then the marker set on the boundary of a temple's or shrine's precinct or of a medieval estate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what boundary markers were made of - posts of wood or stone, sometimes a rock or a tree. Its scope is ancient and medieval precincts and estates, not the Edo village, so it shows the practice's age and materials rather than how an Edo village's line was marked.

Used for: the older boundary markers of estates and precincts (ways)
