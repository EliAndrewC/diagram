What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on tile roofing in Japan, its forms and its use on houses, with the ease of replacing a single broken tile.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a kawara roof is repaired tile by tile, which is why this page counts a tiled roof as a lasting one. A tertiary summary; it gives no lifespan. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks citations and may hold original research.
