小林高英・苦瀬博仁・橋本一明, 江戸期の河川舟運における川舟の運航方法と河岸の立地に関する研究, 日本物流学会誌 11, May 2003 (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/logisticssociety1995/2003/11/2003_11_121/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A peer-reviewed paper in the journal of the Japan Logistics Society on Edo-period river transport: how the river boats were worked, why bulk goods such as tribute rice went by water, and where the landings that served them stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states, from the logistics literature, that bulk went by river because boats carried more than the roads could, the Japanese half of the reason our river capital draws a wharf and a towpath. Its limit is that it is about how the boats and landings worked, not about whether roads followed rivers.

Used for: river boat transport carrying tribute rice in bulk to Edo
