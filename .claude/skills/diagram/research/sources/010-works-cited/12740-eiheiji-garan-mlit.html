Japan Tourism Agency, multilingual explanatory text database, "Eiheiji Temple: shichido garan" (in English; https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/en/R2-01260.html)

What it is: A government-commissioned English explanation of the seven-hall layout of Eiheiji, the head temple of the Soto Zen sect, naming its seven buildings and how they are joined.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the Zen seven halls in English and says the conventions vary by school and era. Its covered corridors are said of Eiheiji, a great mountain head temple, not of Zen temples in general, still less of a town temple.

Used for: the Zen seven-hall plan, and the covered corridors of a head temple (religion-and-death)
