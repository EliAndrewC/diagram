What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Beijing's bell and drum towers, the paired Yuan-founded towers at the north end of the capital's axis, and the routine by which they struck the night watches.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the capital's one pair of towers timing the watches for the whole inner city, the China-first scaling answer this page sets against Edo's many bells. A tertiary summary; it describes the imperial capital, a ceiling rather than a norm.

Used for: Beijing's towers striking the watches for the whole city
