zh.wikipedia 名宦祠 (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/名宦祠)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the mingguanci (名宦祠), the shrine in which a locality offered sacrifice to former officials of the place who had governed it well.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for what the building is and where it belongs - a state institution from the early Ming onward, standing in the Confucian temple precinct east of the Dacheng gate rather than inside the magistrate's own compound - which is how we place and label it on a town plan. It is a tertiary summary whose statements we take only where its own references support them, and it describes the Chinese institution, which our setting adapts rather than copies.

Used for: 「The mingguanci (shrine of eminent officials) serves to offer sacrifice to local officials who had achievements in government.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「名宦祠用以祭祀有政績的地方官」) - the hall venerating local officials of merit, standing at the Confucian temple (east of the Dacheng gate), Ming Hongwu onward - a state institution, not a yamen's own predecessor shrine
