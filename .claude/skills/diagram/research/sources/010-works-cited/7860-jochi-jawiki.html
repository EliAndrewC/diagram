jochi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 除地 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%99%A4%E5%9C%B0) - 「Land of special provenance, such as the precincts of temples and shrines and fields and homesteads holding a document of tax exemption」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺社の境内や、無年貢証文のある田畑・屋敷など特別な由緒のある土地で」)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on jochi, the Edo category of tax-exempt land outside the vermilion- and black-seal grants.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the fact that temple and shrine precincts were exempt land - the tax-free plot the campaign notes give the country monk. Tertiary; it defines the category and does not say how much such land an ordinary village temple held.

Used for: temple and shrine precincts as exempt land (religion-and-death.html).
