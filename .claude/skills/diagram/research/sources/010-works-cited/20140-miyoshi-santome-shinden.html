miyoshi-santome-shinden

Miyoshi Town, Saitama, 埼玉県指定旧跡 三富新田 ("Santome Shinden, a Saitama prefectural historic site", title translated from the Japanese by this project), on the town's history pages (in Japanese; https://www.town.saitama-miyoshi.lg.jp/kanko/rekishi/santomeshinden.html)

What it is: The town government's page on Santome Shinden, part of which lies in Miyoshi: the survey of 1696 and the homesteads it counted in each of the three villages, and what was planted round the homestead and on the fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the homesteads per village at the 1696 survey and the trees planted round a homestead - bamboo, oak and zelkova - and why. It is a short unsigned page for visitors, with no source named, and it speaks of one planned dry-field colony.

Used for: the homesteads counted in each Santome village in 1696, and the grove planted round the homestead (homesteads)
