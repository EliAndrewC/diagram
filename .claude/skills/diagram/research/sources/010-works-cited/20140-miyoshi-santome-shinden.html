miyoshi-santome-shinden

Miyoshi Town, Saitama, 埼玉県指定旧跡 三富新田 ("Santome Shinden, a Saitama prefectural historic site", title translated), on the town's history pages (in Japanese; https://www.town.saitama-miyoshi.lg.jp/kanko/rekishi/santomeshinden.html)

What it is: The town government's page on Santome Shinden, part of which lies in Miyoshi: the survey of 1696 and the homesteads it counted in each of the three villages, and what was planted round the homestead and on the fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the homestead lots per village at the 1696 survey - Kamitome 91, Nakatome 40, Shimotome 49 - and some of the trees planted round a homestead, "bamboo, kashi, zelkova and the like" (kashi is an evergreen oak), so the list is not the whole grove. The count is of lots laid out and surveyed two years after clearing began, not of households shown to be living on them, and the reasons it gives for each tree are the town's present-day telling, not a period record. It is a short unsigned page for visitors, with no source named, and it speaks of one dry-field colony laid out by a domain to a single plan: a pattern for a planned row village, not for the farm count or grove of an old village that grew.

Used for: the homesteads counted in each Santome village in 1696, and the grove planted round the homestead (homesteads)
