Tokyo Museum Collection (ToMuCo), "炭俵" (charcoal bale), Edo-Tokyo Museum accession 90007940 (in Japanese; https://museumcollection.tokyo/works/6531424/)

What it is: The online catalog entry for a charcoal bale in the Edo-Tokyo Museum's collection, dated to the mid-Showa period, with its dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a measured charcoal bale in a museum, the one found. It is a 20th-century bale, not an Edo-period one, made when charcoal was sold in modern graded bales (commonly 4 kan, about 15 kg). The entry gives no weight, material, place of origin or description, and it is a single object. So it shows the order of a charcoal bale's size, not a premodern standard or any one region's practice.

Used for: a charcoal bale of 63 by 39 by 32 cm, about 2 by 1.3 by 1 ft (urban features)
