ja.wikipedia 踏車 ("Treadle wheel", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%B8%8F%E8%BB%8A)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the treadle wheel, summarizing what Okura Nagatsune's farm-tool manual of 1822 says of its origin in Osaka, its spread, its sizes and its price.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dates (made in the 1660s, spread through the provinces by 1751-1780) and the one-person wheel's three sizes, 4.5 to 5.5 shaku across. The article is flagged for missing sources and one of its citations misnames the manual, so it carries the manual's figures at second hand.

Used for: the treadle wheel's date and sizes (water)
