ja.wikipedia 踏車 ("Treadle wheel", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%B8%8F%E8%BB%8A)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the treadle wheel, summarizing what Okura Nagatsune's farm-tool manual of 1822 says of its origin in Osaka, its spread, its sizes and its price.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dates (made in the 1660s, spread through the provinces by 1751-1780) and the one-person wheel's three sizes, 4.5, 5 and 5.5 shaku (the article says only "dimensions", not which one; reading them as the wheel's diameter is ours). It is a tertiary summary: the article has been flagged for missing sources since February 2015, its paragraph on sizes and prices has no footnote and calls the manual 農業便利論 instead of 農具便利論, so it carries the manual's 1822 Kansai figures at second hand. The manual itself is scanned publicly and is the source to read for a number.

Used for: the treadle wheel's date and sizes (water)
