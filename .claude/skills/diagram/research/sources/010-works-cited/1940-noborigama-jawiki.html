What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the noborigama, the multi-chamber climbing kiln of Japan: the firebox at the foot, the chambers stepping up the slope, the flue and chimney at the top.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the chambered tunnel climbing a slope with its firebox low and its chimney high, which is the kiln form these maps draw. A tertiary summary used for the form.

Used for: the climbing kiln's chambers, firebox and chimney
