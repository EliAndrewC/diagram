zhengding-chengqiang-zhwiki

Chinese Wikipedia, 正定城墙 ("Zhengding city wall", translated) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A3%E5%AE%9A%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the wall of Zhengding in Hebei, in the Ming the seat of Zhending prefecture, whose surviving wall is Ming work; it gives the moat's width and depth in zhang.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a prefectural seat's moat given in the old measure, over ten zhang wide, the size of moat the greater end of the band describes; but Zhengding was no common prefectural seat - a circuit of twenty-four li from 1449, triple gates the article calls rare in all the country, a city credited with guarding the capital - so its moat is a great seat's, not an ordinary one's. Its moat sentence carries no footnote and names no period or survey, so although the unit suggests an older record the figure cannot be dated; the article's one gazetteer footnote, to a county gazetteer published in 2008, is on its history section, not on the moat. The article also records the wall largely pulled down from 1958 and the moat (the Zhouhan River) put under a comprehensive rehabilitation from 2013, so the undated figure may describe the moat as it is now, not as the Ming or Qing dug it.

Used for: a prefectural seat's moat, given in zhang without a date (City defenses)
