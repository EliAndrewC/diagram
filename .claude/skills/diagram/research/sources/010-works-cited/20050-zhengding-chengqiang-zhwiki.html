zhengding-chengqiang-zhwiki

Chinese Wikipedia, 正定城墙 ("Zhengding city wall", translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A3%E5%AE%9A%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the wall of Zhengding in Hebei, in the Ming the seat of Zhending prefecture, whose surviving wall is Ming work; it gives the moat's width and depth in zhang.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a prefectural seat's moat given in the old measure, over ten zhang wide, the size of moat the greater end of the band describes. The moat sentence carries no footnote and names no period or survey, so although the unit suggests an older record the figure cannot be dated; the article's one gazetteer footnote, to a county gazetteer published in 2008, is on its history section, not on the moat.

Used for: a prefectural seat's moat, given in zhang without a date (cities/defenses)
