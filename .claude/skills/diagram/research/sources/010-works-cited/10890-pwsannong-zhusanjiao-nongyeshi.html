"Zhujiang sanjiaozhou nongye shi" (珠江三角洲农业史, the agricultural history of the Pearl River delta), an entry in the regional-agricultural-history section of the encyclopedia on Zhihui Sannong (智汇三农), a Chinese agricultural knowledge database published by China Agriculture Press (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/560776.shtml)

What it is: A Chinese encyclopedia entry tracing farming in the Pearl River delta from the Neolithic to the Qing, including the rise of the dike-pond system, the fry trade of Jiujiang and the specialized farming districts of the Ming and Qing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the dike-pond types - fruit dikes first in the mid-Ming, mulberry dikes replacing them from the late Ming and early Qing - and says which livestock belonged to which district, pigs to the dike-pond district and ducks to the sand fields. It is a modern secondary summary posted in 2016 that cites no sources of its own; some of its types (the cane, rice and fan-palm dikes) are named for the Ming-Qing period with no date of their own, and it gives no shares of households or of ponds.

Used for: the order in which the dike-pond types arose, the Qing loop that fed pigs from the ponds, Jiujiang's hold on the fry catch, and ducks as a sand-field trade (archetypes)
