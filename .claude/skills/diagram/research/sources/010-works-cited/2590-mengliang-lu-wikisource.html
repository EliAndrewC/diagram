吳自牧 (Wu Zimu), 夢粱錄 (Mengliang lu), 卷十九 瓦舍, Chinese Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%A4%A2%E7%B2%B1%E9%8C%84/%E5%8D%B719)

What it is: The memoir of Southern Song Hangzhou of about 1274, whose nineteenth chapter gives the city's pleasure precincts as no fewer than seventeen inside and outside the wall and their founding for the garrison's northern soldiers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the second primary count of Hangzhou's precincts, read beside the first so that the page carries the range the record gives rather than one figure. A primary text read from a crowd-transcribed wiki copy.

Used for: Hangzhou's seventeen or more wazi
