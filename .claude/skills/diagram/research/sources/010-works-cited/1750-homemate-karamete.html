homemate-karamete

ホームメイト・リサーチ 城・日本の城 (Homemate Research), 城郭用語辞典 搦手門（からめてもん） (https://www.homemate-research-castle.com/useful/glossary/castle/2125401/)

What it is: A castle-terms glossary entry on a Japanese commercial reference site for the karamete-mon, the rear gate of a castle: its pairing with the front gate and the siege tactic it served, drawing the enemy to the front while the garrison sortied from the rear.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the doctrine behind a castle's two gates, which is why our castle draws a front and a rear gate. Its limit is that it is a commercial hobby reference rather than a military-history source.

Used for: the sortie from the rear gate against attackers held at the front
