弘前市, 弘前城と石垣修理の歴史 ("Hirosaki City: Hirosaki Castle and the history of its stone-wall repairs", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://www.city.hirosaki.aomori.jp/ishigaki/rekishi.html)

What it is: Hirosaki city's own page on its castle, an Edo-period castle completed in 1611, giving its six baileys, its extent and its area, before an account of the stone-wall repairs.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the area as the castle's own, but its round 50 ha matches the present national historic site and park (about 49.2 ha by the park's own page), so it is a modern measure of the grounds that survive inside the outer moats, not a survey of the period; the castle town and the retainers' quarters outside them are not in it, and it is one castle of a northern domain of 100,000 koku, an example rather than a rule. It is a city page for general readers, with round figures and no plan, and it does not say whether the moats are counted.

Used for: Hirosaki Castle's six baileys and about 50 ha (cities/capitals)
