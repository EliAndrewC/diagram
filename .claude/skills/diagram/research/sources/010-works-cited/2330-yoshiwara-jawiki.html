What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Yoshiwara, Edo's licensed pleasure quarter, its founding, its 1656 removal to Nihon-zutsumi behind Senso-ji in Asakusa, and its later history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records where the quarter was moved to, which is one of the three places this page aligns to Edo's northeast. A tertiary summary used for the relocation; it says nothing of the kimon or of direction. The article carries the encyclopedia's own notice that it lacks sufficient sources. And the article gives its own reason for the move - the city had grown and daimyo residences had come up against the quarter - which competes with the northeast reading this page draws from the places.

Used for: the Yoshiwara's removal to Asakusa
