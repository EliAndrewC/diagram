What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the hinin, the Edo-period outcaste status below the eta: who fell into it and what its members did.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the status and lists its occupations, none of them a craft, which is what this page's separation of the potter from the outcaste rests on. A tertiary summary used for the definition. That potters were not of the status is drawn from the list's silence, not stated on the page.

Used for: the hinin status and its occupations
