zh.wikipedia 北京城门 ("the gates of Beijing", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/北京城门)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the gates of Ming and Qing Beijing, gate by gate: the towers, archery towers and barbicans of the inner and outer cities, many with their width and depth in bays and meters.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures both ends of a capital's range, the Yongding Gate's five-bay tower (24 by 10.5 m) and the three-bay tower of a lesser gate such as Zuo'an (16 by 9 m). It describes a capital, not a provincial seat, and several towers as rebuilt - Yongding's in 1766 and again in 2004 - so its figures are ceilings and a lower bound from one city. It is a tertiary summary, and the article cites no source for these dimensions; Zuo'an's tower was pulled down in the 1930s, and the article does not say when or from what its measurements were taken. The numbers rest on the encyclopedia alone until a survey such as Sirén's The Walls and Gates of Peking (1924) confirms them.

Used for: the capital's gate towers, great and lesser - the lesser 52 by 30 ft (defenses)
