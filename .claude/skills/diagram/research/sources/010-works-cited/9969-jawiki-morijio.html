jawiki-morijio

Japanese Wikipedia, "盛り塩" (morijio) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%9B%9B%E3%82%8A%E5%A1%A9)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on morijio, its form and the stories told of its origin.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the form - salt heaped into a cone or a three-sided pyramid and set at the entrance or inside the house. The article flags itself as without verifiable sources, and its claims of an origin in the Nara or Heian period rest on nothing, so it is used for the shape alone, which the other pages do not describe.

Used for: the shape of a morijio heap, a cone or a three-sided pyramid (religion-and-death).
