Takayama Jin'ya (Gifu Prefecture), 見どころ (Highlights), the official site (in Japanese; https://jinya.gifu.jp/highlight/; the site's English page is https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/highlight/)

What it is: The highlights page of the Takayama Jin'ya's own site, run for the prefecture, describing six of its features, among them its two courts and what each was for, and where its torture was done.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the managing body's own account of the one intendant's office whose main buildings survive, and it says the jin'ya had a court for villages' petitions as well as one for criminal cases, and that its jail stood in the town. Its limits are that it is a visitors' page without references, hedged on the torture ("thought to", "reportedly"), and that Takayama was a gundai's office, larger than an ordinary intendant's.

Used for: Takayama's two courts; its torture instruments for show and its torture done in its jail in the town (buildings)
