What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Zuihoden, the mausoleum of Date Masamune at Sendai on the wooded hill of Kyogamine across the Hirose river from the castle, with the mausolea of his son and grandson and the lesser Date tombs around it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one clear case of a ruling family's mortuary precinct standing on a wooded rise across the river from the castle, the form our capital's lineage precinct may take. A tertiary summary used for the site and its buildings; the Date were one of the greatest houses in the country, so the precinct is an upper case, and the buildings that stand are reconstructions after the fire of 1945, which leaves the siting untouched.

Used for: the Zuihoden across the Hirose meander from the honmaru, with the family's mausolea around it
