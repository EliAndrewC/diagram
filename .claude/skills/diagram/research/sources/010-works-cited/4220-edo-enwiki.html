en.wikipedia "Edo" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Edo, the shogunal capital that became Tokyo; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the city's million by 1721, the scale this page sets the great dojos in; it also states, in words and with no figure, that the townsmen's quarter was far more densely peopled than the samurai's, and that great lords' residences took dozens of hectares - Edo was the shogunal capital, so both ends of that contrast are larger than a provincial city's; a tertiary summary; the million is a historians' estimate, not a census; and when it was read the article carried two maintenance banners, one saying it lacks inline citations and one warning that it may include machine-generated text, so it is used for the direction of a contrast and never for a figure.

Used for: Edo's population (government); the density contrast between the townsmen's and the samurai's quarters (sizing)
