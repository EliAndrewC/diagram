nihonminkaen-kanagawa

Kawasaki Municipal Nihon Minka-en (open-air museum of Japanese folk houses), "Kanagawa village" (in Japanese; https://www.nihonminkaen.jp/kanagawa.html)

What it is: The museum's page on the houses of its Kanagawa section, giving for each its former site, its type (farmhouse or headman's house), its roof form and material, its length and depth in meters, and its date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured dimensions and roof forms - hipped, gabled and hip-and-gable - of seventeenth-century farmhouses of Sagami and Musashi, beside the Kanto page. The same survivor bias holds: most of these houses were headmen's, so they show the upper stratum of a village; and it is one region.

Used for: the measured length, depth and roof form of Kanagawa farmhouses (homesteads)
