zh.wikipedia 宏村村 (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/宏村村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Hongcun, a Huizhou village on the World Heritage list, describing its siting and the water channel its villagers dug through it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the rivers pass on one side of the village while a dug channel about a meter wide runs through it to every household - the same pairing the record already shows at Harie, now in a Chinese village. It is a tertiary summary; it does not date the channel in the passage read, and Hongcun is a wealthy lineage village rather than a small farming hamlet.

Used for: the rivers passing west of Hongcun; the channel nearly 1 m wide dug through the village to every household (water)
