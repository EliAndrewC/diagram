Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), "慈溪老人收藏600多杆大秤 最大的可称重175公斤" (A Cixi elder collects more than 600 large steelyards; the largest weighs up to 175 kg), from China Ningbo Net, 2021-05-08 (in Chinese; https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/nbnews/202105/t20210508_22496583.shtml)

What it is: A regional news story on a collector in Cixi, Zhejiang, and his collection of wooden steelyards, the oldest and largest of which he describes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured old steelyard built for heavy loads - over 200 years old, 2.2 m long, weighing up to 175 kg - the scale a depot weighing bales would need. It is a news feature relaying a private collector's account, so the age is his and unverified, and it is one object from one region, bought at Shaoxing; it says nothing of where or how such a steelyard hung.

Used for: an old Chinese steelyard for loads up to 175 kg (urban features)
