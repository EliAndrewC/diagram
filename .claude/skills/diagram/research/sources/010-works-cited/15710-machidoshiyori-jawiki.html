ja.wikipedia 町年寄 ("machi-doshiyori, the town elders", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/町年寄)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the town elders, the townspeople who headed the commoners' administration of an early-modern city under its town magistrate, chiefly Edo's three hereditary houses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where Edo's elders worked: their lots, granted by the shogunate, were the corner lots of Honcho 1 to 3 on Honcho street, and, as with samurai, the residence doubled as the office. It is Edo's case, the largest city, and says nothing of a smaller town's headman.

Used for: that a town's elders worked from their own houses on the main street (towns)
