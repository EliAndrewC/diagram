mizu-no-bunka-32-yanagawa

ミツカン 水の文化センター (Mizkan Water Culture Center), 堀の記憶が成し遂げた、柳川再生物語, 水の文化 32 (https://www.mizu.gr.jp/kikanshi/no32/07.html)

What it is: An article in the Mizkan Water Culture Center's periodical on the revival of Yanagawa's channels: the delta the town stands on, the tidal range of the Ariake sea beside it, the density of its waterways, and the tide barrier above which their water is drawn.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ground Yanagawa's canals stand on and the fact that their water is deliberately kept above the tide, which corrects the reading that such channels are tidal. Its limit is that it is a water-culture magazine report rather than a hydrological survey. The tide barrier and the non-tidal regime are described in the present tense and undated, and the canal lengths are modern measurements of a modernized system, so what is attested is today's managed regime; the delta and the tidal range are geography and transfer freely.

Used for: Yanagawa's delta ground, the 6 m tide beside it, and the channels drawn above the tide barrier
