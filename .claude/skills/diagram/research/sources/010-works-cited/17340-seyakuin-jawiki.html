ja.wikipedia 施薬院 ("seyakuin", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/施薬院)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the seyakuin, the dispensary of ancient Japan, set up at the Kofukuji temple in 723 and in the empress's household in 730, and its companion the hidenin, the almshouse.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that Japan's relief houses began at the capital, at a great temple and at court, which is the Japanese side of the relief-house knob. A tertiary summary of the ancient institution, some thousand years before the castle towns our maps draw on, and it says nothing of the houses' size.

Used for: the ancient dispensaries and almshouses at the capital (urban-features)
