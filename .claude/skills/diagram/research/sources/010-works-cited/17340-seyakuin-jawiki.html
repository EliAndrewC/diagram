ja.wikipedia 施薬院 ("seyakuin", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/施薬院)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the seyakuin, the dispensary of ancient Japan, first securely recorded in the empress's household in 730 (the Shoku Nihongi, a chronicle of the period), with an earlier founding at the Kofukuji temple in 723 known only from the Fuso Ryakki, a chronicle compiled in the late eleventh century; and its companion the hidenin, the almshouse. It is a short stub.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that Japan's relief houses began at the capital, at a great temple and at court, which is the Japanese side of the relief-house knob. A tertiary summary of the ancient institution, some thousand years before the castle towns our maps draw on, and it says nothing of the houses' size.

Used for: the ancient dispensaries and almshouses at the capital (urban-features)
