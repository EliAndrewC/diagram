gymnosperm-densiflora

The Gymnosperm Database, Pinus densiflora (https://www.conifers.org/pi/Pinus_densiflora.php)

What it is: The species page for Pinus densiflora, the Japanese red pine, in the Gymnosperm Database, a long-running online reference on the world's conifers. It describes where the tree grows - rocky and sandy slopes, poor and dry soils, from sea level up into the hills.

Why it applies, and its limits: A species' tolerances are a fact of the species, so a modern description bounds what a premodern map may stand a pine on: red pine is a tree of dry, well-drained and disturbed ground, never a reed edge, and both pages also say what a human landscape does with it - its spread in Japan tracked the rise of agriculture. The limits are that the Gymnosperm Database is one editor's compilation rather than a peer-reviewed flora, though it cites its literature; that the habitat sentence our footnote quotes describes the species at its Russian northern edge rather than in Japan; and that neither page is a historical source, so how a village managed its pines rests elsewhere.

Used for: red pine as a tree of dry, well-drained slopes - never a reed edge
