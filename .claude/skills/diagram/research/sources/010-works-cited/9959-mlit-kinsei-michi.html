mlit-kinsei-michi

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), "道の歴史: 近世の道 - (参考) 江戸の町と道 3: 江戸府内の道路利用" (the history of roads: early modern roads - Edo's streets, 3: how the roads in Edo were used) (in Japanese; https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/michi-re/3-6.htm)

What it is: A reference panel in the national road ministry's online history of Japanese roads, listing in bullet points how Edo's streets were used: on foot and by palanquin, goods by hand cart, horse and ox cart, and the apron and drain every house kept.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how goods moved in the shogun's city and gives the apron a house was required to keep, which bear on the carts and roads the map draws. It is a government summary in note form that cites no sources, and it describes Edo alone, the largest city, not a provincial town or the roads between towns.

Used for: goods moved by hand cart, horse and ox cart in Edo, and the 3 ft apron a house kept (ways)
