What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Shimotsuke provincial temple site, whose precinct dimensions rest on a park explanatory board, the city's website and a gazetteer; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the precinct's dimensions, which set the large end of the kokubunji range against Sanuki's small one; no archaeological report is cited for the figure that sets the top of the range, the site is an eighth-century state foundation, and it is one site, with nothing on shrines.

Used for: the size of a provincial temple precinct (religion and death)
