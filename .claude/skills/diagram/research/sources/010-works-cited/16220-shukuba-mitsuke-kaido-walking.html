旧街道ウォーキング (Kaido Walking), 宿場とは ("What is a post station?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://kaido-walking.com/basic/about-syukuba.php)

What it is: An explanatory page on a walkers' guide site to the old highways, on what an Edo-period post station was, its facilities and the features at its entrances.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the mitsuke at a post town's entrances were - an earthen bank on each side of the road with a fence on top. It is an enthusiasts' guide without citations, its note on the night lamps is hedged, and it describes the post towns of the great highways, not a market town.

Used for: the post town's entrance banks (urban-features)
