Ehime Prefecture, えひめの宝 no. 56 (a designated shrine's honden and haiden) (https://www.pref.ehime.jp/ehimenotakara/56.html) - 「1.98 m along the ridge and 1.82 m across the beams; the pillars are plain squared timber with almost no ornament, giving a spare impression.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桁行1.98m、梁間1.82mで、柱は白木角型でほとんど装飾がなく、清そな感じを受けます。」)

What it is: A prefectural cultural-property page describing a designated village shrine's honden of 1789 and haiden of 1790 with their measured dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: We use it for the two measurements themselves - a honden of 1.98 by 1.82 m and a haiden of 9.78 by 10.62 m at one Edo-period village shrine - which are the anchors for the sanctuary's size and the top of the hall's band. Its limits: one shrine in one province, designated because it survived well, so it is an attested instance rather than a typical one; its honden is irimoya-roofed rather than nagare-zukuri.

Used for: a village honden of 1.98 by 1.82 m; a haiden of 9.78 by 10.62 m (religion-and-death.html).
