xunjiansi-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 巡檢司 ("xunjiansi", title translated from the Chinese by this project) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%B7%A1%E6%A3%80%E5%8F%B8)

What it is: A stub Chinese Wikipedia article on the xunjiansi, the grassroots patrol and inspection office of the Yuan, Ming and Qing below the county, with the rank of its officer and the Ming's use of it against pirates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the Chinese state's own post below the county seat, which a town map might otherwise be tempted to draw at a town gate. A stub citing two older works; it says nothing of where a post stood or what it looked like.

Used for: the xunjiansi's low-ranking officer, the Ming's forty-two in Fujian, and county posts under the magistrate (defenses)
