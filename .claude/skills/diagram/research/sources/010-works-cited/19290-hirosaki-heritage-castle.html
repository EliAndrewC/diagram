HIROSAKI Heritage, 弘前城 東北で唯一の現存天守と城址を活用した弘前公園 ("Hirosaki Castle: the only surviving keep in Tohoku, and Hirosaki Park on the castle site", title translated) (https://www.hirosaki-heritage.com/heritage/h-hirosakijo/)

What it is: The Hirosaki Tourism and Convention Bureau's heritage-site article (a promotional page, citing no references) on Hirosaki Castle and the park laid out on it: its extent, its gates, its keep and its stone walls, with a short building overview and chronology.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the park opens nearly the whole of the domain's castle and that the castle's layout as first built survives entire, which makes today's area the Edo castle's. It gives two areas that disagree, about 49 ha in its text and 385,200 square meters in its overview, and says neither what each counts nor why they differ. And they are one northern domain's castle, not a size for castles in general.

Used for: that Hirosaki Park is nearly the whole Edo castle, its layout surviving, and its two area figures (Domain capitals)
