ja.wikipedia 馬場 ("riding ground", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%A6%AC%E5%A0%B4)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the riding ground: ground kept for riding, for military horses and their training, with sections on the Takada riding ground in Edo and on racecourses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in one sentence that an early-modern castle town had a riding ground in its samurai district, used by the samurai living there. It is a short encyclopedia article that gives no size and no example outside Edo.

Used for: that the riding ground stood in the samurai district (cities/government)
