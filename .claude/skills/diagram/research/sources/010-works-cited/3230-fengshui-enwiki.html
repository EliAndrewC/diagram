en.wikipedia "Feng shui" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feng_shui)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on feng shui, the Chinese practice of siting buildings and graves by landform and orientation; an encyclopedia article with references, read for its sentence on where southern Chinese villages put their graves.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its statement that graves went on the highest hills, far from water and on otherwise unvaluable farmland, is the grave half of this page's per-archetype matrix. It is a tertiary summary of southern China with no period given, and it says nothing of terraces, outcrops or ponds.

Used for: grave siting on the slope rather than in the wet paddy (fields)
