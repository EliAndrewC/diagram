ja.wikipedia 伝馬町 ("tenma-cho, the relay-horse quarter", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BC%9D%E9%A6%AC%E7%94%BA)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tenma-cho, the quarter of an Edo-period castle town that bore the lord's relay-horse duty, with where it stood and how the duty was later paid.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that a castle town, not only a post station, kept a relay, and where in the town it stood. It is a short tertiary article resting on one encyclopedia entry, and it gives no size for the quarter or its stables.

Used for: the relay quarter placed near the castle's main gate, and the duty later carried by a few houses and paid in money by the rest (towns)
