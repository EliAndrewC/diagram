ja.wikipedia 閑谷学校 ("the Shizutani school", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/閑谷学校)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Shizutani school, opened by the Okayama domain in the 1670s for commoners, with its lecture hall, Confucian shrine and other buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows what a domain-built school held besides its Confucian hall. It was a school for commoners in the countryside, not the domain's school for samurai in the castle town, and the article counts its designated structures as 24 in one place and 25 in another.

Used for: what a domain-built school held (religion-and-death)
