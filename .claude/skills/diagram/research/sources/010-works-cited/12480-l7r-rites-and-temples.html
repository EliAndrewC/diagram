The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "The Ministry of Rites", "Temple Organization" and "Demographics" (URL: none - the GM's own unpublished writing, at /host-l7r-repo/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, on how the Ministry of Rites oversees the shrines and temples at each tier of settlement - a country monk in every village district, a preceptor in every county town, a provincial abbot in every provincial city, grand abbots in the domain capital - and on where samurai children are schooled.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: which religious offices and schools a settlement of each tier holds is the setting's to decide, and these maps follow it. The notes name the offices and the tiers, not the buildings' sizes or plans, and they name no Confucian temple, City God temple or state altar.

Used for: the setting's analogue of the state cult's buildings at each seat, and where a provincial city's samurai children are schooled (religion-and-death)
