Edo-Tokyo Museum, special exhibition page "Tamagawa Josui" (English) (https://www.edo-tokyo-museum.or.jp/en/s-exhibition/tamagawajosui/)

What it is: The Edo-Tokyo Museum's English page for its exhibition on the Tamagawa Jōsui: the aqueduct's branching from the Tama river at Hamura, its 43 km run, and the underground network of conduits that carried its water on from the Yotsuya gate to the city's households.

Why it applies, and its limits: A museum's own exhibition text for the aqueduct's length and the change from open channel to buried conduit at the city gate, corroborating the encyclopedia article cited beside it. Its limit is brevity: it is an exhibition summary, not a technical history.

Used for: the 43 km from Hamura and the underground conduits from the Yotsuya gate
