Kotobank, 陳旉農書 (Chen Fu nongshu), the Britannica International Encyclopedia (ブリタニカ国際大百科事典 小項目事典) entry (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%99%B3%E6%97%89%E8%BE%B2%E6%9B%B8-98774)

What it is: A short encyclopedia entry on Chen Fu's farming manual, giving its date of completion, its three books and what each covers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the manual to 1149 and names its subject as the wet-rice farming of the Jiangnan region, the lower Yangtze, which is all it is used for. It is a brief secondary entry that names no sources of its own.

Used for: the date of Chen Fu's manual and the region it describes (fields)
