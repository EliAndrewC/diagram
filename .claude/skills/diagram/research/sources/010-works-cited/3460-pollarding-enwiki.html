en.wikipedia "Pollarding" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollarding)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on pollarding, the pruning system that keeps a tree at a set height and harvests its shoots; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the withy harvest, the held height and the low stub form, which is why this page's dike willows stand low; it names no dike or waterway, and its examples are European; its low stub form is a woodland boundary marker, so the height is borrowed across uses.

Used for: pollarded dike willows (archetypes)
