Independent Chinese PEN Center, old-site articles, "书斋——中国文人的精神家园" (the study: the spiritual home of the Chinese literati), posted 2008 (in Chinese; https://www.chinesepen.org/old-posts/?p=6959)

What it is: An essay posted on the old site of the Independent Chinese PEN Center describing the traditional Chinese scholar's study: what it was for, how it was furnished, and where in the house compound it stood.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states plainly that the study separated from the living rooms and stood in the quiet part of the compound beside the rear garden, the placement a detached garden study takes. It is a general essay for readers, not a scholarly or dated architectural account, and it cites no sources for these statements.

Used for: the Chinese study set apart from the living rooms, beside the rear garden (buildings)
