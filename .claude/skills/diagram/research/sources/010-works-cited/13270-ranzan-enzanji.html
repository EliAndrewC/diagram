ranzan-enzanji

Ranzan Town web museum (嵐山町web博物誌), 比企郡寺院明細帳, 曹洞宗 遠山寺 菅谷村大字遠山 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter12.html)

What it is: The Ranzan town office's transcription of the Hiki district temple register entry for Enzanji (遠山寺), a Sōtō temple of Tōyama, Saitama, with its main hall of 10 ken 3 shaku by 7 ken 3 shaku and its kuri.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its main hall is the widest of the nineteen temples the Ranzan town museum transcribes from the register (those of today's Ranzan town, not the whole Hiki district), the top of the band. Its limits: one rural temple; a village temple, not a town one, and a well-endowed one (a 10-koku shogunal grant); the register dates from the Meiji era, before 1889 (it names Tōyama village, merged into Sugaya village in 1889, and records the temple's grant land being resumed in 1868, with notes added to 1948), and is kept in the prefecture's files for 1963, so the hall it measures stood about a decade after the Edo period and may have been rebuilt since then.

Used for: the widest main hall in the band (religion-and-death.html).
