shirobito-612-goten

Shirobito (Japan Castle Foundation), 超入門！お城セミナー 第45回【構造】御殿には「表」と「奥」があるって本当？ ("Castle seminar for beginners, 45: is it true that a palace had an omote and an oku?", title translated from the Japanese by this project) (https://shirobito.jp/article/612)

What it is: A 2018 introductory article on the site of the Japan Castle Foundation explaining what a castle palace (goten) was for, why it could stand in a bailey other than the honmaru, and its division into a public and a private side.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states in one place the palace's two roles (seat of government and the lord's residence), the omote/oku split, and that in hill-and-plain castles the palace was often built in the wider second or third bailey. It is a general-audience explainer with no references, written around the Nagoya palace reconstruction.

Used for: what a castle palace was and where in the castle it stood (cities/capitals)
