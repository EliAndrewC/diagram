• kotobank-banta - 「Those hired by towns and villages in the Edo period to keep night watch and guard against fire, at sluice gates and the like; many were of hinin status.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代、町や村に雇われ、夜警や火事・水門などの番に当たった者。非人身分の者が多かった。」) (Digital Daijisen)
  • kotobank-banta - 「In some villages, their duties were guarding the hills, fields and sluice gates, running errands, night watch, and controlling vagrants.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村によっては山野水門の警備や走り使い、夜番、浮浪者の取締りなどである。」) and 「These Edo banta were commoners」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「この江戸の番太は平民で」) and 「elsewhere many were of hinin status and were also called ban-hinin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その他の所では非人身分の者が多く番非人ともいわれた。」) (Nipponica)
  • kotobank-banta - 「Normally they sat in a watchman's hut (banya)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「平常は、番人小屋（番屋）に詰め」) and 「a watchman's allowance was paid.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「番人給が支給されていた。」) (Seisen-ban Nihon kokugo daijiten)
  • kotobank-banta - 「In some regions many were of hinin status and handled grave digging, cremation, and disposing of travelers who died on the road.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「地方によっては非人身分の者も多く，穴掘，火葬，行路死者の始末などにあたった。」) (Britannica Japan)
  • kotobank-bangoya - 「In the villages, calling it the advance hawk watch, they build a watch hut at the village's entrance」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一 村々に先御鷹番と号して、其村の入口に番小屋を造り」) (quoted in the dictionary from Minkan seiyo, 1721)
