• kotobank-banta - 「Those hired by towns and villages in the Edo period to keep night watch and guard against fire, at sluice gates and the like; many were of hinin status.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Digital Daijisen)
  • kotobank-banta - 「In some villages, their duties were guarding the hills, fields and sluice gates, running errands, night watch, controlling vagrants and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「These Edo banta were commoners」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「elsewhere many were of hinin status and were also called ban-hinin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Nipponica)
  • kotobank-banta - 「Normally they sat in a watchman's hut (banya)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) and 「a watchman's allowance was paid.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Seisen-ban Nihon kokugo daijiten)
  • kotobank-banta - 「In some regions many were of hinin status and handled grave digging, cremation, and disposing of travelers who died on the road.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Britannica Japan)
  • kotobank-bangoya - 「In the villages, calling it the advance hawk watch, they build a watch hut at the village's entrance」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (quoted in the dictionary from Minkan seiyo, 1721)
