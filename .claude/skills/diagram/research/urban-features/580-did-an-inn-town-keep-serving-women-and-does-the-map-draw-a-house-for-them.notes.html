• meshimori-jawiki - 「Meshimori onna, or meshiuri onna, were unlicensed prostitutes who existed at the post towns of early modern Japan (centered mainly on the Edo period).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「In the Edo period prostitutes were allowed to do business only inside the pleasure quarters the rulers designated, the Yoshiwara of Edo and others, but the meshimori alone were half-tolerated under the name of 'servants of the post town'.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • meshimori-jawiki - 「In 1772 limits were set of 150 each at Senju-juku and Itabashi-juku, 500 at Shinagawa-juku and 250 at Naito-Shinjuku.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • meshimori-jawiki - of the three post towns at the foot of Mount Asama: 「At these inns and the like it was the custom to keep meshimori (also called 'white necks') as employees to entertain the lodgers, and as a result, at Karuizawa-juku and Oiwake-juku in particular, the female population greatly exceeded the male.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • yukaku-jawiki - 「Besides these, in the Edo period, the places outside the licensed quarters where prostitutes gathered were the meshimori inns of the post towns (see meshimori onna) and the okabasho of the temple-gate towns and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
