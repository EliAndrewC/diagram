  • yashio-noma-yogyo - 「According to the survey of Bunsei 12 (1829), of the 166 households of Hachijo village, 66 (four in ten) traded or worked a craft in the intervals of farming.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「the depth of the lots of the machiya facing the street was thus unified at 20 ken」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「In this way the arrangement of holding the front shop to a depth of about 5 ken and placing back-lane tenements behind it was quite standardized」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「if the frontage of the front shop was about 3 ken they lined one side, if about 5 ken both sides」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the ken is taken at about 6 ft)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「The Kyoto machiya of the late Muromachi period was generally a small machiya of about 2 ken frontage and 2 ken depth.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - of the city of Edo in the early Edo period: 「Most machiya were of a small scale, about 1.5 ken frontage and 2 ken depth, smaller than the Kyoto machiya of the late Muromachi period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「In Kyoto, as in Edo, the stratification of the townspeople was marked: they were divided into townspeople holding large machiya with a frontage of 10 ken or more, townspeople holding ordinary machiya with a frontage of 4 or 5 ken, and townspeople holding small machiya with a frontage of 1.5 to 2 ken, or renting」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
