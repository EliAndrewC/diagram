• machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「The machiya is a kind of folk house: the dwelling form, with a shop attached, in which townspeople lived.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町屋（まちや）とは、民家の一種で町人の住む店舗併設の住居形態。」)
  • yashio-noma-yogyo - 「According to the survey of Bunsei 12 (1829), of the 166 households of Hachijo village, 66 (four in ten) traded or worked a craft in the intervals of farming.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「文政12年(1829年)の調査によると、八條村の家数166軒のうち66軒(4割)が農間商い・諸職人。」)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「the depth of the lots of the machiya facing the street was thus unified at 20 ken」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「通りに面する町屋の敷地の奥行きは20間に統一」); 「In this way the arrangement of holding the front shop to a depth of about 5 ken and placing back-lane tenements behind it was quite standardized」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このように、表店を5間程度の奥行きに抑え、その奥に裏長屋を取る構成はかなり定型化されており」); 「if the frontage of the front shop was about 3 ken they lined one side, if about 5 ken both sides」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「表店の間口が3間程度なら片側、5間程度なら両側に並んでいた」) (the ken is taken at about 6 ft)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「The Kyoto machiya of the late Muromachi period was generally a small machiya of about 2 ken frontage and 2 ken depth.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「室町時代末期の京都町屋は、間口2間、奥行き2間ほどの小さな町屋が一般的であった。」)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - of the city of Edo in the early Edo period: 「Most machiya were of a small scale, about 1.5 ken frontage and 2 ken depth, smaller than the Kyoto machiya of the late Muromachi period.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「町屋の多くは間口1間半、奥行き2間ほどの小さな規模であり、室町時代末期の京都町屋より小規模であった。」)
  • machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「In Kyoto, as in Edo, the stratification of the townspeople was marked: they were divided into townspeople holding large machiya with a frontage of 10 ken or more, townspeople holding ordinary machiya with a frontage of 4 or 5 ken, and townspeople holding small machiya with a frontage of 1.5 to 2 ken, or renting」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「京都も江戸と同じく町人の階層分化は顕著であり、間口10間以上の大きな町屋を持つ町人、間口4、5間の一般的な町屋を持つ町人、そして間口1間半から2間の小さな町屋を持ち、または借家をする町人とに分かれており」)
