  • wangzhen-nongshu-18 - 「Over the well a frame is set up carrying an axle, threaded through a long hub」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「井上立架置軸，貫以長轂」) (a crank windlass, not a pulley; from above its frame reads as the map's two posts and a beam)
  • tiangong-shuili - 「Using the well-sweep or the windlass, the work done is very small indeed.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「用桔槔、轆轤，功勞又甚細已。」)
  • shi-zhang-lulu - 「on the well frame there is clearly a drum-shaped pulley.」 (of the Western Han tomb mural from Sandaohao, Liaoyang) (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「井架上显然是一个腰鼓形的滑车。」) and 「many pulleys have been found on Han stone reliefs, carved bricks, tomb murals and excavated pottery wells」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「汉代的画像石、画像砖、墓葬壁画或者出土陶井上发现了很多滑车」) (the frame's size is not given)
  • kotobank-kurumaido - the Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's first attestation, 「Long and short, not one in Kyoto but is a pulley well」 (a comic verse of 1753) (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「長みじか京はのこらず車井戸」), and Nipponica's 「For that reason this method is common in wells where the water is deep.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「そのため水位が深い井戸にはこの方式が多い。」)
