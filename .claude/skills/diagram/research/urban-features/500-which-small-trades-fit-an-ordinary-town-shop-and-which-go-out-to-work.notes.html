• l7r-castes - 「Coopers」, 「Weavers (and other fibercrafters of all sorts, spinners, embroiderers, sewers, tailors, etc)」, 「Soy sauce brewers」, 「Sake brewers」, 「Noodlers」 and 「Tofuya」 (the setting's canon, items of the notes' list of castes)
  • token-edo-shokunin - 「Carpenters and plasterers, who went out to work, were called deshoku; those who worked in a workshop in their own homes, such as cabinetmakers, were called ijoku.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「外に出て働く大工・左官は出職(でしょく)、自宅の仕事場で仕事をする指物師などは居職(いじょく)と呼ばれました。」)
  • kotobank-okeya - 「From the early modern period coopers appeared who worked at home, combining making and selling, and in the castle towns and such places they lived together and formed cooper streets and barrel-maker streets.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「近世からは製造と販売とを兼ねる居職の桶屋があらわれ，城下町などでは集住して桶屋町や樽屋町をつくっていた。」); 「Because the hoops of tubs and barrels often broke, some went out to work at repairs, and some walked the round of the towns and villages.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「桶や樽は箍がこわれることが多いので，修理のために出職するものもあり，町や村をめぐり歩くものもいた。」) (the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry, by Endo Motoo)
  • kotobank-tatamiya - 「They worked mainly at home, but sometimes went out to work.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「居職(いじょく)を主とするが出職(でしょく)のこともあった。」) (the Nipponica entry, by Endo Motoo)
  • kotobank-tofuya - 「In the seventeenth century shop sale began.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「17世紀には店売りが始まった。」); 「In the eighteenth century they appeared not only in the towns but in the surrounding villages too.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「18世紀には都市ばかりでなく周辺の村落にもできた。」); 「Shop sale and peddling are the same today.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「店売りと振り売りは今日も同じである。」) (the Nipponica entry, by Endo Motoo)
  • kotobank-sobaya - 「A shop that makes soba, or mainly noodles, and serves them to eat or sells them.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「蕎麦、または主として麺類をつくって食べさせ、または売る店。」) (the Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
  • kotobank-yakushuya - 「A house that compounds crude drugs and also deals in Chinese medicines.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「生薬を調剤し、また漢方薬を商う家。」) (the Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
  • kusuriya-jawiki - 「Nihonbashi Honcho - from the Edo period drug shops stood eave to eave」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「日本橋本町 - 江戸時代から薬屋が軒を並べ」)
