  • mlit-tokaido-kosatsuba - 「The size of the kosatsuba differs a little from post town to post town, but the height is roughly constant at 1 jo (about 3 m) to 2 ken (about 3.6 m).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高札場の大きさは、宿場によって若干違いは見られるが、高さは1丈（約3ｍ）〜2間（約3.6ｍ）でほぼ一定している。」) and, from the page's table drawn from the Tokaido post-town survey, 「Kanagawa-juku, beside the Takinohashi: height 1 jo, length (frontage) 1 jo 6 shaku, width (depth) 5 shaku」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「神奈川宿 字瀧之橋脇 1丈 1丈6尺 5尺」) and 「Totsuka-juku, Tenno-cho: 1 jo 1 shaku, 3 ken, 1 ken」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「戸塚宿 天王町 1丈1尺 3間 1間」) (the conversion to feet is this project's: a jo of ten shaku is about 10 ft, a ken about 6 ft)

  • kotobank-kosatsu - 「The kosatsuba was surrounded with a yarai palisade, or else stone walls or turf banks were built, and it was strictly managed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高札場には矢来をめぐらし，あるいは石垣，芝土居を築くなどして，厳重な管理が行われた。」) (Heibonsha, Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten)
