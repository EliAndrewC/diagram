  • kanda-josui-jawiki - 「Aqueduct wells were installed in the nagaya as well, and the residents used them in common.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「長屋にも上水井戸が設置され、住人たちが共同でして容姿いた。」); nagaya-jawiki - 「There was a communal privy in the alley, and no bath. There was a communal well for water, but」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「路地に共同トイレがあり、風呂は無い。水は共同の井戸が有ったが」) (the page's own text is garbled at the verb, evidently for 共同で使用していた; the rate of one draw-point per ten to twenty households is this project's own: a search in Japanese for how many nagaya households shared one well found pages that place a shared well in the alley, and none that gives a ratio)
  • kanda-josui-jawiki - 「The order of supply by the aqueduct gave priority to the samurai districts, with the remaining water supplied to the commoner districts.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「上水による給水順次は武家地が優先で、残りの水を町人地へ給水している。」) (the Edo fact is priority of supply; the provincial samurai house's own well is cited from Kakunodate beside it)
  • jta-aoyagi-kakunodate - 「After passing through the gate, visitors will find a roofed well (ido-yakata) and rice storehouse, as well as the main entryway」
  • semboku-bukeyashiki - 「the roof is shingled, and there are shitomi shutters and a sweep-type well」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋根は木羽葺きで、しとみ戸やつるべ式の井戸があり」) (of the Iwahashi house, 「a senior retainer of the Ashina」 - translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「芦名氏の重臣」)
