• eta-jawiki - 「lower-official work such as executioner, arresting officer, watchman, mountain warden and water warden」 (translated; ); eta-jawiki - 「They were often in places unsuited to farming, such as the edge of the village or beside the river.」 (translated; ); suzugamori-jawiki - 「It was an execution ground set along the southern entrance (the Tokaido).」 (translated; )
  • kozukappara-jawiki - 「The bodies were left exposed to the weather, unburied, with nothing but earth thrown over them, and」 (translated; )「In Kanbun 7 (1667) Teiyo Gikan, the abbot of Ekoin in Honjo, founded the Jogyodo hall on ground adjoining the Kozukappara execution ground, in order to bury the dead and hold memorial rites (kuyo) for them.」 (translated; ) (bodies were left exposed, not buried, merely covered with earth; in 1667 the abbot of Honjo Ekoin founded a hall on ground adjoining the execution ground to bury and pray for the dead.)
  • teramachi-jawiki - 「Before the Edo period, with the intent of forming a defensive line in the event of an enemy attack, temples and graveyards were gathered at the outer edge of the town in many castle towns.」 (translated; ) (the page gives defense, not pollution, as the reason; and the executed of the shizai class in Edo were later carried from the Denmachō jail to Ekōin for burial rather than pitted at the ground)
  • suzugamori-jawiki - 「It was the execution ground that had been set along the southern entrance (the Tokaido).」 (translated; )
  • senju-shuku-jawiki - 「The Senju Great Bridge was built over the Arakawa, and the land of Senju is 2 ri from Edo (Nihonbashi)」 (translated; ); senju-shuku-jawiki - 「In Kozukappara-machi, the town south of Senju-juku, the Kozukappara execution ground was placed as Edo's northern execution ground.」 (translated; ) (two ri is about five miles; Suzugamori's distance is on no page read)
  • dosojin-jawiki - 「A deity enshrined mainly as a stone monument or stone image at the boundary of a settlement, at the center of a village, at the boundary between inside and outside the village, at road crossings and three-way junctions」 (translated; ); dosojin-jawiki - 「The reading 'sae-no-kami' (the blocking deity) was applied to it, and it is understood as a deity that prevents intruders from outside」 (translated; )
