• eta-jawiki - 「lower-official work such as executioner, arresting officer, watchman, mountain warden and water warden」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「刑吏・捕吏・番太・山番・水番などの下級官僚的な仕事」); eta-jawiki - 「They were often in places unsuited to farming, such as the edge of the village or beside the river.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村外れや川の側など、農業に適さない場所であったことが多い。」); suzugamori-jawiki - 「It was an execution ground set along the southern entrance (the Tokaido).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南の入口（東海道）沿いに設置されていた刑場であった。」)
  • kozukappara-jawiki - 「The bodies were left exposed to the weather, unburied, with nothing but earth thrown over them, and」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「遺体は野ざらしのまま埋葬せずに土を被せるだけで」)「In Kanbun 7 (1667) Teiyo Gikan, the abbot of Ekoin in Honjo, founded the Jogyodo hall on ground adjoining the Kozukappara execution ground, in order to bury the dead and hold memorial rites (kuyo) for them.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寛文7年（1667年）に本所回向院の住職である弟誉義観（ていよぎかん）が、死者の埋葬と供養を行うため、小塚原刑場隣接地に常行堂を創建した。」) (bodies were left exposed, not buried, merely covered with earth; in 1667 the abbot of Honjo Ekoin founded a hall on ground adjoining the execution ground to bury and pray for the dead.)
  • teramachi-jawiki - 「Before the Edo period, with the intent of forming a defensive line in the event of an enemy attack, temples and graveyards were gathered at the outer edge of the town in many castle towns.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代以前、敵襲に際して防衛線とする意図で、多くの城下町において寺院や墓地が市街の外縁にまとめられた。」) (the page gives defense, not pollution, as the reason; and the executed of the shizai class in Edo were later carried from the Denmachō jail to Ekōin for burial rather than pitted at the ground)
  • suzugamori-jawiki - 「It was the execution ground that had been set along the southern entrance (the Tokaido).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「南の入口（東海道）沿いに設置されていた刑場であった。」)
  • senju-shuku-jawiki - 「The Senju Great Bridge was built over the Arakawa, and the land of Senju is 2 ri from Edo (Nihonbashi)」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「荒川に千住大橋がかけられ、千住の地は江戸（日本橋）から2里」); senju-shuku-jawiki - 「In Kozukappara-machi, the town south of Senju-juku, the Kozukappara execution ground was placed as Edo's northern execution ground.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「千住宿の南の町小塚原町には江戸北の刑場として、小塚原刑場が置かれている。」) (two ri is about five miles; Suzugamori's distance is on no page read)
  • dosojin-jawiki - 「A deity enshrined mainly as a stone monument or stone image at the boundary of a settlement, at the center of a village, at the boundary between inside and outside the village, at road crossings and three-way junctions」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「集落の境や村の中心、村内と村外の境界や道の辻、三叉路などに主に石碑や石像の形態で祀られる神」); dosojin-jawiki - 「The reading 'sae-no-kami' (the blocking deity) was applied to it, and it is understood as a deity that prevents intruders from outside」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「さへのかみ（塞の神）」という音があてられ、外部からの侵入者を防ぐ神であると考えられている」)
