  • nippon-com-terakoya - 「Terakoya, which needed only a master and one teaching room, are said to have been about as many as the 60,000-odd villages of the Japanese archipelago.」 (translated; )

  • terakoya-jawiki - 「I hear that in the Kamigata region they are called terakoya; this is said to be because many studied at nearby temples.」 (translated; ) (a report quoted in the article's footnote from the Nihon kyoikushi shiryo)
  • terakoya-jawiki - 「Taking the Kawaguchi-Tajime area of Fukatsu district, Bingo, as an example, there were 7 terakoya in all in the late Edo period (though not all existed at the same time); the masters were 1 shoya, 2 village officials, 1 doctor, 2 monks, 1 other.」 (translated; )
