• terakoya-jawiki - 「Terakoya is the general name for the private educational establishments where, in the Edo period, writing masters taught commoners' children (townspeople and farmers) elementary reading, writing, arithmetic and the like.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「寺子屋（てらこや）とは、江戸時代に、手習師匠が庶民層（町人・農民）の子弟に初歩の読み書き・計算等を教えた民間の教育施設の総称である。」)
  • terakoya-jawiki - 「I hear that in the Kamigata region they are called terakoya; this is said to be because many studied at nearby temples.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「上方筋ニハ寺子屋ト稱候趣承リ申候右ハ最寄寺院ニテ多ク就學致シ候故之由ニ御座候」) (a report quoted in the article's footnote from the Nihon kyoikushi shiryo)
  • terakoya-jawiki - 「Taking the Kawaguchi-Tajime area of Fukatsu district, Bingo, as an example, there were 7 terakoya in all in the late Edo period (though not all existed at the same time); the masters were 1 shoya, 2 village officials, 1 doctor, 2 monks, 1 other.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「備後国深津郡の川口・多治米地域を例にとると、幕末期には合計7ヶ所(ただし、そのすべてが同時に存在したわけではない)の寺子屋が存在し、師匠の内訳は、庄屋1人、村役人2人、医者1人、僧侶2人、その他1人であった。」)
  • terakoya-jawiki - 「According to the same source there were said to be 16,560 terakoya nationally; in Edo alone there were 400-500 large ones, and about 1,000-1,300 counting small ones.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「同資料によると全国に16560軒の寺子屋があったといい、江戸だけでも大寺子屋が400-500軒、小規模なものも含めれば1000-1300軒ぐらい存在していた｡」) and 「Pupils per school varied from 10 to 100.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「1校当たりの生徒数は、10-100人と様々であった。」)
  • nippon-com-terakoya - 「Terakoya, which needed only a master and one teaching room, are said to have been about as many as the 60,000-odd villages of the Japanese archipelago.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「師匠と教場一部屋さえあれば成立した寺子屋は、列島日本の6万余の村数くらいあったと言われる。」)
  • hannan-terakoya - 「For the late Edo period, the Hannan city area has recorded one terakoya each in Ozaki, Boude, Shimura and Kaikake villages and two in Hakotsukuri village - six in all.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代末期(約165～150年前)の阪南市域では尾崎村、波有手(ぼうで)村、新村(しむら)、貝掛(かいかけ)村にそれぞれ1ヶ所、箱作(はこつくり)村に2ヶ所、合計6ヶ所の寺子屋が記載されています。」)
  • ndl-crd-terakoya-count - 「It records the national number of terakoya by the Nihon kyoikushi shiryo survey as 15,560.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「日本教育史資料」の調査による全国の寺子屋数は15,560校であると記載されている。」) and 「It says many regions - Saitama, Ibaraki, Iwate, Nara, Ehime, Okinawa and others - were missed by the survey.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「埼玉・茨城・岩手・奈良・愛媛・沖縄など、多くの地域が調査漏れになっているとある。」) (the Okayama prefectural library's answer, summarizing three books)
  • sishu-zhwiki - 「The ancestral hall held rites in spring and autumn, and in ordinary times served as a sishu, educating the family's children.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「祠堂春秋举行祭礼，而平常则作为私塾，教育家庭子弟。」)
  • yixue-zhwiki - 「Kangxi 51 (1713): 'ordered the provinces, prefectures, departments and counties to set up many charity schools, gather the orphaned and poor, and engage teachers to teach them'」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「康熙五十一年（1713年），"令各省府州县多立义学，聚集孤寒，延师教读"」) and 「pupils were mostly aged six to eleven」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「学生年龄多为六岁至十一岁」)
