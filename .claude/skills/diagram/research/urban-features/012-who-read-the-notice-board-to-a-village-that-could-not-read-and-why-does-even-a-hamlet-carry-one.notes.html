• kosatsu-jawiki - 「Furegaki of especially high importance were posted on the kosatsu (seisatsu), a wooden standing board.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「特に重要度の高い触は木製の立て札である高札（制札）で掲示された。」); kosatsu-jawiki - 「The shogunate held it to be 'a matter to be made known to all the people', and worked to have the contents thoroughly known to the population.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「幕府は「万民に周知の事」とし、内容が人々に周知徹底されるよう努めていた」)
  • shoya-jawiki - 「Furegaki and circulars from the lord were copied out into a ledger, and the original was then sent on to the neighboring village by order to the standing messenger.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「また領主からの触書、廻状類は、それを帳面に書き写したうえで、原文を定使に命じて隣村へ持って行かせた。」); shoya-jawiki - 「Almost all official documents required the shoya's signature and seal, and transfers of land between villagers (chiefly pledged land) frequently required the shoya's certifying seal as well, and therefore at minimum an ability in reading, writing and the abacus was necessary.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ほとんどの公文書には庄屋の署名・捺印が必要とされ、村人相互の土地移動（主として質地）にも庄屋の証印が必要とする場合が多く、それゆえ最低限の読み書き算盤の能力が必要だった。」) (the literacy is grounded on signing and sealing documents rather than on the circulars)

  • daikan-jawiki - 「The intendants in and around the Kanto in particular were permanently stationed in Edo; they governed through contact with their assistants, and the intendant went out to the territory in his charge only for land surveys, harvest inspections, tours of inspection, and when a serious incident occurred.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「特に関東近辺の代官は江戸定府で、支配は手付と連絡を取り行い、代官は検地、検見、巡察、重大事件発生時にのみ支配地に赴いた。」) (the sentence is about the shogunate's intendants near the Kanto, and the page adds that in distant regions the intendant as a rule lived on the spot; it gives the occasions of a visit, not a count, so twice a year is this page's estimate)
