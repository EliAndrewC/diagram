  • tawara-jawiki - 「In the Iburi district of Hokkaido, a major charcoal-producing area, charcoal for shipping was packed in square bales.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「木炭の一大生産地であった北海道胆振地方では、出荷する木炭を四角い俵詰にしていた。」)
  • tawara-unit-jawiki - 「From the Sengoku to the Edo period one tawara was roughly between 2 and 5 to, differing by time and place; for example the shogunate set one tawara at 3 to 5 sho, but in Kaga domain one tawara was 5 to.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「戦国時代から江戸時代の1俵はおおむね2斗から5斗の間で時代・土地ごとに異なり、例えば江戸幕府は1俵を3斗5升としたが、加賀藩の1俵は5斗であった。」)
  • tawara-unit-jawiki - 「And the bales themselves came in various sizes, 4-to bales, 6-to bales and so on, and were not standardized.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「またそもそも俵自体にも、四斗俵や六斗俵などいろいろなサイズがあって、規格が一定していなかった。」)
  • tawara-unit-jawiki - 「It was applied to various products once traded in bales besides rice: minor grains, charcoal, salt, cotton and so on.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「米以外にも雑穀、木炭、食塩、綿花など、かつて俵(たわら)で流通したさまざまな産物に適用された」)
  • tawara-unit-jawiki - 「The top grade was packed at 4 kan (about 15 kg), good grade at 10 kan (37.5 kg), and a lower grade at 8 kan (30 kg), rolled in straw mats.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「最上級品が四貫目詰め（約15kg）、上物は十貫目詰め（37.5kg）、さらに下の等級ではむしろで丸めた八貫目詰め（30kg）とされていた」) (of the charcoal of the Iburi district of Hokkaido, where the article says the weight of a bale differed by grade; it names no date)
  • tawara-unit-jawiki - 「From then on, one tawara = 4 to became the de facto standard.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「以後は一俵 = 4斗が事実上の統一基準になった。」) (after the prefectural rice-inspection rules of the Meiji period, which the article describes just before)
  • tawara-unit-jawiki - 「In current rice-trade practice one tawara is 60 kg.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「現在の米穀取引の実務における1俵は60 kgとされている」)
