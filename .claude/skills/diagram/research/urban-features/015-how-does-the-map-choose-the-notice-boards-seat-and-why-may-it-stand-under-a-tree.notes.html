• nakatsugawa-kosatsuba - 「The kosatsuba of Nakatsugawa-juku was not at its present position; it was built at the crossroads with the Hida road, about 20 m further up the Chaya-zaka, so as to catch the eyes of more people.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • mlit-tokaido-kosatsuba - 「So as to meet the eyes of many people, they were often set at places with much passing traffic, such as the center of the village or a crossroads where main highways cross.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • shiojiri-iwadare-kosatsuba - 「it stands at a position roughly equidistant from the houses of the headmen of the shogunal domain and of the Takato domain」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the page links this to the village's division between the two lords in 1691)
  • kotobank-kosatsu - 「The kosatsuba was surrounded with a yarai palisade, or else stone walls, turf banks and the like were built, and it was strictly managed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (Heibonsha, Sekai Daihyakka Jiten)
