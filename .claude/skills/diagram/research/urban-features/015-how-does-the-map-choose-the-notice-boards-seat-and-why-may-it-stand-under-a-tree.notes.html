• nakatsugawa-kosatsuba - 「The kosatsuba of Nakatsugawa-juku was not at its present position; it was built at the crossroads with the Hida road, about 20 m further up the Chaya-zaka, so as to catch the eyes of more people.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「中津川宿の高札場は現在の位置ではなく、茶屋坂を20mくらい離れてあがった飛騨街道との交差点に、よりたくさんの人たちの目につくように建てられていました。」)
  • mlit-tokaido-kosatsuba - 「So as to meet the eyes of many people, they were often set at places with much passing traffic, such as the center of the village or a crossroads where main highways cross.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「多くの人々の目に触れるように、村の中心や主要な街道が交錯する交差点といった人通りの多い場所に設置されることが多かった。」)
  • shiojiri-iwadare-kosatsuba - 「it stands at a position roughly equidistant from the houses of the headmen of the shogunal domain and of the Takato domain」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「天領と高遠領との各名主宅からほぼ等距離の位置に建っています。」) (the page links this to the village's division between the two lords in 1691)
  • kotobank-kosatsu - 「The kosatsuba was surrounded with a yarai palisade, or else stone walls, turf banks and the like were built, and it was strictly managed.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「高札場には矢来をめぐらし，あるいは石垣，芝土居を築くなどして，厳重な管理が行われた。」) (Heibonsha, Sekai Daihyakka Jiten)
