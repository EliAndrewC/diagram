Commercial frontage and wealth-stratified housing

Sources: kyomachiya-jawiki, kyototuu-unagi, nagaya-jawiki

In premodern Japanese castle-towns (jokamachi) and Chinese cities the valuable street frontage is COMMERCE: shops and workshops line the high street, each facing the road, while dwellings sit behind them or in the block interior reached by alleys.4376 A merchant family lived over/behind its own shop44 (so merchant residences sit directly behind the storefronts, sharing their orientation). Housing was stratified by wealth - a few rich households in large homes or walled courtyard estates, most in modest/uniform dwellings,45 never one size - and the merchant houses stand as independent shops on the main street while the laborer warren packs dense and uniform in the back-street tenements.173
