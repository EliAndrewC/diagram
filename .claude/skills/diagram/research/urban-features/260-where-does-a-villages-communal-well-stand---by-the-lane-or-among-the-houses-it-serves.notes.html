• kotobank-tsurube-ido - 「Examples of common use by a village, a group within a village and the like are not few」 (translated; ) (Nipponica)
  • kotobank-tsujiido - 「A shared well at the roadside was called a tsuji-ido, and a shared privy at a street corner a tsuji-benjo.」 (translated; ) (Heibonsha, Sekai Daihyakka Jiten, old edition) (the entry speaks of wayside facilities generally and does not say whether villages had them)
  • seiyo-karihama-ido - 「In Karihama, where water sources were once scarce, the main sources of water until simple waterworks were laid in 1952 were dug wells and springs. The dug wells were made at a rate of about one to every 5 to 10 houses, and their number reaches about 150」 (translated; ) and 「it was found that 11 in Honura and 1 in Edaura were used in common. Around the communal wells, many had stones laid into the ground, creating a wellside space.」 (translated; ) (Honura and Edaura, the "main cove" and the "branch cove", are the two parts of Karihama; that the neighbors met there is this page's reading of 井戸端空間, the "wellside space")
