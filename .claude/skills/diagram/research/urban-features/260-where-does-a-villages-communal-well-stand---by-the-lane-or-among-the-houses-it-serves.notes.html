• kotobank-tsurube-ido - 「Examples of common use by a village or a village group are not few」 (Nipponica) (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村・村組などの共同利用の例も少なくなく」)
  • kotobank-tsujiido - 「A shared well at the roadside was called a tsuji-ido, and a shared privy at a street corner a tsuji-benjo.」 (Heibonsha, Sekai Daihyakka Jiten) (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「路傍にある共同の井戸を辻井戸，町かどにある共同便所を辻便所といった。」) (the entry speaks of wayside facilities generally and does not say whether villages had them)
  • seiyo-karihama-ido - 「In Karihama, where water sources were once scarce, the main sources of water until simple waterworks were laid in 1952 were dug wells and springs. The dug wells were made at a rate of about one to every 5 to 10 houses, and their number reaches about 150」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「かつて水源の乏しかった狩浜では、簡易水道が整備された昭和27年まで、主な用水源は堀井戸や湧水でした。堀井戸は5～10軒に1か所ほどの割合でつくられ、その数は約150か所(注1)にも及びますが」) and 「it was found that 11 in Honura and 1 in Edaura were used in common. Around the communal wells, many had stones laid into the ground, creating a wellside space.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「本浦で11か所、枝浦で1か所が共同で使われていたことがわかりました。共同井戸の周りには地面に石を敷き込み、井戸端空間を作り出しているものが多くありました。」) (that the neighbors met there is this page's reading of 井戸端空間, the "wellside space")
