  • joetsu-furoya-pdf - 「In the farming districts called zaikata, each house was fitted with a bath, but they did not heat the bath in each house every day. For the purpose of saving firewood and labor, there was a custom of fixing a rotation within the settlement for heating the bath and taking 'borrowed baths' from one another.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「在方と呼ばれた農村部では、各家に風呂が備え付けられていましたが、毎日それぞれの家で風呂を焚くことはしませんでした。薪や手間を節約する目的で、集落の中で順番を決めて風呂を焚き、互いに「もらい風呂」をする習慣があったからです。」)
