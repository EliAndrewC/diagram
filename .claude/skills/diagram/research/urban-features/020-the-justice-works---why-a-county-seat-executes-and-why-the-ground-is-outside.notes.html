• kozukappara-jawiki - 「In the Edo period there was the Kozukappara execution ground to the north and, to the south, the Suzugamori execution ground along the Tokaido (Minami-Oi, Shinagawa ward, Tokyo), and they were called the two great execution grounds.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸時代は北に小塚原刑場、南に東海道沿いの鈴ヶ森刑場（東京都品川区南大井）があり、二大刑場といわれた。」) (as above.)
  • suzugamori-jawiki - 「Together with the Kozukappara execution ground, which had been set along Edo's northern entrance (the Nikko Kaido), this was the execution ground that had been set along the southern entrance (the Tokaido).」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸の北の入口（日光街道）沿いに設置されていた小塚原刑場とともに、南の入口（東海道）沿いに設置されていた刑場であった。」)
  • rekilabo-shokeijo - 「Anyone going in or out of Edo had no choice but to pass beside the execution ground.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸を出入りする者は、否応なく刑場のそばを通らなくてはなりません。」); rekilabo-shokeijo - 「By exposing so cruel a sight to public view, the shogunate sought to forestall crime.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「このように残酷な光景をひと目に晒すことで、幕府は犯罪を未然に防ごうとしたのです。」); tsuihokei-jawiki - 「Banishment imposed on one who had broken the village's own code or village law was called murabarai or tokorobarai」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「村の掟・村法違反を犯した者に対して科した追放刑を村払・所払と称した」) (that the placement was for deterrence is the popular-history site's reading; no page read gives death pollution as the reason)
  • caishikou-enwiki - "It was located at the crossroads of Xuanwumen Outer Street and Luomashi Street." "also known as Vegetable Market Execution Ground"
  • qiushen-zhwiki - 「The qiushen, or autumn assize system, was the system by which officials of the imperial court in Qing-dynasty China deliberated and decided each year in autumn whether death sentences were to be carried out.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「秋审或秋審制度，是中國清代朝廷官员于每年秋季審決是否執行死刑的制度」)「The autumn assize began with the Three Judicial Offices - that is, the Board of Punishments, the Court of Judicial Review (Dalisi), and the Censorate - hearing the cases jointly.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「秋審開始由三法司即刑部、大理寺、都察院會同審案」)「The procedure applied to the criminals imprisoned in the several provinces and awaiting execution (jianhou) was called qiushen.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「對各省收監監候人犯的程序稱為秋審」) (the autumn assize was the Qing court's yearly autumn review deciding whether death sentences were carried out; it began with the Three Judicial Offices - Board of Punishments, Court of Revision, Censorate - hearing jointly; the procedure for the provinces' jailed prisoners awaiting execution was called qiushen.)
