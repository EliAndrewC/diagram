• kotobank-murazakai - 「The mura, as an ideal type, has at its center a settlement where houses gather, cultivated land (paddy and dry fields) spread around it, and hill and wild land spreading beyond that.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • satoyama-jawiki - 「In the Yoshino mountains of Nara Prefecture, the hills are divided, in order from nearest the village and lowest, into satoyama, uchiyama, okuyama and dake, and what counts as satoyama is the fields and mixed woodland (zokibayashi) on the slopes around the settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • satoyama-enwiki - 「Satoyama (里山) is a Japanese term applied to the border zone or area between mountain foothills and arable flat land.」
  • miura-2019-yashikiyama - 「A forestry paper titled 'Cedar woods of the plains' (近野, 1910) describes a Musashino vegetation landscape arranged around the group of buildings such as the main house: cedar wood and bamboo wood, oak wood and zelkova wood, then out to cultivated land, and further to konara wood (zokibayashi) on the outer edge.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the reading of 近野 is not given)
  • iriaichi-jawiki - 「[Iriai land] is broadly divided into two kinds: iriai-yama, forest from which firewood and charcoal wood, timber and fallen leaves for fertilizer were taken, and kusakariba, open wild land and riverbank from which fodder, roofing thatch and the like were taken.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
