• kotobank-murazakai - 「The mura, as an ideal type, has at its center a settlement where houses gather, cultivated land (paddy and dry fields) spread around it, and hill and wild land spreading beyond that.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ムラは，理念型としては，家々が集合する集落を中心とし，その周囲に田畑という耕地が展開し，その外側に山野が広がっている。」)
  • satoyama-jawiki - 「In the Yoshino mountains of Nara Prefecture, the hills are divided, in order from nearest the village and lowest, into satoyama, uchiyama, okuyama and dake, and what counts as satoyama is the fields and mixed coppice on the slopes around the settlement.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「奈良県の吉野山地では、山を村落から近く標高が低い順に「サトヤマ」「ウチヤマ」「オクヤマ」「ダケ」と区分しており、「サトヤマ」に該当するのは集落の周囲の斜面にある畑や雑木林である」)
  • satoyama-enwiki - 「Satoyama is a Japanese term applied to the border zone or area between mountain foothills and arable flat land.」
  • miura-2019-yashikiyama - 「A forestry paper titled 'Cedar woods of the plains' (近野, 1910) describes a Musashino vegetation landscape arranged around the group of buildings such as the main house: cedar wood and bamboo wood, oak wood and zelkova wood, then out to cultivated land, and further to konara wood (zokibayashi) on the outer edge.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「平地の杉林」と題した林学の論文 （近野 ，1910）には，母屋など家屋群の周囲に，スギ林とタケ類林，カシ類林，ケヤキ林から耕作地へ，さらに外縁のコナラ林（雑木林）へと配列する武蔵野の植生景観が記されている。」) (the reading of 近野 is not given)
  • iriaichi-jawiki - 「[Iriai land] is broadly divided into two kinds: iriai-yama, forest from which firewood and charcoal wood, timber and fallen leaves for fertilizer were taken, and kusakariba, open wild land and riverbank from which fodder, roofing thatch and the like were taken.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「薪炭・用材・肥料用の落葉を採取した山林である入会山と、まぐさや屋根を葺くカヤなどを採取した原野・川原である草刈場の2種類に大別される。」)
