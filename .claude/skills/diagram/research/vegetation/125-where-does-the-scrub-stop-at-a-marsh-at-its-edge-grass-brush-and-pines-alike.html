Where does the scrub stop at a marsh? At its edge, grass, brush and pines alike

Sources: none - the GM's ruling on the drawn edge and measurements on our own maps; what grows at a marsh's margin, which the edge follows, is at The marsh margin

The form the edge takes, and the two forms it is not (the GM, 2026-08-26: "the marshland is not supposed to overlap with the scrubland rendering"). Dry-ground scrub - brush discs, blades, pines - scatters, with no rule, through the reeds in the same pattern as outside them; under the rule, on one hamlet only four scrub marks stand inside its marshes, all of them on the marsh's own edge. Cutting the scatter dead at the marsh's boundary exposed a ruled line and a bare strip along the straight western edge of the toe, because the reeds themselves feather inset from that boundary. Letting every kind of scrub thin INTO the marsh across that feather closed the line, and left a line of pines standing in the bog along some 1,600 ft of the northern edge, which the GM read as the overlap still being there: "it looks like it is still overlapping!" What the map keeps from that: no pine or dry brush stands in a marsh - because, as The marsh margin records, a marsh's margin grades through sedge and grass into reed, and no pine or dry brush stands in it. The reverse rule is not needed: the marsh is drawn after the scrub and its own reeds already keep off crops, water and buildings.

Where the grass meets the reeds, the map draws a line, not a grading. The scrub's grass and the marsh's reeds are each one small block repeated across their ground, and the grass block stops at the marsh's edge, where the reed block begins: a meeting line, not a grading. That line is a map drawing convention, the edge the GM accepted with the blocks (2026-10-01: "I don't mind the issue about edges").

The rule the map follows: no dry-ground scrub is drawn inside a marsh. The scrub's grass, its brush and its pines all stop at the marsh's edge, and every patch of rough ground a map lays down after the marsh keeps out of it the same way.
