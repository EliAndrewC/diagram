• qimin-yaoshu-zhongzhu - 「The elm's nature is to shade the ground; beneath its shade the five grains do not grow.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「榆性扇地，其陰下五穀不植。」) (the chapter on planting elm and white poplar)
  • qimin-yaoshu-zhongzhu - 「According to its height and breadth, what it shades on the three sides - east, west and north - is in each case equal to the tree.」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「隨其高下廣狹，東西北三方，所扇各與樹等。」) (the author's interlinear note)
  • qimin-yaoshu-zhongzhu - 「Whoever plants it should plant on the north edge of the garden ground」 (translated from the Chinese by this project; original: 「種者，宜於園地北畔」)
