No canopy tree stands under another's crown

Sources: crown-shyness-enwiki; the GM's observation (2026-08) and the density entry above (the stems/ha band, see "Forest density and crown size"); the stand structure itself is not separately asserted

The GM, on the interactive map's highlight: "practically every tree might have a smaller tree underneath it ... I understand that real life trees can, in fact, overlap with each other ... but I don't see a single tree which is entirely subsumed within the branch structure of a different tree."

The rule. A crown is drawn only if its center is outside every crown already on the map AND no drawn crown's center lies inside it; two crowns closer than the sum of their radii may still overlap at the edge, a drawing allowance, since the pages read have fully stocked crowns holding a gap rather than interlacing.114 What is removed is the tree drawn wholly under a neighbor: a crown wholly inside another's is not part of the canopy layer, and the canopy layer is what the map draws.108 Measured on Inashiro: 787 crowns, 0 subsumed, 737 (94%) still touching a neighbor - the stand reads as closed canopy at ~one crown per 13 ft grid cell, which is the density entry's own figure: a canopy has one tree per crown, at the density that entry gives.
