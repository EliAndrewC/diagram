• hanamoku-enoki - 「In the old days, a dosojin or a small shrine was worshipped near the entrance of a village, and enoki were planted there. This is thought to have been because the enoki had since ancient times been believed to be a tree that wards off evil spirits, because it spreads its branches wide and makes much shade, and because it bears fruit that can be eaten.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「昔、 村落の出入口付近には道祖神や祠が祭られ、 そこにエノキが植えられました。 それは古来よりエノキが邪霊を避ける樹と信じられていたこと、 大きく枝を広げ、 木陰が沢山できること、 食用としての実がなること等によるものであったと思われます。」) (the page quotes a Chiba prefecture series on its great old trees)
  • ichirizuka-jawiki - 「a mound (a heap of earth) set beside the main highways at every 1 ri (about 3.927 kilometers), with Nihonbashi in Edo as the starting point」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「江戸の日本橋を起点として、主要な街道の側に1里（約3.927キロメートル）毎に設置された塚（土盛り）である。」); 「The milestone mound was made by heaping up earth 5 ken (about 9 meters) square and 1 jo (about 1.7 meters) high, and trees such as enoki were planted on top of it, with care taken that travelers could rest in the shade.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一里塚の大きさは5間（約9メートル）四方、高さ1丈（約1.7メートル）に土を盛り上げてつくられ、一里塚の上にはエノキなどの木が植えられ、木陰で旅人が休息を取れるように配慮されていた」)
  • ichirizuka-jawiki - 「The original form of the milestone mound is one set up as a pair on both sides of the highway.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「一里塚の本来の姿は街道の両側に対で設置されるものである。」)
  • ichirizuka-jawiki - 「The tree species on the five highways were enoki 55%, pine 27% and sugi 8%」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「五街道における樹種は、エノキが55%、マツが27%、スギが8%であり」)
  • kotobank-ichirizuka - 「They were built facing each other on both sides of the road, and as a rule the tree was the enoki.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「道の両側に向かい合わせに築いてあり、木はエノキが原則であった。」) (the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten entry)
