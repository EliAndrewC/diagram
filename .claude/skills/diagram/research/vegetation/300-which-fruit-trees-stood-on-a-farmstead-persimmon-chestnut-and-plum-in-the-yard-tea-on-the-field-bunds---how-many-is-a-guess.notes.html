  • esj-yashikirin-regions - 「Isawa town (9 sites): 42 species in all, an average of 13.3 species per homestead; Iide town (10 sites): 44 species, 9.8; Tonami city (5 sites): 54 species, 17.8; Hikawa town (4 sites): 29 species, 8.8. The most frequent species were sugi, chestnut and hoonoki in Isawa; sugi, chestnut and red pine in Iide; sugi, persimmon and urajirogashi in Tonami; and mochinoki, black pine and matebajii in Hikawa.」 (translated; )
  • chaen-jawiki - 「Keihan-cha is a tea garden planted in a single row on the bunds of paddies and fields; it also served to show the boundary with the neighbor, as a windbreak and to prevent the soil from washing away, and the harvest beyond what the farm household consumed was sometimes shipped to wholesalers.」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-shiboku-sanso - 「They represent the industrial crops whose commodity production developed in the early-modern period: the four trees are tea, mulberry, lacquer and paper mulberry (kozo), the three grasses hemp, safflower and indigo. Unlike grains such as rice and barley they had a high added value, and their mark is that they have both a growing process from planting to harvest and a processing process until they are finished as products.」 (translated; ); 「The shogunate restricted the planting of the four trees and three grasses by its prohibition on the free cropping of fields, but from the middle period onward they were grown as the special products of each region.」 (translated; ) (the Yamakawa entry; the four trees are tea, mulberry, lacquer and paper mulberry)
  • biwa-jawiki - 「A folk belief is recorded that if loquat is planted in a garden or a homestead, the sick will never cease.」 (translated; )
