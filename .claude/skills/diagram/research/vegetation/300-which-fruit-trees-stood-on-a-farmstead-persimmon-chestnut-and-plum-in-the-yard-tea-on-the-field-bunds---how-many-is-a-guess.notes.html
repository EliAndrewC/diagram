  • esj-yashikirin-regions - 「Isawa town (9 sites): 42 species in all, an average of 13.3 species per homestead; Iide town (10 sites): 44 species, 9.8; Tonami city (5 sites): 54 species, 17.8; Hikawa town (4 sites): 29 species, 8.8. The most frequent species were sugi, chestnut and hoonoki in Isawa; sugi, chestnut and red pine in Iide; sugi, persimmon and urajirogashi in Tonami; and mochinoki, black pine and matebajii in Hikawa.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「胆沢町（調査地9ヵ所）で総出現種数42種，屋敷あたり平均13.3種，飯豊町（10ヵ所）で44種，9.8種，砺波市（5ヵ所）で54種，17.8種，斐川町（4ヵ所）で29種，8.8種であった。出現頻度が高い上位の樹種は胆沢町でスギ，クリ，ホオノキ，飯豊町でスギ，クリ，アカマツ，砺波市でスギ，カキノキ，ウラジロガシ，斐川町でモチノキ，クロマツ，マテバジイであった。」)
  • chaen-jawiki - 「Keihan-cha is a tea garden planted in a single row on the bunds of paddies and fields; it also served to show the boundary with the neighbor, as a windbreak and to prevent the soil from washing away, and the harvest beyond what the farm household consumed was sometimes shipped to wholesalers.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦畔茶とは田畑の畦畔に一列に殖栽された茶園のことで、隣との境界を示したり、防風、土の流出防止の目的も兼ねており、農家が自家消費する以上の収穫分は問屋に出荷されたりもした。」)
  • kotobank-shiboku-sanso - 「The shogunate restricted the planting of the four trees and three grasses by its prohibition on the free cropping of fields, but from the middle period onward they were grown as the special products of each region.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「幕府は田畑勝手作の禁令で四木三草の作付を制限したが，中期以降は各地の特産物として栽培された。」) (the Yamakawa entry; the four trees are tea, mulberry, lacquer and paper mulberry)
  • biwa-jawiki - 「A folk belief is recorded that if loquat is planted in a garden or a homestead, the sick will never cease.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「ビワを庭や屋敷に植えると病人が絶えないという俗信が記録されている」)
