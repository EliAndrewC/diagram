• yashikirin-jawiki - 「A yashiki is the lot on which a house stands, and the grove formed on that lot is called a yashikirin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷とは家の建っている敷地であり、その敷地に形成された林群を屋敷林と言う。」)
  • miura-2019-yashikiyama - 「Yashikiyama is found in a document of Jōkyō 1 (1684), from the early part of the early modern period in the Mito domain (Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry ed., 1932), in the entries of the [Ōsato village register of hill land]: 'yashikiyama, 30 ken / upper-grade hill 1 tan, Hachirobei / 10 ken', 'yashikiyama, 9 ken / lower-grade hill 5 se 21 bu, Gon'emon / 9 ken', 'yashikiyama, 40 ken / middle-grade hill 2 tan 6 se, [ ]zaemon / 20 ken', and others. Judging from areas of 1 to 2.6 tan, a yashikiyama was a stand inside the homestead and is taken to be a yashikirin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「屋敷山は， 水戸藩の近世前期， 貞享元年（1684）の文書（農林省編，1932） ， 〔大里村山御改帳〕の記載， 「屋敷山　三十間／上山壹反歩　八郎兵衛／十間」 ， 「屋敷山　九間／下山五畝貳拾壹歩　權衛門／九間」 ， 「屋敷山　四拾間／中山貳反六畝歩　左衛門／貳十間」 などに見いだされる。 面積1～2.6反歩から判断すると，屋敷山は屋敷内の林分であって，屋敷林と考えられる。」) (the author's 1 to 2.6 tan leaves out his own second entry, 5 se 21 bu, which is under 1 tan; 9 by 9 ken is 81 tsubo, not the 171 bu stated; the square feet are this page's arithmetic, a ken being the side of a tsubo)
  • shakkanho-jawiki - 「1 tsubo (bu) = 10 go = 400/121 square meters, approximately 3.305785 m²」; 「1 se = 30 tsubo (bu), approximately 99.173553 m²」; 「1 tan = 10 se, approximately 991.735537 m²」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「1坪（歩）= 10 合 = 400/121平米 ≒ 3.305785 m²」; original: 「1 畝 = 30坪（歩）≒ 99.173553 m²」; original: 「1反（段）= 10 畝 ≒ 991.735537 m²」) (the modern legal values, taken for the Edo units)
  • kotobank-yashikirin-heibonsha - 「Their form, size and species are not necessarily fixed but varied」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「その形，規模，樹種は必ずしも一定しておらず，多様である」)
  • miura-2019-yashikiyama - 「A forestry paper titled 'Cedar woods of the plains' (近野, 1910) describes a Musashino vegetation landscape arranged around the group of buildings such as the main house: cedar wood and bamboo wood, oak wood and zelkova wood, then out to cultivated land, and further to konara wood (zokibayashi) on the outer edge.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「「平地の杉林」と題した林学の論文 （近野 ，1910）には，母屋など家屋群の周囲に，スギ林とタケ類林，カシ類林，ケヤキ林から耕作地へ，さらに外縁のコナラ林（雑木林）へと配列する武蔵野の植生景観が記されている。」) (the reading of 近野 is not given)
  • takehara-2004-yashikirin - 「the most frequent species were ... at Tonami City, sugi, persimmon and urajirogashi」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「出現頻度が高い上位の樹種は胆沢町でスギ，クリ，ホオノキ，飯豊町でスギ，クリ，アカマツ，砺波市でスギ，カキノキ，ウラジロガシ」)
