• yashikirin-jawiki - 「A yashiki is the lot on which a house stands, and the grove formed on that lot is called a yashikirin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
  • miura-2019-yashikiyama - 「Yashikiyama is found in a document of Jōkyō 1 (1684), from the early part of the early modern period in the Mito domain (Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry ed., 1932), in the entries of the [Ōsato village register of hill land]: 'yashikiyama, 30 ken / upper-grade hill 1 tan, Hachirobei / 10 ken', 'yashikiyama, 9 ken / lower-grade hill 5 se 21 bu, Gon'emon / 9 ken', 'yashikiyama, 40 ken / middle-grade hill 2 tan 6 se, [ ]zaemon / 20 ken', and others. Judging from areas of 1 to 2.6 tan, a yashikiyama was a stand inside the homestead and is taken to be a yashikirin.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ) (the author's 1 to 2.6 tan leaves out his own second entry, 5 se 21 bu, which is under 1 tan; 9 by 9 ken is 81 tsubo, not the 171 bu stated; 40 by 20 ken is 800 tsubo against the 780 bu of 2 tan 6 se; the square feet are this page's arithmetic, a ken being the side of a tsubo; the "[ ]" before "zaemon" marks a character the translation read as missing from the third household's name, which the original as quoted here does not show - the PDF has no text layer this record can read, so which is right is not yet checked)
