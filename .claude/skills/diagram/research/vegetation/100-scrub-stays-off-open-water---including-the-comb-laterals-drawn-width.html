Scrub stays off open water - including the comb laterals' drawn width

Sources: not applicable - a rendering rule; nothing physical asserted

  • Vegetation never draws over open water, and every drawn watercourse counts at its drawn (tapering) width - the supply laterals run up to ~14 ft wide - so no grass tuft stands on a head race or crowds its banks.
