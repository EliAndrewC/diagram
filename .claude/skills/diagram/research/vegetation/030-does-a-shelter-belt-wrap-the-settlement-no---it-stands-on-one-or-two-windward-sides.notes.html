  • umca-windbreak-manual - 「Gaps in a windbreak become funnels that concentrate wind flow, creating areas on the downwind side of the gap in which wind speeds often exceed open field wind velocities. Gaps will decrease the windbreak’s effectiveness. Access lanes through a windbreak should be avoided or minimized.」 (chapter 6, "Windbreak Continuity")
  • umca-windbreak-manual - 「Avoid creating gaps with access roads cut through a windbreak. Wind flow increases through gaps decreasing windbreak effectiveness. Where needed, design the opening at an angle to prevailing winds. Lanes or roads through single-row barriers should be avoided; where necessary, locate them 100 to 500 feet from the ends of the windbreak.」 (chapter 6, "General Design Considerations"); 「Locate access roads from 100 to 500 feet from the ends of the windbreak. If a lane must cut a windbreak, it should cut through the windbreak at an angle to prevailing winds to prevent funneling of wind and snow drifting.」 (the same chapter, "Windbreaks for Homes", Design Tips) (a modern United States extension manual: the physics of air through a constriction holds anywhere, the angled crossing is its design practice and is borrowed as such)
