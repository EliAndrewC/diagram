  • mokutan-jawiki - 「From the late Muromachi period into the Edo period, soft black charcoal that catches fire easily was produced by the in-kiln extinguishing method (taking it out after the kiln has cooled). However, it is said that the distinction between white charcoal and black charcoal was established only in the modern period and after.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; ); 「Rough charcoal was mainly white charcoal made by the out-of-kiln extinguishing method (at the last stage of charcoal burning, the kiln mouth is opened wide to let in air, the heat is raised, and the charcoal is taken out and put out by covering it with ash); it lasted long, but was hard and slow to catch fire.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; )
