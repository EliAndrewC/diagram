• kotobank-karishiki - 「that which was made by gathering, in early spring, the green grass and the young shoots of trees growing thickly on hills and fields, paddy bunds and the like, and plowing them straight into the paddies and dry fields as fertilizer」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「春先に山野・畦畔などに繁茂する青草や木の若芽を採取し，そのまま田畑にすきこんで肥料としたもの」) (the Yamakawa Nihonshi Shojiten entry)
  • kotobank-karishiki - 「The method of using grass as fertilizer spread widely from antiquity as seedling grass, and in early modern farming too it held an important place as the farmers' self-supplied fertilizer. For that reason wide grass-cutting ground was needed around the paddies, and it was also gathered from the commons of the hills and fields, which were ground used in common.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「草を肥料とする施肥法は古代から苗草として広く普及し，近世農業でも農民の自給肥料として重要な地位を占めていた。そのため水田の周囲には広大な採草地を必要とし，共同利用地である山野の入会地からも採取した。」) (the same entry; it gives no width for the grass-cutting ground)
  • kotobank-karishiki - 「a kind of self-supplied fertilizer from before the Meiji period, when purchased fertilizer (kinpi) became general: cutting brushwood and grass on the hills and fields and laying it into the paddies and dry fields as fertilizer」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「購入肥料（金肥）が一般化する明治期以前における自給肥料の一種で，山野で刈り取ったしば草を田畑に敷き肥料とすること。」) (the Heibonsha Sekai Daihyakka entry, by Mitsuhashi Tokio)
  • kotobank-azebiki - 「azebiki (noun): In a land survey, surveying a field with the bund width of one shaku (about 30 cm) and one shaku at each side of the bund removed from its area.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「検地の時、耕地の面積から畔幅一尺（約三〇センチメートル）と畔際左右一尺ずつを除いて検地すること。」); 「One shaku at each bund edge is to be excluded; but for the adjoining land, one shaku at the bund edge is likewise deducted; the bund being reckoned at one shaku, the bund deduction, together with the adjoining land, shall be reckoned at one shaku five sun each」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「あせ際壱尺宛可除、但、類地もあせ際壱尺宛引之、あせ壱尺之積、類地共にあせ引壱尺五寸宛之積たるへし」) (the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's definition, then its earliest example, from the Tokugawa Kinreiko, a collection of the shogunate's laws and orders, Kyoho 11 (1726), eighth month; a sun is a tenth of a shaku, about 3 cm; the translation of the order's middle clauses is tentative; a survey reckoning for tax, not a measured strip)
  • hiroshima-keihan-manual - 「To grasp the state of bund management, over 2007 to 2008 a questionnaire survey on bund management was carried out among the collective farms in the prefecture, and answers were received from 81 corporations.」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦畔管理の状況を把握するために，平成 19 年～20 年にかけて，県内の集落法人を対象に畦畔管理に関するアンケート調査を行い，81 法人より回答を得ました。」); 「Bund mowing is done with brush cutters. The number of mowings a year averages 3.4」 (translated from the Japanese by this project; original: 「畦畔の草刈は刈払機を用いて行われています。年間の草刈回数は平均で 3.4 回」) (modern and mechanized)
