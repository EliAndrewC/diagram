  • kotobank-karishiki - 「The method of using grass as fertilizer spread widely from antiquity as seedling grass, and in early modern farming too it held an important place as the farmers' self-supplied fertilizer.   • kotobank-karishiki - 「a kind of self-supplied fertilizer from before the Meiji period, when purchased fertilizer (kinpi) became general: cutting brushwood and grass on the hills and fields and laying it into the paddies and dry fields as fertilizer」 (translated; ) (the Heibonsha Sekai Daihyakka entry, by Mitsuhashi Tokio)
  • kotobank-azebiki - 「azebiki (noun): In a land survey, surveying a field with the bund width of one shaku (about 30 cm) and one shaku at each side of the bund removed from its area.」 (translated; ); 「One shaku at each bund edge is to be excluded; but for the adjoining land, one shaku at the bund edge is likewise deducted; the bund being reckoned at one shaku, the bund deduction, together with the adjoining land, shall be reckoned at one shaku five sun each」 (translated; ) (the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's definition, then its earliest example, from the Tokugawa Kinreiko, a collection of the shogunate's laws and orders, Kyoho 11 (1726), eighth month; a sun is a tenth of a shaku, about 3 cm; the translation of the order's middle clauses is tentative; a survey reckoning for tax, not a measured strip)
