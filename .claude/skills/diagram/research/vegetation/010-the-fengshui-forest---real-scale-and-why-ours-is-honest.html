The fengshui forest - real scale, and why ours is honest

Sources: forests-2020, hu-2011-fengshui-patches, afcd-ncsc-9-06, hk-herbarium-fsw, afcd-hkbio-8, yuan-liu-2009, park-2013-maeulsup, kweon-youn-2021, ma-2017-fengshui-carbon, coggins-minor-2018, fengshui-woodland-enwiki, zhongshan-fsw-2016, huangshan-daily-jiekou

  • Scale - the real numbers. How many: Fujian villages keep about two fengshui forests each (the province-wide figure the Forests 2020 paper reports from Xu, Qiu and Wang 2012)1 and two Buyi villages in Guizhou hold two parcels each,75 so the map's back grove plus water-mouth cluster is the attested count. How dense: stem density ~3,400 stems/ha (「3403 plants/ha」), basal area ~49 m²/ha in well-protected Pearl River Delta patches (one 1200 m² transect each; values the authors call consistent with well-developed evergreen broadleaved forests),2 and a basal area of 30.0-52.5 m²/ha in five Guangzhou fragments.74 How large - the back grove: patch area is highly variable. The one large measured sample readable is Hong Kong's territory-wide survey of 115 village woods with GIS-drawn boundaries - the wood 「seen today behind many villages, typically in a crescent shape」:76 median about 1 ha, mean 1.1 ha, half of them under a hectare, four in ten between one and two, one in ten at two or more, the range 0.06-5.3 ha69 (the Herbarium's summary: 「The average area of a fung shui wood is 1 hectare」, 600 m² to 6 ha).70 Across the delta in Zhongshan, Guangdong, a 2016 survey of the city's fengshui groves counts 66 of them in 66 natural villages totaling 269.85 ha - about 4.1 ha each on average, the record's own arithmetic over a total that the tail of large groves pulls up.79 The great lineage villages of Fujian are the tail: fengshui forests listed as 「A forest over three hectares」 and 「A forest over six hectares」 for two Youxi County villages, and Lingtou's over 20 ha in total, kept for over 800 years,77 a 22-hectare houlongshan grove in Nanjing County saved in 1958-59,3 and a 133 ha lineage forest at Letu.71 A TYPICAL village grove is nevertheless a small forest patch,5 and the ~1-2 ha back grove drawn here sits in the upper half of the measured band - a deliberate choice, recorded: the belt must read as a forest at two feet to the pixel. How large - the water-mouth cluster: no readable Chinese source measures one4 - the nearest analogue is the Korean village-entrance grove (동구숲), the analogue of the water-mouth grove (the Chinese grove type at the village's entrance, 「where the stream flows out of the village」 - Fujian's forests 「are mainly located near a villages’ entrances, ancestral halls, and temples」78): the most numerous type in the field survey (42 of the 100 groves surveyed), averaging 7,149 m² and standing ~730 m from the village center,72 while a 24-grove sample around Mt. Jiri averages 0.22 ha (range 0.025-0.63 ha).73 A Huangshan Daily piece on Jiekou (Shexian County, Anhui) describes that village's water-mouth grove as over a hundred mu - about 7 ha - with nearly a thousand ancient trees, the largest in East China: the one Chinese water-mouth figure found, an extreme rather than a type.80 So the ~0.1-0.5 ha water-mouth cluster is a GUESS that sits inside the analogues' spread, positioned by Coggins' topographic rule.7 How many trees: no readable source counts a Chinese grove's trees (note 6);6 the Korean groves hold 「less than 10 to more than 100」 each,73 and at the Pearl-delta density a 1-2 ha belt would carry some 3,400-6,800 stems of all sizes - so the 100-300 mature canopy trees over a dense bamboo/shrub understory drawn for the belt, and a few dozen big old trees at the water mouth, remain a GUESS bracketed by those figures. The back belt reads as a real small FOREST (clearly the largest vegetation feature, a wall of dozens-to-hundreds of crowns - the grove fills its polygon with overlapping dense clumps so a big belt does not read as a handful of lone trees), the water-mouth as a distinct smaller cluster, plus the bamboo/fruit scatter through the village. Hoshigaoka draws a ~1-2 ha embracing windward belt + a ~0.3 ha water-mouth + the leafy scatter.

  • Why the sizing is honest at this scale. At two feet to the pixel a ~1-2 ha grove is genuinely large relative to the ~11 ha built cluster (~10-18%), so the belt reads as the dominant feature without being cartoonishly oversized.
