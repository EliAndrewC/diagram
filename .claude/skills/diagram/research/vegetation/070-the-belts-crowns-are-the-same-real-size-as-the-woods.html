The belt's crowns are the same real size as the woods'

Sources: the entry above

The GM: "The trees in the woodland commons look bigger than the trees in the windbreak forest. Is that intentional?" They are not different: one crown size, the 5-8 m across taken from two abstracts and on no page read - a GUESS - sizes every stand on the map - the belt's cedars included (measured on Inashiro: belt median radius 7.6 ft, ~15 ft across; 325 crowns in the belt, 505 on the map).

No record read from before modern times gives a crown's width for a belt or any wood, so the belt's crowns rest on the same modern guess as the woods'. What the old record does say of trees against the wind is that an Edo rule for coastal sugi planted them thicker where the wind was fierce (how thickly woods were stocked before modern times) - a denser planting, not a different crown.
