• seimai-jawiki - 「it is said that at the time several waterwheels were set on the Onden river」 (translated; ); 「The waterwheel's power moves pestles up and down and polishes brown rice.」 (translated; ); 「Katsushika Hokusai, Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, "The Waterwheel at Onden". It depicts a waterwheel that stood on the Onden river in Edo. The Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji is a collection of pictures of famous places of the late Edo period (the 1830s)」 (translated; ) (the caption to Hokusai's "Waterwheel at Onden" from the Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, of the 1830s)
  • nta-nengumai-genmai - 「The basis was to pay the annual tax in brown rice. Not only for the tax: in the Edo period the word "rice" meant brown rice. Both the rice that commoners paid to their lords as tax and the rice that commoners shipped as merchandise were rice in the state of brown rice, threshed and hulled (momisuri).」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-tsukigomeya - 「Tsukigome is the work of polishing rice; in the early modern period those who made it their specialty arose, were called tsukigomeya, and took charge of the last stage of the rice trade.」 (translated; ); 「In Edo, as of 1744 (Enkyo 1), there were 18 guilds, some 2,100 tsukigomeya, doing business using some 5,500 mortars.」 (translated; ) (the Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Daihyakka Jiten entry, by Ito Yoshikazu)
