• kotobank-gogura - 「A storehouse set up in villages in the Edo period to hold tax rice, or grain stored against bad harvests, among other uses.」 (translated; ) and 「Not every village had one: there were also cases where several villages shared one, or where the headman's storehouse was used in its place.」 (translated; ) (Seisen-ban Nihon kokugo daijiten)
  • yabu-yamaji-gogura - 「At Yamaji a single gogura building stands, its door turned to the old former prefectural road.」 (translated; ) and 「It is a two-story building with a tiled roof, 340 centimeters across the front and 290 centimeters along the side, and 500 centimeters high to the roof.」 (translated; )
  • kotobank-gogura - 「The gogura is attached within the village's dwellings; mostly it is built on dry-field land; where there is a vacant house site or the like, it is also built on a house plot.」 (translated; ) (quoted in the dictionary from Jikata hanreiroku, 1794, book 6)
