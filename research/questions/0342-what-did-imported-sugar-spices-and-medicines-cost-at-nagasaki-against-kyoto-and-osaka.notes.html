  • yao-2011-edo-sato-shokubunka - 「When the Nagasaki Kaisho was set up in Genroku 10 (1697) and the kakarimono, an import duty, came to be laid on imported goods, sugar was taxed at about 200%, the price at auction became 300% of the original cost, and the retail price in Osaka came to nearly 300 monme, close to 500%.」 (translated; ); 「Further, in the early 1800s, in the later Edo period, sugar became a speculative good, and though the trading price at Dejima in Bunka 5 (1808) fell to 69 monme for 100 kin, the retail price within the country was 660 monme for 100 kin, that is, at times over 5,000 yen a kilogram in today's money.」 (translated; )
