• edo-sanza-jawiki - 「By about the beginning of the Enpo era (the 1670s) the shogunate had come to permit only four troupes - the Nakamura-za, the Ichimura-za, the Morita-za and the Yamamura-za - to raise a yagura.」 (translated; ); shibaigoya-jawiki - 「In the late Edo period, kabuki theaters (shibai-goya) with roofs and seating were established, centered on Edo, Kyoto and Osaka.」 (translated; ) (licensed permanent houses, in a tier of three cities rather than one capital; the festival crowd as the reason is this page's)
  • kotobank-miyaji-shibai - 「In the Edo period, a booth-built play performed under a temporary license in the precincts of a shrine or temple.」 (translated; ); edo-sanza-jawiki - 「Conversely, a playhouse without a yagura was called a miyaji shibai, and various restrictions were imposed on it: that it be a simple booth structure, that no roof be put on anything other than over the stage, that it not use fittings such as a draw curtain, a revolving stage or a hanamichi.」 (translated; ) (the play in a precinct, with the regulation beside it that put no roof on anything but the stage; the audience's position between stage and hall is this page's)
