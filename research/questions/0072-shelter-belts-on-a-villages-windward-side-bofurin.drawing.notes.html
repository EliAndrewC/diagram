  • umca-windbreak-manual - 「Avoid creating gaps with access roads cut through a windbreak. Wind flow increases through gaps decreasing windbreak effectiveness. Where needed, design the opening at an angle to prevailing winds. Lanes or roads through single-row barriers should be avoided; where necessary, locate them 100 to 500 feet from the ends of the windbreak.」 (chapter 6, "General Design Considerations"); 「Locate access roads from 100 to 500 feet from the ends of the windbreak.
