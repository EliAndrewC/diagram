  • thcdc-half-moon-pond - 「Most traditional households of the early agricultural era were located between fields. With no running tap water, most family make use of local materials in the construction of half moon ponds, that is to introduce natural stream (commonly known as ‘field water’) into the household as water source for half moon ponds.」「Therefore, a water channel most commonly known as ‘waterway’ is built from the ‘dragon side’ (left rear) of the house, entering the half moon pond through the drainage channel around the outside of the main building.」「The folk saying is that waterflow represents flow of wealth. If the waterflow is fast and rapid, it means that wealth is coming and leaving fast and cannot be kept.」「you may find that the appropriate height of foundation has been deliberately measured when constructing the ‘waterway’ drainage channel, so as to control the speed of waterflow not to be too fast, causing an overflow, thereby creating a ‘soft flow of water’.」
