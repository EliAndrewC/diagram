How our maps draw baths (furo)

On our building plans a samurai residence's bath is one of its rooms or a small addition to it, never a pavilion standing apart, since no page we read shows a samurai compound's bath as a building of its own. In history a house of middle rank or below often had none, as most such samurai washed from a tub or went to the public bath.

  • How do our maps place the bath?
    On the house's service side, as a small addition abutting the kitchen. The intendant's residence at the Takayama jin'ya listed its earth floor and kitchen, its well and its bath together, and around 1600, as the Edo period began, a warrior family's bath stood in a corner of the doma; setting the bath with the doma, the kitchen and the well is this project's reading of the two. On the magistrate's office compound our maps generate, it abuts the side of the kitchen that faces its court, and never stands in a garden or a court, where it would be the pavilion the record does not find.
  • Where do our maps put the kitchen's well?
    Past the bath, as far as 20 ft out from the kitchen. The Takayama intendant's residence lists its bath together with its well and kitchen, so this project keeps the three together; that the one well also filled the bath is our reading, not something the list says.
  • A fire-water tub stands on the bath's yard side.
    A bath's hearth was a fire risk, so a tub of water stands ready beside it.
  • The bath is drawn 10 by 8 ft - a GUESS.
    No page we read gives a bath's size. It is drawn small so that the whole house keeps to the size of the residence our maps draw, 49 tsubo (~1,740 sq ft) (How our maps lay out samurai residences and their rooms (buke yashiki)).
  • A plan may draw its bath larger as one of its resident's particulars - a GUESS.
    Where a plan's story gives its magistrate a fondness for the bath, the bath is drawn larger, up to about 18 by 12 ft, still on the house's service side; no page we read gives a bath's size to draw it from. Such a house is already larger than the residence the other plans keep to, and the larger bath adds only about 4 tsubo to it.
