  • yokushitsu-kotobank - 「At that time nobles and warrior houses had a small bath for the family, but it was only a tub one person could get into, with a hearth built on, in a corner of the doma; because provision against the danger of fire and the high cost of fuel was inadequate, it was seldom used.」 (translated; ) (the Nipponica entry; "at that time" is the late sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries of the sentence before it)
