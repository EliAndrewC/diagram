• jokamachi-jawiki - 「The townsman district had a smaller area per household than the samurai district, and stood built up along the highway with no gaps between the buildings.」 (translated; ); machiya-shoka-jawiki - 「Compared with a farmhouse, whose main building stands deep inside a gated lot, the characteristic of the machiya is that they stand built in a relatively even row facing the street.」 (translated; ) (the townhouse page setting the street-facing row against the gated farmhouse plot)
  • udatsu-jawiki - 「The part where the roof between one's own house and the neighboring house is raised a little came to be called the udatsu.」 (translated; ) (separate houses built adjoining; the drawn hairline seam is a map drawing convention)
  • roji-jawiki - 「The width of the roji between one back-tenement building and the next was about three to six shaku (about 0.9 m to 1.8 m).」 (translated; ); 「Down the middle of the roji a row of drain boards was laid, and under them a small ditch 6 to 7 sun (18 to 21 cm) wide was dug.」 (translated; ); 「The place just inside behind the merchant houses, or the middle of the roji, widened out, and a shared well and a shared water supply were set there.」 (translated; ); 「Both the waste water from the water place at the well-side and the rainwater from the nagaya roofs flowed into this ditch.」 (translated; ) (the lane between one tenement building and the next, with a drain down its middle; two houses back to back under one ridge share their rear wall, so the gap falls between one row and the next, and the eaves shed the roofs' rainwater into that ditch)
  • bukeyashiki-wiki - 「A buke-yashiki is a residence, enclosed by a hedge, a wall, a gate and the like, in which a warrior household lived.」 (translated; ); 「Because the lower ashigaru lived not in a yashiki but in housing of the nagaya form, these nagaya are called ashigaru nagaya.」 (translated; ) (the enclosed plot of the upper samurai, and the ashigaru in row housing)
  • udatsu-jawiki - 「The wall of the keraba portion (the two ends of a gable roof) where it meets the neighboring house was raised slightly and given an independent small roof; this came to be called an udatsu (hon-udatsu).」 (translated; )
  • nagaya-jawiki - 「A thing in which multiple dwelling units run together horizontally and share walls.」 (translated; ) (the shared wall)
