What does a square meter of housing cost a year in the US today?

Rokugan's money rules tell players what a koku would be worth today partly by what a laborer's room costs now, and housing moves that answer more than any other good. The findings below set out the US figures a room could be priced at, each per square meter a year where the sources allow it, and say what each one measures.

No US survey gives a rent per square meter: the rents come without floor areas and the floor areas without rents, so each figure per square meter below divides one survey's rent by another's floor area, and is this project's arithmetic. None of them sets a koku's value or a Rokugani rent, and the money rules' own list of today's prices is not changed here. The poverty-line allowance of 3 m² (~32 sq ft) a person and its rents are in How do economic historians carry a basket of goods into another time's money?

The asking rent, over a typical home's floor area

  • The median rent asked for a vacant rental home was $1,531 a month in April-June 2026.
    That is the national median for units vacant and on offer in the second quarter of 2026, or $18,372 a year.
  • Over the mean rented home: about $182 a m² (~$17 a sq ft) a year.
    The 40.61 million rented homes of 2020 had 44.22 billion sq ft between them, a mean of 1,089 sq ft (101 m²); the rented count includes households living rent-free. The year's asking rent over that floor area is $182 a m²; it mixes a 2026 rent with a 2020 floor area.
  • Over the median home: about $132 a m² (~$12 a sq ft) a year.
    The median occupied home of 2023, owned or rented, had 1,500 sq ft (139 m²), and the year's asking rent over it is $132 a m². Most US homes are owned, and an owned home averages about twice a rented one's floor area, so this divides a rent by an owner's floor area.
  • The money rules' $269 a m² a year is not rebuilt here.
    Taken over the same asking rent, it would mean a home of about 68 m² (~735 sq ft).

The lowest rents the government sets, with no floor area

  • The housing agency's floor for a modest two-bedroom home in 2027 is $1,014 a month.
    It is the median rent across the country's non-metropolitan areas, and no area's two-bedroom rent may fall below it, or below its state's lower median.
  • An efficiency is set at 0.70 to 0.88 of the two-bedroom rent, and a single room at 0.75 of an efficiency.
    Those are the agency's rules, and in an area at the national floor they give an efficiency $710-892 a month and a single room $532-669 a month, about $6,400-8,000 a year.
  • No rent per m² follows from them.
    The notice gives no floor area for any of its units, so these rents cannot be put per square meter.

The cost to build, without the land

  • Building a new US house cost about $1,741 a m² (~$162 a sq ft) in 2024, without its lot.
    Builders put the construction cost of an average new house of 2,647 sq ft at $428,215, 64.4% of its price; the lot, overhead and profit are counted apart. That is $162 a sq ft, or $1,741 a m².
  • It is a cost paid once, not a rate a year.
    To become a cost per m² a year it needs a building life or a rate of depreciation, which none of these sources gives and which is not chosen here.
