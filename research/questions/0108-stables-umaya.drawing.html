How our maps draw stables (umaya)

An official's compound on our maps keeps its horses in a small stable of its own, a few bays long, with quarters in it for the grooms and an earth-floored passage before the stalls, as a warrior's stable had.

  • The stalls are drawn about one ken (~6 ft) wide, on board floors.
    A warrior's stall was one bay wide, on a raised timber floor, and our maps take a bay as one ken, by convention.
  • A stall's depth, and so its area, is our own guess.
    No page we read gives a warrior's stall its depth, so the depth drawn is a GUESS.
  • The number of horses the stable holds is our own guess.
    No page we read says how many horses a county magistrate's post kept, so the number is a GUESS.
