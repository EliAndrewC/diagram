Wa-raku Web (intojapanwaraku.com), an article on the Edo lending libraries (in Japanese; https://intojapanwaraku.com/rock/culture-rock/128664/)

What it is: A culture magazine's web article on how Edo townspeople rented books from peddling lending librarians, quoting Nagatomo Chiyoji's study of the early modern lending libraries (1997) for its scale of fees.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable quotation found of Nagatomo's scale of reading fees by the size of a work, in silver; the quotation gives no years, Nagatomo's book was not read, and the page's own words on how long a book was kept are not his.

Used for: the lending fee by the number of volumes (Money and prices)
