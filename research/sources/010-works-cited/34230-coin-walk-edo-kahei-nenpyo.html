"江戸時代貨幣年表" ("A chronology of Edo-period money"), coin-walk.site, a website whose author the page does not name (https://coin-walk.site/J063.htm)

What it is: A year-by-year table of the money of the Edo period, giving each year's coin events and its rice price, gold-silver rate and copper rate, the three series taken, by the page's own statement, from the 1986 reference book 『日本史資料総覧』.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only year-by-year gold and copper rate read for the 1690s to the 1780s and for the 1860s. It names Edo for three early years and Osaka only for its rice from 1685, and does not say whose gold and copper rates the reference book printed; the book itself was not read; and in the years of mixed coins, 1706-1721 and 1736, its figures are for coins of different fineness, a few labeled by coin and most not.

Used for: the gold and copper rates before 1787 and in the 1860s (Money and prices)
