What it is: The section on reeling silk from the cocoons in the late Ming encyclopedia of crafts by Song Yingxing, describing the reeling frame and the silk reeled in a day.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one rate read for reeling silk, by weight in a full day: thirty liang of the ordinary silk for damask and gauze, but only twenty of the head-wrap silk, which is reeled from fewer cocoons (ten-odd at a time, against twenty). It is a scholar's account of the best practice he knew - he ranks the Huzhou reeling frame above Sichuan's - not a count of what households did; it times reeling only, not weaving.

Used for: the silk one person could reel in a day (Money and prices)
