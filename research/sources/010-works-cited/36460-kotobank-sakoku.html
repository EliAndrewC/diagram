kotobank-sakoku

"Sakoku" (the closing of the country), Kotobank, which gathers the entries of several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias on one page (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%8E%96%E5%9B%BD-68867)

What it is: The Japanese reference entries on the shogunate's restrictions on foreign contact, among them the Digital Daijisen, the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia and the Nipponica encyclopedia.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dates of the edicts and which foreign trade continued, but the entries do not list the same partners: the Obunsha and Hofu entries name only China and the Dutch, the Digital Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Nipponica add Korea, the Heibonsha, Britannica and Yamakawa entries add Ryukyu and set Korea and Ryukyu apart from the Nagasaki trade, and only a snippet from the older Heibonsha encyclopedia names the Ainu trade through Matsumae. The entries date the policy's start differently, to 1633-39, to 1639 or to 1641, and its end to 1853 or 1854; each note here names the entry it quotes.

Used for: The dates of Japan's closing and the trade left open (Money and prices)
