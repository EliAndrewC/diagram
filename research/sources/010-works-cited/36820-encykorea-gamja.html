Jo Jae-yeong (조재영), "감자" (Potato), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture (한국민족문화대백과사전), the Academy of Korean Studies (in Korean; https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0000815)

What it is: The encyclopedia's signed article on the potato, written in 1995 and last revised in 2024, giving its scientific name, its names in Chinese characters and the reported stories of its arrival.

Why it applies, and its limits: It identifies the plant, and reports the Oju yeonmun, the Wonjeobo and the colonial farm association's journal of 1912, its only authority for potatoes at Seoul in 1879 and 1883. None of those texts was read. It says itself that the exact year of arrival is unknown, so 1824-25 is only the Oju yeonmun's report. Its story of the ban at Musan carries no date. It puts the British ship of 1832 off the coast of modern Jeonbuk, while the visit that fits, the Lord Amherst's, is usually placed at Godae Island in Chungcheong. Its growing directions, varieties and tonnages are twentieth-century figures, with chemical fertilizer, and say nothing of the setting.
