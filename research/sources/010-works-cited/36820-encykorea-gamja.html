Jo Jae-yeong (조재영), "감자" (Potato), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture (한국민족문화대백과사전), the Academy of Korean Studies (in Korean; https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0000815)

What it is: The encyclopedia's signed article on the potato, giving its scientific name, older names and the reported stories of its arrival.

Why it applies, and its limits: It identifies the plant, and reports the Oju yeonmun, the Wonjeobo and a farm journal of 1912. None of those texts was read. Its story of the ban at Musan carries no date.

Used for: The white potato in Korea: names, arrival and spread (Money and prices)
