edo-royashiki-nuka-akidawara

『七十冊物類集』 (Classified collection in seventy volumes), 71 volumes, [43], part 42, on allowances and rewards for the jail's constables and kitchen staff who worked to cut its costs, a manuscript copy among the records the shogunate's Edo town magistrates handed on, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548603/1/34 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548603/1/40 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548603)

What it is: A volume of the Edo town magistrates' precedents on the Kodenmacho jail's running costs. The papers read say that the bran and the empty bales (糠明俵) of the jail's kitchen had been a perquisite of its kitchen staff until the reform of 1843, after which they were sold every month and the money added to the jail's budget, and, in a later year, give the sum the sale of the bran and empty bales left from the prisoners' ration rice had brought in the year before.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows an institution in Edo selling its empty bales month by month for money, and treating them before 1843 as a recognized perquisite. It gives a year's proceeds but not the number of bales, and those proceeds are for the kitchen's bran and its empty bales sold together, so neither the bales' share nor a price a bale comes from it; the paper giving the proceeds is dated only by its zodiac year. The hand is cursive, and only the lines cited were read.

Used for: the Edo jail's sale of its kitchen's bran and empty bales (Money and prices)
