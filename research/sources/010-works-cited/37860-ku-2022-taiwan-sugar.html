ku-2022-taiwan-sugar

Ku Hui-wen 古慧雯, "Qingmo kaigang hou de Taiwan tangye" 清末開港後的台灣糖業 (Taiwan's sugar industry after the opening of its ports in the late Qing), Jingji lunwen congkan 經濟論文叢刊 (Taiwan Economic Review) 50, no. 3 (2022), the journal's PDF (https://econ.ntu.edu.tw/ter/new/data/new/TER50-3/TER503-3.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's study of Taiwan's sugar exports from the 1860s to 1895, from the customs returns and the archive of the trading house Jardine Matheson, printing in its appendix the house's invoice and sales account for one cargo of brown sugar bought at Takow and sold at Yokohama in 1871.

Why it applies, and its limits: The two accounts give one cargo's buying price, every charge at both ends and its selling price, from the trader's own books. It is a foreign house's cargo, in silver dollars, sold in a Japanese treaty port three years after Japan's premodern period ends in this record, so it shows what a sea cargo's gap was made of and says nothing of a Chinese city's price. The 1.77 and the 8% are the author's reckoning from the tables; the 5% average of fourteen accounts is hers, with the accounts not printed.

Used for: one cargo of Taiwan sugar bought at Takow and sold at Yokohama in 1871, with its charges (Money and prices)
