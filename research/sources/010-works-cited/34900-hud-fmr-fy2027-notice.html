US Department of Housing and Urban Development, "Fair Market Rents for the Housing Choice Voucher Program, Moderate Rehabilitation Single Room Occupancy Program, and Other Programs, Fiscal Year 2027", Federal Register notice, public-inspection copy, scheduled for publication on 2026-09-01, PDF (https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-17891.pdf)

What it is: The notice in which the federal housing agency sets the rents its housing vouchers pay for the year, with the rules that derive an efficiency's and a single room's rent from a two-bedroom unit's, and the national rent of non-metropolitan areas that serves as their floor.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: the cheapest rents the US government recognizes, as a yardstick for a laborer's room, never a figure of the setting. Its rents are gross rents, shelter rent plus utilities, set by rule at the 40th percentile of what recent movers paid for a modest two-bedroom unit in the Census Bureau's survey, and its national non-metropolitan floor is a median of those; an efficiency's rent and a single room's are fixed fractions of the two-bedroom's, not observed for a room; it gives no floor area, so no rent per square meter follows from it.

Used for: the lowest US rents, a single room's among them (Comparative money: other societies)
