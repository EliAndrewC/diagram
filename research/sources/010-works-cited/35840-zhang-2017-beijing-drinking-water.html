zhang-2017-beijing-drinking-water

Lei Zhang, "One City with Two Water: Drinking Water in Beijing, 1644-1900", journal article, December 2017, its abstract on Lingnan Scholars, Lingnan University (https://scholars.ln.edu.hk/en/publications/one-city-with-two-water-drinking-water-in-beijing-1644-1900/)

What it is: A historian's article on how Qing Beijing got its drinking water, read here in its abstract: wells of salty water, and two systems of water carriers, the state's in the Inner City and private ones in the Outer City.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that Beijing's households had water carried to them by carriers in the Qing. Only the abstract was read, so it gives no price for the water and no account of which households paid.

Used for: water carriers in Qing Beijing (Money and prices)
