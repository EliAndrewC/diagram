Wang Zhiguo and Wang Siming, an article on the introduction and development of American cotton in China, Chinese Agricultural Science Bulletin 22, no. 3 (2006): 421 (https://www.casb.org.cn/EN/Y2006/V22/I3/421)

What it is: A Chinese agricultural journal's article tracing American cotton in China over nearly 150 years.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one dated statement the record has read of when American cotton entered China, and nothing read confirms it. The date can only bound when New World cotton arrived: the abstract says nothing of the older cottons grown before it, and an "introduction" need not mean the crop was grown at any scale. Only its abstract was read: the abstract names the year and three stages and gives no place, no share of the crop and no evidence for the date.

Used for: The arrival of American cotton in China (Money and prices)
