oamishirasato-tenpo13-bukka

Oami Shirasato Town History (大網白里町史), part 1, chapter 3, the early modern period, section 6, "(2) 酒造業" ("Sake brewing", title translated), pp. 574-578 (in Japanese; Oamishirasato City Digital Museum, https://adeac.jp/oamishirasato-city/texthtml/d100010/mp100010-100010/ht012240)

What it is: The town history's section on village sake brewing in what is now Oamishirasato, Chiba: the shogunate's brewing licenses and limits, one brewing house's license and brewing amounts from 1693 into the 1860s, a brewer's list of tools, and a table of the wages and prices the shogunate set for the village group of Nagata in 1842, drawn from a village document.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its table is the one source found that sets a sake price and a day laborer's wage side by side in the same document. Both are official prices ordered under the Tenpo reforms for a rural village group, not prices found in the market or in Edo, and the table's columns run together on the web page, so which wage is the man's and which the woman's is read from the order they are listed in.

Used for: the official price of local and Itami sake a sho and a farm day laborer's wage in a village of 1842, and its taverns that sold their own brew (Money and prices)
