Tirthankar Roy, "Consumption of Cotton Cloth in India, 1795-1940", CEPR Discussion Paper 8669, November 2011, as saved by the Wayback Machine (https://web.archive.org/web/20240413163623/https://repec.cepr.org/repec/cpr/ceprdp/DP8669.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's estimate of how much cotton cloth India produced and consumed from 1795 to 1940, with imports by date and some family budgets.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only long series found of India's own cloth beside its imports. Its figures before about 1900 are reconstructed from cotton acreage, yields and trade by stated assumptions, not counted, and the discussion-paper series' standard notice calls such papers provisional; it was published in 2012 in the Australian Economic History Review, which was not read. It speaks for India as a whole, and its figures for poor households are a handful of surveys.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
