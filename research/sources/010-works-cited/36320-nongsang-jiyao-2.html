nongsang-jiyao-2

Sinong si (司農司, Office of Agriculture), Nongsang jiyao (農桑輯要, Essentials of Agriculture and Sericulture), chapter 2, the Siku quanshu text on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/農桑輯要_(四庫全書本)/卷2)

What it is: The chapter on sowing in a farming manual, the page's own heading naming the Yuan dynasty's Office of Agriculture as its compiler, with a section arguing that ramie and cotton can be grown outside their old homes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the manual whose date and sponsor the modern accounts dispute, and it says in its own words where cotton had lately been planted. The compiler's name is the Siku edition's heading, and the preface of 1273 is not on this page, so the page cannot settle the dispute by itself.

Used for: cotton newly grown in Shaanxi by the 1270s (Money and prices)
