Dorian Q Fuller, Louis Champion, Cristina Cobo Castillo, Anna den Hollander and others, "Cotton and post-Neolithic investment agriculture in tropical Asia and Africa, with two routes to West Africa", Journal of Archaeological Science: Reports 57 (2024), article 104649 (https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10206456/1/Cotton%20investment%20agriculture%20%26%202%20routes%20thru%20Africa.pdf)

What it is: A review by archaeobotanists of published cotton finds across Asia and Africa, from the earliest fibers to about the 18th century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one work read that sets out the Old World species, their origins and the Indian chronology together. Its authors say seeds of the two species have proven difficult to tell apart, so the early South Asian finds are presumed to be G. arboreum, not identified as it; it says little of East Asia beyond the date of annual forms in China.

Used for: Old World cotton species and India's earliest dates (Money and prices)
