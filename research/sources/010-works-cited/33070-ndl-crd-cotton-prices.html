ndl-crd-cotton-prices

Osaka Prefectural Nakanoshima Library, an answer in the National Diet Library's Collaborative Reference Database on the price of cotton cloth in Osaka in the Edo period (case 6001013626, 2016; in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?page=ref_view&id=1000187424)

What it is: A reference librarian's answer to a reader who asked what cotton cloth cost in Edo-period Osaka, naming the price series that hold it and quoting the dated prices of a tan that a private researcher, Koyaizu Nobuo, compiled from town and city histories and old records.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated prices of a tan of cotton at Ako and Nishinomiya, a 1716 price of white cotton, and a 1692 price of habutae silk. The figures are quoted at two removes, through Koyaizu's compilation from the city histories; the 1692 silk price is taken from Saikaku's fiction; and the 1716 cotton price is in the Yotsuho silver coin of its day, not the coin of the later prices.

Used for: cotton prices a tan at Ako and Nishinomiya, 1781-1790, white cotton in 1716, and habutae in 1692 (Money and prices)
