ndl-crd-cotton-prices

Osaka Prefectural Nakanoshima Library, an answer in the National Diet Library's Collaborative Reference Database on the price of a tan of silk and of cotton goods in Osaka in the mid-Edo period (case 6001013626, 2015; in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?page=ref_view&id=1000187424)

What it is: A reference librarian's answer to a reader who asked what a tan of silk goods and of cotton and hemp cloth cost in mid-Edo Osaka, naming the price series that hold it and quoting the dated prices of a tan that a private researcher, Koyaizu Nobuo, compiled from many sources, centered on the Tokai region.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated prices of a tan of cotton at Ako and Nishinomiya, a 1716 price of white cotton, and a 1692 price of habutae silk. The figures are quoted at two removes, through Koyaizu's compilation from the Ako and Nishinomiya city histories and, for 1716, a record of Yamato commoners, and none is from Osaka itself; the 1692 silk price is taken from Saikaku's fiction; and the 1716 cotton price is for a hiki of two tan, not one, in the Yotsuho silver coin of its day, not the coin of the later prices. It names the tables of the Mitsui volume of 1989 by their titles, Osaka market-price tables, without listing the goods each holds.

Used for: cotton prices a tan at Ako and Nishinomiya, 1781-1790, white cotton in 1716, habutae in 1692, and the Mitsui volume's Osaka market-price tables (Money and prices)
