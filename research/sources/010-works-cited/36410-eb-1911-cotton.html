"Cotton", Encyclopaedia Britannica, 11th edition (1911), as transcribed on Wikisource (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclop%C3%A6dia_Britannica/Cotton)

What it is: The long article on the plant and the trade in an English encyclopedia of 1911.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a contemporary summary of the East India Company's trials of foreign cotton and the trade of about 1900. Its names for the species predate the classification now used, so where it says G. herbaceum for Indian cotton, later work says otherwise; it reports the Company's trials without a date for any single one.

Used for: American cotton in India and Japan's imports of about 1900 (Money and prices)
