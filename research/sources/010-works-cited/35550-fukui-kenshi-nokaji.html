Fukui Prefectural Archives, 『福井県史』通史編4 近世二, 「野鍛冶」 ("Village smiths", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.library-archives.pref.fukui.lg.jp/fukui/07/kenshi/T4/T4-3-01-02-03-13.htm)

What it is: A page of Fukui Prefecture's own history on the village smiths of Echizen and Wakasa, who mended and made farm tools, from village registers and the smiths' tax, with the charges of a price agreement of 1841.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a village smith's charges for named jobs, the one such list found. It does not say which villages the agreement bound or what each job covered, and it says the number of village smiths is not known.

Used for: a village smith's charges in Echizen or Wakasa in 1841 (Money and prices)
