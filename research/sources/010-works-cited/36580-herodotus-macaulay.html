Herodotus, The History of Herodotus, volume 1, translated by G. C. Macaulay (Macmillan, 1890), as Project Gutenberg eBook 2707 (https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2707/pg2707.txt)

What it is: The Greek historian's account of the known world, written in the 5th century BCE, here in an English translation of 1890.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Indians wore cloth from trees that grow wool. Herodotus never saw India and speaks of its inhabitants in general, naming no region or class.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
