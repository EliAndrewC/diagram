Jeong Kee-Taeg (정기택), "Study on Origin of Korea Tobacco by Homogeneity of the Names and Folk Tales in the Tobaccos introduced among Three Countries" (in Korean), Journal of the Korean Society of Tobacco Science 37, no. 1 (2015): 1-7 (https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO201507740043433.pdf)

What it is: A journal article by a researcher at the KT&G Central Research Institute, Korea's tobacco manufacturer, which sets out the earliest Korean texts on tobacco in their classical Chinese and argues for a Japanese origin.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that quotes the Korean texts of 1622, 1635 and 1638, and those texts were not read in their own editions. Its arrival years are its author's subtractions from a work's date of writing, and it argues one side of a dispute it reports.

Used for: Tobacco in Korea (Money and prices)
