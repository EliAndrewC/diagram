農林省山林局 (Forestry Bureau, Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry), 『本邦木炭生産並移動状況調査書』 (Survey of the production and movement of charcoal in Japan, title translated), 1931, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1178389/1/8 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1178389/1/9 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1178389)

What it is: A government survey of Japan's charcoal trade. Its third table gives the standard cost of producing white charcoal, per 100 bales of 10 kan, in 1914, 1920 and 1926, in five parts (standing timber, burning, baling, carriage, sundries) with their total and the standard settlement price; its fourth gives the same for 1914 prefecture by prefecture, naming the district surveyed and the market.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the earliest source found that sets the cost of baling charcoal beside the price of the charcoal. It is of 1914-1926, half a century after the Edo period, in yen, and per 100 bales of 10 kan only because the bureau converted its returns to that basis "for convenience" (便宜上), so its per-bale figure is not the cost of baling a bale as packed in the district surveyed; and its baling cost is one figure: the tables do not divide it into the bale, the rope and the work of packing, nor say whether the bale was bought or made at home. Its figures are "standard" costs the bureau collected, one district standing for each prefecture, and they are counted only from the place of burning to the nearest railway station or market, so the carriage in them is a haul to a railway of the 1910s.

Used for: the cost of baling white charcoal beside its price, Japan and Hikawa village in Tokyo's Nishitama district, 1914 (Money and prices)
