kotobank-kawachi-momen

Kotobank, "河内木綿" ("Kawachi cotton", title translated), gathering the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, Mypedia, Nipponica and Yamakawa dictionary entries, in Japanese (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B2%B3%E5%86%85%E6%9C%A8%E7%B6%BF-468756)

What it is: A Japanese dictionary portal's page gathering several reference works' entries on the thick cotton cloth of Kawachi province, near Osaka.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the growth of the largest cotton cloth district. Who wore its cloth rests on one undated sentence, part of the older Heibonsha encyclopedia's article on Kawachi province: Osaka merchants' apprentices, and commoners in Kyoto and Osaka. It does not say who wore the cloth elsewhere, and the other entries list only uses, such as shop curtains and tabi. Its entries differ on the output of the 1830s, 3 million tan in one and over 2 million in another, and the Mypedia sentence on 46 percent of the land leaves its subject implied.

Used for: Kawachi's output, and who wore Kawachi cloth (Money and prices)
