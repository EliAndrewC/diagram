iijima-jinya-kakiyaku-beya

Iijima Jinya Diary (飯島陣屋日記), "飯島陣屋・書役部屋" ("Iijima Jinya: the writing room", title translated), 2024-05-02, a Hatena Blog post by the Iijima Jinya (in Japanese; https://iijimajinya.hatenablog.com/entry/2024/05/02/105917)

What it is: A short post by the museum that runs the rebuilt Iijima Jin'ya, a shogunate intendant's office in Shinano, on its writing room (kakiyaku-beya): the first room a visitor reaches up from the step and the entrance, where, it says, the office's apprentices kept night duty while they trained.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that sets a writing room in a small office. The building is a rebuild of 1993, so the room's place is the rebuild's, and the post gives no size and no source for who used the room; a visitor's blog says the room held literate local hires taken on by the intendant, from whom senior staff were promoted, which likely describes the same trainee clerks from another side.

Used for: the writing room of a small office's rebuild, its place and who used it (Estates and other compounds)
