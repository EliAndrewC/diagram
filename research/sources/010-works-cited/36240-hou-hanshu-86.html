hou-hanshu-86

後漢書 (History of the Later Han, title translated), volume 86, 南蠻西南夷列傳 ("Account of the southern and southwestern peoples"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷86)

What it is: The chapter on the peoples of the south and southwest in the dynastic history of the Later Han.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the text a modern survey reads as an early mention of cotton cloth, so its own words are given. It does not say cotton: it names cloths whose fiber it leaves unstated, and Pelliot says that where the word for cotton occurs in this history it comes from a later source.

Used for: whether its cloths of the southwest and Hainan were cotton (Money and prices)
