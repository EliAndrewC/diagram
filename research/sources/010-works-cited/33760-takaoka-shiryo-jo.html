takaoka-shiryo-jo

高岡市 (the city of Takaoka), 『高岡史料』 (Historical materials of Takaoka, title translated), 上 (volume 1), 1909, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/899934/1/371; the account's heading at https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/899934/1/370)

What it is: The first volume of the city's 1909 compilation of its records, which prints in full, under the town's dealings with the domain's lords, the town magistrates' papers of 1728 on repairing the lord's lodging house at Takaoka, with the account of what the work paid its carpenters, sawyers and day laborers by the man.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a paid account of work in the town itself, giving a carpenter's and a day laborer's rate side by side, but of 1728, a century before the prices it is set beside in this record, and paid in the full-weight coin of the Kyoho years, before the recoinages of 1736 and of 1818 on cut the metal in the money those later prices are counted in, so the two are not on one yardstick; and of work for the domain, whose rates may have been set rather than bargained. The account pays by the man, which this record reads as a man's day.

Used for: a carpenter's and a day laborer's pay at Takaoka (Money and prices)
