ジャガイモ (Potato), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ジャガイモ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the potato, with sections on its names and its arrival in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It holds nearly all the dates read for Japan, from 1598 to Meiji. It gives two arrival dates of its own (1598, offered as one of several theories, and the early 1600s), credits the 1598 arrival to the Dutch though the first Dutch ship reached Japan only in 1600, places a work of 1807 in the 18th century, and has the plant grown in Hokkaido in 1706 and entering Hokkaido at the end of the 1700s, without reconciling them. The books and articles it cites were not read, and its sentences on Meiji adoption and on Takano Choei carry no footnote.

Used for: The white potato in Japan: names, arrival, planting and who grew it (Money and prices)
