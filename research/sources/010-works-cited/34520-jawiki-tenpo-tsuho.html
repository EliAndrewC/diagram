"天保通宝" ("Tenpo tsuho"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/天保通宝)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the Tenpo tsuho, the oval copper coin of 100 mon first cast in 1835, with its weight, its issue and what it passed for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the coin's value in use, about 80 mon rather than its face of 100, and its fall to a sixtieth and then a hundredth of a ryo in the 1850s and 1860s. These are round figures for whole eras, not dated market quotations, and the hundredth is from the Man'en era (1860-1861), after the ports opened in 1859 and the ryo was recoined lighter in 1860, which the page does not say and which the setting has no counterpart for.

Used for: what the hundred-mon coin passed for (Money and prices)
