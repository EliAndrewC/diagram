zhufan-zhi-xia

Zhao Rukuo (趙汝适), Zhufan zhi (諸蕃志, Records of Foreign Peoples), second chapter, on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/諸蕃志/卷下)

What it is: A Southern Song official's account of foreign goods and of Hainan island; this project takes 1225 as its date from Pelliot.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the cotton plant, its processing and its grades, and names Hainan's jibei (it does not say whether floss or cloth) among the goods that merchants of Quanzhou shipped. It goes through the island prefecture by prefecture, so it shows where weaving was noted and not how much of it there was.

Used for: Hainan's cotton and the trade to Fujian in the 1220s (Money and prices)
