edo-honmaru-enjo-ikken-1859

『炎上一件』 (Records of the fires), 4 volumes, [3], the burning of the main enceinte of Edo castle, tenth month of Ansei 6, part 1, a manuscript copy among the records the shogunate's Edo town magistrates handed on, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547702/1/20 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547702)

What it is: A volume of the Edo town magistrates' correspondence after the main enceinte of Edo castle burned in the tenth month of 1859. The letter read asks that the city's rice merchants be notified at once: a contractor, Seishichi, ordered to deliver 5,000 empty rice bales for clearing the burned ground, finds them hard to buy because they are scarce in the city, and wishes to buy them from the rice dealers by private agreement.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows empty rice bales as a thing bought in Edo by the thousand, ordinarily on a market that could run short, and the rice dealers as holders of them. It gives no price: the price is left to private agreement. It is one emergency order, and it says nothing of who made the bales or of charcoal bales.

Used for: empty rice bales bought by the thousand in Edo in 1859 (Money and prices)
