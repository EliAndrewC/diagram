Fang Yun (方云), "华夏棉路的紫贝芳华" (The purple cotton of China's cotton road), People's Daily (人民日报), 14 June 2024, in Chinese (https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbwap/html/2024-06/14/nw.D110000renmrb_20240614_2-17.htm)

What it is: A newspaper feature on the history of Shanghai's cotton cloth by a researcher at a Shanghai arts and crafts college.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that gives Huang Daopo's return a year range, and it quotes the Kangxi-era gazetteer of Songjiang on the seed's arrival. It names no source for her dates, and its range is only the span of the Yuanzhen (元贞) reign era: 1295 and 1297 are the era's bounds, not dated events. The Yuan-period record behind that era, Wang Feng's 梧溪集, is quoted in 2024 news reports as giving only the era (元贞间), and was not read. The gazetteer as quoted dates the seed only to "the Song" (宋时), with no year, and was not read, only this quotation of it.

Used for: the date of Huang Daopo's return and of the seed's arrival at Songjiang (Money and prices)
