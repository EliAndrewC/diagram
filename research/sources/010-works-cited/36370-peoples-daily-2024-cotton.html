Fang Yun (方云), "华夏棉路的紫贝芳华" (The purple cotton of China's cotton road), People's Daily (人民日报), 14 June 2024, in Chinese (https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbwap/html/2024-06/14/nw.D110000renmrb_20240614_2-17.htm)

What it is: A newspaper feature on the history of Shanghai's cotton cloth by a researcher at a Shanghai arts and crafts college.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that gives Huang Daopo's return a year range, and it quotes the Kangxi-era gazetteer of Songjiang on the seed's arrival. It names no source for her dates, and the gazetteer was not read, only this quotation of it.

Used for: the date of Huang Daopo's return and of the seed's arrival at Songjiang (Money and prices)
