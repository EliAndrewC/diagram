bassino-van-der-eng-2018-rice-markets

Jean-Pascal Bassino and Pierre van der Eng, Hitotsubashi University Center for Economic Institutions working paper 2018-18, its abstract on RePEc/IDEAS (https://ideas.repec.org/p/hit/hitcei/2018-18.html)

What it is: The abstract of two economic historians' study of rice prices in 13 markets across Japan, 1720-1857, asking whether better-integrated markets would have eased the great famines.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says which parts of Japan had well-integrated rice markets and what held the east back. Only the abstract was read, so nothing is taken from it but its stated findings; it gives none of the paper's measures or its markets' names.

Used for: how closely Japan's regional rice markets moved together (Money and prices)
