netease-pingyao-yamen

"堪称皇宫缩影的平遥县衙" ("The Pingyao county yamen, a palace in miniature", title translated), an article posted to the NetEase Hao self-publishing platform by the travel account 云行天下, 2018-03-16 (in Chinese; https://www.163.com/dy/article/DD102NPC0524CT3H.html)

What it is: An article whose body is unsigned (its one credit, "文+图：王永云", text and photographs by Wang Yongyun, stands at its appended note on punishment instruments), walking the Pingyao county yamen in Shanxi from its main gate to its rear tower, dating its buildings by reign (the main gate's drum to early Hongwu, 1368; the ceremonial gate and the great hall's side ranges to 1619) and citing the county gazetteers of 1707 and 1882 for the rooms between the gates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what stood where in a Ming and Qing county yamen and how its gates were used - the drum under the main gate, the ceremonial gate's two side doors and the one prisoners were led through, the terrace before the great hall and the money and arms stores inside it. Its limits are that the body's author is unnamed and cites the gazetteers only for the clerks' rooms, so the gate customs and the use of each door rest on the article alone; and that the compound is a rebuilt museum, the article naming its rear tower the only original building left (so the gate, the drum and the hall's stores are all rebuilds) and noting that the clerks' rooms between the gates were rebuilt with the wrong number, six a side where the 1882 gazetteer shows five.

Used for: the drum under the main gate and the prisoners' west door of the ceremonial gate (Estates and other compounds); the money and arms stores in the great hall's end bays and the terrace before it (Towns)
