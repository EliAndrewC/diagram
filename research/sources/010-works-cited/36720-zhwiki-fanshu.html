Chinese Wikipedia, "番薯" (Sweet potato; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/番薯)

What it is: The Chinese encyclopedia article on the sweet potato, with a short account of how it reached China.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read that gives 1593 for Chen Zhenlong, and it lists the routes into China in a single sentence. Neither statement carries a primary source on the page, and the works it footnotes there include Ho Ping-ti, so its routes may not be independent of Newman's summary of Ho.

Used for: The 1593 date and the routes of the sweet potato into China (Money and prices)
