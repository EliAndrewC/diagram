kimura-1987-gpih-osaka-prices

David Jacks (extended by Ernest Boffy-Ramirez), "Osaka 1600-1650", a spreadsheet for the Global Price and Income History Group, 2006, transcribing Masahiro Kimura, La Revolución de los precios en la cuenca del pacífico, 1600-1650 (Mexico City: UNAM, 1987) (https://gpih.ucdavis.edu/files/Osaka_1600-1650.xls)

What it is: A transcription of the Osaka prices that a Japanese economic historian gathered for a Spanish-language study of prices around the Pacific, 1600-1650: rice, soybeans, salt, oil, sake, cloth, paper and other goods by year, each in its own measure and in silver monme.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one series read that prices goods other than rice in a Japanese city before 1650. Its figures are sparse, many goods priced in only a handful of years; it does not say whether its oil was for lamps or food; its fuel is priced in units it says it cannot convert; and Kimura's own sources, which the file does not name, were not seen.

Used for: Osaka's prices of rice, soybeans, salt, oil and cloth in 1620-1650 (Money and prices)
