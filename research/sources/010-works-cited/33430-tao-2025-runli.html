陶小军 (Tao Xiaojun), 《近代书画润例的商业广告与文化认同：以《申报》刊载为中心》 ("The commercial advertising and cultural identity of modern fee schedules for painting and calligraphy, centered on those printed in the Shenbao", title translated), Academic Monthly 57:8 (August 2025), pp. 160-167 (in Chinese; https://www.xsyk021.com/fileXSYK/journal/article/xsyk/2025/8/PDF/xsyk-2025-8-160.pdf)

What it is: A study of the price lists Chinese painters and calligraphers posted for their work, mostly those printed in the Shenbao newspaper, which opens with their forerunners, among them Zheng Banqiao's list of 1759.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes Zheng Banqiao's list in full, the one calligrapher's price list of the period found, with its prices by format. It quotes the list at second hand from a collected edition of 1979; the list is one celebrated artist's; and it has no line for a plaque.

Used for: what a noted calligrapher charged (Money and prices)
