What it is: A French sinologist's long philological note, in English, on the words for cotton in Chinese and the texts that mention the plant, from the Han dynasty to the Qing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets the Chinese texts on cotton in order, with dates, and it is the only source read that weighs them against one another. Its English renderings of Chinese texts are Pelliot's own; several of its dates are his inferences, among them the Yangzi's 12th century, and "one century after Polo" is his gloss, with no count behind it. The page text is OCR, with broken words, and the footnotes here give the site's page numbers, which run 16 ahead of the printed ones.

Used for: the dated stages of cotton in China, from the Tang silence to the Ming planting order (Money and prices)
