nippon.com, "寿司は江戸の昔からお手軽ファストフードだった : 『守貞漫稿』（その3）" ("Sushi has been easy fast food since old Edo: from the Morisada Manko, part 3", title translated), 17 October 2020 (in Japanese; https://www.nippon.com/ja/japan-topics/g00962/)

What it is: A magazine article on sushi in Edo, from the Morisada Manko (written 1837-1853), with Morisada's drawings of hand-pressed sushi, his price for a piece and his account of the sushi and tempura stalls on busy streets.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a ready-cooked food's price in late-Edo Edo and how common the food stalls were. The prices are the writer's report of Morisada, not a quotation, and they are for sushi and eggs alone, not for a meal.

Used for: what a piece of sushi and an egg cost in Edo, and the food stalls on its streets (Money and prices)
