kaken-ohtomo-shogun-funerals

Ohtomo Kazuo, An Introduction to the History of Funeral-and Ancestral Dedicating Ceremony of the Tokugawa Syogun, KAKEN research project 09610354 (National Institute of Japanese Literature, 1997-1999), English research summary (https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/grant/KAKENHI-PROJECT-09610354/)

What it is: The English summary of a three-year study of the funerals and ancestral rites of the Tokugawa shoguns, from the records of domains, towns and villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that every domain was ordered to stop all music and dancing on a shogun's death. It is a summary in imperfect English, and gives no number of days for the mourning or for any closure of the theaters.

Used for: the stopping of music and dancing on a shogun's death (Money and prices)
