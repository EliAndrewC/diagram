daqing-huidian-zeli-122

欽定大清會典則例 (Precedents of the Collected Statutes of the Great Qing, title translated), volume 122, Board of War, the Wuku office, "Military equipment, part 1", the Siku Quanshu edition (in Chinese; Wikisource transcription; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/欽定大清㑹典則例_(四庫全書本)/卷122)

What it is: A volume of the Qing government's own compilation of the rulings behind its statutes, on the general rules for arms: who made them, the arrowsmiths the Board of War kept and the bowyers of the banner companies, the rules for making bows and arrows, and the prices fixed for arrows by the kind of feather that fletched them.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its ruling of 1727 fixes prices for fine arrows by feather and grade and for war arrows by the hundred, and forbids the shops to raise them, a primary list of ordinary against fine arrows. The ruling does not name the city whose shops it bound; that it was the capital, where the Board sat, is this project's guess. The prices are fixed ones, a ceiling on what the shops could ask, not a record of trade.

Used for: the fixed prices of fine and war arrows in 1727, and the Board of War's ten arrowsmiths (Money and prices)
