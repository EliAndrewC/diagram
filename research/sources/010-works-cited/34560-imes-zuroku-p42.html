imes-zuroku-p42

Bank of Japan Institute for Monetary and Economic Studies, Currency Museum, permanent exhibition catalog (常設展示図録), p. 42, on the three-currency system (in Japanese; https://www.imes.boj.or.jp/cm/collection/tenjizuroku/mod/book/pageindices/index42.html)

What it is: One page of the Bank of Japan currency museum's catalog, setting out the Edo period's gold, silver and copper units, the coins counted in fractions of a ryo, the official rates of 1609, 1700 and 1842, and how Osaka's changers set the rates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the fixed steps within gold, the counted silver coins and the official rates in one chart, and says weighed silver left circulation while its unit stayed in Osaka's accounts. It is a short exhibit text with no references, and its chart gives the official rates without the market's.

Used for: the legal rates, the counted coins and Osaka's rate as the standard (Money and prices)
