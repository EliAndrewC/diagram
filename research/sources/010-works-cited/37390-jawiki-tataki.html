jawiki-tataki

Japanese Wikipedia, "三和土" (Tataki) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E5%92%8C%E5%9C%9F)

What it is: A short encyclopedia article on tataki, the hard surface of earth and gravel mixed with slaked lime and bittern, spread and beaten hard.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what tataki is made of, to gloss the word where an excavation report names it. It names the soils used (from Nagasaki, Mikawa and Fukakusa in Kyoto), says only that tataki was used "in the times when there was no cement", and dates nothing but Hattori Choshichi's improvement of it in the Meiji period; it gives no date for when the technique began and says nothing of garden ponds.

Used for: what tataki was (Estates and other compounds)
