zeitlin-qing-theater-scandals

Judith Zeitlin (蔡九迪), 清代两起戏曲丑闻事件：1689和1874 ("Two theatrical scandals of the Qing: 1689 and 1874", title translated), Theatre Arts Communication vol. 1, Center for Theater Arts Collaboration, Binghamton University, Chinese text (https://www.binghamton.edu/centers/ctac/thea-comm/issues/vol1/zeitlininchinese.html)

What it is: A study by a historian of Chinese theater of two disastrous performances in Qing Beijing, one of them the private staging of Hong Sheng's play in 1689 during a national mourning.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests that staging a play during imperial mourning was an offense in 1689, relaying the Cambridge History of Chinese Literature for the facts. It gives no length for the mourning's ban on plays.

Used for: imperial mourning stopping plays in Qing Beijing (Money and prices)
