liu-ge-song-cotton

Liu Pingsheng (柳平生) and Ge Jinfang (葛金芳), "'农商社会'视野下南宋商品性农业述论" (Commercial farming in the Southern Song seen as an agrarian-commercial society), in Chinese, as posted on Aisixiang (https://www.aisixiang.com/data/121418.html)

What it is: An article by two Chinese scholars on Southern Song cash crops, one section of which traces cotton's spread and its marketing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out, against what it calls the view of most scholars that cotton flourished only from the Yuan, the case for an earlier spread into Zhejiang and the lower Yangzi. That case rests on a few poems and a gloss, read by the authors in support of a thesis about the Song economy, and the page gives its sources as endnotes that this project did not follow.

Used for: cotton in the Yangzi region under the Song, the Guangzhou weaving case of 1078, and the Siming port's cotton cargoes (Money and prices)
