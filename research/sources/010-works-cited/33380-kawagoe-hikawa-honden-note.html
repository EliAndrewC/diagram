kawagoe-hikawa-honden-note

川越氷川神社, 「社史－本殿彫刻にみる川越の粋と江戸の技－」 ("Shrine history: the carving of the sanctuary", title translated), the Kawagoe Hikawa shrine's post on note.com, 6 April 2025 (in Japanese; https://note.com/kawagoe_hikawa/n/n46255682a738)

What it is: The Kawagoe Hikawa shrine's own account of building its sanctuary from 1842 to 1849, wholly of keyaki and carved all over by a house of Edo carvers and in part by a carver of Kumagaya, and of who paid for it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a sum from a document of 1847, 1,369 ryo with the roofing left out, for a large town shrine's richly carved sanctuary, so it is an upper bound for a small one, not a price for it. It says the whole cost is not known, and it does not name or quote the document.

Used for: an upper bound on a carved sanctuary's cost (Money and prices)
