hatogamine-great-torii

鳩ヶ嶺八幡宮 (Hatogamine Hachiman shrine, Iida, Nagano), 「大鳥居」 ("The great torii", title translated), the shrine's own page on its grounds (in Japanese; https://hatogamine.org/keidai/%E8%A1%A8%E7%9F%B3%E5%A4%A7%E9%B3%A5%E5%B1%85/)

What it is: The shrine's short note on its great stone torii: when it was begun and finished, where its stone was cut, and what it is said to have cost.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one cost found for a torii. The shrine hedges its own figure ("is said to have"), names no record behind it, does not say what the sum covered (stone, cutting, hauling, raising) and gives no size; the sum is one of 1708-09, when the ryō was the debased Genroku coin, so it cannot be set beside ryō prices of other decades without adjusting for the coin; and the torii is a great one at a major shrine, so its cost bounds a small torii's from far above.

Used for: what a stone torii cost (Money and prices)
