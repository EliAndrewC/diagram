minami-alps-nishikawa-dozo

Minami-Alps City, 西川家土蔵の戸前口 ("The doorway of the Nishikawa family storehouse", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.city.minami-alps.yamanashi.jp/docs/nishikawake-dozo.html)

What it is: The page of Minami-Alps city (Yamanashi prefecture) for one of its designated cultural properties, the doorway of a family storehouse, designated in 1992, which survives as it was built at the end of the Edo period though the storehouse was renovated in 1988.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a storehouse's two plastered door leaves to the end of the Edo period, and says they are of the pivot-hung (jikuzuri) type, which it calls the earlier form without naming the later one. It is one doorway in one province (Kai), described in a few lines, and the city does not say how it dated it. The doorway was designated as a craft work, and the page calls a storehouse doorway the plasterer's showpiece, so this one may be finer than an ordinary storehouse's. It attests the end of the Edo period only, and gives no width for the leaves or the opening.

Used for: that a storehouse's doorway closed by two plastered leaves is attested at the end of the Edo period (Estates and other compounds)
