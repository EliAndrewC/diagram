"天保丁銀" ("Tenpo chogin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/天保丁銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the chogin issued in 1837, with its prescribed fineness, the Meiji mint's assay, the order of 1842 withdrawing the older silver and its own withdrawal in 1868.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms the prescribed fineness (26% silver) by an assay (26.05%), and dates the order of 1842 that stopped the older silver. But it says only that the exchange deadline was then extended again and again, with the exchange premium raised in 1855; it gives no year when the older silver finally stopped passing. Like the other chogin articles it quotes the Meiji table and assay through the article, and it gives no figure for how much old silver stayed in use.

Used for: the Tenpo silver's fineness, assay and the years it passed (Money and prices)
