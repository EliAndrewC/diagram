Japanese Wikipedia, "石 (単位)" (the koku as a unit of measure) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%9F%B3_(%E5%8D%98%E4%BD%8D))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the koku as a unit of volume: its subdivisions, when its present capacity was fixed, its metric value, the convention that a koku fed one adult for a year, and the history of the same character as a unit in China and Korea.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the koku's capacity from 1669 and its metric value of 1886 in one place, and the Chinese shi's change from a weight to a volume. Its Japanese history section carries no footnotes, so its dates and its consumption convention rest on the article alone; its weights per koku are in the Korean section and are not Japanese evidence.

Used for: the koku's capacity in liters, the one-adult-a-year convention and the Chinese shi (Money and prices)
