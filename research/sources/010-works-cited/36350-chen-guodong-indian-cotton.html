Chen Guodong (陳國棟), "印度棉布與中國: 以十六、七世紀為中心" (Indian cotton cloth and China, centered on the 16th and 17th centuries), 故宮學術季刊 (National Palace Museum Research Quarterly) 34:1, in Chinese (https://www.npm.edu.tw/NewChineseArtDownload.ashx?bid=3896)

What it is: A journal article by a historian at Academia Sinica on Indian cloth reaching China, with a survey of the earlier record.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the fullest account read of imported cotton before the Ming and of the Southern Song customs lists. Its subject is the 1500s and 1600s, so the earlier centuries are background, and he states no date himself for the Minfeng cloth that he calls the earliest known.

Used for: early finds, and the import of cotton cloth under the Southern Song (Money and prices)
