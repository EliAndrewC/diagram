清史稿 (Draft History of the Qing, title translated), volume 124, 食貨五, 錢法 ("Food and goods, part 5: the coinage"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/清史稿/卷124)

What it is: The treatise on the Qing coinage in the draft dynastic history compiled in the 1910s and 1920s, summarizing reign by reign the court's coins, its legal rates of cash to silver, its bans and its paper money and big coins of the 1850s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the legal rates of 1645, 1653 and 1702, the market rates the court complained of, and the measures it took, as the court's own records state them. Its dates are often given only by the order of its entries ("that year", "before this"), its market rates are those of the capital unless it says otherwise, and its account of the 1850s notes is told from the court's side.

Used for: the Qing legal rate of cash to silver against the market, and the 1850s notes and big coins (Money and prices)
