Ja-Yeon Lee (Lee Ja-yeon), "Cloths Culture Study Based on the Trade Goods between Chosun Envoy and a Japanese Envoy in the Early Chosun Dynasty", The Research Journal of the Costume Culture 11, no. 6 (December 2003): 826-834 (https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO200311922064774.pdf)

What it is: A journal article on the cloth Joseon Korea gave Japanese envoys in the 15th and 16th centuries, working from the Korean annals.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated volumes of cotton cloth and a date for cotton as the general dress of Korea. Its figures are one author's readings of the annals, known here from a scanned two-column page whose text layer is machine-read, and the annals entries it cites for them were not read: its "all ranks" statement cites an entry of 1456, and its figures of 1485-86 an entry of 1488.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
