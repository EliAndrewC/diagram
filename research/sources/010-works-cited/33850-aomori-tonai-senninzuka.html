aomori-tonai-senninzuka

Aomori Prefecture, Cultural Properties Protection Division, 「斗内千人塚」 (Tonai Sennin-zuka, "the thousand-man mounds of Tonai", title translated), a page on a prefectural historic site, updated 2023 (in Japanese; https://www.pref.aomori.lg.jp/soshiki/kyoiku/e-bunka/kensiseki_17.html)

What it is: The prefecture's description of four earth mounds and a stone memorial in Sannohe, built in 1785 over the heads of those who died in the Tenmei famine of 1783-1784, with the memorial's inscription and how the burial was paid for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives what the sponsor paid for each head and what the rite of 1784 brought in, and says the mounds and the stone were built from those offerings; it gives no builder's cost, no price for the stone and no fee for the rites, and it does not name the document its figures come from.

Used for: what gathering and burying the famine dead under mounds and a stone cost (Money and prices)
