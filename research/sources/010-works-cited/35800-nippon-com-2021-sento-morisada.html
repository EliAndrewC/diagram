nippon.com, "寛政の改革で混浴禁止に？ お江戸銭湯よもやま話 : 『守貞漫稿』（その10）" ("Mixed bathing banned in the Kansei reforms? Tales of Edo's bathhouses: from the Morisada Manko, part 10", title translated), 22 May 2021 (in Japanese; https://www.nippon.com/ja/japan-topics/g01098/)

What it is: A magazine article on Edo's public bathhouses as Kitagawa Morisada recorded them in the Morisada Manko (written 1837-1853), with his plan of a bathhouse and his dated bath fees.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the adult bath fee from 1841 to 1866 and Morisada's contrast between Kyoto and Osaka, where many houses had a bath, and Edo, where tenement dwellers used the bathhouses. Apart from one sentence on the names of bathhouses it reports Morisada in the writer's own words rather than quoting him.

Used for: Edo bath fees, 1841-1866, and who used the bathhouses in Edo and in Kyoto and Osaka (Money and prices)
