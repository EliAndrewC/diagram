japanknowledge-yakusha

「役者・俳優」 ("Actors", title translated), the general article of the Shinpan Kabuki Jiten (新版歌舞伎事典, Heibonsha), on JapanKnowledge's public pages (in Japanese; https://japanknowledge.com/contents/kabuki/sousetsu/04yakusha.html)

What it is: A kabuki encyclopedia's overview of the actor: his social standing, his yearly pay, the ranks of a company and the kinds of roles.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states how a city actor was paid in the Edo period and the top of the scale, with the cap of the Kansei era and how it was evaded. It gives no ordinary actor's pay and no year for the thousand-ryo benchmark, and speaks of the city theaters only.

Used for: a star kabuki actor's yearly pay and the Kansei cap (Money and prices)
