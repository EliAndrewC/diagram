長孫無忌 (Zhangsun Wuji) and others, 唐律疏議 (Tanglü shuyi, "The Tang Code with Commentary"), 卷第二十六 雜律 (chapter 26, Miscellaneous statutes), Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%94%90%E5%BE%8B%E7%96%8F%E8%AD%B0/%E5%8D%B7%E7%AC%AC%E4%BA%8C%E5%8D%81%E5%85%AD)

What it is: A chapter of the Tang dynasty's penal code with its official commentary; article 404 punishes encroaching on lanes, streets and field paths, and planting or tilling them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that lanes and field paths were protected only as places where the public passed, and that planting in one that was wide, with room to spare, obstructing nothing, was no offense. It is a law of the Tang state, for city lanes and field paths alike, and says how the state judged encroachment, not how often a village lane was in fact planted over or how long that took. Its exemption is for a lane still in use with width to spare, and every lane it punished encroachment on was to be restored; it never speaks of a lane that has stopped serving anyone, so the rule that such a lane is not drawn is the record's own extension of it.

Used for: a lane that serves nothing is not drawn (Ways)
