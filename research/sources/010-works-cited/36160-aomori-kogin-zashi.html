aomori-kogin-zashi

Aomori Prefecture, "青森県の伝統工芸品（こぎん刺し）" ("Aomori's traditional crafts: kogin embroidery", title translated), the prefecture's page, in Japanese with an English version (https://www.pref.aomori.lg.jp/soshiki/sangyo/chikikigyo/aomori_dento-kogei_koginzashi.html)

What it is: A prefectural office's short description of the quilted hemp embroidery of Tsugaru, written to present a local craft.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the common account of why Tsugaru peasants wore hemp. It says flatly that they were not permitted cotton, across the whole Edo period and with no date or order named, which the domain's own orders in a historian's study do not bear out.

Used for: the common account of Tsugaru peasants and cotton (Money and prices)
