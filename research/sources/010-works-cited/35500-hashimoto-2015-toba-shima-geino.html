橋本好史 (Hashimoto), 「近世・近代の鳥羽志摩地方における芸能興行の実態と変遷：歌舞伎・人形芝居を中心として」 ("Performances in the Toba and Shima region in the early modern and modern periods", title translated), master's thesis, Mie University, 2015 (in Japanese; https://mie-u.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/7030/files/2015M004.pdf)

What it is: A master's thesis on the kabuki and puppet plays put on in the fishing villages of the Toba and Shima coast from the late Edo period into Meiji, from the villages' account books and the permits they asked of the domain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the account book of Ura village, 1855-1872, item by item, and a statement of 1832 from the papers of Kawachi village, so what one village paid a hired touring puppet company, with its size and its board and lodging, can be read line by line. The companies are puppet troupes playing shrine festivals in one district's fishing villages; the book shows what the village paid out, not what the company took in a year or how it shared its money, and some sums mix gold and silver in one entry. Its years are the late Edo period, the 1832 statement before the recoinage of 1860 and most of the Ura book after it: the recoinage cut the gold in a ryō by about two-thirds and prices rose steeply after it, so a fee paid in the 1860s runs higher in ryō than the 1832 one, or than the same company would have cost earlier in the Edo period, and the two cannot be set side by side in ryō unadjusted.

Used for: what a village paid a touring puppet company and how large the company was, 1832-1867 (Money and prices)
