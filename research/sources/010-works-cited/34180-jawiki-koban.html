jawiki-koban

"小判" ("Koban"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/小判)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the koban, the oval one-ryo gold coin, with a list of every issue of the Edo period giving its year, the amount issued (counted together with the one-bu coins), its weight and its prescribed gold content.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives each koban's weight and fineness in one list, from which the gold in a ryo follows. The fineness is the prescribed one, from the gold mint's own records, and the article does not give the assays in that list. For the Keichō koban it gives two prescribed finenesses, 84.3% and later 86.8%, so the gold in a Keichō ryo is a range, not one figure.

Used for: the weight and gold of each koban (Money and prices)
