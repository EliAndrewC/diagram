bochum-yunnan-wuliao-jiazhi

Ruhr-Universitat Bochum, Section for the History of China, "Introduction to the Databases of Prices, Wages and Transport Costs in Yunnan Province in the Qianlong Reign", and its transport-cost and wage spreadsheets (in English and Chinese; https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/gc/forschung/yunnan_province.html; the spreadsheets, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/yunnan/yunnan_transport_costs.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/yunnan/yunnan_wages.xls)

What it is: A database, entered row by row by a German and Chinese project, of the Yunnan edition of the Qing Regulations and precedents on prices of materials, an official printing in ten chapters whose second prefatory memorial is dated 27 January 1769: for the province's districts, the prices of building materials, the day wages of craftsmen and laborers, and the rates for hauling materials.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its transport rows price stone by oxcart, timber by porter and porters carrying loads they do not name, by the 100 jin a li, beside each district's craftsman and laborer wage in the same silver. They are the rates official building could claim back, each row marked as the rule already in force; many rows name neither the mode nor the material, and none names both a porter and stone, so stone by porter is not priced. The laborer's wage is written per man, not per day.

Used for: the official rates for stone by oxcart and loads by porter in Yunnan in 1769, and the day wages beside them (Money and prices)
