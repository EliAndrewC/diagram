中国新闻网 (China News Service), 「古代镖局靠强盗“赏”饭吃 行业守则是远离妇人」 ("The old escort agencies lived on the robbers' 'favor'; their rule of the trade was to keep away from women", title translated), 2013-09-12, by way of 人民网 (People's Daily Online), first printed in the popular-history magazine 《国家人文历史》 2013 no. 18 (in Chinese; https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cul/2013/09-12/5277624.shtml)

What it is: A popular-history article on the Qing escort agencies (biaoju): what they carried and guarded, how clients contracted with them, what an escort-master was paid, the agencies' other business of guarding houses, and their decline after banks and railways came, the Tongxinggong agency of Shanxi closing in 1914.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that gives an escort-master's wage and terms and how clients engaged an agency, but it gives no year or place for the wage or the rates, and names its references only at its end, so no figure can be traced to its source.

Used for: an escort agency's terms with its escort-masters and its clients (Money and prices)
