jawiki-chogin

"丁銀" ("Chogin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/丁銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the chogin, the bar silver of the Edo period that passed by weight, with a list of every issue from 1601 to 1859 giving its year, the amount minted and its prescribed silver content.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the prescribed fineness of all eleven issues in one list, citing the Ministry of Finance's 1875 tables and a 1963 history of the silver mint, and says when old and new silver passed side by side. Its figures are the mint's prescribed fineness, which it says are not assays, and its Ansei figure carries an error it flags itself.

Used for: the fineness of each chogin and the dates of the recoinages (Money and prices)
