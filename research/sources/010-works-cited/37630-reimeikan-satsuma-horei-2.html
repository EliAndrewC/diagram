reimeikan-satsuma-horei-2

Kagoshima Prefectural Museum of Culture Reimeikan (鹿児島県歴史・美術センター黎明館), ed., "薩摩藩法令史料集 二" ("Collected laws of Satsuma domain", volume 2, title translated), in the series 鹿児島県史料, first part (in Japanese; https://www.pref.kagoshima.jp/ab23/reimeikan/siroyu/documents/6756_20230221111232-1.pdf)

What it is: A printed edition of Satsuma domain's own compilation of its rules and precedents, the "Institutions of the successive Shimazu lords", from its fifteenth book on. Among them are the domain's reckonings of the rice it owed the Amami islands for the sugar it bought from them, and its schedules of freight for goods shipped from the islands and from Ryukyu.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the domain's own record of the rate at which it bought the islanders' sugar and of the freight on it. The rate is in rice for each kin of sugar, not in money, and several of the reckonings carry no year on the page. They are what the domain set, not what the islanders received in the event; the Ryukyu freight of 1807 is a later note in red recording the shipping office's answer to an inquiry, not a rule. The edition gives no price of the sugar in Osaka.

Used for: the rice Satsuma paid for a kin of Amami sugar, and the freight in kind from the islands and from Ryukyu (Money and prices)
