jawiki-uji-saichashi

Japanese Wikipedia, "宇治採茶使" ("the Uji tea envoys", title translated; 御茶壺道中, the tea-jar procession, redirects to it) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/宇治採茶使)

What it is: The article on the yearly procession that carried the shogun's tea jars from Edo to Uji and back with new tea, from 1633 to 1866.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the route out and back, the season, the number of jars and the size of the procession at its largest, and how the tea went in 1867 without a procession. It gives no weight of tea, no cost of the carriage and no price.

Used for: how the shogun's Uji tea traveled to Edo (Money and prices)
