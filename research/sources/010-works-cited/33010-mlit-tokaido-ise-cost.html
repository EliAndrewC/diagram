Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Kanto Regional Development Bureau, Yokohama office, "東海道への誘い: Q7 東海道を利用して庶民がお伊勢参りする場合にどの程度の費用が必要だったのでしょうか。" ("An invitation to the Tokaido: Q7, how much did a commoner need for a pilgrimage to Ise by the Tokaido?", title translated) (https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/yokohama/tokaido/02_tokaido/04_qa/index7/answer7.htm)

What it is: One answer in a question-and-answer series on the old Tokaido highway by a regional office of Japan's Ministry of Land, reckoning a pilgrim's daily costs on the road from around Edo to the Ise shrines from what travelers wrote in their diaries.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the going rate for a hatago, about 200 mon a night, to the mid-19th century, and prices a lunch on the road. It hedges both figures itself ("seems to have been", "perhaps"), and names none of the diaries it read.

Used for: the going rate for a hatago night in the mid-19th century (Money and prices)
