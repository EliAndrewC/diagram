bunka-ueno-umaya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 旧上野家住宅馬屋 ("former Ueno house, stable", title translated) (https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/118707)

What it is: The national register's entry for the stable of the former Ueno house at Maizuru, Kyoto prefecture, dated 1751-1829: its period, its earthen storehouse-style walls, its tiled gable roof, its length and depth in ken, its building area, and where it stands among the house's buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is one of the few freestanding farm stables of the Edo period whose size can be read, so it measures a premodern byre directly rather than through a modern housing standard. Its limits: one building of a substantial household (it stands beside a gatehouse row, and the register lists the house's several storehouses), built more solidly than an ordinary smallholder's shed; its openings have been altered; the register gives a building area of 30 sq m that its 2.5 by 2 ken frame alone does not reach, and does not say why; and it gives no count of stalls or beasts.

Used for: the size of an Edo farm stable (Homesteads)
