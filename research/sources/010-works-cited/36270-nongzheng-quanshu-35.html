nongzheng-quanshu-35

Xu Guangqi (徐光啟), Nongzheng quanshu (農政全書, Complete Treatise on Agricultural Administration), chapter 35, on cotton (木棉), the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/農政全書/卷35)

What it is: The cotton chapter of the late Ming agricultural compendium that Xu Guangqi left unfinished at his death in 1633 and that Chen Zilong's group amended and rearranged for printing in 1639, gathering older texts and adding remarks marked as Xu's, by a man who calls Songjiang his own.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one acreage share read for a named place and the north-south trade in raw cotton and cloth. The acreage is a round estimate (nearly two million mu, more than half in cotton) in an unsigned note on one official's tour of 1615, made to argue for wider spacing. The statement that Suning county's cloth equalled "a tenth of our Songjiang's" is Xu's estimate of cloth output, not of acreage. The north-south trade is one undated sentence from before his death in 1633, with no quantities. This is a community transcription with visible character errors (the reign name in the 1615 date is garbled), not checked here against a printed edition. The preface by the Yuan writer Wang Zhen, which the chapter reproduces, is read here through Pelliot's rendering.

Used for: Haishang's cotton acreage (Shanghai's, on this project's reading) around 1615 and the north's raw cotton against the south's cloth (Money and prices)
