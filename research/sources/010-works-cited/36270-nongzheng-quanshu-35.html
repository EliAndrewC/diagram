nongzheng-quanshu-35

Xu Guangqi (徐光啟), Nongzheng quanshu (農政全書, Complete Treatise on Agricultural Administration), chapter 35, on cotton (木棉), the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/農政全書/卷35)

What it is: The cotton chapter of the late Ming agricultural compendium printed in 1639, gathering older texts and adding its compiler's remarks, by a man of Shanghai.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one acreage share read for a named place and the north-south trade in raw cotton and cloth. The acreage is a round figure in a note on one official's tour of 1615, and "a tenth of our Songjiang's" is a compiler's estimate. His Wang Zhen preface is read here through Pelliot's rendering.

Used for: Shanghai's cotton acreage in 1615 and the north's raw cotton against the south's cloth (Money and prices)
