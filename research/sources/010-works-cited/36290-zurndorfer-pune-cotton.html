zurndorfer-pune-cotton

Harriet T. Zurndorfer, "Cotton Textiles And Ming/Qing China In The Global Economy (1500-1840)", a paper filed on the London School of Economics' Global Economic History Network pages under its Pune conference, undated on its face (https://www.lse.ac.uk/Economic-History/Assets/Documents/Research/GEHN/GEHNConferences/conf8/PUNEZurndorfer.pdf)

What it is: A Leiden historian's survey of the Ming and Qing cotton industry and its place in world trade, drawn from the secondary literature.

Why it applies, and its limits: It collects the modern estimates of state demand and output in one place. Its first page says "Not for citation without written permission of the author", so it is cited here with that said, and every figure in it is at second hand from Yan Zhongping, Li Bozhong or Xu Dixin and Wu Chengming, whose own tables were not read.

Used for: Ming state demand, Songjiang's commerce, and the output and household figures (Money and prices)
