新版歌舞伎事典 (Heibonsha; served on JapanKnowledge), 興行 ("Performances", title translated) (https://japanknowledge.com/contents/kabuki/sousetsu/06kogyo.html)

What it is: The kabuki encyclopedia's survey article on how kabuki was staged as a business in the Edo period: the licensing of theaters in Edo, Kyoto and Osaka, the managers' system in each, and the theatrical year from the 11th month with its named programs and rests.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the record's one account of the city theaters' year, program by program, and of when the monthly program began. It gives the calendar as a rule of the 18th and 19th centuries, dating only a few customs (the spring Soga plays from the Kyōhō era, 1716-1736; the leading actors' summer programs from Bunka-Bunsei, 1804-1830). It describes only the licensed great theaters - Edo's three and the large houses of Kyoto and Osaka - and names the small shrine-ground theaters without describing their year. It says nothing of the puppet theaters or of how many hours a day was played, and gives no run lengths: how long a program ran is read from the opening dates it gives.

Used for: the city theaters' year, its programs, how long a program ran and the summer rest (Money and prices)
