Tokugawa Institute for Forestry History (徳川林政史研究所), 『人・物・お金にみる山村の暮らし』 ("Life in a mountain village seen through its people, goods and money", title translated), booklet 9 (in Japanese; https://rinseishi.tokugawa.or.jp/pdf_file/booklet_09.pdf)

What it is: A research institute's booklet built on the diary kept by the household of Hikoshichi in Kashimo, an upland village of Mino, from 1763 to 1774, quoting its entries and tabling what the household bought, sold and paid.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the price of salt at each purchase the household made over eleven years, and the day wage the same household paid a hired farm hand, so a price and an unskilled wage of one place and decade stand side by side. It is one household of one inland village, its prices those of the salt peddlers who came to the door, and it gives no craftsman's day wage; its own dates for one entry disagree with its table (an HTML note on the page that cites it says where).

Used for: the price of salt and a farm hand's day wage in an upland Mino village, 1763-1774 (Money and prices)
