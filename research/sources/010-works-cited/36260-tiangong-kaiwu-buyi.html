tiangong-kaiwu-buyi

Song Yingxing, 《天工開物》 (Tiangong kaiwu, 1637), the chapter 「布衣」 ("Cloth garments", title translated), on Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/天工開物/布衣)

What it is: The short section on cotton in the late Ming encyclopedia of crafts: its kinds, the spinning and weaving, and where each stage was best done.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states, in 1637, that cotton cloth for keeping out the cold was the same for high and low and grown across the realm. These are one writer's general statements with no count behind them, and his "ten houses" is a manner of speaking.

Used for: cotton as winter cloth for all ranks by 1637 (Money and prices)
