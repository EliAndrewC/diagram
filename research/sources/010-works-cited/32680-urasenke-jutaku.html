urasenke-jutaku

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 「Urasenke house」 (translated; original: 「裏千家住宅」), the national database of designated cultural properties (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/db/heritages/detail/122434)

What it is: The database listing for the Urasenke house in Kyoto, an Important Cultural Property: the house of the Urasenke line of tea masters, dated to the end of the Edo period (about 1839, its Kan'untei room 1788). It is a list of the house's rooms and a short note, not a description of the grounds.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists the house's well among the parts of its kitchen, at the house of the Urasenke tea masters, whose two most important tea rooms stand in it. It is a tea master's house in the capital, not a samurai's or an official's, and as a room list it says nothing of the garden paths, so it shows where this one house kept its well and no more.

Used for: the well listed with the kitchen of a tea master's house (Estates and other compounds)
