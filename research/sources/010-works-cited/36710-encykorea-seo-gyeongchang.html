Kim Yeong-jin, "서경창" (Seo Gyeong-chang), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture (한국민족문화대백과사전), the Academy of Korean Studies (in Korean; https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0027593)

What it is: The encyclopedia's signed article on the author of a treatise of 1813 on planting the sweet potato.

Why it applies, and its limits: It alone reports the 1633 date for the plant in Korea and the estimate of a still earlier arrival, and says these tell against the 1763 date. It reports the treatise's contents without quoting them, and the treatise itself was not read.

Used for: The dispute over the first planting of the sweet potato in Korea, and the government's printing of 1814 (Money and prices)
