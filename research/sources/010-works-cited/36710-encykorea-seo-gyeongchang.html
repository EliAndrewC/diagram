encykorea-seo-gyeongchang

Kim Yeong-jin, "서경창" (Seo Gyeong-chang), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture (한국민족문화대백과사전), the Academy of Korean Studies (in Korean; https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0027593)

What it is: The encyclopedia's signed article (written 1995, last revised 2023) on the author of a treatise of about 1813 on planting the sweet potato.

Why it applies, and its limits: It reports the estimate that the plant arrived in the reigns of Seonjo and Gwanghaegun and a record of the Border Defense Command's efforts to spread it in 1633, and says these tell against the 1763 date. The encyclopedia's separate article on the treatise (https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0053003) places both in the treatise's own closing discussion, so they are one author's statements of about 180 years after 1633, with no older record named here, and 1633 is a date for spreading the plant, not for its first planting. It reports the treatise's contents without quoting them, and the treatise itself was not read. The government's printing and distribution of 1814 is one sentence here with no record named, in an article that calls the treatise a manuscript, and the encyclopedia's separate article on the treatise mentions only a manuscript and no printing.

Used for: The dispute over the first planting of the sweet potato in Korea, and the government's printing of 1814 (Money and prices)
