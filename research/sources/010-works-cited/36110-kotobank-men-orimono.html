Kotobank, "綿織物" ("Cotton cloth", title translated), the article signed by Ogasawara Sae in the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, in Japanese (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%B6%BF%E7%B9%94%E7%89%A9-141907)

What it is: A Japanese encyclopedia's article on cotton cloth, whose section on Japan is signed by Ogasawara Sae and runs from the Shosoin fragments to the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places cotton's stages in Japan in one signed account and names the Shosoin fragments, the Korean trade and the Chinese cloth that displaced it. Its sentence that cotton wholly overcame hemp from the Edo period on gives no date and no region, and the growing districts it names next, from warm Kyushu to Mikawa, Ise, Yamato and Kawachi, include none in the cold north, and it quotes William Adams only in a Japanese rendering.

Used for: the first cotton in Japan, the import trade, and cotton as commoners' clothing (Money and prices)
