jacks-2008-gpih-beijing-prices

David Jacks and Leticia Arroyo Abad, "Prices in Beijing 1900-1924", a spreadsheet for the Global Price and Income History Group (revised April 2008) (https://gpih.ucdavis.edu/files/Beijing_prices_1900-1924.xls)

What it is: A transcription of the yearly prices of flour, wheat, rice, bean flour, millet (retail and wholesale) and corn flour in Beijing from Meng and Gamble's study of Beijing's prices and wages of 1900-1924, in dollars a hundred catties, with conversions to metric units and grams of silver.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one year-by-year series of Beijing's grain prices found for the years before 1920. The printed study it copies was not read here; its rice begins only in 1913; and its own conversions take a catty as half a kilogram and a dollar at the American price of silver, so this record uses its prices in dollars only. They are the capital's prices, not a market town's or a village's; the sheet calls only its millet retail or wholesale.

Used for: Beijing's grain and flour prices of 1900-1919 (Money and prices)
