Prince Gong's Mansion Museum (恭王府博物馆), 「清代王府演戏」 ("Plays in the princes' mansions of the Qing", title translated), May 2009, on the museum's research pages (in Chinese; https://www.pgm.org.cn/pgm/xsyjou/200905/09b229c3dc224f7c9d56161babe0693d.shtml)

What it is: A museum's research article on the troupes kept and hired by the Qing princes' households, quoting the court's edicts on officials who kept or hired actors.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the edict of 1799 that let a local official hire an outside troupe for a festival but forbade him to keep one in his office. The article's subject is the princes' households in Beijing, it gives the edicts in excerpt with gaps, and its web text breaks off mid-sentence in one quotation.

Used for: whether a Qing local official could hire a troupe, 1799 (Money and prices)
