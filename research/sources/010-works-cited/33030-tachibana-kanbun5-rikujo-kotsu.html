tachibana-kanbun5-rikujo-kotsu

Tachibana Toshio (橘敏夫), 「寛文5年における江戸幕府の陸上交通政策」 ("The Edo shogunate's land transport policy in Kanbun 5", title translated), Aichi Daigaku Sogo Kyodo Kenkyujo Kiyo 57 (2012), pp. 69-80 (in Japanese; Aichi University repository, https://aichiu.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/1320/files/%E6%A9%98%E3%80%80%E6%95%8F%E5%A4%AB.pdf)

What it is: A historian's study of the shogunate's survey of the post stations and their levied villages in 1665, on the Tokaido and the Nakasendo, quoting the stations' petitions, the shogunate's orders and the stations' own statements of that year.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the oldest stage fares found, at Hamamatsu and Takamiya in 1665, and the Nakasendo order's rules for a porter's and a light horse's load. The Hamamatsu fares are those after a rise withdrawn within the year, and it gives no distance for them; the Takamiya statement prints three fares without saying which load each is for.

Used for: the stage fares of 1665 and the rules for a porter's and a light horse's load (Money and prices)
