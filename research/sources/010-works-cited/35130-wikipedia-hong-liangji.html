Wikipedia, "Hong Liangji" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Liangji)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Qing scholar and official Hong Liangji (1746-1809), with his birthplace and a list of his works and their dates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the two facts the record needs to place his essay on livelihoods: that he was of Changzhou, and that his Opinions (Yiyan), the collection it belongs to, date to 1793. Its date of 1793 is given for the collection in a list and for one essay of it in the prose, with no source cited for either.

Used for: Hong Liangji's home and the date of his Yiyan (Money and prices)
