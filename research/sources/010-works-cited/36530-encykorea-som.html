encykorea-som

Lee Eun-jin, "Som" (padding, wadding), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, Academy of Korean Studies, written 2022, last revised 2023 (https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0030605)

What it is: An encyclopedia article by a university professor on padding for clothes and bedding in Korea, cotton and silk.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the "Song of Cotton" by the scholar Yi Ik (1681-1763; his collected works compiled 1774), which was not read in its own edition, so it speaks for Korea in his lifetime, about four centuries after the 1363 introduction of cotton the article gives, and not for how far cotton had reached earlier. The poem, in the lines the article quotes, says cotton clothed even the low and base, in a poet's terms, and gives no count; the article quotes it to show that padded cotton clothes were worn.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
