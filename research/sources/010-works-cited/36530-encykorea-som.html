encykorea-som

Lee Eun-jin, "Som" (padding, wadding), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, Academy of Korean Studies, written 2022, last revised 2023 (https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0030605)

What it is: An encyclopedia article by a university professor on padding for clothes and bedding in Korea, cotton and silk.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes a poem by the scholar Yi Ik on who wore cotton, which was not read in its own edition. The poem, in the lines the article quotes, says cotton clothed even the low and base, in a poet's terms, and gives no count; the article quotes it to show that padded cotton clothes were worn.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
