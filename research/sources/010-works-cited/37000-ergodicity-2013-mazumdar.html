Anand Sarwate, "The history of new foods in India", An Ergodic Walk (blog), 9 March 2013, summarizing and quoting Sucheta Mazumdar, "The Impact of New World Food Crops on the Diet and Economy of China and India, ca. 1600-1900", in Food in Global History, ed. Raymond Grew (Westview Press, 1999), pp. 58-78 (https://ergodicity.net/2013/03/09/the-history-of-new-foods-in-india/)

What it is: A blog post by a reader of Mazumdar's chapter, giving its argument about China and India in a few sentences and quoting short passages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only access here to Mazumdar's comparison, whose chapter was not read. It is one reader's notes, not a historian's review. Only the passages the post attributes to her are her words: the two it sets off after naming her and two short phrases in quotation marks, on inheritable tenancy and on India's population. The rest is the blogger's paraphrase, and "around 150 years ago" is the blogger's own phrase, about India. On China it gives only unnamed tenancy reforms and the sweet potato's advantages, undated, so it cannot show when or how fast the crops spread there.

Used for: What set the pace of new crops (Money and prices)
