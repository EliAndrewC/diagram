Anand Sarwate, "The history of new foods in India", An Ergodic Walk (blog), 9 March 2013, summarizing and quoting Sucheta Mazumdar, "The Impact of New World Food Crops on the Diet and Economy of China and India, ca. 1600-1900", in Food in Global History, ed. Raymond Grew (Westview Press, 1999), pp. 58-78 (https://ergodicity.net/2013/03/09/the-history-of-new-foods-in-india/)

What it is: A blog post by a reader of Mazumdar's chapter, giving its argument about China and India in a few sentences and quoting short passages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only access here to Mazumdar's comparison, whose chapter was not read. Only the passages the post itself puts in quotation marks are her words; the rest is the blogger's paraphrase, and "around 150 years ago" is the blogger's own phrase.
