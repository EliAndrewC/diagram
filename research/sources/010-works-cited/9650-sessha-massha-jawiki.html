ja.wikipedia 摂末社 (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%91%82%E6%9C%AB%E7%A4%BE)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article defining subsidiary shrines, the sessha and massha, attached to a main shrine within or near its precinct.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the feature. Its limits: a stub flagged as unsourced; its formal classes come from the Meiji-to-war shrine-ranking system, and it says it is unknown whether the word was used before modern times; its examples are all great shrines, and it says nothing of how often a village shrine had one.

Used for: subsidiary shrines (Religion and the dead).
