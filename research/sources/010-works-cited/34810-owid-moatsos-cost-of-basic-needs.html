Our World in Data, "Share of population living in extreme poverty: Long-run estimates - Cost of basic needs", a chart page on the data of Michalis Moatsos (2021), archived August 17, 2026 (https://archive.ourworldindata.org/20260817-183715/grapher/share-of-population-living-in-extreme-poverty-lines-cbn.html)

What it is: The chart page of a research publication based at the University of Oxford, showing Moatsos's estimates of the share of the world living in extreme poverty from 1820 to 2018, with his own description of his method quoted and his source named.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only readable account here of how Moatsos's long-run poverty lines are built: one line for every country and year, priced at the local prices of the time. It quotes his method in three sentences and reports his figures after its own processing, so the detail of his work, in his papers of 2016 and 2021, is not on it.

Used for: how a long-run basic-needs poverty count is priced (Money and prices)
