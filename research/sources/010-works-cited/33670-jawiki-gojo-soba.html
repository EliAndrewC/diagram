jawiki-gojo-soba

"御定相場" ("Official exchange rate"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/御定相場)

What it is: A short encyclopedia article on the exchange rates the shogunate fixed by law between gold, silver and copper coin, how each city quoted its rates, and the changes of 1609, 1700 and 1842.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the legal rates, which the market did not keep to, and how Edo and Osaka quoted copper, citing a historical dictionary and studies of the currency. It gives no market figures for copper, and for 1700 it states the silver rate alone (60 monme to the ryō), giving copper only as a floor (money changers might not trade copper at 3,900 mon or fewer to the ryō), not an official copper rate. Its 1700 and 1842 rates and its account of how each city quoted carry no footnote of their own; they rest on its general reference, Miyamoto Mataji's entry in the Kokushi daijiten (1980).

Used for: the official rate of 1700 and the Edo rate of 1842, and how Edo and Osaka quoted copper (Money and prices)
