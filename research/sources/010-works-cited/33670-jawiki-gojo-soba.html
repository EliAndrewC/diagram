"御定相場" ("Official exchange rate"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/御定相場)

What it is: A short encyclopedia article on the exchange rates the shogunate fixed by law between gold, silver and copper coin, how each city quoted its rates, and the changes of 1609, 1700 and 1842.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the legal rates, which the market did not keep to, and how Edo and Osaka quoted copper, citing a historical dictionary and studies of the currency. It is a short summary and gives no market figures for copper.

Used for: the official rate of 1700 and the Edo rate of 1842, and how Edo and Osaka quoted copper (Money and prices)
