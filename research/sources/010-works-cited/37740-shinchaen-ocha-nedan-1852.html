shinchaen-ocha-nedan-1852

Shinchaen (真茶園), "その昔、お茶の値段" ("The price of tea, long ago", title translated), a tea shop's web column of 23 February 2023, reproducing an Edo tea merchant's printed price list of 1852 from Takau Masamitsu (高宇政光), 日本茶の世界 (in Japanese; https://www.shinchaen.com/column/その昔、お茶の値段/)

What it is: A short column by a tea shop that shows a picture of a tea merchant's handbill dated autumn 1852, from a book on Japanese tea, and reads one line of it. The handbill, of a shop in Nihonbashi in Edo, lists teas by name with a price a kin, under headings for powdered tea, for leaf tea of Uji and Shigaraki, and for leaf tea of Suruga.

Why it applies, and its limits: The handbill is the one list found of Edo shop prices under the name of the district the tea came from. It reaches this record as a small picture in a shop's column: the column reads one price, and the rest is read here from the picture, where some figures are not legible. The handbill does not say how heavy its kin was, and the column's 600 g is its own gloss.

Used for: an Edo tea shop's prices of 1852 for the tea of Uji, Shigaraki and Suruga (Money and prices)
