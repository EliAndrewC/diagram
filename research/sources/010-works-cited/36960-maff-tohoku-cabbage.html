maff-tohoku-cabbage

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, Tohoku Regional Agricultural Administration Office (東北農政局), "キャベツ" (Cabbage), a short page on foods of the region (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/tohoku/monosiritai/syokutaku/kyabetu.html)

What it is: A short page, in simple language, from a regional office of Japan's agriculture ministry on where cabbage came from.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read that says an Edo-period arrival did not at that time become a vegetable, and it dates edible cabbage to the mid-19th century. It gives no year for the Edo arrival and no source for its history.

Used for: Cabbage in Japan under the seclusion edicts (Money and prices)
