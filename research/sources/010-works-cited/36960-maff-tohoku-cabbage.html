maff-tohoku-cabbage

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, Tohoku Regional Agricultural Administration Office (東北農政局), "キャベツ" (Cabbage), its page for children on foods (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/tohoku/monosiritai/syokutaku/kyabetu.html)

What it is: A short page for children from a regional office of Japan's agriculture ministry on where cabbage came from.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read that says an Edo-period arrival did not become a vegetable. It gives neither a year nor a source.

Used for: Cabbage in Japan under the seclusion edicts (Money and prices)
