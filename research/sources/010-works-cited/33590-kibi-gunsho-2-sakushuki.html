kibi-gunsho-2-sakushuki

『吉備群書集成』 第2輯 (Collected writings of Kibi, vol. 2), 吉備群書集成刊行会, 1932, 『作州記』 貞の巻 〔七三〕 「諸職人作料値段定帳」 ("Register of fixed charges for the craftsmen's work", title translated), printed pp. 115-117 (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1912986)

What it is: A printed collection of old writings from the Kibi provinces (modern Okayama); its Sakushū-ki is a record of Mimasaka province, and this item in it is a register of the charges set on the first day of the eighth month of 1665 for the work of named craftsmen: three smiths, eight polishers, the makers of metal fittings and scabbards, and others.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices a kitchen knife beside swords, razors and scissors from the same smiths, in silver. The figures are charges fixed by authority, which other smiths were to undercut, so they are ceilings rather than prices paid; the smiths named are swordsmiths, and the register does not say whether the iron was the smith's or the buyer's. It is a modern typeset transcription, not the manuscript.

Used for: a kitchen knife's fixed charge in Mimasaka in 1665, and the polishers' tariff beside it (Money and prices)
