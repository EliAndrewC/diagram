zheng-tianting-qing-natural-economy

Zheng Tianting (郑天挺), 「鸦片战争前清代社会的自然经济」 ("The natural economy of Qing society before the Opium War", title translated), on Aisixiang (in Chinese; https://www.aisixiang.com/data/176107.html)

What it is: An essay by a historian of the Ming and Qing, first published in 《中国社会经济史研究》 in 1982 (no. 1), arguing that the Qing countryside stayed a self-sufficient economy of farming joined to home spinning and weaving, with Shanghai county's cotton weavers as its example and Yang Guangfu's poems, the Songnan yuefu, and the Tongzhi-era Shanghai county gazetteer as its evidence.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the poems on farm women spinning by night and gives a weaver's output in a day. The output is the historian's own statement, citing no count, in the unit duan rather than the bolt; and the essay argues a thesis of its time, so it is read here for its quotations and its one figure. And by the essay's own account, Shanghai county in Songjiang prefecture was the most commercial district of Ming and Qing China, where farmers grew more cotton than rice and sold much of their cloth. Its spinning by night and its cloth a day are therefore the top of the range, not a household's rate elsewhere.

Used for: a weaver's cloth in a day and the farm women's night spinning around Shanghai (Money and prices)
