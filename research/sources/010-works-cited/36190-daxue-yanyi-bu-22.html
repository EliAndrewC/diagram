daxue-yanyi-bu-22

Qiu Jun (丘濬), Daxue yanyi bu (大學衍義補, Supplement to the Extended Meaning of the Great Learning), chapter 22, the Siku quanshu text on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/大學衍義補_(四庫全書本)/卷022), and the memorial presenting the treatise, on its opening page (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/大學衍義補_(四庫全書本))

What it is: A chapter of a Ming minister's statecraft treatise, presented to the throne in 1487, here his comment on cotton under the taxes of cloth and grain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the earliest general statement read that cotton had reached all regions and both rich and poor. It is one official's judgment with no count, set down in a chapter on regular taxes so that later ages would know cotton's profit first flourished in his own time; for cotton as tribute before the Song he is a later reader and no witness, writing more than five centuries after the Tang and naming no instance of such tribute, only a gloss on the Yu gong and cotton's absence from earlier tax rules; and its claim that the Song and Yuan did not levy cotton is contradicted for the Yuan by the Yuan history's own treatise, which has the Jiangnan summer tax of 1296 paid partly in cotton cloth and does not speak to the Song.

Used for: cotton as tribute before the Song, and its spread across the realm by the 1480s (Money and prices)
