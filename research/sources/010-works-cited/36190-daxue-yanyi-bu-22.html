Qiu Jun (丘濬), Daxue yanyi bu (大學衍義補, Supplement to the Extended Meaning of the Great Learning), chapter 22, the Siku quanshu text on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/大學衍義補_(四庫全書本)/卷022)

What it is: A chapter of the statecraft treatise a Ming minister presented to the throne in 1487, here his comment on cotton under the taxes of cloth and grain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the earliest general statement read that cotton had reached all regions and both rich and poor. It is one official's judgment with no count, written to argue for a tax policy; and its claim that the Song and Yuan did not levy cotton is contradicted by the Yuan history's own treatise.

Used for: cotton as tribute before the Song, and its spread across the realm by the 1480s (Money and prices)
