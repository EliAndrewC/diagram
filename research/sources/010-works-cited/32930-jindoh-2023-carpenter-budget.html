jindoh-2023-carpenter-budget

不二考匠 (Fuji Kosho), 「【歴史小話】大工の家計」 ("A short history: a carpenter's household budget", title translated), note.com, 9 March 2023 (in Japanese; https://note.com/takamasa_jindoh/n/nef9a0e8fdb30)

What it is: A blog post by a retired systems engineer that restates the Edo carpenter's household budget given in the popular book 『江戸のお勘定』 (edited by Oishi Manabu): his day wage, his working days, and his spending on rent, rice, food and fuel, tools, clothing and social costs, in silver, for the Bunka-Bunsei years of 1804-1830.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only figure read for an Edo carpenter's day wage early in the 1800s. It is third-hand: the blog reports the book, which it believes drew on the 『文政年間漫録』, and neither the book nor that record was read; it gives one rate for a whole generation and no account of which carpenters it describes.

Used for: an Edo carpenter's day wage early in the 1800s (Money and prices)
