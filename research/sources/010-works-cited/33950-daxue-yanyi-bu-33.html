Qiu Jun (丘濬), Daxue yanyi bu (大學衍義補, Supplement to the Extended Meaning of the Great Learning), chapter 33, "What befits grain transport, part 1" (漕輓之宜上), the Siku quanshu text on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/大學衍義補_(四庫全書本)/卷033)

What it is: A chapter of the statecraft treatise a Ming minister presented to the throne in 1487, quoting the histories on how grain was carried from the Qin dynasty to the Tang, each passage followed by his own comment.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gathers the old histories' figures for what carriage cost against the grain delivered: the Qin and Han armies' supply, and the Tang canal under Liu Yan in the 760s. The figures are the histories' as Qiu Jun quotes them, abridged, not read here in the histories themselves, and the Qin and Han ones are military hauls to the frontier.

Used for: the Qin, Han and Tang costs of carrying grain, as Qiu Jun quotes them (Money and prices)
