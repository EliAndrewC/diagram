川戸貴史 (Kawato Takashi), 「鉄炮1挺60万円、軍事費トータル10億円！？ 戦国大名の懐事情とは」 ("A musket for 600,000 yen and ten billion yen of war costs? The purses of the Sengoku daimyo", title translated), Gendai Shinsho, Kodansha, 18 June 2020, an excerpt from his book 『戦国大名の経済学』 (in Japanese; https://gendai.media/articles/-/72981)

What it is: An excerpt, on the publisher's site, from an economic historian's book on the money of the Sengoku daimyo, pricing a soldier's swords, spear, archer's glove, armor, horse and musket from the documents that survive, and turning each into present-day yen.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page found that speaks to what an arrowhead was worth in Japan, and it says so only as the author's own inference, from the price of a spear, with no document behind it. Its yen equivalents are the author's, and this record takes none of them.
