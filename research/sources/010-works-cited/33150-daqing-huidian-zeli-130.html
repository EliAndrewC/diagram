daqing-huidian-zeli-130

欽定大清會典則例 (Precedents of the Collected Statutes of the Great Qing, title translated), volume 130, Board of War, the Wuku office, "Military equipment, part 2", the Siku Quanshu edition (in Chinese; Wikisource transcription; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/欽定大清㑹典則例_(四庫全書本)/卷130)

What it is: A volume of the Qing government's own compilation of the rulings behind its statutes, finished in the 1760s, setting out year by year what the Board of War ruled on the arms of the banner and provincial troops, among them the cost in silver allowed for making each piece of a provincial soldier's equipment.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its ruling of 1650 sets the silver allowed for a provincial soldier's arrow beside his bow, armor, sabre and musket, a primary figure for an ordinary arrow early in the Qing. It is an allowance for making the arms out of local funds, fixed for every province at once, so it says what the state would pay, not what an arrow cost in any one market.

Used for: the Qing allowance for a provincial soldier's arrow in 1650 (Money and prices)
