kodama-takamatsu-sato-kawase

Kodama Yoichi (兒玉洋一), "高松藩に於ける砂糖為替の研究" ("A study of the sugar bills of exchange of Takamatsu domain", title translated), 香川大学経済論叢 (Kagawa University Economic Review) 17(2-3) (in Japanese; https://kagawa-u.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/4235/files/AN00138785_17_2_36.pdf)

What it is: A mid-twentieth-century economic historian's study of how Takamatsu domain, in Sanuki province on Shikoku, financed its white sugar: the advances to growers, the domain's sugar offices, the shipment to Osaka and the sale there. It quotes a domain record of about 1858 on freight, and reckons the province's output from 1836 to 1871 at a flat value a kin.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the costs between Sanuki and Osaka - freight a cask, a levy on sales - and how the sugar was sold in Osaka. Its value a kin is the author's flat rate for thirty-six years in domain notes, not a dated price at either end, and its cask of 100 kin is his average. No page of it gives an Osaka sale price or a dated price in Sanuki; about half its pages were read here only by a search of their page images for prices and charges.

Used for: the freight, the levy and the way of sale of Sanuki's white sugar (Money and prices)
