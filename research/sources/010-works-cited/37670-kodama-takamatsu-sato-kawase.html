kodama-takamatsu-sato-kawase

Kodama Yoichi (兒玉洋一), "高松藩に於ける砂糖為替の研究" ("A study of the sugar bills of exchange of Takamatsu domain", title translated), 高松高商論叢 (Takamatsu Kōshō Ronsō, the journal of Takamatsu Higher Commercial School, now held by Kagawa University's repository) 17(2-3), pp. 36-84, November 1942 (in Japanese; https://kagawa-u.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/4235/files/AN00138785_17_2_36.pdf)

What it is: A 1942 economic historian's study of how Takamatsu domain, in Sanuki province on Shikoku, financed its white sugar: the advances to growers, the domain's sugar offices, the shipment to Osaka and the sale there. It quotes a domain record of about 1858 on freight, and reckons the province's output from 1836 to 1871 at a flat value a kin.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the costs between Sanuki and Osaka - a freight for white sugar and another by the cask, a levy on sales - and how the sugar was sold in Osaka. The record it quotes lists the two freights apart, states white sugar's as 2 bu 2 rin and then 2 bu 6 rin without saying of what, and names no money for the cask rate; the levy is named among the domain's measures of the Tenpo years with no year of its own. Its value a kin is the author's flat rate for thirty-six years in domain notes, not a dated price at either end, and its cask of 100 kin is his average. No page of it gives an Osaka sale price or a dated price in Sanuki; about half its pages were read here only by a search of their page images for prices and charges.

Used for: the freight, the levy and the way of sale of Sanuki's white sugar (Money and prices)
