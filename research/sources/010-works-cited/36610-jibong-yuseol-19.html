jibong-yuseol-19

Yi Su-gwang (李睟光), Jibong yuseol (芝峯類說), chapter 19, the Korean Wikisource transcription of the classical Chinese text (https://ko.wikisource.org/wiki/번역:지봉유설/19권)

What it is: A Korean scholar's encyclopedia of miscellaneous knowledge, in classical Chinese, whose entry on tobacco is the earliest Korean account the record has read.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a witness of the time on the plant's arrival and planting. The transcription is a volunteer's and carries no date, so the year 1614 is taken from a modern study. That is the year the book is said to have been compiled; this chapter calls the king of 1614 "Gwanghae", a name unlikely before his deposition in 1623, so the text as read may be later than 1614 and the tobacco entry cannot be dated to that year with certainty; "recent years" and "many" are the author's words, with no count and no place.

Used for: Tobacco in Korea (Money and prices)
