zizhi-tongjian-159

Sima Guang (司馬光), Zizhi tongjian (資治通鑑, Comprehensive Mirror in Aid of Governance), chapter 159, on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/資治通鑑/卷159)

What it is: The Song chronicler's account of the years 545 and 546, including a character sketch of the Liang emperor Wu under 545.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the latest of the three histories Pelliot cites for the emperor's black hanging of 木綿. Sima Guang finished it in 1084, more than five centuries after the emperor's reign, but he repeats almost word for word a sentence of the Liang shu (chapter 3, compiled in the 630s), so the testimony is that earlier history's and this text is no independent witness. The sentence says only 木綿, a word also used then of the tree whose floss southerners made into wadding, and it does not say which fibre the cloth was or where it came from; calling it cotton is Pelliot's wording. Its 帳 can mean a tent, as Pelliot renders it, or a curtain. The sentence's point is his austerity, so it says nothing of how common the cloth was.

Used for: cotton at the Liang court (Money and prices)
