Sima Guang (司馬光), Zizhi tongjian (資治通鑑, Comprehensive Mirror in Aid of Governance), chapter 159, on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/資治通鑑/卷159)

What it is: The Song chronicler's account of the years 545 and 546, including a character sketch of the Liang emperor Wu under 545.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the text behind the emperor's cotton curtain, which Pelliot cites. It was written more than five centuries after the emperor's reign, and its point is his austerity, so it says nothing of how common the cloth was.

Used for: cotton at the Liang court (Money and prices)
