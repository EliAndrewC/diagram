gunma-monjokan-keian-ofuregaki

Gunma Prefectural Archives, 「第3回慶安御触書」 ("The Keian edict, installment 3", title translated), updated 2022, with the original's image, a transcription and a reading in modern Japanese (in Japanese; https://www.pref.gunma.jp/site/monjyokan/191712.html; the transcription, https://www.pref.gunma.jp/uploaded/attachment/146048.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural archives' serial presentation of a printed copy of the Keian edict, a few articles an installment, each with a photograph of the pages, a transcription with readings and a modern reading with notes on terms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the edict's own wording of the article telling farmers to rise early and work through the day, which the record otherwise has only in a modern summary. The pages read do not say which copy it is or when it was printed, so the date and authorship of the edict are taken from elsewhere.

Used for: the Keian edict's order to rise early (Money and prices)
