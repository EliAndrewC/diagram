gunma-monjokan-keian-ofuregaki

Gunma Prefectural Archives, 「第3回慶安御触書」 ("The Keian edict, installment 3", title translated), updated 2022, with the original's image, a transcription and a yomikudashi reading (in Japanese; https://www.pref.gunma.jp/site/monjyokan/191712.html; the transcription, https://www.pref.gunma.jp/uploaded/attachment/146048.pdf; the teaching page, https://www.pref.gunma.jp/site/monjyokan/130131.html)

What it is: A prefectural archives' serial presentation of a printed copy of the Keian edict, a few articles an installment, each with a photograph of the pages, a transcription with readings and a yomikudashi (the text rendered in Japanese word order) with notes on terms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the edict's own wording of the article telling farmers to rise early and work through the day, which the record otherwise has only in a modern summary. The installment pages do not say which copy they show; the same archives' teaching page on the edict shows a printing of 1838 (Tenpo 9), block-owned by Yamamoto Daizen, from the Iizuka family papers, and says its text is based on the edition the Iwamura domain printed in 1830. That page adds that no original of the edict's traditional date, 1649, has been found and that whether it was ever issued has been argued since the Meiji period, so the record cites its wording as a late-Edo printed instruction to farmers, never as a law of 1649.

Used for: the Keian edict's order to rise early (Money and prices)
