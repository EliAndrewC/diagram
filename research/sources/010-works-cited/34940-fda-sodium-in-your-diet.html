fda-sodium-in-your-diet

US Food and Drug Administration, "Sodium in Your Diet", consumer guidance page (https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-education-resources-materials/sodium-your-diet)

What it is: The food regulator's page for the public on how much sodium Americans eat, where it comes from, and the daily limit it advises.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: how much salt an American takes in a year, as a yardstick for a premodern laborer's salt, never a figure of the setting. It gives an average day's sodium from all food, most of it from packaged and prepared food, not the salt a household buys; it names no year or survey for its average.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
