jiandao-zdic

漢典 ("Han Dian, an online Chinese dictionary", title translated) zdic.net, 箭道 ("archery strip", title translated) (https://www.zdic.net/hans/箭道)

What it is: The entry for 箭道 (jiandao) in Zdic (漢典), an online Chinese character and word dictionary. It defines the word as an archery practice ground that government offices set up in former times, with one Tang poem as its example of use.

Why it applies, and its limits: What we take is the meaning of the word as the Qing gazetteers use it of a county office's grounds. The entry gives no size, form, date or place for such a ground, and its one example is a Tang poem, not an office's plan.

Used for: what the archery strip (箭道) inside a county office was (Towns)
