tamba-kaibara-nagayamon

Tamba City, 柏原藩主織田家旧邸長屋門 ("The rowhouse gate of the former residence of the Oda, lords of the Kaibara domain", title translated), updated 2024 (in Japanese; https://www.city.tamba.lg.jp/soshiki/shakaikyoikubunkazaika/gyomuannai/7/1/2558.html)

What it is: The Tamba city education board's short page on the Kaibara jin'ya's rowhouse gate as a prefectural cultural property (designated 1962): its building in 1714 with the seat, its escape from the fire of 1818, the order of its rooms, and its dismantling and rebuilding, finished in 1961.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the gate's rooms in order from the gate outward, as a visitor faces it, which the city's page on the whole site does not. Its limits: it is one gate, at the seat of one small domain's lords (the Oda of Kaibara, about 20,000 koku), so its order is one example and not a rule, and a lesser magistrate's office need not have had a horse-viewing room or a gun store; it is a few sentences long, names the rooms without their sizes or how many mats each took, and describes the gate as rebuilt in 1961, so the order is that of the restored building, which the page takes to be the first one.

Used for: the order of the rooms in a jin'ya's rowhouse gate, the horse-viewing room beside the gate and the gun store beyond it (Estates and other compounds)
