Guo Weidong (郭卫东), "印度棉花: 鸦片战争之前外域原料的规模化入华" (Indian cotton: the large-scale import of a foreign raw material into China before the Opium War), a journal article in Chinese filed on a Peking University site (https://fh.pku.edu.cn/docs/2018-11/20181110172100744658.pdf)

What it is: A Peking University historian's article on raw cotton imported into China from India in the 18th and 19th centuries.

Why it applies, and its limits: One sentence of its opening states when cotton replaced hemp and silk, which is the summary the wider literature commonly gives. The sentence is a passing generality with no evidence set beside it, in a paper about a later period.

Used for: a modern summary of when cotton displaced the older fibers (Money and prices)
