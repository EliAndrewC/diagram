Kibe Kazuaki (木部和昭), "萩藩における領主廻米運賃について" ("The shipping expenses of the land-tax rice transportation in the Hagi domain, 1703-1862"), 山口経済学雑誌 (Yamaguchi Journal of Economics) 47-2 (1999), 729-756 (in Japanese; https://petit.lib.yamaguchi-u.ac.jp/8245/files/164377)

What it is: A historian's study of the freight the Hagi domain, at the western tip of Honshu, paid the ships that carried its tax rice to Osaka and to its own castle town, from the domain's freight schedules of 1703 to 1862 and a statement of 1652.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives freight by sea as a share of the rice carried, by district and by year, which is carriage against the good's own value. The shares are the schedules' base rates, which the domain later paid at a discount, they were paid in rice, and they mix distance with the danger of a winter voyage on the Sea of Japan.

Used for: the freight on the Hagi domain's rice by sea, as a share of the rice (Money and prices)
