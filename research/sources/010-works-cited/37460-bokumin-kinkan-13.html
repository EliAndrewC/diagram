bokumin-kinkan-13

荒井顕道 (Arai Akimichi), compiler, 『牧民金鑑』 (Bokumin kinkan, a compilation of the shogunate's orders to its rural intendants), [13], a manuscript copy in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547281/1/25 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547281)

What it is: A volume of a compilation of the shogunate's orders and rulings on governing its own rural lands. The page read copies an order of Kanpo 3 (1743) on the materials villages had long supplied for river-bank and weir works, which lists item by item what was to be village service: short stakes, bamboo and timber, and empty bales, rope, straw matting, vines and wisteria rope.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the rule itself, for the shogunate's own lands: empty bales and rope for river works were a levy on the villages, supplied in kind, not bought. It speaks of public works on rivers and weirs, not of trade, and not of charcoal bales; and a rule says what was ordered, not how every district did it (other pages of the volume, not read on their images, have Kai province charging such goods to the district as a whole). It is a later compiler's copy of the order.

Used for: empty bales and rope as a village levy for river works, 1743 (Money and prices)
