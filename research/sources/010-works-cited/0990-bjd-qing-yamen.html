北京日报 (Beijing Daily), 清代各级地方官署旧址巡礼 ("A Tour of the Former Sites of Local Government Offices at All Levels in the Qing Dynasty", title translated) (https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s68f22db6d5dee3918d4d209e.html)

What it is: A local-history feature by Wang Milin in the Beijing Daily of 2025-10-17, touring the surviving Qing local government compounds: the halls ranged along a central axis, the symmetry of the plan, and the courtyards and room counts of a surviving example.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the surviving Qing yamen's plan in plain terms, the courtyard-and-axis form our maps give the seat of government.

Used for: the axial, symmetrical courtyard plan of a Qing local yamen; the sizes and court-by-court order of a governor-general's, a prefect's and a department's yamen (The government quarter); the Zhili governor-general's three-bay main gate on its platform, and the open front court inside it (Estates and other compounds); the Huozhou department yamen inside its city on the north side of East Main Street (Towns)
