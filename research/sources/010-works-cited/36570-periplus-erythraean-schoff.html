periplus-erythraean-schoff

The Periplus of the Erythraean Sea, translated and edited by W. H. Schoff (London, Bombay and Calcutta, 1912), on the Internet Ancient History Sourcebook (https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/ancient/periplus.asp)

What it is: A merchant's handbook of the first century to the ports of the Red Sea and the Indian Ocean, with what each bought and sold, in English translation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows Indian cotton cloth as a trade good of named places in that century. It lists what merchants handled at the ports it knew, on the west coast above all, and gives no figure for how much was made, only words such as "a great quantity," and does not say who wore it. The cloth words are the 1912 translator's, so his "muslins" show cotton cloth traded, not how fine that cloth was.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
