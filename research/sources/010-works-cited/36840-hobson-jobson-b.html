Henry Yule and A. C. Burnell, Hobson-Jobson, the letter B, the Wikisource transcription of the enlarged edition with William Crooke's additions (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Hobson-Jobson/B)

What it is: The standard glossary of Anglo-Indian words, here its entry on the brinjal, which quotes the traveler John Fryer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used only for Fryer's line naming potatoes with yams in a garden, which it dates 1673. It does not say which plant his word means.

Used for: The white potato in India: Fryer's garden (Money and prices)
