takaoka-shiryo-ge

高岡市 (the city of Takaoka), 『高岡史料』 (Historical materials of Takaoka, title translated), 下 (volume 2), 1909, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/899935/1/486; the note on the mirrors at https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/899935/1/487)

What it is: The second volume of the city's 1909 compilation of its records, which prints in full old documents kept by Takaoka families, among them, under the town's customs of the domain era, a letter of 1836 from the townsman who vowed the Sekino shrine's three great mirrors to the merchant Fukuokaya Seiemon, asking him to help pay for them.

Why it applies, and its limits: The letter is the donor's own word on what one mirror cost, about 17 ryo, but the sum takes in the cost of bringing the mirror from Kyoto and is given as an approximation, and it is one large mirror for a town shrine, not a village's. The letter's chronology (a visit to Kyoto "the year before last", in a letter of 1836) does not match the date of 1833 that a later page reads on the mirrors, and the print gives no word on where the original letter is now.

Used for: what one of a town shrine's great mirrors cost, and how it was paid for (Money and prices)
