moriya-benibana-aidake-2

Moriya Madoka (森谷圓人), "近世中期における紅花荷主の経営構造と上方取引（二）" ("The business of a safflower shipper and its trade with Kyoto and Osaka in the mid Edo period, part 2", title translated), a journal article in the Yamagata University library's collection of writings on safflower (in Japanese; https://www.lib.yamagata-u.ac.jp/database/benibana/img/bunken/76_moriya.pdf)

What it is: The second part of a study of the Aida house, safflower shippers outside the castle town of Yamagata, from the house's own papers. It follows the lots of 1736 to 1738 mark by mark: how they were shipped, what each load cost landed at Otsu, what each sold for, the freight sent with each waybill, and what the Kyoto wholesalers took off the sale.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its table of the 1737 lot sets a load's landed cost beside its sale and the freight, and its reckoning of the 1738 lot shows the wholesalers' two deductions. The landed costs are in the old gold coin and most sales in silver, so the two columns are not in one money; the freight column is what was sent with the waybills, which the article does not say was the whole cost of carriage. One house, three years.

Used for: the route of Yamagata's safflower, what a landed cost included, the freight and the wholesalers' deductions (Money and prices)
