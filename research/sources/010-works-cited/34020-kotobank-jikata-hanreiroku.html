kotobank-jikata-hanreiroku

"地方凡例録" ("Jikata hanreiroku", the Edo handbook of village administration, title translated), entries in the Digital Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Nipponica (by Iijima Chiaki), the revised Heibonsha World Encyclopedia (by Saito Yoichi), Yamakawa's Japanese History Dictionary and others, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%9C%B0%E6%96%B9%E5%87%A1%E4%BE%8B%E9%8C%B2)

What it is: A set of encyclopedia entries on the Jikata hanreiroku, the handbook of land, tax and village administration that Oishi Hisataka, a district officer of the Takasaki domain, began in 1791 and left at eleven books when he died in 1794, and on the different texts of it that circulated.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates and places the handbook whose carriage rule a historian quotes, and says which text the modern edition he quotes follows. Its entries differ on the year (1792 in one, 1794 in others), and none of them gives the carriage rule itself.

Used for: when and by whom the handbook was written, and which text its modern edition follows (Money and prices)
