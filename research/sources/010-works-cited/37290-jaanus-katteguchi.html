jaanus-katteguchi

JAANUS (Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System), "katteguchi" 勝手口 (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/node/3250)

What it is: The entry for katteguchi 勝手口, the kitchen or service door, in JAANUS, an online English-language dictionary of Japanese art and architectural terms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the late Edo houses of middle-ranking warriors as the houses that particularly used the katteguchi, with their small doma and the kitchen door often tucked away at the rear. It says where the door was, not what closed it.

Used for: the kitchen door at the rear of a middle-ranking warrior's house (Estates and other compounds)
