mie-u-tsu-dochu-nikki

Mie University Library, 「道中日記に見る津の記述」 ("Tsu as described in travel diaries", title translated), a one-table exhibit of its online gallery (in Japanese; https://www.lib.mie-u.ac.jp/r_and_d/gallery/gallery3/img/itiranhyou.pdf)

What it is: A table compiled by the university library of the passages that Edo-period travel diaries, written by travelers on their way to the Ise shrines from 1767 to the 1830s, give about the castle town of Tsu, each with its year and the published transcription or archive it was taken from.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives two prices travelers paid for a night at Tsu in their own words, 130 mon in 1790 and a firewood fee of 50 mon in 1823. Each is one traveler's one night at one inn, quoted as an excerpt without the rest of the diary, and the 1790 entry does not say what the 130 mon took in.

Used for: a night's lodging at Tsu in 1790 and 1823 (Money and prices)
