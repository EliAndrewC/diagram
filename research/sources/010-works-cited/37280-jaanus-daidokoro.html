jaanus-daidokoro

JAANUS (Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System), "daidokoro" 台所 (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/node/4838)

What it is: The entry for daidokoro 台所, the kitchen, in JAANUS, an online English-language dictionary of Japanese art and architectural terms, giving its senses from the Heian palace to the Edo farmhouse and town house.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its second sense describes the kitchen of an upper-class residence from the 13th to the 19th century as a building of its own, with a doma, a kamado and a smoke louvre in its roof. That sense is of the great residences, the daimyo's mansions among them; it does not say whether a small samurai house's kitchen had a louvre or stood apart.

Used for: the great residences' kitchen as a building with a smoke louvre (Estates and other compounds)
