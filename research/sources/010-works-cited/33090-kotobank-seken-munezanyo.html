kotobank-seken-munezanyo

コトバンク 世間胸算用 ("Seken munezan'yo", title translated), the entries of Shogakukan's Digital Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Nipponica and other dictionaries (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/世間胸算用)

What it is: Dictionary and encyclopedia entries on Seken munezan'yo, Ihara Saikaku's book of stories of 1692 about townspeople settling their debts on New Year's Eve.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the book is, its author, its date and that it is fiction, which is what a silk price taken from it must carry. It says nothing of the prices in the book itself.

Used for: the book a 1692 silk price comes from (Money and prices)
