hong-liangji-yiyan-shengji

Hong Liangji 洪亮吉, "Shengji pian" 生計篇 (On livelihoods), in Yiyan 意言, in Hong Beijiang shiwen ji 洪北江詩文集, Sibu congkan edition, Juanshi ge wen jia ji, juan 1, Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/zh/洪北江詩文集_(四部叢刊本)/甲集卷第一)

What it is: A short essay by a Qing scholar of Yanghu county, Changzhou, on how a growing population had pressed on livelihoods, setting what he had heard rice and cotton cloth cost in his grandfather's and father's time against what they cost as he wrote, in copper cash.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one cash price of cloth by the length found for the Lower Yangzi in the 1700s that can be read. Its figures are an essayist's round numbers, the older ones by hearsay ("I have heard") and the later ones unattributed to any market, and they serve an argument that prices had risen; it names neither a place nor a grade of cloth, and the transcription carries no date for the essay.

Used for: cotton cloth and rice by the length and the measure in cash, about the 1740s and in the 1790s (Money and prices)
