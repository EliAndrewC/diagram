makishima-2016-village-courier

巻島隆 (Makishima Takashi), 「江戸後期、村名主の通信環境 ―上野国那波郡福島村、渡辺三右衛門を事例に―」 ("A village headman's communications in the late Edo period: the case of Watanabe San'emon of Fukushima village, Kozuke", title translated), 郵政博物館研究紀要 (Bulletin of the Postal Museum) 7, March 2016 (in Japanese; https://www.postalmuseum.jp/publication/research/research_07_03.pdf)

What it is: A postal historian's study of how a village headman in Kozuke sent and received letters, money and goods, from his diary of 1842 to 1869: the village's own courier, couriers hired by the run, the courier houses of the highways and the robberies of their convoys.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the terms on which one village kept its courier and hired others by the run, with the amounts paid, and shows the courier houses' convoys carrying cash without a separate guard; it is one headman's diary in one village of the Kanto, and the robberies it records are those that reached him.

Used for: couriers hired by the run and by the year, and how cash went on the road (Money and prices)
