墙根网, "老北京的旅店与庙寓" ("Old Beijing's inns and temple lodgings", title translated) (https://culture.qianggen.net/2012/0222/22552.html)

What it is: An unsigned article, dated 10 March 2012, on a Chinese website of old-Beijing local culture, describing the city's old lodgings, among them the lowest grade, the "chicken-feather inns", and the temples of Qing Beijing that took lodgers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the cheapest lodging in one small district of a Chinese city and says that everyone who slept there was poor, which is what places a cheap lodging house inside a city's walls on a map. It dates the capital's grand hotels and temple lodgings but gives no date for the chicken-feather inns, which it lists among the lodgings found "since the Republic" (民国以来, after 1912), so what it says of them may describe early-twentieth-century Beijing rather than the imperial city; it concerns the imperial capital rather than a county seat, and it gives no count of the cheap inns.

Used for: the chicken-feather inns clustered in one small district north of Tianqiao, and their lodgers all poor (Urban fabric)
