jawiki-kanei-tsuho

"寛永通宝" ("Kan'ei tsuho"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/寛永通宝)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the Kan'ei tsuho, the shogunate's copper, iron and brass coin of the mon from 1636 to the Meiji period, coinage by coinage, with the copper rate against gold at its turning points and the shogunate's orders on the coins' values.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the copper rate in mon to the ryo in 1694, 1700, 1737 and 1778, the recall of copper coin in 1859 with the changers' fee, and the premiums at which coins of one face passed in 1865 and 1868, each tied to a footnote. Its sentence on prices moving to multiples of four mon after the four-mon coin carries no footnote and is hedged "it is said".

Used for: the copper rate's swings against the legal rate, the 1859 fee, the different values of coins of one face, and the four-mon prices (Money and prices)
