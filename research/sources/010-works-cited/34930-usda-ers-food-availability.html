usda-ers-food-availability

US Department of Agriculture, Economic Research Service, Food Availability (Per Capita) Data System, the files for grains (https://www.ers.usda.gov/media/5352/grains.xlsx?v=20014), pulses (https://www.ers.usda.gov/media/5378/vegetables-pulses.xlsx?v=89557), fish and shellfish (https://www.ers.usda.gov/media/5337/fish-and-shellfish.csv?v=88311) and added fats and oils (https://www.ers.usda.gov/media/5335/fats-and-oils-added.csv?v=45514), XLSX and CSV

What it is: The government's yearly accounts of how much of each food is available to eat in the US per person: what was produced and imported, less what was exported, stored or put to other uses, divided by the population.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: how much of each basket good an American uses in a year, as a yardstick for a premodern laborer's year, never a figure of the setting. Availability is not eating: it counts what reaches the market, waste and spoilage included; and several series end early, rice and added oils in 2010, barley in 2020, fish in 2018, each for the reason its file gives.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
