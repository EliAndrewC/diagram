edo-tatekawa-sarai-kakitome-1808

『日本橋川筋竪川小名木川浚一件書留』 (Notes on the dredging of the Nihonbashi river, the Tatekawa and the Onagigawa), [6], the Tatekawa part, 2, Bunka 5, a manuscript copy among the records the shogunate's Edo town magistrates handed on, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547651/1/169 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547651)

What it is: A volume of the Edo town magistrates' files on dredging the city's canals. The frame read is a page of a day-by-day list of small sums paid out in the eighth month, in the volume's Tatekawa canal part: laborers by the head, empty bales by the bale, tying rope, each with who advanced the money.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets an empty bale's price and a laborer's pay on one page of one works account. The year, 1808, is the library's catalog title for the volume, not a date on the page. It does not say what the bales had held or whether they were new, and its sum for the fifty bales, 572 mon, is fifty times the 11 mon it states only when counted, as its laborers' 1 kan for seven men at 141 mon is, at 96 coins to the hundred (572 = 5 x 96 + 72 = 552 coins); that reading is this record's arithmetic. Other pages of the same list pay laborers 150 and 300 mon a head; those were read only in a machine reading and are not used.

Used for: what an empty bale of unstated kind and a laborer cost at Edo's Tatekawa canal works of 1808 (Money and prices)
