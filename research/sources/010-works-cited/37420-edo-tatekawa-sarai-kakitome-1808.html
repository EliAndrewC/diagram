edo-tatekawa-sarai-kakitome-1808

『日本橋川筋竪川小名木川浚一件書留』 (Notes on the dredging of the Nihonbashi river, the Tatekawa and the Onagigawa), [6], the Tatekawa part, 2, Bunka 5, a manuscript copy among the records the shogunate's Edo town magistrates handed on, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547651/1/169 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2547651)

What it is: A volume of the Edo town magistrates' files on dredging the city's canals. The frame read is a page of a day-by-day list of small sums paid out in the eighth month, in the volume's Tatekawa canal part: laborers by the head, empty bales by the bale, tying rope, each with who advanced the money.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets an empty bale's price and a laborer's pay on one page of one works account. The year, 1808, is the library's catalog title for the volume, not a date on the page. It does not say what the bales had held or whether they were new, and its sums are not its rates times its counts as written: fifty bales at 11 mon is 550 mon where it writes 572, and seven men at 141 mon is 987 mon where it writes 1 kan. Counted at 96 coins to the hundred they come within a coin or two: 572 mon is 552 coins (5 x 96 + 72) against 550, and 1 kan is 960 coins against seven men at 137 coins (141 mon being 96 + 41), 959. That reading is this record's arithmetic, not a statement of the page, and the record takes the rates the page states, 11 mon a bale and 141 mon a head, not its sums. Other pages of the same list pay laborers 150 and 300 mon a head; those were read only in a machine reading and are not used.

Used for: what an empty bale of unstated kind and a laborer cost at Edo's Tatekawa canal works of 1808 (Money and prices)
