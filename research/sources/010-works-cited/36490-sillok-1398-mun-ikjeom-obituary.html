sillok-1398-mun-ikjeom-obituary

Veritable Records of King Taejo (Taejo Sillok), book 14, 13th day of the 6th month of the 7th year of Taejo's reign (1398), obituary of Mun Ik-jeom, in the National Institute of Korean History's online Annals of the Joseon Dynasty (https://sillok.history.go.kr/id/kaa_10706013_001)

What it is: The official annals' obituary of the man credited with bringing cotton seed to Korea, in classical Chinese with a modern Korean translation on the same page.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the fullest early account read of what it presents as the first cotton grown in Korea. It is entered under Mun's death in 1398, thirty-four years after the planting of 1364, and it records a title given to him after that death, so it was written later still. Its "under ten years" for the spread of the spinning and weaving across the country is a retrospective claim with no starting year, not a count. The entry gives no acreage or price, and its one harvest figure is the hundred or so fruits or seeds (實) picked that autumn from the only plant that grew, which is one plant's count and not a yield from a field. Only the English here is this project's; the page's Korean translation was not used.
