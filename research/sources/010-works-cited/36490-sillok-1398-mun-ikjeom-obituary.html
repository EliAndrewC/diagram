sillok-1398-mun-ikjeom-obituary

Veritable Records of King Taejo (Taejo Sillok), book 14, 13th day of the 6th month of the 7th year of Taejo's reign (1398), obituary of Mun Ik-jeom, in the National Institute of Korean History's online Annals of the Joseon Dynasty (https://sillok.history.go.kr/id/kaa_10706013_001)

What it is: The official annals' obituary of the man credited with bringing cotton seed to Korea, in classical Chinese with a modern Korean translation on the same page.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the fullest early account of the first cotton grown in Korea. It was written at Mun's death in 1398, more than thirty years after the planting it describes, as praise of the man, so its "under ten years" for the spread across the country is a retrospective claim, not a count. Only the English here is this project's; the page's Korean translation was not used.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
