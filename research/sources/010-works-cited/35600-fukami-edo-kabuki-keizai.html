fukami-edo-kabuki-keizai

石井正 (Ishii Tadashi), 「江戸歌舞伎の経済学」 ("The economics of Edo kabuki", title translated), 1 April 2018, an essay on the website of the Fukami patent office (in Japanese; https://www.fukamipat.gr.jp/discusses/1908/)

What it is: A patent attorney's short essay on the money of the Edo kabuki theaters, which he says follows Akasaka Chiseki's book on Edo's economic affairs, with a yearly budget of the three Edo theaters taken from a playwright's memoir of the Kansei era (1789-1801).

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only readable account found of a city company's whole year: its takings, what went to actors and to costumes and props, its size, and that stars paid for their own costumes. The budget reaches it at second or third hand from a memoir the record has not read, its figures are round, and the ordinary actor's 25 to 50 ryo is the essayist's own reckoning from them, not a sum anyone recorded.

Used for: an Edo theater company's yearly budget, its size and its actors' pay, 1789-1801 (Money and prices)
