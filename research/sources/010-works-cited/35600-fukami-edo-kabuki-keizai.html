fukami-edo-kabuki-keizai

石井正 (Ishii Tadashi), 「江戸歌舞伎の経済学」 ("The economics of Edo kabuki", title translated), 1 April 2018, an essay on the website of the Fukami patent office (in Japanese; https://www.fukamipat.gr.jp/discusses/1908/)

What it is: A patent attorney's short essay on the money of the Edo kabuki theaters, which names Akasaka Chiseki's book on Edo's economic affairs as describing these matters, with a yearly budget of an Edo theater company whose takings it puts to a playwright's book of the Kansei era (1789-1801), the second Nakamura Jusuke's Shibai noriai-banashi (芝居乗合話); the rest of the budget it gives without a source.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only readable account found of a city company's whole year: its takings, what went to actors and to costumes and props, its size, and that stars paid for their own costumes. The budget reaches it at second or third hand from a book the record has not read, and only its takings are put to that book by name; its figures are round, and the ordinary actor's 25 to 50 ryo is the essayist's own figure, not a sum anyone recorded, and it does not follow from his budget: 6,000 ryo for actors less a 1,000 for the stars that he gives only "for example", spread over a company of about 60, would come to some 85 ryo a head, and his range fits only if several stars each took about 1,000, which he does not say. Nor does the essay say plainly whether its 8,000 ryo of takings is each theater's or the three together; it reads on as one company's, beside a company of about 60 actors.

Used for: an Edo theater company's yearly budget, its size and its actors' pay, 1789-1801 (Money and prices)
