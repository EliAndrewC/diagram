Huang Shujing 黃叔璥, Taihai shicha lu 臺海使槎錄 (A record of the mission to Taiwan and its strait), juan 1, the notes headed "Chikan bitan" 赤嵌筆談, Chinese Wikisource (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/臺海使槎錄/卷1)

What it is: The notes of a Qing official sent to inspect Taiwan, then a prefecture of Fujian, on the island's geography, taxes, shipping and products, among them what its cane sugar sold for and what it cost to ship to Suzhou.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a producer's-end price for sugar with the freight to the city that bought it, from an official on the spot. The passage carries no year: it is dated to about 1723 from the page itself, which calls Zhanghua county newly approved and dates a neighboring item to the fifth month of the first Yongzheng year. Its prices are round ranges for a whole island, it does not say whether they were paid at the mill or at the port, and it gives no price at Suzhou. The Wikisource text was not compared with a printed edition.

Used for: Taiwan's sugar prices and the freight to Suzhou about 1723 (Money and prices)
