baijing-16-zeng-guofan

敗經 (title translated: "The Classic of Failure"), part 16, 亂敗 ("ruin by disorder"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese, mixed character forms; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/敗經/卷十六_亂敗)

What it is: A chapter of a modern thematic anthology of Zeng Guofan's writings (the work's index page names him as its author; its compiler and edition are not given); its text includes his memorial on the people's hardships under dear silver, which this project identifies as his memorial of early 1852.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out, in an official's own words, how the doubling of the silver rate doubled a farmer's tax measured in rice, and what followed in arrears and unrest. The page names neither the memorial nor its date, so its identification as the memorial of early 1852 is this project's; the transcription has machine-conversion errors and stray fragments of a modern translation. Its reckoning per mu is for Suzhou, Songjiang, Changzhou, Zhenjiang and Taicang, which it calls the most heavily taxed land in the empire, and is of the landowner's share after the tenant's half; it says Jiangxi and Huguang were taxed more lightly. Its figures are round numbers given in argument, not counts.

Used for: the farmer's tax burden under dear silver and the responses to it (Money and prices)
