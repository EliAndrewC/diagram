baijing-16-zeng-guofan

敗經 (title translated: "The Classic of Failure"), part 16, 亂敗 ("ruin by disorder"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese, mixed character forms; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/敗經/卷十六_亂敗)

What it is: A modern anthology page of writings on misrule; its text includes a memorial on the people's hardships under dear silver, which this project reads as Zeng Guofan's memorial of early 1852.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets out, in an official's own words, how the doubling of the silver rate doubled a farmer's tax measured in rice, and what followed in arrears and unrest. The page names neither the memorial, nor its author, nor its date, so the attribution is this project's reading; its figures are round numbers given in argument, not counts.

Used for: the farmer's tax burden under dear silver and the responses to it (Money and prices)
