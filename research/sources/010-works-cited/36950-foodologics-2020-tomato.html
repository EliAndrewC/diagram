foodologics-2020-tomato

"Tomato: A foreign eggplant?", Foodologics (blog), 22 August 2020 (https://foodologics.wordpress.com/2020/08/22/eggplants-from-portugal/)

What it is: An unsigned blog post on the tomato's coming to India, which quotes a cookbook of 1801 and colonial botanical works by name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only account of the Indian evidence read here. Every date is the blog's report of a work not read here, and it names those works by title and year but gives no page or edition for its quotations, so they stand only as its own report.

Used for: The tomato in India (Money and prices)
