foodologics-2020-tomato

"Tomato: A foreign eggplant?", Foodologics (blog), 22 August 2020 (https://foodologics.wordpress.com/2020/08/22/eggplants-from-portugal/)

What it is: An unsigned blog post on the tomato's coming to India, which quotes a cookbook of 1801 and colonial botanical works by name.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only account of the Indian evidence read here. Every date is the blog's report of a work not read here, and it names those works by title and year but gives no page or edition for its quotations, so they stand only as its own report. Its 1801 witness is one recipe, which the post itself says does not show typical use, and its conclusion that the tomato was present by the late eighteenth century is hedged with "perhaps". It is not a careful reading of its evidence: it says the tomato did not appear in Indo-Persian cookbooks of that era directly after citing one, and it offers an English cookbook of 1758 as an early Indian recipe. It gives no price, and it says nothing of the tomato in China or Japan.
