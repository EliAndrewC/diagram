handa-museum-otsukawa-1842-prices

Handa City Museum (柴田邦彦, Shibata Kunihiko), 「古文書に見る江戸時代の食材と食生活」 ("Edo-period foods and meals seen in old documents", title translated), the slides of a talk, January 25, 2020 (in Japanese; https://www.city.handa.lg.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/002/711/r1komonnjo.pdf)

What it is: A city museum curator's slides on the food in the old documents of Handa, in the Chita district, each slide setting a photograph of the handwritten page beside the museum's printed transcription; the first document is a list of the prices of goods that the village of Otsukawa submitted in 1842.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the price at which a village dealer bought salt by the ryo and sold it by the sho, in one document of a known village and year. The list answered an order to lower prices, which the museum takes to be part of the Tenpo reforms, so its prices are those a village reported to the authorities; the museum shows only its food prices, with no wage and no rate between gold and copper among them.

Used for: the price of salt in a village of the Chita district in 1842 (Money and prices)
