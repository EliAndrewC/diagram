alic-2004-tomato

Agriculture and Livestock Industries Corporation (ALIC, 農畜産業振興機構), "今月の野菜 トマト" (This month's vegetable: the tomato), its vegetable information series, July 2004 (in Japanese; https://vegetable.alic.go.jp/yasaijoho/yasai/0407_yasai1.html)

What it is: A short profile of the tomato from the Japanese agency that handles vegetable market information, with a paragraph on its origin and arrival in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read that puts arrival latest, at the start of the 18th century, which the dated sketch of 1668 contradicts. It gives no source. Its month and year are read from the page's address.

Used for: The tomato in Japan (Money and prices)
