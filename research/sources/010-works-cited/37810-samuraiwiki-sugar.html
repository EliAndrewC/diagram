samuraiwiki-sugar

"Sugar", an article of SamuraiWiki, the wiki of the Samurai Archives website, last edited 27 February 2020 and marked by the wiki as a stub, in the Internet Archive's copy of 5 November 2024 (https://web.archive.org/web/20241105163011/https://samurai-archives.com/wiki/Sugar)

What it is: A volunteer-written encyclopedia article on sugar in Japan and the Ryukyu kingdom. Of the reform of the Satsuma official Zusho in the 1820s and 1830s it says that the domain bought the Amami islands' sugar at roughly a third of what it would be worth at the Osaka markets, citing pages 127 to 128 of Robert Hellyer's Defining Engagement (2009).

Why it applies, and its limits: It states in one phrase the ratio the question asks for. It gives no figure at either end, no year within the two decades and no unit. Hellyer's book, a print work, was not read, and the footnote to his pages stands at the end of the paragraph, after the next sentence on smuggling, so whether the phrase is his cannot be said. The same paragraph says the islanders were forbidden money and bought their food and necessities through an official channel at inflated prices, so the third is the nominal rate of a forced exchange in goods, and the islanders received less than it implies. It differs from the multiples the Japanese studies give for 1830.

Used for: a second-hand statement that Satsuma bought the islands' sugar at about a third of its Osaka worth (Money and prices)
