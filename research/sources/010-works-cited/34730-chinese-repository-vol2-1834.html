"Journal of Messrs. Lindsay and Gutzlaff", a review of the voyage of the Lord Amherst in 1832, The Chinese Repository, vol. 2 (Canton, May 1833-April 1834), April 1834, p. 534, archive.org copy (https://archive.org/details/chinesereposito01willgoog)

What it is: The second volume of the missionaries' Canton monthly; the passage used is its quotation of the East India Company agent Hugh Hamilton Lindsay's report of trading at Nan'ao, in eastern Guangdong, in March 1832.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one rate read for cash to the dollar in Guangdong outside Canton before 1837. It is a trader's rate in barter, the equivalence stated in passing, at one island port, by a foreign party that sold nothing there.

Used for: cash to the dollar at Nan'ao, 1832 (Money and prices)
