muraoka-edo-izakaya

Muraoka Shoji (pen name), "江戸外食文化の定着-3 居酒屋・小料理屋" ("Eating out in Edo takes hold, part 3: taverns and small restaurants", title translated), in the Edo dining-culture section of the site "日本食文化の醤油を知る" ("learning about soy sauce in Japanese food culture", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.eonet.ne.jp/~shoyu/mametisiki/reference-16d.html)

What it is: A long personal-site article, by the author of muraoka-edo-nagaya-life, on the taverns of late Edo: how they grew out of sake shops and cooked-food stalls, their snacks, vessels and prices, with long quotations from published histories.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the price of a go of good and of cheap sake at an Edo tavern in the Bunsei era, and quotes a study of drinking for a cup's price at a sake shop of the 1750s. It names no source for the Bunsei prices beyond "a record", and its 1750s figure, a cup of sake with grilled tofu for 2 mon at the Toshimaya, is quoted from a work by Ito Maya that could not be found in print. The page's own text elsewhere gives 2 mon as the price of the tofu skewer alone, so it supports only "a few mon" for a cup with a skewer, not 2 mon for the cup.

Used for: the price of a go of sake at an Edo tavern in the Bunsei era, and of a cup at a sake shop in the 1750s (Money and prices)
