mingshi-78-fuyi

明史 (History of the Ming, title translated), volume 78, 食貨二, 賦役 ("Food and goods, part 2: taxes and labor service"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/明史/卷78)

What it is: The treatise on the Ming land tax and labor service in the official dynastic history finished in 1739, from the first registers to the surcharges of the 1620s and 1630s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the 1436 commutation into silver of some four million shi of the grain tax of seven southern provinces, later extended empire-wide, and the Single Whip of 1581, which set the tax in silver. It states the rules, not how far each county carried them out, and was compiled a century after the dynasty fell.

Used for: the Ming land tax set in silver (Money and prices)
