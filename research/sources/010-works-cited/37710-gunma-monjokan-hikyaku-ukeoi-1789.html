Gunma Prefecture, "寛政元年六月 京都・江戸の定飛脚問屋ら糸絹荷物ほか請負証文" ("Sixth month of 1789: the undertaking of the fixed courier houses of Kyoto and Edo for thread, silk and other goods", title translated), a document of the Sakamoto house of Tomioka in transcription, with a page of notes and commentary (in Japanese; https://www.pref.gunma.jp/uploaded/attachment/144001.pdf ; the commentary: https://www.pref.gunma.jp/uploaded/attachment/144003.pdf)

What it is: A contract of 1789 in which courier houses of Kyoto and Edo, with their agent at Fujioka in Kozuke province (Joshu), undertake to carry a Tomioka silk merchant's thread, silk goods and money to Kyoto, Edo, Osaka and elsewhere, and to make good what is lost. The prefecture's commentary says who the parties were.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows by whom Joshu's thread and silk went up to Kyoto: by contract courier, under an undertaking to answer for loss. It names no rate, no road and no number of days, and it is one merchant's contract, for silk cloth as much as for thread.

Used for: how Joshu's thread and silk were carried to Kyoto (Money and prices)
