kepuchina-tang-changan

张伟 (associate professor, Southwest University), for 科普中国 (Kepu China, the China Association for Science and Technology's popular-science site), 唐长安城 ("Tang Chang'an", title translated), 2019 (https://www.kepuchina.cn/wiki/ct/201903/t20190323_1029448.shtml)

What it is: A popular-science encyclopedia entry on Tang Chang'an that summarizes the archaeological survey of the city: its outer wall, its avenues measured one by one, its wards (110 by its usual count, though it also reports 108 and 109) and its two markets.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the wall-side streets on all four sides inside the outer wall, 20 to 25 m wide, which is both the one measured patrol road read and the evidence that Chang'an's wards stood back from the rampart. Its note numbers point to a reference list the page does not print, and it contradicts itself on some counts (108, 109 or 110 wards; 8 or 11 north-south avenues; a perimeter of 35.5 or 36.7 km). The 20-25 m wall-side street figure is the same in all three places it appears. The measurement goes back to the excavators' own report (唐代长安城考古纪略, 《考古》 1963), which would be the closer source if it can be read. It describes a capital planned on a grid in the 580s, not of a provincial seat, and it does not describe what stood on the wall-side street.

Used for: the wall-side street on all four sides of Chang'an, 20-25 m, and the roads dividing the city into wards (City defenses)
