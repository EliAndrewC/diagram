National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, NOAA Fisheries, "Fisheries of the United States", web page (https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/sustainable-fisheries/fisheries-united-states)

What it is: The fisheries agency's summary page for its yearly report on US fishing: landings, their value, trade, and how much seafood Americans eat.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a later and larger figure for the seafood an American eats in a year than the food accounts' last count. The page does not say whether its pounds are edible meat or the fish's whole weight, so the two figures cannot be set side by side with certainty.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
