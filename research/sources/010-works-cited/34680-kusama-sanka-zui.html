kusama-sanka-zui

草間直方 (Kusama Naokata), 『三貨図彙』 ("Illustrated compendium of the three moneys", completed 1815), 物価之部 ("prices") books 5-6, in the printing of 白東社, Tokyo, 1932, pp. 802-823; National Diet Library Digital Collections, internet-public (https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1279564)

What it is: A history of Japan's money, prices and finance from antiquity to the author's day, compiled over twenty years by an Osaka money-changer; its price books give, year by year, the price of a koku of Higo and Chikuzen rice and the market rates of a ryo of gold and a kanmon of copper coin in monme of silver, each naming the silver it is quoted in, with the year's events and decrees.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one year-by-year series read for 1705-1719 that names which silver each rate is quoted in. Its entries for those years were compiled about a century later from records these entries do not name, and it does not say in which city the rates were made; it gives no gold or copper rate for 1715 or 1716, a few entries print figures that run against the coins' finenesses (1717) or against the decree printed beside them (1714, 1718) - and since it was read only in the 1932 typeset printing, not the manuscript, such a figure may be the printer's error rather than Kusama's - and its figures are ranges and round numbers, not daily quotations.

Used for: the market rates of gold and copper in silver, 1705-1719, and the decrees of 1714 and 1718 (Money and prices)
