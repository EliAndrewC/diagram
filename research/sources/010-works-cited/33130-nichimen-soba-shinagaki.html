nichimen-soba-shinagaki

Japan Noodle Industry Association (日本麺類業団体連合会), "江戸時代のそばの品書き" ("An Edo-period soba shop's menu", title translated), no. 30 of its noodle miscellany (in Japanese; https://nichimen.or.jp/know/zatsugaku/30/)

What it is: A short page of the noodle trade's association transcribing the soba shop's menu that the Morisada manko, a record of the customs of its time from 1830 to its completion in 1853, draws and prices, with notes on the dishes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a go of good sake at a soba shop beside the shop's soba, from one menu of one record. The association transcribes the menu without showing the page it is drawn on, and the record dates it only to the years it covers.

Used for: the price of a go of sake at an Edo soba shop, 1830-1853 (Money and prices)
