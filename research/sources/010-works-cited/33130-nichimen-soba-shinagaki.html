nichimen-soba-shinagaki

Japan Noodle Industry Association (日本麺類業団体連合会), "江戸時代のそばの品書き" ("An Edo-period soba shop's menu", title translated), no. 30 of its noodle miscellany (in Japanese; https://nichimen.or.jp/know/zatsugaku/30/)

What it is: A short page of the noodle trade's association transcribing the soba shop's menu that the Morisada manko, Kitagawa Morisada's record of the customs of Edo, Kyoto and Osaka (begun in 1837, completed in 1853 and added to until about 1867), draws and prices for Edo, with notes on the dishes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a go of good sake at a soba shop beside the shop's soba, from one menu of one record. The association transcribes the menu without showing the page it is drawn on, and the record dates it only to the years it covers: it was completed in 1853 but added to until about 1867, so the price may be as late as the 1860s. It is one Edo menu, a big-city price.

Used for: the price of a go of sake at an Edo soba shop, 1837-1853 (perhaps as late as 1867) (Money and prices)
