edo-shussui-ikken-54-1802

『出水一件』 (Records of the floods), volume 54, a manuscript among the records the shogunate's Edo town magistrates handed on, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548494/1/72 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548494/1/74 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548494/1/82 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548494)

What it is: A volume of the Edo town magistrates' files on floods. The part read is the account a group of ward headmen submitted for stopping the water that overflowed the drainage canals of Honjo, Nakanogo and Yokokawa in the seventh month of 1802, place by place: the empty bales made into earth bags, the tying rope, the cedar leaves and the laborers, each with its count, its sum in copper coin and its rate, and the receipt for the money, dated the fourth day of the ninth month of Kyowa 2.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices empty bales and a laborer's day side by side in one dated account of one place. It does not say what the bales had held, whether they were new or used, or from whom they were bought, and it is an emergency purchase by a ward claiming its costs back from the magistrates. Its sums do not equal the stated rate times the count: each is larger by about a twenty-fourth, which fits a "hundred" reckoned as a string of 96 coins; that reading is this record's arithmetic, not a statement of the account. Only the three frames named were read.

Used for: what empty bales of unstated kind and a laborer's day cost in Edo's flood works of 1802 (Money and prices)
