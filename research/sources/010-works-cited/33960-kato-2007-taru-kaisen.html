kato-2007-taru-kaisen

Kato Keiichiro (加藤慶一郎), "樽廻船の加入形態に関する一考察" ("On the forms of investment in the taru cargo ships", title translated), 流通科学大学論集 (Journal of the University of Marketing and Distribution Sciences) 20-1 (2007) (in Japanese; https://ryuka.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/181/files/n20-1kato.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's study of how investors bought shares in the taru ships that carried sake from the Osaka-Kobe coast to Edo, from the shares' contracts of 1798 to 1865.

Why it applies, and its limits: In checking a contract of 1855 it gives the standard freight on sake to Edo, by the horse-load. That standard is taken from another historian's book, not from the contracts, has no year of its own, and rose toward the end of the Edo period, which the note says without giving the later figures.

Used for: the standard freight on sake shipped to Edo (Money and prices)
