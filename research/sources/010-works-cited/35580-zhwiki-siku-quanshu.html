zhwiki-siku-quanshu

Chinese Wikipedia, 「四庫全書」 ("The complete library of the four treasuries", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/四庫全書)

What it is: The encyclopedia's article on the Qianlong emperor's manuscript library, with a section on how its copyists were recruited, held to a daily quota and rewarded, footnoted to a history of the compilation.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the copyists' quota of characters a day and their reward in office rather than pay. They were literate candidates recommended or chosen by test for an imperial project, not copyists hired for pay, so the quota is not a commercial copyist's output, and its book source was not read.

Used for: the Siku copyists' daily quota and reward (Money and prices)
