kujiyado-kotobank

Kotobank, 公事宿 ("kujiyado, litigants' inns", title translated) - Nipponica (entry by 青木美智男, Aoki Michio), the Britannica International and the World Encyclopedia's entry on the suit-broker (公事師) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%85%AC%E4%BA%8B%E5%AE%BF-162055)

What it is: The Kotobank page carrying published reference entries on the Edo-period inns that lodged people come to town for a lawsuit, and on the illegal suit-brokers: both coached litigants and wrote their documents.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows legal documents written for a client case by case, by an inn or a broker, for a fee; it gives no amount for the fee, and the trade it describes is the shogunate's courts' in Edo chiefly.

Used for: legal documents written by the case in Japan (Money and prices)
