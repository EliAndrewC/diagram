tabashio-japan-tobacco

Tobacco & Salt Museum, "Japanese Tobacco", Tobacco History and Culture (https://tabashio.jp/en/collection/tobacco/japan/index.html)

What it is: A page of the Tobacco and Salt Museum, summing up tobacco's place in Japanese society.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says which social levels had taken up tobacco by the end of the bans. It gives no year for the end of the bans more exact than the seventeenth century. "All social levels" is one summary sentence with no count or region, and it does not separate town from village, so it cannot show how far tobacco had reached in the villages. The museum was founded by the Japan Monopoly Corporation, now Japan Tobacco, so the claim that the product was popular comes from the company that sells it.

Used for: Tobacco in Japan (Money and prices)
