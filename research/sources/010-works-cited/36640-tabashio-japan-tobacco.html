tabashio-japan-tobacco

Tobacco & Salt Museum (Tokyo), "Japanese Tobacco", Tobacco History and Culture (https://tabashio.jp/en/collection/tobacco/japan/index.html)

What it is: A page of the history museum run by Japan's tobacco company, summing up tobacco's place in Japanese society.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says which social levels had taken up tobacco by the end of the bans. It gives no year more exact than the seventeenth century, names no source, and speaks for an institution with a stake in the subject.

Used for: Tobacco in Japan (Money and prices)
