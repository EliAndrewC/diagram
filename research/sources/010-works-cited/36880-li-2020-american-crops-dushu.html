Li Xinsheng (李昕升), 《美洲作物的中国故事》 (The story of American crops in China), 《读书》 2020, no. 1, reposted by the magazine on The Paper, 8 January 2020 (in Chinese; https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_5453847)

What it is: An essay for general readers by a historian of Chinese agriculture, surveying the American crops in China and arguing that they spread slowly because of fixed habits of eating and farming.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tomato two sentences, a reign and no figure, and rests on the author's own wider work without notes. It also says in passing that American crops "soon" spread over China, in tension with its own argument for slowness.

Used for: The tomato in China (Money and prices)
