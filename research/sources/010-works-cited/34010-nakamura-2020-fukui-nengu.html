Nakamura Ken (中村賢), "福井藩における年貢収受 －関連資料紹介と諸疑問の整理－" ("Receiving the land tax in the Fukui domain: related documents introduced and open questions sorted", title translated), 福井県文書館研究紀要 (Bulletin of the Fukui Prefectural Archives) 17 (March 2020) (in Japanese; https://www.library-archives.pref.fukui.lg.jp/bunsho/file/615603.pdf)

What it is: An archivist's paper introducing the Fukui domain's tax documents shown in an archives exhibition of 2019, with the questions they leave open: who carried the tax rice, how, to where, and whether it was ever paid in coin.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its note 22 quotes the Edo handbook of village administration, the Jikata hanreiroku, on who paid to carry tax rice to the shipping landing, with the rate the lord paid beyond five ri. It quotes the handbook from Oishi Shinzaburo's edition of 1969; the handbook itself was not read for this record. Its own statement that the Fukui domain followed the rule is one sentence, and it says how the domain's farmers actually carried their tax is still unknown.

Used for: the five-ri rule for carrying tax rice, and the lord's rate beyond it (Money and prices)
