yuanshi-15

Song Lian (宋濂) and others, Yuan shi (元史, History of the Yuan dynasty), chapter 15, the annals of Shizu, on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/元史/卷015)

What it is: The annals of Qubilai's reign for 1288 and 1289 in the official Yuan history, compiled early in the Ming.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the order that set up the cotton offices and the yearly levy in the court's own chronicle. It records the order and not whether the levy was met, and it says nothing of who wore the cloth.

Used for: the cotton levy of 1289 (Money and prices)
