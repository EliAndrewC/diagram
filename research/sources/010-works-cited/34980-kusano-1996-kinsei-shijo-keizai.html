kusano-1996-kinsei-shijo-keizai

Kusano Masahiro, Kinsei no shijō keizai to chiikisa: bukkashi kara no sekkin (The early-modern market economy and regional differences: an approach from price history), doctoral thesis in economics, Kobe University, 1996, published in the university's repository 2011 (https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/D2002030/D2002030.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's thesis that builds consumer price indexes and real wages for Kyoto in the Edo period from the Mitsui house's price tables, in the older edition and in the enlarged revised one, and says, good by good, what units the tables use and how he adjusted them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one reading found of the printed Mitsui Kyoto table, which the spreadsheet transcription this record uses does not annotate: it says the table prices salt and hard charcoal by the bale from 1791, not by the koku and by weight as before. Its bale of 32 sho (3 to and 2 sho, 0.32 koku) is the size Kusano used to convert the older edition's salt, following Umemura Mataji's 1961 study of builders' real wages (his note 6), not a size he says the table states, and for his own index he dropped the earlier figures rather than convert across the change.

Used for: the unit of Kyoto's salt and hard charcoal in the Mitsui books from 1791 (Money and prices)
