pomeranz-east-asia-europe-1998

Kenneth Pomeranz, "East Asia, Europe, and the Industrial Revolution", a conference paper posted by the Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis, undated on its face, its address filing it under 1998 (https://www.minneapolisfed.org/economic-research/conferences/~/media/files/research/events/1998_10-09/Pomeranz_IndustrialRevolution.pdf)

What it is: A historian's draft estimating consumption and earnings in late imperial China to set against what is known for Europe, with a table of cloth output and consumption per head about 1750.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives cotton cloth per head about 1750 for all China and for the Yangzi Delta; its delta leaves out the salt-producing prefectures (about 31 million people remain) and lies in the Lower Yangzi, one of what it calls China's two most advanced regions, and both figures are averages over millions of people, with no household or village behind them. Its first page says "Work in progress: comments welcomed, but please do not cite without permission", so it is cited here with that said. Its table sends the reader to an Appendix F for its sources and data problems, and the posted file ends at its bibliography with no appendix in it, so the sources of these figures were not read (the text says only, of its estimates in general, that they were usually worked back from land under crops and yields, less exports, and that it took steps to keep them conservative); the delta's figure is output, not what the delta consumed, the all-China range (marked "probably nearer low end") is called neither output nor consumption in the table, and the table does not say that its pounds are per year. Pomeranz later wrote that his Jiangnan cloth production estimates were probably worth adjusting "down a bit", and that the adjusted ones were still more than double those Philip Huang takes from Xu Xinwu (his reply in the Journal of Asian Studies, 2003, p. 170); the delta's figure is therefore disputed, and whether the adjustment touches this draft's figure or the all-China range was not checked.

Used for: cotton cloth per head about 1750 (Money and prices)
