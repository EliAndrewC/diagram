ehime-kenshi-ise-shinko

Ehime Prefecture, Ehime kenshi: gakumon, shukyo (1985), part 2, chapter 1, section 3, part 4, 「伊勢信仰の展開」 ("The spread of Ise belief", title translated), in the database Ehime no kioku (in Japanese; https://www.i-manabi.jp/system/regionals/regionals/ecode:2/54/view/7280)

What it is: The prefectural history's account of Ise belief in Iyo in the Edo period: pilgrimage, the Ise confraternities, and the oshi, the Ise shrine agents who each year carried talismans and calendars to the households on their rolls and took their offerings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one oshi's takings and costs for his round of 2,162 households in Ozu domain in 1813, from an account printed in the city history of Uji-Yamada. It gives a total for the whole round, paid in rice or coin in proportion to each household, not what any household gave or what a talisman cost.

Used for: what an Ise oshi took for his talismans in 1813 (Money and prices)
