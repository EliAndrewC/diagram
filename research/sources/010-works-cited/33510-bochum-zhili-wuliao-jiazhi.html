Ruhr-Universitat Bochum, Section for the History of China, "Database of prices, wages, and transport costs in public construction of Zhili province in the Qianlong reign", preface and chapter spreadsheets (in English and Chinese; https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/gc/forschung/zhili_province.html; the chapters, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_1.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_13.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_15.xls)

What it is: A database, entered row by row by a German and Chinese project, of the Zhili edition of the Qing Regulations and precedents on prices of materials, whose introductory memorial is dated 1769: for each of 139 districts, the prices of building materials and the day wages of craftsmen and laborers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a craftsman's and a laborer's day wage for each district of the capital's province in one regulation, often several grades of craftsman beside one or two of laborer. Its preface says these are the rates the province could claim back from the Ministry of Works for public building, which its edicts say reflect market prices at middling activity, some extrapolated from earlier prices; it lists no smith or roofer, and no rows for the two counties of Beijing itself.

Used for: the official day wages of craftsmen and laborers in Zhili districts in 1769 (Money and prices)
