Ruhr-Universitat Bochum, Section for the History of China, "Database of prices, wages, and transport costs in public construction of Zhili province in the Qianlong reign", preface and chapter spreadsheets (in English and Chinese; https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/gc/forschung/zhili_province.html; the chapters, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_1.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_3.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_13.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_15.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_18.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_22.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/zhili/wuliao_jiazhi_zeli_zhili_24.xls)

What it is: A database, entered row by row by a German and Chinese project, of the Zhili edition of the Qing Regulations and precedents on prices of materials, whose introductory memorial is dated 1769: for each of 139 districts, the prices of building materials, the day wages of craftsmen and laborers, and the rates for hauling materials by cart, porter, pack animal and boat.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a craftsman's and a laborer's day wage, and a cart's rate by the 100 li, for each district of the capital's province in one regulation, often several grades of craftsman beside one or two of laborer. Its preface says these are the rates the province could claim back from the Ministry of Works for public building, not fares or wages anyone was seen to pay, which its edicts say reflect market prices at middling activity, some extrapolated from earlier prices; the four chapters read (1, 13, 15 and 22 of 24) list no smith or roofer, and there are no rows for the two counties of Beijing itself. In chapter 13 the database labels the same haulage figures per 100 li at Tianjin and per li in the districts around it, a conflict its spreadsheets cannot settle.

Used for: the official day wages of craftsmen and laborers in Zhili districts in 1769, the official cart rates beside a mule hire, and the official price of charcoal by district (Money and prices)
