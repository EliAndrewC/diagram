yahoo-cbot-rough-rice-daily

Yahoo Finance, daily chart of CBOT rough rice futures (symbol ZR=F), ten years to 2026-10-23, JSON (https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/ZR=F?range=10y&interval=1d)

What it is: A financial site's daily record of the closing price of the Chicago Board of Trade's rough rice futures (the continuous symbol ZR=F, named for its current contract month), with 2,514 daily closes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: a present-day staple traded day by day, read beside the Osaka rice exchange's daily quotes, never a figure of the setting. It is a futures price, not a shop price; it does not say how the symbol passed from one contract month to the next, where the series may step; and the site limits how often a reader may fetch it. The exchange caps how far the price may move in one day (about $1.20 a hundredweight, widened to $1.80 after a close at the limit, in its 2022 notice; reset twice a year), so the largest daily moves stop at the cap and a larger move is spread over several days; and rough rice is thinly traded, so its daily moves can be jumpier than a busier market's.

Used for: how far a staple's price moves from day to day today (Comparative money: other societies)
