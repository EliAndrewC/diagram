nippon-com-2025-tsutaju-kashihon

nippon.com, 「蔦重を取り巻く出版業者 : 書物問屋と地本問屋」 ("The publishers around Tsutaju: the scholarly and the popular booksellers", title translated), in the series 「江戸のメディア王・蔦重が生きた時代」 ("The times of Tsutaju, Edo's media king", title translated), 23 February 2025 (in Japanese; https://www.nippon.com/ja/japan-topics/c14903/)

What it is: An article in a series on the Edo publisher Tsutaya Juzaburo (1750-1797), on the two kinds of Edo bookseller and the lending libraries, with a list of reference books.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the lending fee of Tsutaju's lifetime as 6 to 30 mon a volume, beside Nagatomo Chiyoji's book on the book trade, which was not read; the page does not tie the figure to a page of it, and the range spans a half-century without a year.

Used for: the lending libraries' fee a volume in the later 1700s (Money and prices)
