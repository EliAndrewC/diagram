en.wikipedia "Former Yamauchi Residence" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Former_Yamauchi_Residence)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the rowhouse of the Yamauchi lords' lower residence at Kochi, dated in its text to 1864 and an Important Cultural Property; it gives the building's length and depth, its two stories and its roof.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured size, 33.4 by 5.7 m, of a surviving rowhouse at a lord's residence. The article is two sentences long: it does not say who lived in the row or how it was divided, the row is of two stories where the record's other rows are of one, and it dates from the last years of the Edo period, though not to a settled year: the article's text gives 1864 and its own categories 1866. Its figures could not be checked against the Agency for Cultural Affairs listing it cites.

Used for: the size, stories and date of the Yamauchi residence's rowhouse (Estates and other compounds)
