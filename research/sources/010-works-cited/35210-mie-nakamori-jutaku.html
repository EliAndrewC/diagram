mie-nakamori-jutaku

Mie Prefecture, the registration summary for the 中森家住宅 (Nakamori house), a registered tangible cultural property at Iga-Ueno, a one-page PDF (in Japanese; https://www.pref.mie.lg.jp/common/content/000370740.pdf)

What it is: Mie Prefecture's one-page summary of the Nakamori house in the castle town of Iga-Ueno, the house of a retainer of a senior Todo vassal: each registered building, its date and its building area, among them a well house and board fence of the late Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the building area of a dated Edo-period well house. It is a samurai household's own well, not a commoner's shared one. Its line for the well house and board fence gives 12 sq m and a length of 7 m without saying which belongs to which; that the area is the well house's and the length the fence's is this project's reading, from the gate's line beside it, which gives the gate's frontage and the wall's length.

Used for: the size of a late-Edo well house (Trades and services)
