kotobank-koka-shunju

Kotobank, 耕稼春秋 ("Kōka shunjū", title translated) (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%80%95%E7%A8%BC%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B-1173590)

What it is: Kotobank's page gathering the Japanese dictionaries' and encyclopedias' entries on the Kōka shunjū, an Edo-period farming book: the Digital Daijisen dictionary, the Nihon Dai-hyakka Zensho, Heibonsha's World Encyclopedia, the Yamakawa dictionary of Japanese history and the Britannica.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is cited only to say what the book is - its title, its author, its region and its date - where a paper quoted in the record names the book without explaining it; the entries agree on the facts it is cited for, and the record takes nothing about farming practice from it.

Used for: what the Kōka shunjū is, the Kaga farming book a study of night soil cites for Kanazawa's contracts (Towns)
