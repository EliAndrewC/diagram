arai-2000-boundary-iron

新井宏 (Arai Hiroshi), 「21. 中世の鉄価と製鉄の画期」 ("Medieval iron prices and the turning points of iron-making", title translated), BOUNDARY, September 2000 (in Japanese; http://www.arai-hist.jp/magazine/baundary/b21.pdf)

What it is: A metallurgist's magazine article, not peer-reviewed, in his series on the history of metal prices, which reprints Oka Mitsuo's table of the price of a kan of iron in rice from 1599 to 1844 and re-reads its sources for the rows up to 1719, correcting the two before 1620.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one readable series of Edo-period iron prices found, but in rice, not silver, and of iron of unstated grade; its rows from 1812 on are four single-year prices, two from Osaka and two from Edo, all from one later government compilation of shogunal records, and are reprinted, not re-examined. In both earlier rows Arai did check, Oka had misread his source, taking a steel price for iron and a nominal rice price for a market one. A price stated in rice also moves with the harvest, not just with the price of iron.

Used for: iron's price in rice in Osaka and Edo, 1812-1844 (Money and prices)
