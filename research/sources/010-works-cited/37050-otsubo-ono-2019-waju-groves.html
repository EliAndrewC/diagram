otsubo-ono-2019-waju-groves

Otsubo Eiji and Ono Akihiko, "濃尾平野輪中地域における集落形態からみた屋敷林の空間構成" ("Study on the spatial composition of enclosing forests with houses in relationship with settlement formation in polders, Noubi Plain"), in 環境情報科学 学術研究論文集 (Papers on Environmental Information Science) 33, 2019, pp. 13-18 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ceispapers/ceis33/0/ceis33_13/_pdf/-char/ja)

What it is: A field study of the homestead groves of 11 ring-dike polders (waju) on the Nobi plain: 98 groves, traced on aerial photographs from 1949 or 1961 to 2009 and surveyed in 2018, sorted into four plan forms and set against the form of the settlement each stands in.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one study the record found of where the groves of polder villages stand, in Japan's best-known polder country. Its evidence is the groves as photographed from 1949 on, inherited but already thinned and altered, not a premodern record; the winter wind from the northwest is given as the region's received explanation, not measured. It counts which side of the homestead plot a grove stands on, but never which side of a row village faces the ring dike, and it says nothing of the depth of a grove or of houses set back from a dike. A belt around a whole village is the authors' reconstruction, not something they saw whole: in clustered villages it rests on neighboring groves found beside only 9 of 27 corner-type groves on the 1961 photographs, and in row villages along the ring dike on 4 groves seen joined into one belt on the 1949 photographs.

Used for: where a polder village's shelter belt stands (Field archetypes).
