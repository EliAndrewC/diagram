Goto Takasi, "Map symbols of the Meiji 40s" (in Japanese; https://goto-seikotsuin.sakura.ne.jp/Map_symbols_of_the_Meiji40s.html)

What it is: A short page on a personal site, listing by category the symbols needed to read the old maps the Land Survey Department (the army's mapping office) issued around 1910, among them a bamboo-grove symbol.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows the bamboo-grove symbol in use on the national survey's maps of about 1910, well after 1868. It does not say when the symbol was introduced, and it says nothing of maps before modern times.

Used for: the bamboo-grove map symbol on maps of about 1910 (Vegetation and terrain)
