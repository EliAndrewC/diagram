The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "9th Day (16 Oct): Choyo no sekku (Chrysanthemum Festival)" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#9th-day-16-oct-choyo-no-sekku-chrysanthemum-festival) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, on the Chrysanthemum Festival of the ninth day of the ninth month, the last of the five seasonal holidays, when gardens, temples, shrines and fine homes display the season's flowers, among them chrysanthemums grown in the shape of dolls.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: the festival and its display are the setting's to decide, and these maps follow it. The notes describe the festival's display, not where the flowers were grown or on how much land.

Used for: the setting's reason a town keeps a chrysanthemum field (Fields)
