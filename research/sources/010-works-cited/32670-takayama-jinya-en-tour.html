takayama-jinya-en-tour

Takayama Jin'ya (Gifu Prefecture), "Tour Inside the Building", the official site's English page (https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/architecture/)

What it is: The Takayama Jin'ya's own English tour page, with a bird's-eye illustration of the compound keyed to seventeen numbered places, from the front gate through the office rooms and the official's living rooms to the court and the rice storehouse, each with a line or two of description.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its illustration shows how the office and the residence stand against each other on the ground. Its limits: it draws the compound as restored, its residence rebuilt in 1996 from a drawing of 1830 that the page does not name or reproduce, and the page does not say which wings are joined or by what.

Used for: the office and the residence drawn under joined roofs at Takayama (Estates and other compounds)
