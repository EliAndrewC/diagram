takayama-jinya-en-tour

Takayama Jin'ya (Gifu Prefecture), "Tour Inside the Building", the official site's English page (https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/architecture/)

What it is: The Takayama Jin'ya's own English tour page, with a bird's-eye illustration of the compound keyed to numbered places, from the front gate through the office rooms and the official's living rooms to the court and the rice storehouse, each with a line or two of description.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its illustration shows how the office and the residence stand against each other on the ground. Its limits: it draws the compound as restored, and the page does not say which parts stand from the Edo period and which were restored (the site's About page says only that the Edo-period appearance was recreated by 1996), nor which wings are joined or by what.

Used for: the office and the residence drawn under joined roofs at Takayama (Estates and other compounds)
