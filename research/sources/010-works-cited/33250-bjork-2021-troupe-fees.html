ビュールク・トーヴェ (Tove Björk), 「江戸中期の歌舞伎役者と人形浄瑠璃演者の出演料考（1）」 ("The performance fees of kabuki actors and puppet-theater players in the mid-Edo period, part 1", title translated), in 『書くこと／書かれたもの：表現行為と表現』 (Writing and the Written), Saitama University Faculty of Liberal Arts, Liberal Arts series 12, 2021, pp. 37-47 (in Japanese; https://sucra.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/19334/files/P0000319.pdf)

What it is: A theater historian's paper on what kabuki and puppet-theater troupes earned away from the city theaters in 1690-1737, from the diaries of the Hirosaki domain's Edo mansion and of Funai domain, where touring troupes played at the town of Hamanoichi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives what a troupe's run took in a provincial town, the size of the troupes, and one run's ticket money. Its "fees" are the shows' recorded takings, and its "bought-in plays" are a provincial town's, not a village's; which the author treats as the troupe's fee only as a hypothesis; its per-performance figures assume a run of nine and a half days of one show a day; it covers one domain's port town, and gives no price for a seat.

Used for: bought-in troupes, their takings at Funai and their size (Money and prices)
