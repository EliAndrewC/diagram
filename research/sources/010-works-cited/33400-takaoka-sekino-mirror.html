takaoka-sekino-mirror

山元醸造, 「高岡銅像物語 夜明け前」 ("The story of Takaoka's bronze statues: before the dawn", title translated), a local-history page, 2006, on the site of Yamagen Jozo, a Takaoka miso and soy-sauce maker (in Japanese; https://www.yamagen-jouzou.com/murocho/aji/douzou/douzou1.html)

What it is: A local-history page on the three great mirrors cast in 1833 for the Sekino shrine of Takaoka by a maker of Buddhist altar fittings, and on how the man who vowed them raised the money, from a letter, kept in the Okamoto family papers, that he wrote to a leading Takaoka merchant.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and weight of a shrine's mirrors of known date, and the metals of their alloy (copper, tin and lead, in proportions it does not give), which with a copper price gives a rough floor under a mirror's cost, and it shows that the mirrors were paid for by gifts. It gives no price; it paraphrases the letter rather than quoting it in full; and these are a town shrine's three large mirrors, not a village shrine's one.

Used for: a shrine mirror's size, weight, alloy and date (Money and prices)
