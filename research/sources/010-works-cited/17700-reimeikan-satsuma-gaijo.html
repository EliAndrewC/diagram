reimeikan-satsuma-gaijo

Kagoshima Prefectural Center for History and Art, Reimeikan (鹿児島県歴史・美術センター黎明館), permanent exhibition, "藩政のしくみ" ("How the domain was governed", title translated), on the Kagoshima Prefecture website (in Japanese; http://www.pref.kagoshima.jp/reimeikan/josetsu/theme/kinsei/hansei/index.html)

What it is: A short, unsigned and undated exhibition page of the prefecture's history museum on Satsuma domain's two distinctive institutions: the outer-district system that settled warriors across the domain, and the land system for its farming villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: A public museum states in two sentences that Satsuma set up 113 outer districts besides the Kagoshima castle town, settled warriors in them and had them farm in peacetime. Its one number, the 113, comes with no date and no source, so it cannot say when in the period that count held. The page gives no count of warriors or of households. It also describes Satsuma alone, a domain whose arrangement was unusual.

Used for: Satsuma's warriors settled in 113 outer districts and farming in peacetime (Sizing a city)
