松本勇介 (Matsumoto Yusuke), 「江戸時代の正一位稲荷大明神安鎮証書について」 ("On the Edo-period certificates of installation of the Shoichii Inari Daimyojin", title translated), 『皇學館論叢』 (Kogakkan Ronso) 52:2 (April 2019) (in Japanese; https://kogakkan.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/317/files/%E8%AB%96%E5%8F%A252%282%29-01.pdf)

What it is: A study of the certificates the priestly houses and the temple of the Fushimi Inari shrine in Kyoto issued when they installed a branch of the god in another shrine, from surviving certificates, receipts and the issuers' fee schedules of before and after 1864.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the graded fees for dedicating a shrine to Inari and which grades people chose. It is one shrine's fee for one rite, paid to Fushimi, not what a local priest or monk was given for a dedication; the earlier schedules are undated or dated only as before a year; the schedule of after 1864 is in the debased coin of the years after the 1860 recoinage, so its figures are not the earlier ones at face value, nor mid-Edo prices; and its count of grades is of the certificates that survive and show one.

Used for: what dedicating a small shrine cost (Money and prices)
