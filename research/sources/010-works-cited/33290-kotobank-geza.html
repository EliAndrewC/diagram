kotobank-geza

Britannica Japan concise encyclopedia, Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten (Heibonsha, old edition) and others, s.v. 下座 ("the musicians' corner of the kabuki stage", title translated), aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/下座)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the geza, the corner of the kabuki stage where the music was played and, by extension, that music, carrying several dictionaries' entries on the word's other senses too.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the geza, kabuki's offstage music, has been played by musicians belonging to the theater since it arose, and that late in the Kyoho era (1716-1736) the corner by the stage became the place where it was played. It speaks of the city theaters of Kamigata and Edo, not of touring troupes, and does not say how many musicians a theater kept.

Used for: the theater's own musicians (Money and prices)
