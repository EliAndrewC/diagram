Yang Guoan, 「Ponds, Weirs and Irrigation: Water Management Organizations and Popular Order in Rural Southern Hubei in the Ming and Qing」 (translated; original: 「塘堰與灌溉：明清時期鄂南鄉村的水利組織與民間秩序」), Journal of History and Anthropology 5(1) (2007) (in Chinese; https://schina-publication.hkust.edu.hk/sites/default/files/pubs/Jour-05.1.A03.pdf)

What it is: A historian's study of Chongyang county in southeastern Hubei, built on the Huapi weir's own register of water documents, with the county gazetteers, lineage genealogies and land deeds. It gives the land the county's old ponds and its great weir watered, how the weir's upkeep was shared out by acreage, and how each paddy was tied to the pond that watered it.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its figures for single ponds and for one large weir come from the county's Ming and Qing records, quoted in the original. It is one hilly county of the middle Yangtze, where ponds and weirs ordered the farmland; the acreages are those the records register, in the mu of their day.

Used for: the land single ponds and the Huapi weir watered, and every paddy's tie to one pond (Fields)
