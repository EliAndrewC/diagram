koshikake-chaya-kotobank

Kotobank, 腰掛茶屋 ("koshikake tea-house", title translated) - the Digital Daijisen, the Seisen-ban Nihon kokugo daijiten and Heibonsha's revised new World Encyclopedia (entry by 神保文夫, Jinbo Fumio) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/腰掛茶屋-501056)

What it is: Dictionary and encyclopedia entries on the word: a roadside or shrine-precinct rest tea-house, and the licensed tea-houses kept in the waiting sheds of the shogunate's courts; the World Encyclopedia's, by a historian of Edo law, describes the shed itself.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts the shed before the gate, gives the South Town Magistracy's as 94 tsubo, and tells what the waiting parties did there until called into the court; it describes the courts in Edo only, and the tea-house, not the shed's build; the 94 tsubo is one Edo town magistracy's shed, a court of the shogun's capital, not a figure for a provincial magistrate's office.

Used for: the hearing court's waiting shed (Estates and other compounds)
