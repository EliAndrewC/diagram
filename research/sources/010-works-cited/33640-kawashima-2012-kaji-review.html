kawashima-2012-kaji-review

河島一仁 (Kawashima Kazuhito), 「日本における鋳物師・鍛冶に関する研究の進展 ―歴史地理学的「職人集団研究」の可能性―」 ("Progress in research on founders and smiths in Japan", title translated), 『立命館文學』 627 (in Japanese; https://www.ritsumei.ac.jp/acd/cg/lt/rb/627/627PDF/kawashima.pdf)

What it is: A historical geographer's review of the research on Japan's founders and smiths, which summarizes Yamaguchi Morito's studies of the 1960s on how the cutlery towns of Seki, Sakai, Miki and others took shape.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one source found that names the trades a Sakai blade passed through in the 1750s, but it is a summary of another scholar's summary, its sentence hedged as hearsay, and it gives no figures.

Used for: the trades of Sakai's cutlery about 1757, and when Seki's took shape (Money and prices)
