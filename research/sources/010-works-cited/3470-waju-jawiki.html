ja.wikipedia 輪中 ("ring-levee community", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%BC%AA%E4%B8%AD)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the waju, the ring-diked polder settlements of the Nobi plain; an encyclopedia article citing prefectural and academic works.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the continuous ring dike and the homestead's own windbreak and flood groves sited by the winter wind and the flood direction, which is this page's evidence that a polder village kept its house groves; it says nothing of planting on the dikes themselves. For the wetland at a fan's toe it has one sentence, citing a national river office's bulletin: springs near the lower edge of the Nobi plain's alluvial fans made wetland there, and some waju were built as that land was developed. It says only that such wetland formed, gives no width or extent for the wet belt, and speaks for the Nobi plain's fans only.

Used for: homestead groves inside a polder (Field archetypes); wetland from the springs near a fan's toe (Water)
