nagoya-ninomaru-2023

名古屋市 (Nagoya City), papers on the repair of the Ninomaru garden of Nagoya Castle and on its 2022 excavation (令和５年度二之丸庭園の修復整備について; 令和４年度二之丸庭園の発掘調査成果について), 2022-23 (in Japanese; https://www.nagoyajo.city.nagoya.jp/plan_expert/uploads/b7316da1d745be0a2b7e08930b1a74b8_1.pdf)

What it is: Papers put to Nagoya City's expert committee on the Ninomaru garden, the garden of the second bailey (Ninomaru) of Nagoya Castle: a table of the earlier excavations' findings (2016-17), the 2022 excavation of the North pond's bank, and the garden drawing of the Bunsei years (1818-30) against which the remains are read.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the excavated revetment and floor of an Edo-period garden pond. It is one castle's garden, far grander than a small residence's or an office's, and the excavators could not fix the date of the tataki revetment they found.

Used for: the excavated bank of one castle garden's pond (Estates and other compounds)
