achaya-potatoes-blog

A personal blog, "Potatoes in India, as per Achaya", Arun's Musings, 17 March 2005, quoting a writer named Achaya (https://arunsmusings.blogspot.com/2005/03/potatoes-in-india-as-per-achaya.html)

What it is: A blog post reproducing, with his references, what Achaya wrote on the history of the potato in India.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is read only as a quotation of Achaya, and the blogger warns that he may be wrong or one-sided. It is used for his doubt that the "potatoes" of 1615 and 1675 were white potatoes. The doubt is hedged ("perhaps", "probably"), it reasons from the confusion of names and from the potato's not yet having reached India, and the passages quote no description of the plant; the blogger says most accounts take Fryer to have seen the white potato. The two passages date Fryer differently, 1675 and 1678, and the blog names neither Achaya's book nor a page. They say the sweet potato was "known much earlier in India" but give no date for it.

Used for: The sweet potato in India: the identity of the 1615 and 1675 "potatoes" (Money and prices)
