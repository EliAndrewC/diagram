jacks-2006-gpih-japan-1887-1926

David Jacks, Peter Lindert and Salvadore Puente, "Prices in Japan 1887-1926", a spreadsheet for the Global Price and Income History Group, 2006, from the Financial and Economic Annual of Japan (Tokyo: Department of Finance, various years) (https://gpih.ucdavis.edu/files/Japan_1885-1926.xls)

What it is: A yearly table, 1887-1926, of the prices in yen of rice, wheat, soybeans, salt, "soy" by the koku, sake, cotton cloth, charcoal, coal and a few other goods, each in its Japanese unit, taken mainly from the Finance Ministry's annual, with the same prices converted to pounds sterling a metric unit.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the nearest yearly series found to the Tokyo prices of 1882-1886 for the goods those leave out, salt and fuel. It names no city except that its prices for 1906-1914 are Tokyo's rescaled, its prices begin in 1887 (its wages sheet in 1885), and it holds no rent and no firewood.

Used for: the nearest salt, charcoal, soybean and cloth prices to Tokyo 1882-1886 (Money and prices)
