"宝永四ツ宝丁銀" ("Hoei Yotsu-takara chogin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/宝永四ツ宝丁銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the lowest-grade chogin, issued from 1711, with its fineness, the amounts minted and the effect of its issue on prices and on the silver rate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Edo's silver rate in the fifth month of 1714, which the record compares with the rate from an Osaka compilation. It takes the figure from the 1933 『新稿両替年代記関鍵』 and does not say which silver the 89 monme were counted in; that book was not read here.

Used for: Edo's rate of gold in silver in 1714 (Money and prices)
