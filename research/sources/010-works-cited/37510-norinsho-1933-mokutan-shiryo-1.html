農林省山林局 (Forestry Bureau, Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry), 『木炭関係資料 第一号: 炭材林ノ経営及製炭事業ノ収支関係調査』 (Materials on charcoal, no. 1: survey of the accounts of charcoal-wood forests and of charcoal burning, title translated), September 1933, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1077861/1/13 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1077861/1/14 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1077861/1/3 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1077861)

What it is: The forestry bureau's compilation of the prefectures' reports on what burning charcoal cost and brought in during 1932. For charcoal from natural forest it gives, by kind of wood and for white and black charcoal apart, the cost of making 100 kan in six parts (standing timber, burning, baling, carriage, sundries, interest), the market settlement price and the profit, and then the same six parts as percentages of the cost.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the cost of baling charcoal as a share of the whole cost in the bureau's own percentages, and sets it beside a market price. It is of 1932, in yen; each figure is an average of the prefectures' "representative" districts; and its baling cost is one figure, not divided into the bale, the rope and the work, nor said to be bought or home-made. The share is of cost: a share of the price is this record's division of two of its columns.

Used for: baling as a share of the cost of making charcoal, and beside its market price, Japan, 1932 (Money and prices)
