What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Dutch ship that came ashore in Bungo in 1600.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is used only for the dates of the first Dutch ship to reach Japan, against which this project tests the 1598 date for the potato. It says nothing about the potato.

Used for: The date of the first Dutch ship in Japan, set against the potato's claimed arrival (Money and prices)
