Horibe Tetsuya, "ルーツは家康の「三河木綿の保護」、愛知の繊維産業のこれまでとこれから" ("Rooted in Ieyasu's protection of Mikawa cotton: Aichi's textile industry past and future", title translated), Newswitch (Nikkan Kogyo Shimbun), 4 March 2024, in Japanese (https://newswitch.jp/p/40552)

What it is: A newspaper site's column by a local historian, by trade head of internal audit at the manufacturer Fuji Seiko, on the textile industry of Aichi, opening with Mikawa cotton under Tokugawa Ieyasu.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the document behind the 1510 date, a chronicle of Daijoin at Kofukuji, and says the cloth was collected as tax. The chronicle is the Eisho nenchuki, under the year Eisho 7, which is 1510. Of it the column quotes only the name it gives the cloth, 三川木綿, not the entry, and it does not say who collected the cloth, from whom or how much; the chronicle itself was not read. Its date for the seed, the late fifteenth century, cites no source and is for a variety imported from Ming into Japan; of Mikawa it says only that the seed reached it soon after, so it gives no date for Mikawa.

Used for: what the record of Mikawa cotton in 1510 says, and when the seed arrived (Money and prices)
