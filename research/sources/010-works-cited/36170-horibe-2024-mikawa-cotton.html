Horibe Tetsuya, "ルーツは家康の「三河木綿の保護」、愛知の繊維産業のこれまでとこれから" ("Rooted in Ieyasu's protection of Mikawa cotton: Aichi's textile industry past and future", title translated), Newswitch (Nikkan Kogyo Shimbun), 4 March 2024, in Japanese (https://newswitch.jp/p/40552)

What it is: A newspaper site's column by a local historian on the textile industry of Aichi, opening with Mikawa cotton under Tokugawa Ieyasu.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the document behind the 1510 date, a chronicle of Daijoin at Kofukuji, and says the cloth was collected as tax. It quotes no words of that chronicle, which was not read, and it cites no source for its date of the seed's arrival.

Used for: what the record of Mikawa cotton in 1510 says, and when the seed arrived (Money and prices)
