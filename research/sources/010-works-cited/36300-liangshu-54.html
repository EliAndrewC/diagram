liangshu-54

Yao Silian (姚思廉), Liang shu (梁書, History of the Liang dynasty), chapter 54, the accounts of foreign peoples, on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/梁書/卷54)

What it is: The chapter of the official history of the Liang dynasty (502-557) on the peoples of the southern seas, the east and the northwest, compiled in the early Tang.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an early account that describes a cotton plant and its weaving outright, at Linyi and at Gaochang. It describes foreign lands from court records and earlier envoys' reports, so it says nothing of cotton inside China, and a custom it gives may be older than the 500s. It gives no price or quantity: Gaochang's cloth is said only to pass in trade, and the tribute lists name jibei without saying whether raw or woven. It calls jibei a tree in the south and baidie a herb in the northwest without saying whether the two are one plant, and the names cross: baidie also at Funan and in India, jibei cloth also at Kepantuo in the far northwest.

Used for: cotton at Turfan and in southern-seas tribute in the 500s (Money and prices)
