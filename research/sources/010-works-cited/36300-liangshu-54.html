liangshu-54

Yao Silian (姚思廉), Liang shu (梁書, History of the Liang dynasty), chapter 54, the accounts of foreign peoples, on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/梁書/卷54)

What it is: The chapter of the official history of the Liang dynasty (502-557) on the peoples of the southern seas and the northwest, compiled in the early Tang.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the oldest text read that describes a cotton plant and its weaving outright, at Gaochang. It describes foreign lands from court records, so it says nothing of cotton inside China, and it calls jibei a tree in the south and baidie a herb in the northwest without saying whether the two are one plant.

Used for: cotton at Turfan and in southern-seas tribute in the 500s (Money and prices)
