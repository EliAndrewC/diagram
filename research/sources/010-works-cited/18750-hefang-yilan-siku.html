hefang-yilan-siku

Pan Jixun, Hefang yilan (A General Survey of River Defense), juan 4, "Xiushou shiyi" (Matters of repair and guarding), Siku Quanshu text (in Chinese; http://ab.newdu.com/book/ms219615.html)

What it is: The fourth juan of a Ming manual of the Yellow River and Grand Canal works by Pan Jixun, the official who ran them, transcribed from the Siku Quanshu edition on a classics-reading site; the juan sets out rules for building, guarding and repairing dikes, among them how to plant willows along a dike.

Why it applies, and its limits: A period text, so its planting rules are Ming practice as the river works prescribed it, not a measurement of dikes on the ground; and they are written for the great river dikes of the north, not for a polder's or a fish pond's dike, so they reach a polder map only as an analog. The web transcription carries no date of its own and is not proofed against a printed edition; the juan names years of the Wanli era (1580, 1589).

Used for: the two rows of willow set off a dike's foot, and the long willow's branches supplying fascine material (Field archetypes)
