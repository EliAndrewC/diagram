「Shaanxi Provincial Gazetteer (Siku Quanshu edition), chapter 40, water works」 (translated; original: 「陜西通志 (四庫全書本)/卷040」), the gazetteer of the Yongzheng era (1735), on Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/陜西通志_(四庫全書本)/卷040)

What it is: The water-works chapter of the Shaanxi provincial gazetteer compiled under the Yongzheng emperor and kept in the Qing imperial library, as transcribed on Wikisource. County by county it lists each canal, weir and pond, how far it lies from the county seat, what it draws from, and how many mu of fields it waters, most entries marked as taken from the county's own register, a few from older inscriptions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives, for named canals and ponds, the land each watered, so the span of one irrigation work's tract can be read straight from it. Its figures are registered acreages in the mu of the day, not measured; the Wikisource text is a transcription with some rare characters lost, and the zh-hans view shows it in simplified characters.

Used for: the land one village canal or pond watered in Qianyang county, and the much larger Wumen weir divided into sluices (Fields)
