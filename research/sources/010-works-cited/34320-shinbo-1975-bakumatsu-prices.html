shinbo-1975-bakumatsu-prices

新保博 (Shinbo Hiroshi), 「幕末期の物価変動／1830～67年」 ("Price movements at the end of the shogunate, 1830-67", title translated), 『経済研究』 (The Economic Review) 26:4 (October 1975), pp. 289-301 (in Japanese; https://econ-review.ier.hit-u.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/files/1975/keizaikenkyu2604289.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's index of Osaka wholesale prices from 1802 to 1867, with Kyoto retail prices for sake and soy sauce, split into three periods (1802-1829, 1830-1858, 1859-1867), and a table of each good's coefficient of variation within the year and within each period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only measured comparison read of how much each Edo good swung, good by good, at the scale of a year and of a period. Its period figures are taken on an index of annual means, so they carry each period's trend as well as its swings; it covers only the last 65 years of the Edo period, and salt, silk and several goods only from 1830.

Used for: how far Edo prices moved within a year and over a period, good by good (Money and prices)
