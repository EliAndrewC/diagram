iwahashi-1981-gpih-rice-prices

"The Price of Rice in 14 Areas of Japan", a spreadsheet for the Global Price and Income History Group, from Masaru Iwahashi, Kinsei Nippon bukkashi no kenkyū (Tokyo: Ōhara Shinseisha, 1981), Appendix Table 1, pp. 460-465, with Osamu Saito's clarifications of its places and price types, 2008 (https://gpih.ucdavis.edu/files/Japan_rice_P_1620-1867.xls)

What it is: A transcription of a price historian's yearly table of the price of a koku of rice in fourteen places, 1620-1867, in silver monme in the west and gold ryo in the east, with a note on where and how each place's price was set.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its Osaka series is the Dojima rice market's, year by year from 1701, and the same prices converted into ryo at Osaka's gold-silver rates, so the two together give that rate. It prices rice alone, of one province's crop, at the wholesale exchange for domain warehouse rice, not what a household paid at a shop; its silver and gold are each year's coin, whose metal the shogunate's recoinages (1695, 1706-1714, 1736, 1818 and after) changed, so its prices and the rate they imply are in the coin of that year, not a constant weight of silver or gold; several of its places are domain prices that its notes say probably smoothed the market's swings; and the rate it implies is a conversion, not a quoted rate, with only three years before 1720.

Used for: Osaka's rice from 1701 and its gold-silver rate from 1720; how far the price of rice stood apart from place to place (Money and prices)
