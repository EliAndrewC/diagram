keller-shiue-wang-appendix

Wolfgang Keller, Carol H. Shiue and Xin Wang, "Capital Markets in China and Britain, 1770-1860: Evidence from Grain Prices", Online Appendix, American Economic Association (https://topcat.aeaweb.org/articles/materials/14932)

What it is: The online appendix of three economists' paper on grain storage and interest rates in China and Britain, describing the Qing grain price reports (a prefecture's high and low price each lunar month) and tabulating month-to-month grain price changes for 1770-1860.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only figure read for how much Qing grain prices moved from one month to the next. Its monthly figures are for the storage months between harvests only, and the paper itself, which they serve, was not read.

Used for: how far Qing grain prices moved from month to month (Money and prices)
