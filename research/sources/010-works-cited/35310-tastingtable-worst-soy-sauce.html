Tasting Table, "The Store-Brand Soy Sauce You Shouldn't Even Think About Buying" (https://www.tastingtable.com/1571349/worst-soy-sauce-brand/)

What it is: Wendy Leigh's article in the food magazine Tasting Table (updated July 30, 2026) on the Kroger grocery chain's basic soy sauce, which the magazine's taste tester ranked worst of 13 brands, explaining the difference between brewed soy sauce and soy sauce made quickly by acid hydrolysis.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says, from the label's ingredients, that the basic store-brand soy sauce of one grocery chain is not brewed, so that the cheapest soy sauce on a shelf need not be the brewed kind the basket stands for. It gives no price and does not say the basic bottle is the chain's cheapest, and its reading of the ingredients is the magazine's, not the maker's. It also notes that the same chain sells a brewed soy sauce under its own name, so it shows only that one store brand's basic bottle is not brewed, not that a store brand's soy sauce is never brewed.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today (Comparative money: other societies)
