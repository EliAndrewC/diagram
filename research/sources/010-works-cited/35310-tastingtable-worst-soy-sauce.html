Tasting Table, "The Store-Brand Soy Sauce You Shouldn't Even Think About Buying" (https://www.tastingtable.com/1571349/worst-soy-sauce-brand/)

What it is: A food magazine's article on a supermarket chain's basic soy sauce, which its taste tester ranked worst, explaining the difference between brewed soy sauce and soy sauce made quickly by acid hydrolysis.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says, from the label's ingredients, that the basic store-brand soy sauce of one grocery chain is not brewed, so that the cheapest soy sauce on a shelf need not be the brewed kind the basket stands for. It gives no price, and its reading of the ingredients is the magazine's, not the maker's.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today (Comparative money: other societies)
