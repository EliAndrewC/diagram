jaanus-shikidai

JAANUS (Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System), "shikidai" 式台 (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/shikidai)

What it is: The entry for shikidai 式台 in JAANUS, an online English-language dictionary of Japanese art and architectural terms: the low broad timber step across a formal entrance, with its boards, its depth and its use as a palanquin stand.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that says what a shikidai was made of and how deep it ran, which is what a plan's formal entrance is drawn from. It dates the step as a standard feature of upper-class houses only "by the end of the 17th century", gives its depth as a range rather than a typical figure, names a single example house, and cites no study of its own.

Used for: the shikidai a low timber step, its boards and its depth (Estates and other compounds)
