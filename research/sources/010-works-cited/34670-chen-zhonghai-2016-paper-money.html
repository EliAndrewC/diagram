陈忠海 (Chen Zhonghai), 纸币在中国古代为何难以通行 ("Why paper money was hard to circulate in premodern China", title translated), 2016, on Aisixiang (in Chinese; https://www.aisixiang.com/data/99634.html)

What it is: A short magazine article on the failures of paper money in the Yuan, Ming and Qing, citing the Ming annals and histories of Chinese money for its figures.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated discounts on Ming notes and on the Xianfeng cash notes, each named from the work it cites. Those works were not read here, so its figures stand at second hand.

Used for: the discounts on Ming and Xianfeng paper money (Money and prices)
