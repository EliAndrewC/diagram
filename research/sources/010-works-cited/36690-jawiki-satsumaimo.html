jawiki-satsumaimo

Japanese Wikipedia, "サツマイモ" (Sweet potato; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/サツマイモ)

What it is: The Japanese encyclopedia article on the sweet potato, with a long history of its arrival and spread in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read that gives the Japanese dates, and nearly all of them come through it second-hand, Cocks's diary and Miyazaki's and Kaibara's books among them. Its own editors mark its section on the plant's spread in Japan as over-detailed, under-sourced and possibly original research, and that section's passages on Cocks, Miyazaki, Kaibara and 1773 carry no footnote. The inference that the plant was familiar to commoners in Edo by 1773 rests on one joke book, with no source given for it. The article also sets 1697 beside Genroku 9, which is 1696, so its era-years need checking.
