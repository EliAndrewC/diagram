jawiki-satsumaimo

Japanese Wikipedia, "サツマイモ" (Sweet potato; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/サツマイモ)

What it is: The Japanese encyclopedia article on the sweet potato, with a long history of its arrival and spread in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read that gives the Japanese dates, and nearly all of them come through it second-hand, Cocks's diary and Miyazaki's and Kaibara's books among them. Its own editors mark the history section as over-detailed and under-sourced, and the inference that commoners in Edo ate the plant by 1773 rests on one joke book.

Used for: The sweet potato in Japan (Money and prices)
