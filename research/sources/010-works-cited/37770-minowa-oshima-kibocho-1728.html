minowa-oshima-kibocho-1728

Minowa Yu (箕輪優), "享保十三年「大島規模帳」に関する考察―薩摩藩の奄美諸島支配について―" ("A study of the Oshima regulations of 1728: on Satsuma domain's rule of the Amami islands", title translated), a journal article in Seijo University's repository (in Japanese; https://seijo.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/3744/files/jpn-039-04.pdf)

What it is: A study of the regulations Satsuma domain issued for Amami Oshima in 1728 and of how the domain governed the islands. One of its notes dates the start of paying the land tax in black sugar instead of rice and gives the first rate of conversion.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its note gives the earliest rate of rice for sugar on the islands, of 1747, after a 2011 study by Yuge Masami. The rate is a conversion of tax owed in rice into sugar, not a price paid for sugar, and the article says nothing of what the sugar fetched.

Used for: the first rate of rice for Amami sugar, 1747 (Money and prices)
