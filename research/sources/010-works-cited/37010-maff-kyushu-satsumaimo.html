maff-kyushu-satsumaimo

Japan's Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, Kyushu regional office, "サツマイモの伝来 - 前田利右衛門と青木昆陽" (The arrival of the sweet potato: Maeda Riemon and Aoki Konyo), undated leaflet (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/kyusyu/seibibu/kokuei/13/pdf/satumaimonodennrai.pdf)

What it is: A two-page local-history leaflet on how the sweet potato reached Kagoshima and Edo, listing a city newsletter, a college bulletin, a confectioner's pamphlet and a shrine's signboard as its sources.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is one of the few pages read that says anything about the sweet potato's spread in Japan. It tells the commemorated story of two local heroes, hedges its claims with "it is said" and "there is a theory", and does not itself argue why the crop spread.

Used for: What set the pace of new crops (Money and prices)
