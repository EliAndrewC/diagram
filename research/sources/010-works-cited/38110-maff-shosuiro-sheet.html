maff-shosuiro-sheet

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, "⑦承水路" ("7. The catch channel", title translated), p. 91 of the ministry's guide to environment-friendly paddy improvement (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/j/nousin/keityo/kankyo/attach/pdf/suiden_seibi_kaisetu-29.pdf)

What it is: One sheet of a modern design guide for paddy works that keep room for wildlife, on the channel dug along a hill foot to catch its seepage before it reaches the paddy, with its size, where it goes and its local names.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where the seepage of a valley paddy comes from - the hill foot - that it can make the paddy wet and cold, and that the catch channel goes in valley and sloping paddies. It is a modern standard and gives no date for the practice; its many regional names for the channel suggest an old one, but the sheet does not say so. It speaks only of paddy against a hill foot and gives no reach for the wet ground, and it says nothing of a fan's sides or a brook's bank.

Used for: seepage from the hill foot making a valley paddy wet, caught in a channel along the foot (Water)
