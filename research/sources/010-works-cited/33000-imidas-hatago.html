imidas-hatago

Yamamoto Hirofumi, "旅籠（はたご）" ("Hatago", title translated), in the Jidaigeki yogo shinan, on imidas, 19 January 2009 (in Japanese; https://imidas.jp/jidaigeki/detail/L-57-131-08-04-G252.html)

What it is: A signed dictionary entry by a historian of the Edo period on the hatago, the post-town inn that served meals, in a glossary of terms for period drama.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a night's charge at the better and the lesser hatago on the Tokaido, at the better inns of the Nakasendo, and at a kichin-yado. It dates none of these figures and names no source for them, so they are a historian's summary of the late Edo period, not one year's prices.

Used for: a night at a hatago and a kichin-yado on the Tokaido and the Nakasendo (Money and prices)
