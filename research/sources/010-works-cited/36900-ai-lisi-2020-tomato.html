Ai Lisi (艾栗斯), 《食物有故事：番茄的征服与妥协》 (Food has a story: the tomato's conquest and compromise), first printed in 《北京日报》 as 《番茄，从“毒果”到美食》 (print date not given), posted on The Paper's platform 11 May 2020 (in Chinese; https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_7335825)

What it is: A newspaper feature on the tomato's history around the world, with a section on China and a closing note on the dish of tomato and egg.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the Qunfang pu at more length than the other pages read and gives a date for the dish of tomato and egg. It has no notes, and its European stories are told as anecdote.

Used for: The tomato in China (Money and prices)
