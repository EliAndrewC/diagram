湯浅隆 (Yuasa Takashi), 「江戸における開帳場の構成 - 享和三年善光寺出開帳の事例を中心として -」 ("How the grounds of an unveiling in Edo were laid out: the Zenkoji traveling unveiling of 1803", title translated), 国立歴史民俗博物館研究報告 (Bulletin of the National Museum of Japanese History) 11 (1986) (in Japanese; https://rekihaku.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/332/files/kenkyuhokoku_011_09.pdf)

What it is: A historian's study of one traveling showing of the Zenkoji temple's hidden image, at the Denboin of Asakusa in Edo in 1803: the temporary buildings, the bidding for them, the dealings with the authorities and how it ended.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the winning bid for the temporary buildings of the showing and quotes the temple officer's letter on its takings; it gives no total of takings or costs, but it says the eighty-day showing ended at a loss, which the officer's letter blamed on a measles epidemic and, perhaps, the site, so its takings are a bad year's and not a typical showing's; and it is one showing of the most famous image of the period in the largest city.

Used for: what the temporary buildings for a temple's showing cost, and how the 1803 showing paid (Money and prices)
