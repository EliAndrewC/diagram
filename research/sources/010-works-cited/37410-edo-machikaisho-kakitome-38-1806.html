edo-machikaisho-kakitome-38-1806

『町会所一件書留』 (Notes on the affairs of the town office), [38], the fire-relief part for Bunka 3, a manuscript copy among the records the shogunate's Edo town magistrates handed on, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587102/1/54 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587102/1/65 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587102/1/71 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587102)

What it is: A volume of the files of Edo's town office, the body that held the city's relief fund. The part read is the accounts for the relief sheds put up for those burned out by the great fire of 1806: the straw mats, rope, empty bales and straw bought to roof, floor and tie the sheds, each with its count, its sum in copper coin and its rate, the later repair of leaking roofs, and the craftsmen and laborers paid in silver.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices empty bales, straw mats and rope by the piece in one dated Edo account, bought by the thousand. It does not say what the bales had held or whether they were new, and they were bought as roofing for sheds, not as containers. The laborers' silver rate is entered as covering early starts, late stays and work by day and night, so it is not a plain day's wage. Its copper sums are larger than rate times count by about a twenty-fourth, which fits a "hundred" reckoned as a string of 96 coins; that reading is this record's arithmetic. Only the three frames named were read.

Used for: what empty bales, straw mats and rope cost in Edo in 1806, and the relief sheds' laborers (Money and prices)
