Yamamoto Mitsumasa (山本光正), 「東海道における人馬賃銭について」 ("On the fares for men and horses on the Tokaido", title translated), Hosei Shigaku 21 (1969), from p. 89 (in Japanese; Hosei University repository, https://hosei.ecats-library.jp/da/repository/00010841/shigaku_21_yamamoto.pdf)

What it is: A historian's study of how the official fares for post horses and porters on the Tokaido were made up, from the base fare of 1711 and the later surcharges to how each station shared the surcharges out, from the stations' own documents of the 1820s to the 1850s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the base fare and the surcharged fare for one stage, Maisaka to Hamamatsu, in the 1820s, how the base fare of 1711 was reached, and the negotiated fare against the official one. It prints the fare for that one stage, not a table for the road, and it hedges the negotiated fare's "twice" itself.

Used for: the base and surcharged stage fares, the negotiated fare, and the unpaid way back (Money and prices)
