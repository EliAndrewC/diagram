l7r-bounties

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Registering a bounty" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#registering-a-bounty) and "Example bounties" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#example-bounties) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the sections "Registering a bounty" and "Example bounties": how an Imperial magistrate registers a bounty and gives notice of it, and the outstanding bounties of the latest Imperial bulletin, among them 100 koku on the hermit Shoda.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: that bounties are posted, and on whom, is the setting's to decide, and these maps follow it. The notes send a fugitive's description to the waystations on Imperial roads and the bulletin of outstanding bounties to local authorities, but they do not say that a notice is put up on a board at a town's gate, nor name the seat whose magistrate registered Shoda's bounty; the board at Ubame's gate is these maps' own placing of the bulletin.

Used for: the bounty on the hermit Shoda, behind the second board at Ubame's gate (Trades and services)
