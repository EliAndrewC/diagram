kotobank-daidogei

Hyakka Jiten MyPedia (Heibonsha) and others, s.v. 大道芸 ("street performance", title translated), aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/大道芸)

What it is: The Kotobank page for street performance, mostly in Japan but with sections on Europe, China, Korea and Indonesia, carrying several Japanese encyclopedias' entries on the performers who played at crossroads and in the busy quarters, their kinds of act and their history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how a street performer was paid: by asking the onlookers for coin, or by selling goods; its entries on the medieval performers and the door-to-door ones say they begged rice as well as coin. It gives no amount, and its entries describe the trade from the medieval period into the twentieth century without dating the practice more closely.

Used for: how a street performer was paid (Money and prices)
