shugiin-1950-genmai-koku-question

House of Representatives of Japan, question no. 21 of the 7th Diet, "供出玄米の重量に関する質問主意書" ("Question on the weight of delivered brown rice", title translated), submitted 28 January 1950 (in Japanese; https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_shitsumona.nsf/html/shitsumon/a007021.htm)

What it is: A Diet member's written question asking why rice delivered to the government was reckoned at 150 kg a koku of brown rice, two and a half bales of 60 kg, when a ministry handbook of 1949 fixed a koku of brown rice at 146.3 kg; the Cabinet's answer has its own entry.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes the ministry's conversion table for the weight of a koku of brown rice, a second official figure beside the 150 kg of the answer. The table is of the late 1940s, and the Cabinet's answer calls it unconnected with what the government actually used in buying rice, so it stands for an Edo koku only by analogy.

Used for: a 1949 table's weight of a koku of brown rice (Money and prices)
