"トマト" (Tomato), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%88%E3%83%9E%E3%83%88)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tomato, with a history section on its arrival and use in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It alone dates the tomato's spread as a vegetable to about 1887, and its dates rest on works not read here: the 1887 date on a chronological dictionary of Meiji social history (明治世相編年辞典), cited without a page, and on a popular vegetable book of 2013 from Kodansha; the arrival by the Dutch at Nagasaki on that book alone. Its phrase "early 17th century (around the Kanbun era)" does not agree with itself.
