yabuiri-kotobank

Kotobank, 藪入り ("yabuiri, servants' home leave", title translated) - the World Encyclopedia (entry by 宮田登, Miyata Noboru), Nipponica (entry by 井之口章次, Inokuchi Shoji), the Yamakawa dictionary of Japanese history and other reference works (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%97%AA%E5%85%A5%E3%82%8A-143938)

What it is: The Kotobank page carrying several published reference entries on yabuiri, the days around the 16th of the first and the seventh months when servants were given leave to go home.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates servants' two home-leave days and says they were not servants' only days off; its entries describe the custom of Edo's merchant houses chiefly, and, beyond one entry's two home visits a year, in spring and autumn, give no count of a servant's days off in a year.

Used for: servants' home-leave days (Money and prices)
