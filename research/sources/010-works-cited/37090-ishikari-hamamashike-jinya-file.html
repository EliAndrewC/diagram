City of Ishikari, 石狩ファイル No.0103-01 荘内藩ハママシケ陣屋跡 ("Ishikari File No. 0103-01: the site of the Shonai domain's Hamamashike jin'ya", title translated), 2009 (in Japanese; https://www.city.ishikari.hokkaido.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/004/676/1004676_010.pdf)

What it is: A one-page leaflet of the city of Ishikari's board of education and its museum, signed by Ishibashi Takao, on the Hamamashike jin'ya: why the Shonai domain, granted the land in 1859, built it (1860 by the leaflet's table), the villages it founded, the main buildings inside it, and its dismantling in 1868.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page that names the post's weapons store, in a list of the main buildings. The post was the Shonai domain's seat in Ezo for under ten years (1859-1868). Its magistrate's office at the center governed the domain's settlers, but its arms served a garrison of over two hundred retainers posted against Russia: a larger and more military seat than a county magistrate's compound. So it shows that such a seat kept a separate weapons store, not how a civil magistracy stocked or placed its arms. The leaflet lists one reference (the 1980 history of Hamamasu village) without tying its building list to it, and gives no place, size or build for any building.

Used for: where a seat kept its arms (Estates and other compounds)
