l7r-money

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Money" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#money), "Merchant Families" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#merchant-families), "Rent and taxes" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#rent-and-taxes) and "What does this imply about rent and taxes?" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#what-does-this-imply-about-rent-and-taxes) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the sections on money and on rent and taxes: the koku as both a gold coin and the rice that feeds a person for a year, about 40 gallons, and the 2 koku a year a person is assumed to need; a family's plot of about 15 koku a year, its tax capped at a third of its estimated yield and its rent at a sixth; and a single farm's value of about 50 koku, a figure the notes take from earlier adventures.

Why it applies, and its limits: What a koku coin is worth in Rokugan is the setting's to decide, and the research beside it reports what the historical koku held. The notes do not say which gallon they mean, or whether the rice is brown or polished. They also give the rent's base two ways: one section caps it at a sixth of the land's productive capacity, another sets it at a sixth of the land's total value. The second section's sum of rent and tax as half the yield fits only the first reading.

Used for: the setting's koku of about 40 gallons that feeds a person for a year, set beside the historical koku; the family plot's yield, tax and rent, and a farm's value of about 50 koku, set beside what farmland sold for (Money and prices)
