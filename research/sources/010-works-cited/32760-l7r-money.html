l7r-money

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Money" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#money) and "Rent and taxes" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#rent-and-taxes) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the sections on money and on rent and taxes: the koku as both a gold coin and the rice that feeds a person for a year, about 40 gallons, and the 2 koku a year a person is assumed to need.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: what a koku coin is worth in Rokugan is the setting's to decide, and the research beside it reports what the historical koku held. The notes do not say which gallon they mean, or whether the rice is brown or polished.

Used for: the setting's koku of about 40 gallons that feeds a person for a year, set beside the historical koku (Money and prices)
