US Department of Agriculture, National Agricultural Statistics Service, Agricultural Prices, released September 29, 2026, with prices for August 2026, plain text (https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/796084/agpr0926.txt)

What it is: The government's monthly report of the prices US farmers receive for their crops and livestock, nationwide, by bushel, hundredweight or pound, with the prices farmers pay for what they buy.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: the US farm-gate price of the basket's raw goods, as a yardstick beside a premodern wholesale price, never a figure of the setting. Its prices are for a crop's first sale off the farm, in its raw form (rough rice, whole soybeans, raw cotton lint), not a wholesale price of the milled or finished good; and a month's price moves with the harvest.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
