Song Yingxing, 《天工開物》 (Tiangong kaiwu, 1637), the chapter 「夏服」 ("Summer clothing", title translated), on Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%A4%A9%E5%B7%A5%E9%96%8B%E7%89%A9/%E5%A4%8F%E6%9C%8D)

What it is: The section on ramie and other bast fibers in the late Ming encyclopedia of crafts: growing ramie, splitting and bleaching the fiber, spinning it, and the looms used.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one rate read for preparing ramie, the fiber split in a full day, and says a skilled spinner on a treadle wheel did the work of three. The rate is by weight of fiber, not by bolt, and for the splitting alone; the transcription prints 一女土 where 一女工, "one woman worker", is meant.

Used for: the ramie fiber split in a day and treadle spinning (Money and prices)
