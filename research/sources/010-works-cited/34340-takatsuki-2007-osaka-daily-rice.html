高槻泰郎 (Takatsuki Yasuo), 「近世市場機構の形成過程 ―堂島米会所における米価形成の効率性―」 ("How the early-modern market system formed: the efficiency of rice price formation at the Dojima rice exchange", title translated), Discussion Paper 07-19-Rev., Graduate School of Economics and Osaka School of International Public Policy, Osaka University, December 2007 (in Japanese; https://www2.econ.osaka-u.ac.jp/econ_society/dp/0719R.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's test of whether the Dojima rice exchange in Osaka priced rice efficiently, on daily price series of 1798-1859 built from merchants' price diaries, with notes on how each day's prices were recorded.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its notes say how a trading day ran at Dojima: an opening and a closing price, trading halts, and trading after the official close. It measures market efficiency, not the size of a day's price change, so it gives no figure for how far the price moved within a day or from one day to the next.

Used for: how a day's rice trading ran at Dojima (Money and prices)
