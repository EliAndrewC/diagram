Tosa Masahiko and Kano Ayuko, "『元文年中鉄山仕法書写』 - 解説と翻刻" ("'Copy of the ironworks method book of the Genbun years': commentary and transcription", title translated), ひょうご歴史研究室紀要 (Bulletin of the Hyogo History Research Office) 9, 2024, pp. 98-135 (in Japanese; https://rekihaku.pref.hyogo.lg.jp/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/e84030e2d4bfdbd38e0253caf6f99756.pdf)

What it is: A transcription, with commentary, of a manuscript found among the papers of Mikazuki domain in Harima province (now Hyogo prefecture): a copy of a method book for running an ironworks, dated by its title to the Genbun years (1736 to 1741). It reckons a year of a bellows furnace and its forges item by item: charcoal, ore sand, wages, rice, the iron made, what each kind costs to make at the works, the carriage to Osaka and what the iron should sell for there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets a cost at the works beside a sale in Osaka for the same iron in one document. Both are an operator's estimates for a model year, not prices at which iron changed hands: the figure at the works is a cost of making, and the Osaka figure an expected sale. It comes from a Harima domain's papers, not from Izumo, and the pages read do not name the works; the editors mark one of its sums as doubtful. The weight of its pack-horse load is not stated and is worked out from its own totals.

Used for: what bar iron cost to make at a Harima ironworks against its expected sale in Osaka (Money and prices)
