Tosa Masahiko and Kano Ayuko, "『元文年中鉄山仕法書写』 - 解説と翻刻" ("'Copy of the ironworks method book of the Genbun years': commentary and transcription", title translated), ひょうご歴史研究室紀要 (Bulletin of the Hyogo History Research Office) 9, 2024, pp. 98-135 (in Japanese; https://rekihaku.pref.hyogo.lg.jp/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/e84030e2d4bfdbd38e0253caf6f99756.pdf)

What it is: A transcription, with commentary, of a manuscript found among the papers of Mikazuki domain in Harima province (now Hyogo prefecture): a copy of a method book for running an ironworks, dated by its title to the Genbun years (1736 to 1741). It reckons a year of a bellows furnace and its forges item by item: charcoal, ore sand, wages, rice, the iron made, what each kind costs to make at the works, the carriage to Osaka and what the iron should sell for there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It sets a cost at the works beside a sale in Osaka for the same iron in one document. Both are an operator's estimates for a model year, not prices at which iron changed hands: the figure at the works is a cost of making, and the Osaka figure an expected sale. Both come from its reckoning of the six-blow working, which the editors class with the old established method, not from the four-blow working that the book dates to 1739, so they may be older than the Genbun years its title names. It comes from a Harima domain's papers, not from Izumo, and its model year names no works: the editors tie two later items to a works in the Chigusa valley and suppose the domain had the book from the house that ran the district's ironworks. The editors read one of its sums, the cost of the long bars, as 17 kan 520 monme where the copy writes 19 kan, and the book's own rate of 60 monme a load and its total bear them out. The weight of its pack-horse load is worked out from its own totals, and the editors' table of its contents gives the same 32 kan for a load of pig iron. Nothing in it says how the iron went on from Yamasaki to Osaka.

Used for: what bar iron cost to make at a Harima ironworks against its expected sale in Osaka (Money and prices)
