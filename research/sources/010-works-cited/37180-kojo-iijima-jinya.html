古城盛衰記３ (Kojo seisuiki 3, "The rise and fall of old castles", title translated), "飯島陣屋" (Iijima Jin'ya), a personal site on Google Sites (in Japanese; https://sites.google.com/onodenkan.net/hiro3/%E9%95%B7%E9%87%8E%E7%9C%8C%E3%81%AE%E5%9F%8E/%E9%A3%AF%E5%B3%B6%E9%99%A3%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: A castle enthusiast's page on the Iijima Jin'ya, an intendant's office in Shinano, from a visit of 2015, with its history: its territory, and its rebuilding in 1993 in its appearance at the end of the shogunate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the rebuild whose rooms other pages describe, so a reader knows that what the Iijima pages say of its rooms is a modern reconstruction. It is a hobbyist's page: its history it credits to the Iijima Town Board of Education's signboard, but it names no source for the 1993 date, which stands in its own guide to the site, or for the plan the rebuild followed.

Used for: the Iijima Jin'ya rebuilt in 1993 (Estates and other compounds)
