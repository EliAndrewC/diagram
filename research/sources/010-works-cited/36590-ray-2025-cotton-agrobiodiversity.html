Avik Ray, "The erosion of agrobiodiversity of cotton in India: interplay of politics, science, and technology", Science, Technologie, Développement 5, no. 1 (2025): 84-98, DOI 10.21494/ISTE.OP.2025.1339 (https://openscience.fr/IMG/pdf/iste_std25v5n1_6.pdf)

What it is: A journal article reconstructing how India's cotton species changed over the last century and what drove the change.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the 2-3% share of American cotton in India to 1947-48, the one year its table gives before 1989. That table is taken from a 2014 work not read here, and its 1947-48 column sums to more than 100%, so the shares are not clean. The year falls after the Partition of India, so it may not stand for the undivided country. Only pp. 84, 88 and 91 were read.

Used for: The share of American cotton in India (Money and prices)
