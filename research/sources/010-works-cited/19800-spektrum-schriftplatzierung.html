spektrum-schriftplatzierung

Lexikon der Kartographie und Geomatik (Spektrum Akademischer Verlag), entry "Schriftplatzierung" (https://www.spektrum.de/lexikon/kartographie-geomatik/schriftplatzierung/4424)

What it is: The entry on placing lettering, signed KGR (Konrad Großer, Institut für Länderkunde, Leipzig), in a German reference dictionary of cartography and geomatics, published in print and online by Spektrum (copyright 2001), which sets out the rules for naming point, line and area features and cites Imhof's 1962 work on the arrangement of names.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a readable statement, in a standard reference work, of the German cartographic tradition that follows Imhof, and it names the place to the right of a point symbol ("rechts neben dem Kartenzeichen") as ideal. It does not say whether that means level with the point or at its upper right, and Imhof's own order, as Bobák, Čmolík and Čadík tabulate it, puts the top-right corner first and directly right second; so it supports a right-hand placement, not an order that tries the adjacent positions before the corners. It is a short dictionary entry, not Imhof's own text, and it speaks of point symbols on maps in general, not of drawn objects on building plans.

Used for: the placement of a point's name to its right as the ideal in a German reference work after Imhof (Map conventions)
