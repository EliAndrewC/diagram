ja.wikipedia 出雲大社 ("Izumo Taisha, the Izumo Grand Shrine", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/出雲大社) - 「Its height is 8 jo (about 24 meters), a size out of all proportion for a shrine.」 (translated; original: 「高さは8丈（およそ24メートル）と、神社としては破格の大きさである」); no plan dimension such as 二間四方 ("ni-ken shihō, two ken square", translated)

Used for: the 24 m height of the main hall, and the absence of a floor dimension (Estates and other compounds).
