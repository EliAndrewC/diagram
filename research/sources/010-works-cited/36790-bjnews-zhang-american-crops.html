What it is: A popular article on three American crops by a writer who mentions his own research once, for a count of American crops, reposted on a newspaper's contributor platform.

Why it applies, and its limits: It alone gives 1601 for Japan and reads the older word tu-dou as the potato. It names no source for 1601 beyond "Western historical records", and the texts it quotes for tu-dou were not read; its reading of them conflicts with Laufer's.
