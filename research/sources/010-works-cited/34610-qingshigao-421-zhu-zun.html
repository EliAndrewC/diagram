清史稿 (Draft History of the Qing, title translated), volume 421, biographies, 朱嶟 (Zhu Zun), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/清史稿/卷421)

What it is: A volume of official biographies in the draft dynastic history; the biography of the official Zhu Zun quotes at length his memorial of about 1846 answering a proposal to unify silver and cash.

Why it applies, and its limits: The memorial says who paid in which metal in the 1840s (farmers' taxes, salt, river works, soldiers) and who lost as cash fell. Its figures are an official's estimates made in an argument for paying troops in cash, not counts, and its date is the biography's order of entries.

Used for: who paid in silver and who in copper in the 1840s, and who lost as cash fell (Money and prices)
