naro-2009-tomato

National Agriculture and Food Research Organization (農研機構), "今が旬の"トマト"のお話" (On the tomato, now in season), 23 May 2009 (in Japanese; https://www.naro.go.jp/publicity_report/season/006534.html)

What it is: A seasonal explainer on the tomato from Japan's national agricultural research agency, covering its origin, history in Japan and varieties.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the agency's account of why early varieties failed in Japan and which variety changed that. It gives no year for the Ponderosa's arrival beyond the Showa period and no source for its history.

Used for: The tomato in Japan (Money and prices)
