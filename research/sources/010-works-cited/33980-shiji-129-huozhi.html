shiji-129-huozhi

Sima Qian (司馬遷), Shiji (史記, Records of the Grand Historian), chapter 129, "The money-makers" (貨殖列傳), the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/史記/卷129)

What it is: The chapter of the Han court historian's history, finished about 90 BCE, on the merchants and producers who grew rich, from the Zhou kingdoms to the Han, with Sima Qian's own account of trade and the proverbs he quotes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries the proverb that firewood is not traded beyond 100 li nor grain beyond 1,000, the oldest statement read here of a distance past which carriage priced a good out. It is a proverb quoted in a work of the Han, as advice on where to put one's wealth, not a measurement, and it carries no date of its own.

Used for: the Han proverb on how far firewood and grain were worth carrying (Money and prices)
