kotobank-momen

Kotobank, "木綿" ("Cotton", title translated), with the history section signed by Sasaki Ginya in the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, the Nipponica article and the Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten's dated examples, in Japanese (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9C%A8%E7%B6%BF-142758)

What it is: A Japanese dictionary portal's page gathering several reference works' entries on cotton, among them a medieval historian's short history of it in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates cotton's earliest uses and first domestic traces, including the Echigo mention of about 1494, and its dictionary examples quote period texts. The Echigo line says only that seed cotton (mi-wata) appeared in the Uesugi domain: it names no document, does not say the cotton was grown there and does not call it the earliest, and of what the record takes from the history only the seed story names a source (the Nihon koki). The dictionary examples are one line each with no context, some of them for the same characters read as bark-fibre yū or kapok rather than cotton, and the entries differ in precision, one placing the seed's landing in the Enryaku years and another in 799.

Used for: the seed of 799, the import trade, who first wore cotton, and the first domestic cotton (Money and prices)
