Kano Yoshiaki (鹿野嘉昭), 「銭匁勘定と銭遣い―江戸期幣制の特色を再検討する―」 ("Copper monme accounting and the use of copper: the Edo monetary system reconsidered", title translated), 同志社商学 61(1), 2009 (in Japanese; https://doshisha.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/21421/files/034061010002.pdf)

What it is: An economist's study of how domains in western Japan, short of silver, came to price in silver monme and pay in copper, and of the split between shogunal coin for a domain's outside trade and notes and copper inside it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes which money each kind of trade used and how a silver price was paid in copper. It is a synthesis of other studies, Iwahashi's above all, and where it reads a fixed ratio as a fee it disagrees with Iwahashi, who reads it as a premium.

Used for: domains' outside and inside trade, silver prices paid in copper, and the Miyazu ratio (Money and prices)
