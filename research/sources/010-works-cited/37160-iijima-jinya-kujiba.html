iijima-jinya-kujiba

Iijima Jinya Diary (飯島陣屋日記), "飯島陣屋・公事場" ("Iijima Jinya: the court room", title translated), 2024-05-15, a Hatena Blog post by the Iijima Jinya (in Japanese; https://iijimajinya.hatenablog.com/entry/2024/05/15/093510)

What it is: A short post by the museum that runs the rebuilt Iijima Jin'ya on its court room (kujiba), next to the writing room, with the gravel court before it where offenders sat while the officials sat in the board-floored room.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the rebuilt office's writing room beside its court room. The building is a modern rebuild (the post gives no date; a hobbyist's page gives 1993), so the order of its rooms is the rebuild's, and the post gives no sizes.

Used for: the writing room beside the court room in a small office's rebuild (Estates and other compounds)
