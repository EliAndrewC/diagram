daiwadou-kibyoshi

daiwadou, a post on the kibyoshi on note.com (in Japanese; https://note.com/daiwadou/n/n9825ff587b92)

What it is: A blog post introducing the kibyoshi, the picture booklets of the late 1700s, with a short list of references, among them Suzuki Toshiyuki's book on the publisher Tsutaya Juzaburo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a second retail price of a kibyoshi, 8 mon a volume and about 24 for a three-volume work, attributed to Suzuki's book, which was not read. It gives no year, and its figure differs from the magazine's 6 mon.

Used for: a second price of a kibyoshi volume (Money and prices)
