sagara-2009-iwami-tatara

Sagara Eisuke, "江の川沿いの石見たたら" ("The bellows ironworks of Iwami along the Gonokawa river", title translated), pp. 56-58 of a volume in the Shimane University repository, whose file is dated 2009 (in Japanese; https://ir.lib.shimane-u.ac.jp/38042/files/4947)

What it is: A three-page article by a historian of the ironworks of the San'in coast, printed in the March 2009 report of a Shimane University research project on silver and the country around the Iwami silver mine (銀の流通と石見銀山周辺地域に関する歴史学的研究), and written, its closing note says, for a regional encyclopedia of Iwami (『石見ふるさと大百科』). It describes the bellows ironworks of the shogunate's lands in Iwami province (now western Shimane prefecture) from a petition of 1830: how many works there were, what they made, what it was worth and where it was sold; its other two sections are on the leasing and sale of works (contracts of 1821, 1827 and 1836) and on the workers' households in two temple registers of 1863.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the pig iron of Iwami's shogunal lands as a quantity and a value in silver for about 1830, and the share sold to Osaka. The figures are round ones from a petition pleading hardship, which the author takes from another historian's lecture handout of 2008, not from the document; the value is a total, so a price a load is a division, and nothing says where along the road to the buyer the value was reckoned. It gives no price in Osaka, and it counts the iron in horse-loads (駄) without saying what a load weighed, so a price by weight needs a load's weight from another source.

Used for: the value of Iwami's pig iron about 1830 and where it was sold (Money and prices)
