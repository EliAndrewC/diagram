sagara-2009-iwami-tatara

Sagara Eisuke, "江の川沿いの石見たたら" ("The bellows ironworks of Iwami along the Gonokawa river", title translated), pp. 56-58 of a volume in the Shimane University repository, whose file is dated 2009 (in Japanese; https://ir.lib.shimane-u.ac.jp/38042/files/4947)

What it is: A three-page article by a historian of the ironworks of the San'in coast, written, its closing note says, for a regional encyclopedia of Iwami. It describes the bellows ironworks of the shogunate's lands in Iwami province (now western Shimane prefecture) from a petition of 1830: how many works there were, what they made, what it was worth and where it was sold.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the pig iron of Iwami's shogunal lands as a quantity and a value in silver for about 1830, and the share sold to Osaka. The figures are round ones from a petition pleading hardship, which the author takes from another historian's lecture handout of 2008, not from the document; the value is a total, so a price a load is a division, and nothing says where along the road to the buyer the value was reckoned. It gives no price in Osaka.

Used for: the value of Iwami's pig iron about 1830 and where it was sold (Money and prices)
