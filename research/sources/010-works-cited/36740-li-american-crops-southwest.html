Li Xinsheng (李昕升), "救荒活民：清代民国美洲作物与西南民族地区的民族交融" (American crops and famine relief among the peoples of the southwest in the Qing and Republic), at Aisixiang (in Chinese; https://www.aisixiang.com/data/177018.html)

What it is: A historian's article on the American crops as famine food in southwest China, which reports his own earlier national estimates of their share of the fields.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the under-1% figure and the mid-1800s date for the American crops' value. The study behind those figures was not read, the page does not show how they were calculated, and the article's own subject is the southwest, where it finds the crops much more important locally.

Used for: The share of the sweet potato in Qing cultivated area (Money and prices)
