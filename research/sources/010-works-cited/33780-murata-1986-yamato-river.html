murata-1986-yamato-river

村田路人 (Murata Michihito), 「宝永元年大和川付替手伝普請について」 ("On the assisted works of 1704 for re-routing the Yamato River", title translated), 『待兼山論叢』 史学篇 20, 1986, pp. 21-44 (in Japanese; https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/47993/mrh_020_021.pdf)

What it is: A study of the digging of the Yamato River's new channel in Kawachi in 1704, from village and domain records, among them the account a village on the new channel, Ota in Shiki district, kept of the laborers' wages it paid during the works, set out as a table, and the estimates' wage of a laborer's day.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives what a Kawachi village paid laborers by the man in 1704, for sweeping, carrying and guard duty during an official visit, and the estimates' wage per tsubo of earthwork, a river works on the shogunate's order, far bigger than any shrine's ground. It gives no craftsman's wage, and it does not say how big the tsubo of its earthwork was.

Used for: laborers' day wages for sweeping and earthwork in Kawachi in 1704 (Money and prices)
