kawaguchi-miso-recipe

Kawaguchi City, "手作りみその作り方" (How to make homemade miso, title translated), one page, undated (in Japanese; https://www.city.kawaguchi.lg.jp/material/files/group/183/misorecipe.pdf)

What it is: A one-page recipe for making koji miso at home (the sheet does not name the koji's grain), on Kawaguchi City's site: about 1 kg of soybeans, 1 kg of koji and 500 g of salt, soaked, boiled, mashed, packed and left about a year, which it says makes about 3 kg of miso.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a present-day recipe that states both its ingredients by weight and what they make, the beans dry. Its "about" figures are a cook's round numbers, not a measured yield. The 3 kg is given only to size the container, and it is barely above the 2.5 kg its ingredients weigh dry, before the beans take up water in soaking and boiling and before the cooking liquid is added to bring the mash to "earlobe" softness. Neither of those is weighed, so the yield is probably understated. The koji is weighed as finished koji, not as the grain it was made from. The beans are cooked in a pressure cooker and mashed in a blender, modern tools that change the labor but not, so far as the sheet shows, the weights.

Used for: a present-day yield of miso from a kilogram of dry soybeans, with its proportions (Money and prices)
