jawiki-tabako

Japanese Wikipedia, "タバコ" (Tobacco; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/タバコ)

What it is: The Japanese encyclopedia article on the tobacco plant, with a short account of its arrival in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the years 1601 and 1605 and the places, and it is the only page read that gives the 1601 seed at Hirado. The sources its footnotes name for those years were not read, and the Hirado date is marked on the ground by a monument, whose own date the page does not give.

Used for: Tobacco in Japan (Money and prices)
