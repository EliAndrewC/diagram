jawiki-tabako

Japanese Wikipedia, "タバコ" (Tobacco; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/タバコ)

What it is: The Japanese encyclopedia article on the tobacco plant, with a short account of its arrival in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the years 1601 and 1605 and the places, and it is the only page read that gives the 1601 seed at Hirado. The sources its footnotes name for those years were not read: for 1601 the textbook of a local-knowledge exam from a Hirado tourism guides' association, and for 1605 a travel feature on the tobacco company JT's website about a memorial stone, so the page shows neither year resting on a period record or a historian. The Hirado date is marked on the ground by a monument, whose own date the page does not give. The page's "first" is not settled: a health-ministry page, which cites nothing either, puts tobacco's arrival in the sixteenth century, with rival accounts at Nagasaki and Kagoshima.

Used for: Tobacco in Japan (Money and prices)
