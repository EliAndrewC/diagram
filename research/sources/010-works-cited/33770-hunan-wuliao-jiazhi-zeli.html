《欽定物料價值則例》 (Regulations and precedents on prices of materials, by imperial order), Hunan provincial edition, 卷一 (chapter 1, Changsha prefecture), woodblock print of the Qianlong reign, scanned (SSID-11354707) and placed on Wikimedia Commons as a public-domain scan (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SSID-11354707_欽定物料價值則例_第1卷.pdf; the PDF, https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2f/SSID-11354707_%E6%AC%BD%E5%AE%9A%E7%89%A9%E6%96%99%E5%83%B9%E5%80%BC%E5%89%87%E4%BE%8B_%E7%AC%AC1%E5%8D%B7.pdf)

What it is: The printed original of Hunan's edition of the Qing regulation on what the province's public building could claim back from the Ministry of Works: its memorials, one of them presented in the 12th month of Qianlong 33 (early 1769), then, county by county, the prices of materials, the day wages of craftsmen and laborers, and the rates for hauling materials.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its transport rows state their unit inside each row (per li for a porter, per 10 li for a boat), which a modern transcription of another province's edition flattens into one column. Only chapter 1 was read, and only the joint section of Changsha and Shanhua counties; its notes say the figures are the rules already in force checked against the counties' own registers, so they are reimbursement rates for official building, not fares or wages anyone was seen to pay.

Used for: the official porter's and boat rates, and the craftsman's and laborer's day wages, of Changsha in Hunan (Money and prices)
