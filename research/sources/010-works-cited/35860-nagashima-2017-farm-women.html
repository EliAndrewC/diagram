nagashima-2017-farm-women

Nagashima Junko, 「江戸時代における農村女性の労働・生活とジェンダー」 ("Labor, life and gender of rural women in the Edo period", title translated), 『農業および園芸』 (Agriculture and Horticulture) vol. 92, no. 7 (the page header prints no. 6), 2017, pp. 572-577, on the AgriKnowledge repository (in Japanese; https://agriknowledge.affrc.go.jp/RN/2010912071.pdf)

What it is: A short survey by a historian of women in Tokugawa villages, drawing on farm books and account books: the Seiryoki's passage on the peasant wife, the Imai household's accounts of 1821 in Shinano, the Muramatsu house rules of Noto (late 1700s to early 1800s), and a picture scroll of 1717 from Kaga.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one source read that names which farm tasks fell to women and how their work was valued against men's, with a household's women-only tasks and its wages by sex. Its examples are wealthy households with servants, not small farms, and it counts no days or hours; its figure that ramie took ten times the labor of cotton is reported as "said to" (といわれている), an estimate it does not source on the page.

Used for: a farm wife's round, the division of farm tasks by sex, women's wages and work on rest days in a wealthy household, and ramie's labor against cotton's (Money and prices)
