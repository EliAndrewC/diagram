In-Sung Kim Han, "The Agency of Hoseung (Foreign Monk) in Cotton Dissemination in the Goryeo-Joseon Transition Period: A Korean Case of Medieval Globalism", The Review of Korean Studies 24, no. 2 (December 2021): 173-204 (https://www.accesson.kr/rks/assets/pdf/10087/journal-24-2-173.pdf)

What it is: A historian's article on how cotton growing and cotton processing reached Korea in the later 14th century, working from the Korean annals.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one work read that addresses which species Mun Ik-jeom brought. Its body text and its note 18 do not say the same thing: the body calls the variety a hybrid of G. herbaceum and G. arboreum, and the note says it was not G. herbaceum; it spells the second "arboretum" throughout. Its botany is taken from Hutchinson and from other secondary works, which were not read here.

Used for: The species of Korean cotton (Money and prices)
