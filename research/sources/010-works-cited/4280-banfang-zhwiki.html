zh.wikipedia 班房 ("duty lockup", title translated) (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/班房)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia disambiguation page for 班房 (banfang), which lists the word's two senses: the yamen's lockup, in a short paragraph (first the duty room of its three squads of runners, then where defendants in civil and petty cases were held before trial, as distinct from the prison for the convicted), and the Hong Kong word for a classroom.

Why it applies, and its limits: It attests the runners' station inside the yamen and its use as a holding room, which bears on housing the runners within the yamen; it says nothing of the room's form, of where in the compound it stood, or of where the runners lived; its paragraph on the yamen sense dates it only to "ancient times" and cites nothing.

Used for: the runners' duty room (The government quarter)
