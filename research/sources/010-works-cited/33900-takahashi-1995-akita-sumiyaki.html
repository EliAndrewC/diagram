高橋正 (Takahashi Tadashi), 「秋田県の炭焼き」 ("Charcoal burning in Akita prefecture", title translated), 秋田県立博物館研究報告 (Bulletin of the Akita Prefectural Museum) no. 20, March 1995, pp. 11-18 (in Japanese; the Internet Archive's copy, http://web.archive.org/web/20260210160553/https://www.akihaku.jp/cms/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/aktpmrep20_011-018.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural museum curator's history of charcoal making in Akita, opening with an extract from the Akita house's account of the costs of repairing Minato castle in 1600, which prices 120 bales of smith's charcoal.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one bale price found from the years around the Edo period's opening, and it quotes the account line itself. It is a price of 1600, before the Tokugawa coinage, for charcoal bought for building works at a castle, and neither the line nor the article gives the bale's weight or any wage by the day.

Used for: the price of smith's charcoal by the bale in Akita in 1600 (Money and prices)
