Gu Kuang, "Passing a Mountain Farmer's House" (過山農家), with a modern translation and commentary, at gushiwen (in Chinese; https://www.gushiwen.cn/gushiwen_0a220b88cc.aspx)

What it is: A six-character quatrain by the Tang poet Gu Kuang (8th century), as given by a classical-poetry website with a modern Chinese translation and an appreciation of each line.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its first line has a traveler crossing a plank bridge (板桥) over a spring-fed stream, and the commentary places the bridge close by the farm household (近旁), on the traveler's way to its gate, so it attests a plank bridge on the path to a farmhouse in Tang China. Its limits: it is a poem, one image and no description; the stream is a mountain spring, not a paddy ditch; the page gives no date for the poem; and the translation and commentary are the website's, modern and unsigned.

Used for: a plank bridge beside a farmhouse in Tang China (Ways)
