nippon-com-ooku-kyuryo

Nippon.com, 「大奥のトップ女中は年収1300万円！？」 ("The top women of the inner palace earned 13 million yen a year?", title translated), 30 October 2022, in its series on the shogun's women's quarters (in Japanese; https://www.nippon.com/ja/japan-topics/c10811/)

What it is: A popular history article on what the women serving in the shogun's women's quarters at Edo castle were paid, with a table of rice stipend, rations and cash allowance for each rank under the thirteenth shogun, Iesada (1853-1858), and what came in kind.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its table, which it says is made from Matsuo Mieko's study of the shogunate's registers of its women servants, is the only ladder found of a great house's maids' pay by rank. It is the shogun's own household, the richest in the realm, not a lord's or a retainer's; the article converts the pay to yen by rough rates of its own, which the record does not use.

Used for: the stipends, rations and allowances of the shogun's women servants by rank, 1853-1858 (Money and prices)
