Atlas Obscura, "In India, the British Hyped Potatoes to Justify Colonialism", 9 April 2019 (https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/potato-history-india)

What it is: A popular magazine article on the East India Company's promotion of the potato, drawing on Company ledgers, letters and food historians.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Company's campaigns from 1797 and the 1814 letter on who ate it. It reports the records without quoting most of them, and its evidence is largely what British officials wrote about their own program.

Used for: The white potato in India: the Company's promotion, who grew it and who ate it (Money and prices)
