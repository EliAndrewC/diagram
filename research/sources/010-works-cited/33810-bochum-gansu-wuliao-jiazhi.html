bochum-gansu-wuliao-jiazhi

Ruhr-Universitat Bochum, Section for the History of China, "Introduction to the Databases of Prices, Wages and Transport Costs in Gansu Province in the Qianlong Reign", and its transport-cost and wage spreadsheets (in English and Chinese; https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/gc/forschung/gansu_province.html; the spreadsheets, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/gansu/database_of_transport_cost.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/gansu/database_of_wages.xls)

What it is: A database, entered row by row by a German and Chinese project, of the Gansu edition of the Qing Regulations and precedents on prices of materials, a manuscript of eight chapters held by the Library of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, whose introductory memorial is dated 27 January 1769: for twelve prefectures and independent departments, the prices of building materials, the day wages of craftsmen and laborers, and the rates for hauling materials.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its transport rows price stone and timber by the cart, and timber by porter and pack donkey, beside each district's craftsman and laborer wage, with remarks saying how each rate was checked against the older rule and the district's register. They are the rates official building could claim back, not hires anyone was seen to pay; the remarks say again and again that the rule sets no separate freight for stone, brick or lime, so most districts price no stone at all.

Used for: the official rates for stone by cart and timber by porter in Gansu in 1769, and the day wages beside them (Money and prices)
