kobe-nada-shuzogyo

Kobe City Archives (神戸市文書館), "灘の酒造業" ("The sake-brewing industry of Nada", title translated), a special feature on the Kobe city site (in Japanese; https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/documents/60534/tokusyu-nadanosyuzogyo.pdf)

What it is: The city archives' history of sake brewing on the Nada coast from the Edo period on, with tables drawn from brewers' and shippers' papers: shipments, settlements with the Edo wholesalers and the licenses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Edo price of Nada sake by the ten horse-loads in the early 1800s and a brewery's settlements with an Edo wholesaler in 1858, and says Nada's new licenses of 1832 were for Edo only. It gives no price at the brewery, and its column heading in the 1858 table reads otherwise than its own sums, so its unit is a reading.

Used for: the Edo price of Nada sake (Money and prices)
