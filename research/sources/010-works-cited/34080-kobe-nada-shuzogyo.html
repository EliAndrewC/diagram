kobe-nada-shuzogyo

Kobe City Archives (神戸市文書館), "灘の酒造業" ("The sake-brewing industry of Nada", title translated), a special feature on the Kobe city site (in Japanese; https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/documents/60534/tokusyu-nadanosyuzogyo.pdf)

What it is: The city archives' illustrated history of sake brewing on the Nada coast from the Edo period to the early Meiji years, written for it by Ishikawa Michiko (undated). It draws on the archives' Shibata family papers (a brewery's shipping book of 1850 and its Edo settlements of 1858) and on published compilations and city histories (the Nada sake histories of 1907 and 1950-51, Kobe's city history of 1992), and covers shipments, settlements with the Edo wholesalers and the licenses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Edo price of Nada sake by the ten horse-loads in the early 1800s and a brewery's settlements with an Edo wholesaler in 1858, and says Nada's new licenses of 1832 were for Edo only. Both are wholesale prices in Edo, what the wholesalers got when they sold the sake on to the brokers, not what a drinker paid. The 1858 sums are before the wholesaler's commission and include spoiled lots sold cheap, and the early-1800s price is a round "usual" range given in passing, with no table or citation behind it. It gives no price at the brewery, and its column heading in the 1858 table reads otherwise than its own sums, so its unit is a reading.

Used for: the Edo price of Nada sake (Money and prices)
