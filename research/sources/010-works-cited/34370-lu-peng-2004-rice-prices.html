卢锋 (Lu Feng) and 彭凯翔 (Peng Kaixiang), 《我国长期米价研究（1644-2000）》 ("A study of China's long-run rice prices, 1644-2000", title translated), China Center for Economic Research, Peking University, Working Paper C2004009, July 2004 (in Chinese; https://nsd.pku.edu.cn/attachments/e0f218104a1f41bba495f50835b5d4af.pdf)

What it is: Two economists' survey of Chinese rice prices from 1644 to 2000, assembled from the economic historians' regional series, with trends and correlations of five-year average prices for Suzhou, Guangdong, Hubei, Hunan and Haizhou in the 18th century, and the copper cash to silver rate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the 18th-century trend of rice prices in silver and the long movement of the cash-to-silver rate. Its regional figures are five-year averages, so they hide year-to-year swings, and it builds on others' series rather than new archival work.

Used for: the long trend of Qing rice prices in silver, and the cash-to-silver rate (Money and prices)
