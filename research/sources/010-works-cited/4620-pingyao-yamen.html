pingyao-yamen

"平遥县衙" ("The Pingyao county yamen", title translated), an encyclopedia entry on Baidu Baike (in Chinese; https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%B9%B3%E9%81%A5%E5%8E%BF%E8%A1%99/2409876)

What it is: A collaboratively edited encyclopedia entry on the county government compound at Pingyao, Shanxi, now a museum: its history, the magistrate Yang Tingmo's rebuilding of 1619, which fixed the plan the yamen kept under the Ming and Qing, and a walk through its gates, halls, offices and prisons.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists, building by building, what the rebuilding of 1619 put where - the great hall's end bays as the money-and-grain store and the armory, and the county granary north of the official quarters behind the war, justice and works offices - and names the drum under the main gate. Its limits are that its editors are anonymous and it names no source for the 1619 list, and that the compound is a restored museum, so the list is the entry's account of the rebuilding, not a plan of it.

Used for: the money-and-grain store and armory in the great hall's end bays, and the granary north of the official quarters, in 1619 (Towns)
