『実行容易無資本小資本実業成功法』 (How to succeed in business with no capital or little: easily carried out, title translated), Tokyo, 1915, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/920967/1/36 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/920967)

What it is: A popular handbook of ways to earn a living in Tokyo with little or no capital, trade by trade. Its entry on the empty-bale dealer says how many empty rice bales the city turned out each day, that wholesalers sent buyers round to collect them, how many a buyer could buy in a day and what a buyer earned.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows empty rice bales as a trade of their own in a great city, with wholesalers and walking buyers. It is of about 1915, of rice bales and not charcoal bales, and gives no price for a bale; its figures (ten thousand bales a day, 200 to a buyer, a quarter to three-tenths profit) are the round numbers of a book selling the trade to its reader, and it names no dealer.

Used for: Tokyo's trade in empty rice bales about 1915 (Money and prices)
