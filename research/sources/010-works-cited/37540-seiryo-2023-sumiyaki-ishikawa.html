seiryo-2023-sumiyaki-ishikawa

金沢星稜大学 (Kanazawa Seiryo University), 「炭焼きという労働」 ("The work of charcoal burning", title translated), a column on the university's site, July 2023 (in Japanese; https://www.seiryo-u.ac.jp/u/new/2023/0701.html)

What it is: A short column on charcoal burning as work, written from a film of a village market of 1962 and from the History of forestry in Ishikawa prefecture (1997), which it cites by page for how a charcoal burner's income was divided: wood, packing and sundries, and what was left for the burner's work.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one statement found of packing as a share of a burner's income. It reports the prefectural history at second hand and gives no year for the division; packing and sundries are one figure of 10 to 20 percent, not separated; and the share is of income, not of a bale's price.

Used for: packing and sundries as a share of a charcoal burner's income, at second hand and undated (Money and prices)
