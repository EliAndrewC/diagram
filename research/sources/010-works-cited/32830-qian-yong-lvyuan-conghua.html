錢泳 (Qian Yong), 履園叢話 ("Collected talk from the Lü garden", title translated), chapter 1, 田價 ("Land prices"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/履園叢話/01)

What it is: A Jiangnan scholar's miscellany of the early 1800s, whose short entry on land prices runs from the middle of the Ming to the author's own day, fifty years after his childhood, in taels a mu.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a single observer's series of what good land fetched over about three centuries in the Lower Yangzi, the richest farmland of the Qing. It gives prices only, with no rent or yield beside them, in round ranges, and for its earlier reigns it reports what was said rather than what the author saw.

Used for: how far a Jiangnan land price moved between the late Ming and the early 1800s (Money and prices)
