marukome-tezukuri-miso

Marukome Co., Ltd., "味噌を手作りしてみよう！" ("Let's make miso by hand!", title translated), on the company's site (in Japanese; https://www.marukome.co.jp/miso/handmade/)

What it is: A miso maker's page of two home recipes for rice-koji miso, a sweet one and a salty one, each with its weights of koji, soybeans, salt and bean broth, which it says make about 1.3 kg of miso, with the beans soaked overnight and boiled about six hours.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one miso maker read that states what a recipe makes. It gives the same "about 1.3 kg" for both recipes although they hold different weights of beans and salt, so the figure is rough, and it does not say whether its bean weights (250 g and 430 g) are dry or soaked - the record takes them as dry because the recipe washes the beans and soaks them overnight after weighing them; it names the Miso Health Promotion Committee (みそ健康づくり委員会) and the National Federation of Miso Manufacturers' Cooperatives (全国味噌工業協同組合連合会) among its references.

Used for: a present-day yield of salty and sweet miso from a kilogram of dry soybeans, with its proportions (Money and prices)
