quan-1957-silver-price-revolution

全漢昇 (Chuan Han-sheng), 〈美洲白銀與十八世紀中國物價革命的關係〉 ("American silver and the eighteenth-century price revolution in China", title translated), 《中央研究院歷史語言研究所集刊》 (Bulletin of the Institute of History and Philology, Academia Sinica) 28, part 2 (1957), pp. 517-550 (in Chinese; https://www1.ihp.sinica.edu.tw/storage/w2_file/4232vsthcJJ.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's classic article arguing that the inflow of American silver, with population growth, drove a price revolution in 18th-century China, from rice prices in local records and officials' writings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of the 18th-century rise in rice prices. Its figure is a summary of many local series, several of them in copper cash, so it mixes the rise in silver with the fall of cash against silver, and it predates the grain price databases later scholars used.

Used for: the 18th-century rise in Chinese rice prices (Money and prices)
