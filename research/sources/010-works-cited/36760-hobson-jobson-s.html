Henry Yule and A. C. Burnell, Hobson-Jobson, the letter S, the Wikisource transcription of the enlarged edition with William Crooke's additions (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Hobson-Jobson/S)

What it is: The standard glossary of Anglo-Indian words, here its entry on the sweet potato.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where the plant was grown when the glossary was written, what it was called, and how its names overlapped with the potato and the yam. It gives no date for its arrival or for its becoming common.

Used for: The sweet potato in India (Money and prices)
