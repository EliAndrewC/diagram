Baba Norie, "アイロンに漂白、オーガニック石鹸。江戸の洗濯事情を洗いざらいご紹介" ("Irons, bleach and organic soap: all about washing clothes in Edo", title translated), Waraku web, Shogakukan, 2 February 2026 (in Japanese; https://intojapanwaraku.com/culture/294401/)

What it is: A magazine article on how clothes were washed in the Edo period, the lye and other cleaners used, and the first washing shops, drawn from Ochiai Shigeru's history of washing (1984) and Kaibara Ekiken's moral writings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the washing trade to the first years of the Genroku era (about 1688) and says who worked in it in Edo and Kyoto. It gives no price, and does not say whose clothes the shops washed.

Used for: washing shops in Edo and Kyoto from about 1688 (Money and prices)
