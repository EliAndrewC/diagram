matsue-monozukuri-cotton

Koyama Shoko, "木綿" ("Cotton", title translated), pp. 21-22 of a City of Matsue local-history booklet on the crafts of the Matsue domain, in Japanese (https://www.city.matsue.lg.jp/material/files/group/32/history_mono_6.pdf)

What it is: Two pages by the city's historical staff on the cotton cloth of Izumo province, with a short national background, resting on the city's published history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one source read that places cotton as commoners' main clothing in the later Edo period, and the one that reports household weaving in Izumo by the late 1400s. It says so without a citation for either sentence, and the Izumo date comes from a work written about two centuries later, in 1682.

Used for: when cotton arrived, when it became commoners' main clothing, and household weaving in Izumo (Money and prices)
