鷲崎俊太郎 (Washizaki Shuntarō), 「江戸の土地市場と不動産投資―収益還元法による地代・地価分析―」 ("The land market of Edo and investment in real estate: an analysis of rents and land prices by the income-capitalization method", title translated), 社会経済史学 (Socio-Economic History) 73, no. 2, July 2007 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/sehs/73/2/73_KJ00008198243/_pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's study of what town lots in Edo sold for against the ground rents they earned, from the 1710s to the 1840s, treating a lot's price as its net rent capitalized at the rate of interest.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a price in years of net income attested from the time, the Edo town office's valuation of a town lot, and it names the studies that found farmland priced the same way. Its own figures are for town lots in the shogun's capital, not for farmland; its passage on farmland reports others' findings without a rate; and its text layer is machine-read, with some characters misread.

Used for: a town lot's price as twenty years of its owner's net income, and farmland priced as its capitalized net yield (Money and prices)
