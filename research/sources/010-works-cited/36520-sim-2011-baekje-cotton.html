sim-2011-baekje-cotton

Sim Yeon Ok, Chung Yong Jae, Yu Ji A and Namgung Seung, "Study on the Baekje's Cotton Fabrics Excavated in Neungsan-ri Temple Site" (Korean), Munhwajae: Korean Journal of Cultural Heritage Studies 44, no. 3 (2011): 4-17 (https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO201165251815602.pdf)

What it is: A laboratory and documentary study of a piece of cotton cloth excavated in 1999-2000 at a Baekje temple site in Buyeo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one study read that identifies an early cotton cloth from Korea by its fibers. It rests on one small find surviving as two remnants, only one of them fiber-tested, dated by what was found at the site and by the temple's founding, and it says it cannot yet tell whether the cloth was woven in Baekje, while judging that it could have been. It also leaves the cotton's species and its route to Korea unresolved and reports no seed or field evidence, so it does not show the plant was grown there; its statements that the early cotton was grown in Korea without spreading and woven in small amounts for gifts are the authors' inference from written records. It gives no price, and the find is a coarse crepe weave the authors call the first reported in cotton, so it may not be typical cloth.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
