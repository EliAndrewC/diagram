sim-2011-baekje-cotton

Sim Yeon Ok, Chung Yong Jae, Yu Ji A and Namgung Seung, "Study on the Baekje's Cotton Fabrics Excavated in Neungsan-ri Temple Site" (Korean), Korean Journal of Heritage: History and Science 44, no. 3 (2011): 4-17 (https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO201165251815602.pdf)

What it is: A laboratory and documentary study of a piece of cotton cloth excavated in 1999-2000 at a Baekje temple site in Buyeo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one study read that identifies an early cotton cloth from Korea by its fibers. It rests on a single small piece, dated by what was found with it and by the temple's founding, and it says it cannot yet tell where the piece was woven.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
