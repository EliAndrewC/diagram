saketimes-edo-sake-prices

Yanai Hiroshi, "9年熟成で17,000円！江戸時代のチラシにみる日本酒の価格" ("17,000 yen for nine years' aging: sake prices in Edo-period flyers", title translated), part 9 of the series "熟成古酒の失われた100年", SAKETIMES, 3 August 2018 (in Japanese; https://jp.sake-times.com/knowledge/culture/sake_g_lost-100years_09)

What it is: An article in a sake trade magazine's online series on aged sake, quoting the prices of sake by brand from two Edo guides of the Bunsei era (1818-1829): the shopping guide Edo kaimono hitori annai, a sho at a time, and the food guide Edo machinaka kuimono chohoki.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page found that quotes the Edo shopping guide's sake prices by the sho. Its author writes about aged sake, so it picks out the dearest and cheapest lines rather than giving the list whole; it does not say for what measure the food guide's prices are given; and its yen equivalents are its own.

Used for: the price of named sake brands a sho at an Edo shop in the Bunsei era (Money and prices)
