saketimes-edo-sake-prices

Yanai Hiroshi, "9年熟成で17,000円！江戸時代のチラシにみる日本酒の価格" ("17,000 yen for nine years' aging: sake prices in Edo-period flyers", title translated), part 9 of the series "熟成古酒の失われた100年", SAKETIMES, 3 August 2018 (in Japanese; https://jp.sake-times.com/knowledge/culture/sake_g_lost-100years_09)

What it is: An article in an online series on aged sake in SAKETIMES, a sake web magazine for drinkers, quoting the prices of sake by brand from two Edo guides: the shopping guide Edo kaimono hitori annai (1824, in the Bunsei era), a sho at a time, and the food guide Edo machinaka kuimono chohoki, which the article places in the same period without giving its date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page found that quotes the Edo shopping guide's sake prices by the sho. Its author writes about aged sake, so it picks out the dearest and cheapest lines rather than giving the list whole; it does not say for what measure the food guide's prices are given; its yen equivalents are its own, and it converts between the silver (monme) and copper (mon) prices at the shogunate's nominal rate of 60 monme or 4,000 mon to the ryo, not the market rate of the 1820s. The 1824 shopping guide itself is in the National Diet Library's digital collection, in woodblock script; the record takes its figures from this article and did not read the guide directly.

Used for: the price of named sake brands a sho at an Edo shop in the Bunsei era (Money and prices)
