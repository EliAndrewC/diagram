喜田川季荘 (Kitagawa Morisada), 『類聚近世風俗志 : 原名守貞漫稿』 (Customs of recent times, by topic: the Morisada manko, title translated), 上 (volume 1), 國學院大學出版部, 1908, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/991466/1/88; the polishers' entry at https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/991466/1/94)

What it is: The 1908 printed edition, arranged by topic, of the notebook an Osaka-born townsman kept on the customs of Edo, Kyoto and Osaka from 1837 to 1853, with additions until about 1867. This first volume holds his chapters on the trades and street sellers, with the day's hire of the carpenter and the building helper in Osaka and in Edo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is one observer's account of the wages and trades he saw in the three cities, set down from 1837 and added to until about 1867 without saying which year a figure is of, so a wage may be from the 1860s, when prices and wages rose steeply; he lived in Osaka until 1840 and in Edo after, so his figures for the two cities are likely of different years; its wages are what he gives as usual, not a ledger's payments. A printed transcription of a manuscript, it may differ from his hand in places.

Used for: what a carpenter and a laborer were paid in Edo and Osaka, and the street sharpener's trade (Money and prices)
