What it is: The national retailer's online listing of bags of dried soybeans, each with the price it charged that day.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices the soybeans of the basket as a US shopper buys them dry. No own-brand pack is listed, and the listed bags are small specialty packs sold for soy milk and tofu, so their price per kilogram is several times that of a staple like dried beans; each is the price for buying online on the day it was seen, and the page keeps no history.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
