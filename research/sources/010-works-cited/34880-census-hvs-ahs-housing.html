US Census Bureau, Quarterly Residential Vacancies and Homeownership, Second Quarter 2026, press release, PDF (https://www.census.gov/housing/hvs/files/qtr226/Q226press.pdf); and the American Housing Survey 2023, table specifications and estimates workbook, Table C-02-AO, sheet "Rooms, Size, Amenities", XLSX (https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/ahs/2023/AHS%202023%20Table%20Specifications%20and%20PUF%20Estimates%20for%20User%20Verification.xlsx)

What it is: The Census Bureau's quarterly survey of vacant homes, which gives the median rent asked for vacant rental units nationwide; and its biennial survey of occupied homes, whose workbook gives the median and mean floor area of a unit and per person.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: what a US home rents for and how big it is, as a yardstick for a premodern laborer's room, never a figure of the setting. The asking rent is for units vacant and on offer, not what sitting tenants pay, and comes with no floor area; the floor areas are the occupants' own estimates of their homes' size, which run smaller than measured areas, for all occupied units, owners' and renters' together, and give no rent.

Used for: housing per square meter a year in the US (Comparative money: other societies)
