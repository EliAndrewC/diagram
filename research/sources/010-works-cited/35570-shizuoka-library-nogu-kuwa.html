Shizuoka Prefectural Library, 「農業ジャーナリスト大蔵永常と遠州 ～『農具便利論』と『広益国産考』～」 ("The agricultural journalist Okura Nagatsune and Enshu", title translated), a leaflet (in Japanese; https://www.tosyokan.pref.shizuoka.jp/data/open/cnt/3/50/1/ssr3-32.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural library's leaflet on Okura Nagatsune's books on farm tools and local products, picturing two Edo-period hoes with their weights.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the weights of a flat hoe and a three-pronged Bitchu hoe, but not where the weights come from, nor how much of each is iron.

Used for: the weights of two hoes (Money and prices)
