miyamoto-1975-osaka-rice-prices

宮本又郎 (Miyamoto Matao), 「近世後期大阪における米価変動と米穀取引機構」 ("Rice price movements and the rice trading system in late-Edo Osaka", title translated), 『経済研究』 (The Economic Review) 26:4 (October 1975), pp. 359-368 (in Japanese; https://econ-review.ier.hit-u.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/files/1975/keizaikenkyu2604359.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's study of the Osaka rice market from 1756 to 1864, comparing the spot price of rice with the price on the Dojima exchange's book-entry futures market, with an appendix table giving each year's coefficient of variation of both prices.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives, year by year, how much Osaka's rice price moved within the year, from quiet years to famines. Its own note warns that the brand of rice quoted changes from year to year, so its figures compare less well between years than within one; its coefficients of variation end in 1859, and several years, the 1830s among them, are blank. Its prices are of rice bills traded at Dojima, the most closely linked rice market in Japan, where short swings were damped, so a year's movement there is a floor for a provincial market, not a measure of one.

Used for: how far Osaka's rice price moved within a year (Money and prices)
