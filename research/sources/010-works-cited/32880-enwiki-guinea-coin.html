English Wikipedia, "Guinea (coin)" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea_(coin))

What it is: The encyclopedia's article on the English gold coin called the guinea: its history from 1663, its value fixed at twenty-one shillings from 1717, its replacement by the sovereign in 1816, and its use afterward as a unit of account.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the value of the guinea in which The Complete Servant quotes most wages, a book of 1825 written after the coin had ceased to circulate. It says nothing of wages or prices themselves.

Used for: a guinea as twenty-one shillings (Comparative money: other societies)
