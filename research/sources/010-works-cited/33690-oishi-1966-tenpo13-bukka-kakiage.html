大石慎三郎 (Oishi Shinzaburo), 「〈資料紹介〉天保13年8月"銭相場公定に伴う物価引下げ令"による「物価書上」について」 ("Source introduction: the price returns made under the price-reduction order that came with the fixing of the copper rate, Tenpo 13 [1842], 8th month", title translated), 学習院大学経済論集 2-2 (1966), pp. 69-128 (in Japanese; https://glim-re.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/202/files/keizai_2_2_69_128.pdf)

What it is: A historian's edition of the Edo price returns of 1842, opening with a table of the market rate of copper coin in mon to the gold ryo, two figures a year from 1751 to the fifth month of 1842, with their months from 1781, which he takes from Mitsui Takasumi's 1933 table of the gold, silver and copper rates of the three cities "centered on Edo".

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one public printed series of the copper rate in Edo's own form of quotation found for these years. It is a copy of Mitsui's table, not of the money-changers' books, with ranges averaged and fractions dropped by its editor; that its rates are Edo's is this record's reading of the table's title and form, since the paper does not say so in those words; and it stops in 1842, when the official rate replaced the market.

Used for: what copper coin fetched in gold in Edo, 1781-1842 (Money and prices)
