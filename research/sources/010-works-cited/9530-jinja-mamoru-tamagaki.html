jinja-mamoru-tamagaki

note.com, 神社を守るということ (a series on shrine upkeep) (https://note.com/jinja_mamoru/n/nf6c05b296cea)

What it is: A single post (16 August 2026) in the note.com blog 神社経営研究 〜神社を守るということ〜, by Ebisu Takeshi, a small-business consultant who advises shrines. It is about the stone tamagaki as a roll of donors' names, and says in passing that the older fences were wooden rails or hedges and stone ones spread later.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the order wood-then-stone. Its limits: the order is one passing sentence by a consultant, not a historian, with no dates or sources for the change, so it gives the order and no chronology.

Used for: wood and hedge fences before stone (Religion and the dead).
