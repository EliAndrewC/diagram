mlit-kaga-ashigaru

The Japan Tourism Agency's multilingual commentary database, "Ashigaru (Foot Soldiers) Residences" (in English; https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/common/001559629.pdf)

What it is: An English visitor text, written for the national tourism agency, on the Edo-period houses of the Kaga domain's foot soldiers (ashigaru) in Kanazawa: their plots, size, plan and roofing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the roof of a foot soldier's house (wood shingles weighted with field stones, as the light frame could not carry heavier clay tiles, which were kept for buildings of higher status) and puts the kitchen among its rooms. It describes one domain's foot soldiers, and by its own account Kaga's detached houses were rare: in most domains foot soldiers lived in rowhouses (nagaya), so the house it describes is Kaga's, not the usual one.

Used for: the foot soldier's house under a shingled roof, its kitchen one of its rooms (Estates and other compounds)
