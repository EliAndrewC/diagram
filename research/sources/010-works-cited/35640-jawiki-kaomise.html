jawiki-kaomise

Japanese Wikipedia, 顔見世 ("Kaomise", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/顔見世)

What it is: The encyclopedia's article on kaomise, the first program of the kabuki year, given in the 11th month by the newly contracted company: its origins in the 1650s-1670s, the ceremonies leading to its opening day, and the form of its plays.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the opening day's drum at four in the morning and the kaomise's usual length of about ten days. Its account of the ceremonies cites a dictionary of yearly observances of 1958; the figures are not dated to a particular year.

Used for: the kaomise program's length and the opening day's first drum (Money and prices)
