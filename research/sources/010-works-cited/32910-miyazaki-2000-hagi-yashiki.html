miyazaki-2000-hagi-yashiki

宮崎勝美 (Miyazaki Katsumi), 「大名屋敷の作事・普請と江戸遺跡」 ("Building and earthworks at daimyo mansions, and Edo's archaeological sites", title translated), the revised summary of a paper to the 13th meeting of the Edo Iseki Kenkyukai, 2000 (in Japanese; https://edo.jpn.org/cyber/2000/miyazaki2000.htm)

What it is: A historian of the University of Tokyo's Historiographical Institute summarizes the Hagi (Choshu) domain's records of rebuilding its three Edo mansions after the great fire of 1772: what the work cost, and the timber, tiles, nails and earth it shipped from the domain or bought in Osaka and Edo, with quantities and prices.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one price found for copper roof tiles, and shows cypress-bark nails shipped for the same work. Its buildings are a great domain's mansions bought for in bulk, not a village shrine. It prices the copper tiles only as one lot: 11,000 tiles of 1 shaku by 1.2 shaku, bought in Osaka in 1772 for "a little under 30 kan" of silver. So a price per tile (a little under 2.7 monme) is our own division of a rounded total, and it is the cost of buying the tiles alone, without their freight to Edo or the roofer's labour. It gives the tiles' size but not how many covered a tsubo. And it is a meeting summary: it quotes several passages of the domain's records, but gives the copper figure only in the author's own words (the full record was printed in a 1999 research-grant report).

Used for: what copper roofing cost, and whether cypress-bark roofs were laid (Money and prices)
