"Prohibitions", Tobacco in History and Culture: An Encyclopedia, on Encyclopedia.com (https://encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/prohibitions)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on attempts to ban tobacco around the world, with a sentence or two on each country.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the end of Japan's bans and the Chinese edicts in one place. It cites no source for those dates at the point it gives them, and its dates for the Chinese edicts differ in one detail from those of Laufer's study.

Used for: Tobacco in China and Japan (Money and prices)
