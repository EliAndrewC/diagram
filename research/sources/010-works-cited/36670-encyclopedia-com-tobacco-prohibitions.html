"Prohibitions", Tobacco in History and Culture: An Encyclopedia, on Encyclopedia.com (https://encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/prohibitions)

What it is: An article in a reference work on tobacco (ed. Jordan Goodman, Thomson Gale, 2005) on attempts to ban tobacco from the seventeenth century to the present, mostly in Europe and North America, with a sentence or two each on the bans in Japan and China.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the end of Japan's bans and the Chinese edicts in one place. It cites no source for those dates at the point it gives them, its bibliography lists no work on Japan, China or Asia, and it calls European travelers' accounts the only sources for that period, so its dates for Japan and China rest at best on general Western histories of tobacco. It says the Japanese bans on growing and using tobacco were all lifted in 1625, a flat date that could not be checked against a Japanese source here. Its dates for the Chinese edicts differ in one detail from those of Laufer's study.

Used for: Tobacco in China and Japan (Money and prices)
