sena-site-shizuoka

Shizuoka Prefectural Library, "弥生水田のつくり方としごとの仕方 ～静岡平野を中心に～" ("How Yayoi paddies were built and worked, chiefly on the Shizuoka plain", title translated), pp. 12-13 of a prefectural-history reader (in Japanese; https://www.tosyokan.pref.shizuoka.jp/data/open/cnt/3/50/1/ssr1-4.pdf)

What it is: A short illustrated text, with no author named, drawing on the Shizuoka prefectural history and the excavation report of the Sena site (Shizuoka city) to describe how the Yayoi paddies there were laid out, watered and worked.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes channels with solid banks, water openings cut in a bund, weirs leading water from large channels into small ones, and channel banks and large bunds reinforced with stakes and sheet boards, and it says which of these belong to which period: the reinforced banks and large bunds are Late Yayoi into the Kofun period, while the site's Middle Yayoi paddies had none. It is one site, many centuries before the setting's period, in a popular reader rather than the report itself; it gives no widths.

Used for: a channel bank and a large bund built and reinforced together, and water openings cut in the bund (Water)
