唐順之 (Tang Shunzhi), 武編 (Wubian, "a compilation on war", title translated), first part, volume 5, the Siku Quanshu edition (in Chinese; Wikisource transcription; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/武編_(四庫全書本)/前集卷5)

What it is: A volume of a Ming military compendium by the scholar-official Tang Shunzhi, who died in 1560, on weapons and how they were made, with an itemized costing in silver of a matchlock musket: its iron and charcoal, and the days of each kind of worker at the forge and the bench with the pay of each.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives mid-Ming day wages of craftsmen, the forge's tongs-man, its ordinary smiths and a carpenter, the nearest Ming wages to the arrow prices in the record. They are the pay of skilled hands at a weapons workshop, so they are not an unskilled laborer's wage; and the costing names no place or year, only that it was written before 1560.

Used for: mid-Ming craftsmen's day wages beside the arrow prices, and its charcoal by the jin (Money and prices)
