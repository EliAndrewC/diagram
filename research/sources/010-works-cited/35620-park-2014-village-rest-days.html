朴晋煥 (Park Jinhwan), translated by 田中俊光 (Tanaka Toshimitsu), 「江戸時代の上層農民の余暇と旅行」 ("Leisure and travel of upper-class peasants in the Edo period", title translated), 国文学研究資料館紀要 アーカイブズ研究篇 (Bulletin of the National Institute of Japanese Literature, archival studies) 10, 2014 (in Japanese; https://kokubunken.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/843/files/KA1064.pdf)

What it is: A historian's study of the festivals, entertainments and travel of one well-off farming family, the Tamao of Kagami village in Omi, from its own chronicle, with a summary of Furukawa Sadao's 1986 book on village rest days.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Furukawa's usual number of a village's rest days and his account of hired servants pressing for more, but at second hand, in a sentence and a footnote, dating the number only to the Edo period as a whole; the paper's own evidence is one village's chronicle, not a count of days.

Used for: the number of a village's rest days (Money and prices)
