mencius-14-21-chinakongzi

孟子 (Mencius), 孟子·尽心下 (Mengzi, "Jinxin" part two) 14.21, in the 《孟子》中·英对照版 (Chinese-English edition) on the China Confucius site (in classical Chinese with modern Chinese notes and an English translation; https://chinakongzi.org/eBook/worksmencius/four/files/basic-html/page207.html)

What it is: One saying of the Confucian classic of the late fourth century BCE, as printed with notes and a facing English translation on the China Confucius site: Mencius tells a disciple that a footpath on a hillside becomes a road when it is walked and is choked by grass when it is left a while.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states, as a commonplace in a Confucian classic, that a footpath lasts only while it is used. It is a metaphor for the mind, about a hill path rather than a village lane, and gives no time or measure; that a hamlet's lanes behaved the same way is this project's reading.

Used for: a lane that serves nothing is not drawn (Ways)
