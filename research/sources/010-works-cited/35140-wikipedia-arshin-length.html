Wikipedia, "Arshin (length)" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arshin_(length))

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the arshin, the old Russian unit of length, and its Turkish counterpart.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the length a Russian visitor's arshin stood for in the 1820s, 28 inches (71.12 cm), as Peter the Great set it, so that cloth he measured in arshins can be put in meters. It dates the standard only to "the 18th century" and cites no source for the length.

Used for: the length of the Russian arshin (Money and prices)
