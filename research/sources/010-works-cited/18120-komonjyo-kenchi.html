komonjyo-kenchi

古文書ネット (komonjyo.net), 検地とは 検地帳を通して知る百姓の権利義務 ("What was the land survey: a farmer's rights and duties read through the survey register", title translated) (in Japanese; https://komonjyo.net/kenchitoha.html)

What it is: A page on a site teaching the reading of old documents, transcribing lines of a 1591 survey register of Mahiki village (Ebara district, Musashi) and of the 1678 survey register of Yaho village (Tama district, Musashi) and explaining what each line records.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its lines give single fields with their sides and areas, from 7-and-6 by 6 ken (1 se 12 bu, an uneven plot) and 14 by 7 ken (3 se 8 bu) to 41 by 10 ken (1 tan 3 se 20 bu), and it reads a line as one field - dated, measured premodern plots. Its limits: the fields it transcribes are dry fields, not paddy; the lines are an excerpt of one village's register; the areas are the surveyors' assessed areas, which the page itself says could come out larger or smaller than the ground depending on how the rope and rod were handled; and it is an educational site working from published textbooks (Kodama Kōta; a Sanseido/NHK Gakuen reading course), not a published edition.

Used for: the sizes of single dry fields in survey registers of 1591 and 1678, the premodern figure a drawn dry parcel is calibrated to (Fields)
