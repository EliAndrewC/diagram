Liu Yanchun (刘艳春), 《薰风昼长话番茄》 (On the tomato in the long days of the warm wind), Shanghai Botanical Garden, on The Paper's platform, 10 May 2022 (in Chinese; https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_18019776)

What it is: A popular article on the tomato published by the Shanghai Botanical Garden, with its botany, naming and a short history of its planting in China.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the plainest statement read of the stages of planting in China: grown for food in the Northeast, North and Central China only from the 1930s, and a main vegetable across the country from the 1950s. It gives each stage in a single sentence, with no area or count before 2005. It names two older texts but gives no source for its dates of planting, and it repeats European legends about the tomato without testing them.

Used for: The tomato in China (Money and prices)
