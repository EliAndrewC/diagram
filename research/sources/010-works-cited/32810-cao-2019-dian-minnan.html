曹树基 (Cao Shuji), 典地与典租：清代闽南地区的土地市场与金融市场 ("Pledged land and pledged rent: the land market and the financial market of southern Fujian in the Qing", title translated), 清史研究 (Studies in Qing History) 2019, no. 4, reposted on Aisixiang 2019-12-09 (in Chinese; https://www.aisixiang.com/data/119372.html)

What it is: An economic historian's article on newly found contracts, most from Yongchun county in southern Fujian (the author notes the same practice in papers from Yongding, in the southwestern hills), dated from 1704 to 1904, the pledged-rent contracts from 1746, in which a holder pledged or sold the right to a field's rent rather than the field itself; it works out each contract's yearly return from the rent grain and the price of rice in the Qing grain-price records, with comparisons from Shicang in southern Zhejiang.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that turns a Qing land price into a yearly return, case by case, for 36 contracts. Its returns are the author's reckoning, resting on an assumed 70% milling yield, on silver-to-copper rates estimated for some years and on rice prices that swung from year to year, so that, as the author says, they have only a relative meaning; the 36 cases are rent rights from the papers of one county, Yongchun, in coastal southern Fujian, often a slice of a larger rent, which the author treats as loans, not for whole fields bought outright; its table of the 36 cases is not on the repost, only their summary; and its one figure for a pledge's price against an outright sale, about two-thirds, is from Shicang in southern Zhejiang, stated from the author's earlier work and not worked out from the Fujian contracts.

Used for: the yearly return on a pledged or sold rent right in Qing Fujian, and a pledge's price against an outright sale (Money and prices)
