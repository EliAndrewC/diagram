曹树基 (Cao Shuji), 典地与典租：清代闽南地区的土地市场与金融市场 ("Pledged land and pledged rent: the land market and the financial market of southern Fujian in the Qing", title translated), posted on Aisixiang 2019-12-09 (in Chinese; https://www.aisixiang.com/data/119372.html)

What it is: An economic historian's article on newly found contracts from Yongchun and Yongding in southern Fujian, dated from the 1740s to 1904, in which a holder pledged or sold the right to a field's rent rather than the field itself; it works out each contract's yearly return from the rent grain and the price of rice in the Qing grain-price records, with comparisons from Shicang in southern Zhejiang.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that turns a Qing land price into a yearly return, case by case, for 36 contracts. Its returns are the author's reckoning, resting on an assumed 70% milling yield and on silver-to-copper rates estimated for some years; they are for rent rights in two hill counties of Fujian, often a slice of a larger rent, not for whole fields bought outright; and its table of the 36 cases is not on the repost, only their summary.

Used for: the yearly return on a pledged or sold rent right in Qing Fujian, and a pledge's price against an outright sale (Money and prices)
