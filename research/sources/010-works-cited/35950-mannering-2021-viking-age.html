Ulla Mannering, "Fashioning the Viking Age: status after the first three years", Archaeological Textiles Review no. 63, 2021, pp. 139-142 (https://tidsskrift.dk/atr/article/download/166728/207807/371498)

What it is: A project report on reconstructing Viking Age textiles and clothing, with tools copied from finds at Haithabu: wool sorted, combed and spun on drop spindles, and samples woven on a warp-weighted loom, with the weaving speeds measured.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured speed of hand weaving from hand-spun yarn, the only modern rate read. It is wool, a European loom and modern weavers learning reconstructed tools, so it is a yardstick for how slow such work was, never a figure for hemp, ramie, cotton or silk or for Rokugan; it gives no spinning speed.

Used for: hand-weaving speeds on a warp-weighted loom, as a comparison (Comparative money: other societies)
