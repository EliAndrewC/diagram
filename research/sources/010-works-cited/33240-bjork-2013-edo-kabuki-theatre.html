ビュールク・トーヴェ (Tove Björk), 「二代目市川団十郎と劇場経営―享保十九年の江戸歌舞伎―」 ("Ichikawa Danjuro II and theater management: Edo kabuki in Kyoho 19", title translated), 立教大学日本文学 (Rikkyo University Japanese Literature) 109, January 2013, pp. 104-117 (in Japanese; https://rikkyo.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/5105/files/AN0024904X_109_13.pdf)

What it is: A theater historian's paper on how the Edo kabuki theaters were run in the 1710s-1730s, from the diary of the actor Ichikawa Danjuro II, the town magistrate's records and the theaters' own returns: their size, their seating and what they took at the door.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Edo's kabuki seat prices by class, the list prices of 1803 and what was actually charged in 1714 and 1737. The 1803 list prices are another historian's (Hattori Yukio's), drawn from an illustrated theater guide and a late-Edo miscellany and reported, not read here at first hand; the paper itself finds no source of the Kyoho era (1716-1736) that names a list price, and says that what was charged moved with the times and the house; its copper equivalents of the silver box prices are its own conversion.

Used for: Edo kabuki's seat prices by class in 1714, 1737 and 1803, and how many sat in a box (Money and prices)
