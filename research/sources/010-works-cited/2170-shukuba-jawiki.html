ja.wikipedia 宿場 ("post station", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%AE%BF%E5%A0%B4)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the shukuba, the post station of the Edo highways: its transport office, its inns for officials and for ordinary travelers, and the porters and horses it kept on hand for the relay.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that a post station kept horses and porters permanently for the official relay beside its inns, which is the wayside facility with stabling this page names. It says nothing of pasture.

Used for: the post station's inns and standing horses; the crank and the gates at a post town's two ends (Ways)
