谷釜尋徳 (Tanigama Hironori), 「近世後期における江戸庶民の勧進相撲興行見物の実際」 ("How Edo commoners watched the fund-raising sumo tournaments in the late Edo period", title translated), スポーツ健康科学紀要 (Bulletin of Sport and Health Science, Toyo University) 11, 2014, pp. 55-77 (in Japanese; https://toyo.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/6852/files/sportskenkokagaku11_055-077.pdf)

What it is: A sport historian's paper on what it cost an Edo commoner to watch sumo in the late Edo period, which sets the price of admission beside the daily income and the monthly outgoings of a street peddler and a carpenter, taken from 『文政年間漫録』, a miscellany describing Edo around the Bunsei era (1818-1830).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a carpenter's day wage and his household's monthly rent from the same period account, so a rent and a skilled wage stand side by side. The figures are one writer's sketch of a typical household, not an account book; the paper does not say what kind of house the rent bought or how big it was; and its copper equivalents rest on a rate of exchange it chooses for the late Edo period, not on the account's own.

Used for: an Edo carpenter's day wage and monthly rent in the Bunsei era (Money and prices)
