encykorea-gwangmok

Min Gil-ja, "Gwangmok" (broad cotton cloth), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, Academy of Korean Studies, written 1995, last revised 2026 (https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0005115)

What it is: A short encyclopedia article by a textile engineer on wide unbleached cotton cloth.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates imported cotton cloth in Korea and says when cotton cloth became general. Its sentence on the Goryeo period is one line with no source named beside it, and the article's subject is a later cloth, so it is given beside the fuller account in a study of the annals.

Used for: Cotton in India and Korea (Money and prices)
