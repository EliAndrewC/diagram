"Excursion to the Tea Hills" (June 1835, p. 78) and a review of Joseph Thompson's Considerations respecting the Trade with China (April 1836, p. 543), The Chinese Repository, vol. 4 (Canton, May 1835-April 1836), archive.org copy (https://archive.org/details/thechineserepository18351836v4.112)

What it is: The fourth volume of the missionaries' Canton monthly; the passages used are a land tax quoted in cash in a Fujian tea district, with the cash to the dollar beside it, from the journal of a trip made in November 1834, and a correspondent's review of a London book on the China trade.

Why it applies, and its limits: The review, printed at Canton and citing its prices current, says that the 1,000 cash to the tael in the book's table was only nominal; it gives no market number in its place. The tea-district rate is a traveler's parenthesis for one inland district of Fujian, not Canton's.

Used for: cash to the dollar in a Fujian tea district, 1834, and that 1,000 cash to the tael was nominal at Canton (Money and prices)
