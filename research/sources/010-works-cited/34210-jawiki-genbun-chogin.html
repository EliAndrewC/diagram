"元文丁銀" ("Genbun chogin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/元文丁銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the chogin issued in 1736 and withdrawn in 1827, with its prescribed fineness, the Meiji mint's assay and the dates of its withdrawal.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms the prescribed fineness by an assay and says how long the coin passed after the next recoinage. Its fineness and assay are from the Meiji government's table of old coins and a mint chemist's analysis, quoted through the article, not read in the original.

Used for: the Genbun silver's fineness, assay and withdrawal (Money and prices)
