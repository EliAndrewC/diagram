taketora-bamboo-kinds

竹虎 (Taketora, the bamboo-craft maker of Susaki, Kochi), 「Bamboo, its kinds」 (translated; original: 「竹について（竹の種類）」) (https://www.taketora.co.jp/c/special/bamboo)

What it is: A bamboo-craft company's page describing the kinds of bamboo found in Japan - madake, hachiku, moso and thirteen other bamboos and bamboo grasses, most of them smaller, four of them finishes rather than kinds (smoked, fungus-marked or acid-patterned culms) - giving the height and diameter of the culms for most, not all, of them.

Why it applies, and its limits: Bamboo's height is the plant's, not a period's, so a present-day maker's figures stand for the bamboos of a premodern farmstead. Its limits are that it gives culm heights of grown stands, not of a stand kept short by cutting, and that moso, by the page's own account, came to Japan from China only in the Edo period, so a moso grove is an Edo-or-later form in Japan, though native in China.

Used for: the heights of madake, hachiku and moso
