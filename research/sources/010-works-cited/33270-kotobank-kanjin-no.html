kotobank-kanjin-no

Kaitei Shinpan Sekai Dai-hyakka Jiten (Heibonsha), Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica), Seisen-ban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and others, s.v. 勧進能 ("fund-raising noh", title translated), aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/勧進能)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the fund-raising noh performance, a large noh show open to the public for an entrance fee, carrying several encyclopedias' entries on its history from the medieval period to the last such show in 1848.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is where the noh's few public prices are found: an admission of 30 mon in a jokebook of the 1610s-1620s, and how Edo's shows sold their tickets by allotting them to the wards. The 30 mon comes from a collection of comic tales, not an account; and the page gives no price list by seat class for any show.

Used for: what a fund-raising noh charged and how its tickets were sold (Money and prices)
