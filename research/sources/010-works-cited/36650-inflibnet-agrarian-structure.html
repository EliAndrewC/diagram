"Agrarian Structure on the Eve of the British Rule II", module 7 of Agrarian Relations and Social Structure in India, an online book on INFLIBNET (https://ebooks.inflibnet.ac.in/socp6/?p=36)

What it is: A 2018 module by Manish Thakur, published online by the INFLIBNET Center, on the agrarian society of India before British rule, which says of itself that it heavily reproduces the work of historians, and that Irfan Habib is present in each and every sentence on Mughal India.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read that gives the spread of tobacco growing to almost all parts of the Mughal empire by 1650. That is not all of India: the sentence stands in a section on North India, and the empire did not then hold Bijapur, Golconda or the far south. It gives that in one sentence citing Habib 1982 (the only 1982 work in its reference list is Raychaudhuri and Habib, eds., The Cambridge Economic History of India, vol. 1), which was not read, and it speaks for cultivation, not for who smoked.

Used for: Tobacco in India (Money and prices)
