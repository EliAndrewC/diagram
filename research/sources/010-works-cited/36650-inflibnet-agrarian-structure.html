"Agrarian Structure on the Eve of the British Rule", module 7 of Agrarian Relations and Social Structure in India, an open university course book on INFLIBNET (https://ebooks.inflibnet.ac.in/socp6/?p=36)

What it is: A university course module on the agrarian society of India before British rule, which says of itself that it closely reproduces the work of historians including Irfan Habib.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page read that gives the spread of tobacco growing across the Mughal empire by 1650. It gives that in one sentence citing Habib 1982, which was not read, and it speaks for cultivation, not for who smoked.

Used for: Tobacco in India (Money and prices)
