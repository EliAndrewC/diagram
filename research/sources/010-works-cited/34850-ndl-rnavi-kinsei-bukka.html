ndl-rnavi-kinsei-bukka

National Diet Library, Research Navi, 「近世以前の物価・貨幣価値を調べる」 ("Researching prices and the value of money before the modern era", title translated), updated July 27, 2023 (in Japanese; https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/rnavi/humanities/post_101127)

What it is: A research guide of Japan's national library listing the reference works for prices, exchange rates and the value of money from ancient times to the Edo period, with a short note on converting old prices.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states how Japanese reference practice regards converting an Edo price through the price of rice: a convenient method, with no exact value to be had. It is a guide to other works and gives no conversion or reasoning of its own beyond that note.

Used for: rice as a convenient standard for converting old prices (Money and prices)
