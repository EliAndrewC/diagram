potatopro-india

PotatoPro, "India" regional page, History section, updated 9 October 2026 (https://www.potatopro.com/node/136)

What it is: An online potato-industry directory's page on India, whose history section summarizes two reviews and other accounts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read saying when the potato became an established vegetable, and it reports a review of the International Potato Center and one of 2019, which were not read. It says itself that the date and route of arrival are uncertain. It does not say whether the potato of the gardens of 1675 was the white potato, though the word then also meant the sweet potato. It names no source for its dates in the hills (Shimla about 1828, the Nilgiris about 1830) or for its statement on when the crop became established, and that statement, the late 18th and early 19th centuries in both hills and plains, begins before its own dates for the hills.

Used for: The white potato in India: the gardens of 1675, the hills and its becoming established (Money and prices)
