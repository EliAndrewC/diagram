potatopro-india

PotatoPro, "India" regional page, History section, updated 9 October 2026 (https://www.potatopro.com/node/136)

What it is: An online potato-industry directory's page on India, whose history section summarizes two reviews.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only page read saying when the potato became an established vegetable, and it reports a review of the International Potato Center and one of 2019, which were not read. It says itself that the date and route of arrival are uncertain, and it gives no evidence for its statement on when the crop became established.

Used for: The white potato in India: the gardens of 1675, the hills and its becoming established (Money and prices)
