sakai-shishi-1930-3

堺市 (Sakai City), ed., 『堺市史』 第3巻 本編第三 (History of Sakai City, vol. 3, main text part 3), 1930, the section on the city's products and special trades in the later Edo period (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1049487)

What it is: The city's official history, written from the guild's and the founding family's papers, here tracing the Sakai tobacco-knife smiths' guild from its licensed stamp of 1730 through its rules, its quarrels with polishers and middlemen, and its troubles at the end of the shogunate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the trades around the smiths and dates their quarrels, and gives the guild's shares and dues; it gives no price of a knife and no wage. It is about tobacco knives, which Sakai was known for, not kitchen knives, and its levy per knife for 1730 disagrees with the 1761 rules as Takekoshi quotes them.

Used for: the Sakai tobacco-knife guild's stamp, shares and levy, and the trades around its smiths (Money and prices)
