kotobank-han-senbaisei

Yoshinaga Akira (吉永昭), 「藩専売制」 ("Domain monopolies", title translated), Nihon Daihyakka Zensho (Nipponica), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/藩専売制-118212)

What it is: An encyclopedia article by a historian of the Edo domains on the monopolies by which a domain bought up or controlled the sale of a product of its own territory or of goods brought in from outside it, with their early and late examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the Kanazawa domain's salt monopoly as an early one, kept almost without a break to the end of the shogunate, with the import of salt from the Inland Sea banned, and Sendai's as another. It gives no price of salt under either monopoly, and no date for Sendai's.

Used for: Japan's domain salt monopolies (Money and prices)
