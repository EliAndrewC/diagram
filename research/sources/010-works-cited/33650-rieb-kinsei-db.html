rieb-kinsei-db

Research Institute for Economics and Business Administration, Kobe University, and the Mitsui Bunko, 「近世経済データベース」 (Early modern economic database, title translated), 2018 (in Japanese; https://www.rieb.kobe-u.ac.jp/project/kinsei-db/about.html)

What it is: A day-by-day transcription of the business diary of the Mitsui house's Osaka exchange office, 1787-1871: the weather, the events its compilers took from it (economic matters, disasters and disturbances), and the closing rates on the Osaka floors for gold against silver, copper coin against silver, and rice, in silver until Osaka's silver reckoning was abolished in the fifth month of 1868 (after which copper is quoted in coin per ryō, rice in gold per hundred bales, and gold hardly at all).

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the record's one continuous series of what copper coin fetched in silver on the market, every trading day, as it was struck on the Kitahama exchange the guild of principal money-changers set up, and the Mitsui office wrote it down. It is the dealers' wholesale rate, not what a coin-changer's stall gave a townsman or villager for their coppers, which would differ by the changer's margin. It is Osaka's market alone, at its closing session, which the diary gives as the day's low and high rather than one figure; some years of the diary are lost; from 1843 to 1850 the copper rate sits at a floor the authorities set; and from the sixth month of 1859 its gold is quoted in the smaller coins rather than the koban.

Used for: the Osaka market rates of copper coin and gold in silver, 1787-1859 (Money and prices)
