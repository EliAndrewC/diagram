rieb-kinsei-db

Research Institute for Economics and Business Administration, Kobe University, and the Mitsui Bunko, 「近世経済データベース」 (Early modern economic database, title translated), 2018 (in Japanese; https://www.rieb.kobe-u.ac.jp/project/kinsei-db/about.html)

What it is: A day-by-day transcription of the business diary of the Mitsui house's Osaka exchange office, 1787-1871: the weather, the day's events, and the closing rates on the Osaka floors for gold against silver, copper coin against silver, and rice.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the record's one continuous series of what copper coin fetched in silver on the market, every trading day, from a money-changer who traded at those rates. It is Osaka's market alone, at its closing rate; some years of the diary are lost; from 1843 to 1850 the copper rate sits at a floor the authorities set; and from the sixth month of 1859 its gold is quoted in the smaller coins rather than the koban.

Used for: the Osaka market rates of copper coin and gold in silver, 1787-1859 (Money and prices)
