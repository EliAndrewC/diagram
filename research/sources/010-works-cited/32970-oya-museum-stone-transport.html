大谷資料館 (Oya History Museum, Utsunomiya), 「大谷石搬出、輸送の移り変わり」 ("How Oya stone was carried out and transported", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.oya909.co.jp/contents/%E5%A4%A7%E8%B0%B7%E7%9F%B3%E6%90%AC%E5%87%BA%E3%80%81%E8%BC%B8%E9%80%81%E3%81%AE%E7%A7%BB%E3%82%8A%E5%A4%89%E3%82%8F%E3%82%8A/)

What it is: The quarry museum's page on how Oya stone, a soft volcanic tuff of Shimotsuke, was carried out of the quarries and to market, from hand quarrying and pack horses before Meiji to carts, push-railways and trucks.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of a pack-horse load of cut stone and the weight one quarryman carried. Its account of the times before Meiji names no record; Oya stone is light for a stone, so a load of a denser stone would be smaller; and it gives no price for carriage.

Used for: how much cut stone a horse or a man carried (Money and prices)
