Iwahashi Masaru (岩橋勝), 「近世銭匁遣い成立の要因―津軽地方を事例として―」 ("Why reckoning in copper monme arose in early modern Japan: the case of Tsugaru", title translated), 松山大学論集 22(4), October 2010 (in Japanese; https://matsuyama-u-r.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/1605/files/KJ00008731859.pdf)

What it is: A study by a price historian of why domains in northern Japan came to keep accounts in "copper monme", with a merchant house's holdings and a domain mine's payroll in Akita, and the Tsugaru domain's notes of 1837-1838 from a merchant's diary.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows laborers paid in copper and goods priced both in coin and in domain notes, from documents it quotes. Its cases are from the far north, where copper served for sums the west reckoned in silver, so they show how a domain economy worked there, not in Edo or Osaka.

Used for: mine wages paid in copper, and prices in coin and in notes in Tsugaru (Money and prices)
