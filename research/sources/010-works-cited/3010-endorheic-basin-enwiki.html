en.wikipedia "Endorheic basin" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endorheic_basin)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the endorheic basin, the closed drainage whose water leaves only by evaporation and seepage, and where such basins occur.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that closed basins can occur in any climate but are most commonly found in deserts, which this page reads as why a terminal moat with no outlet belongs to an arid site and not to rice country. It describes natural drainage basins at the scale of a region, from a lake's watershed to the Caspian, and says nothing of moats or other dug water; the step from basin to moat is this page's own, and the article itself names closed basins in wet country (the valley of Mexico City, waterlogged at 850 mm of rain a year).
