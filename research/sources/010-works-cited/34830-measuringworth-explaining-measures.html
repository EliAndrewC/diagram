measuringworth-explaining-measures

Lawrence H. Officer and Samuel H. Williamson, "Explaining the Measures of Worth", MeasuringWorth (https://www.measuringworth.com/explaining_measures_of_worth.php)

What it is: The method essay of MeasuringWorth, a site run by two economic historians that turns past amounts of US and British money into later ones, explaining its several measures of relative worth with worked examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the practice of answering with several measures at once, each against a different yardstick, and shows one past price coming out at a range of values. Its examples are American, of the 1900s, and the year of their modern values is not stated beside the table.

Used for: several modern values for one past price, each named for its yardstick (Money and prices)
