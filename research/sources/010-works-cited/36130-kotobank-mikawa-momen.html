kotobank-mikawa-momen

Kotobank, "三河木綿" ("Mikawa cotton", title translated), the article signed by Seta Katsuya in the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, in Japanese (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B8%89%E6%B2%B3%E6%9C%A8%E7%B6%BF-637305)

What it is: A Japanese encyclopedia's article on the white cotton cloth of Mikawa province, from the cotton seed of 799 to the Meiji period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates an appearance of the name Mikawa cotton, in Nara in 1510, as the first of its examples of the cloth's fame, without saying that no earlier record exists. It says the name appears but not in what document or transaction, so it cannot settle whether the cloth was sold or levied as tax. The 1510 date rests on this article alone: another encyclopedia's entry on the same Kotobank page, Yamanobe Tomoyuki's in Nipponica, says it is reported that the cloth was sold under this name only from the Meiji period.

Used for: Mikawa cotton named at Nara in 1510 (Money and prices)
