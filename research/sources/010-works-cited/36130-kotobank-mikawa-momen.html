kotobank-mikawa-momen

Kotobank, "三河木綿" ("Mikawa cotton", title translated), the article signed by Seta Katsuya in the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, in Japanese (https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B8%89%E6%B2%B3%E6%9C%A8%E7%B6%BF-637305)

What it is: A Japanese encyclopedia's article on the white cotton cloth of Mikawa province, from its first named appearance to the Meiji period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the first appearance of Mikawa cotton by name, in Nara in 1510. It says the name appears but not in what document or transaction, so it cannot settle whether the cloth was sold or levied as tax.

Used for: the first named domestic cotton cloth (Money and prices)
