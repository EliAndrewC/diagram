hu-yukun-2008-lu-village

Hu Yukun (胡玉坤), a review of Laurel Bossen's Chinese Women and Rural Development: Sixty Years of Change in Lu Village, Yunnan (2002), on Aisixiang (in Chinese; https://www.aisixiang.com/data/11673.html)

What it is: A Chinese sociologist's review of an American anthropologist's restudy of Lu Village, the Yunnan village Fei Xiaotong surveyed in 1938, summarizing both studies' findings on women's farm work.

Why it applies, and its limits: It reports, from Fei's 1938 survey, that women did more of the staple-crop work than men there and earned half a man's day pay. It is third-hand - the reviewer summarizing Bossen summarizing Fei - and one poor village of southwest China in the 1930s, where many men had left farming.

Used for: women's farm work and pay in Lu Village, 1938 (Money and prices)
