koshikake-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 腰掛 (江戸幕府) ("the koshikake (Edo shogunate)", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/腰掛_(江戸幕府))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia stub on the koshikake, the waiting place for parties summoned to the shogunate's courts in Edo, resting on 大石学 (ed.), 『江戸幕府大事典』, 高橋敏, 『江戸の訴訟』, 古川愛哲, 『悪代官は実はヒーローだった江戸の歴史』, and 村上一博・西村安博 (eds.), 『史料で読む日本法史』.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says where the shed stood (within a court's grounds or before its gate), gives the town magistracies' sheds a size, and tells how parties lodged at inns were summoned to wait there; it speaks only of the shogunate's courts in Edo, names no intendant's office, says nothing of the shed's form (its roof, walls or benches), and gives the 94-tsubo figure with no footnote and no date, nor says whether it is each magistracy's shed or the two together.

Used for: the hearing court's waiting shed (Estates and other compounds)
