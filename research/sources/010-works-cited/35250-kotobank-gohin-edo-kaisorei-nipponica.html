kotobank-gohin-edo-kaisorei-nipponica

津田秀夫 (Tsuda Hideo), 「五品江戸廻送令」 ("the order to ship five goods by way of Edo", title translated), 『日本大百科全書（ニッポニカ）』 (Shogakukan), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%BA%94%E5%93%81%E6%B1%9F%E6%88%B8%E5%BB%BB%E9%80%81%E4%BB%A4-1537389)

What it is: A historian's encyclopedia article on the shogunate's order of 1860 that five goods - grains, rapeseed oil, wax, dry goods and raw silk - be shipped first to Edo's wholesalers and only then to Yokohama for export.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one source read that ties the price of a named everyday good in Edo to the export trade of 1859-60, through goods sent straight to Yokohama instead of to Edo's markets. It names no price and no size of rise, says the surge centered on export goods without saying which, and does not say whether its "grains" took in soybeans.

Used for: which goods' prices the opening of the ports changed in Edo (Money and prices)
