宋史 (Songshi, History of the Song), 卷181, 食貨志下三 (Treatise on Food and Money, second part, 3), the first section on salt (鹽上), compiled under the Yuan and presented in 1345 (Wikisource, in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷181)

What it is: The official history's account of the Song state's salt monopoly: how each kind of salt was made, what the state paid the makers, what it sold for, and how the rules changed reign by reign.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the spread of the Song's official selling prices of sea salt, from 47 cash a jin down to 8 in twenty-one grades. It states the grades without saying which place or year each belongs to, and they are the state's set prices, not what private salt fetched.

Used for: the Song state's selling prices of salt (Money and prices)
