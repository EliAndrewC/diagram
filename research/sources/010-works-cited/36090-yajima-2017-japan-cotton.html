yajima-2017-japan-cotton

Yajima Iwao, "日本における綿栽培の盛衰と加古川西部地域における綿栽培の歴史が有する意味" ("The rise and fall of cotton growing in Japan and the meaning of the history of cotton growing in western Kakogawa", title translated), a report of the Kobe Gakuin University regional research center, March 2017, in Japanese (https://www.human.kobegakuin.ac.jp/yajisoffice/papers_presentation/20170320kogawamenka.pdf)

What it is: A geographer's survey of how cotton growing spread and vanished in Japan, set before a study of one district in Harima, resting mostly on published histories.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the record's readable account of Nagahara Keiji's 2004 history of ramie, silk and cotton, which was not read, so what it says of Nagahara is a summary and may shorten him. It prints some dates that do not fit their period names: the Kan'ei period as 1624-29 and as 1620-29, when it ran 1624-44, and a table year as "1763 (天文元)", probably a slip for 1736 (元文元). It dates Ōkura Nagatsune's 『綿圃要務』 to 1697, the year of Miyazaki Yasusada's 『農業全書』, when that book appeared in 1833. It also gives the spread of cotton to all but the northeast two ways, both from Nagahara: within the sixteenth-century Sengoku and Shokuhō years in its opening, and in the late fifteenth to early sixteenth century in its section on Japan (citing his pp. 323-324) and its conclusion.

Used for: when cotton growing spread, when its stages of work divided, and the shogunal order of 1628 (Money and prices)
