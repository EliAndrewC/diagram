saito-2022-tama-no-sumi

齋藤慎一 (Saito Shin'ichi), 「多摩の炭」 ("Tama charcoal", title translated), 『郷土あれこれ』 (Local odds and ends) no. 33, 五日市郷土館 (Itsukaichi Local Museum, Akiruno city), 30 March 2022 (in Japanese; https://www.city.akiruno.tokyo.jp/cmsfiles/contents/0000005/5474/33.pdf)

What it is: A local museum's bulletin article by a member of a survey team for the documents of the Musashi Mitake shrine, on the charcoal of the Tama hills west of Edo from the Middle Ages to the 1950s: its kilns, its straw bales and their weights, the horse-loads and brokers' levies of Itsukaichi and Ome, and Edo's demand for it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one Edo-period charcoal price by the bale found for the Tama district, for 1716, but at second hand: the author reports it from the antiquarian Mitamura Engyo's study of a merchant's diary, which the article does not quote, and it prints the year once as 1716 and once as 1712. Its bale weights, horse-loads and kiln yields are stated without dates or sources, except a weight of Hikawa charcoal "in a record of the Meiji 20s" (1887-1896).

Used for: Edo charcoal prices of 1716 by the bale and the ryo, Tama bale weights, horse-loads, the brokers' levy, and kiln yields of black and white charcoal (Money and prices)
