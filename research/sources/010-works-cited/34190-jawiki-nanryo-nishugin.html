jawiki-nanryo-nishugin

"南鐐二朱銀" ("Nanryo nishugin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/南鐐二朱銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the nanryo two-shu piece, a silver coin counted in gold units (eight to the ryo), issued from 1772, with its weight, the mint's assay and the shogunate's own justification of its value.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how much pure silver a ryo of these coins held against a ryo of chogin, and quotes the order of 1773 setting a ryo at 24 monme of pure silver. Its reckonings in monme of pure silver are the article's, cited to Mikami's 1996 and Hisamitsu's 1976 histories of Japanese money, which were not read, and they count the coin at its prescribed fine silver, not its assay.

Used for: the silver in the nanryo and the order of 1773 (Money and prices)
