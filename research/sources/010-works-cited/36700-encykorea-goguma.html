Yi Seong-u, "고구마" (Sweet potato), Encyclopedia of Korean Culture (한국민족문화대백과사전), the Academy of Korean Studies (in Korean; https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0003335)

What it is: The Korean national encyclopedia's signed article on the sweet potato, written in 1995 by a historian of food and last revised in 2026, giving its names, its arrival and the people who promoted it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It identifies 감저 with the plant by its Latin name and is the source for the annals' report of 1663 and the 1763 planting, neither of which was read in its own text. It takes the 1763 planting from the Unseok yugo, a collection printed in 1868 that calls it the first, and then says Yi Gwang-ryeo had already tried to grow the plant, so 1763 is a later book's date and not a settled first. It says nothing of the 1633 date that another article of the same encyclopedia gives.

Used for: The sweet potato in Korea (Money and prices)
