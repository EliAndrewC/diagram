rieb-kinsei-db-data

Research Institute for Economics and Business Administration, Kobe University, and the Mitsui Bunko, 「近世経済データベース（2018年3月1日）」 (Early modern economic database, March 1, 2018, title translated), the database's Excel file, 2018 (in Japanese; https://www.rieb.kobe-u.ac.jp/project/kinsei-db/data/data_180301.xlsx)

What it is: The data of the database that rieb-kinsei-db describes, one row a day of the Mitsui house's Osaka exchange office's business diary from 1787 to 1871: the date in the Japanese calendar and the Western year, the weather, the day's economic events, disasters and disturbances in modern Japanese summary, the lowest and highest rates of gold and of copper coin in silver (the day's closing rates, by the database's about page), the rice price and the rice's kind, and in its remarks column passages of the diary in its own words.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the record's day-by-day series of what copper coin and gold fetched in silver on the Osaka market, kept by a money-changer who traded at those rates, and the diary's own note of the 1843 order that set a floor under the copper rate. The events column is the editors' modern summary of the diary, not its words; a few rate cells hold text (a range, a note) rather than a number; and its limits as a series are those of the database it carries.

Used for: the 1843 order forbidding copper trades under 10 monme a kanmon, and the Osaka decade means of the copper and gold rates computed from it (Money and prices)
