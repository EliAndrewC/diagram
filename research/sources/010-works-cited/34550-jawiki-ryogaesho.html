jawiki-ryogaesho

"両替商" ("ryogaesho", money changers), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/両替商)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on Japan's money changers from the medieval period to the Meiji period: the main changers who dealt in gold and silver, the copper shops, their fees, and their banking.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names who used which kind of changer and the kinds of fee they charged. Its figure of about 1-2% for a changer's fee, and its account of the fees' kinds, carry no footnote on the page, so the figure is a reading this record could not trace to a study.

Used for: the main changers and the copper shops, and the 1-2% fee (Money and prices)
