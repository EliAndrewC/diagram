jta-himeji-kurumamon

Japan Tourism Agency, multilingual commentary database, "車門跡" ("Kurumamon Gate", the site of Himeji castle's cart gate; title translated), a sign text of 2020 written for Himeji city (in English and Japanese; https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/R2-00200.html)

What it is: The national tourism agency's commissioned sign text for the site of Himeji castle's Kurumamon, the gate by which supplies entered the castle, with what the gate was for and how its three gateways and two courtyards were laid out.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that a gate named for the carts that came through it was a gate of its own in Japan, set apart from the castle's other gates and serving goods brought in by road and river. Its limits: it is a castle's gate complex, a lord's great works, not a gate in a compound's wall; it gives the gate no width or size; and it is a short sign text of 2020 for the site of a gate that no longer stands, and the only thing it offers as evidence that the name came from carts is the name itself.

Used for: a cart gate as a gate of its own name in Japan, at a castle (Ways)
