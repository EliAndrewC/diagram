kaken-matsushiro-sensui

Sasaki Kunihiro (principal investigator), with Okubo Kumiko, Uehara Misoto and Oishi Yoshitaka, Shinshu University, final report of KAKEN grant 24560743, Jokamachi no niwaike o musubu mizu riyo shisutemu no genjo to tamenteki na riyo jittai, oyobi sono hozen rikatsuyo keikaku (its own English title: The present water systems connecting garden ponds, their actual state with many uses, and planning to conserve and utilize them), research 2012-2014, report dated June 2015 (in Japanese; https://soar-ir.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/18449/files/24560743seika.pdf)

What it is: A six-page research report on the channels (sensuiro, 泉水路 - sensui, 泉水, is the pond itself) that run from garden pond to garden pond through the samurai-house lots of Matsushiro, the castle town of the Matsushiro domain, with a survey of the water plants along them and interviews with the ponds' owners.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the channels between Matsushiro's garden ponds are commonly walled with today. It speaks of the channels, not the ponds' own banks; it says stacked small stone is common, not the only walling; it gives no date for the stonework, which the landowners maintain today; it records channels lost, rerouted and straightened where ponds were filled since its 1984 baseline survey, so some of today's courses and walls are recent; and it is one castle town's water system.

Used for: the small-stone walling of the channels between Matsushiro's garden ponds (Estates and other compounds)
