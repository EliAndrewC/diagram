lawrence-1912-inn-yards

W. J. Lawrence, The Elizabethan Playhouse and Other Studies (Stratford: Shakespeare Head Press, 1912), the chapter on the playhouse's evolution from the inn yards, as reproduced by Shakespeare Online, 2011 (https://shakespeare-online.com/theatre/theinnyardsexp.html)

What it is: A theater historian's account, published in 1912, of how London's first playhouses grew out of the inn yards where companies had played, reproduced on a Shakespeare reference website with its footnotes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source, read only as a yardstick for players. Its "secondary gathering in the galleries" is Lawrence's own reading, in a footnote, of practices three centuries before him; the page quotes no document of the period for it and does not say when in a performance the gathering came.

Used for: payment at the door and a second gathering in the galleries as relics of the inn-yard period (Comparative money: other societies)
