Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries of Japan, 令和5年産米において等級格付けされた水稲うるち玄米の機械鑑定による測定結果 ("Machine-assessment measurements of graded non-glutinous paddy brown rice of the 2023 crop", title translated), September 2024 (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/j/seisan/syoryu/kensa/attach/pdf/kikaikantei-3.pdf)

What it is: A ministry report of the measurements taken by machine on some 9,600 inspected samples of the 2023 brown-rice crop, by grade, among them the bulk density in grams per liter.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a measured weight per liter of brown rice from thousands of samples, which an older official figure of 150 kg a koku can be checked against. It is today's rice, of modern varieties dried and hulled by machine, so it stands for the weight of Edo-period brown rice only by analogy.

Used for: the weight of a liter of brown rice (Money and prices)
