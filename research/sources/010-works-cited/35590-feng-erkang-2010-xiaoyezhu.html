冯尔康 (Feng Erkang), 「乾嘉之际小业主的经济状况和社会生活 ——兼述嘉庆朝刑科题本档案史料的价值」 ("The economic condition and social life of small proprietors around the Qianlong-Jiaqing transition", title translated), posted on sinoss.net, 2010 (in Chinese; https://www.sinoss.net/uploadfile/2010/1130/4960.pdf)

What it is: A social historian's survey of small masters, shopkeepers and farmers who hired labor, around 1800, drawn from the Board of Punishments' case reports of the Jiaqing reign (1796-1820), trade by trade.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one source found on how many hands a Qing smithy hired. Its cases are those that came to a criminal trial, its figures for hired hands are for small masters of every trade together, and it gives no smith's takings.

Used for: the hired hands of Qing smithies (Money and prices)
