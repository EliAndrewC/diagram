Avik Ray, "Does Indian Cotton Crisis Have Historical Allusions? Technological Development Sheds New Light", an essay on the blog of the Research Network on Innovation (https://rri.univ-littoral.fr/en/does-indian-cotton-crisis-have-historical-allusions-technological-development-sheds-new-light/)

What it is: A short modern web essay on Indian cotton policy and the history behind it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a share of American cotton in India, 2-3% of "the acreage" from the 19th to the mid-20th century, without saying of what acreage and with no citation on the sentence. The range is looser than its evidence: the same author's 2025 journal article, listed among the essay's references, says the acreage "reached only about 2-3 percent by 1947-48", and its table gives 3% of India's cotton acreage for that one year. So this is a figure for the end of the period, not a measured share for the 19th century or any earlier date. The essay takes today's trade dispute as its occasion, and its history is a brief one of how modern cotton technology came to India.

Used for: The share of American cotton in India (Money and prices)
