gao-shouxian-jiuqing-wuliao

高寿仙 (Gao Shouxian), 「清代《九卿议定物料价值》的分类整理——兼比较明代《工部广库须知》同类物品的价格」 ("Sorting the Qing-Dynasty Material Value as Agreed by the Nine Chief Ministers: In Comparison with Similar Items in the Ming-Dynasty Essential Knowledge of the Ministry of Public Works' Workshops and Warehouses", the paper's own English title), Gugong Xuekan 2022, pp. 23-37 (in Chinese; the Palace Museum, https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/02/10/u63e60647c73ae.pdf)

What it is: A historian's tables of the official price list for the materials the Ministry of Works bought in Beijing, settled by the Nine Chief Ministers and printed in 1736, sorted by kind, with the wages the list sets for craftsmen and laborers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Beijing's official prices of silk and cotton cloth by width and length, of clothing, and a laborer's day wage, in the same list. The prices are those the ministry would pay, based on the market but set by officials; the wages are in copper cash, which the author converts at an estimated 800 cash to the tael; and most of its garments are costume pieces for the yangge folk dance, which it marks as differing from everyday dress.

Used for: Beijing's silk and cotton prices, its clothing prices and its laborer's wage in 1736 (Money and prices)
