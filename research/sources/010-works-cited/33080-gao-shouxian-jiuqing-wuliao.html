gao-shouxian-jiuqing-wuliao

高寿仙 (Gao Shouxian), 「清代《九卿议定物料价值》的分类整理——兼比较明代《工部广库须知》同类物品的价格」 ("Sorting the Qing-Dynasty Material Value as Agreed by the Nine Chief Ministers: In Comparison with Similar Items in the Ming-Dynasty Essential Knowledge of the Ministry of Public Works' Workshops and Warehouses", title translated by its author), Gugong Xuekan 2022, pp. 23-37 (in Chinese; the Palace Museum, https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/02/10/u63e60647c73ae.pdf)

What it is: A historian's tables of the official price list for the materials the Ministry of Works bought in Beijing, settled by the Nine Chief Ministers and submitted to the emperor in 1736, sorted by kind, with the wages the list sets for craftsmen and laborers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Beijing's official prices of silk and cotton cloth by width and length, of clothing, of paper and ink, and of bows and arrows, with a laborer's and a craftsman's day wage, in the same list. The prices are those the ministry would pay, based on the market but set by officials; the wages are in copper cash, which the author converts at the 800 cash to the tael that a Board of Revenue memorial of 1737 reports for Beijing (the official rate was 1,000, and the market rate varied); and most of its garments are costume pieces for the yangge folk dance, which it marks as differing somewhat from everyday dress. Its silks run from plain raw silk at 0.36 tael a zhang to palace-made golden-yellow satin with five-clawed mang (the dragon of rank robes) at 7.935; this project takes the dragon satins for court silk and only the plain grades as what a townsman might buy. These are the imperial capital's prices, which the author says follow Beijing's market; a provincial town's ran differently, and the 1768 provincial price regulations he names (1,557 jurisdictions, Beijing excluded) are the closer record for those.

Used for: Beijing's silk and cotton prices, its clothing prices, its paper, ink and arrow prices, and its laborer's and craftsman's wages in 1736 (Money and prices)
