jawiki-erizeni

"撰銭" ("erizeni", coin selection), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/撰銭)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the medieval and Sengoku practice of refusing or discounting bad copper coins, and on the laws that tried to regulate it, up to the Kan'ei tsuho of the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the discount at which bad coin once passed, four to a mon, and Nobunaga's grading of coins in 1569-1570. The four-to-one agreement is given without a place or a year, and its grading in three classes differs from Kobayashi's four.

Used for: bad coin passing at a discount before 1600 (Money and prices)
