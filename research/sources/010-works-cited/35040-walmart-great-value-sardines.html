Walmart.com, search results for "great value sardines", seen October 25, 2026 (https://www.walmart.com/search?q=great+value+sardines)

What it is: The national retailer's online listing of its own-brand canned sardines, each with the price it charged that day.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices the cheapest small whole fish a US shopper buys. The listing gives the can's weight but not the drained weight of the fish, so the price per kilogram of fish alone may be higher than the figure worked from it; it is the price for buying online on the day it was seen, and the page keeps no history.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
