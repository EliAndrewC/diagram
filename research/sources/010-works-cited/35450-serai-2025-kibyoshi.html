serai-2025-kibyoshi

Serai editorial staff, 「セリフ付きの絵物語で人情、笑い、色恋などを描いた「黄表紙」は江戸のコミック誌だ」 ("The kibyoshi, picture stories with dialogue of feeling, laughter and love, were Edo's comic magazines", title translated), Serai, February 2025 issue, on serai.jp, 9 January 2025 (in Japanese; https://serai.jp/news/magazine-news/1213187)

What it is: A general-interest magazine's feature on the kibyoshi picture booklets, with a fact box of their format, price and years and comments by the kibyoshi scholar Tanahashi Masahiro.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the booklet's format, five leaves to a volume, and a retail price of 6 mon a volume. The price is in the fact box with no source and no year beyond the genre's span of 1772 to about 1818, and it is not said to be Tanahashi's.

Used for: the kibyoshi's format and one price of a volume (Money and prices)
