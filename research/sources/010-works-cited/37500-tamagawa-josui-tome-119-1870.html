『玉川上水留』 (Records of the Tamagawa aqueduct), volume 119, a manuscript file of the works office at Hamura, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587455/1/36 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587455/1/59 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587455/1/2 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587455/1/62 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2587455)

What it is: A volume of the files kept on the Tamagawa aqueduct, which carried Edo's and then Tokyo's drinking water from its intake at Hamura on the Tama river. Its cover dates it Meiji 3 (1870) and names its subject, the floods at Hamura in the seventh and ninth months of that year; inside are the works office's settled accounts for the emergency repairs, item by item: timber, bamboo, mats, empty bales, rope and laborers, each with its count and, for most, its sum in silver monme and its rate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices an empty bale, a mat, a bundle of rope and a laborer's pay in the accounts of one office in one year; the one entry whose heading was read is for the bank in Fussa village. It is of 1870, two years after the Edo period ended, so it gives no Edo-period price; it is an emergency purchase by a government works office; and it does not say what the bales had held or whether they were new. Its sums are in monme of silver, the unit of account, and it does not say in what money they were paid. Only the four frames named were read.

Used for: what an empty bale, a straw mat, a bundle of kaya rope and a laborer's pay came to in the accounts of the Tamagawa aqueduct's works office at Hamura in 1870 (Money and prices)
