hanno-edojo-shintan-yore-092

"江戸城の薪炭" ("Firewood and charcoal for Edo castle", title translated), parts 1-8 of the series 昔の飯能 (Hanno in the old days), an unsigned local-history column in Hanno City's bulletin 広報はんのう (Kōhō Hannō), installments 92-99, 15 May to 1 September 1982, as retyped on a personal website (in Japanese; https://ghosts.xrea.jp/daylight/yore/yore-092.htm)

What it is: A 1982 local-history series in the Hanno city bulletin on the men of the hills west of Edo who contracted to supply firewood and charcoal to Edo castle. Its fifth and sixth parts follow Soemon of Kami-Inoue village through a diary-like record of 1763 in the Ono Yoshisuke family papers: his purchase of rope at the market town of Hanno, and the 4,930 bales of charcoal he sent out between the eighth and twelfth months.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes one line of a charcoal contractor's own record: a count of rope bundles and what they cost, at a named market in a named month. It is at third hand: the family papers were quoted by an unnamed writer of a 1982 city-bulletin column, who gives no reference a reader could follow to them, and are read here in a retyped web copy. That copy has slips (役入 for 役人, 御番屋 where 御舂屋 is meant), so its count and price cannot be checked against the print. That the rope was for making charcoal bales is the writer's guess, said as one ("it seems"); the record as quoted says only that rope was bought, and gives no length or weight for a bundle.

Used for: rope bought by a charcoal contractor at Hanno in 1763 (Money and prices)
