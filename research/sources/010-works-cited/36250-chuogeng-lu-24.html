chuogeng-lu-24

Tao Zongyi (陶宗儀), Nancun chuogeng lu (南村輟耕錄, Notes Written While Resting from the Plow), chapter 24, the Sibu congkan text on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/南村輟耕錄_(四部叢刊本)/卷之二十四)

What it is: A chapter of miscellaneous notes by a writer of the late Yuan who lived near Songjiang, with the earliest telling read of Huang Daopo's story.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that Songjiang's poor land took up cotton with seed from the south, and that an old woman from Yazhou taught its tools. It dates her only as "early in the dynasty", gives no figures, and writes of one locality.

Used for: Songjiang's cotton and Huang Daopo (Money and prices)
