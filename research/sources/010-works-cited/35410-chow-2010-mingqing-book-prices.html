周启荣 (Kai-wing Chow), 「明清印刷书籍成本、价格及其商品价值的研究」 ("A study of the cost, price and commercial value of printed books in the Ming and Qing", title translated), 『浙江大学学报（人文社会科学版）』 40:1 (2010), p. 5 on (in Chinese; https://www.zjujournals.com/soc/CN/article/downloadArticleFile.do?attachType=PDF&id=9314)

What it is: A historian of the Chinese book's study of what printed books cost to make and to buy from the Wanli reign (1573-1620) into the early Qing, from the prices marked on surviving Ming imprints, two collectors' catalogs and printing accounts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gathers the few marked book prices of the late Ming and weighs what they can show. Its marked prices are publishers' list prices without the dealer's costs, so they show no difference by region or by year, and its catalog prices are of collectors' books and pledges, which run dearer; its range of a tenth of a tael to a tael for an ordinary book is its own conclusion from these.

Used for: book prices and the price of a hand copy in the late Ming and early Qing (Money and prices)
