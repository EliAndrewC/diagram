kawakami-1991-shrine-funding

河上信行 (Kawakami Nobuyuki), 「江戸時代後期における中小寺社造営の費用調達 - 名主吉野家文書にみる旧武蔵国多摩郡の中小寺社造営 -」 ("Raising the costs of building minor temples and shrines in the later Edo period: the Yoshino headman family papers of the former Tama district of Musashi", title translated), 日本建築学会計画系論文報告集 (Journal of Architecture, Planning and Environmental Engineering, AIJ) 420 (February 1991) (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/aijax/420/0/420_KJ00004076677/_pdf)

What it is: An architectural historian's study of the income and spending of the building and repair of four small temples and shrines of the Ome area, from the mid-1700s to 1858, from the account books in two headman families' papers, with each job's total and the shares of its spending.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the totals and the cost shares of repairs to two small one-bay sanctuaries, one of them the size asked about, and of a copper re-roofing. Its figures are repairs, not new building; its shares are the author's grouping of the account books, and its totals are what the surviving books confirm, which the author says leave points unclear; and the larger sanctuary's copper roof makes its materials share unusually high.

Used for: what a small shrine's sanctuary and roof cost (Money and prices)
