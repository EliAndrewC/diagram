jikata-hanreiroku-9

『地方凡例録』 (Jikata hanreiroku, the Edo handbook of village administration), book 9, a manuscript copy held by the Ministry of Justice branch of the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1365507/1/75 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1365507)

What it is: Book 9 of the handbook of land, tax and village administration begun in 1791 by Oishi Hisataka, a district officer of the Takasaki domain (kotobank-jikata-hanreiroku). The page read is among its standard allowances for river and dike works: a rule headed as the standard for carrying empty bales and rope, which says what it counts as an empty bale.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a working handbook's own definition of the empty bale that dike works reckoned with: a bale of the 4-to size, its end caps included. It is a standard for counting loads, not a price and not an account of any purchase, and it does not say the bale had held rice, only its size. The copy is undated and the texts of the handbook differ, so the rule is of the 1790s only as far as this copy keeps Oishi's words. The numbers of the rule itself were not read with confidence and are not used.

Used for: what a handbook of the 1790s counted as an empty bale in dike works (Money and prices)
