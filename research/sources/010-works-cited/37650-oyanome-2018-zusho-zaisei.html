Oyano Eiji (大矢野栄次), "薩摩藩の財政改革と調所広郷" ("The fiscal reform of Satsuma domain and Zusho Hirosato", title translated), 久留米大学 経済社会研究 (Kurume University, Journal of Economics and Social Research) 58(1-2), June 2018, pp. 1-19 (in Japanese; https://kurume.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/455/files/keisya58_1-2_1-19.pdf)

What it is: An economist's article on how Satsuma domain repaired its finances from 1827 into the 1840s under its minister Zusho Hirosato. Its section on the sugar monopoly gives the rates at which the domain exchanged rice for the Amami islanders' black sugar at several dates, and average prices of rice and of black sugar in silver.

Why it applies, and its limits: It puts the islands' exchange rate beside silver prices of rice and sugar, the one place a silver ratio can be reckoned. It does not say where or in which years its two average prices were taken, nor show how it reaches the multiple it states, which its own figures do not give. It dates the 5.07-kin rate only by setting it in its account of the 1839 plan to raise the sugar take. It is a survey written from published books, not from the documents, and it gives no source for the rate or the prices. Its reading list sets Haraguchi Torao's 1966 history of the reform beside popular lives of Zusho, one by a novelist, and its own dates are often misprinted (文化11年 given as 1828), so these figures are only as good as a secondary report whose origin is not traced.

Used for: the rate of rice for Amami sugar in the 1830s and the silver prices set beside it (Money and prices)
