kurima-ryukyu-sato-ryutsu

Kurima Yasuo (来間泰男), "17～19世紀琉球の砂糖生産とその流通" ("Sugar Production and Circulation in Ryukyu Islands, 17-19 cent.", the article's own English title), a journal article in Okinawa International University's repository (in Japanese; https://okiu1972.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/3096/files/18801706_12_1_YasuoKurima.pdf)

What it is: A review of what earlier historians wrote on Ryukyu's sugar from the 1600s to the 1800s: sugar taken by the royal government in place of tax grain, sent to the kingdom's office at Kagoshima and sold there, and from there to Osaka.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says by what hands Ryukyu's sugar reached Osaka, the route this record's other sources leave out. It is a critical reading of older histories, quoting them at length, and gives quantities of sugar; its only prices are of the Meiji years, the government's purchase price of 3 yen 20 sen for 100 kin, raised to 4 yen, and an Osaka market price of 6 or 4 yen with no unit. It gives no price for the kingdom's route through Kagoshima.

Used for: the route of Ryukyu's sugar to Kagoshima and Osaka and how it was sold (Money and prices)
