khan-2020-tomato

Ko Young (고영), "[고영의 문헌 속 '밥상'] 토마토" (The table in the texts: the tomato), Kyunghyang Shinmun, 24 July 2020 (in Korean; https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202007240300035)

What it is: A newspaper column on the tomato in Korean texts by a researcher of Korean food culture, who quotes the older authors and a 1935 newspaper.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only account read of the tomato in Korea from the 17th century to the 1930s. It is a short column with no notes, and its readings of Yi Gyu-gyeong and of the 1935 newspaper were not checked against those texts.

Used for: The tomato in Korea (Money and prices)
