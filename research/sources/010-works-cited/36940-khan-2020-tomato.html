khan-2020-tomato

Ko Young (고영), "[고영의 문헌 속 '밥상'] 토마토" (The table in the texts: the tomato), Kyunghyang Shinmun, 24 July 2020 (in Korean; https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202007240300035)

What it is: A newspaper column on the tomato in Korean texts by a researcher of Korean food culture, who reports the older authors and quotes a 1935 newspaper.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the only account read of the tomato's course in Korea after its arrival, from Yi Gyu-gyeong in the 19th century to the 1930s; for the 17th-century arrival, Yi Su-gwang's own entry in Jibong yuseol 19 is the closer source. Its premodern evidence is a few sentences: the plant came by way of China, was not grown in earnest, and was a weed to Yi Gyu-gyeong. The tomato as a crop and a market good belongs to the 20th century under Japanese rule (imported varieties, canneries, a ketchup factory) and cannot stand for the setting, and the column gives no price, acreage or yield. It is a short column with no notes, and its readings of Yi Gyu-gyeong and of the 1935 newspaper were not checked against those texts.
