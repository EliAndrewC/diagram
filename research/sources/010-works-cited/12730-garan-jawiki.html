garan-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 伽藍 ("garan, a temple's buildings", title translated) (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/伽藍)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the garan, the set of buildings of a Buddhist temple, giving the older seven-hall list from a Kamakura-period text and the Zen seven-hall list.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states which halls a full Japanese temple was held to have, old and Zen. The article itself says the lists vary by sect and era and that the name was used loosely of any large temple; it gives no sizes, and on an ordinary small temple only that Nichiren temples began as one small hall and added buildings over time. It is an article with one reference (the Iwanami Buddhist dictionary, footnoted only for the Zen list); the older list names its Kamakura text but is not cited. Its China section traces the shift from a pagoda-centred to a hall-centred temple but lists no halls.

Used for: which buildings a full temple precinct held (Religion and the dead)
