l7r-lineages

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Provincial and cosmopolitan lineages" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#provincial-and-cosmopolitan-lineages) and "Samurai Lineages" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#samurai-lineages) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, on the lineages a ruling house's samurai belong to. They describe two kinds: lineages tied to a single province (provincial lineages; their one example is the Aki of the Daidoji, most of whose samurai live in Akishi province, whose governor is always an Aki) and lineages based in their domain's capital (cosmopolitan lineages), and they note that the Ryusei domain, the notes' worked example of a domain, has only the second kind.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: what kinds of lineage the setting's houses have is the setting's to decide. The notes do not say where a lineage's chancellor lives, whether a lineage keeps a compound in the capital, or how large one is. Nor do these sections say how common provincial lineages are: they name one, the Aki of the Daidoji, and one domain with none, the Ryusei, so which other domains have a provincial lineage is also this project's decision.

Used for: which domains have a provincial lineage, behind the smaller capital compound of a provincial lineage's chancellor (Domain capitals)
