chinanews-beijing-biaoju

China News Service, 「北京的镖局」 ("Beijing's escort agencies", title translated), 14 May 2001, in its folk-customs series (in Chinese; https://www.chinanews.com/zhuanzhu/2001-05-14/509.html)

What it is: A short popular article on the Beijing escort agencies that carried silver under armed guard, from their Qing heyday to their last years in the early Republic, partly from what an old man who had worked in one remembered.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how an agency was run and paid: a yearly settlement, silver in packets, liability for loss. It gives no rate per trip or per tael carried and no guard's wage, its yearly totals are round figures with no year, and its memory reaches the agencies' last decades rather than the eighteenth century.

Used for: how a Beijing escort agency was paid (Money and prices)
