kotobank-shuson

Kotobank, 集村 ("clustered village", title translated), the entry from Heibonsha's Hyakka jiten Mypedia (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%9B%86%E6%9D%91-77146)

What it is: Kotobank's page for the clustered village, gathering dictionary and encyclopedia entries; the Mypedia entry says the clustered village is the general form in Japan and describes its dense and loose regional variants.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the plainest statement read of which settlement form was usual in Japan, and of how it differed between the west and the east. "general" and "common" are its words, with no figure and no period.

Used for: the clustered village as the general form in Japan, dense in the Kinai and Setouchi and loose in the Kanto and Tohoku (Field archetypes)
