Lu Mingshan, 『農桑衣食撮要』 ("Essentials of farming, sericulture, clothing and food", title translated), Yuan dynasty, begun 1314, chapter 2, on Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/農桑衣食撮要/卷下; the main page with the Siku catalog's summary, https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/農桑衣食撮要)

What it is: A Yuan-dynasty farming almanac arranged by the twelve months, written by a Uighur official while supervising Shou commandery from 1314 and, by the imperial catalog's reading of a preface signed in 1330, printed again then, in a Wikisource transcription of the Siku edition from the Yongle dadian.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its closing "plain words in common speech", addressed to those who farm, is the earliest place read where the sayings on diligence, wealth and early rising stand together. It is an official's advice written for farmers, so it shows what was said to them, not what farmers said among themselves; the transcription leaves one character it could not encode as a placeholder.

Used for: the old sayings on rising early and on diligence and wealth (Money and prices)
