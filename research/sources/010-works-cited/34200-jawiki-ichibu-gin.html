jawiki-ichibu-gin

"一分銀" ("Ichibu-gin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/一分銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the one-bu silver piece, a quarter-ryo silver coin issued from 1837, nearly pure in the Tenpo issue (98.86% silver) and made to the Mexican dollar's fineness in the Ansei issue of 1859 (89.36%), with the prescribed weight of each issue and an assay by the Mint that the article cites to Kōga Yoshimasa (1930).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the silver in a ryo of these coins against a ryo of Tenpo chogin, which shows how far the coin passed above its metal. That comparison is not like for like: the 15.6 monme is Tenpo chogin (26% silver) at the official 60 monme to the ryo, not the market rate, and the 9.2 monme set against it is the Tenpo coin's gross weight, not its silver. The article makes the comparison for the Tenpo issue only. Its table gives assays only, not a prescribed fineness.

Used for: the silver in the Tenpo and Ansei one-bu pieces (Money and prices)
