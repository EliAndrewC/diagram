jawiki-ichibu-gin

"一分銀" ("Ichibu-gin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/一分銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the one-bu silver piece, a quarter-ryo coin of nearly pure silver issued from 1837, with the weight and the Meiji mint's assay of each issue.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the silver in a ryo of these coins against a ryo of Tenpo chogin, which shows how far the coin passed above its metal. Its table gives assays only, not a prescribed fineness.

Used for: the silver in the Tenpo and Ansei one-bu pieces (Money and prices)
