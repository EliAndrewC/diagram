湯浅隆 (Yuasa Takashi), 「江戸の開帳における十八世紀後半の変化」 ("Changes in the unveilings of Edo in the later eighteenth century", title translated), 国立歴史民俗博物館研究報告 (Bulletin of the National Museum of Japanese History) 33 (1991), pp. 171-189 (in Japanese; https://rekihaku.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/493/files/kenkyuhokoku_033_07.pdf)

What it is: A historian's study of the public showings of temples' hidden images in Edo, and how the shogunate's licensing and the temples' aims changed over the 1700s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It reports, from another historian's study of the Zenkoji temple, the takings and costs of that temple's travelling showings in Edo in 1692 and 1820. Those are the figures of the most famous image of the period on its tours of the largest city, at second hand, and not what an ordinary temple's showing took in.

Used for: what a temple's showing of its hidden image took in and cost (Money and prices)
