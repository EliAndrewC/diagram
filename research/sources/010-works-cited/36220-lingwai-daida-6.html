Zhou Qufei (周去非), Lingwai daida (嶺外代答, Answers in Place of Letters from beyond the Passes), chapter 6, the Siku quanshu text on Chinese Wikisource (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/嶺外代答_(四庫全書本)/卷06)

What it is: The chapter on clothing and goods in a Song official's account of the far south, finished in 1178 from his service in Guangxi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the earliest dated first-hand account read of cotton grown and woven on the south coast and Hainan. Two words in its key sentence can be read as "plentiful" or as "some of the rich", and it counts nothing.

Used for: cotton in Guangdong's coast and Hainan in 1178 (Money and prices)
