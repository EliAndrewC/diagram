kotobank-douza

kotobank 銅座 ("Douza, the copper office", title translated), the entry by 佐々木潤之介 (Sasaki Junnosuke) in 『改訂新版世界大百科事典』 (Heibonsha) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%8A%85%E5%BA%A7-103544)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry on the shogunate's copper office in Osaka, which from 1738 bought up the country's copper and divided it between export at Nagasaki and sale at home.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the office's buying prices for copper from five mines and the fixed export prices, all from one record of 1774, the one price of raw copper found. It does not name the record; its export prices were set by policy and held almost fixed; and it gives the home-market price only as about twice the China export price, in its account of the years after 1738, not as a figure for 1774.

Used for: the price of raw copper as a floor under a cast object's cost (Money and prices)
