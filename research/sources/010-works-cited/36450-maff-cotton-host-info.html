maff-cotton-host-info

Japan, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, "Wata (rikuchimen) no shukushu joho" (host information on upland cotton), a regulatory sheet (https://www.maff.go.jp/j/syouan/nouan/carta/tetuduki/attach/pdf/index-28.pdf)

What it is: The ministry's background sheet on the cotton plant, written for the review of genetically modified crops, with a paragraph on cotton's history in Japan.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that names the species grown across early modern Japan. The history is one paragraph, resting on two secondary works it names and that were not read; it says nothing of when an American species was first planted in Japan.

Used for: The species of Japanese cotton (Money and prices)
