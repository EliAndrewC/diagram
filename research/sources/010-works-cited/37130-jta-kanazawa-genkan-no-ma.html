jta-kanazawa-genkan-no-ma

Japan Tourism Agency multilingual commentary database, Entrance Hall (Genkan no Ma), Reception Room (Zashiki), and Garden (Niwa) (H30-00662) (https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/en/H30-00662.html)

What it is: An interpretive sign of 2018 for the Kanazawa City Tourism Association, in the Japan Tourism Agency's multilingual database, on the Shimizu family house, a foot soldier's (ashigaru) house of the Kaga domain shown to visitors: it says what the genkan room and the reception room were for, and who came in by the formal entrance.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that says what a genkan room was used for. It speaks of a foot soldier's house in one domain, not of an official's or a senior retainer's, its account of who visited is hedged ("It is said"), and it gives no date for the house.

Used for: what a genkan room was for, at a Kaga foot soldier's house (Estates and other compounds)
