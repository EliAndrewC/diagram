takatsuki-2012-osaka-otsu

Yasuo Takatsuki, "Informational Efficiency under the Shogunate Governance: Concentration and Integration of the Rice Market in Tokugawa Japan", Research Institute for Economics and Business Administration, Kobe University, Discussion Paper DP2012-22, September 2012 (https://www.rieb.kobe-u.ac.jp/academic/ra/dp/English/DP2012-22.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's test, on daily rice prices of 1798-1856, of how quickly the rice market at Otsu, about 50 km from Osaka, followed the Dojima price in Osaka.

Why it applies, and its limits: It measures how fast a price moved from Osaka to a nearby market, and gives how long letters took between Edo, Osaka and Otsu. It tests the timing of price changes, not how far the two markets' price levels stood apart, and covers one pair of markets close together. Both its series are futures prices on rice bills at two exchanges chartered by the shogunate, so it shows how fast a price passed between merchants' exchanges, not how it reached a shop or a village; it is a discussion paper in draft.

Used for: how quickly prices moved from Osaka to a nearby market (Money and prices)
