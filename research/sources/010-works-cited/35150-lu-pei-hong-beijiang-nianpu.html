Lü Pei 呂培 and others, Hong Beijiang xiansheng nianpu 洪北江先生年譜 (A chronology of Mr. Hong Beijiang), in Hong Beijiang shiwen ji 洪北江詩文集, Sibu congkan edition, Wikisource transcription (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/洪北江詩文集_(四部叢刊本)/年譜)

What it is: A year-by-year chronology of the life of Hong Liangji, compiled by his followers and printed with his collected works, saying where he was and held office in each year and what he wrote in it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates his writings and places him in each year, so it says where he was when he wrote the essays the record cites. It is a chronology by his own circle, compiled after his death, and dates a work by the year it lists it under, with no month; it says where he was, not what place an essay's figures describe.

Used for: the year and place Hong Liangji wrote his Yiyan (Money and prices)
