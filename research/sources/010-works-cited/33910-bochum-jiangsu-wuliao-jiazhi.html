bochum-jiangsu-wuliao-jiazhi

Ruhr-Universitat Bochum, Section for the History of China, "Preface to the Database on Prices and Wages of Jiangsu Province", and its transport-cost and wage spreadsheets (in English and Chinese; https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/gc/forschung/jiangsu_province.html; the spreadsheets, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/jiangsu/jiangsu_transport.xls, https://www.oaw.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/mam/gc/content/db/jiangsu/jiangsu_wages.xls)

What it is: A database, entered row by row by a German and Chinese project, of the Jiangsu edition of the Qing Regulations and precedents on prices of materials, an official printing in eight chapters held in the Naikaku Bunko in Tokyo: for the province's districts, the prices of building materials, the wages of craftsmen (by the workday) and laborers (by the man), and the allowances for carrying materials, each row with the regulation's own remark.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one regulation read here that prices boats and porters in the same districts, in the same silver, beside the same districts' wages: stone, bricks and lime by the boatload over 100 to 200 li, and porters from the landing to the site by 100 jin a li. The edition is undated: the preface says only that a 1940 study, whose title places it in the Qianlong reign (1736-1795), used a copy with an 1830 imprint that this one lacks. Its porter rows are short carries of 1 to 4 li from the river to the site, some rows give a share of the materials' value rather than a rate by weight, and chapters 6 and 7 have no transport rows.

Used for: the official boat and porter rates of Jiangsu and the wages beside them (Money and prices)
