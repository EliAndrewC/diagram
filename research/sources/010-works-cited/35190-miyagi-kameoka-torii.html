miyagi-kameoka-torii

Miyagi Prefecture, 指定文化財〈県指定有形文化財〉亀岡八幡宮石鳥居 ("Designated cultural property: the stone torii of Kameoka Hachimangu", title translated), the prefecture's cultural-property page (in Japanese; https://www.pref.miyagi.jp/soshiki/bunkazai/15kameoka.html)

What it is: Miyagi Prefecture's page on the stone torii of Kameoka Hachimangu in Sendai, built in 1683 with the shrine's halls, giving its height and its width between the posts' centers at their feet and at their tops.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates and measures one arch of the Edo period. It is one of the three stone torii of Sendai, its plaque the gift of the fifth lord of the Sendai domain, so it shows how large an arch could be, not what a village shrine had. Its widths run from post center to post center, and it gives neither the posts' thickness nor the top beam's length, so the clear opening and the beam's overhang cannot be taken from it.

Used for: the span of a large dated arch of 1683 (Religion and the dead)
