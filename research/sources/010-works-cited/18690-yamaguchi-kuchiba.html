yamaguchi-kuchiba

Yamaguchi Prefecture, cultural properties database, 「The Kuchiba house」 (translated; original: 「口羽家住宅」) (in Japanese; https://bunkazai.pref.yamaguchi.lg.jp/sp/bunkazai/summary.asp?mid=20027)

What it is: The prefecture's page on the Kuchiba house at Hagi, an Important Cultural Property: a description of the household's rank, the main house's probable date, its original plan and its kitchen wing, a short passage for elementary-school children, and the register entry with the dimensions of the main house, its two wings and its front gate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one account read of a surviving house of a retainer of about 1,000 koku, the Matsue house's rank, with dimensions of a house built before 1868 - though measured as it stood when listed in 1974, after its move in the 1840s and a later extension (the page names an addition on the north side, where the entrance now is), so they are not surely the house's first size. It dates the house only by its style ("no documents"), gives the household's 1,018 koku as report, and as its rank at the end of the shogunate, not when the house was built, and does not say whether the two wings are original; its dimensions are far larger than the national register's for the same house (14.9 by 11.9 m against 12.9 by 10.0 m, and a kitchen wing of 5.9 by 6.9 m against the national register's single east wing of 2.0 by 3.9 m), without saying why, and it gives the house's move from facing east to facing south only as tradition ("it is said"), dated "1845 (Koka 3)", two dates a year apart.

Used for: the Kuchiba house's rank, probable date, size by the prefecture's measure, and its kitchen of board floor and earth floor (Estates and other compounds)
