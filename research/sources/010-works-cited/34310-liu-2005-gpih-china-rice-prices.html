liu-2005-gpih-china-rice-prices

Peter Lindert (transcriber), "China rice prices 961-1910", a spreadsheet for the Global Price and Income History Group (May 2006), from Guanglin William Liu, "Wrestling for Power: The State and the Economy in Later Imperial China, 1000-1770", doctoral dissertation, Harvard University, 2005 (https://gpih.ucdavis.edu/files/China_rice_prices_%20961-1910.xls)

What it is: Peng Xinwei's averages of China's rice prices, by Ming reign in taels a shi and by decade from the 960s to the 1900s in grams of silver a hectoliter as Liu converted them, with Kishimoto Mio's Suzhou prices of single years.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one rice series found that reaches the Song. Its figures are averages of scattered quotations from across the empire, not one market's; Peng's monetary history and Liu's dissertation, which made them, were not read here; and the Song figures were quoted in copper cash, which Liu turned into silver at a rate the file does not give, so they are a modern conversion, not a Song price in silver.

Used for: Song and Ming rice prices (Money and prices)
