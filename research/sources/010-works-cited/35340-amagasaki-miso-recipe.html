amagasaki-miso-recipe

Amagasaki City, "みそづくり" (a home miso recipe sheet, title translated), undated (in Japanese; https://www.city.amagasaki.hyogo.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/021/517/misodukuri.pdf)

What it is: A recipe sheet from Amagasaki City in Hyogo prefecture for making rice-koji miso at home: 500 g of dry soybeans, 1,000 g of rice koji and 300 g of coarse salt, for about 2.5 kg of finished miso, with a formula for the finished salt content.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states its ingredients and its finished weight. It uses twice as much koji as beans, a sweet, koji-rich miso, so its yield a kilogram of beans stands for that kind of miso only.

Used for: a present-day yield of a koji-rich miso from a kilogram of dry soybeans, with its proportions (Money and prices)
