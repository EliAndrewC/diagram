miso-kenko-qa50

みそ健康づくり委員会 (the Miso Health Promotion Committee), 『みそ知り博士のQ&A 50』 ("Dr. Miso's 50 questions and answers", title translated), revised printing of March 2014 (first printed 2007; in Japanese; https://miso.or.jp/misoonline/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/misoqa1.pdf)

What it is: A booklet of questions and answers about miso published by the committee under the supervision of the Central Miso Research Institute (中央味噌研究所), with a table of the main kinds of miso by region, each with its usual koji ratio, salt content and aging time, and the definition of the koji ratio.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the usual proportions of an ordinary salty red rice miso, measuring the koji by the rice or barley it is made from. They are the proportions of today's factory-made miso: the booklet describes the standard salty rice miso it says makes nearly 80% of industrial output, and says home miso-making, once general, is now uncommon. So it shows nothing of what an Edo-period or earlier household used. Its salt figure is the salt content of the finished miso, not an amount put in, and it does not say whether the beans and grain of its koji ratio are weighed dry. It states no yield, how much miso a measure of beans makes.

Used for: the proportions of an ordinary present-day rice miso (Money and prices)
