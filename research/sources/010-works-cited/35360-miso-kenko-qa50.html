みそ健康づくり委員会 (the Miso Health Committee), 『みそ知り博士のQ&A 50』 ("Dr. Miso's 50 questions and answers", title translated), 2011 (in Japanese; https://miso.or.jp/misoonline/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/misoqa1.pdf)

What it is: A booklet of questions and answers about miso from the committee set up by the miso industry, with a table of the main kinds of miso by region, each with its usual koji ratio, salt content and aging time, and the definition of the koji ratio.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the industry's own proportions for an ordinary salty red rice miso. It states no yield, how much miso a measure of beans makes.

Used for: the proportions of an ordinary present-day rice miso (Money and prices)
