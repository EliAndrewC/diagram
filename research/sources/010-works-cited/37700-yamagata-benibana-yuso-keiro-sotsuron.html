yamagata-benibana-yuso-keiro-sotsuron

"近世後期最上紅花の輸送経路に関する一考察" ("A study of the routes by which Mogami safflower was carried in the late Edo period", title translated), a graduation thesis held in Yamagata University's repository, its author not named on the pages read (in Japanese; https://yamagata.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2979/files/Graduation_Thesis.pdf)

What it is: A student thesis on how safflower went from the Yamagata basin to Kyoto: down the Mogami river, by sea to Tsuruga and across Lake Biwa, or round by the western sea route to Osaka. It prints seventeen documents, among them a waybill of 1763 with the freight sent along, and explains how the dried safflower was bagged and loaded.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one work read that states the weight of a load of safflower and a freight a load from the river port to Kyoto. It is a graduation thesis, not a reviewed study: its load of 32 kan is the standard packing, which it says actual shipments departed from, and its comparison of the two routes' days is its own inference from a rice ship's log.

Used for: the weight of a load of safflower, the freight of 1763 and the Kyoto wholesalers' commission (Money and prices)
