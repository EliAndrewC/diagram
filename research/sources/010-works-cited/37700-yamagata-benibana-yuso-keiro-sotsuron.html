yamagata-benibana-yuso-keiro-sotsuron

"近世後期最上紅花の輸送経路に関する一考察" ("A study of the routes by which Mogami safflower was carried in the late Edo period", title translated), a graduation thesis by 森田昌一 (Morita Shoichi) for the 2008 academic year, History and Heritage course of Kyoto University of Art and Design's correspondence division, held in Yamagata University's repository (in Japanese; https://yamagata.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2979/files/Graduation_Thesis.pdf)

What it is: A student thesis on how safflower went from the Yamagata basin to Kyoto: down the Mogami river, by sea to Tsuruga and across Lake Biwa, or round by the western sea route to Osaka. It prints seventeen documents, among them a waybill of 1763 with the freight sent along, and explains how the dried safflower was bagged and loaded.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one work read that states the weight of a load of safflower and a freight a load from the river port to Kyoto. It is a graduation thesis, not a reviewed study: its load of 32 kan is the standard packing, which it says actual shipments departed from, and its comparison of the two routes' days is its own inference from a rice ship's log. Its freight a load is its own division of one waybill's sum, 2 ryō 2 bu 500 mon sent from Ōishida with three loads in 1763. Its commission of 1 or 1.5 percent rests on one wholesaler's account of sale of 1854, which deducts 1.5.

Used for: the weight of a load of safflower, the freight of 1763 and the Kyoto wholesalers' commission (Money and prices)
