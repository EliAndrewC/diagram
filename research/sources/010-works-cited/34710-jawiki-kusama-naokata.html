"草間直方" ("Kusama Naokata"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/草間直方)

What it is: An encyclopedia biography of the Osaka money-changer and economic writer Kusama Naokata (1753-1831), author of 『三貨図彙』.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the compiler of the record's 1705-1719 rate series in Osaka and dates the book's completion to 1815, which is what sets those rates as a later merchant's compilation. It says nothing of the sources he compiled the early rates from.

Used for: who compiled the 1705-1719 rates, where and when (Money and prices)
