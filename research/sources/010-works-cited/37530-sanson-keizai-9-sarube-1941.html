sanson-keizai-9-sarube-1941

全国山林会聯合会 (National Federation of Forestry Associations), 『第九回山村経済実態調査報告書: 山村経済の構造と解体過程 - 青森県猿辺村に於ける実例』 (Ninth report of the survey of mountain-village economies: the structure and dissolution of a mountain village's economy, the case of Sarube village, Aomori, title translated), Tokyo, December 1941, in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1140088/1/29 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1140088/1/42 https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1140088)

What it is: A field study of one mountain village of Aomori prefecture, made with the agriculture ministry's support by university researchers who revisited a village first surveyed in 1935. Its table 22 gives what a bale of charcoal cost the village's burners to make in 1939, item by item in sen, beside a Tokyo-market survey's percentages; the text beside it says who burned charcoal there, and a later chapter lists what the village bought with a state grant of 1936.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices the baling of one bale of charcoal beside the other costs of that bale, in a named village and year, and says the baling had been largely bought. It is of 1939, in sen, in a village selling through a cooperative under inspection, and in a wartime year when charcoal was already under state price control (from 1938, with its distribution controlled from the end of 1939), so the shares of a bale's cost are those of a controlled market, not a free or a premodern one; it gives no weight for the bale; its "bale at 1 yen" is a supposition of its own text, not a recorded price; and where it says baling moved from purchase to home production it does not say whether the baling was for charcoal, rice or both.

Used for: what baling cost in a bale of charcoal at an Aomori village in 1939, and that the village's baling had been largely bought (Money and prices)
