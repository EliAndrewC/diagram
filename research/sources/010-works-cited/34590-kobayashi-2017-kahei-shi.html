kobayashi-2017-kahei-shi

Kobayashi Noburu (小林延人), 「近世・近代日本貨幣史の基礎的研究」 ("Basic studies in the history of money in early modern and modern Japan", title translated), in Bank of Japan Institute for Monetary and Economic Studies, Currency Museum, 『常設展示リニューアルの記録』, 2017 (in Japanese; https://www.imes.boj.or.jp/cm/research/kinken/mod/cm_201703kinsei.pdf)

What it is: A historian's survey, written for the currency museum's renewed exhibition, of Japanese money from the late medieval period to the Meiji period: copper coin, gold and silver, the recoinages and their aims, the changers and the domains' notes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how Osaka's copper rate was set, how bad coin passed after 1569 with a surcharge added to its count, at a discount to good coin,, and the recoinages' effects on the money supply, prices and the shogunate's gains. Its figures for the recoinages' gains are estimates it takes from other studies, some named and some not.

Used for: Osaka's daily copper rate, bad coin passed at a discount, and who gained from the recoinages (Money and prices)
