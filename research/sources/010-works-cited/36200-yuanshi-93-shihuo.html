元史 (History of the Yuan, title translated), volume 93, 食貨一 ("Food and goods, part 1"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/元史/卷093)

What it is: The first part of the revenue treatise in the official history of the Yuan dynasty, compiled in the early Ming, on land registers, farming, the grain tax and the summer tax.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the summer tax of 1296 in the south, payable in cotton cloth among other goods. It states the rule and not how much cotton was paid, and, beyond setting Huguang apart until 1299 and exempting official land, it does not say how far the rule was carried out.

Used for: the Yuan summer tax payable in cotton cloth (Money and prices)
