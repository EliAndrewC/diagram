yuanshi-93-shihuo

元史 (History of the Yuan, title translated), volume 93, 食貨一 ("Food and goods, part 1"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/元史/卷093)

What it is: The first part of the revenue treatise in the official history of the Yuan dynasty, compiled in the early Ming, on land registers, farming, the grain and summer taxes, household levies, the sea transport of grain and paper money.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the summer tax of 1296 in the south, payable in cotton cloth among other goods. It states the rule and not how much cotton was paid: the tax was reckoned in paper money per shi of grain (one to three guan by region, outside Huguang), the goods were taken in its place at current prices, and its only totals, for 1328, are in paper money. So it shows that cotton cloth was accepted in payment, not that any quantity was paid. It says Huguang paid a household levy in place of the summer tax until 1299, and it exempts official land.

Used for: the Yuan summer tax payable in cotton cloth (Money and prices)
