宇野功一 (Uno Koichi), 「近世博多祇園山笠における当番町制度と当番費用徴収法」 ("The duty-ward system and the levying of duty costs in the early-modern Hakata Gion Yamakasa", title translated), 『国立歴史民俗博物館研究報告』 (Bulletin of the National Museum of Japanese History) 121 (March 2005) (in Japanese; https://rekihaku.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/1336/files/kenkyuhokoku_121_02.pdf)

What it is: A study of how the wards of Hakata took turns at building the floats of the Gion festival in the Edo period and how a ward on duty raised the money, from ward records and rules.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one budget found for a festival's cost to the townsmen who put it on: Gyo-machi's estimate of 1838 for building its float, and the contribution the other wards gave. It is one ward's estimate of a float, not an account of what was spent nor of a priests' rite; its share of the whole duty rests on another ward's budget of 1861 taken as Gyo-machi's; and its figure for the other wards' contribution is the author's reckoning from labor quotas of 1691, not a recorded payment. It is also one city's festival, run in a way the author calls nearly without parallel in Japan: a ward's turn came only once in several years to more than a decade, so the figure is what a ward had saved up to spend on one turn, not a yearly cost; and it falls in late Edo, when he says these costs had soared and wards that were not rich had to find new ways to raise them.

Used for: what a ward's festival float cost (Money and prices)
