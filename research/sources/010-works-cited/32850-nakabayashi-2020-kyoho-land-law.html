nakabayashi-2020-kyoho-land-law

中林真幸 (Nakabayashi Masaki), 「享保元文農地法の成立―市場拡大と社会的安定の平衡―」 ("The making of the farmland law of the Kyōhō and Genbun eras: balancing market growth and social stability", title translated), 経済研究 (The Economic Review) 71, no. 2, April 2020 (in Japanese; https://hit-u.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2048820/files/keizaikenkyu07102144.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's study of the shogunate's law on pledged farmland as it was settled between the 1720s and the 1740s, read from the edicts and the court precedents, and of how it set who paid the land tax on a pledged field.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the two arrangements that decide what a lender on land took home: whether the holder of a pledge paid the field's tax and levies, or made the farmer pay them. It is about the law on the shogunate's own lands, and it gives no rate of return or price.

Used for: who paid the tax on a pledged field, and the pledge leased back to its debtor as tenant (Money and prices)
