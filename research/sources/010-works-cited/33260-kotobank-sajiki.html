kotobank-sajiki

Britannica Japan concise encyclopedia, Digital Daijisen (Shogakukan) and others, s.v. 桟敷 ("boxes, raised seats", title translated), aggregated at kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/桟敷)

What it is: The Kotobank page for the raised boxes from which festivals and plays were watched, carrying several dictionaries' entries on their history from the Heian period to the modern theater.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its Britannica entry gives Edo's pit and box prices for 1803 and how many a box seated, and its Daijisen entry quotes a line of the novel Eitaigura on boxes at a fund-raising noh. The 1803 figures are given without their source, and they are the list prices that bjork-2013-edo-kabuki-theatre traces to an illustrated theater guide of that year, which were not always what was charged, and the novel's line is fiction, so it shows what a reader of the 1680s found plausible, not a recorded price.

Used for: Edo's pit and box prices in 1803, and a fund-raising noh's box price in fiction (Money and prices)
