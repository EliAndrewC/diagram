fukui-kenshi-takefu-sales

Fukui Prefectural Archives, 『福井県史』通史編4 近世二, 「販路の拡張と打刃物問屋」 ("Widening markets and the forged-blade wholesalers", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.library-archives.pref.fukui.lg.jp/fukui/07/kenshi/T4/T4-3-01-02-03-04.htm)

What it is: A page of Fukui Prefecture's own history, 『福井県史』通史編4 近世二 (chapter 3, section 2, part 3), reading period documents, on how Takefu's sickles and vegetable knives were sold, by peddlers and then by wholesalers, with the domain's tax count and its sales figures of the 1860s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the number of sickles and vegetable knives taxed and the value sold outside the domain, both for the two kinds together, in the 1860s; it gives no price of a single blade, and its figures of the 1860s do not split knives from sickles. It does not say whether the 20,000 ryō of the Genji and Keiō years (1864-68) is one year's sales or the total for those years. The count of 807,000 blades is the domain's whole taxed output, while the 20,000 ryō is only what was sold outside the domain, so one divided by the other is not a price. These are sums of 1864-68, in a ryō debased in 1860 and swollen by the inflation after 1859, and do not compare with earlier Edo prices.

Used for: Takefu's sickle and vegetable-knife trade in the 1860s (Money and prices)
