Fukui Prefectural Archives, 『福井県史』通史編4 近世二, 「販路の拡張と打刃物問屋」 ("Widening markets and the forged-blade wholesalers", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.library-archives.pref.fukui.lg.jp/fukui/07/kenshi/T4/T4-3-01-02-03-04.htm)

What it is: The same history's page on how Takefu's sickles and vegetable knives were sold, by peddlers and then by wholesalers, with the domain's tax count and its sales figures of the 1860s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the number of sickles and vegetable knives taxed and the value sold outside the domain, both for the two kinds together, in the 1860s; it gives no price of a single blade and does not split knives from sickles.

Used for: Takefu's sickle and vegetable-knife trade in the 1860s (Money and prices)
