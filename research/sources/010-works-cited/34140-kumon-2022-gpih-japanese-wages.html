kumon-2022-gpih-japanese-wages

Yuzuru Kumon, "Japanese wages, prices, and living standards 1600-1938", a spreadsheet for the Global Price and Income History Group, the data of his "The Labor-Intensive Path: Wages, Incomes, and the Work Year in Japan, 1610-1890", Journal of Economic History 82.2 (2022) (https://gpih.ucdavis.edu/files/Japanese%20Wages.xlsx)

What it is: An economic historian's yearly series, 1600-1938, of a farm servant's cash wage and the retail prices of the goods of a living-standard basket in Japan, in silver monme to 1867 and yen after, each good's sources named and every estimated figure marked with a fill.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Osaka's rice in every year and, from the later 1700s, recorded prices of soybeans, barley, edible and lamp oil and cloth, taken mostly from the Mitsui Bunko's printed price tables, which were not read here. Many of its cells are estimates from the price of rice, and every one for fish, other beans and buckwheat before the 1880s; its cloth is headed "linen" without saying what fiber or width, and its wage is a farm servant's year, not a town laborer's day.

Used for: Osaka's recorded prices of rice, soybeans, barley, oils and cloth by era (Money and prices)
