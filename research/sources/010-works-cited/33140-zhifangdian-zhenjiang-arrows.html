zhifangdian-zhenjiang-arrows

Gujin Tushu Jicheng (欽定古今圖書集成), 方輿彙編, 職方典, volume 731, "Zhenjiang prefecture: land tax, part 3" (in Chinese; Wikisource transcription; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/欽定古今圖書集成/方輿彙編/職方典/第0731卷)

What it is: A volume of the imperial encyclopedia compiled in the early eighteenth century, copying from the gazetteers of Zhenjiang prefecture, on the lower Yangzi, each county's land-tax accounts: the land on the registers and the goods and silver each county owed the throne, as the old Ming fiscal book set them and as the Qing commuted them into silver from 1646.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the price per arrow at which Jintan county's levy of arrows was booked in the Ming fiscal book, a primary figure for what an ordinary war arrow was reckoned to cost. It is an accounting price, set by officials to value a levy, not a price paid in a shop; the fiscal book is not dated beyond being the old one the Qing replaced; and the silver rate per arrow at which the Qing commuted the levy in 1646 is a tax rate, not a cost. The Wikisource punctuation was added by a machine.

Used for: the Ming book price of a war arrow in Jintan county (Money and prices)
