吴海波 (Wu Haibo), 「清代私盐盛行的经济视角思考」 ("An economic view of the spread of private salt in the Qing", title translated), 盐文化研究论丛 (Studies in salt culture), volume 4, pp. 41-46 (in Chinese; https://tsjyb.jsbc.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/75/69/7346c62b453cbd4126bdc45fd5a0/bca8ad31-2170-4285-b227-c25c94f43143.pdf)

What it is: An economic historian's essay on why smuggled salt flourished under the Qing monopoly, arguing from how far the official price stood above the cost of making salt.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the spread between the price of official salt at the saltworks and what consumers paid, and quotes the historian Xu Hong's figure for the Huai district. Its own figures are given as a range for the dynasty as a whole, with no year or place, and the volume's year is not printed on the pages read.

Used for: how far the official price of salt rose from the saltworks to the consumer in Qing China (Money and prices)
