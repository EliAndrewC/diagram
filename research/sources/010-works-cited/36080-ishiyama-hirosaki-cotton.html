ishiyama-hirosaki-cotton

Ishiyama Akiko, "弘前藩領における木綿・古手類の移入および流通について" ("The import and circulation of cotton and old clothes in the Hirosaki domain", title translated), 弘前大学國史研究 (Hirosaki University Journal of National History) no. 144, pp. 37-52, in Japanese; the year is not given on the pages read (https://web.archive.org/web/20250918060503/https://hirosaki.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/6322/files/HirodaiKokushi_144_37.pdf)

What it is: A regional historian's article on how cotton and secondhand clothing reached the Hirosaki domain at the northern tip of Honshu, built on the domain's diary, its clothing orders and port records from the 1660s to the 1850s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one study read of how a domain too cold to grow cotton clothed itself, and the only one that follows one domain's clothing orders class by class. Its summary of the orders leans on Kurotaki Jujiro's earlier studies, the orders say what was allowed rather than what anyone owned, and its Morioka material is at second hand from a study by Nishimura. Its port figure is one port's cotton for one year, and its author's remark on the Furukawa travel diary concerns one traveler's inquiry.

Used for: cotton's arrival in the cold north, the clothing orders by rank, and old clothes among the peasants of Tsugaru (Money and prices)
