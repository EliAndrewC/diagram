National Association of Home Builders, Cost of Constructing a Home - 2024, from its Construction Cost Survey of fall 2024, PDF (https://nahb.org/-/media/AB4EFC742624475A97A0A62189986FF8.ashx)

What it is: The builders' trade association's survey of what goes into the price of a new single-family house: the lot, the construction, the builder's overhead and profit, with the average finished floor area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: what it costs to build a square meter of US house without its land, as a yardstick for a laborer's room, never a figure of the setting. It is an average from 41 builders' answers for a typical new detached house of theirs, which the survey says is not meant to represent the average home built that year, and not for rental rooms; it is a cost of building once, not a rent.

Used for: what a square meter of US house costs to build (Comparative money: other societies)
