Berthold Laufer, 《马铃薯在中国的传播及其影响》 (The spread of the potato in China and its influence), a chapter of his history of the potato in the Chinese translation of Guo Yanhua and Zeng Ruiyun 《马铃薯的全球史》 (Chongqing Press, May 2026), as excerpted by the 新京报 (Beijing News), 23 May 2026 (in Chinese; https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1779527473169840.html)

What it is: A newspaper's authorized excerpt from a Chinese translation of the American anthropologist and sinologist Laufer's chapter on the potato in China, which reports Chinese gazetteers and Western witnesses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the fullest account read of the potato in China, and it does not hide the gaps: Laufer says no absolute date can be fixed. His English was not read, so a passage quoted here is an English rendering of a Chinese rendering of him, and the translation names one letter-writer as both Cunningham and Campbell. The gazetteers and Wu Qijun's book are his reports, unread here.

Used for: The white potato in China: its names, its first records, where it was grown and who ate it (Money and prices)
