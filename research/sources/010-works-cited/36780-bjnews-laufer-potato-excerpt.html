Berthold Laufer, 《马铃薯在中国的传播及其影响》 (The spread of the potato in China and its influence), a chapter of his history of the potato in the Chinese translation of Guo Yanhua and Zeng Ruiyun 《马铃薯的全球史》 (Chongqing Press, May 2026), as excerpted by the 新京报 (Beijing News), 23 May 2026 (in Chinese; https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1779527473169840.html)

What it is: A newspaper's authorized excerpt from a Chinese translation of the American anthropologist and sinologist Laufer's chapter on the potato in China, which reports Chinese gazetteers and Western witnesses. The original is evidently his The American Plant Migration, Part I: The Potato (Field Museum, 1938), edited from his manuscript after his death in 1934.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the fullest account read of the potato in China, and it does not hide the gaps: Laufer says no absolute date can be fixed. His English was not read, so a passage quoted here is an English rendering of a Chinese rendering of him, and the translation has a James Cunningham writing on Chusan and, at the same date, a James Campbell on the coast near Ningbo, apparently one man under two surnames. The excerpt is abridged by the newspaper's editor and shows no notes, the gazetteers and Wu Qijun's book are his reports, unread here, and it gives Japan one sentence, pointing ahead to a discussion it leaves out. His reading of which plant an old record means is not settled. He takes the 马铃薯 of the 1700 Songxi gazetteer as the earliest Chinese record of the potato, but Zhai Qianxiang and Huang Heqing (或問 WAKUMON 33, 2018, pp. 13-14) hold it is the air yam 黄独 (Dioscorea bulbifera), a bitter climber on trees, and the excerpt's wording of that entry (菜依树生, 略如铃子) differs from Huang's (叶依树生, 果如铃). The excerpt's section on the foreign potato also quotes a Dong'a gazetteer on 番薯, sown by order from 1752, which is the ordinary name of the sweet potato.

Used for: The white potato in China, its names, first records, growing and eaters, and Laufer's doubt on a Dutch introduction to Japan (Money and prices)
