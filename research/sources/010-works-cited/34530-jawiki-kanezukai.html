jawiki-kanezukai

"金遣い" ("kanezukai", gold use), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/金遣い)

What it is: A short encyclopedia article on the custom of reckoning in gold in eastern Japan, against silver in the west, in the Edo period, citing the Kokushi daijiten and two histories of money, and quoting the Kinsei fuzokushi of 1853.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the border between the gold-using east and silver-using west, which it says cannot be drawn strictly, the two ways of quoting rice, and the Kanto's land tax reckoned in a unit of old copper coin and paid in gold. It gives no dates for when the border held.

Used for: the regional split between gold and silver, and the Kanto's tax paid in gold (Money and prices)
