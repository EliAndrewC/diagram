guangdong-xinyu-15

Qu Dajun (屈大均), Guangdong xinyu (廣東新語, New Sayings of Guangdong), chapter 15, on goods, the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/廣東新語/卷15)

What it is: The goods chapter of a late-seventeenth-century description of Guangdong by a scholar of the Pearl River delta, here on the province's cloths.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the far south wore in summer and where its winter cloth came from. It is one observer's account of one province; it gives no quantities, and what it says of the Han-era word is his gloss, made some fifteen centuries later.

Used for: the far south's summer cloths and its bought-in winter cotton (Money and prices)
