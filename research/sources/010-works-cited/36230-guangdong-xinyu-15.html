guangdong-xinyu-15

Qu Dajun (屈大均), Guangdong xinyu (廣東新語, New Sayings of Guangdong), chapter 15, on goods, the Chinese Wikisource transcription (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/廣東新語/卷15)

What it is: The goods chapter of a late-seventeenth-century description of Guangdong by a scholar of the Pearl River delta, here on the province's cloths.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what Guangdong wore in summer and where its winter cloth came from. It is one observer's account of one province; it gives no amount for the cloth worn or brought in from Wu and Chu, and its figures are for other things (the finest Lei kudzu at a hundred cash the foot, the length of a bolt, one county's shares of women's fibre work), and what it says of the Han-era word langan (蘭幹) is a gloss he gives without naming a source, some fifteen centuries later. It was read only in the Wikisource transcription, which the page files at 50% text quality and which has copying slips in these sections; no print edition was compared.

Used for: Guangdong's summer cloths and its bought-in winter cloth (Money and prices)
