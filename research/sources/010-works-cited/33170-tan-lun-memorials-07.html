譚綸 (Tan Lun), 譚襄敏奏議 (The Memorials of Tan Xiangmin, title translated), volume 7, the Siku Quanshu edition (in Chinese; Wikisource transcription; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/譚襄敏奏議_(四庫全書本)/卷07)

What it is: A volume of the memorials to the throne of Tan Lun, the Ming official who commanded the defense of the Jizhou frontier north of Beijing late in the 1560s, on the frontier's defenses and its troops, among them an itemized estimate, in silver for materials and labor together, of the carts, guns, powder, blades and bows needed to train and equip the troops of his command, drawn up by his subordinates and put before the throne by him.

Why it applies, and its limits: It prices a set of a bow, a string, thirty arrows and a quiver as one item, a primary late-Ming figure that bounds what an arrow cost. It prices the set, not the arrow, so a single arrow's share is this project's arithmetic and an upper bound; it is an estimate, not an account of money spent; and the Wikisource transcription drops a character or two, so that the set's string is read from the sense.

Used for: the late-Ming cost of a bow with thirty arrows and a quiver (Money and prices)
