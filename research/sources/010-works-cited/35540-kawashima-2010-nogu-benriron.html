kawashima-2010-nogu-benriron

河島一仁 (Kawashima Kazuhito), 「『農具便利論』における堺の「農具鍛冶」と「其処の鍛冶」」 ("The 'farm-tool smiths' of Sakai and 'the local smiths' in the Nogu benriron", title translated), 『立命館地理学』 22 (2010), pp. 75-87 (in Japanese; https://www.ritsumei.ac.jp/lt/area/assets/file/research/geo/letter/22/22-2010-kawashima.pdf)

What it is: A historical geographer's reading of Okura Nagatsune's book on farm tools, 『農具便利論』, published in 1822, on the Sakai and Osaka smiths whose tools it priced and the local smiths it told readers to have copy them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes one hoe's price from the book: the Settsu Nishinari-district hoe for loam, 5 monme 6 bu of silver beside its picture and 5 monme 5 bu in the price table, two figures the book itself gives for the same hoe. It also quotes the book's note that the table is the Sakai and Osaka farm-tool smiths' prices, for tools ordered through an Osaka agent, so the price is a Kinai maker's, not a village smith's. Its list of the table's 40 items gives their names only; any other price is only in its picture of the table.

Used for: a Sakai or Osaka hoe's price in 1822, and the wooden-bodied hoes the book pictures first (Money and prices)
