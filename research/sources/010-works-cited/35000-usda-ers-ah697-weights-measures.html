US Department of Agriculture, Economic Research Service, with the Agricultural Marketing Service, the Agricultural Research Service and the National Agricultural Statistics Service, Weights, Measures, and Conversion Factors for Agricultural Commodities and Their Products, Agricultural Handbook No. 697, 1992, PDF (https://www.ers.usda.gov/media/7780/ah-697.pdf?v=67628)

What it is: The government's handbook of the standard weights of farm goods in their trade units, such as the pounds in a bushel of each grain, and the factors that turn one unit into another.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the bushel weight that turns the farm price of wheat and soybeans per bushel into a price per kilogram. Its weights are approximate net weights, the trade's standard for each unit rather than a weighing of any one load, and the handbook dates from 1992.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
