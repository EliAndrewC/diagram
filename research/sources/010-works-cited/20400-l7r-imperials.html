l7r-imperials

The GM's campaign notes, l7r.md, "Imperials" (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md#imperials) (https://github.com/EliAndrewC/l7r/blob/master/setting/l7r.md)

What it is: The setting's own notes, written by the game master, in the section "Imperials": that every domain has an Imperial samurai appointed as its Imperial Magistrate, with duties the Emerald Charter defines, and a staff of roughly 25 assistants at the magistrate's office in the domain capital.

Why it applies, and its limits: This is canon rather than evidence: what the Imperial Magistrate is, and how large a staff the office keeps, are the setting's to decide. There is no historical limitation to state, because the notes are not making a historical claim; they do not say how large the magistrate's compound is or where its staff live, which the capitals page works out from Edo's town magistracies and a domain's estates in Edo as analogues.

Used for: what the Imperial Magistrate is and the size of its staff, behind the Imperial Magistrate's compound in a capital (Domain capitals)
