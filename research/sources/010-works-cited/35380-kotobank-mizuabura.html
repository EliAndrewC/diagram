kotobank-mizuabura

「水油」 ("liquid oil", title translated), on Kotobank: the entries of 『デジタル大辞泉』 and 『精選版日本国語大辞典』 (Shogakukan), and the passages on 水油 from the articles 「油」 ("oil", title translated) and 「ナタネ」 ("rapeseed", title translated) in 『世界大百科事典（旧版）』 (Heibonsha) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B0%B4%E6%B2%B9)

What it is: Two dictionary definitions of the word 水油, a liquid oil for the hair or for lamps, and an encyclopedia's account of how pressed rapeseed oil (水油) and cottonseed oil (白油) became the chief oils of the Edo period, with 水油 the one good shipped out of Osaka in 1714 worth over 10,000 kan of silver.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says which oil the Edo period meant by 水油 and by 白油, the names a trade order of 1860 and a table of Osaka prices use. Its encyclopedia passages are sentences in longer articles, not a study of the oil trade, and they say nothing of the 1860s.

Used for: which goods' prices the opening of the ports changed in Edo (Money and prices)
