takaoka-maeda-toshinaga-nuno-letter

Takaoka City Museum, 「年未詳4月29日付前田利長書状（九兵へ宛）」 ("Letter of Maeda Toshinaga to Kyube, 29th day of the 4th month, year unknown", title translated), catalog entry with transcription and notes, in Bunka Isan Online, the Agency for Cultural Affairs (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/614440)

What it is: The museum's entry for a letter in the hand of Maeda Toshinaga (1562-1614), lord of Kaga and founder of Takaoka, ordering his retainer to pay for twenty rolls of cloth bought at Nakata, with the museum's transcription, its gist of the letter and its notes on Etchu hemp cloth.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one lot of Etchu hemp cloth and the silver paid for it in the early 1600s, from a primary letter. The letter carries no year, so it is dated only by Toshinaga's death in 1614; it counts the cloth in rolls of unknown length, not in tan; and it is a domain lord's purchase of a lot he judged worse than another, not a market price list.

Used for: the price of a lot of Etchu hemp cloth before 1614 (Money and prices)
