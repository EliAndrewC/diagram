"江戸時代の三貨制度" ("The three-currency system of the Edo period"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/三貨制度)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the Edo period's gold, silver and copper currencies, their official rates and their market rates over time, with single-day price lists drawn from published price histories.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the official rate of 1700 and two single days' rates, Osaka in 1766 and Kyoto in 1867, citing the Mitsui Bunko's price study for them. Its rates are single days, and its account of the long fall of copper is a summary without figures by year.

Used for: the official rate of 1700 and the Osaka rate of 1766 (Money and prices)
