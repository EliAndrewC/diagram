fuchi-kotobank

"扶持" (fuchi, "rice stipend"), Kotobank, the entries of several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%89%B6%E6%8C%81-124992)

What it is: The Kotobank page gathering several reference works' entries on fuchi, the rice allowance paid to retainers in the Edo period, reckoned in units of one person's daily ration.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its entries agree on the ration the stipend was reckoned in: 5 gō a person a day, 1 koku 8 to a year, and three of them name it as brown rice. It is the official allowance by which samurai were paid, not a measurement of what anyone ate, and the entries do not say whether a stipend was meant to feed its holder alone.

Used for: the Edo-period one-person ration of 5 gō of brown rice a day (Money and prices)
