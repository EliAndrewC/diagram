shomonya-saiko-1851-tools

『諸問屋再興調』 (Records of the restoration of the wholesale guilds), 24 volumes, [41] 「二十 諸色引下直段」 ("20: reduced prices of goods", title translated), a manuscript copy in the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/2548713)

What it is: The returns the Edo wholesale guilds filed in 1851, the year the guilds were restored, of their prices cut, each guild listing its goods at the old price in black ink and the reduced price in red. This volume holds the returns of cloth, paper, lacquer, candle, drug and other guilds, among them the tool and blade wholesalers' (大工道具打物問屋) list of carpenters' tools, knives, needles and whetstones, dated the ninth month of 1851.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a price list of the time, from the city, for kitchen knives by kind and grade. Its prices are what a wholesaler charged, not what a household paid in a shop, and they were cut by order, so the old prices are the market's and the new ones a guild's promise to the magistrate. It is handwritten in a cursive hand and its knife entries are priced by the ten (十枚ニ付) by this project's reading of where that note stands on the page.

Used for: Edo's wholesale prices of kitchen knives in 1851 (Money and prices)
