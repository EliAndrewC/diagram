北野博司 (Kitano Hiroshi), 「元和7年に行われた徳川期大坂城石垣修築工事の組織と技術」 ("The Management and Technology of the Tokugawa-era Osaka Castle Stonewall Reconstruction Project in 1621", the paper's own English title), 『東北芸術工科大学紀要』 (Bulletin of Tohoku University of Art and Design) 33 (2026) (in Japanese; http://archives.tuad.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/tuad-bulletin33_1_KITANO.pdf)

What it is: An archaeologist's study of how the daimyo houses organized the 1621 (Genna 7) repair of three stretches of Osaka Castle's walls that had collapsed after the first phase of the Tokugawa rebuilding in 1620. It follows chiefly one house, the Nakagawa of Oka in Bungo, through copies of its letters, and checks them against the walls as they stand: the retainers it sent and the day laborers it hired, how the foundations were relaid, and who bore the costs.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its notes give the day wages of hauling stone and of quarry labor in 1620-1621, set against each other. Its wages are those of one shogunal castle works at Osaka, the country's great market, in the castle-building boom of the 1620s. They were paid to great numbers of men hired at the site for a rushed repair, with hundreds of day laborers at its height, so they can run above what a village paid for the same work. They are given in a note citing documents the paper does not reprint.

Used for: what carrying stone and quarry labor were paid (Money and prices)
