北野博司 (Kitano Hiroshi), 「元和7年に行われた徳川期大坂城石垣修築工事の組織と技術」 ("The organization and techniques of the repair of the stone walls of Tokugawa Osaka Castle in Genna 7", title translated), 『東北芸術工科大学紀要』 (Bulletin of Tohoku University of Art and Design) 33 (2026) (in Japanese; http://archives.tuad.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/tuad-bulletin33_1_KITANO.pdf)

What it is: An archaeologist's study of how the daimyo houses organized the first phase of rebuilding Osaka Castle's walls in 1620-1621, from their letters and accounts: the men they sent, the stone they quarried and hauled, and the wages they paid.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its notes give the day wages of hauling stone and of quarry labor in 1620-1621, set against each other. Its wages are castle work's, set by the houses for great numbers of men, and are given in a note citing documents the paper does not reprint.

Used for: what carrying stone and quarry labor were paid (Money and prices)
