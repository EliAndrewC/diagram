kokugakuin-eos-torii

Kokugakuin University, Encyclopedia of Shinto, "Torii" (https://d-museum.kokugakuin.ac.jp/eos/detail/?id=9635)

What it is: The Kokugakuin University Encyclopedia of Shinto entry on the torii, by Mori Mizue, a scholarly reference work in English.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the first, largest arch at the approach to the grounds and others within the precinct, and says a torii may be built into a sacred fence or a corridor. Its limits: it describes shrines as they stand today (it names concrete and plastic torii) and dates none of this arrangement, so the record takes from it the long-standing form, not any one period's practice; and it is normative and general, with no distances or village examples.

Used for: the first arch at the entry to the grounds, and the torii built into a sacred fence (Religion and the dead).
