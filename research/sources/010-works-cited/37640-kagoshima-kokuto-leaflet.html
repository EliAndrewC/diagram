kagoshima-kokuto-leaflet

Kagoshima Prefecture, a one-page illustrated leaflet on the history of black sugar in the islands south of Kyushu, published after the islands' way of making black sugar was listed as a cultural property in 2024 (in Japanese; https://www.pref.kagoshima.jp/ba08/kokuto/documents/128908_20260617134239-1.pdf)

What it is: A prefectural leaflet for the general reader, with history columns that name the studies they draw on. One column reports, from two documents of 1830 as a 1983 study by Matsushita Shiro reads them, how much dearer goods were for the Amami islanders than at Osaka's market, and what share of the sugar reaching Osaka was Satsuma's.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the one ratio found between the islands' end and Osaka's for Satsuma's sugar: how much more sugar an islander gave for a koku of rice than the Osaka market would ask. It is a summary at second hand, with no price at either end, and it does not say which grades, islands or months the range covers. The study it rests on was not read.

Used for: how much more sugar an Amami islander gave for rice than Osaka's market asked, in 1830 (Money and prices)
