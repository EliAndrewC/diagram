korea-archives-tobacco

National Archives of Korea, "담배" (Tobacco), in 기록으로 만나는 대한민국 (Meeting the Republic of Korea through its records; https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/koreaOfRecord/domesticSmoke.do)

What it is: A government archive's short public page on the history of smoking in Korea, most of it on the twentieth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the usual view of when tobacco arrived, against which the earliest texts can be set, and names an early royal-annals mention. It ties no source to the usual view, ending only with a general list (Lee Young-hak's 2005 article on tobacco's spread in late Joseon, the annals site and Naver), and the annals entry was not read.

Used for: Tobacco in Korea (Money and prices)
