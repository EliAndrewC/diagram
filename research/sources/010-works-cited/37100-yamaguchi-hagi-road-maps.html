yamaguchi-hagi-road-maps

Yamada Minoru, 「Special commentary: the maps of the Hagi domain, part two - three Bocho maps that drew the highways」 (translated; original: 「特別解説｜萩藩の絵図【後編】街道を描いた防長絵図三選」), Yamaguchi Prefecture's official tourism site (in Japanese; https://yamaguchi-tourism.jp/feature/kochizu-special2)

What it is: An article on the prefecture's tourism site by Yamada Minoru, a curator at the Yamaguchi Prefectural Museum and formerly a researcher at the prefectural archives, revised from his commentary in the museum's 2021 exhibition catalog 『絵図で見る防長の街道』. It describes three sets of Hagi-domain maps that show the highways, held in the Yamaguchi Prefectural Archives: two road maps (a map of 1742 drawn for the domain lord's tour of the domain, and the road maps from Hagi to Edo, made at different times by route, the Sanyodo volumes about 1764) and the domain's village maps begun in 1720. It also describes how the shogunate's provincial maps showed the highways.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes what period maps of Japan drew on a road - its red line on the provincial and village maps, and the marks the 1742 road map put on it - by a specialist in the region's maps who has handled the originals. It speaks for maps of one domain, Hagi, and for the shogunate's provincial maps in general, and says nothing of how a town's own plans drew its streets.

Used for: how period maps drew a road, and the one mark found drawn on one, a horizontal line at a steep slope (Ways)
