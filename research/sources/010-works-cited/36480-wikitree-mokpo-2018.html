wikitree-mokpo-2018

A Wikitree news report on the new official history of the city of Mokpo (https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/339948)

What it is: A Korean news site's report listing the firsts recorded for the city of Mokpo in its new city history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page found that dates the first successful trial of American upland cotton in Korea, to 1904 on Gohado island. It gives the date in one unsourced line of a list of firsts, which the city says it will use to raise civic pride and its brand image and to promote tourism, and a claim to be first is what such a list flatters. It reports the city history at second hand, and that history was not read; a monument of 1936 it mentions shows the claim is an old one, not that it is right. The trial came after the period the setting is modeled on, so the page can carry only when this cotton reached Korea, and nothing about how cotton was grown, what it yielded or what it cost.

Used for: The first upland cotton in Korea (Money and prices)
