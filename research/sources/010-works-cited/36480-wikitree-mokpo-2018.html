wikitree-mokpo-2018

A Wikitree news report on the new official history of the city of Mokpo (https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/339948)

What it is: A Korean news site's report listing the firsts recorded for the city of Mokpo in its new city history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page found that dates the first successful trial of American upland cotton in Korea, to 1904 on Gohado island. It reports the city history at second hand, and that history was not read; a monument of 1936 it mentions shows the claim is an old one, not that it is right.

Used for: The first upland cotton in Korea (Money and prices)
