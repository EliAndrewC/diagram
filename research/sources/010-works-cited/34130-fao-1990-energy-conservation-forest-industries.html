Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, Energy conservation in the mechanical forest industries, FAO Forestry Paper 93 (Rome, 1990), Appendix IV, "Comparison of energy values" (https://www.fao.org/4/T0269E/t0269e0c.htm)

What it is: An FAO manual on the use of energy in sawmills and wood-processing plants, whose appendix tabulates the heating values of commercial fuels and of wood and wood residues at typical moisture levels.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the heating value of seasoned, air-dried wood that turns a price of firewood by weight into a price by energy. It is a modern engineering figure that depends on species and moisture; it measures no wood of Edo-period Japan, and the record uses it only as an assumption.

Used for: the heat in a kilogram of firewood (Money and prices)
