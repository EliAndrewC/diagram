清稗類鈔 (Classified Anecdotes of the Qing, title translated), part 022, the entry 湘淮軍餉 ("the pay of the Hunan and Huai armies"), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/清稗類鈔/022)

What it is: A collection of anecdotes on Qing institutions and life compiled by Xu Ke and published in 1917; this entry tells how Hu Linyi paid the Hunan Army in Hubei from 1859.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the scale the army was paid by and the rates at which pay in silver and cash were mixed against the market. It is an anecdote written down decades later, with no source named, for one army and province.

Used for: soldiers' pay in silver and cash against the market rate (Money and prices)
