takayama-jinya-viewing-guide

Takayama Jin'ya (Gifu Prefecture), 見学ガイド ("Visitor's guide", title translated), the official guide to the restored office (in Japanese; https://jinya.gifu.jp/wp/wp-content/themes/original_theme/images/viewing-guide/viewing-guide-ja.pdf)

What it is: The Takayama Jin'ya's own numbered guide to its rooms, saying what each was used for and by whom: among them the binding room (chotojiba), where official documents and ledgers were drawn up and bound and then kept in the ledger storehouse beside it, the writing room, where the officials it calls shoyaku drew up documents, and the genkan room, where the intendant met the shogunate's envoys.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the office's own account of the rooms where its paperwork was done, which is what a plan's writing and binding rooms are drawn from. It gives no room a size in mats, says the ledger storehouse survives only as remains, so it gives where that storehouse stood but not its form or size, describes the office of a whole province as restored, not a small office, and reads 書役 as shoyaku where other pages read kakiyaku.

Used for: the binding room and the ledger storehouse beside it; who worked in the writing room; the genkan room as the place of official greetings, and the envoys' room (Estates and other compounds)
