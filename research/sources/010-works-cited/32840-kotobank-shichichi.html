kotobank-shichichi

佐藤常雄 (Satō Tsuneo), 質地 ("Pledged land", title translated), in 改訂新版 世界大百科事典 (Heibonsha's encyclopedia, revised edition), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/質地-520863)

What it is: A signed encyclopedia article on land pledged as security for a loan in Japan, chiefly in the Edo period: how pledging took the place of the sale of fields the shogunate banned in 1643, the shogunate's rules on pledges from 1666 to 1744, and how the lender was paid.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the page that says how a pledge of land paid its lender, from the land's yield rather than from interest. It describes the shogunate's own rules, not the domains', and it gives no figure for what land was pledged or sold for.

Used for: how a pledge of farmland worked in Edo Japan and how its lender was paid (Money and prices)
