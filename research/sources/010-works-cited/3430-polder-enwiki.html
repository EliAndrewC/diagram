What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the polder, the diked tract below the surrounding water level; an encyclopedia article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives full enclosure by dikes, the floor below the surrounding water some or all of the time, exchange through sluices and the permanent flood risk, which is the enclosure rule this page states; Netherlands-weighted and spanning medieval to twentieth-century reclamation, but carrying the Chinese case (Kunshan's polders, the Jiangnan polders built mostly from the tenth to thirteenth centuries); its drainage sentence does not say what drives the pumps, and only hand-, foot- or ox-driven pumps and the tidal sluice belong to our setting.

Used for: why a polder is enclosed on every side (Field archetypes)
