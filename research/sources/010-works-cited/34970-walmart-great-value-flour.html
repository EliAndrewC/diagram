Walmart.com, search results for "great value flour", seen October 25, 2026 (https://www.walmart.com/search?q=great+value+flour)

What it is: The national retailer's online listing of its own-brand wheat flours, all-purpose and whole wheat, in bags of 2 to 25 lb, each with the price it charged that day.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: what the cheapest plain household bag of flour costs a US shopper, as a yardstick beside a premodern price of wheat, never a figure of the setting. Each is the price for buying online on the day it was seen, not a price in a store; the page keeps no history, so a later reading may differ.

Used for: what the standard basket's goods cost in the US today and how much of each an American uses (Comparative money: other societies)
