sagara-2012-matsue-tatara

Sagara Eisuke (相良英輔), "松江藩における近世中・後期たたら製鉄業の展開" ("The course of tatara ironmaking in Matsue domain in the middle and later Edo period", title translated), 2012, in Hiroshima University of Economics' repository (in Japanese; https://hue.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/33/files/sagara201203_2.pdf)

What it is: A study of the ironmasters of Matsue domain, in Izumo, from the 1700s to the first years of Meiji, written from the Tanabe house's papers and the domain's iron office records: the domain's buying of their iron, their debts, and their turn from pig iron to bar iron.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how Izumo's iron met the Osaka market in the 1780s: through an iron office the shogunate set up there, which fixed what the iron was bought for. It gives no price at either end, and its account of how the office worked is a few sentences, though its first section is on what the office did to the ironmasters: two leading houses had their lands, goods and furnaces taken by the domain, in 1781 and 1785. The paper's own fifth section argues that such a seizure was a mortgage written into the loan, not a ruin: the house went on farming and worked its furnaces as the domain's appointed manager, and repaid. It is the author who lays both failures to the iron office, which he describes as already well known, citing no record of the office itself.

Used for: the shogunate's iron office at Osaka, 1780 to 1787 (Money and prices)
