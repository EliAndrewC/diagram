sagara-2009-tagi-sakuraike

Sagara Eisuke, "田儀桜井家史料についての紹介と若干の分析" ("An introduction to the papers of the Sakurai house of Tagi, with some analysis", title translated), pp. 59-62 of a volume in the Shimane University repository, whose file is dated 2009 (in Japanese; https://ir.lib.shimane-u.ac.jp/38043/files/5060)

What it is: A four-page article, printed after the same author's article on Iwami, on the papers of the Sakurai house, ironmasters at Tagi in Izumo province (now Shimane prefecture). From the house's accounts it gives the yearly average, over the five years 1831 to 1835, of the pig iron its two furnaces made, the bar iron its two forges made, and what each brought in.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives an Izumo ironmaster's receipts for pig iron and for bar iron beside the loads sold, in the 1830s. They are five-year averages of one house, under the domain's management in years of famine, and the chapter does not say where or to whom the iron was sold, so the price a load is a division at an unnamed place of sale. It gives no price in Osaka.

Used for: what an Izumo ironmaster's pig iron and bar iron brought in the 1830s (Money and prices)
