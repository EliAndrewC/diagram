Li Shizhong, "列女书写、妇德规训与地域秩序——以明清乐清县志为中心的考察" ("The Writing of Chaste Females, Female Virtues Discipline and Local Order: A Case Study of Yueqing County Annals in the Ming and Qing Dynasties", its own English title), Journal of East China Normal University (Philosophy and Social Sciences) 48, no. 4 (2016): 81-91, its abstract page (in Chinese and English; https://xbzs.ecnu.edu.cn/CN/Y2016/V48/I4/81)

What it is: A journal article by a historian at East China Normal University comparing the biographies of exemplary women in successive Ming and Qing editions of the gazetteer of Yueqing county, near Wenzhou; read here only in its Chinese and English abstracts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that the gazetteers' lives of women were shaped by the local gentry's ideals of virtue. It is one county, read only in an abstract, so the record takes from it only the abstract's own conclusions.

Used for: what Chinese gazetteers' lives of women were written for (Money and prices)
