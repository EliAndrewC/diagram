JAANUS (Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System), "shimodaidokoro" 下台所 (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/node/1283)

What it is: The entry for shimodaidokoro 下台所, the lower kitchen, in JAANUS, an online English-language dictionary of Japanese art and architectural terms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the service kitchen of the leading warriors' and aristocrats' mansions from medieval times to the end of the Edo period, entered by its kitchen door onto a doma, and an upper kitchen sometimes in a separate building. Its first sense is of those great mansions only, not of a small samurai house; its second is of the lower kitchen in late Edo vernacular houses (minka).

Used for: the great mansions' kitchen door onto the doma and their kitchens in separate buildings (Estates and other compounds)
