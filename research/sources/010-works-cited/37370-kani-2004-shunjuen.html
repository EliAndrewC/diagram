kani-2004-shunjuen

可児市教育委員会 (Kani City Board of Education), Shi meisho Shunjuen: iseki hozon no tame no hakkutsu chosa hokokusho (The city Place of Scenic Beauty Shunjuen: report of the excavation for the site's preservation), 2004 (in Japanese; https://sitereports.nabunken.go.jp/files/attach/65/65477/40733_1_%E5%B8%82%E5%90%8D%E5%8B%9D%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B%E5%9C%92.pdf)

What it is: The report of the 2001-03 excavation of Shunjuen, the garden of the lower residence of the Chimura, a hatamoto family of 4,400 koku, at Kukuri in Kani (Gifu): its trenches, the pond edges, stone walls and pond beds they found, and a family account of the garden's making in 1756-65 and its remaking with an enlarged pond in about 1804-28.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the banks and beds of excavated Edo-period garden ponds at a samurai residence. It is one garden, a stroll garden of several ponds at a wealthy hatamoto's lower residence, larger than a small residence's or an office's pond garden; the passages read do not date the banks it found, so they are the garden's as it survives, not dated to its making or its remaking.

Used for: the excavated banks of one hatamoto's garden ponds (Estates and other compounds)
