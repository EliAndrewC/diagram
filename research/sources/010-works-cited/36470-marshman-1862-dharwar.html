marshman-1862-dharwar

J. C. Marshman, "Notes on the Cultivation of Cotton in the District of Dharwar; Past, Present, and Future", Journal of the Royal Asiatic Society of Great Britain and Ireland 19 (January 1862): 351-360 (https://zenodo.org/record/1742888/files/article.pdf)

What it is: A British writer's account, written while the events were recent, of the East India Company's experiments with American cotton in one district of the Bombay Presidency.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated acreages for the one district in India found where American seed took hold. Those acreages are for Dharwar alone, though it reports the seed spreading into neighboring territories, and it gives its largest acreage as "stated", not as measured.

Used for: American cotton at Dharwar (Money and prices)
