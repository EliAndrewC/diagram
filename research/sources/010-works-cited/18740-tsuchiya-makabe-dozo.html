tsuchiya-makabe-dozo

Cultural Heritage Online (Agency for Cultural Affairs), 土谷家住宅土蔵 ("The storehouse of the Tsuchiya family house", title translated) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/194369)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's entry for a storehouse at Makabe (Sakuragawa city, Ibaraki prefecture), registered as a tangible cultural property in 2003 and dated to the Edo period, 1830-1867, with a short commentary on its build.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an official register's entry for a surviving late-Edo storehouse with massive paired door leaves on its ground-floor doorway. It is one merchant town's storehouse of 29 square meters; its origin as relief building after a typhoon of 1833 is given as hearsay, and it gives no size for the doors. Its commentary lists the "massive paired doors" among the building's features without saying which opening they close (it names the east ground-floor side as the doorway), and it dates the building by the register's range of 1830-1867, not the doors themselves, which a surviving building may have had renewed.

Used for: that a storehouse's doorway closed by massive paired leaves is attested in the late Edo period (Estates and other compounds)
