shugiin-1950-genmai-koku

House of Representatives of Japan, question no. 21 of the 7th Diet, "供出玄米の重量に関する質問主意書" ("Question on the weight of delivered brown rice", title translated), submitted 28 January 1950, and the Cabinet's written answer of 7 February 1950 (in Japanese; https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_shitsumona.nsf/html/shitsumon/b007021.htm and https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_shitsumona.nsf/html/shitsumon/a007021.htm)

What it is: A Diet member's written question on why rice delivered to the government was reckoned at 150 kg a koku of brown rice when a ministry handbook said 146.3 kg, and the Cabinet's answer giving the basis of the 150 kg figure: an inspection standard of at least 400 monme for a shō of grade-3 brown rice.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an official statement of what a koku of brown rice weighed, and of the weight per shō it was derived from. It describes the rice and the grading of 1946-1950, three generations after the Edo period, so it stands for an Edo koku only by analogy, and it is a minimum for grading, not an average of what a measure held.

Used for: the weight of a koku and a shō of brown rice (Money and prices)
