shugiin-1950-genmai-koku

House of Representatives of Japan, the Cabinet's written answer of 7 February 1950 to question no. 21 of the 7th Diet, on the weight of delivered brown rice (in Japanese; https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_shitsumona.nsf/html/shitsumon/b007021.htm)

What it is: The Cabinet's answer to a Diet member's question on why rice delivered to the government was reckoned at 150 kg a koku of brown rice when a ministry handbook said 146.3 kg (the question has its own entry), giving the basis of the 150 kg figure: an inspection standard of at least 400 monme for a shō of grade-3 brown rice.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is an official statement of what a koku of brown rice weighed, and of the weight per shō it was derived from. It describes the rice and the grading of 1946-1950, three generations after the Edo period, so it stands for an Edo koku only by analogy, and it is a minimum for grading, not an average of what a measure held.

Used for: the weight of a koku and a shō of brown rice (Money and prices)
