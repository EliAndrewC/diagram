mingshi-60-li

明史 (History of the Ming, title translated), volume 60, 志第三十六 禮十四 ("Treatises 36: rites 14", the rites of mourning), Wikisource text (in classical Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/明史/卷60)

What it is: The volume of the Ming official history's treatise on rites that covers mourning and burial: the funerals of officials by rank and of commoners, with the rules of 1370 and 1372 on how much ground a grave could take and how high its mound could rise.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the grave sizes the Ming allowed each rank and the common people, so it gives a ceiling on a grave's ground. It states the rule, not what villagers actually built, gives a commoner's ground but no height for a commoner's mound, and was compiled by the Qing court, finished in 1739, from the dynasty's statutes. A commoner's ground is given only as eighteen bu "around" (塋地圍十八步): the text does not say whether "around" is the plot's whole circuit or its measure each way, nor how long its bu is, so any area taken from it is the record's own reading of the figure.

Used for: the ground a commoner's grave was allowed (Fields)
