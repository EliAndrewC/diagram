bunka-yokota-jutaku

Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, 「The former Yokota residence: main house, front gate, retirement house, two storehouses」 (translated; original: 「旧横田家住宅主屋表門隠居屋土蔵２棟」) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/182500)

What it is: The national cultural-heritage database's record of the five buildings of the former Yokota residence at Matsushiro, an Important Cultural Property: each building's date and its size, the retirement house dated to 1820.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates and measures a retirement house that still stands beside its main house. It is a middle-ranking samurai household's house in a castle town, not a farmer's, and the record gives sizes and dates but not where each building stands on the grounds.

Used for: the size and date of a retirement house of 1820 (Homesteads)
