laufer-1924-tobacco-asia

Berthold Laufer, Tobacco and Its Use in Asia, Field Museum of Natural History, Anthropology Leaflet 18, Chicago, 1924, full text on the Internet Archive (https://archive.org/stream/tobaccoitsuseina18lauf/tobaccoitsuseina18lauf_djvu.txt)

What it is: A short non-technical museum leaflet by a sinologist, by his own account a sketch of a longer unpublished manuscript, tracing how tobacco and pipes spread across Asia. It quotes Chinese, Persian and European witnesses in English and reports unnamed Japanese records; for how tobacco reached Korea it has only the Dutch captive Hamel and a Korean folk tale, beside an unsourced line on Korean tribute tobacco sent to Peking and notes on Korean pipes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one work read that covers all four countries, and it holds the words of Cocks, Asad Beg, Terry and Jahangir, and Hamel in Laufer's summary. It gives no notes and, for its Asian writers and European witnesses, never a chapter or page: a few Chinese readings carry an author or title in the running text (Chang Kiai-pin, Lu Yao, a herbal, the Hiang tsu pi ki) and can be sought only by that, and its other Chinese readings and all its Japanese ones cannot be traced. Its Chinese authors are in Laufer's own English and were not read in the original; several are unnamed, among them the writer it reports on the 1638 edict and on boys smoking by the 1640s, and it names no emperor or dynasty for its edicts of 1638 and 1641. No Japanese or Korean writer is named: the "Japanese records" are only reported, and Korea's arrival date rests on Hamel, a Dutchman. Its firsts are a 1924 synthesis: "about 1605" for Japan is inferred from Cocks's "not ten years" and said to agree with the unnamed Japanese records, which put the first Nagasaki plantations then and Portuguese leaf before 1600, and the first author it credits with "an interesting account" is Chang Kiai-pin (Zhang Jiebin), whose account it does not date and whom it places in Shan-si, though he came from Shanyin in Zhejiang.

Used for: Tobacco in China, Japan, Korea and India (Money and prices)
