laufer-1924-tobacco-asia

Berthold Laufer, Tobacco and Its Use in Asia, Field Museum of Natural History, Anthropology Leaflet 18, Chicago, 1924, full text on the Internet Archive (https://archive.org/stream/tobaccoitsuseina18lauf/tobaccoitsuseina18lauf_djvu.txt)

What it is: A short museum study by a sinologist tracing how tobacco and pipes spread across Asia, quoting Chinese, Persian and European witnesses in English and reporting Japanese and Korean ones.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one work read that covers all four countries, and it holds the words of Cocks, Asad Beg, Terry and Jahangir, and Hamel in Laufer's summary. Its Chinese and Japanese authors are in Laufer's own English and were not read in the original, one Chinese writer is left unnamed, and the "Japanese records" it reports are not named at all.

Used for: Tobacco in China, Japan, Korea and India (Money and prices)
