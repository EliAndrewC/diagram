tani-1989-osaka-shakuya

Tani Naoki (谷直樹), 「近世大坂の居住事情 ―借家と長屋建を中心に―」 ("Housing in early modern Osaka: rented houses and row houses", title translated), 『大阪市立大学生活科学部紀要』 (Bulletin of the Faculty of Human Life Science, Osaka City University) 37, 1989, pp. 160-168 (in Japanese; https://ocu-omu.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2015149/files/111H0000001-37-14.pdf)

What it is: An architectural historian's study of how Osaka's townsmen owned and rented houses in the Edo period, how its rented houses and row houses were built, and how the Mitsui house's Osaka exchange branch ran the rented houses on one of its lots, from the Mitsui house's own records.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the rents the Mitsui house charged in Osaka by the mat of floor a month, front and back houses apart, from 1814 and from a building of 1846, with a general average from a letter-book of 1832, and the month's rent of one lot worked out from them. They are a great landlord's rates, not what a named tenant paid for a room of stated size, and the author assumes rather than shows that they held into 1848-1854; nothing in it is from Kyoto or from the 1740s.

Used for: Osaka rents by the mat a month, 1814 and 1846 (Money and prices)
