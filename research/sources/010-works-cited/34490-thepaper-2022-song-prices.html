Song Yun (宋赟), 「《梦华录》一块玉200贯，北宋的一贯钱到底值多少钱？」 ("A piece of jade at 200 strings in A Dream of Splendor: what was a Northern Song string of cash really worth?", title translated), first published by the Dushi Kuaibao, reposted by Liaowang Zhiku on The Paper, 10 June 2022 (in Chinese; https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_18498645)

What it is: A newspaper feature on what Song money bought, written around a television drama, drawing on the historian Cheng Minsheng's study of Song prices.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is the one page read that gives a Song laborer's day wage, 40 to 50 cash in the southeast in the middle Northern Song, quoting an old text it does not name. The wage is second-hand twice over, from a book not read here and a period text not identified, and is used only beside a Song salt price as a rough measure.

Used for: a Song laborer's day wage (Money and prices)
