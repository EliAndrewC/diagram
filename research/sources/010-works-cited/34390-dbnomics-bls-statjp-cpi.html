dbnomics-bls-statjp-cpi

DBnomics (CEPREMAP), seven series in one request: the US Bureau of Labor Statistics' consumer price index for all urban consumers (CUUR0000SA0) and its average prices of rice, flour and eggs (APU0000701312, APU0000701111, APU0000708111), and the Statistics Bureau of Japan's consumer price index, all items annual and monthly and cereals monthly (STATJP CPIa/001, CPIm/001, CPIm/003), JSON (https://api.db.nomics.world/v22/series?series_ids=BLS/cu/CUUR0000SA0,BLS/ap/APU0000701312,BLS/ap/APU0000701111,BLS/ap/APU0000708111,STATJP/CPIa/001,STATJP/CPIm/001,STATJP/CPIm/003&observations=1&format=json)

What it is: A public mirror of official statistics, giving each series' name and every monthly or annual value as the agency published it: the US index from 1913, the US staple prices from 1980, Japan's index from 1970.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a comparative source: the price level of two present-day economies under paper money, the yardstick beside the Edo and Qing series, never a figure of the setting. It is a mirror, read where the agencies' own files could not be; the US series are not seasonally adjusted, and the US staple series have gaps of some months.

Used for: how far prices move today, month to month and year to year (Comparative money: other societies)
