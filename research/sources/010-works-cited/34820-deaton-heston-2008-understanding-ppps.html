deaton-heston-2008-understanding-ppps

Angus Deaton and Alan Heston, "Understanding PPPs and PPP-based National Accounts", NBER Working Paper 14499, November 2008 (https://www.nber.org/papers/w14499.pdf)

What it is: An NBER working paper (revised November 2009), which by NBER's own notice was not peer-reviewed. It is two economists' account of how the purchasing-power comparisons of the International Comparison Program are made, with the 2005 round's data, and of where they are uncertain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states how the gap between the two baskets' price indexes measures the uncertainty of a comparison where relative prices differ, and gives that gap between real countries. Its comparisons are of whole economies' prices across countries in one year, not of a household's basket across centuries.

Used for: the gap between two baskets' indexes as a comparison's uncertainty, and how wide it ran in 2005 (Money and prices)
