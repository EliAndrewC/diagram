江戸雑記録 (Edo miscellany), 天保十三寅年二月 南町奉行所住居向絵図面 (The plan of the South Town Magistracy's residence and offices, Tenpō 13 (1842), second month), a personal blog (in Japanese; http://edo.ashigaru.net/?eid=20)

What it is: A blog post (2017, by the pen name 琉次郎) reproducing a plan of the Edo South Town Magistracy dated 1842, which the blogger says comes from the shogunate's handed-over records in the National Diet Library's digital collections and was joined from four sheets.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its plan shows the order of the rooms behind the magistracy's court, which bounds where our office hall puts its clerks' room. Its limits are that the blogger joined the four sheets by hand and says the joins are slightly off, that the typed red room names are the blogger's overlay on the brush labels, that the post's text describes only the gate and the retainers' rowhouses, so every room order taken from it is read off the image, and that it is the capital's town magistracy, not a country office.

Used for: the room behind the seat that faced the court at Edo's South Town Magistracy (Estates and other compounds)
