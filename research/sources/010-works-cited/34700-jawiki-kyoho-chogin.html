jawiki-kyoho-chogin

"享保丁銀" ("Kyoho chogin"), Japanese Wikipedia (https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/享保丁銀)

What it is: An encyclopedia article on the fine chogin issued from 1714, with its fineness, its exchange against the older silver and the market rates in Edo while the coins were exchanged.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives Edo's rates of 1717 and 1718, naming the gold and the silver of the later two, beside the Osaka compilation's. It takes them from Tanaya Hiroshi's 1963 study of the silver mint, which was not read here, and gives a mean for spring and summer of 1717 without saying how it was taken.

Used for: Edo's rates of gold in silver in 1717 and 1718 (Money and prices)
