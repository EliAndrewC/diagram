nippon-com-temizuya

nippon.com, "神社空間を読み解く⑤手水舎" ("Reading the shrine space, 5: the temizuya", title translated), in the series "神社に行こう！" ("Let's go to a shrine!", title translated), 9 June 2016 (in Japanese; https://www.nippon.com/ja/views/b05205/)

What it is: A short magazine article on the temizuya, the water basin at a shrine where visitors wash their hands and mouth. It gives the order of the washing and says what the basin stands in for.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the basin stands before the last torii on the approach, and that it is a greatly simplified form of full-body purification, the proper form being to purify at a spring or stream in the precinct, as at the Isuzu river at Ise. It describes present practice and does not date the basin or say how many shrines had one, and it gives no size or layout.
