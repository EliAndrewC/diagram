zh.wikipedia 倒夜香 ("dao yexiang", emptying the night soil, title translated; the page is reached by redirect from 夜香) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/夜香)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the trade of collecting night soil, centered on Hong Kong, describing how households without flush toilets used chamber pots and a sealed bucket, and how the collectors emptied them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the practice in the old Hong Kong tenement, where women collectors called at each door, carried the buckets on a shoulder pole to a collection cart at the street, and sometimes sold the night soil as compost for crops. It is about Hong Kong in the age of tenements and gives no date but 2013, when five places still needed the service; the article's own banners say it needs more sources and is not a universal view, and it describes nothing about collection in a farm village.
