ja.wikipedia 小物成 ("komononari", the minor levies of the Edo period, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/小物成)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on komononari, the miscellaneous taxes laid on land outside the surveyed rice land in Edo-period Japan, with a list of the kinds, such as mountain, field, tea, lacquer, pond and net levies.

Why it applies, and its limits: It separates these taxes from the main land tax and lists what they fell on (mountain land, wild grass, tea, lacquer, fishing and river uses), and says one source counts 307 kinds and the 1875 abolition covered 1,554 across the country. It gives no rates, no shares of a village's burden and no region for any one kind.
