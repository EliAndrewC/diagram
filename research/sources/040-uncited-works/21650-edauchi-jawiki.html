edauchi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 枝打ち ("edauchi", branch pruning, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9E%9D%E6%89%93%E3%81%A1)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on pruning the lower branches of a tree trunk, a tending task in planted forests, with its aims, its tools and its present-day subsidies.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says pruning is done in planted stands once the canopy closes, to get knot-free timber and a trunk of even thickness, and that a person cuts low branches with a billhook or saw and higher ones with a rope-and-pole tool, a ladder or a long pole saw. It describes the practice in modern plantation sugi and hinoki, with its 1990s market and pollen-policy context, and it does not say when pruning began or how it was done before then.
