komenet-rice-culture-5-3

Rice and Rice-Eating Database (お米・ごはん食データベース), "お米と文化 5-3 ごはん料理アラカルト" ("Rice and culture 5-3: a sampler of rice dishes", title translated), on Komenet (in Japanese; https://www.komenet.jp/database/culture/culture05/culture05-3.html)

What it is: A database page surveying Japanese rice dishes - barley rice, bean rice, greens rice, rice porridge, rice balls, gruel and others - with the history of each, from the Nara period to the twentieth century.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says barley rice was the commoner's staple, that peasants' rice was diverted to military rations from the Sengoku period on, and that rice cost 80 to 120 mon per sho in the late Edo period, which bears on what villagers and low-ranking samurai ate. It names period works for some claims (the Seisuisho, a 1731 farmers' book, an 1802 book of rice dishes, the Oan monogatari) but gives no source for the late-Edo prices or for the diversion of peasants' rice to army rations, its recipes mix old and present-day forms, and it names no single region.
