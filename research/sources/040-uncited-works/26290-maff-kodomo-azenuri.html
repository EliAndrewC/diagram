Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, "あぜぬりとは何ですか。" ("What is azenuri?", title translated), in its children's consultation pages (こどもそうだん), on rice growing (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/j/heya/kodomo_sodan/0611/01.html)

What it is: A children's question-and-answer page of Japan's agriculture ministry explaining azenuri, the plastering of the earth bund (aze) between paddies after the first plowing. It says what it is for and how it is done by hand.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the bund is mended after plowing, that mole runs are blocked and leaks stopped, and that mud made of soil scraped from the bund itself is spread on the inside and top with a paddy hoe (taguwa) and pressed down, or trodden in by foot. It is a short answer for children, updated in 2023 from a children's book, giving no dimensions, no dates, no regions and no word on how old the method is, so it shows only that the work is done with hand tools and the bund's own earth.
