sakata-seed-hikage

Sakata Seed Corporation, "日陰" ("hikage", shade), in its horticultural glossary (園芸用語集) of the Engei Tsushin gardening site (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/words/detail_99)

What it is: A one-entry page of a seed company's gardening glossary defining "hikage" (shade) in one sentence, with a usage example.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a working definition of shade as brightness enough to read a book without strain, which is a usable plain-language threshold for what counts as shade for a garden plant, and its example says white-stemmed mitsuba grows well in shade. It is a modern home-gardening definition with no measurement, no light level and no date or place, and it says nothing about premodern gardens or field crops.
