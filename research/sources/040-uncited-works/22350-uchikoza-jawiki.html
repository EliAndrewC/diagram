ja.wikipedia 内子座 ("Uchiko-za", a theater in Uchiko, Ehime, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/内子座)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Uchiko-za, a wooden kabuki-style theater built in Uchiko, Ehime, in 1916 by local backers, with its history, restorations, and building area and capacity.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows that a town grown rich on wax and silk built its own theater, a two-story wooden hall of 785 square meters with floor boxes, a revolving stage and room for 650, used for kabuki, puppet plays, films and meetings. It is a building of 1915 to 1916 with later alterations and restorations, so it records a late and prosperous merchant town, not the Edo-period theaters or any theater of a village.
