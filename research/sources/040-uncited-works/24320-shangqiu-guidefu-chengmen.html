shangqiu-guidefu-chengmen

Shangqiu Local Documents Library (商丘地方文献馆), "商丘归德府古城城门特点探析" ("An analysis of the gates of the old city of Guide Prefecture, Shangqiu", title translated), reposted on Tencent News (in Chinese; https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20210613A080V700)

What it is: A study of the four surviving gates of the Ming walled city of Guide Prefecture (Shangqiu, Henan), giving each gate's platform and arch measurements, construction and layout, and the city's wall circuit of 4,476 m.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured gate platforms of about 34 to 43 m by 18 to 21 m, arched passages 4 to 5.4 m wide, and says the inner half of a passage is taller and wider than the outer half, and that the west gate is offset from the east gate. It records only the gates as they stand: the towers are lost, the floors are modern concrete, and the city was rebuilt on higher ground in 1511 after a Yellow River flood, so it speaks for one Ming prefecture on a flood plain. The offset gates are explained there by five-element belief.
