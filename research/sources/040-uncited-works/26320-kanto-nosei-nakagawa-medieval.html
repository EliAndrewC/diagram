kanto-nosei-nakagawa-medieval

Kanto Regional Agricultural Administration Office, MAFF, Nakagawa Coastal Agricultural Water Project Office, "2. 中世の農" ("2. Farming in the medieval period", title translated), from "Farming and history" (in Japanese; https://www.maff.go.jp/kanto/nouson/sekkei/kokuei/nakagawa/rekishi/002.html)

What it is: A history page of a national irrigation project office on the Naka River in Ibaraki, covering the founding of Hitachi province and the warrior-ruled medieval centuries along the river's lower course.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that under the Taika-era land system the five Kanto provinces including Hitachi paid mostly silk and cloth rather than rice, that the Naka riverside may have favored upland dry fields over lowland paddies, and that the Daijo clan's 1193 residence on the right-bank plateau was the origin of Mito castle. It marks the paddy claim as a guess (no clear records survive) and says large-scale reclamation of the riverside had to wait for the Edo period, so it describes one river valley and gives no sizes or yields.
