sogen-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 草原 ("sogen", grassland, title translated), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/草原)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on grassland, defining it as land covered in grass with few or no trees, and covering the conditions that produce it, its place in plant succession, its ecology and animal life, and its relation to human life.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the ecology the record can lean on: grassland forms where dryness, cold, wind or wet soil keep trees out, or where cutting, grazing or burning clears them, and its grass can be anything from lawn height to susuki taller than 2 m. The page carries a banner saying it has no verifiable references, and gives no Japanese region or period of its own beyond the general account.
