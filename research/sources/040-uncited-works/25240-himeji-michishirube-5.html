himeji-michishirube-5

Himeji City Castle Research Office, "姫路城への道しるべ 第5章 城下町、港、街道" ("A guide to Himeji Castle, chapter 5: the castle town, port and highways", title translated), on the Himeji City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.himeji.lg.jp/jyokakuken/0000017240.html)

What it is: A reading guide, updated in 2024, from a municipal castle research office, listing the books and papers on Himeji's castle town, its outer port Shikama and the Saigoku highway, with short notes on how scholars classify the town and lay out its streets.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Himeji's town was classed as moving from an all-enclosing (sogamae) form to an inner-town and outer-town form under Ikeda, that the Edo-period town was a side-street type, that the town magistrate's residence lay inside the Uchi-Kyoguchi gate and the county magistrate's outside the Kisai gate, and that the street plan follows three lines, so it points a reader to the scholarship on one castle town's plan. It is a bibliography, so each claim is a pointer to a book or paper the page names, and it gives no dimensions or counts for the streets, only the age of two of the base lines (the castle line set when Ikeda built the castle, the Soja line traced to the mid-Nara period); beyond Himeji it names other castles (Gifu, Yoshida, Gochaku, Kanki, Kiyosu, Komaki) only in passing, on the all-enclosing form.
