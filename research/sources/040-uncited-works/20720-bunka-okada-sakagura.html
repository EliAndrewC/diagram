bunka-okada-sakagura

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧岡田家住宅(兵庫県伊丹市宮ノ前) 酒蔵" (the sake brewery of the former Okada residence, Itami, Hyogo, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/201289)

What it is: A national important-cultural-property entry for the sake brewery of the former Okada residence in Itami, Hyogo, of about 1715: a two-story, gabled, tile-roofed earthen-walled building 16.2 m by 15.0 m behind the shop.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of a brewery building and its place behind the shop in one continuous townhouse lot, in a town noted for brewing, and calls the shop one of few securely dated seventeenth-century townhouses. It covers one wealthy brewer's lot in early eighteenth-century Itami and does not describe the brewing or its yard. Its 16.2 m by 15.0 m is the main body only: the entry lists lean-tos on the east, west and north sides, so the building's footprint is larger.
