Shi Nai'an (施耐庵), "水滸傳/第010回" (Water Margin, chapter 10, title translated), Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/水滸傳/第010回)

What it is: The text of chapter 10 of the Ming novel Water Margin on Wikisource, in which the exiled Lin Chong is sent to guard a fodder depot outside Cangzhou and escapes its burning.

Why it applies, and its limits: In passing it shows a garrison's fodder depot drawn as a walled yard with a two-leaf gate, seven or eight thatched sheds used as granaries and stacks of fodder, two thatched halls, 15 li east of the camp, with a wayside temple half a li off, a market two or three li down the road marked by a broom hung out and a village of thatched houses. It is a novel, written long after the Song period it is set in, so its distances and buildings are told for the plot and give no measured layout, and its fire and its snow-crushed roofs are a story's events.
