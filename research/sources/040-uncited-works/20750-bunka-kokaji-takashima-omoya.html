bunka-kokaji-takashima-omoya

"小鍛冶屋（旧高島家住宅）主屋" ("Kokajiya (the former Takashima house), main house", title translated), Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/575652)

What it is: A catalog entry in the national Cultural Heritage Online database for a registered tangible cultural property: the main house of a townhouse-type dwelling at Iwamuro hot spring, Niigata, built 1830-1868, with its building area (247 square meters, the footprint as extended) and a short description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the house stands at the north end of the Iwamuro hot-spring settlement as a two-story gabled townhouse, and that a front garden (zensai) lies between the old house's front and the highway. That gives one dated Edo-period example of a roadside house with a planted strip before it, in Niigata. It describes a single house that has been extended and remodeled repeatedly, so its plan and its footprint are not the original, and its tile roof was once boarded. It does not date the front garden, which may be later than the house, and gives no lot size or dimensions for it.
