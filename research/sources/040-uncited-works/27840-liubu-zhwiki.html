zh.wikipedia "六部" ("The Six Ministries", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/六部)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the Six Ministries of Personnel, Revenue, Rites, War, Justice and Works, the chief organs of the central government from the Sui and Tang to the late Qing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It traces the ministries from the Han secretariat offices, says the Sui and Tang fixed the six under the Department of State Affairs, that the Yuan put them under the Zhongshu secretariat, and that the Ming founder dropped the chancellor so the ministers answered to the emperor, with Nanjing keeping a second set. It lists the offices and ranks only in a table (a minister of the second rank, vice-ministers of the third), and the page covers the central court, not the offices of a county or prefecture.
