ja.wikipedia 日本橋本町 ("Nihonbashi Honcho", a ward district of Chuo, Tokyo, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/日本橋本町)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the present-day town of Nihonbashi Honcho in Chuo ward, Tokyo, the old Edo Honcho. It gives its boundaries, a short history from the 1590 development of the town ground through the Edo period, and modern household, population, business and transport tables.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its history paragraph says Honcho was developed as a town ground in 1590, that by the Kan'ei era large shops from Kyoto and Osaka had moved in, that the name means the first, original town made in Edo, and that it ran from first to fourth block with many drug wholesalers and drapers. It says the Honcho road from Edo Castle through Tokiwa Bridge was Edo's main street until early Meiji. The page gives no street widths, plot sizes or plan for the Edo town, and its tables are of 1995-2023.
