en.wikipedia "Nagao Shrine" (長尾神社, Nagao-jinja, Katsuragi, Nara, title translated) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagao_Shrine)

What it is: A short English Wikipedia article on Nagao Shrine, a Shinto shrine in Katsuragi, Nara Prefecture, describing its approach path between two torii, its hand-washing pavilion, its hall of worship and its annual festival dates.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives one shrine's approach in order: an outer torii, a 200 meter sando to the second torii, a temizusha just before that gate, and the haiden just after it. It also lists a festival calendar that includes a spring harvest-praying rite on 4 March and a harvest thanksgiving on 23 November. The page cites no sources at all, gives its establishment date as unknown, and its layout and festival descriptions are of the shrine as it stands today (its summer festival lists karaoke), with no dated evidence for how it looked before Meiji.
