ranhaku-fudokiko-intro

Ranzan Town (嵐山町), "新編武蔵風土記稿" ("The Shinpen Musashi Fudokiko", title translated), from the History of Ranzan Town (嵐山町誌, 1968), introduction, in the Ranzan Town Web Museum, vol. 6 (嵐山町web博物誌), on the Ranzan town site (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/10_004.html)

What it is: A passage of the 1968 history of Ranzan town, Saitama, in which the editors test the Edo-period gazetteer of Musashi against a village's own statement (kojosho) and explain how they will use it. It quotes the gazetteer's entries for one village: its boundaries, 60 households, successive lords, shrines, temples and notice boards.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports reading the gazetteer's village entries as a summary of what each village itself reported: the editors find the entries for the village they check match the village statement, with lords' changes from 1698 to 1704 shortened and the temple Koshoji's founding dates, names and years identical. They add that the shogunate required every village to submit material, so coverage is uniform, and that the town's history is therefore built mostly on the Edo period. That is the editors' argument from one village, in 1968, and the page does not give the gazetteer's own date or how many villages were compared.
