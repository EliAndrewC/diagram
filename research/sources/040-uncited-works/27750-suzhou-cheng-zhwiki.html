zh.wikipedia "苏州城" ("Suzhou city", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8B%8F%E5%B7%9E%E5%8F%A4%E5%9F%8E)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the walled city of Suzhou in Jiangsu, giving its wall history from the legendary Helu city of 514 BCE through the Song, Yuan and Ming to the Qing rebuild and the modern demolition.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Ming and Qing prefectural city's wall length (45 li after the Kangxi rebuild of 1662, against 47 li for the Helu wall as the Wu Yue chunqiu records it) and names the Song Pingjiang map, so it fixes the scale of a major Jiangnan prefectural seat. It gives no wall height, thickness or gate plan, and says the 514 BCE city's very site is disputed; only about 2 km of wall survives today.
