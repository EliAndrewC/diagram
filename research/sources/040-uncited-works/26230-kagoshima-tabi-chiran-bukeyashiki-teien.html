kagoshima-tabi-chiran-bukeyashiki-teien

Kagoshima Prefecture and the Kagoshima Prefectural Tourism Federation, "知覧武家屋敷庭園群" ("Chiran buke-yashiki teien-gun", The Chiran samurai-residence gardens, title translated), on the official Kagoshima tourism site (in Japanese; https://www.kagoshima-kankou.com/guide/11053)

What it is: A tourism-site entry on the Chiran fumoto (samurai settlement) in southern Satsuma, with its hedged streets, borrowed-view gardens and national designations.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the fumoto is a typical Kagoshima samurai settlement, with winding streets, hedges of inumaki and tea, arm-bracket gates (udegimon) in front of the gardens and gardens kept since the Edo period, with an 18.6 ha preservation district and seven gardens open to the public as nationally designated scenic sites. It gives no house plot sizes, counts or dates, and describes what remains after preservation, not the settlement as it stood.
