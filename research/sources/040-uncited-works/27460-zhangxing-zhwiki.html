zh.wikipedia 杖刑 (the heavy-stick beating, redirecting to the article on caning, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/杖刑)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on judicial caning (the bamboo or rattan beating of the buttocks, back or soles), reached here by its redirect from 杖刑 (the heavy-stick beating). It covers the punishment's use in Rome, China, Britain and its colonies, Japan and its colonies, and the countries that keep it today.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its Chinese section gives the ranks of the old penalty: under the Sui and Tang five punishments 10 to 50 blows were the light bamboo beating (chi) and 60 to 100 the heavy stick (zhang), on the back, buttocks and legs, with the Song splitting it into buttock and spine beatings and the Yuan to Qing beating only the buttocks. Most of the article is modern colonial and present-day practice (Hong Kong, Taiwan, Korea, Singapore, Malaysia), and it names no source for the Chinese ranks.
