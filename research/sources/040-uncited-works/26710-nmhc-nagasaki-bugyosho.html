nmhc-nagasaki-bugyosho

Nagasaki Museum of History and Culture, "長崎奉行所ゾーン" ("Nagasaki Magistrate's Office zone", title translated), on the museum's site (in Japanese; https://www.nmhc.jp/permanent/bugyosho/)

What it is: A museum page on the part of the permanent exhibition built on the site of the Nagasaki magistrate's Tateyama office, partly reconstructed. It lists the exhibits on the magistrate's duties, the court record, the Christian-suppression items and the rooms of the office.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Nagasaki magistrate's duties covered administration, justice, foreign relations, trade and military matters and the policing of Christians and smuggling, and that 127 magistrates served. It also names the rooms of the reconstructed office: a reception hall, a meeting room used for inspecting imported goods, and a gravel courtyard (the shirasu) rebuilt from drawings and excavated gravel. It is a visitor page giving no plan or dimensions, and Nagasaki's magistracy was an unusual post because of its foreign trade, so it is not a model for an ordinary magistrate's office.
