hakone-sekisho-tategu

Hakone Town, "伝統的な建具1" ("traditional fittings, part 1", title translated), in "よみがえった箱根関所" ("the revived Hakone barrier", title translated), on the Hakone Sekisho site (in Japanese; https://www.hakonesekisyo.jp/db/hs_disp_fr.html?hdid=data_04_02_03_23)

What it is: A short page from the town's restoration record on the traditional doors and sliding screens rebuilt at the Hakone barrier: the plank doors and shoji of the main guardhouse, the stable's great door, and the huge gate leaves at the Kyoto and Edo gates. It says the work followed a detailed Edo-period repair record.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the barrier's fittings were rebuilt to the measurements and materials of an Edo-period repair record (the Soshu Hakone Gosekisho Goshufuku Dekigata-cho), and that the great gate leaves, each with ten thick vertical bars, resemble those of Edo Castle's Shimizu Gate and are mostly of hemlock. It describes a modern restoration, gives no sizes and treats only doors, so it is no source for the barrier's plan or for barriers elsewhere.
