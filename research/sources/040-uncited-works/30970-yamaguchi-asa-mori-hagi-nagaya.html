yamaguchi-asa-mori-hagi-nagaya

Yamaguchi Prefecture Cultural Promotion Division, "旧厚狭毛利家萩屋敷長屋" ("The long row-house of the former Asa Mori family's Hagi residence"), in the prefecture's Designated Cultural Properties search (in Japanese; https://bunkazai.pref.yamaguchi.lg.jp/sp/bunkazai/summary.asp?mid=20019)

What it is: A prefectural record for an Important Cultural Property: the surviving long row-house of the Asa Mori family's Hagi residence, in front of the Hagi castle's main gate, with its dimensions and designation history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a retainer-row of the Choshu domain: a single-storey tiled-roof building 51.4 m long and 5.0 m deep (257.2 square meters of floor), raised in 1856, with a verandah on the south and rooms, a mezzanine store and an earth-floor room inside. The page says it is only the surviving part, that the west end was cut off so the original was longer, and that it is said to have been a servants' row-house though this is not certain, and it is a late Edo building of one of the domain's branch houses. Its 1966 listing gave the length as 43.6 m; the 51.4 m is the length after a change to its present state recorded in 1973.
