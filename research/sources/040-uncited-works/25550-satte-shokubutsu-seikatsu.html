satte-shokubutsu-seikatsu

Satte City Environment Division, "植物と生活" ("Plants and Daily Life", title translated), in "Satte City Environment Navigation: Let's Learn About Satte's Environment", on the Satte city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.satte.lg.jp/soshiki/kankyou/1/sattecityenvironmentnavigation/sattenokankyowomanabou/seibutusyokubutu/6679.html)

What it is: A municipal environmental-education page on the plants of Satte, Saitama: farmhouse and settlement woods (yashikirin), hedges, shrine and temple groves, the cherry embankment at Gongendo, and the everyday uses of bamboo, alder, palm and wild plants.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that homestead woods were planted chiefly as windbreaks and also supplied building timber, fuel and saleable wood, that hedges marked boundaries and protected thatch from fire, that shrine and temple groves stand out over open paddy and are mostly zelkova, and that bamboo and alder served for flood protection, tools and rows. The tree counts for the cherry embankment come from a 1994 survey and have no bearing on the setting, and the page gives no ages, widths or acreage for any grove and speaks of a single town in the Saitama lowland.
