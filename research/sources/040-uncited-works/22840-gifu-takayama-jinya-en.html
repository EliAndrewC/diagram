Gifu Prefecture, "Takayama Jinya", the official English site of the Takayama magistrate's office (https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/)

What it is: The prefecture's official English page on Takayama Jinya, the former Edo-period shogunal magistrate's office at Takayama, Hida, describing its history, its principal rooms and its rice granary.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the shogunate put Hida under direct control in 1692, that the office was used until 1969, and that the Onkura granary stored the annual rice tax of neighboring villages and was moved from Takayama Castle's outer bailey in 1695. It names the reception hall as 49 mats in three rooms, and the courtroom room, but gives no dimensions for the compound, and it describes one site that it calls the only surviving one of about sixty.
