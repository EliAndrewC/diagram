zh.wikipedia "中國度量衡" ("Chinese weights and measures", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/中国度量衡)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on China's units of length, capacity, weight and area, with a table for each, dynasty by dynasty from the Shang to the Qing, and the present-day metric and market systems.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the foot (chi) in centimeters by period, for instance 31.2 cm for Song and Yuan, and for the Ming 32 cm for the building foot and 32.7 cm for the land-measuring foot, with the Qing 32 cm and 34.5 cm, and the capacity of the Ming and Qing shi as 100 liters. Some entries carry "source requested" flags, the page's own banner asks for more sources, and it does not say which of a period's several feet a given source used, so a figure must be matched to the right foot before it is applied.
