en.wikipedia "Kōnoike Shinden" (the Konoike new paddy settlement) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%8Dnoike_Shinden)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on a merchant-developed paddy settlement in Kawachi, now Higashiosaka, begun after a flood-control project of 1704, and on its management office, the Kaisho, completed in 1707.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a 119 hectare development with farmers settled from as far as Ise Province, many waterways used to boat rice to a central warehouse, and a management office inside a moated, walled trapezoid with five buildings, which can illustrate a planned reclaimed-land village and its steward's office. It is one merchant-financed settlement on drained lake land in the Osaka plain in 1704-1707, not a typical village, and gives no layout figures for the farmland.
