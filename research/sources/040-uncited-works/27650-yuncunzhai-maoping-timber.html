Guizhou Digital Publishing Cloud Village Platform, "茅坪：千家木商古镇" (Maoping: the old town of a thousand timber merchants, title translated), from the book 名镇名村 (Famous towns and villages), published 1 December 2017 (in Chinese; https://www.yuncunzhai.com/book/183385.jhtml)

What it is: A provincial heritage-platform chapter on Maoping, a timber-trading town at the meeting of the Qingshui and Liang rivers in Jinping county, Guizhou, covering its timber wharf, the Ming and Qing timber market, its merchant guildhalls and surviving buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says rafts floated down were tied at Maoping into standard square stacked rafts (duozi pai) before going on to Hongjiang and Hankou, that a timber market was set up there in 1514, that the Yongzheng-era governor licensed three river timber markets, that over a thousand merchant households gathered at the peak and annual sales ran above three million taels, about 70 percent of the three markets' trade. Its figures are unsourced, and it describes a frontier river entrepot for imperial timber, not an ordinary county town or village.
