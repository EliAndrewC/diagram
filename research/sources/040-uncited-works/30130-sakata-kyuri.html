sakata-kyuri

Sakata Seed Corporation, "キュウリの育て方・栽培方法" ("Kyuri no sodatekata, saibai hoho", how to grow cucumber, title translated), in the Engei Tsushin gardening site, "Shippai shinai saibai lesson (yasai)" (updated 2025-05-13) (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/lesson-vegetable/detail_31)

What it is: A seed company's home-garden growing guide for cucumber, giving its spacing (50 cm), trellising on frames set 50 cm apart, vine pruning, seedling raising of about 30 days, and harvest about a week after flowering in summer.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives spacing, the support frame interval, how fast the fruit matures, and that the plant is set out after the last frost, with a trailing (ground-creeping) form as an alternative to trellising. Its grafted seedlings, black mulch and chemical sprays are modern, so its schedule is for today's cultivars and not for the Edo period.
