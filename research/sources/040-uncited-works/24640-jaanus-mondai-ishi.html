jaanus-mondai-ishi

Mary Neighbour Parent (compiler), "mondai-ishi 問台石" (gate foundation stones, title translated), in JAANUS, the on-line Dictionary of Japanese Architectural and Art Historical Terminology, Media Center for Art History, Columbia University (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/mondaiishi)

What it is: A dictionary entry on the mondai-ishi, a large flat stepping stone laid under a middle gate of a tea garden (roji), also called tozuri-ishi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the stone sits under a sarudo or kido type of middle gate and often replaces the host, guest and gate-crossing stones. It is about the tea garden's inner gate; it gives no date or size and says nothing about the gates of a house or compound.
