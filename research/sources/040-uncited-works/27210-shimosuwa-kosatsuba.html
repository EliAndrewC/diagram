shimosuwa-kosatsuba

Shimosuwa Town, "高札場について" ("About the notice-board site", title translated), on the Shimosuwa town site, updated 2015-04-27 (in Japanese; https://www.town.shimosuwa.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000000780/index.html)

What it is: A short town page on the kosatsu, the ink-written law boards of the shogunate and domains, and on the notice-board site (kosatsuba) of Shimo-Suwa post station on the Nakasendo, which the town reconstructed in its Tachimachi district in 2015.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a kosatsuba stood in conspicuous places such as road forks and checkpoints, and that the boards recorded the laws that governed commoners' lives, with a 1711 horse-fee board among its examples. It gives no size, layout or number of boards, and does not say what evidence the 2015 reconstruction followed.
