Koshina Yoshitaka (神品芳孝), "関東平野北西部の集村集落における屋敷林の変化" ("Transition of Woodlands around Farmyards at Settlements in the Northwestern Part of the Kanto Plain", the article's own English title), Journal of Geography (Chigaku Zasshi) 132(3), 2023 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jgeography/132/3/132_132.197/_article/-char/ja/)

What it is: The J-STAGE record, with an English abstract, of a journal article on the homestead woods of compact villages in northern Saitama, using aerial photographs, land registers and interviews.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the woods at the north edge of such a village act as a windbreak for the whole village, and that when the owner lives far from the wood it is hard to manage and tends to decline. It reports the present-day state, where the woods serve only as windbreaks or house boundaries, and the abstract gives no measurements. Its villages, in northern Saitama, face strong, cold, dry winter winds, so the north-edge windbreak speaks for such windswept plains first.
