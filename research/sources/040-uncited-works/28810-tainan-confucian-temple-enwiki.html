en.wikipedia "Tainan Confucian Temple" (臺南孔子廟, title translated) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tainan_Confucian_Temple)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Confucian temple in Tainan, Taiwan, founded in 1665 under the Kingdom of Tungning. It lists the halls, gates and shrines added under the Qing, the temple's later renovations, and its present ceremonies.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the parts of a prefectural temple and school compound as they stood after the 1712 renovation: the temple (Wen Miao) with its main hall, gate, and rear shrine beside the academy (Guo Xue) with its lecture hall, gate, courtyard of side chambers and faculty quarters behind. It gives no dimensions or areas, the compound was cut back in 1917 and renovated again in 1987-1989, and it is a southern-Taiwan prefectural seat, not a county town.
