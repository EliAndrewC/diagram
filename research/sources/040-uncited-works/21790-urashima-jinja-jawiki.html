ja.wikipedia 浦嶋神社 ("Urashima Jinja", the shrine of Urashima, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B5%A6%E5%B6%8B%E7%A5%9E%E7%A4%BE)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Urashima shrine at Ine in Kyoto Prefecture, which holds the Urashima Taro legend, with its founding tradition, festivals and listed cultural properties.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the shrine by tradition to 825 and by the Engishiki to its listing in 927, and gives a calendar of its yearly rites, including a monthly rite on the 7th and 17th. It also lists a medieval picture scroll of the shrine's origin. The present worship hall and main hall were built in 1884, the worship hall altered in 1946 to 1965; the page gives their building areas (93 and 26 square meters, the main hall thatched) but no precinct plan.
