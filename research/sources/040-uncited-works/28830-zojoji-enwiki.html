en.wikipedia "Zōjō-ji" (増上寺) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zōjō-ji)

What it is: The English Wikipedia article on Zojo-ji, the Jodo-shu temple in Shiba, Tokyo that was the Tokugawa family temple. It covers the temple's history from 1393, the main gate of 1622, the shoguns' mausoleums, the buildings and the annual events.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the grounds held more than 120 buildings at their peak (a figure cited to the temple's own English pamphlet) and that the temple was moved in 1590 when Edo Castle was expanded (the Japanese article dates the move to Shiba to 1598), which shows the scale of a shogunal family temple of the Edo period. It gives no site area, the buildings were lost in the 1945 air raids except the 21 m gate, and a clan temple of the shogun is the extreme case, so it says nothing about a temple of a county or village.
