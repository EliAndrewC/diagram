kakine-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 垣根 ("kakine", fences and hedges, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9E%A3%E6%A0%B9)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kakine, the fences and hedges that mark a plot or garden, with a section on European field hedgerows and a section on Japanese bamboo fences, hedges, board and earthen walls.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the kinds of boundary a Japanese house or garden used (bamboo fences, living hedges, earthen and stone walls, board fences) and sorts the bamboo ones into those that block the view and those that do not. Its European section describes English hawthorn hedgerows, which do not bear on an East Asian setting. The Japanese section describes present-day houses, listing concrete, brick and wire-mesh fences beside the traditional ones, and draws on a seed company's gardening magazine. It gives names and forms with no dates, no sizes and no account of which a farm or a samurai house used, and the garden-fence types it lists are mostly ornamental, many named for Kyoto temples and villas.
