yanagi-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "ヤナギ" (willow, the genus Salix, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ヤナギ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on willows (genus Salix), covering their form, species, riverbank habitat, uses, medicine and cultural associations in Japan, China and Europe.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says willows are planted along rivers and ponds in Japan as flood protection, that cuttings root easily and are used as stakes in present-day erosion-control works (治山の土留工・伏工), which the page does not give as an old practice, and that Europe wove willow shoots into baskets and fences from prehistory; it also names the Chang'an custom of giving a willow twig to a departing traveler. The page carries a 2011 notice that its sources are missing or insufficient, its statements on Japanese riverside planting and on willow stakes in bank work carry no footnote, its Japanese farming content is brief and undated, and its detailed craft material is about Europe (withy beds on old European waterway banks, pollarded willows in the Benelux), not East Asia.
