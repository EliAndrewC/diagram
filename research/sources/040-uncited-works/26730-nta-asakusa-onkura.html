nta-asakusa-onkura

National Tax Agency, National Tax College, "江戸幕府の年貢と米蔵（答え）" ("The Edo shogunate's land tax and rice granaries, answer", title translated), in its tax-history quiz (in Japanese; https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/quiz/2212/answer.htm)

What it is: A short answer page of the National Tax College's tax-history quiz, signed by a staff researcher, on the shogunate's rice granaries in Edo: the Asakusa granary, the Honjo, Takebashi and Hama granaries, and how stored rice was paid out to retainers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a size and a capacity for the largest shogunal granary: Asakusa, set on the west bank of the Sumida in 1620, with a site of more than 20,000 tsubo and a stored amount said to be 300,000 to 500,000 koku, and the Honjo granary of 1734 at 100,000 to 200,000 koku. These are the shogunate's own central granaries in Edo, not a domain's or a village's store, and the page words the capacity as what "is said" and gives no source for it.
