kotobank-take

"竹" ("take", bamboo, title translated), entries from the Digital Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and Jitsu, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%AB%B9-559058)

What it is: A page of dictionary entries on bamboo: what it is as a plant (a grass with a creeping rhizome and hollow jointed culms), its uses, and the character's meaning, with a word-history note on Japanese bamboo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that Moso bamboo was brought from China in the eighteenth century and that earlier Japanese bamboo was largely madake and hachiku, and lists building material, crafts and food among its uses. It is a general definition with no stand size, spacing or yield, and much of the page (the compound words, a song title and sponsored job listings) is unrelated to groves.
