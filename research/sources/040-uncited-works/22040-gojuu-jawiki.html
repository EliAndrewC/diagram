ja.wikipedia 郷中 ("gojuu", the Satsuma ward youth associations, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%83%B7%E4%B8%AD%E6%95%99%E8%82%B2)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the gojuu, the neighborhood groups of middle- and lower-ranking samurai boys in the Satsuma domain, with the age grades, the code attributed to them, the list of wards and their later influence.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows how a castle town's samurai quarter was divided into named wards (18 at first, 33 by the late Edo period) and how boys were organized by age inside them, with older boys teaching younger. It is about Satsuma, a domain known for its own customs, much of its text concerns sexuality and a Meiji-era legacy, and the article carries a notice that it cites too few sources.
