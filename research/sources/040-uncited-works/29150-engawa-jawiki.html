engawa-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 縁側 ("engawa", the veranda of a Japanese house, title translated), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/縁側)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the engawa, the plank-floored passage along the edge of a Japanese house that also lets one step in from the garden, with short descriptions of the nure-en and kure-en types.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the engawa as a feature peculiar to Japanese houses and says how the open nure-en (outside the wall or shutters, under the eaves) differs from the kure-en (a lean-to room under the eaves behind rain shutters), enough to know what a house's edge holds. It gives no dimensions, no date and no region, and its construction notes (posts on foundation stones, modern plywood unsuitable) are generic.
