kojodan-kaibara-nagayamon

Kojodan (攻城団), "柏原藩主織田家旧邸長屋門" ("The nagaya gate of the former residence of the Oda lords of Kaibara", title translated), from the Kaibara Jinya guide, quoting the Hyogo Board of Education signboard (in Japanese; https://kojodan.jp/castle/281/memo/442.html)

What it is: A castle-fan site's note on the gatehouse of the Kaibara domain's jinya in Tanba (Tamba, Hyogo), with the text of the prefectural board's signboard and its English translation. It gives the date, size and the three rooms inside.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a small domain's official gate: built in 1714 as the front gate of the lord's residence, 13.5 ken long and 2 ken deep (about 24 m by 3.6 m), with a tatami gatekeeper's room on the left and a wood-floored horse-viewing room (the English panel says watch room) and an arms room on the right. It survived a fire of 1818 that burned the residence, and the signboard calls the structure simple and one of few surviving daimyo residence remains; it describes a lord of a small domain and one gate, and says nothing of the rest of the compound.
