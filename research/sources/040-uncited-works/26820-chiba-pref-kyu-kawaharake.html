Chiba Prefecture Board of Education, "旧河原家住宅" ("The former Kawahara house", title translated), a designated cultural property in Sakura (in Japanese; https://www.pref.chiba.lg.jp/kyouiku/bunkazai/bunkazai/p111-039.html)

What it is: A prefectural page on a samurai house of Sakura moved and restored in 1989: its plan before restoration, the documentary evidence for its date, and what the restoration put back (an entrance with a raised step, a next room, a middle room and a veranda).

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the house was already standing in 1845, and that its plan as donated in 1987 separated the reception rooms at the front (a two-mat entrance and a six-mat guest room) from the family's living room and kitchen at the back, which it calls characteristic of warrior houses. The page gives no dimensions, the owner's rank or income, and the entrance and rooms shown today are a restoration from traces and a handover document, the formal entrance having been lost from the house as donated.
