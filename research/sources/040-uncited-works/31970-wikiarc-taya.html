wikiarc-taya

WikiArc, "多屋" ("taya", the lodgings at Yoshizaki, title translated), a dictionary entry from the annotated Jodo Shinshu scriptures (浄土真宗聖典 注釈版), Hongwanji Publishing (in Japanese; https://labo.wikidharma.org/index.php/多屋)

What it is: A dictionary entry on the term taya, the lodging houses at the Yoshizaki temple where followers from the provinces stayed to hear the teaching when they visited.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that major temples of Echizen and southern Kaga each built a taya at Yoshizaki and left part of a household there to look after the guests. It gives no date, no size and no number of lodgings, and the entry is a gloss to a passage of the scriptures, so it names the institution of a lodging for pilgrims at a temple town but says nothing of what one looked like.
