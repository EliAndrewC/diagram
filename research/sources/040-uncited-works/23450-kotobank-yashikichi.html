kotobank-yashikichi

"屋敷地" ("yashikichi", house-lot land, title translated), from the Daijisen and the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, with a mention in the older Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%B1%8B%E6%95%B7%E5%9C%B0-648233)

What it is: A short Kotobank page defining 屋敷地 as the land a house is built on, or land where a house once stood, with one dated example and one mention from the entry on koku assessment.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the word and nothing more about the lot: the example is a shogunal edict of 1719 forbidding a person who had suffered a fire from lending out the lot, and the mention shows that survey officers assessed house lots together with fields and gardens. It gives no size, no layout and no region.
