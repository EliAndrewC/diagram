shikata-1996-ina-slope-forests

Shikata Keiichiro (四方 圭一郎), "長野県伊那市を事例とした農村集落における段丘崖斜面樹林の保全" ("Nagano-ken Ina-shi o jirei to shita noson shuraku ni okeru dankyugai shamen jurin no hozen", conservation of the slope forests of terrace scarps in rural communities, a case study of Ina City, Nagano Prefecture, title translated), Journal of the Japanese Institute of Landscape Architecture (ランドスケープ研究) 59(5): 85-88, 1996, on J-STAGE (in Japanese; https://jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jila1994/59/5/59_5_85/_pdf)

What it is: The full text of a four-page paper on the belts of forest on river-terrace scarps beside 21 farming settlements in Ina City, Nagano. It sorts the forest into house-grove, shrine-grove and production types and reports who owns each, with area shares by owner and a count of which settlements keep shrine groves, temple land and graves there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows what a Japanese settlement's slope forest was used for (firewood and charcoal, fallen leaves, timber) and that shrine grounds kept large trees as a shrine grove, with graves scattered in and around the forest. Its data are from 1990-1995 (aerial photos, a 1992 forest register, field survey) in one upland valley, and it says that use of the production woods has since declined, so the forest it records is a modern remnant.
