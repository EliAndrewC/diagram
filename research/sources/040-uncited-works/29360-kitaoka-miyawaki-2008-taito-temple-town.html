kitaoka-miyawaki-2008-taito-temple-town

Kitaoka Katsue and Miyawaki Masaru (北岡 勝江, 宮脇 勝), "台東区における寺町の道路と街区と寺院の歴史的変遷に関する研究" ("Taito-ku ni okeru teramachi no doro to gaiku to jiin no rekishiteki hensen ni kansuru kenkyu", historical change in the roads, blocks and temples of the temple town in Taito Ward, title translated), City Planning Review (都市計画論文集) 43.2: 1-10, 2008, on J-STAGE (in Japanese; https://jstage.jst.go.jp/article/journalcpij/43.2/0/43.2_1/_article/-char/ja)

What it is: The J-STAGE record page of a 2008 paper, giving its bibliographic data and Japanese abstract; the full text is a separate PDF that this page does not carry. It maps roads, blocks and temples of Tokyo's Taito Ward from temple histories, 88 historical maps, 3 fire-insurance maps and a road register, with Yanaka and Asakusa as case areas.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its abstract says 253 of the 550 temples founded in the ward (46%) survive from the Edo period and that only about 2% of blocks, roads and temples survive from Edo, in Yanaka, so it bears on how much of an Edo temple district can be recovered. It is about one Edo-period city's temple district, not a village or a provincial town, and the page gives only the abstract, not the maps or methods.
