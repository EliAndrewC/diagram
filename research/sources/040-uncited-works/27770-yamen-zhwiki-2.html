zh.wikipedia "衙門" ("The yamen", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%A1%99%E9%96%80)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the yamen, the office of a local official, tracing the word from "牙門" (the gate where a general's banner stood) to its later uses and explaining the "six-leaf gate" (六扇門).

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the name and the sense of the yamen, with quoted passages from the Beishi, the Jiu Tangshu and Liaozhai zhiyi, and says its great gate was opened in six leaves to show authority. It describes no layout and gives no sizes; its only plan is the caption of an 1803 Shaoxing print, and its folk-tale paragraph on the Liushanmen is about fiction, not offices.
