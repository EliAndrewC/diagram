kameyama-seki-rekishi-bunka

Kameyama City, Culture Division, "関宿の歴史と文化" ("The history and culture of Seki-juku", title translated), on the city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.kameyama.mie.jp/docs/2014112311914/)

What it is: A city page on the history of Seki, a Tokaido post town in Mie, from the ancient Suzuka barrier and the medieval Jizo temple settlement through its Edo-period prosperity to its decline after the railway of 1890 and its selection as a preservation district in 1984.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a post town's figures from the 1843 Tokaido survey - Seki 632 households and 1,942 people, Sakashita 153 households and 564 people - and says Seki stood where two side roads branched off the highway, which brought traffic beyond the daimyo processions. It also names the town's fuse-cord trade, with dozens of shops, and its summer festival floats. The counts are for two Tokaido towns in one year, and the page gives no street widths, lot sizes or inn counts.
