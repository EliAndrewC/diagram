bonsho-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 梵鐘 ("bonsho", the Buddhist temple bell, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/梵鐘)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the bonsho, the hanging bronze bell of East Asian Buddhist temples, covering its origin, the parts of a Japanese bell, a list of famous bells from the Nara period on, bell towers and present-day makers.

Why it applies, and its limits: It can carry what a temple bell is for (calling services, marking morning and evening, and in the smaller hansho form sounding fire alarms), how its form was fixed from the Nara to the Edo period, and a measured dated example, the Osaka town time bell of 1634 at 1.9 m high, 1.1 m across and about 3 tons. The article itself carries a template saying it lacks verifiable sources, and it says over 90 percent of Japan's bells were lost to the wartime metal requisition, so surviving examples skew to protected ones.
