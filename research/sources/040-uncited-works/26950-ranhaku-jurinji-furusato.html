ranhaku-jurinji-furusato

"曹洞宗 重輪寺 古里村" ("Jurinji, Soto school, Furusato village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register, in Ranzan Town Web Natural and Local History, volume 6 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter01.html)

What it is: A transcription of one temple's entry in the Hiki County register of temples (Saitama prefectural records): the Soto temple Jurinji in Furusato village. It gives the main hall and priest's residence sizes, the precinct area, the lands it owns outside the precinct, and the number of parishioners (danto, counted in persons).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a hall 6 ken by 6 ken 5 shaku, a priest's residence 9 by 4 ken, a precinct of 1,558 tsubo and 100 parishioners, and records that the temple burned in 1894 and was rebuilt by 1897. The sizes are those of the rebuilt Meiji buildings, the entry gives its origin as unknown, and its land values are Meiji land-tax figures in yen.
