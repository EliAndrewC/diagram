satte-chikei-tochi-riyo

Satte City Environment Division, "地形と土地利用" ("Landform and Land Use", title translated), in "Satte City Environment Navigation: Let's Learn About Satte's Environment", on the Satte city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.satte.lg.jp/soshiki/kankyou/1/sattecityenvironmentnavigation/sattenokankyowomanabou/sattenoter/6576.html)

What it is: A municipal environmental-education page on the landform of Satte, Saitama, in the Nakagawa lowland, explaining river deposition, natural levees and back marshes. It says where the old town and farmland were put, and describes the dug-up fields (horiage-da) made in the marshy Kamiougi district from the seventeenth century, with the dug ditches and raised strips.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports siting settlements and fields on a natural levee with the poorly drained back marsh behind it, and gives a worked case of reclaiming marsh by digging ditches and heaping the soil into planting strips linked by boat canals. Its evidence is one lowland in eastern Saitama, the diagrams are schematic and no ditch width, strip width or yield is given, and the page says the district was rebuilt with modern improvement work in the 1960s and 1970s.
