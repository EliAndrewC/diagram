bunka-asa-mori-hagi-nagaya

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "旧厚狭毛利家萩屋敷長屋" (the long house of the former Asa Mori family's Hagi residence, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/124024)

What it is: A database entry for a long row house (nagaya) in Hagi, Yamaguchi, an Important Cultural Property designated in 1966: a one-story hip-and-gable building with a tiled roof, 5.0 m deep and over 50 m long. The short note explains that it stood in the Hagi town residence of the Asa branch of the Mori family, the lords of Hagi.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the length of a row house in a domain lord's branch-family town residence in a castle town, and calls its length unusually rare. The page is inconsistent with itself: the data block says 51.4 m and dates it 1856, while the note says 53.1 m and 1855 (Ansei 3), so the length can only be taken as about 51 to 53 m. It does not say how many households or retainers the row held.
