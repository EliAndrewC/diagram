tohoku-rekishi-sumidawara

Tohoku History Museum, "炭俵" ("Charcoal bag", title translated), item ID 25908, in the museum's collection catalogue (館蔵資料目録) (in Japanese; https://jmapps.ne.jp/thm2/det.html?data_id=25908)

What it is: A museum catalogue record for two straw charcoal bags (sumidawara), classed as folklore material and as a storage container, with the item number 000514 and one set of three measurements (56 by 31 by 36, units not shown in the text).

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms that straw charcoal bags are a collected folk object and gives one set of dimensions for a pair. The record gives no date, place of use, owner, units or capacity, so it supports only that the object existed, and any use of its size has to assume its units.
