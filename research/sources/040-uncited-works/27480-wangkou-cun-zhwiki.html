zh.wikipedia 汪口村 (Wangkou village, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/汪口村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Wangkou, a village in Wuyuan County, Jiangxi, listing its protected buildings, with a table of national, provincial and county heritage sites.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records a Huizhou-style lineage village of the Yu family, founded in 1110 on a site ringed by water on three sides, with an ancestral hall of 1736, a weir, and a county-listed group of some twenty old buildings (houses, halls, a study, two bridges and landing steps). It gives no plan, house count or field data, and most of the page is a heritage register and a list of other villages.
