ja.wikipedia 幡羅郡 ("Hara-gun", Hara County of Musashi, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/幡羅郡)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Hara County, a vanished county of Musashi province in present Saitama, covering its territory, its ancient county office site, its medieval families and its villages from the late shogunate to its abolition in 1896.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows how one county was laid out: the largest in northern Musashi with seven townships in the Wamyosho, a county office and temple site of the 7th to 11th centuries, 58 villages in the early Meiji survey with the shrine and temple lands marked, and twelve villages formed by merging in 1889. It describes one county on an alluvial fan, and its village lists give names and rulers, not populations or areas.
