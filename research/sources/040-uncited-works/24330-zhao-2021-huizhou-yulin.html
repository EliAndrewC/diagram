Zhao Siyuan (赵思渊), "清前期徽州乡村社会秩序中的土地登记" ("Land registration in the rural social order of Huizhou in the early Qing", title translated), Lishi yanjiu (历史研究) 2021, no. 3, reposted on Tencent News by the Chinese Academy of History (in Chinese; https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20210825A08QKO00)

What it is: The text of a journal article on the fish-scale land registers of Shexian county, Huizhou, from the Wanli survey of 1581 through the early Qing, compared with lineage genealogies and land contracts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It argues the early Qing registers copied the 1581 survey and were not remeasured, that the area unit was 224 bu to the mu, and that tenant entries mark rights in the field surface. It rests on a few villages of one mountainous county (the 26th du of Shexian), and it finds that the mu in these registers are tax mu, not measured field areas, so they cannot give a plot size. The tables and figures it cites are not in the reposted text.
