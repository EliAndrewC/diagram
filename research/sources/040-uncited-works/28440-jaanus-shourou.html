jaanus-shourou

Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System (JAANUS), "shourou 鐘楼" ("belfry", title translated), entry in the online dictionary of Japanese architecture and art (https://aisf.or.jp/~jaanus/deta/s/shourou.htm)

What it is: A dictionary entry on the belfry of a Buddhist temple: its types from the Nara period on, its usual position, its roof forms and named examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Nara belfries were two-storied, three bays by two, with the bell in the upper story, that two stood in front of the lecture hall to left and right, that a trapezoidal-based two-storied type appeared late in the Heian period, and that an open single-story type introduced at Todaiji at the start of the 13th century, often with inward-leaning corner pillars, became very popular. It also says the location was not strictly prescribed from the start of the Heian period on; its examples are great temples and it gives no sizes, so it shows the forms, not the common belfry of a village temple.
