en.wikipedia "Yichun Confucian Temple" (宜春文庙, title translated) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yichun_Confucian_Temple)

What it is: A short English Wikipedia article on a Confucian temple it places at Fengcheng, Yichun, Jiangxi (its one reference is a chinakongmiao.org page on the Confucian Temple of Fengcheng County), founded in 1143 under the Southern Song and restored last in 1934. It lists the buildings it once had and gives the size of the one surviving hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists a Song-founded local temple's former buildings (gate, pond, lecture hall, library pavilion, Wenchang palace, Kuixing pavilion, over ten halls and rooms) and the main hall's size, 22.5 m wide, 16.6 m deep and 13.5 m high. That hall is the rebuilt, largest-in-Jiangxi hall, not a typical one, its dimensions are of the restored building, and the article gives no ground plan, site area or dates for the lost buildings. Its title says Yichun but its location and its one reference say Fengcheng county, so whether it was a prefectural or a county temple is unclear.
