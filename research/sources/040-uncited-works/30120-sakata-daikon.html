sakata-daikon

Sakata Seed Corporation, "ダイコンの育て方・栽培方法" ("Daikon no sodatekata, saibai hoho", how to grow daikon, title translated), in the Engei Tsushin gardening site, "Shippai shinai saibai lesson (yasai)" (updated 2025-05-13) (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/lesson-vegetable/detail_25)

What it is: A seed company's home-garden growing guide for daikon, giving its spacing (25-30 cm), digging to 30-35 cm, thinning in three steps to one plant, and harvest 60-90 days after sowing for autumn daikon and 50-60 days for summer daikon.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives spacing, the depth to which the soil must be worked, thinning stages and days to harvest for each season, which bears on how large a daikon plot and how long its crop stands. Its varieties (such as the green-necked type) and its fertilizer and insect netting are modern, and the page states no Edo-period practice.
