zh.wikipedia 拱桥 (arch bridge, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/石拱桥)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the arch bridge, reached by its redirect from 石拱桥 (stone arch bridge), defining the form and giving its oldest stone examples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It explains how a stone arch passes load sideways to its piers, and names the Mycenaean Arkadiko bridge of about 1300 BC, the Caravan Bridge of 850 BC and the Sui-dynasty Zhaozhou Bridge. The page is short, general and mostly about Europe and the Mediterranean, and gives no span, rise or width.
