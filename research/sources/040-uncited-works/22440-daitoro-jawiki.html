ja.wikipedia 大当郎 ("Daitoro", a town district of Nakagawa ward, Nagoya, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/大当郎)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Daitoro and Daitoro-cho, town districts in Nakagawa ward, Nagoya, giving their geography, the list of old small-area (koaza) names, the origin of the name, the sequence of administrative changes since 1889, and population from 1995 to 2019.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its old field names - arata (荒田, read "areta" in an 1882 list), santanda (三反田, three-tan field), furunaeshiro (old seedbed), maruike (round pond), higashi-yashiki and nishi-yashiki (east and west house plots) - show how land near the Shonai river was named, and it says the area marked 常不 on a 14th-century estate map of Tomita is presumed to be this place, which the page defines as fields left unplanted and exempt from tax. The page dates the field names no earlier than an 1882 Aichi prefectural list of koaza names, and its figures (2,896 residents in 2019) describe a modern city district, so only the names and the medieval note speak to the premodern countryside.
