shasou-gakkai-home

Shasou Gakkai (社叢学会, the Society for Shrine Forest Studies), "お知らせ" (Announcements), home page of the society's website (in Japanese; https://shasou.org)

What it is: The news and home page of the Shasou Gakkai, a Kyoto-based nonprofit that studies shrine forests (chinju no mori, temple and shrine woods, burial-mound groves and Okinawan utaki), listing its meetings, seminars and newsletter issues from 2024 to 2026. Its introduction and logo note describe the society's aims and the typical layout of a shrine forest.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its logo note gives the society's schematic of a shrine forest: a first torii at the entrance from the public road, an approach leading to a precinct with the hall, a precinct mostly of planted trees (sometimes with a sacred tree, garden and pond), a surrounding flat-land forest of primeval or climax woodland often forbidden to enter, and beyond it a hillside secondary or mixed forest. That is a generalized diagram with no site, date or measurement behind it, and the rest of the page is announcements of present-day events, so it cannot show how large any grove was or how common each layer was in a premodern village.
