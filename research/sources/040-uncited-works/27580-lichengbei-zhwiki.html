zh.wikipedia 里程碑 (milestone, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/里程碑)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the milestone, a roadside marker giving a distance, with sections by country and on railway mileposts, mostly left in English.

Why it applies, and its limits: It covers Roman, Byzantine and early Islamic milestones, the Mughal Kos Minar about 9 meters tall, and modern highway markers in several countries. It has no section on China, Japan or any premodern East Asian road, and its own banner says it lists no sources.
