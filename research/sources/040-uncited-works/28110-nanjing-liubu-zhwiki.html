zh.wikipedia 南京六部 (Nanjing liubu, the Six Ministries at Nanjing, title translated), simplified-character edition (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/南京六部)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the second set of Six Ministries kept at Nanjing after the Ming capital moved to Beijing, with each ministry's duties and a table of its offices.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how a Ming secondary capital kept a full set of ministries, that each handled affairs for the Southern Metropolitan Area (Nan Zhili), and that the posts were mostly honorary, quoting the Ming shi that holders only 'nurtured a pure reputation'. It lists staffing (one minister and one right vice-minister each) but says nothing about buildings or where they stood, so it supports the staffing and function of the offices, not their grounds.
