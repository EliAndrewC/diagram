kotobank-yadake

Shogakukan, "矢竹(ヤダケ)" ("yadake, the arrow bamboo", title translated), in the Digital Daijisen (デジタル大辞泉) and the Seisen edition of the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (精選版 日本国語大辞典), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%9F%A2%E7%AB%B9-648513)

What it is: A Kotobank dictionary entry defining yadake, a grass-bamboo (Pseudosasa japonica) whose straight, long-jointed stems were used for arrow shafts, with its height and stem size.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says yadake grows wild on hills and fields, was also planted to make arrows, and was already recorded in the Hitachi Fudoki (717-724), and gives its stem as about 4 m by 1 cm in one dictionary and 2-6 m by 1-2 cm with joints 25-60 cm long in the other. The two dictionaries differ on size, and the entry says nothing of where it was grown, how large a planting was, or who tended it.
