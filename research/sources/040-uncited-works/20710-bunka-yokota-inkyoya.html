bunka-yokota-inkyoya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧横田家住宅（長野県長野市松代町松代） 隠居屋" (the retirement house of the former Yokota residence, Matsushiro, Nagano, title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/185066)

What it is: A national important-cultural-property entry for the retirement house (inkyoya) of the former Yokota residence in Matsushiro, Nagano, of about 1820, a thatched hipped-roof building 7.7 m by 4.6 m with a 5.4 m by 2.7 m projection, belonging to a middle-ranking retainer of the Sanada house of Matsushiro domain.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size of a retirement house on a middle samurai's lot of the late Edo period and says the lot keeps its main house, front gate and outbuildings with little alteration, called one of few such lots surviving nationwide. It describes one lot in one castle town and rank in 1820, and states only the retirement house's dimensions, not those of the rest of the lot.
