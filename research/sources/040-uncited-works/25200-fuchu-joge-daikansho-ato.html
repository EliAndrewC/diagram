fuchu-joge-daikansho-ato

Fuchu City (Hiroshima), "天領上下代官所跡（広島県の指定文化財）" ("Site of the Joge intendant's office, a shogunal domain (a Hiroshima Prefecture designated cultural property)", title translated), on the Fuchu City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.fuchu.hiroshima.jp/soshiki/kyoiku_iinkai/kyoikuseisakuka/bingokokufu/shinobunkazai/kenshitei_bunkazai/925.html)

What it is: A municipal designation record, updated in 2018, for the site of the Joge daikansho in Fuchu, Hiroshima, which states how the shogunate's share of the old Fukuyama domain was set and administered from 1698 to the end of the Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that after the Mizuno house's extinction in 1698, 50,000 koku of the 150,000-koku Fukuyama lands became shogunal land under a daikansho at Joge governing 71 villages (about 40,000 koku), and that in 1717 it was reduced to a branch jin'ya of the Iwami Omori daikansho, which gives the scale of an intendant's district. It says nothing of the office's layout, buildings or staff; the site is a designation entry of a few lines, and its 1717 form is only stated as continuing to the end of the shogunate.
