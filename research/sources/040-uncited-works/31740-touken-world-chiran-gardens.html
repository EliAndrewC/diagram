touken-world-chiran-gardens

Touken World (刀剣ワールド), "鹿児島・知覧町の「知覧武家屋敷庭園」" ("The Chiran samurai-residence gardens of Chiran, Kagoshima", title translated), in the guide to daimyo gardens (大名庭園の豆知識), on Touken World, the sword site of the Nagoya Token Museum (in Japanese; https://touken-world.jp/tips/127771)

What it is: A visitor's guide to the seven publicly opened gardens of the samurai quarter at Chiran in southern Kyushu, with the Satsuma domain's outer-castle (tojo) system that produced the quarter. It gives each garden's period of making, its area and its features, then three nearby sights.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Satsuma divided its land into 113 districts and set outer castles in 102, that samurai lived scattered in a settlement at each castle's foot (fumoto), and that the Chiran lane is lined with stone walls and hedges. It gives garden areas of about 198 to 446 square meters with dates from 1741 to the early 19th century; the page prints one garden's dates as 1764 to 1711, which cannot be right, and it covers the Satsuma samurai quarter only, plus a modern war museum that has no bearing here.
