What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the street through the old samurai quarter of Kakunodate in Akita, covering its founding as a castle town, its black board fences and weeping cherries, and its present road and tourism status.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Kakunodate's castle town was laid out in 1620 on the south side of the castle hill, with the samurai quarter in the north and a right-angled jog in the middle street (a masugata) taken from castle design, and that 6.9 hectares around the street are preserved as a samurai quarter. The street is the quarter of one branch family's retainers (the page names another quarter for the main lord's direct vassals), and the page is mostly present-day tourism, road and award detail.
