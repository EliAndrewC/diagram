kannabi-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 神奈備 ("kannabi", the place where a god dwells, a sacred mountain or wood, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/神奈備)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on kannabi, in Shinto the domain that holds a seat or vessel of a god, which can be a mountain, a wood, a sacred tree or a rock, with its word origins and the four kannabi mountains the Izumo no kuni fudoki names.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines kannabi as a god's dwelling place, a mountain or wood or sacred wood, and says old shrines may have neither worship hall nor main hall and worship the natural kannabi itself; its example, Mount Miwa, is the god-body of the Omiwa shrine, which has no main hall. It also lists the four kannabi mountains of Izumo's fudoki with the places they are identified with today; the page carries a notice that it lacks verifiable sources, says nothing of a village's own shrine wood or its size, and several of its claims are marked as needing a citation.
