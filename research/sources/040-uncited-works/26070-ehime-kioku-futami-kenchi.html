Ehime Prefectural Lifelong Learning Center, "双海町誌 第二編 第三章 第六節 検地と石高" ("Futami Town history, part 2, chapter 3, section 6: land surveys and yields", title translated), in the database Memories of Ehime (in Japanese; https://www.i-manabi.jp/system/regionals/regionals/ecode:3/40/view/11591)

What it is: A section of a town history on the land surveys of Futami on the Iyo coast: the Taiko survey, the 1700 village yield register and the 1752 survey with paddy and upland acreage and yields for Kaminada, Takagishi and Takanogawa villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It lists 1700 yields for five villages (Kaminada 667.562 koku) and 1752 acreages, such as Kaminada's 53 cho 5 tan of paddy at 598 koku and 67 cho of upland rated in soybeans at 421 koku. It also records a four-year tax holiday on new fields and 26 mon per tan paid for clearing, and that 41 sites (magistrate's house, shrines, temples) were exempt. It covers four or five coastal villages of one domain. The town history calls the registers of about 1700 vague, with no split between rice and soybeans, so only the 1752 survey separates paddy yields from upland yields. Ratings of upland by soybean yield are local.
