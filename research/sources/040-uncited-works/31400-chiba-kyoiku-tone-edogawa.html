Chiba Prefectural Board of Education, "「ちば文化的景観」(2) 利根川・江戸川と水運のゾーン" ("Chiba bunkateki keikan 2: Tone-gawa, Edo-gawa to suiun no zon", The Chiba cultural landscapes 2: the Tone and Edo rivers and water transport zone, title translated), Chiba Prefecture website, updated 2023-01-06 (in Japanese; https://pref.chiba.lg.jp/kyouiku/bunkazai/bunkaisan/keikan/edogawa.html)

What it is: A prefectural page listing seven cultural landscapes along the Tone and Edo rivers in Chiba: the Yagiri ferry, the rerouting of the Tone, the castle town of Sekiyado, the soy sauce brewing of Noda, Fuse Benten, the hedged homesteads of Nagareyama, and the fisheries of Lake Teganuma.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its entry on the kune says that along the Edo River from Nagareyama to Matsudo a tall clipped hedge of mochi holly and camellia ringed a homestead, and that the raised house ground was planted to stabilize it in the early Edo flood-prone new fields; it also gives Teganuma's present area as 6.5 square kilometers and its greatest depth as 3.8 m, a lake it says was once an inlet. It names the origin of the hedge as what is thought to be, with no study cited, and the hedged houses it describes are the few left after river works of the postwar years.
