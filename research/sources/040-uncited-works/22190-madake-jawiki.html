madake-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "マダケ" (the madake bamboo, Phyllostachys bambusoides, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/マダケ)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on madake, the giant timber bamboo, covering its range, whether it is native to Japan, its form, its roughly 120-year mass flowering, its groves and its uses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the culm size (10 to 20 m tall, up to 10 cm thick), says madake groves on unembanked riverside fields catch driftwood and coarse gravel and so enrich the soil, and lists its uses for bows, ladders, baskets and building trim; it also says Japanese archaeology puts madake-sized bamboo goods in ancient (Nara to Heian) sites, and that whether madake is native or introduced is still disputed. The figures for the mass die-off (about 40,000 ha, a third of Japan's madake groves, from 1962 to 1966) are twentieth-century, and the page dates none of the craft uses.
