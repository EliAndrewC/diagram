Palace Museum, "御花园东井亭" ("East Well Pavilion of the Imperial Garden", title translated), in the museum's building guide (in Chinese; https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236530.html)

What it is: A building entry of the Palace Museum on a Ming-dated well pavilion in the Imperial Garden of the Forbidden City, with its plan, roof, structure and decoration and a glossary of terms.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a pavilion over a well, square in plan with a bay width of 1.94 m, a stone cover on the wellhead and an open hole in the roof above the well, which the page says lets in light and lets a long pole reach the well for dredging. It is a single palace pavilion, which the page calls a rare surviving well pavilion, glazed-tile roofed and painted for the imperial garden, so it is no model for a village well; it gives no depth or water use.
