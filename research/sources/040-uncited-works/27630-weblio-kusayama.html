weblio-kusayama

Weblio Dictionary, "草山(クサヤマ)の意味や使い方" (Meaning and use of kusayama, title translated), entries from the Dictionary of Historical Folk Terms and the Wikipedia subheading dictionary (in Japanese; https://www.weblio.jp/content/草山)

What it is: A dictionary aggregator page for 'kusayama' (grass mountain), giving a one-line historical-folk-terms definition as a place to gather fodder in the early modern period and a passage excerpted from Wikipedia's satoyama article on how such hills were kept.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a kusayama was a mountain deliberately cleared of trees and covered only in grass, that dried grass was an important paddy fertilizer in early modern farming, that villages set aside kusayama to supply it and kept them from regrowing trees, and that some paddies had their own small 'tazuke kusayama'. It gives no area per paddy, no region and no date within the period, and the page says its Wikipedia excerpts are generated mechanically.
