zh.wikipedia "子孫叢林" ("Hereditary monasteries", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/子孫廟)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the zisun conglin, the hereditary kind of Buddhist and Daoist monastery owned by the monks who were tonsured there, set against the open "ten directions" monastery.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that in a hereditary monastery the temple is the private property of its own ordained monks, that its abbot is chosen from within, that Shaolin is the model case, that such temples were very common in the Ming and Qing, and that the hereditary system was abolished after 1949. It gives no counts or sizes, and its three sources are news pieces of 2014 and 2015 and a 2025 association page, not a study of the Ming and Qing temples.
