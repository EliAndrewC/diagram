hirosaki-nakamachi-bukejutaku

Hirosaki Nakamachi Traditional Buildings Preservation Association (弘前市仲町地区伝統的建造物群保存会), "公開武家住宅の概要" ("Overview of the publicly open samurai houses", title translated), on the Hirosaki Nakamachi preservation district site (in Japanese; https://nakachou.main.jp/bukejyutaku/)

What it is: A preservation association's page describing four surviving samurai houses open to visitors in the Nakamachi district of Hirosaki, Aomori, with each house's date, structure, plan and history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a lot of about 16 m frontage and 43 m depth, an owner's house set about 9 m back from the street with a viewing garden in front and a kitchen garden of vegetables and herbs behind, and the ceremonial entrance with reception rooms reached without passing the living rooms. It dates the oldest house to the 18th century and records a 1756 register of the domain's samurai houses. All four houses were moved or restored, and they are middle and lower rank houses of one snowy northern domain, so the page speaks for that rank and climate first.
