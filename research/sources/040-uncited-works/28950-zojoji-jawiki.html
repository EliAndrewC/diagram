ja.wikipedia 増上寺 ("Zojoji", the Tokugawa family temple in Shiba, Tokyo, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/増上寺)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Zojoji, a great head temple (daihonzan) of the Jodo sect, not the sect's head temple (Chion-in in Kyoto), in Minato ward, Tokyo, covering its founding in 1393, its move to Shiba in 1598, the Tokugawa mausolea, surviving buildings and its old main gate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It records one large temple's patrons (the Chiba, Ota and Hojo houses before the Tokugawa), its move when Edo castle was enlarged, and a feng shui reading that set it against the unlucky southwest of the city as Kan'eiji stood against the northeast, which the article gives without a source and only as what "is thought". It is a shogunal temple of the capital, most buildings were lost in 1945 air raids and rebuilt, and it gives no overall precinct size.
