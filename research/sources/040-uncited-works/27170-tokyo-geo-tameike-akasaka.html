tokyo-geo-tameike-akasaka

"（2）溜池" ("(2) Tameike", title translated), page 21 of a technical note on the site tokyo-geo.or.jp; the page names no author (in Japanese; https://www.tokyo-geo.or.jp/technical_note/bv/No39/24.html)

What it is: A one-paragraph note on the Tameike pond in Akasaka, Edo, giving its dating and uses from 1606 to its reclamation in the Meiji 40s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the pond was dammed in 1606 by the lord Asano Yukinaga to secure drinking water and to serve as Edo Castle's outer moat, was larger than Shinobazu pond, stopped serving as a reservoir when the Tamagawa aqueduct opened in 1654 and was then turned to farmland. It gives no dimensions, cites only a map figure, and is one pond of the shogun's capital.
