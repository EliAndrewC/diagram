ja.wikipedia "煮売屋" ("niuriya", a shop selling simmered food, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%85%AE%E5%A3%B2%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the niuriya, an Edo-period shop or stall selling simmered fish, beans and vegetables, with its growth in Edo after the Meireki fire of 1657 and its kinds.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that niuriya multiplied in Edo after 1657 to feed laborers brought in for the rebuilding, that orders against night trading recurred from 1661 to 1799 because cooking fires were a fire risk, and it quotes an 1810 account of four-mon stalls lining Ryogoku. It also names the tea-house, sake-shop and side-dish kinds. It speaks of Edo only, and its footnote notice says the page is poorly sourced; it says nothing of niuriya in smaller towns or villages.
