bunka-minami-jutaku-nagayamon

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "南家住宅門屋及び庭門" ("Minamike jutaku mon'ya oyobi niwamon", the gate house and garden gate of the Minami residence, title translated), on Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/520427)

What it is: A Cultural Heritage Online record of the gate house and garden gate of the Minami residence in Gose, Nara, built in 1912 and registered in 2021, with its floor area and a short description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a long-house gate (nagayamon) on the north edge of the lot, a hip-and-gable roofed east-west building whose gateway of two bays sits west of center with a storeroom on each side, and a wall running south from its corner into a garden gate; it calls it the formal front of an old house. It is a Taisho-year building with no earlier form recorded, and the page gives the gate house's area (35 square meters) but not the lot.
