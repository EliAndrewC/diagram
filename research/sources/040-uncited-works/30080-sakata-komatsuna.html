sakata-komatsuna

Sakata Seed Corporation, "コマツナの育て方・栽培方法" ("Komatsuna no sodatekata, saibai hoho", how to grow komatsuna, title translated), in the Engei Tsushin gardening site, "Shippai shinai saibai lesson (yasai)" (updated 2025-05-13) (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/lesson-vegetable/detail_17)

What it is: A seed company's home-garden growing guide for komatsuna, a non-heading leaf mustard, giving its spacing (5-6 cm), sowing and thinning, pests, and harvest at 25-30 cm, 30-40 days after sowing in spring and 50-80 days in autumn.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the growing season, sowing depth, spacing and days to harvest of a leaf vegetable, and says the name comes from Komatsugawa in Tokyo and that regional varieties differ. It describes modern varieties with row cover, vinyl tunnels, lime and chemical fertilizer, so its schedule is for today's cultivars and methods, and the page gives no Edo-period practice.
