suginami-shiosho-gura

Suginami City Board of Education, "75 塩硝蔵地跡 【その他】(永福1丁目2番)" ("no. 75, the site of the saltpeter store", title translated), cultural-property signboard text of March 1984, on the Suginami ward site (in Japanese; https://city.suginami.tokyo.jp/kyouiku/bunkazai/hyouji/1007877.html)

What it is: The text of a ward cultural-property signboard for the site of the Edo shogunate's gunpowder (saltpeter) store at Izumi Shinden, now in Eifuku, Suginami, Tokyo, covering its founding, size, staffing, and later use as an army powder depot.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the store was set up on this site in the 1750s with a ground of about 18,896 tsubo (about 62,000 square meters), a storage area of 5 buildings and about 23,000 square meters, kept by three resident guards who served by turns, with three laborers a day levied in rotation from 16 nearby villages. It adds that the surroundings were wood and zelkova forest until the end of Meiji. These figures are the signboard's own, given as "said to be", with no source, and cover one shogunate store of the 1750s.
