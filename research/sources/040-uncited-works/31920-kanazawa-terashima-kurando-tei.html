Kanazawa Cultural Foundation (金沢文化振興財団), "武家屋敷 寺島蔵人邸: 寺島蔵人邸について" ("Samurai residence, the Terashima Kurando house: about the house", title translated), Kanazawa Museums site (in Japanese; https://kanazawa-museum.jp/terashima/residence/index.html)

What it is: A museum page on the Terashima Kurando house in Otemachi, Kanazawa, a designated historic site representing the house of a middle-ranking samurai of the Kaga domain, with the garden, rooms and the site's area.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the lot as 1,179.53 m2 with a 244.59 m2 house on two floors and a 52.04 m2 storehouse, and places the house about 230 m east-northeast of the Kanazawa Castle's Otemon gate in a district of equal-sized middle-ranking samurai lots. It is one house of one domain's castle town and says nothing of how many such lots there were or of lower or upper ranks, and it does not say how much of the original lot or buildings survive.
