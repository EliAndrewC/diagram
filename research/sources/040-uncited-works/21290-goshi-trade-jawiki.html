ja.wikipedia 互市 ("goshi", the officially sanctioned border and port trade of Chinese dynasties, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BA%92%E5%B8%82)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on goshi, the officially recognized foreign trading places of Chinese dynasties, and the Ming and Qing trade system built on them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places state-run frontier and port markets in China from the Han onward, and says the Ming at first banned private trade outside tribute missions, then allowed it after the late sixteenth-century pirate raids. It says Edo Japan traded with the Qing under this system and lists Kyakhta, Shanghai, Ningbo, Xiamen and Guangzhou as the main Qing trading points. It stays at the level of policy and gives no market layouts, sizes or customs, so it says nothing of what such a place looked like.
