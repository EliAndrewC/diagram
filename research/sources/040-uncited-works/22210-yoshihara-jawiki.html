yoshihara-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "ヨシ原" (reed bed, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ヨシ原)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the reed bed, the stand of reed (yoshi) that grows on lake shores, riverbanks and coasts, covering how it forms, what lives in it, and its use in treating wastewater.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a reed bed takes up nutrients from the water, builds up humus, and serves as spawning ground for fish such as carp and as habitat for waterbirds, and that it forms in floodplains, wet hollows, river mouths and brackish water. Its opening statements (where reed beds form, their species, the name Yoshiwara) are marked as needing a citation, and the rest has no footnotes, only three field guides listed as references; it gives no date, no region beyond Japan and Europe, and nothing on reed cutting or thatching.
