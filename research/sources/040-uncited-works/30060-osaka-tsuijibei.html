osaka-tsuijibei

Osaka Bunkazai Navi, "築地塀" ("tsuijibei", the plastered earthen wall, title translated), in its glossary (用語集) (in Japanese; https://osaka-bunkazainavi.org/glossary/築地塀)

What it is: A glossary entry on the tsuijibei, a wall built in one piece of earth, set against the nerihei, which stacks roof tiles in earth; it also names the wall's parts and notes the number of horizontal lines on the walls of the palace, official temples and imperial monastic temples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how a tsuijibei differs in structure from a nerihei and that under the ancient ritsuryo system only holders of the fifth rank or higher could put such a wall around a residence, which bears on who walled a compound. It states the rank rule for the ancient period only and gives no wall height or thickness.
