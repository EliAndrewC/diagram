iijima-jinya-goyoba

Iijima Jinya Diary (飯島陣屋日記), "飯島陣屋・御用場" ("Iijima Jinya: the goyoba, the business room", title translated), 2024-05-28, a Hatena Blog post by the Iijima Jinya (in Japanese; https://iijimajinya.hatenablog.com/entry/2024/05/28/105018)

What it is: A short blog post by the Iijima Jinya, a Shinano magistrate's office, on the room in it called the goyoba, where deputies and assistants did the office's clerical work, with the gravel court in front of it where complaints were received.

Why it applies, and its limits: It divides a jinya's duties into jikata (tax collection and civil administration) and kujikata (police and trials), calls the goyoba the center of the jikata, and says the gravel area before it was where village-group headmen's petitions and civil suits were received. It is a few lines about one office in Shinano with no date for the layout it describes and no room sizes.
