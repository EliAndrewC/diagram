funabashi-library-kenchi-nengu

Funabashi City West Library, "江戸時代の農民の負担―検地と年貢―" ("The burdens of Edo-period farmers: land surveys and tribute", title translated), in its local history pages (in Japanese; https://www.lib.city.funabashi.lg.jp/viewer/info.html?idSubTop=2&id=1096&g=23)

What it is: A municipal library essay on the Edo-period land survey and annual rice tribute in what is now Funabashi, Chiba: the survey units, field grades, a table of assumed yields per tan for ten villages, how tribute was levied on the village and what labor levies fell on it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the survey units (one ken, about 180 cm, square being a bu; 30 bu a se; 10 se a tan), and shows assumed yields for the same field grade differ from village to village, with a village's tribute split among households by their holdings and met by the village as a whole. Its table covers only the Funabashi area, and the essay itself says the assumed yields no longer count as a measure of real soil.
