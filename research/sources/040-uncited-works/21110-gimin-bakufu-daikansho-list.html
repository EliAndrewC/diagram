gimin-bakufu-daikansho-list

Muramatsu Fuko, "全国の幕府代官所・郡代役所の一覧" ("List of the shogunate's daikansho and gundai offices nationwide", title translated), on the blog Gimin no Ashiato (in Japanese; https://gimin.travelogues.jp/magistrates_office/)

What it is: A blog page listing dozens of shogunal daikansho and gundai offices as of 1863, each with its province, intendant, whether a main or branch (debari) office, and what stands on the site today.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows how many shogunal intendant offices there were and that main offices were joined by branch offices, and it names the one that survives with its main buildings, Takayama. It lists the situation of 1863 only, says that more offices existed in other years, gives the present condition of the sites rather than their layouts, and sources its list to a 1981 document collection and a 1863 directory.
