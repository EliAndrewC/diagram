What it is: The English Wikipedia article on the Takayama Jin'ya in Hida Province, the office-residence of the shogunate's administrator from 1692 to 1871, with its history, rebuildings and 1996 restoration.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows what a shogunal daikansho looked like and how it came to be: a former castle-foot shimoyashiki converted into a combined residence and office, with storehouses moved from the castle in 1695 and wooden-shingle roofs explained by Hida's snow and timber. It is one site in one snowy timber province, the main building stands as rebuilt in 1816 and restored in 1996 from an 1830 drawing, and it is the only such building left standing, so it cannot say how typical it was.
