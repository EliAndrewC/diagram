ja.wikipedia 錦帯橋 ("Kintaikyo", the Kintai bridge at Iwakuni, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/錦帯橋)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kintai bridge, a five-span wooden arch bridge over the Nishiki River at Iwakuni, Yamaguchi, built in 1673 by the Iwakuni domain lord, with its dimensions, repair record and later history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It documents a wooden bridge 193.3 m long and 5.0 m wide over a river about 200 m wide, built after earlier bridges were washed out, rebuilt on a fixed cycle (arches about every 20 years, girder spans every 40) under domain control, and open only to domain retainers while others used a ferry. Much of the page is about the post-1950 rebuilds, tourism and the bridge's engineering, and it is a single showpiece, not an ordinary river crossing.
