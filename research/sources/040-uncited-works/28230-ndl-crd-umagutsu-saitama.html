Kuki Library, Saitama Prefecture (埼玉県立久喜図書館), "江戸時代末期まで用いられた馬沓（馬草鞋）について知りたい。" ("I want to know about the horse shoes (horse straw sandals) used until the end of the Edo period", title translated), reference case 埼久-2007-040, in the Reference Collaborative Database (レファレンス協同データベース) of the National Diet Library (in Japanese; https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000041427&page=ref_view)

What it is: A library reference answer, registered 2008, that lists the books a librarian found on Edo-period horse shoes (umagutsu, umawaraji) and summarizes three of them in a line each. It also lists books with photographs or drawings of straw horse sandals, and a later comment from another library adds one more title.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states, citing the Nihonshi Daijiten, that horse shoes were mostly of straw, washi paper, horse hair or human hair, and, citing Kiko-shako no rekishi, that no iron horseshoes were used in the Edo period and straw shoes were put on horses in training. It gives only these one-line summaries and a bibliography, with no counts, sizes or places, and the librarian says searches of two magazine and book databases found nothing.
