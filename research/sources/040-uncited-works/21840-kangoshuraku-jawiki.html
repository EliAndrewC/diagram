kangoshuraku-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "環濠集落" ("kango shuraku", moated settlement, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%92%B0%E6%BF%A0%E9%9B%86%E8%90%BD)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on moated settlements: the Yayoi-period ditched villages of Kyushu and Kinki and the medieval ones of the Nara basin and Osaka plain, with the early Chinese roots, site sizes and a list of surviving villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: For the medieval form it says such villages ran to 40 or 50 households, were built for defense or water control as the village leagues (so) developed, and sometimes centered on a temple, which became a temple town; it names Hieda in Yamato-Koriyama as a village whose crooked lanes survive. It also gives Yayoi ditch widths and village lengths from named sites. The page flags itself as lacking references, and its medieval section is marked as needing expansion, so its figures are leads to check, not measurements.
