"Cryptomeria", English Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptomeria)

What it is: An English Wikipedia article on Cryptomeria japonica (sugi, Japanese cedar), the conifer native to Japan: its size, range, growing conditions, timber qualities and uses, ornamental forms, and its place in Japanese culture.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tree's height (up to 60 m) and trunk diameter (up to 4 m), says it grows fast on deep, well-drained, warm and moist soils but not poor soils or cold, dry climates, and says its timber is light, scented and rot-resistant and is used in Japan for all types of construction. It also says sugi is commonly planted around temples and shrines, giving the Nikko cedar avenue as an example, with the date the record of that avenue rests on being the 1894 Sargent account. Several statements on the page are flagged as lacking a citation, and its tree sizes are for the species at its largest, not for a village grove.
