Henan Daily, "内乡县：“天下第一县衙”，常留人间作史鉴" ("Neixiang xian: 'tianxia diyi xianya', chang liu renjian zuo shijian", Neixiang County: the "first county yamen under heaven", kept as a lesson of history, title translated), 10 May 2023, on the Dahe Cube news app (in Chinese; https://app.dahecube.com/nweb/news/20230510/162109n2453f0e8c89.htm?artid=162109)

What it is: A provincial newspaper feature on the Neixiang county yamen in Henan, the best-preserved county government compound in China, now a museum. It recounts its founding in 1304, its destruction and rebuilding under the Ming and Qing, its 1892 rebuilding, its rediscovery in 1983 and its present layout.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the layout and size of a county seat's government compound: 18 courtyards and over 260 rooms on about 47,000 square meters, in three parallel rows, civil offices on the left and military on the right, residence behind the offices, the jail in the south, and the Qing official who rebuilt it in 1892 from the statutory plans. What survives is that Qing rebuilding after a fire in 1857, built to the Ming and Qing codes, so it shows the Qing form, not a Yuan or earlier one; the compound was restored to its "old scale" after 1984, with later buildings cleared, so its counts of courtyards, rooms and area describe the museum as restored, not a survey of the 1892 work; and it gives no plan or dimensions of single buildings.
