agano-mizuhara-rokusaiichi

Agano City, "水原露店市場(水原六斎市)に行こう!" ("Let's go to the Mizuhara open-air market (Mizuhara six-times-a-month market)", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www.city.agano.niigata.jp/soshiki/shokokankoka/sangyoshinko/2/3023.html)

What it is: A municipal page on the Mizuhara street market in Agano, Niigata, held on the days with a 4 or an 8 each month, which says the market is said to be over 300 years old and describes it as it runs today.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that once a daikansho (intendant's office) was placed at Mizuhara, in the middle of the Edo period, its market grew to rank with Shibata and Sanjo as one of the three great markets of Echigo, and that it served the people of Agakita (阿賀北) and the Gozu foothills, which supports a six-times-a-month market beside an intendant's seat. Its history is one sentence of tradition ("said to") with no Edo-era figures; the six market days a month (the days with a 4 or an 8), the stall count of about 30 over about 600 m, the hours of 7 am to 2 pm and the products are those of the market today; for the Edo-period market the page gives only its name, rokusaiichi, and no schedule.
