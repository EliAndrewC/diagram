kagawa-pref-mannoike

Kagawa Prefecture, Land Improvement Division, "満濃池" ("Mannoike", title translated), in its introduction of the main ponds (in Japanese; https://www.pref.kagawa.lg.jp/tochikai/about_tameike/introduction/mannouike.html)

What it is: A prefectural page on Mannoike, the largest irrigation pond in Japan, in Kagawa: its founding in 701-704, repairs by Kukai and Nishijima Hachibei, the breaches, the wooden then stone then tunnel bottom conduit of 1849 to 1870, and the 1940 raising of the dam by 6 m, with the work's figures.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the pond to the Taiho era (701-704), says that it stayed broken from 1184 for about 450 years until Nishijima Hachibei rebuilt it, that the wooden outlet pipe had to be replaced regularly at the cost of its users, and that it breached at least five times. Its figures (storage 15,400 thousand cubic meters, an irrigated area of 3,003 ha, a dam raised 6 m) belong to the 1940-69 project and the modern pond, not to the premodern one.
