kochi-tabi-hayase-ippon-bashi

Kochi Visitors and Convention Association (こうち旅ネット), "早瀬の一本橋" ("The Hayase one-plank bridge", title translated), tourist spot page, updated 2026-03-16 (in Japanese; https://kochi-tabi.jp/search_spot.html?id=13550)

What it is: A tourist-spot page on a simple wooden plank bridge in Tsuno, Kochi, said to be the prototype of the sinking bridge (chinka-bashi) of the Shimanto River.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the bridge as three timber planks about 9 m long, 60 cm wide and 30 cm thick laid across supports, called a washed-away bridge, and says that dozens of years earlier many such bridges stood on tributaries of the Shimanto but this is the only one left in Tsuno. It gives no date for the bridge and nothing of its supports, so it shows the form and size of a surviving plank bridge and does not show how old it is.
