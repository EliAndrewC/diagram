ja.wikipedia 沖端水天宮 ("Okinohata Suitengu", a Suitengu shrine in Yanagawa, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B2%96%E7%AB%AF%E6%B0%B4%E5%A4%A9%E5%AE%AE)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on a small Suitengu shrine in Yanagawa, Fukuoka, founded in 1869 by splitting off the Kurume head shrine's spirit and merging two older local shrines, with its festivals.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a village or district shrine made in 1869 by a branch spirit joined to an Inari shrine and a Gion shrine already in the district, and a May festival with a boat stage. It is a founding after the setting's era, a stub with no layout, and it dates the Inari shrine to 1587 while naming the era Bunroku 3, which is 1594, so its dates are not consistent.
