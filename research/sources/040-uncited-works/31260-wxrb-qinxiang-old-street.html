Jiangnan Evening News, "秦巷老街：百年当铺藏" ("Qinxiang old street: the hundred-year pawnshop hidden away", title translated), 24 October 2024, on Wuxi Xinchuanmei (wxrb.com) (in Chinese; https://wxrb.com/doc/2024/10/24/371610.shtml)

What it is: A 2024 regional newspaper feature on Qinxiang old street in Luoshe town, Huishan district, Wuxi, Jiangsu: its growth from a Ming-era pawnshop street into a market town, its Qing pawnshop house, and its temple fair.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the street's formation to the mid-Ming, names the town's growth as a market for the northern district of Wuxi by the mid-Qing (the saying "Qinxiang by morning, Doumen by night"), and describes a Qing pawnshop house said to be over 200 years old and the largest old pawnshop found in Wuxi. It also records a local-earth-god temple at the street's east end and a fair on the second day of the second lunar month said to run nearly 300 years, with the god's procession every two to three years. The dates are the reporter's and village staff's accounts with no source cited, the pawnshop is a restored building, and it is the Yangtze delta, not northern China.
