Hida Takayama tourist information site (飛騨高山の観光情報), "「高山陣屋」の七不思議（前編）" ("The seven wonders of Takayama Jinya, part 1", title translated), in 飛騨のたばる箱, 2016-02-24 (in Japanese; https://www.takayama-gh.com/tabaru/article/jinya_mystery01/)

What it is: A tourist-site interview in which the director of Takayama Jinya, the Edo-period magistrate's office in Takayama, walks a reporter through the building and explains four of its features.

Why it applies, and its limits: The director says that Hida passed to the shogunate after the Kanamori lords' six-generation, 107-year rule because of its timber, charcoal and mines, that tatami edges differed by rank, that the building has seven entrances and twelve toilets divided by status, and that servants' rooms had no rain shutters. These are the guide's spoken statements with no source given, and the page does not say which fittings are original to the Edo building.
