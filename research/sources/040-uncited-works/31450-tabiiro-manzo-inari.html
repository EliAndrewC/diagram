tabiiro-manzo-inari

Tabiiro (旅色), "萬蔵稲荷神社" ("Manzo Inari Jinja", Manzo Inari Shrine, title translated), tourist guide page for Shiroishi, Miyagi (in Japanese; https://tabiiro.jp/leisure/s/215878-shiroishi-manzouinarijinja)

What it is: A travel-guide page on Manzo Inari Shrine on Mount Batou in Shiroishi, Miyagi, giving its founding legend, a hall built in steps on a mountain slope, more than a hundred small red torii along the approach, a stone fox pair, festivals and visitor details.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a shrine founded about 1785 by a packhorse driver on the old Yamanaka Shichigashuku road (the former Ushu highway), later ranked a village shrine in 1909 after merging sixteen neighboring shrines, and shows a mountain shrine of a stepped plan with more than a hundred small votive torii. The page says the founding year is unknown and the story is a legend, and it was written from the shrine's own information for visitors, so the torii and the plan are those of today.
