Qian Guohong (钱国宏), "小桥流水李坑村" ("Likeng village of little bridges and flowing water", title translated), People's Daily Overseas Edition, on People's Daily Online (人民网), 15 September 2022 (in Chinese; https://env.people.com.cn/big5/n1/2022/0915/c1010-32526522.html)

What it is: A travel essay on a visit to Likeng, a surviving Ming-Qing village in Wuyuan county, Jiangxi, describing its Hui-style houses, the stream that runs through the lanes, its bridges, its tea houses and the family-compound layout.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes a surviving southern village: houses built along the stream and against the hill, with fire walls around each compound, a front court and light well and a rear hall, a bamboo-fenced vegetable garden with spring water piped in by bamboo in nearly every yard, and dozens of stone, wood and brick bridges. It is one writer's visit in 2022, not a survey: its figures are the village's sayings (36 light wells, a family of nine metropolitan graduates), and it mixes Ming-Qing buildings with present-day tourism such as tea houses and bamboo rafts.
