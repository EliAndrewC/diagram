zh.wikipedia 叶县县衙 ("the Ye County magistrate's yamen", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/叶县县衙)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the county yamen at Ye County, Henan, the only surviving Ming-dynasty government office in China.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the yamen was begun in 1396, that only the five-bay main hall survived the wars at the end of the Ming and the other buildings are Qing, and that it is laid out as a central axis with two side groups. It prints the area as over 16,000 square kilometers, which cannot be one compound, so the figure is unusable as written, and the article has no room plan or building dimensions.
