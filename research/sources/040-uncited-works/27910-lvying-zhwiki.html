zh.wikipedia 綠營 (清朝) ("the Green Standard Army of the Qing", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/%E7%B6%A0%E7%87%9F_(%E6%B8%85%E6%9C%9D))

What it is: The Traditional-Chinese Wikipedia article on the Green Standard Army, the Han-staffed regular army of the Qing (1644-1912). It covers the army's origin, its decline, its chain of commands from the provincial commander down to the local post, and its strength.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the ladder of commands and the size of each rung: a provincial army of ten thousand to sixty or seventy thousand, a garrison of a thousand to two or three thousand, and a post (汛) of a dozen to over a hundred soldiers at one station. For the mid-Qing it counts 66 garrisons, 1,169 camps and 648,345 men by the Qianlong code, and 861,671 men in 1812. All of this is Qing, not an earlier dynasty's army, and the article itself describes the force as hollowed out by absent men and rented-out war junks by the 1830s-1840s, so its later figures are for a paper strength.
