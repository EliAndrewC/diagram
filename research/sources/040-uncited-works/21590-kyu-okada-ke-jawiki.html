ja.wikipedia 旧岡田家住宅 ("kyu Okada-ke jutaku", the former Okada house, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%97%A7%E5%B2%A1%E7%94%B0%E5%AE%B6%E4%BD%8F%E5%AE%85)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the former Okada house in Itami, Hyogo, a townhouse built in 1674 with a sake brewery added in 1715, designated an Important Cultural Property in 1992 and opened to the public after a dismantling survey and restoration.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates a merchant townhouse to 1674 and its brewery to about 1715, which it calls the oldest surviving brewery of certain date in Japan, and records that the shop and brewery were pulled down and rebuilt as a restoration after 1987. It is a single urban sake-brewing house of the Kansai, so it speaks for a town merchant's house and not a village one, and the page gives no dimensions or plan.
