kanjipedia-shigarami

Japan Kanji Aptitude Testing Foundation (公益財団法人 日本漢字能力検定協会), "しがらみ" ("shigarami", a brushwood fence, title translated), entry under the character 柵, in Kanjipedia (漢字ペディア) (in Japanese; https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kotoba/0002568200)

What it is: A dictionary entry for the word shigarami, written with the character 柵, with its two senses: a row of driven stakes with bamboo or wood laid across to hold back water, and, in the figurative sense, whatever holds one back.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the water-holding sense as stakes driven in a row with bamboo or wood laid across them, which names the structure and its materials. It gives no date, no place, no dimensions and no use of the structure beyond damming water, so it supports only what the word means.
