Ma Yanru (马彦如), "焦山定慧寺大雄宝殿重修纪实" ("An account of the rebuilding of the Great Hall of Dinghui Temple on Jiaoshan", title translated), Zhenjiang News, Jinshan Net (jsw.com.cn), April 23, 2023 (in Chinese; https://jsw.com.cn/2023/0423/1780424.shtml)

What it is: A local newspaper report on the 2021-2022 repair of the Great Hall (大雄宝殿) of Dinghui Temple on Jiaoshan, Zhenjiang, Jiangsu, a provincial-level protected timber building. It gives the temple's history from its stated founding in AD 194 and describes the hall as a Ming-style double-eaved hip-roofed building, with the repair method and the areas of the hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the hall's footprint, 780 square meters of site with 630 of building and a 150-square-meter terrace, and that its main timber frame is Ming survival with the highest-grade roof form of Ming and Qing halls, so it anchors the scale and form of a principal Buddhist hall in a Jiangnan temple. The figures are those of the present repaired hall, not of any earlier state; the page says no drawings survive from earlier repairs, and its claims of 98 jian (bays) of halls (殿宇98间) and 3,000 monks in the Ming and of an AD 194 founding are stated without a source. The page calls Dinghui a famous Jiangnan monastery and its hall carries the highest-grade roof, so the hall marks the top of the range for a principal hall, not an ordinary town temple's.
