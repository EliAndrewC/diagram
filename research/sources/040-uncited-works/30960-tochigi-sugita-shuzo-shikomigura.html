tochigi-sugita-shuzo-shikomigura

Tochigi Prefecture Cultural Promotion Division, "【杉田酒造仕込蔵】" ("Sugita Brewery brewing storehouse"), in the prefecture's Cultural Properties search (in Japanese; https://bunkazai.pref.tochigi.lg.jp/cultural/【杉田酒造仕込蔵】)

What it is: A prefectural cultural-property record for the brewing storehouse of Sugita Brewery in Oyama, Tochigi, a nationally registered building, with its location, ownership and a description of its structure.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a sake brewery's storehouse as a mud-walled kura of 5 by 7.5 bays with a hipped tiled roof, moved in the mid-Meiji period from another brewery in the city, with a two-storey middle part, and it places the brewery beside the Uzuma River. It gives no floor area in meters and no brewing equipment, and the page dates only the building's move to this site in the mid-Meiji period, not when it was built, so it shows a brewery as laid out in the Meiji and Taisho periods (a Taisho storehouse is joined to it), not an Edo-period one.
