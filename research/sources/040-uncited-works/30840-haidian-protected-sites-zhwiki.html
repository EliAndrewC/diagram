zh.wikipedia 北京市海淀区文物保护单位 ("Beijing shi Haidian qu wenwu baohu danwei", the district-protected heritage sites of Haidian District, Beijing, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/北京市海淀区文物保护单位)

What it is: A Chinese Wikipedia list of the district-level protected heritage sites of Haidian, Beijing, announced in four batches from 1981 to 2014, with a paragraph for most entries on its history, layout and surviving buildings: temples, shrines, tombs, old courtyard houses and gardens, and a few memorials.

Why it applies, and its limits: Its entries give layouts and sizes of many Ming and Qing temples and shrines on the outskirts of Beijing, such as a temple's quoted stele listing its gate, hall, bell and drum towers and monks' rooms, a village shrine's stage, and a teahouse for pilgrims on the road to Miaofeng Mountain. They are descriptions of what survives and was restored, drawn from the district's own record without notes by entry, and many entries are of the late Qing and the 1920s to 1960s, such as eunuch temples and tombs, so they speak for the capital's suburbs and not an ordinary village.
