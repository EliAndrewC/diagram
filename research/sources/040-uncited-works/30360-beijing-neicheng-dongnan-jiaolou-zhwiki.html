zh.wikipedia 北京内城东南角楼 ("the southeast corner tower of Beijing's inner city", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/北京内城东南角楼)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on the corner arrow tower at the southeast of Beijing's Ming and Qing inner city, with its building dates, repair history and a full set of dimensions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the tower's measurements: built 1437 to 1439, a platform 12 m high and 39.45 m on a side under a 17 m tower of 701.3 square meters, 144 arrow windows in four rows (14 per row on the wide faces, 4 on the narrow), and a footprint of 3,654 square meters with the connected wall. It is the capital's corner tower, damaged in 1900 and rebuilt by dismantling in 1981 to 1983, with the 1935 repair replacing parts, so it is an upper bound for a city-corner tower and not a county city's.
