Semboku City, "武家屋敷" ("Samurai Residences", title translated), in the sightseeing information for the Kakunodate area, on the Semboku city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.semboku.akita.jp/sightseeing/spot/07_buke.html)

What it is: A municipal sightseeing page on six preserved samurai houses of Kakunodate in Akita: the Iwahashi, Matsumoto, Aoyagi, Ishiguro, Kawarada and Odano houses. For each it gives the household's rank and lord (the Satake Kita house), a few features of the building, and opening hours.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies the stipend of one upper retainer (75 koku, the Iwahashi), the house features the page names (a black-boarded fence with peep windows, a medicine-gate with a dated roof board of 1809 or 1860, separate main and side entrances used by the rank of the guest, thatched hip roofs), and a lowly household in the foot-soldier quarter. The houses are late Edo and Meiji (the Kawarada house is of 1891, the Odano main house was rebuilt after the great fire of 1900), several were altered or restored, and the page gives no plot sizes or plans.
