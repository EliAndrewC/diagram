nagano-cvb-higuchi

Nagano Tourism and Convention Bureau (公財 ながの観光コンベンションビューロー), "旧樋口家住宅" ("kyu Higuchi-ke jutaku", the former Higuchi family residence, title translated), on its Nagano sightseeing site (in Japanese; https://nagano-cvb.or.jp/modules/sightseeing/page/40)

What it is: A short tourism-bureau page on a surviving samurai house in Matsushiro, Nagano, once a retainer's residence of the Sanada family's 100,000-koku Matsushiro domain. It describes the plot and the buildings now standing on it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places an upper samurai house (a 230-koku retainer in the last years of Edo) in Tonomachi, the samurai quarter near the castle's front gate and beside the lord's mansion, calls such houses a kind of public housing, and says the south of the plot was fields in the Higuchi era, with the main house, storehouse, row house, household shrine, gate and walls on the north. It describes one house of one rank as it survives, so the field and the buildings are stated for that plot only, and it says the front gate was moved there from elsewhere.
