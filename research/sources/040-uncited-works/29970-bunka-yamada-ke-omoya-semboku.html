Agency for Cultural Affairs, "山田家住宅主屋" ("Yamada house, main house," title translated), Cultural Heritage Online (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/187530)

What it is: A national database entry for the main house of the Yamada family in Shinke village, Sennan, Osaka, rebuilt in 1863, single-story with a partial second floor, 119 square meters, registered in 2002.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Yamada were wealthy farmers who served generations as village headmen near the Kumano road, and that the house keeps old timbers upstairs and an old staggered three-room plan. The commentary is brief, it gives one headman's house of the Osaka plain, and the building is a rebuild of 1863: the old timbers upstairs are taken to come from the earlier house and the plan keeps an older form, but the page does not date that earlier house.
