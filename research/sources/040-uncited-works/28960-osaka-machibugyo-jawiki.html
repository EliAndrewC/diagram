ja.wikipedia 大坂町奉行 ("Osaka machibugyo", the Osaka city magistrates, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/大坂町奉行)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the two Osaka city magistrates of the Edo shogunate, with the office's founding in 1619, its staff, court days, turnover and lists of the holders of both offices.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a magistrate's staff of 30 yoriki and 50 doshin each at the East and West offices, the yoriki's grant of 500 tsubo and the doshin's of 200 tsubo, a rotating monthly duty, and eight civil court days a month. It describes a shogunal city magistrate, not a provincial one, and the page itself says its footnotes are too thin to trace its sources.
