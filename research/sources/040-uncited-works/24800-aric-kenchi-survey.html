aric-kenchi-survey

ARIC (一般社団法人農業農村整備情報総合センター), "土地を量る、検地" ("Measuring the land: the cadastral survey", title translated), in "国土づくりを支えた技術" (Techniques that supported land-making), "技術の歩み", in Suido no Ishizue (水土の礎) (in Japanese; https://suido-ishizue.jp/daichi/part3/01/11.html)

What it is: A short page on the Japanese land survey (kenchi), centered on Hideyoshi's survey of 1594-95: its units, the registers it produced, the surveying tools and how areas were worked out.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the survey's units in modern equivalents (1 ken about 1.8 m = 6 shaku 3 sun; 1 tan about 10 a = 10 se = 300 bu), says each plot was entered with its grade, area, assessed yield and cultivator, and describes tools such as the measuring pole and the sighting bamboo, with an allowance for the sag of the measuring rope. It states that the survey "seems" to have unified standards nationwide, and it does not say how plots were shaped or sized or how other surveys differed.
