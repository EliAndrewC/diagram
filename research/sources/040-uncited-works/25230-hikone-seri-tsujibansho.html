hikone-seri-tsujibansho

Hikone City, "61 旧彦根藩足軽組辻番所（善利組）" ("No. 61, the former Hikone domain ashigaru-unit street guardhouse (Seri unit)", title translated), in the Hikone City cultural properties list (in Japanese; https://www.city.hikone.lg.jp/kakuka/kanko_bunka/8/2_2/4642.html)

What it is: A municipal designation entry, updated in 2024, for a surviving street guardhouse (tsujibansho) in the front garden of a former ashigaru house in Hikone's Seri district, designated a city property in 2009 and dated to the late Edo period.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places the guardhouse at the south end of the front garden of the former Isojima house, which stands at the north-west corner of the crossing with Nakatsuji street in the middle of the former Serihashi 12-chome, jutting slightly into the street for a clear view, with watch windows on two streets and entry from the street rather than through the house, and reasons that one such post stood at each ashigaru unit's block, perhaps 37, manned in turn. That count and the post's use are the page's own inferences ("it can be considered", "it is presumed"), drawn from the single guardhouse that survives; the page gives no size and speaks only of Hikone.
