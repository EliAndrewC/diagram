setagaya-bukeyashiki-mon

Setagaya Ward, "武家屋敷門（ぶけやしきもん）" ("The Samurai Residence Gate", title translated), in its introduction to the cultural properties within the ward, on the Setagaya ward website (in Japanese; https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02059/3398.html)

What it is: A ward cultural-property entry on a gatehouse of the nagayamon type, a roofed gate with rooms along its sides, a designated ward property at Okamoto, Tokyo. It gives its building area (151.8 square meters, one story, hip-and-gable tiled roof) and its moves, from the lower estate of Iki, chief retainer of the Okayama domain, to Kioicho in 1937, to Okamoto in 1963 and to its present site in 1978.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured floor area for a nagayamon of a domain's chief retainer's lower estate and dates it to the middle of the Edo period. The page says the gate is only "said to" come from the Iki estate, it has been moved three times and now serves as a condominium's management office and meeting room, and the building's width, depth and gate opening are not given.
