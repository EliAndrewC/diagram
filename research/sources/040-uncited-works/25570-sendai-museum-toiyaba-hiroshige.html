Sendai City Museum, "キッズコーナー（宿駅の様子（浮世絵））" ("Kids' Corner: A Post Station (Ukiyo-e)", title translated), in its children's section on Edo-period travel and the alternate-attendance system, on the Sendai city website (in Japanese; https://www.city.sendai.jp/museum/kidscorner/kids-08/kidscorner-04/kids-28.html)

What it is: A children's explanation by a city museum of a Hiroshige print of the toiyaba (post-station office) at Shono, on the Tokaido, explaining what a toiyaba was and the relay system of carrying goods and travelers from one station to the next by porters and horses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the toiyaba as the station office that gathered horses, palanquins and porters to forward travelers and baggage, and the relay (jinba tsugitate) from station to adjoining station. It is a few sentences written for children about one print, which the page says shows the porters humorously, and it gives no numbers of horses or porters, fees or building sizes.
