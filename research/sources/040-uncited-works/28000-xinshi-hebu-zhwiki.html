zh.wikipedia 新市河埠群 ("the Xinshi river landing steps", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E6%96%B0%E5%B8%82%E6%B2%B3%E5%9F%A0%E7%BE%A4)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the row of Ming-Qing river landing steps along the west bank of the market river at Xinshi town, Deqing county, Zhejiang, a provincially protected site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Ming-Qing stone quay runs about 1,100 m along the market river, is built of local Wukang stone about 2 m high, and holds 34 landing steps of several forms, double-drop and single-drop among them, so it shows how a water-town street met its canal. It is a few lines long with one source, gives no date for any step and does not describe the steps' dimensions or the houses behind them.
