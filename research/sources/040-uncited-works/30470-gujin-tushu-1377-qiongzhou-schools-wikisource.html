Gujin tushu jicheng (欽定古今圖書集成), Fangyu huibian, Zhifang dian, juan 1377, "瓊州府學校考" (Qiongzhou prefecture schools, title translated), Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/欽定古今圖書集成/方輿彙編/職方典/第1377卷)

What it is: Scroll 1377 of the Qing imperial encyclopedia, on Wikisource: the school record of Qiongzhou prefecture on Hainan, giving for the prefectural school and each county and department school its founding, moves and rebuildings from the Song to about 1686, with its community schools, academies and the school lands that supported them.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows what a prefecture's schools consisted of and how they moved and were rebuilt (hall, side halls, gate, ponds, shooting ground, dormitories), and lists endowment fields and ponds by plot, area in mu and rent in silver, with a few lot measurements in zhang and chi. It is a Hainan frontier prefecture quoting its gazetteers up to the Kangxi reign, it describes buildings in terms of names and dates and not plans or dimensions, and the Wikisource punctuation was added by a program, so the page itself invites corrections.
