Edo-Tokyo Museum Library, "北町奉行所、南町奉行所はどこにあったのか、史跡はないか。" ("Where were the North and South town magistrates' offices, and are there historic sites?", title translated), a reference answer in its reference service (in Japanese; https://www.edo-tokyo-museum.or.jp/library/reference/5768.html)

What it is: A library reference answer saying where Edo's North and South town magistrates' offices (machi-bugyosho) finally stood, and where memorial stones mark them in Chiyoda ward today.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the South office ended at Sukiyabashi and the North office inside Gofukubashi, that both lasted until their abolition in 1868, and that the offices moved several times before that. It names the books it relies on and gives no map, plan, ground size or building layout, only the places and the two stone markers.
