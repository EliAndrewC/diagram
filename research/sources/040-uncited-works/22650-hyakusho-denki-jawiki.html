ja.wikipedia 百姓伝記 ("Hyakusho denki", Record of the Farmers, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/百姓伝記)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on the Hyakusho denki, a manuscript farming treatise of the late seventeenth century from the Tokai region, with no printed edition in its own day.

Why it applies, and its limits: It tells what the treatise is: written from the cultivator's side, centered on rice with little on cash crops, with much on flood control and a detailed account of storm surges, and dated by its editor to about 1680 and tied to the Totomi Yokosuka domain, its author guessed to be of village-official rank. The article carries none of the treatise's own measurements or practices, so it can only point to the book as the place to look, and the dating and authorship are the editor's inference, since the manuscript names neither author nor year.
