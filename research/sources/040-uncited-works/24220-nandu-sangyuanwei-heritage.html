Guan Wanling, "揭秘:与都江堰齐名的佛山桑园围,竟是这么牛!" ("Revealed: Foshan's Sangyuanwei, which rivals Dujiangyan, is this impressive!", title translated), Nandu Foshan (南都佛山), 2020-12-09, on the Nandu mobile news site (in Chinese; https://m.mp.oeeee.com/a/BAAFRD000020201209388957.html)

What it is: A newspaper feature on the Sangyuanwei, the diked polder system of Nanhai and Shunde in the Pearl River delta, written when it was named a World Heritage Irrigation Structure in December 2020, with a history from the Song to its 2020 listing and a table of its parts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a dated history (Song dikes of 1102 to 1110, the closing of the last reverse-flow gap in 1396, the first use of the name in 1642) and a count of the works: a 83.86 km perimeter, 265.4 square km inside, 57 channels with a recorded history (by an incomplete count) and 63 historical sluices, of which 30 survive intact and 10 were rebuilt on their sites. Its figures are those of the system as it stood in 2020, after the steel and concrete gates of 1925-1926 and its joining with the Qiaobei dike in 1985, not a Ming or Song layout, and beyond a Yuan dike height of about 3 to 4 m it gives no cross-sections of dike or sluice.
