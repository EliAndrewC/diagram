Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Chubu Regional Development Bureau (国土交通省中部地方整備局), "木曽三川の歴史 輪中（わじゅう）＜昔の人のくらしと知恵（ちえ）＞" ("History of the Kiso Three Rivers: the wajuu, the life and wisdom of people long ago", title translated), a children's page (kisojyo/child) on the bureau's site (in Japanese; https://www.cbr.mlit.go.jp/kisojyo/child/012.html)

What it is: A very short children's page on the wajuu, a settlement enclosed by an encircling embankment against flooding in the Kiso Three Rivers country. It describes the embankment, the raised flood storehouse (mizuya), the boat hung under the eaves, and the dug-up raised rice plots (horita) with their ditches.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the wajuu people built the embankment and helped one another in floods, that a mizuya stood higher than the main house and held rice and miso, that a boat was hoisted under the eaves for escape, and that low wet land was farmed on raised plots with the dug-out ditches used as water lanes. It gives no date, no size and no place within the three rivers' lowland, and it says the land has since been drained into ordinary paddies, so it names the forms without measuring them.
