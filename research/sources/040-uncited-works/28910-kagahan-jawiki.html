ja.wikipedia 加賀藩 ("Kaga-han", the Kaga domain, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/加賀藩)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kaga domain (Kanazawa), the Maeda domain of Kaga, Noto and Etchu, rated at 1,025,000 koku. It covers the domain's history, its lords, its retainer ranks and the long lists of its senior houses with their stipends, its Edo residences, and its villages at the end of the shogunate.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the largest domain's retainer structure: eight top houses of 10,000 koku or more, about seventy houses of the jinmochi rank from about 1,000 koku to over 10,000, then ordinary samurai and foot soldiers, and the village counts by district at the end of the Edo period. It also lists the Edo residences (upper, middle, lower and rice-store) with their areas, such as 60,000 tsubo. Kaga was the largest domain by far and a favored one, so these are upper bounds, and the article gives no breakdown of the numbers of ordinary samurai or of how they lived in the castle town.
