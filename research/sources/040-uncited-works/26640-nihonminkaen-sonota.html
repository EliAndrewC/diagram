nihonminkaen-sonota

Kawasaki City Japan Open-Air Folk House Museum, "その他建造物" ("Other buildings", title translated), on the museum's site (in Japanese; https://www.nihonminkaen.jp/other.html)

What it is: A museum page listing three relocated buildings: the Hara house, a large two-story Meiji house from Kawasaki; the Funakoshi kabuki stage, a fishing village's stage from Shima, Mie; and an Okinoerabu raised granary from Kagoshima. Each has its building type, structure, floor dimensions and date.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives measured floor dimensions and dates for a village stage built in 1857 (9.1 m by 10.8 m main body, with a revolving stage and a space below) and a thatched raised granary of the late 19th century (2.7 m by 2.5 m, posts wrapped in iron to keep out rats). The Hara house dates from 1911 and so shows Meiji, not Edo, building, and each building is a single example moved to a museum, not a typical one for its region.
