ugomachi-tashiro-kura

Ugo Town tourism site (羽後町ならでは), "日本一の蔵 !" ("Japan's finest storehouses!", title translated), with the plasterer Yamauchi Toru, on the two landlord storehouses of Tashiro, Ugo, Akita (in Japanese; https://ugomachi.jp/tashironokura/)

What it is: A tourism-site feature in which a specialist earthen-storehouse plasterer explains the two landlord storehouses of the Tashiro district of Ugo, Akita: the former Hasegawayama house storehouse (black polished plaster, six-step door rebates) and the Asen family storehouse (polished white plaster doors). It also says what a storehouse was for and how it was built.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states the purpose of an earthen storehouse as protecting a household's valuables from fire, theft and damp, when the main house had only paper-screen doors and wooden shutters, and describes the construction: closely set posts, thick earth walls, very small windows, lime plaster, and a wooden outer shell (saya) against snow. It dates neither storehouse and gives no dimensions, and both are rich landlord storehouses in a snowy mountain district of northern Honshu, so they are the upper end of what a village held, not the common granary.
