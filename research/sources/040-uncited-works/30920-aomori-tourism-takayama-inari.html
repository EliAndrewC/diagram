aomori-tourism-takayama-inari

Aomori Prefecture tourism site (Aomori Tourism and International Exchange Organization), "高山稲荷神社" ("Takayama Inari Shrine"), in 観光スポット (sightseeing spots) (in Japanese; https://aomori-tourism.com/spot/detail_530.html)

What it is: A tourism-board page on Takayama Inari Shrine in Tsugaru City, Aomori, known for a long run of vermilion torii gates, with its enshrined deities, hours and access.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the torii are about 2 m high and run on without end, that stone fox figures stand among them, that the shrine is said to date from Kamakura to Muromachi times, and that its gods are asked for good harvests, safe seas and trade. The founding date is a tradition the page does not support, and it describes the shrine as a visitor sees it now, with no layout figures.
