jtb-seki-juku

Japan Travel Bureau Foundation (公益財団法人日本交通公社), "関宿" ("Seki-juku", the Seki post town), in the National Tourism Resource Ledger (全国観光資源台帳), as of August 2024 (in Japanese; https://tabi.jtb.or.jp/res/240049-)

What it is: A tourism-resource ledger entry on Seki-juku in Kameyama, Mie, the 47th post town of the Tokaido, with its roughly 1.8 km street and its surviving townhouses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports a long, single-street post town: about 1.8 km long with some 200 townhouses of the Edo to Meiji years still standing on both sides, sited at the east foot of the Suzuka range, where an ancient barrier stood. It adds that two inns were famed (Tsuruya and Tamaya) and that the town's float festival once had 16 floats in the Bunka years and now has four. It gives no lot width, street width or house count from the Edo period itself, and the 200 houses are what has survived into the present.
