fuchu-kanko-jouge-daikansho

Fuchu City Tourism Association, "天領上下代官所跡" ("Site of the shogunal-domain Jouge daikansho", title translated), on its FUN! FAN! FUCHU! tourism site (in Japanese; https://fuchu-kanko.jp/recommended/recommended-842/)

What it is: A tourism-association page on the site of the Jouge daikansho in Fuchu, Hiroshima, the intendant's office for shogunal land in Bingo and Bitchu, with its dates and the villages it governed.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a worked case of how shogunal land was administered locally: after 1698, 50,000 koku of the former Fukuyama domain went to the shogunate, and the Jouge office governed 71 villages (about 40,000 koku) in three districts, with a resident intendant from 1700 to 1717 and then a branch office of the Iwami Omori intendancy to the end of the period. It is a short visitor page, it says only that the surrounding stone wall is original, and it gives no layout, building sizes or staffing.
