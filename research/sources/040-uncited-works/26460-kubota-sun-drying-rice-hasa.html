kubota-sun-drying-rice-hasa

Kubota Corporation, "天日での乾燥と稲の運搬" ("Sun-drying rice and carrying it", title translated), from 稲作の歴史とそれを支えた伝統農具, on Kubota no Tanbo (in Japanese; https://www.kubota.co.jp/kubotatanbo/history/tools/drying_02.html)

What it is: A short company educational page on drying cut rice in bundles on a drying rack for about two weeks, the stake stack called a nio, and the two-person carrying basket and cart that carried it to the threshing place.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a time and a figure for the drying rack - about two weeks, grain falling from 20-25 percent water to about 15 - which supports the drying rack as a harvest feature. It does not date the practice, name a region beyond the regional terms it lists, or say how far the rice was carried; its cart dimensions are of the one cart pictured, credited to a book published in Akita (渡部景俊, 農を支えて).
