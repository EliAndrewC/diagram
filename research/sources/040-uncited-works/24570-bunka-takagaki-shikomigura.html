bunka-takagaki-shikomigura

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "高垣酒造仕込蔵" ("Takagaki shuzo shikomigura", the Takagaki brewery's brewing storehouse, title translated), on Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/index.php/heritages/detail/185563)

What it is: A Cultural Heritage Online record of the brewing storehouse of the Takagaki sake brewery in Aridagawa town, Wakayama, dated 1926 and registered in 2006, with its floor area and a short description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the measured form of a brewery's main building: ten bays long by six wide, two stories, 351 square meters, with a lean-to three bays wide along the whole south face and regular openings on the north face along the road. It is a Taisho building, so it speaks for a brewer's building of the 1920s, and the page has no earlier brewery plan.
