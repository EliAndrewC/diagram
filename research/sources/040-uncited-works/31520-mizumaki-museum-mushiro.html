mizumaki-museum-mushiro

Mizumaki Town Historical Museum, "むしろ" ("Mushiro", the straw mat, title translated), in the museum's collection, folk materials, agriculture (in Japanese; https://town.mizumaki.lg.jp/museum/040/020/020/470/1249.html)

What it is: A collection record from a town history museum in Fukuoka for a straw mat (mushiro): what it is made of, how it is used, and the object's size (200 by 93 cm), material (straw) and date (postwar Showa).

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the mushiro as an ordinary farm article: woven of straw, rush, sedge or bamboo, laid on a farmhouse's plank or earthen floor, hung at a doorway against wind and rain, used for drying grain and as packing. The catalog object itself is a postwar piece, so its measurements show a mat of the postwar Showa years, not an Edo one, and the page gives no area of mat per yard or per household.
