ranhaku-ochihata-1693

"御公儀様差出帳 元禄6年：越畑 船戸悟兵衛家文書" ("Report submitted to the shogunate, Genroku 6: Ochihata, the Funato Gohei family papers", title translated), reprinted from the Ranzan Town History, in Ranzan Town Web Natural and Local History, volume 6 (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/01_06.html)

What it is: A transcription of the 1693 village report of Ochihata in Musashi province (now Ranzan, Saitama), an answer to a list of questions put to a village when it passed under new administration. It lists the village's land, trades, shrines and temples, households, inhabitants and horses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives 84 farm households (4 landless), 478 inhabitants, 59 horses and no oxen, a village 8 chō east to west and 18 chō north to south, mulberry and silk and cotton cloth, 3 carpenters, 1 dyer, 2 oil makers and 1 sake brewer, and a granary 4 ken by 2 ken. It is one village's own account to officials in 1693 and counts only what the questions asked, and the transcription has a damaged character and an uncertain reading in places.
