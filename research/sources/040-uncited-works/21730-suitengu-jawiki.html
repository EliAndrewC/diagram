ja.wikipedia 水天宮 ("Suitengu", the shrines of the water deity, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E5%A4%A9%E5%AE%AE)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Suitengu shrine family headquartered at Kurume in Fukuoka, giving its deities, what it is prayed to for, its festival days and a regional list of its branch shrines.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the shrines guard water and children, with prayers against drowning and for fishing, shipping, farming and safe birth, and that the monthly fair falls on the 5th. Its list names 46 present-day shrines, ten of them in Fukuoka, and says 27 religious corporations in Japan bear the name; the list gives only the principal shrines, so it shows how widely the cult spread but not when or where each shrine stood in the past. It marks two statements as lacking a source.
