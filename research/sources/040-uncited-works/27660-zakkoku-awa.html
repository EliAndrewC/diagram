zakkoku-awa

Japan Millet Association (一般社団法人 日本雑穀協会), "アワ（粟）" (Foxtail millet, title translated), project page on cereal grains (in Japanese; https://www.zakkoku.jp/archives/project/foxtail_millet)

What it is: A trade association's short profile of foxtail millet, giving its classification, origin, the prefectures that grow it in Japan today and its place in Japanese food history.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says foxtail millet, with barnyard millet, is Japan's oldest grain, grown since the Jomon period and 'apparently' the staple before rice arrived, that over two thousand varieties exist and most grown now are glutinous. It gives no yields, field forms or dates when rice displaced it, hedges the staple claim, and lists present producing prefectures (Iwate, Hokkaido, Nagano, Tokushima, Nagasaki) that need not match the setting's.
