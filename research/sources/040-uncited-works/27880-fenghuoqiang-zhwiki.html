fenghuoqiang-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia "封火墙" ("The fire-sealing wall", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/封火墙)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the fenghuo qiang, the raised gable wall of a traditional Chinese house or village that stops fire passing from roof to roof, with its regional shapes.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says fire was seen to climb the wooden posts, so the first remedy was plaster over timber and the wall followed once posts were set into brick gables, that houses joined by such walls checked fire well, and that Huizhou's form is the horse-head wall and Fuzhou's the saddle wall. The page itself carries banners saying it has no sources and needs a specialist, it gives no dates or heights, and its five-element naming is geomancy, so it cannot date the form or say how common it was.
