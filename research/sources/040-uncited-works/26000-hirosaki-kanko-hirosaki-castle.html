hirosaki-kanko-hirosaki-castle

Hirosaki Tourism and Convention Association, "史跡 弘前城" ("Historic site Hirosaki Castle", title translated), on the association's Hirosaki tourist information site (in Japanese; https://www.hirosaki-kanko.or.jp/details.html?id=CNT00403281549012844)

What it is: A tourist-association page on Hirosaki Castle in Aomori, giving the castle's founding dates, the extent of the grounds and its surviving structures, followed by visitor information and a notice that the keep's interior is closed from November 2025.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the castle's plan: planned in 1603 by Tsugaru Tamenobu, begun in 1610 and finished in 1611, about 600 m east-west and 1000 m north-south on about 50 ha, six enclosures within three moats and earthen ramparts, with a keep, three turrets and five gates surviving, and used as the Tsugaru domain's center for 260 years. The figures are for a northern Honshu castle and its whole present grounds, and most of the page is visiting information.
