Nagano City history, "鐘楼" ("the bell tower", title translated), in 第一二章 "Architecture and stonework", section 1 "The buildings of Zenkoji", part 3 "The main gate, sutra repository, bell tower and Nio gate", on the Adeac digital archive (in Japanese; https://adeac.jp/nagano-city/texthtml/d100040/ct00000004/ht001970)

What it is: A section of the Nagano city history on the bell tower of Zenkoji: where it stands, the buildings that preceded it, the history of its bell, and its six inward-leaning pillars and carved bracket ends.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the bell tower stands diagonally southeast in front of the main hall on a high stone base, and that the present bell is 1.8 m tall and 1.16 m across, cast in 1666 after earlier bells were lost, so it places a great temple's belfry relative to its hall and sizes a large bell. The present tower dates from 1853, the page says only that the tower before it was built in 1753 and later damaged, not where that tower stood, and it is a single great temple's building, so it does not show where belfries stood at smaller temples.
