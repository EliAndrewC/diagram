kotobank-komoridou

Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (Selected Edition), "籠堂" ("komoridou", the retreat hall, title translated), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/籠堂-2040496)

What it is: A one-sentence dictionary entry from the Selected Edition of the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, reproduced on Kotobank, defining the komoridou as a hall attached to a shrine or temple where believers and ascetics shut themselves in to pray or train.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms that shrines and temples had such a hall and gives one dated usage, from the 1667 printing of the sermon-ballad Sansho Dayu, which names a komoridou on the left. It gives no size, position within the precinct or number, so it supports the hall's existence and not any drawn dimension.
