huchenghe-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 護城河 ("hucheng he", the city moat, title translated), Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8A%A4%E5%9F%8E%E6%B2%B3)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article defining the city moat as a dug channel around a city, palace city or temple, with examples from China, Taiwan, Japan and Europe and a note on the cost of its upkeep in present-day cities.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in general terms that a moat was dug by hand around a whole city and served defense against enemies or animals, and that Ming Nanjing used Xuanwu Lake as its moat rather than a river. It gives no dimensions, no dates beyond that one case and no account of how a moat was supplied with water; the article is a stub, and its section on upkeep concerns present-day cities, not the premodern ones.
