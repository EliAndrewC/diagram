kamishikimi-kumanoza-jawiki

Japanese Wikipedia, "上色見熊野座神社" (Kamishikimi Kumanoza Shrine, title translated), Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/上色見熊野座神社)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article on one village shrine, Kamishikimi Kumanoza Shrine in Takamori, Aso District, Kumamoto, on the south-east of the Aso caldera rim, giving its setting, founding period, former names and deities.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the shrine stands on the path up from a road to a large holed rock, that it was founded between the end of the Kamakura period and the Muromachi period from rock worship joined to the Kumano faith brought by mountain ascetics, and that it was a former village shrine (kyu sonsha) with its festival in mid-July. It describes one mountain shrine in Kyushu and gives no precinct size, layout, grove or count of households, so it speaks to a village shrine's origin and site, not its grounds.
