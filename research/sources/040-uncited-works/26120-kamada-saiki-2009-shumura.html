kamada-saiki-2009-shumura

Kamata Masashi and Saiki Takahito, "近世末期・沖縄の間切番所が置かれた村落（主村）における空間構成の復元に関する研究" ("Kinsei makki, Okinawa no magiri bansho ga okareta sonraku (shumura) ni okeru kukan kosei no fukugen ni kansuru kenkyu", A study restoring the spatial composition of the principal villages of late early-modern Okinawa where the magiri office stood, title translated), Journal of Architecture and Planning (Transactions of AIJ) 74(635), 2009 (in Japanese; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/aija/74/635/74_635_75/_article/-char/ja/)

What it is: The J-STAGE record, with an English abstract, of an architectural-planning paper reconstructing the layout of the principal villages (shumura) of Ryukyu in the last years of the early modern era, each with its magiri bansho (district office).

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports a government office as the core of a village plan, with the village's arrangement following the Ryukyu government's policy; the abstract says the restoration rests on research, hearings, fieldwork and a restoration simulation. It is Ryukyu, not mainland Japan, and the page shows only the abstract, so no plan dimensions or counts are visible.
