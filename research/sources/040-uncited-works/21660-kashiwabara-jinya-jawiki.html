ja.wikipedia 柏原陣屋 ("Kaibara jin'ya", the Kaibara daimyo's administrative compound, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9F%8F%E5%8E%9F%E9%99%A3%E5%B1%8B)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the Kaibara jin'ya in Tamba (modern Hyogo), the seat of the small Kaibara domain, giving its lords, the 1713 permission to build it, the surviving gatehouse and the fate of the main hall.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the founding of a small domain's seat (permission in 1713 for a 20,000-koku house) and names what stood in it: a gatehouse with guardroom, horse-viewing room and gun store, a main hall, a drum turret at the main gate. It is a short stub with no plan or dimensions, and only the gatehouse (1714) is original: the main hall was rebuilt after an 1818 fire and mostly demolished after the Restoration (about a fifth survives), the undated drum turret was moved to a shrine in Meiji, and the site is now a school, so it gives a list of parts and not a layout.
