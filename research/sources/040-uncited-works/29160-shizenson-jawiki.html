ja.wikipedia 村 ("mura", village; reached from the redirect "自然村", natural village; title translated), Japanese Wikipedia (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/自然村)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the village (mura) as a unit of local government, whose opening section describes the premodern "natural village" and the Meiji mergers that made administrative villages, followed by tables of present-day villages.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the premodern village, also called a natural village, was the living community and the unit of self-government of the farming class, that it carried on the medieval soson, that more than 60,000 existed in the Edo period, and that today's oaza areas largely continue them; it adds that whether a place counted as a village or a town was often the lord's choice. Its history section cites no source for any of these claims, the count of 60,000 included, and it gives no size, layout or region for a natural village, and most of the page is the 2025 list of municipalities and their readings, which concerns the modern state.
