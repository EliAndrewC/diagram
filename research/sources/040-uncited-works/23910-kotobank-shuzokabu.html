kotobank-shuzokabu

"酒造株" ("shuzokabu", sake-brewing license), in the Concise Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, with a Heibonsha World Encyclopedia mention, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%85%92%E9%80%A0%E6%A0%AA)

What it is: A Kotobank dictionary entry on the Edo shogunate's sake-brewing license system of 1657, which capped each brewer's output and taxed it, with an excerpt from the encyclopedia's sake-shop article.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the shogunate set the system in 1657 to steady rice prices, that licenses were later bought and sold, and, from the encyclopedia, that early Edo brewing was a town trade centered on castle-town shops while farmers were forbidden even to drink sake. It speaks of shogunal territory and domains in general and gives no counts of brewers or of how a license showed on the ground.
