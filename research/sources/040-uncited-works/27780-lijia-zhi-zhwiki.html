zh.wikipedia "里甲" ("Lijia", title translated), on Chinese Wikipedia (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%87%8C%E7%94%B2%E5%88%B6)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the lijia system, the Ming household register and labor-service unit set up by Zhu Yuanzhang after 1368, as a redirect from "里甲制".

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the unit's structure: 110 neighboring households formed one li of ten jia of ten households each, ten li-head households and 100 jia-head households, with one li-head and his ten households taking the labor-service duty each year in a ten-year rotation. It says the register was falsified and the system faded by late Ming, when the Single Whip reform replaced it, and it says nothing of how a li sat on the ground or how large its land was.
