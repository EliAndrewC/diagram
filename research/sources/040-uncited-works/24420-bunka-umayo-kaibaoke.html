Agency for Cultural Affairs, "馬用飼葉桶" ("horse fodder trough", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/12824)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record, from the collection of the Tohoku History Museum (東北歴史博物館), of an item of folk material culture from Kannari, Miyagi: a 47-centimeter wooden fodder trough for horses, made by a local cooper.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a width for a horse's trough and says cattle troughs were larger than horses', and that horses there were kept for farm work. It is one object, undated, from Tohoku. Its commentary labels only the width, and the unlabeled size line (0×47×30) gives no sure height or depth. The commentary says the trough was made to order by the Kannari cooper Chiba, so it may be a twentieth-century piece in the old form rather than an old trough.
