hiroshima-bunka-jouge-daikansho

Hiroshima Prefecture, "天領上下代官所跡" ("Site of the Joge magistrate's office on shogunal land", title translated), in the Hiroshima Culture Encyclopedia (ひろしま文化大百科) (in Japanese; https://www.hiroshima-bunka.jp/modules/newdb/detail.php?id=701)

What it is: A prefectural encyclopedia entry on the Joge daikansho in Hiroshima, a shogunate magistrate's office set up in 1700 over shogunal land split off from Fukuyama domain, with its later reduction and the post town that grew around it.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the numbers for a small shogunal district: 50,000 koku of Fukuyama's land taken as direct rule in 1698, an office over 10 villages of Yasuna, 37 of Jinseki and 24 of Koyo, cut by 1717 to 22 villages and 13,000 koku and made a branch office of the Iwami Omori magistrate. It also says the office stood on the Sekishu road, drew merchants and finance and became a post town, and that the site now holds a city branch office within the old stone walls. It gives no layout of the office.
