kotobank-kuchiireya-nihon-kokugo

"口入屋" ("kuchiireya", the employment-placement agent), in the Shogakukan Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten (精選版 日本国語大辞典) and the Digital Daijisen Plus (デジタル大辞泉プラス), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/口入屋)

What it is: A one-line dictionary definition of the kuchiireya, a house or person whose trade is arranging positions in service, with an illustrated Edo example and a 1817 verse, and a second entry saying the word is also the title of a Kamigata comic-storytelling piece.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms that placing servants through an agent was a trade by the early nineteenth century, quoting a 1817 comic verse as the earliest example it gives. The definition says nothing of where such agents stood, how many a town held or what their premises looked like, and half the page is about a rakugo title.
