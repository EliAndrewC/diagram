kotobank-shamusho

"社務所" ("shamusho, the shrine office", title translated), a headword compiled on Kotobank from several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/社務所-517940)

What it is: A short Kotobank headword for shamusho, the building where a shrine's business is handled, from Daijisen and the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, with a note from the old Heibonsha encyclopedia on shamu (shrine administration).

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines the shamusho as the shrine office and says shamu or shamushiki was also the old title for the head of a shrine's affairs, but its only dated citation is an official notice of 1874. It gives no layout, size or position within the grounds, and does not say that shrines had such a building before the Meiji period.
