nta-komedawara-zatsuzei-quiz

National Tax Agency (国税庁), National Tax College, "年貢の米俵をめぐる雑税" ("The miscellaneous taxes around the tribute rice bale", title translated), a quiz in its tax-history series (税の歴史クイズ) (in Japanese; https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/quiz/2208/index.htm)

What it is: A quiz page of the Japanese tax agency's college on the size of the rice bale in which Edo-period tribute was delivered. This capture holds the question only; the answer is on a linked page that was not saved.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that bales differed in size from one lord to the next throughout the Edo period, and that on shogunate land a bale held 37 sho of rice (0.37 koku): 35 sho of tribute plus 2 sho of surcharge rice, weighing about 60 kg. That is a bale size for the shogun's own domains, but the page gives no other lord's bale, and it does not say which of its three offered reasons accounts for the surcharge, so it cannot back any claim about what the surcharge paid for.
