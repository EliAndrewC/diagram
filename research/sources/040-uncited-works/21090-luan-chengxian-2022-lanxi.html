Luan Chengxian, "兰溪鱼鳞图册：一座待开掘的学术富矿" ("The Lanxi fish-scale registers: an academic gold mine yet to be dug", title translated), in Zhonghua Dushubao, 7 December 2022, p. 9, on Guangming Net (in Chinese; https://epaper.gmw.cn/zhdsb/html/2022-12/07/nw.D110000zhdsb_20221207_1-09.htm)

What it is: A review by a Chinese Academy of Social Sciences historian of the 2022 published collection of the Lanxi county (Zhejiang) fish-scale land registers, explaining what such registers are and what they record.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what a fish-scale register entered for each plot (lot number, field name, grade, shape, four boundaries, measured area) and that the system ran from the Southern Song to the Republic, so it shows what a county land record could hold. The Lanxi registers were compiled mainly in the Tongzhi years with Guangxu and Republican additions, 746 volumes survive, and the page reports no plot sizes or counts from them.
