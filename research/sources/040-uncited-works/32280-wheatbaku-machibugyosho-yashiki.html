wheatbaku-machibugyosho-yashiki

Yumemiru Baku (夢見る獏), "奉行所の建物の様子（町奉行6）" ("The buildings of the magistrate's office (City Magistrate 6)", title translated), on the blog 気ままに江戸, Excite Blog, 2013-11-14 (in Japanese; https://wheatbaku.exblog.jp/20939714)

What it is: A blog post describing the Edo city magistrate's compound, with the ground and building areas of the Minami (south) and Kita (north) offices, which parts held the office, the magistrate's residence and the white-gravel court, and how the gates were used.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the Minami office as 2,617 tsubo of ground against the Kita office's 2,560, a nagaya gate on the east side opened at dawn and closed at dusk, a 6-shaku stone path from the gate to the entrance, and the office joined to the magistrate's private residence behind it. It is a compound of the shogun's capital, not a provincial one, and its building figures disagree within the post (1,819 tsubo built in one sentence, 580 tsubo of buildings in the next); it names its plan, the Ooka Echizen-no-kami residence plan, but cites nothing else.
