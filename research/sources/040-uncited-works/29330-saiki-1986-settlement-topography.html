Saiki Takahito (齊木 崇人), "集落空間の構成原理と地形立地" ("shuraku kukan no kosei genri to chikei ricchi", principles of settlement-space composition and topographic location, title translated), Journal of Rural Planning Association (農村計画学会誌) 4(4): 19-32, 1986, on J-STAGE (in Japanese; https://jstage.jst.go.jp/article/arp1982/4/4/4_4_19/_article/-char/ja)

What it is: The J-STAGE record page of a 1986 paper, giving its bibliographic data and English abstract; the full text is a separate PDF that this page does not carry. The paper classifies the topographic location of farming settlements in Ibaraki Prefecture and analyzes their space by six elements (drainage, land use, roads, common ground and facilities, open space, residences).

Why it applies, and its limits: It could bear on how a Japanese farming settlement sits on the land and what elements make up its space, using twelve location classes drawn from old Japanese topographic terms. The page gives only the abstract and no findings or figures, so nothing here can be quoted for a rule; it is a 1980s survey of one prefecture.
