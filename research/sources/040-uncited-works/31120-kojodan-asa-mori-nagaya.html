kojodan-asa-mori-nagaya

攻城団 (Kojodan), "旧厚狭毛利家萩屋敷長屋" ("kyū Asa Mōri-ke Hagi yashiki nagaya", the former row house of the Asa Mori family's Hagi residence, title translated), a castle memo under Hagi Castle, Yamaguchi, on the Kojodan castle site, with the Hagi city sign text reproduced on the page (in Japanese; https://kojodan.jp/castle/86/memo/1371.html)

What it is: A castle-enthusiast site's memo on the one surviving building of the Asa Mori family's Hagi residence, giving its size, roof and history and reproducing the Hagi city and Cultural Properties sign texts (with an English version).

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dimensions of a high-ranking retainer family's long row house in a domain castle town: built 1856, 51.5 m by 5 m, the largest surviving samurai building in Hagi, standing on an estate the page puts at about 15,500 square meters before the main house was pulled down after the Restoration. The page itself gives the family's holding as 10,500 koku in one passage and 8,370 koku in another, the building dates from the last decade of the shogunate, and the estate's other buildings are gone, so the 15,500 square meter figure is the page's report of a lost layout.
