bunka-akai-dobei

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "赤井家住宅土塀" ("Akai house, earthen wall", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/145834)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of the registered earthen wall of the Akai house in Ueno, Iga, Mie, dated 1895: 54 meters long by its description (its summary line says 48 m), about 2 meters high, tile-capped, on a cut-stone footing, with a gate in the south run.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the length, height, cap and footing of an earthen wall finished at its middle coat of plaster (中塗仕上げ) around a house lot in a former samurai quarter, running between the nagaya gate and a storehouse. It dates from 1895 with a 1957 repair, so it records a Meiji-period wall in the older form, not an Edo wall, and the page does not say whether it replaced an earlier one.
