Ito Masatoshi, "旧樋口家住宅庭園" ("kyu Higuchi-ke jutaku teien", the garden of the former Higuchi family residence, title translated), on Oniwasan, the garden magazine run by Karenfuji (in Japanese; https://oniwa.garden/higuchike-nagano-旧樋口家住宅)

What it is: A garden-magazine page on the former Higuchi house in Matsushiro, Nagano, an upper samurai residence near the Sanada lord's mansion, now open free of charge. It describes the house and its pond garden fed by the town's water channel.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the garden within the plot was fields in the Edo period and is now a stroll garden of woods and bamboo, that the pond is fed by the senzui-ro, a channel that has supplied each samurai residence in Matsushiro's castle town since Edo, and that neighboring houses are laid out the same way; it also says the main house was repaired in 1862. It is one visitor's account from 2019 of a restored property, with no source given for its dates, and the garden shown is the present one, not the Edo layout.
