hiejinja-inari-sando

Hie Shrine (日枝神社), "稲荷参道について" ("About the Inari approach", title translated), in the shrine's news archive, 26 October 2020 (in Japanese; https://www.hiejinja.net/news_archives/2020/about_inari_sando.html)

What it is: A shrine's own notice, in Japanese, English, Chinese and Korean, describing the Inari approach that runs west from the precinct, lined with vermilion torii (the 'thousand torii') and dedicated banners, and why Inari torii are painted vermilion.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that Inari torii are customarily painted vermilion ('Inari paint') like the shrine buildings, that the color carries the faith in Inari's power over life, earth and production, and that the path is narrow and crowded. It records the present approach and a custom of color, not a date or the age of the path, and does not name where the shrine stands.
