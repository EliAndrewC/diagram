bunka-honhaga-dozo

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "本芳我家住宅(愛媛県喜多郡内子町) 土蔵" ("Honhaga house (Uchiko, Ehime), storehouse", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/187556)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of the storehouse of the Honhaga house in Uchiko, Ehime, an Important Cultural Property of about 1884: a two-story plastered building 11.8 by 7.9 meters beside the main house on a highway frontage.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and layout of a merchant house's plastered storehouse standing beside the main house on the street, with a kitchen behind and a garden to the north. It is a wealthy town house of the Meiji period (about 1884) in a preserved district, not a farm storehouse.
