kokufu-jawiki

ja.wikipedia "国府" ("kokufu", the provincial capital of the ritsuryo state, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/国府)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the kokufu, the seat of each province's governor in the Nara and Heian periods, covering the layout of the government hall and its surrounding offices, the staff, how the sites were excavated, and a table of every province's capital site.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what stood at a provincial seat - a walled-off hall court with a front gate, offices, the governor's residence, workshops, pit-dwellings of workers, a market and sometimes a port - and that there was no outer boundary wall; it puts the staff at tens of people in a small province and hundreds in a large one, and the whole population at up to two or three thousand in the largest, though the article gives no citation for either the staff or the population figure. Its evidence is Nara and Heian (the article says the sites faded by the Muromachi period), so it describes the ancient state's provincial seats, not a later domain's, and it names no figure for any site's area except the Musashi site's 2.2 km by 1.8 km.
