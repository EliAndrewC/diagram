ja.wikipedia 新寺町 (弘前市) ("Shinteramachi, Hirosaki", the New Temple Town of Hirosaki, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/新寺町_(弘前市))

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on Shinteramachi, a town district of Hirosaki in Aomori Prefecture, a street of temples in the castle town. It gives the district's origin in the 1650s, its Edo-period maps, and a list of its temples.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Hirosaki's temples burned in a great fire and were moved here, after which the lots were divided: an old map of 1673 shows the moved temples and 46 townhouses, and the name sets it against Mototeramachi, whose temples are all Soto, while this street holds many sects. That is a dated, specific case of a castle town's temple quarter laid out as a planned street in northern Honshu. The page gives no sizes or plan, takes its Edo facts at second hand from old maps and a gazetteer, and its temple list and 2017 population are modern.
