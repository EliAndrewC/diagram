tojosei-jawiki

ja.wikipedia 外城制 ("tojosei", the Satsuma domain's system of outer castles, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/外城制)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the tojo system, by which the Satsuma domain settled its samurai in outer castle districts around the province, with a list of the 113 districts and their rice yields in 1744 and after.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a case where samurai lived dispersed in the countryside in small castle-foot towns (fumoto) beside the farm villages they governed, and gives each district's yield in koku, so it supports a samurai-in-the-countryside form for a domain. It is the page's own statement that Satsuma was exceptional: samurai were 26 percent of its population in 1871 against a national average of about 5 percent, so its figures are not a norm, and it is a south Kyushu domain with its own governing posts.
