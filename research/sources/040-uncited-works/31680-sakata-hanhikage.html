sakata-hanhikage

Sakata Seed Corporation (サカタのタネ), "半日陰" ("han-hikage", half shade, title translated), in its horticulture glossary on the Engei Tsushin site (in Japanese; https://sakata-tsushin.com/words/detail_96)

What it is: A one-entry glossary page from a seed company's gardening site, defining half shade as a growing condition.

Why it applies, and its limits: It defines half shade as ground that gets sun for about half of the day, or only the dappled light that comes through leaves, with the example of keeping a plant in half shade in midsummer to avoid leaf scorch. It is a gardener's definition with no hours, no latitude and no plant-specific data, and says nothing of East Asian or premodern gardens.
