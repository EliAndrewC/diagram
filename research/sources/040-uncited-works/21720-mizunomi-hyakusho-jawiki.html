ja.wikipedia 水呑百姓 ("mizunomi hyakusho", the landless peasant, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E5%91%91%E7%99%BE%E5%A7%93)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the mizunomi hyakusho, the Edo-period peasant who held no assessed land, covering their status in the village and how they lived.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says such peasants had no assessed yield and so no land tax, but were not counted as full village members and had no voice, and that they lived as tenants or went to cities for work. It also says that from the mid-17th century a peasant was one who held assessed yield and rights to commons and water. The page carries a 2013 banner warning that it may contain original research, it cites two books only by title, and it gives no counts of such households.
