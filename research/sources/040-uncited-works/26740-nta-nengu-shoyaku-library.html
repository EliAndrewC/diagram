nta-nengu-shoyaku-library

National Tax Agency, National Tax College, "１ 年貢と諸役" ("1. Annual land tax and miscellaneous levies", title translated), in its Tax History Library (租税史料ライブラリー) (in Japanese; https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/shiryou/library/01.htm)

What it is: A signed introductory page of the National Tax College's tax-history library on Edo-period taxes, explaining nengu, the miscellaneous levies and the village-contract system, with eight captioned documents from 1672 to 1866 (survey orders, a survey register, a tax assessment notice, a payment receipt, a notice board, a post-station labor return, a Morioka survey manual and a crop-failure inspection).

Why it applies, and its limits: It says in plain terms how the tax was assessed on the village as a whole, collected by the village and receipted by the intendant, and that post-station labor (denma and sukego levies) fell on the post town and its nearby villages. The documents it shows come from Yamagata, Chiba, Ishikawa, Saitama, Nagano, Yamanashi and Iwate, each described in a line or two, so it is a guide to the forms of the system and gives no rates, yields or village sizes.
