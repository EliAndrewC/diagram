kotobank-taniguchi-shuraku

Takizawa Yumiko, "谷口集落" ("taniguchi shuraku", the valley-mouth settlement), in the revised edition of the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia (改訂新版 世界大百科事典), with the Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Nipponica and Britannica entries on the same page, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/谷口集落)

What it is: A page of Japanese encyclopedia entries on the valley-mouth settlement: a market village at the point where a river leaves the hills for the plain, with examples around the western Kanto mountains such as Ome, Itsukaichi, Hanno and Ogose.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the form such a settlement took: a street village on the river terrace with narrow plots at right angles to a wide road once used as a market ground, trading hill goods for plain goods, and some with water-wheel mills. It says many lie in the Kanto, Niigata and Toyama and that after the Meiji era changes in transport and shops ended their market role; it gives no street width or plot size and does not date the first market.
