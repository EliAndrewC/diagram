fuse-umaya-harima-united

Harima Wide-Area Cooperation Council (播磨広域連携協議会), "古の播磨を訪ねて～たつの市編 その４" ("Visiting ancient Harima: Tatsuno City, part 4", title translated), in the column Harima Fudoki Kiko, on the council's site (in Japanese; https://www.harima-united.jp/column/climate/contents000171.html)

What it is: A column by a regional council of Harima municipalities on the Fuse post station (Fuse no umaya) at the Koinumaru site in Ibonosho, Tatsuno, which excavation from 1986 identified as a post station on the ancient Sanyodo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says how the site was identified: tile-roofed buildings found along the Sanyodo, ink-written pottery marked 'station' and 'Fuse', and the match with the seven Harima stations in the Engishiki of 927. It concerns a state post station of the Nara and Heian periods, centuries before the Edo-period roads, and gives no layout, size or building plan.
