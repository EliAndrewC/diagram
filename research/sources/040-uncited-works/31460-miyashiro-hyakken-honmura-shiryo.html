miyashiro-hyakken-honmura-shiryo

Miyashiro Town (Saitama), "企画展「江戸時代の百間本村」関係史料集" ("Source collection for the exhibition 'Hyakken Honmura in the Edo Period'", title translated), transcriptions from the Orihara family papers (折原家文書) and the Odaka family papers (大高家文書), a PDF on the town's website (in Japanese; https://www.town.miyashiro.lg.jp/cmsfiles/contents/0000002/2519/hyakkanhonmurakankei.pdf)

What it is: A municipal PDF of typeset transcriptions of dated village documents of Hyakken village, Saitama county, Musashi province (now Miyashiro, Saitama), from a register extract of 1698 (Genroku 11) through Edo-period petitions, tax warrants and religious registers to village-office papers of 1925. Its longest item lists the village's plots one by one, with each plot's dimensions in ken, grade, area and rated yield.

Why it applies, and its limits: The 1698 extract gives, for one Kanto lowland village, plot sizes in ken with grade and area, the rated yields (paddies 0.8, 0.6 and 0.4 koku per tan by grade; fields 0.7, 0.5, 0.3 and 0.1), and village totals of about 69.3 cho and 300 koku, of which only about 15 cho are paddy, so it can anchor plot shapes and the paddy-to-dry-field mix of such a village; an 1842 petition adds that its water came by ditch from more than ten ri away, with 41 bridges and about 4,080 person-days of upkeep labor a year. It is transcription only, with no commentary, map or photograph, the saved text interleaves the document's columns and marks unreadable characters, and its evidence is this one village, shared among several lords, so it speaks for its own place and not for Rokugan at large.
