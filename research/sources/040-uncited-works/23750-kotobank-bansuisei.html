kotobank-bansuisei

"番水制" ("bansuisei", the rotational water-turn system, title translated), in the Heibonsha Hyakka Jiten Maypedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%95%AA%E6%B0%B4%E5%88%B6-862504)

What it is: A single short encyclopedia entry defining the bansui system, in which an irrigation district is divided and water is handed out by turns in time, with a Muromachi example.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the turn system was an intermittent method used when and where water was scarce, with a recorded case between villages on rivers of the Kofukuji estate in Yamato in the Muromachi period, and that the order and time of switching often caused water disputes. It gives no turn lengths, no shares and no map of a rotation, so it supports the practice but not any rule for it.
