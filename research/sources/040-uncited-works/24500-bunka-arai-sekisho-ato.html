bunka-arai-sekisho-ato

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "新居関跡" ("Arai checkpoint site", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/216539)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of the designated historic site of the Arai checkpoint on the Tokaido, Shizuoka, on the west shore of Lake Hamana: a barrier founded in 1600 and moved twice, with its 1855 main building surviving.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives how a Tokaido barrier was laid out: a main inspection building of three rooms with a hipped-gable roof and three attached buildings, a boat office, a women's inspection row and a great gate, and a landing on the lake. It says it is the only surviving main-road barrier building, and that its site stood at 2 meters elevation, but gives no sizes of the grounds. The building is the 1855 rebuilding: one of its three attached wings is lost, and it was a school and town office until 1951 and taken apart and repaired in 1971. The lakeshore and landing have been filled in, so the page gives the boat office, women's inspection row and great gate only as places where they stood, not as buildings.
