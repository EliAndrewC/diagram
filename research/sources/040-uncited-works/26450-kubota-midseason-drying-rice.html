kubota-midseason-drying-rice

Kubota Corporation, "中干し(土用干し)と稲の開花" ("Mid-season drainage and the flowering of rice", title translated), from 稲作の歴史とそれを支えた伝統農具, on Kubota no Tanbo (in Japanese; https://www.kubota.co.jp/kubotatanbo/history/tools/drying_01.html)

What it is: A short company educational page on the midsummer draining of a paddy until it cracks, the heading of the ear and the flowering of the rice, ending with the sedge hat as the tool of this stage.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supports the practice of draining the paddy in the hot season and that rice flowers in the morning of fair days and over about a week, so the paddy cannot be entered then. It gives no date or region for the practice, does not say whether premodern farmers drained at this point, and its only tool, the sedge hat, is described by size alone.
