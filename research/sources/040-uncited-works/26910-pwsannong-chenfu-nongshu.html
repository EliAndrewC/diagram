pwsannong-chenfu-nongshu

"陈旉《农书》" ("Chen Fu's Book of Agriculture", title translated), in the encyclopedia of agricultural history on Zhihui Sannong, the agricultural knowledge service of China Agriculture Press (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/560175.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry on the Nongshu of Chen Fu (1076-1154), written in 1149 in three parts on rice farming, oxen and silk in the Song-era Jiangnan. It gives the author's life, the writing, a summary of the contents and the editions.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the book is the earliest surviving regional farming treatise on Song Jiangnan paddy work, and describes its plan for high fields: a pond dug where water gathers, its bank planted with mulberry and tied with cattle, so that one pond waters the fields, shades the cattle and is fertilized by them. It is a modern summary of one hermit author's account of the lower Yangzi, gives no sizes for the pond (only that its size should follow the water the fields need), and paraphrases the pond passage rather than quoting it.
