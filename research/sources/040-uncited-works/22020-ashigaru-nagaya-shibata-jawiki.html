What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on the one surviving ashigaru row house of the Shibata domain in Niigata, a designated Important Cultural Property.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a concrete form: a thatched, ridge-divided row house for eight households, dated by a ridge plaque to 1842, one of about four said to have stood nearby, and lived in until 1968. It gives no floor area or room layout, and a house restored in 1969 shows the restoration, not necessarily how the row looked when new.
