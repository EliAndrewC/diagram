zh.wikipedia 大同城墙 ("Datong chengqiang", the city wall of Datong, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%A4%A7%E5%90%8C%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%99)

What it is: A Chinese Wikipedia article on the walls of Datong in Shanxi, giving their size and gates, the Ming rebuilding of 1372, their damage in 1649, demolition from 1952 and restoration from 2008 to 2016.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a perimeter of 7,270.7 meters, a height of about 14 meters, an inner area of 2.63 square kilometers, a moat 40 meters outside the wall and about 10 meters wide, four named gates, and side lengths from 1,771 to 1,873 meters. These are for a prefectural city. The page gives the four side lengths as the original lengths, but does not say whether the perimeter, height, area and moat are the Ming figures or those of the wall rebuilt by 2016 (the moat was joined up again only in 2016), and it says five chi were cut off the wall in 1649 and its bricks stripped by about 1952.
