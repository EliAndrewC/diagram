ranhaku-myokoji

Ranzan Town (嵐山町), "天台宗 明光寺 菅谷村大字将軍沢" ("Tendai sect, Myoko-ji, Shogunzawa in Sugaya Village", title translated), from the Hiki County temple register (比企郡寺院明細帳), in the Ranzan Town Web Museum, vol. 6 (嵐山町web博物誌), on the Ranzan town site (in Japanese; http://www.ranhaku.com/web06/01chishi/09ter19.html)

What it is: A town-hosted transcription of the Meiji-era Hiki County temple register entry for Myoko-ji, a Tendai (Jimon branch) temple in Shogunzawa village, Musashi, with the town's footnote on the sect branch. It lists the buildings, precinct, outlying land and parishioner count.

Why it applies, and its limits: It documents a small village temple: a main hall of 6 ken by 5, a priest's residence of 5 ken 3 shaku by 2 ken 3 shaku, a gate of 1.5 ken by 1, a precinct of 462 tsubo (494 in the 1948 restatement) and only 20 parishioners (danto, counted in persons), yet a long list of forest plots alongside paddy and fields. The pairing of few parishioners with many woodland plots is the page's data, and it gives no reason for it; the origin is given as unknown, and the page covers one temple.
