bunka-yano-naya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "矢野家住宅（本宅）納屋" (the barn of the Yano house (main residence), title translated), in Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/181063)

What it is: A registered-cultural-property entry for the barn of the Yano house in Nishihara, Kumamoto, built in 1889 of stone, single-storied in part two-storied, tile-roofed, 58 square meters, with a board fence, standing on the road frontage north of the miso storehouse.

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows a farm barn placed at the front of the house lot facing the road, a simpler building than the storehouses and finished with clapboard on the road side so the street view is looked after. It records a Meiji-era stone building on a substantial house lot in Kyushu, one with its own miso storehouse, storehouses and several gates (the entry does not say the household made miso for sale), so its material and finish are not those of a poor farm's outbuilding in the premodern setting.
