zh.wikipedia 圍墾 (weiken, land reclamation by enclosure, title translated), traditional-character edition (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hant/%E5%9C%A9%E7%94%B0)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on enclosure reclamation (weiken), diking a coast, bay or lakeshore and draining it to make a polder, with three kinds and examples from China, Japan and the Netherlands.

Why it applies, and its limits: It explains the method in a few steps (build a dike, drain at low tide, leach salt with a freshwater system or plant salt-tolerant crops) and names reclaimed lake and coast examples such as Dongting Lake, Ogura Pond and Isahaya Bay. It gives no date for any example, no area or height, and carries a notice that it lists no sources, so it supports the definition and the sequence of the work but not any premodern scale.
