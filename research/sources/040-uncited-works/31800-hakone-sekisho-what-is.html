hakone-sekisho-what-is

Hakone Town (箱根町), "関所ってなに？" ("What is a checkpoint?", title translated), in the materials for "The Revived Hakone Checkpoint" (『よみがえった箱根関所』資料), on the Hakone Sekisho site (in Japanese; https://www.hakonesekisyo.jp/db/hs_disp_fr.html?hdid=data_01_02)

What it is: A one-paragraph introduction from the Hakone checkpoint's site to the Edo-period barrier set to watch the Tokaido road, and to the inspection of travelers, especially of women going out from Edo.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the barrier was set to watch the Tokaido, that its duties were posted on a notice board, that some travelers needed a pass, and that a woman leaving Edo for the west had to carry a certificate issued by the shogunate's caretaker officials and have it match her description. It is a few lines, with no plan, no staffing and no dates, so it supports the barrier's purpose and the women's rule at Hakone only.
