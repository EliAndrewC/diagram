Shen Shengyi, "农书中的“农书”" ("The 'Nongshu' among the farming books", title translated), in Zhonghua Dushubao (China Reading Weekly), 2 March 2016, p. 13, on Guangming Net (in Chinese; https://epaper.gmw.cn/zhdsb/html/2016-03/02/nw.D110000zhdsb_20160302_2-13.htm)

What it is: A short newspaper essay introducing three Chinese farming books titled or known as Nongshu: Wang Zhen's Yuan-era comprehensive treatise, Chen Fu's Southern Song book on Jiangnan farming, and the Ming-Qing Shenshi Nongshu with Zhang Lüxiang's supplement.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates and places the books the record may cite for Jiangnan farming: Chen Fu's as the earliest surviving account of Jiangnan agriculture (three parts, on rice fields, oxen and sericulture), and the Shenshi Nongshu as a late-Ming record of small-scale farming. It describes the books and their authors rather than quoting their figures, so it can only point to where such figures sit, and it counts 542 or 2,084 old farming books depending on the catalog used.
