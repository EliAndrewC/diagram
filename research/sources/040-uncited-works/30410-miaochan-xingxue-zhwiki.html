zh.wikipedia 庙产兴学 ("Using temple property to found schools", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/庙产兴学)

What it is: A short Chinese Wikipedia article on the late-Qing and early-Republic campaign to turn Buddhist and Daoist temples and Confucian temples into schools, centered on Zhang Zhidong's 1898 proposal in his Quanxue pian.

Why it applies, and its limits: It quotes Zhang's proposal that temples be converted to schools, noting that there were far more than tens of thousands of temples, over a hundred in a metropolis, dozens in a large county and a dozen in a small one, each with fields, and that seven tenths of a county's temples be converted and three tenths left for monks. This is an argument of 1898, after the premodern cut-off, not a count, and the article's statement that the nation's temples fell from over seven million to about ten thousand households carries no footnote.
