Ishikawa Prefecture, Board of Education, "白米の千枚田" ("The Shiromai thousand-paddy terraces", title translated), national place of scenic beauty (in Japanese; https://www.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kyoiku/bunkazai/siseki/2-4.html)

What it is: A prefectural page on the Shiromai terraces at Wajima on the Noto coast, a nationally designated scenic site: the designated area of 18,136 square meters, the slope, the average paddy of about 20 square meters, the 1684 landslide, the Meiji re-clearing, and the weir-cutting method still practiced.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a figure for how small a terrace paddy was here: about 20 square meters on average, over 1,000 paddies in about 2 ha, on a slope with a height difference of about 50 m, with overflow irrigation from paddy to paddy. It says the settlement is recorded only from the 17th century, that the 1684 landslide took most of the paddies and that, as tradition has it, they were re-cleared gradually from the Meiji era, so the layout seen is largely later than the setting's period and is one landslide-ground site.
