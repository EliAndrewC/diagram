ja.wikipedia 居城 ("kyojo", the castle a lord lives in; reached from the 居館 "kyokan" address, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/居館)

What it is: A short Japanese Wikipedia article defining the kyojo, the castle where a lord lives, and the kyokan, a lord's residence that is a mansion and not a castle, with examples of each.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that until about the middle Sengoku period a lord lived in a residence at the foot of the hills and withdrew to a mountain castle in war, and that from the late period large plain castles joined the two. It names examples (the Ouchi and Takeda residences, Yoshida-Koriyama, Azuchi, Osaka) but gives no sizes, and its dates stop at the Sengoku period.
