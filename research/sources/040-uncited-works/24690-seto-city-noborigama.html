seto-city-noborigama

Seto City, "連房式登窯（洞本業窯・一里塚本業窯・古窯）" ("renboshiki noborigama", the chambered climbing kilns of Hora, Ichirizuka and the old kiln, title translated), in the Seto City Historical and Cultural Basic Plan, Aichi Prefecture (in Japanese; http://seto-guide.jp/setostory/setomono/noborigama)

What it is: A municipal page on three surviving chambered climbing kilns of Seto, Aichi, each a city-designated cultural property. It tells how Seto's kilns changed in the early Edo period from the older cave and large kilns to a row of firing chambers up a slope, and gives the makers, the dates the three kilns were built (1947 to 1950) and last fired, and what each fired.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the move to the chambered climbing kiln at Seto to the early Edo period and describes the form: firing chambers in steps up a slope, with a wider-chambered kind for earthenware and a long-chambered kind added for porcelain in the late Edo period. The kilns it describes were built or rebuilt in the twentieth century from older kiln materials, and the page gives no dimensions, so it shows the form and its dates, not the size of an Edo kiln.
