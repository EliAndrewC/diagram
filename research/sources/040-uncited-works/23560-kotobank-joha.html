kotobank-joha

"条播" ("jōha", drill sowing in rows, title translated), in the Digital Daijisen (デジタル大辞泉) and the Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, concise edition (精選版 日本国語大辞典), on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9D%A1%E6%92%AD-532924)

What it is: A Kotobank dictionary page defining jōha, sowing seed in even rows, with two dictionary entries and a one-line quotation from the old Heibonsha World Encyclopedia that lists it among the ways of sowing.

Why it applies, and its limits: Both dictionary entries define it as making parallel furrows in a field (畑) and sowing at a fixed spacing, also called sujimaki, and the encyclopedia excerpt sets it beside broadcast sowing (散播), dibbling and others, so it names a row-sowing method for dry fields. The page gives no date, no crop, no spacing and no region, and the encyclopedia passage is a fragment that opens with a general account of sowing in which pasture grasses are the broadcast example.
