sayama-hikeshi-nenshi

Sayama City Fire Corps (狭山市消防団), "第1章 江戸時代の火消部隊" ("Chapter 1: the firefighting units of the Edo period", title translated), in its fifty-year history (50年史), part 1 (in Japanese; https://city.sayama.saitama.jp/kurashi/anshin/bosai/bousaitaisei/shobodan/50nenshi/50nensi.files/1-1.pdf)

What it is: A chapter of a Saitama city fire corps' anniversary history on Edo's firefighting: the daimyo, jo and town brigades and their dates (1629 to 1720), the Meireki fire's fire-prevention works, lookout towers and bells, the guard posts and the town officers. It also notes that other domains' castle towns soon set up similar systems.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives dated detail on Edo's organization and gear: the 1658 permanent brigade of four hatamoto, 100 laborers each, the lookout towers of three jo (about 9 m) for the brigades and one per ten blocks for the town ladders, the post-Meireki widening of streets to 5 ken (about 10 m), and demolition as the main tactic. It is about the shogun's capital only, with the provincial castle towns given one sentence, and it says itself that the shogunate's measures served regime stability more than the townspeople.
