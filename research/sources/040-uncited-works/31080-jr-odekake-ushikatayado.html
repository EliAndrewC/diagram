jr-odekake-ushikatayado

West Japan Railway Company (JR-Odekake.net), "牛方宿" ("Ushikata-yado, the ox-drivers' inn", title translated), spot information supplied by the Shinshu Nagano Prefecture Tourism Association, listing current as of 28 April 2022 (in Japanese; https://guide.jr-odekake.net/spot/10714)

What it is: A tourist-guide entry for the Ushikata-yado in Otari village, Nagano, a restored inn on the Chikuni road from Itoigawa on the Sea of Japan to Matsumoto, where ox-drivers hauling salt stopped. It gives the building's frame, size and date and its cultural-property status.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the dimensions and form of a surviving travelers' lodging for pack-ox drivers on a mountain trade road: a hip-roofed thatched building of 6 ken by 10 ken, estimated early nineteenth century, the only one left on the Chikuni road, designated an Otari village cultural property in 1997 and later a prefectural treasure. It does not say what was restored or rebuilt (it calls the museum a restoration), says nothing of the interior, and speaks of one inn on a mountain pass road, not of lodgings on a post-town street.
