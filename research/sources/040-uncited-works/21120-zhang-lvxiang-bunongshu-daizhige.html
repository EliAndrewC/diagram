zhang-lvxiang-bunongshu-daizhige

Zhang Lüxiang, "补农书" ("Supplement to the Nongshu", title translated), with the "沈氏农书" (Shenshi Nongshu, Mr. Shen's farming book), as transcribed in the daizhigev20 corpus on GitHub (in Chinese; https://github.com/garychowcmu/daizhigev20/blob/master/%E5%AD%90%E8%97%8F/%E5%86%9C%E5%AE%B6/%E8%A1%A5%E5%86%9C%E4%B9%A6.txt)

What it is: A transcribed text of the Bu Nongshu: the first scroll of Mr. Shen's farming book for the Huzhou (Zhejiang) lake country, with its month-by-month farm tasks, its method for working fields and mulberry ground, and Shen's account of the famine and floods of 1640-1643. Its mulberry section breaks off at a 'to be continued' mark, after which come the famine account and an 1815 colophon; the file holds none of Zhang's own supplement, and its numbered notes on the monthly tasks are a modern editor's glosses, not the Ming text.

Why it applies, and its limits: It is a first-hand late-Ming record of Jiangnan paddy and mulberry farming by a landowner who hired monthly and short-term labourers and whose family alone ran a famine gruel kitchen, so it speaks for a large managed holding more than for a smallholder's: rice transplanted with rows 7 cun (about 22 cm) apart, fields dried in the sixth month, mulberry set 7 chi apart each way at about 200 trees per mu, and a year's schedule of manuring, mud-dredging and silk work. It is a typed corpus copy with some characters missing or rendered as placeholders, it covers only the Huzhou area, and its famine chapter shows the author's years were a disaster, not a normal run of seasons.
