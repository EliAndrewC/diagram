ja.wikipedia 郷士 ("goshi", country samurai, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/郷士)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on goshi, Edo-period lower samurai who lived in the countryside and farmed, with how such men came to the status and domain-by-domain examples (Mito, Yonezawa, Awa, Tosa, Higo, Satsuma).

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows samurai status held by men who lived on and managed farmland, in the countryside, with the surname-and-sword privilege, which supports a rural samurai household beside a village. Its examples are by domain and the status varied widely (some had stipends, some were bought into by donations), so it gives no single form and no household or plot sizes; one footnote on Tosa is itself a correction of a popular belief.
