"仙台藩花山村寒湯番所跡" ("Sendai-han Hanayama-mura Nuruyu bansho ato", the site of the Nuruyu checkpoint at Hanayama village in Sendai domain, title translated), in the Kodansha guide to nationally designated historic sites, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%BB%99%E5%8F%B0%E8%97%A9%E8%8A%B1%E5%B1%B1%E6%9D%91%E5%AF%92%E6%B9%AF%E7%95%AA%E6%89%80%E8%B7%A1-1444267)

What it is: A short guide entry on the Nuruyu border guard station of Sendai domain at Hanayama, Kurihara, Miyagi, set on a mountain pass in the upper Ichihasama river valley where it meets the Akita domain. It describes the thatched four-pillar front gate that straddled the road, the residence about 23 m by 12 m, and the lost inspection house.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the layout of a surviving domain-border checkpoint: a gate spanning the road, an inspection house beside it, and a residence of ordinary house form with a formal reception room, on a pass at the domain boundary. The entry says the checkpoint was rebuilt in the early Ansei years (1854-60) and the inspection house is gone, so what survives is the late Edo gate and residence of one Tohoku border pass; it says nothing of the staff or of the inspection buildings' size.
