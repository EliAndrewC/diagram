kotobank-kyuko

"休耕" ("kyuko", fallowing a field, title translated), entries in the Digital Daijisen and Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%BC%91%E8%80%95-476799)

What it is: Two short dictionary definitions of kyuko, the stopping of cultivation of a field for a time, one for a year or more in the case of rice. The page's cut-off snippet on the related term kyukoden refers to rice surplus in the 1960s and 1970s.

Why it applies, and its limits: It confirms the term's sense, that a field is deliberately left uncultivated for a year or longer, as a gloss for a reader meeting the word. It gives no practice, period, region or rotation, and the only dated item, the fallow-paddy snippet tied to a rice-surplus policy of the Showa 40s (1965 onward), is modern and unrelated to premodern fallowing.
