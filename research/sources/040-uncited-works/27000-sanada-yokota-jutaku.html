sanada-yokota-jutaku

Sanada Treasure Museum, "旧横田家住宅" ("The former Yokota family house", title translated), in its guide to the facilities, on the Sanada Treasure Museum site (in Japanese; https://www.sanadahoumotsukan.com/facility_detail.php?n=6)

What it is: The museum's page on the former Yokota house in Matsushiro (Nagano), a designated Important Cultural Property that the page calls the house of a middle-rank samurai, built in the early to middle 1800s. It lists the gate-with-barracks, the main house, the retirement house, the storehouse, the garden and the kitchen garden.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the house keeps the typical plan and arrangement of a middle-rank samurai house in Matsushiro, and that about half the plot, 1,600 square meters, was kitchen garden growing the household's own crops, which the page calls a feature of Matsushiro's samurai houses. It also says a channel (segi) runs along the plots' rear boundary line, and a garden watercourse (sensui) links each house's pond. It describes the house as it was preserved and opened to the public in 1992, and it speaks for one castle town's middle-rank retainers; it gives no plan dimensions beyond the garden area.
