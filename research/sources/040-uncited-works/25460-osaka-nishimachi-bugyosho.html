osaka-nishimachi-bugyosho

Osaka City, "43．西町奉行所（にしまちぶぎょうしょ）跡" ("No. 43: the site of the Nishimachi magistrate's office", title translated), in the History Walks series (Uemachi Plateau North Course), Osaka City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/kensetsu/page/0000009649.html)

What it is: A short city walking-guide entry on the site of the west magistrate's office of Osaka, saying what the Osaka town magistrates did, how the two offices alternated, when the west office moved to this site, and what stood here afterward.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the shogunate's Osaka town magistrates, under a senior councilor, handled policing, justice and administration, that an east and a west office served alternate months, and that the west office moved here after the great fire of 1724 and that excavation is still establishing its size. It gives no dimensions or layout of the compound, and as a guide stop it states the office's duties and dates and little else.
