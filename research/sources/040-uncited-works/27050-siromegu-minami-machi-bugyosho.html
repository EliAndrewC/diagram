siromegu-minami-machi-bugyosho

Siromegu (a castle-visiting site), "南町奉行所" ("The Minami town magistrate's office", title translated), in its Tokyo castle pages (in Japanese; http://www.siromegu.com/castle/tokyo/minamibugyo/minamibugyo.htm)

What it is: A castle-visitor's page on the site of the Edo south town magistrate's office in Yurakucho, Tokyo, that reproduces the wording of the site's information board and adds the visitor's own impressions. It describes the office's history and the objects found in the 2005 dig.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Edo town magistracy was one of the shogunate's three magistracies, held two posts that alternated by month, and that the south office moved to this site in 1707 and stayed until the end of the shogunate. It gives what the 2005 dig found: a drain facing the front gate, a well, storehouses, and a plank-lined underground cellar with a waterproofed frame and a drain hole. It is a short site-board text, with no plan or dimensions of the office, so it supports the dates and the finds, not the layout.
