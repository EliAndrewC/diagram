fukui-osagoe-joji-house

Fukui City, "【おさごえ民家園】旧城地家住宅と昔の暮らしの特色" ("Osagoe Minkaen: the former Joji house and the features of old life", title translated), on the Fukui City website (in Japanese; https://www.city.fukui.lg.jp/kankou/bunka/sisetu/p012624.html)

What it is: A municipal page on the former Joji house at the Osagoe open-air farmhouse museum in Fukui, a village headman's (shoya) house of 1852 moved from Ono, which goes room by room through the earth-floor area, the hearth, the sink and bath, the guest rooms, the toilets and manure use.

Why it applies, and its limits: It supplies a snow-country headman's house of Echizen: thick posts and a steep roof with high eaves for snow, a stable at the entrance with farm animals kept in the sunny part of the house, an earth floor for hulling and straw work, about ten male and female servants sleeping on mezzanines, a 12.5-mat main room, and separate guest and family toilets with the family one sited to draw off manure. The page itself calls it the largest in the prefecture, so it is not a typical house, and it is a museum's account of a moved and restored building that gives no dimensions of the house or its yard.
