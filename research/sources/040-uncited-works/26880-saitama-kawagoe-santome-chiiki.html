saitama-kawagoe-santome-chiiki

Saitama Prefecture, Kawagoe Agriculture and Forestry Promotion Center, "300年の歴史を誇る「三富地域」" ("The Santome area, proud of 300 years of history", title translated), on the Saitama Prefecture website (in Japanese; https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/b0902/santome/santome-history.html)

What it is: A prefectural office page on the Santome area of Saitama, centered on the Santome new fields (Kamitomi, Nakatomi, Shimotomi) opened in 1694-1696 and spanning five cities and towns over about 3,200 ha. It gives the area's land use, the planned strip allotment, and the later efforts to keep its woods.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the new-field lots were laid out as strips of house plot, field and flat woodland, 40 ken (72 m) wide and 375 ken (675 m) deep and 5 chō in area, and that the woodland supplied firewood, leaf-mold compost and house timber. It describes an upland dry-field colony of the Edo period seen through present-day preservation, so it gives no paddy, and its counts (farmland half, woodland a fifth) are today's, not those of 1700.
