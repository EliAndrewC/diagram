zh.wikipedia 棚樓 ("penglou", the wall shed, title translated; the address 敌楼 redirects to it) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/敌楼)

What it is: A very short Chinese Wikipedia article on the timber shelter built on a city wall, a wall tower or a bastion to cover the defenders, naming the shed on a bastion, on a barbican and on a corner.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the wall-top shed by position (the enemy tower on a bastion, the fighting shed on a barbican, the corner tower on a corner of the wall) and by its materials of timber posts, boards, lime plaster and wet felt, citing the Wujing zongyao and compares it to the European hoarding. It gives no size, no spacing along the wall and no date for any example, so it settles what the parts are called and not how many a wall carried.
