gifu-kanko-takayama-jinya

Gifu Prefecture tourism site, "高山陣屋" ("Takayama jinya", Takayama magistrate's office, title translated), on the official Gifu travel guide (in Japanese; https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1242.html)

What it is: A tourism-site entry on the Takayama jinya in Hida, the Edo shogunate's regional office, with a visitor guide to its rooms and storehouses.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says Hida was a shogunal holding rich in forest, raised to a gundai (district governor's) seat in 1777, and that the jinya is the only gundai or daikan office whose buildings survive almost whole of more than sixty at the end of the shogunate. It lists the office hall, entrance hall (with entries by rank), the local daikan's office, interrogation room, great hall, kitchen, residence rooms, garden and the rice storehouse, called among the oldest and largest surviving, and counts 40 rooms. It gives no dimensions or plan, and it does not say how much of the present building is original.
