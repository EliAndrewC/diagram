geng-ju-zhwiki

zh.wikipedia 耿橘 ("Geng Ju", title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%80%BF%E6%A9%98)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia biography of Geng Ju (born 1571), a Ming magistrate of Changshu county in the Wanli reign, who dredged the county's rivers and wrote a twelve-chapter book on its water control.

Why it applies, and its limits: It credits Geng Ju as magistrate of Changshu from 1604 to 1608 with dredging 214 rivers and writing the Changshu County Water Conservancy Book, and lists the steps of its method - feeding the laborers, assigning work along a line, setting out the earth heaps, building the banks, installing sluices - for a county where the north was dry and the south low. The article only names the steps and never describes them, so it points to a primary source and a magistrate's role in drainage works but gives no figure for a channel, bank or sluice.
