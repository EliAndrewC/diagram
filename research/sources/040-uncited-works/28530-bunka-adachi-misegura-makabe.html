bunka-adachi-misegura-makabe

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "安達家住宅見世蔵及び主屋" (the shop storehouse and main house of the Adachi house, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/245828)

What it is: A database entry for the earthen-walled shop (misegura) and main house of a merchant house in Makabe, Sakuragawa, Ibaraki, a registered tangible cultural property dated 1830-1868, two stories, 135 square meters. The note describes the layout of the shop front and living rooms and the heavily plastered facade.

Why it applies, and its limits: It describes the form of a late Edo townhouse of a small town in the Kanto plain: a five-bay shop on the street with the main house at right angles to it, both two-story and earthen walled, a dirt floor on the east and a ledger room and living room on the west, and a low story height. The note speaks of the house as showing this region's townhouses at the end of the Edo period; it gives no plot size and the 135 square meters is the building area only.
