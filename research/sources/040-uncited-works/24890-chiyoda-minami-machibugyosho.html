chiyoda-minami-machibugyosho

Chiyoda City Tourism Association, "南町奉行所跡" ("Site of the Minami (South) Town Magistrate's Office", title translated), spot guide on the Visit Chiyoda site (in Japanese; https://visit-chiyoda.tokyo/app/spot/detail/637)

What it is: A four-sentence spot guide to the site of Edo's South Town Magistrate's Office, now the plaza before Yurakucho station in Chiyoda, Tokyo, with its address. It says the office handled administration, justice and fire-fighting for Edo's townsfolk.

Why it applies, and its limits: It names the three duties of the town magistrate's office (administration, trials and fire-fighting) in one line, and says excavation found drains and wells on the site, with a stone wall and a cellar restored on view. It gives no dates, sizes or plan of the office, and does not say where the wells and drains lay within it, so it is a pointer to what such an office held, not a layout.
