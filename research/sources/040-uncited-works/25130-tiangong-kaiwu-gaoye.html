tiangong-kaiwu-gaoye

"《天工開物》:膏液第十二卷" (Tiangong kaiwu, volume 12, "Oils and fats", title translated), a transcription of the classical text on the Digital Classics site (數位經典) (in Chinese; https://www.chineseclassic.com/content/1266)

What it is: A proofread online transcription of the twelfth volume of the Tiangong kaiwu, a Chinese technical treatise, on oils and fats: which seeds are pressed for food, lamps and candles, the oil each yields per picul, and how the press and the mill are built and worked.

Why it applies, and its limits: It carries oil yields (for example 30 jin per shi of rape seed, 27 of radish seed, 9 of soybean), the dimensions of the wedge-press trough (three or four feet long and three or four inches wide), a stone ox-mill that does the work of ten men, and the use of the soybean cake as pig feed, so it can anchor a village oil-press and its inputs. It is a Chinese text, speaking of Jiangnan, Shaanxi, Beijing and Korea as places, not Japan; the transcription gives no date, and it is a general account of methods rather than a count of presses in any place.
