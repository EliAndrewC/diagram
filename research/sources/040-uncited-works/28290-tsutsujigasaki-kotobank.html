tsutsujigasaki-kotobank

Britannica International Encyclopedia, small-item edition (ブリタニカ国際大百科事典 小項目事典), "躑躅ヶ崎" ("Tsutsujigasaki", the Takeda residence site, title translated), with excerpts from the old Heibonsha World Encyclopedia (世界大百科事典), on Kotobank (the page saved under this address is the Tsutsujigasaki entry) (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B8%AD%E9%96%80-99316)

What it is: A short encyclopedia entry, reproduced on Kotobank, on Tsutsujigasaki in Kofu, the site of the residence of three generations of the Takeda of Kai, from 1519 until 1581. It adds two excerpts from the old Heibonsha encyclopedia on Kofu's founding there.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says that the residence there was a narrow, plain one with a single moat, and that Takeda Shingen did not build a great castle, quoting his verse "people are the castle". It gives no dimensions and no layout, and the address given here ends in the word for "inner gate" while the saved page is this entry, so it carries no word on gates.
