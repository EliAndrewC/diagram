iwakura-saobakari

Iwakura City, "棹秤[サオバカリ]" ("saobakari", the beam balance, title translated), in the city's folk-materials database (民俗資料データベース) (in Japanese; https://www.city.iwakura.aichi.jp/bunkazai_db/0000000484.html)

What it is: A municipal database record for one item in Iwakura City's folk-material collection (Aichi), a beam balance dated before Meiji 2 (1869), stamped with the name 石町十助, with its length, the way it was read and the loads it weighed (12 to 25 kan).

Why it applies, and its limits: It shows how a pre-Meiji merchant weighed heavy goods: the object hung from a hook, a weight on the other arm, the scale read where the beam hung level. It records a single surviving object with its dimensions, not how common such scales were, what trade used it or where it was used, and its range of 12 to 25 kan is the page's own figure with no stated basis.
