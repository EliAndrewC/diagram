Miyagi Prefecture Association of Shinto Shrines (宮城県神社庁), "日枝神社（ひえじんじゃ）" ("Hie Jinja", Hie Shrine, title translated), in "宮城県の神社を探す" (Find a Shrine in Miyagi Prefecture), shrine search (in Japanese; https://miyagi-jinjacho.or.jp/jinja-search/detail.php?code=310020507)

What it is: The prefectural shrine association's database entry for Hie Shrine at Tajiri-Onuki, Osaki City, Miyagi, giving its deity, annual festival date and a history of the shrine from its founding to its rebuilding in 1941.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a worked history for one rural shrine in Tohoku: founded about 680 as the guardian of a temple on Kagobo mountain, a mountain deity worshipped as protector of mountain and water works and of farming, the grave of the first lord of Wakuya in 1594 marked by five planted cedars (blown down in storms in early Taisho, with replacement trees planted since), donations of armor, swords and fields by later lords, ranking as a village shrine in 1872, a gift of 2 cho of mountain forest in 1908 for upkeep, and a rebuild in 1941. The 680 founding is the shrine's own tradition and carries no source, and the page gives no precinct size, layout or grove.
