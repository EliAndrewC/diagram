tokyo-jinjacho-nakameguro-chozubachi

Tokyo Association of Shrines, "手水鉢" ("The hand-washing basin", title translated), in ご社宝めぐり, on the page for Nakameguro Hachiman Shrine, contributed March 2023 (in Japanese; http://www.tokyo-jinjacho.or.jp/goshahou/chouzubachi/)

What it is: A page on a stone hand-washing basin dated 1827 at Nakameguro Hachiman Shrine in Meguro, Tokyo, with a short account of the shrine's standing in Edo-period records.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says village registers of 1763 and 1845 and the gazetteer Shinpen Musashi fudokiko list the shrine as the whole village's tutelary shrine, with a festival on the eighteenth of the ninth month at which twelve-part kagura was played. It is one village near Edo, the shrine's founding is unknown because fires burned its records, and the present building dates from 1936.
