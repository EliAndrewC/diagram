ja.wikipedia レンコン ("lotus root", title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%AC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B3%E3%83%B3)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia article on lotus root, the edible rhizome of the lotus, covering its appearance, history in Japan, varieties, cultivation in mud fields, harvest statistics, and cooking.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says lotus root is grown in marshes and in paddy-like lotus fields, planted in late spring and harvested from autumn into the next spring, and that the lotus reached Japan by the Nara period and was brought from China and Korea with Buddhism. It also names the pressurized-water harvester and the prefectures that now lead production, which are present-day figures. It does not say how large a premodern lotus pond was, and it notes that the varieties sold today are mostly Meiji-era or later imports from China.
