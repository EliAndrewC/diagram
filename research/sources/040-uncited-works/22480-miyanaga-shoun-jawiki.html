ja.wikipedia 宮永正運 ("Miyanaga Shoun", the Edo-period Etchu farmer-scholar, title translated) (in Japanese; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/宮永正運)

What it is: The Japanese Wikipedia biography of Miyanaga Shoun (1732-1803), the fifth-generation head of a model-farmer family and agricultural writer of Shimokawasaki village, Tonami district, Etchu, who served the Kaga domain as a mountain-patrol and local-products official, with notes on his farming manual, famine-food book and travel writing.

Why it applies, and its limits: It places an Edo-period farming manual, the 1789 Shika nogyodan, in the Tonami plain of Toyama and says it covers rice, cotton, mulberry, tea and vegetables and was written from his own practice there, which makes the author a witness to Etchu agriculture in the late 1700s. It also quotes him on Gokayama, where, lacking paddies, people ate millet porridge from mountain fields. The article is a short biography that gives no figures from the manual, so it points to the work but does not carry its contents, and the page itself notes it lacks sources.
