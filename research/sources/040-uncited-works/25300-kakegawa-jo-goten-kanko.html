kakegawa-jo-goten-kanko

Kakegawa City, "掛川城御殿" ("The palace of Kakegawa Castle", title translated), on the city's tourism site (in Japanese; https://www.city.kakegawa.shizuoka.jp/kanko/spot-list/kakegawajyogoten.html)

What it is: A city tourism page on the lord's residence (goten) of Kakegawa Castle, a shoin-style building of tatami rooms divided by sliding doors, rebuilt from 1855 to 1861 after the 1854 earthquake, with its later uses and its designation as an Important Cultural Property.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says what the goten was used for - the lord's residence, the domain office and the place of official ceremonies - and that it first stood in the main bailey before moving to the second bailey. It also lists its reuse after 1869 as a school, town office and fire station, which shows the building was kept in use. It describes one castle's palace as rebuilt in 1855-1861 and gives no room sizes or floor plan.
