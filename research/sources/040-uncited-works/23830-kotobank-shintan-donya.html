kotobank-shintan-donya

"薪炭問屋" ("shintan donya", the firewood and charcoal wholesaler, title translated), in the old Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%96%AA%E7%82%AD%E5%95%8F%E5%B1%8B-1344218)

What it is: A page that carries only a short extract from the encyclopedia's article on fuel, covering the Edo-period firewood and charcoal wholesaler.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says firewood and charcoal were bought and sold from the Muromachi period and that the trade split into wholesale and retail in the early modern period, which dates the trade. It is two sentences from another article, with no wholesaler's location, size or customers, so it supports the existence and rough date of the trade only.
