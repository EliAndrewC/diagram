bunka-kamigamo-shakemachi

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "京都市上賀茂（社家町 京都）" ("Kyotoshi Kamigamo (shakemachi Kyoto)", Kamigamo in Kyoto City, the Kyoto shake town, title translated), on Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/46988)

What it is: A Cultural Heritage Online record of Kamigamo, the shrine-gate settlement east of Kamigamo Shrine in northern Kyoto, a row of priests' houses (shake) along the Myojin River.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the settlement grew from the Muromachi period, that the priests' houses stand along the river, each main house set back on its lot with a gable-entered, single-story, gabled form, with an earthen wall, garden, gate and an earthen bridge over the river. It is a short description of the townscape as it survives, without dates for the houses themselves or any measurements.
