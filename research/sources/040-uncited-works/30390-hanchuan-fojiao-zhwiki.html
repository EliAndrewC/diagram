zh.wikipedia 汉传佛教 ("Han-tradition Buddhism", title translated), the article the address 宋代佛教 ("Song-dynasty Buddhism") redirects to (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/宋代佛教)

What it is: The long Chinese Wikipedia article on Chinese-tradition Buddhism from its arrival in Han China through the present, with sections on each dynasty, on Japan, Korea and Vietnam, the schools, and monastic rules and offices; the Song address opens it as a redirect.

Why it applies, and its limits: The Song material is a few paragraphs: a central office for monks under the Court of State Ceremonial, the ordination certificate system that from the reign of Shenzong was sold to raise revenue, the printing of the Kaibao Tripitaka, and the decline of all schools but Chan and Pure Land under the Southern Song. It gives no count of Song temples or monks, and it carries a banner saying it leans too heavily on primary sources and needs editing, so it only outlines the period.
