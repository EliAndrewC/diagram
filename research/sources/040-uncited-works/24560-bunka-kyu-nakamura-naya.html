bunka-kyu-nakamura-naya

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "旧中村家住宅納屋" ("kyu Nakamurake jutaku naya", the barn of the former Nakamura residence, title translated), on Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/572532)

What it is: A Cultural Heritage Online record of the barn of the former Nakamura residence in Koshigaya, Saitama, dated 1888, registered in 2021, with its floor area and a short description.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the barn's plan: 23 square meters, a single story northeast of the main house and facing south, its east half an earthen-floored miso room and its west half a plank-floored tool room with a closet. It records one small Meiji outbuilding, not a typical farm's full set of buildings, and says nothing of what the barn replaced.
