kanazawa-teramachidai

Kanazawa City Cultural Properties Protection Division, "寺町台伝統的建造物群保存地区" ("Teramachidai Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings", title translated) (in Japanese; https://www4.city.kanazawa.lg.jp/soshikikarasagasu/bunkazaihogoka/gyomuannai/3/1/1/siteibunkazai/7/5810.html)

What it is: A city page describing the Teramachidai district of Kanazawa, a temple quarter selected in 2012 as a nationally Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings, with its location, area and two temple-town layouts.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the district is about 22.0 ha, lies where the Hokkoku road brought travelers from Echizen into the castle town, and is made of two temple quarters: Noda Teramachi, with temples in an orderly row along straight streets on the old road to the Maeda family tombs, and Izumi Teramachi, with townhouses along the street front of temple grounds on the old road to Mount Hakusan. The page dates only the 2012 selection, not the layouts, and gives no count of temples or size of precincts, and it is a castle-town edge quarter, not a village.
