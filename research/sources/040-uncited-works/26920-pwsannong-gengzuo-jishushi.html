pwsannong-gengzuo-jishushi

"耕作技术史" ("History of tillage technique", title translated), in the encyclopedia of agricultural history on Zhihui Sannong, the agricultural knowledge service of China Agriculture Press (in Chinese; https://www.pwsannong.com/c/2016-04-13/560252.shtml)

What it is: An encyclopedia entry outlining four stages of Chinese soil tillage from the Zhou period to the Ming and Qing: the ridge-and-furrow method, ridge and flat tillage together, improved wet and dry tillage, and rotation tillage.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says paddy tillage in the south had by the Sui to Yuan era become a plow, harrow and leveler sequence, and that Ming and Qing farmers set ridge shape and sowing place by how high the land stood, sowing in the furrow on high dry land and on the ridge on low wet land. It spans some two thousand years of China and dates a practice only by its broad stage (Western Zhou to Warring States, Qin to the Northern and Southern Dynasties, Sui to Yuan, Ming and Qing), naming a region only now and then (the southern paddies, the northern dry land, Guanzhong), so it cannot date a practice closely. The page drops its linked terms, so the Ming-Qing ridge summary names no source work.
