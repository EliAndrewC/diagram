Tao Zhu Gong (attributed; Fan Li), "養魚經" ("Classic of Fish Raising", title translated), in the Yuhan shanfang congshu, on Chinese Wikisource (in Chinese; https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/%E9%A4%8A%E9%AD%9A%E7%B6%93)

What it is: The Wikisource text of a short fish-raising classic attributed to Tao Zhu Gong (Fan Li), with a preface by the editor Ma Guohan. It sets out a six-mu carp pond with nine islets and a second note on stocking a pond with lake-bottom soil.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a pond plan with a size and a stocking rule: six mu, nine islets and eight channels, water 2 chi deep over the islets and 6 chi in the channels, 20 pregnant and 4 male carp put in on the first geng day of the second month, and turtles added later "to guard". It also sets out a three-year yield in thousands of fish and a price per fish. The preface says the book is lost and rebuilt from quotations in the Qimin yaoshu (scroll 6), that a later copy added words by guess, and the attribution to Fan Li and the profit figures are the text's own idealized claims, not a measured pond.
