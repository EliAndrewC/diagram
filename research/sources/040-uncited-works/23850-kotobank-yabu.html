kotobank-yabu

"藪" ("yabu", thicket or bamboo grove, title translated), entries from the Digital Daijisen, Nihon Kokugo Daijiten and the Heibonsha World Encyclopedia, on Kotobank (in Japanese; https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%97%AA-551746)

What it is: A page of dictionary entries on yabu, an overgrown place of shrubs or bamboo, with a botanical article on thicket vegetation and a 1495 estate document quoted for a bamboo grove.

Why it applies, and its limits: The Nihon Kokugo Daijiten quotes a 1495 Toji document recording a five-tan yabu with a house lot at Kamikuze estate in Yamashiro, which shows a bamboo grove held and counted as land in the late Muromachi period. The botanical article is general (light reaches the ground, low productivity) and the rest of the entry is slang senses, so the page gives no grove yield, use or management.
