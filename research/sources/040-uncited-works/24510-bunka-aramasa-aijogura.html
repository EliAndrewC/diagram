bunka-aramasa-aijogura

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "新政酒造愛醸蔵" ("Aramasa brewery, Aijo storehouse", title translated), in Bunka Isan Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/230808)

What it is: A Bunka Isan Online record of a registered brewing storehouse of 1883-1897 in Akita: a plastered single-story building of 116 square meters, 16.2 by 7.2 meters, gabled, with doors in both ends.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the size and form of a Meiji-period brewery's core brewing storehouse, with plastered walls, a tile roof, a plaster band under the eaves finished in black plaster on its lower half and over the door lintels, and the brewery's mark on the gable wall. It is a late nineteenth-century building and says nothing of the brewing process or the storehouse's date within the 1883-1897 range.
