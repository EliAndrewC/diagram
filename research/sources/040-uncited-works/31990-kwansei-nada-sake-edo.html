kwansei-nada-sake-edo

Kwansei Gakuin University Library (関西学院大学図書館), "江戸時代の灘酒造業" ("The Nada sake-brewing industry in the Edo period", title translated), introduction to a digital exhibition of the Yunoki collection (in Japanese; https://library.kwansei.ac.jp/archives/nadashu/edo.html)

What it is: The introduction to a university library's digital exhibition on the sake brewers of the Nada coast between Nishinomiya and Kobe in the Edo period, and the 2,500 old documents of the collection.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Nada brewers grew in the eighteenth century as rural brewers shipping sake to Edo, after the shogunate's 1754 repeal of limits on brewing, and joined a brewers' guild in 1772, and credits the success to the spring water, rice, cedar casks, Tanba master brewers, water-powered rice polishing and a shipping port. It is a short introduction of one region's brewing, and it gives no output, brewery size or workforce figures.
