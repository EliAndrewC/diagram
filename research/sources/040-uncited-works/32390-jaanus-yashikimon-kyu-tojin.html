JAANUS (Japanese Architecture and Art Net Users System), "yashikimon 屋敷門", dictionary entry, Media Center for Art History, Columbia University (https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/node/186)

What it is: A short dictionary entry defining yashikimon as the front gate of a warrior's manor house, and describing one dated 1740, the former Chinese-quarter gate moved to Kofukuji in Nagasaki.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the one-line definition of a warrior manor's front gate, and the second sense describes a small 1x1-bay tiled gable gate on a raised earthen podium with three steps, so it fixes what the term names and shows one built example. That example is a Nagasaki Chinese-residence gate, not a samurai one, and the entry gives no size, count or rank rule for manor gates.
