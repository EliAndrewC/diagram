imidas-tenma-yaku

Yamamoto Hirofumi, "伝馬役（てんまやく）" ("Tenma-yaku, the post-horse duty", title translated), in the Jidaigeki yogo shinan, on imidas, 1 October 2009 (in Japanese; https://imidas.jp/jidaigeki/detail/L-57-191-08-04-G252.html)

What it is: A signed dictionary entry by a historian on the duty of a castle town or post station to supply porters and horses for official travel.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the early Edo requirement that each Tokaido station keep 100 people and 100 horses ready and each Nakasendo station 50 and 50, with official travelers carried free or at a set rate and the levy of nearby villages (sukego) when the standing stock ran short. The numbers are for the early period and two named highways only, and the page gives no costs for the standing stock or the later growth of the levy.
