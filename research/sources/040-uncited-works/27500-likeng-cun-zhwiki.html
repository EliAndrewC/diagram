zh.wikipedia 理坑村 (Likeng village, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/理坑村)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Likeng, a village of the Yu family in Tuochuan, Wuyuan County, Jiangxi (not the Likeng, 李坑, of Qiukou, which has its own entry), with its heritage listings and a table of its Ming and Qing buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It dates the village to the late Yuan or early Ming and says over fifty Ming and Qing buildings survive, naming halls, ancestral halls, a gatehouse, nine Ming bridges and one well dated to the Song, so it shows what a Huizhou lineage village kept. Its chief listed buildings are the houses and halls of the village's Ming and Qing officials, a Ming Minister of Personnel among them, so it shows a village rich in officeholders, not an ordinary one. It lists buildings by name and date only: no plan, spacing or measurement.
