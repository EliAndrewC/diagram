bunka-senshoji-kuri-iwaki

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "専称寺 庫裏" (Senshoji temple: the kuri, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/124654)

What it is: A database entry for the thatched kuri of Senshoji, a Pure Land (Jodo) temple in eastern Iwaki, Fukushima, an Important Cultural Property designated in 2004 and dated to about 1700 (rebuilt in 1690). It gives the building's size (18.2 m by 10.3 m, one story with a partial second story) and a note on the temple's history and layout.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the temple was founded in 1395, lost most of its halls to fire in 1668 and was rebuilt, with the main compound near the top of a low hill behind a front gate facing east, and that it was the center of the Jodo sect in the Tohoku region and a leading training temple, with the sizes of its main hall and kuri showing that rank. It speaks for a major sect headquarters in Tohoku in the late 17th century, not for a village temple, and gives no figures for its monks or grounds.
