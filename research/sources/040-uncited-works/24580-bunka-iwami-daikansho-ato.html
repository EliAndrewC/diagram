bunka-iwami-daikansho-ato

Agency for Cultural Affairs, "代官所跡" ("daikansho ato", the site of the magistrate's office, title translated), on Cultural Heritage Online (文化遺産オンライン) (in Japanese; https://online.bunka.go.jp/special_content/component/92)

What it is: A Cultural Heritage Online entry, filed under World Heritage, on the site of the shogunate's magistrate's office (daikansho) at Omori in the Iwami Ginzan silver mine landscape, with its area and its surviving buildings.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the office governed the mine and some 150 villages around it from the 17th to the mid-19th century, covers 2,657 square meters, and keeps a tiled single-story front gate with gate row houses on either side, about 4 meters wide and 33 meters long overall, built in 1815 after the fire of 1800 and shown in an 1841 map. It is one shogunal magistrate's seat, tied to a silver-mining district, so its size need not be typical of such offices. Its main hall was demolished in 1879 and a district office was built at the site's center in 1902, so the page records only the gate and its row houses, not the hall's plan or how the grounds were laid out.
