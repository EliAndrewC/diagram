sengoku-oki-tama-bukeyashiki

Yamagata Okitama Tourism Council, "武家屋敷" ("bukeyashiki", samurai residences), page on the Sengoku kanko Yamagata site (in Japanese; https://sengoku.oki-tama.jp/?p=log&l=92615)

What it is: A short tourism page on the lower-samurai residences (harakata-shu) of Yonezawa, which stand in the southern districts of the city.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says the Uesugi placed lower samurai outside the castle town after moving from Aizu because the town could not hold every retainer, that they lived half as farmers and half as warriors and opened wasteland, and that a stone wall kept out Matsukawa floods while an ukogi (うこぎ) hedge kept out intruders, so it ties a lower-samurai house to a hedge and a flood wall. The page credits both the wall and the hedge to Naoe Kanetsugu 400 years ago, a date it gives no evidence for, and its posts are dated 2007.
