ochiaijuku-kosatsuba

Ochiaijuku homepage (落合宿 ホームページ), "高札場を復元しました" ("kosatsuba o fukugen shimashita", The notice-board site has been restored, title translated), by the Ochiai Machizukuri Promotion Council (in Japanese; https://ochiaijuku.com/5647)

What it is: A short post on a local community site announcing the restoration of the kosatsuba, the notice-board site, of the Ochiai post station. It explains what a kosatsu was and names two boards of the station.

Why it applies, and its limits: It says a kosatsu was a board carrying a law, used from ancient times to the early Meiji period, that the shogunate and the domains made it a national system, that important notices were posted on such wooden boards for travelers to read, and that at a post station the site was also the reference point for measuring the distances between stations. It is a few sentences and gives no size, no position within the town and no date for the original, and what stands now is a restoration.
