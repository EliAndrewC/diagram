National Tax Agency, National Tax College, "3. 年貢の課税・納入（その１）" ("3. Assessment and payment of the land tax, part 1", title translated), in its 2000 special exhibition on tax history (in Japanese; https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/sozei/tokubetsu/h12shiryoukan/03.htm)

What it is: A page of the National Tax College's 2000 exhibition on how Edo-period land tax was assessed and paid: the assessment notice, the village's per-farmer apportionment register, the two methods of assessment (annual crop inspection and fixed rate) and the village-contract collection, with a Kaga-domain farming book showing the harvest and the rice carried to the lord's granary.

Why it applies, and its limits: It states that the village collected the tax, paid it in one or several installments, received a receipt from the lord's officer, and bore the cost of collecting and of carrying the rice to a nearby town or port. Its farm-year scenes come from Kaga's castle-town countryside, around Kanazawa, so they speak for one domain's villages, and the page gives no rates or quantities.
