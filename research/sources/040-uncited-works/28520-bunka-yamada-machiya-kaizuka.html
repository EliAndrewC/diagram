bunka-yamada-machiya-kaizuka

Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁), "山田家住宅主屋" (the main house of the Yamada house, title translated), in the Agency's cultural heritage database (文化遺産データベース) at bunka.nii.ac.jp (in Japanese; https://bunka.nii.ac.jp/heritages/detail/183183)

What it is: A database entry for a townhouse (machiya) in Kaizuka, Osaka, a registered tangible cultural property dated 1830-1867, one story with a tiled roof and a floor area of 183 square meters. The note describes it as standing on the north side of the street north of the temple Gansenji, filling its plot of 8 ken frontage and 12 ken depth.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a measured plot of a late Edo townhouse in a temple town of the Osaka plain, with its gabled roof and a second-floor wall now covered in copper sheet that was earlier tiled and plastered. It is one house, and the entry says nothing of the household's trade or of how common such a plot was in the town. Its own figures do not agree: it lists the house as single-story yet describes a second-floor wall, and its building area of 183 square meters is well short of the 8-by-12-ken plot (96 tsubo, about 317 square meters) it says the house fills.
