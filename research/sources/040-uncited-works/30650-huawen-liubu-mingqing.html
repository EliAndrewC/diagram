huawen-liubu-mingqing

"明清两代六部的职能" ("The functions of the Six Ministries in the Ming and Qing", title translated), in the Chinese History section of the teaching materials of 中国华文教育网 (China Huawen Education Net) (in Chinese; https://hwjyw.com/hwjc/171.html)

What it is: A teaching-material page of seven short paragraphs listing what each of the Six Ministries (Personnel, Revenue, Rites, War, Punishments, Works) managed in the Ming and Qing, with a modern ministry each resembles, and naming the minister (shangshu) and vice-minister (shilang). It names no author.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives a one-line scope for each ministry, such as Revenue handling household registers, field acreage, currency, taxes and official salaries, and Works handling water works and major building projects, which fits a quick check on which central office would deal with a county's land, taxes or construction. It treats the Ming and Qing as one, gives no dates or sources, and explains every ministry by a modern government department, so it cannot show how the ministries differed between dynasties or how they reached a county.
