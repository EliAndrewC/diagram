zh.wikipedia 左安门 ("Zuoanmen", the left-peace gate of Beijing's outer city, title translated) (in Chinese; https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/左安门)

What it is: The Chinese Wikipedia article on Zuoanmen, one of the seven gates of Beijing's Ming-Qing outer city, built in 1553, with the dimensions of its gate tower, barbican and arrow tower and its surviving guard house.

Why it applies, and its limits: It gives the gate's dimensions: gate tower three bays wide (16 m) and one deep (9 m), 15 m high with its platform; a semicircular barbican 29 m by 23 m; an arrow tower 13 m by 6 m; and a surviving five-bay guard house of 148.35 square meters, once the garrison's quarters. All but the guard house was torn down between the 1930s and 1953, so the dimensions rest on a 2012 magazine article and not on a measurement, and it is a capital's outer-city gate, not a county gate.
